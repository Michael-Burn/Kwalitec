## 3. Why this is the standard all future packages must match

Future authors and certifiers should treat the following properties as **non-negotiable norms**, not aspirational extras.

### Norm G1 — Tutor Intent before copy

Every Mission has a Mission-unique Tutor Intent; every Session has a Session-unique Tutor Purpose that extends it. Interchangeable coaching notes are automatic fails.

### Norm G2 — Continuity is structural

Yesterday (4.1) → Today (GLM structure) → Tomorrow (5.1) is a skill story, not unlock theatre. Gate TP light-prep discipline is part of premium.

### Norm G3 — Guidance multiplies CMP

Open / stop / out-of-scope / exit packet / sparse pauses / re-entry to retrieval. Kwalitec never becomes a second textbook; reading completion never becomes mastery.

### Norm G4 — Cognition after exposure

At least one serious Active Recall / Practice / Checkpoint after reading, with feedback and lawful advance. Reflection does not substitute for retrieval.

### Norm G5 — Honesty of progress language

Study Progress and Session complete are spoken clearly. Topic Complete and Estimated Mastery are not awarded by vibes.

### Norm G6 — Exam seriousness without panic

Success checks target examiner-real moves (here: justify a link) without engagement bait or software-default excuses.

### Norm G7 — Joint certification

Mission and Session certify together. A beautiful brief over empty stages — or a polished Session contradicting its Mission — cannot be Golden.

### Norm G8 — Revision trail required

Premium includes surviving reject-and-revise. Golden designation records that Critical defects were fixed, not that the first draft was perfect.
