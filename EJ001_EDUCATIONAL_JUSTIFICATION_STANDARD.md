# EJ-001 — Educational Justification Standard

**Programme:** Educational Production Enhancement EJ-001 — Educational Justification Standard  
**Status:** Binding — mandatory production artefact for every authored Educational Mission  
**Effective:** 2026-08-01  
**Authority:** Educational Excellence Framework (Frozen) · Educational Operations (Frozen) · TV-001 PASS  
**Nature:** Editorial production law only — **no** redesign of Educational Excellence, Educational Operations, Runtime A/C, SCI, Student Digital Twin, or recommendations; **no** application code; **no** curriculum redesign; **no** student-facing surface change  

**Companion deliverables:** `EJ001_MISSION_JUSTIFICATION_TEMPLATE.md` · `EJ001_AUTHORING_GUIDELINES.md` · `EJ001_IMPLEMENTATION_REPORT.md`

---

## 1. Purpose

Introduce **Educational Justification** as a mandatory production artefact for every authored Educational Mission.

The goal is **not** to explain the Mission to the student.

The goal is to ensure that **Kwalitec itself can always justify why the Mission exists exactly as authored** — so every educational decision can be defended, reviewed, audited, and improved.

> **Nothing educational is published without a documented educational rationale.**  
> **Every Mission must be traceable to a defendable justification before Publication Approval.**

---

## 2. What Educational Justification is

### 2.1 Definition

An **Educational Justification** is an editorial artefact that records the evidence-based educational rationale for an authored Mission’s design decisions: placement, workload, CMP reading, revision timing, stopping point, tomorrow continuity, applied educational science, syllabus/CMP support, misconception prevention, and expected session outcome.

### 2.2 What it is not

| Not this | Why |
|----------|-----|
| Student-facing Mission copy | Students receive Tutor Voice briefs under EA-001/EA-003; Justification is internal |
| A redesign of Excellence or Operations | Frozen law remains superior; EJ-001 strengthens production discipline under it |
| Runtime / Twin / SCI / Recommendation logic | No automatic surfacing; no planning math; no recommendation redesign |
| A syllabus rewrite | Topic identity and CMP truth remain curriculum/CMP authority |
| Marketing or inventory narrative | Coverage metrics and campaign volume do not substitute for educational rationale |

### 2.3 Audience

| Audience | Use |
|----------|-----|
| Educational Authors | Capture design rationale before review |
| Educational / Tutor Reviewers | Audit decisions objectively against stated intent |
| Quality Gate / Publication Approvers | Block publication when rationale is missing or indefensible |
| Founder / Educational Trust Validation | Corroborate T1–T7 expert-defence with authored evidence |
| Future Subject Leads | Apply subject-specific strategies while remaining explainable |

Students are **not** an audience for this artefact under Version 1.

## 4. Mandatory relationship to Mission publication

### 4.1 Hard rule

> **A Mission may not receive Publication Approval without a complete Educational Justification pack for that Mission.**

This rule is **editorial / operational**. It does not amend Gate MG criteria text, Mission schema fields, or Runtime behaviour. It adds a required dossier artefact in the publishing spine.

### 4.2 Lifecycle placement

```text
INPUTS
  → AUTHORING (Mission pack under EA-003)
    → EDUCATIONAL JUSTIFICATION (this standard — editorial dossier)
      → EDUCATIONAL REVIEW / TUTOR REVIEW / CERTIFICATION (EA-002 / EA-003)
        → PUBLICATION APPROVAL (EO-001)
```

Justification is authored **with** the Mission (or immediately after the first complete draft) and must be current at Certification and Publication Approval.

### 4.3 Joint publication

EA-002 joint publication remains binding: Mission + linked Session + Episodes.  
EJ-001 adds: Mission Justification must be present for the Mission under review. Session/Episode justifications may be referenced or summarised inside the Mission Justification when decisions are shared; they are not a separate EJ-001 class in this programme.

### 4.4 Status values

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| `draft` | Author working; incomplete fields allowed |
| `complete` | All J1–J10 present; ready for Educational Review |
| `review_pass` | Educational Reviewer accepts rationale quality |
| `review_fail` | Rationale incomplete, boilerplate, or educationally indefensible |
| `locked` | Publication Approval granted for this Mission version — Justification frozen with package version |
| `superseded` | Replaced by a later Mission / edition Justification |

## 5. Justification dimensions (J1–J10)

Every Mission Justification **must** answer all ten dimensions. Empty, “N/A” without reason, or reusable boilerplate fails.

| ID | Dimension | Governing question | Primary frozen law | Trust corroboration |
|----|-----------|--------------------|--------------------|---------------------|
| **J1** | Topic placement | Why does this topic appear at this point? | EA-001 M4; Constitution VI; EP-02 | TV-001 T1 |
| **J2** | Workload | Why is this workload educationally appropriate? | EA-001 Session Philosophy; EP-03; load fields | TV-001 T2 |
| **J3** | CMP reading selection | Why was this CMP reading selected? | EP-01; EP-04; Guidance-Over-Content | TV-001 T3 |
| **J4** | Revision timing | Why is revision scheduled — or not — today? | Mode honesty; EP-05; Gate RV culture | TV-001 T4 |
| **J5** | Stopping point | Why is today’s stopping point correct? | EP-03; Guided Reading stop; wrap integrity | TV-001 T5 |
| **J6** | Tomorrow continuity | Why does tomorrow naturally follow from today? | EP-02; M10; Continuity Bundle | T7 pathway defence |
| **J7** | Educational science | Which educational science principles are intentionally applied? | EP-01–EP-10; deliberate practice / retrieval / load | TV-001 T6 |
| **J8** | Syllabus & CMP support | Which syllabus objectives and CMP sections does this Mission support? | Curriculum truth; CMP edition pin; U1 | Audit / coverage honesty |
| **J9** | Misconception prevention | Which common misconceptions is the Mission designed to prevent? | EA-003 `common_misconceptions`; EP-05/EP-07 | Teaching defensibility |
| **J10** | Expected outcome | What is the expected educational outcome by end of Session? | M6/M9; success criteria; Constitution VIII honesty | T7 + student benefit |

### 5.1 Completeness rule

All ten dimensions are **mandatory** for `complete` status. Subject strategies may differ in *how* they answer (e.g. heavier retrieval in revision subjects; denser worked examples in calculation-heavy topics), but none may omit a dimension.

### 5.2 Specificity rule

Each answer must be **Mission-unique** enough that two different Missions cannot share identical J1–J10 text. Reused “highest-value next step” / “builds exam readiness” boilerplate is a hard fail (aligns EA-001 P5 / TB-004 culture).

### 5.3 Evidence rule

Where a claim depends on prior learning, CMP edition, exam weight, Reflection residual, or mode, cite the concrete source (topic code, CMP locus, prior Mission ID, sitting cue). Opinion without locus fails Educational Review.

### 5.4 Honesty rule

Justification may not claim mastery from Session completion, invent prerequisites the student lacks without disclosure, or pretend revision is learning (or learning is revision). Align Constitution VIII and One Educational Truth (EP-10).

## 6. Relationship to student-facing Mission fields

Student-facing Mission fields (EA-003) and Educational Justification are **related but distinct**:

| Student-facing field (examples) | Justification role |
|---------------------------------|--------------------|
| `why_now`, `explainability` | Brief student-facing rationale; Justification holds the full defensive case |
| `tutor_intent` | Coaching move; Justification explains *why that move* is the right professional choice today |
| `cmp_reading_scope` | What the student opens/stops; Justification defends *why that locus* |
| `estimated_cognitive_load` / time | Stated load; Justification defends appropriateness |
| `revision_signals` / mode | Student-truth signals; Justification defends timing decision |
| `common_misconceptions` | Teaching targets; Justification records prevention design |
| `success_criteria` / `expected_benefit` | Student “done when…”; Justification states expected outcome with honesty limits |
| `prior_bridge` / `tomorrow_bridge` | Continuity prose; Justification defends pathway logic |

**Rule:** Student copy may be shorter. Justification may not contradict student-facing claims. If they diverge, fix the Mission or the Justification before certification.

**Rule:** Do not paste Justification prose onto Home, Mission hero, Session Overview, or any student surface as a substitute for Tutor Voice.

## 7. Subject flexibility (explainable strategy)

Different subjects may apply different educational strategies (e.g. proof-heavy vs calculation-heavy; early-chapter orientation vs late-chapter exam craft) **provided**:

1. J1–J10 remain complete and Mission-specific.  
2. Cited EP IDs in J7 are accurate for the design actually used.  
3. Mode (Learning / Revision) is honest.  
4. CMP and syllabus citations in J8 resolve to lawful nodes / pinned edition.  
5. An educational expert could defend the pathway using the Justification alone (TV-001 T7 spirit).

Subject variation is allowed. Unexplainable variation is not.

## 8. Review and audit

### 8.1 Educational Reviewer checks (Justification)

| ID | Criterion |
|----|-----------|
| EJ-R01 | All J1–J10 present and non-empty |
| EJ-R02 | Answers are Mission-unique (no cross-Mission boilerplate) |
| EJ-R03 | J1 coherent with mode, prior bridge, and lawful syllabus order |
| EJ-R04 | J2 load/time claims match Mission scope and Session design |
| EJ-R05 | J3 CMP open/stop/out-of-scope defended; Guidance-Over-Content respected |
| EJ-R06 | J4 revision-or-not decision explicit and mode-honest |
| EJ-R07 | J5 stop protects learning and tomorrow; no sprawl theatre |
| EJ-R08 | J6 tomorrow follows or honest horizon / cold-start absence stated |
| EJ-R09 | J7 cites concrete EP / science moves actually designed into the day |
| EJ-R10 | J8 syllabus objectives + CMP sections resolve and match pack |
| EJ-R11 | J9 misconceptions match Mission pack and Session design intent |
| EJ-R12 | J10 outcome assessable; no false mastery claim |
| EJ-R13 | No contradiction with EA-003 Mission pack student-facing fields |

A single EJ-R fail ⇒ Justification `review_fail` ⇒ Mission may not publish.

### 8.2 Audit uses

- Pre-publication certification evidence  
- Post-publication defect investigation (“why was this Mission authored this way?”)  
- Educational Trust Validation corroboration (T1–T7)  
- Edition / CMP bump impact analysis (which justifications depend on retired loci)

---

## 9. Versioning and maintenance

- Justification is versioned with the Mission / educational package version.  
- CMP edition bump, syllabus node change, Session redesign, or mode change triggers Justification revisit.  
- Errata that change educational decisions require Justification update before re-approval.  
- Cosmetic Tutor Voice polish that does not change J1–J10 decisions does not require rewrite — reviewer discretion, recorded.

---

## 10. Explicit non-goals (this programme)

EJ-001 does **not**:

- Redesign Educational Excellence, Educational Operations, Runtime A, Runtime C, SCI, Twin, or recommendations  
- Change application code, templates, or curriculum JSON  
- Auto-display Justification to students  
- Replace Gate MG, EA-003 schema, or EO-001 stages  
- Author live Missions or subject content  
- Declare Version 1 production-ready or validated KSI thresholds
