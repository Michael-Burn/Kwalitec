# PX-001 — Student Experience Audit

**Programme:** PX-001 Product Experience Elevation  
**Date:** 2026-07-31

---

## Goal

Students identify today’s task immediately. Nothing delays studying with essays, duplicates, or self-explaining chrome.

---

## Home

| Check | Result |
|-------|--------|
| Today’s task immediate? | Yes — greeting → Mission → primary CTA (UX-001) |
| Educational essays? | No |
| Subject duplicated in Progress? | **Fixed** — hidden when mission shows subject |
| Empty state guides? | Yes |

---

## Revision

| Check | Result |
