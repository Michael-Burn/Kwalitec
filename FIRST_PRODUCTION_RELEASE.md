## Why we exist

Kwalitec was created for people preparing for demanding professional examinations—actuarial, legal, medical, engineering, and the like. Their hardest problem is rarely a shortage of material. It is the daily question: *what should I study next?* Months of syllabus, limited hours, and an immovable exam date turn preparation into decision fatigue.

Our thesis is simple: **reduce decisions; increase learning.**

We are not a generic planner and not a black-box AI tutor. We exist to give students a trustworthy next step, grounded in official curriculum structure and their own evidence—and to give founders a disciplined way to turn real syllabuses into that experience.

## What we solve

- The fog of “what next” under long, high-stakes syllabuses  
- Plans that ignore readiness, time, and exam urgency  
- Recommendations that cannot be explained or reproduced  
- Curriculum that enters a product without quality gates  
- A gap between “we built engines” and “a student can actually study”

## Why 2.0.0-beta.1 is the first true product release

Earlier work built foundations, engines, and dogfood proofs. This version is different: it is **versioned, tagged, deployed to production**, and frozen for Private Beta.

For the first time, in one release:

- A Founder can create, certify, and publish curriculum into a live Student Runtime  
- Students meet a single Education OS—Home, Mission, Session, Journey, Tutor, Knowledge Map—on **certified** catalogue authority  
- The product runs at https://kwalitec.onrender.com as invite-only Private Beta  

That is product, not laboratory. The historical archive lives at `knowledge/archive/releases/v2_0_0_beta_1/`.

## Principles that must not be compromised

1. **Curriculum first** — official syllabus structure is the educational source of truth.  
2. **Explainable and deterministic cores** — planning, readiness, and recommendations must be reproducible from the same inputs; no opaque scores pretending to be wisdom.  
3. **Certification before students** — do not enrol learners into uncertified noise; publish gates exist for a reason.  
4. **Layered honesty** — HTTP in blueprints, domain rules in services, schema through Alembic; small scope over cleverness.  
5. **Student value over feature theatre** — ship what helps someone study tomorrow morning.  
6. **Preserve the past you claim to improve** — archive baselines; do not rewrite history to flatter the future.

## Lessons from the journey here

- A strong engine with a noisy published package is still a blocked product. Certification must stick through publish, not only in a review pack.  
- Educational quality is structural: thousands of spurious topics are not “more curriculum.” Syllabus-first coherence matters more than extraction volume.  
- Dogfood finds the truth that dashboards miss—Founder → Student end-to-end is the only proof that counts.  
- Premium UX does not require new intelligence; it requires answering “what next?” in seconds.  
- Empty-cohort honesty beats fabricated adoption. Private Beta starts when the platform is ready—and when you admit you have not yet earned the metrics.  
- Feature freeze is a gift: packaging a shippable identity is work, not an afterthought.
