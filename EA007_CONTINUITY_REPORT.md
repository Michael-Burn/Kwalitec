## 1. Executive continuity verdict

# FAIL

Educational continuity is **not** maintained across the audited campaign.

A student who follows Kwalitec every day would **not** still trust Kwalitec after Day 20.

---

## 2. What “continuity” means here

Continuity is not merely syllabus order. For EA-007 it means:

1. **Narrative** — yesterday → today → tomorrow feels tutor-authored  
2. **Pedagogical** — Session standard holds day after day  
3. **Voice** — one Study Sensei across weeks  
4. **Cognitive** — load and dependency track the syllabus honestly  
5. **Memory** — revision spacing protects earlier chapters  
6. **Trust** — quality does not spike once then vanish  

Structural topic sequencing without these layers is **artificial continuity**.

## 9. Conditions to reopen continuity PASS

All required:

1. Contiguous certified package arc covering a full 10–20 day campaign (not orphan nodes).  
2. Reciprocal prior_bridge / Tomorrow Preview pairs across every day boundary.  
3. Reading Guidance + topic-faithful Knowledge Checks on every Learning Mode day.  
4. Student-visible revision days spaced inside the campaign.  
5. Removal or quarantine of curriculum contaminants on the published path.  
6. Progress / confidence language aligned with sitting evidence (successor to EV-001 residuals).  
7. Re-audit under EA-007 method with mean dimension score ≥ 7 and no recurring LTB pattern families.

Until then: **large-scale publication must not proceed under a continuity PASS claim.**
