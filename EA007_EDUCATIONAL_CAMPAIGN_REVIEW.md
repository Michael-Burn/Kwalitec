## 4. Chapter-level campaign notes

### Chapter 1 — Data analysis (Days 1–2)

Entry is lawful. Cold-start Mission can be simple. Even here, Reading Guidance and assessable checks are missing. Board expects more than “read the material / note one idea” for a commercial primary system.

### Chapter 2 — Distributions (Days 3–8)

Longest continuous template stretch. Highest risk of **motivation and reflection fatigue**. Concept hinges (joint → expectation → MGF → CLT → sampling) are exactly where authored bridges should shine — and do not.

### Chapter 3 — Inference (Days 9–11)

Syllabus weight peaks early here (estimators, intervals, testing). Uniform Session shell under-serves exam-critical technique. Board flags **pacing and cognitive load** as severe.

### Chapter 4 — Regression (Days 12–13)

Day 12 (4.1) remains templated while Day 13 (4.2) is Golden. Asymmetric handoff (LTB-016): premium day assumes a finished classical-linear yesterday the product may not have delivered. Board treats orphan 4.2 publication as **pedagogically incomplete**.

### Chapter 5 — Bayesian (Day 14)

Receives Tomorrow Preview courtesy from 4.2 but opens without reciprocal bridge. Relapse day. Symbolically ends the first-pass spine on a template — unacceptable for continuity certification.
