## Lessons learned for student value

Student Intelligence value will not come from stacking more tips or declaring Twin-live prematurely. The roadmap privileges **trustworthy next-action quality (H1)** and **honest incompleteness** before Twin-first authority (H2) and outcome research (H4). That sequencing mirrors Vision AI philosophy and Product Constitution claim honesty: students benefit first from guidance they can understand and safely refuse, then from deeper models that remain provisional.
