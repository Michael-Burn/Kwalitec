## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not rewrite yesterday’s syllabus rule because today’s ticket closed, erase the history of a superseded teaching rule mid-pipeline, treat a product roadmap as educational law, or declare “evolution complete” because a stage board turned green. After the Constitution and EIP have defined *educational law*, after Programmes VI–VIII have published *meaning, orchestration, and runtime law*, after WS1–WS4 have defined *how implementations may be judged against that law*, and after WS5 / MS001 has defined *under what principles the corpus may lawfully evolve*, the platform still needs one lifecycle answer:

> **“How are constitutional evolution activities lawfully organised from proposal through constitutional disposition?”**

That answer must ensure evolution consumes **evolution governance principles**; evaluates **proposed constitutional changes**; coordinates **constitutional evolution activities**; produces **evolution dispositions**; and preserves **constitutional provenance** — without directly amending constitutional specifications, redefining constitutional meaning, bypassing constitutional authority, or governing implementation behaviour.

This document records that posture so every future constitutional evolution lifecycle (and successor) has a single constitutional reference for *how evolution work may be sequenced without becoming the amendment act, a second constitution, or an implementation bureau*.

> **The Constitutional Evolution Lifecycle Model describes how evolution activities are lawfully coordinated from proposal through constitutional disposition.  
> It does not create educational meaning, invent ownership, mint tips, invent runtime behaviour, directly amend specifications, redefine constitutional meaning, or become constitutional authority.**

## 2. What the Constitutional Evolution Lifecycle Is

The **constitutional evolution lifecycle** is the binding organisation by which **constitutional evolution activities** may proceed through recognised stages — consuming published evolution governance principles (CEG-01…CEG-07 and CEGO objectives), classifying proposals, assessing them under continuity / integrity / authority constraints, and yielding **evolution dispositions** and **historical provenance records** that preserve constitutional continuity, governance transparency, repeatability, and explainability — without becoming a silent amendment act, a rewrite of CEG types, an implementation governance bureau, a freeze of any stack as permanent constitutional authority, or a substitute for constitutional authorship.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Published constitutional corpus** | Constitution, EIP, Programme VI / VII / VIII / IX specifications that publish educational and execution law | Which artefacts may the lifecycle organise evolution against? |
| **Evolution governance principles** | Published WS5 / MS001 CEG types, CEGO objectives, boundaries, and explainability | Which governance principles does the lifecycle consume? |
| **Evolution proposal** | Named proposed change against identifiable published artefacts | What change is proposed? |
| **Evolution category** | A recognised CEG-01…CEG-07 change class from WS5 / MS001 | Which evolution category applies? |
| **Lifecycle stage** | A recognised CEL-01…CEL-07 organisational step | Which coordination step is active? |
| **Evolution disposition** | Lawful outcome: permitted / refused / deferred / escalated — never a silent rewrite of history or corpus text | May this evolution proceed under published principles? |
| **Historical provenance** | Reconstructable record of prior corpus identity, successor relationships, and preserved boundaries | Which historical relationships are preserved? |
| **Constitutional continuity** | Prior lawful meaning remains speakable after change; history is not erased for convenience | Does continuity survive? |
| **Constitutional authority** | The exclusive right of published corpora to define educational and execution meaning | Who owns the standard after the lifecycle? |
| **Repeatability** | Same published artefacts, same proposal, same CEG classification, same stage sequence → same disposition | Can the judgement be reproduced? |
| **Explainability** | Affected artefacts, category, stages, provenance, and boundaries are speakable | Can the lifecycle be narrated honestly? |

The constitutional evolution lifecycle is:

- **coordination-centred** — it organises evolution activities; it never silently enacts corpus rewrites under the label “lifecycle”;
- **governance-consuming** — it consumes WS5 / MS001 evolution governance principles; it never invents, rewrites, or replaces them;
- **proposal-bound** — it evaluates named proposals against published artefacts; it never evolves unpublished customs;
- **catalogue-closed** — only CEL-01…CEL-07 may be applied as constitutional evolution lifecycle stages;
- **type-faithful** — stages apply only published CEG-01…CEG-07 types from WS5 / MS001; they never invent types;
- **non-enacting** — dispositions coordinate lawful change readiness; they never themselves rewrite corpus text;
- **authority-preserving** — published corpora remain the sole source of constitutional meaning;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by lifecycle law;
- **corpus-bound** — every material lifecycle cites identifiable published artefacts;
- **provenance-preserving** — every material lifecycle leaves reconstructable historical trails;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which artefacts, category, stage, provenance, and boundaries applied;
- **non-legislative** — an evolution disposition never becomes constitutional law by proximity;
- **non-substitutive** — lifecycle machinery never becomes the constitutional standard for the next judgement;
- **non-mutating** — the lifecycle never directly amends constitutional specifications or redesigns implementations under coordination pretext.

The constitutional evolution lifecycle is **not**:

- a second Educational Constitution, EIP, Programme VI / VII / VIII / IX, WS1–WS4, or WS5 / MS001 amendment path;
- a licence to reinterpret coach meaning, ownership maps, tip warrants, runtime contracts, conformance criteria, traceability meaning, verification meaning, compliance meaning, certification types, or CEG types in lifecycle prose;
- a testing framework, CI/CD pipeline, GitHub Action, release train, or product roadmap authority;
- a claim that “lifecycle complete” guarantees learning, mastery, exam readiness, product success, better implementations, or frozen stack authority;
- a substitute for MS001 conformance evaluation — CEL coordinates evolution; CC defines when conformity may be claimed;
- a substitute for MS002 traceability — CT preserves relationships; CEL preserves them in provenance;
- a substitute for WS2 verification — CV evaluates evidence; CEL never performs verification;
- a substitute for WS3 compliance — CCM determines obligation status; CEL never determines compliance;
- a substitute for WS4 certification — CRT recognises satisfaction; CEL never certifies and never treats seals as evolution authority;
- a substitute for WS5 / MS001 evolution governance meaning — CEG defines evolution kinds; CEL sequences when governance activities occur;
- a substitute for Programme VIII evidence validation (EV-xx) — EV validates educational evidence eligibility; CEL coordinates *evolution organisation*;
- a substitute for Educational Validation Framework quality review — quality release remains a distinct horizon;
- a licence to modify implementation artefacts “so evolution can proceed.”

## 3. Lifecycle Posture (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material constitutional evolution lifecycle travels through seven constitutional stages. These are **normative coordination obligations**, not an implementation architecture and not a CI job graph.

```
CEL-01 Evolution Proposal
        │
        ▼
CEL-02 Proposal Classification
        │
        ▼
CEL-03 Constitutional Assessment
        │
        ├── refused / deferred / escalated (lawful decision — law unchanged; no authority freeze)
        │
        └── permitted (or other lawful disposition) →
                │
                ▼
CEL-04 Evolution Decision
        │
        ▼
CEL-05 Provenance Recording
        │
        ▼
CEL-06 Publication Preparation
        │
        ▼
CEL-07 Evolution Closure
        │
        ▼
Explain artefacts / category / stages / provenance / boundaries
```

| Stage | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **CEL-01 Evolution Proposal** | Open a named evolution concern against identifiable published artefacts | Subject is unpublished custom, tribal knowledge, or “how main works today” |
| **CEL-02 Proposal Classification** | Bind the proposal to published CEG-01…CEG-07 types | Classification would invent a type, privilege a stack as law, or soft-amend meaning in classification prose |
| **CEL-03 Constitutional Assessment** | Assess continuity, integrity, authority, and provenance constraints under published governance principles | Assessment would redefine meaning, erase history, bypass authority, redesign implementations as law, or replace WS1–WS4 |
| **CEL-04 Evolution Decision** | Emit permitted / refused / deferred / escalated under published criteria | Decisions would become silent amendments, educational quality grades, or substitutes for constitutional authority |
| **CEL-05 Provenance Recording** | Preserve audit-speakable trails of artefacts, category, stages, and dispositions | Path would erase history, forge provenance, or treat records as the corpus rewrite |
| **CEL-06 Publication Preparation** | Prepare honest publication of dispositions and provenance under published honesty rules | Publication would invent permission, hide refused history, rewrite boundaries, or freeze a stack as law |
| **CEL-07 Evolution Closure** | Close the lifecycle honestly when duties are exhausted | Closure would enact amendments, replace constitutional authority, govern implementation behaviour, or pretend incomplete work is done |

> **The lifecycle never “best-effort invents” an eighth stage called silent soft amendment, retrospective history rewrite, stack canonisation, implementation redesign, or corpus enactment under coordination colour.**

## 6. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Evolution Lifecycle Model exists so that:

1. **Law remains upstream of coordination** — stages organise evolution against published artefacts and governance principles; progress does not quietly become the specifications.
2. **Governance remains honest** — classification and assessment consume CEG principles; they do not invent types or soft-amend meaning in stage prose.
3. **Proposals remain honest** — the lifecycle evaluates named published artefacts; it does not evolve tribal knowledge so change can proceed.
4. **Evolution remains sequenced** — CEG dispositions crystallise under CEL-04 after lawful proposal, classification, and assessment — not as random rewrite theatre.
5. **Provenance remains intact** — prior versions and successor relationships remain reconstructable after every material lifecycle.
6. **Authority remains honest** — no team may treat a closed lifecycle as the new constitutional source or as the amendment act itself.
7. **Implementations remain replaceable** — lifecycle law does not freeze Flask, SQLAlchemy, a particular API shape, or a CI vendor as law.
8. **Repeatability remains honest** — the same published artefacts, proposal, CEG classification, and stage sequence yield the same disposition.
9. **Transparency remains intact** — governance decisions and stops are speakable, not hidden in product urgency.
10. **Explainability remains intact** — lifecycle speech describes coordination of evolution; it does not redefine what learning means.
11. **Students remain protected** — product theatre cannot launder silent soft amendment as “lifecycle evolved.”
