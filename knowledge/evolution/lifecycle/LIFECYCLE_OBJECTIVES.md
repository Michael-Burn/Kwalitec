## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “rewrite shipped,” “stages skipped for speed,” or “history cleaned.” The tutor optimises **honest organisation of constitutional evolution work**: only a continuity-preserving, provenance-preserving, transparent, repeatable, and explainable sequence — consuming published evolution governance principles — may support lawful evolution dispositions.

These objectives bind every constitutional evolution lifecycle and every successor coordination machinery that claims constitutional evolution lifecycle meaning.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### CELO-01 — Preserve constitutional continuity

**Definition.** Ensure that every material constitutional evolution lifecycle organises activities through recognised CEL-01…CEL-07 stages so that prior lawful constitutional meaning remains speakable and reconstructable after every disposition — consuming published CEG-01…CEG-07 classifications and governance principles — without erasing history, silently soft-amending corpora, or becoming constitutional authority.

**Includes:**

- Binding every material lifecycle to identifiable published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII / IX artefacts.
- Consuming published WS5 / MS001 evolution governance principles (CEG types, CEGO objectives, boundaries, explainability) as inputs.
- Applying only published CEG-01…CEG-07 types during CEL-02 / CEL-03.
- Preferring lawful refused / deferred / escalated / refuse-progress outcomes over rewriting law, inventing types, erasing history, redesigning implementations as law, or mutating artefacts so the lifecycle “completes.”
- Keeping lifecycle progress distinct from educational quality, mastery, readiness, product success, verification / compliance / certification theatre, or constitutional amendment by proximity.
- Preserving the Model’s posture: stages coordinate evolution; they do not enact amendments or become constitutional authority.

**Excludes:**

- Treating green CI, coverage percentages, linter scores, Twin estimates, Adaptive scores, UI ticks, certification seals, or product urgency as lifecycle fulfilment or amendment authority.
- Soft-amending specifications in stage prose, inventing unpublished customs, inventing lineage, erasing prior versions, mutating artefacts, or privileging “how main works” to force progress.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for continuity-preserving coordination.
- Declaring “close enough continuity” without documenting preserved historical relationships.
- Freezing an implementation as permanently lawful because the lifecycle closed.
- Treating an evolution disposition as the silent corpus rewrite.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software pretends yesterday’s educational rule never existed because today’s release needs a clean story. Continuity honesty is care; history erasure is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CELO-02 — Preserve historical provenance

**Definition.** Ensure that every material evolution lifecycle leaves reconstructable predecessor / successor / deprecation / archival / restoration / consolidation relationships — so students, developers, and auditors can say what the law *was*, what disposition was issued, and how historical identity was preserved.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later say *which prior rule* a change relates to cannot be trusted with educational law.

**Manifestations:**

- CEL-05 records bind artefacts, CEG categories, stages, and dispositions to historical identity.
- Missing provenance identity, missing artefact refs, missing stage-to-decision binding, or missing disposition identity is a defect.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing prior lawful evolution / lifecycle history to simplify the next report.
- Provenance records never themselves become the corpus rewrite.

---

### CELO-03 — Preserve governance transparency

**Definition.** Ensure that evolution coordination remains speakable: which proposal was opened, which CEG category was applied, which assessment constraints bound the decision, which disposition issued, and which stops occurred — so product urgency cannot hide governance behind opaque “trust us” evolution theatre.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who hides why a syllabus rule changed — or why change was refused — destroys institutional trust even if the rewrite looks tidy.

**Manifestations:**

- Refuse / defer / escalate outcomes are first-class and narratable.
- Stage skips and invented private “fast paths” are defects.
- Transparency never authorises inventing unpublished stages to “harmonise” product processes.
- Student / developer / auditor projections share one truth with different vocabulary.

---

### CELO-04 — Preserve repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that the same published constitutional artefacts, the same named proposal, the same published CEG classification, the same published governance constraints, and the same CEL stage sequence yield the same evolution disposition (permitted / refused / deferred / escalated) and the same lawful stage outcomes — absent a published non-deterministic exception (none are granted by this Model for constitutional meaning or evolution status).

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who treats the same proposal as permitted or refused depending on which reviewer woke first — or skips stages randomly — is not coaching; they are gambling.

**Manifestations:**

- Stage transitions and outcomes are corpus-bound and reproducible.
- Parallel CI runners, sharding, and cache races must not change constitutional disposition or invent stage skips.
- Explicit multi-type CEG sets at CEL-02 / CEL-03 use published conjunction / published stop rules, not random reviewer races.
- “Eventually consistent lifecycle meaning” is a constitutional defect.
- Invented or unstable proposal inputs, and invented classifications, are hard stops — not soft variables.

---

### CELO-05 — Preserve explainability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material evolution lifecycle can honestly answer the mandatory explainability questions in `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md` — so students, developers, and auditors hear the same constitutional truth in appropriate vocabulary.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who cannot say *which artefacts* were affected under *which category* at *which stage* that produced the disposition — and *which provenance* was preserved — is performing, not coordinating evolution.

**Manifestations:**

- Explainability obligations apply to all material lifecycles.
- Student / developer / auditor projections share one truth with different vocabulary.
- Lifecycle progress is never narrated as mastery, readiness, educational quality, silent soft amendment, or a freeze of stack authority.
- Stack preference and race-dependent outcomes are defects to disclose, not mysteries to hide.

---

### CELO-06 — Preserve implementation neutrality

**Definition.** Ensure that lifecycle coordination organises evolution against published artefacts independently of programming language, framework, datastore, CI vendor, cloud topology, or preferred Runtime A shape — so no stack becomes constitutional by proximity to lifecycle progress or closure.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who declares only one notebook brand “correct” has abandoned educational law for vendor preference.

**Manifestations:**

- CEL criteria speak to constitutional coordination obligations, not “must use Flask / SQLAlchemy / OpenAPI / GitHub Actions.”
- Equivalent lawful implementations under the same published law may both be subjects of lawful evolution lifecycles without becoming the law.
- Replacing a stack never itself creates or refuses evolution authority — and never freezes either stack as constitutional authority.
- “Our reference implementation does it this way” is never a substitute for a published specification or a published stage.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CELO-01 conflicts with “helping” incomplete history look lifecycle-complete | Continuity honesty wins — refused / deferred / escalated / refuse-progress |
| CELO-02 conflicts with log volume reduction that erases provenance | Provenance wins |
| CELO-03 conflicts with opaque “trust us” evolution theatre | Governance transparency wins |
| CELO-04 conflicts with race-dependent reviewer outcomes | Repeatability wins |
| CELO-05 conflicts with opaque stage narration | Explainability wins |
| CELO-06 conflicts with privileging a reference stack as law | Implementation neutrality wins |

No supporting objective authorises violating CELO-01.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — Constitution / EIP integrity obligations, Programme VI–VIII authority preservation, WS1–WS4 assessment honesty, and WS5 / MS001 evolution governance objectives for the lifecycle horizon.

---

## 5. Non-Objectives

The constitutional evolution lifecycle does **not** optimise for:

- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- CI throughput, flaky-test suppression, or coverage theatre as educational success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when artefacts, proposals, or classifications are incomplete;
- collapsing CEL stages into one mega-step that owns meaning or enacts amendments;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics signals as substitutes for published constitutional artefacts or CEG classifications;
- freezing implementations, releases, or stacks as permanently lawful;
- treating documentation as optional commentary on whatever rewrite already shipped;
- replacing MS001 conformance evaluation, MS002 lineage preservation, WS2 verification, WS3 compliance, WS4 certification, or WS5 / MS001 evolution governance meaning;
- directly amending constitutional specifications under lifecycle colour;
- governing implementation behaviour as if it were constitutional change.

---

## 6. Closing Statement

> **A lifecycle that becomes “complete” by rewriting constitutional law, erasing history, redefining meaning, bypassing authority, governing implementations, or replacing constitutional authority is a failed educational system.  
> A lifecycle that coordinates only published evolution — with continuity, provenance, transparency, repeatability, and explainability intact — is doing its only coordination job.**
