## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which constitutional artefacts* were affected — which evolution category applied, which lifecycle stage produced the decision, which historical provenance was preserved, and which constitutional boundaries remained intact — is not coaching; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–VIII have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, and runtime speech speakable, after WS1–WS4 have made conformance, lineage, verification, compliance, and certification speech speakable, and after WS5 / MS001 has made evolution-governance speech speakable, Programme IX still needs one evolution-lifecycle-speech answer:

> **“Which constitutional artefacts were affected, which evolution category was applied, which lifecycle stage produced the decision, which historical provenance was preserved, and which constitutional boundaries remained intact?”**

That answer must cover every material evolution lifecycle without inventing educational meaning, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, silently rewriting specifications in explanation prose, erasing history, freezing a stack as law, inventing unpublished CEG or CEL types, replacing WS1–WS4 evaluation, or presenting closure as educational quality.

---

## 2. What Lifecycle Explainability Is

**Constitutional Evolution Lifecycle Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **coordination of named evolution activities through published CEL stages** — consuming published evolution governance principles, applying published CEG types, and producing dispositions and provenance — not to invent a second tutor voice from rewrite machinery, not to grade the student, and not to replace constitutional authority.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Artefacts affected** | Named published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII / IX artefacts (and clauses as applicable) under evolution | Which constitutional artefacts were affected? |
| **Evolution category applied** | Named CEG-01…CEG-07 binding(s) | Which evolution category was applied? |
| **Lifecycle stage producing the decision** | Named CEL stage (especially CEL-03 / CEL-04) bound to each disposition | Which lifecycle stage produced the decision? |
| **Historical provenance preserved** | Named predecessor / successor / deprecation / archival / restoration / consolidation relationships and stage trails | Which historical provenance was preserved? |
| **Boundaries remaining intact** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden amendment / redefinition / authority substitution / implementation governance / history rewrite did not occur | Which constitutional boundaries remained intact? |

Lifecycle Explainability is:

- **coordination-centred** — it narrates artefacts → category → stages → dispositions → provenance under law;
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CEL and CEG identities;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published artefacts;
- **stage-honest** — every material disposition is bound to the producing lifecycle stage;
- **provenance-honest** — every material lifecycle cites which historical provenance was preserved;
- **non-legislative-by-prose** — explanation never becomes a back door to soft-amend corpora;
- **non-enacting** — dispositions and closure are never narrated as the corpus rewrite itself;
- **non-substitutive** — evolution coordination is never narrated as replacement of WS1–WS4 assessment;
- **quality-honest** — closure is never narrated as mastery, readiness, or educational quality;
- **authority-honest** — closure is never narrated as absorption of constitutional authorship by machinery or stacks;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence / validation speech, WS1–WS4 assessment speech, or WS5 / MS001 evolution-governance speech;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match CELO-02 / CELO-05 reconstruction needs;
- **continuity-aligned** — explanation components match EIP-005 / CELO-01 continuity needs;
- **repeatability-honest** — explanations must not paper over race-dependent dispositions or private stage shortcuts.

Lifecycle Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–VIII / WS1–WS4 / WS5 MS001 meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a substitute for CC findings, CT lineage speech, CV verification speech, CCM compliance speech, CRT certification speech, or CEG evolution-governance speech;
- a CI status page, coverage report, or linter summary presented as constitutional coordination;
- a freeze of implementations, stacks, vendors, or releases as constitutional law;
- a licence to narrate silent soft amendment, history erasure, or implementation redesign as if they were lawful lifecycle outcomes.

## 3. Mandatory Explainability Questions

Every material constitutional evolution lifecycle must be able to answer:

### CELEQ-01 — Which constitutional artefacts were affected?

**Required content**

- Named published artefact / clause identities under the evolution concern
- Binding between artefact identities and intended / applied CEG types
- Confirmation that unpublished customs, tribal knowledge, or “main as law” were not used as subjects

**Defect if**

- “The constitution evolved” with no artefact references
- Anonymous “the docs” or “the rules” without identifiable published artefacts
- Subjects invented or soft-amended in explanation prose without disclosure and hard stop

---

### CELEQ-02 — Which evolution category was applied?

**Required content**

- Named CEG-01…CEG-07 binding(s) from CEL-02
- Classification rationale under published type meanings
- Explicit refuse-classify disposition when no published type applied

**Defect if**

- Assessment or decision proceeds with “category assumed”
- Unpublished evolution customs invented mid-lifecycle to force CEL-03
- Silent redefinition of CEG type meaning in explanation prose

---

### CELEQ-03 — Which lifecycle stage produced the decision?

**Required content**

- Binding of each evolution disposition to the producing CEL stage (normally CEL-03 assessment crystallised at CEL-04)
- Honest recording of refuse-open / refuse-classify / refuse-prepare / refuse-close dispositions to the stages that produced them

**Defect if**

- Decisions appear without stage binding
- Closure or publication narrated as the producing stage for substantive disposition claims that actually arose earlier
- Silent stage skips when dispositions are claimed

---

### CELEQ-04 — Which historical provenance was preserved?

**Required content**

- Named provenance records produced at CEL-05
- Predecessor / successor / deprecation / archival / restoration / consolidation relationships as applicable
- Publication-preparation artefacts from CEL-06 where material (or refuse-prepare identity)
- Continuity sufficient to reconstruct artefacts, category, stages, dispositions, and boundaries

**Defect if**

- “Evolved” with no reconstructable provenance identity
- Records that drop stage-to-decision binding or artefact identity
- Silent erasure of prior lawful evolution / lifecycle history

---

### CELEQ-05 — Which constitutional boundaries remained intact?

**Required content**

- Explicit confirmation against `LIFECYCLE_BOUNDARIES.md` checks material to the concern (especially non-enactment, non-redefinition, non-substitution, non-implementation-governance, non-history-rewrite)
- Disclosure of any hard stop that prevented progress or closure

**Defect if**

- Boundaries are implied, not speakable
- Closure speech smuggles silent soft amendment, authority substitution, implementation redesign, or history erasure
- Artefact mutation or invented classification is hidden

## 4. Audience Projections

| Audience | Vocabulary emphasis | Same truth |
|----------|---------------------|------------|
| **Student-facing** | Plain speech that learning rules may change only under published care — not that the student mastered a topic or that the product is “evolved forever as law” | Artefacts / category / stage / provenance / boundaries remain reconstructable |
| **Developer-facing** | Corpus paths, CEG / CEL / CT identities, disposition codes, provenance identities | No private “trust the rewrite” meaning |
| **Auditor-facing** | Full reconstruction: CELEQ-01…CELEQ-05 answers, stage traces, boundary checks | Continuity of prior lawful records |

Projections differ in vocabulary. They must not differ in constitutional facts.

## 5. Forbidden Explainability Moves

| Forbidden narration | Why | Lawful alternative |
|---------------------|-----|--------------------|
| “Lifecycle closed ⇒ constitution amended” | Enacts amendment via closure | “Disposition recorded; corpora unchanged by lifecycle act” |
| “Lifecycle closed ⇒ meaning redefined” | Soft amendment via prose | “Assessed against published meaning; meaning unchanged by explanation” |
| “Lifecycle closed ⇒ new constitutional authority” | Authority substitution | “Coordination closed; published corpora remain the standard” |
| “Lifecycle closed ⇒ implementation redesign authorised” | Implementation governance | “Evolution coordinated; delivery remains delivery” |
| “Evolved ⇒ student ready / mastered” | Quality / mastery out of scope | Keep coordination speech distinct from educational claim classes |
| “Stage skipped because CI was green” | Unpublished stage customs | Apply CEL-01…CEL-07 or refuse |
| “We cleaned history so the story is simpler” | History rewrite | Preserve prior versions; refuse erasure theatre |
| “This stack is now the standard” | Authority substitution / neutrality breach | Keep implementation as subject |
| “Decisions update the constitution” | Legislation via explanation | Propose writer action upstream when *permitted*; leave corpora unchanged by lifecycle |
| “Certification seal authorised this evolution” | CRT seals are not evolution authority | Refuse seal-as-amendment theatre |
| “We verified / complied again in evolution” | Replaces WS2 / WS3 | Never perform verification or determine compliance under evolution colour |

## 7. Audit Alignment

Lifecycle explainability records must support reconstruction of:

1. Constitutional artefacts affected (CELEQ-01)
2. Evolution category applied (CELEQ-02)
3. Lifecycle stage producing each evolution decision (CELEQ-03)
4. Historical provenance preserved (CELEQ-04)
5. Constitutional boundaries remaining intact (CELEQ-05)
6. Applied CEG-01…CEG-07 identities (from WS5 / MS001 speech)
7. Decision dispositions (permitted / refused / deferred / escalated / refuse-*)
8. Continuity of prior lawful lifecycle history (EIP-005)

Missing any material element is an explainability defect under CELO-02 / CELO-03 / CELO-05.
