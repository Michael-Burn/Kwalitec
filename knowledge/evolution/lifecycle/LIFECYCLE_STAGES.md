## 1. Purpose

Evolution without a closed stage catalogue invents law by proximity: whichever ticket status, merge habit, or reviewer custom happens to finish becomes the tutor’s “proof the system ran a constitutional evolution.”

This catalogue names the only lawful constitutional evolution lifecycle stages — and binds each stage to purpose, inputs, outputs, and permitted / prohibited activities.

---

## 2. Stage Principles

1. **Ordered and skip-disciplined.** Stages may be brief under thin scope; mandatory proposal, classification, assessment, decision, provenance, publication preparation, and closure obligations may not be silently omitted when an evolution disposition is claimed.
2. **Published artefacts before classification.** Identifiable published constitutional artefacts are named before CEG classification.
3. **Classification before assessment.** CEG-01…CEG-07 binding precedes continuity / integrity / authority assessment.
4. **Explainability accumulates.** Each stage leaves traceable artefacts for `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.
5. **Refusal is a stage outcome.** Refused, deferred, escalated, and refuse-progress are first-class conclusions.
6. **Deterministic transitions.** Same published inputs ⇒ same next stage outcomes.
7. **Stages coordinate; they do not legislate.** No stage creates, modifies, or redefines constitutional law.
8. **Stages do not become constitutional authority.** Closure never freezes an implementation as permanently lawful or replaces published corpora.
9. **Stages do not enact amendments.** Dispositions coordinate lawful change readiness; they never themselves rewrite corpus text.
10. **Stages do not govern implementation behaviour.** Evolution coordination never redesigns delivery under constitutional colour.

## 3. Catalogue Overview

| ID | Stage | Constitutional purpose (short) | Primary inputs | Primary outputs |
|----|-------|--------------------------------|----------------|-----------------|
| **CEL-01** | Evolution Proposal | Open a named evolution concern against published artefacts | Published artefact identities + proposal scope + constitutional justification seed | Initiated proposal record (or refuse-open) |
| **CEL-02** | Proposal Classification | Bind the proposal to CEG-01…CEG-07 | Initiated proposal + published CEG catalogue | Classified proposal with CEG binding(s) (or refuse-classify) |
| **CEL-03** | Constitutional Assessment | Assess continuity, integrity, authority, and provenance constraints | Classified proposal + CEGO / boundary / EIP constraints | Assessment dispositions under published principles |
| **CEL-04** | Evolution Decision | Emit evolution dispositions under published criteria | Assessment dispositions | Evolution decisions (permitted / refused / deferred / escalated) |
| **CEL-05** | Provenance Recording | Preserve audit-speakable historical trails | Decisions + stage traces | Provenance records bound to artefacts / category / stages / dispositions |
| **CEL-06** | Publication Preparation | Prepare honest publication of dispositions and provenance | Provenance records + boundary confirmations | Publication-ready artefacts (or refuse-prepare) |
| **CEL-07** | Evolution Closure | Close the lifecycle honestly when duties are exhausted | Publication-ready artefacts + boundary confirmations | Closed lifecycle (or refuse-close) — never a freeze of constitutional authority or silent amendment |

Material evolution claiming constitutional dispositions must map to this sequence as published law requires. Stages may be brief; none may invent a stage outside this catalogue.

**Relation to CEG / CC / CT / CV / CCM / CRT / EV:** Those catalogues remain defined solely by their owners. CEL stages *organise when* evolution activities occur; they do not replace, reclassify, re-verify, re-determine compliance, invent, certify, or amend those catalogues.

## 4. Canonical Stage Sequence

```text
CEL-01  Evolution Proposal
           ↓
CEL-02  Proposal Classification
           ↓
CEL-03  Constitutional Assessment
           ↓
CEL-04  Evolution Decision
           ↓
CEL-05  Provenance Recording
           ↓
CEL-06  Publication Preparation
           ↓
CEL-07  Evolution Closure
```

Lawful early stop dispositions (refuse-open / refuse-classify / deferred / escalated / refused) may exhaust remaining *enactment-readiness* duties while still requiring honest provenance recording, publication preparation (or refuse-prepare), and closure of what occurred — never silent disappearance of the concern.

## 5. Stage Definitions

### CEL-01 — Evolution Proposal

**Constitutional purpose**  
Open a material evolution concern by naming the published constitutional artefacts proposed for change and the scope of the concern — without inventing law, opening against unpublished customs, treating initiation as amendment, or treating proposal as disposition.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Identifiable published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII / IX artefact and specification references for the concern scope
- Concern identity (what constitutional-change question is under evolution)
- Constitutional justification seed (integrity / continuity / authority / educational care — never mere product urgency)
- Optional published indication of candidate CEG-01…CEG-07 types for the scope
- Optional identifiable supporting authority / lineage references (not rewritten)

**Constitutional outputs**

- An initiated evolution proposal record naming artefacts, scope, and justification seed — **or**
- Lawful refuse-open when subjects are unpublished, scope is theatrical, or initiation would invent law

**Permitted activities**

- Name published artefacts as evolution subjects
- Bound the concern scope without amending corpora
- Record candidate CEG types from the published catalogue
- Refuse initiation when constitutional preconditions fail
- Preserve proposal audit identity for later stages

**Prohibited activities**

- Treat “how main works,” tribal knowledge, or stack preference as the subject of evolution
- Create or modify constitutional specifications by opening a proposal
- Issue a formal evolution disposition at proposal
- Invent unpublished evolution types
- Skip proposal when a material disposition will later be claimed
- Govern implementation redesign as if it were constitutional proposal

**Authority preserved**  
Published corpora remain the sole standard. Proposal organises; it does not legislate.

---

### CEL-02 — Proposal Classification

**Constitutional purpose**  
Bind the initiated proposal to one or more published **CEG-01…CEG-07** evolution categories — so assessment proceeds under recognised change kinds, not anonymous rewrite theatre — without inventing types, soft-amending meaning in classification prose, or enacting amendments.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Initiated proposal record from CEL-01
- Published CEG-01…CEG-07 catalogue (`../EVOLUTION_TYPES.md`)
- Published CEGO objectives and evolution boundaries
- Supporting artefact identities and justification seed

**Constitutional outputs**

- Classified proposal with explicit CEG binding(s) and classification rationale — **or**
- Lawful refuse-classify when no published type applies, classification would invent customs, or soft amendment is being smuggled as “clarification”

**Permitted activities**

- Apply only published CEG-01…CEG-07 types
- Compose multiple CEG types when published law requires (for example CEG-04 + CEG-06)
- Refuse classification of unpublished evolution customs
- Record classification identity for assessment and provenance
- Preserve classification audit trail

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent unpublished evolution categories
- Redefine CEG type meaning during classification
- Treat product urgency, CI greens, or certification seals as classification authority
- Soft-amend constitutional meaning in classification prose
- Skip classification when a material disposition will later be claimed
- Enact corpus rewrites under classification colour

**Authority preserved**  
WS5 / MS001 owns CEG meaning. Classification applies; it does not rewrite the catalogue.

---

### CEL-03 — Constitutional Assessment

**Constitutional purpose**  
Assess the classified proposal against published continuity, integrity, authority, provenance, and boundary constraints — so dispositions rest on constitutional care, not convenience — without redefining constitutional meaning, erasing history, replacing WS1–WS4, or enacting amendments.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Classified proposal with CEG binding(s)
- Published evolution governance principles (CEGO objectives, `EVOLUTION_BOUNDARIES.md`, EIP-001 / EIP-005 / EIP-003)
- Historical identity of prior published versions / relationships where material
- Supporting CT lineage and authority map references (preserved, not invented)

**Constitutional outputs**

- Assessment dispositions under published principles (pass / fail constraints with speakable rationale) — never a silent corpus rewrite
- Hard-stop notes when continuity, integrity, authority, or provenance would be violated

**Permitted activities**

- Evaluate continuity, integrity, authority, and provenance constraints as published
- Prefer refuse / defer / escalate over history erasure or soft amendment
- Cite established CT relationships without inventing them
- Record assessment rationale for decision and explainability
- Compose cross-cutting CEG constraints when published

**Prohibited activities**

- Redefine Programme VI educational meaning or sibling constitutional meaning during assessment
- Alter constitutional history retrospectively to force a pass
- Bypass constitutional authority or replace WS1–WS4 workstreams
- Privilege a stack, vendor, or reference implementation as the assessment standard
- Treat Twin / Adaptive / UI / CI noise as constitutional producers
- Enact amendments under assessment colour
- Govern implementation behaviour as constitutional assessment

**Authority preserved**  
Published corpora and WS5 / MS001 principles remain the sole assessment standard. Assessment judges readiness for disposition; it does not rewrite law.

---

### CEL-04 — Evolution Decision

**Constitutional purpose**  
Emit lawful **evolution dispositions** — permitted / refused / deferred / escalated — under published criteria crystallising CEL-03 assessment — without treating dispositions as silent soft amendments, educational quality grades, implementation redesign licences, or substitutes for constitutional authority.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Assessment dispositions from CEL-03
- Published decision criteria under CEG types and CEGO / boundary rules
- Stage identity binding assessment to decision

**Constitutional outputs**

- Evolution decisions: **permitted** / **refused** / **deferred** / **escalated**
- Explicit non-enactment note: disposition is not itself the corpus rewrite
- Boundary preservation note (authority, continuity, non-history-rewrite, non-implementation-governance)

**Permitted activities**

- Crystallise assessment into published disposition codes
- Prefer honest refuse / defer / escalate over improvisation
- Bind each decision to producing stage identity (CEL-03 → CEL-04)
- Record decision rationale for provenance and explainability
- Escalate when published law requires human / corpus stewardship beyond automated disposition

**Prohibited activities**

- Treat “permitted” as automatic silent amendment of corpus text
- Treat decisions as educational quality, mastery, or readiness grades
- Freeze stacks, vendors, or releases as constitutional law via disposition
- Invent unpublished disposition kinds that smuggle soft amendment
- Skip decision when later publication claims a disposition occurred
- Replace constitutional authority with decision machinery

**Authority preserved**  
Dispositions coordinate lawful change readiness. Published corpora remain the sole source of meaning; permitted writers remain the sole enacting path outside this lifecycle’s non-enacting posture.

---

### CEL-05 — Provenance Recording

**Constitutional purpose**  
Preserve **audit-speakable historical provenance** of artefacts affected, CEG category applied, lifecycle stages traversed, dispositions issued, and boundaries intact — so continuity and explainability survive — without treating provenance records as the amendment act or erasing inconvenient prior law.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Evolution decisions from CEL-04
- Stage traces from CEL-01…CEL-04
- Prior published version / relationship identities
- Boundary confirmations material to the concern

**Constitutional outputs**

- Provenance records binding artefacts / category / stages / dispositions / boundaries
- Continuity-sufficient trails for CELO-02 / CELO-05 reconstruction
- Refuse-record notes when provenance would require forgery or history erasure

**Permitted activities**

- Record predecessor / successor / deprecation / archival / restoration / consolidation relationships as applicable
- Preserve refuse / defer / escalate history honestly
- Cite established CT relationships in provenance without inventing them
- Keep reconstructable constitutional references, not only technical CI logs

**Prohibited activities**

- Erase prior lawful versions to simplify the next report
- Forge provenance to force a permitted narrative
- Treat provenance records as silent soft amendments of corpora
- Drop stage-to-decision binding or artefact identity
- Privilege stack preference as provenance “truth”
- Govern implementation redesign via provenance theatre

**Authority preserved**  
Provenance preserves history under EIP-005 / CEGO / CELO duties. It never becomes legislation.

---

### CEL-06 — Publication Preparation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Prepare **honest publication artefacts** for evolution dispositions and provenance under published honesty rules — so students, developers, and auditors may later receive speakable governance outcomes — without inventing permission, hiding refused history, rewriting boundaries, freezing a stack as law, or enacting corpus amendments under “publication” colour.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Provenance records from CEL-05
- Boundary confirmations
- Published honesty / explainability rules (EIP-003 / CEGEQ / CELEQ families)

**Constitutional outputs**

- Publication-ready artefacts (disposition summaries, provenance summaries, boundary notes) — **or**
- Lawful refuse-prepare when publication would invent permission, hide stops, or rewrite boundaries

**Permitted activities**

- Assemble speakable disposition and provenance packages under published rules
- Disclose refuse / defer / escalate outcomes honestly
- Prepare audience projections that share one truth with different vocabulary
- Refuse preparation when honesty rules would be violated

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent “permitted” publication when disposition was refused / deferred / escalated
- Hide incomplete classification, assessment stops, or missing provenance
- Publish stack preference as constitutional law
- Soft-amend corpora in publication prose
- Treat publication preparation as the amendment act
- Present green CI or seals as constitutional evolution authority

**Authority preserved**  
Publication preparation communicates coordination outcomes. It never legislates.

---

### CEL-07 — Evolution Closure

**Constitutional purpose**  
Close the lifecycle honestly when constitutional coordination duties are exhausted — recording that proposal, classification, assessment, decision, provenance, and publication-preparation obligations have been completed or lawfully refused — without enacting amendments, replacing constitutional authority, governing implementation behaviour, or pretending incomplete work is done.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Publication-ready artefacts (or refuse-prepare identity) from CEL-06
- Boundary confirmations against `LIFECYCLE_BOUNDARIES.md`
- Completeness of required CEL-01…CEL-06 duties for the concern

**Constitutional outputs**

- Closed lifecycle identity with speakable duty exhaustion — **or**
- Lawful refuse-close when duties remain incomplete, boundaries failed, or closure would smuggle amendment / authority substitution

**Permitted activities**

- Confirm required stages executed or lawfully early-stopped
- Close refused / deferred / escalated concerns honestly after provenance and preparation duties
- Preserve closure audit identity for explainability
- Refuse closure when coordination duties remain theatrical or incomplete

**Prohibited activities**

- Treat closure as silent soft amendment of constitutional specifications
- Treat closure as replacement of constitutional authority
- Treat closure as licence to redesign implementations
- Treat closure as waiver of WS1–WS4 assessment horizons
- Skip provenance or publication-preparation while claiming closed evolution
- Freeze stacks, vendors, or releases as law via closure

**Authority preserved**  
Closure ends coordination. Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard; permitted writers remain the sole enacting path for any *permitted* disposition that later requires textual change outside this lifecycle’s non-enacting posture.

## 6. Stage Transition Rules

| From | To | Lawful when … | Hard stop when … |
|------|----|---------------|------------------|
| CEL-01 | CEL-02 | Proposal names published artefacts | Unpublished subjects / invent law |
| CEL-02 | CEL-03 | CEG-01…CEG-07 binding exists | Invented types / soft-amend classification |
| CEL-03 | CEL-04 | Assessment under published principles complete | History erasure / authority bypass / meaning redefinition |
| CEL-04 | CEL-05 | Disposition issued (including refuse / defer / escalate) | Disposition treated as silent amendment |
| CEL-05 | CEL-06 | Provenance reconstructable | Forged or erased history |
| CEL-06 | CEL-07 | Publication artefacts honest (or refuse-prepare recorded) | Invented permission / hidden stops |
| CEL-07 | — | Duties exhausted or refuse-close recorded | Closure smuggles amendment / authority substitution / implementation governance |

---

## 7. Closing Statement

> **CEL-01…CEL-07 organise constitutional evolution from proposal through disposition.  
> They consume evolution governance principles and produce dispositions and provenance.  
> They never directly amend constitutional specifications, redefine constitutional meaning, bypass constitutional authority, or govern implementation behaviour.**
