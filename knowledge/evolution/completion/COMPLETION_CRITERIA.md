## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor needs bright conditions for saying “this constitutional evolution governance work is finished” — and equally bright refusals when it is not. After WS5 / MS001 has named under what principles the corpus may lawfully evolve and WS5 / MS002 has named how evolution activities are organised, Programme IX Workstream 5 still needs one fulfilment checklist:

> **What must be true before constitutional speech may affirm that the required evolution lifecycle has been fully executed, evolution dispositions recorded, provenance preserved, and publication preparation concluded where applicable?**

These criteria answer that question. They are **normative constitutional conditions**, not database predicates, CI job statuses, ticket states, merge badges, or API status codes.

---

## 2. Criterion Catalogue

All material affirmed completions require **CEC-01 through CEC-05** to hold. Failure of any criterion ⇒ **not yet** or **refuse close**. Do not invent a partial “mostly complete” amendment, educational, governance, or constitutional status.

| ID | Criterion | Primary question |
|----|-----------|------------------|
| **CEC-01** | Required lifecycle stages completed | Were required CEL-01…CEL-07 stages completed for the concern? |
| **CEC-02** | Evolution disposition recorded | Was a required evolution disposition lawfully recorded under published CEG / CEL decision rules? |
| **CEC-03** | Provenance preserved | Do reconstructable historical provenance records exist for the completed evolution activity? |
| **CEC-04** | Publication preparation completed where applicable | Was CEL-06 publication preparation (or refuse-prepare) concluded where required for the concern? |
| **CEC-05** | Evolution activity formally closed | Was the evolution concern formally closed under published CEL-07 / boundary honesty? |

Optional supporting honesty checks (do not replace CEC-01…CEC-05):

| ID | Check | Primary question |
|----|-------|------------------|
| **CEC-06** | Catalogue honesty | Were stages and dispositions typed under published CEL-01…CEL-07 and CEG-01…CEG-07 only? |
| **CEC-07** | Continuity honesty | Was prior lawful evolution / corpus history preserved (EIP-005)? |
| **CEC-08** | Non-claim honesty | Does the judgement refuse amendment-enactment / meaning-redefinition / authority-replacement / history-rewrite / implementation-governance overclaim? |
| **CEC-09** | Neutrality honesty | Does the judgement refuse stack / vendor canonisation as completion law? |
| **CEC-10** | Repeatability honesty | Would the same published inputs yield the same completion disposition? |

## 3. Criterion Definitions

### CEC-01 — Required lifecycle stages completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every lifecycle stage required by the concern’s published WS5 / MS002 scope has been executed — or that a published refuse / defer / escalate / refused disposition exhausted remaining *evolution-governance* duties for that stage set with honest progression to provenance recording, publication preparation (or refuse-prepare), and closure of what occurred.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Identifiable evolution concern opened under WS5 / MS001–MS002.
- Required CEL-01…CEL-07 stage identities for the concern scope.
- Stage traces / disposition records for each required stage (including lawful early stops).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed stage-fulfilment record naming which required stages completed.
- Or a lawful stop record naming which stages were refused / deferred / escalated and why remaining evolution-governance duties are exhausted.

**Completion conditions.**

- Required stages map to published CEL-01…CEL-07 only.
- Mandatory proposal, classification, assessment, decision, provenance, publication preparation (where applicable), and closure obligations for the concern are not silently omitted.
- Lawful early stops still leave honest stage outcomes; silent disappearance of a concern is a defect.
- Refusal of “pipeline skipped assessment” or “we know it ran” without stage identity as completion.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That stage completion amends constitutional specifications or enacts corpus change.
- That stage completion replaces constitutional authority or freezes a stack as law.
- That stage completion rewrites constitutional history retrospectively.
- That stage completion means the learner succeeded or implementation redesign is authorised.
- That skipped stages may be invented or implied from CI theatre.
- That verification was performed, compliance was determined, or certification was minted under stage progress.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who declares evolution “finished” without confirming each required governance step occurred has not finished lawful care — even if the desk looks tidy.

---

### CEC-02 — Evolution disposition recorded

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every evolution disposition required by the concern’s published scope has been lawfully recorded under published CEG / CEL decision rules — including permitted, refused, deferred, and escalated dispositions — without rewriting those dispositions into constitutional amendment enactment, authority transfer, history rewrite, or implementation redesign, and without silently replacing prior lawful dispositions.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Assessment dispositions under applicable CEG-01…CEG-07 types (CEL-03 outputs).
- Published decision-issuance rules under WS5 / MS001–MS002 (especially CEL-04).
- Boundary confirmations that dispositions do not invent law, erase history, or freeze stack authority.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Identifiable evolution disposition identities with lawful outcomes.
- Or a lawful stop record naming why required dispositions could not be recorded without invention.

**Completion conditions.**

- Dispositions cite published constitutional references, named artefacts, and applied CEG types.
- Outcomes remain within published vocabulary (permitted / refused / deferred / escalated / refuse-*).
- “Assessment happened somewhere” without named disposition identity is not completion.
- Lawful refused / deferred / escalated dispositions still satisfy *recording* when they are the honest outcome — completeness of *recording* is not completeness of *permitted disposition*, and neither is constitutional amendment enactment.
- Prior lawful dispositions for the same published concern are not silently replaced under close theatre.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That recording a disposition amends constitutional specifications or enacts corpus change.
- That a permitted disposition freezes the implementation, stack, vendor, or release as permanent constitutional law.
- That a refused / deferred / escalated disposition may be silently erased or replaced so close can look green.
- That MS001 conformance evaluation was replaced, skipped, or rewritten by disposition recording.
- That Educational Validation Framework quality review is complete.
- That constitutional history was rewritten because a disposition was recorded.

**Tutor rationale.** Recording a hard evolution disposition is not rewriting the syllabus — but omitting a required disposition while claiming the session finished is dishonest. Completeness of *disposition recording* is not completeness of *constitutional change*.

---

### CEC-03 — Provenance preserved

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that reconstructable historical provenance records exist for the completed evolution activity: which artefacts were affected, which CEG category applied, which lifecycle stages ran, which disposition was recorded, which prior/successor relationships were preserved, and which boundaries remained intact — sufficient for student / developer / auditor reconstruction under `COMPLETION_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Stage traces (CEL-01…CEL-07).
- Disposition records (CEC-02).
- Provenance recording artefacts under CEL-05.
- Continuity obligations under EIP-005.
- Boundary preservation confirmations.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Audit-speakable provenance records aligned with CECQ-01…CECQ-05.
- Honest refuse / defer / escalate records when fulfilment was not affirmed.

**Completion conditions.**

- Material completion judgements leave provenance that cites published constitutional references, not only technical job logs or CI annotations.
- Missing CEG / CEL identity, missing artefact identity, missing disposition identity, missing prior/successor refs, or missing boundary honesty for a material affirmed close is a defect.
- Continuity forbids erasing prior lawful evolution / corpus trails to make close look tidy.
- Refusal of log volume alone as a substitute for constitutional content.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That a particular database schema, log shipper, CI annotation format, or UI widget is required.
- That provenance preservation equals constitutional amendment enactment, authority replacement, educational success, or implementation redesign.
- That volume of logs substitutes for constitutional content.
- That Twin / Adaptive / analytics origin stories may replace published constitutional identity.
- That preserving provenance silently replaces prior lawful dispositions or rewrites history.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later reconstruct why evolution was declared finished cannot be trusted — and neither can a platform that celebrates mute “complete” flags.

---

### CEC-04 — Publication preparation completed where applicable

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that CEL-06 publication preparation obligations required by the concern’s published scope were concluded — assembling honest publication-ready disposition / provenance packages under published honesty rules, or recording refuse-prepare when preparation would invent permission, hide refused history, rewrite boundaries, or freeze a stack as law — without treating publication preparation as the amendment act itself.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Provenance records (CEC-03 / CEL-05).
- Published CEL-06 publication-preparation rules and honesty constraints.
- Boundary confirmations that publication artefacts do not invent permission or erase refused history.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed publication-preparation record naming which publication-ready artefacts were assembled — or a refuse-prepare identity with reconstructable reason.
- Explicit non-claim that preparation ≠ enactment of constitutional change.

**Completion conditions.**

- Where CEL-06 is required for the concern scope, preparation (or refuse-prepare) is not silently omitted.
- Publication-ready artefacts cite dispositions and provenance honestly — including refused / deferred / escalated outcomes where those are the truth.
- “Looks published enough” without named preparation identity is not completion.
- Where published law states CEL-06 is not applicable for a thin refuse-open concern that never reached disposition publication duties, the non-applicability itself must be reconstructable — silent omission is a defect.
- Refusal of ticket closure, PR merge, or pipeline greens alone as publication preparation.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That publication preparation amends constitutional specifications or enacts corpus change.
- That publication preparation replaces constitutional authority or freezes a stack as law.
- That refuse-prepare may be silently erased so close can look green.
- That MS001 conformance, WS2 verification, WS3 compliance, or WS4 certification was performed under preparation.
- That educational quality or implementation redesign was authorised.

**Tutor rationale.** Preparing honest publication of what the evolution governance process decided is care; pretending preparation rewrote the syllabus — or skipping preparation while claiming the session finished — is not.

---

### CEC-05 — Evolution activity formally closed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that the evolution concern was formally closed under published CEL-07 obligations and boundary honesty — exhausting remaining *evolution lifecycle* duties without inventing a constitutional amendment enactment, redefining constitutional meaning, replacing constitutional authority, rewriting constitutional history, or governing implementation behaviour.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Recorded dispositions and preserved provenance (CEC-02 / CEC-03).
- Publication-preparation outcomes where applicable (CEC-04).
- CEL-07 closure rules and `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md` hard stops.
- Explicit non-claim confirmations (amendment enactment / meaning redefinition / authority replacement / history rewrite / implementation governance).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Formal close disposition: affirmed complete / not yet / refuse close / defer / escalate.
- Boundary preservation record confirming forbidden overclaims did not occur.

**Completion conditions.**

- Closure occurs only after required stages, dispositions, provenance, and publication-preparation obligations for the concern are satisfied — or after a published stop exhausts remaining evolution-governance duties with honest recording.
- Closure speech answers CECQ-01…CECQ-05 or explicitly refuses mute theatre.
- Closure never silently replaces disposition outcomes or upgrades them into constitutional law.
- Refusal of ticket closure, PR merge, merge badges, or pipeline greens alone as formal constitutional close.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That formal close amends constitutional specifications or enacts corpus change.
- That formal close replaces constitutional authority or freezes the implementation, release, stack, or vendor as law.
- That formal close silently replaces prior lawful evolution dispositions.
- That formal close rewrites constitutional history retrospectively.
- That formal close means the student succeeded or is exam-ready.
- That formal close means implementation behaviour has been redesigned as constitutional law.

**Tutor rationale.** Closing the evolution folder is care only when the required steps, dispositions, provenance, and publication preparation exist — and only when nobody pretends the close rewrote the syllabus, erased history, froze the product, or finished the candidate’s education.

## 4. Criterion Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CEC-01 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CEC-02 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CEC-03 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — mute complete badges are defects |
| CEC-04 fails | Affirmation is unlawful when publication preparation is required (or non-applicability is not reconstructable) |
| CEC-05 fails | Affirmation is unlawful even if earlier stages appear present |
| Optional CEC-06…CEC-10 fail | Treat as honesty defects; do not soft-pass CEC-01…CEC-05 |
| Any criterion “passes” only by amending specs / redefining meaning / replacing authority / rewriting history / governing implementation | The implied overclaim voids lawful affirmation |

All of CEC-01…CEC-05 are **conjunctive** for material affirmed completion. There is no lawful “four of five” educational, amendment, governance, or constitutional status.

## 5. Lawful Stop Dispositions

Not every incomplete concern is a defect. Published WS5 / MS002 refuse / defer / escalate / refused outcomes may **exhaust remaining evolution-governance duties** for a concern without affirming lifecycle fulfilment as “complete” in the celebratory sense — while still requiring honest provenance recording, publication preparation (or refuse-prepare), and formal close of what occurred.

| Disposition | Constitutional meaning | May affirm CEC completion? |
|-------------|------------------------|----------------------------|
| **Affirm complete** | CEC-01…CEC-05 hold for the concern | Yes |
| **Not yet** | Required stages, dispositions, provenance, or publication preparation remain outstanding | No |
| **Refuse close** | Affirmation would invent stages / dispositions / provenance, erase history, replace dispositions, or overclaim | No |
| **Defer** | Published wait for upstream corpus / artefact identity / governance attention | No (unless published law says remaining evolution-governance duties are exhausted) |
| **Escalate** | Published stop requiring human / corpus governance attention | No (unless published law says remaining evolution-governance duties are exhausted) |

A lawful stop that exhausts evolution-governance duties is **honest unfinished fulfilment or honest refusal** — never a stealth constitutional amendment, history rewrite, authority transfer, educational success, or implementation redesign warrant.

---

## 6. Non-Criteria

The following are **not** constitutional completion criteria:

- CI pipeline greens, flaky-merge suppression, or job exit codes;
- ticket closure, PR merge, or documentation checklist ticks alone;
- coverage percentages, lint scores, merge badges, or elapsed time;
- Twin / Adaptive / Experience / Mission confidence signals;
- UI celebration states or analytics milestones;
- “looks evolved enough for the demo”;
- MS001 conformant disposition (that is fidelity law, not CEC);
- WS1 / MS003 traceability completion (CTC) alone (orthogonal lineage fulfilment);
- WS2 / MS003 verification completion (CVC) alone (orthogonal verification lifecycle fulfilment);
- WS3 / MS003 compliance completion (CCC) alone (orthogonal compliance lifecycle fulfilment);
- WS4 / MS003 certification completion (CRTC) alone (orthogonal — and must not initiate evolution);
- Educational Validation Framework quality release (orthogonal);
- any implication that constitutional specifications changed because evolution closed;
- any implication that constitutional history was rewritten because evolution closed;
- any implication that an implementation has permanently become constitutional law because evolution closed.

---

## 7. Closing Statement

> **Constitutional evolution is complete only when required lifecycle stages are completed, an evolution disposition is recorded, provenance is preserved, publication preparation is completed where applicable, and the evolution activity is formally closed.  
> Satisfying CEC-01…CEC-05 never means constitutional law has been amended, constitutional meaning has been redefined, constitutional authority has been replaced, constitutional history has been rewritten, or implementation behaviour has been governed as constitutional law.**
