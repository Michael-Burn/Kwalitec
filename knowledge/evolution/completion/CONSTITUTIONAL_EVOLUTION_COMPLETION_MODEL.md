## 2. What Constitutional Evolution Completion Is

**Constitutional Evolution Completion** is confirmation that the **required constitutional evolution lifecycle** for a published evolution concern has been **fully executed according to constitutional governance principles** — such that **evolution dispositions** have been **recorded**, **provenance** has been **preserved**, and **publication preparation** has **concluded where applicable** — without claiming that any constitutional-amendment enactment, history rewrite, authority replacement, educational, or implementation-governance question was resolved by the close itself.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Evolution concern** | A bounded constitutional unit of evolution work opened under published CEG / CEL law | What evolution work is being assessed for completion? |
| **Completion warrant** | Judgement that lifecycle fulfilment may be assessed | May we ask whether evolution is complete? |
| **Completion criteria** | Constitutional conditions that must hold (`COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md`) | What must be true for fulfilment? |
| **Completion judgement** | Affirmed / not yet / refused (no lawful close) | Is constitutional evolution complete for this concern? |
| **Lifecycle fulfilment** | Required CEL-01…CEL-07 obligations executed (or lawfully stopped with honest recording) | Did the published lifecycle run? |
| **Disposition recording** | Lawful evolution dispositions recorded under CEG / CEL decision rules | Were dispositions recorded? |
| **Provenance preservation** | Audit-speakable historical provenance records preserved | Was provenance preserved? |
| **Publication preparation** | Honest publication-preparation (or refuse-prepare) concluded where applicable under CEL-06 | Was publication preparation concluded where required? |
| **Audit continuity** | Reconstructable constitutional records of the completed evolution activity | Can the close be audited? |
| **Boundary preservation record** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden overclaims / amendments / substitutions did not occur | Did constitutional integrity survive close? |

Constitutional Evolution Completion is:

- **governance-scoped** — it concerns fulfilment of the published evolution governance process, not enactment of constitutional change, educational outcomes, or implementation redesign;
- **lifecycle-bound** — fulfilment references CEL-01…CEL-07 stages actually required for the concern under WS5 / MS002;
- **criteria-gated** — fulfilment requires stated constitutional conditions (`COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md` CEC-01…CEC-05);
- **principle-consuming** — completion confirms published WS5 / MS001 evolution governance principles were applied; it never invents or rewrites them;
- **disposition-honest** — completion confirms dispositions were recorded; it never silently replaces them or upgrades disposition into silent soft amendment or authority transfer;
- **provenance-preserving** — fulfilment cites reconstructable historical provenance, not ticket theatre or CI greens;
- **publication-honest** — where CEL-06 applies, completion confirms publication preparation (or refuse-prepare) concluded; it never invents permission or hides refused history;
- **audit-capable** — fulfilment cites reconstructable constitutional records;
- **repeatable** — same published artefacts, same proposal, same CEG classification, same stage sequence ⇒ same completion disposition for the same concern scope;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by completion;
- **non-legislative** — completion never amends constitutional specifications or constitutes constitutional change itself;
- **authority-preserving** — published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard after close;
- **history-preserving** — completion never rewrites constitutional history retrospectively;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which principles / stages / dispositions / provenance / boundaries applied;
- **corpus-bound** — every material completion cites identifiable published specifications, artefacts, named dispositions, and provenance records;
- **non-substitutive** — a completed evolution activity never becomes the constitutional standard or a substitute for MS001 evaluation.

Constitutional Evolution Completion is **not**:

- a constitutional amendment, soft rewrite of specifications, or enactment of corpus change;
- redefinition of constitutional meaning outside lawful amendment processes;
- replacement or transfer of constitutional authority;
- a rewrite of constitutional history to make today’s product look always-correct;
- governance of implementation behaviour, release trains, or product roadmaps as constitutional law;
- educational success, learner mastery, progress, or exam readiness;
- a Programme VI / VII / VIII educational, orchestration, or runtime completion judgement;
- transfer of authority, minting of tips, invention of EST/CST postures, or amendment of constitutional specifications;
- a claim that pipeline greens, ticket closure, elapsed time, or merge badges equal fulfilment;
- a UI celebration, analytics milestone, or “evolved forever” flag as educational or constitutional law;
- a licence to skip outstanding published lifecycle duties because a product surface looks finished;
- a substitute for WS5 / MS001 evolution governance meaning or WS5 / MS002 lifecycle law — completion *judges fulfilment of* those corpora; it does not redefine CEG or CEL catalogues;
- a substitute for WS4 / MS003 certification completion — CRTC confirms certification lifecycle fulfilment and must not initiate evolution; CEC confirms evolution lifecycle fulfilment;
- a substitute for WS3 / MS003 compliance completion, WS2 / MS003 verification completion, or WS1 / MS003 traceability completion — those horizons remain orthogonal.

## 4. Completion Lifecycle (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material evolution concern that claims lawful completion travels through constitutional assessment phases. These are **normative judgement obligations**, not an implementation architecture.

```
Identify published evolution concern opened under CEG / CEL law
        │
        ▼
Confirm required CEL-01…CEL-07 stages and CEG-01…CEG-07 applications for the concern
        │
        ├── refuse / defer / escalate (lawful stop — law unchanged; no invented “complete”)
        │
        └── assess → Apply CEC-01…CEC-05
                │
                ├── not yet / refuse close (outstanding duties or boundary breach)
                │
                └── affirm → Emit completion judgement; preserve audit continuity and repeatability
                        │
                        ▼
                Explain principles / stages / dispositions / provenance / boundaries
```

| Phase | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **Identify** | Name only published evolution concerns opened under WS5 / MS001–MS002 | Concern is unpublished custom, tribal knowledge, or “how CI works today” |
| **Confirm lifecycle** | Verify required CEL stages and CEG applications for the concern | Stages would invent law, rewrite specs, reinterpret meaning, erase history, or privilege a stack as law |
| **Assess criteria** | Apply CEC-01…CEC-05 without softening into product theatre | Any required criterion fails; or close would claim amendment enactment / history rewrite / authority replacement / implementation governance |
| **Preserve & explain** | Record audit continuity; answer CECQ-01…CECQ-05 | Path would erase provenance trails, treat completion as constitutional amendment, or narrate “complete therefore law changed / history rewritten / stack frozen” |

> **Evolution completion never “best-effort invents” a fifth phase called constitutional amendment enactment, history rewrite, authority replacement, or implementation redesign.**

## 7. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Evolution Completion Model exists so that:

1. **Lifecycle honesty remains intact** — “complete” means the published evolution lifecycle ran, not that a pipeline glowed green.
2. **Dispositions remain honest** — close confirms dispositions were recorded; it never silently replaces them or upgrades disposition into silent soft amendment or authority transfer.
3. **Provenance survives close** — completion leaves reconstructable historical provenance.
4. **Audit continuity survives close** — completion leaves reconstructable constitutional records.
5. **Repeatability remains intact** — the same published inputs yield the same completion disposition.
6. **Authority remains upstream** — completed evolution never becomes the constitutional source for the next judgement.
7. **History remains intact** — completion does not rewrite prior lawful corpus identity for convenience.
8. **Implementations remain replaceable** — completion does not freeze Flask, SQLAlchemy, a particular API shape, or a CI vendor as law.
9. **Explainability remains intact** — completion speech describes governance-process fulfilment; it does not redefine what learning means or what conformity is.
10. **Students remain protected** — product theatre cannot launder unfinished evolution work as “evolution complete therefore law changed / history rewritten / permanently frozen.”
