## 1. Purpose

Evolution machinery that silently turns “the evolution lifecycle finished” into “the Constitution now says X,” “yesterday’s history is rewritten,” “the stack has permanently become law,” “student succeeded,” or “implementation redesign is authorised” destroys student trust and educational integrity.

This document draws a bright line between **lawful constitutional evolution completion** (Programme IX / WS5 / MS003) and **constitutional authorship / governance replacement / authority redefinition / history alteration / educational judgement / implementation governance** owned elsewhere.

---

## 2. Boundary Principles

1. **Confirm lifecycle fulfilment, do not legislate.** Completion affirms fulfilment of published evolution lifecycle duties; it does not create or enact constitutional law.
2. **Governance process only.** Constitutional amendment enactment, history rewrite, educational success, and implementation redesign are never inferred from close.
3. **Not conformance evaluation.** WS1 / MS001 owns fidelity disposition.
4. **Not verification.** Completion never performs verification or re-authors CV findings.
5. **Not compliance determination.** Completion never becomes a CCM engine.
6. **Not certification.** Completion never certifies and never treats CRTC seals as evolution authority.
7. **Not constitutional amendment.** Close never patches law or enacts corpus change.
8. **Not meaning redefinition.** Close never soft-amends Programme VI–VIII / EIP meaning.
9. **Not evolution-governance replacement.** Close never replaces WS5 / MS001 CEG meaning or WS5 / MS002 CEL law.
10. **Not authority redefinition.** Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard after close.
11. **Not history alteration.** Close never rewrites constitutional history retrospectively.
12. **Not implementation governance.** Close never redesigns delivery under constitutional colour.
13. **Exactly as published.** Close never redefines CEG / CEL catalogues.
14. **Published criteria only.** Pipelines, tickets, merges, and elapsed time are not fulfilment law.
15. **Published records only.** Audit continuity and provenance are constitutional, not free-form log theatre.
16. **Reference dispositions honestly.** Recorded dispositions may be cited; closing never upgrades disposition into amendment / authority transfer / history rewrite.
17. **Meaning non-reinterpretation.** Programme VI questions and EIP claim classes survive close intact.
18. **Implementation neutrality.** No stack becomes law by proximity to a complete flag.
19. **Evolution-complete ≠ quality.** Completion never grades learning, mastery, readiness, or product success.
20. **Explain the stop.** Students, developers, and auditors should hear when close must be refused.
21. **No emergency exemption.** Deadlines and demos never mint completion power or amendment power.
22. **Repeatability survives close.** Close must not invent contradictory dispositions from identical published inputs.

## 3. What Evolution Completion May Do (Lawful)

| Lawful action | Constitutional meaning |
|---------------|------------------------|
| **Confirm lifecycle completion** | Affirm CEC-01…CEC-05 when published criteria hold for a lawful evolution concern |
| **Confirm disposition recording** | Cite that required evolution dispositions were lawfully recorded under published CEG / CEL rules — without rewriting or silently replacing outcomes |
| **Confirm provenance preservation** | Cite that reconstructable historical provenance was preserved under CEL-05 / EIP-005 |
| **Confirm publication preparation where applicable** | Cite that CEL-06 preparation (or refuse-prepare) concluded where required |
| **Preserve governance records** | Leave reconstructable constitutional records of principles, stages, dispositions, provenance, and boundaries |
| **Preserve audit continuity** | Leave reconstructable constitutional records of affirmed or refused close |
| **Refuse unlawful close** | Prefer honest not-yet / refuse / defer / escalate over improvisation |
| **Compose required stages and types** | Confirm fulfilment across published CEL / CEG conjunctions without collapsing failed duties |
| **Remain implementation-neutral** | Honour completion law across lawful equivalent stacks |
| **Preserve governance repeatability** | Ensure same published inputs do not yield contradictory completion stories |
| **Explain completion** | Answer principles / stages / dispositions / provenance / boundaries without rewriting meaning |
| **Propose corpus amendments upstream** | Recommend (not enact) amendments when law itself must change before evolution can complete |

These actions **confirm evolution lifecycle fulfilment**. They do **not** publish a new Constitution, invent coach warrants, mint tips, author ownership maps, invent CEG / CEL / CEC types, author runtime behaviour, evaluate fidelity in place of MS001, amend constitutional specifications, replace evolution governance, redefine constitutional authority, rewrite constitutional history, redesign implementations as constitutional law, or judge educational quality.

| Forbidden action | Why | Lawful alternative |

| **Amend constitutional specifications** | Only corpus writers under lawful amendment processes may enact text | Leave specs unchanged; propose amendment upstream or refuse incomplete evolution |

| **Redefine constitutional authority** | Published corpora remain the standard after any close | Keep dispositions as governance outcomes, never as next-cycle law |
| **Alter constitutional history** | EIP-005 continuity forbids retrospective erasure / rewrite | Preserve prior lawful provenance; refuse destructive “cleanup” |
| **Redefine constitutional meaning** | Close never soft-amends Programme VI–VIII / EIP meaning | Preserve identity of published meaning; never invent it |
| **Create constitutional law** | Completion judgements are not legislation | Cite published law only; refuse unpublished anchors |
| **Govern implementation behaviour** | Delivery redesign is not CEC fulfilment | Affirm lifecycle fulfilment only; never redesign stacks as law |
| **Replace conformance evaluation** | MS001 owns CC disposition | Never claim conformity-from-complete as new law |
| **Perform verification** | WS2 owns verification | Never re-author CV findings under close |
| **Determine compliance** | WS3 owns compliance | Never rewrite CCC / CCM under close |
| **Certify or treat CRTC as evolution authority** | WS4 owns certification; CRTC must not initiate evolution | Compose with CRTC; never collapse CRTC into CEC (or vice versa) |

## 5. Boundary Tests (Pre-Affirmation)

Before material constitutional evolution completion is treated as affirmed, close requires affirmative answers:

| # | Test | Fail ⇒ |
|---|------|--------|
| 1 | Are all required published CEL stages completed (CEC-01)? | not yet / refuse |
| 2 | Was a required evolution disposition lawfully recorded without silent replacement (CEC-02)? | not yet / refuse |
| 3 | Do provenance records support CECQ-01…CECQ-05 (CEC-03)? | refuse |
| 4 | Was publication preparation completed where applicable, or is non-applicability reconstructable (CEC-04)? | not yet / refuse |
| 5 | Was evolution formally closed under CEL-07 / boundary honesty (CEC-05)? | refuse |
| 6 | Would affirmation amend constitutional specifications, replace evolution governance, redefine authority, or alter history? | refuse overclaim |
| 7 | Would affirmation imply educational success or implementation redesign as constitutional law? | refuse |
| 8 | Would affirmation privilege a stack / vendor as law? | refuse |
| 9 | Would affirmation erase prior lawful evolution / corpus history for convenience? | refuse |
| 10 | Can the stop or affirmation be explained honestly to student and auditor? | refuse mute theatre |

Any single fail blocks lawful affirmation.
