## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “pipeline greens,” “tickets closed,” or “merges shown.” The tutor optimises **honest closing of constitutional evolution governance work**: when the published lifecycle for a concern has been fully executed, dispositions recorded, provenance preserved, and publication preparation concluded where applicable, the tutor can say so — without pretending the Constitution was amended by the close, without pretending history was rewritten, without pretending the stack is permanently frozen, without pretending the student has mastered the topic, and without rewriting evolution meaning because the machinery stopped cleanly.

These objectives bind every constitutional evolution completion judgement and every successor evolution machinery that claims constitutional evolution fulfilment.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### CECO-01 — Confirm lifecycle completion

**Definition.** Ensure that runtime / assessment / audit speech affirms evolution completion only when a published evolution concern under WS5 / MS001–MS002 has executed the required CEL-01…CEL-07 lifecycle obligations — or honestly refuses close when fulfilment criteria are not met.

**Includes:**

- Assessing only concerns that lawfully opened under WS5 / MS001–MS002 proposal → classification → assessment → decision → provenance → publication preparation → closure law.
- Affirming completion only when `COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md` conditions (CEC-01…CEC-05) hold.
- Preferring lawful “not yet” / refuse-close over inventing a “done enough” status for product convenience.
- Distinguishing lawful stop dispositions (refuse / defer / escalate / refused as published outcomes that exhaust remaining *evolution-governance* duties with honest recording) from abandoned lifecycle duties.
- Binding every material completion judgement to reconstructable constitutional references.

**Excludes:**

- Declaring complete because a CI job exited, a ticket closed, a merge badge glowed, or a UI spinner stopped.
- Treating Twin / Adaptive / Experience / Mission heuristics as completion warrants.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for lifecycle honesty.
- Collapsing “evolution complete” into “constitution amended,” “history rewritten,” “stack frozen,” “student succeeded,” or “authority transferred.”

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software celebrates unfinished evolution work — or celebrates finished pipeline machinery as finished law-making or history erasure. Honest confirmation is care; completion theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CECO-02 — Preserve provenance

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming evolution completion never occurs without preservation of reconstructable historical provenance under published CEL-05 / continuity obligations — prior corpus identity, successor relationships, category bindings, stage traces, dispositions, and boundary confirmations — without treating provenance records as the corpus rewrite itself.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who declares evolution “finished” without leaving a speakable trail of what was proposed, classified, decided, and historically related has not finished care — and a coach who erases yesterday’s lawful version to tidy the close has abandoned honesty.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion leaves provenance identities reconstructable.
- Close never substitutes pipeline exit codes or ticket states for published provenance content.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing or silently rewriting prior lawful provenance to simplify the next report.
- Missing required provenance after an affirmed close is a defect.

---

### CECO-03 — Preserve explainability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material completion judgement can answer the mandatory explainability questions — which evolution governance principles, which stages, which disposition, which provenance records, which boundaries — without redefining constitutional meaning or implying amendment enactment / history rewrite / authority replacement / implementation governance.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who can only say “it’s fully evolved somewhere” is performing, not teaching. Speakable completion is care.

**Manifestations:**

- CECQ-01…CECQ-05 answers are producible for every material affirmed or refused close.
- Explanations never soft-amend law or narrate completion as authority transfer, history rewrite, or constitutional enactment.
- Audience projections differ in vocabulary, not in constitutional facts.
- Opaque “trust the complete badge” theatre is a defect.

---

### CECO-04 — Preserve audit continuity

**Definition.** Ensure that every material completion judgement leaves a reconstructable trail: which evolution governance principles were applied, which lifecycle stages were completed, which disposition was recorded, which provenance records were preserved, and which boundaries remained intact.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later say *why* evolution was declared finished cannot be trusted. Silent “complete” badges destroy student and auditor trust.

**Manifestations:**

- Audit records preserve constitutional references, not only technical job logs or CI annotations.
- Explainability obligations in `COMPLETION_EXPLAINABILITY.md` apply to all material completion judgements.
- Missing CEC identity against criteria, missing principle identity, missing disposition identity, missing stage identity, or missing provenance identity is a defect.
- Student / developer / auditor projections share one truth with different vocabulary.

---

### CECO-05 — Preserve governance repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming completion preserves the constitutional repeatability obligation: same published artefacts, same proposal, same CEG classification, same stage sequence ⇒ same completion disposition for the same concern scope — so close cannot invent contradictory “done” and “not done” stories from identical published inputs.

**Tutor rationale.** An evolution session that finishes differently for the same published inputs under the same rules is not care; it is lottery dressed as law.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion judgements remain reproducible from published constitutional inputs.
- Hidden stack-specific shortcuts that flip completion for one vendor only are defects.
- Continuity forbids deleting prior lawful stage / disposition / provenance artefacts to force a different close next time.
- Non-deterministic “best effort complete” theatre is a defect.

---

### CECO-06 — Preserve implementation neutrality

**Definition.** Ensure that completion judgements speak to constitutional lifecycle fulfilment independently of programming language, framework, datastore, CI vendor, cloud topology, or preferred Runtime A shape — so no stack becomes constitutional by proximity to a “complete” flag.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who declares only one notebook brand “fully evolution-complete” has abandoned educational law for vendor preference.

**Manifestations:**

- CEC criteria speak to constitutional identities and obligations, not “must use Flask / SQLAlchemy / OpenAPI.”
- Equivalent lawful implementations under the same published law may both reach lawful completion.
- Replacing a stack that still obeys published law does not itself create unlawful completion.
- “Our reference implementation completes this way” is never a substitute for published criteria.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CECO-01 conflicts with inventing unpublished stages so close can complete | Lifecycle honesty wins — refuse / defer / escalate |
| CECO-02 conflicts with mute close that skips or silently rewrites provenance | Provenance honesty wins |
| CECO-03 conflicts with unspeakable “trust us” completion | Explainability wins |
| CECO-04 conflicts with opaque complete badges | Audit continuity wins |
| CECO-05 conflicts with stack-specific non-reproducible close | Governance repeatability wins |
| CECO-06 conflicts with privileging a reference stack as completion law | Implementation neutrality wins |
| Any CECO conflicts with amending specs / redefining meaning / replacing authority / rewriting history / governing implementation | Non-implication honesty wins — refuse the overclaim |

No supporting objective authorises violating CECO-01.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — Constitution / EIP integrity obligations, Programme VI–VIII authority preservation, WS5 / MS001 CEGO duties, and WS5 / MS002 CELO duties for the *completion* horizon.

## 5. Non-Objectives

Constitutional evolution completion does **not** optimise for:

- constitutional amendment enactment, soft rewrite of specifications, or silent corpus change via close;
- redefinition of constitutional meaning outside lawful amendment processes;
- replacement or transfer of constitutional authority;
- retrospective rewrite of constitutional history;
- governing implementation behaviour, release management, or product roadmap authority as constitutional law;
- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- freezing a stack, vendor, or topology as permanent constitutional law;
- CI throughput, flaky-merge suppression, or demo continuity as educational or constitutional success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when required lifecycle stages, dispositions, provenance, or publication preparation remain incomplete;
- collapsing CEC criteria into one mega-flag that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics signals as substitutes for published completion criteria;
- treating documentation of a pipeline as optional commentary on whatever code already shipped;
- declaring constitutional amendment enactment, history rewrite, authority replacement, or implementation redesign solely because completion was affirmed;
- collapsing certification completion (CRTC), compliance completion (CCC), verification completion (CVC), or traceability completion (CTC) into evolution completion.

## 6. Closing Statement

> **A concern that becomes “evolution complete” by rewriting constitutional law — or by redefining meaning, replacing authority, rewriting history, or governing implementation behaviour — is a failed educational system.  
> A concern that confirms only that the required published lifecycle ran according to constitutional governance principles — with dispositions recorded, provenance preserved, publication preparation concluded where applicable, and audit continuity, explainability, repeatability, and neutrality intact — is doing its only completion job.**
