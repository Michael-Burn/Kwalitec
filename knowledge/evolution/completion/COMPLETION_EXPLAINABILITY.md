## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which evolution governance principles* were applied — which lifecycle stages were completed, which disposition was recorded, which provenance records were preserved, and which limits remained intact — is not closing honestly; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–VIII have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, and runtime speech speakable, after WS1–WS4 have made conformance, verification, compliance, and certification speech speakable, and after WS5 / MS001–MS002 have made evolution governance and lifecycle speech speakable, Programme IX still needs one evolution-completion-speech answer:

> **“Which evolution governance principles were applied, which lifecycle stages were completed, which disposition was recorded, which provenance records were preserved, and which constitutional boundaries remained intact?”**

That answer must cover every material affirmed (or refused) completion without inventing educational success, amending constitutional specifications, rewriting history, redefining constitutional authority, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, freezing a stack as law, or modifying constitutional law.

## 2. What Evolution Completion Explainability Is

**Constitutional Evolution Completion Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **fulfilment (or lawful refusal of fulfilment) of the required constitutional evolution lifecycle according to constitutional governance principles** — not to invent a second tutor voice from CI complete badges, and not to grade the student, amend constitutional law, rewrite history, freeze a stack, or redesign implementations as constitutional law.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Evolution governance principles applied** | Named WS5 / MS001 CEG / CEGO / boundary principles consumed for the concern | Which evolution governance principles were applied? |
| **Lifecycle stages completed** | Named CEL-01…CEL-07 stages executed (or lawfully stopped) for the concern | Which lifecycle stages were completed? |
| **Disposition recorded** | Named evolution disposition with lawful outcome | Which disposition was recorded? |
| **Provenance records preserved** | Named reconstructable historical provenance surviving close | Which provenance records were preserved? |
| **Boundaries intact** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden overclaims / amendments / substitutions did not occur | Which constitutional boundaries remained intact? |

Evolution Completion Explainability is:

- **fulfilment-centred** — it narrates why CEC criteria hold (or why close is refused);
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CEG / CEL / CEC identities where applicable;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published specifications, artefacts, named dispositions, and provenance records;
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to amend corpora;
- **non-enacting** — explanation never narrates completion as the corpus rewrite itself;
- **history-honest** — explanation never narrates completion as retrospective history rewrite;
- **disposition-honest** — explanation never silently replaces recorded dispositions;
- **quality-honest** — complete is never narrated as mastery, readiness, or educational quality;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence / validation speech, MS001 conformance speech, MS002 relationship speech, WS2 verification speech, WS3 compliance speech, WS4 certification speech, WS5 / MS001 evolution speech, or WS5 / MS002 lifecycle speech;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match CEC-03 reconstruction needs;
- **authority-honest** — explanations must not paper over authority inference or stack canonisation.

Evolution Completion Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–VIII / WS1–WS5 meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a constitutional amendment instrument or history-rewrite warrant;
- a silent disposition-replacement mechanism;
- an implementation-redesign report as constitutional law;
- a substitute for WS5 / MS001 evolution explainability or WS5 / MS002 lifecycle explainability (compose with; do not replace);
- a substitute for WS4 / MS003 certification completion explainability, WS3 / MS003 compliance completion explainability, WS2 / MS003 verification completion explainability, or WS1 / MS003 traceability completion explainability (compose with; do not collapse);
- a rendering system, UI template, CI badge, merge visualiser, or analytics dashboard;
- a claim that clear completion speech guarantees learning, a pass, conformity, constitutional amendment, or history rewrite;
- a licence to present coverage, lint scores, ticket graphs, or pipeline greens as constitutional proof or educational quality.

## 3. Mandatory Explanation Questions

Every material completion judgement (affirm or refuse close) should answer all five questions:

### CECQ-01 — Which evolution governance principles were applied?

Name the primary published WS5 / MS001 evolution governance principles consumed for the concern — including applicable CEG-01…CEG-07 type bindings, relevant CEGO objectives, and boundary principles — or name which required principles remain unavailable / unapplied when refusing close. Never narrate invented principles or unpublished customs as lawful support. “Governance happened somewhere” is not a constitutional principle identity.

### CECQ-02 — Which lifecycle stages were completed?

Name the primary CEL-01…CEL-07 stages completed for the concern — or name which required stages remain incomplete / refused when refusing close. Include lawful early-stop stage outcomes honestly. Never narrate a skipped mandatory stage as completed because a later pipeline job glowed green. “It ran in CI” is not a constitutional stage identity.

### CECQ-03 — Which disposition was recorded?

Name the primary evolution disposition identities and outcomes recorded under the concern (permitted / refused / deferred / escalated / refuse-*) — or name which required dispositions remain unrecorded when refusing close. Never narrate a refused / deferred / escalated disposition as upgraded by completion, and never narrate a silent replacement of a prior lawful disposition. “Looks permitted enough” is not a constitutional disposition identity.

### CECQ-04 — Which provenance records were preserved?

Name the primary historical provenance records preserved under the concern (artefact refs, CEG category, prior/successor relationships, stage traces, disposition refs, publication-preparation artefacts where applicable, boundary confirmations) — or name which required provenance remains missing when refusing close. Never narrate log volume, ticket graphs, or CI annotations alone as constitutional provenance. Distinguish:

- **disposition records** — which outcome was recorded and under which CEG type;
- **lifecycle records** — which CEL stages ran and with what outcomes;
- **continuity honesty** — prior lawful corpus / evolution history not erased for convenience (EIP-005).

Never narrate preserved provenance as “student is ready,” “mastery confirmed,” “law updated by close,” “this stack is now constitutional,” or “history rewritten.”

### CECQ-05 — Which constitutional boundaries remained intact?

Confirm the hard stops in `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md` that were checked — especially non-amendment of constitutional specifications, non-replacement of evolution governance, non-redefinition of constitutional authority, non-alteration of constitutional history, implementation neutrality, governance repeatability honesty, and non-equation of completion with educational success, implementation redesign, or constitutional truth.

## 5. Audience Rules

| Audience | May hear | Must not hear as educational law |
|----------|----------|----------------------------------|
| **Student** | That formal evolution-governance work against published educational rules has finished (or honestly stopped), why a stop occurred when evolution is incomplete, and what limits apply — in plain educational language | Internal CEC IDs as theatre; CI traces; merge badges as “proof you are ready”; complete as mastery, as constitutional amendment, as history rewrite, or as stack freeze |
| **Developer / auditor** | Full CECQ-01…05 answers, corpus paths, CEG / CEL / CEC IDs, principle refs, disposition identities, provenance inventories, publication-preparation outcomes, boundary checks, neutrality and non-amendment notes | Product success metrics, pipeline greens, or coverage counts substituted for constitutional refs; completion framed as educational quality, new law, history rewrite, or authority replacement |

Student and developer traces must describe the **same constitutional truth**. Vocabulary may differ; facts may not.
