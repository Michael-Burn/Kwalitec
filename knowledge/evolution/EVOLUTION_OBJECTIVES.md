## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “ship the rewrite” or “hide the old rule.” The tutor optimises **honest, continuous, authority-preserving change of educational law**: only published constitutional artefacts that can evolve under recognised CEG-01…CEG-07 types — with continuity, integrity, provenance, explainability, and governance transparency intact — may support a lawful evolution disposition.

These objectives bind every constitutional evolution activity and every successor evolution machinery that claims constitutional evolution governance.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### CEGO-01 — Preserve constitutional continuity

**Definition.** Ensure that every material constitutional evolution preserves the speakability and reconstructability of prior lawful constitutional meaning — so students, developers, and auditors can still say what the law *was*, what it *is*, and how those states relate — without erasing history for product convenience.

**Includes:**

- Binding every material evolution to identifiable published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII / IX artefacts.
- Applying the closed CEG catalogue required by published law for the evolution scope.
- Preferring lawful refused / deferred / escalated dispositions over silent soft amendment or history erasure so a roadmap “passes.”
- Keeping evolution distinct from educational quality, mastery, readiness, product success, implementation redesign, verification / compliance / certification theatre, or authority absorption.
- Preserving the Model’s posture: governance governs change; it does not silently rewrite law or freeze a stack as the next constitution.

**Excludes:**

- Treating green CI, coverage percentages, linter scores, Twin estimates, Adaptive scores, UI ticks, certification seals, or product urgency as constitutional amendment authority.
- Soft-amending specifications in governance prose, inventing unpublished customs, inventing lineage, or privileging “how main works” to force evolution.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for continuity-preserving evolution.
- Declaring “close enough continuity” without documenting preserved historical relationships.
- Erasing prior lawful versions so today’s product looks always-correct.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software pretends yesterday’s educational rule never existed because today’s release needs a clean story. Continuity honesty is care; history erasure is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CEGO-02 — Preserve constitutional integrity

**Definition.** Ensure that evolution never collapses constitutional layering, authority maps, claim honesty, or educational/runtime separation — so change strengthens lawful structure rather than dissolving it.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who “simplifies” by letting runtime invent educational meaning has abandoned integrity.

**Manifestations:**

- Layering duties published by Constitution / EIP / Programmes VI–VIII remain intact across CEG types.
- Authority maps remain owned by published corpora; evolution does not transfer ownership by proximity.
- Claim honesty and mutation rights survive extension, supersession, consolidation, and restoration.
- “Ship a flatter constitution later” improvisation is a constitutional defect unless published under a lawful CEG process that preserves integrity notes.
- Integrity notes compose across multi-bound CEG types without collapsing failed checks.

---

### CEGO-03 — Preserve historical provenance

**Definition.** Ensure that every material evolution leaves a reconstructable trail of prior artefact identity, successor / deprecation / archival / restoration relationships, and disposition — so constitutional lineage remains auditable after change.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later say *which law superseded which* cannot be trusted with syllabus care.

**Manifestations:**

- Provenance records preserve constitutional references, not only git commit hashes or release notes.
- Prior version identity, CEG category, and successor relationships are recorded when material.
- Missing CEG identity, missing artefact refs, missing historical relationship notes, or missing disposition is a defect.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing prior lawful evolution history to simplify the next release story.
- CT relationships that bind specs across change are preserved; invented lineage is a hard stop.

---

### CEGO-04 — Preserve explainability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material constitutional evolution can honestly answer the mandatory explainability questions in `EVOLUTION_EXPLAINABILITY.md` — so students, developers, and auditors hear the same constitutional truth in appropriate vocabulary.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who cannot say *which artefacts* evolved under *which category* and *why* is performing, not governing.

**Manifestations:**

- Explainability obligations apply to all material evolutions.
- Student / developer / auditor projections share one truth with different vocabulary.
- Evolution is never narrated as mastery, readiness, educational quality, silent soft amendment, stack freeze, or WS1–WS4 replacement.
- Stack preference and race-dependent dispositions are defects to disclose, not mysteries to hide.

---

### CEGO-05 — Preserve governance transparency

**Definition.** Ensure that evolution governance remains publicly speakable as governance of *constitutional change* — never disguised as implementation redesign, certification theatre, product urgency override, or retrospective history correction.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who hides a syllabus rewrite inside a “bugfix” has abandoned educational governance.

**Manifestations:**

- Evolution dispositions name the CEG category and constitutional justification explicitly.
- Product urgency never waives CEG requirements or invents amendment power.
- Governance notes never soft-amend Constitution / EIP / Programmes VI–VIII / WS1–WS4 under another workstream’s label.
- “Ship anyway / evolve later / rewrite history quietly” improvisation is a constitutional defect.
- Transparency composes with CEGO-01…CEGO-04 without replacing them.

---

### CEGO-06 — Preserve constitutional authority

**Definition.** Ensure that evolution preserves published corpora as the sole constitutional standard and never lets evolution machinery, preferred stacks, certification seals, or assessment findings absorb constitutional authorship.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who declares “whatever shipped is now the syllabus” has abandoned educational law for vendor preference.

**Manifestations:**

- CEG criteria speak to lawful corpus change, not “must use Flask / SQLAlchemy / OpenAPI.”
- Equivalent lawful corpus evolutions under the same published principles remain speakable without privileging a delivery stack.
- A permitted evolution never makes the implementing team, release, or runtime the next constitutional standard.
- “Our reference implementation needs this rewrite” is never a substitute for a published CEG justification.
- Evolution dispositions never replace Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–VIII authorship rules.

---

### CEGO-07 — Govern change without replacing assessment workstreams

**Definition.** Ensure that evolution strengthens lawful change of constitutional law without rewriting CC / CT / CV / CCM / CRT meaning, inventing assessment findings, or presenting evolution as a substitute for conformance, verification, compliance, or certification.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who says “we evolved the law so we no longer need verification” has abandoned honesty.

**Manifestations:**

- Evolution may consume assessment findings as *motivating evidence* for proposals; it may not amend those findings or treat them as automatic amendment authority.
- Evolution may preserve CT relationships across change; it may not invent lineage.
- “Evolved” must never be narrated as “permanently conformant,” “verification skipped,” “compliance waived,” or “certified forever.”
- Missing published artefact identity or missing CEG category remains a hard stop even when a team wants a fast rewrite story.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CEGO-01 conflicts with “helping” by erasing prior law | Continuity wins — refuse / defer / escalate |
| CEGO-02 conflicts with flattening authority maps for convenience | Integrity wins |
| CEGO-03 conflicts with log volume reduction that erases provenance | Historical provenance wins |
| CEGO-04 conflicts with opaque “trust us” rewrites | Explainability wins |
| CEGO-05 conflicts with urgency that hides change as bugfix | Governance transparency wins |
| CEGO-06 conflicts with privileging a reference stack as next-cycle law | Authority preservation wins |
| CEGO-07 conflicts with treating evolution as a waiver of WS1–WS4 | Govern-without-replacement wins |

No supporting objective authorises violating CEGO-01.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — Constitution / EIP integrity and continuity obligations, Programme VI–VIII authority preservation, and WS1–WS4 assessment honesty for the evolution horizon.

---

## 5. Non-Objectives

Constitutional evolution governance does **not** optimise for:

- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- CI throughput, flaky-test suppression, coverage theatre, or seal theatre as educational success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when artefact identity or justification is incomplete;
- collapsing CEG types into one mega-rewrite that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics / certification signals as substitutes for published CEG justification;
- directly amending constitutional specifications under the label “evolution governance”;
- performing verification, compliance, or certification under the label “evolution”;
- treating documentation as optional commentary on whatever code already shipped;
- replacing MS001 conformance evaluation, MS002 lineage preservation, WS2 verification, WS3 compliance, or WS4 certification;
- retrospectively altering constitutional history for product narrative convenience.

## 6. Closing Statement

> **An evolution that becomes “lawful” by silently rewriting constitutional text, erasing history, or absorbing authority is a failed educational system.  
> An evolution that governs only published artefacts under recognised CEG types — with continuity, integrity, provenance, explainability, and governance transparency intact — is doing its only evolution job.**
