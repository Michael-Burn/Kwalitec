## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which constitutional artefacts* are affected — which evolution category applies, why evolution is constitutionally justified, which historical relationships are preserved, and which constitutional boundaries remain intact — is not coaching; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–VIII have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, and runtime speech speakable, and after WS1–WS4 have made conformance, lineage, verification, compliance, and certification speech speakable, Programme IX still needs one evolution-speech answer:

> **“Which constitutional artefacts are affected, which evolution category applies, why is evolution constitutionally justified, which historical relationships are preserved, and which constitutional boundaries remain intact?”**

That answer must cover every material evolution activity without inventing educational meaning, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, silently rewriting specifications in explanation prose, erasing history, freezing a stack as law, inventing unpublished CEG types, replacing WS1–WS4 evaluation, or presenting evolution as educational quality.

## 2. What Evolution Explainability Is

**Constitutional Evolution Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **lawful governance of named published constitutional artefacts under published CEG types, with constitutional justification, preserved historical relationships, and intact boundaries** — not to invent a second tutor voice from rewrite machinery, not to grade the student, and not to freeze an implementation as constitutional law.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Artefacts affected** | Named published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII / IX artefacts (and clauses as applicable) under evolution | Which constitutional artefacts are affected? |
| **Evolution category** | Named CEG-01…CEG-07 binding(s) | Which evolution category applies? |
| **Constitutional justification** | Published integrity / continuity / authority / educational-care rationale — never mere urgency | Why is evolution constitutionally justified? |
| **Historical relationships preserved** | Named predecessor / successor / deprecation / archival / restoration / consolidation relationships | Which historical relationships are preserved? |
| **Boundaries intact** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden bypass / history rewrite / workstream replacement / implementation redesign did not occur | Which constitutional boundaries remain intact? |

Evolution Explainability is:

- **change-centred** — it narrates artefacts → category → justification → history → boundaries under law;
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CEG identities;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published artefacts;
- **history-honest** — every material justification preserves prior version / relationship identity (or names the hard stop when provenance would be erased);
- **non-legislative-by-prose** — explanation never becomes a back door to soft-amend corpora;
- **non-substitutive** — evolution is never narrated as replacement of WS1–WS4 assessment;
- **quality-honest** — evolution is never narrated as mastery, readiness, or educational quality;
- **authority-honest** — evolution is never narrated as absorption of constitutional authorship by machinery or stacks;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence / validation speech, or WS1–WS4 assessment speech;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match CEGO-03 reconstruction needs;
- **continuity-aligned** — explanation components match EIP-005 / CEGO-01 continuity needs.

Evolution Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–VIII / WS1–WS4 meaning in explanatory prose;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a substitute for CC / CV / CCM / CRT findings;
- a substitute for CT lineage speech;
- a rendering system, UI template, CI badge, release note generator, log shipper, or analytics dashboard;
- a claim that clear evolution speech guarantees learning, a pass, better implementations, or silent soft amendment legitimacy;
- a licence to present coverage, lint scores, pipeline greens, certification seals, or roadmaps as constitutional proof;
- a retrospective history rewrite narrated as “clarification.”

## 3. Mandatory Explanation Questions

Every material constitutional evolution activity should answer all five questions:

### CEGEQ-01 — Which constitutional artefacts are affected?

Name the primary published constitutional artefacts (corpus paths, catalogue IDs, and clauses as applicable) under evolution. Unpublished tribal norms and “how main works” are not constitutional artefacts.

### CEGEQ-02 — Which evolution category applies?

Name the primary CEG-01…CEG-07 type(s) and the published permitted / prohibited governance criteria applied. Cross-cutting evolution names all bound types (for example CEG-04 + CEG-06). “We updated the docs” is not a constitutional category.

### CEGEQ-03 — Why is evolution constitutionally justified?

Name the constitutional justification — integrity, continuity, authority preservation, educational care, conflict resolution under published law — that supports the disposition. “Product deadline,” “CI green,” “certificate issued,” or “preferred stack needs this” are not constitutional justifications.

### CEGEQ-04 — Which historical relationships are preserved?

Name the primary predecessor / successor / deprecation / archival / restoration / consolidation relationships preserved (or required) by the evolution. If required provenance would be erased or invented, say so and record the hard stop. “Git history somewhere” is not sufficient when constitutional provenance is required.

### CEGEQ-05 — Which constitutional boundaries remain intact?

Confirm the hard stops in `EVOLUTION_BOUNDARIES.md` that were checked — especially non-bypass of constitutional authority, non-redefinition of implementation behaviour as law, non-replacement of WS1–WS4 workstreams, non-retrospective history alteration, implementation neutrality, and non-equation of evolution with educational quality or silent soft amendment.

## 5. Audience Rules

| Audience | May hear | Must not hear as educational law |
|----------|----------|----------------------------------|
| **Student** | Plain speech that study-system rules for a named scope changed deliberately (or honestly did not), with continuity of what still matters | “You have mastered the topic,” “you are exam-ready,” “the old rules never counted,” “this app is now the law” |
| **Developer** | CEG identities, artefact refs, justification, historical relationships, disposition, boundary notes | Soft amendments by prose, history erasure, “evolve from green CI,” stack-as-law |
| **Auditor** | Full reconstructable trail answering CEGEQ-01…CEGEQ-05 with CEGEC components | Missing CEG / artefact / justification / history identities; erased prior lawful versions |

Vocabulary may differ; truth must not.

## 6. Refusal and Deferral Speech

When evolution governance must refuse a proposed change, explanation remains mandatory.

Lawful refusal speech:

- names the missing or unpublished artefacts and/or missing justification;
- names the CEG type(s) that could not lawfully govern;
- names the boundary that stopped evolution (for example history-rewrite risk, authority bypass, workstream replacement risk);
- refuses to invent provenance or soft-amend specifications in prose.

Lawful deferral / escalation speech:

- names what published law is waiting for (for example conflict unresolved under continuity; owning Programme stewardship required);
- names the escalation owner when published law requires human / corpus escalation;
- does not narrate deferral as “evolved enough for demo.”

## 7. Non-Explanation Catalogue

The following are **not** constitutional evolution explanations:

- release notes, changelog bullets, or PR titles without CEGEQ answers;
- coverage percentages, lint scores, pipeline greens, or certification seals presented as evolution authority;
- Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics narratives presented as constitutional justification;
- “trusted because Runtime A shipped” or “trusted because main looks like this”;
- quality-release speech from Educational Validation Framework presented as CEG disposition;
- conformance, verification, compliance, or certification speech silently re-labelled as evolution;
- any prose that soft-amends Constitution / EIP / Programmes VI–VIII / WS1–WS4 under an “explanation” label;
- any prose that erases or rewrites constitutional history retrospectively.
