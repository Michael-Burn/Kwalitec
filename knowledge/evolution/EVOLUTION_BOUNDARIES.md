## 1. Purpose

Evolution governance that silently becomes a second constitution — rewriting specs under “clarification,” erasing superseded history so today’s product looks always-correct, licensing runtime redesign as constitutional change, treating certification seals as amendment authority, replacing WS1–WS4 with evolution theatre, or freezing a preferred stack as next-cycle law — destroys student trust and educational integrity.

This document draws a bright line between **lawful constitutional evolution governance** (Programme IX / WS5 / MS001) and **corpus authorship / educational judgement / runtime authorship / assessment authorship / implementation governance** (Constitution / EIP / Programmes VI–VIII / WS1–WS4 / permitted writers / delivery owners).

---

## 2. Boundary Principles

1. **Govern, do not silently enact.** Evolution governance classifies and dispositions change; it does not itself silently rewrite corpus text.
2. **Published artefacts only.** Unpublished customs and “main as law” are hard stops as evolution subjects.
3. **Published evolution types only.** Unpublished CEG customs are unlawful.
4. **Continuity first.** Prior lawful meaning remains historically speakable after every permitted change.
5. **History preserved.** Retrospective alteration of constitutional history is a hard stop.
6. **Provenance required.** Deprecation, supersession, consolidation, archival, and restoration leave reconstructable relationships.
7. **Authority first.** Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard after every disposition.
8. **Implementation independence.** No stack, vendor, or reference implementation becomes law by proximity to evolution.
9. **Evolved ≠ quality.** Evolution never grades learning, mastery, readiness, or product success.
10. **Evolved ≠ assessment waiver.** Evolution never replaces conformance, verification, compliance, or certification.
11. **No silent soft amendment.** Governance prose never becomes an unpublished rewrite of meaning.
12. **No authority substitution.** Evolution machinery never replaces the corpus for the next judgement.
13. **No workstream replacement.** WS1–WS4 remain assessment owners; evolution does not absorb them.
14. **No bypass.** Product urgency never waives CEG requirements or invents amendment power.
15. **No implementation redesign under constitutional colour.** Evolution governs law change, not delivery fashion.
16. **Explain the stop.** Students, developers, and auditors should hear when evolution must refuse.
17. **No emergency exemption.** Deadlines and demos never mint constitutional amendment power via evolution theatre.

## 3. What Evolution Governance May Do (Lawful)

| Lawful action | Constitutional meaning |
|---------------|------------------------|
| **Govern lawful constitutional evolution** | Apply CEG-01…CEG-07 to classify and disposition proposed corpus change under published principles |
| **Preserve constitutional history** | Keep prior lawful versions speakable across amendment, deprecation, supersession, archival, and restoration |
| **Preserve provenance** | Keep reconstructable trails of artefact identity, category, justification, relationships, and disposition |
| **Preserve continuity** | Honour EIP-005 so students / developers / auditors can relate prior and current law honestly |
| **Classify under the closed catalogue** | Bind material change to CEG-01…CEG-07 only |
| **Refuse unlawful evolution claims** | Prefer honest refused / deferred / escalated over improvisation |
| **Compose required types** | Apply published CEG conjunctions without collapsing failed checks |
| **Remain implementation-neutral** | Honour evolution law across lawful equivalent stacks |
| **Support without replacing neighbours** | Strengthen change honesty without replacing CC / CT / CV / CCM / CRT meaning |
| **Explain evolution** | Answer artefacts / category / justification / history / boundaries without rewriting meaning |
| **Authorise lawful amendment processes upstream** | Permit (not silently enact) corpus change under owning Programme / Constitution / EIP authorship rules |

These actions **govern constitutional change**. They do **not** publish a new Constitution by proximity, invent coach warrants, mint tips, author ownership maps, invent CEG types, invent CT links, invent CV / CCM / CRT artefacts, author runtime behaviour, judge educational quality, redesign implementations as law, or freeze stacks as constitutional authority.

| Forbidden action | Why | Lawful alternative |
|------------------|-----|--------------------|
| **Bypass constitutional authority** | Published corpora remain the standard | Cite owning Constitution / EIP / Programme authorship; refuse urgency overrides |
| **Redefine implementation behaviour** | Evolution governs law, not delivery fashion | Keep runtime redesign in implementation governance; evolve law only when published justification exists |
| **Replace governance workstreams** | WS1–WS4 own assessment horizons | Govern corpus change; never waive or re-run CC / CV / CCM / CRT under CEG labels |
| **Alter constitutional history retrospectively** | Continuity and audit depend on intact prior law | Preserve prior versions; use CEG-03 / CEG-04 / CEG-06 / CEG-07 honestly |
| **Directly amend constitutional specifications under the governance label** | Governance is not silent enactment | Disposition under CEG; enact only via lawful authorship processes |
| **Redefine educational meaning outside lawful amendment processes** | Programme VI / EIP own meaning | Require named CEG-01 / CEG-02 / CEG-04 process with owning authority |
| **Replace constitutional authority** | Evolution machinery is not the corpus | Keep dispositions subordinate to published law |
| **Invent unpublished evolution types** | Catalogue is closed | Use CEG-01…CEG-07 only; propose catalogue amendment under this Model if needed |
| **Invent traceability / erase lineage** | CT and provenance own lawful relationships | Preserve established relationships; refuse invented or erased history |
| **Treat certification / CI / seals as amendment authority** | Assessment and delivery proxies are not evolution law | Require published CEG justification |
| **Privilege a technology as constitutional** | Implementation neutrality | Govern meaning change, not Flask / SQLAlchemy / REST / OpenAPI fashion |

| **Become irreplaceable by evolution topology** | Replaceability | Keep evolution law implementation-independent |

## 6. Hard-Stop Catalogue

Evolution **must stop** (refused / deferred / escalated as published law requires) when any of the following hold:

1. Required published constitutional artefacts under evolution are unidentified or unpublished.
2. Required CEG type(s) for the scope cannot be applied without inventing a type.
3. Disposition would require bypassing constitutional authority or owning Programme authorship rules.
4. Disposition would redefine educational meaning outside a lawful amendment process.
5. Disposition would redefine implementation behaviour as constitutional law.
6. Disposition would replace WS1–WS4 governance workstreams or waive their meaning.
7. Disposition would alter constitutional history retrospectively or erase provenance.
8. Disposition would privilege a stack, vendor, or reference implementation as constitutional law.
9. Disposition would treat educational quality, mastery, readiness, or product success as evolution authority.
10. Disposition would treat green CI, coverage, lint, Twin / Adaptive / UI proxies, or certification seals as substitutes for published CEG justification.

> **Evolution governance may govern lawful constitutional evolution, preserve constitutional history, preserve provenance, and preserve continuity.  
> Evolution governance must never bypass constitutional authority, redefine implementation behaviour, replace governance workstreams, or alter constitutional history retrospectively.
