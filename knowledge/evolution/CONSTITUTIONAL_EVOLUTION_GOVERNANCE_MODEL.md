Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not quietly rewrite yesterday’s syllabus rule because today’s code is convenient, erase the history of a superseded teaching rule to hide inconsistency, or treat a product roadmap as educational law. After the Constitution and EIP have defined *educational law*, after Programmes VI–VIII have published *meaning, orchestration, and runtime law*, and after WS1–WS4 have defined *how implementations may be judged against that law*, the platform still needs one evolution answer:

> **“Under what constitutional principles may the constitutional corpus be amended, extended or superseded?”**

That answer must ensure evolution **governs constitutional change**; **preserves constitutional continuity**; **preserves constitutional integrity**; **preserves constitutional authority**; and **preserves historical traceability** — without directly amending constitutional specifications, redefining educational meaning outside lawful amendment processes, replacing constitutional authority, or governing implementation details.

This document records that posture so every future constitutional evolution (and successor) has a single constitutional reference for *how constitutional law may change without becoming product fiction or silent soft amendment*.

> **The Constitutional Evolution Governance Model describes the constitutional framework governing lawful evolution of the constitutional corpus.  
> It does not create educational meaning by improvisation, invent ownership, mint tips, invent runtime behaviour, directly amend specifications under the label “governance,” or become a substitute for constitutional authority.**

## 2. What Constitutional Evolution Governance Is

**Constitutional evolution governance** is the binding framework by which **constitutional change** may be **lawfully organised and classified**, yielding **evolution dispositions** and **historical provenance records** that preserve constitutional continuity, integrity, authority, and historical traceability — without becoming a silent amendment act, a second constitution, an implementation governance bureau, or a freeze of any stack as permanent constitutional authority.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Published constitutional corpus** | Constitution, EIP, Programme VI / VII / VIII / IX specifications that publish educational and execution law | Which artefacts may lawfully evolve? |
| **Constitutional evolution** | Lawful change of published constitutional artefacts under recognised CEG types | What kind of change is proposed? |
| **Evolution category** | A recognised CEG-01…CEG-07 change class | Which evolution category applies? |
| **Evolution disposition** | Lawful governance outcome: permitted / refused / deferred / escalated — never a silent rewrite of history | May this evolution proceed under published principles? |
| **Historical provenance** | Reconstructable record of prior corpus identity, successor relationships, and preserved boundaries | Which historical relationships are preserved? |
| **Constitutional continuity** | Prior lawful meaning remains speakable after change; history is not erased for convenience | Does continuity survive? |
| **Constitutional integrity** | Change does not collapse layering, authority maps, or claim honesty | Does integrity survive? |
| **Constitutional authority** | The exclusive right of published corpora to define educational and execution meaning | Who owns the standard after evolution? |
| **Historical traceability** | Prior versions, supersessions, deprecations, archives, and restorations remain reconstructable | Can the lineage of law be reconstructed? |
| **Explainability** | Affected artefacts, category, justification, history, and boundaries are speakable | Can the evolution be narrated honestly? |

Constitutional evolution governance is:

- **change-governing** — it governs how the corpus may lawfully evolve; it never silently rewrites the corpus under the label “governance”;
- **continuity-preserving** — prior lawful meaning remains historically speakable after change;
- **integrity-preserving** — layering, authority maps, and claim honesty survive evolution pressure;
- **authority-preserving** — published corpora remain the sole source of constitutional meaning; evolution machinery never absorbs that role;
- **history-preserving** — supersession, deprecation, archival, and restoration leave reconstructable provenance;
- **catalogue-closed** — only CEG-01…CEG-07 may be applied as constitutional evolution types;
- **corpus-bound** — every material evolution cites identifiable published artefacts;
- **non-legislative-by-proximity** — product urgency, CI greens, certification seals, and preferred stacks never invent amendment power;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool becomes law by proximity to evolution;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which artefacts changed under which category and why;
- **non-substitutive** — evolution governance never replaces WS1–WS4 assessment horizons or Programme VI–VIII authorship rules.

Constitutional evolution governance is **not**:

- a licence to directly edit constitutional specifications outside lawful amendment processes;
- a second Educational Constitution, EIP, or Programme VI / VII / VIII / IX soft-amendment bureau;
- a rewrite of educational meaning disguised as “clarification” without a published CEG category;
- a testing framework, CI/CD pipeline, GitHub Action, release train, or product roadmap authority;
- a claim that “evolved” guarantees learning, mastery, exam readiness, product success, or better implementations;
- a substitute for MS001 conformance, MS002 traceability, WS2 verification, WS3 compliance, or WS4 certification;
- a retrospective rewrite of constitutional history to make today’s product look always-correct;
- a freeze of implementations, stacks, vendors, or releases as constitutional law;
- implementation governance dressed as constitutional care.

## 3. Evolution Governance Posture (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material constitutional evolution activity travels through four constitutional phases. These are **normative governance obligations**, not an implementation architecture.

```
Identify published constitutional artefacts proposed for evolution
        │
        ▼
Classify under CEG-01…CEG-07 and bind constitutional justification
        │
        ▼
Govern disposition (permitted / refused / deferred / escalated)
        │
        ├── refused / deferred / escalated (lawful stop — history and authority intact)
        │
        └── permitted → Preserve historical provenance and continuity records
                │
                ▼
        Explain artefacts / category / justification / history / boundaries
```

| Phase | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **Identify** | Name only published constitutional artefacts as subjects of evolution | Subject is unpublished custom, tribal knowledge, or “how main works today” |
| **Classify** | Apply CEG-01…CEG-07 and bind a constitutional justification | Evolution would invent a type, redefine meaning outside lawful process, or privilege a stack as law |
| **Govern** | Issue a disposition without silently rewriting history or absorbing authority | Path would bypass constitutional authority, alter history retrospectively, or replace WS1–WS4 |
| **Record** | Preserve provenance, continuity, and explainability without becoming the amendment act itself | Path would treat governance prose as a silent corpus rewrite or erase prior lawful versions |

> **Evolution governance never “best-effort invents” a fifth phase called silent soft amendment, retrospective history rewrite, stack canonisation, or implementation redesign under constitutional colour.**
