## Authority

This document is the constitutional overview of the **Educational Recommendation Model** for Kwalitec.

It is subordinate to the Educational Constitution, specialised Programme VI educational models, and Programme VII Workstreams 1–2. It governs **what a constitutional educational recommendation is** — its objectives, lawful sources, structure, boundaries, and explainability. It does not authorise implementation shortcuts that contradict the Constitution, and it does not invent educational meaning, alter ownership, or execute workflows.

Authority order for educational recommendations:

> Constitution defines educational truth and curriculum primacy.  
> Knowledge & Mastery defines coverage ≠ understanding ≠ mastery.  
> Evidence Model defines what may warrant educational claims.  
> Continuity Standard preserves rightful educational history.

> Programme VII Workstream 2 defines who owns decisions, how valid recommendations compete for action, and how permission is explained.  
> **This Educational Recommendation Model (Programme VII / Workstream 3 / MS001) defines what a constitutional educational recommendation is.**  
> Downstream Runtime A, product surfaces, and narration must consume this recommendation law — never invent tips from undocumented sources or silent product preference.

## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not invent advice in a vacuum. After Programme VI has defined *what each educational question means*, after Workstream 1 has defined *how reasoning flows*, and after Workstream 2 has defined *who owns decisions and how concurrent valid guidance is dispositioned*, the tutor still needs one communication answer:

> **“What is a constitutional educational recommendation?”**

That answer must communicate lawful educational guidance, preserve constitutional meaning, unify lawful educational outputs into one speakable artefact, support learner decision-making, and remain fully explainable — without creating educational meaning, altering constitutional authority, or executing workflows.

This document records that tutor posture so every future Educational Recommendation Engine subsystem has a single educational reference for *what recommendations are and are not*.

> **The Educational Recommendation Model describes constitutional educational recommendations.  
> It does not create educational meaning, alter ownership, or implement Runtime A.**

## 2. What a Constitutional Educational Recommendation Is

A **constitutional educational recommendation** is the authorised communicative artefact by which Kwalitec **surfaces lawful educational guidance** to a learner (or to a developer/auditor trace) — derived only from documented constitutional sources, carrying a named owner, supporting evidence, orchestration context, and constitutional references — without inventing meaning, rewriting ownership, or performing orchestration.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Educational guidance** | The speakable next-step or posture the student may lawfully treat as coach advice | What is recommended? |
| **Constitutional owner** | Programme VI (or other named) component accountable for the educational decision | Who owns this guidance? |
| **Supporting educational evidence** | Observational or constitutional warrants that justify the claim under EIP-002 / EIP-006 | What supports it? |
| **Orchestration context** | Workflow / authority / conflict facts that situate the guidance without becoming the guidance | Under what coordination? |
| **Constitutional references** | Documented models, principles, and boundaries that make the artefact valid | Why is it lawful? |
| **Recommendation validity** | Satisfaction of sources, structure, boundaries, and explainability in this corpus | Is this a recommendation or an unlawful tip? |

A constitutional educational recommendation is:

- **communicative** — it conveys guidance already authorised; it does not mint new educational truth;
- **owner-bound** — every material tip names a constitutional owner from the Authority Model;
- **source-bound** — provenance is limited to documented sources (`RECOMMENDATION_SOURCES.md`);
- **structured** — required components are present (`RECOMMENDATION_STRUCTURE.md`);
- **meaning-preserving** — Programme VI educational meanings are unchanged by communication itself;
- **non-executing** — recommendations do not run workflows, write states, or pack calendars;
- **unifyable** — they may aggregate lawful outputs under published structure without silent merge of meanings;
- **explainable** — students and developers can answer why the recommendation exists and why it is constitutionally valid;
- **honest about limits** — refusal, deferral, and “no recommendation” remain first-class when no warrant exists.

A constitutional educational recommendation is **not**:

- a second Daily Coach, Learning Coach, Recovery Coach, Revision Coach, Exam Coach, or Master Planner;
- a licence to invent tips, scores, or “AI tutor” speech without Programme VI warrant;
- reinterpretation of Educational Evidence under a recommendation label;
- transfer or absorption of constitutional ownership;
- workflow execution, stage advancement, or conflict resolution itself;
- a UI card, notification payload, API schema, or analytics event;
- a claim that following the recommendation guarantees learning or a pass.

## 3. Educational Purpose

The Educational Recommendation Model exists so that:

1. **Guidance remains lawful** — every material tip traces to Programme VI meaning and Programme VII coordination, never to undocumented heuristics.
2. **Constitutional meaning survives packaging** — surfacing a recommendation never rewrites coach questions or plan contracts.
3. **Lawful outputs can be unified** — workflow context, ownership, conflict disposition, and coach artefacts compose into one speakable recommendation without becoming a mega-coach.
4. **Learners can decide** — recommendations support informed educational action; they do not coerce or obscure limits.
5. **Explainability is end-to-end** — why / contributors / owner / evidence / validity are always answerable (EIP-003; EL-008).

The Educational Recommendation Model is educationally responsible for:

The Educational Recommendation Model must **not**:

- redefine Programme VI educational meaning or coach questions;
- invent workflow stages, transition conditions, or completion criteria;
- amend Authority Model domains or Conflict Resolution CT/RP/RO catalogues by fiat;
- grant or invent EIP-001 mutation rights;
- implement ranking, scoring, personalisation mathematics, or Runtime A services;

- absorb ownership of Educational Evidence, Estimated Knowledge, or Estimated Mastery under a “recommendation engine” label;
- treat Version 2 Adaptive / Twin / Mission authorities as replacements for Programme VI / VII recommendation law;
- silently merge competing coach meanings into a third mega-recommendation without a published constitutional merge pathway (WS2 / MS002 RO-03).

## 7. Integrity Rules

1. **Communicate, do not create.** Recommendations surface authorised guidance; they do not invent educational meaning.
2. **Documented sources only.** Undocumented provenance makes a tip educationally unlawful.
3. **Owner required.** Every material recommendation names its constitutional owner.
4. **Evidence honest.** Supporting evidence remains observational / constitutional warrant — never re-labelled coverage as understanding or mastery.
5. **Context is not meaning.** Orchestration, authority, and conflict context situate guidance; they do not replace Programme VI reasoning.
6. **Structure before speech.** Incomplete artefacts must not be presented as constitutional recommendations.
7. **Boundaries over packaging.** Product pressure to “just show a tip” never overrides `RECOMMENDATION_BOUNDARIES.md`.
8. **Conflict law untouched.** When concurrency exists, disposition remains WS2 / MS002; this Model does not invent ranking.
9. **Explain or refuse.** Material recommendations must satisfy `RECOMMENDATION_EXPLAINABILITY.md` or must not ship as constitutional.
10. **Amend before invent.** Missing source, structure, or objective → amend this corpus (or Programme VI / VII siblings) — do not invent in Runtime A.

## 11. Closing

Educational recommendations are how Kwalitec *speaks* lawful tutor guidance — not how it *invents* a tutor.

When a tip would lack a documented source, **refuse**.  
When ownership or meaning would need rewriting to “make the tip work,” **amend the owning corpora** — do not recommend by reinterpretation.  
When concurrency arises, **apply Conflict Resolution** — do not invent a ranking tip.  
When the artefact is complete and lawful, **communicate and explain**.

> **Recommendations communicate constitutional outcomes.  
> They never create educational meaning.**
