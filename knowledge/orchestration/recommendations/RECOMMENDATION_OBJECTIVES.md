## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “number of tips shown.” The tutor optimises **lawful, coherent educational guidance**: the student hears the right constitutional voice, with honest evidence and limits, so they can decide what to do next.

These objectives bind every Educational Recommendation Engine behaviour and surface.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### ERO-01 — Communicate educational guidance

**Definition.** Ensure that constitutionally authorised educational guidance is speakable to the learner as a clear recommendation — or as an honest refusal / deferral / escalation — without inventing a tip when no Programme VI warrant exists.

**Includes:**

- Surfacing Daily Coach “what today” guidance under an Active-class Canonical Study Plan.
- Surfacing Learning Coach progression, obstacle, or intervention guidance when that question is primary.
- Surfacing Recovery, Revision, or Exam Coach guidance when those warrants lead.
- Surfacing Master Planner / structural handoff speech when orchestration escalates beyond coach envelopes.
- Communicating deferred, queued, or superseded dispositions when WS2 / MS002 has lawfully dispositioned concurrency.
- Preferring “no recommendation yet” over fabricated certainty.

**Excludes:**

- Generating motivational filler that is not grounded in an authorised artefact.
- Optimising for tip volume, click-through, or engagement as a substitute for educational honesty.
- Packaging multiple coach meanings into one undifferentiated “AI tutor” tip.
- Communicating guidance that bypasses workflow authority checks or ownership law.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when the student cannot tell what to do next — or when advice is invented to fill a UI slot. Communication is educational care; invention is educational harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### ERO-02 — Preserve constitutional meaning

**Definition.** Ensure that packaging and communicating a recommendation never redefines Programme VI educational questions, Canonical Study Plan commitments, Evidence claim types, or Authority Model domains.

**Tutor rationale.** A tip that “helpfully” rewrites revision as first learning, or plan intent as daily convenience, destroys constitutional layering even if the wording feels smoother.

**Manifestations:**

- Recommendation text consumes coach / planner meaning; it does not amend it.
- Claim ladder honesty (coverage ≠ understanding ≠ mastery) is preserved in speech (EIP-006).
- Ownership labels on the artefact match WS2 / MS001 — never product proximity.
- Forbidden actions in `RECOMMENDATION_BOUNDARIES.md` are hard stops.

---

### ERO-03 — Unify lawful educational outputs

**Definition.** Ensure that Programme VI guidance, Authority ownership, Workflow context, and (when applicable) Conflict Resolution disposition compose into **one** constitutional recommendation artefact under published structure — without silent merge of competing meanings.

**Tutor rationale.** Students should not assemble a tutor from scattered coach fragments. Unification is packaging of already-lawful outputs, not creation of a fourth tutor.

**Manifestations:**

- Required structural components assembled per `RECOMMENDATION_STRUCTURE.md`.
- Concurrent valid tips dispositioned under WS2 / MS002 before a single acted-upon recommendation is communicated as primary.
- Orchestration context attached as context, not as substitute meaning.
- Merge allowed only where Conflict Resolution publishes a constitutional merge pathway (RO-03).

---

### ERO-04 — Support learner decision-making

**Definition.** Ensure recommendations help the student choose a lawful next educational action — including understanding limits, alternatives not taken, and when to wait or escalate — rather than obscure choice behind opaque certainty.

**Tutor rationale.** An expert tutor coaches judgement. Recommendations that coerce, hide trade-offs, or overclaim readiness undermine learner agency and exam integrity.

**Manifestations:**

- Clear next-step or clear pause / refuse / escalate posture.
- Honest limits when evidence is thin or warrants fail.
- No punitive catch-up theatre or exam theatre from calendar alone.
- Decision support speech remains subordinate to EIP-003 student-language rules.

---

### ERO-05 — Remain fully explainable

**Definition.** Ensure every material recommendation can answer why it exists, which constitutional components contributed, who owns it, what evidence supports it, and why it is constitutionally valid (`RECOMMENDATION_EXPLAINABILITY.md`).

**Tutor rationale.** Opaque tips feel like a black box even when Programme VI reasoning underneath is sound. Explainability is part of educational integrity (EIP-003; EL-008).

**Manifestations:**

- Trace links from sources → owner → evidence → context → validity.
- Student speech without internal IDs; developer traces with document and component IDs.
- Explainability never used to invent certainty the underlying artefact did not claim.

## 5. Priority Among Objectives

When objectives appear to tension:

1. **ERO-02 (meaning preservation)** and boundary honesty precede packaging convenience.
2. **ERO-05 (explainability)** precedes motivational brevity that would hide provenance.
3. **ERO-01 (communication)** prefers honest refusal over invented tips that would satisfy engagement metrics.
4. **ERO-03 (unification)** never overrides Conflict Resolution or single-owner law to force a mega-tip.
5. **ERO-04 (decision support)** never authorises overclaiming mastery, readiness, or plan rewrites.

Product metrics may proxy these objectives. Proxies that invert this order are educationally unlawful.

---

## 6. Non-Objectives

The following are **not** recommendation objectives under this Model:

- Maximising tip count, session length, or notification frequency
- Ranking coaches by undocumented scores
- Optimising calendar density or “coverage theatre”
- Substituting Twin / Adaptive infrastructure authority for Programme VI meaning
- Treating recommendation communication as state mutation or workflow execution
- Guaranteeing exam passes or learning outcomes

---

## 7. Closing

Recommendation objectives keep Kwalitec’s tips tutor-like: clear, lawful, unified, decision-supporting, and explainable.

When a surface would optimise engagement over ERO-02 or ERO-05, **stop**.  
When unification would require inventing meaning, **refuse or amend Programme VI / conflict law** — do not unify by reinterpretation.

> **Communicate lawful guidance. Preserve meaning. Explain everything.**
