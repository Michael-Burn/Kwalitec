## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not only *decide* who may speak — the tutor can also *say why* that voice was the rightful one, and why another voice was not. After MS001 has named owners and MS002 has dispositioned concurrency, the platform still needs one constitutional explanation question answered consistently:

> **“Why was this constitutional component permitted to make this decision, and why were alternative components not permitted to do so?”**

That answer must cover ordinary authority decisions, delegated exercise, conflict resolution, superseded recommendations, and constitutionally permitted merges — without inventing educational meaning, transferring ownership, performing orchestration, or publishing undeclared rules.

This document records that tutor posture so every future Educational Authority Engine subsystem has a single educational reference for *how authority decisions are explained*.

> **Authority Decision Explainability describes constitutional reasoning about permission and refusal.  
> It does not redefine educational meaning, alter ownership, or orchestrate workflows.**

## 2. What Authority Decision Explainability Is

**Authority Decision Explainability** is the constitutional contract for narrating **why a named component was entitled to decide** (or entitled to exercise a bounded delegation), **why alternatives were refused or non-primary**, and **which published constitutional facts support that narration**.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Decision owner** | Constitutional component accountable for the decision class (MS001) | Who owned this decision? |
| **Permission** | Constitutional warrant that allowed that owner (or delegate under AP-04) to act | Why was this component permitted? |
| **Refusal / non-permission** | Constitutional reason another component could not decide | Why were alternatives not permitted? |
| **Constitutional authority invoked** | Published principles, domains, boundaries, and (when applicable) conflict rules cited | Which law was applied? |
| **Consumed recommendations** | Authorised outputs of other owners read as inputs (AP-05) | What peer artefacts informed the decision without transferring ownership? |
| **Conflict disposition** | Lawful RO outcome set when concurrency arose (MS002) | How was action selected without rewriting owners? |
| **Lawful outcome** | The acted-upon (or refused) result that remains ownership-true and meaning-true | What happened constitutionally? |

Authority Decision Explainability is:

- **constitutional** — it cites published ownership and resolution law, not product preference;
- **permission-centred** — its core job is *why permitted / why not*, not *why this tip is educationally good* (Programme VI owns that);
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in underlying truth;
- **layer-honest** — it frames ownership and disposition; it does not replace coach meaning or workflow narration;
- **non-inventive** — it never creates owners, merges meanings, or invents unpublished precedence;
- **consistent** — ordinary, delegated, and conflict paths share one explanation contract.

Authority Decision Explainability is **not**:

- a second Educational Authority Model;
- a second Conflict Resolution Framework;
- a rewrite of Programme VI educational meaning;
- a workflow stage sequencer;
- a rendering, UI, template, or analytics system;
- a claim that a clear explanation guarantees student success or mastery.

## 3. Educational Purpose

Authority Decision Explainability exists so that:

1. **Permission is speakable** — students and developers can hear why *this* component could decide.
2. **Refusal is dignified** — alternatives are named as constitutionally non-permitted, not as mysterious product failure.
3. **Delegation stays honest** — temporary exercise is narrated without alienating ownership.
4. **Conflict stays ownership-true** — disposition of action never sounds like a coach-domain rewrite.
5. **Precedence stays published** — only MS001 / MS002 / Constitution / EIP rules appear as justification.
6. **Layers stay distinct** — meaning, ownership, orchestration, and conflict disposition remain separable in speech.

## 5. Four Authority Explanation Questions (Binding)

Every material authority decision explanation must answer these four questions.

### AEQ1 — Why was this component permitted to decide?

Name the owner (or AP-04 delegate under that owner’s warrant) and the constitutional domain / principle that authorised the decision class.

### AEQ2 — Why were alternative components not permitted?

Name material refused or non-primary alternatives and the constitutional reason (prohibited domain, not primary question, boundary, non-owner for this class, RP-08 ownership dispute refusal, RO-05 unlawful exclusion, etc.).

### AEQ3 — Which constitutional facts were applied?

Cite ownership principles/domains/boundaries and, when concurrency arose, conflict types/principles/outcomes — plus any higher EIP / Constitution obligations that constrained the path.

### AEQ4 — What lawful outcome resulted, with ownership intact?

State the acted-upon or refused result, any delegation or conflict dispositions, and explicit preservation of ownership and educational meaning.

All four AEQs are mandatory for material authority narrations. Omitting AEQ2 when alternatives would confuse the student (or when an auditor would expect refused owners) is an architectural defect. Omitting AEQ4 ownership preservation is always an architectural defect.

Every material authority explanation must satisfy:

| ID | Invariant |
|----|-----------|
| **AXI-01** | Decision owner matches MS001 for the decision class |
| **AXI-02** | Alternatives refused for published constitutional reasons only |
| **AXI-03** | No ownership transfer implied by speech |
| **AXI-04** | No educational meaning rewrite implied by speech |
| **AXI-05** | No unpublished precedence, scoring, or discretionary winner story |
| **AXI-06** | Delegation (if any) remains temporary and bounded in speech |
| **AXI-07** | Conflict disposition (if any) uses only published CT/RP/RO vocabulary |
| **AXI-08** | Orchestration references remain coordination context, not educational ownership |
| **AXI-09** | Student and developer traces describe the same constitutional truth |
| **AXI-10** | AEQ1–AEQ4 answered for the audience |

Violation of any invariant makes the explanation **unlawful** regardless of product convenience or motivational polish.
