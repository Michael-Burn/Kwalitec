## Authority

This document is the constitutional overview of the **Workflow Transition Framework** for Kwalitec.

It is subordinate to the Educational Constitution, specialised Programme VI educational models, and the Educational Workflow Model (MS001). It governs **when an educational workflow may move from one constitutional stage to another**. It does not authorise implementation shortcuts that contradict the Constitution, and it does not invent educational meaning absent from Programme VI or MS001.

Authority order for educational workflow transitions:

> Constitution defines educational truth and curriculum primacy.  
> Evidence Model defines what may warrant educational claims.  
> Continuity Standard preserves rightful educational history.  
> State Authority Matrix defines who may mutate educational states.  
> Programme VI defines educational reasoning for planning, today, learning, recovery, revision, and examination.  
> Educational Workflow Model (MS001) defines events, stages, objectives, and orchestration boundaries.  
> **This Workflow Transition Framework (Programme VII / Workstream 1 / MS002) defines when lawful movement between those stages may occur.**  
> Downstream Runtime A, product surfaces, and narration must consume this transition law — never redefine Programme VI meaning while advancing stages.

## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not leap from “something happened” to “here is advice” without recognisable steps — nor invent advice while waiting for missing evidence. After MS001 has defined *events*, *stages*, and *orchestration boundaries*, the tutor still needs an answer to one living coordination question:

> **“When should an educational workflow move from one constitutional stage to another?”**

That answer must coordinate educational flow, preserve each Programme VI component’s authority, ensure deterministic stage movement, support explainable transitions, and prevent conflicting educational actions — without creating educational meaning, modifying coach recommendations, rewriting the Canonical Study Plan, or introducing independent educational decisions.

This document records that tutor posture so every future Educational Workflow Engine subsystem has a single educational reference for *when workflows transition*.

> **The Workflow Transition Framework describes orchestration of stage movement.  
> It does not implement Runtime A logic, algorithms, scheduling, or execution engines.**

## 2. What a Workflow Transition Is

A **workflow transition** is the constitutional act by which an educational workflow **moves** (advances, pauses, resumes, escalates, supersedes, or concludes) between MS001 orchestration stages — or between recognised orchestration lifecycle postures — without redefining any participating Programme VI component’s educational meaning.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Current stage / posture** | Where the open workflow sits in MS001 S0–S7 (or awaiting / concluded) | Where are we now? |
| **Transition kind** | Named lawful movement class (`TRANSITION_CATALOGUE.md`) | What kind of move is this? |
| **Permitting conditions** | Constitutional facts that must hold before movement (`TRANSITION_CONDITIONS.md`) | Why may we move now? |
| **Participating components** | Programme VI authorities and MS001 artefacts involved in the move | Who is present in this step? |
| **Post-transition stage / posture** | Lawful destination after the move | Where do we go? |
| **Authority preservation record** | Explicit confirmation that coach / plan / evidence meaning was not altered by the move | Did authority stay intact? |

A workflow transition is:

- **orchestrating** — it moves flow between constitutional stages;
- **condition-gated** — movement requires stated constitutional conditions, not convenience;
- **authority-preserving** — coach recommendations and Programme VI meanings remain unchanged by the move itself;
- **deterministic** — the same stage + same lawful conditions ⇒ the same transition kind and destination;
- **non-deciding on meaning** — transitions do not invent educational recommendations;
- **explainable** — students and developers can see why movement occurred and that authority was preserved;
- **continuity-safe** — transitions do not erase learner-owned educational history (EIP-005).

A workflow transition is **not**:

- a second Daily Coach, Learning Coach, Recovery Coach, Revision Coach, Exam Coach, or Master Planner;
- reinterpretation of Educational Evidence under a transition label;
- modification of a Programme VI recommendation already emitted;
- a rewrite of the Canonical Study Plan;
- an independent educational decision engine;
- a scheduler, job queue, saga, or runtime state machine;
- a claim that completing a transition guarantees learning or a pass.

## 3. Binding Distinction: Transitions vs Neighbours

| Layer | Educational / orchestration job | Unlawful collapse |
|-------|----------------------------------|-------------------|
| **Event (WE-xx)** | Stimulus that may initiate or continue a workflow | Treating an event as a stage skip or a recommendation |
| **Stage (S0–S7)** | Ordered constitutional step in the orchestration | Treating the stage list as optional theatre |
| **Transition (WT-xx)** | Lawful *movement* between stages / postures | Inventing educational meaning during the move |
| **Programme VI reasoning** | Educational answer at S3 | Letting a transition substitute for coach reasoning |
| **Outcome (S5)** | Recommend / hand off / refuse / escalate | Using a transition to mint a fifth “workflow tip” |

MS001 without MS002 risks stages that never lawfully connect.  
MS002 without MS001 risks transitions without stages, events, or orchestration boundaries.

## 4. Educational Purpose

The Workflow Transition Framework exists so that:

1. **Stage movement stays lawful and auditable** — workflows do not jump from recognition to recommendation without completing mandatory authority checks.
2. **Waiting is first-class** — when educational outputs or evidence are not yet available, the workflow may pause without inventing meaning.
3. **Resume is honest** — continuation requires the awaited constitutional outputs, not product impatience.
4. **Escalation remains structural** — envelope / plan problems move to Master Planner pathways; they do not become silent plan rewrites.
5. **Authority survives complexity** — advancing a stage never quietly absorbs coach or planner rights.
6. **Flow remains deterministic** — same educational situation ⇒ same lawful transition path.

## 5. Core Responsibilities

The Workflow Transition Framework is educationally responsible for:

| Responsibility | Meaning |
|----------------|---------|
| **Catalogue lawful moves** | Name transition kinds that coordinate flow (`TRANSITION_CATALOGUE.md`) |
| **Gate movement on conditions** | Permit transitions only when constitutional conditions hold (`TRANSITION_CONDITIONS.md`) |
| **Preserve authority during movement** | Ensure transitions do not alter coach meaning, plans, or evidence (`TRANSITION_BOUNDARIES.md`) |
| **Coordinate pause / resume** | Await Programme VI or evidence outputs without inventing them |
| **Coordinate escalate / conclude** | Move structural questions upstream; close when authorised outcomes exist |
| **Explain transitions** | Make reason, condition, participants, and authority preservation speakable (`TRANSITION_EXPLAINABILITY.md`) |

### 5.1 Binding non-responsibility

Workflow transitions must **not**:

- redefine coach or Master Planner educational meaning;
- modify, invalidate, or silently rewrite the Canonical Study Plan;
- reinterpret Educational Evidence, Estimated Knowledge, or Estimated Mastery;
- modify Programme VI recommendations already produced;
- introduce educational recommendations independently of Programme VI authorities;
- select Educational Strategy, re-diagnose the Student Educational Profile, or pack calendar cells;
- mint understanding or readiness claims from transition completion alone;
- absorb State Authority Matrix mutation rights under a transition label;
- invent runtime algorithms, schedules, or execution semantics as constitutional law.

## 6. Educational Guarantees

A lawful Workflow Transition posture **guarantees** the following educational properties — not exam outcomes.

| Guarantee | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Condition fidelity** | Every material transition cites permitting conditions |
| **Stage discipline** | Mandatory MS001 authority checks are not silently skipped when a recommendation is claimed |
| **Authority preservation** | The transition itself does not change coach meaning, plan intent, or evidence reading |
| **Deterministic movement** | Same stage + same conditions ⇒ same transition kind and destination |
| **Honest waiting** | Pause does not invent educational outputs |
| **Honest resume** | Resume requires availability of awaited outputs |
| **Plan non-mutation** | Transitions never modify Canonical Study Plan educational intent |
| **Evidence non-reinterpretation** | Evidence remains owned by Evidence Model / permitted writers |
| **Explainability** | Every material transition can answer the transition explainability questions |
| **Continuity** | Transition changes do not erase learner-owned educational history |

## 8. Integrity Rules

1. **Move flow, do not educate by substitute.** Transitions advance orchestration; Programme VI owns educational answers.
2. **Conditions before convenience.** Do not advance stages to smooth a UI if constitutional conditions fail.
3. **No silent skips.** Claiming a recommendation without completing S1–S5 remains unlawful even if a transition “shortcut” looks elegant.
4. **Pause rather than invent.** Missing educational outputs ⇒ await; do not fabricate.
5. **Escalate rather than rewrite.** Broken envelopes ⇒ Master Planner pathways; never silent plan mutation.
6. **Speak educationally.** Explanations cite stages, conditions, and authorities — not job queues or optimiser names.
7. **Amend before contradicting.** Implementation that needs different transition law must amend this corpus first.
