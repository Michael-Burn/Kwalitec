This document is the constitutional overview of the **Educational Workflow Model** for Kwalitec.

It is subordinate to the Educational Constitution and specialised Programme VI educational models. It governs **how educational decisions flow between constitutional educational components**. It does not authorise implementation shortcuts that contradict the Constitution, and it does not invent educational meaning absent from Programme VI.

## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not leave coaches to shout over each other. After Programme VI has defined *what each educational question means*, the tutor still needs an answer to one living coordination question:

> **“How do educational decisions flow between constitutional educational components?”**

That answer must coordinate educational reasoning, preserve each component’s authority, ensure deterministic flow, support explainable outcomes, and prevent conflicting educational actions — without inventing new educational recommendations or rewriting the Canonical Study Plan.

This document records that tutor posture so every future Educational Workflow Engine subsystem has a single educational reference for *how workflows are orchestrated*.

> **The Educational Workflow Model describes orchestration of educational reasoning.  
> It does not implement Runtime A logic, algorithms, scheduling, or execution engines.**

## 2. What an Educational Workflow Is

An **educational workflow** is the constitutional sequence by which Kwalitec **initiates, progresses, and concludes** participation of Programme VI educational components in response to an educational event — producing an authorised educational outcome (recommendation, handoff, refusal, or escalation) without redefining any participating component’s meaning.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Educational event** | Observed stimulus that may start or continue a workflow | What happened educationally? |
| **Workflow warrant** | Judgement that orchestration is needed (vs. no-op / already decided) | Does this event require coordinated flow? |
| **Participating authorities** | Programme VI components invited into the flow | Who may reason here? |
| **Workflow stage** | Ordered constitutional step in the orchestration | Where are we in the flow? |
| **Orchestrated outcome** | Authorised result of the flow (not a new educational meaning) | What may the student or system lawfully do next? |

An educational workflow is:

- **coordinating** — it sequences and hands off among constitutional components;
- **authority-preserving** — each Programme VI component retains its educational question and limits;
- **deterministic** — the same lawful inputs yield the same stage path and participation set;
- **event-initiated** — workflows begin or continue from educational events, not from opaque timers alone;
- **non-deciding on meaning** — events and stages do not invent educational recommendations;
- **conflict-preventing** — concurrent or competing coach claims are resolved by stage order and boundaries, not by merger of meanings;
- **explainable** — students and developers can see why the flow started, who participated, and why the outcome emerged;
- **plan-faithful** — Canonical Study Plan remains Master Planner / Scheduling educational contract.

An educational workflow is **not**:

- a second Daily Coach, Learning Coach, Recovery Coach, Revision Coach, Exam Coach, or Master Planner;
- a licence to modify the Canonical Study Plan;
- reinterpretation of Educational Evidence under an orchestration label;
- an independent recommendation engine;
- a UI journey map, product funnel, or analytics pipeline;
- a claim that completing a workflow guarantees learning or a pass.

## 3. Educational Purpose

The Educational Workflow Model exists so that:

1. **Educational reasoning stays coherent across components** — login, session close, new evidence, disruption, revision completion, and examination proximity do not produce competing tutor voices.
2. **Constitutional authority survives product complexity** — orchestration never quietly absorbs coach or planner rights.
3. **Flow remains deterministic and auditable** — same educational situation → same lawful participation path.
4. **Recommendations remain explainable end-to-end** — from initiating event through stages to the Programme VI authority that owns the educational answer.
5. **Conflicting educational actions are prevented** — only one primary educational question is active at a time unless a documented parallel-read pattern applies (read-only inputs to a primary decider).

## 4. Core Responsibilities

The Educational Workflow Engine is educationally responsible for:

| Responsibility | Meaning |
|----------------|---------|
| **Recognise educational events** | Classify stimuli that may initiate or continue workflows (`WORKFLOW_EVENTS.md`) |
| **Open and progress workflows** | Move through constitutional stages without skipping mandatory authority checks (`WORKFLOW_STAGES.md`) |
| **Invite the correct authorities** | Route to Master Planner or the coach whose educational question is primary |
| **Preserve authority boundaries** | Refuse actions that redefine coach meaning, rewrite plans, or invent recommendations (`WORKFLOW_BOUNDARIES.md`) |
| **Prevent conflicting actions** | Ensure one primary educational decision path per active workflow instance |
| **Conclude or hand off** | End the workflow when an authorised outcome exists, or escalate to the owning Programme VI authority |
| **Explain the orchestration** | Make start reason, participants, authority preservation, and outcome provenance speakable (`WORKFLOW_EXPLAINABILITY.md`) |

### 4.1 Binding non-responsibility

The Educational Workflow Engine must **not**:

- redefine coach or Master Planner educational meaning;
- modify, invalidate, or silently rewrite the Canonical Study Plan;
- reinterpret Educational Evidence, Estimated Knowledge, or Estimated Mastery;
- introduce educational recommendations independently of Programme VI authorities;
- select Educational Strategy, re-diagnose the Student Educational Profile as a substitute for consulting it, or pack calendar cells;
- mint understanding or readiness claims from workflow completion alone;
- absorb State Authority Matrix mutation rights under an orchestration label.

## 5. Educational Guarantees

A lawful Educational Workflow posture **guarantees** the following educational properties — not exam outcomes.

| Guarantee | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Meaning fidelity** | Orchestration consumes Programme VI meaning; it never redefines it |
| **Authority preservation** | Each participating component keeps its constitutional question and limits |
| **Deterministic flow** | Same classified event + same educational context ⇒ same stage path and participation set |
| **Single primary decision** | At most one primary educational decider is active per workflow instance |
| **Plan non-mutation** | Workflows never modify Canonical Study Plan educational intent |
| **Evidence non-reinterpretation** | Evidence remains owned by Evidence Model / permitted writers |
| **Explainability** | Every material orchestration can answer the workflow explainability questions |
| **Honest escalation** | When no Programme VI authority can answer within envelopes, the workflow escalates — it does not invent |
| **Continuity** | Orchestration changes do not erase learner-owned educational history (EIP-005) |

## 6. Constitutional Components Orchestrated

These Programme VI components are **participants**, not subordinates redefined by this Model:

| Component | Constitutional educational question | Workflow role |
|-----------|-------------------------------------|---------------|
| **Master Planner** | How should the long-term journey be designed, decided, blueprinted, scheduled, and contracted? | Structural change, plan publication, reschedule / replan handoff target |

| **Recovery Coach** | How should progress be restored after meaningful disruption? | Restorative warrant and recovery journey meaning |
| **Revision Coach** | What should be revised now, and why? | Consolidating emphasis meaning |
| **Exam Coach** | How should the learner prepare for and approach the examination? | Assessment-facing preparation meaning |
| **Student Educational Profile** | Where is the student educationally now? | Diagnostic input; not rewritten by workflow |
| **Educational Evidence / Twin estimates** | What observational trail and estimates exist? | Inputs under EIP-001 / EIP-002 / EIP-006; workflow does not author |

## 8. Integrity Rules

1. **Orchestrate, do not educate by substitute.** Workflows route reasoning; Programme VI owns educational answers.
2. **One primary question at a time.** Prefer explicit handoff over blended coach speech.
3. **Events initiate; authorities decide.** Login, completion, evidence, disruption, proximity never mint recommendations alone.
4. **Determinism before convenience.** Do not introduce non-reproducible orchestration branches for product theatre.
5. **Escalate rather than invent.** Missing authority or broken envelopes ⇒ handoff / refusal, not a workflow-authored tip.
6. **Speak educationally.** Explanations cite events, stages, and Programme VI authorities — not job queues or optimiser names.
7. **Amend before contradicting.** Implementation that needs different orchestration law must amend this corpus first.
