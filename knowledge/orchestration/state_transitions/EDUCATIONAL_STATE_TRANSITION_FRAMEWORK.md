## Authority

This document is the constitutional overview of the **Educational State Transition Framework** for Kwalitec.

It is subordinate to the Educational Constitution, specialised Programme VI educational models, and the Educational State Model (WS4 / MS001). It governs **when one constitutional educational context may lawfully transition into another**. It does not authorise implementation shortcuts that contradict the Constitution, and it does not invent educational meaning, alter ownership, execute workflows, or mint recommendations.

Authority order for educational state transitions (constitutional context succession):

> Constitution defines educational truth and curriculum primacy.  
> Knowledge & Mastery defines coverage ≠ understanding ≠ mastery.  
> Evidence Model defines what may warrant educational claims.  
> Continuity Standard preserves rightful educational history.  
> State Authority Matrix defines who may mutate Article IV educational states.  
> Programme VI (Master Planner and coaches) defines educational meaning for planning, today, learning, recovery, revision, and examination.  
> Programme VII Workstream 1 defines how that reasoning is orchestrated across components.  
> Programme VII Workstream 2 defines who owns decisions, how valid recommendations compete, and how permission is explained.  
> Programme VII Workstream 3 defines what recommendations and recommendation sets are.  
> Educational State Model (WS4 / MS001) defines what constitutional educational context may exist and what each state represents.  
> **This Educational State Transition Framework (Programme VII / Workstream 4 / MS002) defines when one constitutional educational context may lawfully transition into another.**  
> Downstream Runtime A, product surfaces, and narration must consume this transition law — never treat contextual succession as success, mastery, workflow completion, or authority transfer.

## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not confuse *changing educational focus* with *having mastered a topic*, *having finished a workflow*, or *having transferred coaching ownership*. After MS001 has defined *what constitutional educational context may exist*, the tutor still needs one living context-succession answer:

> **“When may one constitutional educational context transition into another?”**

That answer must update constitutional context representation, reference lawful workflow progression where appropriate, preserve contextual continuity, and enable constitutional explainability — without redefining educational meaning, implying learner success or mastery, modifying authority ownership, creating recommendations, or executing educational workflows.

This document records that tutor posture so every future Educational State Engine subsystem has a single educational reference for *when contextual state may transition*.

> **The Educational State Transition Framework describes lawful succession of constitutional educational context.  
> It does not redefine Programme VI meaning, implement Runtime A, or execute workflows.**

## 2. What a Contextual State Transition Is

A **contextual state transition** is the constitutional act by which the Educational State Engine **updates the representation** of live educational context — changing which published EST type is primary (or which parallel-read siblings are observed) — without asserting educational success, learner mastery, workflow completion, Article IV mutation, authority transfer, or recommendation creation.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Originating contextual state** | Prior primary EST-xx (and any parallel-read siblings) | Where were we? |
| **Destination contextual state** | Successor primary EST-xx (and any parallel-read siblings) | Where are we now? |
| **Transition type** | Named lawful succession class (`TRANSITION_TYPES.md`) | What kind of context move is this? |
| **Permitting conditions** | Constitutional facts that must hold before succession (`TRANSITION_CONDITIONS.md`) | Why may context move now? |
| **Context warrant** | Plan class, Programme VI warrant, WS1 progression / completion fact, WS2 disposition, continuity hold | What evidence of *context* supports the move? |
| **Continuity record** | Explicit preservation that prior context history was not erased (EIP-005) | Did we pretend the prior focus never existed? |
| **Prohibited interpretation** | Readings that turn succession into success, mastery, completion, tips, ownership transfer, or workflow execution | What must this transition *never* mean? |

A contextual state transition is:

- **representational** — it updates context labels; it does not invent educational truth;
- **type-gated** — only published CST types and EST origins/destinations are lawful;
- **condition-gated** — succession requires stated constitutional conditions, not convenience;
- **non-evaluative of success** — context move ≠ pass, mastery, or “good student”;
- **non-executing** — CST labels do not run workflows, write Article IV states, or pack calendars;
- **authority-preserving** — succession never transfers WS2 ownership;
- **meaning-preserving** — Programme VI questions remain owned by their corpora;
- **recommendation-neutral** — succession never creates, ranks, or assembles tips;
- **continuity-aware** — context changes do not erase learner-owned educational history;
- **explainable** — students and developers can see why context moved, what warranted it, which rules applied, and how continuity was preserved.

A contextual state transition is **not**:

- redefinition of Programme VI educational meaning or coach questions;
- Constitution Article IV meaning-bearing state mutation;
- a workflow transition (WT-xx) or workflow completion judgement;
- transfer or absorption of constitutional ownership;
- creation of educational recommendations or recommendation sets;
- a claim that leaving Recovery Context means recovery “succeeded”;
- a claim that entering Day Priority Context means today’s mission is complete;
- a claim that any CST completion equals learner mastery;
- a state machine implementation, scheduler, or Runtime A service;
- a UI mode switch, feature flag, analytics segment, or database enum contract.

### 2.1 Binding distinctions

| Horizon | Object | Owns |
|---------|--------|------|
| **Constitution Article IV / EIP** | Meaning-bearing educational states | Educational definitions and (via EIP-001) mutation rights |
| **WS4 / MS001 Educational State Model** | Constitutional educational *context* states (EST-xx) | Representation catalogue, entry/exit, non-claim prohibitions |
| **This Framework (VII / WS4 / MS002)** | Lawful *succession* among EST contexts (CST-xx) | When representation may change |
| **WS1 Workflow Transitions** | Stage / posture movement (WT-xx) | Orchestration flow — may be *referenced* as condition evidence, never executed by CST |

Naming collision risk (“state transition”) is historical and **must be disambiguated in speech**:

- Student / tutor speech: prefer “focus change / educational context change” language.
- Developer / auditor speech: cite **CST-xx** for this Framework; cite **EST-xx** for context types; cite **WT-xx** for workflow stage movement; cite **Article IV / EIP-001 rows** for meaning-bearing states.

## 3. Educational Purpose

The Educational State Transition Framework exists so that:

1. **Context succession stays lawful and auditable** — focus does not jump from day to exam, or from holding to recovery, without published types and conditions.
2. **Representation remains the only thing that moves** — succession updates EST posture; it does not educate by substitute.
3. **Workflow facts remain inputs, not executions** — lawful WS1 progression / completion may *permit* a context change; CST never *runs* the workflow.
4. **Authority survives focus change** — naming a new EST primary never quietly absorbs coach or planner rights.
5. **Continuity survives product pressure** — prior context remains speakable; history is not erased by mode refresh.
6. **Explainability is end-to-end** — why, warrants, published rules, and continuity are always answerable (EIP-003).
7. **Forbidden readings are blocked by design** — success, mastery, workflow completion, tip minting, and ownership transfer are never inferred from CST labels.

The Educational State Transition Framework is educationally responsible for:

| **Catalogue lawful contextual moves** | Name transition types that update context representation (`TRANSITION_TYPES.md`) |

### 4.1 Binding non-responsibility

The Educational State Transition Framework must **not**:

- redefine Programme VI educational meaning or coach questions;
- redefine Constitution Article IV educational states or EIP-001 mutation rows;
- invent, amend, or execute workflow stages, WT transitions, or completion criteria;
- amend Authority Model domains or Conflict Resolution catalogues by transition fiat;
- create, rank, or assemble educational recommendations;
- grant or invent EIP-001 mutation rights;
- implement Runtime A services, state machines, schedulers, databases, UI, or analytics;
- select Educational Strategy, pack calendars, or mint mastery from transition labels;
- treat Version 2 operational state machines as replacements for this constitutional succession law;
- invent undocumented EST types — amend MS001 `STATE_TYPES.md` first.

## 5. Educational Guarantees

A lawful contextual state transition **guarantees** the following educational properties — not exam outcomes.

| Guarantee | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Catalogue fidelity** | Only published CST types may be treated as constitutional contextual transitions |
| **EST fidelity** | Origin and destination are published EST types (or documented holding / absent postures from MS001) |
| **Meaning fidelity** | Succession never redefines Programme VI or Article IV meanings |
| **Non-success reading** | CST labels never mean educational success |
| **Non-mastery reading** | CST labels never mean learner mastery or Estimated Mastery |
| **Non-completion reading** | CST labels never mean workflow completion (even when WS1 completion is cited as condition evidence) |
| **Authority preservation** | Succession never transfers WS2 ownership |
| **Non-execution** | Succession never executes educational workflows or educational actions |
| **Recommendation neutrality** | Succession never creates or assembles tips |
| **Boundary honesty** | Forbidden actions in `TRANSITION_BOUNDARIES.md` are hard stops |
| **Explainability** | Every material transition can answer the transition explainability questions |
| **Continuity** | Succession does not erase learner-owned educational history (EIP-005) |
| **Honest refusal** | When conditions fail, remain in current primary context rather than invent successor focus |

## 7. Integrity Rules

1. **Published types only.** Undocumented “mode switches” are not constitutional contextual transitions.
2. **EST catalogue supremacy.** Origin and destination must be EST-01…EST-12 (including parallel-read patterns from MS001).
3. **Exit before succession where required.** MS001 exit conditions for the prior primary must hold when the catalogue requires exit.
4. **Entry before speech.** Destination may not be narrated as live without satisfying MS001 entry conditions and this Framework’s permitting conditions.
5. **One primary after the move.** At most one primary EST type remains constitutive, unless a documented parallel-read pattern applies.
6. **Observers consume; they do not own.** Workflow, authority, and recommendation engines may observe post-transition context; they do not gain mutation or tip-creation rights from succession.
7. **Evidence of context ≠ Educational Evidence of understanding.** Warrants for succession may cite plan class, Programme VI warrants, WS1 progression / completion facts, or WS2 disposition — they do not redefine EIP-002 observations.
8. **Reference ≠ execute.** Citing lawful workflow progression as a condition is not workflow execution by the State Engine.
9. **No silent Article IV writes.** Context succession never mutates Study Progress, Evidence, or estimates.
10. **Amend before inventing.** New transition types require amending this corpus first; new EST types require amending MS001 first.
