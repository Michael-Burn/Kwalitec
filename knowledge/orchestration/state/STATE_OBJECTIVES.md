## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “number of mode switches.” The tutor optimises **honest educational context**: the student and the system share a lawful answer to *where we are educationally right now*, so orchestration and guidance can proceed without pretending that context is mastery or success.

These objectives bind every Educational State Engine behaviour and surface.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### ESO-01 — Represent constitutional educational context

**Definition.** Ensure that the live class of constitutional educational situation is named as a published educational state (EST-xx) — or as an honest absence / holding posture — without inventing undocumented modes or reading context as success, mastery, or workflow completion.

**Includes:**

- Naming Day Priority, Recovery, Revision, Examination, Structural Planning, Session, Reflection, Learning Progression, Conflict Disposition, Escalation Await, Continuity Holding, or Absent Plan contexts when warrants hold.
- Preferring Continuity Holding or Absent Plan over fabricated “focus” when no primary warrant exists.
- Keeping Article IV meaning-bearing states distinct from EST context labels.
- Making context speakable to students in plain educational language and to developers as EST-xx.

**Excludes:**

- Treating context labels as Estimated Mastery, Study Progress, or exam-pass forecasts.
- Inventing product “modes” not published in `STATE_TYPES.md`.
- Collapsing multiple primary educational questions into one silent mega-context.
- Using engagement or UI navigation state as a substitute for constitutional context.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when the student cannot tell whether they are recovering, revising, studying today’s priority, or waiting for a plan change — or when “context” is used to imply they have already succeeded. Representation is educational honesty; evaluative overclaim is educational harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### ESO-02 — Support lawful orchestration

**Definition.** Ensure educational state provides observable constitutional context that the Educational Workflow Engine (WS1) may consume when recognising events, selecting primary authority, assembling inputs, and concluding — without the state corpus becoming a second Workflow Engine.

**Tutor rationale.** Orchestration that invents its own posture vocabulary drifts from educational law. Shared context vocabulary keeps stage paths auditable.

**Manifestations:**

- WS1 stages may *observe* EST types as educational context inputs.
- State labels never skip mandatory authority checks or invent stages.
- Conflict-await and escalation-await contexts support honest parking without tip invention.
- Forbidden actions in `STATE_BOUNDARIES.md` remain hard stops for orchestration consumers.

---

### ESO-03 — Support recommendation assembly

**Definition.** Ensure educational state can be referenced as constitutional context when Workstream 3 packages lawful recommendations into artefacts and sets — without the state corpus creating tips, ranking coaches, or assembling sets itself.

**Tutor rationale.** Students should hear guidance situated in the live educational focus. Context references orient assembly; they do not mint recommendations.

**Manifestations:**

- Recommendation structure / assembly may cite EST-xx as orchestration / context references.
- State never supplies undocumented recommendation sources.
- Empty or deferred guidance remains preferred when context is Conflict Disposition or Escalation Await without a dispositioned primary tip.
- Assembly consumers must not treat EST labels as ownership or evidence rewrites.

---

### ESO-04 — Preserve educational continuity

**Definition.** Ensure changes of constitutional educational context do not erase learner-owned educational history, silently rewrite Article IV states, or pretend a prior educational situation never existed (EIP-005 specialised to context narration).

**Tutor rationale.** Moving from recovery back to ordinary daily focus is continuity care — not amnesia. Context succession must remain explainable.

**Manifestations:**

- Exit conditions in `STATE_TYPES.md` precede successor primary context where required.
- Context change narration names the prior focus when material.
- Continuity Holding is a first-class lawful posture, not a failure.
- State representation never deletes Study Progress, Evidence, or plan history.

---

### ESO-05 — Enable constitutional explainability

**Definition.** Ensure every material educational state can answer — for students and developers — what the current context is, why it exists, what constitutional warrants support it, and which workflows / recommendations reference it (`STATE_EXPLAINABILITY.md`; EIP-003).

**Tutor rationale.** Opaque “modes” feel arbitrary even when underlying coaching is sound. Explainable context is part of educational integrity.

**Manifestations:**

- Student speech uses plain focus language; developer traces cite EST-xx and warrants.
- Supporting warrants distinguish context evidence from Educational Evidence of understanding.
- Consumers (WS1 / WS2 / WS3) are nameable when they reference the state.
- Explainability never legitimises an unlawful context invention.

---

## 4. Objective Priority (When Targets Tension)

When objectives appear to conflict, apply this order:

1. **ESO-01** — honest context representation (never invent or overclaim)
2. **ESO-04** — continuity (do not erase history to simplify narration)
3. **ESO-05** — explainability (make the lawful posture speakable)
4. **ESO-02** — orchestration support (observe, do not replace workflows)
5. **ESO-03** — recommendation assembly support (reference, do not create tips)

**Rule:** Supporting orchestration or assembly never authorises inventing context, claiming mastery, or transferring authority. Explainability never legitimises an unlawful state. Continuity never legitimises fabricating a primary focus.
