## 1. Purpose

Principles prevent context narration from becoming a second empire: motivational copy that invents mastery, “helpful” mode stories that hide succession rules, ownership absorption into “the app,” or developer traces that cite UX preference instead of published EST/CST law.

These principles bind every educational-state explanation path — documentation, design, and future Runtime A behaviour. They deliberately **do not** specify rendering, templates, or generation algorithms.

## 2. Principle Catalogue

| ID | Principle | One-line rule |
|----|-----------|---------------|
| **ESEP-01** | Contextual transparency | Every material explanation names the current constitutional educational context |
| **ESEP-02** | Transition traceability | Lawful succession is reconstructible from published CST steps and EST postures |
| **ESEP-03** | Constitutional continuity | Prior contextual history is preserved in speech; succession is never amnesia |

| **ESEP-10** | Audience fidelity | Student and developer speech share one truth; vocabulary differs by audience |

## 3. ESEP-01 — Contextual Transparency

**Rule:** Every material educational-state explanation must identify the live primary constitutional educational context (EST posture) in audience-appropriate language.

| Lawful | Unlawful |
|--------|----------|
| “Right now we’re focusing on today’s priorities under your Study Plan.” | “The app is in some mode…” with no named focus |
| Developer: `primary_est=EST-03` | Substitute UI screen names for EST postures |
| Name parallel-read siblings when observational only | Collapse Article IV Study Progress into an EST label |

**Relationship to MS001:** ESEP-01 is the explanation-face of EST catalogue discipline and ESQ-01. Transparency describes context; it does not create EST types.

---

## 4. ESEP-02 — Transition Traceability

**Rule:** When context evolved, the succession path must be reconstructible from published CST types, from/to EST postures, and permitting conditions. When context did not evolve, refuse/remain (or initial posture) must be explicit.

| Lawful | Unlawful |
|--------|----------|
| Trace EST-03 → CST-07 → EST-07 with warrants | “Focus flipped somehow” |
| Record CST-13 remain with condition failure | Silent mode change with no CST citation |
| Ordered multi-step chain for audits | Hide intermediate EST postures when they were material |

**Traceability tests:**

1. Can a developer reconstruct ESEQ-01…ESEQ-05 without reading implementation code?
2. Can a student hear *why focus moved* (or why it did not) without architecture jargon?
3. If the answer depends on unpublished tribal knowledge, the explanation is not yet constitutional — amend owning corpora or refuse.

---

## 5. ESEP-03 — Constitutional Continuity

**Rule:** Succession speech must preserve that prior contextual history remains learner-owned educational history (EIP-005 specialised to context narration). Returning from recovery, closing a sitting, or leaving conflict-await must never sound like the prior focus never existed.

| Lawful | Unlawful |
|--------|----------|
| “We’re returning to today’s priorities — recovery focus has concluded for now.” | “Forget recovery; only today exists.” |
| Developer: `prior_est_recorded=true; eip005_pass=true` | History erasure or overwrite fiction |
| Holding as continuity care (EST-12) | Holding narrated as “nothing / broken product” |

**Relationship to MS002:** ESEP-03 is the explanation-face of STQ-04 / C-CONT.

---

## 6. ESEP-04 — Provenance Preservation

**Rule:** Supporting contextual evidence and observing consumers must remain attributable — plan class, Programme VI warrant IDs, WS1 progression/completion facts, WS2 dispositions, continuity holds, and WS1/WS2/WS3 observers — without absorbing those sources into the State Engine.

| Lawful | Unlawful |
|--------|----------|
| “Given your Study Plan and today’s coaching warrant…” | “The State Engine decided today’s topic.” |
| `context_warrants=[plan, programme_vi, …]` | Stripping warrant provenance to a single anonymous “system” |
| List workflows / tips that *reference* EST-xx | Claim EST created the tip or ran the workflow |

---

## 7. ESEP-05 — Published-Rule Transparency

**Rule:** The only lawful justifications for live context or succession are published constitutional rules — Constitution, EIP obligations, MS001 EST entry/exit / SB boundaries, and MS002 CST catalogue / conditions / STB boundaries.

| Lawful | Unlawful |
|--------|----------|
| “Under recovery rules, restorative focus leads before ordinary daily tips.” | “Recovery scored higher than Daily.” |
| Cite CST-07 + EST-03 exit + EST-07 entry | Invented “emergency focus” under time pressure |
| Cite CST-13 when conditions fail | Soft hierarchy not written in MS001/MS002 |

**No unpublished precedence:** Explanation may not introduce ranking tables, optimiser confidence, or “usually X mode wins” customs that are not already written in MS001/MS002 or higher law.

---

## 8. ESEP-06 — Non-Evaluative Honesty

**Rule:** Explanation treats EST posture and CST succession as *focus / context*, never as proof of mastery, pass certainty, educational success, or workflow completion.

| Lawful | Unlawful |
|--------|----------|
| “This is about where we are, not a judgement that you’ve mastered the topic.” | “You’re in revision mode — you must have mastered the chapter.” |
| CST-07 Day ← Recovery: return of focus | “You succeeded at recovery” from CST alone |
| Workflow completion cited only via WS1 speech | Completing a sitting narrated as mastery via EST-05 |

**Relationship to boundaries:** ESEP-06 is the explanation-face of SB-06…SB-08 / STB-02 / STB-03 / STB-07 and EIP-006 claim honesty.

---

## 9. ESEP-07 — Meaning Non-Invention

**Rule:** Educational-state explanation frames *constitutional context and succession*. It does not invent, reinterpret, or silently edit Programme VI educational meanings, Evidence classifications, or tip substance.

| Lawful | Unlawful |
|--------|----------|
| Point to Programme VI explainability for educational warrant of guidance shown under this focus | Rewrite a tip’s warrant so succession “explains” the tip |
| Consume Evidence / Twin estimates as published inputs when they situate context warrants | Reinterpret Evidence inside context speech |
| Keep coverage ≠ understanding ≠ mastery in any adjacent educational claims | Imply mastery from focus clarity alone |

---

## 10. ESEP-08 — Authority Non-Transfer

**Rule:** Explanation must never imply that EST labels, CST succession, UI proximity, or temporary focus transferred decision ownership.

| Lawful | Unlawful |
|--------|----------|
| “Recovery focus is live; Daily still owns day-priority decisions when that question returns.” | “Recovery now owns your daily plan.” |
| “Workflow handed off coordination around this focus.” | “The workflow / State Engine decided what you should study.” |
| EST-10 conflict-await as situation | Conflict-await narrated as a new mega-coach owner |

**Relationship to WS2:** ESEP-08 aligns with AP-06 / RP-01 / authority explainability AEP-08 — context speech must not contradict ownership speech.

---

## 11. ESEP-09 — Layer Honesty

**Rule:** Speech must keep layers distinct: Programme VI meaning, WS1 orchestration, WS2 ownership/disposition, WS3 tips/sets, and WS4 context/succession.

| Layer | Explanation may | Explanation must not |
|-------|-----------------|----------------------|
| Meaning | Reference owner’s Programme VI explainability | Substitute context speech for educational warrant |
| Ownership | Reference permission speech when material | Invent domains via EST labels |
| Orchestration | Reference participation / progression as supporting context | Claim State Engine executed workflow stages |
| Tips / sets | Note that guidance references this focus | Claim EST invented the tip |
| Context | Answer ESEQ-01…ESEQ-05 | Invent EST/CST types |

---

## 12. ESEP-10 — Audience Fidelity

**Rule:** Student-facing and developer/auditor-facing explanations describe the **same constitutional facts**. Vocabulary differs; truth does not.

| Audience | Language | Must include | Must exclude |
|----------|----------|--------------|--------------|
| **Student** | Plain educational speech | Current focus; why; how focus arrived (when material); honest limits; continuity | EST/CST/ESEP IDs, Twin facets, optimiser jargon, internal document paths, mastery rhetoric from context alone |
| **Developer / auditor** | Precise constitutional references | EST-xx, CST-xx, warrants, rules, continuity, consumers, SB/STB pass | Student motivational fluff as a substitute for audit fields |

Student copy that invents mastery from focus is unlawful even if warm. Developer traces that only say “UX switched to recovery tab” are unlawful even if precise about screens.

## 13. Application Order (Conceptual)

When composing a material educational-state explanation:

1. Name current EST posture (ESEP-01 / ESEQ-01).
2. State why it exists now (ESEQ-02) from published warrants (ESEP-05).
3. If succession occurred (or was refused), narrate the CST path with continuity (ESEP-02 / ESEP-03 / ESEQ-03).
4. Cite supporting contextual evidence and published rules; keep Evidence distinct (ESEP-04 / ESEQ-04).
5. Name observing workflows / authority / recommendations when material (ESEQ-05).
6. Enforce non-evaluative, meaning, and ownership honesty (ESEP-06…ESEP-09).
7. Emit audience-appropriate speech with shared truth (ESEP-10).

This order is constitutional guidance for explanation composition. It is **not** a runtime algorithm.

---

## 14. Closing

Principles keep context speech honest: **current focus visible, succession traceable, continuity preserved, provenance attributable, justification published — without inventing meaning, mastery, or ownership.**

> **Transparent context. Traceable progression. Published rules only.**
