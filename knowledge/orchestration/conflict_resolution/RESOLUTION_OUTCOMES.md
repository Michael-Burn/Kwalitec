## 1. Purpose

Without a closed outcome catalogue, resolution invents informal endings: soft-delete tips, silent merges, or “both primary.” This document names what may lawfully happen to each artefact after RP application.

---

## 2. Outcome Catalogue

| ID | Outcome | One-line definition |
|----|---------|---------------------|
| **RO-01** | Recommendation deferred | Valid artefact remains lawful; primary action is postponed for this coordination moment |
| **RO-02** | Recommendation superseded | Later valid artefact replaces the earlier one’s *acted-upon* status; earlier remains owned and meaningful |
| **RO-03** | Recommendation merged | Composite acted-upon outcome where a **published constitutional merge pathway** explicitly permits it |
| **RO-04** | Recommendation queued | Valid artefact awaits a later lawful action window without losing owner or meaning |
| **RO-05** | Recommendation rejected as unlawful | Artefact fails constitutional validity; excluded from peer resolution |
| **RO-06** | Recommendation acted upon | Valid artefact becomes the primary educational outcome for this coordination moment |

RO-06 is the **acted-upon** designation. RO-01–RO-05 describe dispositions of competitors (and of unlawful claims). A resolution record typically names **one RO-06** (or one RO-03 composite) plus dispositions for other artefacts.

Additional outcomes may be added only by amending this document.

---

## 3. Invariants for Every Outcome

Every RO-xx disposition must satisfy:

| Invariant | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **I1 — Ownership intact** | Decision owners named by MS001 remain owners (RP-01) |
| **I2 — Meaning intact** | Programme VI meanings unchanged by the disposition itself (RP-02) |
| **I3 — Boundary respect** | AB-01…AB-10 and related limits are not crossed to “make the outcome work” |
| **I4 — Explainable** | Disposition is narratable under `RESOLUTION_EXPLAINABILITY.md` (RP-10) |
| **I5 — No silent dual primary** | Unless RO-03 applies, at most one primary acted-upon outcome (RP-06) |

Violation of any invariant makes the disposition **unlawful** regardless of product convenience.

## 4. RO-01 — Recommendation Deferred

**Definition:** A constitutionally valid recommendation is **not** acted upon as primary in this coordination moment. It remains valid under its owner; primary action is postponed relative to another acted-upon outcome or to a higher obligation.

| Property | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| Validity | Artefact remains constitutionally valid |
| Ownership | Unchanged |
| Meaning | Unchanged |
| Student posture | “This guidance waits while another obligation leads” |
| Distinct from RO-04 | Defer emphasises *not now because another leads*; queue emphasises *await a later window* — both preserve validity; choose the clearer educational story |
| Distinct from RO-05 | Deferred ≠ rejected |

**Typical use:** CT-01 / CT-02 / CT-03 when a standing valid recommendation yields primary action to a temporary focus or higher obligation.

**Unlawful lookalikes:** Relabelling deferred as “invalid”; transferring its domain to the winner; rewriting its warrant.

---

## 5. RO-02 — Recommendation Superseded

**Definition:** A **later** constitutionally valid recommendation replaces the **acted-upon status** of an **earlier** valid recommendation. The earlier artefact’s owner and educational meaning remain on record; its primary action status does not.

| Property | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| What is replaced | Acted-upon / primary-action status |
| What is not replaced | Ownership of the earlier decision class; educational meaning of the earlier artefact |
| Trace | Must cite earlier artefact, later artefact, and preservation of earlier owner |
| Typical conflict type | CT-04 (may co-occur with CT-01 / CT-03) |

**Typical use:** Same-day disruption causes Recovery (or another warranted temporary primary) to supersede action on an earlier Daily Coach day recommendation.

**Unlawful lookalikes:** Silent overwrite without supersession record; claiming the later owner now owns the earlier domain; erasing the earlier artefact’s meaning.

---

## 6. RO-03 — Recommendation Merged (Where Constitutionally Permitted)

**Definition:** Two or more valid artefacts contribute to a **single composite acted-upon outcome** only when a **published constitutional merge pathway** already authorises that composition — naming owners, bounds, and accountability.

| Property | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| Permission | Merge pathway must be published in constitutional corpora (Authority Model delegation patterns, Programme VI “informs but does not commandeer” pathways, or an explicit amendment) — **not invented at resolution time** |
| Accountability | Composite still cites contributing owners; no anonymous mega-coach |
| Meaning | Each contributor’s meaning remains distinguishable in explainability |
| Default | **Merge is exceptional.** Absence of a published pathway ⇒ do **not** merge; use RO-01 / RO-02 / RO-04 / RO-06 instead |

**Lawful merge pattern (illustrative):** Daily Coach owns today’s primary priority; Revision Coach’s warrant *informs* day emphasis within Daily’s domain (AP-04 / consumed authority) — composite day guidance names both roles without transferring ownership.

**Unlawful merge patterns:**

- Silent collapse of Recovery + Revision + Daily into one tip (AB-08)
- “Average the coaches” without a published pathway
- Merge used to dodge RP-08 ownership disputes

---

## 7. RO-04 — Recommendation Queued

**Definition:** A constitutionally valid recommendation awaits a **later lawful action window**. Ownership and meaning are preserved; the artefact is recognised as pending action rather than rejected or rewritten.

| Property | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| Validity | Remains valid |
| Ownership | Unchanged |
| Meaning | Unchanged |
| Window | Later window must itself be constitutionally lawful (not a fiction invented to hide concurrency) |
| Distinct from RO-01 | Queue emphasises ordered future action; defer emphasises yielding now — both are lawful; narration should not pretend a queue exists if the artefact is merely ignored |

**Typical use:** CT-05 timing collisions; CT-01 when a secondary valid warrant should follow the leading obligation.

**Unlawful lookalikes:** Infinite “queue” that never returns (continuity / honesty failure); queue as soft-delete; queue that rewrites meaning “for later packaging.”

---

## 8. RO-05 — Recommendation Rejected as Unlawful

**Definition:** An artefact **fails** constitutional validity (out of domain, no Programme VI warrant, ownership invention, boundary violation, or similar). It is **excluded** from peer resolution among valid recommendations.

| Property | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| Peer status | Not a CT-xx peer |
| Ownership | Rejection does not invent a new owner |
| Meaning | Rejection does not rewrite a valid sibling’s meaning |
| Explainability | Must name why the claim was unlawful and which owner (if any) would be rightful |
| Typical triggers | AB-04 / AB-05 violations; Workflow Engine independent tip; dual ownership attempt (also RP-08 refuse) |

**Typical use:** When P2 fails in `CONFLICT_TYPES.md` preconditions.

**Unlawful lookalikes:** Rejecting a valid peer because it lost concurrency (that is RO-01 / RO-02 / RO-04); rejecting to hide an ownership dispute without naming AP-01.

---

## 9. RO-06 — Recommendation Acted Upon

**Definition:** A constitutionally valid recommendation becomes the **primary educational outcome** for this coordination moment — the guidance the student may lawfully treat as leading *now*.

| Property | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| Singularity | At most one RO-06 per moment unless RO-03 composite is the acted-upon form |
| Ownership | Acted-upon status ≠ new or transferred ownership |
| Meaning | Unchanged by selection |
| Competitors | Other valid artefacts dispositioned via RO-01 / RO-02 / RO-04 (not silently dropped) |

**Typical use:** The winner of rule-bound selection under RP-03…RP-07 after RP-08 / RP-05 gates.

---

## 10. Outcome Combinations (Lawful Patterns)

| Pattern | Example disposition set |
|---------|-------------------------|
| **Lead + defer** | RO-06 for temporary primary; RO-01 for standing day tip |
| **Supersede + act** | RO-02 for earlier day tip’s action status; RO-06 for later recovery tip |
| **Lead + queue** | RO-06 for leading obligation; RO-04 for secondary warrant |
| **Permitted merge** | RO-03 composite as acted-upon; contributing owners cited |
| **Reject + act** | RO-05 for unlawful tip; RO-06 for remaining valid owner’s artefact |
| **Refuse ownership war** | No RO-06 among disputants; escalate to Authority Model (RP-08) |

Unlawful pattern: **dual RO-06** without RO-03 pathway.

---

## 11. What Outcomes Must Never Do

No RO-xx may:

| Prohibition | Bound |
|-------------|-------|
| Transfer decision ownership | RP-01 / MS001 |
| Rewrite Programme VI meaning | RP-02 |
| Mutate Canonical Study Plan as a side-effect | AB-02 |
| Redefine Educational Evidence | AB-03 |
| Invent an independent educational tip as the outcome | AB-05 |
| Mint mastery / understanding from disposition alone | AB-06 / EIP-006 |
| Erase educational history to clear concurrency | EIP-005 |
| Collapse multiple primary questions into a silent mega-decision | AB-08 |
| Amend Authority domains by outcome fiat | MS001 amendment process |

---

## 12. Closing

Lawful outcomes are few and speakable: **act upon, defer, supersede, queue, merge only when published, or reject as unlawful.**

> **Disposition ends concurrency for action. Ownership and meaning continue.**
