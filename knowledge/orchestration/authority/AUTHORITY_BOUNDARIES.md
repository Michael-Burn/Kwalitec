## 1. Purpose

Authority without bounds becomes quiet empire-building: one coach “helps” by rewriting the plan, orchestration “simplifies” by inventing tips, and evidence is relabelled until mastery means completion.

This document draws bright lines that **no domain owner, delegate, orchestrator, adapter, or product surface** may cross.

---

## 2. Boundary Principles

1. **Meaning non-reinterpretation.** Sibling educational meanings stay as classified by their owners.
2. **Plan non-mutation outside authority.** Canonical Study Plan changes only via Master Planner / Scheduling pathways.
3. **Evidence non-redefinition.** Observational truth stays with Evidence / permitted writers.
4. **Domain non-overclaim.** Ownership stops at the published domain edge.
5. **Orchestration non-education.** Flow is not a substitute tutor.
6. **Mutation non-ownership.** Write rights (EIP-001) do not grant coaching ownership — and vice versa.
7. **Presentation non-authority.** UI may display; it may not decide.
8. **Explicit refusal.** Boundary violation → refuse and name the rightful owner.
9. **No emergency exemption.** Time pressure does not mint authority.
10. **Explain the stop.** Students and developers should hear when authority ends.

---

## 3. Universal Prohibitions (All Components)

No constitutional component, Runtime A service, Version 2 engine, adapter, or product surface may:

| ID | Prohibition | Lawful alternative |
|----|-------------|--------------------|
| **AB-01** | Reinterpret another component’s educational meaning | Consume the meaning as published; escalate if conflict |
| **AB-02** | Modify the Canonical Study Plan outside Master Planner / Scheduling authority | Escalate via rescheduling / replanning pathways |
| **AB-03** | Redefine Educational Evidence (including treating coverage, time, or confidence as understanding) | Consume Evidence Model classifications; leave writers to EIP-001 |
| **AB-04** | Claim educational ownership beyond the component’s domain in `AUTHORITY_DOMAINS.md` | Refuse; name the owner; amend the Model if a new class is truly needed |
| **AB-05** | Introduce educational recommendations without a Programme VI owner warrant | Invoke the rightful owner; surface *its* artefact |
| **AB-06** | Mint Estimated Knowledge / Mastery from coaching, workflow, or UI completion alone | Leave estimates to Evidence-driven Twin paths |
| **AB-07** | Erase or rewrite educational history to simplify ownership or narration | Obey EIP-005 Continuity |
| **AB-08** | Collapse multiple primary educational questions into one silent mega-decision | Single primary question; explicit handoff / supersede |
| **AB-09** | Treat Version 2 Adaptive / Twin / Mission / Experience authorities as replacements for Programme VI decision ownership | Consume V2 for delivery/state; Programme VI remains educational decision law |
| **AB-10** | Invent authority by inference from code proximity, performance, or product convenience | Publish ownership in this corpus first |

## 4. What Authority May Do (Lawful)

Within these boundaries, components may:

| Lawful action | Educational meaning |
|---------------|---------------------|
| **Own listed decisions** | Decide within `AUTHORITY_DOMAINS.md` owned classes |
| **Consume sibling outputs** | Use authorised artefacts as inputs without absorbing ownership |
| **Delegate narrowly** | Temporary exercise under AP-04 with restoration |
| **Refuse out-of-domain asks** | Honest refusal naming the owner |
| **Escalate structurally** | Hand structural questions to Master Planner pathways |
| **Invite owners (Workflow Engine)** | Select whose question is live without answering it |
| **Explain ownership** | Narrate why this owner decided and others could not |
| **Present authorised outcomes (UI)** | Display without becoming the owner |

## 8. Relationship to EIP-001

| EIP-001 | This Model |
|---------|------------|
| Who may **write** Study Progress, Evidence, Mastery, Current Learning, Today’s Mission | Who may **decide** educational coaching / planning / orchestration questions |
| Forbidden writers table | Forbidden decision overclaims (AB-01–AB-10) |

A component may be a lawful **reader** of a state and still be **prohibited** from owning a coaching decision about it.  
A component may **own a decision** and still be a **forbidden writer** of Twin estimates.

Crossing either map is unconstitutional.

---

## 9. Violation Response

When a proposed behaviour would cross a boundary:

1. **Stop** — do not ship the behaviour as “temporary.”
2. **Classify** — name the AB-xx (and AD prohibited row if applicable).
3. **Refuse or escalate** — return to the rightful owner or pathway.
4. **Explain** — student- and/or developer-facing per `AUTHORITY_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.
5. **Amend if needed** — if the boundary is wrong, change this corpus deliberately; do not bypass it.

## 10. Closing

Boundaries are how educational authority stays trustworthy under product pressure.

> **Coordinate without absorbing. Consume without rewriting. Decide only what you own. Refuse what you do not.**
