## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not leave orchestration hanging forever — nor celebrate “workflow done” as if the student had mastered the syllabus. After MS001 has defined *stages* and MS002 has defined *when stages may move*, the tutor still needs an answer to one living coordination question:

> **“When is an educational workflow constitutionally complete?”**

That answer must confirm that orchestration responsibilities are fulfilled, preserve each Programme VI component’s authority, ensure deterministic and auditable close, support explainable completion, and permit lawful post-completion moves — without evaluating educational success, inventing mastery, rewriting the Canonical Study Plan, or minting coach outcomes from orchestration alone.

This document records that tutor posture so every future Educational Workflow Engine subsystem has a single educational reference for *when workflows conclude constitutionally*.

> **The Workflow Completion Model describes orchestration fulfilment.  
> It does not evaluate educational success, mastery, or coach outcomes.  
> It does not implement Runtime A logic, algorithms, scheduling, or execution engines.**

## 2. What Workflow Completion Is

**Workflow completion** is the constitutional judgement that an educational workflow instance has **fulfilled its orchestration responsibilities** for the educational concern it was opened to coordinate — such that no outstanding orchestration duties remain for that instance — without claiming that any Programme VI educational question was “successfully” answered in an educational-outcomes sense.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Completion warrant** | Judgement that orchestration fulfilment may be assessed | May we ask whether this workflow is complete? |
| **Completion criteria** | Constitutional conditions that must hold (`COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md`) | What must be true for fulfilment? |
| **Completion evidence** | Constitutional trail supporting those conditions (`COMPLETION_EVIDENCE.md`) | What proves fulfilment? |
| **Completion judgement** | Affirmed / not yet / redirected (successor or await) | Is orchestration complete? |
| **Post-completion transition** | Lawful move after the judgement (`COMPLETION_TRANSITIONS.md`) | What may follow? |
| **Authority preservation record** | Explicit confirmation that Programme VI meaning was not altered by completion | Did authority stay intact? |

Workflow completion is:

- **orchestration-scoped** — it concerns coordination duties, not educational outcomes;
- **criteria-gated** — fulfilment requires stated constitutional conditions;
- **evidence-bound** — fulfilment cites constitutional orchestration trail, not timers;
- **authority-preserving** — coach recommendations, plan intent, and evidence meaning remain unchanged by the close itself;
- **deterministic** — the same stage trail + same lawful conditions ⇒ the same completion judgement class;
- **non-certifying** — completion never certifies mastery, readiness, or coach educational success;
- **explainable** — students and developers can see why orchestration is complete and what remains;
- **continuity-safe** — completion does not erase learner-owned educational history (EIP-005).

Workflow completion is **not**:

- educational success, learner mastery, or exam readiness;
- certification that Daily Coach, Learning Coach, Recovery Coach, Revision Coach, Exam Coach, or Master Planner “succeeded”;
- reinterpretation of Educational Evidence under a completion label;
- modification of a Programme VI recommendation already emitted;
- a rewrite of the Canonical Study Plan;
- an independent educational decision engine;
- a claim that elapsed time or execution duration equals fulfilment;
- a UI celebration, analytics milestone, or job-queue “done” flag as educational law.

## 3. Binding Distinction: Completion vs Neighbours

| Layer | Educational / orchestration job | Unlawful collapse |
|-------|----------------------------------|-------------------|
| **Event (WE-xx)** | Stimulus that may initiate or continue a workflow | Treating an event as completion proof |
| **Stage (S0–S7)** | Ordered constitutional step in the orchestration | Treating stage presence as educational success |
| **Transition (WT-xx)** | Lawful *movement* between stages / postures | Treating any WT-xx as mastery; treating WT-06 as automatic without MS003 criteria |
| **Programme VI reasoning** | Educational answer at S3 | Letting workflow completion certify that answer’s educational success |
| **Programme VI completion models** | Educational completion (recovery / revision / etc.) | Collapsing educational completion into orchestration close |
| **Workflow completion (WCC / WCE / WCT)** | Orchestration fulfilment and post-close moves | Inventing educational meaning during close |

Hard separation:

> **Programme VI completion models (e.g. Recovery Completion, Revision Completion) judge educational restorative or consolidating success.  
> This Workflow Completion Model judges only whether Programme VII orchestration duties are done.  
> The two must never be collapsed.**

MS002 WT-06 is the *conclude transition kind*.  
MS003 supplies the *fulfilment judgement* that makes WT-06 constitutionally honest when claiming orchestration complete — and names what may follow.

## 4. Educational / Orchestration Purpose

The Workflow Completion Model exists so that:

1. **Workflows close honestly** — orchestration does not hang indefinitely after authorised outcomes, nor close before mandatory stages and handoffs are done.
2. **Completion stays non-educational** — closing a workflow never becomes a mastery or coach-success ceremony.
3. **Authority survives close** — finishing orchestration never quietly absorbs coach or planner rights.
4. **Post-completion moves stay lawful** — archive, successor, await, and audit are named and authority-safe.
5. **Audits remain possible** — same educational concern ⇒ same completion criteria and evidence posture.
6. **Explainability spans the full arc** — students and developers can see why the flow is finished as coordination.

## 5. Core Responsibilities

The Workflow Completion Model is constitutionally responsible for:

| Responsibility | Meaning |
|----------------|---------|
| **Define fulfilment conditions** | Name criteria under which orchestration is complete (`COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md`) |
| **Bind completion to evidence** | Require constitutional trail for stage completion, transitions, authority preservation (`COMPLETION_EVIDENCE.md`) |
| **Name post-completion moves** | Catalogue archive / successor / await / audit transitions (`COMPLETION_TRANSITIONS.md`) |
| **Refuse temporal theatre** | Reject elapsed-time or duration-only completion grounds |
| **Preserve Programme VI authority** | Ensure completion does not alter educational meaning |
| **Explain completion** | Make why-complete, evidence, participants, and remaining duties speakable (`COMPLETION_EXPLAINABILITY.md`) |

### 5.1 Binding non-responsibility

Workflow completion must **not**:

- evaluate or certify educational success, understanding, or mastery;
- certify that a coach “completed” its educational job successfully (Programme VI owns that meaning where defined);
- redefine coach or Master Planner educational meaning;
- modify, invalidate, or silently rewrite the Canonical Study Plan;
- reinterpret Educational Evidence, Estimated Knowledge, or Estimated Mastery;
- modify Programme VI recommendations already produced;
- introduce educational recommendations independently of Programme VI authorities;
- mint readiness or pass claims from orchestration close alone;
- absorb State Authority Matrix mutation rights under a completion label;
- invent runtime algorithms, schedules, or execution semantics as constitutional law.

A lawful Workflow Completion posture **guarantees** the following orchestration properties — not exam outcomes.

| Guarantee | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|

| **Criteria fidelity** | Every material completion cites applicable WCC criteria |

| **Authority preservation** | The completion judgement itself does not change coach meaning, plan intent, or evidence reading |

| **Evidence non-reinterpretation** | Evidence remains owned by Evidence Model / permitted writers |
| **Explainability** | Every material completion can answer the completion explainability questions |
| **Continuity** | Completion does not erase learner-owned educational history |

## 8. Integrity Rules

1. **Complete orchestration, do not certify education.** Closing a workflow is not mastery theatre.
2. **Criteria and evidence before convenience.** Do not close for UI polish if WCC/WCE fail.
3. **No temporal shortcuts.** Elapsed time and execution duration never complete a workflow by themselves.
4. **WT-06 without fulfilment is theatre.** Conclude transition requires honest MS003 judgement when claiming orchestration complete.
5. **Outstanding duties block completion.** Open consultations, unfinished required stages, or incomplete lawful handoffs ⇒ not complete.
6. **Escalate rather than invent.** Missing authority or broken envelopes use MS002 escalation / handoff — not fake close.
7. **Speak educationally about coordination.** Explanations cite stages, transitions, and authorities — not job queues.
8. **Amend before contradicting.** Implementation that needs different completion law must amend this corpus first.
