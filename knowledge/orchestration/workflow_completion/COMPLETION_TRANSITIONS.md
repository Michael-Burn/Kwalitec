> Successful orchestration completion closes coordination duties — it does not rewrite educational intent.**

## 1. Purpose

Without post-completion transition law, “workflow complete” becomes a badge with no constitutional consequence — or a silent plan rewrite / mastery ceremony.

With transition law, an expert tutor can:

- archive a fulfilled workflow instance without inventing educational claims;
- initiate a successor workflow when a new orchestration concern begins;
- await future educational events when the journey continues but this instance’s duties are done or parked;
- generate a constitutional audit record for explainability and continuity.

---

## 2. Relationship to MS002 Transitions

| MS002 class | MS003 completion transition | Relationship |
|-------------|----------------------------|--------------|
| **WT-06 Conclude** | **WCT-01** (archive) and often paired with **WCT-04** (audit) | Specialised success close with WCC/WCE gates |
| **WT-01 Initiate** (new concern) | **WCT-02** | Successor workflow after prior instance fulfilment or handoff boundary |
| **WT-10 Park continuation** | **WCT-03** | Await future events — *not* false completion when duties remain |
| **WT-08 / WT-05** | May precede **WCT-02** | Handoff/escalate completed; successor owns next primary |
| **Remain / resume (WT-02–WT-04)** | **WCT-05** | Non-completion: continue orchestration |

MS002 remains the stage-movement vocabulary. WCT-XX are completion-judgement transitions that **align with** WT-xx and add criteria/evidence discipline for post-fulfilment moves. They do not author a rival educational stack, and they do not invent new educational meaning.

---

## 3. Transition Principles

1. **Continuity of learner history.** Completion does not wipe educational identity (EIP-005).  
2. **Explain before material close.** Completion speech under `COMPLETION_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.  
3. **Orchestration-only effects.** WCT moves change coordination posture — not coach meaning or plan intent.  
4. **No new educational meaning.** Archive / successor / await / audit must not mint recommendations.  
5. **Outstanding duties block archive.** Incomplete WCC ⇒ WCT-05 continue, not WCT-01.  
6. **Await ≠ complete.** Parking for future events is honest waiting, not fulfilment theatre.  
7. **Successor is explicit.** New concerns open new instances; they do not silently mutate the old one.  
8. **Audit is constitutional, not analytics theatre.** Audit records preserve authority trail.  
9. **No timer gates.** Transitions are orchestration judgements, not SLA expiry.  
10. **One primary story** after the judgement — no blended coach speech from the close itself.

## 4. Transition Catalogue

### WCT-01 — Archive workflow

**Orchestration meaning.** Applicable completion criteria are honestly met on constitutional evidence. This workflow instance’s orchestration responsibilities are fulfilled. The instance moves to `concluded` and may be archived as a closed coordination record.

**Requires:**

- Core WCC set (WCC-01, WCC-02, WCC-04, WCC-05, WCC-06) affirmed;
- WCC-03 affirmed when handoff/escalate applied;
- Supporting WCE trail without WCE-06 blockers;
- Alignment with MS002 WT-06 conditions;
- Explainability can honestly say orchestration for this concern is complete — without mastery speech.

**Lawful effects:**

- Apply WT-06 → `concluded`;
- Retain learner-owned educational history;
- Preserve Programme VI artefacts by reference;
- Typically emit WCT-04 audit record;
- Allow future unrelated WE-xx to open new instances (not reopen this one’s duties without new warrant).

**Unlawful effects:**

- Archiving because a timer, duration, or UI funnel finished;
- Claiming educational success / mastery / coach success from archive;
- Silently rewriting plan envelopes or coach recommendations “on close”;
- Erasing continuity records to tidy the archive.

**Authority boundary:** Programme VII closes orchestration; Programme VI meaning unchanged; Master Planner intent unchanged.

---

### WCT-02 — Initiate successor workflow

**Orchestration meaning.** Either (a) this instance has fulfilled its duties and a *new* educational concern requires fresh orchestration, or (b) a completed handoff/escalate transfers ongoing coordination to a new instance under a named primary — without inventing educational meaning in the transition itself.

**Typical uses:**

- Day orchestration concludes (WCT-01) and a later session-complete event will open a reflection workflow (successor when that WE-xx arrives — may be deferred via WCT-03 until then);
- S5 hand off completed; successor instance continues under the receiving Programme VI authority;
- Escalate path opens Master Planner–primary successor after prior coach-primary instance closes its transfer duty.

**Requires:**

- Clear separation of prior instance duties vs successor concern;
- Named primary authority for the successor (when immediately initiated);
- No dual-primary across instances for the same living question;
- No educational recommendation minted by the successor-initiation act itself.

**Lawful effects:**

- Prior instance may WCT-01 if its own WCC set is met;
- Successor opens via WT-01 under MS001/MS002 law when the new concern is live;
- Explainability names the transfer of coordination — not a new coach tip from Programme VII.

**Unlawful effects:**

- Using “successor” to bypass incomplete WCC on the prior instance while pretending it archived cleanly;
- Merging two primaries across instances;
- Rewriting Canonical Study Plan as the successor’s first act without Master Planner authority;
- Treating successor initiation as educational completion of the prior coach question.

**Authority boundary:** Orchestration may sequence instances; educational meaning remains with the named Programme VI primary of each instance.

---

### WCT-03 — Await future educational events

**Orchestration meaning.** Further progress on the educational concern requires a future WE-xx stimulus. The instance parks (`awaiting_continuation`) **or** this instance has concluded its present duties while the journey simply waits for ordinary future events without an open parked instance — without inventing interim independent recommendations.

**Typical uses:**

- S7 continue decision: session must complete before reflection (WT-10);
- Day guidance delivered; next orchestration awaits tomorrow’s login / plan-faithful event;
- Thin context: refuse/no-op now; await richer educational event before reopening.

**Requires:**

- Named expected event class (or explicit “any lawful continuation WE-xx for this concern”);
- No interim independent Programme VII recommendations while awaiting;
- Honest distinction: parked incomplete instance vs archived instance with future unrelated events.

**Lawful effects:**

- WT-10 park when this instance remains the owner of the open concern;
- Or WCT-01 archive when duties for *this* instance are done and future events will open *new* instances;
- Resume later via WT-04 when parked.

**Unlawful effects:**

- Labelling await as “complete” while required consultations/stages remain open;
- Emitting filler coaching to cover the wait;
- Using await to hide outstanding WCC failures;
- Inferring that waiting time itself advances completion criteria.

**Authority boundary:** Waiting is orchestration honesty; Programme VI does not lose meaning during the wait; no plan mutation to “keep the product moving.”

---

### WCT-04 — Generate constitutional audit record

**Orchestration meaning.** Produce an auditable completion record binding event → stages → transitions → participants → outcome → WCC/WCE trail → authority preservation → non-certification of educational success.

**Requires:**

- Material completion judgement (typically with WCT-01);
- Trace links from `COMPLETION_EVIDENCE.md` §8;
- Explicit non-reliance on elapsed time / duration alone.

**Lawful effects:**

- Persist constitutional audit artefacts for developers and educational continuity;
- Support student-facing explainability without exposing internal IDs;
- Enable later review that Programme VII did not invent meaning.

**Unlawful effects:**

- Analytics vanity events without WCE trail presented as constitutional audit;
- Audit that rewrites Programme VI content;
- Audit that claims mastery or coach educational success;
- Secret audit fields that mutate plan or Twin estimates.

**Authority boundary:** Audit observes and records orchestration; it does not become a State Authority Matrix writer.

---

### WCT-05 — Continue orchestration (non-completion)

**Orchestration meaning.** Completion cannot be honestly affirmed. The workflow remains active: continue stages, pause awaiting output, resume, hand off, or escalate under MS002 — do not archive.

**Typical uses:**

- Applicable WCC criterion missing;
- WCE-06 blockers present;
- Still `awaiting_output` for required consultation;
- Conflict check unresolved.

**Requires:**

- Explicit refusal of WCT-01;
- Speakable reason under `COMPLETION_EXPLAINABILITY.md` when students might otherwise expect “done.”

**Lawful effects:**

- Remain in MS002 movement law (WT-02…WT-05, WT-08, WT-03/WT-04);
- Gather missing stage/consultation trail;
- Prefer honest incomplete over false archive.

**Unlawful effects:**

- Soft-closing in the UI while leaving constitutional duties open;
- Forcing WT-06 despite failed WCC;
- Inventing missing coach artefacts to unlock archive.

**Authority boundary:** Non-completion preserves the open educational concern; it does not punish the learner or invent educational failure from unfinished orchestration.

## 5. Combination Patterns (Illustrative)

| Situation | Typical WCT pattern |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Ordinary day path finished; guidance explained | **WCT-01** + **WCT-04**; future login is a new instance (**WCT-03** as journey posture, not a parked duty) |
| Session path must wait for sitting end | **WCT-03** via WT-10; later WT-04 resume — not WCT-01 yet |
| Hand off to Learning Coach completed | Prior instance **WCT-01**/**WCT-04**; **WCT-02** successor under Learning Coach primary |
| Escalation to Master Planner | Transfer duty complete → **WCT-02**; structural meaning stays Master Planner |
| Criteria not met | **WCT-05** only |

---

## 6. Binding Combination Rules

1. **WCT-01 requires applicable WCC affirmation.** No archive on hope.  
2. **WCT-04 accompanies material WCT-01.** Audit is not optional theatre for material closes.  
3. **WCT-02 must not launder incomplete priors.** Prior archive only if prior WCC met.  
4. **WCT-03 must not be labelled complete** when the same instance still owns open duties.  
5. **WCT-05 is mandatory** when WCE-06 blockers remain.  
6. **No WCT introduces educational meaning.** Recommendations stay Programme VI–owned.  
7. **No WCT mutates Canonical Study Plan intent.** Structural change uses Master Planner pathways only.

## 7. Explicit Non-Transitions (Forbidden)

| Forbidden move | Why |
|----------------|-----|
| Auto-archive on timer / SLA | Temporal theatre |
| Auto-archive on session complete tick | Event ≠ orchestration fulfilment |
| Mastery ceremony on workflow close | Scope violation |
| Plan rewrite “now that workflow finished” | Authority violation |
| Silent coach-tip edit on archive | Authority violation |
| Merge archive + successor into dual-primary | Integrity violation |
| Analytics-only “completed” without WCT-04 trail | Audit evasion |

---

## 8. Anti-Patterns (Forbidden)

- Celebrating workflow completion as exam readiness
- Keeping an instance “soft open” forever to avoid audit
- Opening successors that redefine the prior coach’s artefact
- Using await parking to emit independent Programme VII tips
- Narrating WCT IDs to students
