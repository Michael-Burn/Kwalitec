# Beta Expectations

Student-facing expectations for the Kwalitec Internal Alpha / private beta.

---

## What should work

You should be able to:

- Sign in with the credentials your Kwalitec coordinator gave you
- Complete (or skip) the short Alpha onboarding
- Create a study plan for a **Supported** IFoA paper: **CS1**, **CB2**, or **CM1**
- See today’s study focus on the Dashboard and on Session
- Start a study session from **Dashboard → Session**, work through the checklist, and finish
- Record practice results after the session
- Open Journey, History, Revision, Analytics, Study Plan, Settings, and Help
- Switch Light / Dark / System appearance
- Send Product Check-in and Alpha feedback forms
- Sign out cleanly

If something in this list fails for you, please report it — that is a defect relative to Alpha expectations.

---

## What is experimental

Treat these as early and changeable:

- **Home (`/student/`)** layout with Readiness, Journey story, Coach insight, and milestones
- **Coach insight** wording and usefulness day to day
- **Revision** options (may be empty until you have enough syllabus progress)
- **History** and **Analytics** charts while you still have little study data
- **Educational history** step after creating a plan
- **Home → Start Session** Session Overview (works; briefing may be thinner than Learning Workspace Session)
- Dual navigation (sidebar Learning Workspace vs Student Experience top nav)
- Backup / restore and weekly report export in Settings
- Subject list entries marked Coming Soon or Not Supported

Experimental does **not** mean “ignore it.” It means your honest reaction is especially valuable.

---

## What feedback we want from students

Please tell us, in plain language:

1. **Clarity** — Did you always know what to do next within 30 seconds of opening the app?
2. **Trust** — Did today’s mission feel justified for your exam, or arbitrary?
3. **Session quality** — Was the study session screen helpful while you actually studied, or did you ignore it?
4. **After the session** — Was recording practice results easy? Did reflection/check-in feel worth the time?
5. **Progress** — Did Journey / History / Analytics help you feel on track, or create anxiety / confusion?
6. **Coach** — Was the Coach insight useful, vague, or wrong?
7. **Coverage** — Did missing papers (Coming Soon) or blocked subjects frustrate you?
8. **Friction** — Where did you get stuck, click the wrong place, or need to ask someone for help?
9. **Tone** — Does Internal Alpha / Founding Cohort labelling feel appropriate for how you are using the product?
10. **Must-fix** — If we could fix only three things before wider beta, what would they be?

You can send feedback via:

- **Share Feedback** / Product Check-in  
- **Help → Report a problem** or **Suggest an improvement**  
- Post-session feedback links  
- Directly to your Kwalitec coordinator

---

## What we are not asking you to evaluate

Please do not spend review time on:

- How the product is engineered internally
- Staff / Console areas (you should not have access)
- Speculating about future redesigns

Evaluate Kwalitec as you would any study tool you were asked to use for real exam preparation.
