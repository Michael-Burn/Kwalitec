# Known Limitations

Only issues a student can realistically see or feel in the current product. Developer-only debt is omitted.

---

## Access and accounts

1. **No public registration**  
   Sign in states Internal Alpha is invite-only. There is no Sign up / Register screen. Students who arrive without coordinator credentials cannot create an account.

2. **Email cannot be changed in Settings**  
   Profile shows email as read-only (“cannot be changed here”).

3. **Password reset is not self-service**  
   Sign in tells students to contact their Kwalitec coordinator for lost details or resets.

---

## Subject coverage

4. **Only some IFoA papers are Supported**  
   Full plans work for **CS1**, **CB2**, and **CM1**. Other IFoA papers may appear as **Coming Soon** and block plan creation.

5. **Non-IFoA categories are listed but Not Supported**  
   The wizard shows CFA, ACCA, CIMA, university, school, and Other options that cannot produce a Version 1 plan.

---

## Incomplete or placeholder student content

6. **Study plan roadmap outcomes: “Not available yet”**  
   Plan detail may show outcome rows that are not filled in.

7. **Early empty Journey / History / Readiness / Milestones**  
   New or lightly used accounts show empty or 0% states until sessions produce evidence. Copy explains this, but the panels can feel unfinished on day one.

8. **Coach is not its own page**  
   Reviewers looking for a Coach destination in the nav will not find one. Insight lives on Home only.

9. **No dedicated Reflection nav item**  
   Reflection happens after finishing a session (practice outcome + feedback) and via Product Check-in.

---

## Workflow friction

10. **Two homes exist**  
    `/dashboard/` (sidebar Learning Workspace) and `/student/` (Home / Student Experience) both present “what to do next.” Students may be unsure which is authoritative. Default post-login home is the Learning Workspace Dashboard.

11. **Session duration can disagree across surfaces**  
    Student Experience Home may show **30 minutes** for today’s mission while Learning Workspace Session briefing shows **90 min** for the same day’s topic. Students must choose which estimate to trust.

12. **Session Experience Overview can be thin**  
    After Home → **Start Session**, Session Overview may show placeholder-style copy (e.g. “your topic”), generic topic labels (e.g. “Core methods”), and **No activities listed**, while the Learning Workspace Session path shows a fuller briefing and activity checklist.

13. **Daily goal hours may not feel permanent**  
    Preferences allow a daily goal, but students should not assume it behaves like a fully saved account preference across all devices/sessions.

14. **Weekly report “PDF” is plain text**  
    Settings Data export that sounds like a PDF downloads as a text report.

15. **Profile examination may disagree with Dashboard**  
    Student Profile can show **Current Examination: Not set** while Dashboard / Study Plan clearly show an active paper (e.g. IFoA CS1).

---

## Feedback and Alpha chrome

16. **Internal Alpha labelling is persistent**  
    Badges and footers repeatedly show Internal Alpha / Founding Cohort / Build RC2. Expected for this programme, but not a polished GA consumer look.

17. **Some feedback entry points are easy to miss**  
    Mission-helpful and explanation-clear forms are reachable from post-session / Help paths rather than primary nav.

---

## Errors and recovery

18. **403 Access Denied for staff areas**  
    Visiting Console/staff URLs as a student correctly shows Access Denied — expected, but abrupt if a link is followed by mistake.

19. **Native browser confirm dialogs**  
    Archive/Delete plan actions use browser confirm prompts, which are easy to miss and not styled like the rest of the app.

---

## Loading states

20. **No durable full-page loading screen for reviewers to “open”**  
    Loading / skeleton UI may flash briefly during navigation but is not a stable student destination. Not captured as a standing screen in this package.
