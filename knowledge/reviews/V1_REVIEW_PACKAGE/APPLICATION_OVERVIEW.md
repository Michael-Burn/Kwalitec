# Application Overview

**Product name:** Kwalitec  
**Student-facing description:** Education Operating System  
**Value line:** Know exactly what to study next.  
**Programme status shown in the product:** Internal Alpha · Founding Cohort  
**Build shown in the product:** Kwalitec v2.0.0 · Build RC2

This overview describes Kwalitec as a private-beta student would encounter it. It does not describe how the product is built.

---

## Purpose

Kwalitec helps actuarial students prepare for Institute and Faculty of Actuaries (IFoA) exams by answering one daily question clearly:

> What should I study next?

Students set an exam goal and schedule once. After that, Kwalitec proposes a focused study session for the day, keeps a record of progress toward the exam, and surfaces simple coaching insight based on what the student has already done.

The product is designed to reduce decision fatigue during long exam seasons — not to replace official syllabus materials or tutoring.

---

## Supported IFoA subjects

Students can create a full study plan for these IFoA papers when the matching syllabus is available in the product:

| Paper | Student-visible status |
|---|---|
| **CS1** | Supported |
| **CB2** | Supported |
| **CM1** | Supported |

Other IFoA papers may appear in the study-plan wizard as **Coming Soon** (for example CS2, CM2, CB1, CB3, CP1, CP2, CP3, SP, SA). Selecting a Coming Soon paper does not create a usable plan.

The wizard also lists other examination families (for example CFA, ACCA, CIMA, university courses, A-Level, GCSE). In this release those options are shown as **Not Supported** for plan creation.

---

## Intended users

- IFoA students preparing for a supported paper (CS1, CB2, or CM1)
- Invite-only Internal Alpha / Founding Cohort participants
- Students who already have login credentials from a Kwalitec coordinator

Kwalitec is **not** open to public self-registration in this release.

---

## Problems the application claims to solve

From the sign-in experience and in-product copy, Kwalitec claims to help students:

1. **Always know what to study next** — a clear daily mission instead of an open-ended syllabus.
2. **Plan around a real exam date** — study time and sitting chosen by the student.
3. **Run focused sessions** — one objective for the day, with suggested activities.
4. **See honest progress** — readiness, journey, and history views that fill in as study happens.
5. **Keep a sustainable pace** — tips and workload cues during exam season.

---

## Student value proposition

Kwalitec presents itself as an Education Operating System whose job is to **reduce decisions and increase learning**.

A student who completes onboarding and creates a study plan should be able to:

- Open the app and immediately see today’s recommended study focus
- Start a study session, work through suggested activities, and record practice results
- Check Journey / History to see how preparation is progressing
- Adjust preferences and give product feedback during the Internal Alpha

Accounts, passwords, and access are managed outside the public web UI by the Kwalitec coordinator.
