# Application Walkthrough

A realistic ~60-minute first study block for an invited Internal Alpha student preparing for **IFoA CS1**.

---

## Minute 0–5 — First login

Alex opens the Kwalitec URL on a laptop (desktop width).

The Sign in screen shows the Kwalitec logo, “Education Operating System,” and “Know exactly what to study next.” An **Internal Alpha · Founding Cohort** badge makes the programme status clear. The form explains there is no public sign-up.

Alex enters the email and password provided by the coordinator, checks Remember me, and signs in.

If this is the first Alpha login, a short onboarding walkthrough appears. Alex clicks through (or skips) and continues.

Because Alex already has a study plan for IFoA CS1 (April sitting), the app opens on the **Student Dashboard**: today’s study session, progress cues, and whether they are on track.

---

## Minute 5–12 — Planning check

Alex opens **Study Plan** from the sidebar to confirm:

- Exam: IFoA CS1  
- Sitting and exam date  
- Weekday / weekend minutes  
- Current topic focus  

Nothing needs changing today. Alex returns to the Dashboard.

Optionally, Alex opens `/student/` (Home) to see the same day framed as **Today’s Mission**, with Readiness, Journey, and Coach panels. Coach insight restates why today’s topic is the highest-value next step.

---

## Minute 12–45 — Studying

From the Dashboard, Alex clicks **Start Study Session** and lands on **Today’s Study Session**.

The briefing shows:

- Topic: e.g. “Review CS1-A: Descriptive statistics foundations”  
- Estimated time (aligned to the plan)  
- Why this session exists  
- What success looks like  
- Recommended activities (read, examples, practice, review mistakes)

Alex clicks **Start Study Session**. The active session screen starts a timer, lists today’s tasks, and lets Alex tick recommended activities while working from official notes and question banks (outside Kwalitec).

Alex studies for roughly half an hour: reading syllabus notes, working examples, and attempting practice questions.

---

## Minute 45–52 — Completing work

Alex clicks **Finish Study Session**.

On **Practice Outcome Capture**, Alex records:

- Questions attempted  
- Questions correct  
- Optional duration and notes  

Alex submits. **Study Session Feedback** confirms the session is recorded and offers Alpha feedback links and Product Check-in.

---

## Minute 52–57 — Reflection

Alex spends a minute on **Product Check-in** (Share Feedback / post-session prompt): quick answers about the study experience, then **Thank you**.

Alex also glances at the Coach panel and Journey page: progress may still be low early on, which is expected. History is mostly empty until more sessions accumulate — also expected.

---

## Minute 57–60 — Reviewing progress and leaving

Alex opens **Analytics** (sidebar) to see charts beginning to populate, then **Settings → Preferences** to confirm Light/Dark/System appearance and a daily goal.

Satisfied, Alex clicks **Sign out** in the sidebar and returns to the Sign in screen.

---

## What “done” looks like after one hour

| Outcome | Visible result |
|---|---|
| Signed in successfully | Dashboard / Home available |
| Confirmed plan | Study Plan shows IFoA CS1 |
| Completed a session | Mission moves through In Progress → completed path |
| Recorded practice | Practice outcome saved; feedback screen shown |
| Optional reflection | Product Check-in thank-you |
| Signed out cleanly | Sign in screen |

This is a complete, realistic Alpha study hour using the **Learning Workspace Session path**. Home → **Start Session** also opens Session Overview; that surface may show a thinner briefing than the Learning Workspace Session page (see Known Limitations).
