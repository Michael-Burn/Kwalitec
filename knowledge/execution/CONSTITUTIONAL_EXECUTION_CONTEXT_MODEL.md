## Authority

This document is the constitutional overview of the **Constitutional Execution Context Model** for Kwalitec.

It is subordinate to the Educational Constitution, Educational Interpretation Principles (EIP), Programme VI educational meaning corpora, Programme VII orchestration corpora, Programme VIII runtime constitutional corpora, and Programme IX conformance / verification / compliance / certification / evolution corpora. It governs **what constitutional information must exist before any constitutional execution may begin** — its objectives, recognised context components, hard boundaries, and explainability. It does not authorise implementation shortcuts that contradict the Constitution, and it does not execute constitutional logic, interpret constitutional law, produce constitutional decisions, modify constitutional specifications, redefine constitutional meaning, replace constitutional authority, mint recommendations, invent educational state, perform verification, determine compliance, certify implementations, or amend constitutional law.

Authority order for constitutional execution context:

> Constitution defines educational truth and curriculum primacy.  
> EIP defines evidence classification, continuity, explainability, claim honesty, and mutation rights.  
> Programme VI defines educational meaning and may publish learning / assessment warrants.  
> Programme VII defines orchestration flow, ownership, recommendations, context, and orchestration evidence artefacts.  
> Programme VIII defines how runtime implementations may lawfully execute published law (contracts, events, evidence, services, interfaces).  
> Programme IX defines when implementations may be judged conformant, traced, verified, found compliant, certified, and how the corpus may lawfully evolve.  
> **This Constitutional Execution Context Model (Programme X / Workstream 1 / MS001) defines the complete constitutional information environment available prior to constitutional execution.**  
> Downstream constitutional execution activities, Runtime A, product surfaces, Twin, Adaptive, and narration must consume this information environment — never become constitutional producers by assembling context, and never treat context assembly as execution, interpretation, or decision.

## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not begin a formal examination of a student’s plan without knowing which syllabus rules apply, which interpretation principles constrain speech, which prior governance artefacts exist, which concern is in scope, and which hard limits must not be crossed. After the Constitution and EIP have defined *educational law*, after Programmes VI–VIII have published *meaning, orchestration, and runtime law*, and after Programme IX has published *how implementations may be judged and how the corpus may evolve*, the platform still needs one pre-execution answer:

> **“What constitutional information must exist before any constitutional execution may begin?”**

That answer must establish the complete constitutional environment available to execution — authority references, interpretation principles, specifications, governance artefacts, execution identity, scope, assumptions, and constraints — while remaining fully consistent, implementation-neutral, explainable, and repeatable — without executing constitutional rules, producing constitutional decisions, modifying constitutional specifications, or replacing constitutional authority.

This document records that posture so every future constitutional execution activity (and successor) has a single constitutional reference for *what information environment must be assembled before execution is lawful to begin*.

> **The Constitutional Execution Context Model describes the complete constitutional information environment available prior to execution.  
> It does not execute constitutional logic, interpret constitutional law, produce constitutional decisions, or become constitutional authority.**

## 2. What Constitutional Execution Context Is

The **Constitutional Execution Context** is the binding **information environment** by which **published constitutional artefacts, interpretation principles, governance artefacts, execution identity, scope, assumptions, and constraints** may be **assembled and made available prior to constitutional execution** — without becoming a source of constitutional law, an interpreter of that law, an executor of constitutional rules, or a producer of constitutional decisions.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Published constitutional corpus** | Constitution, EIP, Programme VI / VII / VIII / IX specifications that publish educational, execution, and governance law | Which law may be referenced? |
| **Constitutional authority reference** | Identifiable citation of the corpora that own meaning for the concern | Who owns the standard? |
| **Interpretation context** | Applicable Educational Interpretation Principles and claim-honesty constraints | Which EIP principles apply? |
| **Specification context** | Named constitutional specifications in force for the concern | Which specifications are available? |
| **Governance context** | Named Programme IX (and related) governance artefacts available as information | Which governance artefacts are available? |
| **Execution identity** | Identifiable unit of intended constitutional execution (concern, instance, or activity identity) | Which execution is this? |
| **Execution scope** | Bound of what the intended execution may lawfully address | What is in / out of scope? |
| **Execution assumptions** | Explicit, non-legislative premises under which the intended execution may proceed | What is assumed without rewriting law? |
| **Execution constraints** | Hard limits that remain intact for the intended execution | Which limits may not be crossed? |
| **Information environment** | The assembled CECX-01…CECX-07 whole available *before* execution begins | Is the environment complete enough to begin? |
| **Implementation neutrality** | Context assembly does not privilege a stack, vendor, or topology as law | Remains the stack replaceable? |
| **Repeatability** | Same published artefacts, identity, scope, assumptions, and constraints → same assembled context | Can the environment be reproduced? |
| **Explainability** | Artefacts, principles, governance, constraints, and boundaries are speakable | Can the environment be narrated honestly? |

Constitutional Execution Context is:

- **information-centred** — it assembles what execution may know; it never executes what execution must do;
- **pre-execution** — it exists *before* constitutional execution begins; assembly is not a disguised first act of execution;
- **authority-preserving** — published corpora remain the sole source of constitutional meaning;
- **interpretation-referencing** — it may name applicable EIP principles; it never reinterprets them;
- **catalogue-closed** — only CECX-01…CECX-07 may be applied as constitutional execution context components;
- **corpus-bound** — every material context cites identifiable published specifications and artefacts;
- **assumption-honest** — assumptions are explicit and non-legislative; they never soft-amend law;
- **constraint-preserving** — hard constitutional boundaries remain intact and speakable;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by context law;
- **repeatable** — identical published inputs yield the same assembled information environment;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which artefacts, principles, governance items, constraints, and boundaries were available;
- **non-executive** — context never runs constitutional rules;
- **non-interpretive** — context never produces constitutional interpretation outcomes;
- **non-decisional** — context never produces constitutional decisions, findings, dispositions, or seals;
- **non-legislative** — context never modifies constitutional specifications or replaces constitutional authority.

Constitutional Execution Context is **not**:

- a second Educational Constitution, EIP, or Programme VI / VII / VIII / IX amendment path;
- a licence to reinterpret coach meaning, ownership maps, tip warrants, runtime contracts, or Programme IX judgements in “context” prose;
- a runtime execution engine, interpreter, workflow runner, scheduler, or decision service;
- a substitute for Programme VII educational / orchestration context — those corpora define educational or orchestration context meaning; CECX defines the *pre-execution constitutional information environment*;
- a substitute for Programme VIII runtime contracts or execution completion — RC / CE / REC govern *software execution of published law*; CECX governs *information available before constitutional execution*;
- a substitute for Programme IX conformance, verification, compliance, certification, or evolution — those corpora produce judgements; CECX may *reference* their published artefacts as information and never re-produce them;
- a claim that “context complete” guarantees learning, mastery, exam readiness, product success, conformity, verification, compliance, certification, or lawful execution outcome;
- a testing framework, CI/CD pipeline, GitHub Action, linter policy, or OpenAPI checker.

## 3. Execution Context Posture (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material constitutional execution context travels through three constitutional phases. These are **normative information-environment obligations**, not an implementation architecture.

```
Identify published constitutional artefacts and principles for the concern
        │
        ▼
Assemble CECX-01…CECX-07 information components
        │
        ├── incomplete / refused (lawful stop — execution must not begin)
        │
        └── complete enough → Preserve identity, scope, assumptions, constraints
                │
                ▼
        Explain artefacts / principles / governance / constraints / boundaries
                │
                ▼
        Make information available to constitutional execution (successor activities)
                │
                └── never execute, interpret, or decide within this Model
```

| Phase | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **Identify** | Name only published constitutional artefacts, EIP principles, and governance identities as available information | Standard is unpublished custom, tribal knowledge, or “how main works today” |
| **Assemble** | Apply CECX-01…CECX-07 to form the information environment | Assembly would invent law, rewrite specs, reinterpret educational meaning, execute rules, or produce decisions |
| **Preserve & explain** | Keep identity, scope, assumptions, constraints, and boundaries speakable without amending corpora | Path would treat context as execution, interpretation, decision, soft amendment, or stack canonisation |

> **Execution Context never “best-effort invents” a fourth phase called execution, interpretation, decision, or law-making.**

## 6. What Execution Context May Include

Execution Context may include — when published and identifiable — the following classes of information:

- **Constitutional authority references** — which corpora own meaning for the concern
- **Educational Interpretation Principles** — which EIP principles constrain honest speech and evidence claims
- **Constitutional specifications** — which Programme VI–IX (and Constitution / EIP) specifications are in force
- **Governance artefacts** — which Programme IX (and related) governance records are available as information
- **Execution identity** — which intended constitutional execution instance or concern this is
- **Execution scope** — what the intended execution may lawfully address
- **Execution assumptions** — explicit non-legislative premises carried into the environment
- **Execution constraints** — hard limits that remain intact

These classes map onto CECX-01…CECX-07. Assumptions are carried as explicit, non-legislative information within the assembled environment (typically alongside scope and constraints) and never as soft amendments.

## 7. What Execution Context Never Does

Execution Context never:

- **executes** constitutional rules or constitutional logic;
- **interprets** constitutional law into new meaning outcomes;
- **produces** constitutional decisions, findings, dispositions, seals, or warrants;
- **modifies** constitutional specifications;
- **replaces** constitutional authority;
- **determines** conformance, verification, compliance, or certification outcomes;
- **amends** constitutional law under context pretext;
- **freezes** a preferred stack, vendor, or reference implementation as law.

## 8. Integrity Rules

1. **Information before execution.** No constitutional execution may lawfully begin without an assembled information environment under this Model.
2. **Published artefacts only.** Unpublished customs are not constitutional information.
3. **Closed catalogue.** Only CECX-01…CECX-07 assemble constitutional execution context.
4. **No execution-by-assembly.** Assembling context is not performing execution.
5. **No interpretation theatre.** Naming EIP principles is not producing interpretation outcomes.
6. **No decision theatre.** Listing governance artefacts is not minting new judgements.
7. **Authority first.** Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard.
8. **Implementation independence.** No stack becomes law by proximity to context assembly.
9. **Assumptions are not law.** Explicit assumptions never soft-amend specifications.
10. **Constraints survive.** Hard boundaries remain intact and explainable.
11. **Repeatability.** Race-dependent or opaque context assembly is a defect.
12. **Explain the stop.** Incomplete or refused context must be speakable; silence is not permission to begin.

---

## 9. Architectural Requirement

> **Execution Context provides constitutional information to execution.  
> It never performs constitutional execution itself.**

Downstream constitutional execution activities (successor Programme X corpora and any consuming machinery) may *consume* this information environment. They remain bound by published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–IX law. Context assembly never becomes a second constitution, a soft interpreter, or a hidden decision engine.
