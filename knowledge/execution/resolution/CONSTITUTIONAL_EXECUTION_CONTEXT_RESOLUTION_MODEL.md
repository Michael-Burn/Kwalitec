## Authority

This document is the constitutional overview of the **Constitutional Execution Context Resolution Model** for Kwalitec.

It is subordinate to the Educational Constitution, Educational Interpretation Principles (EIP), Programme VI educational meaning corpora, Programme VII orchestration corpora, Programme VIII runtime constitutional corpora, Programme IX conformance / verification / compliance / certification / evolution corpora, and the Constitutional Execution Context Model (Programme X / WS1 / MS001). It governs **how the relevant constitutional information is selected and assembled from the available Execution Context for a specific constitutional execution** — its objectives, recognised stages, hard boundaries, and explainability. It does not authorise implementation shortcuts that contradict the Constitution, and it does not execute constitutional logic, interpret constitutional law independently, produce constitutional decisions, modify constitutional specifications, redefine constitutional meaning, replace constitutional authority, mint recommendations, invent educational state, perform verification, determine compliance, certify implementations, or amend constitutional law.

Authority order for constitutional execution context resolution:

> Constitution defines educational truth and curriculum primacy.  
> EIP defines evidence classification, continuity, explainability, claim honesty, and mutation rights.  
> Programme VI defines educational meaning and may publish learning / assessment warrants.  
> Programme VII defines orchestration flow, ownership, recommendations, context, and orchestration evidence artefacts.  
> Programme VIII defines how runtime implementations may lawfully execute published law (contracts, events, evidence, services, interfaces).  
> Programme IX defines when implementations may be judged conformant, traced, verified, found compliant, certified, and how the corpus may lawfully evolve.  
> The Constitutional Execution Context Model (Programme X / Workstream 1 / MS001) defines the complete constitutional information environment available prior to constitutional execution.  
> **This Constitutional Execution Context Resolution Model (Programme X / Workstream 1 / MS002) defines how that Execution Context is lawfully resolved into a constitutionally valid execution view for a specific constitutional execution.**  
> Downstream constitutional execution activities, Runtime A, product surfaces, Twin, Adaptive, and narration must consume the resolved execution context — never become constitutional producers by resolving context, and never treat resolution as execution, independent interpretation, or decision.

## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not dump every book, past paper, and corridor rumour onto the desk for every sitting. After the desk has been prepared (the Execution Context), the tutor still selects *which* syllabus rules, interpretation principles, governance records, and hard limits apply *to this specific examination act* — without inventing new meaning, without running the exam inside the selection step, and without declaring a pass because the right papers were chosen. After the Constitution and EIP have defined *educational law*, after Programmes VI–IX have published *meaning, orchestration, runtime, and governance law*, and after MS001 has assembled the *complete information environment available prior to execution*, the platform still needs one resolution answer:

> **“How is the relevant constitutional information selected and assembled from the available Execution Context for a specific constitutional execution?”**

That answer must consume the Execution Context; identify relevant constitutional artefacts; assemble a **resolved execution context**; preserve constitutional authority and implementation neutrality; and remain fully deterministic, repeatable, explainable, and faithful to published law — without executing constitutional rules, producing constitutional decisions, modifying constitutional specifications, or replacing constitutional authority.

This document records that posture so every future constitutional execution activity (and successor) has a single constitutional reference for *how an available information environment becomes a constitutionally valid execution view without becoming law, an interpreter, or an execution engine*.

> **The Constitutional Execution Context Resolution Model describes how an Execution Context is resolved into a constitutionally valid execution view.  
> It does not execute constitutional logic, interpret constitutional law independently, produce constitutional decisions, or become constitutional authority.**

## 2. What Constitutional Execution Context Resolution Is

**Constitutional Execution Context Resolution** is the binding **selection and assembly process** by which **relevant constitutional information** is **chosen from an available Constitutional Execution Context** and **assembled into a resolved execution context** suitable for a specific constitutional execution — without becoming a source of constitutional law, an independent interpreter of that law, an executor of constitutional rules, or a producer of constitutional decisions.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Execution Context** | The MS001 information environment (CECX-01…CECX-07) available prior to execution | What constitutional information is available? |
| **Resolution** | The lawful process of selecting and assembling relevant information from that environment for a specific execution | How is the relevant subset chosen and assembled? |
| **Resolved execution context** | The constitutionally valid execution view produced by resolution | What information is bound for *this* execution? |
| **Selected artefacts** | Published constitutional artefacts included in the resolved view | Which artefacts were selected? |
| **Excluded artefacts** | Available artefacts deliberately left out of the resolved view (with reconstructable reason) | Which artefacts were excluded? |
| **Assembled dependencies** | Published dependency relationships carried into the resolved view without inventing new law | Which dependencies were assembled? |
| **Consistency validation** | Checks that the resolved view does not contradict itself under published law | Is the resolved view coherent? |
| **Constitutional authority** | The exclusive right of published corpora to define educational and execution meaning | Who owns the standard? |
| **Implementation neutrality** | Resolution does not privilege a stack, vendor, or topology as law | Remains the stack replaceable? |
| **Determinism** | Same Execution Context + same execution identity / scope ⇒ same resolved view (or same honest refuse) | Is resolution operator-independent? |
| **Repeatability** | The resolved view is reproducible from reconstructable inputs | Can the view be reconstructed? |
| **Explainability** | Selection, exclusion, dependencies, checks, and boundaries are speakable | Can resolution be narrated honestly? |
| **Constitutional fidelity** | Resolution preserves published meaning exactly; it never soft-amends law | Does the resolved view remain faithful to law? |

Constitutional Execution Context Resolution is:

- **selection-centred** — it chooses relevant information from what is available; it never invents unpublished artefacts;
- **assembly-centred** — it builds a resolved execution context; it never executes that context;
- **context-consuming** — it consumes the MS001 Execution Context; it never replaces or redefines CECX components;
- **authority-preserving** — published corpora remain the sole source of constitutional meaning;
- **catalogue-closed** — only CECR-01…CECR-06 may be applied as constitutional resolution stages;
- **corpus-bound** — every material resolution cites identifiable published specifications and artefacts;
- **exclusion-honest** — exclusions are reconstructable; silence is not a lawful exclusion reason;
- **consistency-bound** — contradictory resolved views are refused, not silently merged;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by resolution law;
- **deterministic** — identical published inputs yield the same resolved view (or the same honest refuse disposition);
- **repeatable** — resolution is reproducible and not race-dependent or operator-dependent;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which artefacts were selected, excluded, depended upon, checked, and which boundaries remained intact;
- **non-executive** — resolution never runs constitutional rules;
- **non-interpretive** — resolution never produces independent constitutional interpretation outcomes;
- **non-decisional** — resolution never produces constitutional decisions, findings, dispositions, or seals;
- **non-legislative** — resolution never modifies constitutional specifications or replaces constitutional authority.

Constitutional Execution Context Resolution is **not**:

- a second Educational Constitution, EIP, Programme VI / VII / VIII / IX, or MS001 amendment path;
- a licence to reinterpret coach meaning, ownership maps, tip warrants, runtime contracts, Programme IX judgements, or CECX components in “resolution” prose;
- a runtime execution engine, interpreter, workflow runner, scheduler, or decision service;
- a substitute for MS001 Execution Context assembly — MS001 defines *what is available*; CECR defines *how what is available is resolved for a specific execution*;
- a substitute for Programme VII educational / orchestration context — those corpora define educational or orchestration context meaning; CECR resolves *constitutional* information for execution;
- a substitute for Programme VIII runtime contracts or execution completion — RC / CE / REC govern *software execution of published law*; CECR governs *selection and assembly of constitutional information*;
- a substitute for Programme IX conformance, verification, compliance, certification, or evolution — those corpora produce judgements; CECR may *select* their published artefacts as information and never re-produce them;
- a claim that “resolved” guarantees learning, mastery, exam readiness, product success, conformity, verification, compliance, certification, or lawful execution outcome;
- a testing framework, CI/CD pipeline, GitHub Action, linter policy, or OpenAPI checker.

## 3. Resolution Posture (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material constitutional execution context resolution travels through recognised stages CECR-01…CECR-06. These are **normative selection-and-assembly obligations**, not an implementation architecture.

```
Consume available Execution Context (MS001 / CECX-01…CECX-07)
        │
        ▼
CECR-01  Resolution Initiation
        │
        ▼
CECR-02  Scope Determination
        │
        ▼
CECR-03  Artefact Selection
        │
        ▼
CECR-04  Dependency Assembly
        │
        ▼
CECR-05  Consistency Validation
        │
        ├── inconsistent / refused (lawful stop — resolved view must not be published as complete)
        │
        └── consistent → CECR-06 Resolved Context Publication
                │
                ▼
        Make resolved execution context available to constitutional execution
                │
                └── never execute, interpret independently, or decide within this Model
```

| Phase family | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|--------------|------------------------|----------------|
| **Initiate & bound** (CECR-01…CECR-02) | Open resolution against a named execution identity and bound the resolution scope under published law | Execution Context absent / incomplete; identity theatrical; scope invents unpublished concerns |
| **Select & assemble** (CECR-03…CECR-04) | Choose relevant artefacts and assemble published dependencies into a candidate resolved view | Selection invents artefacts; exclusion is silent; dependencies invent new law |
| **Validate & publish** (CECR-05…CECR-06) | Confirm internal consistency and publish the resolved execution context (or refuse) | Consistency would require soft-amendment; publication would present resolution as execution / decision / law |

> **Resolution never “best-effort invents” a seventh stage called execution, independent interpretation, decision, or law-making.**

## 6. What Resolution Consumes and Produces

### Consumes

Resolution consumes — when published and identifiable — the **Constitutional Execution Context** assembled under MS001, including:

- Constitutional authority references (CECX-01)
- Interpretation context (CECX-02)
- Specification context (CECX-03)
- Governance context (CECX-04)
- Execution identity (CECX-05)
- Execution scope and explicit assumptions (CECX-06)
- Execution constraints (CECX-07)

Resolution may also consume published dependency relationships already present among those artefacts. It never invents missing CECX components to force a resolved view.

### Produces

Resolution produces a **resolved execution context**: a constitutionally valid execution view that:

- names selected artefacts and exclusions;
- carries assembled dependencies;
- records consistency-validation outcomes;
- preserves authority, neutrality, and constraints;
- is suitable for consumption by successor constitutional execution activities.

Resolution does **not** produce constitutional decisions, interpretation outcomes, compliance determinations, certifications, or amended specifications.

## 7. What Resolution Never Does

Resolution never:

- **executes** constitutional rules or constitutional logic;
- **interprets** constitutional law independently into new meaning outcomes;
- **produces** constitutional decisions, findings, dispositions, seals, or warrants;
- **determines** conformance, verification, compliance, or certification outcomes;
- **modifies** constitutional specifications;
- **replaces** constitutional authority;
- **amends** constitutional law under resolution pretext;
- **freezes** a preferred stack, vendor, or reference implementation as law;
- **redefines** MS001 CECX components or invents an eighth resolution stage outside CECR-01…CECR-06.

## 8. Integrity Rules

1. **Context before resolution.** No resolution may lawfully claim completeness without a consumable MS001 Execution Context.
2. **Published artefacts only.** Unpublished customs are not selectable constitutional information.
3. **Closed catalogue.** Only CECR-01…CECR-06 organise constitutional execution context resolution.
4. **No execution-by-resolution.** Selecting and assembling information is not performing execution.
5. **No interpretation theatre.** Naming EIP principles in the resolved view is not producing interpretation outcomes.
6. **No decision theatre.** Selecting governance artefacts is not minting new judgements.
7. **Authority first.** Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard.
8. **Implementation independence.** No stack becomes law by proximity to resolution.
9. **Exclusions are speakable.** Silent omission of available artefacts is a defect.
10. **Consistency or refuse.** Material inconsistency yields refused / incomplete resolution — never silent merge into execution.
11. **Determinism and repeatability.** Race-dependent or opaque resolution is a defect.
12. **Explain the stop.** Incomplete or refused resolution must be speakable; silence is not permission to treat an unresolved view as resolved.

## 9. Architectural Requirement

> **Resolution assembles constitutional information for execution.  
> It never performs constitutional execution or constitutional interpretation independently.**

Downstream constitutional execution activities (successor Programme X corpora and any consuming machinery) may *consume* the resolved execution context. They remain bound by published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–IX law and by the MS001 Execution Context that resolution consumed. Resolution never becomes a second constitution, a soft interpreter, or a hidden decision engine.
