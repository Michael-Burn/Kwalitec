## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “pipeline greens,” “tickets closed,” or “desk looks tidy enough.” The tutor optimises **honest closing of constitutional preparation work**: when the published resolution for a concern has been fully executed, dependencies assembled, consistency validated, and the resolved execution context published, the tutor can say so — without pretending constitutional rules ran, without pretending a decision was made, without pretending the student has mastered the topic, and without rewriting the syllabus because the preparation folder closed cleanly.

These objectives bind every constitutional execution context completion judgement and every successor preparation machinery that claims constitutional preparation fulfilment.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### CECCO-01 — Confirm context readiness

**Definition.** Ensure that runtime / assessment / audit speech affirms preparation completion only when a published preparation concern under WS1 / MS001–MS002 has fulfilled the required resolution, dependency-assembly, consistency-validation, and publication obligations — or honestly refuses close when fulfilment criteria are not met — so a resolved execution context may be treated as ready for successor constitutional execution activities.

**Includes:**

- Assessing only concerns that lawfully opened under WS1 / MS001–MS002 context → resolution law.
- Affirming completion only when `COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md` conditions (CECC-01…CECC-05) hold.
- Preferring lawful “not yet” / refuse-close over inventing a “ready enough” status for product convenience.
- Distinguishing lawful stop dispositions (refuse / defer / escalate as published outcomes that exhaust remaining *preparation* duties with honest recording) from abandoned resolution duties.
- Binding every material completion judgement to reconstructable constitutional references.
- Keeping “ready for execution” distinct from “execution occurred.”

**Excludes:**

- Declaring complete because a CI job exited, a ticket closed, a cache warmed, or a UI spinner stopped.
- Treating Twin / Adaptive / Experience / Mission heuristics as completion warrants.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for preparation honesty.
- Collapsing “context prepared” into “executed,” “interpreted,” “decided,” “compliant,” “certified,” “student succeeded,” or “constitution updated.”

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software celebrates unfinished desk preparation — or celebrates a tidy desk as finished marking, decision, or learning. Honest confirmation is care; readiness theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CECCO-02 — Preserve determinism

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming completion preserves the constitutional determinism obligation: same published Execution Context, same execution identity / scope, same resolution inputs ⇒ same completion disposition for the same concern scope — so close cannot invent contradictory “ready” and “not ready” stories from identical published inputs.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who declares the desk prepared differently for the same sitting under the same rules is not care; it is lottery dressed as law.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion judgements remain reproducible from published constitutional inputs.
- Hidden stack-specific shortcuts that flip completion for one vendor only are defects.
- Operator-dependent or race-dependent “ready” flags are defects.
- Non-deterministic “best effort ready” theatre is a defect.

---

### CECCO-03 — Preserve repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material completion judgement is reconstructable and re-affirmable from the same published constitutional records — so preparation close survives audit continuity under EIP-005 and does not depend on ephemeral operator memory, ambient product state, or deleted intermediate traces.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who cannot later reconstruct why the desk was declared prepared cannot be trusted.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion leaves reconstructable references to resolution outcomes, assembled dependencies, consistency results, and published resolved-context identity.
- Continuity forbids deleting prior lawful resolution / publication records to force a different close next time.
- Missing CECC identity against criteria, missing artefact refs, missing dependency identity, or missing publication identity is a defect.
- Student / developer / auditor projections share one truth with different vocabulary.

---

### CECCO-04 — Preserve explainability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material completion judgement can answer the mandatory explainability questions — which artefacts were resolved, which dependencies were assembled, which validations were completed, which resolved execution context was published, which boundaries remained intact — without redefining constitutional meaning or implying execution / interpretation / decision / compliance / certification / educational success.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who can only say “it’s ready somewhere” is performing, not teaching. Speakable completion is care.

**Manifestations:**

- CECCQ-01…CECCQ-05 answers are producible for every material affirmed or refused close.
- Explanations never soft-amend law or narrate completion as authority, execution, decision, compliance, certification, or approval.
- Audience projections differ in vocabulary, not in constitutional facts.
- Opaque “trust the ready badge” theatre is a defect.

---

### CECCO-05 — Preserve auditability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material completion judgement leaves a reconstructable constitutional audit trail sufficient for student / developer / auditor reconstruction under `COMPLETION_EXPLAINABILITY.md` — citing published references rather than technical job logs alone.

**Tutor rationale.** Silent “ready” badges destroy student and auditor trust. Auditable preparation close is care.

**Manifestations:**

- Audit records preserve constitutional references, not only technical job logs or CI annotations.
- Explainability obligations in `COMPLETION_EXPLAINABILITY.md` apply to all material completion judgements.
- Refusal of log volume alone as a substitute for constitutional content.
- Missing boundary-preservation honesty for a material affirmed close is a defect.

---

### CECCO-06 — Preserve implementation neutrality

**Definition.** Ensure that completion judgements speak to constitutional preparation fulfilment independently of programming language, framework, datastore, CI vendor, cloud topology, or preferred Runtime A shape — so no stack becomes constitutional by proximity to a “ready” flag.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who declares only one notebook brand “fully prepared” has abandoned educational law for vendor preference.

**Manifestations:**

- CECC criteria speak to constitutional identities and obligations, not “must use Flask / SQLAlchemy / OpenAPI.”
- Equivalent lawful implementations under the same published law may both reach lawful completion.
- Replacing a prepared stack with another that still obeys published law does not itself create unlawful completion.
- “Our reference implementation completes this way” is never a substitute for published criteria.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CECCO-01 conflicts with inventing unpublished stages so close can complete | Context-readiness honesty wins — refuse / defer / escalate |
| CECCO-02 conflicts with stack-specific non-reproducible close | Determinism wins |
| CECCO-03 conflicts with ephemeral “ready” that cannot be reconstructed | Repeatability wins |
| CECCO-04 conflicts with unspeakable “trust us” completion | Explainability wins |
| CECCO-05 conflicts with mute complete badges | Auditability wins |
| CECCO-06 conflicts with privileging a reference stack as completion law | Implementation neutrality wins |
| Any CECCO conflicts with implying execution / interpretation / decision / compliance / certification / educational success / law change | Non-implication honesty wins — refuse the overclaim |

No supporting objective authorises violating CECCO-01.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — Constitution / EIP integrity obligations, Programme VI–IX authority preservation, WS1 / MS001 CECXO duties, and WS1 / MS002 CECRO duties for the *completion* horizon.

---

## 5. Non-Objectives

Constitutional execution context completion does **not** optimise for:

- constitutional execution of rules, logic, workflows, or decision engines;
- independent constitutional interpretation outcomes;
- production of constitutional decisions, findings, dispositions, or seals;
- constitutional compliance disposition, fidelity scoring, or “prepared therefore compliant” theatre;
- certification of releases, stacks, vendors, or implementations;
- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- CI throughput, cache warming, or demo continuity as educational or constitutional success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when required resolution, validation, or publication remains incomplete;
- collapsing CECC criteria into one mega-flag that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics signals as substitutes for published completion criteria;
- treating documentation of a pipeline as optional commentary on whatever code already shipped;
- declaring execution, interpretation, decision, compliance, certification, educational success, or constitutional amendment solely because completion was affirmed.

---

## 6. Closing Statement

> **A concern that becomes “context prepared” by rewriting constitutional law — or by implying execution, interpretation, decision, compliance, certification, or educational success — is a failed educational system.  
> A concern that confirms only that published preparation completed and a resolved execution context was lawfully published — with determinism, repeatability, explainability, auditability, and neutrality intact — is doing its only completion job.**
