## 1. Purpose

Preparation machinery that silently turns “the resolved execution context was published” into “constitutional rules ran,” “a decision was made,” “the implementation is compliant,” “the release is certified,” “the student succeeded,” or “the Constitution now says X” destroys student trust and educational integrity.

This document draws a bright line between **lawful constitutional execution context completion** (Programme X / WS1 / MS003) and **constitutional execution / independent interpretation / decision / compliance determination / certification / educational judgement / constitutional authorship** owned elsewhere.

---

## 2. Boundary Principles

1. **Confirm preparation fulfilment, do not legislate.** Completion affirms fulfilment of published preparation duties; it does not create constitutional law.
2. **Preparation only.** Execution, independent interpretation, decision, compliance, certification, and educational success are never inferred from close.
3. **Not constitutional execution.** Successor Programme X execution corpora (and Runtime A) own execution of published law where authorised; completion never performs that execution.
4. **Not independent interpretation.** Naming EIP principles in a published resolved view is not producing interpretation outcomes.
5. **Not decision.** Selecting or publishing governance artefacts is not minting constitutional decisions, findings, dispositions, or seals.
6. **Not compliance determination.** Programme IX owns fidelity / compliance / certification judgements.
7. **Not constitutional amendment.** Close never patches law or modifies constitutional authority.
8. **Exactly as published.** Close never redefines constitutional specifications or meaning.
9. **Published criteria only.** Pipelines, tickets, caches, and elapsed time are not fulfilment law.
10. **Published records only.** Auditability is constitutional, not free-form log theatre.
11. **Reference publication honestly.** A published resolved context may be cited; publishing it never upgrades disposition to executed / decided / compliant / certified.
12. **Authority non-transfer.** Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard after close.
13. **Meaning non-reinterpretation.** Programme VI questions and EIP claim classes survive close intact.
14. **Implementation neutrality.** No stack becomes law by proximity to a ready flag.
15. **Prepared ≠ quality.** Completion never grades learning, mastery, readiness, or product success.
16. **Prepared ≠ executed.** Completion never replaces constitutional execution.
17. **Explain the stop.** Students, developers, and auditors should hear when close must be refused.
18. **No emergency exemption.** Deadlines and demos never mint completion power or amendment power.
19. **Determinism and repeatability survive close.** Close must not invent contradictory dispositions from identical published inputs.

## 3. What Execution-Context Completion May Do (Lawful)

| Lawful action | Constitutional meaning |
|---------------|------------------------|
| **Confirm context readiness** | Affirm CECC-01…CECC-05 when published criteria hold for a lawful preparation concern |
| **Confirm dependency assembly** | Cite that required published dependencies were assembled under CECR-04 — without inventing law |
| **Confirm consistency validation** | Cite that required consistency checks completed under CECR-05 — without soft-amending contradictions |
| **Confirm publication** | Cite that a resolved execution context was published under CECR-06 — without upgrading publication into execution |
| **Refuse unlawful close** | Prefer honest not-yet / refuse / defer / escalate over improvisation |
| **Compose required stages** | Confirm fulfilment across published CECR conjunctions without collapsing failed duties |
| **Remain implementation-neutral** | Honour completion law across lawful equivalent stacks |
| **Preserve determinism and repeatability** | Ensure same published inputs do not yield contradictory completion stories |
| **Explain completion** | Answer artefacts / dependencies / validations / publication / boundaries without rewriting meaning |
| **Propose corpus amendments upstream** | Recommend (not enact) amendments when law itself must change before preparation can complete |

These actions **confirm constitutional preparation fulfilment**. They do **not** publish a new Constitution, invent coach warrants, mint tips, author ownership maps, invent CECX / CECR / CECC types, author runtime behaviour, execute constitutional rules, produce constitutional decisions, evaluate fidelity in place of Programme IX, certify releases, approve implementations, or judge educational quality.

## 4. What Execution-Context Completion Must NEVER Do

| Forbidden action | Why | Lawful alternative |
|------------------|-----|--------------------|
| **Execute constitutional logic** | Completion is preparation close, not an execution engine | Affirm preparation fulfilment only; never narrate “ready therefore executed” |
| **Interpret constitutional law independently** | EIP / Programme VI own meaning; resolution may *name* principles, completion may *confirm naming*, neither produces interpretation outcomes | Preserve identity of published principles; never invent outcomes |
| **Produce constitutional decisions** | Decisions / findings / dispositions / seals are not preparation products | Affirm publication of resolved information only; never mint decisions |
| **Determine compliance** | Programme IX owns compliance / conformance dispositions | Confirm preparation fulfilment only; never narrate “prepared therefore compliant” |
| **Amend constitutional law** | Only corpus governance may amend meaning | Leave specs unchanged; propose amendment upstream or refuse incomplete preparation |
| **Replace constitutional authority** | Published corpora remain the standard after any close | Keep published resolved context as information, never as next-cycle law |
| **Create constitutional law** | Completion judgements are not legislation | Cite published law only; refuse unpublished anchors |
| **Perform verification or certification** | Programme IX owns those judgements | Supply / confirm preparation support for honesty; never claim verification or certification from completion |
| **Create educational meaning** | Programme VI owns coach / planner questions | Preserve identity of published meaning; never invent it |
| **Author runtime behaviour** | Programme VIII + permitted writers own execution law | Confirm information readiness; do not mint behaviour |
| **Bypass audit / publication requirements** | Requirements are educational care, not optional polish | Prefer understatement / refuse over invented green readies |
| **Privilege a technology as constitutional** | Implementation neutrality | Confirm obligations, not Flask / SQLAlchemy / REST / OpenAPI fashion |
| **Erase preparation trails to tidy close** | EIP-005 continuity | Preserve prior lawful stage / assembly / publication records; refuse destructive “cleanup” |
| **Upgrade publication by closing** | CECC-04 preserves identity; close does not rewrite meaning into execution | Record publication honestly including refuse-publish |
| **Present CI / tickets / caches as fulfilment law** | Theatre is not constitution | Require CECC-01…CECC-05 content |
| **Imply educational success** | Learning outcomes are not preparation fulfilment | Keep student speech honest: prepared ≠ ready-to-pass |
| **Emergency-amend via deadline** | Urgency never mints amendment power | Refuse incomplete close; escalate to corpus governance |
| **Collapse with Programme VIII runtime completion** | Software-cycle fulfilment is orthogonal | Compose with runtime completion; do not treat REC as CECC (or vice versa) |

## 5. Boundary Tests (Pre-Affirmation)

Before material constitutional execution context completion is treated as affirmed, close requires affirmative answers:

| # | Test | Fail ⇒ |
|---|------|--------|
| 1 | Was resolution completed (CECC-01)? | not yet / refuse |
| 2 | Were dependencies assembled (CECC-02)? | not yet / refuse |
| 3 | Was consistency validated (CECC-03)? | not yet / refuse |
| 4 | Was the resolved execution context published (CECC-04)? | not yet / refuse |
| 5 | Was preparation formally closed under boundary honesty (CECC-05)? | refuse |
| 6 | Would affirmation imply execution, independent interpretation, decision, compliance, certification, educational success, or law change? | refuse overclaim |
| 7 | Would affirmation redefine specifications or replace constitutional authority? | refuse |
| 8 | Would affirmation privilege a stack / vendor as law? | refuse |
| 9 | Would affirmation erase prior lawful preparation trails for convenience? | refuse |
| 10 | Can the stop or affirmation be explained honestly to student and auditor? | refuse mute theatre |

Any single fail blocks lawful affirmation.

## 6. Collisions with Neighbour Corpora

| Neighbour claim | Lawful coexistence | Unlawful collapse |
|-----------------|--------------------|-------------------|
| CECX “context assembled” | May coexist with completed preparation for the same subjects | Treating any single CECX component as whole-preparation completion |
| CECR “stage closed” | Completion judges *required stages complete together* | Treating any single CECR stage as whole-preparation completion |
| CECR-06 “published” | Completion judges *publication as part of CECC-01…CECC-05* | Treating publication alone as complete when resolution / assembly / consistency / close fail |
| Programme VIII runtime complete | Orthogonal fulfilment horizons | Treating context prepared as runtime executed |
| Programme IX “conformant / verified / compliant / certified” | May coexist as distinct judgements over related subjects | Treating CECC complete as CC / CV / CCM / CRT disposition |
| Programme IX completion families (CVC / CTC / CRTC / …) | Orthogonal fulfilment horizons | Treating verification / certification close as context-preparation close |
| Educational Validation Framework release | Quality lens | Treating CECC complete as quality certified |
| CI green / cache warm | Engineering signals | Treating signals as CECC criteria |

## 7. Closing Statement

> **Completion may confirm context readiness, confirm dependency assembly, and confirm publication.  
> It must never execute constitutional logic, produce constitutional decisions, determine compliance, or amend constitutional law.**

This document does not replace sibling boundaries. It binds **constitutional execution context completion** so those boundaries survive preparation machinery, CI, demos, and replacements.
