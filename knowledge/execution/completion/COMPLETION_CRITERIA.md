## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor needs bright conditions for saying “this constitutional desk preparation is finished” — and equally bright refusals when it is not. After WS1 / MS001 has named what information environment must exist and WS1 / MS002 has named how that environment is resolved, Programme X Workstream 1 still needs one fulfilment checklist:

> **What must be true before constitutional speech may affirm that context resolution has completed, dependencies have been assembled, consistency has been validated, the resolved execution context has been published, and preparation has been formally closed?**

These criteria answer that question. They are **normative constitutional conditions**, not database predicates, CI job statuses, ticket states, cache states, or API status codes.

---

## 2. Criterion Catalogue

All material affirmed completions require **CECC-01 through CECC-05** to hold. Failure of any criterion ⇒ **not yet** or **refuse close**. Do not invent a partial “mostly prepared” execution, decision, compliance, certification, educational, or constitutional status.

| ID | Criterion | Primary question |
|----|-----------|------------------|
| **CECC-01** | Resolution completed | Were required CECR-01…CECR-06 resolution obligations completed for the concern? |
| **CECC-02** | Dependencies assembled | Were required published dependency relationships assembled into the resolved view? |
| **CECC-03** | Consistency validated | Was consistency of the candidate resolved view validated under published law? |
| **CECC-04** | Resolved execution context published | Was a constitutionally valid resolved execution context published (or lawfully refused)? |
| **CECC-05** | Preparation formally closed | Was preparation formally closed under published boundary honesty? |

Optional supporting honesty checks (do not replace CECC-01…CECC-05):

| ID | Check | Primary question |
|----|-------|------------------|
| **CECC-06** | Catalogue honesty | Were stages typed under published CECR-01…CECR-06 only? |
| **CECC-07** | Continuity honesty | Was prior lawful preparation history preserved (EIP-005)? |
| **CECC-08** | Non-claim honesty | Does the judgement refuse execution / interpretation / decision / compliance / certification / educational / amendment overclaim? |
| **CECC-09** | Neutrality honesty | Does the judgement refuse stack / vendor canonisation as completion law? |
| **CECC-10** | Determinism / repeatability honesty | Would the same published inputs yield the same completion disposition? |

## 3. Criterion Definitions

### CECC-01 — Resolution completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every resolution stage required by the concern’s published WS1 / MS002 scope has been executed — or that a published refuse / defer / escalate disposition exhausted remaining *resolution* duties for that stage set with honest recording — so preparation speech may treat resolution as completed for the concern.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Identifiable preparation concern opened under WS1 / MS001–MS002.
- Consumable MS001 Execution Context (CECX-01…CECX-07) for the concern.
- Required CECR-01…CECR-06 stage identities for the concern scope.
- Stage traces / disposition records for each required stage (including lawful early stops).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed resolution-fulfilment record naming which required stages completed.
- Or a lawful stop record naming which stages were refused / deferred / escalated and why remaining resolution duties are exhausted.

**Completion conditions.**

- Required stages map to published CECR-01…CECR-06 only.
- Mandatory initiation, scope determination, artefact selection, dependency assembly, consistency validation, and publication obligations for the concern are not silently omitted when a complete preparation is claimed.
- Lawful early stops still leave honest stage outcomes; silent disappearance of a concern is a defect.
- Refusal of “pipeline skipped prep” or “we know it resolved” without stage identity as completion.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That resolution completion means constitutional rules were executed.
- That resolution completion produces constitutional decisions or independent interpretation outcomes.
- That skipped stages may be invented or implied from CI theatre.
- That constitutional specifications were amended by progressing stages.
- That the learner understood, progressed, or mastered anything.
- That the implementation is compliant, verified, or certified.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who declares desk preparation “finished” without confirming each required selection-and-assembly step occurred has not finished lawful care — even if the desk looks tidy.

---

### CECC-02 — Dependencies assembled

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every published dependency relationship required by the concern’s resolution scope was assembled into the resolved view under CECR-04 — without inventing new law, inventing unpublished dependencies, or silently omitting required relationships.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Selected artefact set from CECR-03.
- Published dependency relationships among those artefacts.
- CECR-04 assembly rules and boundary confirmations.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed dependency-assembly record naming which dependencies were carried into the resolved view.
- Or a lawful refuse / defer / escalate record naming which required dependencies remain unassembled and why.

**Completion conditions.**

- Dependencies cite published constitutional relationships, not unpublished tribal “everyone knows X depends on Y.”
- Assembly does not invent new constitutional meaning or soft-amend dependency law.
- “Dependencies somehow resolved” without named relationship identity is not completion.
- Lawful refuse-assemble still satisfies *honest recording* when assembly cannot lawfully proceed — completeness of *assembly production* is not completeness of *forced invention*.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That assembled dependencies execute constitutional rules.
- That assembling dependencies produces constitutional decisions or findings.
- That a particular stack, language, or framework is privileged as law by dependency shape.
- That product quality or educational quality was judged.
- That constitutional specifications were modified to fit assembled dependencies.
- That Programme IX judgement outcomes were re-determined by assembling governance artefacts.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who claims “the papers hang together” without naming which syllabus clauses and interpretation principles were linked has performed assembly theatre, not preparation fulfilment.

---

### CECC-03 — Consistency validated

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that the candidate resolved view was validated for internal consistency under published law (CECR-05) — so contradictory artefacts, scopes, assumptions, or constraints are refused rather than silently merged into a “ready” fiction.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Selected artefact set and assembled dependencies.
- Execution constraints and assumptions from the consumed Execution Context / resolution scope.
- Published consistency-validation rules under WS1 / MS002.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Consistency outcome record: consistent / inconsistent / refuse.
- Affirmed validation-fulfilment record when consistency held; or lawful stop when inconsistency exhausted remaining preparation duties with honest recording.

**Completion conditions.**

- Validation cites published constitutional rules, not unpublished “looks fine” customs.
- Material inconsistency yields refused / incomplete preparation — never silent merge into affirmed readiness.
- Soft-amendment of law to force consistency is a hard stop.
- Refusal of Twin confidence, lint scores, or demo continuity as substitutes for named consistency validation.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That consistency validation executes constitutional rules or produces constitutional decisions.
- That a consistent resolved view is therefore correct educationally, compliant, verified, or certified.
- That inconsistent artefacts may be silently dropped so close can look green.
- That constitutional specifications were amended to remove contradiction.
- That Programme VIII EV eligibility equals preparation consistency fulfilment.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who staples contradictory syllabus extracts together and calls the desk “ready” has abandoned honesty. Consistency is care; forced tidiness is harm.

---

### CECC-04 — Resolved execution context published

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that a constitutionally valid resolved execution context was published under CECR-06 — or that a published refuse-publish disposition exhausted remaining *publication* duties with honest recording — so successor constitutional execution activities may consume a named published view (or may honestly refuse to begin).

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Consistent candidate resolved view (or lawful inconsistency stop).
- CECR-06 publication rules and `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md` / resolution boundary honesty.
- Explicit non-claim confirmations (no execution / interpretation / decision / compliance / certification / law change by publication).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Identifiable published resolved execution context identity (artefacts, exclusions, dependencies, consistency outcome, constraints).
- Or a lawful refuse-publish record naming why publication could not proceed without invention or overclaim.

**Completion conditions.**

- Publication names a reconstructable resolved-context identity, not “the context somewhere.”
- Publication preserves selected artefacts, exclusions, assembled dependencies, and consistency outcomes.
- Publication never silently upgrades preparation into execution, decision, compliance, or certification.
- Refusal of cache warm, UI ready-state, or ticket closure alone as constitutional publication.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That publication means constitutional execution occurred.
- That publication produces constitutional decisions or independent interpretation outcomes.
- That publication certifies, approves, or finds compliant the implementation or the learner.
- That publication amends constitutional specifications or transfers constitutional authority.
- That a refuse-publish may be narrated as “ready enough for demo.”

**Tutor rationale.** Publishing the prepared desk is care only when the published pile is named, consistent, and honest — and only when nobody pretends publication is the examination itself.

---

### CECC-05 — Preparation formally closed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that the preparation concern was formally closed under published boundary honesty — exhausting remaining *preparation* duties without inventing execution, independent interpretation, decision, compliance disposition, certification, educational success, or constitutional amendment.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Resolution, dependency, consistency, and publication records (CECC-01…CECC-04).
- `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md` hard stops.
- Explicit non-claim confirmations (execution / interpretation / decision / compliance / certification / educational success / law change).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Formal close disposition: affirmed complete / not yet / refuse close / defer / escalate.
- Boundary preservation record confirming forbidden overclaims did not occur.

**Completion conditions.**

- Closure occurs only after required resolution, dependency assembly, consistency validation, and publication obligations for the concern are satisfied — or after a published stop exhausts remaining preparation duties with honest recording.
- Closure speech answers CECCQ-01…CECCQ-05 or explicitly refuses mute theatre.
- Closure never silently upgrades publication into execution or decision.
- Refusal of ticket closure, PR merge, or pipeline greens alone as formal constitutional close.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That formal close means constitutional rules were executed.
- That formal close interprets constitutional law independently or produces constitutional decisions.
- That formal close means the implementation is constitutionally compliant, verified, or certified.
- That formal close amends constitutional specifications or transfers constitutional authority.
- That formal close means the student succeeded or is exam-ready.

**Tutor rationale.** Closing the preparation folder is care only when the required steps, assemblies, validations, and publication records exist — and only when nobody pretends the close is a marked script, a pass certificate, or a new syllabus.

|------|--------|

| CECC-03 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |

| CECC-05 fails | Affirmation is unlawful even if earlier preparation appears present |
| Optional CECC-06…CECC-10 fail | Treat as honesty defects; do not soft-pass CECC-01…CECC-05 |
| Any criterion “passes” only by implying execution / interpretation / decision / compliance / certification / educational success / law change | The implied overclaim voids lawful affirmation |

All of CECC-01…CECC-05 are **conjunctive** for material affirmed completion. There is no lawful “four of five” educational, execution, decision, compliance, certification, or constitutional status.

## 5. Lawful Stop Dispositions

Not every incomplete concern is a defect. Published WS1 / MS002 refuse / defer / escalate outcomes may **exhaust remaining preparation duties** for a concern without affirming preparation fulfilment as “complete” in the celebratory sense — while still requiring honest recording and formal close of what occurred.

| Disposition | Constitutional meaning | May affirm CECC completion? |
|-------------|------------------------|----------------------------|
| **Affirm complete** | CECC-01…CECC-05 hold for the concern | Yes |
| **Not yet** | Required resolution, dependency assembly, consistency validation, or publication remain outstanding | No |
| **Refuse close** | Affirmation would invent stages / dependencies / consistency / publication, erase audit trails, or overclaim | No |
| **Defer** | Published wait for upstream corpus / context / dependency identity | No (unless published law says remaining preparation duties are exhausted) |
| **Escalate** | Published stop requiring human / corpus governance attention | No (unless published law says remaining preparation duties are exhausted) |

A lawful stop that exhausts preparation duties is **honest unfinished fulfilment or honest refusal** — never stealth execution, decision, compliance certificate, product certification, educational success, or constitutional amendment.

---

## 6. Non-Criteria

The following are **not** constitutional completion criteria:

- CI pipeline greens, cache warming, or job exit codes;
- ticket closure, PR merge, or documentation checklist ticks alone;
- elapsed time, “desk looks tidy,” or UI ready-states;
- Twin / Adaptive / Experience / Mission confidence signals;
- UI celebration states or analytics milestones;
- “looks prepared enough for the demo”;
- Programme VIII runtime execution completion alone (orthogonal software-cycle fulfilment);
- Programme IX conformance / verification / compliance / certification dispositions (those are judgements, not CECC);
- Educational Validation Framework quality release (orthogonal);
- any implication that constitutional specifications changed because preparation closed;
- any implication that constitutional execution occurred because preparation closed.

---

## 7. Closing Statement

> **Constitutional preparation of an Execution Context is complete only when resolution is completed, dependencies are assembled, consistency is validated, the resolved execution context is published, and preparation is formally closed.  
> Satisfying CECC-01…CECC-05 never means constitutional rules were executed, law was independently interpreted, decisions were produced, the implementation is compliant or certified, the student succeeded, or that constitutional law has changed.**
