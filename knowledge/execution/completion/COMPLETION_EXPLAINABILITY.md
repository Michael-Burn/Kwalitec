## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which constitutional artefacts* were resolved — which dependencies were assembled, which validations were completed, which resolved execution context was published, and which limits remained intact — is not closing honestly; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–IX have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, runtime, and governance speech speakable, after MS001 has made information-environment speech speakable, and after MS002 has made resolution speech speakable, Programme X still needs one preparation-completion-speech answer:

> **“Which constitutional artefacts were resolved, which dependencies were assembled, which validations were completed, which resolved execution context was published, and which constitutional boundaries remained intact?”**

That answer must cover every material affirmed (or refused) completion without inventing educational success, implying execution, implying independent interpretation, producing or implying decisions, implying compliance, implying certification, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, freezing a stack as law, or modifying constitutional law.

## 2. What Execution-Context Completion Explainability Is

**Constitutional Execution Context Completion Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **fulfilment (or lawful refusal of fulfilment) of constitutional preparation of an Execution Context** — not to invent a second tutor voice from CI ready badges, and not to grade the student, execute constitutional rules, or mint a decision.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Artefacts resolved** | Named constitutional artefacts selected into the resolved view under CECR | Which constitutional artefacts were resolved? |
| **Dependencies assembled** | Named published dependency relationships carried into the resolved view | Which dependencies were assembled? |
| **Validations completed** | Named consistency (and related preparation) validations under CECR-05 | Which validations were completed? |
| **Resolved execution context published** | Named published resolved-context identity under CECR-06 | Which resolved execution context was published? |
| **Boundaries intact** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden overclaims / amendments / substitutions did not occur | Which constitutional boundaries remained intact? |

Execution-Context Completion Explainability is:

- **fulfilment-centred** — it narrates why CECC criteria hold (or why close is refused);
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CECR / CECC identities where applicable;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published specifications and named resolution / publication records;
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to amend corpora;
- **non-executive** — completion is never narrated as constitutional execution;
- **non-interpretive / non-decisional** — completion is never narrated as independent interpretation or decision;
- **quality-honest** — prepared is never narrated as mastery, readiness, or educational quality;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence / validation speech, MS001 context speech, or MS002 resolution speech;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match auditability reconstruction needs;
- **authority-honest** — explanations must not paper over authority inference or stack canonisation.

Execution-Context Completion Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–IX / WS1 meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a claim that constitutional execution occurred;
- a claim that constitutional decisions were produced;
- a compliance certificate or substitute for Programme IX findings;
- a certification instrument or implementation approval;
- a substitute for WS1 / MS001 CECXEQ context explainability or WS1 / MS002 CECREQ resolution explainability (compose with; do not replace);
- a rendering system, UI template, CI badge, cache visualiser, or analytics dashboard;
- a claim that clear completion speech guarantees learning, a pass, conformity, certification, approval, or lawful execution outcome;
- a licence to present cache states, ticket graphs, or pipeline greens as constitutional proof or educational quality.

## 3. Mandatory Explanation Questions

Every material completion judgement (affirm or refuse close) should answer all five questions:

### CECCQ-01 — Which constitutional artefacts were resolved?

Name the primary published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII / IX artefacts (paths and identities) selected into the resolved execution context under the preparation concern — or name which required artefacts remain unresolved when refusing close. When a clause or catalogue ID is material, name it (for example EIP-002 claim class; CECX-03 specification context; CECR-03 selection outcome). Unpublished tribal norms and “how main works” are not constitutional artefacts.

### CECCQ-02 — Which dependencies were assembled?

Name the primary published dependency relationships assembled into the resolved view (for example Constitution → EIP principle set; Programme VI warrant → Programme VII ownership map; runtime contract → evidence category) — or name which required dependencies remain unassembled when refusing close. Vendor labels alone are not sufficient without constitutional mapping. If a published refuse-assemble disposition applied, say so explicitly and do not invent dependency theatre.

### CECCQ-03 — Which validations were completed?

Name the primary consistency (and related preparation) validations completed under CECR-05 for the concern — or name which required validations remain incomplete / refused when refusing close. Include lawful early-stop validation outcomes honestly. Never narrate a skipped mandatory validation as completed because a later pipeline job glowed green. “It looked consistent in CI” is not a constitutional validation identity.

### CECCQ-04 — Which resolved execution context was published?

Name the primary published resolved execution context identity (selected artefacts, exclusions, assembled dependencies, consistency outcome, constraints, execution identity / scope) — or name why publication was refused when refusing close. Never narrate a refuse-publish as “ready enough.” “Context warmed somewhere” is not a constitutional publication identity.

### CECCQ-05 — Which constitutional boundaries remained intact?

Confirm the hard stops in `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md` that were checked — especially non-execution of constitutional logic, non-independent interpretation, non-production of constitutional decisions, non-determination of compliance, non-implication of certification, non-redefinition of constitutional law, non-replacement of constitutional authority, implementation neutrality, determinism / repeatability honesty, and non-equation of completion with educational success or constitutional truth.

## 5. Audience Rules

| Audience | May hear | Must not hear as educational law |
|----------|----------|----------------------------------|
| **Student** | That the learning system prepared which published educational rules apply to this activity, why a stop occurred when preparation is incomplete, and what limits apply — in plain educational language | Internal CECC IDs as theatre; CI traces; cache maps as “proof you are ready”; prepared as mastery, as executed, as decided, as compliant, or as certified |
| **Developer / auditor** | Full CECCQ-01…05 answers, corpus paths, CECR / CECC IDs, stage traces, dependency / validation / publication identities, boundary checks, neutrality and non-implication notes | Product success metrics, pipeline greens, or cache counts substituted for constitutional refs; completion framed as educational quality, new law, execution, decision, compliance, certification, or approval |

Student and developer traces must describe the **same constitutional truth**. Vocabulary may differ; facts may not.
