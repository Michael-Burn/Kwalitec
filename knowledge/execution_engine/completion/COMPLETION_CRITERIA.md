## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor needs bright conditions for saying “this constitutional execution work is finished” — and equally bright refusals when it is not. After WS2 / MS001 has named how completed context is lawfully executed and WS2 / MS002 has named how execution activities are organised, Programme X Workstream 2 still needs one fulfilment checklist:

> **What must be true before constitutional speech may affirm that the required execution lifecycle has been fully executed, constitutional rules and constraints have been evaluated, execution outcomes have been published, and execution has been formally closed?**

These criteria answer that question. They are **normative constitutional conditions**, not database predicates, CI job statuses, ticket states, process exit codes, or API status codes.

## 3. Criterion Definitions

### CEEC-01 — Lifecycle completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every lifecycle stage required by the concern’s published WS2 / MS002 scope has been executed — or that a published refuse / defer / escalate disposition exhausted remaining *execution* duties for that stage set with honest progression through recording, publication (or refuse-publish), and closure of what occurred — so execution speech may treat the lifecycle as completed for the concern.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Identifiable execution concern opened under WS2 / MS001–MS002.
- Named completed-context identity (WS1 / MS003) for the concern.
- Required CEEL-01…CEEL-07 stage identities for the concern scope.
- Stage traces / disposition records for each required stage (including lawful early stops).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed stage-fulfilment record naming which required stages completed.
- Or a lawful stop record naming which stages were refused / deferred / escalated and why remaining execution duties are exhausted.

**Completion conditions.**

- Required stages map to published CEEL-01…CEEL-07 only.
- Mandatory initiation, context loading, rule evaluation, constraint evaluation, outcome assembly, publication, and closure obligations for the concern are not silently omitted when a complete execution is claimed.
- Lawful early stops still leave honest stage outcomes; silent disappearance of a concern is a defect.
- Refusal of “pipeline skipped stages” or “we know it ran” without stage identity as completion.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That lifecycle completion means constitutional law was interpreted independently.
- That lifecycle completion produces constitutional decisions or governance dispositions.
- That skipped stages may be invented or implied from CI theatre.
- That constitutional specifications were amended by progressing stages.
- That the learner understood, progressed, or mastered anything.
- That the implementation is compliant, verified, or certified.
- That CECC preparation completion alone equals CEEC execution completion.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who declares marking “finished” without confirming each required marking step occurred has not finished lawful care — even if the desk looks tidy.

---

### CEEC-02 — Rule evaluation completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every published constitutional rule required by the concern’s published scope was evaluated under CEEL-03 using published CEE-01 / CEE-02 discipline — without inventing new law, inventing unpublished rules, interpreting independently, or silently omitting required rules.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Loaded completed execution context from CEEL-02.
- Published constitutional rule identities assembled in that completed context (Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–IX as applicable).
- Rule-evaluation traces under CEEL-03 / CEE-01 / CEE-02.
- Any lawful refuse-evaluate / stop dispositions for out-of-scope or boundary-violating attempted rules.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed rule-evaluation-fulfilment record naming which required published rules were evaluated against which completed-context identity.
- Or a lawful refuse / defer / escalate record naming which required rules remain unevaluated and why remaining evaluation duties are exhausted.

**Completion conditions.**

- Evaluated rules are named published identities (not “rules happened somewhere,” “the pipeline,” or vendor labels alone).
- Evaluation cites published constitutional specifications, not unpublished customs.
- Rules consumed were identified in the completed context — invented or rewritten rules are a hard stop.
- Evaluation invoked only published CEE-01…CEE-07 components — unpublished engine customs are a hard stop.
- Refusal of coverage percentages, lint scores, Twin confidence, or CI greens as substitutes for named rule evaluation.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That rule evaluation completed means independent constitutional interpretation occurred.
- That evaluated rules produced constitutional decisions, findings, dispositions, or seals.
- That a particular stack, language, or framework is privileged as law.
- That product quality or educational quality was judged.
- That constitutional specifications were soft-amended so evaluation could finish.
- That Programme IX judgements were re-determined under evaluation progress.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who claims “the rules were applied” without naming which published syllabus obligations were evaluated against which prepared desk has performed evaluation theatre, not rule fulfilment.

### CEEC-03 — Constraint evaluation completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every hard constitutional constraint required by the concern’s published scope was evaluated under CEEL-04 using published CEE-03 discipline — without waiving constraints, inventing soft limits, minting amendment power, or silently omitting required constraint checks.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Loaded constraints preserved in the completed execution context.
- Interim rule-evaluation results from CEEL-03 where published composition requires them.
- Constraint-evaluation records under CEEL-04 / CEE-03.
- Any lawful stop / escalate dispositions for constraint violations.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed constraint-evaluation-fulfilment record naming which required constraints were evaluated and with what published outcomes.
- Or a lawful refuse / defer / escalate / stop record naming which required constraints remain unevaluated or which violations exhausted remaining evaluation duties.

**Completion conditions.**

- Constraints are named published hard limits from the completed context (not ambient product preferences).
- Constraint evaluation never waives published limits to force close.
- Constraint evaluation never invents soft “guidance-only” substitutes for hard stops published as hard stops.
- Lawful constraint-stop dispositions still leave honest records; silent disappearance of a constraint is a defect.
- Refusal of “constraints assumed OK” without evaluation identity as completion.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That constraint evaluation completed means constraints were waived or rewritten.
- That constraint outcomes are constitutional decisions or compliance dispositions.
- That constraint evaluation certifies implementations or freezes a stack as law.
- That constraint evaluation amends constitutional specifications.
- That educational success or product quality was judged by constraint checks.
- That unfinished constraint evaluation may be implied from later publication theatre.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who declares “limits were checked” without naming which hard syllabus boundaries were evaluated — or who quietly softens a hard stop because closing is convenient — has abandoned care.

### CEEC-04 — Execution outcomes published

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that required execution outcomes assembled under CEEL-05 were published under CEEL-06 / CEE-07 for downstream decision architecture — without inventing satisfaction, hiding incomplete evaluation, rewriting boundaries, freezing a stack as law, or upgrading publication into a constitutional decision, compliance seal, or certification.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Assembled execution outcome set and preserved execution-state record from CEEL-05.
- Boundary confirmations and exception coordination records as required.
- Publication artefacts / refuse-publish dispositions under CEEL-06 / CEE-07.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed publication-fulfilment record naming which execution outcomes were published (or which refuse-publish disposition lawfully exhausted publication duties).
- Reconstructable publication identity suitable for downstream decision architecture consumption.

**Completion conditions.**

- Published artefacts are named execution outcomes (not compliance seals, certifications, or constitutional decisions).
- Publication cites reconstructable rule / constraint / stage / context references.
- Incomplete evaluation cannot be hidden behind publication theatre.
- Refuse-publish is a first-class lawful outcome when publication would invent satisfaction or rewrite boundaries.
- Refusal of “outcomes somewhere in logs” without publication identity as completion.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That published execution outcomes are constitutional decisions.
- That publication determines compliance or certifies implementations.
- That publication amends constitutional specifications or replaces EIP.
- That publication freezes a stack, vendor, or release as constitutional law.
- That publication means the learner succeeded or governance finished.
- That CECC publication of a resolved context equals CEEC publication of execution outcomes.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who claims “results were issued” without naming which reconstructable marking outcomes were published — or who treats a published mark sheet as a new constitution — has left educational honesty.

### CEEC-05 — Execution formally closed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that the execution concern was formally closed under CEEL-07 when duties were exhausted — with boundary honesty intact — so execution speech may treat the concern as closed without creating constitutional authority, producing constitutional decisions, determining compliance, certifying implementations, or pretending incomplete work is done.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Publication (or refuse-publish) artefacts from CEEL-06.
- Boundary-preservation confirmations for the concern.
- Closure / refuse-close dispositions under CEEL-07.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed formal-closure record naming the closed execution concern and preserved audit continuity.
- Or a lawful refuse-close record naming which outstanding duties or boundary breaches prevent close.

**Completion conditions.**

- Closure follows published CEEL-07 meaning; unpublished “done” customs are hard stops.
- Outstanding required CEEC-01…CEEC-04 duties are not silently waived to force close.
- Boundary preservation is explicit — especially non-interpretation, non-decision, non-compliance, non-certification, non-amendment, and non-authority-replacement.
- Continuity (EIP-005) preserves prior lawful execution history; erasing trails to simplify close is a defect.
- Refusal of ticket closure, process exit, or elapsed time as substitutes for formal constitutional closure.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That formal closure creates or replaces constitutional authority.
- That formal closure produces constitutional decisions.
- That formal closure determines compliance or certifies implementations.
- That formal closure amends constitutional specifications.
- That formal closure implies educational success or governance finish.
- That incomplete work may be narrated as closed because a demo deadline arrived.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who stamps “finished” while required marking steps remain open — or who treats the stamp as a new source of syllabus authority — has not closed lawfully.

## 4. Composition Rules

1. **All five required.** Material affirmed completion requires CEEC-01…CEEC-05 simultaneously.
2. **No invented sixth criterion as law.** Optional honesty checks CEEC-06…CEEC-11 support audit; they do not replace the five.
3. **Lawful early stops still close honestly.** A refuse / defer / escalate disposition that exhausts remaining *execution* duties with honest recording may support CEEC-01…CEEC-05 for *that* concern scope; abandoned duties may not.
4. **Context before fulfilment.** Invented or incomplete context is a hard stop for CEEC-01…CEEC-05.
5. **Catalogue closed.** Stages and components outside CEEL / CEE catalogues cannot satisfy criteria.
6. **Non-implication.** Satisfaction never implies interpretation, decision, compliance, certification, educational success, or law change.
7. **Determinism / repeatability.** Identical published inputs must not yield contradictory CEEC dispositions for the same concern.

> **These criteria are the only lawful conditions under which constitutional speech may affirm that a constitutional execution activity has completed.  
> They confirm lifecycle, rule evaluation, constraint evaluation, outcome publication, and formal closure — never interpretation, decision, compliance, certification, or constitutional authority.**
