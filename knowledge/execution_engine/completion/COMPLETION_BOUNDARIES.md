## 1. Purpose

Execution machinery that silently turns “the execution lifecycle finished” into “a constitutional decision was made,” “the implementation is compliant,” “the release is certified,” “student succeeded,” or “the Constitution now says X” destroys student trust and educational integrity.

This document draws a bright line between **lawful constitutional execution engine completion** (Programme X / WS2 / MS003) and **independent interpretation / constitutional decision / compliance determination / certification / educational judgement / constitutional authorship** owned elsewhere.

---

## 2. Boundary Principles

1. **Confirm execution fulfilment, do not legislate.** Completion affirms fulfilment of published execution lifecycle duties; it does not create constitutional law.
2. **Execution only.** Independent interpretation, constitutional decisions, compliance, certification, and educational success are never inferred from close.
3. **Not independent interpretation.** Applying published rules under the lifecycle is not inventing new constitutional meaning; completion never narrates independent interpretation outcomes.
4. **Not constitutional decision.** Published execution outcomes are not findings, dispositions, seals, or warrants.
5. **Not compliance determination.** Programme IX owns fidelity / compliance / certification judgements.
6. **Not certification.** Close never certifies implementations, releases, stacks, or vendors.
7. **Not constitutional amendment.** Close never patches law or modifies constitutional authority.
8. **Not authority replacement.** Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard after close.
9. **Exactly as published.** Close never redefines CEE / CEEL catalogues or soft-amends Programme VI–IX meaning.
10. **Published criteria only.** Pipelines, tickets, process exits, and elapsed time are not fulfilment law.
11. **Published records only.** Audit continuity is constitutional, not free-form log theatre.
12. **Reference outcomes honestly.** Published execution outcomes may be cited; publishing or closing never upgrades disposition to decided / compliant / certified / law-changed.
13. **Meaning non-reinterpretation.** Programme VI questions and EIP claim classes survive close intact.
14. **Implementation neutrality.** No stack becomes law by proximity to a complete flag.
15. **Execution-complete ≠ quality.** Completion never grades learning, mastery, readiness, or product success.
16. **Execution-complete ≠ prepared.** CECC preparation completion is a prerequisite horizon; it is not CEEC fulfilment.
17. **Execution-complete ≠ runtime-complete.** Programme VIII runtime completion remains orthogonal.
18. **Explain the stop.** Students, developers, and auditors should hear when close must be refused.
19. **No emergency exemption.** Deadlines and demos never mint completion power or amendment power.
20. **Determinism and repeatability survive close.** Close must not invent contradictory dispositions from identical published inputs.

## 3. What Execution-Engine Completion May Do (Lawful)

| Lawful action | Constitutional meaning |
|---------------|------------------------|
| **Confirm execution completion** | Affirm CEEC-01…CEEC-05 when published criteria hold for a lawful execution concern |
| **Confirm outcome publication** | Cite that required execution outcomes were published under CEEL-06 / CEE-07 — without upgrading publication into decision / compliance / certification |
| **Confirm execution closure** | Cite that the concern was formally closed under CEEL-07 / boundary honesty — without creating constitutional authority |
| **Confirm rule / constraint evaluation** | Cite that required published rules and constraints were evaluated under CEEL-03 / CEEL-04 — without inventing interpretation or decision meaning |
| **Preserve audit continuity** | Leave reconstructable constitutional records of context, stages, rules, constraints, outcomes, and boundaries |
| **Refuse unlawful close** | Prefer honest not-yet / refuse / defer / escalate over improvisation |
| **Compose required stages and components** | Confirm fulfilment across published CEEL / CEE conjunctions without collapsing failed duties |
| **Remain implementation-neutral** | Honour completion law across lawful equivalent stacks |
| **Preserve determinism and repeatability** | Ensure same published inputs do not yield contradictory completion stories |
| **Explain completion** | Answer context / rules / constraints / outcomes / boundaries without rewriting meaning |
| **Propose corpus amendments upstream** | Recommend (not enact) amendments when law itself must change before execution can complete |

These actions **confirm constitutional execution fulfilment**. They do **not** publish a new Constitution, invent coach warrants, mint tips, author ownership maps, invent CEE / CEEL / CEEC types, author runtime behaviour, interpret law independently, produce constitutional decisions, evaluate fidelity in place of Programme IX, certify releases, approve implementations, or judge educational quality.

## 4. What Execution-Engine Completion Must NEVER Do

| Forbidden action | Why | Lawful alternative |
|------------------|-----|--------------------|
| **Produce constitutional decisions** | Decisions / findings / dispositions / seals are not execution-completion products | Affirm publication of execution outcomes only; never mint decisions |
| **Interpret constitutional law independently** | EIP / Programme VI own meaning; lifecycle may *apply* published rules, completion may *confirm application*, neither invents interpretation outcomes | Preserve identity of published rules; never invent outcomes |
| **Determine compliance** | Programme IX owns compliance / conformance dispositions | Confirm execution fulfilment only; never narrate “executed therefore compliant” |
| **Certify implementations** | Programme IX / CRT owns recognition | Confirm execution fulfilment only; never narrate “executed therefore certified” |
| **Amend constitutional law** | Only corpus governance may amend meaning | Leave specs unchanged; propose amendment upstream or refuse incomplete execution |
| **Create or replace constitutional authority** | Published corpora remain the sole standard | Cite published law; never elevate close into authority |
| **Replace EIP** | Educational Interpretation Principles are applied as published, never superseded by close | Preserve EIP identity; never narrate “execution replaces EIP” |
| **Re-prepare or rewrite completed context** | WS1 owns preparation; inventing context to force close is defective | Consume named CECC-completed context only |
| **Invent stages or components** | Only CEEL-01…CEEL-07 and CEE-01…CEE-07 are published | Refuse unpublished customs |
| **Imply educational success** | Mastery / readiness are not execution-completion products | Narrate fulfilment only |
| **Freeze a stack as law** | Implementation neutrality survives close | Keep stacks replaceable |
| **Treat CI greens as fulfilment** | Pipelines are not constitutional criteria | Apply CEEC-01…CEEC-05 only |
| **Erase audit trails** | EIP-005 continuity forbids convenience deletion | Preserve reconstructable records |
| **Collapse CECC into CEEC** | Preparation complete ≠ execution complete | Keep horizons distinct |
| **Collapse Programme VIII runtime completion into CEEC** | Software-cycle fulfilment is orthogonal | Keep horizons distinct |
| **Replace Programme IX judgements with outcomes** | Execution outcomes feed decision architecture; they do not become governance dispositions | Publish outcomes honestly; never re-author CC / CV / CCM / CRT / CEG |

## 5. Boundary Stop Conditions

Refuse close (or refuse overclaim) when any of the following hold:

1. Required CEEL stages remain incomplete without a published lawful stop that exhausts remaining execution duties.
2. Required rule or constraint evaluation remains incomplete or would invent / waive / soft-amend law.
3. Required execution outcomes remain unpublished without a lawful refuse-publish disposition.
4. Formal closure would create constitutional authority, produce constitutional decisions, determine compliance, certify implementations, or amend law.
5. Completion speech would narrate interpretation / decision / compliance / certification / educational success / authority replacement.
6. Completed context is missing, invented, or rewritten.
7. Stages or components fall outside published CEEL / CEE catalogues.
8. Determinism or repeatability would be violated for identical published inputs.
9. Audit continuity would be erased or replaced by mute badges.
10. A preferred stack, vendor, or CI tool would be elevated as completion law.

---

## 6. Closing Statement

> **Execution Engine Completion may confirm that constitutional execution finished under the published lifecycle.  
> The moment it produces constitutional decisions, determines compliance, certifies implementations, or amends constitutional law, it has left constitutional education and entered product fiction.**
