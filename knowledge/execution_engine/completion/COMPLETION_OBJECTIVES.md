## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “pipeline greens,” “tickets closed,” or “engine stopped.” The tutor optimises **honest closing of constitutional execution work**: when the published lifecycle for a concern has been fully executed, rules and constraints evaluated, outcomes published, and execution formally closed, the tutor can say so — without pretending a constitutional decision was made, without pretending the implementation is compliant or certified, without pretending the student has mastered the topic, and without rewriting the syllabus because the machinery stopped cleanly.

These objectives bind every constitutional execution engine completion judgement and every successor execution machinery that claims constitutional execution fulfilment.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### CEECO-01 — Confirm execution completion

**Definition.** Ensure that runtime / assessment / audit speech affirms execution completion only when a published execution concern under WS2 / MS001–MS002 has executed the required CEEL-01…CEEL-07 lifecycle obligations — including rule evaluation, constraint evaluation, outcome publication, and formal closure — or honestly refuses close when fulfilment criteria are not met.

**Includes:**

- Assessing only concerns that lawfully opened under WS2 / MS001–MS002 initiation → context loading → rule evaluation → constraint evaluation → outcome assembly → publication → closure law.
- Affirming completion only when `COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md` conditions (CEEC-01…CEEC-05) hold.
- Preferring lawful “not yet” / refuse-close over inventing a “done enough” status for product convenience.
- Distinguishing lawful stop dispositions (refuse / defer / escalate as published outcomes that exhaust remaining *execution* duties with honest recording) from abandoned lifecycle duties.
- Binding every material completion judgement to reconstructable constitutional references.
- Keeping “execution complete” distinct from “decided,” “compliant,” “certified,” “context prepared,” and “constitution amended.”

**Excludes:**

- Declaring complete because a CI job exited, a ticket closed, an engine process exited, or a UI spinner stopped.
- Treating Twin / Adaptive / Experience / Mission heuristics as completion warrants.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for lifecycle honesty.
- Collapsing “execution complete” into “interpreted independently,” “decided,” “compliant,” “certified,” “student succeeded,” or “constitution updated.”

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software celebrates unfinished execution work — or celebrates finished pipeline machinery as finished decision-making, compliance, or learning. Honest confirmation is care; completion theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CEECO-02 — Preserve determinism

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming completion preserves the constitutional determinism obligation: same completed execution context, same published constitutional rules, same evaluated constraints, same stage sequence ⇒ same completion disposition for the same concern scope — so close cannot invent contradictory “complete” and “not complete” stories from identical published inputs.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who declares the same script marked under the same rules “finished” differently depending on which runner woke first is not care; it is lottery dressed as law.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion judgements remain reproducible from published constitutional inputs.
- Hidden stack-specific shortcuts that flip completion for one vendor only are defects.
- Operator-dependent or race-dependent “complete” flags are defects.
- Non-deterministic “best effort complete” theatre is a defect.

---

### CEECO-03 — Preserve repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material completion judgement is reconstructable and re-affirmable from the same published constitutional records — so execution close survives audit continuity under EIP-005 and does not depend on ephemeral operator memory, ambient product state, or deleted intermediate traces.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who cannot later reconstruct why execution was declared finished cannot be trusted.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion leaves reconstructable references to completed-context identity, rule-evaluation traces, constraint-evaluation records, published outcomes, stage outcomes, and boundary confirmations.
- Continuity forbids deleting prior lawful execution / publication records to force a different close next time.
- Missing CEEC identity against criteria, missing context identity, missing rule identity, missing constraint identity, missing outcome identity, or missing closure identity is a defect.
- Student / developer / auditor projections share one truth with different vocabulary.

---

### CEECO-04 — Preserve explainability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material completion judgement can answer the mandatory explainability questions — which execution context was consumed, which constitutional rules were executed, which constraints were evaluated, which execution outcomes were published, which boundaries remained intact — without redefining constitutional meaning or implying interpretation / decision / compliance / certification / educational success.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who can only say “it’s fully executed somewhere” is performing, not teaching. Speakable completion is care.

**Manifestations:**

- CEECQ-01…CEECQ-05 answers are producible for every material affirmed or refused close.
- Explanations never soft-amend law or narrate completion as authority, independent interpretation, decision, compliance, certification, or approval.
- Audience projections differ in vocabulary, not in constitutional facts.
- Opaque “trust the complete badge” theatre is a defect.

---

### CEECO-05 — Preserve audit continuity

**Definition.** Ensure that every material completion judgement leaves a reconstructable trail: which completed execution context was consumed, which lifecycle stages were completed, which rules and constraints were evaluated, which execution outcomes were published, which execution state was closed, and which boundaries remained intact.

**Tutor rationale.** Silent “complete” badges destroy student and auditor trust. Auditable execution close is care.

**Manifestations:**

- Audit records preserve constitutional references, not only technical job logs or CI annotations.
- Explainability obligations in `COMPLETION_EXPLAINABILITY.md` apply to all material completion judgements.
- Refusal of log volume alone as a substitute for constitutional content.
- Missing boundary-preservation honesty for a material affirmed close is a defect.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CEECO-01 conflicts with inventing unpublished stages so close can complete | Execution-completion honesty wins — refuse / defer / escalate |
| CEECO-02 conflicts with stack-specific non-reproducible close | Determinism wins |
| CEECO-03 conflicts with ephemeral “complete” that cannot be reconstructed | Repeatability wins |
| CEECO-04 conflicts with unspeakable “trust us” completion | Explainability wins |
| CEECO-05 conflicts with mute complete badges | Audit continuity wins |
| Any CEECO conflicts with implying interpretation / decision / compliance / certification / educational success / law change / authority replacement | Non-implication honesty wins — refuse the overclaim |

No supporting objective authorises violating CEECO-01.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — Constitution / EIP integrity obligations, Programme VI–IX authority preservation, WS1 CECC duties, WS2 / MS001 CEEO duties, and WS2 / MS002 CEELO duties for the *completion* horizon.

---

## 5. Non-Objectives

Constitutional execution engine completion does **not** optimise for:

- independent constitutional interpretation outcomes;
- production of constitutional decisions, findings, dispositions, or seals;
- constitutional compliance disposition, fidelity scoring, or “executed therefore compliant” theatre;
- certification of releases, stacks, vendors, or implementations;
- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- CI throughput, engine exit codes, or demo continuity as educational or constitutional success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when required lifecycle, evaluation, publication, or closure remains incomplete;
- collapsing CEEC criteria into one mega-flag that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics signals as substitutes for published completion criteria;
- treating documentation of a pipeline as optional commentary on whatever code already shipped;
- declaring interpretation, decision, compliance, certification, educational success, authority replacement, or constitutional amendment solely because completion was affirmed;
- re-preparing or rewriting completed execution contexts under an “execution complete” badge;
- replacing Programme IX judgements with published execution outcomes.

## 6. Closing Statement

> **A concern that becomes “execution complete” by rewriting constitutional law — or by implying interpretation, decision, compliance, certification, or educational success — is a failed educational system.  
> A concern that confirms only that the published execution lifecycle completed, rules and constraints were evaluated, outcomes were published, and execution was formally closed — with determinism, repeatability, explainability, and audit continuity intact — is doing its only completion job.**
