## 1. Purpose

Execution without a closed stage catalogue invents law by proximity: whichever pipeline step, ticket status, or ambient product habit happens to finish becomes the tutor’s “proof the system ran constitutional execution.”

This catalogue names the only lawful constitutional execution engine lifecycle stages — and binds each stage to purpose, inputs, outputs, and permitted / prohibited activities.

---

## 2. Stage Principles

1. **Ordered and skip-disciplined.** Stages may be brief under thin scope; mandatory initiation, context loading, rule evaluation, constraint evaluation, outcome assembly, publication, and closure obligations may not be silently omitted when execution outcomes are claimed.
2. **Completed context before evaluation.** Identifiable completed execution context is loaded before rule and constraint evaluation.
3. **Explainability accumulates.** Each stage leaves traceable artefacts for `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.
4. **Refusal is a stage outcome.** Refuse-open, refuse-load, stop, escalate, and refuse-publish / refuse-close are first-class conclusions.
5. **Deterministic transitions.** Same published inputs ⇒ same next stage outcomes.
6. **Stages coordinate; they do not legislate.** No stage creates, modifies, or redefines constitutional law.
7. **Stages do not become constitutional authority.** Closure never freezes an implementation as permanently lawful or replaces published corpora.
8. **Stages do not interpret independently.** Applying published rules is not inventing new constitutional meaning.
9. **Stages do not produce constitutional decisions.** Governance dispositions remain outside this catalogue.
10. **Stages invoke published engine components only.** CEE-01…CEE-07 may be applied; unpublished engine customs may not.

## 3. Catalogue Overview

| ID | Stage | Constitutional purpose (short) | Primary inputs | Primary outputs |
|----|-------|--------------------------------|----------------|-----------------|
| **CEEL-01** | Execution Initiation | Open an execution concern against a named completed-context identity and published scope | Completed-context identity + published scope + optional rule-set indication | Initiated execution record (or refuse-open) |
| **CEEL-02** | Context Loading | Load and bind the identifiable completed execution context | Initiated record + candidate completed-context identity | Loaded completed-context binding (or refuse-load) |
| **CEEL-03** | Rule Evaluation | Evaluate published constitutional rules under CEE-01 / CEE-02 | Loaded context + published rule identities | Rule-evaluation traces / interim results |
| **CEEL-04** | Constraint Evaluation | Evaluate hard constitutional constraints under CEE-03 | Loaded constraints + interim rule results | Constraint-evaluation records |
| **CEEL-05** | Outcome Assembly | Assemble execution outcomes and preserve execution state under CEE-04 / CEE-05 / CEE-06 | Rule traces + constraint records + state / exception signals | Execution outcome set + execution-state record |
| **CEEL-06** | Outcome Publication | Publish execution outcomes under CEE-07 for downstream decision architecture | Outcome set + state / boundary confirmations | Published execution artefacts (or refuse-publish) |
| **CEEL-07** | Execution Closure | Close the lifecycle honestly when duties are exhausted | Published artefacts + boundary confirmations | Closed lifecycle (or refuse-close) — never a freeze of constitutional authority |

Material constitutional execution claiming lifecycle outcomes must map to this sequence as published law requires. Stages may be brief; none may invent a stage outside this catalogue.

**Relation to CEE / CECX / CECR / CECC / Programme VIII / IX catalogues:** Those catalogues remain defined solely by their owners. CEEL stages *organise when* constitutional execution activities occur; they do not replace, reclassify, re-prepare context, invent, or amend those catalogues.

```text
CEEL-01  Execution Initiation
           ↓
CEEL-02  Context Loading
           ↓

           ↓
CEEL-04  Constraint Evaluation
           ↓

           ↓
```

## 5. Stage Definitions

### CEEL-01 — Execution Initiation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Open a material constitutional execution concern by naming the completed execution context identity under which execution will proceed and the published scope of the concern — without inventing law, opening against unpublished customs, or treating initiation as completed execution or a constitutional decision.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Identifiable completed resolved execution context identity (WS1 / MS003 fulfilment claimed or to be confirmed at CEEL-02)
- Concern identity (what constitutional execution question is under organisation)
- Optional published indication of rule identities / CEE components required for the scope
- Optional identifiable supporting authority / lineage references (not rewritten)

**Constitutional outputs**

- An initiated execution concern record naming completed-context identity, scope, and intended rule / component set — **or**
- Lawful refuse-open when standards are unpublished, scope is theatrical, completed-context identity cannot be established, or initiation would invent law

**Permitted activities**

- Name published completed-context identity and scope as execution standards
- Bound the concern scope without amending corpora
- Record intended published rule identities and CEE components from published catalogues
- Refuse initiation when constitutional preconditions fail
- Preserve initiation audit identity for later stages

**Prohibited activities**

- Treat “how main works,” tribal knowledge, or stack preference as the standard
- Create or modify constitutional specifications by opening a concern
- Issue execution outcomes or constitutional decisions at initiation
- Invent unpublished engine components or lifecycle stages
- Skip initiation when material outcomes will later be claimed
- Interpret constitutional law independently or determine governance at initiation

**Authority preserved**  
Published corpora remain the sole standard. Initiation organises; it does not legislate.

---

### CEEL-02 — Context Loading

**Constitutional purpose**  
Load and bind the **identifiable completed Constitutional Execution Context** for the concern — so subsequent stages execute against real completed preparation, not anonymous theatre — without inventing, rewriting, or re-completing the context, and without performing WS1 preparation.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Initiated concern from CEEL-01
- Candidate completed execution context identity (WS1 / MS003 completion confirmed, with supporting resolution / context references as applicable)
- Published loading prerequisites from WS2 / MS001 and related WS1 completion law
- Optional supporting artefact identities already established under upstream law

**Constitutional outputs**

- A loaded completed-context binding for the concern, plus an explicit load-confirmation record — **or**
- Lawful refuse-load when completed context is missing, forged, invented, rewritten, incomplete, or would require unlawful re-preparation under WS1

**Permitted activities**

- Identify and name the identifiable completed execution context
- Validate published loading prerequisites for the concern
- Bind the completed context to the initiated concern and intended rule / CEE set
- Refuse loading when completed context cannot be honestly affirmed
- Preserve loading records for audit and explainability

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent missing completed context
- Rewrite context contents or completion judgements to force later evaluation
- Re-run WS1 preparation / resolution / completion under the label “lifecycle loading”
- Treat CI noise, coverage percentages, UI ticks, or seals as completed context without published mapping
- Redefine educational or constitutional meaning while “loading”
- Present loading as itself completed execution, a constitutional decision, or a freeze of stack authority

**Authority preserved**  
Completed context remains an upstream input. Loading organises identity honesty; it does not author preparation, educational truth, or governance.

---

### CEEL-03 — Rule Evaluation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Evaluate **published constitutional rules** identified in the loaded completed context — under CEE-01 (Rule Dispatcher) and CEE-02 (Rule Executor) discipline — exactly as published, without inventing rules, soft-amending specifications, or interpreting constitutional law independently.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Loaded completed context from CEEL-02
- Published constitutional rule identities assembled in that context
- Published CEE-01 / CEE-02 component law from WS2 / MS001
- Published composition / precedence already stated in constitutional corpora

**Constitutional outputs**

- Rule-evaluation traces and interim results bound to named rule identities — **or**
- Lawful stop / escalate when required published rules cannot be evaluated without invention or independent interpretation

**Permitted activities**

- Dispatch published rules named in the completed context (CEE-01)
- Execute published rules exactly as published (CEE-02)
- Record which rules were evaluated and which were out of scope under the completed context
- Refuse evaluation when rules are unpublished or would require soft amendment
- Preserve evaluation traces for later assembly and explainability

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent unpublished rules or “implied house rules”
- Soft-amend specification text to make evaluation convenient
- Reorder rules contrary to published precedence to force a preferred outcome
- Interpret constitutional law independently under the label “evaluation”
- Produce constitutional decisions, compliance determinations, or certifications at this stage
- Begin evaluation against an unloaded or incomplete context

**Authority preserved**  
Published rules remain the sole evaluation standard. Evaluation applies; it does not author or reinterpret.

---

### CEEL-04 — Constraint Evaluation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Evaluate **hard constitutional constraints** preserved in the loaded completed context — under CEE-03 (Constraint Evaluator) discipline — so hard limits remain intact and speakable, without waiving constraints, inventing soft limits, or minting amendment power.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Loaded constraints from the completed context (including CECX-07 and published boundary corpora as assembled)
- Interim rule-evaluation results from CEEL-03
- Published CEE-03 component law from WS2 / MS001
- Published boundary corpora (engine boundaries and upstream authority limits)

**Constitutional outputs**

- Constraint-evaluation records bound to named constraint identities — **or**
- Lawful stop / escalate when constraint evaluation would require waiver, invention, or soft amendment

**Permitted activities**

- Evaluate hard constraints exactly as preserved in the completed context
- Record satisfied / violated / inapplicable dispositions under published meaning
- Refuse progress when constraint evaluation cannot be performed honestly
- Preserve constraint records for outcome assembly and explainability

**Prohibited activities**

- Waive hard constraints because a demo needs progress
- Invent soft constraints that rewrite published limits
- Treat constraint evaluation as authority to amend constitutional specifications
- Collapse constraint evaluation into a governance decision or compliance seal
- Hide constraint violations so later publication looks clean

**Authority preserved**  
Hard constraints survive evaluation intact. Evaluation assesses; it does not rewrite limits.

---

### CEEL-05 — Outcome Assembly

**Constitutional purpose**  
Assemble **reconstructable execution outcomes** and preserve **execution state** — under CEE-04 (Outcome Collector), CEE-05 (Execution State Manager), and CEE-06 (Exception Coordinator) discipline — so downstream decision architecture receives honest outcomes without inventing governance dispositions, erasing history, or treating assembly as new law.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Rule-evaluation traces from CEEL-03
- Constraint-evaluation records from CEEL-04
- Execution-state and exception signals under published CEE-05 / CEE-06 law
- Boundary confirmations from prior stages

**Constitutional outputs**

- Execution outcome set bound to named rules, constraints, context identity, and stages
- Execution-state record describing lawful progress (not educational state, not governance disposition)
- Exception dispositions (stop / escalate / record) where material — **or**
- Lawful refuse-assemble when outcomes would invent governance, forge provenance, or erase prior lawful traces

**Permitted activities**

- Collect reconstructable execution outcomes from rule and constraint traces
- Maintain speakable execution-state transitions for the run
- Coordinate lawful stops, refusals, and boundary violations without inventing new law
- Bind outcomes to completed-context identity, rule identities, constraint identities, and CEEL stage identities
- Preserve assembly artefacts for publication and audit

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent compliance, certification, or other constitutional decisions as “outcomes”
- Erase stage history to simplify the next report
- Forge provenance so incomplete evaluation looks finished
- Treat assembled outcomes as constitutional amendments or EIP replacements
- Mutate implementation artefacts under assembly pretext
- Invent educational state under the label “execution state”

**Authority preserved**  
Outcomes remain execution outcomes. Assembly organises reconstructability; it does not legislate or govern.

---

### CEEL-06 — Outcome Publication

**Constitutional purpose**  
Publish execution outcomes and supporting state / exception records — under CEE-07 (Execution Publisher) discipline — for downstream decision architecture under published honesty rules, without inventing satisfaction, hiding incomplete evaluation, rewriting boundaries, or freezing a stack as law.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Execution outcome set and execution-state record from CEEL-05
- Boundary confirmations from CEEL-01…CEEL-05
- Published honesty / explainability rules for publication
- Downstream consumer identity (decision architecture) as information only — not as a licence to invent law

**Constitutional outputs**

- Published execution artefacts suitable for lawful downstream consumption — **or**
- Lawful refuse-publish when publication would invent satisfaction, hide incomplete work, rewrite boundaries, or freeze stack authority

**Permitted activities**

- Publish reconstructable execution outcomes under published honesty rules
- Include stage, context, rule, constraint, and boundary identities required for explainability
- Refuse publication when honesty requirements cannot be met
- Preserve publication audit identity for closure

**Prohibited activities**

- Publish invented outcomes or silent stage skips as if they were lawful
- Present publication as compliance determination, certification, or constitutional decision
- Freeze implementations, stacks, vendors, or releases as constitutional law by publication
- Hide constraint violations or refuse dispositions
- Soft-amend specifications in publication prose

**Authority preserved**  
Publication communicates execution results. It does not mint authority or governance.

---

### CEEL-07 — Execution Closure

**Constitutional purpose**  
Close the lifecycle honestly when coordination duties for the concern are exhausted — recording what was initiated, loaded, evaluated, assembled, and published — without creating constitutional authority, producing constitutional decisions, determining compliance, certifying implementations, or pretending incomplete work is done.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Published artefacts (or refuse-publish disposition) from CEEL-06
- Boundary confirmations across CEEL-01…CEEL-06
- Explicit confirmation that remaining evaluation / publication duties are exhausted or lawfully refused

**Constitutional outputs**

- Closed lifecycle record bound to context / rules / constraints / outcomes / stages / boundaries — **or**
- Lawful refuse-close when closure would invent completion, replace constitutional authority, or launder incomplete work

**Permitted activities**

- Close when published stage duties are exhausted or lawfully refused
- Record final stage disposition without amending corpora
- Preserve closure identity for audit and explainability
- Prefer refuse-close over theatrical “done”

**Prohibited activities**

- Create constitutional authority by closing
- Produce constitutional decisions at closure
- Determine compliance or certify implementations under a closed seal
- Amend constitutional specifications or EIP under closure pretext
- Freeze a stack as law because the lifecycle closed
- Treat “lifecycle complete” as mastery, readiness, product success, or perpetual conformity

**Authority preserved**  
Closure ends coordination of this concern. Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard for the next act.
