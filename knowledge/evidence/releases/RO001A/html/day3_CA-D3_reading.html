Open: CMP · Syllabus 1.2.2 correlation measures for bivariate data

Stop: Through the CMP treatment of Pearson's, Spearman's, and Kendall's measures for Syllabus 1.2.2 (stop before principal component analysis 1.2.3)


Focus questions:

• How does the CMP define each of Pearson, Spearman, and Kendall in plain language?

• When would you prefer a rank-based measure over Pearson?

• Where does the CMP (or sound practice) warn that association is not causation?

• You will later distinguish the three measures, choose one for a situation, and refuse one overclaim.


Misconception watch:

• Watch for treating all three coefficients as interchangeable labels.

• Watch for reading r = 0.6 as '60% causal effect.'

• Watch for skipping scatter inspection before trusting a single number.
