## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which completed constitutional decision* was consumed — which provenance was preserved, which integrity validations were performed, which audit record was published, and which limits remained intact — is not coaching; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–IX have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, runtime, conformance, verification, compliance, certification, and evolution speech speakable, after Workstream 1 has made preparation speech speakable, after Workstream 2 has made execution speech speakable, after Workstream 3 has made decision speech speakable, and after WS4 / MS001 has made audit-component speech speakable, Programme X still needs one lifecycle-speech answer:

> **“Which constitutional decision was consumed, which provenance was preserved, which integrity validations were performed, which audit record was published, and which constitutional boundaries remained intact?”**

That answer must cover every material audit lifecycle without inventing educational meaning, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, inventing completed decisions, re-executing rules, re-producing decisions, reinterpreting EIP, amending specifications, creating constitutional authority, freezing a stack as law, inventing unpublished CAA or CAAL types, replacing WS2 execution, replacing WS3 decision production, or presenting closure as educational quality.

## 2. What Lifecycle Explainability Is

**Constitutional Audit Lifecycle Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **coordination of named constitutional audit activities through published CAAL stages** — consuming completed constitutional decisions, constructing / provenance-recording / integrity-validating / publishing constitutional audit records under published CAA components — not to invent a second tutor voice from CI machinery, not to grade the student, and not to create or replace constitutional authority.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Constitutional decision consumed** | Named completed WS3 constitutional decision used as input | Which constitutional decision was consumed? |
| **Provenance preserved** | Named reconstructable lineage recorded at CAAL-04 under CAA-03 | Which provenance was preserved? |
| **Integrity validations performed** | Named integrity disposition and checks at CAAL-05 under CAA-04 | Which integrity validations were performed? |
| **Audit record published** | Named reconstructable published audit record from CAAL-06 (or refuse-publish) | Which audit record was published? |
| **Boundaries remaining intact** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden execution / decision production / reinterpretation / amendment / authority creation did not occur | Which constitutional boundaries remained intact? |

Lifecycle Explainability is:

- **coordination-centred** — it narrates completed decisions → provenance → integrity → published records under law;
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CAAL and CAA identities;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published specifications and authority identities;
- **decision-honest** — every material justification cites identifiable completed decisions consumed (or names the hard stop when none exist);
- **provenance-honest** — every material justification cites which provenance was preserved (or names the hard stop);
- **integrity-honest** — every material publication cites which integrity validations were performed;
- **record-honest** — every material lifecycle cites which constitutional audit record was published (or names refuse / invalid);
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to amend corpora;
- **non-executive** — explanation never presents rule re-execution as “audit”;
- **non-decisive** — explanation never presents decision re-production as “audit”;
- **non-interpretive** — explanation never presents soft amendment or EIP reinterpretation as “recording”;
- **non-authoritative** — closure is never narrated as a freeze of stack authority, a substitute for published corpora, or creation of constitutional authority;
- **quality-honest** — closure is never narrated as mastery, readiness, or educational quality;
- **mutation-honest** — explanation never hides artefact changes performed “so audit could proceed”;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence / validation speech, WS1 preparation speech, WS2 execution speech, WS3 decision speech, WS4 / MS001 audit-component speech, or Programme IX governance speech;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match CAALO-04 / CAALO-05 reconstruction needs;
- **repeatability-honest** — explanations must not paper over race-dependent dispositions or private stage shortcuts.

Lifecycle Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–IX / WS1 / WS2 / WS3 / WS4 MS001 meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a substitute for CC findings, CT lineage speech, CV verification speech, CCM compliance-determination speech, CRT certification-recognition speech, CEE / CEEL execution speech, CDA / CDAL decision speech, or CAA audit-component speech;
- a CI status page, coverage report, or linter summary presented as constitutional coordination;
- a freeze of implementations, stacks, vendors, or releases as constitutional law;
- a licence to narrate invented completed decisions, rule re-execution, decision re-production, EIP reinterpretation, or authority creation as if they were lawful inputs or outcomes.

## 3. Mandatory Explainability Questions

Every material constitutional audit lifecycle must be able to answer:

### CAALEQ-01 — Which constitutional decision was consumed?

**Required content**

- Named completed constitutional decision identities (WS3 / MS003 fulfilment, with supporting architecture / lifecycle references as applicable)
- Binding between completed-decision identities and the initiated CAAL-01 concern
- Confirmation that unpublished customs, invented decisions, incomplete decision production, or re-produced decisions were not used as completed decisions

**Defect if**

- “The system audited” with no completed-decision reference
- Anonymous “the build” or “the pipeline” without identifiable completed decisions
- Completed decisions invented, rewritten, or re-produced as audit inputs without disclosure and hard stop

---

### CAALEQ-02 — Which provenance was preserved?

**Required content**

- Named provenance record identity from CAAL-04 under CAA-03
- Binding from admitted completed decisions through upstream outcome / authority citations to the candidate / published audit record
- Explicit note of lineage identities excluded as out of scope under the concern
- Confirmation that provenance was reconstructable and not invented or forged

**Defect if**

- “Provenance was recorded” with no reconstructable lineage identity
- Soft-amended or invented lineage presented as published provenance
- Stack preference or “how main works” presented as a provenance source
- Erasure of inconvenient completed-decision or authority citations

---

### CAALEQ-03 — Which integrity validations were performed?

**Required content**

- Named integrity disposition from CAAL-05 (valid / invalid / refuse) with reconstructable reasons
- Binding to CAA-04 Integrity Validator expectations
- Confirmation that publication as valid did not occur without successful integrity validation
- Explicit note of which integrity checks were applied (decision citation, provenance preservation, authority preservation, boundary integrity)

**Defect if**

- “It was validated” with no disposition or check identities
- Integrity validation skipped so publication could proceed
- Forged or theatrical integrity presented as constitutional validity

---

### CAALEQ-04 — Which audit record was published?

**Required content**

- Named constitutional audit record identity (or lawful refuse-publish / invalid / stop disposition)
- Binding to construction at CAAL-03 and publication (or refuse-publish) at CAAL-06
- Citation of completed decisions, provenance, and integrity disposition that ground the published record
- Confirmation that the record is not narrated as new constitutional law, EIP replacement, re-decision, rule re-execution, stack freeze, or authority creation

**Defect if**

- “An audit was published” with no reconstructable audit-record identity or refuse record
- Unvalidated candidate presented as published valid audit record
- Audit record narrated as constitutional amendment, mastery grade, re-decision, or authority creation

---

### CAALEQ-05 — Which constitutional boundaries remained intact?

**Required content**

- Explicit confirmation against `LIFECYCLE_BOUNDARIES.md` hard stops (especially no rule execution, no decision production, no EIP reinterpretation, no specification amendment, no authority creation / substitution, no stack freeze, no artefact mutation, no forged provenance)
- Named hard stops triggered, if any, and the lawful refuse / stop / escalate / invalid / refuse-publish / refuse-close disposition taken
- Confirmation that lifecycle closure did not create or replace published corpora as the next-cycle standard

**Defect if**

- Silent assumption that “lifecycle closed ⇒ boundaries held”
- Hidden artefact mutation, stack canonisation, decision re-production, or EIP reinterpretation
- Closure narrated as constitutional authority creation or replacement

## 5. Explainability Defects

The following are constitutional explainability defects regardless of UX polish:

1. Narrating lifecycle progress as educational mastery, readiness, or product success.
2. Narrating closure as constitutional amendment, EIP replacement, decision re-production, or authority creation.
3. Omitting completed-decision identities when an audit record is claimed.
4. Omitting provenance identities when provenance preservation is claimed.
5. Omitting integrity disposition when valid publication is claimed.
6. Presenting CI / coverage / linter / Twin / Adaptive signals as constitutional audit explanations.
7. Hiding race-dependent or private stage-shortcut dispositions behind a single seal.
8. Collapsing WS2 execution speech into “audit lifecycle” speech.
9. Collapsing WS3 decision speech into “audit lifecycle” speech.
10. Collapsing Programme IX governance speech into “audit lifecycle” speech outside published provenance recording.
