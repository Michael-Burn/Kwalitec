## 2. What the Constitutional Audit Lifecycle Is

The **constitutional audit lifecycle** is the binding organisation by which **constitutional audit activities** may proceed through recognised stages — consuming completed constitutional decisions, constructing and validating constitutional audit records under WS4 / MS001 component law, recording provenance, publishing those records for accountability and explainability, and closing honestly — while preserving accountability, provenance, implementation neutrality, repeatability, explainability, and constitutional fidelity — without becoming a source of constitutional law, a substitute for EIP, a re-execution engine, a second decision producer, or an independent governance producer outside constitutional authority.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Completed constitutional decision** | A reconstructable WS3 decision whose required decision lifecycle has lawfully completed and whose disposition has been published | Which completed decisions may the lifecycle consume? |
| **Lifecycle stage** | A recognised CAAL-01…CAAL-07 organisational step | Which coordination step is active? |
| **Audit architecture component** | A recognised CAA-01…CAA-06 audit mechanism from WS4 / MS001 applied under lifecycle stages | Which audit machinery may stages invoke? |
| **Constitutional audit record** | Reconstructable record produced under published CAA components for accountability and explainability — never new law, never a re-decision, never stack freeze | What audit record was produced? |
| **Constitutional provenance** | Reconstructable lineage binding the audit record to completed decisions, upstream outcomes / authority citations, and published corpora — never invented lineage | Which lineage was preserved? |
| **Audit integrity** | Confirmation that a constructed audit record cites completed decisions, preserves provenance, and respects Audit Architecture / Lifecycle boundaries | Which integrity validations were performed? |
| **Constitutional authority** | The exclusive right of published corpora to define educational and execution meaning | Who owns the standard? |
| **Implementation neutrality** | Lifecycle coordination is judged against law and completed decisions, not against a preferred stack, vendor, or topology | Remains the stack replaceable? |
| **Accountability** | Completed constitutional activities remain attributable to named decisions, provenance, and authority | Can responsibility be reconstructed? |
| **Repeatability** | Audit organisation is reproducible from reconstructable completed decisions and provenance inputs | Can the run be reproduced? |
| **Explainability** | Decisions consumed, provenance preserved, integrity validations, published records, and boundaries are speakable | Can the run be narrated honestly? |
| **Constitutional fidelity** | Stages apply audit components exactly as published; they never soft-amend law or re-produce decisions | Did authority survive coordination? |

The constitutional audit lifecycle is:

- **coordination-centred** — it organises constitutional audit activities; it never authors constitutional obligations;
- **decision-consuming** — it consumes completed constitutional decisions; it never invents, rewrites, or re-produces them;
- **architecture-faithful** — stages invoke only published CAA-01…CAA-06 components from WS4 / MS001; they never invent audit machinery;
- **catalogue-closed** — only CAAL-01…CAAL-07 may be applied as constitutional audit lifecycle stages;
- **provenance-preserving** — it records lineage exactly as reconstructable from completed decisions and published corpora; it never invents provenance theatre;
- **integrity-bound** — constructed audit records must survive constitutional integrity validation before publication;
- **accountability-honest** — outputs are constitutional audit records for accountability / explainability, not constitutional amendments, re-decisions, or stack freezes;
- **authority-preserving** — published corpora remain the sole source of constitutional meaning;
- **EIP-faithful** — Educational Interpretation Principles are referenced as published; they are never reinterpreted or replaced;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by lifecycle law;
- **repeatable** — organisation is reproducible from reconstructable inputs;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which decisions, provenance, integrity validations, records, and boundaries applied;
- **non-executive** — lifecycle progress never performs constitutional rule execution;
- **non-decisive** — lifecycle progress never produces constitutional decisions;
- **non-legislative** — lifecycle progress never amends constitutional specifications;
- **non-substitutive** — lifecycle machinery never becomes the constitutional standard for the next act;
- **non-authority-creating** — lifecycle progress never creates constitutional authority;
- **non-mutating** — the lifecycle never modifies constitutional specifications or implementation artefacts under coordination pretext.

The constitutional audit lifecycle is **not**:

- a second Educational Constitution, EIP, or Programme VI / VII / VIII / IX amendment path;
- a licence to reinterpret coach meaning, ownership maps, tip warrants, runtime contracts, or Programme IX judgements in “lifecycle” prose;
- a Runtime A workflow runner, scheduler, Python interpreter, Flask request cycle, or CI job;
- a substitute for WS1 context / resolution / completion — those corpora prepare context; CAAL never prepares context;
- a substitute for WS2 execution engine / lifecycle / completion — those corpora execute rules and complete execution; CAAL never re-executes rules;
- a substitute for WS3 decision architecture / lifecycle / completion — those corpora produce decisions; CAAL consumes *completed decisions* and never re-produces them;
- a substitute for WS4 / MS001 audit architecture meaning — CAA defines components; CAAL sequences when those activities occur;
- a substitute for Programme IX conformance, verification, compliance, certification, or evolution — those corpora produce governance judgements under their own catalogues; CAAL may *preserve* published identities as provenance and never becomes an independent producer of those judgements;
- a substitute for Programme VIII evidence validation (EV-xx) — EV validates educational evidence eligibility; CAAL coordinates *constitutional audit sequencing* of completed decisions;
- a substitute for Educational Validation Framework quality review — quality release remains a distinct horizon;
- a claim that “audit published” or “lifecycle closed” guarantees learning, mastery, exam readiness, product success, conformity, verification, compliance, certification, or frozen stack authority;
- a testing framework, CI/CD pipeline, GitHub Action, linter policy, or OpenAPI checker.

## 3. Lifecycle Posture (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material constitutional audit lifecycle travels through seven constitutional stages. These are **normative coordination obligations**, not an implementation architecture and not a CI job graph.

```
CAAL-01 Audit Initiation
        │
        ▼
CAAL-02 Decision Intake
        │
        ├── incomplete / refused (lawful stop — audit production must not proceed)
        │
        └── admitted →
                │
                ▼
CAAL-03 Audit Construction
        │
        ▼
CAAL-04 Provenance Recording
        │
        ▼
CAAL-05 Integrity Validation
        │
        ├── invalid / refused (lawful stop — do not publish as valid)
        │
        └── valid →
                │
                ▼
CAAL-06 Audit Publication
        │
        ▼
CAAL-07 Audit Closure
        │
        ▼
Explain decisions / provenance / integrity / records / boundaries
```

| Stage | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **CAAL-01 Initiation** | Open an audit concern against named completed-decision identity and published scope | Scope is unpublished custom, tribal knowledge, or “how main works today” |
| **CAAL-02 Decision intake** | Admit identifiable completed constitutional decisions under CAA-01 | Decisions are missing, invented, rewritten, incomplete, or would require re-production |
| **CAAL-03 Audit construction** | Assemble a candidate constitutional audit record under CAA-02 | Construction would invent law, rewrite specs, reinterpret EIP, re-produce decisions, or privilege a stack as law |
| **CAAL-04 Provenance recording** | Preserve reconstructable constitutional provenance under CAA-03 | Provenance would be invented, forged, erased, or treated as new law |
| **CAAL-05 Integrity validation** | Confirm the candidate is constitutionally intact under CAA-04 | Validation would be skipped, forged, or treated as new law |
| **CAAL-06 Audit publication** | Publish valid constitutional audit records under CAA-05 (with CAA-06 state) | Publication would invent integrity, hide failed validation, rewrite boundaries, or freeze a stack as law |
| **CAAL-07 Closure** | Close the lifecycle honestly when duties are exhausted | Closure would execute rules, produce decisions, reinterpret EIP, create / replace constitutional authority, or pretend incomplete work is done |

> **The lifecycle never “best-effort invents” an eighth stage called law-making, reinterpretation, rule re-execution, decision re-production, authority creation, stack canonisation, or artefact mutation.**

## 6. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Audit Lifecycle Model exists so that:

1. **Law remains upstream of coordination** — stages organise audit production against published components and completed decisions; progress does not quietly become the specifications.
2. **Decisions remain honest** — intake admits completed constitutional decisions; it does not invent or re-produce them to force audit records.
3. **Provenance remains honest** — the lifecycle records reconstructable lineage; it does not invent provenance theatre so publication can proceed.
4. **Audit production remains sequenced** — CAA activities occur under CAAL stages after lawful initiation and decision intake — not as random green theatre.
5. **Execution remains untouched** — lifecycle progress never becomes a second execution engine.
6. **Decision production remains untouched** — lifecycle progress never becomes a second decision engine.
7. **Integrity remains required** — publication never skips constitutional integrity validation.
8. **Authority remains honest** — no team may treat a closed lifecycle as the new constitutional source.
9. **EIP remains intact** — audit coordination never reinterprets or replaces Educational Interpretation Principles.
10. **Implementations remain replaceable** — lifecycle law does not freeze Flask, SQLAlchemy, a particular API shape, or a CI vendor as law.
11. **Accountability / repeatability remain honest** — the same published inputs and stage sequence yield the same lawful audit disposition.
12. **Explainability remains intact** — lifecycle speech describes coordination of audit production; it does not redefine what learning means.
13. **Students remain protected** — product theatre cannot launder incomplete decisions or unvalidated constructions as “lifecycle audited.”
