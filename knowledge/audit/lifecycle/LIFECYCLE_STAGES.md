## 1. Purpose

Audit production without a closed stage catalogue invents law by proximity: whichever pipeline step, ticket status, or ambient product habit happens to finish becomes the tutor’s “proof the system audited.”

This catalogue names the only lawful constitutional audit lifecycle stages — and binds each stage to purpose, inputs, outputs, and permitted / prohibited activities.

---

## 2. Stage Principles

1. **Ordered and skip-disciplined.** Stages may be brief under thin scope; mandatory initiation, decision intake, construction, provenance recording, integrity validation, publication, and closure obligations may not be silently omitted when published constitutional audit records are claimed.
2. **Completed decisions before construction.** Identifiable completed constitutional decisions are admitted before audit-record construction.
3. **Explainability accumulates.** Each stage leaves traceable artefacts for `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.
4. **Refusal is a stage outcome.** Refuse-open, refuse-intake, stop, escalate, invalid, refuse-publish, and refuse-close are first-class conclusions.
5. **Repeatable transitions.** Same published inputs ⇒ same next stage outcomes.
6. **Stages coordinate; they do not legislate.** No stage creates, modifies, or redefines constitutional law.
7. **Stages do not become constitutional authority.** Closure never freezes an implementation as permanently lawful or replaces published corpora.
8. **Stages do not execute constitutional rules.** Recording audit information is not performing WS2 execution.
9. **Stages do not produce constitutional decisions.** Recording audit information is not performing WS3 decision production.
10. **Stages do not reinterpret EIP.** Educational Interpretation Principles remain as published.
11. **Stages invoke published audit components only.** CAA-01…CAA-06 may be applied; unpublished audit customs may not.

## 3. Catalogue Overview

| ID | Stage | Constitutional purpose (short) | Primary inputs | Primary outputs |
|----|-------|--------------------------------|----------------|-----------------|
| **CAAL-01** | Audit Initiation | Open an audit concern against a named completed-decision identity and published scope | Completed-decision identity + published scope + optional CAA indication | Initiated audit record (or refuse-open) |
| **CAAL-02** | Decision Intake | Admit identifiable completed constitutional decisions under CAA-01 | Initiated record + candidate completed decisions | Admitted decision set (or refuse-intake) |
| **CAAL-03** | Audit Construction | Construct a candidate constitutional audit record under CAA-02 | Admitted decisions + authority references | Candidate constitutional audit record |
| **CAAL-04** | Provenance Recording | Preserve reconstructable constitutional provenance under CAA-03 | Candidate record + lineage identities | Provenance record bound to the candidate |
| **CAAL-05** | Integrity Validation | Validate the candidate under CAA-04 | Candidate + provenance + integrity expectations | Integrity disposition (valid / invalid / refuse) |
| **CAAL-06** | Audit Publication | Publish valid constitutional audit records under CAA-05 (with CAA-06 state) | Validated record + state / boundary confirmations | Published constitutional audit record (or refuse-publish) |
| **CAAL-07** | Audit Closure | Close the lifecycle honestly when duties are exhausted | Published artefacts + boundary confirmations | Closed lifecycle (or refuse-close) — never a freeze of constitutional authority |

Material constitutional audit production claiming lifecycle outcomes must map to this sequence as published law requires. Stages may be brief; none may invent a stage outside this catalogue.

**Relation to CAA / CECX / CECR / CECC / CEE / CEEL / CEEC / CDA / CDAL / CDAC / Programme VIII / IX catalogues:** Those catalogues remain defined solely by their owners. CAAL stages *organise when* constitutional audit activities occur; they do not replace, reclassify, re-execute, invent, or amend those catalogues.

## 4. Canonical Stage Sequence

```text
CAAL-01  Audit Initiation
           ↓
CAAL-02  Decision Intake
           ↓
CAAL-03  Audit Construction
           ↓
CAAL-04  Provenance Recording
           ↓
CAAL-05  Integrity Validation
           ↓
CAAL-06  Audit Publication
           ↓
CAAL-07  Audit Closure
```

Lawful early stop dispositions (refuse-open / refuse-intake / stop / escalate / invalid) may exhaust remaining *audit-production* duties while still requiring honest publication (or refuse-publish) and closure — never silent disappearance of the concern.

## 5. Stage Definitions

### CAAL-01 — Audit Initiation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Open a material constitutional audit concern by naming the completed constitutional decision identity under which audit production will proceed and the published scope of the concern — without inventing law, opening against unpublished customs, treating initiation as a published audit record, executing constitutional rules, or producing constitutional decisions.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Identifiable completed constitutional decision identity (WS3 / MS003 fulfilment claimed or to be confirmed at CAAL-02)
- Concern identity (what constitutional audit question is under organisation)
- Optional published indication of CAA components required for the scope
- Optional identifiable supporting authority / lineage references (not rewritten)

**Constitutional outputs**

- An initiated audit concern record naming completed-decision identity, scope, and intended CAA component set — **or**
- Lawful refuse-open when standards are unpublished, scope is theatrical, completed-decision identity cannot be established, or initiation would invent law

**Permitted activities**

- Name published completed-decision identity and scope as audit standards
- Bound the concern scope without amending corpora
- Record intended published CAA components from published catalogues
- Refuse initiation when constitutional preconditions fail
- Preserve initiation audit identity for later stages

**Prohibited activities**

- Treat “how main works,” tribal knowledge, or stack preference as the standard
- Create or modify constitutional specifications by opening a concern
- Issue published constitutional audit records at initiation
- Execute constitutional rules or produce constitutional decisions at initiation
- Reinterpret EIP at initiation
- Invent unpublished audit components or lifecycle stages
- Skip initiation when material published audit records will later be claimed
- Create constitutional authority at initiation

**Authority preserved**  
Published corpora remain the sole standard. Initiation organises; it does not legislate.

---

### CAAL-02 — Decision Intake

**Constitutional purpose**  
Admit only **identifiable completed constitutional decisions** for the concern under CAA-01 — so subsequent stages audit against real completed decisions, not anonymous theatre — without inventing, rewriting, or re-producing decisions, and without performing WS2 execution or WS3 decision production.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Initiated concern from CAAL-01
- Candidate completed constitutional decision identities (WS3 / MS003 completion confirmed, with supporting architecture / lifecycle references as applicable)
- Reconstructable decision traces sufficient to bind audit records
- Published CAAO / CAALO expectations for completed-decision honesty

**Constitutional outputs**

- An admitted decision set bound to the initiated concern — **or**
- Lawful refuse-intake when decisions are missing, invented, rewritten, incomplete, unpublished, or would require re-production to “complete”

**Permitted activities**

- Identify and bind named completed constitutional decisions
- Confirm WS3 completion identity where published law requires
- Refuse intake of incomplete, invented, or theatrical decisions
- Preserve decision provenance for later stages and explainability
- Invoke CAA-01 Decision Intake as published by WS4 / MS001

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent, rewrite, or complete unfinished constitutional decisions under an audit badge
- Re-produce constitutional decisions to manufacture inputs
- Re-execute constitutional rules to manufacture decisions
- Treat CI greens, Twin estimates, or UI ticks as completed constitutional decisions
- Soft-select convenient decisions and omit inconvenient completed decisions without lawful scope record
- Begin audit construction against anonymous or incomplete decisions
- Treat intake itself as a published constitutional audit record

**Authority preserved**  
WS3 remains the owner of constitutional decisions. Intake admits; it does not decide.

---

### CAAL-03 — Audit Construction

**Constitutional purpose**  
Construct a **candidate constitutional audit record** from admitted completed decisions under CAA-02 — assembling reconstructable audit information for provenance recording and integrity validation — without becoming new law, EIP replacement, rule re-execution, decision re-production, or creation of constitutional authority.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Admitted decision set from CAAL-02
- Named constitutional authority references already available through completed decisions and published corpora
- Published CAA-02 Audit Record Constructor expectations

**Constitutional outputs**

- A candidate constitutional audit record citing admitted decisions and authority — **or**
- Lawful stop / refuse when construction would invent disposition, omit required citations, or violate published construction rules

**Permitted activities**

- Assemble candidate audit records from admitted decisions exactly as published construction rules allow
- Cite completed decisions and constitutional authority
- Record audit-state progress under CAA-06 as applicable
- Invoke CAA-02 Audit Record Constructor as published by WS4 / MS001
- Prefer refuse over inventing a convenient candidate for demo continuity

**Prohibited activities**

- Construct a candidate that amends constitutional specifications
- Construct a candidate that reinterprets or replaces EIP
- Construct a candidate that re-executes constitutional rules
- Construct a candidate that re-produces constitutional decisions
- Present an unvalidated candidate as a published valid constitutional audit record
- Invent missing admitted decisions to force construction
- Create constitutional authority via construction theatre

**Authority preserved**  
Construction assembles candidates under published law. It does not create authority.

---

### CAAL-04 — Provenance Recording

**Constitutional purpose**  
Preserve **reconstructable constitutional provenance** for the candidate audit record under CAA-03 — binding the record to completed decisions, upstream outcome / authority citations, and published corpora — without inventing lineage, forging trails, erasing inconvenient history, or treating provenance as new law.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Candidate constitutional audit record from CAAL-03
- Admitted decision identities and upstream lineage identities available through completed decisions and published corpora
- Published CAA-03 Provenance Recorder expectations

**Constitutional outputs**

- A provenance record bound to the candidate audit record — **or**
- Lawful stop / refuse when provenance would require invention, forgery, or soft-amendment of lineage meaning

**Permitted activities**

- Record reconstructable links from completed decisions through upstream identities to the candidate
- Preserve CT / sibling lineage identities where already established — never invent them
- Invoke CAA-03 Provenance Recorder as published by WS4 / MS001
- Refuse to proceed when required provenance cannot be reconstructed honestly
- Preserve provenance artefacts for integrity validation and explainability

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent provenance theatre or forge lineage links
- Erase inconvenient completed-decision or authority citations
- Treat provenance records as constitutional amendments or re-decisions
- Soft-amend CT / sibling lineage meaning under an audit badge
- Skip provenance recording when a published audit record will later be claimed
- Privilege a stack preference as provenance identity

**Authority preserved**  
Provenance preserves published lineage. It does not invent law or create authority.

---

### CAAL-05 — Integrity Validation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Validate that the candidate constitutional audit record is **constitutionally intact** under CAA-04 — confirming completed-decision citation, provenance preservation, authority preservation, and boundary integrity — before any claim of valid publication.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Candidate constitutional audit record from CAAL-03
- Provenance record from CAAL-04
- Published integrity expectations (CAA-04 / CAAO / CAALO / boundary corpus)
- Boundary confirmations required by `LIFECYCLE_BOUNDARIES.md`

**Constitutional outputs**

- Integrity disposition: valid / invalid / refuse — with reconstructable reasons — **or**
- Escalation when validation itself would require inventing law or mutating artefacts

**Permitted activities**

- Confirm candidate cites identifiable completed decisions and preserved provenance
- Confirm EIP was not reinterpreted and specifications were not amended
- Confirm no constitutional rule execution or decision re-production occurred under audit pretence
- Confirm implementation neutrality and authority preservation (no authority creation)
- Invoke CAA-04 Integrity Validator as published by WS4 / MS001
- Refuse to mark invalid candidates as valid

**Prohibited activities**

- Skip integrity validation so publication can proceed
- Forge integrity dispositions
- Treat integrity success as constitutional amendment, educational quality, or authority creation
- Soft-waive hard boundaries for demo urgency
- Re-execute rules or re-produce decisions “to make integrity pass”
- Publish invalid candidates as constitutionally valid

**Authority preserved**  
Integrity validation confirms fidelity to published law. It does not rewrite law.

---

### CAAL-06 — Audit Publication

**Constitutional purpose**  
Publish **constitutionally valid** constitutional audit records under CAA-05 for accountability and explainability — communicating the audit honestly — without inventing integrity, hiding failed validation, rewriting boundaries, freezing a stack as law, or creating constitutional authority.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Validated constitutional audit record (valid disposition from CAAL-05)
- Audit-state records under CAA-06 as applicable
- Boundary confirmations and explainability obligations

**Constitutional outputs**

- Published constitutional audit record artefacts bound to decisions, provenance, integrity validations, and authority — **or**
- Lawful refuse-publish when integrity is absent, boundaries are broken, or publication would invent satisfaction

**Permitted activities**

- Publish valid audit records under published honesty rules
- Preserve reconstructable links to decisions, provenance, and integrity validations
- Record publication identity for closure and explainability
- Invoke CAA-05 Audit Publisher (and CAA-06 Audit State Manager) as published by WS4 / MS001
- Refuse publication of invalid or unvalidated candidates

**Prohibited activities**

- Publish invalid / unvalidated constructions as valid
- Present publication as new constitutional law, EIP replacement, or authority creation
- Present publication as constitutional rule execution or decision re-production
- Freeze implementations, stacks, vendors, or releases as constitutional law
- Erase prior stage history to simplify the published story
- Determine independent governance outside published constitutional authority via publication theatre

**Authority preserved**  
Publication communicates audit records. It does not become the corpus.

---

### CAAL-07 — Audit Closure

**Constitutional purpose**  
Close the constitutional audit lifecycle honestly when remaining *audit-production* duties for the concern are exhausted — recording closure without pretending incomplete work is done, without executing rules, without producing decisions, without reinterpreting EIP, and without creating or replacing constitutional authority.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Published artefacts (or refuse-publish record) from CAAL-06
- Stage history for CAAL-01…CAAL-06
- Boundary confirmations required by `LIFECYCLE_BOUNDARIES.md`
- Explainability readiness under `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md`

**Constitutional outputs**

- Closed lifecycle record with reconstructable stage trail — **or**
- Lawful refuse-close when mandatory duties remain, boundaries are broken, or closure would invent completion

**Permitted activities**

- Affirm that required audit-lifecycle duties for the concern are exhausted (or honestly refuse-close)
- Preserve full stage / decision / provenance / integrity / publication trail
- Distinguish lawful early stops from abandoned duties
- Hand off published audit records to downstream explainability consumers without transferring legislative power

**Prohibited activities**

- Close while pretending incomplete intake, construction, provenance, integrity validation, or publication succeeded
- Execute constitutional rules, produce constitutional decisions, reinterpret EIP, or amend specifications under a closed seal
- Create or replace constitutional authority because the lifecycle closed
- Freeze a stack as law because the lifecycle closed
- Narrate closure as mastery, readiness, educational quality, or independent governance outside authority
- Invent an eighth stage of law-making after closure

**Authority preserved**  
Closure ends coordination. Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard for the next act.

## 6. Stage Interaction Rules

1. **No silent skips.** Mandatory duties may be brief; they may not vanish when a published audit record is claimed.
2. **Intake before construction.** CAAL-03 must not begin against refused or anonymous decisions.
3. **Provenance before valid publication.** CAAL-04 must leave reconstructable lineage when a published record is claimed.
4. **Integrity before valid publication.** CAAL-06 must not publish as valid without CAAL-05 success.
5. **Refuse is first-class.** Refuse-open / refuse-intake / invalid / refuse-publish / refuse-close are lawful outcomes.
6. **CAA fidelity.** CAAL-02…CAAL-06 invoke only published CAA-01…CAA-06 components.
7. **No execution bleed.** No stage may perform WS2 constitutional rule execution.
8. **No decision bleed.** No stage may perform WS3 constitutional decision production.
9. **No EIP bleed.** No stage may reinterpret or replace Educational Interpretation Principles.
10. **Explainability trail.** Each stage contributes artefacts required by `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.
