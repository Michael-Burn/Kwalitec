## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “pipeline finished,” “stages skipped for speed,” or “audit badge green.” The tutor optimises **honest organisation of constitutional auditing**: only an accountable, provenance-preserving, repeatable, explainable, and constitutionally faithful sequence — consuming completed constitutional decisions and applying published CAA components — may support lawful published constitutional audit records.

These objectives bind every constitutional audit lifecycle and every successor coordination machinery that claims constitutional audit lifecycle meaning.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### CAALO-01 — Preserve accountability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material audit lifecycle organises activities through recognised CAAL-01…CAAL-07 stages such that completed constitutional activities remain attributable to named completed constitutional decisions, named provenance, named integrity validations, and named published authority — so constitutional audit organisation never collapses into anonymous ambient logs, green theatre, or unowned seals.

**Includes:**

- Binding every material lifecycle to identifiable completed WS3 constitutional decisions and published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–IX authority identities.
- Consuming identifiable completed constitutional decisions as inputs — never inventing or rewriting them.
- Invoking only published CAA-01…CAA-06 components during CAAL-02…CAAL-06 as WS4 / MS001 requires.
- Preferring lawful refuse / stop / escalate / invalid outcomes over rewriting law, inventing decisions, inventing provenance, re-executing constitutional rules, re-producing decisions, reinterpreting EIP, amending specifications, or mutating artefacts so the lifecycle “completes.”
- Keeping lifecycle progress distinct from educational quality, mastery, readiness, product success, constitutional rule execution, decision re-production, EIP replacement, constitutional amendment, or creation of constitutional authority.
- Preserving the Model’s posture: stages coordinate audit production; they do not become law or constitutional authority.

**Excludes:**

- Treating green CI, coverage percentages, linter scores, Twin estimates, Adaptive scores, UI ticks, or seals as lifecycle fulfilment.
- Soft-amending specifications, inventing unpublished customs, inventing or rewriting completed decisions, inventing provenance, mutating artefacts, re-executing rules, re-producing decisions, or privileging “how main works” to force progress.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for lawful accountable coordination.
- Declaring “close enough” closure without documenting a corpus amendment.
- Freezing an implementation as permanently lawful because the lifecycle closed.
- Re-running WS2 execution or WS3 decision production under a lifecycle badge.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software pretends a mark was filed without naming which completed decision was recorded or who owned the standard. Accountable coordination is care; anonymous audit theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CAALO-02 — Preserve provenance

**Definition.** Ensure that every material audit lifecycle preserves **reconstructable constitutional provenance** — lineage binding admitted completed decisions through upstream outcome / authority citations to published audit records — so auditors and successor machinery can follow the trail without tribal knowledge or ambient log archaeology.

**Tutor rationale.** An IFoA examiner who cannot follow how yesterday’s mark reached today’s file has no defensible process.

**Manifestations:**

- Provenance recording at CAAL-04 under CAA-03 is mandatory when a published audit record is claimed.
- Provenance cites identifiable completed decisions and published corpora; invented lineage is a hard stop.
- Soft-selecting convenient lineage and omitting inconvenient completed-decision links is a defect.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing prior lawful provenance to simplify the next report.
- Provenance never becomes new constitutional law, a re-decision, or a stack freeze.

---

### CAALO-03 — Preserve repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that the same published constitutional inputs, the same consumed completed constitutional decisions, the same published CAA component discipline, the same provenance inputs, the same integrity outcomes, and the same CAAL stage sequence yield the same constitutional audit dispositions and the same lawful stage outcomes — absent a published non-deterministic exception (none are granted by this Model for constitutional meaning or audit disposition).

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who treats the same completed decisions as audited or not depending on which runner woke first — or skips stages randomly — is not coaching; they are gambling.

**Manifestations:**

- Stage transitions and outcomes are corpus-bound and reproducible.
- Parallel CI runners, sharding, and cache races must not change constitutional disposition or invent stage skips.
- Explicit multi-decision intake at CAAL-02 uses published composition / published stop rules, not random operator races.
- “Eventually consistent lifecycle meaning” is a constitutional defect.
- Invented or unstable completed-decision inputs, and invented provenance, are hard stops — not soft variables.

---

### CAALO-04 — Preserve explainability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material audit lifecycle can honestly answer the mandatory explainability questions in `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md` — so students, developers, and auditors hear the same constitutional truth in appropriate vocabulary.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who cannot say *which completed decisions* were consumed, *which provenance* was preserved, *which integrity validations* were performed, *which audit record* was published, and *which boundaries* remained intact — is performing, not coordinating audit production.

**Manifestations:**

- Explainability obligations apply to all material lifecycles.
- Student / developer / auditor projections share one truth with different vocabulary.
- Lifecycle progress is never narrated as mastery, readiness, educational quality, new law, rule re-execution, decision re-production, EIP replacement, authority creation, or a freeze of stack authority.
- Stack preference and race-dependent outcomes are defects to disclose, not mysteries to hide.

---

### CAALO-05 — Preserve constitutional fidelity

**Definition.** Ensure that every material audit lifecycle applies published CAA components and preserves published constitutional meaning **exactly as published** — preserving constitutional authority, EIP integrity, and implementation neutrality — so coordination never soft-amends law, invents independent interpretation, re-executes rules, re-produces decisions, or elevates lifecycle machinery into constitutional authority.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who quietly rewrites the syllabus mid-filing so today’s audit badge looks finished has abandoned educational law for product fiction.

**Manifestations:**

- CAA components cited at CAAL-02…CAAL-06 remain unchanged by stage progress.
- EIP claim classes, Programme VI meaning, and WS1 / WS2 / WS3 / WS4 MS001 meanings survive coordination intact.
- Constitutional audit records remain audit records — never amendments, EIP replacements, re-executions, re-decisions, or stack freezes.
- Implementation neutrality is preserved; no stack becomes constitutional by proximity to closure.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing prior lawful lifecycle history to simplify the next report.
- Audit records preserve constitutional references, completed-decision identities, CAA / CAAL identities, provenance identities, integrity dispositions, and published audit-record identities when material.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CAALO-01 conflicts with “helping” incomplete decisions look lifecycle-complete | Accountability / lawful refuse wins |
| CAALO-02 conflicts with inventing convenient lineage | Provenance honesty wins |
| CAALO-03 conflicts with race-dependent runner outcomes | Repeatability wins |
| CAALO-04 conflicts with opaque “trust us” seals | Explainability wins |
| CAALO-05 conflicts with soft-amending specs so stages finish | Constitutional fidelity wins |

No supporting objective authorises violating CAALO-01 when that violation would destroy reconstructable accountability — and none authorises violating CAALO-05 to manufacture progress.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — Constitution / EIP integrity obligations, Programme VI–IX authority preservation, WS1 preparation honesty, WS2 execution honesty, WS3 decision honesty, and WS4 / MS001 audit objectives for the lifecycle horizon.

---

## 5. Non-Objectives

The constitutional audit lifecycle does **not** optimise for:

- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- CI throughput, flaky-test suppression, or coverage theatre as educational success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when specifications or completed decisions are incomplete;
- collapsing CAAL stages into one mega-step that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics signals as substitutes for completed decisions or published CAA components;
- freezing implementations, releases, or stacks as permanently lawful;
- treating documentation as optional commentary on whatever code already shipped;
- replacing WS1 preparation, WS2 execution, WS3 decision production, or WS4 / MS001 audit architecture meaning;
- modifying implementation artefacts to manufacture passable audit records;
- executing constitutional rules or producing constitutional decisions under an audit seal;
- reinterpreting Educational Interpretation Principles or creating constitutional authority under lifecycle closure.

## 6. Closing Statement

> **A lifecycle that becomes “complete” by rewriting constitutional law, inventing completed decisions, re-executing rules, re-producing decisions, reinterpreting EIP, mutating artefacts, or creating constitutional authority is a failed educational system.  
> A lifecycle that coordinates only published constitutional auditing — with accountability, provenance, repeatability, explainability, and constitutional fidelity intact — is doing its only coordination job.**
