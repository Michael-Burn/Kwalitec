## 2. What the Constitutional Audit Architecture Is

The **Constitutional Audit Architecture** is the binding **constitutional mechanism** by which **completed constitutional decisions** may be **recorded into constitutional audit records** — consuming those decisions, constructing and validating audit records, recording provenance, managing audit state, and publishing audit records for explainability — while preserving accountability, traceability, provenance, implementation neutrality, repeatability, explainability, and constitutional fidelity — without becoming a source of constitutional law, a substitute for EIP, a re-execution engine, a second decision producer, or an independent governance producer outside constitutional authority.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Completed constitutional decision** | A reconstructable WS3 decision whose required decision lifecycle has lawfully completed and whose disposition has been published | Which completed decisions may be consumed? |
| **Constitutional audit information** | Speakable facts required to reconstruct which completed decision was recorded, under which provenance, with which integrity confirmation | What must be recorded? |
| **Constitutional provenance** | Reconstructable lineage binding the audit record to completed decisions, upstream outcomes / authority citations, and published corpora — never invented lineage | Which lineage was preserved? |
| **Constitutional audit record** | Reconstructable record produced under published CAA components for accountability and explainability — never new law, never a re-decision, never stack freeze | What audit record was produced? |
| **Audit integrity** | Confirmation that a constructed audit record cites completed decisions, preserves provenance, and respects Audit Architecture boundaries | Is the audit record constitutionally intact? |
| **Audit state** | Speakable progress of audit recording under CAA-06 (not educational state, not independent governance disposition, not a re-decision) | Where is audit recording in its lawful course? |
| **Constitutional authority** | The exclusive right of published corpora to define educational and execution meaning | Who owns the standard? |
| **Implementation neutrality** | Audit law does not privilege a stack, vendor, or topology | Remains the stack replaceable? |
| **Accountability** | Completed constitutional activities remain attributable to named decisions, provenance, and authority — not to anonymous ambient logs | Can responsibility be reconstructed? |
| **Traceability** | Material audit runs leave reconstructable links from decisions through provenance to published records | Can the trail be followed? |
| **Repeatability** | Audit recording is reproducible from reconstructable completed decisions and provenance inputs | Can the record be reproduced? |
| **Explainability** | Decisions recorded, provenance preserved, audit records produced, and boundaries intact are speakable | Can the audit be narrated honestly? |
| **Constitutional fidelity** | Audit recording preserves published meaning exactly; it never soft-amends law or re-produces decisions | Did authority survive audit recording? |

The Constitutional Audit Architecture is:

- **decision-consuming** — it consumes completed constitutional decisions; it never invents, rewrites, or re-produces them;
- **record-centred** — it produces constitutional audit records; it never authors constitutional obligations or decisions;
- **provenance-preserving** — it records lineage exactly as reconstructable from completed decisions and published corpora; it never invents provenance theatre;
- **authority-preserving** — published corpora remain the sole source of constitutional meaning;
- **EIP-faithful** — Educational Interpretation Principles are referenced as published; they are never reinterpreted or replaced;
- **catalogue-closed** — only CAA-01…CAA-06 may be applied as constitutional audit architecture components;
- **integrity-bound** — constructed audit records must survive constitutional integrity validation before publication;
- **accountability-honest** — outputs are constitutional audit records for accountability / explainability, not constitutional amendments, re-decisions, or stack freezes;
- **state-honest** — audit state describes Audit Architecture progress; it never invents educational state, independent governance, or re-decision dispositions;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by audit law;
- **repeatable** — audit recording is reproducible from reconstructable inputs;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which decisions, provenance, records, and boundaries applied;
- **non-executive** — audit recording never performs constitutional rule execution;
- **non-decisive** — audit recording never produces constitutional decisions;
- **non-legislative** — audit recording never amends constitutional specifications;
- **non-substitutive** — audit machinery never becomes the constitutional standard for the next act;
- **non-authority-creating** — audit recording never creates constitutional authority.

The Constitutional Audit Architecture is **not**:

- a second Educational Constitution, EIP, or Programme VI / VII / VIII / IX amendment path;
- a licence to reinterpret coach meaning, ownership maps, tip warrants, runtime contracts, or Programme IX judgements in “audit” prose;
- a Runtime A workflow runner, scheduler, Python interpreter, Flask request cycle, or CI job;
- a substitute for WS1 context / resolution / completion — those corpora prepare context; CAA never prepares context;
- a substitute for WS2 execution engine / lifecycle / completion — those corpora execute rules and complete execution; CAA never re-executes rules;
- a substitute for WS3 decision architecture / lifecycle / completion — those corpora produce decisions; CAA consumes *completed decisions* and never re-produces them;
- a substitute for Programme IX conformance, verification, compliance, certification, or evolution — those corpora produce governance judgements under their own catalogues; CAA may *record* published identities as provenance and never becomes an independent producer of those judgements;
- a substitute for Programme VIII evidence validation (EV-xx) — EV validates educational evidence eligibility; CAA records *constitutional audit* of completed decisions;
- a substitute for Educational Validation Framework quality review — quality release remains a distinct horizon;
- a claim that “audit published” guarantees learning, mastery, exam readiness, product success, conformity, verification, compliance, certification, or frozen stack authority;
- a testing framework, CI/CD pipeline, GitHub Action, linter policy, or OpenAPI checker.

## 3. Audit Posture (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material constitutional audit travels through constitutional phases. These are **normative audit obligations**, not an implementation architecture.

```
Confirm completed constitutional decisions (WS3 / MS003)
        │
        ├── incomplete / refused (lawful stop — audit recording must not begin)
        │
        └── completed → Intake completed constitutional decisions (CAA-01)
                │
                ▼
        Construct constitutional audit record (CAA-02)
                │
                ▼
        Record constitutional provenance (CAA-03)
                │
                ▼
        Validate audit integrity (CAA-04)
                │
                ├── invalid / refused (lawful stop — do not publish as valid)
                │
                └── valid → Publish constitutional audit record (CAA-05)
                        │
                        ├── manage audit state (CAA-06)
                        │
                        └── never execute constitutional rules, produce constitutional
                            decisions, reinterpret EIP, amend specifications,
                            or replace / create constitutional authority
```

**Refuse is lawful.** Prefer honest stop over inventing unpublished audit customs, re-producing decisions under an audit label, or minting independent governance so a demo can “audit anyway.”

## 4. Integrity Rules

1. **Completed decisions only.** Audit recording may begin only against WS3-completed published constitutional decisions.
2. **Published components only.** Unpublished CAA customs are unlawful.
3. **Exactly as published.** Successful audit recording never rewrites specification meaning.
4. **Authority first.** Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard; the Audit Architecture never becomes one.
5. **EIP intact.** Educational Interpretation Principles are never reinterpreted or replaced by audit prose.
6. **No rule execution.** Recording audit information is not performing WS2 constitutional rule execution.
7. **No decision production.** Recording audit information is not producing WS3 constitutional decisions.
8. **Implementation independence.** No stack, vendor, or reference implementation becomes law by proximity to an audit record.
9. **Audit records ≠ legislation.** Constitutional audit records never amend constitutional specifications.
10. **Audit records ≠ decisions.** Constitutional audit records never become substitute constitutional decisions.
11. **No authority substitution.** A published audit record never replaces the corpus for the next act.
12. **No authority creation.** Audit recording never creates constitutional authority.
13. **No bypass.** Product urgency never waives required audit components or completed-decision prerequisites.
14. **Repeatability.** Operator-dependent or ambient-only audit recording that cannot be reconstructed is a defect.
15. **Explain the stop.** Students, developers, and auditors should hear when audit recording must refuse.
16. **No emergency exemption.** Deadlines and demos never mint constitutional amendment or re-decision power via the Audit Architecture.
17. **Integrity before publication.** Invalid constructed audit records must not be published as constitutionally valid.
18. **Upstream honesty.** Upstream constitutional decisions remain decisions; audit recording never rewrites them into new law or new dispositions.

## 6. Relationship to Upstream Decision and Downstream Consumers

Completed constitutional decisions produced under WS3 (CDA-05 / CDAC completion) are **inputs** to this Audit Architecture. The Audit Architecture may:

- read reconstructable completed constitutional decisions;
- bind audit records to published constitutional authority already named in the completed decisions and published corpora;
- refuse to audit when decisions are incomplete or boundary-violating.

The Audit Architecture must never:

- re-execute constitutional rules under an audit label;
- re-produce constitutional decisions under an audit label;
- treat audit records as a licence to reinterpret EIP;
- treat published audit records as constitutional amendments;
- elevate a preferred stack because an audit record was published on it;
- invent independent governance dispositions outside published constitutional authority;
- create constitutional authority by proximity to a published audit record.

Downstream explainability and product surfaces may consume published constitutional audit records. They inherit no legislative, executive, decisive, or independent-governance power from proximity to a published audit record.
