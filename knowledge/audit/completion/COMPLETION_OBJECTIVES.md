## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “pipeline greens,” “tickets closed,” or “audit badges shown.” The tutor optimises **honest closing of constitutional audit work**: when the published lifecycle for a concern has concluded, audit records have been constructed and validated, provenance has been preserved, records have been published, and the process has been formally closed, the tutor can say so — without pretending rules were re-executed, without pretending decisions were remade, without pretending EIP was reinterpreted, without pretending the Constitution was amended, without pretending authority was created, and without rewriting audit meaning because the machinery stopped cleanly.

These objectives bind every constitutional audit completion judgement and every successor audit machinery that claims constitutional audit fulfilment.

## 2. Primary Objective

### CAACO-01 — Confirm lawful audit completion

**Definition.** Ensure that runtime / assessment / audit speech affirms audit completion only when a published audit concern under WS4 / MS001–MS002 has concluded the required CAAL-01…CAAL-07 lifecycle obligations with constructed, validated, published, and formally closed audit records — or honestly refuses close when fulfilment criteria are not met.

**Includes:**

- Assessing only concerns that lawfully opened under WS4 / MS001–MS002 initiation → decision intake → construction → provenance → integrity → publication → closure law.
- Affirming completion only when `COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md` conditions (CAAC-01…CAAC-05) hold.
- Preferring lawful “not yet” / refuse-close over inventing a “done enough” status for product convenience.
- Distinguishing lawful stop dispositions (refuse / defer / escalate as published outcomes that exhaust remaining *audit-production* duties with honest recording) from abandoned lifecycle duties.
- Binding every material completion judgement to reconstructable constitutional references.

**Excludes:**

- Declaring complete because a CI job exited, a ticket closed, an audit badge glowed, or a UI spinner stopped.
- Treating Twin / Adaptive / Experience / Mission heuristics as completion warrants.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for lifecycle honesty.
- Collapsing “audit complete” into “rules re-executed,” “decisions remade,” “EIP reinterpreted,” “constitution amended,” or “authority created.”

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software celebrates unfinished audit work — or celebrates finished pipeline machinery as finished rule execution, decision remaking, or law-making. Honest confirmation is care; completion theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CAACO-02 — Preserve accountability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material completion judgement leaves a reconstructable trail of responsibility: which completed constitutional decisions were audited, which lifecycle stages were completed, which audit records were constructed and published, which integrity validations were completed, and which boundaries remained intact — so accountability survives close.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later say *who* and *what* the completed audit attributed has not finished care. Silent “complete” badges destroy student and auditor trust.

**Manifestations:**

- Accountability records preserve constitutional references, not only technical job logs or CI annotations.
- Close never substitutes pipeline exit codes or ticket states for named decision / record / stage identities.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing or silently replacing prior lawful audit records to simplify the next report.
- Missing required reconstructable accountability references after an affirmed close is a defect.

---

### CAACO-03 — Preserve provenance

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming audit completion never erases, forges, truncates, invents, or replaces published constitutional identity, completed-decision identity, or succession history required to reconstruct where the audited activity came from.

**Tutor rationale.** A close that “helpfully” drops which completed decision was audited — or invents a prettier lineage so the filing looks tidy — destroys educational honesty even if the completion UX looks clean.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion leaves specification provenance and decision provenance intact.
- Provenance cited under CAAC-03 remains reconstructable after close.
- Invented provenance theatre or truncated lineage to force a green close is a defect.
- Completion never invents Programme IX judgements or WS2 / WS3 outcomes as provenance substitutes.

---

### CAACO-04 — Preserve repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that the same published obligations, same consumed completed decisions, same provenance inputs, and same required stage sequence yield the same completion disposition for the same concern scope — without stack-specific non-reproducible close theatre.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who marks the same script “audit complete” on Monday and “not yet” on Tuesday without a published input change has abandoned professional care.

**Manifestations:**

- Same published inputs ⇒ same CAAC disposition for the same concern.
- Stack / vendor / topology differences never become completion law.
- Ephemeral “we remember it completed” theatre without reconstructable records is a defect.
- Continuity forbids deleting prior lawful publication / closure records to force a different close next time.

---

### CAACO-05 — Preserve explainability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material completion judgement can answer the mandatory explainability questions — which constitutional decision was audited, which provenance was preserved, which integrity validations were completed, which audit records were published, which boundaries remained intact — without redefining constitutional meaning or implying rule execution / decision production / EIP reinterpretation / law amendment / authority creation.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who can only say “it’s fully audited somewhere” is performing, not teaching. Speakable completion is care.

**Manifestations:**

- CAACQ-01…CAACQ-05 answers are producible for every material affirmed or refused close.
- Explanations never soft-amend law or narrate completion as rule re-execution, decision remaking, EIP replacement, or authority transfer.
- Audience projections differ in vocabulary, not in constitutional facts.
- Opaque “trust the complete badge” theatre is a defect.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CAACO-01 conflicts with inventing unpublished stages so close can complete | Lifecycle honesty wins — refuse / defer / escalate |
| CAACO-02 conflicts with opaque complete badges | Accountability wins |
| CAACO-03 conflicts with truncated or invented lineage theatre | Provenance honesty wins |
| CAACO-04 conflicts with stack-specific non-reproducible close | Repeatability wins |
| CAACO-05 conflicts with unspeakable “trust us” completion | Explainability wins |
| Any CAACO conflicts with executing rules / producing decisions / reinterpreting EIP / amending law / creating authority | Non-implication honesty wins — refuse the overclaim |

No supporting objective authorises violating CAACO-01.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — Constitution / EIP integrity obligations, Programme VI–IX authority preservation, WS4 / MS001 CAAO duties, and WS4 / MS002 CAALO duties for the *completion* horizon.

## 5. Non-Objectives

Constitutional audit completion does **not** optimise for:

- constitutional rule execution or re-authoring of completed execution outcomes;
- production, rewriting, or silent replacement of constitutional decisions;
- reinterpretation or replacement of Educational Interpretation Principles;
- constitutional amendment, soft rewrite of specifications, or creation / replacement of constitutional authority;
- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- determining governance outside published constitutional authority;
- freezing a stack, vendor, or topology as permanent constitutional law;
- CI throughput, flaky-audit suppression, or demo continuity as educational or constitutional success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when required lifecycle stages, records, provenance, integrity validations, publications, or closures remain incomplete;
- collapsing CAAC criteria into one mega-flag that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics signals as substitutes for published completion criteria;
- treating documentation of a pipeline as optional commentary on whatever code already shipped;
- declaring rule re-execution, decision remaking, EIP replacement, law amendment, or authority creation solely because completion was affirmed;
- collapsing decision completion (CDAC), execution completion (CEEC), or context completion (CECC) into audit completion.

---

## 6. Closing Statement

> **A concern that becomes “audit complete” by re-executing constitutional rules — or by remaking decisions, reinterpreting EIP, amending law, or creating authority — is a failed educational system.  
> A concern that confirms only that the required published audit lifecycle concluded, audit records were constructed and validated, provenance was preserved, records were published, and the process was formally closed — with accountability, repeatability, and explainability intact — is doing its only completion job.**
