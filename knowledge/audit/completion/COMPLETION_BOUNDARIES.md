## 1. Purpose

Audit machinery that silently turns “the audit lifecycle finished” into “constitutional rules were re-executed,” “decisions were remade,” “EIP now means X,” “the Constitution now says Y,” or “we are the new constitutional authority” destroys student trust and educational integrity.

This document draws a bright line between **lawful constitutional audit completion** (Programme X / WS4 / MS003) and **constitutional rule execution / decision production / EIP reinterpretation / specification amendment / authority creation / educational judgement / independent governance outside constitutional authority** owned elsewhere.

---

## 2. Boundary Principles

1. **Confirm audit fulfilment, do not legislate.** Completion affirms fulfilment of published audit lifecycle duties; it does not create constitutional law.
2. **Audit production only.** Rule execution, decision production, EIP reinterpretation, constitutional amendment, and authority creation are never inferred from close.
3. **Not rule execution.** WS2 owns constitutional rule execution; completion never re-executes rules.
4. **Not decision production.** WS3 owns constitutional decision production; completion never remakes decisions.
5. **Not EIP reinterpretation.** Educational Interpretation Principles remain as published.
6. **Not constitutional amendment.** Close never patches law or redefines constitutional meaning.
7. **Not authority creation.** Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard after close; completion never creates constitutional authority.
8. **Not conformance / verification / compliance / certification re-determination.** Programme IX judgements may appear as provenance identities only.
9. **Exactly as published.** Close never redefines CAA / CAAL catalogues or soft-amends Programme VI–IX meaning.
10. **Published criteria only.** Pipelines, tickets, badges, and elapsed time are not fulfilment law.
11. **Published records only.** Accountability continuity is constitutional, not free-form log theatre.
12. **Reference decisions honestly.** Completed decisions may be cited; closing never upgrades citation into re-production or amendment.
13. **Meaning non-reinterpretation.** Programme VI questions and EIP claim classes survive close intact.
14. **Implementation neutrality.** No stack becomes law by proximity to a complete flag.
15. **Audit-complete ≠ quality.** Completion never grades learning, mastery, readiness, or product success.
16. **Audit-complete ≠ independent governance.** Completion never finishes programme or release governance as constitutional law.
17. **Explain the stop.** Students, developers, and auditors should hear when close must be refused.
18. **No emergency exemption.** Deadlines and demos never mint completion power or amendment power.
19. **Repeatability survives close.** Close must not invent contradictory dispositions from identical published inputs.
20. **Provenance survives close.** Close must not erase, forge, truncate, or invent required lineage.

## 3. What Audit Completion May Do (Lawful)

| Lawful action | Constitutional meaning |
|---------------|------------------------|
| **Confirm audit completion** | Affirm CAAC-01…CAAC-05 when published criteria hold for a lawful audit concern |
| **Confirm publication** | Cite that required valid constitutional audit records were published under CAAL-06 / CAA-05 — without rewriting dispositions or inventing records |
| **Confirm formal closure** | Cite that the concern was formally closed under CAAL-07 with boundary honesty |
| **Confirm provenance preservation** | Cite that reconstructable provenance was preserved under CAAL-04 / CAA-03 |
| **Confirm integrity validation** | Cite that required integrity validations completed under CAAL-05 / CAA-04 |
| **Preserve accountability** | Leave reconstructable constitutional records of decisions audited, stages, records, validations, and boundaries |
| **Refuse unlawful close** | Prefer honest not-yet / refuse / defer / escalate over improvisation |
| **Compose required stages and components** | Confirm fulfilment across published CAAL / CAA conjunctions without collapsing failed duties |
| **Remain implementation-neutral** | Honour completion law across lawful equivalent stacks |
| **Preserve repeatability** | Ensure same published inputs do not yield contradictory completion stories |
| **Explain completion** | Answer decisions audited / provenance / integrity / records / boundaries without rewriting meaning |
| **Propose corpus amendments upstream** | Recommend (not enact) amendments when law itself must change before auditing can complete |

These actions **confirm audit-production fulfilment**. They do **not** publish a new Constitution, invent coach warrants, mint tips, author ownership maps, invent CAA / CAAL / CAAC types, author runtime behaviour, execute rules, produce decisions, reinterpret EIP, amend constitutional law, create constitutional authority, finish governance as constitutional law, or judge educational quality.

## 4. What Audit Completion Must NEVER Do

| Forbidden action | Why | Lawful alternative |
|------------------|-----|--------------------|
| **Execute constitutional rules** | WS2 owns execution | Cite completed decisions as inputs; never re-run rules under completion pretext |
| **Produce constitutional decisions** | WS3 owns decision production | Cite completed decisions as audited subjects; never remake or rewrite them |
| **Reinterpret constitutional meaning** | Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–IX own meaning | Leave meaning unchanged; narrate fulfilment against published meaning only |
| **Reinterpret Educational Interpretation Principles** | EIP corpora own interpretation law | Reference EIP as published; refuse soft-replacement in completion prose |
| **Amend constitutional specifications** | Only corpus governance may amend meaning | Leave specs unchanged; propose amendment upstream or refuse incomplete audit |
| **Create constitutional authority** | Published corpora remain the sole standard | Refuse “audit complete therefore we are the law” theatre |
| **Replace constitutional authority** | Authority stays upstream after close | Keep completion subordinate; never freeze a stack as the next constitution |
| **Invent unpublished stages / components / criteria** | Catalogues are closed | Use CAAL / CAA / CAAC only; refuse custom completion law |
| **Erase / forge / invent provenance** | Accountability depends on honest lineage | Preserve reconstructable provenance or refuse close |
| **Publish invalid audit records to force close** | Integrity precedes publication | Refuse-publish / refuse-close when integrity fails |
| **Imply educational success** | Learning outcomes are not audit fulfilment | Separate educational speech from completion speech |
| **Imply independent governance finish** | Programme / release governance is not CAAC | Keep governance horizons distinct |
| **Canonise a stack / vendor / topology** | Implementation neutrality is invariant | Remain technology-independent |
| **Treat CI greens / tickets / badges as fulfilment** | Theatre is not law | Require CAAC-01…CAAC-05 with reconstructable records |
| **Skip outstanding lifecycle duties** | Completion theatre is harm | Prefer not-yet / refuse-close |
| **Collapse CDAC / CEEC / CECC into CAAC** | Sibling horizons remain distinct | Compose inputs; do not conflate fulfilment |

## 5. Boundary Tests

Before affirming completion, every material concern must pass:

1. **Lifecycle test** — Were required CAAL stages completed (or lawfully stopped) without inventing stages?
2. **Record test** — Were required constitutional audit records constructed under published CAA components?
3. **Provenance test** — Was reconstructable provenance preserved without erasure, forgery, truncation, or invention?
4. **Integrity / publication test** — Were integrity validations completed and required valid records published (or refuse-publish recorded)?
5. **Closure test** — Was formal closure honest under CAAL-07 without elevating close into authority?
6. **Non-execution test** — Does the judgement refuse implying constitutional rule execution?
7. **Non-decision test** — Does the judgement refuse implying constitutional decision production?
8. **EIP / amendment / authority test** — Does the judgement refuse EIP reinterpretation, specification amendment, and authority creation?
9. **Neutrality / repeatability test** — Would the same published inputs yield the same disposition without stack canonisation?
10. **Explainability test** — Can CAACQ-01…CAACQ-05 be answered without rewriting meaning?

Failure of any test ⇒ **not yet** or **refuse close**.

---

## 6. Closing Statement

> **Audit completion may confirm that constitutional auditing has lawfully completed — including publication and formal closure.  
> It must never execute constitutional rules, produce constitutional decisions, reinterpret constitutional meaning, or amend constitutional specifications.  
> The moment completion becomes execution, decision production, EIP replacement, legislation, or authority creation, it has left constitutional education.**
