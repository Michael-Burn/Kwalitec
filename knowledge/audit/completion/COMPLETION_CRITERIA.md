## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor needs bright conditions for saying “this constitutional audit work is finished” — and equally bright refusals when it is not. After WS4 / MS001 has named how completed decisions become constitutional audit records and WS4 / MS002 has named how audit activities are organised, Programme X Workstream 4 still needs one fulfilment checklist:

> **What must be true before constitutional speech may affirm that the required audit lifecycle has concluded, audit records have been constructed and validated, provenance has been preserved, audit records have been published, and the audit process has been formally closed?**

These criteria answer that question. They are **normative constitutional conditions**, not database predicates, CI job statuses, ticket states, process exit codes, or API status codes.

All material affirmed completions require **CAAC-01 through CAAC-05** to hold. Failure of any criterion ⇒ **not yet** or **refuse close**. ## 3. Criterion Definitions

### CAAC-01 — Audit lifecycle completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every lifecycle stage required by the concern’s published WS4 / MS002 scope has been executed — or that a published refuse / defer / escalate disposition exhausted remaining *audit-production* duties for that stage set with honest progression through recording, publication (or refuse-publish), and closure of what occurred — so audit speech may treat the lifecycle as completed for the concern.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Identifiable audit concern opened under WS4 / MS001–MS002.
- Named completed-constitutional-decision identities (WS3 / MS003) for the concern.
- Required CAAL-01…CAAL-07 stage identities for the concern scope.
- Stage traces / disposition records for each required stage (including lawful early stops).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed stage-fulfilment record naming which required stages completed.
- Or a lawful stop record naming which stages were refused / deferred / escalated and why remaining audit-production duties are exhausted.

**Completion conditions.**

- Required stages map to published CAAL-01…CAAL-07 only.
- Mandatory initiation, decision intake, construction, provenance recording, integrity validation, publication, and closure obligations for the concern are not silently omitted when complete audit production is claimed.
- Lawful early stops still leave honest stage outcomes; silent disappearance of a concern is a defect.
- Refusal of “pipeline skipped stages” or “we know it ran” without stage identity as completion.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That stage completion executes constitutional rules or re-authors completed execution outcomes.
- That stage completion produces or rewrites constitutional decisions.
- That stage completion reinterprets Educational Interpretation Principles.
- That stage completion amends constitutional specifications or creates constitutional authority.
- That stage completion means the learner succeeded or governance finished outside published authority.
- That skipped stages may be invented or implied from CI theatre.
- That CDAC decision completion alone equals CAAC audit completion (CDAC is the decision-input horizon; CAAC is audit-production fulfilment).

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who declares auditing “finished” without confirming each required audit step occurred has not finished lawful care — even if the desk looks tidy.

---

### CAAC-02 — Audit records completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every constitutional audit record required by the concern’s published scope was constructed under published CAA-01…CAA-06 components — especially decision intake, audit construction, and integrity-bound record assembly — without inventing unpublished audit customs, re-executing WS2 rules under an “audit” badge, re-producing WS3 decisions, or treating record construction as constitutional amendment.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Named completed-constitutional-decision identities admitted at CAAL-02.
- Published CAA component identities required for the concern.
- Candidate / constructed constitutional audit record identities under CAAL-03 / CAA-02.
- Boundary confirmations that construction did not invent law, re-execute rules, or re-produce decisions.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed audit-record-construction record naming which constitutional audit records were completed against which completed decisions and CAA components.
- Or a lawful refuse / defer / escalate record naming which required records remain incomplete and why.

**Completion conditions.**

- Audit records cite completed constitutional decisions by identity; invented or incomplete decisions are a hard stop.
- Construction invokes published CAA-01…CAA-06 only — not unpublished customs, tribal knowledge, or “how main audits today.”
- Construction never silently becomes WS2 rule re-execution or WS3 decision re-production.
- Refusal of coverage percentages, lint scores, Twin confidence, or CI greens as substitutes for named audit-record construction.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That constructing audit records executes constitutional (WS2) rules.
- That constructing audit records produces constitutional (WS3) decisions.
- That constructing audit records reinterprets or replaces Educational Interpretation Principles.
- That constructing audit records amends constitutional specifications or transfers constitutional authority.
- That a particular stack, language, or framework is privileged as law.
- That product quality or educational quality was judged.
- That unpublished audit customs may be invented so close can succeed.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who claims “the audit records were completed” without naming which records were constructed against which completed decisions has performed audit theatre, not record construction.

### CAAC-03 — Provenance preserved

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that reconstructable constitutional provenance required by the concern was preserved under CAAL-04 / CAA-03 — binding published audit records to completed decisions, upstream outcomes / authority citations, and published corpora — without inventing lineage theatre, erasing succession history, or treating provenance preservation as constitutional amendment or authority creation.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Constructed / candidate constitutional audit record identities.
- Completed-decision identities and authority references for the concern.
- Provenance records bound under CAAL-04 / CAA-03.
- Continuity expectations under EIP-005 for prior lawful audit history where applicable.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed provenance-preservation record naming which lineage identities were preserved with which audit records.
- Or a lawful refuse / defer / escalate record naming which required provenance remains missing, truncated, forged, or invented.

**Completion conditions.**

- Provenance cites reconstructable constitutional identities — not free-form log theatre or “we remember the source.”
- Provenance binds audit records to named completed decisions and published corpora.
- Erasure, forgery, truncation, or invention of required provenance is a hard stop.
- Refusal of graph aesthetics, ticket graphs, or CI annotations as substitutes for constitutional provenance.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That preserving provenance executes constitutional rules or produces constitutional decisions.
- That preserving provenance reinterprets Educational Interpretation Principles.
- That preserving provenance amends constitutional specifications or creates constitutional authority.
- That invented or incomplete provenance may be narrated as lawful so close can succeed.
- That Programme IX judgements were re-run or re-determined under provenance speech.
- That stack / vendor identity is constitutional provenance law.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who celebrates “provenance done” after dropping which decision was audited has destroyed the only trail that makes the filing honest.

### CAAC-04 — Audit publication completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every valid constitutional audit record required by the concern was published under CAAL-06 / CAA-05 (with CAA-06 state honesty where applicable) — after required integrity validation under CAAL-05 / CAA-04 — without publishing invalid records, inventing publication theatre, or treating publication as rule execution, decision production, EIP reinterpretation, amendment, or authority creation.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Integrity dispositions under CAAL-05 / CAA-04 for candidate records.
- Validated constitutional audit record identities eligible for publication.
- Publication records / refuse-publish dispositions under CAAL-06 / CAA-05.
- Boundary confirmations that publication did not invent law or elevate records into authority.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed publication record naming which constitutional audit records were published (or which were refused publication and why).
- Reconstructable publication identity usable for accountability and explainability.

**Completion conditions.**

- Publication follows required integrity validation; unpublished invalid records must not be narrated as published.
- Published identities are reconstructable constitutional audit records — not ticket closures or CI greens.
- Refuse-publish is a first-class lawful outcome when integrity fails or boundaries would be breached.
- Refusal of “published somewhere” without record identity as completion.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That publication executes constitutional rules or produces constitutional decisions.
- That publication reinterprets Educational Interpretation Principles.
- That publication amends constitutional specifications or creates constitutional authority.
- That publication freezes a stack, vendor, or topology as permanent constitutional law.
- That educational success or independent governance was achieved by publication.
- That invalid records may be published to force completion.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who claims “audit published” without naming which validated records were published — or who publishes invalid filings to look finished — has abandoned accountability.

---

### CAAC-05 — Audit formally closed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that the audit concern was formally closed under CAAL-07 with boundary honesty — exhausting remaining *audit-production* duties for what occurred, preserving accountability continuity, and refusing to elevate closure into rule execution, decision production, EIP reinterpretation, specification amendment, or creation of constitutional authority.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Affirmed or refused dispositions for CAAC-01…CAAC-04 (or lawful early-stop records that exhaust remaining duties).
- Closure records under CAAL-07.
- Boundary confirmations that forbidden overclaims did not occur.
- Explainability readiness for CAACQ-01…CAACQ-05.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed formal-closure record for the concern.
- Or a refuse-close record naming which duties or boundaries remain outstanding.

**Completion conditions.**

- Closure cites reconstructable lifecycle, record, provenance, integrity, and publication outcomes for the concern.
- Closure never silently invents missing CAAC-01…CAAC-04 fulfilment.
- Closure preserves accountability and refuses stack canonisation as close law.
- Refuse-close is preferred over improvisation when duties remain outstanding or boundaries would be breached.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That formal closure executes constitutional rules or produces constitutional decisions.
- That formal closure reinterprets Educational Interpretation Principles.
- That formal closure amends constitutional specifications or creates constitutional authority.
- That formal closure means the learner succeeded or governance finished outside published authority.
- That formal closure freezes an implementation as permanently lawful.
- That pipeline exit, elapsed time, or badge glow equals formal closure.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who stamps “closed” while outstanding audit duties remain — or who treats the closed folder as the new constitution — has finished the filing theatre, not the care.

## 4. Criterion Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CAAC-01 fails | Refuse close — lifecycle not fulfilled |
| CAAC-02 fails | Refuse close — records not completed |
| CAAC-03 fails | Refuse close — provenance not preserved |
| CAAC-04 fails | Refuse close — publication not completed |
| CAAC-05 fails | Refuse close — formal closure not honest |
| Optional CAAC-06…CAAC-12 fail while CAAC-01…CAAC-05 hold | Affirmation may proceed only with explicit honesty notes; material honesty failures that would invent overclaims become refuse-close |
| Any criterion conflicts with rule execution / decision production / EIP reinterpretation / amendment / authority creation | Boundary honesty wins — refuse the overclaim |

All five primary criteria are required for material affirmed completion. Optional honesty checks strengthen refusal discipline; they do not create a sixth primary criterion outside Programme X amendment.

---

## 5. Closing Statement

> **Constitutional auditing is complete only when CAAC-01…CAAC-05 hold.  
> Anything less is unfinished audit production — and anything that satisfies those criteria by executing rules, remaking decisions, reinterpreting EIP, amending law, or creating authority is not completion at all.**
