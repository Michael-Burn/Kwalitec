## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which constitutional decision was audited* — which provenance was preserved, which integrity validations were completed, which audit records were published, and which limits remained intact — is not closing honestly; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–IX have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, runtime, and governance speech speakable, after WS1–WS3 have made context, execution, and decision speech speakable, and after WS4 / MS001–MS002 have made audit architecture and lifecycle speech speakable, Programme X still needs one audit-completion-speech answer:

> **“Which constitutional decision was audited, which provenance was preserved, which integrity validations were completed, which audit records were published, and which constitutional boundaries remained intact?”**

That answer must cover every material affirmed (or refused) completion without inventing educational success, executing constitutional rules, remaking decisions, reinterpreting EIP, amending constitutional law, creating constitutional authority, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, freezing a stack as law, or modifying constitutional law.

## 2. What Audit Completion Explainability Is

**Constitutional Audit Completion Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **fulfilment (or lawful refusal of fulfilment) of the required constitutional audit lifecycle** — not to invent a second tutor voice from CI complete badges, and not to grade the student, remake decisions, amend constitutional law, freeze a stack, or create constitutional authority.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Constitutional decision audited** | Named completed constitutional decision(s) consumed as audit subjects | Which constitutional decision was audited? |
| **Provenance preserved** | Named reconstructable lineage identities bound to those decisions and records | Which provenance was preserved? |
| **Integrity validations completed** | Named integrity dispositions under CAAL-05 / CAA-04 | Which integrity validations were completed? |
| **Audit records published** | Named reconstructable published constitutional audit records | Which audit records were published? |
| **Boundaries intact** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden overclaims / executions / decisions / amendments / authority-creation did not occur | Which constitutional boundaries remained intact? |

Audit Completion Explainability is:

- **fulfilment-centred** — it narrates why CAAC criteria hold (or why close is refused);
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CAA / CAAL / CAAC identities where applicable;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published specifications, completed decisions, and named audit records;
- **non-executive** — explanation never becomes a back door to re-execute constitutional rules;
- **non-decisive** — explanation never remakes or silently replaces constitutional decisions;
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to amend corpora;
- **non-authority-creating** — explanation never narrates completion as creation of constitutional authority;
- **EIP-faithful** — explanation never narrates completion as EIP reinterpretation or replacement;
- **quality-honest** — complete is never narrated as mastery, readiness, or educational quality;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence / validation speech, WS1–WS3 speech, WS4 / MS001 audit speech, or WS4 / MS002 lifecycle speech;
- **accountability-aligned** — explanation components match CAAC reconstruction needs;
- **authority-honest** — explanations must not paper over authority inference or stack canonisation.

Audit Completion Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–IX / WS1 / WS2 / WS3 / WS4 meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a constitutional amendment instrument or authority-creation warrant;
- a silent decision-replacement or rule-re-execution mechanism;
- a governance-finish report as constitutional law;
- a substitute for WS4 / MS001 audit explainability or WS4 / MS002 lifecycle explainability (compose with; do not replace);
- a substitute for WS3 / MS003 decision completion explainability, WS2 / MS003 execution completion explainability, or WS1 / MS003 context completion explainability (compose with; do not collapse);
- a rendering system, UI template, CI badge, audit visualiser, or analytics dashboard;
- a claim that clear completion speech guarantees learning, a pass, conformity, constitutional amendment, or authority creation;
- a licence to present coverage, lint scores, ticket graphs, or pipeline greens as constitutional proof or educational quality.

## 3. Mandatory Explanation Questions

Every material completion judgement (affirm or refuse close) should answer all five questions:

### CAACQ-01 — Which constitutional decision was audited?

Name the primary completed constitutional decision identities (WS3 / MS003 CDAC-complete decisions) consumed as audit subjects under the concern — or name which required completed decisions remain unavailable when refusing close. Never narrate invented, incomplete, or re-produced decisions as lawful audit subjects. “A decision happened somewhere” is not a constitutional decision identity.

### CAACQ-02 — Which provenance was preserved?

Name the reconstructable provenance identities bound under CAAL-04 / CAA-03 — including completed-decision lineage, authority citations, and published corpus references required for the concern — or name which required provenance remains missing, truncated, forged, or invented when refusing close. Never narrate free-form logs or invented lineage as constitutional provenance.

### CAACQ-03 — Which integrity validations were completed?

Name the integrity dispositions under CAAL-05 / CAA-04 for candidate audit records — valid / invalid / refuse — including which integrity expectations were applied — or name which required integrity validations remain outstanding when refusing close. Never narrate CI greens, lint scores, or Twin confidence as integrity validation.

### CAACQ-04 — Which audit records were published?

Name the published constitutional audit record identities under CAAL-06 / CAA-05 (or refuse-publish dispositions with reasons) — or name which required publications remain incomplete when refusing close. Never narrate ticket closures, badge glows, or pipeline exits as published constitutional audit records.

### CAACQ-05 — Which constitutional boundaries remained intact?

Name the boundary confirmations that close did **not** execute constitutional rules, produce constitutional decisions, reinterpret Educational Interpretation Principles, amend constitutional specifications, create constitutional authority, freeze a stack as law, or invent educational success / independent governance — or name which boundary would be breached if close were affirmed. Boundary silence is a defect for material completion speech.

## 4. Audience Projections

| Audience | Vocabulary posture | Same truth requirement |
|----------|--------------------|------------------------|
| **Student-facing** | Plain language: what was filed, what trail was kept, what was published, what limits still apply | Must not imply re-marking, syllabus rewrite, or mastery from “audit complete” |
| **Developer-facing** | Catalogue IDs (CAA / CAAL / CAAC), completed-decision identities, record identities, stage traces | Must not treat stack choices as constitutional law |
| **Auditor-facing** | Reconstructable references to decisions, provenance, integrity dispositions, publications, closures, and boundary confirmations | Must enable independent reconstruction without product theatre |

Vocabulary may differ. Constitutional facts must not.

---

## 5. Explainability Defects

The following are constitutionally defective completion explanations:

- “Audit complete” with no named completed decision.
- “Provenance preserved” with no reconstructable lineage identity.
- “Integrity validated” because CI was green.
- “Published” because a ticket closed or a badge glowed.
- Narrating completion as rule re-execution, decision remaking, EIP reinterpretation, constitutional amendment, or authority creation.
- Collapsing decision completion, execution completion, or context completion into audit completion speech.
- Presenting stack / vendor / topology preference as the reason close was lawful.
- Refusing to answer any of CAACQ-01…CAACQ-05 for a material affirmed close.
