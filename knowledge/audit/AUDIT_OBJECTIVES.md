## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “audit badge green” or “pipeline finished.” The tutor optimises **lawful audit honesty**: only when completed constitutional decisions are recorded into constitutionally valid audit records that preserve accountability, traceability, provenance, repeatability, and fidelity to published authority may constitutional audit recording be said to have occurred.

These objectives bind every Constitutional Audit Architecture run and every successor machinery that claims to produce constitutional audit records from completed constitutional decisions.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### CAAO-01 — Preserve accountability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material audit run produces a **constitutionally accountable audit record** — a reconstructable record that (a) consumes identifiable completed constitutional decisions, (b) attributes recorded activities to named decision identities and governing authority, (c) survives audit integrity validation (CAA-04), and (d) preserves closed CAA-01…CAA-06 component discipline — so audit theatre never masquerades as lawful constitutional accountability.

**Includes:**

- Binding every material audit record to identifiable completed WS3 constitutional decisions.
- Naming which completed activities were recorded and under which published authority.
- Preferring lawful refuse / invalid disposition over inventing unpublished audit customs so a demo can “audit anyway.”
- Keeping constitutionally accountable audit recording distinct from constitutional rule execution, decision re-production, EIP reinterpretation, specification amendment, educational quality, mastery, readiness, or independent governance outside constitutional authority.

**Excludes:**

- Treating CI greens, ticket fields, Twin estimates, Adaptive scores, or UI ticks as constitutionally accountable audit records.
- Soft-selecting convenient decisions and omitting inconvenient completed decisions.
- Publishing unvalidated constructions as valid constitutional audit records.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for constitutional accountability.
- Declaring accountability “close enough” without documenting which completed decisions were recorded.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software pretends a mark was filed without naming which completed decision was recorded or who owned the standard. Accountability is care; audit theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CAAO-02 — Preserve traceability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material constitutional audit leaves **reconstructable links** from completed constitutional decisions through provenance to published audit records — so auditors and successor machinery can follow the trail without tribal knowledge or ambient log archaeology.

**Tutor rationale.** An IFoA examiner who cannot follow how yesterday’s mark reached today’s file has no defensible process.

**Manifestations:**

- Audit records retain enough identity of completed decisions, provenance links, integrity results, and state transitions to reconstruct the trail.
- Material gaps that force “ask whoever ran it” are defects.
- Traceability remains distinct from Programme IX CT lineage law: CAA preserves *audit trail of completed decisions*; it never redefines CT meaning.

**Excludes:**

- Relying on ephemeral logs, operator memory, or stack-local caches as the sole reconstructability path.
- Treating one-shot demo theatre as traceable constitutional audit recording.
- Confusing operational log shipping of Runtime A with constitutional traceability of audit records.
- Soft-inventing missing links to force a continuous trail.

---

### CAAO-03 — Preserve provenance

**Definition.** Ensure that constitutional audit recording preserves **constitutional provenance** — reconstructable lineage binding each audit record to completed decisions, upstream outcome / authority citations already named therein, and published corpora — so provenance theatre never replaces lawful lineage.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who invents a pedigree for a mark that was never completed has abandoned educational integrity.

**Manifestations:**

- Provenance recorded under CAA-03 cites identifiable completed decision identities and authority references already available through those decisions and published corpora.
- Provenance remains append-faithful: it records what was consumable; it never invents upstream completion.
- Implementation neutrality is preserved: no stack becomes provenance law by proximity to a published audit record.

**Excludes:**

- Inventing unpublished provenance chains or “implied house lineage.”
- Soft-amending upstream decisions or outcomes to manufacture convenient provenance.
- Treating product analytics events or Twin narratives as constitutional provenance without published identity.
- Claiming provenance while omitting reconstructable decision or authority identities.

---

### CAAO-04 — Preserve repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that constitutional audit recording is **reproducible** from reconstructable completed decisions, provenance inputs, authority citations, and component traces — so auditors and successor machinery can re-run or re-narrate the same audit record without tribal knowledge.

**Tutor rationale.** An IFoA examiner who cannot reproduce a filing from the same completed mark sheet and published filing criteria has no defensible process.

**Manifestations:**

- Audit records retain enough identity of decisions, provenance, integrity results, and state transitions to reconstruct the record.
- Repeat runs against the same reconstructable inputs produce the same audit disposition (or the same honest refuse / invalid disposition) and the same explainability answers.
- Incomplete records that force “ask whoever ran it” are defects.

**Excludes:**

- Relying on ephemeral logs, operator memory, or stack-local caches as the sole reconstructability path.
- Treating one-shot demo theatre as repeatable constitutional audit recording.
- Confusing operational retry of Runtime A with constitutional repeatability of audit records.
- Allowing race-dependent or ambient-only recording to alter material audit content without published warrant.

---

### CAAO-05 — Preserve constitutional fidelity

**Definition.** Ensure that audit recording remains **faithful to published constitutional authority** — completed decisions are recorded exactly as published; EIP is not reinterpreted; specifications are not amended; constitutional authority is not replaced or created; decisions are not re-produced; governance outside constitutional authority is not invented — so Audit Architecture never becomes a second constitution.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who changes the syllabus mid-filing to justify today’s preferred record has abandoned the profession.

**Manifestations:**

- Decisions cited under CAA-01 match published completed-decision identities; no silent soft-amendment or re-disposition.
- EIP remains referenced as published under authority maps — never rewritten in audit notes.
- Boundaries in [`AUDIT_BOUNDARIES.md`](AUDIT_BOUNDARIES.md) remain intact for every material run.
- Implementation neutrality is preserved: no stack becomes law by proximity to a published audit record.
- Explainability questions in [`AUDIT_EXPLAINABILITY.md`](AUDIT_EXPLAINABILITY.md) remain honestly answerable without fabricating intactness.

**Excludes:**

- Soft-amending completed decisions or provenance to force a preferred audit record.
- Reinterpreting EIP principles under “audit clarification” labels.
- Elevating Audit Architecture machinery, Runtime A, Twin, Adaptive, or CI into constitutional authority.
- Re-producing constitutional decisions or executing constitutional rules under an audit badge.
- Creating constitutional authority by declaring an audit record to be law.

All five objectives bind together. None may be waived for product urgency.
