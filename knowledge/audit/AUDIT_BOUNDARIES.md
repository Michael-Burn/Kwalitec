## 1. Purpose

An Audit Architecture that silently becomes a second constitution — re-executing rules under the label “audit,” re-producing decisions under “record construction,” reinterpreting EIP in “notes,” amending specifications so a preferred record appears, or inventing governance outside published authority because a badge was needed — destroys student trust and educational integrity.

This document draws a bright line between **lawful constitutional audit recording** (Programme X / WS4 / MS001) and **constitutional execution / decision production / legislation / independent interpretation / independent governance** (Constitution / EIP / Programmes VI–IX / WS2 / WS3 / permitted writers).

---

## 2. Boundary Principles

1. **Record, do not execute.** The Audit Architecture records completed activities; it does not perform constitutional rule execution.
2. **Record, do not decide.** The Audit Architecture records completed decisions; it does not produce constitutional decisions.
3. **Completed decisions only.** Incomplete or unpublished decision production is a hard stop.
4. **Published components only.** Unpublished CAA customs are unlawful.
5. **Exactly as published.** Successful audit recording never rewrites specification meaning.
6. **Authority first.** Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard.
7. **EIP intact.** Educational Interpretation Principles are never reinterpreted or replaced by audit prose.
8. **Implementation independence.** No stack, vendor, or reference implementation becomes law by proximity.
9. **Audit records ≠ legislation.** Published constitutional audit records never amend constitutional specifications.
10. **Audit records ≠ decisions.** Published constitutional audit records never become substitute constitutional decisions.
11. **No rule execution.** Recording audit information is not WS2 constitutional rule execution.
12. **No meaning reinterpretation.** Audit prose never redefines Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–IX meaning.
13. **No authority substitution.** A published audit record never replaces the corpus for the next act.
14. **No authority creation.** Audit recording never creates constitutional authority.
15. **No bypass.** Product urgency never waives required audit components or completed-decision prerequisites.
16. **Repeatability.** Operator-dependent or ambient-only audit recording that cannot be reconstructed is a defect.
17. **Explain the stop.** Students, developers, and auditors should hear when audit recording must refuse.
18. **No emergency exemption.** Deadlines and demos never mint constitutional amendment or re-decision power via the Audit Architecture.
19. **Integrity before publish.** Invalid constructions must not be published as constitutionally valid.
20. **Upstream honesty.** Constitutional decisions remain decisions; audit recording never rewrites them into new law or new dispositions.

| **Manage audit state** | Record speakable Audit Architecture progress via CAA-06 |
| **Refuse incomplete audit recording** | Prefer honest refuse / invalid over improvisation |
| **Cite constitutional authority** | Name governing published corpora without replacing them |
| **Remain repeatable** | Same completed decisions + same provenance inputs ⇒ same audit disposition |
| **Remain implementation-neutral** | Honour audit law across lawful equivalent stacks |
| **Explain audits** | Answer decisions recorded / provenance / records / boundaries without rewriting meaning |
| **Compose required components** | Bind published CAA conjunctions without collapsing missing components |
| **Propose corpus amendments upstream** | Recommend (not enact) amendments when law itself must change before a lawful audit can be recorded |

## 4. What the Audit Architecture Must Never Do (Unlawful)

| Unlawful action | Why it is forbidden |
|-----------------|---------------------|
| **Execute constitutional rules** | Rule execution belongs to WS2 Constitutional Execution Engine; audit recording consumes completed decisions |
| **Produce constitutional decisions** | Decision production belongs to WS3 Constitutional Decision Architecture; audit recording consumes completed decisions |
| **Reinterpret constitutional meaning** | Meaning remains owned by Constitution / EIP / Programmes VI–IX |
| **Reinterpret Educational Interpretation Principles** | EIP is referenced as published; audit notes never author new interpretation |
| **Amend constitutional specifications** | Audit records never soft-amend published law |
| **Modify constitutional specifications** | Clarifying notes must not rewrite published text |
| **Replace constitutional authority** | Audit machinery never becomes the standard for the next act |
| **Create constitutional authority** | Audit recording never mints new constitutional ownership |
| **Determine constitutional decisions** | Dispositions remain WS3 products; CAA never re-disposes |

## 6. Hard Stops

Audit recording **must stop** (refuse / invalid) when any of the following hold:

1. Required constitutional decisions are incomplete, unpublished, or non-reconstructable.
2. Required provenance cannot be recorded without inventing unpublished lineage.
3. Applying the only available “custom” would require executing constitutional rules, producing constitutional decisions, reinterpreting EIP, or amending specifications.
4. The candidate audit record would replace or create constitutional authority, or determine constitutional decisions.
5. Integrity validation (CAA-04) fails and no lawful repair exists without violating boundaries.
6. Required CAA components cannot be applied without inventing unpublished component identities.
7. Repeatability cannot be preserved for the material concern.
8. Explainability questions cannot be answered honestly without fabricating traces.

Product urgency, demo schedules, CI pressure, and founder preference never waive these hard stops.
