## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *what was filed after the script was marked* — which completed decision was recorded, which provenance was preserved, which audit record resulted, and which hard stops remained intact — is not coaching; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–IX have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, runtime, conformance, verification, compliance, certification, and evolution speech speakable, after Workstream 1 has made preparation speech speakable, after Workstream 2 has made execution speech speakable, and after Workstream 3 has made decision speech speakable, Programme X still needs one audit-speech answer:

> **“Which constitutional decision was recorded, which provenance was preserved, which audit record was produced, and which constitutional boundaries remained intact?”**

That answer must cover every material constitutional audit without inventing educational meaning, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, freezing a stack as law, inventing unpublished CAA components, re-executing constitutional rules, re-producing constitutional decisions, reinterpreting EIP, or presenting audit publication as educational quality or independent governance outside constitutional authority.

## 2. What Audit Explainability Is

**Constitutional Audit Architecture Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **lawful recording of completed constitutional decisions into constitutional audit records under published CAA components** — not to invent a second tutor voice from ambient product logs, and not to grade the student, re-decide, or mint independent governance outside constitutional authority.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Constitutional decision recorded** | Named completed WS3 decision identities admitted under CAA-01 and bound into the record | Which constitutional decision was recorded? |
| **Provenance preserved** | Reconstructable lineage recorded under CAA-03 | Which provenance was preserved? |
| **Audit record produced** | Reconstructable record constructed / integrity-validated / published under CAA-02…CAA-05 | Which audit record was produced? |
| **Boundaries remaining intact** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden execution / decision production / reinterpretation / amendment / substitution / authority creation did not occur | Which constitutional boundaries remained intact? |

Audit Explainability is:

- **audit-centred** — it narrates how completed decisions became audit records, not how rules were executed or decisions were produced upstream;
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CAA identities;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published specifications, completed decisions, and authority;
- **non-executive** — explanation never pretends that auditing equals re-running WS2 rule execution;
- **non-decisive** — explanation never pretends that auditing equals re-running WS3 decision production;
- **non-interpretive** — explanation never smuggles EIP reinterpretation into “audit notes”;
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to amend corpora;
- **non-authority-creating** — explanation never presents audit publication as creation of constitutional authority;
- **quality-honest** — audit publication is never narrated as mastery, readiness, or educational quality;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence speech, Programme IX judgement speech, WS1 preparation speech, WS2 execution speech, or WS3 decision speech;
- **repeatability-honest** — explanations must not paper over ambient-only or anonymous audit recording.

Audit Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–IX / WS1 / WS2 / WS3 meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a substitute for Programme VIII runtime explainability or Programme IX judgement explainability corpora;
- a substitute for WS1 CECXEQ / CECREQ / CECCQ preparation speech;
- a substitute for WS2 CEEEQ / lifecycle / completion explainability speech;
- a substitute for WS3 CDAEQ / lifecycle / completion explainability speech;
- a rendering system, UI template, CI badge, log shipper, or analytics dashboard;
- a claim that clear audit speech guarantees learning, a pass, conformity, compliance, or certification;
- a licence to present pipeline greens, stack traces, Twin narratives, or ambient logs as constitutional proof.

## 3. Mandatory Explanation Questions

Every material constitutional audit should answer all four questions:

### CAAEQ-01 — Which constitutional decision was recorded?

Name the primary completed WS3 constitutional decision identities admitted under CAA-01 (paths, decision-run identity, decision identities, and completion confirmation as applicable). Distinguish *decisions available* from *decisions admitted and recorded*. Incomplete, unpublished, or invented ambient logs are not completed constitutional decisions. Lawful refuse must name which required decisions could not be admitted without invention.

### CAAEQ-02 — Which provenance was preserved?

Name the reconstructable provenance recorded under CAA-03 (completed-decision identities, upstream outcome / decision-rule / authority identities already available through those decisions and published corpora, and binding to the audit record). Distinguish *provenance preserved* from *provenance invented*. Unpublished tribal lineage and “how main works” are not constitutional provenance. Lawful refuse must name which required provenance could not be preserved without invention.

### CAAEQ-03 — Which audit record was produced?

Name the reconstructable constitutional audit record constructed under CAA-02, integrity-validated under CAA-04, and published under CAA-05 (or the honest invalid / refuse disposition). Distinguish *candidate constructed* from *validated and published*. Distinguish *constitutional audit record* from constitutional decisions, Programme IX governance dispositions produced outside this architecture, and educational quality claims. Downstream consumers must not hear a soft upgrade into seals, amendments, or re-decisions.

### CAAEQ-04 — Which constitutional boundaries remained intact?

Explicitly confirm that forbidden acts did not occur — especially: no constitutional rule execution under an audit label; no constitutional decision production under an audit label; no EIP reinterpretation; no specification amendment; no replacement of constitutional authority; no creation of constitutional authority; no unpublished CAA components; no stack freeze. If a boundary would have been violated, the explanation must narrate the lawful refuse / invalid stop rather than a fabricated intactness claim.

## 4. Audience Postures

| Audience | Speech posture | Must still answer |
|----------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Student** | Plain language about what was recorded and why the filing was allowed under published rules — without syllabus-amendment or re-marking theatre | All four CAAEQ questions in accessible form |

| **Auditor** | Reconstructable traces: decisions → provenance → audit record → boundaries | All four CAAEQ questions with enough identity to reproduce the record |

Truth is invariant across audiences. Vocabulary may change; fabrication may not.

---

## 5. Defective Explanations

An explanation is constitutionally defective when it:

- omits any mandatory CAAEQ question for a material audit;
- cites unpublished customs as decisions or provenance;
- presents audit publication as constitutional rule execution;
- presents audit publication as constitutional decision production;
- reinterprets EIP under “clarification” labels;
- soft-amends specifications in explanatory prose;
- upgrades a constitutional audit record into independent governance outside published authority;
- claims educational quality, mastery, or exam readiness from publication alone;
- privileges a stack, vendor, or repository topology as constitutional authority;
- papers over refuse / invalid dispositions with demo-continuity narration;
- cannot be repeated from reconstructable inputs (violates CAAO-04).
