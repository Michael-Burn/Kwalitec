## 1. Purpose

Audit recording without a closed component catalogue invents law by proximity: whichever services, queues, or “ambient” product logs happen to finish become the tutor’s “proof the system audited.”

This catalogue names the only lawful constitutional audit architecture components a run may apply — and binds each component to constitutional purpose, inputs, outputs, and permitted / prohibited responsibilities.

---

## 2. Catalogue Overview

| ID | Component | Constitutional purpose (short) | Primary inputs | Primary outputs |
|----|-----------|--------------------------------|----------------|-----------------|
| **CAA-01** | Decision Intake | Admit only completed, reconstructable constitutional decisions | Published WS3 completed decisions + completion identity | Admitted decision set (or lawful refuse) |
| **CAA-02** | Audit Record Constructor | Assemble a candidate constitutional audit record from admitted decisions | Admitted decisions + authority references | Candidate constitutional audit record |
| **CAA-03** | Provenance Recorder | Preserve reconstructable constitutional provenance for the record | Admitted decisions + upstream lineage identities | Provenance record bound to the candidate |
| **CAA-04** | Integrity Validator | Confirm the candidate audit record is constitutionally intact | Candidate record + provenance + published integrity expectations | Integrity disposition (valid / invalid / refuse) |
| **CAA-05** | Audit Publisher | Publish valid constitutional audit records for accountability and explainability | Validated record + state records | Published constitutional audit record |
| **CAA-06** | Audit State Manager | Maintain speakable audit-state transitions for the run | Component progress signals | Audit-state record (not educational / decision / independent-governance state) |

Material constitutional audit recording must map to one or more of these components as published law requires. Cross-cutting situations may bind multiple CAA components; none may invent a component outside this catalogue.

**Relation to WS1:** CECX / CECR / CECC prepare, resolve, and complete context. CAA never redefines preparation catalogues and never begins against incomplete preparation disguised as decisions.

**Relation to WS2:** CEE / CEEL / CEEC execute rules, organise execution, and complete execution into outcomes. CAA never re-executes constitutional rules and never redefines engine catalogues.

**Relation to WS3:** CDA / CDAL / CDAC produce, organise, and complete constitutional decisions. CAA consumes *completed decisions*; it never re-produces decisions and never redefines decision catalogues.

**Relation to Programme VIII / IX catalogues:** Those catalogues remain defined solely by Programmes VIII / IX. CAA may *record published identities as provenance* under constitutional authority; it does not replace, re-run, or independently invent them.

**Relation to educational quality:** No CAA component judges whether learning was good, whether a tip was wise, or whether the student is ready. Audit ≠ quality. Constitutional audit record ≠ constitutional decision ≠ constitutional amendment.

## 3. CAA-01 — Decision Intake

### Constitutional purpose

Admit only **completed, reconstructable constitutional decisions** as lawful inputs to audit recording — so the Audit Architecture knows *which completed decisions may be consumed* without inventing decisions, completing unfinished decision production, or beginning against incomplete publication.

### Constitutional inputs

- Published WS3 constitutional decisions whose required decision lifecycle has lawfully completed (WS3 / MS003 fulfilment confirmed where required by published law).
- Decision identities, decision-run identity, and reconstructable traces sufficient to bind audit records.
- Published CAAO-01 / CAAO-05 expectations.

### Constitutional outputs

- Admitted decision set (references and reconstructable content as published).
- Explicit exclusion of incomplete, unpublished, or invented decisions.
- Honest refuse when required completed decisions cannot be admitted without invention.

### Permitted responsibilities

- Verify completion identity / publication status of candidate decisions under WS3 completion law.
- Admit reconstructable completed decisions for downstream CAA components.
- Record which decisions were admitted and which were refused.
- Refuse intake when decisions are incomplete, unpublished, or non-reconstructable.

### Prohibited responsibilities

- Invent unpublished decisions or “implied house dispositions.”
- Complete unfinished WS3 decision production under an intake label.
- Re-produce constitutional decisions to manufacture missing inputs.
- Execute constitutional rules to manufacture missing decisions.
- Soft-amend decision content to make audit recording convenient.
- Begin intake against ambient product logs presented as completed constitutional decisions.

## 4. CAA-02 — Audit Record Constructor

### Constitutional purpose

Assemble a **candidate constitutional audit record** from admitted completed decisions and governing authority references — so a speakable audit artefact exists for provenance binding and integrity validation without pretending construction alone confers validity, legislation, decision production, or independent governance.

### Constitutional inputs

- Admitted decision set from CAA-01.
- Named constitutional authority references governing the recorded activities.
- Published CAAO-01 / CAAO-02 / CAAO-05 expectations.

### Constitutional outputs

- Candidate constitutional audit record: reconstructable draft citing admitted decisions and authority.
- Explicit non-claim: construction is not publication of a validated audit record.
- Honest incomplete construction note when required elements cannot be assembled without invention.

### Permitted responsibilities

- Compose candidate audit records from admitted decisions exactly as published audit law warrants.
- Bind the candidate to admitted decision identities and authority references.
- Preserve reconstructable construction traces for provenance, integrity validation, and explainability.
- Refuse construction when admitted decisions are insufficient under published law.

### Prohibited responsibilities

- Amend constitutional specifications in construction prose.
- Reinterpret EIP to invent a preferred candidate record.
- Produce constitutional decisions or re-disposition completed decisions under a construction label.
- Execute constitutional rules as part of construction.
- Publish the candidate as valid without CAA-04 integrity validation.
- Elevate construction machinery into constitutional authority.

## 5. CAA-03 — Provenance Recorder

### Constitutional purpose

Preserve **reconstructable constitutional provenance** for the candidate audit record — so lineage from completed decisions (and authority / upstream identities already named therein) remains speakable without inventing pedigree theatre or soft-amending upstream artefacts.

### Constitutional inputs

- Admitted decision set from CAA-01.
- Candidate audit record from CAA-02 (or construction-in-progress binding as published law requires).
- Upstream lineage identities already reconstructable from completed decisions and published corpora (outcomes, decision rules, authority citations as applicable).
- Published CAAO-02 / CAAO-03 / CAAO-05 expectations.

### Constitutional outputs

- Provenance record bound to the candidate audit record.
- Explicit exclusion of invented or non-reconstructable lineage.
- Honest refuse when required provenance cannot be recorded without invention.

### Permitted responsibilities

- Record reconstructable provenance links from admitted decisions to the candidate audit record.
- Cite authority and upstream identities already available through completed decisions and published corpora.
- Preserve provenance traces for integrity validation and explainability.
- Refuse provenance recording when required lineage is incomplete or non-reconstructable.

### Prohibited responsibilities

- Invent unpublished provenance chains or “implied house lineage.”
- Soft-amend upstream decisions, outcomes, or authority citations to manufacture convenient provenance.
- Reinterpret EIP under provenance “clarification” labels.
- Treat product analytics, Twin narratives, or ambient logs as constitutional provenance without published identity.
- Use provenance recording as a licence to re-produce decisions or execute rules.

## 6. CAA-04 — Integrity Validator

### Constitutional purpose

Confirm that a candidate constitutional audit record is **constitutionally intact** — that it cites completed decisions, preserves provenance, names governing authority, and preserves Audit Architecture boundaries — before any publication as a lawful constitutional audit record.

### Constitutional inputs

- Candidate constitutional audit record from CAA-02.
- Provenance record from CAA-03.
- Admitted decision set.
- Published integrity expectations under CAAO-01 and [`AUDIT_BOUNDARIES.md`](AUDIT_BOUNDARIES.md).
- Published CAAO-01 / CAAO-05 expectations.

### Constitutional outputs

- Integrity disposition: valid / invalid / refuse (with reconstructable reasons).
- Explicit boundary-integrity confirmation or named boundary defect.
- Honest refuse when integrity validation cannot proceed without inventing unpublished criteria.

### Permitted responsibilities

- Check that required decision identities, provenance links, and authority references are present and consistent.
- Confirm the candidate does not claim rule execution, decision production, EIP reinterpretation, specification amendment, authority replacement, or authority creation.
- Record integrity disposition reconstructably.
- Block publication of invalid candidates as constitutionally valid.

### Prohibited responsibilities

- Soft-pass invalid candidates for demo continuity.
- Invent unpublished integrity criteria.
- Re-execute constitutional rules or re-produce constitutional decisions as a substitute for integrity validation.
- Treat CI greens, UI ticks, or Twin narratives as integrity under constitutional law.
- Amend specifications so a candidate becomes “valid.”

## 7. CAA-05 — Audit Publisher

### Constitutional purpose

Publish **validated constitutional audit records** for accountability and explainability — so lawful audit records become available to consumers without upgrading them into constitutional amendments, EIP replacements, re-decisions, stack freezes, or independent governance outside constitutional authority.

### Constitutional inputs

- Validated constitutional audit record (CAA-04 disposition = valid).
- Provenance record from CAA-03.
- Audit-state records from CAA-06 as required for reconstructability.
- Published CAAO-01 / CAAO-02 / CAAO-03 / CAAO-04 / CAAO-05 expectations.

### Constitutional outputs

- Published constitutional audit record (identity, cited decisions, preserved provenance, cited authority, integrity confirmation).
- Explicit non-claims: not new law; not a re-decision; not EIP replacement; not stack freeze; not independent governance outside authority; not creation of constitutional authority.
- Honest non-publication when integrity is not valid.

### Permitted responsibilities

- Publish only integrity-validated constitutional audit records.
- Preserve reconstructable publication records for accountability and explainability.
- Make published audit records available to lawful downstream consumers.
- Refuse publication when integrity validation failed or required traces are missing.

### Prohibited responsibilities

- Publish invalid or unvalidated candidates as constitutionally valid.
- Soft-amend published audit content after integrity validation without a new lawful cycle.
- Present publication as constitutional amendment, certification seal, re-decision, or EIP replacement.
- Freeze a preferred stack as law because an audit record was published on it.
- Suppress publication of lawful refuse / invalid dispositions when honesty requires them to be speakable.

## 8. CAA-06 — Audit State Manager

### Constitutional purpose

Maintain **speakable audit-state transitions** for the Audit Architecture run — so progress is reconstructable without inventing educational state, minting independent governance dispositions, re-producing decisions, or treating state machinery as constitutional authority.

### Constitutional inputs

- Progress signals from CAA-01…CAA-05.
- Published audit-state vocabulary for this architecture (intake → construction → provenance → integrity → publication / refuse).
- Published CAAO-01 / CAAO-04 / CAAO-05 expectations.

### Constitutional outputs

- Audit-state record: ordered transitions with timestamps / identities as published law requires.
- Explicit distinction from educational state, from WS3 decision state, and from Programme IX governance dispositions.
- Honest stop-state when the run refuses or invalidates.

### Permitted responsibilities

- Record lawful state transitions corresponding to published CAA component progress.
- Expose state for explainability and audit reconstruction.
- Mark refuse / invalid terminal states when audit recording cannot lawfully continue.
- Keep state vocabulary closed and published.

### Prohibited responsibilities

- Invent educational state (mastery, readiness, continuity mutations) under audit-state labels.
- Mint independent governance dispositions (compliance, certification, verification) as audit state.
- Re-produce constitutional decisions or execute constitutional rules via state machinery.
- Use state transitions to waive required components or completed-decision prerequisites.
- Treat state machinery as constitutional authority or as a licence to amend specifications.
- Hide refuse / invalid states to preserve demo continuity.

## 9. Composition Rules

1. **Closed catalogue.** Only CAA-01…CAA-06 may be applied.
2. **Intake first.** CAA-02…CAA-05 may not lawfully proceed without CAA-01 admission (or lawful refuse).
3. **Provenance before intact publication.** CAA-05 may publish as valid only after CAA-03 provenance binding and CAA-04 valid disposition.
4. **Integrity before publish.** CAA-05 may publish as valid only after CAA-04 valid disposition.
5. **State accompanies the run.** CAA-06 records progress; it never substitutes for missing components.
6. **No parallel invention.** Ambient product workflows are not constitutional components unless mapped to this catalogue.
7. **Cross-binding allowed.** A material concern may require multiple components; it may not invent a seventh component identity.
8. **Refuse is a lawful product.** Incomplete / invalid / refused runs remain constitutionally preferable to improvised audit records.
