## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “explanation badge green” or “narration finished.” The tutor optimises **lawful explanation honesty**: only when completed constitutional audit records are assembled into constitutionally faithful explanations that preserve transparency, fidelity, provenance, repeatability, and constitutional understanding may constitutional explanation be said to have occurred.

These objectives bind every Constitutional Explainability Architecture run and every successor machinery that claims to produce constitutional explanations from completed constitutional audit records.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### CEAO-01 — Preserve transparency

**Definition.** Ensure that every material explanation run produces a **constitutionally transparent explanation** — a reconstructable narration that (a) consumes identifiable completed constitutional audit records, (b) makes the explained constitutional activities speakable without anonymous ambient storytelling, (c) survives explanation fidelity validation (CEA-04), and (d) preserves closed CEA-01…CEA-06 component discipline — so explanation theatre never masquerades as lawful constitutional transparency.

**Includes:**

- Binding every material explanation to identifiable completed WS4 constitutional audit records.
- Naming which completed activities are explained and under which published authority.
- Preferring lawful refuse / invalid disposition over inventing unpublished explanation customs so a demo can “explain anyway.”
- Keeping constitutionally transparent explanation distinct from constitutional rule execution, decision re-production, audit re-recording, EIP reinterpretation, specification amendment, educational quality, mastery, readiness, or independent governance outside constitutional authority.

**Excludes:**

- Treating CI greens, ticket fields, Twin estimates, Adaptive scores, UI copy, or ambient logs as constitutionally transparent explanations.
- Soft-selecting convenient audit records and omitting inconvenient completed audit records.
- Publishing unvalidated constructions as valid constitutional explanations.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for constitutional transparency.
- Declaring transparency “close enough” without documenting which completed audit records were consumed.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software pretends a mark was explained without naming which completed filing was consumed or who owned the standard. Transparency is care; narration theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CEAO-02 — Preserve fidelity

**Definition.** Ensure that explanation remains **faithful to published constitutional authority** — completed audit records are narrated exactly as published; EIP is not reinterpreted; specifications are not amended; constitutional authority is not created; decisions are not re-produced; audits are not re-recorded; governance outside constitutional authority is not invented — so Explainability Architecture never becomes a second constitution.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who changes the syllabus mid-narration to justify today’s preferred story has abandoned the profession.

**Manifestations:**

- Audit records cited under CEA-01 match published completed-audit identities; no silent soft-amendment or re-disposition.
- EIP remains referenced as published under authority maps — never rewritten in explanation notes.
- Boundaries in [`EXPLAINABILITY_BOUNDARIES.md`](EXPLAINABILITY_BOUNDARIES.md) remain intact for every material run.
- Implementation neutrality is preserved: no stack becomes law by proximity to a published explanation.
- Explainability-of-explainability questions in [`EXPLAINABILITY_EXPLAINABILITY.md`](EXPLAINABILITY_EXPLAINABILITY.md) remain honestly answerable without fabricating intactness.

**Excludes:**

- Soft-amending completed audit records, decisions, or provenance to force a preferred explanation.
- Reinterpreting EIP principles under “explanation clarification” labels.
- Elevating Explainability Architecture machinery, Runtime A, Twin, Adaptive, UI, or CI into constitutional authority.
- Re-producing constitutional decisions, executing constitutional rules, or re-recording audits under an explanation badge.
- Creating constitutional authority by declaring an explanation to be law.

---

### CEAO-03 — Preserve provenance

**Definition.** Ensure that constitutional explanation preserves **constitutional provenance** — reconstructable lineage binding each explanation to completed audit records, upstream decision / outcome / authority citations already named therein, and published corpora — so provenance theatre never replaces lawful lineage.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who invents a pedigree for a narration that was never filed has abandoned educational integrity.

**Manifestations:**

- Provenance resolved under CEA-03 cites identifiable completed audit-record identities and authority references already available through those audit records and published corpora.
- Provenance remains append-faithful: it resolves what was consumable; it never invents upstream completion.
- Implementation neutrality is preserved: no stack becomes provenance law by proximity to a published explanation.

**Excludes:**

- Inventing unpublished provenance chains or “implied house lineage.”
- Soft-amending upstream audit records, decisions, or outcomes to manufacture convenient provenance.
- Treating product analytics events, Twin narratives, or UI strings as constitutional provenance without published identity.
- Claiming provenance while omitting reconstructable audit-record or authority identities.

---

### CEAO-04 — Preserve repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that constitutional explanation is **reproducible** from reconstructable completed audit records, provenance inputs, authority citations, and component traces — so auditors and successor machinery can re-run or re-narrate the same explanation without tribal knowledge.

**Tutor rationale.** An IFoA examiner who cannot reproduce a narration from the same completed filing and published explanation criteria has no defensible process.

**Manifestations:**

- Explanations retain enough identity of audit records, activities, provenance, fidelity results, and state transitions to reconstruct the narration.
- Repeat runs against the same reconstructable inputs produce the same explanation disposition (or the same honest refuse / invalid disposition) and the same explainability-of-explainability answers.
- Incomplete records that force “ask whoever narrated it” are defects.

**Excludes:**

- Relying on ephemeral logs, operator memory, or stack-local caches as the sole reconstructability path.
- Treating one-shot demo theatre as repeatable constitutional explanation.
- Confusing operational retry of Runtime A or UI refresh with constitutional repeatability of explanations.
- Allowing race-dependent or ambient-only narration to alter material explanation content without published warrant.

---

### CEAO-05 — Preserve constitutional understanding

**Definition.** Ensure that every material explanation run advances **constitutional understanding** — audiences can reconstruct what completed constitutional activity occurred, under which published authority, with which provenance, without soft-amending meaning — so explanation never substitutes theatrical clarity for lawful comprehension.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who makes a student feel they understand while silently changing what the syllabus meant has not explained; they have deceived.

**Manifestations:**

- Explanations make speakable which audit records were consumed, which activities were explained, which provenance supports the narration, which explanation was produced, and which boundaries remained intact.
- Understanding remains distinct from educational quality, mastery, or readiness claims: CEA explains *what constitutionally occurred*; it never grades the student.
- Audience vocabulary may change (student / developer / auditor); truth and authority citations must not.

**Excludes:**

- Softening or omitting inconvenient completed activities to manufacture comforting understanding.
- Presenting explanation publication as proof of learning, mastery, exam readiness, conformity, compliance, or certification.
- Collapsing WS1 / WS2 / WS3 / WS4 speech into a single “explained” badge that erases catalogue distinctions.
- Declaring understanding complete while any mandatory CEAEQ question remains unanswerable.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If this is weak… | …this fails |
|------------------|-------------|
| CEAO-01 Transparency | Faithful theatre that attributes nothing honestly |
| CEAO-02 Fidelity | Transparent narrations that replace constitutional authority |
| CEAO-03 Provenance | Faithful narrations with invented lineage |
| CEAO-04 Repeatability | Unauditable “transparent” narrations |
| CEAO-05 Understanding | Repeatable narrations that still soft-amend meaning for comfort |

All five objectives bind together. None may be waived for product urgency.

## 5. Non-Goals of These Objectives

These objectives do not:

- authorise constitutional rule execution (WS2);
- authorise constitutional decision production (WS3);
- authorise constitutional audit re-recording (WS4);
- authorise EIP reinterpretation or specification amendment;
- redefine Programme IX governance catalogues;
- redefine EIP-003 educational explainability meaning;
- grade learning, mastery, or exam readiness;
- privilege a particular implementation stack;
- create constitutional authority.
