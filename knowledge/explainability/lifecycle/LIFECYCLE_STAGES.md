## 1. Purpose

Explanation production without a closed stage catalogue invents law by proximity: whichever pipeline step, ticket status, or ambient product habit happens to finish becomes the tutor’s “proof the system explained.”

This catalogue names the only lawful constitutional explainability lifecycle stages — and binds each stage to purpose, inputs, outputs, and permitted / prohibited activities.

---

## 2. Stage Principles

1. **Ordered and skip-disciplined.** Stages may be brief under thin scope; mandatory initiation, audit intake, construction, provenance validation, fidelity validation, publication, and closure obligations may not be silently omitted when published constitutional explanations are claimed.
2. **Completed audit records before construction.** Identifiable completed constitutional audit records are admitted before explanation construction.
3. **Explainability accumulates.** Each stage leaves traceable artefacts for `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.
4. **Refusal is a stage outcome.** Refuse-open, refuse-intake, stop, escalate, invalid, refuse-publish, and refuse-close are first-class conclusions.
5. **Repeatable transitions.** Same published inputs ⇒ same next stage outcomes.
6. **Stages coordinate; they do not legislate.** No stage creates, modifies, or redefines constitutional law.
7. **Stages do not become constitutional authority.** Closure never freezes an implementation as permanently lawful or replaces published corpora.
8. **Stages do not execute constitutional rules.** Constructing explanations is not performing WS2 execution.
9. **Stages do not produce constitutional decisions.** Constructing explanations is not performing WS3 decision production.
10. **Stages do not re-record audits.** Constructing explanations is not performing WS4 audit production.
11. **Stages do not reinterpret EIP.** Educational Interpretation Principles remain as published.
12. **Stages invoke published explainability components only.** CEA-01…CEA-06 may be applied; unpublished explainability customs may not.

## 3. Catalogue Overview

| ID | Stage | Constitutional purpose (short) | Primary inputs | Primary outputs |
|----|-------|--------------------------------|----------------|-----------------|
| **CEAL-01** | Explanation Initiation | Open an explanation concern against a named completed-audit identity and published scope | Completed-audit identity + published scope + optional CEA indication | Initiated explanation record (or refuse-open) |
| **CEAL-02** | Audit Intake | Admit identifiable completed constitutional audit records under CEA-01 | Initiated record + candidate completed audit records | Admitted audit-record set (or refuse-intake) |
| **CEAL-03** | Explanation Construction | Construct a candidate constitutional explanation under CEA-02 | Admitted audit records + authority references | Candidate constitutional explanation |
| **CEAL-04** | Provenance Validation | Validate reconstructable constitutional provenance under CEA-03 | Candidate explanation + lineage identities | Provenance validation bound to the candidate |
| **CEAL-05** | Fidelity Validation | Validate the candidate under CEA-04 | Candidate + provenance + fidelity expectations | Fidelity disposition (valid / invalid / refuse) |
| **CEAL-06** | Explanation Publication | Publish valid constitutional explanations under CEA-05 (with CEA-06 state) | Validated explanation + state / boundary confirmations | Published constitutional explanation (or refuse-publish) |
| **CEAL-07** | Explanation Closure | Close the lifecycle honestly when duties are exhausted | Published artefacts + boundary confirmations | Closed lifecycle (or refuse-close) — never a freeze of constitutional authority |

Material constitutional explanation production claiming lifecycle outcomes must map to this sequence as published law requires. Stages may be brief; none may invent a stage outside this catalogue.

**Relation to CEA / CECX / CECR / CECC / CEE / CEEL / CEEC / CDA / CDAL / CDAC / CAA / CAAL / CAAC / Programme VIII / IX catalogues:** Those catalogues remain defined solely by their owners. CEAL stages *organise when* constitutional explainability activities occur; they do not replace, reclassify, re-execute, invent, or amend those catalogues.

```text
CEAL-01  Explanation Initiation
           ↓
CEAL-02  Audit Intake
           ↓
CEAL-03  Explanation Construction
           ↓
CEAL-04  Provenance Validation
           ↓
CEAL-05  Fidelity Validation
           ↓
CEAL-06  Explanation Publication
           ↓
```

Lawful early stop dispositions (refuse-open / refuse-intake / stop / escalate / invalid) may exhaust remaining *explanation-production* duties while still requiring honest publication (or refuse-publish) and closure — never silent disappearance of the concern.

## 5. Stage Definitions

### CEAL-01 — Explanation Initiation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Open a material constitutional explanation concern by naming the completed constitutional audit record identity under which explanation production will proceed and the published scope of the concern — without inventing law, opening against unpublished customs, treating initiation as a published explanation, executing constitutional rules, producing constitutional decisions, or re-recording audits.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Identifiable completed constitutional audit record identity (WS4 / MS003 fulfilment claimed or to be confirmed at CEAL-02)
- Concern identity (what constitutional explanation question is under organisation)
- Optional published indication of CEA components required for the scope
- Optional identifiable supporting authority / lineage references (not rewritten)

**Constitutional outputs**

- An initiated explanation concern record naming completed-audit identity, scope, and intended CEA component set — **or**
- Lawful refuse-open when standards are unpublished, scope is theatrical, completed-audit identity cannot be established, or initiation would invent law

**Permitted activities**

- Name published completed-audit identity and scope as explanation standards
- Bound the concern scope without amending corpora
- Record intended published CEA components from published catalogues
- Refuse initiation when constitutional preconditions fail
- Preserve initiation identity for later stages

**Prohibited activities**

- Treat “how main works,” tribal knowledge, or stack preference as the standard
- Create or modify constitutional specifications by opening a concern
- Issue published constitutional explanations at initiation
- Execute constitutional rules or produce constitutional decisions at initiation
- Re-record audits at initiation
- Reinterpret EIP at initiation
- Invent unpublished explainability components or lifecycle stages
- Skip initiation when material published explanations will later be claimed
- Create constitutional authority at initiation

**Authority preserved**  
Published corpora remain the sole standard. Initiation organises; it does not legislate.

---

### CEAL-02 — Audit Intake

**Constitutional purpose**  
Admit only **identifiable completed constitutional audit records** for the concern under CEA-01 — so subsequent stages explain against real completed audit records, not anonymous theatre — without inventing, rewriting, or re-recording audits, and without performing WS2 execution or WS3 decision production.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Initiated concern from CEAL-01
- Candidate completed constitutional audit record identities (WS4 / MS003 completion confirmed, with supporting architecture / lifecycle references as applicable)
- Reconstructable audit traces sufficient to bind explanations
- Published CEAO / CEALO expectations for completed-audit honesty

**Constitutional outputs**

- An admitted audit-record set bound to the initiated concern — **or**
- Lawful refuse-intake when audit records are missing, invented, rewritten, incomplete, unpublished, or would require re-recording to “complete”

**Permitted activities**

- Identify and bind named completed constitutional audit records
- Confirm WS4 completion identity where published law requires
- Refuse intake of incomplete, invented, or theatrical audit records
- Preserve audit provenance for later stages and explainability
- Invoke CEA-01 Audit Intake as published by WS5 / MS001

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent, rewrite, or complete unfinished constitutional audit records under an explanation badge
- Re-record constitutional audits to manufacture inputs
- Re-execute constitutional rules or re-produce constitutional decisions to manufacture audit records
- Treat CI greens, Twin estimates, or UI ticks as completed constitutional audit records
- Soft-select convenient audit records and omit inconvenient completed audits without lawful scope record
- Begin explanation construction against anonymous or incomplete audit records
- Treat intake itself as a published constitutional explanation

**Authority preserved**  
WS4 remains the owner of constitutional audit records. Intake admits; it does not audit.

---

### CEAL-03 — Explanation Construction

**Constitutional purpose**  
Construct a **candidate constitutional explanation** from admitted completed audit records under CEA-02 — assembling reconstructable explanation information for provenance and fidelity validation — without becoming new law, EIP replacement, rule re-execution, decision re-production, audit re-recording, or creation of constitutional authority.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Admitted audit-record set from CEAL-02
- Named constitutional authority references already available through completed audit records and published corpora
- Published CEA-02 Explanation Constructor expectations

**Constitutional outputs**

- A candidate constitutional explanation citing admitted audit records and authority — **or**
- Lawful stop / refuse when construction would invent disposition, omit required citations, or violate published construction rules

**Permitted activities**

- Assemble candidate explanations from admitted audit records exactly as published construction rules allow
- Cite completed audit records and constitutional authority
- Record explanation-state progress under CEA-06 as applicable
- Invoke CEA-02 Explanation Constructor as published by WS5 / MS001
- Prefer refuse over inventing a convenient candidate for demo continuity

**Prohibited activities**

- Construct a candidate that amends constitutional specifications
- Construct a candidate that reinterprets or replaces EIP
- Construct a candidate that re-executes constitutional rules
- Construct a candidate that re-produces constitutional decisions
- Construct a candidate that re-records constitutional audits
- Present an unvalidated candidate as a published valid constitutional explanation
- Invent missing admitted audit records to force construction
- Create constitutional authority via construction theatre

**Authority preserved**  
Construction assembles candidates under published law. It does not create authority.

---

### CEAL-04 — Provenance Validation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Validate **reconstructable constitutional provenance** for the candidate explanation under CEA-03 — confirming the explanation binds to completed audit records, upstream decision / outcome / authority citations, and published corpora — without inventing lineage, forging trails, erasing inconvenient history, or treating provenance as new law.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Candidate constitutional explanation from CEAL-03
- Admitted audit-record identities and upstream lineage identities available through completed audit records and published corpora
- Published CEA-03 Provenance Resolver expectations

**Constitutional outputs**

- A provenance validation bound to the candidate explanation — **or**
- Lawful stop / refuse when provenance would require invention, forgery, or soft-amendment of lineage meaning

**Permitted activities**

- Validate reconstructable links from completed audit records through upstream identities to the candidate
- Preserve CT / sibling lineage identities where already established — never invent them
- Invoke CEA-03 Provenance Resolver as published by WS5 / MS001
- Refuse to proceed when required provenance cannot be reconstructed honestly
- Preserve provenance artefacts for fidelity validation and explainability

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent provenance theatre or forge lineage links
- Erase inconvenient completed-audit or authority citations
- Treat provenance validations as constitutional amendments, re-decisions, or re-audits
- Soft-amend CT / sibling lineage meaning under an explanation badge
- Skip provenance validation when a published explanation will later be claimed
- Privilege a stack preference as provenance identity

**Authority preserved**  
Provenance validation confirms published lineage. It does not invent law or create authority.

---

### CEAL-05 — Fidelity Validation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Validate that the candidate constitutional explanation is **constitutionally faithful** under CEA-04 — confirming completed-audit citation, provenance preservation, meaning preservation, authority preservation, and boundary integrity — before any claim of valid publication.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Candidate constitutional explanation from CEAL-03
- Provenance validation from CEAL-04
- Published fidelity expectations (CEA-04 / CEAO / CEALO / boundary corpus)
- Boundary confirmations required by `LIFECYCLE_BOUNDARIES.md`

**Constitutional outputs**

- Fidelity disposition: valid / invalid / refuse — with reconstructable reasons — **or**
- Escalation when validation itself would require inventing law or mutating artefacts

**Permitted activities**

- Confirm candidate cites identifiable completed audit records and preserved provenance
- Confirm EIP was not reinterpreted and specifications were not amended
- Confirm no constitutional rule execution, decision re-production, or audit re-recording occurred under explainability pretence
- Confirm implementation neutrality and authority preservation (no authority creation)
- Invoke CEA-04 Fidelity Validator as published by WS5 / MS001
- Refuse to mark invalid candidates as valid

**Prohibited activities**

- Skip fidelity validation so publication can proceed
- Forge fidelity dispositions
- Treat fidelity success as constitutional amendment, educational quality, or authority creation
- Soft-waive hard boundaries for demo urgency
- Re-execute rules, re-produce decisions, or re-record audits “to make fidelity pass”
- Publish invalid candidates as constitutionally valid

**Authority preserved**  
Fidelity validation confirms faithfulness to published law. It does not rewrite law.

---

### CEAL-06 — Explanation Publication

**Constitutional purpose**  
Publish **constitutionally valid** constitutional explanations under CEA-05 for transparency and understanding — communicating the explanation honestly — without inventing fidelity, hiding failed validation, rewriting boundaries, freezing a stack as law, or creating constitutional authority.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Validated constitutional explanation (valid disposition from CEAL-05)
- Explanation-state records under CEA-06 as applicable
- Boundary confirmations and explainability obligations

**Constitutional outputs**

- Published constitutional explanation artefacts bound to audit records, provenance, fidelity validations, and authority — **or**
- Lawful refuse-publish when fidelity is absent, boundaries are broken, or publication would invent satisfaction

**Permitted activities**

- Publish valid explanations under published honesty rules
- Preserve reconstructable links to audit records, provenance, and fidelity validations
- Record publication identity for closure and explainability
- Invoke CEA-05 Explanation Publisher (and CEA-06 Explanation State Manager) as published by WS5 / MS001
- Refuse publication of invalid or unvalidated candidates

**Prohibited activities**

- Publish invalid / unvalidated constructions as valid
- Present publication as new constitutional law, EIP replacement, or authority creation
- Present publication as constitutional rule execution, decision re-production, or audit re-recording
- Freeze implementations, stacks, vendors, or releases as constitutional law
- Erase prior stage history to simplify the published story
- Determine independent governance outside published constitutional authority via publication theatre

**Authority preserved**  
Publication communicates explanations. It does not become the corpus.

---

### CEAL-07 — Explanation Closure

**Constitutional purpose**  
Close the constitutional explainability lifecycle honestly when remaining *explanation-production* duties for the concern are exhausted — recording closure without pretending incomplete work is done, without executing rules, without producing decisions, without re-recording audits, without reinterpreting EIP, and without creating or replacing constitutional authority.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Published artefacts (or refuse-publish record) from CEAL-06
- Stage history for CEAL-01…CEAL-06
- Boundary confirmations required by `LIFECYCLE_BOUNDARIES.md`
- Explainability readiness under `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md`

**Constitutional outputs**

- Closed lifecycle record with reconstructable stage trail — **or**
- Lawful refuse-close when mandatory duties remain, boundaries are broken, or closure would invent completion

**Permitted activities**

- Affirm that required explainability-lifecycle duties for the concern are exhausted (or honestly refuse-close)
- Preserve full stage / audit-record / provenance / fidelity / publication trail
- Distinguish lawful early stops from abandoned duties
- Hand off published explanations to downstream product / narration consumers without transferring legislative power

**Prohibited activities**

- Close while pretending incomplete intake, construction, provenance validation, fidelity validation, or publication succeeded
- Execute constitutional rules, produce constitutional decisions, re-record audits, reinterpret EIP, or amend specifications under a closed seal
- Create or replace constitutional authority because the lifecycle closed
- Freeze a stack as law because the lifecycle closed
- Narrate closure as mastery, readiness, educational quality, or independent governance outside authority
- Invent an eighth stage of law-making after closure

**Authority preserved**  
Closure ends coordination. Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard for the next act.

## 6. Stage Interaction Rules

1. **No silent skips.** Mandatory duties may be brief; they may not vanish when a published explanation is claimed.
2. **Intake before construction.** CEAL-03 must not begin against refused or anonymous audit records.
3. **Provenance before valid publication.** CEAL-04 must leave reconstructable lineage validation when a published explanation is claimed.
4. **Fidelity before valid publication.** CEAL-06 must not publish as valid without CEAL-05 success.
5. **Refuse is first-class.** Refuse-open / refuse-intake / invalid / refuse-publish / refuse-close are lawful outcomes.
6. **CEA fidelity.** CEAL-02…CEAL-06 invoke only published CEA-01…CEA-06 components.
7. **No execution bleed.** No stage may perform WS2 constitutional rule execution.
8. **No decision bleed.** No stage may perform WS3 constitutional decision production.
9. **No audit bleed.** No stage may perform WS4 constitutional audit re-recording.
10. **No EIP bleed.** No stage may reinterpret or replace Educational Interpretation Principles.
11. **Explainability trail.** Each stage contributes artefacts required by `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.
