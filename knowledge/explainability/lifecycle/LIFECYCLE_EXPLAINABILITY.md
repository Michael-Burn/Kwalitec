## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which completed constitutional audit record* was consumed — which provenance was validated, which explanation was produced, which fidelity validations were performed, and which limits remained intact — is not coaching; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–IX have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, runtime, conformance, verification, compliance, certification, and evolution speech speakable, after Workstream 1 has made preparation speech speakable, after Workstream 2 has made execution speech speakable, after Workstream 3 has made decision speech speakable, after Workstream 4 has made audit speech speakable, and after WS5 / MS001 has made explainability-component speech speakable, Programme X still needs one lifecycle-speech answer:

> **“Which audit record was consumed, which provenance was validated, which explanation was produced, which fidelity validations were performed, and which constitutional boundaries remained intact?”**

That answer must cover every material explainability lifecycle without inventing educational meaning, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, inventing completed audit records, re-executing rules, re-producing decisions, re-recording audits, reinterpreting EIP, amending specifications, creating constitutional authority, freezing a stack as law, inventing unpublished CEA or CEAL types, replacing WS2 execution, replacing WS3 decision production, replacing WS4 audit production, or presenting closure as educational quality.

## 2. What Lifecycle Explainability Is

**Constitutional Explainability Lifecycle Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **coordination of named constitutional explainability activities through published CEAL stages** — consuming completed constitutional audit records, constructing / provenance-validating / fidelity-validating / publishing constitutional explanations under published CEA components — not to invent a second tutor voice from CI machinery, not to grade the student, and not to create or replace constitutional authority.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Audit record consumed** | Named completed WS4 constitutional audit record used as input | Which audit record was consumed? |
| **Provenance validated** | Named reconstructable lineage validated at CEAL-04 under CEA-03 | Which provenance was validated? |
| **Explanation produced** | Named reconstructable published explanation from CEAL-06 (or refuse-publish) | Which explanation was produced? |
| **Fidelity validations performed** | Named fidelity disposition and checks at CEAL-05 under CEA-04 | Which fidelity validations were performed? |
| **Boundaries remaining intact** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden execution / decision production / reinterpretation / amendment / authority creation did not occur | Which constitutional boundaries remained intact? |

Lifecycle Explainability is:

- **coordination-centred** — it narrates completed audit records → provenance → explanations → fidelity under law;
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CEAL and CEA identities;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published specifications and authority identities;
- **audit-honest** — every material justification cites identifiable completed audit records consumed (or names the hard stop when none exist);
- **provenance-honest** — every material justification cites which provenance was validated (or names the hard stop);
- **explanation-honest** — every material lifecycle cites which constitutional explanation was produced (or names refuse / invalid);
- **fidelity-honest** — every material publication cites which fidelity validations were performed;
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to amend corpora;
- **non-executive** — explanation never presents rule re-execution as “explainability”;
- **non-decisive** — explanation never presents decision re-production as “explainability”;
- **non-auditing** — explanation never presents audit re-recording as “explainability”;
- **non-interpretive** — explanation never presents soft amendment or EIP reinterpretation as “narration”;
- **non-authoritative** — closure is never narrated as a freeze of stack authority, a substitute for published corpora, or creation of constitutional authority;
- **quality-honest** — closure is never narrated as mastery, readiness, or educational quality;
- **mutation-honest** — explanation never hides artefact changes performed “so explanation could proceed”;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence / validation speech, WS1 preparation speech, WS2 execution speech, WS3 decision speech, WS4 audit speech, WS5 / MS001 explainability-component speech, or Programme IX governance speech;
- **understanding-aligned** — explanation components match CEALO-01 / CEALO-05 reconstruction needs;
- **repeatability-honest** — explanations must not paper over race-dependent dispositions or private stage shortcuts.

Lifecycle Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–IX / WS1 / WS2 / WS3 / WS4 / WS5 MS001 meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a substitute for CC findings, CT lineage speech, CV verification speech, CCM compliance-determination speech, CRT certification-recognition speech, CEE / CEEL execution speech, CDA / CDAL decision speech, CAA / CAAL audit speech, or CEA explainability-component speech;
- a CI status page, coverage report, or linter summary presented as constitutional coordination;
- a freeze of implementations, stacks, vendors, or releases as constitutional law;
- a licence to narrate invented completed audit records, rule re-execution, decision re-production, audit re-recording, EIP reinterpretation, or authority creation as if they were lawful inputs or outcomes.

## 3. Mandatory Explainability Questions

Every material constitutional explainability lifecycle must be able to answer:

### CEALEQ-01 — Which audit record was consumed?

**Required content**

- Named completed constitutional audit record identities (WS4 / MS003 fulfilment, with supporting architecture / lifecycle references as applicable)
- Binding between completed-audit identities and the initiated CEAL-01 concern
- Confirmation that unpublished customs, invented audit records, incomplete audit production, or re-recorded audits were not used as completed audit records

**Defect if**

- “The system explained” with no completed-audit reference
- Anonymous “the build” or “the pipeline” without identifiable completed audit records
- Completed audit records invented, rewritten, or re-recorded as explanation inputs without disclosure and hard stop

---

### CEALEQ-02 — Which provenance was validated?

**Required content**

- Named provenance validation identity from CEAL-04 under CEA-03
- Binding from admitted completed audit records through upstream decision / outcome / authority citations to the candidate / published explanation
- Explicit note of lineage identities excluded as out of scope under the concern
- Confirmation that provenance was reconstructable and not invented or forged

**Defect if**

- “Provenance was validated” with no reconstructable lineage identity
- Soft-amended or invented lineage presented as published provenance
- Stack preference or “how main works” presented as a provenance source
- Erasure of inconvenient completed-audit or authority citations

---

### CEALEQ-03 — Which explanation was produced?

**Required content**

- Named constitutional explanation identity (or lawful refuse-publish / invalid / stop disposition)
- Binding to construction at CEAL-03 and publication (or refuse-publish) at CEAL-06
- Citation of completed audit records, provenance, and fidelity disposition that ground the published explanation
- Confirmation that the explanation is not narrated as new constitutional law, EIP replacement, re-decision, re-audit, rule re-execution, stack freeze, or authority creation

**Defect if**

- “An explanation was published” with no reconstructable explanation identity or refuse record
- Unvalidated candidate presented as published valid explanation
- Explanation narrated as constitutional amendment, mastery grade, re-decision, re-audit, or authority creation

---

### CEALEQ-04 — Which fidelity validations were performed?

**Required content**

- Named fidelity disposition from CEAL-05 (valid / invalid / refuse) with reconstructable reasons
- Binding to CEA-04 Fidelity Validator expectations
- Confirmation that publication as valid did not occur without successful fidelity validation
- Explicit note of which fidelity checks were applied (audit citation, provenance preservation, meaning preservation, authority preservation, boundary integrity)

**Defect if**

- “It was validated” with no disposition or check identities
- Fidelity validation skipped so publication could proceed
- Forged or theatrical fidelity presented as constitutional validity

---

### CEALEQ-05 — Which constitutional boundaries remained intact?

**Required content**

- Explicit confirmation against `LIFECYCLE_BOUNDARIES.md` hard stops (especially no rule execution, no decision production, no audit re-recording, no EIP reinterpretation, no specification amendment, no authority creation / substitution, no stack freeze, no artefact mutation, no forged provenance)
- Named hard stops triggered, if any, and the lawful refuse / stop / escalate / invalid / refuse-publish / refuse-close disposition taken
- Confirmation that lifecycle closure did not create or replace published corpora as the next-cycle standard
- Confirmation that the lifecycle remained entirely downstream of constitutional auditing

**Defect if**

- Silent assumption that “lifecycle closed ⇒ boundaries held”
- Hidden artefact mutation, stack canonisation, decision re-production, audit re-recording, or EIP reinterpretation
- Closure narrated as constitutional authority creation or replacement

## 5. Explainability Defects

The following are constitutional explainability defects regardless of UX polish:

1. Narrating lifecycle progress as educational mastery, readiness, or product success.
2. Narrating closure as constitutional amendment, EIP replacement, decision re-production, audit re-recording, or authority creation.
3. Omitting completed-audit identities when an explanation is claimed.
4. Omitting provenance identities when provenance validation is claimed.
5. Omitting fidelity disposition when valid publication is claimed.
6. Presenting CI / coverage / linter / Twin / Adaptive signals as constitutional explainability-lifecycle explanations.
7. Hiding race-dependent or private stage-shortcut dispositions behind a single seal.
8. Collapsing WS2 execution speech into “explainability lifecycle” speech.
9. Collapsing WS3 decision speech into “explainability lifecycle” speech.
10. Collapsing WS4 audit speech into “explainability lifecycle” speech outside published audit consumption.
11. Collapsing Programme IX governance speech into “explainability lifecycle” speech outside published provenance validation.
