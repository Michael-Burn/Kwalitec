## 1. Purpose

A lifecycle that silently becomes a second constitution — rewriting specs so stages “complete,” inventing educational meaning in “explanations,” licensing runtime behaviour under a closed seal, inventing completed audit records to force CEAL-03, re-executing constitutional rules under an explanation badge, re-producing decisions under an explanation badge, re-recording audits under an explanation badge, reinterpreting EIP mid-lifecycle, treating a preferred implementation as the new standard, mutating artefacts mid-lifecycle, creating constitutional authority, or freezing a stack as law — destroys student trust and educational integrity.

This document draws a bright line between **lawful constitutional explainability lifecycle coordination** (Programme X / WS5 / MS002) and **constitutional authorship / educational judgement / runtime authorship / execution / decision production / audit production / explainability-component authorship / independent governance authorship / artefact mutation / authority creation** (Constitution / EIP / Programmes VI–IX / WS1 / WS2 / WS3 / WS4 / WS5 MS001 / permitted writers).

## 2. Boundary Principles

1. **Coordinate, do not legislate.** The lifecycle organises constitutional explainability; it does not create law.
2. **Published specifications only.** Unpublished customs and “main as law” are hard stops as standards.
3. **Published stages only.** Unpublished CEAL customs are unlawful.
4. **Published explainability components only.** CEAL-02…CEAL-06 invoke CEA-01…CEA-06 only.
5. **Completed audit records only.** Lifecycles without identifiable completed constitutional audit records are theatre.
6. **Exactly as published.** Successful progress never rewrites specification meaning.
7. **No constitutional rule execution.** Constructing explanations is not performing WS2 *execution*.
8. **No constitutional decision production.** Constructing explanations is not performing WS3 *decision production*.
9. **No audit re-recording.** Constructing explanations is not performing WS4 *audit production*.
10. **No EIP reinterpretation.** Educational Interpretation Principles remain as published.
11. **Authority first.** Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard; the lifecycle never creates authority.
12. **Implementation independence.** No stack, vendor, or reference implementation becomes law by proximity to closure.
13. **Closed ≠ quality.** Lifecycle closure never grades learning, mastery, readiness, or product success.
14. **Closed ≠ execution.** The lifecycle never re-executes constitutional rules or replaces WS2.
15. **Closed ≠ decision.** The lifecycle never re-produces constitutional decisions or replaces WS3.
16. **Closed ≠ audit.** The lifecycle never re-records constitutional audits or replaces WS4.
17. **Published ≠ amendment.** Constitutional explanations never become constitutional law.
18. **No authority creation.** The lifecycle never creates constitutional authority.
19. **No redefinition.** Programme VI questions, EIP claim classes, WS1 / WS2 / WS3 / WS4 meanings, and CEA component meanings survive coordination intact.
20. **No authority substitution.** A closed lifecycle never replaces the corpus for the next judgement.
21. **No artefact mutation.** Coordination never rewrites implementation artefacts to manufacture passable explanations.
22. **No bypass.** Product urgency never waives constitutional lifecycle requirements.
23. **Repeatability / transparency.** Race-dependent or private stage shortcuts are defects.
24. **Explain the stop.** Students, developers, and auditors should hear when the lifecycle must refuse progress or closure.
25. **No emergency exemption.** Deadlines and demos never mint constitutional amendment power or authority creation via lifecycle closure.
26. **Fidelity before valid publication.** Invalid constructions must not be published as constitutionally valid.
27. **Downstream of auditing only.** The lifecycle remains entirely downstream of constitutional auditing.

## 3. What the Lifecycle May Do (Lawful)

| Lawful action | Constitutional meaning |
|---------------|------------------------|
| **Coordinate constitutional explainability** | Organise activities through CEAL-01…CEAL-07 |
| **Consume completed constitutional audit records** | Bind named WS4 / MS003 completed audit records without inventing or rewriting them |
| **Construct constitutional explanations** | At CEAL-03, assemble candidate explanations under CEA-02 |
| **Validate / preserve provenance** | At CEAL-04, validate reconstructable lineage under CEA-03 |
| **Validate explanation fidelity** | At CEAL-05, confirm constitutional fidelity under CEA-04 |
| **Publish constitutional explanations** | At CEAL-06, publish valid explanations under CEA-05 for transparency / understanding |
| **Invoke published explainability components** | Apply CEA-01…CEA-06 as published by WS5 / MS001 |
| **Refuse unlawful progress** | Prefer honest refuse / stop / escalate / invalid over improvisation |
| **Remain transparent and repeatable** | Same published inputs → same stage outcomes and dispositions |
| **Remain implementation-neutral** | Honour lifecycle law across lawful equivalent stacks |
| **Preserve constitutional fidelity** | Apply explainability components exactly as published; never soft-amend |
| **Explain lifecycle progress** | Answer audit records / provenance / explanations / fidelity / boundaries without rewriting meaning |
| **Propose corpus amendments upstream** | Recommend (not enact) amendments when law itself must change |

These actions **coordinate constitutional explainability**. They do **not** publish a new Constitution, invent coach warrants, mint tips, author ownership maps, invent CEA or CEAL types, invent CT links, author runtime behaviour, judge educational quality, execute constitutional rules, produce constitutional decisions, re-record audits, reinterpret EIP, amend specifications, create or replace constitutional authority, determine independent governance outside constitutional authority, or modify artefacts under coordination pretext.

## 4. What the Lifecycle Must NEVER Do

| Forbidden action | Why | Lawful alternative |
|------------------|-----|--------------------|
| **Execute constitutional rules** | WS2 / CEE / CEEL / CEEC own rule execution | Consume completed audit records; never re-execute |
| **Produce constitutional decisions** | WS3 / CDA / CDAL / CDAC own decision production | Consume completed audit records; never re-produce decisions |
| **Reinterpret constitutional meaning** | Programme VI / EIP / sibling corpora own meaning | Explain under published meaning; never redefine it |
| **Reinterpret Educational Interpretation Principles** | EIP is upstream law | Apply EIP as published; never rewrite claim classes |
| **Amend constitutional specifications** | Only corpus governance may amend law | Leave specs unchanged; propose amendment upstream or refuse |
| **Create constitutional authority** | Published corpora remain the standard after any closure | Keep artefacts as recognised subjects, never as next-cycle law |
| **Replace constitutional authority** | Lifecycle machinery never becomes the corpus | Keep closure as coordination end, not law replacement |
| **Invent completed constitutional audit records** | WS4 owns audit / completion | Consume completed audit records; refuse invented inputs |
| **Re-record constitutional audits** | WS4 owns audit recording | Consume completed audit records; never re-audit |
| **Invent explainability components** | WS5 / MS001 owns CEA-01…CEA-06 | Invoke published components only |
| **Invent lifecycle stages** | Only CEAL-01…CEAL-07 are lawful | Refuse unpublished stage customs |
| **Invent provenance** | Provenance must be reconstructable from published lineage | Validate established lineage only; refuse invented links |
| **Modify implementation artefacts** | Coordination is not a mutation licence | Consume audit records and provenance as found; refuse when identity is insufficient |
| **Invent traceability** | CT owns lawful lineage | Preserve established relationships in explanations; refuse invented links |
| **Freeze a stack as law** | Implementation neutrality is mandatory | Honour equivalent lawful stacks; refuse stack canonisation |
| **Skip fidelity validation** | CEAL-05 is mandatory before valid publication | Validate; refuse-publish when invalid |
| **Publish invalid constructions as valid** | Fidelity is a constitutional precondition | Refuse-publish; escalate or stop |
| **Perform verification** | CV / CVL / CVC own evidence evaluation | Never re-author verification under an explanation badge |
| **Determine compliance** | CCM / CCL / CCC own obligation status | Never narrate closure as a compliance determination |
| **Certify implementations** | CRT / CRTL / CRTC own recognition | Never narrate closure as certification |
| **Re-run WS1 preparation, WS2 execution, WS3 decision production, or WS4 audit production** | Preparation, execution, decision, and audit are upstream | Consume completed audit records; refuse incomplete audit theatre |
| **Replace WS5 / MS001 explainability architecture** | CEA defines components; CEAL sequences them | Invoke published CEA components; never rewrite CEA meaning |
| **Grade educational quality** | Educational Validation Framework / Programme VI own quality speech | Keep closure distinct from mastery / readiness |

| **HS-CEAL-09** | Attempt to amend constitutional specifications via stage progress |

| **HS-CEAL-11** | Attempt to publish an invalid or unvalidated candidate as constitutionally valid |
| **HS-CEAL-12** | Attempt to create or replace constitutional authority because the lifecycle closed |
| **HS-CEAL-13** | Attempt to freeze a stack, vendor, or implementation as constitutional law |

## 6. Boundary Preservation Confirmations

Every material explainability lifecycle must be able to confirm:

1. Completed constitutional audit records were consumed, not invented or re-recorded.
2. Only published CEA / CEAL catalogues were applied.
3. Educational Interpretation Principles were not reinterpreted or replaced.
4. Constitutional specifications were not amended.
5. Constitutional authority was not created or replaced by lifecycle machinery.
6. Constitutional rules were not executed under explainability pretence.
7. Constitutional decisions were not produced under explainability pretence.
8. Implementation neutrality remained intact.
9. Invalid constructions were not published as valid.
10. Provenance remained reconstructable and non-forged.
11. The lifecycle remained entirely downstream of constitutional auditing.

These confirmations feed `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md` (especially CEALEQ-05).

> **The constitutional explainability lifecycle may coordinate constitutional explainability, validate explanation fidelity, preserve provenance, and publish constitutional explanations.
