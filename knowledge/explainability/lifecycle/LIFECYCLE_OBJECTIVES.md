## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “pipeline finished,” “stages skipped for speed,” or “explanation badge green.” The tutor optimises **honest organisation of constitutional explainability**: only a transparent, fidelity-preserving, provenance-preserving, repeatable, and constitutionally understanding sequence — consuming completed constitutional audit records and applying published CEA components — may support lawful published constitutional explanations.

These objectives bind every constitutional explainability lifecycle and every successor coordination machinery that claims constitutional explainability lifecycle meaning.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### CEALO-01 — Preserve transparency

**Definition.** Ensure that every material explainability lifecycle organises activities through recognised CEAL-01…CEAL-07 stages such that completed constitutional activities remain speakable from named completed constitutional audit records, named provenance, named fidelity validations, and named published authority — so constitutional explanation organisation never collapses into anonymous ambient narration, green theatre, or unowned seals.

**Includes:**

- Binding every material lifecycle to identifiable completed WS4 constitutional audit records and published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–IX authority identities.
- Consuming identifiable completed constitutional audit records as inputs — never inventing or rewriting them.
- Invoking only published CEA-01…CEA-06 components during CEAL-02…CEAL-06 as WS5 / MS001 requires.
- Preferring lawful refuse / stop / escalate / invalid outcomes over rewriting law, inventing audit records, inventing provenance, re-executing constitutional rules, re-producing decisions, re-recording audits, reinterpreting EIP, amending specifications, or mutating artefacts so the lifecycle “completes.”
- Keeping lifecycle progress distinct from educational quality, mastery, readiness, product success, constitutional rule execution, decision re-production, audit re-recording, EIP replacement, constitutional amendment, or creation of constitutional authority.
- Preserving the Model’s posture: stages coordinate explanation production; they do not become law or constitutional authority.

**Excludes:**

- Treating green CI, coverage percentages, linter scores, Twin estimates, Adaptive scores, UI ticks, or seals as lifecycle fulfilment.
- Soft-amending specifications, inventing unpublished customs, inventing or rewriting completed audit records, inventing provenance, mutating artefacts, re-executing rules, re-producing decisions, re-recording audits, or privileging “how main works” to force progress.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for lawful transparent coordination.
- Declaring “close enough” closure without documenting a corpus amendment.
- Freezing an implementation as permanently lawful because the lifecycle closed.
- Re-running WS2 execution, WS3 decision production, or WS4 audit production under a lifecycle badge.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software pretends a mark was explained without naming which completed audit record was narrated or who owned the standard. Transparent coordination is care; anonymous explanation theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CEALO-02 — Preserve fidelity

**Definition.** Ensure that every material explainability lifecycle applies published CEA components and preserves published constitutional meaning **exactly as published** — so constructed explanations survive CEAL-05 fidelity validation, and coordination never soft-amends law, invents independent interpretation, re-executes rules, re-produces decisions, re-records audits, or elevates lifecycle machinery into constitutional authority.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who quietly rewrites the syllabus mid-narration so today’s explanation badge looks finished has abandoned educational law for product fiction.

**Manifestations:**

- CEA components cited at CEAL-02…CEAL-06 remain unchanged by stage progress.
- EIP claim classes, Programme VI meaning, and WS1 / WS2 / WS3 / WS4 / WS5 MS001 meanings survive coordination intact.
- Constitutional explanations remain explanations — never amendments, EIP replacements, re-executions, re-decisions, re-audits, or stack freezes.
- Fidelity validation at CEAL-05 under CEA-04 is mandatory before valid publication.
- Soft-waiving fidelity for demo urgency is a hard stop.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing prior lawful lifecycle history to simplify the next report.

---

### CEALO-03 — Preserve provenance

**Definition.** Ensure that every material explainability lifecycle validates **reconstructable constitutional provenance** — lineage binding admitted completed audit records through upstream decision / outcome / authority citations to published explanations — so auditors and successor machinery can follow the trail without tribal knowledge or ambient log archaeology.

**Tutor rationale.** An IFoA examiner who cannot follow how yesterday’s filing reached today’s explanation has no defensible process.

**Manifestations:**

- Provenance validation at CEAL-04 under CEA-03 is mandatory when a published explanation is claimed.
- Provenance cites identifiable completed audit records and published corpora; invented lineage is a hard stop.
- Soft-selecting convenient lineage and omitting inconvenient completed-audit links is a defect.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing prior lawful provenance to simplify the next report.
- Provenance never becomes new constitutional law, a re-decision, a re-audit, or a stack freeze.

---

### CEALO-04 — Preserve repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that the same published constitutional inputs, the same consumed completed constitutional audit records, the same published CEA component discipline, the same provenance inputs, the same fidelity outcomes, and the same CEAL stage sequence yield the same constitutional explanation dispositions and the same lawful stage outcomes — absent a published non-deterministic exception (none are granted by this Model for constitutional meaning or explanation disposition).

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who treats the same completed audit records as explained or not depending on which runner woke first — or skips stages randomly — is not coaching; they are gambling.

**Manifestations:**

- Stage transitions and outcomes are corpus-bound and reproducible.
- Parallel CI runners, sharding, and cache races must not change constitutional disposition or invent stage skips.
- Explicit multi-audit intake at CEAL-02 uses published composition / published stop rules, not random operator races.
- “Eventually consistent lifecycle meaning” is a constitutional defect.
- Invented or unstable completed-audit inputs, and invented provenance, are hard stops — not soft variables.

---

### CEALO-05 — Preserve constitutional understanding

**Definition.** Ensure that every material explainability lifecycle can honestly answer the mandatory explainability questions in `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md` — so students, developers, and auditors reconstruct what happened under published law without soft-amending meaning — and that published explanations support constitutional understanding rather than theatrical narration.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who cannot say *which completed audit records* were consumed, *which provenance* was validated, *which explanation* was produced, *which fidelity validations* were performed, and *which boundaries* remained intact — is performing, not coordinating explanation production.

**Manifestations:**

- Explainability obligations apply to all material lifecycles.
- Student / developer / auditor projections share one truth with different vocabulary.
- Lifecycle progress is never narrated as mastery, readiness, educational quality, new law, rule re-execution, decision re-production, audit re-recording, EIP replacement, authority creation, or a freeze of stack authority.
- Stack preference and race-dependent outcomes are defects to disclose, not mysteries to hide.
- Published explanations preserve constitutional references, completed-audit identities, CEA / CEAL identities, provenance identities, fidelity dispositions, and published explanation identities when material.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CEALO-01 conflicts with “helping” incomplete audits look lifecycle-complete | Transparency / lawful refuse wins |
| CEALO-02 conflicts with soft-amending specs so stages finish | Fidelity wins |
| CEALO-03 conflicts with inventing convenient lineage | Provenance honesty wins |
| CEALO-04 conflicts with race-dependent runner outcomes | Repeatability wins |
| CEALO-05 conflicts with opaque “trust us” seals | Constitutional understanding wins |

No supporting objective authorises violating CEALO-01 when that violation would destroy reconstructable transparency — and none authorises violating CEALO-02 to manufacture progress.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — Constitution / EIP integrity obligations, Programme VI–IX authority preservation, WS1 preparation honesty, WS2 execution honesty, WS3 decision honesty, WS4 audit honesty, and WS5 / MS001 explainability objectives for the lifecycle horizon.

## 5. Non-Objectives

The constitutional explainability lifecycle does **not** optimise for:

- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- CI throughput, flaky-test suppression, or coverage theatre as educational success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when specifications or completed audit records are incomplete;
- collapsing CEAL stages into one mega-step that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics signals as substitutes for completed audit records or published CEA components;
- freezing implementations, releases, or stacks as permanently lawful;
- treating documentation as optional commentary on whatever code already shipped;
- replacing WS1 preparation, WS2 execution, WS3 decision production, WS4 audit production, or WS5 / MS001 explainability architecture meaning;
- modifying implementation artefacts to manufacture passable explanations;
- executing constitutional rules or producing constitutional decisions under an explanation seal;
- re-recording constitutional audits under an explanation seal;
- reinterpreting Educational Interpretation Principles or creating constitutional authority under lifecycle closure.

## 6. Closing Statement

> **A lifecycle that becomes “complete” by rewriting constitutional law, inventing completed audit records, re-executing rules, re-producing decisions, re-recording audits, reinterpreting EIP, mutating artefacts, or creating constitutional authority is a failed educational system.  
> A lifecycle that coordinates only published constitutional explainability — with transparency, fidelity, provenance, repeatability, and constitutional understanding intact — is doing its only coordination job.**
