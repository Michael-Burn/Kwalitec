An expert IFoA tutor does not finish filing a marked script and then invent a new story about what the mark meant, rewrite the syllabus so today’s narration sounds friendlier, pretend that explaining the filing is the same as re-marking the paper, treat the explanation as a new constitution, skip fidelity checks so a demo can “explain anyway,” or claim that finishing a narration pipeline creates ownership of the syllabus. This document records that posture so every future constitutional explanation activity (and successor) has a single constitutional reference for *how explanation work may be sequenced without becoming law, a second execution engine, a second decision producer, a second auditor, or an independent governance producer*.

## 2. What the Constitutional Explainability Lifecycle Is

The **constitutional explainability lifecycle** is the binding organisation by which **constitutional explainability activities** may proceed through recognised stages — consuming completed constitutional audit records, constructing and validating constitutional explanations under WS5 / MS001 component law, validating provenance, validating fidelity, publishing those explanations for transparency and understanding, and closing honestly — while preserving transparency, fidelity, provenance, implementation neutrality, repeatability, constitutional understanding, and constitutional fidelity — without becoming a source of constitutional law, a substitute for EIP, a re-execution engine, a second decision producer, a second auditor, or an independent governance producer outside constitutional authority.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Completed constitutional audit record** | A reconstructable WS4 audit record whose required audit lifecycle has lawfully completed and whose record has been published | Which completed audit records may the lifecycle consume? |
| **Lifecycle stage** | A recognised CEAL-01…CEAL-07 organisational step | Which coordination step is active? |
| **Explainability architecture component** | A recognised CEA-01…CEA-06 explainability mechanism from WS5 / MS001 applied under lifecycle stages | Which explainability machinery may stages invoke? |
| **Constitutional explanation** | Reconstructable narration produced under published CEA components for transparency and understanding — never new law, never a re-decision, never a re-audit, never stack freeze | What explanation was produced? |
| **Constitutional provenance** | Reconstructable lineage binding the explanation to completed audit records, upstream decisions / outcomes / authority citations, and published corpora — never invented lineage | Which provenance was validated? |
| **Explanation fidelity** | Confirmation that a constructed explanation cites completed audit records, preserves meaning and provenance, and respects Explainability Architecture / Lifecycle boundaries | Which fidelity validations were performed? |
| **Constitutional authority** | The exclusive right of published corpora to define educational and execution meaning | Who owns the standard? |
| **Implementation neutrality** | Lifecycle coordination is judged against law and completed audit records, not against a preferred stack, vendor, or topology | Remains the stack replaceable? |
| **Transparency** | Completed constitutional activity remains speakable from named audit records and provenance — not from anonymous ambient narration | Can the activity be seen honestly? |
| **Constitutional understanding** | Audiences can reconstruct what happened under published law without soft-amending meaning | Can authority be understood as published? |
| **Repeatability** | Explanation organisation is reproducible from reconstructable completed audit records and provenance inputs | Can the run be reproduced? |
| **Explainability** | Audit records consumed, provenance validated, explanations produced, fidelity validations, and boundaries are speakable | Can the run be narrated honestly? |
| **Constitutional fidelity** | Stages apply explainability components exactly as published; they never soft-amend law, re-produce decisions, or re-record audits | Did authority survive coordination? |

The constitutional explainability lifecycle is:

- **coordination-centred** — it organises constitutional explainability activities; it never authors constitutional obligations;
- **audit-consuming** — it consumes completed constitutional audit records; it never invents, rewrites, or re-records them;
- **architecture-faithful** — stages invoke only published CEA-01…CEA-06 components from WS5 / MS001; they never invent explainability machinery;
- **catalogue-closed** — only CEAL-01…CEAL-07 may be applied as constitutional explainability lifecycle stages;
- **provenance-preserving** — it validates lineage exactly as reconstructable from completed audit records and published corpora; it never invents provenance theatre;
- **fidelity-bound** — constructed explanations must survive constitutional fidelity validation before publication;
- **transparency-honest** — outputs are constitutional explanations for transparency / understanding, not constitutional amendments, re-decisions, re-audits, or stack freezes;
- **authority-preserving** — published corpora remain the sole source of constitutional meaning;
- **EIP-faithful** — Educational Interpretation Principles are referenced as published; they are never reinterpreted or replaced;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by lifecycle law;
- **repeatable** — organisation is reproducible from reconstructable inputs;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which audit records, provenance, explanations, fidelity validations, and boundaries applied;
- **non-executive** — lifecycle progress never performs constitutional rule execution;
- **non-decisive** — lifecycle progress never produces constitutional decisions;
- **non-auditing** — lifecycle progress never re-records or re-validates constitutional audits;
- **non-legislative** — lifecycle progress never amends constitutional specifications;
- **non-substitutive** — lifecycle machinery never becomes the constitutional standard for the next act;
- **non-authority-creating** — lifecycle progress never creates constitutional authority;

## 3. Lifecycle Posture (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material constitutional explainability lifecycle travels through seven constitutional stages. These are **normative coordination obligations**, not an implementation architecture and not a CI job graph.

```
CEAL-01 Explanation Initiation
        │
        ▼
CEAL-02 Audit Intake
        │
        ├── incomplete / refused (lawful stop — explanation production must not proceed)
        │
        └── admitted →
                │
                ▼
CEAL-03 Explanation Construction
        │
        ▼
CEAL-04 Provenance Validation
        │
        ▼
CEAL-05 Fidelity Validation
        │
        ├── invalid / refused (lawful stop — do not publish as valid)
        │
        └── valid →
                │
                ▼
CEAL-06 Explanation Publication
        │
        ▼
CEAL-07 Explanation Closure
        │
        ▼
Explain audit records / provenance / explanations / fidelity / boundaries
```

| Stage | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **CEAL-01 Initiation** | Open an explanation concern against named completed-audit identity and published scope | Scope is unpublished custom, tribal knowledge, or “how main works today” |
| **CEAL-02 Audit intake** | Admit identifiable completed constitutional audit records under CEA-01 | Audit records are missing, invented, rewritten, incomplete, or would require re-recording |
| **CEAL-03 Explanation construction** | Assemble a candidate constitutional explanation under CEA-02 | Construction would invent law, rewrite specs, reinterpret EIP, re-produce decisions, re-record audits, or privilege a stack as law |
| **CEAL-04 Provenance validation** | Validate reconstructable constitutional provenance under CEA-03 | Provenance would be invented, forged, erased, or treated as new law |
| **CEAL-05 Fidelity validation** | Confirm the candidate is constitutionally faithful under CEA-04 | Validation would be skipped, forged, or treated as new law |
| **CEAL-06 Explanation publication** | Publish valid constitutional explanations under CEA-05 (with CEA-06 state) | Publication would invent fidelity, hide failed validation, rewrite boundaries, or freeze a stack as law |
| **CEAL-07 Closure** | Close the lifecycle honestly when duties are exhausted | Closure would execute rules, produce decisions, reinterpret EIP, create / replace constitutional authority, or pretend incomplete work is done |

> **The lifecycle never “best-effort invents” an eighth stage called law-making, reinterpretation, rule re-execution, decision re-production, audit re-recording, authority creation, stack canonisation, or artefact mutation.**

## 6. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Explainability Lifecycle Model exists so that:

1. **Law remains upstream of coordination** — stages organise explanation production against published components and completed audit records; progress does not quietly become the specifications.
2. **Audit records remain honest** — intake admits completed constitutional audit records; it does not invent or re-record them to force explanations.
3. **Provenance remains honest** — the lifecycle validates reconstructable lineage; it does not invent provenance theatre so publication can proceed.
4. **Explanation production remains sequenced** — CEA activities occur under CEAL stages after lawful initiation and audit intake — not as random green theatre.
5. **Execution remains untouched** — lifecycle progress never becomes a second execution engine.
6. **Decision production remains untouched** — lifecycle progress never becomes a second decision engine.
7. **Auditing remains untouched** — lifecycle progress never becomes a second auditor.
8. **Fidelity remains required** — publication never skips constitutional fidelity validation.
9. **Authority remains honest** — no team may treat a closed lifecycle as the new constitutional source.
10. **EIP remains intact** — explanation coordination never reinterprets or replaces Educational Interpretation Principles.
11. **Implementations remain replaceable** — lifecycle law does not freeze Flask, SQLAlchemy, a particular API shape, or a CI vendor as law.
12. **Transparency / repeatability remain honest** — the same published inputs and stage sequence yield the same lawful explanation disposition.
13. **Explainability remains intact** — lifecycle speech describes coordination of explanation production; it does not redefine what learning means.
14. **Students remain protected** — product theatre cannot launder incomplete audits or unvalidated constructions as “lifecycle explained.”
