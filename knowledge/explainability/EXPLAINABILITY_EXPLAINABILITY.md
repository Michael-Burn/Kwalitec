## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *what was narrated after the filing was completed* — which completed audit record was consumed, which constitutional activities were explained, which provenance supported the narration, which explanation resulted, and which hard stops remained intact — is not coaching; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–IX have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, runtime, conformance, verification, compliance, certification, and evolution speech speakable, after Workstream 1 has made preparation speech speakable, after Workstream 2 has made execution speech speakable, after Workstream 3 has made decision speech speakable, and after Workstream 4 has made audit speech speakable, Programme X still needs one explanation-of-explanation answer:

> **“Which audit record was consumed, which constitutional activities are being explained, which provenance supports the explanation, which explanation was produced, and which constitutional boundaries remained intact?”**

That answer must cover every material constitutional explanation without inventing educational meaning, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, freezing a stack as law, inventing unpublished CEA components, re-executing constitutional rules, re-producing constitutional decisions, re-recording audits, reinterpreting EIP, or presenting explanation publication as educational quality or independent governance outside constitutional authority.

## 2. What Explainability of Explainability Is

**Constitutional Explainability Architecture Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **lawful assembly of completed constitutional audit records into constitutional explanations under published CEA components** — not to invent a second tutor voice from ambient product logs, and not to grade the student, re-decide, re-audit, or mint independent governance outside constitutional authority.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Audit record consumed** | Named completed WS4 audit-record identities admitted under CEA-01 and bound into the explanation | Which audit record was consumed? |
| **Constitutional activities explained** | Completed activities already bound into the consumed audit record and narrated under CEA-02 | Which constitutional activities are being explained? |
| **Provenance supporting the explanation** | Reconstructable lineage resolved under CEA-03 | Which provenance supports the explanation? |
| **Explanation produced** | Reconstructable explanation constructed / fidelity-validated / published under CEA-02…CEA-05 | Which explanation was produced? |
| **Boundaries remaining intact** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden execution / decision production / audit re-recording / reinterpretation / amendment / authority creation did not occur | Which constitutional boundaries remained intact? |

Explainability of Explainability is:

- **explanation-centred** — it narrates how completed audit records became explanations, not how rules were executed, decisions were produced, or audits were recorded upstream;
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CEA identities;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published specifications, completed audit records, and authority;
- **non-executive** — explanation-of-explanation never pretends that explaining equals re-running WS2 rule execution;
- **non-decisive** — explanation-of-explanation never pretends that explaining equals re-running WS3 decision production;
- **non-auditing** — explanation-of-explanation never pretends that explaining equals re-running WS4 audit recording;
- **non-interpretive** — explanation-of-explanation never smuggles EIP reinterpretation into “explanation notes”;
- **non-legislative** — explanation-of-explanation never becomes a back door to amend corpora;
- **non-authority-creating** — explanation-of-explanation never presents explanation publication as creation of constitutional authority;
- **quality-honest** — explanation publication is never narrated as mastery, readiness, or educational quality;
- **technology-honest** — explanation-of-explanation never presents a stack preference as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence speech, Programme IX judgement speech, WS1 preparation speech, WS2 execution speech, WS3 decision speech, or WS4 audit speech;
- **repeatability-honest** — explanations must not paper over ambient-only or anonymous explanation production.

Explainability of Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–IX / WS1 / WS2 / WS3 / WS4 meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a substitute for Programme VIII runtime explainability or Programme IX judgement explainability corpora;
- a substitute for EIP-003 educational explainability speech;
- a substitute for WS1 CECXEQ / CECREQ / CECCQ preparation speech;
- a substitute for WS2 CEEEQ / lifecycle / completion explainability speech;
- a substitute for WS3 CDAEQ / lifecycle / completion explainability speech;
- a substitute for WS4 CAAEQ / CAALEQ / CAACQ audit speech;
- a rendering system, UI template, CI badge, log shipper, analytics dashboard, or Twin narrative engine;
- a claim that clear explanation speech guarantees learning, a pass, conformity, compliance, or certification;
- a licence to present pipeline greens, stack traces, Twin narratives, UI copy, or ambient logs as constitutional proof.

## 3. Mandatory Explanation Questions

Every material constitutional explanation should answer all five questions:

### CEAEQ-01 — Which audit record was consumed?

Name the primary completed WS4 constitutional audit-record identities admitted under CEA-01 (paths, audit-run identity, audit-record identities, and completion confirmation as applicable). Distinguish *audit records available* from *audit records admitted and consumed*. Incomplete, unpublished, or invented ambient logs are not completed constitutional audit records. Lawful refuse must name which required audit records could not be admitted without invention.

### CEAEQ-02 — Which constitutional activities are being explained?

Name the completed constitutional activities already bound into the consumed audit records and narrated under CEA-02 (decision identities, outcome identities, authority citations, and other published activity identities as applicable). Distinguish *activities recorded upstream* from *activities explained in this run*. Soft-inventing activities not present in the consumed audit records is unlawful. Lawful refuse must name which required activities could not be explained without invention.

### CEAEQ-03 — Which provenance supports the explanation?

Name the reconstructable provenance resolved under CEA-03 (completed-audit identities, upstream decision / outcome / authority identities already available through those audit records and published corpora, and binding to the explanation). Distinguish *provenance resolved* from *provenance invented*. Unpublished tribal lineage and “how main works” are not constitutional provenance. Lawful refuse must name which required provenance could not be resolved without invention.

### CEAEQ-04 — Which explanation was produced?

Name the reconstructable constitutional explanation constructed under CEA-02, fidelity-validated under CEA-04, and published under CEA-05 (or the honest invalid / refuse disposition). Distinguish *candidate constructed* from *validated and published*. Distinguish *constitutional explanation* from constitutional decisions, constitutional audit records, Programme IX governance dispositions produced outside this architecture, and educational quality claims. Downstream consumers must not hear a soft upgrade into seals, amendments, re-decisions, or re-audits.

### CEAEQ-05 — Which constitutional boundaries remained intact?

Explicitly confirm that forbidden acts did not occur — especially: no constitutional rule execution under an explanation label; no constitutional decision production under an explanation label; no constitutional audit re-recording under an explanation label; no EIP reinterpretation; no specification amendment; no replacement of constitutional authority; no creation of constitutional authority; no unpublished CEA components; no stack freeze. If a boundary would have been violated, the explanation must narrate the lawful refuse / invalid stop rather than a fabricated intactness claim.

| Audience | Speech posture | Must still answer |

| **Auditor** | Reconstructable traces: audit record → activities → provenance → explanation → boundaries | All five CEAEQ questions with enough identity to reproduce the explanation |

Truth is invariant across audiences. Vocabulary may change; fabrication may not.

## 5. Defective Explanations

An explanation-of-explanation is constitutionally defective when it:

- omits any mandatory CEAEQ question for a material explanation;
- cites unpublished customs as audit records, activities, or provenance;
- presents explanation publication as constitutional rule execution;
- presents explanation publication as constitutional decision production;
- presents explanation publication as constitutional audit re-recording;
- reinterprets EIP under “clarification” labels;
- soft-amends specifications in explanatory prose;
- upgrades a constitutional explanation into independent governance outside published authority;
- claims educational quality, mastery, or exam readiness from publication alone;
- privileges a stack, vendor, or repository topology as constitutional authority;
- papers over refuse / invalid dispositions with demo-continuity narration;
- cannot be repeated from reconstructable inputs (violates CEAO-04).
