## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor needs bright conditions for saying “this constitutional explainability work is finished” — and equally bright refusals when it is not. After WS5 / MS001 has named how completed audit records become constitutional explanations and WS5 / MS002 has named how explainability activities are organised, Programme X Workstream 5 still needs one fulfilment checklist:

> **What must be true before constitutional speech may affirm that the required explainability lifecycle has concluded, explanations have been constructed, provenance has been validated, fidelity has been verified, explanations have been published, and the explainability process has been formally closed?**

These criteria answer that question. They are **normative constitutional conditions**, not database predicates, CI job statuses, ticket states, process exit codes, or API status codes.

All material affirmed completions require **CEAC-01 through CEAC-05** to hold. Failure of any criterion ⇒ **not yet** or **refuse close**. ## 3. Criterion Definitions

### CEAC-01 — Lifecycle completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every lifecycle stage required by the concern’s published WS5 / MS002 scope has been executed — or that a published refuse / defer / escalate disposition exhausted remaining *explainability-production* duties for that stage set with honest progression through construction, validation, publication (or refuse-publish), and closure of what occurred — so explainability speech may treat the lifecycle as completed for the concern.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Identifiable explainability concern opened under WS5 / MS001–MS002.
- Named completed-constitutional-audit-record identities (WS4 / MS003) for the concern.
- Required CEAL-01…CEAL-07 stage identities for the concern scope.
- Stage traces / disposition records for each required stage (including lawful early stops).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed stage-fulfilment record naming which required stages completed.
- Or a lawful stop record naming which stages were refused / deferred / escalated and why remaining explainability-production duties are exhausted.

**Completion conditions.**

- Required stages map to published CEAL-01…CEAL-07 only.
- Mandatory initiation, audit intake, construction, provenance validation, fidelity validation, publication, and closure obligations for the concern are not silently omitted when complete explainability production is claimed.
- Lawful early stops still leave honest stage outcomes; silent disappearance of a concern is a defect.
- Refusal of “pipeline skipped stages” or “we know it ran” without stage identity as completion.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That stage completion executes constitutional rules or re-authors completed execution outcomes.
- That stage completion produces or rewrites constitutional decisions.
- That stage completion re-records or re-validates constitutional audits.
- That stage completion reinterprets Educational Interpretation Principles.
- That stage completion amends constitutional specifications or creates constitutional authority.
- That stage completion means the learner succeeded or governance finished outside published authority.
- That skipped stages may be invented or implied from CI theatre.
- That CAAC audit completion alone equals CEAC explainability completion (CAAC is the audit-input horizon; CEAC is explainability-production fulfilment).

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who declares explainability “finished” without confirming each required explanation step occurred has not finished lawful care — even if the desk looks tidy.

### CEAC-02 — Explanation construction completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every constitutional explanation required by the concern’s published scope was constructed under published CEA-01…CEA-06 components — especially audit intake and explanation construction — without inventing unpublished explainability customs, re-executing WS2 rules under an “explanation” badge, re-producing WS3 decisions, re-recording WS4 audits, or treating explanation construction as constitutional amendment.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Named completed-constitutional-audit-record identities admitted at CEAL-02 / CEA-01.
- Published CEA component identities required for the concern.
- Candidate / constructed constitutional explanation identities under CEAL-03 / CEA-02.
- Boundary confirmations that construction did not invent law, re-execute rules, re-produce decisions, or re-record audits.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed explanation-construction record naming which constitutional explanations were completed against which completed audit records and CEA components.
- Or a lawful refuse / defer / escalate record naming which required explanations remain incomplete and why.

**Completion conditions.**

- Explanations cite completed constitutional audit records by identity; invented or incomplete audit records are a hard stop.
- Construction invokes published CEA-01…CEA-06 only — not unpublished customs, tribal knowledge, or “how main explains today.”
- Construction never silently becomes WS2 rule re-execution, WS3 decision re-production, or WS4 audit re-recording.
- Refusal of coverage percentages, lint scores, Twin confidence, or CI greens as substitutes for named explanation construction.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That constructing explanations executes constitutional (WS2) rules.
- That constructing explanations produces constitutional (WS3) decisions.
- That constructing explanations re-records constitutional (WS4) audits.
- That constructing explanations reinterprets or replaces Educational Interpretation Principles.
- That constructing explanations amends constitutional specifications or transfers constitutional authority.
- That a particular stack, language, or framework is privileged as law.
- That product quality or educational quality was judged.
- That unpublished explainability customs may be invented so close can succeed.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who claims “the explanations were completed” without naming which explanations were constructed against which completed audit records has performed explanation theatre, not construction.

### CEAC-03 — Provenance validation completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that reconstructable constitutional provenance required by the concern was validated under CEAL-04 / CEA-03 — binding published explanations to completed audit records, upstream decisions / outcomes / authority citations, and published corpora — without inventing lineage theatre, erasing succession history, or treating provenance validation as constitutional amendment or authority creation.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Constructed / candidate constitutional explanation identities.
- Completed-audit-record identities and authority references for the concern.
- Provenance validation records bound under CEAL-04 / CEA-03.
- Continuity expectations under EIP-005 for prior lawful explanation history where applicable.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed provenance-validation record naming which lineage identities were validated with which explanations.
- Or a lawful refuse / defer / escalate record naming which required provenance remains missing, truncated, forged, or invented.

**Completion conditions.**

- Provenance cites reconstructable constitutional identities — not free-form log theatre or “we remember the source.”
- Provenance binds explanations to named completed audit records and published corpora.
- Erasure, forgery, truncation, or invention of required provenance is a hard stop.
- Refusal of graph aesthetics, ticket graphs, or CI annotations as substitutes for constitutional provenance validation.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That validating provenance executes constitutional rules or produces constitutional decisions.
- That validating provenance re-records constitutional audits.
- That validating provenance reinterprets Educational Interpretation Principles.
- That validating provenance amends constitutional specifications or creates constitutional authority.
- That invented or incomplete provenance may be narrated as lawful so close can succeed.
- That Programme IX judgements were re-run or re-determined under provenance speech.
- That stack / vendor identity is constitutional provenance law.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who celebrates “provenance done” after dropping which audit record was explained has destroyed the only trail that makes the narration honest.

### CEAC-04 — Explanation publication completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every valid constitutional explanation required by the concern was published under CEAL-06 / CEA-05 (with CEA-06 state honesty where applicable) — after required fidelity validation under CEAL-05 / CEA-04 — without publishing invalid explanations, inventing publication theatre, or treating publication as rule execution, decision production, EIP reinterpretation, amendment, or authority creation.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Fidelity dispositions under CEAL-05 / CEA-04 for candidate explanations.
- Provenance validation outcomes under CEAL-04 / CEA-03.
- Validated constitutional explanation identities eligible for publication.
- Publication records / refuse-publish dispositions under CEAL-06 / CEA-05.
- Boundary confirmations that publication did not invent law or elevate explanations into authority.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed publication record naming which constitutional explanations were published (or which were refused publication and why).
- Reconstructable publication identity usable for transparency and explainability.

**Completion conditions.**

- Publication follows required fidelity validation; unpublished invalid explanations must not be narrated as published.
- Published identities are reconstructable constitutional explanations — not ticket closures or CI greens.
- Refuse-publish is a first-class lawful outcome when fidelity fails or boundaries would be breached.
- Refusal of “published somewhere” without explanation identity as completion.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That publication executes constitutional rules or produces constitutional decisions.
- That publication reinterprets Educational Interpretation Principles.
- That publication amends constitutional specifications or creates constitutional authority.
- That publication freezes a stack, vendor, or topology as permanent constitutional law.
- That educational success or independent governance was achieved by publication.
- That invalid explanations may be published to force completion.
- That fidelity validation may be skipped because a product surface looks finished.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who claims “explanation published” without naming which fidelity-validated explanations were published — or who publishes invalid narrations to look finished — has abandoned transparency.

---

### CEAC-05 — Explainability formally closed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that the explainability concern was formally closed under CEAL-07 with boundary honesty — exhausting remaining *explainability-production* duties for what occurred, preserving transparency continuity, and refusing to elevate closure into rule execution, decision production, EIP reinterpretation, specification amendment, or creation of constitutional authority.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Affirmed or refused dispositions for CEAC-01…CEAC-04 (or lawful early-stop records that exhaust remaining duties).
- Closure records under CEAL-07.
- Boundary confirmations that forbidden overclaims did not occur.
- Explainability readiness for CEACQ-01…CEACQ-05.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed formal-closure record for the concern.
- Or a refuse-close record naming which duties or boundaries remain outstanding.

**Completion conditions.**

- Closure cites reconstructable lifecycle, construction, provenance, fidelity, and publication outcomes for the concern.
- Closure never silently invents missing CEAC-01…CEAC-04 fulfilment.
- Closure preserves transparency and refuses stack canonisation as close law.
- Refuse-close is preferred over improvisation when duties remain outstanding or boundaries would be breached.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That formal closure executes constitutional rules or produces constitutional decisions.
- That formal closure reinterprets Educational Interpretation Principles.
- That formal closure amends constitutional specifications or creates constitutional authority.
- That formal closure means the learner succeeded or governance finished outside published authority.
- That formal closure freezes an implementation as permanently lawful.
- That pipeline exit, elapsed time, or badge glow equals formal closure.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who stamps “closed” while outstanding explainability duties remain — or who treats the closed folder as the new constitution — has finished the narration theatre, not the care.

## 4. Criterion Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CEAC-01 fails | Refuse close — lifecycle not fulfilled |
| CEAC-02 fails | Refuse close — explanations not completed |
| CEAC-03 fails | Refuse close — provenance not validated |
| CEAC-04 fails | Refuse close — publication not completed |
| CEAC-05 fails | Refuse close — formal closure not honest |
| Optional CEAC-06…CEAC-12 fail while CEAC-01…CEAC-05 hold | Affirmation may proceed only with explicit honesty notes; material honesty failures that would invent overclaims become refuse-close |
| Any criterion conflicts with rule execution / decision production / EIP reinterpretation / amendment / authority creation | Boundary honesty wins — refuse the overclaim |

All five primary criteria are required for material affirmed completion. Optional honesty checks strengthen refusal discipline; they do not create a sixth primary criterion outside Programme X amendment.

Fidelity validation is constitutionally required before publication claims and is recorded as supporting honesty check **CEAC-12**; it is not a separate primary catalogue ID outside CEAC-01…CEAC-05. Publication under CEAC-04 may not be affirmed when required fidelity validation remains outstanding.

---

## 5. Closing Statement

> **Constitutional explainability is complete only when CEAC-01…CEAC-05 hold.  
> Anything less is unfinished explainability production — and anything that satisfies those criteria by executing rules, remaking decisions, reinterpreting EIP, amending law, or creating authority is not completion at all.**
