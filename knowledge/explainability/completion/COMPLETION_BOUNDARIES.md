## 1. Purpose

Explainability machinery that silently turns “the explainability lifecycle finished” into “constitutional rules were re-executed,” “decisions were remade,” “EIP now means X,” “the Constitution now says Y,” or “we are the new constitutional authority” destroys student trust and educational integrity.

This document draws a bright line between **lawful constitutional explainability completion** (Programme X / WS5 / MS003) and **constitutional rule execution / decision production / EIP reinterpretation / specification amendment / authority creation / educational judgement / independent governance outside constitutional authority** owned elsewhere.

---

## 2. Boundary Principles

1. **Confirm explainability fulfilment, do not legislate.** Completion affirms fulfilment of published explainability lifecycle duties; it does not create constitutional law.
2. **Explainability production only.** Rule execution, decision production, audit re-recording, EIP reinterpretation, constitutional amendment, and authority creation are never inferred from close.
3. **Not rule execution.** WS2 owns constitutional rule execution; completion never re-executes rules.
4. **Not decision production.** WS3 owns constitutional decision production; completion never remakes decisions.
5. **Not audit re-recording.** WS4 owns constitutional audit production; completion never re-records audits.
6. **Not EIP reinterpretation.** Educational Interpretation Principles remain as published.
7. **Not constitutional amendment.** Close never patches law or redefines constitutional meaning.
8. **Not authority creation.** Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard after close; completion never creates constitutional authority.
9. **Not conformance / verification / compliance / certification re-determination.** Programme IX judgements may appear as provenance identities only.
10. **Exactly as published.** Close never redefines CEA / CEAL catalogues or soft-amends Programme VI–IX meaning.
11. **Published criteria only.** Pipelines, tickets, badges, and elapsed time are not fulfilment law.
12. **Published records only.** Transparency continuity is constitutional, not free-form log theatre.
13. **Reference audit records honestly.** Completed audit records may be cited; closing never upgrades citation into re-recording or amendment.
14. **Meaning non-reinterpretation.** Programme VI questions and EIP claim classes survive close intact.
15. **Implementation neutrality.** No stack becomes law by proximity to a complete flag.
16. **Explainability-complete ≠ quality.** Completion never grades learning, mastery, readiness, or product success.
17. **Explainability-complete ≠ independent governance.** Completion never finishes programme or release governance as constitutional law.
18. **Explain the stop.** Students, developers, and auditors should hear when close must be refused.
19. **No emergency exemption.** Deadlines and demos never mint completion power or amendment power.
20. **Repeatability survives close.** Close must not invent contradictory dispositions from identical published inputs.
21. **Provenance and fidelity survive close.** Close must not erase, forge, truncate, invent required lineage, or skip required fidelity validation.

## 3. What Explainability Completion May Do (Lawful)

| Lawful action | Constitutional meaning |
|---------------|------------------------|
| **Confirm explainability completion** | Affirm CEAC-01…CEAC-05 when published criteria hold for a lawful explainability concern |
| **Confirm publication** | Cite that required valid constitutional explanations were published under CEAL-06 / CEA-05 — without rewriting dispositions or inventing explanations |
| **Confirm formal closure** | Cite that the concern was formally closed under CEAL-07 with boundary honesty |
| **Confirm provenance validation** | Cite that reconstructable provenance was validated under CEAL-04 / CEA-03 |
| **Confirm fidelity validation** | Cite that required fidelity validations completed under CEAL-05 / CEA-04 |
| **Preserve transparency** | Leave reconstructable constitutional records of audit records explained, stages, explanations, validations, and boundaries |
| **Refuse unlawful close** | Prefer honest not-yet / refuse / defer / escalate over improvisation |
| **Compose required stages and components** | Confirm fulfilment across published CEAL / CEA conjunctions without collapsing failed duties |
| **Remain implementation-neutral** | Honour completion law across lawful equivalent stacks |
| **Preserve repeatability** | Ensure same published inputs do not yield contradictory completion stories |
| **Explain completion** | Answer audit records / provenance / fidelity / explanations / boundaries without rewriting meaning |
| **Propose corpus amendments upstream** | Recommend (not enact) amendments when law itself must change before explainability can complete |

These actions **confirm explainability-production fulfilment**. They do **not** publish a new Constitution, invent coach warrants, mint tips, author ownership maps, invent CEA / CEAL / CEAC types, author runtime behaviour, execute rules, produce decisions, re-record audits, reinterpret EIP, amend constitutional law, create constitutional authority, finish governance as constitutional law, or judge educational quality.

## 4. What Explainability Completion Must NEVER Do

| Forbidden action | Why | Lawful alternative |
|------------------|-----|--------------------|
| **Execute constitutional rules** | WS2 owns execution | Cite completed audit records as inputs; never re-run rules under completion pretext |
| **Produce constitutional decisions** | WS3 owns decision production | Cite upstream decisions only as provenance subjects; never remake or rewrite them |
| **Reinterpret constitutional meaning** | Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–IX own meaning | Leave meaning unchanged; narrate fulfilment against published meaning only |
| **Reinterpret Educational Interpretation Principles** | EIP corpora own interpretation law | Reference EIP as published; refuse soft-replacement in completion prose |
| **Amend constitutional specifications** | Only corpus governance may amend meaning | Leave specs unchanged; propose amendment upstream or refuse incomplete explainability |
| **Create constitutional authority** | Published corpora remain the sole standard | Refuse “explainability complete therefore we are the law” theatre |
| **Replace constitutional authority** | Authority stays upstream after close | Keep completion subordinate; never freeze a stack as the next constitution |
| **Invent unpublished stages / components / criteria** | Catalogues are closed | Use CEAL / CEA / CEAC only; refuse custom completion law |
| **Erase / forge / invent provenance** | Transparency depends on honest lineage | Preserve reconstructable provenance or refuse close |
| **Skip / forge fidelity validation** | Fidelity precedes publication | Refuse-publish / refuse-close when fidelity fails |
| **Publish invalid explanations to force close** | Fidelity precedes publication | Refuse-publish / refuse-close when fidelity fails |
| **Re-record or re-validate audits** | WS4 owns audit production | Consume completed audit records only |
| **Imply educational success** | Learning outcomes are not explainability fulfilment | Separate educational speech from completion speech |
| **Imply independent governance finish** | Programme / release governance is not CEAC | Keep governance horizons distinct |
| **Canonise a stack / vendor / topology** | Implementation neutrality is invariant | Remain technology-independent |
| **Treat CI greens / tickets / badges as fulfilment** | Theatre is not law | Require CEAC-01…CEAC-05 with reconstructable records |
| **Skip outstanding lifecycle duties** | Completion theatre is harm | Prefer not-yet / refuse-close |
| **Collapse CAAC / CDAC / CEEC / CECC into CEAC** | Sibling horizons remain distinct | Compose inputs; do not conflate fulfilment |

## 5. Boundary Tests

Before affirming completion, every material concern must pass:

1. **Lifecycle test** — Were required CEAL stages completed (or lawfully stopped) without inventing stages?
2. **Construction test** — Were required constitutional explanations constructed under published CEA components?
3. **Provenance test** — Was reconstructable provenance validated without erasure, forgery, truncation, or invention?
4. **Fidelity / publication test** — Were fidelity validations completed and required valid explanations published (or refuse-publish recorded)?
5. **Closure test** — Was formal closure honest under CEAL-07 without elevating close into authority?
6. **Non-execution test** — Does the judgement refuse implying constitutional rule execution?
7. **Non-decision test** — Does the judgement refuse implying constitutional decision production?
8. **EIP / amendment / authority test** — Does the judgement refuse EIP reinterpretation, specification amendment, and authority creation?
9. **Neutrality / repeatability test** — Would the same published inputs yield the same disposition without stack canonisation?
10. **Explainability test** — Can CEACQ-01…CEACQ-05 be answered without rewriting meaning?

Failure of any test ⇒ **not yet** or **refuse close**.

---

## 6. Closing Statement

> **Explainability completion may confirm that constitutional explainability has lawfully completed — including publication and formal closure.  
> It must never execute constitutional rules, produce constitutional decisions, reinterpret constitutional meaning, or amend constitutional specifications.  
> The moment completion becomes execution, decision production, EIP replacement, legislation, or authority creation, it has left constitutional education.**
