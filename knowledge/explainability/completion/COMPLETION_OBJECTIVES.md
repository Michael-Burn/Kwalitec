## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “pipeline greens,” “tickets closed,” or “explanation badges shown.” The tutor optimises **honest closing of constitutional explainability work**: when the published lifecycle for a concern has concluded, explanations have been constructed, provenance has been validated, fidelity has been verified, explanations have been published, and the process has been formally closed, the tutor can say so — without pretending rules were re-executed, without pretending decisions were remade, without pretending audits were re-recorded, without pretending EIP was reinterpreted, without pretending the Constitution was amended, without pretending authority was created, and without rewriting explainability meaning because the machinery stopped cleanly.

These objectives bind every constitutional explainability completion judgement and every successor explainability machinery that claims constitutional explainability fulfilment.

## 2. Primary Objective

### CEACO-01 — Confirm lawful explainability completion

**Definition.** Ensure that runtime / assessment / explainability speech affirms explainability completion only when a published explainability concern under WS5 / MS001–MS002 has concluded the required CEAL-01…CEAL-07 lifecycle obligations with constructed, provenance-validated, fidelity-verified, published, and formally closed constitutional explanations — or honestly refuses close when fulfilment criteria are not met.

**Includes:**

- Assessing only concerns that lawfully opened under WS5 / MS001–MS002 initiation → audit intake → construction → provenance → fidelity → publication → closure law.
- Affirming completion only when `COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md` conditions (CEAC-01…CEAC-05) hold.
- Preferring lawful “not yet” / refuse-close over inventing a “done enough” status for product convenience.
- Distinguishing lawful stop dispositions (refuse / defer / escalate as published outcomes that exhaust remaining *explainability-production* duties with honest recording) from abandoned lifecycle duties.
- Binding every material completion judgement to reconstructable constitutional references.

**Excludes:**

- Declaring complete because a CI job exited, a ticket closed, an explanation badge glowed, or a UI spinner stopped.
- Treating Twin / Adaptive / Experience / Mission heuristics as completion warrants.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for lifecycle honesty.
- Collapsing “explainability complete” into “rules re-executed,” “decisions remade,” “audits re-recorded,” “EIP reinterpreted,” “constitution amended,” or “authority created.”

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software celebrates unfinished explanation work — or celebrates finished pipeline machinery as finished rule execution, decision remaking, or law-making. Honest confirmation is care; completion theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CEACO-02 — Preserve transparency

**Definition.** Ensure that every material completion judgement leaves a reconstructable trail of what was explained: which completed constitutional audit records were explained, which lifecycle stages were completed, which explanations were constructed and published, which provenance and fidelity validations were completed, and which boundaries remained intact — so transparency survives close.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later say *what* was explained and *against which audit trail* has not finished care. Silent “complete” badges destroy student and auditor trust.

**Manifestations:**

- Transparency records preserve constitutional references, not only technical job logs or CI annotations.
- Close never substitutes pipeline exit codes or ticket states for named audit-record / explanation / stage identities.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing or silently replacing prior lawful explanation records to simplify the next report.
- Missing required reconstructable transparency references after an affirmed close is a defect.

---

### CEACO-03 — Preserve fidelity

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming explainability completion never skips, forges, softens, or substitutes for required fidelity validations under CEAL-05 / CEA-04 — so published explanations remain constitutionally faithful to completed audit records and published meaning.

**Tutor rationale.** A close that “helpfully” skips fidelity so a demo can “explain anyway” destroys educational honesty even if the completion UX looks clean.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion confirms required fidelity dispositions before publication claims under CEAC-04.
- Softened, forged, or skipped fidelity theatre to force a green close is a defect.
- Twin confidence, lint scores, and CI greens never substitute for named fidelity validation.
- Completion never narrates fidelity as educational quality scoring or Programme IX re-determination.

---

### CEACO-04 — Preserve provenance

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming explainability completion never erases, forges, truncates, invents, or replaces published constitutional identity, completed-audit identity, or succession history required to reconstruct where the explained activity came from.

**Tutor rationale.** A close that “helpfully” drops which completed audit record was explained — or invents a prettier lineage so the narration looks tidy — destroys educational honesty even if the completion UX looks clean.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion leaves specification provenance and audit provenance intact.
- Provenance cited under CEAC-03 remains reconstructable after close.
- Invented provenance theatre or truncated lineage to force a green close is a defect.
- Completion never invents Programme IX judgements or WS2 / WS3 / WS4 outcomes as provenance substitutes.

---

### CEACO-05 — Preserve repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that the same published obligations, same consumed completed audit records, same provenance inputs, and same required stage sequence yield the same completion disposition for the same concern scope — without stack-specific non-reproducible close theatre.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who marks the same script “explainability complete” on Monday and “not yet” on Tuesday without a published input change has abandoned professional care.

**Manifestations:**

- Same published inputs ⇒ same CEAC disposition for the same concern.
- Stack / vendor / topology differences never become completion law.
- Ephemeral “we remember it completed” theatre without reconstructable records is a defect.
- Continuity forbids deleting prior lawful publication / closure records to force a different close next time.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CEACO-01 conflicts with inventing unpublished stages so close can complete | Lifecycle honesty wins — refuse / defer / escalate |
| CEACO-02 conflicts with opaque complete badges | Transparency wins |
| CEACO-03 conflicts with skipped or forged fidelity theatre | Fidelity honesty wins |
| CEACO-04 conflicts with truncated or invented lineage theatre | Provenance honesty wins |
| CEACO-05 conflicts with stack-specific non-reproducible close | Repeatability wins |
| Any CEACO conflicts with executing rules / producing decisions / reinterpreting EIP / amending law / creating authority | Non-implication honesty wins — refuse the overclaim |

No supporting objective authorises violating CEACO-01.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — Constitution / EIP integrity obligations, Programme VI–IX authority preservation, WS5 / MS001 CEAO duties, and WS5 / MS002 CEALO duties for the *completion* horizon.

## 5. Non-Objectives

Constitutional explainability completion does **not** optimise for:

- constitutional rule execution or re-authoring of completed execution outcomes;
- production, rewriting, or silent replacement of constitutional decisions;
- re-recording or re-validation of constitutional audits;
- reinterpretation or replacement of Educational Interpretation Principles;
- constitutional amendment, soft rewrite of specifications, or creation / replacement of constitutional authority;
- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- determining governance outside published constitutional authority;
- freezing a stack, vendor, or topology as permanent constitutional law;
- CI throughput, flaky-explanation suppression, or demo continuity as educational or constitutional success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when required lifecycle stages, explanations, provenance, fidelity validations, publications, or closures remain incomplete;
- collapsing CEAC criteria into one mega-flag that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics signals as substitutes for published completion criteria;
- treating documentation of a pipeline as optional commentary on whatever code already shipped;
- declaring rule re-execution, decision remaking, audit re-recording, EIP replacement, law amendment, or authority creation solely because completion was affirmed;
- collapsing audit completion (CAAC), decision completion (CDAC), execution completion (CEEC), or context completion (CECC) into explainability completion.

## 6. Closing Statement

> **A concern that becomes “explainability complete” by re-executing constitutional rules — or by remaking decisions, re-recording audits, reinterpreting EIP, amending law, or creating authority — is a failed educational system.  
> A concern that confirms only that the required published explainability lifecycle concluded, explanations were constructed, provenance was validated, fidelity was verified, explanations were published, and the process was formally closed — with transparency, fidelity, provenance, and repeatability intact — is doing its only completion job.**
