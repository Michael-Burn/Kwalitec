## 2. What Constitutional Explainability Completion Is

**Constitutional Explainability Completion** is confirmation that the **required constitutional explainability lifecycle** for a published explainability concern has **lawfully concluded** and that **constitutional explanations** have been **constructed, provenance-validated, fidelity-verified, published, and formally closed** — such that transparency, fidelity, provenance, and repeatability hold for that concern under WS5 / MS001–MS002 — without claiming that any rule-execution, decision-production, audit-re-recording, EIP-reinterpretation, amendment, authority-creation, educational, or independent-governance question was resolved by the close itself.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Explainability concern** | A bounded constitutional unit of explanation work opened under published CEA / CEAL law | What explainability work is being assessed for completion? |
| **Completion warrant** | Judgement that lifecycle fulfilment may be assessed | May we ask whether explainability is complete? |
| **Completion criteria** | Constitutional conditions that must hold (`COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md`) | What must be true for fulfilment? |
| **Completion judgement** | Affirmed / not yet / refused (no lawful close) | Is constitutional explainability complete for this concern? |
| **Lifecycle fulfilment** | Required CEAL-01…CEAL-07 obligations executed (or lawfully stopped with honest recording) | Did the published lifecycle run? |
| **Explanation construction** | Required constitutional explanations constructed under published CEA components | Were explanations completed? |
| **Provenance validation** | Reconstructable constitutional provenance validated against completed audit records and published corpora | Was provenance validated? |
| **Fidelity verification** | Required fidelity validations completed under CEAL-05 / CEA-04 | Were fidelity validations completed? |
| **Explanation publication** | Required valid constitutional explanations published under CEAL-06 / CEA-05 | Were explanations published? |
| **Formal closure** | Honest close under CEAL-07 / boundary honesty | Was explainability formally closed? |
| **Transparency continuity** | Reconstructable constitutional records of the completed explainability process | Can the close be seen honestly? |
| **Boundary preservation record** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden overclaims / executions / decisions / amendments / authority-creation did not occur | Did constitutional integrity survive close? |

Constitutional Explainability Completion is:

- **explainability-scoped** — it concerns fulfilment of the published explainability lifecycle, not rule execution, decision production, audit re-recording, EIP reinterpretation, constitutional amendment, or authority creation;
- **lifecycle-bound** — fulfilment references CEAL-01…CEAL-07 stages actually required for the concern under WS5 / MS002;
- **criteria-gated** — fulfilment requires stated constitutional conditions (`COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md` CEAC-01…CEAC-05);
- **audit-consuming** — completion confirms completed constitutional audit records were explained; it never invents or re-records them;
- **explanation-honest** — completion confirms explanations were constructed, validated, and published; it never silently replaces prior explanations or upgrades publication into constitutional amendment or authority transfer;
- **provenance-preserving** — fulfilment never erases, forges, truncates, or invents provenance required to reconstruct the explained audit lineage;
- **fidelity-bound** — fulfilment confirms required fidelity validations completed before publication claims;
- **transparency-capable** — fulfilment cites reconstructable constitutional records, not ticket theatre or CI greens;
- **repeatable** — same published obligations, same consumed completed audit records, same provenance, same stage sequence ⇒ same completion disposition for the same concern scope;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by completion;
- **non-executive** — completion never performs constitutional rule execution;
- **non-decisive** — completion never produces constitutional decisions;
- **non-auditing** — completion never re-records or re-validates constitutional audits;
- **non-legislative** — completion never amends constitutional specifications;
- **non-authority-creating** — completion never creates constitutional authority;
- **EIP-faithful** — Educational Interpretation Principles remain as published; they are never reinterpreted during close;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which audit records / provenance / fidelity validations / explanations / boundaries applied;
- **corpus-bound** — every material completion cites identifiable published specifications, completed audit records, and named constitutional explanations;
- **non-substitutive** — a completed explainability activity never becomes the constitutional standard or a substitute for MS001 evaluation / MS002 lifecycle law.

Constitutional Explainability Completion is **not**:

- constitutional rule execution or re-authoring of completed execution outcomes;
- production, rewriting, or silent replacement of constitutional decisions;
- re-recording or re-validation of constitutional audits;
- reinterpretation or replacement of Educational Interpretation Principles;
- a constitutional amendment, soft rewrite of specifications, or creation of constitutional authority;
- educational success, learner mastery, progress, or exam readiness;
- independent governance outside published constitutional authority;
- implementation evolution that freezes a stack, vendor, or topology as permanent constitutional law;
- a Programme VI / VII / VIII educational, orchestration, or runtime completion judgement;
- transfer of authority, minting of tips, invention of EST/CST postures, or amendment of constitutional specifications;
- a claim that pipeline greens, ticket closure, elapsed time, or explanation badges equal fulfilment;
- a UI celebration, analytics milestone, or “explained forever” flag as educational or constitutional law;
- a licence to skip outstanding published lifecycle duties because a product surface looks finished;
- a substitute for WS5 / MS001 explainability architecture meaning or WS5 / MS002 lifecycle law — completion *judges fulfilment of* those corpora; it does not redefine CEA or CEAL catalogues;
- a substitute for WS4 / MS003 audit completion — CAAC confirms audit-production fulfilment; CEAC confirms explainability-production fulfilment and *requires* completed audit records as input;
- a substitute for WS3 / MS003 decision completion, WS2 / MS003 execution completion, or WS1 / MS003 context completion — those horizons remain orthogonal.

## 4. Completion Lifecycle (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material explainability concern that claims lawful completion travels through constitutional assessment phases. These are **normative judgement obligations**, not an implementation architecture.

```
Identify published explainability concern opened under CEA / CEAL law
        │
        ▼
Confirm required CEAL-01…CEAL-07 stages and CEA-01…CEA-06 applications for the concern
        │
        ├── refuse / defer / escalate (lawful stop — law unchanged; no invented “complete”)
        │
        └── assess → Apply CEAC-01…CEAC-05
                │
                ├── not yet / refuse close (outstanding duties or boundary breach)
                │
                └── affirm → Emit completion judgement; preserve transparency and repeatability
                        │
                        ▼
                Explain audit records / provenance / fidelity / explanations / boundaries
```

| Phase | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **Identify** | Name only published explainability concerns opened under WS5 / MS001–MS002 | Concern is unpublished custom, tribal knowledge, or “how CI explains today” |
| **Confirm lifecycle** | Verify required CEAL stages and CEA applications for the concern | Stages would invent law, rewrite specs, reinterpret EIP, re-execute rules, re-produce decisions, re-record audits, or privilege a stack as law |
| **Assess criteria** | Apply CEAC-01…CEAC-05 without softening into product theatre | Any required criterion fails; or close would claim rule execution / decision production / EIP reinterpretation / amendment / authority creation |
| **Preserve & explain** | Record transparency continuity; answer CEACQ-01…CEACQ-05 | Path would erase trails, treat completion as constitutional amendment, or narrate “complete therefore rules re-ran / decisions remade / audits re-recorded / EIP changed / authority created” |

> **Explainability completion never “best-effort invents” a fifth phase called constitutional rule execution, decision production, EIP reinterpretation, specification amendment, or authority creation.**

## 7. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Explainability Completion Model exists so that:

1. **Lifecycle honesty remains intact** — “complete” means the published explainability lifecycle ran, not that a pipeline glowed green.
2. **Explanations remain honest** — close confirms explanations were constructed, validated, and published; it never silently replaces them or upgrades publication into constitutional amendment or authority transfer.
3. **Provenance survives close** — completion leaves reconstructable audit-to-explanation lineage.
4. **Fidelity survives close** — completion confirms required fidelity validations completed before publication claims.
5. **Transparency survives close** — completion leaves reconstructable constitutional records.
6. **Repeatability remains intact** — the same published inputs yield the same completion disposition.
7. **Authority remains upstream** — completed explanations never become the constitutional source for the next judgement.
8. **Implementations remain replaceable** — completion does not freeze Flask, SQLAlchemy, a particular API shape, or a CI vendor as law.
9. **Explainability remains intact** — completion speech describes explainability-production fulfilment; it does not redefine what learning means or remake decisions.
10. **Students remain protected** — product theatre cannot launder unfinished explanation work as “explainability complete therefore rules re-ran / decisions remade / lawful forever.”
