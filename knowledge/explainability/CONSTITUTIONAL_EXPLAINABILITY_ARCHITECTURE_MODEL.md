## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not finish filing a marked script and then invent a new story about what the mark meant, rewrite the syllabus so today’s narration sounds friendlier, pretend that explaining the filing is the same as re-marking the paper, treat the explanation as a new constitution, or claim that finishing the narration creates ownership of the syllabus. After the Constitution and EIP have defined *educational law*, after Programmes VI–IX have published *meaning, orchestration, runtime, and governance law*, after Workstream 1 has *prepared* the constitutional execution context, after Workstream 2 has *executed* published rules into *completed execution outcomes*, after Workstream 3 has *transformed* those outcomes into *completed constitutional decisions*, and after Workstream 4 has *recorded* those decisions into *completed constitutional audit records*, the platform still needs one explainability answer:

> **“How are completed constitutional activities explained without altering constitutional meaning or authority?”**

That answer must ensure the Explainability Architecture consumes **completed constitutional audit records**; assembles **constitutional explanations**; preserves **constitutional meaning**, **provenance**, and **implementation neutrality**; and produces **constitutional explanations** for lawful audiences — while remaining fully repeatable, explainable, and constitutionally faithful — without executing constitutional rules, producing constitutional decisions, reinterpreting EIP, amending constitutional specifications, creating constitutional authority, re-recording audits, or becoming constitutional authority.

This document records that posture so every future constitutional explanation activity (and successor) has a single constitutional reference for *how completed constitutional audit records may become explanations without becoming law, a second execution engine, a second decision producer, a second auditor, or an independent governance producer*.

> **The Constitutional Explainability Architecture Model describes how completed constitutional audit records are assembled into constitutional explanations.  
> It does not execute constitutional rules, produce constitutional decisions, reinterpret Educational Interpretation Principles, amend constitutional specifications, or create constitutional authority.**

## 2. What the Constitutional Explainability Architecture Is

The **Constitutional Explainability Architecture** is the binding **constitutional mechanism** by which **completed constitutional audit records** may be **assembled into constitutional explanations** — consuming those audit records, constructing and validating explanations, resolving provenance, managing explanation state, and publishing explanations for lawful audiences — while preserving constitutional meaning, provenance, implementation neutrality, transparency, fidelity, repeatability, and constitutional understanding — without becoming a source of constitutional law, a substitute for EIP, a re-execution engine, a second decision producer, a second auditor, or an independent governance producer outside constitutional authority.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Completed constitutional audit record** | A reconstructable WS4 audit record whose required audit lifecycle has lawfully completed and whose record has been published | Which completed audit records may be consumed? |
| **Constitutional activity explained** | Speakable completed activities already bound into the consumed audit record (decisions, outcomes, authority citations as published) | Which constitutional activities are being explained? |
| **Constitutional provenance** | Reconstructable lineage binding the explanation to completed audit records, upstream decisions / outcomes / authority citations, and published corpora — never invented lineage | Which provenance supports the explanation? |
| **Constitutional explanation** | Reconstructable narration produced under published CEA components for transparency and understanding — never new law, never a re-decision, never a re-audit, never stack freeze | What explanation was produced? |
| **Explanation fidelity** | Confirmation that a constructed explanation cites completed audit records, preserves meaning and provenance, and respects Explainability Architecture boundaries | Is the explanation constitutionally faithful? |
| **Explanation state** | Speakable progress of explanation production under CEA-06 (not educational state, not independent governance disposition, not a re-decision, not a re-audit) | Where is explanation production in its lawful course? |
| **Constitutional authority** | The exclusive right of published corpora to define educational and execution meaning | Who owns the standard? |
| **Implementation neutrality** | Explainability law does not privilege a stack, vendor, or topology | Remains the stack replaceable? |
| **Transparency** | Completed constitutional activity remains speakable from named audit records and provenance — not from anonymous ambient narration | Can the activity be seen honestly? |
| **Constitutional understanding** | Audiences can reconstruct what happened under published law without soft-amending meaning | Can authority be understood as published? |
| **Repeatability** | Explanation production is reproducible from reconstructable completed audit records and provenance inputs | Can the explanation be reproduced? |
| **Explainability of explainability** | Audit consumed, activities explained, provenance supporting, explanation produced, and boundaries intact are speakable | Can the explanation itself be narrated honestly? |
| **Constitutional fidelity** | Explanation preserves published meaning exactly; it never soft-amends law, re-produces decisions, or re-records audits | Did authority survive explanation? |

The Constitutional Explainability Architecture is:

- **audit-consuming** — it consumes completed constitutional audit records; it never invents, rewrites, or re-records them;
- **explanation-centred** — it produces constitutional explanations; it never authors constitutional obligations, decisions, or audits;
- **meaning-preserving** — it narrates published meaning exactly; it never soft-amends Constitution / EIP / Programmes VI–IX / WS1–WS4;
- **provenance-preserving** — it resolves lineage exactly as reconstructable from completed audit records and published corpora; it never invents provenance theatre;
- **authority-preserving** — published corpora remain the sole source of constitutional meaning;
- **EIP-faithful** — Educational Interpretation Principles are referenced as published; they are never reinterpreted or replaced;
- **catalogue-closed** — only CEA-01…CEA-06 may be applied as constitutional explainability architecture components;
- **fidelity-bound** — constructed explanations must survive constitutional fidelity validation before publication;
- **understanding-honest** — outputs are constitutional explanations for transparency / understanding, not constitutional amendments, re-decisions, re-audits, or stack freezes;
- **state-honest** — explanation state describes Explainability Architecture progress; it never invents educational state, independent governance, re-decision dispositions, or re-audit dispositions;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by explainability law;
- **repeatable** — explanation production is reproducible from reconstructable inputs;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which audit records, activities, provenance, explanations, and boundaries applied;
- **non-executive** — explanation never performs constitutional rule execution;
- **non-decisive** — explanation never produces constitutional decisions;
- **non-legislative** — explanation never amends constitutional specifications;
- **non-auditing** — explanation never re-records or re-validates constitutional audits;
- **non-substitutive** — explainability machinery never becomes the constitutional standard for the next act;
- **non-authority-creating** — explanation never creates constitutional authority;
- **downstream-only** — the architecture remains entirely downstream of constitutional auditing.

The Constitutional Explainability Architecture is **not**:

- a second Educational Constitution, EIP, or Programme VI / VII / VIII / IX amendment path;
- a licence to reinterpret coach meaning, ownership maps, tip warrants, runtime contracts, or Programme IX judgements in “explanation” prose;
- a Runtime A workflow runner, scheduler, Python interpreter, Flask request cycle, UI template engine, or CI job;
- a substitute for WS1 context / resolution / completion — those corpora prepare context; CEA never prepares context;
- a substitute for WS2 execution engine / lifecycle / completion — those corpora execute rules and complete execution; CEA never re-executes rules;
- a substitute for WS3 decision architecture / lifecycle / completion — those corpora produce decisions; CEA never re-produces them;
- a substitute for WS4 audit architecture / lifecycle / completion — those corpora produce audit records; CEA consumes *completed audit records* and never re-records them;
- a substitute for Programme IX conformance, verification, compliance, certification, or evolution — those corpora produce governance judgements under their own catalogues; CEA may *cite* published identities as provenance and never becomes an independent producer of those judgements;
- a substitute for Programme VIII evidence validation (EV-xx) — EV validates educational evidence eligibility; CEA explains *constitutional activity* already recorded in audit records;
- a substitute for EIP-003 educational explainability speech — EIP-003 governs educational claim honesty; CEA governs *constitutional explanation of completed audit records*;
- a substitute for Educational Validation Framework quality review — quality release remains a distinct horizon;
- a claim that “explanation published” guarantees learning, mastery, exam readiness, product success, conformity, verification, compliance, certification, or frozen stack authority;
- a testing framework, CI/CD pipeline, GitHub Action, linter policy, OpenAPI checker, analytics dashboard, or Twin narrative engine.

## 3. Explainability Posture (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material constitutional explanation travels through constitutional phases. These are **normative explainability obligations**, not an implementation architecture.

```
Confirm completed constitutional audit records (WS4 / MS003)
        │
        ├── incomplete / refused (lawful stop — explanation must not begin)
        │
        └── completed → Intake completed constitutional audit records (CEA-01)
                │
                ▼
        Construct constitutional explanation (CEA-02)
                │
                ▼
        Resolve constitutional provenance (CEA-03)
                │
                ▼
        Validate explanation fidelity (CEA-04)
                │
                ├── invalid / refused (lawful stop — do not publish as valid)
                │
                └── valid → Publish constitutional explanation (CEA-05)
                        │
                        ├── manage explanation state (CEA-06)
                        │
                        └── never execute constitutional rules, produce constitutional
                            decisions, reinterpret EIP, amend specifications,
                            re-record audits, or create constitutional authority
```

**Refuse is lawful.** Prefer honest stop over inventing unpublished explanation customs, re-producing decisions under an explanation label, re-recording audits under an explanation label, or minting independent governance so a demo can “explain anyway.”

## 4. Integrity Rules

1. **Completed audit records only.** Explanation may begin only against WS4-completed published constitutional audit records.
2. **Published components only.** Unpublished CEA customs are unlawful.
3. **Exactly as published.** Successful explanation never rewrites specification meaning.
4. **Authority first.** Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard; the Explainability Architecture never becomes one.
5. **EIP intact.** Educational Interpretation Principles are never reinterpreted or replaced by explanation prose.
6. **No rule execution.** Assembling explanations is not performing WS2 constitutional rule execution.
7. **No decision production.** Assembling explanations is not producing WS3 constitutional decisions.
8. **No audit re-recording.** Assembling explanations is not producing or re-validating WS4 constitutional audit records.
9. **Implementation independence.** No stack, vendor, or reference implementation becomes law by proximity to an explanation.
10. **Explanations ≠ legislation.** Constitutional explanations never amend constitutional specifications.
11. **Explanations ≠ decisions.** Constitutional explanations never become substitute constitutional decisions.
12. **Explanations ≠ audits.** Constitutional explanations never become substitute constitutional audit records.
13. **No authority substitution.** A published explanation never replaces the corpus for the next act.
14. **No authority creation.** Explanation never creates constitutional authority.
15. **No bypass.** Product urgency never waives required explainability components or completed-audit prerequisites.
16. **Repeatability.** Operator-dependent or ambient-only explanation that cannot be reconstructed is a defect.
17. **Explain the stop.** Students, developers, and auditors should hear when explanation must refuse.
18. **No emergency exemption.** Deadlines and demos never mint constitutional amendment, re-decision, or re-audit power via the Explainability Architecture.
19. **Fidelity before publication.** Invalid constructed explanations must not be published as constitutionally valid.
20. **Upstream honesty.** Upstream constitutional audit records remain audit records; explanation never rewrites them into new law, new dispositions, or new audits.
21. **Downstream-only.** The Explainability Architecture remains entirely downstream of constitutional auditing.

## 6. Relationship to Upstream Audit and Downstream Consumers

Completed constitutional audit records produced under WS4 (CAA-05 / CAAC completion) are **inputs** to this Explainability Architecture. The Explainability Architecture may:

- read reconstructable completed constitutional audit records;
- bind explanations to published constitutional authority already named in the completed audit records and published corpora;
- refuse to explain when audit records are incomplete or boundary-violating.

The Explainability Architecture must never:

- re-execute constitutional rules under an explanation label;
- re-produce constitutional decisions under an explanation label;
- re-record or re-validate constitutional audits under an explanation label;
- treat explanations as a licence to reinterpret EIP;
- treat published explanations as constitutional amendments;
- elevate a preferred stack because an explanation was published on it;
- invent independent governance dispositions outside published constitutional authority;
- create constitutional authority by proximity to a published explanation.

Downstream product narration, UI, Twin, and Adaptive surfaces may consume published constitutional explanations. They inherit no legislative, executive, decisive, auditing, or independent-governance power from proximity to a published explanation.
