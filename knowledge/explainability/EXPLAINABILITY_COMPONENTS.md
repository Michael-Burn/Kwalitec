## 1. Purpose

Explanation without a closed component catalogue invents law by proximity: whichever services, queues, UI strings, or “ambient” product logs happen to finish become the tutor’s “proof the system explained.”

This catalogue names the only lawful constitutional explainability architecture components a run may apply — and binds each component to constitutional purpose, inputs, outputs, and permitted / prohibited responsibilities.

---

## 2. Catalogue Overview

| ID | Component | Constitutional purpose (short) | Primary inputs | Primary outputs |
|----|-----------|--------------------------------|----------------|-----------------|
| **CEA-01** | Audit Intake | Admit only completed, reconstructable constitutional audit records | Published WS4 completed audit records + completion identity | Admitted audit-record set (or lawful refuse) |
| **CEA-02** | Explanation Constructor | Assemble a candidate constitutional explanation from admitted audit records | Admitted audit records + authority references | Candidate constitutional explanation |
| **CEA-03** | Provenance Resolver | Resolve reconstructable constitutional provenance supporting the explanation | Admitted audit records + upstream lineage identities | Provenance resolution bound to the candidate |
| **CEA-04** | Fidelity Validator | Confirm the candidate explanation is constitutionally faithful | Candidate explanation + provenance + published fidelity expectations | Fidelity disposition (valid / invalid / refuse) |
| **CEA-05** | Explanation Publisher | Publish valid constitutional explanations for transparency and understanding | Validated explanation + state records | Published constitutional explanation |
| **CEA-06** | Explanation State Manager | Maintain speakable explanation-state transitions for the run | Component progress signals | Explanation-state record (not educational / decision / audit / independent-governance state) |

Material constitutional explanation must map to one or more of these components as published law requires. Cross-cutting situations may bind multiple CEA components; none may invent a component outside this catalogue.

**Relation to WS1:** CECX / CECR / CECC prepare, resolve, and complete context. CEA never redefines preparation catalogues and never begins against incomplete preparation disguised as audit records.

**Relation to WS2:** CEE / CEEL / CEEC execute rules, organise execution, and complete execution into outcomes. CEA never re-executes constitutional rules and never redefines engine catalogues.

**Relation to WS3:** CDA / CDAL / CDAC produce, organise, and complete constitutional decisions. CEA never re-produces decisions and never redefines decision catalogues.

**Relation to WS4:** CAA / CAAL / CAAC produce, organise, and complete constitutional audit records. CEA consumes *completed audit records*; it never re-records audits and never redefines audit catalogues.

**Relation to Programme VIII / IX catalogues:** Those catalogues remain defined solely by Programmes VIII / IX. CEA may *cite published identities as provenance* under constitutional authority; it does not replace, re-run, or independently invent them.

**Relation to educational quality:** No CEA component judges whether learning was good, whether a tip was wise, or whether the student is ready. Explanation ≠ quality. Constitutional explanation ≠ constitutional decision ≠ constitutional audit ≠ constitutional amendment.

## 3. CEA-01 — Audit Intake

### Constitutional purpose

Admit only **completed, reconstructable constitutional audit records** as lawful inputs to explanation — so the Explainability Architecture knows *which completed audit records may be consumed* without inventing audit records, completing unfinished audit production, or beginning against incomplete publication.

### Constitutional inputs

- Published WS4 constitutional audit records whose required audit lifecycle has lawfully completed (WS4 / MS003 fulfilment confirmed where required by published law).
- Audit-record identities, audit-run identity, and reconstructable traces sufficient to bind explanations.
- Published CEAO-01 / CEAO-02 / CEAO-05 expectations.

### Constitutional outputs

- Admitted audit-record set (references and reconstructable content as published).
- Explicit exclusion of incomplete, unpublished, or invented audit records.
- Honest refuse when required completed audit records cannot be admitted without invention.

### Permitted responsibilities

- Verify completion identity / publication status of candidate audit records under WS4 completion law.
- Admit reconstructable completed audit records for downstream CEA components.
- Record which audit records were admitted and which were refused.
- Refuse intake when audit records are incomplete, unpublished, or non-reconstructable.

### Prohibited responsibilities

- Invent unpublished audit records or “implied house filings.”
- Complete unfinished WS4 audit production under an intake label.
- Re-record or re-validate constitutional audits to manufacture missing inputs.
- Re-produce constitutional decisions or execute constitutional rules to manufacture missing audit records.
- Soft-amend audit-record content to make explanation convenient.
- Begin intake against ambient product logs, UI strings, or Twin narratives presented as completed constitutional audit records.

## 4. CEA-02 — Explanation Constructor

### Constitutional purpose

Assemble a **candidate constitutional explanation** from admitted completed audit records and governing authority references — so a speakable explanation artefact exists for provenance resolution and fidelity validation without pretending construction alone confers validity, legislation, decision production, audit re-recording, or independent governance.

### Constitutional inputs

- Admitted audit-record set from CEA-01.
- Named constitutional authority references governing the explained activities.
- Constitutional activities already bound into the admitted audit records (decisions, outcomes, authority citations as published).
- Published CEAO-01 / CEAO-02 / CEAO-05 expectations.

### Constitutional outputs

- Candidate constitutional explanation: reconstructable draft citing admitted audit records, explained activities, and authority.
- Explicit non-claim: construction is not publication of a validated explanation.
- Honest incomplete construction note when required elements cannot be assembled without invention.

### Permitted responsibilities

- Compose candidate explanations from admitted audit records exactly as published explainability law warrants.
- Bind the candidate to admitted audit-record identities, explained activities, and authority references.
- Preserve reconstructable construction traces for provenance resolution, fidelity validation, and explainability of explainability.
- Refuse construction when admitted audit records are insufficient under published law.

### Prohibited responsibilities

- Amend constitutional specifications in construction prose.
- Reinterpret EIP to invent a preferred candidate explanation.
- Produce constitutional decisions or re-disposition completed decisions under a construction label.
- Re-record or re-validate constitutional audits under a construction label.
- Execute constitutional rules as part of construction.
- Publish the candidate as valid without CEA-04 fidelity validation.
- Elevate construction machinery into constitutional authority.

## 5. CEA-03 — Provenance Resolver

### Constitutional purpose

Resolve **reconstructable constitutional provenance** supporting the candidate explanation — so lineage from completed audit records (and decision / outcome / authority identities already named therein) remains speakable without inventing pedigree theatre or soft-amending upstream artefacts.

### Constitutional inputs

- Admitted audit-record set from CEA-01.
- Candidate explanation from CEA-02 (or construction-in-progress binding as published law requires).
- Upstream lineage identities already reconstructable from completed audit records and published corpora (decisions, outcomes, authority citations as applicable).
- Published CEAO-02 / CEAO-03 / CEAO-05 expectations.

### Constitutional outputs

- Provenance resolution bound to the candidate explanation.
- Explicit exclusion of invented or non-reconstructable lineage.
- Honest refuse when required provenance cannot be resolved without invention.

### Permitted responsibilities

- Resolve reconstructable provenance links from admitted audit records to the candidate explanation.
- Cite authority and upstream identities already available through completed audit records and published corpora.
- Preserve provenance traces for fidelity validation and explainability of explainability.
- Refuse provenance resolution when required lineage is incomplete or non-reconstructable.

### Prohibited responsibilities

- Invent unpublished provenance chains or “implied house lineage.”
- Soft-amend upstream audit records, decisions, outcomes, or authority citations to manufacture convenient provenance.
- Reinterpret EIP under provenance “clarification” labels.
- Treat product analytics, Twin narratives, UI strings, or ambient logs as constitutional provenance without published identity.
- Use provenance resolution as a licence to re-produce decisions, execute rules, or re-record audits.

## 6. CEA-04 — Fidelity Validator

### Constitutional purpose

Confirm that a candidate constitutional explanation is **constitutionally faithful** — that it cites completed audit records, preserves meaning and provenance, names governing authority, and preserves Explainability Architecture boundaries — before any publication as a lawful constitutional explanation.

### Constitutional inputs

- Candidate constitutional explanation from CEA-02.
- Provenance resolution from CEA-03.
- Admitted audit-record set.
- Published fidelity expectations under CEAO-02 and [`EXPLAINABILITY_BOUNDARIES.md`](EXPLAINABILITY_BOUNDARIES.md).
- Published CEAO-01 / CEAO-02 / CEAO-05 expectations.

### Constitutional outputs

- Fidelity disposition: valid / invalid / refuse (with reconstructable reasons).
- Explicit boundary-integrity confirmation or named boundary defect.
- Honest refuse when fidelity validation cannot proceed without inventing unpublished criteria.

### Permitted responsibilities

- Check that required audit-record identities, explained activities, provenance links, and authority references are present and consistent.
- Confirm the candidate does not claim rule execution, decision production, audit re-recording, EIP reinterpretation, specification amendment, authority replacement, or authority creation.
- Record fidelity disposition reconstructably.
- Block publication of invalid candidates as constitutionally valid.

### Prohibited responsibilities

- Soft-pass invalid candidates for demo continuity.
- Invent unpublished fidelity criteria.
- Re-execute constitutional rules, re-produce constitutional decisions, or re-record audits as a substitute for fidelity validation.
- Treat CI greens, UI ticks, Twin narratives, or ambient logs as fidelity under constitutional law.
- Amend specifications so a candidate becomes “valid.”

## 7. CEA-05 — Explanation Publisher

### Constitutional purpose

Publish **validated constitutional explanations** for transparency and constitutional understanding — so lawful explanations become available to consumers without upgrading them into constitutional amendments, EIP replacements, re-decisions, re-audits, stack freezes, or independent governance outside constitutional authority.

### Constitutional inputs

- Validated constitutional explanation (CEA-04 disposition = valid).
- Provenance resolution from CEA-03.
- Explanation-state records from CEA-06 as required for reconstructability.
- Published CEAO-01 / CEAO-02 / CEAO-03 / CEAO-04 / CEAO-05 expectations.

### Constitutional outputs

- Published constitutional explanation (identity, cited audit records, explained activities, resolved provenance, cited authority, fidelity confirmation).
- Explicit non-claims: not new law; not a re-decision; not a re-audit; not EIP replacement; not stack freeze; not independent governance outside authority; not creation of constitutional authority.
- Honest non-publication when fidelity is not valid.

### Permitted responsibilities

- Publish only fidelity-validated constitutional explanations.
- Preserve reconstructable publication records for transparency and understanding.
- Make published explanations available to lawful downstream consumers.
- Refuse publication when fidelity validation failed or required traces are missing.

### Prohibited responsibilities

- Publish invalid or unvalidated candidates as constitutionally valid.
- Soft-amend published explanation content after fidelity validation without a new lawful cycle.
- Present publication as constitutional amendment, certification seal, re-decision, re-audit, or EIP replacement.
- Freeze a preferred stack as law because an explanation was published on it.
- Suppress publication of lawful refuse / invalid dispositions when honesty requires them to be speakable.

## 8. CEA-06 — Explanation State Manager

### Constitutional purpose

Maintain **speakable explanation-state transitions** for the Explainability Architecture run — so progress is reconstructable without inventing educational state, minting independent governance dispositions, re-producing decisions, re-recording audits, or treating state machinery as constitutional authority.

### Constitutional inputs

- Progress signals from CEA-01…CEA-05.
- Published explanation-state vocabulary for this architecture (intake → construction → provenance → fidelity → publication / refuse).
- Published CEAO-01 / CEAO-04 / CEAO-05 expectations.

### Constitutional outputs

- Explanation-state record: ordered transitions with timestamps / identities as published law requires.
- Explicit distinction from educational state, from WS3 decision state, from WS4 audit state, and from Programme IX governance dispositions.
- Honest stop-state when the run refuses or invalidates.

### Permitted responsibilities

- Record lawful state transitions corresponding to published CEA component progress.
- Expose state for explainability of explainability and explanation reconstruction.
- Mark refuse / invalid terminal states when explanation cannot lawfully continue.
- Keep state vocabulary closed and published.

### Prohibited responsibilities

- Invent educational state (mastery, readiness, continuity mutations) under explanation-state labels.
- Mint independent governance dispositions (compliance, certification, verification) as explanation state.
- Re-produce constitutional decisions, execute constitutional rules, or re-record audits via state machinery.
- Use state transitions to waive required components or completed-audit prerequisites.
- Treat state machinery as constitutional authority or as a licence to amend specifications.
- Hide refuse / invalid states to preserve demo continuity.

## 9. Composition Rules

1. **Closed catalogue.** Only CEA-01…CEA-06 may be applied.
2. **Intake first.** CEA-02…CEA-05 may not lawfully proceed without CEA-01 admission (or lawful refuse).
3. **Provenance before intact publication.** CEA-05 may publish as valid only after CEA-03 provenance resolution and CEA-04 valid disposition.
4. **Fidelity before publish.** CEA-05 may publish as valid only after CEA-04 valid disposition.
5. **State accompanies the run.** CEA-06 records progress; it never substitutes for missing components.
6. **No parallel invention.** Ambient product workflows, UI strings, or Twin narratives are not constitutional components unless mapped to this catalogue.
7. **Cross-binding allowed.** A material concern may require multiple components; it may not invent a seventh component identity.
8. **Refuse is a lawful product.** Incomplete / invalid / refused runs remain constitutionally preferable to improvised explanations.
9. **Downstream-only.** CEA components never participate in WS2 execution, WS3 decision production, or WS4 audit recording.
