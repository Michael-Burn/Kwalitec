## Purpose

This folder defines **the permanent constitutional mechanism responsible for producing faithful explanations of completed constitutional activity**.

It answers *how completed constitutional activities are lawfully explained*, *what explainability must optimise*, *which recognised explainability components perform that explanation*, *what the Explainability Architecture may and must never do*, and *how constitutional explanations themselves are explained* — without implementing Runtime A, executing constitutional rules, producing constitutional decisions, reinterpreting Educational Interpretation Principles, amending constitutional specifications, creating constitutional authority, or becoming a second constitution.

It does **not** implement Runtime A, test runners, CI/CD pipelines, GitHub Actions, Python, Flask, SQLAlchemy, REST, OpenAPI, infrastructure, or application services.

> **The Constitutional Explainability Architecture consumes completed constitutional audit records and produces constitutional explanations.  
> It never executes constitutional rules.  
> It never produces constitutional decisions.  
> It never reinterprets Educational Interpretation Principles.  
> It never amends constitutional specifications.  
> It never creates constitutional authority.**

Hard separation:

> **Programmes VI–IX publish constitutional law and governance judgements.  
> WS1 prepares, resolves, and completes the constitutional execution context.  
> WS2 executes published constitutional rules against that completed context and produces completed execution outcomes.  
> WS3 transforms those completed execution outcomes into constitutional decisions.  
> WS4 records those completed constitutional decisions into constitutional audit records.  
> This Constitutional Explainability Architecture Model assembles those completed constitutional audit records into constitutional explanations while preserving meaning and provenance.  
> Constitutional explanations are not constitutional decisions, constitutional amendments, EIP reinterpretations, re-execution of rules, audit re-recording, or independent governance outside constitutional authority.  
> The Explainability Architecture remains subordinate to the Constitution, Educational Interpretation Principles, Constitutional Specifications, and Constitutional Governance.  
> It remains entirely downstream of constitutional auditing.**

## Architectural requirement

The Constitutional Explainability Architecture explains **completed constitutional activity** while remaining entirely **downstream of constitutional auditing**.

| Lawful | Unlawful |
|--------|----------|
| Consume completed constitutional audit records | Begin explanation against incomplete or unpublished audit records |
| Assemble constitutional explanations | Execute constitutional rules (WS2 engine responsibility) |
| Preserve constitutional meaning | Produce constitutional decisions (WS3 Decision Architecture responsibility) |
| Preserve provenance | Re-record or re-validate constitutional audits (WS4 Audit Architecture responsibility) |
| Preserve implementation neutrality | Reinterpret Educational Interpretation Principles |
| Produce constitutional explanations | Amend constitutional specifications |
| Publish constitutional explanations | Create constitutional authority |
| Explain audit consumed / activities / provenance / explanation / boundaries | Elevate explainability machinery into a constitutional producer, executor, auditor, or decision engine |

**The Constitutional Explainability Architecture explains completed constitutional activity while remaining entirely downstream of constitutional auditing.  
It never participates in execution, decision production, constitutional governance or constitutional authority.**

## How to use this corpus

1. Confirm completed constitutional audit records exist under WS4 / MS001–MS003 — refuse explanation theatre against incomplete audit production.
2. Read `CONSTITUTIONAL_EXPLAINABILITY_ARCHITECTURE_MODEL.md` for stack position and integrity rules.
3. Optimise under `EXPLAINABILITY_OBJECTIVES.md`.
4. Explain under `EXPLAINABILITY_COMPONENTS.md` (CEA-01…CEA-06 only).
5. Enforce hard stops under `EXPLAINABILITY_BOUNDARIES.md`.
6. Require explainability-of-explainability contracts from `EXPLAINABILITY_EXPLAINABILITY.md` before student-, developer-, or auditor-facing explanation narration.
7. Do not implement explainability machinery that contradicts this corpus without amending it first — and do not treat constitutional explanations as constitutional execution, decision production, audit re-recording, EIP replacement, or constitutional amendment.
