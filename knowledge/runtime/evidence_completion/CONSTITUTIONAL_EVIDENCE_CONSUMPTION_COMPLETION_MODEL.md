## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not confuse “the evidence was lawfully handled” with “the student has mastered the syllabus,” nor treat a finished storage read as finished learning, nor rewrite the syllabus because a warrant passed through cleanly. After the Constitution and EIP have defined *what Educational Evidence is*, after Programme VI and VII have published *which warrants and orchestration artefacts may exist*, after Programme VIII WS1 has defined *which contracts authorise execution*, and after WS2 / MS001–MS002 have defined *how evidence is consumed and validated*, the platform still needs one evidence-handling completion answer:

> **“When has runtime fulfilled its constitutional responsibilities for consuming published evidence?”**

That answer must confirm that required validation, authorised consumption, provenance preservation, required execution hand-off, and required evidence records are satisfied — with no outstanding evidence-handling duties for that published concern — while remaining fully explainable, auditable, and deterministic — without implying educational success, learner progress, evidence quality, workflow completion, or constitutional modification.

This document records that posture so every future Runtime A (and successor) subsystem has a single constitutional reference for *when software evidence-handling concludes lawfully*.

> **The Constitutional Evidence Consumption Completion Model describes fulfilment of constitutional *evidence-handling* responsibilities.  
> It does not evaluate educational success, certify learner progress, judge evidence quality, claim Programme VII workflow completion, or amend constitutional law.  
> It does not implement Runtime A, storage engines, databases, services, queues, APIs, UI, analytics, or algorithms.**

## 2. What Constitutional Evidence Consumption Completion Is

**Constitutional evidence consumption completion** is the constitutional judgement that runtime has **fulfilled its published constitutional responsibilities for consuming published evidence** — such that no outstanding *evidence-handling* duties remain for that published concern under WS2 / MS001–MS002 and applicable RC bindings — without claiming that any educational, quality, orchestration, or constitutional-authorship question was resolved by the close itself.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Evidence-handling concern** | A bounded constitutional unit of evidence work opened under published EC / EV / RC law (receive → validate → consume → hand off → record) | What evidence handling is being assessed? |
| **Completion warrant** | Judgement that evidence-handling fulfilment may be assessed | May we ask whether this handling is complete? |
| **Completion criteria** | Constitutional conditions that must hold (`COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md`) | What must be true for fulfilment? |
| **Completion judgement** | Affirmed / not yet / refused (no lawful close) | Is evidence consumption complete? |
| **Evidence record** | Published audit / continuity artefacts that reconstruct the handling | Can the close be audited? |
| **Boundary preservation record** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden reinterpretations / modifications / overclaims did not occur | Did constitutional integrity survive close? |

Constitutional evidence consumption completion is:

- **evidence-handling-scoped** — it concerns fulfilment of published evidence duties, not educational outcomes or evidence quality;
- **catalogue-bound** — fulfilment references EC-01…EC-07 instances actually received / consumed;
- **validation-honest** — fulfilment requires required EV-01…EV-07 outcomes under MS002 where applicable;
- **contract-bound** — fulfilment requires satisfied RC-01…RC-07 obligations applicable to the handling (especially RC-02);
- **criteria-gated** — fulfilment requires stated constitutional conditions (`COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md`);
- **provenance-preserving** — origin, claim class, and succession survive close intact;
- **integrity-preserving** — meaning, classification, and constitutional text remain unchanged by the close itself;
- **record-bound** — fulfilment cites published evidence / consumption records, not timers or ack theatre;
- **deterministic** — the same published handling facts + same published law ⇒ the same completion judgement class;
- **non-certifying** — completion never certifies mastery, progress, evidence quality, workflow fulfilment, or law change;
- **explainable** — students and developers can see which evidence handling completed and what was preserved;
- **continuity-safe** — completion does not erase learner-owned educational history (EIP-005);
- **implementation-independent** — stores, queues, and frameworks are delivery details, not completion law.

Constitutional evidence consumption completion is **not**:

- educational success, learner mastery, progress, or exam readiness;
- a judgement that evidence is educationally *good*, *strong*, *sufficient for a pass*, or *high quality*;
- Programme VII workflow completion (orchestration fulfilment remains that corpus’s job);
- Programme VIII / WS1 runtime execution completion (execution-cycle fulfilment remains that corpus’s job);
- Programme VI educational completion (recovery / revision / planning / etc. where defined);
- reinterpretation or modification of Educational Evidence under a “consumption done” label;
- transfer of authority, minting of tips, invention of EST/CST postures, or amendment of constitutional specifications;
- a claim that elapsed time, latency, retries, cache hits, or broker acknowledgements equal fulfilment;
- a UI celebration, analytics milestone, or storage “done” flag as educational or constitutional law;
- a licence to skip outstanding published evidence-handling responsibilities because a product surface looks finished.

## 4. Completion Lifecycle (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material evidence-handling concern that claims lawful completion travels through constitutional assessment phases. These are **normative judgement obligations**, not an implementation architecture.

```
Lawful evidence handling opened under WS2 / MS001–MS002 (+ applicable RCs)
        │
        ▼
Assess required validation outcomes
        │
        ▼
Assess authorised consumption & provenance preservation
        │
        ▼
Assess required execution hand-off & evidence records
        │
        ├── not yet / refuse close (outstanding responsibilities or failed criteria)
        │
        └── Affirm evidence consumption completion
                │
                ▼
        Preserve audit continuity + explain the close
```

| Phase | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **Open warrant** | Confirm published EC instance(s) entered lawful receive / validate / consume under MS001–MS002 | No lawful handling; completion theatre for never-started work |
| **Validation assessment** | Confirm required EV outcomes completed under published law | Required validation missing, forged, or still open |
| **Consumption & provenance assessment** | Confirm authorised consumption completed; provenance intact | Consumption abandoned; provenance erased / forged; evidence altered |
| **Hand-off & record assessment** | Confirm required authorised hand-off and published evidence records exist | Missing hand-off; missing RC-07 / consumption trails |
| **Affirm & explain** | Emit completion judgement with explainability and continuity | Explanation would invent success, quality, workflow close, or law change |

> **Completion never “best-effort invents” a shortcut called product-done.**

Evidence consumption completion is the terminal judgement for a published evidence-handling concern — not a parallel constitution and not a substitute for consumption or validation law.

| Completion concern | Primary upstream binding |
|--------------------|--------------------------|
| Lawful handling existence | WS2 / MS001 receive / consume law + WS2 / MS002 validation law + applicable RC-01…RC-07 |
| Validation honesty | EV-01…EV-07 outcomes required for the contemplated act |
| Consumption honesty | EC-01…EC-07 instances consumed exactly as published under MS001 |
| Provenance honesty | Producer, claim class, succession / continuity refs preserved |
| Hand-off honesty | Authorised constitutional processes received the evidence under published contracts |
| Record honesty | RC-07 Audit Contract (+ EC-07 / EV-07 when applicable) |
| Integrity at close | MS001 `EVIDENCE_BOUNDARIES.md` + MS002 `VALIDATION_BOUNDARIES.md` + this corpus |

A handling concern may lawfully complete with a **refuse / defer / escalate** disposition when that disposition is itself a published evidence-handling outcome and required records exist. Unlawful improvisation that “looks finished” is never completion.

## 7. Educational / Constitutional Purpose

The Constitutional Evidence Consumption Completion Model exists so that:

1. **Evidence handling closes honestly** — runtime does not hang indefinitely after published evidence duties are done, nor declare complete while published responsibilities remain.
2. **Completion stays non-educational** — closing evidence handling never becomes a mastery, progress, or pass ceremony.
3. **Completion stays non-quality** — finishing handling never silently becomes a judgement that evidence was educationally “good enough.”
4. **Completion stays non-orchestrational** — finishing evidence handling never silently absorbs Programme VII workflow-completion rights.
5. **Authority and law survive close** — finishing handling never quietly amends corpora, rewrites warrants, or transfers ownership.
6. **Audits remain possible** — same published handling facts ⇒ same completion judgement class and reconstructable trail.
7. **Explainability spans the full arc** — students and developers can see which evidence handling finished and what was preserved.
8. **Runtimes remain replaceable** — completion law outlives any particular store, queue, or service topology.
