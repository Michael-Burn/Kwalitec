## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which constitutional evidence* was consumed — which validations were satisfied, which contracts authorised completion, which execution responsibilities were fulfilled, and which limits were preserved — is not closing honestly; they are performing.

After Programmes VI and VII have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, and orchestration fulfilment speakable, and after WS1 / WS2 MS001–MS002 have made contracts, consumption, and validation speakable, Programme VIII Workstream 2 still needs one completion-speech answer:

> **“Which constitutional evidence was consumed, under which validations, authorised by which contracts, fulfilling which execution responsibilities, while preserving which boundaries?”**

That answer must cover every material affirmed (or refused) completion without inventing educational success, judging evidence quality, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting evidence, claiming workflow close, or modifying constitutional law.

---

## 2. What Evidence Consumption Completion Explainability Is

**Constitutional Evidence Consumption Completion Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **fulfilment (or lawful refusal of fulfilment) of published evidence-handling responsibilities** — not to invent a second tutor voice from storage exits or celebration UI.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Evidence consumed** | Named EC-01…EC-07 instance(s) (and upstream warrant / artefact ID when applicable) | Which constitutional evidence was consumed? |
| **Validations satisfied** | Named EV-01…EV-07 outcomes required for the handling | Which constitutional validations were satisfied? |
| **Contracts authorising completion** | Named RC-01…RC-07 binding(s) whose obligations were met for the handling | Which constitutional contracts authorised completion? |
| **Execution responsibilities fulfilled** | Named published evidence-handling responsibilities / hand-offs exhausted for the concern | Which execution responsibilities were fulfilled? |
| **Boundaries preserved** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden reinterpretations / modifications / overclaims did not occur | Which constitutional boundaries were preserved? |

Evidence Consumption Completion Explainability is:

- **fulfilment-centred** — it narrates why ECC criteria hold (or why close is refused);
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published EC / EV / RC identities where applicable;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published producers and path law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip / educational-completion speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / workflow-completion speech, WS1 execution-completion speech, or MS001 / MS002 consumption / validation speech;
- **non-inventive** — explanation never becomes a back door to mint meaning, tips, mastery, evidence quality, workflow close, or law change;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match RC-07 reconstruction needs;
- **determinism-honest** — explanations must not paper over race-dependent completion flags.

Evidence Consumption Completion Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Programme VI educational meaning or educational-completion explainability;
- a substitute for Programme VII workflow-completion explainability;
- a substitute for WS1 execution-completion explainability;
- a substitute for MS001 evidence-consumption or MS002 validation explainability corpora;
- a rendering system, UI template, log shipper, broker console, or analytics dashboard;
- a claim that clear completion speech guarantees learning, a pass, or evidence quality;
- a licence to present scores, timers, acks, cache hits, or optimiser confidence as constitutional proof.

## 3. Mandatory Explanation Questions

Every material completion judgement (affirm or refuse close) should answer all five questions:

### ECCQ-01 — Which constitutional evidence was consumed?

Name the primary EC-01…EC-07 category(ies) and instance identity(ies). When an upstream warrant or Programme VII artefact applies, also name it (for example EC-01 ← EIP-002 observation class; EC-03 ← workflow completion evidence ID). If completion is refused, name the evidence that was assessed and state that fulfilment was not affirmed. Unpublished payload names are not constitutional evidence.

### ECCQ-02 — Which constitutional validations were satisfied?

Name the EV-01…EV-07 outcomes required for the handling (or which failed when refusing close). If a published refuse / defer / escalate validation disposition exhausted remaining duties, say so explicitly. “Schema happy” is not a constitutional validation.

### ECCQ-03 — Which constitutional contracts authorised completion?

Name the primary RC-01…RC-07 contract(s) whose obligations were met for the handling (or which failed when refusing close). Cross-cutting concerns name all bound contracts (for example RC-02 + RC-05 + RC-07). “Table was readable” is not a constitutional contract.

### ECCQ-04 — Which execution responsibilities were fulfilled?

Name the published evidence-handling responsibilities exhausted for the concern: authorised consumption acts, required hand-offs to constitutional processes, and required record creation — or name which remain outstanding when refusing close. Do not collapse this answer into WS1 REC execution-cycle speech or Programme VII workflow-completion speech.

### ECCQ-05 — Which constitutional boundaries were preserved?

Confirm the hard stops in `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md` that were checked — especially non-reinterpretation of evidence, non-modification of evidence, non-implication of educational success, non-implication of workflow completion, and non-alteration of constitutional specifications.

## 5. Audience Rules

| Audience | May hear | Must not hear as educational or constitutional law |
|----------|----------|----------------------------------------------------|
| **Student** | That the system finished the constitutional evidence-handling steps it was allowed to take, what happened next (if anything), and what was *not* being claimed — in plain educational language | Internal ECC/EC/EV/RC IDs as theatre; “you have mastered this”; “this evidence proves you are ready”; “your study plan is complete” from evidence-handling close alone; broker / cache traces; optimiser scores as “proof you are ready” |
| **Developer / auditor** | Full ECCQ-01…05 answers, corpus paths, EC/EV/RC IDs, ECC criteria map, boundary checks, determinism / continuity notes, provenance | Product success metrics, ack counts, cache hits, or latency SLOs substituted for constitutional refs; silent collapse into Programme VII workflow-completion or WS1 REC speech |

Student and developer traces must describe the **same constitutional truth**. Vocabulary may differ; facts may not.
