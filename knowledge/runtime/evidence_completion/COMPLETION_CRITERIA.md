## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor needs bright conditions for saying “this constitutional evidence work is finished” — and equally bright refusals when it is not. After MS001 has named how evidence is consumed and MS002 has named how evidence is validated, Programme VIII Workstream 2 still needs one fulfilment checklist:

> **What must be true before runtime may affirm that its constitutional responsibilities for consuming published evidence are fulfilled?**

These criteria answer that question. They are **normative constitutional conditions**, not database predicates, API status codes, cache keys, or job-queue exit semantics.

---

## 2. Criterion Catalogue

All material affirmed completions require **ECC-01 through ECC-05** to hold. Failure of any criterion ⇒ **not yet** or **refuse close**. Do not invent a partial “mostly complete” educational, quality, or constitutional status.

| ID | Criterion | Primary question |
|----|-----------|------------------|
| **ECC-01** | Required validation completed | Were required EV outcomes completed under published law? |
| **ECC-02** | Authorised consumption completed | Was published evidence consumed exactly as authorised? |
| **ECC-03** | Provenance preserved | Did producer / claim / succession refs survive intact? |
| **ECC-04** | Required execution hand-off completed | Was evidence handed only to authorised constitutional processes? |
| **ECC-05** | Required evidence records created | Do reconstructable constitutional evidence records exist? |

Optional supporting honesty checks (do not replace ECC-01…ECC-05):

| ID | Check | Primary question |
|----|-------|------------------|
| **ECC-06** | Contract honesty | Were applicable RC-01…RC-07 obligations (especially RC-02) satisfied? |
| **ECC-07** | Continuity honesty | Was prior lawful history preserved (EIP-005)? |
| **ECC-08** | Non-claim honesty | Does the judgement refuse educational / quality / workflow / amendment overclaim? |

## 3. Criterion Definitions

### ECC-01 — Required validation completed

**Definition.** Every validation category (EV-01…EV-07) required by published law for the contemplated consumption / execution has either:

1. completed with a published eligibility disposition that authorises the subsequent consumption acts actually performed; or  
2. terminated in a **published** refuse / defer / escalate disposition that exhausts the concern’s remaining *evidence-handling* duties for that instance.

**Requires:**

- Identifiable EV outcomes mapped to the EC instance(s) under assessment.
- Evidence that required validation steps were executed or lawfully stopped under MS002.
- Refusal of “skipped validation but storage looked fine” as completion.

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- That the evidence is educationally strong, sufficient, or high quality.
- That the learner understood, progressed, or mastered anything.
- That a Programme VII workflow instance is orchestration-complete.
- That constitutional law was updated.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who applies a warrant without checking that it is still the warrant law authorised has not finished lawful evidence care — even if the desk looks tidy.

---

### ECC-02 — Authorised consumption completed

**Definition.** Published constitutional evidence instances (EC-01…EC-07) belonging to the concern have been consumed **exactly as published** under MS001 — under applicable RC bindings — or the concern lawfully stopped under published refusal rules when authorised consumption was impossible.

**Requires:**

- Named EC-01…EC-07 identities (and upstream warrant / artefact IDs when applicable).
- Consumption acts match published authorised paths; no unpublished EC customs were treated as constitutional evidence.
- Absence of required authorised consumption acts (where law required them) blocks completion — unless a published refuse / defer / escalate disposition exhausted remaining duties.

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- That consumption caused educational success.
- That consuming a warrant completed a coach’s educational job.
- That consuming orchestration evidence closed a Programme VII workflow.
- That the evidence was reinterpreted into a “better” form to make consumption succeed.

**Tutor rationale.** Completing evidence care includes doing the authorised consumption acts — not inventing souvenir consumption, and not declaring done while the authorised acts never happened.

---

### ECC-03 — Provenance preserved

**Definition.** Producer identity, claim class, succession / continuity references, and corpus paths required by MS001 / MS002 / EIP-002 / EIP-005 remain intact through validation, consumption, hand-off, and close — without forgery, erasure, or silent replacement.

**Requires:**

- Provenance reconstructable for every material EC instance in the concern.
- No completion path that “fixes” missing provenance by inventing it.
- Continuity across retries / replacements does not invent amnesia as fulfilment.

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- A particular database schema, lineage product, or analytics pipeline.
- That preserving provenance equals learning.
- That a complete provenance trail certifies mastery, evidence quality, or workflow close.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later say where a warrant came from has not closed evidence handling honestly — even if the student has left the room.

---

### ECC-04 — Required execution hand-off completed

**Definition.** Evidence belonging to the concern has been handed only to **authorised constitutional processes** under published contracts and mutation rights — and every required hand-off for the concern’s published path has occurred (including explicit lawful-stop hand-offs when the path refused / deferred / escalated).

**Requires:**

- Hand-off targets are corpus-authorised (for example coach envelopes, Twin succession under EIP-001, workflow engines under RC-03, audit trails under RC-07) — never improvised sinks.
- No unpublished tip mint, ownership transfer, state invention, or evidence rewrite occurred under the label “hand-off.”
- Absence of required published hand-offs blocks completion.

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- That the receiving process finished *its* educational, orchestration, or execution-cycle job.
- That hand-off implies educational success or evidence quality.
- That hand-off amends constitutional specifications.

**Tutor rationale.** Completing evidence care includes delivering the warrant only where law already said it may go — not scattering it into convenient product sinks, and not declaring done while the authorised recipient never received it.

---

### ECC-05 — Required evidence records created

**Definition.** Published evidence / consumption / validation records required by RC-07 (and EC-07 / EV-07 when applicable) exist and are sufficient to reconstruct the concern’s constitutional trail.

**Requires:**

- Records identify the evidence instances, validation outcomes, authorising contracts, hand-offs, and boundary preservation.
- Continuity across retries / replacements does not erase prior lawful record obligations (EIP-005).
- Technical logs without constitutional references do **not** satisfy this criterion.

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- A particular database schema, log shipper, or analytics pipeline.
- That recording equals learning or evidence quality.
- That a complete audit trail certifies mastery or workflow close.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later reconstruct what evidence was handled has not closed the educational machine honestly — even if the product looks finished.

## 4. Supporting Honesty Checks

### ECC-06 — Contract honesty

Every RC-01…RC-07 contract applicable to the concern’s material evidence-handling acts has been satisfied for those acts — including RC-02 when observational Educational Evidence is at stake — or the concern lawfully stopped under published refusal rules when satisfaction was impossible.

### ECC-07 — Continuity honesty

Affirming completion must not erase, silently rewrite, or orphan prior lawful educational / constitutional history (EIP-005). Replacement handling re-enters at published points; it does not invent amnesia as fulfilment.

### ECC-08 — Non-claim honesty

The completion judgement must refuse overclaims:

| Forbidden overclaim | Lawful statement |
|---------------------|------------------|
| Learner success / mastery / progress | Evidence-handling responsibilities for this concern are fulfilled |
| Evidence quality / educational sufficiency | Validation and consumption completed under published eligibility law — quality was not judged |
| Programme VII workflow complete | Evidence consumption for this concern is complete (orchestration fulfilment requires Programme VII corpus) |
| WS1 execution-cycle complete | Evidence-handling fulfilment is orthogonal to REC-xx execution completion |
| Constitutional amendment occurred | No constitutional specification was modified by this close |
| Evidence reinterpreted / modified by close | Evidence remains exactly as published producers left it |

---

## 5. Judgement Classes

| Judgement | When | Effect |
|-----------|------|--------|
| **Affirm evidence consumption complete** | ECC-01…ECC-05 hold (and ECC-06…ECC-08 honesty checks pass where applicable) | Concern may be recorded as evidence-consumption-complete under this Model |
| **Not yet** | One or more criteria fail because published duties remain in progress under law | Do not affirm; continue published path or await published inputs |
| **Refuse close** | Criteria fail because the handling was unlawful, abandoned, or would require invention to “look done” | Do not affirm; escalate / remediate under published governance — never invent fulfilment |

There is no fourth judgement called “educationally complete because evidence was handled,” nor “evidence was high quality because consumption finished.”

---

## 6. Explicit Non-Criteria

The following are **never** sufficient (and often never relevant) as completion criteria:

- elapsed time, wall-clock duration, or SLA success;
- process exit codes, HTTP 200, cache hits, or broker acknowledgements;
- queue depth zero or “no messages remaining”;
- UI spinner stopped / celebration animation shown;
- Adaptive scores, Twin estimates, engagement metrics, or streak theatre;
- presence of *any* log line mentioning “complete” or “validated”;
- evidence-quality scores or Educational Validation Framework release ticks as substitutes for ECC-01…ECC-05;
- Programme VII workflow completion alone (orthogonal; may co-occur but does not replace ECC-01…ECC-05);
- Programme VIII / WS1 execution completion alone (orthogonal; REC-xx never substitutes for ECC-xx);
- Programme VI educational completion alone (orthogonal; never inferred from evidence-handling close);
- majority vote of services, feature-flag theatre, or demo urgency.

Honest systems keep these judgements named and separate.

## 8. Closing Statement

> **Constitutional evidence consumption is complete only when required validation, authorised consumption, provenance preservation, required execution hand-off, and required evidence records are satisfied.  
> Anything less is unfinished constitutional evidence work.  
> Anything more — learning success, evidence quality, workflow close, or law change — is a different judgement owned elsewhere.**
