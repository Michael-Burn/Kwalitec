## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “rows drained,” “validators green,” or “green checkmarks shown.” The tutor optimises **honest closing of constitutional evidence work**: when the published evidence-handling duties for a concern are done, the tutor can say so — without pretending the student has mastered the topic, without pretending the warrant was educationally “good enough,” without pretending a whole educational workflow is finished, and without rewriting the syllabus because the machinery stopped cleanly.

These objectives bind every Runtime A evidence-consumption completion behaviour and every successor runtime that claims constitutional compliance.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### ECCO-01 — Confirm lawful evidence handling

**Definition.** Ensure that runtime affirms evidence consumption completion only when a published evidence-handling concern under WS2 / MS001–MS002 (and applicable RC bindings) has fulfilled its constitutional evidence-handling responsibilities — or honestly refuses close when fulfilment criteria are not met.

**Includes:**

- Assessing only concerns that lawfully opened under EC-01…EC-07 receive / validate / consume law and applicable RC-01…RC-07 bindings (especially RC-02).
- Affirming completion only when `COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md` conditions hold.
- Preferring lawful “not yet” / refuse-close over inventing a “done enough” status for product convenience.
- Distinguishing lawful stop dispositions (refuse / defer / escalate as published outcomes) from abandoned evidence-handling duties.
- Binding every material completion judgement to reconstructable constitutional references.

**Excludes:**

- Declaring complete because a store returned rows, a cache hit, a process exited, or a UI spinner stopped.
- Treating Twin / Adaptive / Experience / Mission heuristics as completion warrants.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or engagement as a substitute for fulfilment honesty.
- Collapsing “evidence consumption complete” into “student succeeded,” “evidence was high quality,” “workflow complete,” or “constitution updated.”

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software celebrates unfinished evidence work — or celebrates finished machinery as finished learning or finished quality judgement. Honest confirmation is care; completion theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### ECCO-02 — Preserve provenance

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming evidence consumption completion never erases, forges, truncates, or replaces producer identity, claim class, succession history, or continuity references required to reconstruct where the evidence came from.

**Tutor rationale.** A close that “helpfully” drops who published a warrant — or invents a prettier origin so the audit looks tidy — destroys educational honesty even if the completion UX looks clean.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion cites preserved provenance; it does not invent it at close.
- Missing provenance blocks affirmation (see `COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md` ECC-03).
- MS001 ECO-03 and MS002 EVO provenance obligations remain binding at close.
- Affirmed completion never grants EIP-001 mutation rights by proximity to a finished handling.

---

### ECCO-03 — Preserve integrity

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming evidence consumption completion never redefines Programme VI meaning, reinterprets or modifies constitutional evidence, reclassifies warrants, invents tips, invents EST/CST postures, bypasses Programme VII workflow law, or modifies constitutional specifications.

**Tutor rationale.** A close that upgrades coverage into mastery, softens an ineligible warrant into “good enough,” or rewrites ownership because “the evidence is done” destroys constitutional layering even if storage looks settled.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion consumes published law and published evidence; it does not amend either.
- Forbidden actions in `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md` are hard stops.
- MS001 ECO-02 / MS002 EVO-02 integrity obligations remain binding at close.
- Affirmed completion never becomes a back door to reclassify EC-01 / EC-02 claim ladders.

---

### ECCO-04 — Preserve deterministic execution

**Definition.** Ensure that the same published handling facts, the same published learner constitutional inputs, and the same published corpora yield the same completion judgement class (affirm / not yet / refuse close) — absent a published non-deterministic exception (none are granted by this Model for educational, evidence-quality, or constitutional meaning).

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who sometimes declares evidence handling complete and sometimes not for the same published facts, depending on which replica woke first, is not coaching; they are gambling with educational honesty.

**Manifestations:**

- Criteria evaluation is corpus-bound and reproducible.
- Infrastructure concurrency, sharding, caching, and retry jitter must not change the completion judgement class.
- Race-dependent “evidence done” flags are constitutional defects.
- “Eventually consistent evidence-handling fulfilment” is a constitutional defect.

---

### ECCO-05 — Support auditability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material completion judgement leaves a reconstructable constitutional trail: which evidence was consumed, which validations were satisfied, which contracts authorised completion, which hand-offs and records were produced, and which boundaries were preserved.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later say *why* evidence handling was closed cannot be trusted. Completion without audit is educational amnesia dressed as cleanliness.

**Manifestations:**

- Audit records preserve constitutional references, not only technical exit codes or cache keys.
- Continuity (EIP-005) is preserved across retries, replacements, and redeploys.
- Missing provenance or missing evidence records is a defect, not an acceptable optimisation.
- RC-07 Audit Contract obligations apply to all material completion judgements.

---

### ECCO-06 — Support explainability

**Definition.** Ensure that evidence consumption completion remains speakable under EIP-003: students and developers can answer the mandatory completion explainability questions without receiving redefined educational meaning, false learning claims, false quality claims, false workflow claims, or invented constitutional amendments.

**Tutor rationale.** Opaque “evidence done” recreates black-box tutoring. Clear completion speech is part of educational honesty.

**Manifestations:**

- Explanations cite evidence identity, validations, contracts, hand-offs / responsibilities, and boundaries (`COMPLETION_EXPLAINABILITY.md`).
- Student speech and developer traces share one truth with different vocabulary.
- Scores, timers, ack counts, cache hits, and optimiser confidence are never presented as constitutional fulfilment warrant.
- Explanation never becomes a back door to invent tips, mastery, evidence quality, workflow close, or law change.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| ECCO-01 conflicts with declaring done for product theatre | Lawful confirmation wins — refuse / not-yet |
| ECCO-02 conflicts with dropping provenance to shrink logs | Provenance wins |
| ECCO-03 conflicts with convenience rewriting at close | Integrity wins |
| ECCO-04 conflicts with race-dependent completion flags | Determinism wins |
| ECCO-05 conflicts with log volume reduction that erases constitutional refs | Auditability wins |
| ECCO-06 conflicts with cryptic internal jargon as student completion speech | Explainability honesty wins |

No supporting objective authorises violating ECCO-01.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — MS001 ECO-01…ECO-05, MS002 EVO-01…EVO-05, and MS001 RCO / WS1 RECO families for the evidence-consumption-completion horizon. They remain orthogonal to WS1 execution-completion objectives (RECO-xx).

---

## 5. Non-Objectives

Evidence consumption completion does **not** optimise for:

- learner progress theatre, mastery badges, or pass certainty as a side-effect of close;
- evidence-quality scores, sufficiency theatre, or “strong enough warrant” judgements as a side-effect of close;
- collapsing Programme VII workflow completion into evidence-handling exit;
- collapsing WS1 execution-cycle completion into evidence-handling exit;
- inventing educational certainty when warrants are incomplete;
- making a particular store, queue, or service irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / broker / cache signals as substitutes for fulfilment criteria;
- treating documentation as optional commentary on “done” flags in code.

---

## 6. Closing Statement

> **A runtime that closes evidence handling quickly, quietly, and constitutionally unfaithfully is a failed educational system.  
> A runtime that affirms completion only when published evidence-handling responsibilities are fulfilled — with provenance, integrity, determinism, audit, and explainability intact — is doing its only evidence-completion job.**
