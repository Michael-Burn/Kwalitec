## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “facades composed” or “SDK methods batched.” The tutor optimises **faithful coordinated interaction**: each specialist interface keeps its identity, the student experiences one constitutional truth, history remains reconstructable, and the machinery stays replaceable.

These objectives bind every Runtime A interface composition and every successor runtime that claims constitutional compliance.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### RICO-01 — Coordinate constitutional interaction

**Definition.** Ensure that runtime interface composition arranges authorised RI-01…RI-07 interactions so that published constitutional exposures are fulfilled together when law requires composition — or honestly refuses when no published pattern and contracts authorise the coordination.

**Includes:**

- Selecting only recognised RIC-01…RIC-07 patterns for material compositions.
- Ensuring every participating interaction maps to a recognised RI interface under MS001.
- Exchanging only authorised constitutional artefacts between participants.
- Producing only authorised constitutional outputs (dispositions, artefacts, audit trails, diagnostic records).
- Preferring lawful refusal / deferral / escalation over invented composition certainty.

**Excludes:**

- Inventing unpublished composition patterns, mega-interfaces, or “implied facades” to keep the product moving.
- Optimising for latency, fan-in, or engagement as a substitute for constitutional fidelity.
- Treating Version 2 Adaptive / Twin / Experience heuristics as replacements for Programmes VI–VIII or MS001.
- Coordinating “close enough” approximations of constitutional interfaces without documenting a corpus amendment.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software collapses specialist boundaries for convenience. Composition is care; identity-collapse is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### RICO-02 — Preserve interface identity

**Definition.** Ensure that every composing RI participant retains the named identity and permitted / prohibited exposures established by the Runtime Interface Model (MS001). Composition may coordinate; it must never merge, rename-away, or absorb interface identities into an unpublished mega-interface.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who “helps” by speaking as every specialist at once has not coordinated — they have erased accountability. Interfaces that dissolve into one facade become unlawful mega-authorities.

**Manifestations:**

- Composed interactions name each participating RI distinctly in audit and explanation.
- Pattern selection never invents a new RI type by silent merger.
- Callers do not inherit callees’ exposures or expand their own prohibited lists.
- MS001 catalogue rules (composition, not absorption) remain binding at every handoff.

---

### RICO-03 — Preserve implementation independence

**Definition.** Ensure that RIC-01…RIC-07 meanings bind *coordination forms*, not languages, frameworks, protocols, SDKs, BFF layers, API gateways, or deployment topologies.

**Tutor rationale.** If “Sequential Composition” only exists as a GraphQL stitch or gRPC gateway chain, that transport has become unlawful constitutional furniture.

**Manifestations:**

- Pattern entries describe purpose, participating interfaces, permitted / prohibited composition, and outputs — never required stacks.
- REST, GraphQL, gRPC, HTTP, WebSockets, SDKs, and auth stacks are delivery options, not constitutional pattern types.
- Cross-cutting composition is described constitutionally, not as a mandatory facade architecture.

---

### RICO-04 — Preserve explainability

**Definition.** Ensure that composition remains speakable under EIP-003: students and developers can answer the constitutional composition explainability questions without receiving redefined educational meaning or merged interface identities.

**Tutor rationale.** Opaque facade graphs recreate black-box tutoring. Clear composition speech is part of educational honesty.

**Manifestations:**

- Explanations cite participating interfaces, patterns, artefacts, outputs, and preserved boundaries (`COMPOSITION_EXPLAINABILITY.md`).
- Student speech and developer traces share one truth with different vocabulary.
- Scores and optimiser confidence are never presented as constitutional warrant for composition.
- Explanation never becomes a back door to invent tips, mastery, or mega-interfaces.

---

### RICO-05 — Preserve auditability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material composition leaves a reconstructable constitutional trail: which RIC pattern applied, which RI interfaces participated, which artefacts were exchanged, which outputs were produced, and which boundaries were preserved.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor team that cannot later say *which specialist boundaries held* cannot be trusted. Composition without audit is educational amnesia with a prettier facade.

**Manifestations:**

- Audit records preserve constitutional references for the whole composition graph, not only technical hop logs.
- Continuity (EIP-005) is preserved across retries, replacements, and redeploys.
- Missing provenance on any material participant is a defect, not an acceptable optimisation.
- RI-06 Audit Interface obligations apply to composed interactions (directly or via RIC-07 Diagnostic Composition when inspection is the primary act).

---

### RICO-06 — Preserve technology neutrality

**Definition.** Ensure that no networking choice, framework, authentication stack, SDK, or gateway becomes part of the constitutional architecture. Composition law describes coordination among RI contracts; transports obey those contracts.

**Tutor rationale.** Treating HTTP aggregators, GraphQL schemas, or SDK clients as educational authorities recreates the technology lock-in Programme VIII exists to prevent.

**Manifestations:**

- Constitutional documents name RIC patterns and RI participants, not route tables or client method graphs.
- AuthN/AuthZ mechanisms may gate access operationally; they never redefine who owns educational meaning or which RI identities exist.
- Protocol migrations must preserve RIC catalogue semantics.
- Framework convenience never rewrites Programme VIII WS4 MS001 identities.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| RICO-01 conflicts with engagement or latency metrics | Constitutional coordination wins |
| RICO-02 conflicts with “one mega-API is simpler” | Interface identity preservation wins |
| RICO-03 conflicts with locking RIC meanings to one protocol facade | Implementation independence wins |
| RICO-04 conflicts with cryptic internal facade jargon as student speech | Explainability honesty wins |
| RICO-05 conflicts with log volume reduction that erases participant / artefact refs | Auditability wins |
| RICO-06 conflicts with elevating a transport into architecture law | Technology neutrality wins |

No supporting objective authorises violating RICO-01.  
No composition objective authorises violating MS001 RIO-01…RIO-06 for any participant.

---

## 5. Non-Objectives

Runtime interface composition does **not** optimise for:

- endpoint aggregation count, SDK method batching, or protocol fashion as educational success;
- inventing educational certainty when warrants are incomplete;
- collapsing Programmes VI–VIII or RI-01…RI-07 into one mega-interface;
- making any facade, gateway, or SDK irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive estimates as substitutes for constitutional producers;
- treating documentation as optional commentary on wire behaviour;
- elevating REST, GraphQL, gRPC, HTTP, WebSockets, SDKs, or auth stacks into constitutional architecture;
- transferring responsibilities “temporarily” for product convenience;
- redefining constitutional capabilities by composing convenient endpoints.

---

## 6. Closing Statement

> **A composition that is fast, elegant, and constitutionally unfaithful is a failed educational boundary.  
> A composition that preserves interface identities, remains auditable and explainable, and coordinates only published interaction contracts is doing its only job.**
