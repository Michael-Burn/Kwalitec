## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor needs bright conditions for saying “this constitutional machine work is finished” — and equally bright refusals when it is not. After MS001 has named contracts and MS002 has named event processing, Programme VIII still needs one fulfilment checklist:

> **What must be true before runtime may affirm that an execution cycle’s constitutional execution responsibilities are fulfilled?**

These criteria answer that question. They are **normative constitutional conditions**, not database predicates, API status codes, or job-queue exit semantics.

---

## 2. Criterion Catalogue

All material affirmed completions require **REC-01 through REC-05** to hold. Failure of any criterion ⇒ **not yet** or **refuse close**. Do not invent a partial “mostly complete” educational or constitutional status.

| ID | Criterion | Primary question |
|----|-----------|------------------|
| **REC-01** | Published execution path completed | Did the published path finish or lawfully stop? |
| **REC-02** | Authorised outputs produced | Were only published authorised outputs emitted? |
| **REC-03** | Required execution records created | Do reconstructable constitutional records exist? |
| **REC-04** | Constitutional contracts satisfied | Were applicable RC-01…RC-07 obligations met? |
| **REC-05** | No outstanding execution responsibilities | Are published execution duties for this cycle exhausted? |

Optional supporting honesty checks (do not replace REC-01…REC-05):

| ID | Check | Primary question |
|----|-------|------------------|
| **REC-06** | Event-processing honesty | If events belonged to the cycle, were MS002 phases closed under published law? |
| **REC-07** | Continuity honesty | Was prior lawful history preserved (EIP-005)? |
| **REC-08** | Non-claim honesty | Does the judgement refuse educational / workflow / amendment overclaim? |

## 3. Criterion Definitions

### REC-01 — Published execution path completed

**Definition.** The execution path(s) authorised for this cycle under published MS001 / MS002 / upstream corpora have either:

1. reached their published terminal step for the cycle’s concern; or  
2. terminated in a **published** refuse / defer / escalate disposition that exhausts the cycle’s remaining *execution* duties.

**Requires:**

- Identifiable published path identity (contract-bound; corpus-referenced).
- Evidence that path steps required for the cycle were executed or lawfully stopped.
- Refusal of “abandoned mid-duty but process exited” as completion.

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- That the learner understood, progressed, or mastered anything.
- That a Programme VII workflow instance is orchestration-complete.
- That constitutional law was updated.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who walks away mid-explanation because the clock rang has not completed the lesson’s lawful tutoring duties — even if the room is empty.

---

### REC-02 — Authorised outputs produced

**Definition.** Every material output produced by the cycle belongs to a **published authorised output class** for the bound contracts / event processing — and required outputs for the path have been produced (including explicit lawful-stop outputs when the path refused / deferred / escalated).

**Requires:**

- Outputs map to published classes (workflow advances, authority dispositions, recommendation artefacts / sets, EST/CST postures, audit records, refuse/defer/escalate outcomes, or other corpus-published results).
- No unpublished tips, owners, evidence classes, state postures, or event types were emitted as if authorised.
- Absence of required published outputs blocks completion.

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- That outputs caused educational success.
- That surfacing a tip completed a coach’s educational job.
- That producing an audit record amended the Constitution.

**Tutor rationale.** Completing a tutoring act includes producing the authorised artefacts of that act — not inventing souvenir outputs, and not declaring done while the authorised artefacts never appeared.

---

### REC-03 — Required execution records created

**Definition.** Published execution records required by RC-07 (and CE-06 when audit events apply) exist and are sufficient to reconstruct the cycle’s constitutional trail.

**Requires:**

- Records identify the cycle / path, authorising contracts, consumed artefacts (with claim honesty), produced outputs, and boundary preservation.
- Continuity across retries / replacements does not erase prior lawful record obligations (EIP-005).
- Technical logs without constitutional references do **not** satisfy this criterion.

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- A particular database schema, log shipper, or analytics pipeline.
- That recording equals learning.
- That a complete audit trail certifies mastery or workflow close.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later reconstruct what was attempted has not closed the educational machine honestly — even if the student has left the room.

---

### REC-04 — Constitutional contracts satisfied

**Definition.** Every RC-01…RC-07 contract applicable to the cycle’s material acts has been satisfied for those acts — including evaluation, execution limits, and audit obligations — or the cycle lawfully stopped under published refusal rules when satisfaction was impossible.

**Requires:**

- Primary and cross-cutting RC bindings named for the cycle.
- No silent skipping of applicable contracts.
- Boundary obligations from MS001 / MS002 observed through the cycle’s end.

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- Satisfaction of contracts invents new educational meaning.
- Satisfying RC-03 equals Programme VII workflow completion.
- Satisfying RC-05 equals Programme VI tip warrant success.

**Tutor rationale.** Contracts are the published permissions for software tutoring acts. Declaring the act complete while ignoring the permissions is forgery.

---

### REC-05 — No outstanding execution responsibilities

**Definition.** For this cycle’s published constitutional concern, no material *execution* responsibility remains outstanding under MS001 / MS002 / upstream published path law.

**Requires:**

- Residual published steps, required outputs, required records, or pending published evaluations for *this cycle* are absent or explicitly deferred to a **new** published cycle / lawful await — not silently dropped.
- Concurrent duties belonging to **other** cycles are not falsely treated as residuals of this cycle (and vice versa).
- “Outstanding” is judged by published law, not by product backlog taste.

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- That all future educational work for the learner is finished.
- That all open Programme VII workflows are closed.
- That the Constitution needs no further amendment ever.

**Tutor rationale.** Completion is the absence of remaining *this-cycle* duties — not the absence of the student’s entire exam journey.

## 4. Supporting Honesty Checks

### REC-06 — Event-processing honesty

When the cycle included constitutional events, MS002 receive → evaluate → execute (and record) obligations for those events must be closed under published law. Reception alone never satisfies cycle completion.

### REC-07 — Continuity honesty

Affirming completion must not erase, silently rewrite, or orphan prior lawful educational / constitutional history (EIP-005). Replacement cycles re-enter at published points; they do not invent amnesia as fulfilment.

### REC-08 — Non-claim honesty

The completion judgement must refuse overclaims:

| Forbidden overclaim | Lawful statement |
|---------------------|------------------|
| Learner success / mastery / progress | Execution responsibilities for this cycle are fulfilled |
| Programme VII workflow complete | Runtime execution for this cycle is complete (orchestration fulfilment requires Programme VII corpus) |
| Constitutional amendment occurred | No constitutional specification was modified by this close |
| Evidence reclassified by close | Evidence classifications remain as published writers left them |

---

## 5. Judgement Classes

| Judgement | When | Effect |
|-----------|------|--------|
| **Affirm execution complete** | REC-01…REC-05 hold (and REC-06…REC-08 honesty checks pass where applicable) | Cycle may be recorded as execution-complete under this Model |
| **Not yet** | One or more criteria fail because published duties remain in progress under law | Do not affirm; continue published path or await published inputs |
| **Refuse close** | Criteria fail because the cycle was unlawful, abandoned, or would require invention to “look done” | Do not affirm; escalate / remediate under published governance — never invent fulfilment |

There is no fourth judgement called “educationally complete because runtime finished.”

---

## 6. Explicit Non-Criteria

The following are **never** sufficient (and often never relevant) as completion criteria:

- elapsed time, wall-clock duration, or SLA success;
- process exit codes, HTTP 200, or broker acknowledgements;
- queue depth zero or “no messages remaining”;
- UI spinner stopped / celebration animation shown;
- Adaptive scores, Twin estimates, engagement metrics, or streak theatre;
- presence of *any* log line mentioning “complete”;
- Programme VII workflow completion alone (orthogonal; may co-occur but does not replace REC-01…REC-05);
- Programme VI educational completion alone (orthogonal; never inferred from runtime close);
- majority vote of services, feature-flag theatre, or demo urgency.

Honest systems keep both judgements named and separate.

---

## 8. Closing Statement

> **Runtime execution is complete only when published paths, authorised outputs, required records, and applicable contracts are satisfied — and no outstanding execution responsibilities remain for the cycle.  
> Anything less is unfinished constitutional work.  
> Anything more — learning success, workflow close, or law change — is a different judgement owned elsewhere.**
