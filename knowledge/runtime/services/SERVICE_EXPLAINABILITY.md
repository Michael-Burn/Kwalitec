## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *why* a study action happened — which capability executed, which contracts authorised it, what evidence was used, what resulted, and what limits were preserved — is not coaching; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–VII and Programme VIII WS1 / WS2 have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, events, and evidence speakable, Programme VIII WS3 still needs one service-speech answer:

> **“What constitutional service executed, under which contracts and evidence, producing which outputs, while preserving which boundaries?”**

That answer must cover every material runtime service act without inventing educational meaning, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, or rewriting evidence.

---

## 2. What Runtime Service Explainability Is

**Runtime Service Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **execution of published responsibilities by named RS capabilities** — not to invent a second tutor voice or elevate a technology stack into educational law.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Service executed** | Named RS-01…RS-07 capability (and compositions) | Which constitutional service executed? |
| **Contracts authorising** | Named RC-01…RC-07 binding(s) | Which constitutional contracts authorised execution? |
| **Evidence consumed** | Educational Evidence / warrants / published conditions actually used | Which constitutional evidence was consumed? |
| **Outputs produced** | Authorised execution records, dispositions, surfaced artefacts, audit trails | Which constitutional outputs were produced? |
| **Boundaries preserved** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden redefinitions / inventions / bypasses did not occur | Which constitutional boundaries were preserved? |

Runtime Service Explainability is:

- **capability-centred** — it narrates which RS service ran under law;
- **contract-bound** — every justification cites RC authorisation;
- **evidence-honest** — claim classes survive packaging into speech;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, or Programme VIII contract / evidence speech;
- **non-inventive** — explanation never becomes a back door to mint meaning, tips, or mastery;
- **technology-silent** — explanation never treats Python, Flask, DI, microservices, REST, queues, or databases as constitutional warrant;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match RS-07 / RC-07 reconstruction needs.

Runtime Service Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Programme VI educational meaning explainability;
- a substitute for workflow, authority, recommendation, state, contract, or evidence explainability corpora;
- a rendering system, UI template, log shipper, or analytics dashboard;
- a claim that clear service speech guarantees learning or a pass;
- a licence to present scores or optimiser confidence as constitutional proof.

## 3. Mandatory Explanation Questions

Every material runtime service execution should answer all five questions:

### RSEQ-01 — Which constitutional service executed?

Name the primary RS-01…RS-07 service(s). Cross-cutting acts name all composed services (for example RS-04 + RS-05 + RS-07).

### RSEQ-02 — Which constitutional contracts authorised execution?

Cite the RC-01…RC-07 binding(s). “Service was available,” “queue delivered,” or “endpoint was called” are not constitutional authorisation.

### RSEQ-03 — Which constitutional evidence was consumed?

Distinguish:

- EIP-002 Educational Evidence of understanding (when actually used);
- Programme VI warrants / artefacts;
- Programme VII orchestration, ownership, recommendation, or context facts;
- published condition evaluations;
- WS2 validation eligibility outcomes when they gated consumption.

Never present coverage, time, engagement, or Twin estimates as understanding evidence unless the Evidence Model permits that classification.

### RSEQ-04 — Which constitutional outputs were produced?

Name only published output classes: execution dispositions, workflow events, authority dispositions, recommendation artefacts / sets, EST/CST postures, evidence-consumption records, audit records, or explicit refuse/defer/escalate outcomes.

### RSEQ-05 — Which constitutional boundaries were preserved?

Confirm the hard stops in `SERVICE_BOUNDARIES.md` that were checked — especially non-redefinition of meaning, authority, evidence, tips, state, specifications, and non-bypass of contracts.

## 5. Audience Rules

| Audience | May hear | Must not hear as educational law |
|----------|----------|----------------------------------|
| **Student** | What happened next, why it was allowed, what evidence supported it, and what limits apply — in plain educational language | Internal RS/RC IDs as theatre; stack traces; optimiser scores as “proof you are ready” |
| **Developer / auditor** | Full RSEQ-01…05 answers, corpus paths, service IDs, contract IDs, boundary checks, provenance, composition graph | Product success metrics or framework names substituted for constitutional refs |

Student and developer traces must describe the **same constitutional truth**. Vocabulary may differ; facts may not.

A complete material journey may carry every layer that applied. Service speech without upstream warrant is hollow. Upstream warrant without service identity invites silent reimplementation drift.

## 9. Out of Scope

This milestone does **not** implement:

- explanation renderers or UI copy systems;
- logging pipelines or analytics products;
- Runtime A services;
- Python classes, Flask services, DI, microservices, REST APIs, queues, schedulers, workers, or databases;
- prompt templates or LLM narration layers.

Those may later *obey* this explainability law. They do not *define* it.
