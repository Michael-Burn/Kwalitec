This document is the constitutional overview of the **Constitutional Evidence Consumption Model** for Kwalitec.

It is subordinate to the Educational Constitution, Educational Interpretation Principles (EIP) — especially EIP-002 Educational Evidence Model — Programme VI educational meaning corpora, Programme VII orchestration corpora, and Programme VIII Workstream 1 Runtime Contract, Event Processing, and Execution Completion Models. ## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not invent a performance record because the UI needs a badge, quietly upgrade coverage into mastery because a dashboard looks empty, or forge yesterday’s warrant because today’s tip wants a stronger story. After the Constitution and EIP have defined *what Educational Evidence is*, after Programme VI and VII have published *which warrants and orchestration artefacts may exist*, and after Programme VIII WS1 has defined *which contracts authorise execution*, the platform still needs one consumption answer:

> **“How is constitutional evidence lawfully received and consumed by runtime implementations?”**

That answer must ensure runtime implementations consume only published evidence, preserve integrity and provenance, support deterministic and auditable execution, and stay fully explainable — without creating, modifying, reinterpreting, or reclassifying constitutional evidence.

This document records that posture so every future Runtime A (and successor) subsystem has a single constitutional reference for *how software may consume constitutional evidence*.

> **The Constitutional Evidence Consumption Model describes constitutional reception and consumption of published evidence.  
> It does not create educational meaning, invent ownership, mint tips, invent evidence classes, or implement Runtime A.**

## 2. What Constitutional Evidence Consumption Is

**Constitutional evidence consumption** is the binding obligation by which a **runtime implementation** may **receive published constitutional evidence**, **validate its integrity and provenance**, and **pass it only to authorised constitutional processes** — without becoming a source of educational truth or a mint of new evidence law.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Constitutional evidence** | A published warrant, observation, orchestration artefact, ownership record, recommendation trail, context posture, or runtime execution record whose meaning originates in Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / Programme VII / Programme VIII WS1 corpora | What was consumed? |
| **Constitutional producer** | The corpus / permitted writer that published the evidence class and its meaning | Who authored the evidence? |
| **Constitutional consumer** | The runtime implementation bound to consume the evidence under RC contracts | Who consumes? |
| **Reception** | Acceptance of a published evidence instance into constitutional consumption | Was it published and intact? |
| **Integrity validation** | Checking that the evidence instance has not been altered, truncated, or stripped of meaning / provenance contrary to published law | Is the warrant honest? |
| **Provenance preservation** | Keeping producer, claim class, succession history, and continuity references intact through consumption | Can origin be reconstructed? |
| **Authorised handoff** | Passing evidence only to processes already authorised by published contracts and mutation rights | Who may lawfully receive it next? |
| **Deterministic consumption** | Same published evidence facts and same published law yield the same consumption disposition | Is improvisation forbidden? |

Constitutional evidence consumption is:

- **law-subordinate** — runtime consumes published evidence; it never invents it;
- **producer-respecting** — every material consumption cites a constitutional producer;
- **contract-bound** — every material consumption maps to RC-01…RC-07 (especially RC-02) as applicable;
- **catalogue-closed** — only EC-01…EC-07 may be consumed as constitutional evidence categories;
- **integrity-preserving** — meaning, classification, and claim honesty survive consumption;
- **provenance-preserving** — origin and succession are not fabricated or erased;
- **audit-capable** — every material consumption leaves reconstructable constitutional traces;
- **explainable** — students and developers can answer which evidence was consumed under which contract;
- **implementation-independent** — storage, queues, and frameworks are delivery details, not evidence law.

Constitutional evidence consumption is **not**:

- a second Educational Evidence Model (EIP-002 remains the law for observational understanding warrants);
- a Programme VII completion-evidence author (orchestration evidence remains Programme VII);
- a licence to invent unpublished EC types, reclassify coverage as mastery, or forge provenance;
- a storage engine, database schema, message queue, analytics pipeline, or UI surface;
- a claim that successful consumption guarantees learning or a pass;
- a replacement for RC-02 — RC-02 authorises consumption; this Model specialises *how* published evidence categories are received and consumed.

## 3. Consumption Pipeline (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material constitutional evidence instance travels through three constitutional phases. These are **normative consumption obligations**, not an implementation architecture.

```
Receive published constitutional evidence
        │
        ▼
Validate integrity / provenance / category / authorising contract
        │
        ├── refuse / defer / escalate (lawful stop)
        │
        └── Consume / hand off to authorised constitutional processes
                │
                ▼
        Preserve provenance and produce published consumption records
```

| Phase | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **Receive** | Accept only EC-01…EC-07 instances whose class and producer are published | Class is unpublished, forged, or infrastructure-only without constitutional mapping |
| **Validate** | Confirm integrity, provenance, claim-class honesty, and RC binding | Integrity broken; provenance missing/forged; no authorising contract; boundary check fails |
| **Consume** | Apply evidence exactly as published; hand off only to authorised processes; emit published records | Path would invent, alter, reclassify, fabricate provenance, or bypass evidence requirements |

> **Consumption never “best-effort invents” a fourth phase called reinterpretation.**

## 6. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Evidence Consumption Model exists so that:

1. **Evidence remains upstream of code** — software consumes published warrants; it does not quietly become the warrant.
2. **Claim honesty survives delivery** — coverage ≠ understanding ≠ mastery remains intact under load and iteration.
3. **Provenance survives packaging** — origin and succession can be reconstructed after handoff.
4. **Audit remains possible** — every material consumption can be reconstructed against producers, contracts, and outputs.
5. **Determinism remains honest** — the same published evidence under the same published law yields the same disposition.
6. **Explainability remains intact** — consumption speech describes what was received and used; it does not redefine what learning means.
7. **Runtimes remain replaceable** — constitutional evidence law outlives any particular storage or service topology.
