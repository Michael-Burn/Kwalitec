## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “rows read” or “caches warmed.” The tutor optimises **faithful educational honesty**: the student experiences only warrants that published law already authorised — intact, attributable, claim-class honest, and reconstructable.

These objectives bind every Runtime A evidence-consumption behaviour and every successor runtime that claims constitutional compliance.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### ECO-01 — Consume published constitutional evidence

**Definition.** Ensure that runtime receives and consumes only published constitutional evidence categories (EC-01…EC-07) whose producers and meanings originate in the Constitution, EIP, Programme VI, Programme VII, or Programme VIII WS1 — or honestly refuse when no published evidence mapping authorises consumption.

**Includes:**

- Accepting only catalogue evidence with identifiable constitutional producers.
- Binding every material consumption to RC-01…RC-07 as applicable (RC-02 when Educational Evidence of understanding is at stake).
- Mapping Programme VII orchestration evidence artefacts into EC-03 consumption under published law — without inventing rival learning meaning.
- Preferring lawful refusal / deferral / escalation over inventing an “almost EC” for storage or product convenience.
- Preserving claim-ladder honesty when EC-01 / EC-02 learning and assessment evidence is consumed.

**Excludes:**

- Inventing unpublished evidence classes “to keep the product moving.”
- Treating Twin estimates, Adaptive scores, UI ticks, cache payloads, or analytics events as constitutional evidence without a published EC mapping.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or engagement as a substitute for constitutional fidelity.
- Executing “close enough” approximations of evidence law without documenting a corpus amendment.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software invents educational warrants from convenience. Consumption is care; improvisation is harm.

---

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### ECO-02 — Preserve evidence integrity

**Definition.** Ensure that constitutional evidence meaning, classification, and claim honesty survive reception, validation, handoff, and audit — so runtime never silently alters, truncates, or softens published warrants.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who quietly upgrades “attempted” into “mastered” because a dashboard looks thin has abandoned educational care for product theatre.

**Manifestations:**

- Evidence is consumed exactly as published; meaning is not rewritten in transit.
- Forbidden actions in `EVIDENCE_BOUNDARIES.md` are hard stops.
- Claim ladder (coverage ≠ understanding ≠ mastery) survives consumption (EIP-006 / EIP-002).
- MS001 RCO-02 / RCO-03 integrity obligations remain binding during evidence consumption.

---

### ECO-03 — Preserve provenance

**Definition.** Ensure that producer identity, claim class, succession history, continuity references, and authorising corpus paths remain intact and attributable through consumption — never fabricated, stripped, or replaced with operational metadata.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who cannot say *where yesterday’s warrant came from* cannot be trusted today. Provenance loss is educational amnesia.

**Manifestations:**

- Producer and corpus references survive packaging into runtime and audit records.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing prior lawful evidence history to simplify UX.
- Missing or forged provenance is a defect, not an acceptable optimisation.
- Storage keys, cache hits, and delivery acks are never substitutes for constitutional provenance.

---

### ECO-04 — Support deterministic execution

**Definition.** Ensure that the same published evidence instance facts, the same published learner constitutional inputs, and the same published corpora yield the same consumption disposition (consume / hand off / refuse / defer / escalate) — absent a published non-deterministic exception (none are granted by this Model for educational meaning).

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who treats the same warrant differently depending on which replica woke first is not coaching; they are gambling.

**Manifestations:**

- Classification and validation are corpus-bound and reproducible.
- Infrastructure concurrency, sharding, and cache races must not change constitutional disposition.
- Explicit multi-evidence warrant sets use published conflict / authority / claim-honesty rules, not random reader races.
- “Eventually consistent educational evidence meaning” is a constitutional defect.

---

### ECO-05 — Support auditability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material evidence consumption leaves a reconstructable constitutional trail: which EC was consumed, which RC / corpus authorised consumption, which provenance was preserved, which outputs relied on the evidence, and which boundaries were maintained.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later say *which warrant* supported yesterday’s advice cannot be trusted. Evidence consumption without audit is educational amnesia.

**Manifestations:**

- Audit records preserve constitutional references, not only technical read logs.
- Continuity (EIP-005) is preserved across retries, replacements, and redeploys.
- Missing provenance or missing EC/RC identity is a defect.
- RC-07 Audit Contract obligations apply to all material EC consumption.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| ECO-01 conflicts with reading an unpublished artefact | Constitutional consumption wins — refuse / remap under amendment |
| ECO-02 conflicts with convenience rewriting inside a reader | Integrity wins |
| ECO-03 conflicts with stripping refs to shrink payloads | Provenance wins |
| ECO-04 conflicts with race-dependent reader outcomes | Determinism wins |
| ECO-05 conflicts with log volume reduction that erases constitutional refs | Auditability wins |

No supporting objective authorises violating ECO-01.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — MS001 RCO-01…RCO-06 and RC-02 obligations for the evidence-consumption horizon.

---

## 5. Non-Objectives

Constitutional evidence consumption does **not** optimise for:

- storage throughput, cache hit rate, or query concurrency as educational success;
- inventing educational certainty when warrants are incomplete;
- collapsing EC categories into one mega-blob that owns meaning;
- making a particular database or store irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics signals as substitutes for constitutional producers;
- treating documentation as optional commentary on storage behaviour.

---

## 6. Closing Statement

> **A runtime that consumes evidence quickly, quietly, and constitutionally unfaithfully is a failed educational system.  
> A runtime that consumes only published evidence, with integrity, provenance, determinism, and audit intact, is doing its only evidence-consumption job.**
