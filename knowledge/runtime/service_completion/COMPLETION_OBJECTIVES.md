## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “saga finished,” “bus drained,” or “green mesh checkmarks shown.” The tutor optimises **honest closing of coordinated constitutional work**: when the published collaboration duties among specialists are done, the tutor can say so — without pretending the student has mastered the topic, without pretending a lone execution cycle or whole educational workflow is finished, without rewriting who owns which coaching duty because the handoffs stopped cleanly, and without elevating an orchestration technology into law.

These objectives bind every Runtime A collaboration-completion behaviour and every successor runtime that claims constitutional compliance.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### RSCCO-01 — Confirm lawful collaboration

**Definition.** Ensure that runtime affirms collaboration completion only when a published collaboration under MS001 / MS002 has fulfilled its constitutional collaboration responsibilities — or honestly refuses close when fulfilment criteria are not met.

**Includes:**

- Assessing only collaborations that lawfully opened under RS-01…RS-07 participants, RSC-01…RSC-07 patterns, and RC-01…RC-07 authorisation.
- Affirming completion only when `COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md` conditions hold.
- Preferring lawful “not yet” / refuse-close over inventing a “coordinated enough” status for product convenience.
- Distinguishing lawful stop dispositions (refuse / defer / escalate as published collaborative outcomes) from abandoned collaboration duties.
- Binding every material completion judgement to reconstructable constitutional references.

**Excludes:**

- Declaring complete because a bus drained, a saga coordinator exited, DI resolved, or a UI spinner stopped.
- Treating Twin / Adaptive / Experience / Mission heuristics as collaboration completion warrants.
- Optimising for latency, fan-out, or engagement as a substitute for fulfilment honesty.
- Collapsing “collaboration complete” into “execution succeeded,” “student succeeded,” “workflow complete,” or “constitution updated.”

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software celebrates unfinished coordination — or celebrates finished mesh choreography as finished learning or finished tutoring duties. Honest confirmation is care; collaboration-completion theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### RSCCO-02 — Preserve service responsibilities

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming collaboration completion never redefines, transfers, absorbs, or merges the permitted and prohibited responsibilities established by the Runtime Service Model (MS001) for any participant.

**Tutor rationale.** A close that “helpfully” lets the initiator keep the specialist’s duty, or invents a mega-service because the saga ended, destroys the RS catalogue even if the completion UX looks tidy.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion consumes MS001 responsibilities; it does not amend them.
- Forbidden actions in `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md` are hard stops — especially ownership transfer and responsibility merger.
- MS001 RSO / boundary obligations and MS002 RSCO-02 remain binding at close.
- Affirmed completion never grants EIP-001 mutation rights or new RS catalogue entries by proximity to a finished collaboration.

---

### RSCCO-03 — Preserve execution determinism

**Definition.** Ensure that the same published collaboration facts — participating services, RSC pattern, contracts, exchanged artefacts, learner constitutional inputs as classified, and published corpora — yield the same completion judgement class (affirm / not yet / refuse close) — absent a published non-deterministic exception (none are granted by this Model for educational or constitutional meaning).

**Tutor rationale.** A coach team that sometimes declares coordination complete and sometimes not for the same published facts, depending on which worker woke first or which bus message won, is not coaching; they are gambling with educational honesty under composed load.

**Manifestations:**

- Criteria evaluation is corpus-bound and reproducible.
- Infrastructure concurrency, sharding, retry jitter, and race-dependent “saga winners” must not change the completion judgement class.
- Race-dependent “collaboration done” flags are constitutional defects.
- “Eventually consistent collaboration fulfilment” is a constitutional defect.

---

### RSCCO-04 — Preserve auditability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material collaboration completion judgement leaves a reconstructable constitutional trail: which services collaborated, which RSC pattern completed, which contracts authorised the acts, which artefacts were exchanged, which collaboration records were created, and which boundaries were preserved.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor team that cannot later say *why* coordinated work was closed cannot be trusted. Collaboration completion without audit is educational amnesia with more moving parts.

**Manifestations:**

- Audit records preserve constitutional references for the whole collaboration graph, not only technical hop logs or orchestrator exit codes.
- Continuity (EIP-005) is preserved across retries, replacements, and redeploys.
- Missing provenance on any material participant is a defect, not an acceptable optimisation.
- RS-07 / RSC-07 Audit Collaboration obligations apply to all material collaboration completion judgements.

---

### RSCCO-05 — Preserve implementation independence

**Definition.** Ensure that RSCC completion meanings bind *fulfilment of published collaboration responsibilities*, not languages, frameworks, DI containers, message buses, process topologies, storage engines, saga engines, or API styles.

**Tutor rationale.** If “collaboration complete” only exists as a Kafka topic drained or a Temporal workflow succeeded, the orchestrator has become unlawful constitutional furniture.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion criteria describe constitutional conditions — never required stacks.
- Orchestration engines, DI, microservices, REST, queues, and workers are delivery options, not completion law.
- Replaceability of Runtime A (and successors) survives any particular collaboration topology.

---

### RSCCO-06 — Support explainability

**Definition.** Ensure that runtime service collaboration completion remains speakable under EIP-003: students and developers can answer the mandatory collaboration-completion explainability questions without receiving redefined educational meaning, false learning claims, false execution claims, false workflow claims, redistributed authority, or invented constitutional amendments.

**Tutor rationale.** Opaque “collaboration done” recreates black-box tutoring with a mesh costume. Clear completion speech is part of educational honesty.

**Manifestations:**

- Explanations cite participants, patterns, contracts, artefacts, and boundaries (`COMPLETION_EXPLAINABILITY.md`).
- Student speech and developer traces share one truth with different vocabulary.
- Scores, hop counts, ack counts, DI resolution, and optimiser confidence are never presented as constitutional fulfilment warrant.
- Explanation never becomes a back door to invent tips, mastery, execution success, workflow close, responsibility merger, or law change.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| RSCCO-01 conflicts with declaring done for product theatre | Lawful confirmation wins — refuse / not-yet |
| RSCCO-02 conflicts with “one mega-service is simpler after close” | Responsibility preservation wins |
| RSCCO-03 conflicts with race-dependent collaboration-done flags | Determinism wins |
| RSCCO-04 conflicts with log volume reduction that erases participant / artefact refs | Auditability wins |
| RSCCO-05 conflicts with locking completion meanings to one orchestrator | Implementation independence wins |
| RSCCO-06 conflicts with cryptic mesh jargon as student completion speech | Explainability honesty wins |

No supporting objective authorises violating RSCCO-01.  
No collaboration-completion objective authorises violating MS001 RSO-01…RSO-06 for any participant, or MS002 RSCO-01…RSCO-06 for the collaboration being closed.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — MS001 service objectives, MS002 collaboration objectives, and sibling Programme VIII completion objectives for the *collaboration-completion* horizon.

---

## 5. Non-Objectives

Runtime service collaboration completion does **not** optimise for:

- learner progress theatre, mastery badges, or pass certainty as a side-effect of close;
- collapsing Runtime Execution Completion (REC) or Evidence Consumption Completion (ECC) into collaboration close;
- collapsing Programme VII workflow completion into collaboration mesh exit;
- inventing educational certainty when warrants are incomplete;
- making a particular orchestrator, bus, DI graph, or service topology irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / broker signals as substitutes for fulfilment criteria;
- transferring ownership “temporarily” because collaboration ended;
- treating documentation as optional commentary on “collaboration done” flags in code.

---

## 6. Closing Statement

> **A runtime that closes collaborations quickly, quietly, and constitutionally unfaithfully is a failed educational system.  
> A runtime that affirms collaboration completion only when published collaboration responsibilities are fulfilled — with responsibilities, determinism, audit, independence, and explainability intact — is doing its only collaboration-completion job.**
