> **This Runtime Service Collaboration Completion Model (Programme VIII / Workstream 3 / MS003) defines when published constitutional collaboration responsibilities among participating services are fulfilled.**  
> Downstream Runtime A, product surfaces, Twin, Adaptive, and narration must consume this completion law — never become constitutional authors by declaring “collaboration done,” and never collapse collaboration close into learning, execution success, orchestration fulfilment, responsibility redistribution, or law change.

## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not confuse “the specialists finished their coordinated handoffs” with “the student has mastered the syllabus,” nor treat a finished collaboration graph as a finished curriculum, nor rewrite who owns which coaching duty because the handoffs closed cleanly. After the Constitution and EIP have defined *educational truth*, after Programme VI has defined *what educational questions mean*, after Programme VII has defined *how orchestration flows*, after Programme VIII WS1 / WS2 have defined *contracts, events, execution completion, and evidence handling*, after WS3 / MS001 has defined *which execution capabilities may exist*, and after MS002 has defined *how those capabilities may lawfully collaborate*, the platform still needs one collaboration-completion answer:

> **“When have collaborating runtime services fulfilled their published constitutional collaboration responsibilities?”**

That answer must confirm that participating services fulfilled their assigned collaboration responsibilities, the authorised RSC pattern completed, required constitutional artefacts were exchanged, collaboration records exist, and no outstanding collaboration duties remain — while remaining fully explainable, auditable, and deterministic — without implying execution success, educational success, workflow completion, or constitutional modification, and without redefining or merging service responsibilities.

This document records that posture so every future Runtime A (and successor) subsystem has a single constitutional reference for *when collaborative coordination among named RS capabilities concludes lawfully*.

> **The Runtime Service Collaboration Completion Model describes fulfilment of constitutional *collaboration* responsibilities.  
> It does not evaluate execution success, educational success, certify learner progress, claim Programme VII workflow completion, redefine service responsibilities, or amend constitutional law.  
> It does not implement Runtime A, orchestrators, buses, DI, microservices, REST APIs, queues, schedulers, workers, databases, analytics, or UI.**

## 2. What Runtime Service Collaboration Completion Is

**Runtime service collaboration completion** is the constitutional judgement that a **material collaboration** among **authorised RS-01…RS-07 services** under a **recognised RSC-01…RSC-07 pattern** has **fulfilled its published constitutional collaboration responsibilities** — such that no outstanding *collaboration* duties remain for that collaboration under MS001 responsibilities and MS002 pattern / boundary law — without claiming that any educational, execution-cycle, orchestration, or constitutional-authorship question was resolved by the close itself.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Collaboration instance** | A bounded constitutional unit of coordinated RS work opened under published RSC / RC / RS law | What collaboration is being assessed? |
| **Completion warrant** | Judgement that collaboration fulfilment may be assessed | May we ask whether this collaboration is complete? |
| **Completion criteria** | Constitutional conditions that must hold (`COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md`) | What must be true for fulfilment? |
| **Completion judgement** | Affirmed / not yet / refused (no lawful close) | Is collaboration complete? |
| **Collaboration record** | Published audit / continuity artefacts that reconstruct the collaboration | Can the close be audited? |
| **Boundary preservation record** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden transfers / mergers / overclaims / amendments did not occur | Did constitutional integrity survive close? |

Runtime service collaboration completion is:

- **collaboration-scoped** — it concerns fulfilment of published coordination responsibilities, not educational outcomes or lone-cycle execution success;
- **participant-bound** — fulfilment requires named RS-01…RS-07 participants that kept their MS001 responsibilities;
- **pattern-bound** — fulfilment requires a recognised RSC-01…RSC-07 pattern that completed under published law;
- **contract-bound** — fulfilment requires satisfied RC obligations applicable to the collaborative acts;
- **artefact-honest** — fulfilment requires required published constitutional artefacts to have been exchanged;
- **criteria-gated** — fulfilment requires stated constitutional conditions (`COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md`);
- **record-bound** — fulfilment cites published collaboration records, not hop counts or bus acks;
- **responsibility-preserving** — close never redefines, transfers, or merges MS001 duties;
- **integrity-preserving** — meaning, ownership, evidence, tips, state, and constitutional text remain unchanged by the close itself;
- **deterministic** — the same published collaboration facts + same published law ⇒ the same completion judgement class;
- **non-certifying** — completion never certifies mastery, progress, execution success, workflow fulfilment, or law change;
- **explainable** — students and developers can see which services collaborated and what was preserved;
- **continuity-safe** — completion does not erase learner-owned educational history (EIP-005);
- **implementation-independent** — orchestrators, buses, DI, and frameworks are delivery details, not completion law.

Runtime service collaboration completion is **not**:

- execution success or Runtime Execution Completion (REC) affirmation by itself;
- educational success, learner mastery, progress, or exam readiness;
- Programme VII workflow completion (orchestration fulfilment remains that corpus’s job);
- Programme VI educational completion (recovery / revision / planning / etc. where defined);
- Evidence Consumption Completion (ECC) affirmation by itself;
- reinterpretation of Educational Evidence under a “collaboration done” label;
- transfer of authority, merger of RS responsibilities, minting of tips, invention of EST/CST postures, or amendment of constitutional specifications;
- a claim that hop counts, bus acknowledgements, DI resolution, or mesh health equal fulfilment;
- a UI celebration, analytics milestone, or orchestration-framework “saga complete” flag as educational or constitutional law;
- a licence to skip outstanding published collaboration responsibilities because a product surface looks finished;
- a redefinition of the Runtime Service Model catalogue by proximity or convenience.

## 4. Completion Lifecycle (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material collaboration that claims lawful completion travels through constitutional assessment phases. These are **normative judgement obligations**, not an implementation architecture.

```
Lawful collaboration opened under MS001 / MS002
        │
        ▼
Assess participating services & assigned responsibilities
        │
        ▼
Assess authorised RSC pattern completion
        │
        ▼
Assess exchanged artefacts & collaboration records
        │
        ▼
Assess residual collaboration duties
        │
        ├── not yet / refuse close (outstanding responsibilities or failed criteria)
        │
        └── Affirm collaboration completion
                │
                ▼
        Preserve audit continuity + explain the close
```

| Phase | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **Open warrant** | Confirm a published collaboration existed under RS / RSC / RC law | No lawful collaboration; completion theatre for never-started coordination |
| **Participant assessment** | Confirm named RS participants fulfilled assigned collaboration responsibilities without absorption | Unpublished participants; responsibility merger; abandoned assigned duties |
| **Pattern assessment** | Confirm the authorised RSC-01…RSC-07 pattern completed or lawfully stopped | Pattern abandoned mid-duty without published refuse/defer/escalate |
| **Artefact & record assessment** | Confirm required published artefacts were exchanged and collaboration records exist | Missing published exchanges / audit trails |
| **Residual assessment** | Confirm no outstanding collaboration responsibilities remain | Residual published collaboration duty remains |
| **Affirm & explain** | Emit completion judgement with explainability and continuity | Explanation would invent success, merge duties, transfer ownership, or amend law |

> **Completion never “best-effort invents” a shortcut called mesh-done.**

## 5. Relationship to MS001 and MS002

Collaboration completion is the terminal judgement for a published collaboration instance — not a parallel constitution and not a substitute for the Runtime Service Model or Collaboration Model.

| Completion concern | Primary upstream binding |
|--------------------|--------------------------|
| Lawful collaboration existence | MS001 RS-01…RS-07 + MS002 RSC-01…RSC-07 + RC authorisation |
| Participant honesty | MS001 responsibilities preserved for each participant |
| Pattern completion | Published RSC pattern steps / roles under MS002 |
| Artefact honesty | Published constitutional artefacts only (MS002 exchange law) |
| Record honesty | RS-07 / RSC-07 audit collaboration obligations |
| Integrity at close | MS001 `SERVICE_BOUNDARIES.md` + MS002 `COLLABORATION_BOUNDARIES.md` + this corpus |

A collaboration may lawfully complete with a **refuse / defer / escalate** disposition when that disposition is itself a published collaborative outcome and required records exist. Unlawful improvisation that “looks coordinated-finished” is never completion.

MS001 without MS002 risks *capabilities without lawful interaction law*.  
MS002 without MS003 risks *collaboration without closed fulfilment judgement*.  
MS003 without MS001 / MS002 risks *completion theatre without participants or patterns*.

## 7. Educational / Constitutional Purpose

The Runtime Service Collaboration Completion Model exists so that:

1. **Collaboration closes honestly** — runtime does not hang indefinitely after published collaboration duties are done, nor declare complete while published collaboration responsibilities remain.
2. **Completion stays collaboration-scoped** — closing coordination never becomes a mastery, execution-success, or workflow ceremony.
3. **Responsibilities remain distinct** — finishing collaboration never silently merges RS catalogue duties or transfers ownership.
4. **Authority and law survive close** — finishing collaboration never quietly amends corpora or redistributes MS001 responsibilities.
5. **Audits remain possible** — same published collaboration facts ⇒ same completion judgement class and reconstructable trail.
6. **Explainability spans the full arc** — students and developers can see which services collaborated and what was preserved.
7. **Implementations remain replaceable** — collaboration completion law outlives any particular orchestrator, bus, DI graph, or service topology.

## 12. Closing Statement

Runtime service collaboration completion exists solely to confirm that published constitutional collaboration responsibilities have been fulfilled.

When a product surface says “collaboration done” and published law still shows outstanding collaboration duties, law wins — and the close must be refused. When collaboration is complete and a product wants to celebrate mastery, execution success, workflow close, responsibility merger, or a constitutional rewrite, law wins again — and those claims must be refused. Runtime never settles honesty by inventing educational success, execution success, orchestration fulfilment, responsibility redistribution, or new constitutional text at the moment of collaboration close.
