## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which specialists finished coordinating* — which pattern completed, which contracts authorised the collaboration, which artefacts were exchanged, and which limits were preserved — is not closing honestly; they are performing with a mesh costume.

After Programmes VI and VII have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, and orchestration fulfilment speakable, after Programme VIII WS1 / WS2 have made contracts, events, execution completion, and evidence handling speakable, and after WS3 / MS001–MS002 have made services and collaboration speakable, Programme VIII WS3 / MS003 still needs one collaboration-completion-speech answer:

> **“Which runtime services collaborated, under which pattern and contracts, exchanging which artefacts, while preserving which constitutional boundaries?”**

That answer must cover every material affirmed (or refused) collaboration completion without inventing educational success, implying execution success, transferring authority, merging responsibilities, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting evidence, claiming workflow close, or modifying constitutional law.

## 2. What Collaboration Completion Explainability Is

**Runtime Service Collaboration Completion Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **fulfilment (or lawful refusal of fulfilment) of published collaboration responsibilities** — not to invent a second tutor voice from saga exits, hop counts, or celebration UI.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Services collaborated** | Named RS-01…RS-07 capabilities and collaboration roles | Which runtime services collaborated? |
| **Pattern completed** | Named RSC-01…RSC-07 coordination form(s) whose published steps finished or lawfully stopped | Which collaboration pattern completed? |
| **Contracts authorising** | Named RC-01…RC-07 binding(s) that authorised the collaborative acts | Which constitutional contracts authorised collaboration? |
| **Artefacts exchanged** | Published constitutional artefacts handed between participants | Which constitutional artefacts were exchanged? |
| **Boundaries preserved** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden transfers / mergers / overclaims / inventions / amendments did not occur | Which constitutional boundaries were preserved? |

Collaboration Completion Explainability is:

- **fulfilment-centred** — it narrates why RSCC criteria hold (or why close is refused);
- **participant-centred** — it names which RS services collaborated under which RSC pattern;
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published RS / RSC / RC identities where applicable;
- **artefact-honest** — only published constitutional artefacts appear in the exchange story;
- **responsibility-honest** — explanation confirms non-absorption, non-transfer, and non-redefinition of MS001 duties;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip / educational-completion speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / workflow-completion speech, Programme VIII REC / ECC speech, MS001 service speech, or MS002 collaboration speech;
- **non-inventive** — explanation never becomes a back door to mint meaning, tips, mastery, execution success, workflow close, mega-services, or law change;
- **technology-silent** — explanation never treats buses, DI, meshes, queues, or orchestrators as constitutional warrant;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match RS-07 / RSC-07 reconstruction needs;
- **determinism-honest** — explanations must not paper over race-dependent collaboration-done flags.

Collaboration Completion Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Programme VI educational meaning or educational-completion explainability;
- a substitute for Programme VII workflow-completion explainability;
- a substitute for WS1 execution-completion or WS2 evidence-consumption-completion explainability;
- a substitute for MS001 service or MS002 collaboration explainability corpora;
- a rendering system, UI template, log shipper, tracing product, orchestrator console, or analytics dashboard;
- a claim that clear collaboration-completion speech guarantees learning, execution success, or a pass;
- a licence to present scores, hop counts, acks, DI resolution, or optimiser confidence as constitutional proof;
- a narrative that “the caller now owns what the callee decided because collaboration closed.”

## 3. Mandatory Explanation Questions

Every material collaboration completion judgement (affirm or refuse close) should answer all five questions:

### RSCCAQ-01 — Which runtime services collaborated?

Name the RS-01…RS-07 services and their collaboration roles (initiator, participant, specialist, gate, recorder). If completion is refused, name the participants that were assessed and state that fulfilment was not affirmed. “Mesh healthy” or “services were up” is not a constitutional participant identity.

### RSCCAQ-02 — Which collaboration pattern completed?

Name the RSC-01…RSC-07 pattern(s) that applied and whether they reached published terminal coordination or a published refuse / defer / escalate stop. If refusing close, name which pattern duties remained outstanding. “Saga succeeded” or “orchestrator exited” is not a constitutional pattern identity.

### RSCCAQ-03 — Which constitutional contracts authorised collaboration?

Name the primary RC-01…RC-07 contract(s) whose obligations were met for the collaborative acts (or which failed when refusing close). Cross-cutting collaborations name all bound contracts. “Service was available,” “bus delivered,” “DI resolved,” or “endpoint was called” are not constitutional authorisation.

### RSCCAQ-04 — Which constitutional artefacts were exchanged?

Name only published artefact classes handed between participants: contract dispositions, evidence records, validation outcomes, CE processing records, recommendation artefacts / sets, EST/CST postures, workflow events, audit refs, or explicit refuse/defer/escalate outcomes. Missing required exchanges must be spoken when refusing close.

### RSCCAQ-05 — Which constitutional boundaries were preserved?

Confirm the hard stops in `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md` that were checked — especially non-transfer of ownership, non-merger of service responsibilities, non-implication of execution completion, non-implication of educational success, non-implication of workflow completion, and non-modification of constitutional specifications.

## 5. Audience Rules

| Audience | May hear | Must not hear as educational or constitutional law |
|----------|----------|----------------------------------------------------|
| **Student** | That the system finished the coordinated constitutional machine steps it was allowed to take among its specialists, what happened next (if anything), and what was *not* being claimed — in plain educational language | Internal RSCC/RSC/RS/RC IDs as theatre; “you have mastered this”; “your study plan is complete” from collaboration close alone; “the engine finished learning for you”; hop traces; optimiser scores as “proof you are ready” |
| **Developer / auditor** | Full RSCCAQ-01…05 answers, corpus paths, RS/RSC/RC IDs, RSCC criteria map, boundary checks, determinism / continuity notes, provenance | Product success metrics, hop counts, ack counts, DI resolution, or latency SLOs substituted for constitutional refs; silent collapse into REC / ECC / Programme VII workflow-completion speech; silent claim that responsibilities merged |

Student and developer traces must describe the **same constitutional truth**. Vocabulary may differ; facts may not.
