## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor needs bright conditions for saying “this coordinated constitutional work among specialists is finished” — and equally bright refusals when it is not. After MS001 has named services and MS002 has named collaboration patterns, Programme VIII Workstream 3 still needs one fulfilment checklist:

> **What must be true before runtime may affirm that collaborating services have fulfilled their published constitutional collaboration responsibilities?**

These criteria answer that question. They are **normative constitutional conditions**, not database predicates, API status codes, saga exit semantics, or bus-drain signals.

---

## 2. Criterion Catalogue

All material affirmed completions require **RSCC-01 through RSCC-05** to hold. Failure of any criterion ⇒ **not yet** or **refuse close**. Do not invent a partial “mostly complete” educational, execution, or constitutional status.

| ID | Criterion | Primary question |
|----|-----------|------------------|
| **RSCC-01** | Participating services fulfilled assigned responsibilities | Did each named RS participant complete its collaboration duties without absorption? |
| **RSCC-02** | Authorised collaboration pattern completed | Did the recognised RSC pattern finish or lawfully stop? |
| **RSCC-03** | Required constitutional artefacts exchanged | Were required published artefacts handed between participants? |
| **RSCC-04** | Collaboration records created | Do reconstructable constitutional collaboration records exist? |
| **RSCC-05** | No outstanding collaboration responsibilities remain | Are published collaboration duties for this instance exhausted? |

Optional supporting honesty checks (do not replace RSCC-01…RSCC-05):

| ID | Check | Primary question |
|----|-------|------------------|
| **RSCC-06** | Contract honesty | Were applicable RC-01…RC-07 obligations for collaborative acts satisfied? |
| **RSCC-07** | Continuity honesty | Was prior lawful history preserved (EIP-005)? |
| **RSCC-08** | Non-claim honesty | Does the judgement refuse educational / execution / workflow / merger / amendment overclaim? |

## 3. Criterion Definitions

### RSCC-01 — Participating services fulfilled assigned responsibilities

**Definition.** Every named RS-01…RS-07 participant in the collaboration has fulfilled the *collaboration responsibilities assigned to it under published MS001 / MS002 law* for this instance — without absorbing another participant’s constitutional duties, and without inventing unpublished participant types.

**Requires:**

- Identifiable published participant identities (RS catalogue; roles such as initiator / participant / specialist / gate / recorder as published).
- Evidence that each participant’s assigned collaborative acts were executed or lawfully stopped under that participant’s MS001 permitted responsibilities.
- Refusal of “caller finished so callee duties vanished” as fulfilment.
- Confirmation that MS001 permitted / prohibited responsibilities remain intact for every participant at close.

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- That any participant’s lone execution cycle is REC-complete (orthogonal; may co-occur).
- That the learner understood, progressed, or mastered anything.
- That a Programme VII workflow instance is orchestration-complete.
- That constitutional law or the RS catalogue was updated.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutoring team that walks away mid-handoff because one specialist left the room has not completed coordinated duties — even if the room looks quiet.

---

### RSCC-02 — Authorised collaboration pattern completed

**Definition.** The RSC-01…RSC-07 pattern(s) authorised for this collaboration under published MS002 law have either:

1. reached their published terminal coordination step for the collaboration’s concern; or  
2. terminated in a **published** refuse / defer / escalate disposition that exhausts the collaboration’s remaining *collaboration* duties.

**Requires:**

- Identifiable published pattern identity (RSC-01…RSC-07; corpus-referenced).
- Evidence that pattern roles and coordination forms required for the instance were fulfilled or lawfully stopped.
- Refusal of “abandoned mid-pattern but orchestrator exited” as completion.
- No silent substitution of an unpublished “implied orchestration” pattern.

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- That pattern completion certifies educational success.
- That pattern completion affirms REC or ECC completion by itself.
- That pattern completion amended collaboration law.

**Tutor rationale.** Completing coordinated tutoring includes finishing the published coordination form — not inventing a new choreography mid-flight and calling the improvisation done.

---

### RSCC-03 — Required constitutional artefacts exchanged

**Definition.** Every published constitutional artefact required for the collaboration’s material handoffs has been exchanged between the participants that law requires — and only published constitutional artefacts were exchanged.

**Requires:**

- Exchanges map to published artefact classes (contract dispositions, evidence records, validation outcomes, CE processing records, recommendation artefacts / sets, EST/CST postures, workflow events, audit refs, refuse/defer/escalate outcomes, or other corpus-published results).
- No unpublished tips, owners, evidence classes, state postures, mega-service payloads, or event types were treated as lawful collaborative exchange.
- Absence of required published exchanges blocks completion.
- Artefacts were not reinterpreted in transit (MS002 exchange honesty).

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- That exchanged artefacts caused educational success.
- That exchanging an audit ref completed a coach’s educational job.
- That exchanging artefacts amended the Constitution or merged RS duties.

**Tutor rationale.** Completing coordinated work includes passing the authorised artefacts of that coordination — not inventing souvenir payloads, and not declaring done while the required handoffs never occurred.

---

### RSCC-04 — Collaboration records created

**Definition.** Published collaboration records required by RS-07 / RSC-07 (and applicable RC-07 / CE-06 obligations) exist and are sufficient to reconstruct the collaboration’s constitutional trail.

**Requires:**

- Records identify participants, RSC pattern(s), authorising contracts, artefacts exchanged, collaborative outputs / dispositions, and boundary preservation.
- Continuity across retries / replacements does not erase prior lawful record obligations (EIP-005).
- Technical hop logs, tracing spans, or orchestrator journals without constitutional references do **not** satisfy this criterion.

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- A particular database schema, log shipper, tracing product, or analytics pipeline.
- That recording equals learning or execution success.
- That a complete collaboration audit trail certifies mastery or workflow close.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor team that cannot later reconstruct who coordinated what under which warrant has not closed the educational machine honestly — even if the mesh is quiet.

---

### RSCC-05 — No outstanding collaboration responsibilities remain

**Definition.** For this collaboration instance’s published constitutional concern, no material *collaboration* responsibility remains outstanding under MS001 / MS002 / upstream published pattern law.

**Requires:**

- Residual published coordination steps, required exchanges, required records, or pending published collaborative evaluations for *this instance* are absent or explicitly deferred to a **new** published collaboration / lawful await — not silently dropped.
- Concurrent duties belonging to **other** collaborations (or to lone execution cycles / evidence-handling concerns) are not falsely treated as residuals of this instance (and vice versa).
- “Outstanding” is judged by published law, not by product backlog taste or orchestrator backlog depth.

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- That all future educational work for the learner is finished.
- That all open Programme VII workflows are closed.
- That all related REC / ECC concerns are closed.
- That the Constitution needs no further amendment ever.
- That MS001 responsibilities have been rewritten to “reduce leftovers.”

**Tutor rationale.** Collaboration completion is the absence of remaining *this-instance* coordination duties — not the absence of the student’s entire exam journey, nor the silent absorption of leftover duties into one mega-service.

## 4. Supporting Honesty Checks

### RSCC-06 — Contract honesty

Every RC-01…RC-07 contract applicable to the collaboration’s material acts must have been satisfied for those acts — or the collaboration lawfully stopped under published refusal rules when satisfaction was impossible. Calling another service never substitutes for RC authorisation.

### RSCC-07 — Continuity honesty

Affirming collaboration completion must not erase, silently rewrite, or orphan prior lawful educational / constitutional history (EIP-005). Replacement collaborations re-enter at published points; they do not invent amnesia as fulfilment.

### RSCC-08 — Non-claim honesty

The completion judgement must refuse overclaims:

| Forbidden overclaim | Lawful statement |
|---------------------|------------------|
| Learner success / mastery / progress | Collaboration responsibilities for this instance are fulfilled |
| Execution success / REC complete | Collaboration responsibilities are fulfilled (execution-cycle fulfilment requires WS1 / MS003) |
| Evidence-handling complete / ECC complete | Collaboration responsibilities are fulfilled (evidence-handling fulfilment requires WS2 / MS003) |
| Programme VII workflow complete | Collaboration for this instance is complete (orchestration fulfilment requires Programme VII corpus) |
| Responsibilities merged / ownership transferred | Each participant retains MS001 responsibilities; no ownership transfer occurred |
| Constitutional amendment occurred | No constitutional specification was modified by this close |

## 5. Judgement Classes

| Judgement | When | Effect |
|-----------|------|--------|
| **Affirm collaboration complete** | RSCC-01…RSCC-05 hold (and RSCC-06…RSCC-08 honesty checks pass where applicable) | Collaboration instance may be recorded as collaboration-complete under this Model |
| **Not yet** | One or more criteria fail because published collaboration duties remain in progress under law | Do not affirm; continue published pattern or await published inputs |
| **Refuse close** | Criteria fail because the collaboration was unlawful, abandoned, merged duties, or would require invention to “look done” | Do not affirm; escalate / remediate under published governance — never invent fulfilment |

There is no fourth judgement called “educationally / execution / workflow complete because collaboration finished.”

---

## 6. Explicit Non-Criteria

The following are **never** sufficient (and often never relevant) as collaboration completion criteria:

- elapsed time, wall-clock duration, or SLA success;
- process exit codes, HTTP 200, broker acknowledgements, or saga coordinator success;
- queue depth zero, bus drained, or “no messages remaining”;
- DI container resolved / mesh healthy / hop count reached;
- UI spinner stopped / celebration animation shown;
- Adaptive scores, Twin estimates, engagement metrics, or streak theatre;
- presence of *any* log line mentioning “collaboration complete”;
- Runtime Execution Completion (REC) alone (orthogonal; may co-occur but does not replace RSCC-01…RSCC-05);
- Evidence Consumption Completion (ECC) alone (orthogonal; never substitutes);
- Programme VII workflow completion alone (orthogonal; may co-occur but does not replace RSCC-01…RSCC-05);
- Programme VI educational completion alone (orthogonal; never inferred from collaboration close);
- majority vote of services, feature-flag theatre, or demo urgency;
- silent merger of leftover duties into the initiator “to clear the backlog.”

| Concern | Sibling corpus | This document |
|---------|----------------|---------------|
| Runtime *execution* responsibilities | WS1 `execution_completion/` (REC-xx) | Collaboration responsibilities (RSCC-xx) |
| *Evidence-handling* responsibilities | WS2 `evidence_completion/` (ECC-xx) | Collaboration responsibilities (RSCC-xx) |

| May co-occur? | Yes — judgements may land in the same window | Yes — but each corpus judges its own fulfilment |
| May substitute? | **Never** | **Never** |

A collaboration may affirm RSCC completion while a related execution cycle remains REC-incomplete, or while a related workflow remains orchestration-incomplete.  
An execution cycle or workflow may affirm its own completion while an unrelated collaboration remains open.  
Honest systems keep all judgements named and separate — and never treat collaboration close as licence to redefine RS responsibilities.

---

## 8. Closing Statement

> **Runtime service collaboration is complete only when participating services fulfilled assigned responsibilities, the authorised pattern completed, required constitutional artefacts were exchanged, collaboration records exist, and no outstanding collaboration responsibilities remain.  
> Anything less is unfinished constitutional coordination.  
> Anything more — learning success, execution success, workflow close, responsibility merger, or law change — is a different judgement owned elsewhere.**
