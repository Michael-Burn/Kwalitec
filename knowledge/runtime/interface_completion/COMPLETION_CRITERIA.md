## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor needs bright conditions for saying “this coordinated constitutional interaction among specialist interfaces is finished” — and equally bright refusals when it is not. After MS001 has named interfaces and MS002 has named composition patterns, Programme VIII Workstream 4 still needs one fulfilment checklist:

> **What must be true before runtime may affirm that participating interfaces have fulfilled their published composition responsibilities?**

These criteria answer that question. They are **normative constitutional conditions**, not database predicates, API status codes, GraphQL stitch semantics, or gateway-drain signals.

---

## 2. Criterion Catalogue

All material affirmed completions require **RICC-01 through RICC-05** to hold. Failure of any criterion ⇒ **not yet** or **refuse close**. Do not invent a partial “mostly complete” educational, execution, collaboration, or constitutional status.

| ID | Criterion | Primary question |
|----|-----------|------------------|
| **RICC-01** | Participating interfaces fulfilled published responsibilities | Did each named RI participant complete its composition duties without identity merger? |
| **RICC-02** | Authorised composition pattern completed | Did the recognised RIC pattern finish or lawfully stop? |
| **RICC-03** | Required constitutional inputs exchanged | Were required published constitutional inputs handed between participants? |
| **RICC-04** | Required constitutional outputs produced | Were required published constitutional outputs produced by the composition? |
| **RICC-05** | Required composition records created | Do reconstructable constitutional composition records exist? |

Optional supporting honesty checks (do not replace RICC-01…RICC-05):

| ID | Check | Primary question |
|----|-------|------------------|
| **RICC-06** | Residual honesty | Are published composition duties for this instance exhausted? |
| **RICC-07** | Contract honesty | Were applicable RC-01…RC-07 obligations for composed interactions satisfied? |
| **RICC-08** | Continuity honesty | Was prior lawful history preserved (EIP-005)? |
| **RICC-09** | Non-claim honesty | Does the judgement refuse educational / execution / collaboration / workflow / merger / amendment overclaim? |

## 3. Criterion Definitions

### RICC-01 — Participating interfaces fulfilled published responsibilities

**Definition.** Every named RI-01…RI-07 participant in the composition has fulfilled the *composition responsibilities assigned to it under published MS001 / MS002 law* for this instance — without absorbing another participant’s constitutional identity or duties, and without inventing unpublished participant types.

**Requires:**

- Identifiable published participant identities (RI catalogue; roles such as initiator / participant / specialist / gate / recorder / inspector as published).
- Evidence that each participant’s assigned composition acts were executed or lawfully stopped under that participant’s MS001 permitted exposures.
- Refusal of “caller finished so callee duties vanished” as fulfilment.
- Confirmation that MS001 identities and permitted / prohibited exposures remain intact for every participant at close.

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- That any participant’s lone execution cycle is REC-complete (orthogonal; may co-occur).
- That any related service collaboration is RSCC-complete (orthogonal; may co-occur).
- That the learner understood, progressed, or mastered anything.
- That a Programme VII workflow instance is orchestration-complete.
- That constitutional law or the RI catalogue was updated.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutoring team that walks away mid-handoff because one specialist conversation left the room has not completed coordinated duties — even if the facade looks quiet.

---

### RICC-02 — Authorised composition pattern completed

**Definition.** The RIC-01…RIC-07 pattern(s) authorised for this composition under published MS002 law have either:

1. reached their published terminal coordination step for the composition’s concern; or  
2. terminated in a **published** refuse / defer / escalate disposition that exhausts the composition’s remaining *composition* duties.

**Requires:**

- Identifiable published pattern identity (RIC-01…RIC-07; corpus-referenced).
- Evidence that pattern roles and coordination forms required for the instance were fulfilled or lawfully stopped.
- Refusal of “abandoned mid-pattern but gateway exited” as completion.
- No silent substitution of an unpublished “implied facade” pattern.

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- That pattern completion certifies educational success.
- That pattern completion affirms REC, ECC, or RSCC completion by itself.
- That pattern completion amended composition law.

**Tutor rationale.** Completing coordinated tutoring includes finishing the published composition form — not inventing a new facade mid-flight and calling the improvisation done.

---

### RICC-03 — Required constitutional inputs exchanged

**Definition.** Every published constitutional input required for the composition’s material interactions has been exchanged between the participants that law requires — and only published constitutional inputs were exchanged.

**Requires:**

- Exchanges map to published input classes (constitutional requests citing contracts, evidence, events, service responsibilities, dispositions, EST/CST postures, workflow events, audit refs, refuse/defer/escalate outcomes, or other corpus-published inputs).
- No unpublished tips, owners, evidence classes, state postures, mega-interface payloads, or event types were treated as lawful composition inputs.
- Absence of required published input exchanges blocks completion.
- Inputs were not reinterpreted in transit (MS002 exchange honesty).

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- That exchanged inputs caused educational success.
- That exchanging an audit ref completed a coach’s educational job.
- That exchanging inputs amended the Constitution or merged RI identities.

**Tutor rationale.** Completing coordinated work includes passing the authorised inputs of that coordination — not inventing souvenir payloads, and not declaring done while the required handoffs never occurred.

---

### RICC-04 — Required constitutional outputs produced

**Definition.** Every published constitutional output required for the composition’s material concern has been produced — and only authorised constitutional outputs were produced.

**Requires:**

- Outputs map to published classes (dispositions, artefacts, audit trails, diagnostic records, refuse/defer/escalate outcomes, or other corpus-published results).
- No unpublished tips, mastery claims, ownership transfers, mega-interface products, or evidence reclassifications were treated as lawful composition outputs.
- Absence of required published outputs blocks completion.
- Outputs remain attributable to the participating RI identities that lawfully produced them — never collapsed into an unpublished mega-output author.

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- That produced outputs caused educational success.
- That producing an audit trail completed execution, collaboration, or workflow fulfilment.
- That producing outputs amended the Constitution or redefined constitutional capabilities.

**Tutor rationale.** Completing coordinated interface work includes producing the authorised results of that coordination — not inventing celebration payloads, and not declaring done while required outputs never appeared.

---

### RICC-05 — Required composition records created

**Definition.** Published composition records required by RI-06 / RIC audit obligations (and applicable RC-07 / CE-06 obligations) exist and are sufficient to reconstruct the composition’s constitutional trail.

**Requires:**

- Records identify participants, RIC pattern(s), authorising contracts, inputs exchanged, outputs produced, composition dispositions, and boundary preservation.
- Continuity across retries / replacements does not erase prior lawful record obligations (EIP-005).
- Technical request logs, tracing spans, or gateway journals without constitutional references do **not** satisfy this criterion.

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- A particular database schema, log shipper, tracing product, or analytics pipeline.
- That recording equals learning, execution completion, or collaboration completion.
- That a complete composition audit trail certifies mastery or workflow close.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor team that cannot later reconstruct which interfaces composed under which warrant has not closed the educational machine honestly — even if the facade is quiet.

## 4. Supporting Honesty Checks

### RICC-06 — Residual honesty

For this composition instance’s published constitutional concern, no material *composition* responsibility remains outstanding under MS001 / MS002 / upstream published pattern law. Residual published coordination steps, required exchanges, required outputs, required records, or pending published composition evaluations for *this instance* are absent or explicitly deferred to a **new** published composition / lawful await — not silently dropped. Concurrent duties belonging to **other** compositions (or to lone execution cycles / evidence-handling / collaboration concerns) are not falsely treated as residuals of this instance (and vice versa).

### RICC-07 — Contract honesty

Every RC-01…RC-07 contract applicable to the composition’s material interactions must have been satisfied for those interactions — or the composition lawfully stopped under published refusal rules when satisfaction was impossible. Calling another interface never substitutes for RC authorisation.

### RICC-08 — Continuity honesty

Affirming composition completion must not erase, silently rewrite, or orphan prior lawful educational / constitutional history (EIP-005). Replacement compositions re-enter at published points; they do not invent amnesia as fulfilment.

### RICC-09 — Non-claim honesty

The completion judgement must refuse overclaims:

| Forbidden overclaim | Lawful statement |
|---------------------|------------------|
| Learner success / mastery / progress | Composition responsibilities for this instance are fulfilled |
| Execution completion / REC complete | Composition responsibilities are fulfilled (execution-cycle fulfilment requires WS1 / MS003) |
| Evidence-handling complete / ECC complete | Composition responsibilities are fulfilled (evidence-handling fulfilment requires WS2 / MS003) |
| Service collaboration complete / RSCC complete | Composition responsibilities are fulfilled (collaboration fulfilment requires WS3 / MS003) |
| Programme VII workflow complete | Composition for this instance is complete (orchestration fulfilment requires Programme VII corpus) |
| Interface identities merged / capabilities redefined | Each participant retains MS001 identity; no capability redefinition occurred |
| Constitutional amendment occurred | No constitutional specification was modified by this close |

## 5. Judgement Classes

| Judgement | When | Effect |
|-----------|------|--------|
| **Affirm composition complete** | RICC-01…RICC-05 hold (and RICC-06…RICC-09 honesty checks pass where applicable) | Composition instance may be recorded as composition-complete under this Model |
| **Not yet** | One or more criteria fail because published composition duties remain in progress under law | Do not affirm; continue published pattern or await published inputs |
| **Refuse close** | Criteria fail because the composition was unlawful, abandoned, merged identities, or would require invention to “look done” | Do not affirm; escalate / remediate under published governance — never invent fulfilment |

There is no fourth judgement called “educationally / execution / collaboration / workflow complete because composition finished.”

---

## 6. Explicit Non-Criteria

The following are **never** sufficient (and often never relevant) as composition completion criteria:

- elapsed time, wall-clock duration, or SLA success;
- process exit codes, HTTP 200, GraphQL stitch success, gRPC status OK, or BFF aggregate success;
- queue depth zero, gateway drained, or “no requests remaining”;
- SDK batch completed / auth middleware passed / network healthy;
- UI spinner stopped / celebration animation shown;
- Adaptive scores, Twin estimates, engagement metrics, or streak theatre;
- presence of *any* log line mentioning “composition complete”;
- Runtime Execution Completion (REC) alone (orthogonal; may co-occur but does not replace RICC-01…RICC-05);
- Evidence Consumption Completion (ECC) alone (orthogonal; never substitutes);
- Service Collaboration Completion (RSCC) alone (orthogonal; may co-occur but does not replace RICC-01…RICC-05);
- Programme VII workflow completion alone (orthogonal; may co-occur but does not replace RICC-01…RICC-05);
- Programme VI educational completion alone (orthogonal; never inferred from composition close);
- majority vote of interfaces, feature-flag theatre, or demo urgency;
- silent merger of leftover duties into the initiator “to clear the backlog.”

A composition may affirm RICC completion while a related execution cycle remains REC-incomplete, a related collaboration remains RSCC-incomplete, or a related workflow remains orchestration-incomplete.  
An execution cycle, collaboration, or workflow may affirm its own completion while an unrelated composition remains open.  
Honest systems keep all judgements named and separate — and never treat composition close as licence to merge RI identities or redefine constitutional capabilities.

---

## 8. Closing Statement

> **Runtime interface composition is complete only when participating interfaces fulfilled published responsibilities, the authorised composition pattern completed, required constitutional inputs were exchanged, required constitutional outputs were produced, and required composition records exist.  
> Anything less is unfinished constitutional interface coordination.  
> Anything more — learning success, execution completion, service collaboration completion, workflow close, identity merger, capability redefinition, or law change — is a different judgement owned elsewhere.**
