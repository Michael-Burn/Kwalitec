An expert IFoA tutor does not hand a colleague their professional licence because two lessons run in sequence, nor merge two coaching roles into one voice because a calendar is convenient. After the Constitution and EIP have defined *educational truth*, after Programme VI has defined *what educational questions mean*, after Programme VII has defined *how decisions flow*, after Programme VIII WS1 / WS2 have defined *which contracts, events, completions, and evidence rules bind software*, and after WS3 / MS001 has defined *which execution capabilities may exist*, the platform still needs one collaboration answer:

> **“How may constitutional runtime services interact while preserving constitutional responsibilities and boundaries?”**

That answer must ensure collaboration coordinates execution under published contracts; exchanges only published constitutional artefacts; preserves each service’s responsibilities; remains deterministic, auditable, explainable, and implementation-independent — without transferring constitutional ownership, merging responsibilities, or elevating an orchestration technology into constitutional architecture.

This document records that posture so every future Runtime A (and successor) subsystem has a single constitutional reference for *how named RS capabilities may lawfully work together*.

## 2. What Runtime Service Collaboration Is

**Runtime service collaboration** is a named **coordination relationship** among **authorised constitutional runtime services** (RS-01…RS-07) that **coordinates execution of published constitutional responsibilities** — without becoming a source of educational truth, transferring ownership, or collapsing distinct service duties into one mega-capability.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Participating services** | Named RS-01…RS-07 capabilities that take part | Which services collaborate? |
| **Collaboration pattern** | A recognised RSC-01…RSC-07 coordination form | How do they coordinate? |
| **Authorising contracts** | RC-01…RC-07 bindings that authorise each material act in the collaboration | What authorised collaboration? |
| **Exchanged artefacts** | Published constitutional artefacts (contracts, evidence, events, dispositions, surfaced outputs, audit records) passed between participants | What was exchanged? |
| **Preserved responsibilities** | Each participant’s MS001 permitted / prohibited responsibilities remain intact | Did anyone absorb another’s duty? |
| **Collaborative outputs** | Authorised execution outputs produced by the coordinated acts | What resulted? |
| **Replaceability** | Collaboration patterns name coordination forms, not orchestration engines | Is collaboration law, or merely glue? |

Runtime service collaboration is:

- **coordination-only** — it sequences, parallels, delegates, conditions, or audits execution; it does not author law;
- **responsibility-preserving** — each RS participant keeps its MS001 catalogue duties;
- **contract-consuming** — every material collaborative act cites one or more RC bindings;
- **artefact-honest** — only published constitutional artefacts are exchanged;
- **authority-non-transferring** — invocation never transfers constitutional ownership;
- **non-merging** — composition never collapses RS types into a new unpublished mega-service;
- **implementation-independent** — patterns name coordination forms, not buses, DI graphs, or topologies;
- **integrity-preserving** — collaboration never quietly rewrites meaning, ownership, tips, evidence, or state;
- **audit-capable** — collaborative acts leave reconstructable constitutional traces;
- **explainable** — students and developers can answer which services collaborated and under which law.

Runtime service collaboration is **not**:

- a second Educational Constitution, EIP, or Runtime Service Model;
- a transfer of constitutional ownership between services or components;
- a merger of RS-01…RS-07 into a single undifferentiated executor;
- a licence to invent unpublished collaboration patterns, tips, mastery, or educational context;
- a service orchestration engine, dependency-injection container, message bus, microservice mesh, REST API, queue, scheduler, worker, database schema, or UI surface;
- a claim that successful collaboration guarantees learning or a pass;
- an amendment to MS001 service responsibilities by proximity or convenience.

## 5. Educational Purpose

The Runtime Service Collaboration Model exists so that:

1. **Composition remains subordinate to law** — services may work together only under published patterns and contracts.
2. **Responsibilities remain distinct** — collaboration never dissolves the RS catalogue into one mega-executor.
3. **Authority remains with owners** — invocation is coordination, not ownership transfer.
4. **Contracts remain the authorisation layer** — calling another service never substitutes for RC bindings.
5. **Determinism remains possible** — the same published collaborative inputs yield the same authorised outputs.
6. **Audit remains possible** — every material collaboration can be reconstructed against participants and producers.
7. **Explainability remains honest** — collaboration speech describes who coordinated; it does not redefine what learning means.
8. **Implementations remain replaceable** — constitutional coordination outlives any particular orchestration technology.
