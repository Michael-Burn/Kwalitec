## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “services wired” or “bus messages delivered.” The tutor optimises **faithful coordinated execution**: each specialist keeps their role, the student experiences one constitutional truth, history remains reconstructable, and the machinery stays replaceable.

These objectives bind every Runtime A collaborative composition and every successor runtime that claims constitutional compliance.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### RSCO-01 — Coordinate constitutional execution

**Definition.** Ensure that runtime service collaboration arranges authorised RS-01…RS-07 acts so that published constitutional responsibilities are fulfilled together when law requires composition — or honestly refuses when no published pattern and contracts authorise the coordination.

**Includes:**

- Selecting only recognised RSC-01…RSC-07 patterns for material collaborations.
- Ensuring every participating act maps to a recognised RS service under MS001.
- Consuming RC-01…RC-07 contracts before each material collaborative act.
- Exchanging only published constitutional artefacts between participants.
- Producing only authorised collaborative outputs (records, dispositions, surfaced artefacts, audit trails).
- Preferring lawful refusal / deferral / escalation over invented coordination certainty.

**Excludes:**

- Inventing unpublished collaboration patterns, mega-services, or “implied orchestration” to keep the product moving.
- Optimising for latency, fan-out, or engagement as a substitute for constitutional fidelity.
- Treating Version 2 Adaptive / Twin / Experience heuristics as replacements for Programmes VI–VIII or MS001.
- Coordinating “close enough” approximations of constitutional responsibilities without documenting a corpus amendment.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software merges coaching roles for convenience. Collaboration is care; role-collapse is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### RSCO-02 — Preserve service responsibilities

**Definition.** Ensure that every collaborating RS participant retains the permitted and prohibited responsibilities established by the Runtime Service Model (MS001). Collaboration may invoke; it must never absorb, transfer, or merge constitutional duties.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who “helps” by taking over a colleague’s professional judgement has not coordinated — they have usurped. Services that absorb callees become unlawful mega-authorities.

**Manifestations:**

- Callers do not inherit callees’ authorship or expand their own prohibited lists.
- Required specialised responsibilities (evidence, audit, authority, state) cannot be omitted by composition convenience.
- Pattern selection never invents a new RS type by silent merger.
- MS001 catalogue rules (composition, not absorption) remain binding at every handoff.

---

### RSCO-03 — Preserve deterministic behaviour

**Definition.** Ensure that the same published collaborative inputs — participating services, RSC pattern, contracts, evidence, events, and learner circumstances as classified — yield the same authorised collaborative outputs, absent a published corpus amendment.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor team that gives contradictory advice for identical warrants is not adaptive; they are unreliable. Determinism is educational honesty under composed load.

**Manifestations:**

- Collaborative outcomes are reconstructable from published inputs and catalogue rules.
- Hidden heuristics, A/B winners, race-dependent “winners,” or undocumented “smart defaults” are not constitutional inputs.
- Non-determinism introduced by infrastructure (clocks, buses, caches, parallel schedulers) must not invent educational meaning or ownership disposition.
- Refusal / deferral / escalate dispositions are first-class deterministic outcomes when law requires them.

---

### RSCO-04 — Preserve auditability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material collaboration leaves a reconstructable constitutional trail: which RSC pattern applied, which RS services participated, which RC contracts authorised each act, which artefacts were exchanged, and which authorised outputs were produced.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor team that cannot later say *who did what under which warrant* cannot be trusted. Collaboration without audit is educational amnesia with more moving parts.

**Manifestations:**

- Audit records preserve constitutional references for the whole collaboration graph, not only technical hop logs.
- Continuity (EIP-005) is preserved across retries, replacements, and redeploys.
- Missing provenance on any material participant is a defect, not an acceptable optimisation.
- RS-07 Audit Service obligations apply to collaborative acts (directly or via RSC-07 Audit Collaboration).

---

### RSCO-05 — Remain implementation independent

**Definition.** Ensure that RSC-01…RSC-07 meanings bind *coordination forms*, not languages, frameworks, DI containers, message buses, process topologies, storage engines, or API styles.

**Tutor rationale.** If “Sequential Collaboration” only exists as a Kafka topic chain, the bus has become unlawful constitutional furniture.

**Manifestations:**

- Pattern entries describe purpose, roles, permitted / prohibited collaboration, and outputs — never required stacks.
- Orchestration engines, DI, microservices, REST, queues, and workers are delivery options, not constitutional pattern types.
- Cross-cutting composition is described constitutionally, not as a mandatory mesh.

---

### RSCO-06 — Preserve explainability

**Definition.** Ensure that collaboration remains speakable under EIP-003: students and developers can answer the constitutional collaboration explainability questions without receiving redefined educational meaning or redistributed authority.

**Tutor rationale.** Opaque service graphs recreate black-box tutoring. Clear collaboration speech is part of educational honesty.

**Manifestations:**

- Explanations cite participants, contracts, artefacts, outputs, and preserved boundaries (`COLLABORATION_EXPLAINABILITY.md`).
- Student speech and developer traces share one truth with different vocabulary.
- Scores and optimiser confidence are never presented as constitutional warrant for collaboration.
- Explanation never becomes a back door to invent tips, mastery, or merged responsibilities.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| RSCO-01 conflicts with engagement or latency metrics | Constitutional coordination wins |
| RSCO-02 conflicts with “one mega-service is simpler” | Responsibility preservation wins |
| RSCO-03 conflicts with “smart” race-dependent personalisation without published warrant | Determinism / honest refusal wins |
| RSCO-04 conflicts with log volume reduction that erases participant / artefact refs | Auditability wins |
| RSCO-05 conflicts with locking RSC meanings to one orchestration engine | Implementation independence wins |
| RSCO-06 conflicts with cryptic internal mesh jargon as student speech | Explainability honesty wins |

No supporting objective authorises violating RSCO-01.  
No collaboration objective authorises violating MS001 RSO-01…RSO-06 for any participant.

---

## 5. Non-Objectives

Runtime service collaboration does **not** optimise for:

- tip volume, click-through, or streak theatre as educational success;
- inventing educational certainty when warrants are incomplete;
- collapsing Programmes VI–VIII or RS-01…RS-07 into one mega-service;
- making any collaboration graph or orchestration engine irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive estimates as substitutes for constitutional producers;
- treating documentation as optional commentary on code behaviour;
- elevating buses, schedulers, workers, or DI into constitutional architecture;
- transferring ownership “temporarily” for product convenience.

---

## 6. Closing Statement

> **A collaboration that is fast, elegant, and constitutionally unfaithful is a failed educational system.  
> A collaboration that preserves responsibilities, remains auditable and deterministic, and coordinates only published duties is doing its only job.**
