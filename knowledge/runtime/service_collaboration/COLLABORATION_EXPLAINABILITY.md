## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *why* a coordinated study action happened — which services participated, which contracts authorised the collaboration, which artefacts were exchanged, what resulted, and which boundaries were preserved — is not coaching; they are performing with more moving parts.

After Programmes VI–VII, Programme VIII WS1 / WS2, and WS3 / MS001 have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, events, evidence, and individual service execution speakable, Programme VIII WS3 / MS002 still needs one collaboration-speech answer:

> **“Which runtime services participated, under which contracts and artefacts, producing which outputs, while preserving which constitutional boundaries?”**

That answer must cover every material collaboration without inventing educational meaning, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting evidence, or merging responsibilities.

---

## 2. What Collaboration Explainability Is

**Runtime Service Collaboration Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **coordination among named RS capabilities under recognised RSC patterns** — not to invent a second tutor voice, elevate an orchestration stack into educational law, or claim that calling another service transferred ownership.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Services participated** | Named RS-01…RS-07 capabilities and collaboration roles | Which runtime services participated? |
| **Contracts authorising** | Named RC-01…RC-07 binding(s) for each material act | Which constitutional contracts authorised collaboration? |
| **Artefacts exchanged** | Published constitutional artefacts passed between participants | Which constitutional artefacts were exchanged? |
| **Outputs produced** | Authorised collaborative execution records, dispositions, surfaced artefacts, audit trails | Which execution outputs were produced? |
| **Boundaries preserved** | Explicit confirmation that ownership was not transferred, responsibilities not merged, contracts not bypassed, evidence not reinterpreted, specifications not modified | Which constitutional boundaries were preserved? |

Collaboration explainability is:

- **participant-centred** — it narrates which RS services collaborated under which RSC pattern;
- **contract-bound** — every justification cites RC authorisation for material acts;
- **artefact-honest** — only published constitutional artefacts appear in the exchange story;
- **responsibility-honest** — explanation confirms non-absorption and non-transfer;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence speech, or MS001 service speech;
- **non-inventive** — explanation never becomes a back door to mint meaning, tips, mastery, or mega-services;
- **technology-silent** — explanation never treats buses, DI, meshes, queues, or frameworks as constitutional warrant;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match RS-07 / RSC-07 reconstruction needs.

Collaboration explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Programme VI educational meaning explainability;
- a substitute for MS001 service explainability or Programme VIII contract / event / evidence explainability;
- a rendering system, UI template, log shipper, tracing product, or analytics dashboard;
- a claim that clear collaboration speech guarantees learning or a pass;
- a licence to present scores, hop counts, or optimiser confidence as constitutional proof;
- a narrative that “the caller owns what the callee decided.”

## 3. Mandatory Explanation Questions

Every material runtime service collaboration should answer all five questions:

### RSCQ-01 — Which runtime services participated?

Name the RS-01…RS-07 services and their collaboration roles (initiator, participant, specialist, gate, recorder). Name the RSC-01…RSC-07 pattern(s) that applied.

### RSCQ-02 — Which constitutional contracts authorised collaboration?

Cite the RC-01…RC-07 binding(s) for each material act. “Service was available,” “bus delivered,” “DI resolved,” or “endpoint was called” are not constitutional authorisation.

### RSCQ-03 — Which constitutional artefacts were exchanged?

Distinguish:

- Contract dispositions and evaluation records;
- EIP-002 Educational Evidence / Programme VI warrants / Programme VII orchestration artefacts;
- WS2 validation eligibility outcomes when they gated collaboration;
- Published CE event processing records when event-driven;
- Recommendation artefacts / sets, EST/CST postures, workflow events as applicable;
- Explicit refuse / defer / escalate artefacts.

Never present coverage, time, engagement, or Twin estimates as understanding evidence unless the Evidence Model permits that classification. Never treat stack payloads as constitutional artefacts by default.

### RSCQ-04 — Which execution outputs were produced?

Name only published output classes: collaborative execution dispositions, workflow events, authority dispositions, recommendation artefacts / sets, EST/CST postures, evidence-consumption records, audit records, or explicit refuse/defer/escalate outcomes for the collaboration as a whole and for material participants.

### RSCQ-05 — Which constitutional boundaries were preserved?

Confirm the hard stops in `COLLABORATION_BOUNDARIES.md` that were checked — especially:

- no transfer of constitutional ownership;
- no merger of constitutional responsibilities;
- no bypass of runtime contracts;
- no reinterpretation of constitutional evidence;
- no modification of constitutional specifications;
- no elevation of technology into constitutional warrant.

## 5. Audience Rules

| Audience | May hear | Must not hear as educational law |
|----------|----------|----------------------------------|
| **Student** | What happened next, which kinds of help coordinated, why it was allowed, what evidence supported it, and what limits apply — in plain educational language | Internal RSC/RS/RC IDs as theatre; mesh traces; optimiser scores as “proof you are ready”; “the system owns this now” |
| **Developer / auditor** | Full RSCQ-01…05 answers, corpus paths, pattern IDs, service IDs, contract IDs, artefact exchange graph, boundary checks, non-absorption record | Product success metrics or framework / bus names substituted for constitutional refs |

Student and developer traces must describe the **same constitutional truth**. Vocabulary may differ; facts may not.

## 9. Out of Scope

This milestone does **not** implement:

- explanation renderers or UI copy systems;
- logging pipelines, distributed tracing products, or analytics;
- Runtime A services;
- service orchestration engines, dependency injection, message buses;
- microservices, REST APIs, queues, schedulers, workers, or databases;
- prompt templates or LLM narration layers.

Those may later *obey* this explainability law. They do not *define* it.
