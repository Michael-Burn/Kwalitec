## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor needs bright conditions for saying “this coordinated constitutional exposure among specialist APIs is finished” — and equally bright refusals when it is not. After MS001 has named APIs and MS002 has named composition patterns, Programme VIII Workstream 5 still needs one fulfilment checklist:

> **What must be true before runtime may affirm that participating APIs have fulfilled their published composition responsibilities?**

These criteria answer that question. They are **normative constitutional conditions**, not database predicates, API status codes, GraphQL stitch semantics, OpenAPI aggregate outcomes, or gateway-drain signals.

---

## 2. Criterion Catalogue

All material affirmed completions require **RACC-01 through RACC-05** to hold. Failure of any criterion ⇒ **not yet** or **refuse close**. Do not invent a partial “mostly complete” educational, interface-composition, execution, collaboration, or constitutional status.

| ID | Criterion | Primary question |
|----|-----------|------------------|
| **RACC-01** | Participating APIs fulfilled published responsibilities | Did each named RA participant complete its composition duties without identity merger? |
| **RACC-02** | Authorised composition pattern completed | Did the recognised RAC pattern finish or lawfully stop? |
| **RACC-03** | Required Runtime Interfaces exposed | Were required published RI contracts exposed without redefinition? |
| **RACC-04** | Authorised constitutional responses produced | Were required published constitutional responses produced by the composition? |
| **RACC-05** | Required composition records created | Do reconstructable constitutional composition records exist? |

Optional supporting honesty checks (do not replace RACC-01…RACC-05):

| ID | Check | Primary question |
|----|-------|------------------|
| **RACC-06** | Residual honesty | Are published composition duties for this instance exhausted? |
| **RACC-07** | Contract honesty | Were applicable RC-01…RC-07 obligations for composed exposures satisfied? |
| **RACC-08** | Request honesty | Were authorised constitutional requests processed under published law? |
| **RACC-09** | Continuity honesty | Was prior lawful history preserved (EIP-005)? |
| **RACC-10** | Non-claim honesty | Does the judgement refuse educational / interface-composition / execution / collaboration / workflow / merger / amendment overclaim? |

## 3. Criterion Definitions

### RACC-01 — Participating APIs fulfilled published responsibilities

**Definition.** Every named RA-01…RA-07 participant in the composition has fulfilled the *composition responsibilities assigned to it under published MS001 / MS002 law* for this instance — without absorbing another participant’s constitutional identity or duties, and without inventing unpublished participant types.

**Requires:**

- Identifiable published participant identities (RA catalogue; roles such as initiator / participant / specialist / gate / recorder / inspector as published).
- Evidence that each participant’s assigned composition acts were executed or lawfully stopped under that participant’s MS001 permitted exposures.
- Refusal of “caller finished so callee duties vanished” as fulfilment.
- Confirmation that MS001 identities and permitted / prohibited exposures remain intact for every participant at close.

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- That any participant’s lone execution cycle is REC-complete (orthogonal; may co-occur).
- That any related interface composition is RICC-complete (orthogonal; may co-occur).
- That any related service collaboration is RSCC-complete (orthogonal; may co-occur).
- That the learner understood, progressed, or mastered anything.
- That a Programme VII workflow instance is orchestration-complete.
- That constitutional law or the RA catalogue was updated.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutoring team that walks away mid-handoff because one specialist exposure left the room has not completed coordinated duties — even if the gateway looks quiet.

---

### RACC-02 — Authorised composition pattern completed

**Definition.** The RAC-01…RAC-07 pattern(s) authorised for this composition under published MS002 law have either:

1. reached their published terminal coordination step for the composition’s concern; or  
2. terminated in a **published** refuse / defer / escalate disposition that exhausts the composition’s remaining *composition* duties.

**Requires:**

- Identifiable published pattern identity (RAC-01…RAC-07; corpus-referenced).
- Evidence that pattern roles and coordination forms required for the instance were fulfilled or lawfully stopped.
- Refusal of “abandoned mid-pattern but gateway exited” as completion.
- No silent substitution of an unpublished “implied gateway” pattern.

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- That pattern completion certifies educational success.
- That pattern completion affirms RICC, REC, ECC, or RSCC completion by itself.
- That pattern completion amended composition law.

**Tutor rationale.** Completing coordinated tutoring includes finishing the published composition form — not inventing a new gateway mid-flight and calling the improvisation done.

---

### RACC-03 — Required Runtime Interfaces exposed

**Definition.** Every published Runtime Interface required for the composition’s material exposures has been exposed through the participating APIs — and only published RI-01…RI-07 contracts were exposed, without redefinition, absorption, or mega-interface invention.

**Requires:**

- Exposures map to published RI catalogue identities bound to participating RA APIs under MS001 / WS4 law.
- No unpublished interfaces, mega-interfaces, or “gateway-improved” interface meanings were treated as lawful composition exposures.
- Absence of required published interface exposures blocks completion.
- Interfaces retain their published WS4 meanings at close (interface integrity).

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- That exposing interfaces caused educational success.
- That exposing interfaces completed interface composition (RICC) by itself.
- That exposing interfaces amended the Constitution or merged RA identities.
- That exposing interfaces redefined RI contracts.

**Tutor rationale.** Completing coordinated API work includes surfacing the authorised interfaces of that coordination — not inventing souvenir endpoints, and not declaring done while required interfaces never appeared.

---

### RACC-04 — Authorised constitutional responses produced

**Definition.** Every published constitutional response required for the composition’s material concern has been produced — and only authorised constitutional responses were produced.

**Requires:**

- Responses map to published classes (dispositions, artefacts, audit trails, diagnostic records, refuse/defer/escalate outcomes, or other corpus-published results).
- No unpublished tips, mastery claims, ownership transfers, mega-API products, interface redefinitions, or evidence reclassifications were treated as lawful composition responses.
- Absence of required published responses blocks completion.
- Responses remain attributable to the participating RA identities that lawfully produced them — never collapsed into an unpublished mega-response author.

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- That produced responses caused educational success.
- That producing an audit trail completed interface composition, execution, collaboration, or workflow fulfilment.
- That producing responses amended the Constitution, merged RA identities, or redefined Runtime Interfaces.

**Tutor rationale.** Completing coordinated API work includes producing the authorised results of that coordination — not inventing celebration payloads, and not declaring done while required responses never appeared.

---

### RACC-05 — Required composition records created

**Definition.** Published composition records required by RA-06 / RAC audit obligations (and applicable RC-07 / CE-06 obligations) exist and are sufficient to reconstruct the composition’s constitutional trail.

**Requires:**

- Records identify participants, RAC pattern(s), exposed Runtime Interfaces, authorising contracts, constitutional requests processed, constitutional responses produced, composition dispositions, and boundary preservation.
- Continuity across retries / replacements does not erase prior lawful record obligations (EIP-005).
- Technical request logs, tracing spans, OpenAPI journals, or gateway journals without constitutional references do **not** satisfy this criterion.

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- A particular database schema, log shipper, tracing product, or analytics pipeline.
- That recording equals learning, interface composition completion, execution completion, or collaboration completion.
- That a complete composition audit trail certifies mastery or workflow close.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor team that cannot later reconstruct which APIs composed under which warrant has not closed the educational machine honestly — even if the gateway is quiet.

## 4. Supporting Honesty Checks

### RACC-06 — Residual honesty

For this composition instance’s published constitutional concern, no material *API composition* responsibility remains outstanding under MS001 / MS002 / upstream published pattern law. Residual published coordination steps, required interface exposures, required responses, required records, or pending published composition evaluations for *this instance* are absent or explicitly deferred to a **new** published composition / lawful await — not silently dropped. Concurrent duties belonging to **other** compositions (or to interface-composition / lone execution / evidence-handling / collaboration concerns) are not falsely treated as residuals of this instance (and vice versa).

### RACC-07 — Contract honesty

Every RC-01…RC-07 contract applicable to the composition’s material exposures must have been satisfied for those exposures — or the composition lawfully stopped under published refusal rules when satisfaction was impossible. Calling another API never substitutes for RC authorisation.

### RACC-08 — Request honesty

Every authorised constitutional request required for the composition’s material exposures has been processed under published MS001 / MS002 law — and only authorised constitutional requests were processed. Unpublished tips, mega-API payloads, redefined interface inputs, or invented event types are not lawful composition requests. Absence of required published request processing blocks completion.

### RACC-09 — Continuity honesty

Affirming composition completion must not erase, silently rewrite, or orphan prior lawful educational / constitutional history (EIP-005). Replacement compositions re-enter at published points; they do not invent amnesia as fulfilment.

### RACC-10 — Non-claim honesty

The completion judgement must refuse overclaims:

| Forbidden overclaim | Lawful statement |
|---------------------|------------------|
| Learner success / mastery / progress | Composition responsibilities for this instance are fulfilled |
| Interface composition complete / RICC complete | Composition responsibilities are fulfilled (interface-composition fulfilment requires WS4 / MS003) |
| Execution completion / REC complete | Composition responsibilities are fulfilled (execution-cycle fulfilment requires WS1 / MS003) |
| Evidence-handling complete / ECC complete | Composition responsibilities are fulfilled (evidence-handling fulfilment requires WS2 / MS003) |
| Service collaboration complete / RSCC complete | Composition responsibilities are fulfilled (collaboration fulfilment requires WS3 / MS003) |
| Programme VII workflow complete | Composition for this instance is complete (orchestration fulfilment requires Programme VII corpus) |
| API identities merged / Runtime Interfaces redefined | Each participant retains MS001 identity; no interface redefinition occurred |
| Constitutional amendment occurred | No constitutional specification was modified by this close |

## 5. Judgement Classes

| Judgement | When | Effect |
|-----------|------|--------|
| **Affirm composition complete** | RACC-01…RACC-05 hold (and RACC-06…RACC-10 honesty checks pass where applicable) | Composition instance may be recorded as API-composition-complete under this Model |
| **Not yet** | One or more criteria fail because published composition duties remain in progress under law | Do not affirm; continue published pattern or await published inputs |
| **Refuse close** | Criteria fail because the composition was unlawful, abandoned, merged identities, redefined interfaces, or would require invention to “look done” | Do not affirm; escalate / remediate under published governance — never invent fulfilment |

There is no fourth judgement called “educationally / interface-composition / execution / collaboration / workflow complete because API composition finished.”

---

## 6. Explicit Non-Criteria

The following are **never** sufficient (and often never relevant) as composition completion criteria:

- elapsed time, wall-clock duration, or SLA success;
- process exit codes, HTTP 200, GraphQL stitch success, gRPC status OK, OpenAPI aggregate success, or BFF aggregate success;
- queue depth zero, gateway drained, or “no requests remaining”;
- SDK batch completed / auth middleware passed / network healthy;
- UI spinner stopped / celebration animation shown;
- Adaptive scores, Twin estimates, engagement metrics, or streak theatre;
- presence of *any* log line mentioning “composition complete”;
- Runtime Interface Composition Completion (RICC) alone (orthogonal; may co-occur but does not replace RACC-01…RACC-05);
- Runtime Execution Completion (REC) alone (orthogonal; may co-occur but does not replace RACC-01…RACC-05);
- Evidence Consumption Completion (ECC) alone (orthogonal; never substitutes);
- Service Collaboration Completion (RSCC) alone (orthogonal; may co-occur but does not replace RACC-01…RACC-05);
- Programme VII workflow completion alone (orthogonal; may co-occur but does not replace RACC-01…RACC-05);
- Programme VI educational completion alone (orthogonal; never inferred from composition close);
- majority vote of APIs, feature-flag theatre, or demo urgency;
- silent merger of leftover duties into the initiator “to clear the backlog.”

| May co-occur? | Yes — judgements may land in the same window | Yes — but each corpus judges its own fulfilment |

An API composition may affirm RACC completion while a related interface composition remains RICC-incomplete, a related execution cycle remains REC-incomplete, a related collaboration remains RSCC-incomplete, or a related workflow remains orchestration-incomplete.  
An interface composition, execution cycle, collaboration, or workflow may affirm its own completion while an unrelated API composition remains open.  
Honest systems keep all judgements named and separate — and never treat API composition close as licence to merge RA identities or redefine Runtime Interfaces.

---

## 8. Closing Statement

> **Runtime API composition is complete only when participating APIs fulfilled published responsibilities, the authorised composition pattern completed, required Runtime Interfaces were exposed, authorised constitutional responses were produced, and required composition records exist.  
> Anything less is unfinished constitutional API coordination.  
> Anything more — learning success, interface composition completion, execution completion, service collaboration completion, workflow close, identity merger, interface redefinition, or law change — is a different judgement owned elsewhere.**
