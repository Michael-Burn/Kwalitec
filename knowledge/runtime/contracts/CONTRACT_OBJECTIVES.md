## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “requests served” or “tips shown.” The tutor optimises **faithful educational execution**: the student experiences the constitutional voice that was already authorised — with honest evidence, intact ownership, and reconstructable history.

These objectives bind every Runtime A behaviour and every successor runtime that claims constitutional compliance.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### RCO-01 — Execute constitutional law

**Definition.** Ensure that software actions apply only published constitutional rules from the Constitution, EIP, Programme VI, and Programme VII — or honestly refuse when no published rule authorises the action.

**Includes:**

- Evaluating published workflow conditions and advancing only through published stages.
- Consuming published ownership maps before acting on educational decisions.
- Surfacing only constitutionally structured recommendations with named owners and sources.
- Applying published educational-state entry/exit and transition law.
- Writing or reading educational facts only under EIP-001 / Evidence Model rights.
- Preferring lawful refusal / deferral / escalation over invented certainty.

**Excludes:**

- Inventing unpublished conditions, tips, owners, evidence classes, or state postures “to keep the product moving.”
- Optimising for engagement, latency, or conversion as a substitute for constitutional fidelity.
- Treating Version 2 Adaptive / Twin / Experience heuristics as replacements for Programmes VI–VII.
- Executing “close enough” approximations of constitutional rules without documenting a corpus amendment.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when the software coach improvises educational law. Execution is care; improvisation is harm.

---

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### RCO-02 — Preserve educational meaning

**Definition.** Ensure that runtime execution never redefines Programme VI educational questions, Canonical Study Plan commitments, Knowledge & Mastery claim ladders, or coach envelopes.

**Tutor rationale.** A service that “helpfully” rewrites revision as first learning, or coverage as mastery, destroys constitutional layering even if the UX feels smoother.

**Manifestations:**

- Runtime consumes coach / planner meaning; it does not amend it.
- Claim ladder honesty (coverage ≠ understanding ≠ mastery) survives execution (EIP-006).
- Plan contracts remain Master Planner / Scheduling artefacts.
- Forbidden actions in `CONTRACT_BOUNDARIES.md` are hard stops.

---

### RCO-03 — Preserve governance

**Definition.** Ensure that ownership, permission, refusal, delegation, conflict disposition, and mutation rights remain as published — never absorbed by runtime convenience, code proximity, or product urgency.

**Tutor rationale.** Silent empire-building by services (“this endpoint owns the tip now”) recreates the very authority confusion Programme VII exists to prevent.

**Manifestations:**

- Authority domains and conflict outcomes are consumed, not rewritten.
- EIP-001 mutation rights are not granted by renaming a writer.
- Workflow participation and handoffs remain WS1 law.
- Emergency load or deadlines never mint new owners.

---

### RCO-04 — Preserve auditability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material runtime action leaves a reconstructable constitutional trail: which contract ran, which corpus authorised it, which evidence was consumed, and which outputs were produced.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later say *why* yesterday’s advice was given cannot be trusted. Runtime without audit is educational amnesia.

**Manifestations:**

- Audit records preserve constitutional references, not only technical logs.
- Continuity (EIP-005) is preserved across retries, replacements, and redeploys.
- Missing provenance is a defect, not an acceptable optimisation.
- RC-07 Audit Contract obligations apply to all material executions.

---

### RCO-05 — Preserve explainability

**Definition.** Ensure that runtime execution remains speakable under EIP-003: students and developers can answer the constitutional explainability questions without receiving redefined educational meaning.

**Tutor rationale.** Opaque execution recreates black-box tutoring. Clear execution speech is part of educational honesty.

**Manifestations:**

- Explanations cite contracts, corpora, evidence, outputs, and boundaries (`CONTRACT_EXPLAINABILITY.md`).
- Student speech and developer traces share one truth with different vocabulary.
- Scores and optimiser confidence are never presented as constitutional warrant.
- Explanation never becomes a back door to invent tips or mastery.

---

### RCO-06 — Remain replaceable

**Definition.** Ensure that Runtime A (and any named runtime) is treated as a replaceable executor. Constitutional truth must outlive frameworks, services, databases, and deployment topology.

**Tutor rationale.** If the product can only tell educational truth while a particular codebase exists, the codebase has become an unlawful constitution.

**Manifestations:**

- Contracts are implementation-independent.
- Successor runtimes must honour the same RC catalogue and boundaries.
- Storage formats and APIs are delivery details; they are not educational law.
- “Only this service can decide” is a governance failure unless the Constitution / Programmes say so.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| RCO-01 conflicts with engagement metrics | Constitutional execution wins |
| RCO-02 conflicts with convenience rewriting | Meaning preservation wins |
| RCO-03 conflicts with “temporary” ownership shortcuts | Governance wins |
| RCO-04 conflicts with log volume reduction that erases constitutional refs | Auditability wins |
| RCO-05 conflicts with cryptic internal jargon as student speech | Explainability honesty wins |
| RCO-06 conflicts with lock-in to a single stack | Replaceability wins |

No supporting objective authorises violating RCO-01.

---

## 5. Non-Objectives

Runtime contracts do **not** optimise for:

- tip volume, click-through, or streak theatre as educational success;
- inventing educational certainty when warrants are incomplete;
- collapsing Programmes VI–VII into one mega-service;
- making Runtime A irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive estimates as substitutes for constitutional producers;
- treating documentation as optional commentary on code behaviour.

---

## 6. Closing Statement

> **A runtime that is fast, polished, and constitutionally unfaithful is a failed educational system.  
> A runtime that is replaceable, auditable, and faithful to published law is doing its only job.**
