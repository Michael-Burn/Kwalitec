## 1. Purpose

Runtime without a closed contract catalogue invents law by proximity: whichever service happens to hold the data becomes the tutor.

This catalogue names the only lawful execution relationships between constitutional producers and runtime consumers.

## 3. RC-01 — Execution Contract

### Constitutional producer

Educational Constitution, EIP corpus, and the Programme VI / VII specifications that publish the rule being applied.

### Constitutional consumer

Runtime A (and lawful successor runtime implementations).

### Contract purpose

Bind software so that every material educational action is an **execution of published constitutional law**, not an improvisation.

### Permitted execution

- Evaluate published constitutional conditions against concrete learner circumstances.
- Perform actions explicitly authorised by a published rule.
- Refuse, defer, or escalate when published law requires refusal / deferral / escalation.
- Compose multiple published rules when the corpora themselves allow composition.
- Emit only published constitutional event classes when law requires emission.

### Prohibited execution

- Invent unpublished rules, conditions, or educational meanings.
- Treat engineering heuristics, A/B winners, or product preference as constitutional authorisation.
- Redefine Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII text in code comments or constants.
- Claim execution success as proof of mastery, pass certainty, or educational truth amendment.

## 4. RC-02 — Evidence Consumption Contract

### Constitutional producer

[`../../educational/EDUCATIONAL_EVIDENCE_MODEL.md`](../../educational/EDUCATIONAL_EVIDENCE_MODEL.md) (EIP-002), Knowledge & Mastery (EIP-006), and permitted writers under the State Authority Matrix (EIP-001).

### Constitutional consumer

Runtime A (and lawful successors) when reading or applying observational educational warrants.

### Contract purpose

Ensure runtime **consumes Educational Evidence as classified**, and never reinterprets coverage, time-on-task, confidence, or engagement as understanding or mastery.

### Permitted execution

- Read evidence artefacts and claim types as published.
- Apply published claim-ladder honesty in downstream speech and decisions.
- Route mutation requests only to EIP-001-permitted writers.
- Surface honest understatement when evidence is incomplete or conflicting.
- Preserve continuity of evidence history (EIP-005).

### Prohibited execution

- Reclassify evidence classes in runtime (e.g. treating session completion as mastery).
- Mint Estimated Knowledge / Mastery from coaching convenience alone.
- Erase or rewrite evidence history to simplify UX.
- Present Twin / Adaptive estimates as if they were primary Evidence Model observations when they are not.
- Bypass Evidence Model succession rules with “temporary” certainty.

## 5. RC-03 — Workflow Execution Contract

### Constitutional producer

Programme VII Workstream 1 corpora under [`../../orchestration/workflows/`](../../orchestration/workflows/), [`../../orchestration/workflow_transitions/`](../../orchestration/workflow_transitions/), and [`../../orchestration/workflow_completion/`](../../orchestration/workflow_completion/).

### Constitutional consumer

Runtime A (and lawful successors) when initiating, advancing, handing off, or concluding educational orchestration.

### Contract purpose

Ensure runtime **executes published workflow law** — events, stages, transitions, and completion — without becoming a substitute tutor or inventing orchestration shortcuts.

### Permitted execution

- Accept published workflow events as initiation / continuation triggers.
- Advance only through published stages under published transition conditions.
- Record published participation and outcome classes.
- Conclude workflows only under published completion criteria.
- Generate published constitutional workflow events / traces required by WS1 explainability.

### Prohibited execution

- Bypass stages, transitions, or completion criteria for product convenience.
- Invent workflow stages, events, or completion meanings in runtime.
- Use orchestration to redefine Programme VI educational meaning.
- Treat workflow progress as mastery, success, or plan completion unless published law says so.
- Skip authority checks (RC-04) while claiming lawful workflow advancement.

## 6. RC-04 — Authority Consumption Contract

### Constitutional producer

Programme VII Workstream 2 corpora under [`../../orchestration/authority/`](../../orchestration/authority/), [`../../orchestration/conflict_resolution/`](../../orchestration/conflict_resolution/), and [`../../orchestration/authority_explainability/`](../../orchestration/authority_explainability/).

### Constitutional consumer

Runtime A (and lawful successors) when deciding who may act, refuse, delegate, or disposition concurrent valid guidance.

### Contract purpose

Ensure runtime **consumes published ownership and conflict law**, and never absorbs educational domains by code proximity.

### Permitted execution

- Resolve the constitutional owner of a decision class from the Authority Model.
- Apply published permission, refusal, and delegation outcomes.
- Apply published conflict-resolution outcomes among valid competing recommendations.
- Preserve owner labels through packaging and delivery.
- Explain permission / refusal using published authority explainability obligations.

### Prohibited execution

- Invent or transfer ownership outside published domains.
- Rank or merge coaches by undocumented heuristics when conflict law applies.
- Treat UI, adapters, Twin, or Adaptive as educational decision owners unless the Authority Model says so.
- Grant mutation rights as a side effect of display or orchestration proximity.
- Silence rightful refusal by inventing a tip to fill a slot.

## 7. RC-05 — Recommendation Consumption Contract

### Constitutional producer

Programme VII Workstream 3 corpora under [`../../orchestration/recommendations/`](../../orchestration/recommendations/), [`../../orchestration/recommendation_assembly/`](../../orchestration/recommendation_assembly/), and [`../../orchestration/recommendation_explainability/`](../../orchestration/recommendation_explainability/), plus the Programme VI owner artefacts that warrant each tip.

### Constitutional consumer

Runtime A (and lawful successors) when packaging, assembling, surfacing, or refusing educational recommendations.

### Contract purpose

Ensure runtime **consumes constitutional recommendations as communicative artefacts**, and never invents tips, rewrites ownership, or executes workflows under a recommendation label.

### Permitted execution

- Surface structured recommendations that already satisfy WS3 structure, sources, and boundaries.
- Assemble lawful recommendations into published recommendation sets.
- Communicate refusal / deferral / no-recommendation when warrants fail.
- Preserve provenance, owner, evidence, and constitutional references through delivery.
- Explain recommendation sets under published set-explainability obligations.

### Prohibited execution

- Invent tips from undocumented heuristics, engagement models, or “AI tutor” improvisation.
- Reinterpret Educational Evidence while packaging speech.
- Alter constitutional ownership by merging coaches into one silent mega-tip.
- Execute workflows, mutate plans, or advance state under recommendation authority alone.
- Present ranking scores as constitutional educational warrant.

## 8. RC-06 — State Consumption Contract

### Constitutional producer

Programme VII Workstream 4 corpora under [`../../orchestration/state/`](../../orchestration/state/), [`../../orchestration/state_transitions/`](../../orchestration/state_transitions/), and [`../../orchestration/state_explainability/`](../../orchestration/state_explainability/), distinct from EIP-001 meaning-bearing educational-state mutation rights.

### Constitutional consumer

Runtime A (and lawful successors) when reading, applying, or transitioning constitutional educational **context** postures.

### Contract purpose

Ensure runtime **consumes published educational-context (EST/CST) law**, and never invents focus postures, implies mastery/success from context alone, or erases continuity.

### Permitted execution

- Represent live educational context only via published EST postures.
- Apply published CST succession under published conditions.
- Hold / wait / refuse-remain when law requires continuity over motion.
- Explain live context and lawful succession under WS4 explainability obligations.
- Keep context speech distinct from Programme VI tip warrant and EIP-002 understanding evidence.

### Prohibited execution

- Invent unpublished EST/CST types in runtime.
- Treat context posture as mastery, pass certainty, workflow completion, or tip authorisation.
- Transfer ownership or mint recommendations via state labels.
- Erase prior lawful context to simplify narration (EIP-005).
- Confuse EIP-001 meaning-bearing mutation rights with WS4 contextual representation.

## 9. RC-07 — Audit Contract

### Constitutional producer

Educational Constitution; EIP-003 / EIP-005; this Programme VIII Runtime Contract Model; and the producer corpora of the audited act (RC-01…RC-06).

### Constitutional consumer

Runtime A (and lawful successors) for every material educational execution.

### Contract purpose

Ensure runtime **preserves reconstructable constitutional audit records** for material actions — so execution remains accountable without becoming a second constitution.

### Permitted execution

- Record which RC contract(s) executed.
- Record which constitutional corpus / artefact authorised the act.
- Record which constitutional evidence (if any) was consumed.
- Record which constitutional outputs / events were produced.
- Record which boundaries were checked and preserved.
- Retain continuity-safe history across retries and replacements.

### Prohibited execution

- Emit audit theatre that invents constitutional refs after the fact.
- Strip constitutional provenance while keeping technical logs only.
- Use audit records to redefine educational meaning or ownership.
- Treat missing audit as acceptable for “low-risk” educational mutations or primary student-facing tips.
- Present analytics dashboards as substitutes for constitutional audit obligations.

## 10. Composition Rules

1. **Primary mapping required.** Every material runtime act names at least one primary RC.
2. **Cross-cutting honesty.** Workflow acts that surface tips also bind RC-03 + RC-05 (+ RC-04 when ownership is at stake).
3. **Evidence before certainty.** Acts that claim understanding or mastery bind RC-02.
4. **Context ≠ meaning.** RC-06 never substitutes for Programme VI meaning (RC-01 consumption of VI) or tip law (RC-05).
5. **Audit always.** RC-07 binds every material act in RC-01…RC-06.
6. **Closed catalogue.** New RC types require a Programme VIII constitutional amendment — not a silent service invention.
