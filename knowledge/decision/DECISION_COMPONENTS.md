## 1. Purpose

Decision production without a closed component catalogue invents law by proximity: whichever services, queues, or “ambient” product steps happen to finish become the tutor’s “proof the system decided.”

This catalogue names the only lawful constitutional decision architecture components a run may apply — and binds each component to constitutional purpose, inputs, outputs, and permitted / prohibited responsibilities.

---

## 2. Catalogue Overview

| ID | Component | Constitutional purpose (short) | Primary inputs | Primary outputs |
|----|-----------|--------------------------------|----------------|-----------------|
| **CDA-01** | Outcome Intake | Admit only completed, reconstructable execution outcomes | Published WS2 completed outcomes + completion identity | Admitted outcome set (or lawful refuse) |
| **CDA-02** | Decision Rule Evaluator | Apply published constitutional decision rules to admitted outcomes | Admitted outcomes + published decision-rule identities | Decision-rule evaluation records |
| **CDA-03** | Decision Constructor | Assemble a candidate constitutional decision from evaluation records | Evaluation records + authority references | Candidate constitutional decision |
| **CDA-04** | Decision Validator | Confirm the candidate decision is constitutionally valid | Candidate decision + published validity expectations | Validation disposition (valid / invalid / refuse) |
| **CDA-05** | Decision Publisher | Publish valid constitutional decisions for audit and explainability | Validated decision + state records | Published constitutional decision |
| **CDA-06** | Decision State Manager | Maintain speakable decision-state transitions for the run | Component progress signals | Decision-state record (not educational / independent-governance state) |

Material constitutional decision production must map to one or more of these components as published law requires. Cross-cutting situations may bind multiple CDA components; none may invent a component outside this catalogue.

**Relation to WS1:** CECX / CECR / CECC prepare, resolve, and complete context. CDA never redefines preparation catalogues and never begins against incomplete preparation disguised as outcomes.

**Relation to WS2:** CEE / CEEL / CEEC execute rules, organise execution, and complete execution into outcomes. CDA consumes *completed outcomes*; it never re-executes constitutional rules and never redefines engine catalogues.

**Relation to Programme VIII / IX catalogues:** Those catalogues remain defined solely by Programmes VIII / IX. CDA may *apply published decision rules that reference* their identities under constitutional authority; it does not replace, re-run, or independently invent them outside that authority.

**Relation to educational quality:** No CDA component judges whether learning was good, whether a tip was wise, or whether the student is ready. Decision ≠ quality. Constitutional decision ≠ constitutional amendment.

## 3. CDA-01 — Outcome Intake

### Constitutional purpose

Admit only **completed, reconstructable execution outcomes** as lawful inputs to decision production — so the Decision Architecture knows *which outcomes may be consumed* without inventing outcomes, completing unfinished execution, or beginning against incomplete publication.

### Constitutional inputs

- Published WS2 execution outcomes whose required execution lifecycle has lawfully completed (WS2 / MS003 fulfilment confirmed where required by published law).
- Outcome identities, execution identity, and reconstructable traces sufficient to bind decisions.
- Published CDAO-01 / CDAO-05 expectations.

### Constitutional outputs

- Admitted outcome set (references and reconstructable content as published).
- Explicit exclusion of incomplete, unpublished, or invented outcomes.
- Honest refuse when required completed outcomes cannot be admitted without invention.

### Permitted responsibilities

- Verify completion identity / publication status of candidate outcomes under WS2 completion law.
- Admit reconstructable completed outcomes for downstream CDA components.
- Record which outcomes were admitted and which were refused.
- Refuse intake when outcomes are incomplete, unpublished, or non-reconstructable.

### Prohibited responsibilities

- Invent unpublished outcomes or “implied house results.”
- Complete unfinished WS2 execution under an intake label.
- Re-execute constitutional rules to manufacture missing outcomes.
- Soft-amend outcome content to make decision production convenient.
- Begin intake against ambient product logs presented as completed constitutional outcomes.

## 4. CDA-02 — Decision Rule Evaluator

### Constitutional purpose

Identify and apply **published constitutional decision rules** to the admitted outcome set — so the Decision Architecture knows *which decision rules map outcomes to lawful dispositions* without re-executing WS2 constitutional rules, inventing decision customs, or soft-amending specifications.

### Constitutional inputs

- Admitted outcome set from CDA-01.
- Named published constitutional decision-rule identities / specification clauses in force for the concern.
- Authority references already available through completed outcomes and published corpora.
- Published CDAO-01 / CDAO-02 / CDAO-05 expectations.

### Constitutional outputs

- Decision-rule evaluation records: which published decision rules applied, to which admitted outcomes, with what reconstructable evaluation result.
- Explicit exclusion of unpublished decision customs from evaluation.
- Honest refuse when required published decision rules cannot be identified without invention.

### Permitted responsibilities

- Select published decision rules applicable to the admitted outcomes.
- Evaluate those decision rules exactly as published against the admitted outcomes.
- Record rule identities, application order as published, and evaluation results.
- Refuse evaluation when required decision rules are unpublished or contradictory under published law.

### Prohibited responsibilities

- Execute constitutional rules (WS2 CEE responsibility) under a “decision rule” label.
- Invent unpublished decision rules or soft-amend published decision-rule text.
- Reinterpret Educational Interpretation Principles as part of evaluation.
- Reorder or omit published decision rules to force a preferred disposition.
- Treat Programme IX judgement production as free-form evaluation outside published decision-rule and authority constraints.

## 5. CDA-03 — Decision Constructor

### Constitutional purpose

Assemble a **candidate constitutional decision** from decision-rule evaluation records and governing authority references — so a speakable disposition exists for validation without pretending construction alone confers validity, legislation, or independent governance.

### Constitutional inputs

- Decision-rule evaluation records from CDA-02.
- Admitted outcome identities from CDA-01.
- Named constitutional authority references governing the decision.
- Published CDAO-01 / CDAO-03 / CDAO-05 expectations.

### Constitutional outputs

- Candidate constitutional decision: reconstructable disposition draft citing outcomes, decision rules, and authority.
- Explicit non-claim: construction is not publication of a validated decision.
- Honest incomplete construction note when required elements cannot be assembled without invention.

### Permitted responsibilities

- Compose candidate dispositions from evaluation records exactly as published decision rules warrant.
- Bind the candidate to admitted outcome identities and authority references.
- Preserve reconstructable construction traces for validation and explainability.
- Refuse construction when evaluation records are insufficient under published law.

### Prohibited responsibilities

- Amend constitutional specifications in construction prose.
- Reinterpret EIP to invent a preferred candidate disposition.
- Mint educational recommendations, tips, or ownership transfers as if they were CDA decisions (Programme VII remains owner of those meanings).
- Publish the candidate as valid without CDA-04 validation.
- Elevate construction machinery into constitutional authority.

## 6. CDA-04 — Decision Validator

### Constitutional purpose

Confirm that a candidate constitutional decision is **constitutionally valid** — that it cites completed outcomes, applies published decision rules, names governing authority, and preserves Decision Architecture boundaries — before any publication as a lawful constitutional decision.

### Constitutional inputs

- Candidate constitutional decision from CDA-03.
- Admitted outcome set and evaluation records.
- Published validity expectations under CDAO-01 and [`DECISION_BOUNDARIES.md`](DECISION_BOUNDARIES.md).
- Published CDAO-01 / CDAO-05 expectations.

### Constitutional outputs

- Validation disposition: valid / invalid / refuse (with reconstructable reasons).
- Explicit boundary-integrity confirmation or named boundary defect.
- Honest refuse when validation cannot proceed without inventing unpublished criteria.

### Permitted responsibilities

- Check that required outcome identities, decision-rule identities, and authority references are present and consistent.
- Confirm the candidate does not claim rule re-execution, EIP reinterpretation, specification amendment, or authority replacement.
- Record validation disposition reconstructably.
- Block publication of invalid candidates as constitutionally valid.

### Prohibited responsibilities

- Soft-pass invalid candidates for demo continuity.
- Invent unpublished validity criteria.
- Re-execute constitutional rules as a substitute for decision validation.
- Treat CI greens, UI ticks, or Twin narratives as validation under constitutional law.
- Amend specifications so a candidate becomes “valid.”

## 7. CDA-05 — Decision Publisher

### Constitutional purpose

Publish **validated constitutional decisions** for downstream audit and explainability — so lawful decisions become available to consumers without upgrading them into constitutional amendments, EIP replacements, stack freezes, or independent governance outside constitutional authority.

### Constitutional inputs

- Validated constitutional decision (CDA-04 disposition = valid).
- Decision-state records from CDA-06 as required for reconstructability.
- Published CDAO-01 / CDAO-03 / CDAO-04 / CDAO-05 expectations.

### Constitutional outputs

- Published constitutional decision (identity, disposition, cited outcomes, cited decision rules, cited authority, validation confirmation).
- Explicit non-claims: not new law; not EIP replacement; not stack freeze; not independent governance outside authority.
- Honest non-publication when validation is not valid.

### Permitted responsibilities

- Publish only validated constitutional decisions.
- Preserve reconstructable publication records for audit and explainability.
- Make published decisions available to lawful downstream consumers.
- Refuse publication when validation failed or required traces are missing.

### Prohibited responsibilities

- Publish invalid or unvalidated candidates as constitutionally valid.
- Soft-amend published decision content after validation without a new lawful cycle.
- Present publication as constitutional amendment, certification seal, or EIP replacement.
- Freeze a preferred stack as law because a decision was published on it.
- Suppress publication of lawful refuse / invalid dispositions when honesty requires them to be speakable.

## 8. CDA-06 — Decision State Manager

### Constitutional purpose

Maintain **speakable decision-state transitions** for the Decision Architecture run — so progress is reconstructable without inventing educational state, minting independent governance dispositions, or treating state machinery as constitutional authority.

### Constitutional inputs

- Progress signals from CDA-01…CDA-05.
- Published decision-state vocabulary for this architecture (intake → evaluation → construction → validation → publication / refuse).
- Published CDAO-03 / CDAO-05 expectations.

### Constitutional outputs

- Decision-state record: ordered transitions with timestamps / identities as published law requires.
- Explicit distinction from educational state and from Programme IX governance dispositions.
- Honest stop-state when the run refuses or invalidates.

### Permitted responsibilities

- Record lawful state transitions corresponding to published CDA component progress.
- Expose state for explainability and audit reconstruction.
- Mark refuse / invalid terminal states when decision production cannot lawfully continue.
- Keep state vocabulary closed and published.

### Prohibited responsibilities

- Invent educational state (mastery, readiness, continuity mutations) under decision-state labels.
- Mint independent governance dispositions (compliance, certification, verification) as decision state.
- Use state transitions to waive required components or completed-outcome prerequisites.
- Treat state machinery as constitutional authority or as a licence to amend specifications.
- Hide refuse / invalid states to preserve demo continuity.

## 9. Composition Rules

1. **Closed catalogue.** Only CDA-01…CDA-06 may be applied.
2. **Intake first.** CDA-02…CDA-05 may not lawfully proceed without CDA-01 admission (or lawful refuse).
3. **Validate before publish.** CDA-05 may publish as valid only after CDA-04 valid disposition.
4. **State accompanies the run.** CDA-06 records progress; it never substitutes for missing components.
5. **No parallel invention.** Ambient product workflows are not constitutional components unless mapped to this catalogue.
6. **Cross-binding allowed.** A material concern may require multiple components; it may not invent a seventh component identity.
7. **Refuse is a lawful product.** Incomplete / invalid / refused runs remain constitutionally preferable to improvised decisions.
