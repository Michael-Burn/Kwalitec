## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *why a decision was reached* after the script was marked — which completed outcomes were used, which decision criteria applied, what disposition resulted, who owned the standard, and which hard stops remained intact — is not coaching; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–IX have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, runtime, conformance, verification, compliance, certification, and evolution speech speakable, after Workstream 1 has made preparation speech speakable, and after Workstream 2 has made execution speech speakable, Programme X still needs one decision-speech answer:

> **“Which execution outcomes were consumed, which decision rules were applied, which constitutional decision was produced, which constitutional authority governed the decision, and which constitutional boundaries remained intact?”**

That answer must cover every material constitutional decision without inventing educational meaning, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, freezing a stack as law, inventing unpublished CDA components, re-executing constitutional rules, reinterpreting EIP, or presenting decision publication as educational quality or independent governance outside constitutional authority.

## 2. What Decision Explainability Is

**Constitutional Decision Architecture Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **lawful transformation of completed execution outcomes into constitutional decisions under published CDA components** — not to invent a second tutor voice from ambient product logs, and not to grade the student or mint independent governance outside constitutional authority.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Execution outcomes consumed** | Named completed WS2 outcome identities admitted under CDA-01 | Which execution outcomes were consumed? |
| **Decision rules applied** | Named published constitutional decision-rule identities evaluated under CDA-02 | Which decision rules were applied? |
| **Constitutional decision produced** | Reconstructable disposition constructed / validated / published under CDA-03…CDA-05 | Which constitutional decision was produced? |
| **Constitutional authority governing** | Named published corpora that owned the standard for the decision | Which constitutional authority governed the decision? |
| **Boundaries remaining intact** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden execution / reinterpretation / amendment / independent governance / substitution did not occur | Which constitutional boundaries remained intact? |

Decision Explainability is:

- **decision-centred** — it narrates how completed outcomes became decisions, not how rules were executed upstream;
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CDA identities;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published specifications, decision rules, and authority;
- **non-executive** — explanation never pretends that deciding equals re-running WS2 rule execution;
- **non-interpretive** — explanation never smuggles EIP reinterpretation into “decision notes”;
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to amend corpora;
- **non-independent-governance** — explanation never presents decision publication as free-form governance outside published authority;
- **quality-honest** — decision publication is never narrated as mastery, readiness, or educational quality;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence speech, Programme IX judgement speech, WS1 preparation speech, or WS2 execution speech;
- **repeatability-honest** — explanations must not paper over race-dependent or anonymous ambient decision production.

Decision Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–IX / WS1 / WS2 meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a substitute for Programme VIII runtime explainability or Programme IX judgement explainability corpora;
- a substitute for WS1 CECXEQ / CECREQ / CECCQ preparation speech;
- a substitute for WS2 CEEEQ / lifecycle / completion explainability speech;
- a rendering system, UI template, CI badge, log shipper, or analytics dashboard;
- a claim that clear decision speech guarantees learning, a pass, conformity, compliance, or certification;
- a licence to present pipeline greens, stack traces, Twin narratives, or Programme II simulation diffs as constitutional proof.

## 3. Mandatory Explanation Questions

Every material constitutional decision should answer all five questions:

### CDAEQ-01 — Which execution outcomes were consumed?

Name the primary completed WS2 execution outcome identities admitted under CDA-01 (paths, execution identity, outcome identities, and completion confirmation as applicable). Distinguish *outcomes available* from *outcomes admitted*. Incomplete, unpublished, or invented ambient logs are not completed constitutional execution outcomes. Lawful refuse must name which required outcomes could not be admitted without invention.

### CDAEQ-02 — Which decision rules were applied?

Name the primary published constitutional decision-rule identities evaluated under CDA-02 (paths, catalogue identities, and clauses as applicable). Distinguish *decision rules applied* from *constitutional rules executed* (the latter is WS2 speech). Unpublished tribal norms and “how main works” are not constitutional decision rules. Lawful refuse must name which required decision rules could not be applied without invention.

### CDAEQ-03 — Which constitutional decision was produced?

Name the reconstructable constitutional decision constructed under CDA-03, validated under CDA-04, and published under CDA-05 (or the honest invalid / refuse disposition). Distinguish *candidate constructed* from *validated and published*. Distinguish *constitutional decision* from educational recommendations, Programme IX governance dispositions produced outside this architecture, and educational quality claims. Downstream consumers must not hear a soft upgrade into seals or amendments.

### CDAEQ-04 — Which constitutional authority governed the decision?

Name the primary published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII / IX / WS1 / WS2 authority references that governed the decision. Distinguish *authority cited* from *authority replaced*. Decision explanation must not present Decision Architecture machinery, Runtime A, Twin, Adaptive, or CI as the constitutional standard.

### CDAEQ-05 — Which constitutional boundaries remained intact?

Explicitly confirm that forbidden acts did not occur — especially: no constitutional rule execution under a decision label; no EIP reinterpretation; no specification amendment; no replacement of constitutional authority; no governance determination outside constitutional authority; no unpublished CDA components; no stack freeze. If a boundary would have been violated, the explanation must narrate the lawful refuse / invalid stop rather than a fabricated intactness claim.

| Audience | Speech posture | Must still answer |

| **Student** | Plain language about what was decided and why it was allowed under published rules — without syllabus-amendment theatre | All five CDAEQ questions in accessible form |

| **Auditor** | Reconstructable traces: outcomes → decision rules → decision → authority → boundaries | All five CDAEQ questions with enough identity to reproduce the disposition |

Truth is invariant across audiences. Vocabulary may change; fabrication may not.

---

## 5. Defective Explanations

An explanation is constitutionally defective when it:

- omits any mandatory CDAEQ question for a material decision;
- cites unpublished customs as decision rules or outcomes;
- presents decision publication as constitutional rule execution;
- reinterprets EIP under “clarification” labels;
- soft-amends specifications in explanatory prose;
- upgrades a constitutional decision into independent governance outside published authority;
- claims educational quality, mastery, or exam readiness from publication alone;
- privileges a stack, vendor, or repository topology as constitutional authority;
- papers over refuse / invalid dispositions with demo-continuity narration;
- cannot be repeated from reconstructable inputs (violates CDAO-03).
