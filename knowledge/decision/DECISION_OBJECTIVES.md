## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “decision badge green” or “pipeline finished.” The tutor optimises **lawful decision honesty**: only when completed outcomes are transformed into constitutionally valid decisions that remain deterministic, repeatable, explainable, and faithful to published authority may constitutional decision production be said to have occurred.

These objectives bind every Constitutional Decision Architecture run and every successor machinery that claims to produce constitutional decisions from completed execution outcomes.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### CDAO-01 — Produce constitutionally valid decisions

**Definition.** Ensure that every material decision run produces a **constitutionally valid decision** — a reconstructable disposition that (a) consumes identifiable completed execution outcomes, (b) applies published constitutional decision rules, (c) cites governing constitutional authority, (d) survives decision validation (CDA-04), and (e) preserves closed CDA-01…CDA-06 component discipline — so decision theatre never masquerades as lawful constitutional decision production.

**Includes:**

- Binding every material decision to identifiable completed WS2 execution outcomes.
- Applying only published constitutional decision rules (never unpublished customs).
- Citing Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–IX / WS1–WS2 authority references already available through completed outcomes and published corpora.
- Preferring lawful refuse / invalid disposition over inventing unpublished decision customs so a demo can “decide anyway.”
- Keeping constitutionally valid decision production distinct from constitutional rule execution, EIP reinterpretation, specification amendment, educational quality, mastery, readiness, or independent governance outside constitutional authority.

**Excludes:**

- Treating CI greens, ticket fields, Twin estimates, Adaptive scores, or UI ticks as constitutionally valid decisions.
- Soft-selecting convenient outcomes and omitting inconvenient completed outcomes.
- Publishing unvalidated constructions as valid constitutional decisions.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for constitutional validity.
- Declaring validity “close enough” without documenting which completed outcomes and published decision rules were applied.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software pretends a grade was decided without a completed mark sheet or published decision criteria. Validity is care; decision theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CDAO-02 — Preserve determinism

**Definition.** Ensure that the same completed execution outcomes and the same published constitutional decision rules yield the same decision disposition — so constitutional decision production is fixed by reconstructable inputs, not by race, operator whim, ambient product state, or hidden randomness.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who cannot say why yesterday’s decision differed from today’s identical completed outcomes has abandoned educational law for chance.

**Manifestations:**

- Identical completed outcome set + identical published decision-rule set ⇒ identical intake (CDA-01), evaluation (CDA-02), construction (CDA-03), validation (CDA-04), and publication (CDA-05) dispositions — or the same honest refuse / invalid disposition.
- Material non-determinism (race-dependent ordering that changes decisions; ambient unread state; silent randomisation) is a defect.
- Operator overrides that change dispositions without published decision-rule warrant are defects.

**Excludes:**

- Treating “best effort under load” as deterministic decision law.
- Allowing hidden personalisation, A/B flips, or ambient product flags to alter constitutional decisions without published decision-rule identity.
- Claiming determinism while omitting reconstructable outcome or decision-rule identities.

---

### CDAO-03 — Preserve repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that constitutional decision production is **reproducible** from reconstructable completed outcomes, published decision rules, authority citations, and component traces — so auditors and successor machinery can re-run or re-narrate the same decision without tribal knowledge.

**Tutor rationale.** An IFoA examiner who cannot reproduce a marking decision from the same scripts and published criteria has no defensible process.

**Manifestations:**

- Decision records retain enough identity of outcomes, decision rules, authority, validation results, and state transitions to reconstruct the disposition.
- Repeat runs against the same reconstructable inputs produce the same disposition (CDAO-02) and the same explainability answers (CDAO-04).
- Incomplete records that force “ask whoever ran it” are defects.

**Excludes:**

- Relying on ephemeral logs, operator memory, or stack-local caches as the sole reconstructability path.
- Treating one-shot demo theatre as repeatable constitutional decision production.
- Confusing operational retry of Runtime A with constitutional repeatability of decision dispositions.

---

### CDAO-04 — Preserve explainability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material constitutional decision can honestly answer which completed execution outcomes were consumed, which decision rules were applied, which constitutional decision was produced, which constitutional authority governed the decision, and which constitutional boundaries remained intact — without rewriting meaning or presenting decision publication as educational quality.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot explain a decision is not teaching; they are performing.

**Manifestations:**

- Mandatory explainability questions in [`DECISION_EXPLAINABILITY.md`](DECISION_EXPLAINABILITY.md) (CDAEQ-01…CDAEQ-05) are answerable for every material decision.
- Student-facing speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth.
- Lawful refuse / invalid dispositions remain speakable (why decision production stopped).

**Excludes:**

- Opaque “decision score” narration without outcome / decision-rule / authority identities.
- Presenting decision publication as mastery, readiness, conformity, compliance, or certification without published warrant.
- Using explanation prose to soft-amend specifications or reinterpret EIP.

---

### CDAO-05 — Preserve constitutional fidelity

**Definition.** Ensure that decision production remains **faithful to published constitutional authority** — decision rules are applied exactly as published; EIP is not reinterpreted; specifications are not amended; constitutional authority is not replaced; governance outside constitutional authority is not invented — so Decision Architecture never becomes a second constitution.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who changes the syllabus mid-decision to justify today’s preferred answer has abandoned the profession.

**Manifestations:**

- Decision rules cited under CDA-02 match published identities; no silent soft-amendment.
- EIP remains referenced as published under authority maps — never rewritten in decision notes.
- Boundaries in [`DECISION_BOUNDARIES.md`](DECISION_BOUNDARIES.md) remain intact for every material run.
- Implementation neutrality is preserved: no stack becomes law by proximity to a published decision.

**Excludes:**

- Soft-amending decision rules or upstream execution outcomes to force a preferred disposition.
- Reinterpreting EIP principles under “decision clarification” labels.
- Elevating Decision Architecture machinery, Runtime A, Twin, Adaptive, or CI into constitutional authority.
- Determining governance outside published constitutional authority under a decision badge.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If this is weak… | …this fails |
|------------------|-------------|
| CDAO-01 Validity | Deterministic theatre that is not a lawful decision |
| CDAO-02 Determinism | Repeatable records of chance |
| CDAO-03 Repeatability | Unauditable “valid” decisions |
| CDAO-04 Explainability | Silent fidelity breaches |
| CDAO-05 Fidelity | Valid-looking decisions that replace constitutional authority |

All five objectives bind together. None may be waived for product urgency.

---

## 5. Non-Goals of These Objectives

These objectives do not:

- authorise constitutional rule execution (WS2);
- authorise EIP reinterpretation or specification amendment;
- redefine Programme IX governance catalogues;
- grade learning, mastery, or exam readiness;
- privilege a particular implementation stack;
- create constitutional authority.
