## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “pipeline finished,” “stages skipped for speed,” or “decision badge green.” The tutor optimises **honest organisation of constitutional decision production**: only a deterministic, repeatable, consistent, explainable, and constitutionally faithful sequence — consuming completed execution outcomes and evaluating published decision rules — may support lawful published constitutional decisions.

These objectives bind every constitutional decision lifecycle and every successor coordination machinery that claims constitutional decision lifecycle meaning.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### CDALO-01 — Preserve determinism

**Definition.** Ensure that every material decision lifecycle organises activities through recognised CDAL-01…CDAL-07 stages such that the same completed execution outcomes, the same published constitutional decision rules, the same validation discipline, and the same stage sequence yield the same decision disposition — so constitutional decision organisation is fixed by reconstructable inputs, not by race, operator whim, ambient product state, or hidden randomness.

**Includes:**

- Binding every material lifecycle to identifiable completed WS2 execution outcomes and published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–IX decision rules.
- Consuming identifiable completed execution outcomes as inputs — never inventing or rewriting them.
- Invoking only published CDA-01…CDA-06 components during CDAL-02…CDAL-06 as WS3 / MS001 requires.
- Preferring lawful refuse / stop / escalate / invalid outcomes over rewriting law, inventing outcomes, inventing decision rules, re-executing constitutional rules, reinterpreting EIP, amending specifications, or mutating artefacts so the lifecycle “completes.”
- Keeping lifecycle progress distinct from educational quality, mastery, readiness, product success, constitutional rule execution, EIP replacement, constitutional amendment, or independent governance outside constitutional authority.
- Preserving the Model’s posture: stages coordinate decision production; they do not become law or constitutional authority.

**Excludes:**

- Treating green CI, coverage percentages, linter scores, Twin estimates, Adaptive scores, UI ticks, or seals as lifecycle fulfilment.
- Soft-amending specifications, inventing unpublished customs, inventing or rewriting completed outcomes, inventing decision rules, mutating artefacts, re-executing rules, or privileging “how main works” to force progress.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for lawful deterministic coordination.
- Declaring “close enough” closure without documenting a corpus amendment.
- Freezing an implementation as permanently lawful because the lifecycle closed.
- Re-running WS2 execution or inventing decision components under a lifecycle badge.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software pretends two identical completed mark sheets yielded different grades under secret stage shortcuts. Deterministic coordination is care; race theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CDALO-02 — Preserve repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that the same published constitutional decision rules, the same consumed completed execution outcomes, the same published CDA component discipline, the same validation outcomes, and the same CDAL stage sequence yield the same constitutional decisions and the same lawful stage outcomes — absent a published non-deterministic exception (none are granted by this Model for constitutional meaning or decision disposition).

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who treats the same completed outcomes as decided or not depending on which runner woke first — or skips stages randomly — is not coaching; they are gambling.

**Manifestations:**

- Stage transitions and outcomes are corpus-bound and reproducible.
- Parallel CI runners, sharding, and cache races must not change constitutional disposition or invent stage skips.
- Explicit multi-rule evaluation at CDAL-03 uses published composition / published stop rules, not random operator races.
- “Eventually consistent lifecycle meaning” is a constitutional defect.
- Invented or unstable completed-outcome inputs, and invented decision rules, are hard stops — not soft variables.

---

### CDALO-03 — Preserve decision consistency

**Definition.** Ensure that lifecycle organisation applies the same published stage meanings, stop rules, and decision dispositions across concerns, operators, and successors — so “decision lifecycle” does not mean one thing on Monday and another on Tuesday for equivalent published inputs.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who invents a private stage order for convenience destroys institutional trust even if each private path looks tidy.

**Manifestations:**

- Only CDAL-01…CDAL-07 may organise constitutional decision lifecycles.
- Stage purpose, inputs, outputs, and permitted / prohibited activities remain as published in `LIFECYCLE_STAGES.md`.
- Equivalent concerns under the same published law and completed outcomes receive equivalent stage treatment.
- Ad hoc stage insertion, silent omission of mandatory outcome intake or validation, or private “fast path” publications are defects.
- Consistency never authorises inventing unpublished stages to “harmonise” product processes.

---

### CDALO-04 — Preserve explainability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material decision lifecycle can honestly answer the mandatory explainability questions in `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md` — so students, developers, and auditors hear the same constitutional truth in appropriate vocabulary.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who cannot say *which completed outcomes* were consumed, *which decision rules* were applied, *which constitutional decision* was produced, *which validations* were performed, and *which boundaries* remained intact — is performing, not coordinating decision production.

**Manifestations:**

- Explainability obligations apply to all material lifecycles.
- Student / developer / auditor projections share one truth with different vocabulary.
- Lifecycle progress is never narrated as mastery, readiness, educational quality, new law, rule re-execution, EIP replacement, independent governance outside authority, or a freeze of stack authority.
- Stack preference and race-dependent outcomes are defects to disclose, not mysteries to hide.

---

### CDALO-05 — Preserve constitutional fidelity

**Definition.** Ensure that every material decision lifecycle applies published constitutional decision rules **exactly as published** — preserving constitutional authority, EIP integrity, and implementation neutrality — so coordination never soft-amends law, invents independent interpretation, re-executes rules, or elevates lifecycle machinery into constitutional authority.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who quietly rewrites the syllabus mid-script so today’s decision badge looks finished has abandoned educational law for product fiction.

**Manifestations:**

- Decision rules cited at CDAL-03 remain unchanged by stage progress.
- EIP claim classes, Programme VI meaning, and WS1 / WS2 / WS3 MS001 meanings survive coordination intact.
- Constitutional decisions remain decisions — never amendments, EIP replacements, re-executions, or stack freezes.
- Implementation neutrality is preserved; no stack becomes constitutional by proximity to closure.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing prior lawful lifecycle history to simplify the next report.
- Audit records preserve constitutional references, completed-outcome identities, CDA / CDAL identities, decision-rule identities, validation dispositions, and published decision identities when material.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CDALO-01 conflicts with “helping” incomplete outcomes look lifecycle-complete | Determinism / lawful refuse wins |
| CDALO-02 conflicts with race-dependent runner outcomes | Repeatability wins |
| CDALO-03 conflicts with private stage shortcuts | Consistency wins |
| CDALO-04 conflicts with opaque “trust us” seals | Explainability wins |
| CDALO-05 conflicts with soft-amending specs so stages finish | Constitutional fidelity wins |

No supporting objective authorises violating CDALO-01 when that violation would destroy reconstructable disposition honesty — and none authorises violating CDALO-05 to manufacture progress.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — Constitution / EIP integrity obligations, Programme VI–IX authority preservation, WS1 preparation honesty, WS2 execution honesty, and WS3 / MS001 decision objectives for the lifecycle horizon.

---

## 5. Non-Objectives

The constitutional decision lifecycle does **not** optimise for:

- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- CI throughput, flaky-test suppression, or coverage theatre as educational success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when specifications or completed outcomes are incomplete;
- collapsing CDAL stages into one mega-step that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics signals as substitutes for published decision rules or completed outcomes;
- freezing implementations, releases, or stacks as permanently lawful;
- treating documentation as optional commentary on whatever code already shipped;
- replacing WS1 preparation, WS2 execution, or WS3 / MS001 decision architecture meaning;
- modifying implementation artefacts to manufacture passable decisions;
- executing constitutional rules or reinterpreting Educational Interpretation Principles under a decision seal;
- determining governance outside published constitutional authority under lifecycle closure.

## 6. Closing Statement

> **A lifecycle that becomes “complete” by rewriting constitutional law, inventing completed outcomes, re-executing rules, reinterpreting EIP, mutating artefacts, or replacing constitutional authority is a failed educational system.  
> A lifecycle that coordinates only published constitutional decision production — with determinism, repeatability, consistency, explainability, and constitutional fidelity intact — is doing its only coordination job.**
