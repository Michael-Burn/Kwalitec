## 1. Purpose

Decision production without a closed stage catalogue invents law by proximity: whichever pipeline step, ticket status, or ambient product habit happens to finish becomes the tutor’s “proof the system produced a constitutional decision.”

This catalogue names the only lawful constitutional decision lifecycle stages — and binds each stage to purpose, inputs, outputs, and permitted / prohibited activities.

---

## 2. Stage Principles

1. **Ordered and skip-disciplined.** Stages may be brief under thin scope; mandatory initiation, outcome intake, decision-rule evaluation, construction, validation, publication, and closure obligations may not be silently omitted when published constitutional decisions are claimed.
2. **Completed outcomes before evaluation.** Identifiable completed execution outcomes are admitted before decision-rule evaluation.
3. **Explainability accumulates.** Each stage leaves traceable artefacts for `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.
4. **Refusal is a stage outcome.** Refuse-open, refuse-intake, stop, escalate, invalid, refuse-publish, and refuse-close are first-class conclusions.
5. **Deterministic transitions.** Same published inputs ⇒ same next stage outcomes.
6. **Stages coordinate; they do not legislate.** No stage creates, modifies, or redefines constitutional law.
7. **Stages do not become constitutional authority.** Closure never freezes an implementation as permanently lawful or replaces published corpora.
8. **Stages do not execute constitutional rules.** Applying published *decision* rules is not performing WS2 *execution* of constitutional rules.
9. **Stages do not reinterpret EIP.** Educational Interpretation Principles remain as published.
10. **Stages invoke published decision components only.** CDA-01…CDA-06 may be applied; unpublished decision customs may not.

## 3. Catalogue Overview

| ID | Stage | Constitutional purpose (short) | Primary inputs | Primary outputs |
|----|-------|--------------------------------|----------------|-----------------|
| **CDAL-01** | Decision Initiation | Open a decision concern against a named completed-outcome identity and published scope | Completed-outcome identity + published scope + optional decision-rule indication | Initiated decision record (or refuse-open) |
| **CDAL-02** | Outcome Intake | Admit identifiable completed execution outcomes under CDA-01 | Initiated record + candidate completed outcomes | Admitted outcome set (or refuse-intake) |
| **CDAL-03** | Decision Rule Evaluation | Evaluate published constitutional decision rules under CDA-02 | Admitted outcomes + published decision-rule identities | Decision-rule evaluation records |
| **CDAL-04** | Decision Construction | Construct a candidate constitutional decision under CDA-03 | Evaluation records + authority references | Candidate constitutional decision |
| **CDAL-05** | Decision Validation | Validate the candidate under CDA-04 | Candidate decision + published validity expectations | Validation disposition (valid / invalid / refuse) |
| **CDAL-06** | Decision Publication | Publish valid constitutional decisions under CDA-05 (with CDA-06 state) | Validated decision + state / boundary confirmations | Published constitutional decision (or refuse-publish) |
| **CDAL-07** | Decision Closure | Close the lifecycle honestly when duties are exhausted | Published artefacts + boundary confirmations | Closed lifecycle (or refuse-close) — never a freeze of constitutional authority |

Material constitutional decision production claiming lifecycle outcomes must map to this sequence as published law requires. Stages may be brief; none may invent a stage outside this catalogue.

**Relation to CDA / CECX / CECR / CECC / CEE / CEEL / CEEC / Programme VIII / IX catalogues:** Those catalogues remain defined solely by their owners. CDAL stages *organise when* constitutional decision activities occur; they do not replace, reclassify, re-execute, invent, or amend those catalogues.

## 4. Canonical Stage Sequence

```text
CDAL-01  Decision Initiation
           ↓
CDAL-02  Outcome Intake
           ↓
CDAL-03  Decision Rule Evaluation
           ↓
CDAL-04  Decision Construction
           ↓
CDAL-05  Decision Validation
           ↓
CDAL-06  Decision Publication
           ↓
CDAL-07  Decision Closure
```

Lawful early stop dispositions (refuse-open / refuse-intake / stop / escalate / invalid) may exhaust remaining *decision-production* duties while still requiring honest publication (or refuse-publish) and closure — never silent disappearance of the concern.

## 5. Stage Definitions

### CDAL-01 — Decision Initiation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Open a material constitutional decision concern by naming the completed execution outcome identity under which decision production will proceed and the published scope of the concern — without inventing law, opening against unpublished customs, treating initiation as a published decision, or performing constitutional rule execution.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Identifiable completed execution outcome identity (WS2 / MS003 fulfilment claimed or to be confirmed at CDAL-02)
- Concern identity (what constitutional decision question is under organisation)
- Optional published indication of decision-rule identities / CDA components required for the scope
- Optional identifiable supporting authority / lineage references (not rewritten)

**Constitutional outputs**

- An initiated decision concern record naming completed-outcome identity, scope, and intended decision-rule / component set — **or**
- Lawful refuse-open when standards are unpublished, scope is theatrical, completed-outcome identity cannot be established, or initiation would invent law

**Permitted activities**

- Name published completed-outcome identity and scope as decision standards
- Bound the concern scope without amending corpora
- Record intended published decision-rule identities and CDA components from published catalogues
- Refuse initiation when constitutional preconditions fail
- Preserve initiation audit identity for later stages

**Prohibited activities**

- Treat “how main works,” tribal knowledge, or stack preference as the standard
- Create or modify constitutional specifications by opening a concern
- Issue published constitutional decisions at initiation
- Execute constitutional rules or reinterpret EIP at initiation
- Invent unpublished decision components or lifecycle stages
- Skip initiation when material published decisions will later be claimed
- Determine governance outside constitutional authority at initiation

**Authority preserved**  
Published corpora remain the sole standard. Initiation organises; it does not legislate.

---

### CDAL-02 — Outcome Intake

**Constitutional purpose**  
Admit only **identifiable completed execution outcomes** for the concern under CDA-01 — so subsequent stages decide against real completed execution, not anonymous theatre — without inventing, rewriting, or re-executing outcomes, and without performing WS2 execution.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Initiated concern from CDAL-01
- Candidate completed execution outcome identities (WS2 / MS003 completion confirmed, with supporting engine / lifecycle references as applicable)
- Reconstructable outcome traces sufficient to bind decisions
- Published CDAO / CDALO expectations for completed-outcome honesty

**Constitutional outputs**

- An admitted outcome set bound to the initiated concern — **or**
- Lawful refuse-intake when outcomes are missing, invented, rewritten, incomplete, unpublished, or would require re-execution to “complete”

**Permitted activities**

- Identify and bind named completed execution outcomes
- Confirm WS2 completion identity where published law requires
- Refuse intake of incomplete, invented, or theatrical outcomes
- Preserve outcome provenance for later stages and explainability
- Invoke CDA-01 Outcome Intake as published by WS3 / MS001

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent, rewrite, or complete unfinished execution outcomes under a decision badge
- Re-execute constitutional rules to manufacture outcomes
- Treat CI greens, Twin estimates, or UI ticks as completed execution outcomes
- Soft-select convenient outcomes and omit inconvenient completed outcomes without lawful scope record
- Begin decision-rule evaluation against anonymous or incomplete outcomes
- Treat intake itself as a published constitutional decision

**Authority preserved**  
WS2 remains the owner of execution outcomes. Intake admits; it does not execute.

---

### CDAL-03 — Decision Rule Evaluation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Evaluate **published constitutional decision rules** against admitted completed outcomes under CDA-02 — producing reconstructable evaluation records — without inventing unpublished decision customs, re-executing constitutional rules, reinterpreting EIP, or amending specifications.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Admitted outcome set from CDAL-02
- Named published constitutional decision-rule identities
- Named constitutional authority references already available through completed outcomes and published corpora
- Published CDA-02 Decision Rule Evaluator expectations

**Constitutional outputs**

- Decision-rule evaluation records binding outcomes → decision rules → interim dispositions — **or**
- Lawful stop / escalate when published decision rules are missing, unpublished customs would be required, or evaluation would violate boundaries

**Permitted activities**

- Apply published decision rules exactly as published
- Record which decision rules applied and which were out of scope
- Prefer lawful stop over inventing unpublished decision customs
- Invoke CDA-02 Decision Rule Evaluator as published by WS3 / MS001
- Preserve evaluation traces for construction, validation, and explainability

**Prohibited activities**

- Execute WS2 constitutional rules under a “decision rule evaluation” label
- Invent unpublished decision rules or soft-amend published ones
- Reinterpret Educational Interpretation Principles
- Privilege a stack, vendor, or “main branch” as a decision-rule source
- Treat evaluation records as constitutional amendments or published decisions
- Skip evaluation when a published decision will later be claimed

**Authority preserved**  
Published decision rules remain unchanged. Evaluation applies; it does not legislate.

---

### CDAL-04 — Decision Construction

**Constitutional purpose**  
Construct a **candidate constitutional decision** from decision-rule evaluation records under CDA-03 — assembling a reconstructable disposition for validation — without becoming new law, EIP replacement, rule re-execution, or independent governance outside constitutional authority.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Decision-rule evaluation records from CDAL-03
- Admitted outcome identities and authority references
- Published CDA-03 Decision Constructor expectations

**Constitutional outputs**

- A candidate constitutional decision citing outcomes, decision rules, and authority — **or**
- Lawful stop / refuse when construction would invent disposition, omit required citations, or violate published construction rules

**Permitted activities**

- Assemble candidate dispositions from evaluation records exactly as published construction rules allow
- Cite completed outcomes, decision rules, and constitutional authority
- Record decision-state progress under CDA-06 as applicable
- Invoke CDA-03 Decision Constructor as published by WS3 / MS001
- Prefer refuse over inventing a convenient candidate for demo continuity

**Prohibited activities**

- Construct a candidate that amends constitutional specifications
- Construct a candidate that reinterprets or replaces EIP
- Construct a candidate that re-executes constitutional rules
- Construct independent governance dispositions outside published constitutional authority
- Present an unvalidated candidate as a published valid constitutional decision
- Invent missing evaluation records to force construction

**Authority preserved**  
Construction assembles candidates under published law. It does not create authority.

---

### CDAL-05 — Decision Validation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Validate that the candidate constitutional decision is **constitutionally valid** under CDA-04 — confirming completed-outcome citation, published decision-rule application, authority preservation, and boundary integrity — before any claim of valid publication.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Candidate constitutional decision from CDAL-04
- Published validity expectations (CDA-04 / CDAO / CDALO / boundary corpus)
- Boundary confirmations required by `LIFECYCLE_BOUNDARIES.md`

**Constitutional outputs**

- Validation disposition: valid / invalid / refuse — with reconstructable reasons — **or**
- Escalation when validation itself would require inventing law or mutating artefacts

**Permitted activities**

- Confirm candidate cites identifiable completed outcomes and published decision rules
- Confirm EIP was not reinterpreted and specifications were not amended
- Confirm no constitutional rule execution occurred under decision pretence
- Confirm implementation neutrality and authority preservation
- Invoke CDA-04 Decision Validator as published by WS3 / MS001
- Refuse to mark invalid candidates as valid

**Prohibited activities**

- Skip validation so publication can proceed
- Forge validation dispositions
- Treat validation success as constitutional amendment or educational quality
- Soft-waive hard boundaries for demo urgency
- Re-execute rules or reinterpret EIP “to make validation pass”
- Publish invalid candidates as constitutionally valid

**Authority preserved**  
Validation confirms fidelity to published law. It does not rewrite law.

---

### CDAL-06 — Decision Publication

**Constitutional purpose**  
Publish **constitutionally valid** constitutional decisions under CDA-05 for downstream audit and explainability — communicating the decision honestly — without inventing validity, hiding failed validation, rewriting boundaries, or freezing a stack as law.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Validated constitutional decision (valid disposition from CDAL-05)
- Decision-state records under CDA-06 as applicable
- Boundary confirmations and explainability obligations

**Constitutional outputs**

- Published constitutional decision artefacts bound to outcomes, decision rules, validations, and authority — **or**
- Lawful refuse-publish when validity is absent, boundaries are broken, or publication would invent satisfaction

**Permitted activities**

- Publish valid decisions under published honesty rules
- Preserve reconstructable links to outcomes, decision rules, and validations
- Record publication identity for closure and explainability
- Invoke CDA-05 Decision Publisher (and CDA-06 Decision State Manager) as published by WS3 / MS001
- Refuse publication of invalid or unvalidated candidates

**Prohibited activities**

- Publish invalid / unvalidated constructions as valid
- Present publication as new constitutional law or EIP replacement
- Present publication as constitutional rule execution
- Freeze implementations, stacks, vendors, or releases as constitutional law
- Erase prior stage history to simplify the published story
- Determine governance outside published constitutional authority via publication theatre

**Authority preserved**  
Publication communicates decisions. It does not become the corpus.

---

### CDAL-07 — Decision Closure

**Constitutional purpose**  
Close the constitutional decision lifecycle honestly when remaining *decision-production* duties for the concern are exhausted — recording closure without pretending incomplete work is done, without executing rules, without reinterpreting EIP, and without replacing constitutional authority.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Published artefacts (or refuse-publish record) from CDAL-06
- Stage history for CDAL-01…CDAL-06
- Boundary confirmations required by `LIFECYCLE_BOUNDARIES.md`
- Explainability readiness under `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md`

**Constitutional outputs**

- Closed lifecycle record with reconstructable stage trail — **or**
- Lawful refuse-close when mandatory duties remain, boundaries are broken, or closure would invent completion

**Permitted activities**

- Affirm that required decision-lifecycle duties for the concern are exhausted (or honestly refuse-close)
- Preserve full stage / outcome / decision-rule / validation / publication trail
- Distinguish lawful early stops from abandoned duties
- Hand off published decisions to downstream audit / explainability consumers without transferring legislative power

**Prohibited activities**

- Close while pretending incomplete intake, evaluation, construction, validation, or publication succeeded
- Execute constitutional rules, reinterpret EIP, or amend specifications under a closed seal
- Replace constitutional authority because the lifecycle closed
- Freeze a stack as law because the lifecycle closed
- Narrate closure as mastery, readiness, educational quality, or independent governance outside authority
- Invent an eighth stage of law-making after closure

**Authority preserved**  
Closure ends coordination. Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard for the next act.

---

## 6. Stage Interaction Rules

1. **No silent skips.** Mandatory duties may be brief; they may not vanish when a published decision is claimed.
2. **Intake before evaluation.** CDAL-03 must not begin against refused or anonymous outcomes.
3. **Validation before valid publication.** CDAL-06 must not publish as valid without CDAL-05 success.
4. **Refuse is first-class.** Refuse-open / refuse-intake / invalid / refuse-publish / refuse-close are lawful outcomes.
5. **CDA fidelity.** CDAL-02…CDAL-06 invoke only published CDA-01…CDA-06 components.
6. **No execution bleed.** No stage may perform WS2 constitutional rule execution.
7. **No EIP bleed.** No stage may reinterpret or replace Educational Interpretation Principles.
8. **Explainability trail.** Each stage contributes artefacts required by `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.

---

## 7. Closing Statement

> **CDAL-01…CDAL-07 are the only lawful stages for organising constitutional decision production after completed constitutional execution.  
> Stages that invent law, re-execute rules, reinterpret EIP, skip validation, or replace constitutional authority are constitutionally defective regardless of product polish.**
