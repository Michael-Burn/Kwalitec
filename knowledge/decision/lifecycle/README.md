## Purpose

This folder defines **the permanent constitutional lifecycle coordinating constitutional decision production** from initiation through outcome intake, decision-rule evaluation, construction, validation, publication, and honest closure.

It answers *how constitutional decision production is organised after completed constitutional execution*, *what the lifecycle must optimise*, *which recognised stages organise decision production*, *what the lifecycle may and must never do*, and *how lifecycle progress is constitutionally explained* — without implementing Runtime A, executing constitutional rules, reinterpreting Educational Interpretation Principles, amending constitutional specifications, replacing constitutional authority, determining governance outside constitutional authority, or elevating lifecycle machinery into a constitutional authority.

It does **not** implement Runtime A, test runners, CI/CD pipelines, GitHub Actions, Python, Flask, SQLAlchemy, REST, OpenAPI, infrastructure, or application services.

> **The Constitutional Decision Lifecycle coordinates constitutional decision production.  
> It consumes completed execution outcomes and produces published constitutional decisions without executing constitutional rules or creating constitutional authority.  
> It never becomes constitutional authority.**

## Architectural requirement

The Constitutional Decision Lifecycle coordinates **constitutional decision production**.

| Lawful | Unlawful |
|--------|----------|
| Coordinate decision production through recognised stages | Execute constitutional rules (WS2 engine responsibility) |
| Consume completed execution outcomes | Begin against incomplete or unpublished outcomes |
| Evaluate published constitutional decision rules | Reinterpret Educational Interpretation Principles |
| Construct and validate constitutional decisions | Amend constitutional specifications |
| Publish constitutional decisions | Replace constitutional authority |
| Preserve determinism, repeatability, consistency, explainability, fidelity | Determine governance outside constitutional authority |
| Explain outcomes / decision rules / decisions / validations / boundaries | Elevate lifecycle machinery into a constitutional producer or executor |

**The Constitutional Decision Lifecycle coordinates constitutional decision production.  
It never performs constitutional execution or creates constitutional authority.**

## How to use this corpus

1. Confirm completed execution outcomes exist under WS2 / MS001–MS003 — refuse decision-lifecycle theatre against incomplete execution.
2. Confirm the published Constitutional Decision Architecture Model (WS3 / MS001) is in force — refuse lifecycle theatre that invents decision components outside CDA-01…CDA-06.
3. Read `CONSTITUTIONAL_DECISION_LIFECYCLE_MODEL.md` for stack position and integrity rules.
4. Optimise under `LIFECYCLE_OBJECTIVES.md`.
5. Sequence under `LIFECYCLE_STAGES.md` (CDAL-01…CDAL-07 only).
6. Enforce hard stops under `LIFECYCLE_BOUNDARIES.md`.
7. Require explainability contracts from `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md` before student-, developer-, or auditor-facing lifecycle narration.
8. Do not implement lifecycle machinery that contradicts this corpus without amending it first — and do not treat lifecycle closure as constitutional execution, EIP replacement, or constitutional amendment.
