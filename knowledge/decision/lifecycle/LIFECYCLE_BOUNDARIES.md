## 1. Purpose

A lifecycle that silently becomes a second constitution — rewriting specs so stages “complete,” inventing educational meaning in “decisions,” licensing runtime behaviour under a closed seal, inventing completed outcomes to force CDAL-03, re-executing constitutional rules under a decision badge, reinterpreting EIP mid-lifecycle, treating a preferred implementation as the new standard, mutating artefacts mid-lifecycle, or freezing a stack as law — destroys student trust and educational integrity.

This document draws a bright line between **lawful constitutional decision lifecycle coordination** (Programme X / WS3 / MS002) and **constitutional authorship / educational judgement / runtime authorship / execution / decision-component authorship / independent governance authorship / artefact mutation / authority substitution** (Constitution / EIP / Programmes VI–IX / WS1 / WS2 / WS3 MS001 / permitted writers).

---

## 2. Boundary Principles

1. **Coordinate, do not legislate.** The lifecycle organises constitutional decision production; it does not create law.
2. **Published specifications only.** Unpublished customs and “main as law” are hard stops as standards.
3. **Published stages only.** Unpublished CDAL customs are unlawful.
4. **Published decision components only.** CDAL-02…CDAL-06 invoke CDA-01…CDA-06 only.
5. **Completed outcomes only.** Lifecycles without identifiable completed execution outcomes are theatre.
6. **Exactly as published.** Successful progress never rewrites specification meaning.
7. **No constitutional rule execution.** Applying published *decision* rules is not performing WS2 *execution*.
8. **No EIP reinterpretation.** Educational Interpretation Principles remain as published.
9. **Authority first.** Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard.
10. **Implementation independence.** No stack, vendor, or reference implementation becomes law by proximity to closure.
11. **Closed ≠ quality.** Lifecycle closure never grades learning, mastery, readiness, or product success.
12. **Closed ≠ execution.** The lifecycle never re-executes constitutional rules or replaces WS2.
13. **Published ≠ amendment.** Constitutional decisions never become constitutional law.
14. **No independent governance.** The lifecycle never determines governance outside published constitutional authority.
15. **No redefinition.** Programme VI questions, EIP claim classes, WS1 / WS2 meanings, and CDA component meanings survive coordination intact.
16. **No authority substitution.** A closed lifecycle never replaces the corpus for the next judgement.
17. **No artefact mutation.** Coordination never rewrites implementation artefacts to manufacture passable decisions.
18. **No bypass.** Product urgency never waives constitutional lifecycle requirements.
19. **Determinism / repeatability / consistency.** Race-dependent or private stage shortcuts are defects.
20. **Explain the stop.** Students, developers, and auditors should hear when the lifecycle must refuse progress or closure.
21. **No emergency exemption.** Deadlines and demos never mint constitutional amendment power or authority replacement via lifecycle closure.
22. **Validation before valid publication.** Invalid constructions must not be published as constitutionally valid.

## 3. What the Lifecycle May Do (Lawful)

| Lawful action | Constitutional meaning |
|---------------|------------------------|
| **Coordinate decision production** | Organise activities through CDAL-01…CDAL-07 |
| **Consume completed execution outcomes** | Bind named WS2 / MS003 completed outcomes without inventing or rewriting them |
| **Evaluate published decision rules** | At CDAL-03, apply published decision rules under CDA-02 |
| **Construct constitutional decisions** | At CDAL-04, assemble candidate decisions under CDA-03 |
| **Validate constitutional decisions** | At CDAL-05, confirm constitutional validity under CDA-04 |
| **Publish constitutional decisions** | At CDAL-06, publish valid decisions under CDA-05 for audit / explainability |
| **Invoke published decision components** | Apply CDA-01…CDA-06 as published by WS3 / MS001 |
| **Refuse unlawful progress** | Prefer honest refuse / stop / escalate / invalid over improvisation |
| **Remain deterministic, repeatable, and consistent** | Same published inputs → same stage outcomes and dispositions |
| **Remain implementation-neutral** | Honour lifecycle law across lawful equivalent stacks |
| **Preserve constitutional fidelity** | Apply decision rules exactly as published; never soft-amend |
| **Explain lifecycle progress** | Answer outcomes / decision rules / decisions / validations / boundaries without rewriting meaning |
| **Propose corpus amendments upstream** | Recommend (not enact) amendments when law itself must change |

These actions **coordinate constitutional decision production**. They do **not** publish a new Constitution, invent coach warrants, mint tips, author ownership maps, invent CDA or CDAL types, invent CT links, author runtime behaviour, judge educational quality, execute constitutional rules, reinterpret EIP, amend specifications, replace constitutional authority, determine governance outside constitutional authority, or modify artefacts under coordination pretext.

## 4. What the Lifecycle Must NEVER Do

| Forbidden action | Why | Lawful alternative |
|------------------|-----|--------------------|
| **Execute constitutional rules** | WS2 / CEE / CEEL / CEEC own rule execution | Consume completed outcomes; never re-execute |
| **Reinterpret constitutional meaning** | Programme VI / EIP / sibling corpora own meaning | Decide under published meaning; never redefine it |
| **Reinterpret Educational Interpretation Principles** | EIP is upstream law | Apply EIP as published; never rewrite claim classes |
| **Amend constitutional specifications** | Only corpus governance may amend law | Leave specs unchanged; propose amendment upstream or refuse |
| **Determine governance outside constitutional authority** | Governance dispositions belong to published Programme IX / sibling authority under published law | Apply published decision rules that *reference* that authority; never invent independent governance |
| **Create constitutional authority** | Published corpora remain the standard after any closure | Keep artefacts as recognised subjects, never as next-cycle law |
| **Replace constitutional authority** | Lifecycle machinery never becomes the corpus | Keep closure as coordination end, not law replacement |
| **Invent completed execution outcomes** | WS2 owns execution / completion | Consume completed outcomes; refuse invented inputs |
| **Invent decision components** | WS3 / MS001 owns CDA-01…CDA-06 | Invoke published components only |
| **Invent lifecycle stages** | Only CDAL-01…CDAL-07 are lawful | Refuse unpublished stage customs |
| **Invent unpublished decision rules** | Decision rules must be published | Cite published rules only; refuse unpublished customs |
| **Modify implementation artefacts** | Coordination is not a mutation licence | Consume outcomes and rules as found; refuse when identity is insufficient |
| **Invent traceability** | CT owns lawful lineage | Preserve established relationships in records; refuse invented links |
| **Freeze a stack as law** | Implementation neutrality is mandatory | Honour equivalent lawful stacks; refuse stack canonisation |
| **Skip validation** | CDAL-05 is mandatory before valid publication | Validate; refuse-publish when invalid |
| **Publish invalid constructions as valid** | Validity is a constitutional precondition | Refuse-publish; escalate or stop |
| **Perform verification** | CV / CVL / CVC own evidence evaluation | Never re-author verification under a decision badge |
| **Determine compliance** | CCM / CCL / CCC own obligation status | Never narrate closure as a compliance determination |
| **Certify implementations** | CRT / CRTL / CRTC own recognition | Never narrate closure as certification |
| **Re-run WS1 preparation or WS2 execution** | Preparation and execution are upstream | Consume completed outcomes; refuse incomplete execution theatre |
| **Replace WS3 / MS001 decision architecture** | CDA defines components; CDAL sequences them | Invoke published CDA components; never rewrite CDA meaning |
| **Grade educational quality** | Educational Validation Framework / Programme VI own quality speech | Keep closure distinct from mastery / readiness |

|-----------|---------|

| **HS-CDAL-08** | Attempt to amend constitutional specifications via stage progress |
| **HS-CDAL-09** | Attempt to invent unpublished decision rules |

| **HS-CDAL-11** | Attempt to determine governance outside published constitutional authority |
| **HS-CDAL-12** | Attempt to replace constitutional authority because the lifecycle closed |

## 6. Boundary Preservation Confirmations

Every material decision lifecycle must be able to confirm:

1. Completed execution outcomes were consumed, not invented or re-executed.
2. Only published decision rules and CDA / CDAL catalogues were applied.
3. Educational Interpretation Principles were not reinterpreted or replaced.
4. Constitutional specifications were not amended.
5. Constitutional authority was not replaced by lifecycle machinery.
6. Governance outside published constitutional authority was not determined.
7. Implementation neutrality remained intact.
8. Invalid constructions were not published as valid.

These confirmations feed `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md` (especially CDALEQ-05).
