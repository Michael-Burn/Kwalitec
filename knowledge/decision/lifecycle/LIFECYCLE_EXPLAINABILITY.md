## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which completed execution outcomes* were consumed — which decision rules were applied, which constitutional decision was produced, which validations were performed, and which limits remained intact — is not coaching; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–IX have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, runtime, conformance, verification, compliance, certification, and evolution speech speakable, after Workstream 1 has made preparation speech speakable, after Workstream 2 has made execution speech speakable, and after WS3 / MS001 has made decision-component speech speakable, Programme X still needs one lifecycle-speech answer:

> **“Which execution outcomes were consumed, which decision rules were applied, which constitutional decision was produced, which validations were performed, and which constitutional boundaries remained intact?”**

That answer must cover every material decision lifecycle without inventing educational meaning, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, inventing completed outcomes, re-executing rules, reinterpreting EIP, amending specifications, replacing constitutional authority, determining governance outside constitutional authority, freezing a stack as law, inventing unpublished CDA or CDAL types, replacing WS2 execution, or presenting closure as educational quality.

## 2. What Lifecycle Explainability Is

**Constitutional Decision Lifecycle Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **coordination of named constitutional decision activities through published CDAL stages** — consuming completed execution outcomes, evaluating published decision rules under published CDA components, constructing / validating / publishing constitutional decisions — not to invent a second tutor voice from CI machinery, not to grade the student, and not to replace constitutional authority.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Execution outcomes consumed** | Named completed WS2 execution outcomes used as input | Which execution outcomes were consumed? |
| **Decision rules applied** | Named published decision-rule identities evaluated at CDAL-03 under CDA-02 | Which decision rules were applied? |
| **Constitutional decision produced** | Named reconstructable decision from CDAL-04…CDAL-06 | Which constitutional decision was produced? |
| **Validations performed** | Named validation disposition and checks at CDAL-05 under CDA-04 | Which validations were performed? |
| **Boundaries remaining intact** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden execution / reinterpretation / amendment / substitution / independent governance did not occur | Which constitutional boundaries remained intact? |

Lifecycle Explainability is:

- **coordination-centred** — it narrates completed outcomes → decision rules → decisions under law;
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CDAL and CDA identities;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published decision rules and specifications;
- **outcome-honest** — every material justification cites identifiable completed outcomes consumed (or names the hard stop when none exist);
- **decision-rule-honest** — every material justification cites which published decision rules were applied (or names the hard stop);
- **decision-honest** — every material lifecycle cites which constitutional decision was produced (or names refuse / invalid);
- **validation-honest** — every material publication cites which validations were performed;
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to amend corpora;
- **non-executive** — explanation never presents rule re-execution as “decision”;
- **non-interpretive** — explanation never presents soft amendment or EIP reinterpretation as “evaluation”;
- **non-authoritative** — closure is never narrated as a freeze of stack authority or a substitute for published corpora;
- **non-independent-governance** — decisions are never narrated as governance outside published constitutional authority;
- **quality-honest** — closure is never narrated as mastery, readiness, or educational quality;
- **mutation-honest** — explanation never hides artefact changes performed “so decision could proceed”;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence / validation speech, WS1 preparation speech, WS2 execution speech, WS3 / MS001 decision-component speech, or Programme IX governance speech;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match CDALO-04 / CDALO-05 reconstruction needs;
- **repeatability-honest** — explanations must not paper over race-dependent dispositions or private stage shortcuts.

Lifecycle Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–IX / WS1 / WS2 / WS3 MS001 meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a substitute for CC findings, CT lineage speech, CV verification speech, CCM compliance-determination speech, CRT certification-recognition speech, CEE / CEEL execution speech, or CDA decision-component speech;
- a CI status page, coverage report, or linter summary presented as constitutional coordination;
- a freeze of implementations, stacks, vendors, or releases as constitutional law;
- a licence to narrate invented completed outcomes, rule re-execution, EIP reinterpretation, or authority replacement as if they were lawful inputs or outcomes.

## 3. Mandatory Explainability Questions

Every material constitutional decision lifecycle must be able to answer:

### CDALEQ-01 — Which execution outcomes were consumed?

**Required content**

- Named completed execution outcome identities (WS2 / MS003 fulfilment, with supporting engine / lifecycle references as applicable)
- Binding between completed-outcome identities and the initiated CDAL-01 concern
- Confirmation that unpublished customs, invented outcomes, incomplete execution, or re-executed outcomes were not used as completed outcomes

**Defect if**

- “The system decided” with no completed-outcome reference
- Anonymous “the build” or “the pipeline” without identifiable completed outcomes
- Completed outcomes invented, rewritten, or re-executed as decision inputs without disclosure and hard stop

---

### CDALEQ-02 — Which decision rules were applied?

**Required content**

- Named published constitutional decision-rule identities evaluated at CDAL-03
- Binding to CDA-02 component application where material
- Explicit note of decision rules excluded as out of scope under the concern
- Confirmation that unpublished customs were not evaluated as decision rules
- Confirmation that WS2 constitutional *execution* rules were not re-run under a decision-rule label

**Defect if**

- “Rules were applied” with no published decision-rule identities
- Soft-amended or invented decision rules presented as published
- Stack preference or “how main works” presented as a decision-rule source
- Rule *execution* narrated as decision-rule *evaluation*

---

### CDALEQ-03 — Which constitutional decision was produced?

**Required content**

- Named constitutional decision identity (or lawful refuse / invalid / stop disposition)
- Binding to construction at CDAL-04 and publication (or refuse-publish) at CDAL-06
- Citation of completed outcomes and decision rules that ground the decision
- Confirmation that the decision is not narrated as new constitutional law, EIP replacement, or stack freeze

**Defect if**

- “A decision was made” with no reconstructable decision identity or refuse record
- Unvalidated candidate presented as published valid decision
- Decision narrated as constitutional amendment, mastery grade, or independent governance outside authority

---

### CDALEQ-04 — Which validations were performed?

**Required content**

- Named validation disposition from CDAL-05 (valid / invalid / refuse) with reconstructable reasons
- Binding to CDA-04 Decision Validator expectations
- Confirmation that publication as valid did not occur without successful validation
- Explicit note of which validity checks were applied (outcome citation, decision-rule citation, authority preservation, boundary integrity)

**Defect if**

- “It was validated” with no disposition or check identities
- Validation skipped so publication could proceed
- Forged or theatrical validation presented as constitutional validity

---

### CDALEQ-05 — Which constitutional boundaries remained intact?

**Required content**

- Explicit confirmation against `LIFECYCLE_BOUNDARIES.md` hard stops (especially no rule execution, no EIP reinterpretation, no specification amendment, no authority substitution, no independent governance outside authority, no stack freeze, no artefact mutation)
- Named hard stops triggered, if any, and the lawful refuse / stop / escalate / invalid / refuse-publish / refuse-close disposition taken
- Confirmation that lifecycle closure did not replace published corpora as the next-cycle standard

**Defect if**

- Silent assumption that “lifecycle closed ⇒ boundaries held”
- Hidden artefact mutation, stack canonisation, or EIP reinterpretation
- Closure narrated as constitutional authority replacement

## 5. Explainability Defects

The following are constitutional explainability defects regardless of UX polish:

1. Narrating lifecycle progress as educational mastery, readiness, or product success.
2. Narrating closure as constitutional amendment, EIP replacement, or authority substitution.
3. Omitting completed-outcome identities when a decision is claimed.
4. Omitting decision-rule identities when evaluation is claimed.
5. Omitting validation disposition when valid publication is claimed.
6. Presenting CI / coverage / linter / Twin / Adaptive signals as constitutional decision explanations.
7. Hiding race-dependent or private stage-shortcut dispositions behind a single seal.
8. Collapsing WS2 execution speech into “decision lifecycle” speech.
9. Collapsing Programme IX governance speech into “decision lifecycle” speech outside published decision-rule authority.
