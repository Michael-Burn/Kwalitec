## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “pipeline greens,” “tickets closed,” or “decision badge green.” The tutor optimises **honest closing of constitutional decision-production work**: when the published lifecycle for a concern has concluded, published decision rules have been applied, decisions validated and published, and the process formally closed, the tutor can say so — without pretending rules were re-executed, without pretending EIP was rewritten, without pretending the Constitution was amended, without pretending the student has mastered the topic, and without transferring constitutional authority because the machinery stopped cleanly.

These objectives bind every constitutional decision completion judgement and every successor decision machinery that claims constitutional decision-production fulfilment.

## 2. Primary Objective

### CDACO-01 — Confirm lawful decision completion

**Definition.** Ensure that runtime / assessment / audit speech affirms decision completion only when a published decision concern under WS3 / MS001–MS002 has concluded the required CDAL-01…CDAL-07 lifecycle obligations — including decision-rule application, decision validation, decision publication, and formal closure — or honestly refuses close when fulfilment criteria are not met.

**Includes:**

- Assessing only concerns that lawfully opened under WS3 / MS001–MS002 initiation → outcome intake → decision-rule evaluation → construction → validation → publication → closure law.
- Affirming completion only when `COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md` conditions (CDAC-01…CDAC-05) hold.
- Preferring lawful “not yet” / refuse-close over inventing a “done enough” status for product convenience.
- Distinguishing lawful stop dispositions (refuse / defer / escalate as published outcomes that exhaust remaining *decision-production* duties with honest recording) from abandoned lifecycle duties.
- Binding every material completion judgement to reconstructable constitutional references.
- Keeping “decision production complete” distinct from “rules re-executed,” “EIP reinterpreted,” “constitution amended,” “authority created,” and “student succeeded.”

**Excludes:**

- Declaring complete because a CI job exited, a ticket closed, a decision process exited, or a UI spinner stopped.
- Treating Twin / Adaptive / Experience / Mission heuristics as completion warrants.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for lifecycle honesty.
- Collapsing “decision complete” into “rules re-executed,” “EIP rewritten,” “law amended,” “authority transferred,” or “student succeeded.”

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software celebrates unfinished decision work — or celebrates finished pipeline machinery as finished law-making, EIP rewrite, or learning. Honest confirmation is care; completion theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CDACO-02 — Preserve determinism

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming completion preserves the constitutional determinism obligation: same completed execution outcomes, same published decision rules, same validations, same stage sequence ⇒ same completion disposition for the same concern scope — so close cannot invent contradictory “complete” and “not complete” stories from identical published inputs.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who declares the same outcomes decided under the same published decision rules “finished” differently depending on which runner woke first is not care; it is lottery dressed as law.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion judgements remain reproducible from published constitutional inputs.
- Hidden stack-specific shortcuts that flip completion for one vendor only are defects.
- Operator-dependent or race-dependent “complete” flags are defects.
- Non-deterministic “best effort complete” theatre is a defect.

---

### CDACO-03 — Preserve repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material completion judgement is reconstructable and re-affirmable from the same published constitutional records — so decision close survives audit continuity under EIP-005 and does not depend on ephemeral operator memory, ambient product state, or deleted intermediate traces.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who cannot later reconstruct why decision production was declared finished cannot be trusted.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion leaves reconstructable references to completed-outcome identities, decision-rule application traces, validation records, published decisions, stage outcomes, and boundary confirmations.
- Continuity forbids deleting prior lawful decision / publication records to force a different close next time.
- Missing required reconstructable references after an affirmed close is a defect.
- “We remember it completed” without records is a defect.

---

### CDACO-04 — Preserve explainability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material completion judgement can answer the mandatory explainability questions — which execution outcomes were consumed, which decision rules were applied, which validations were completed, which constitutional decisions were published, which boundaries remained intact — without redefining constitutional meaning or implying rule execution / EIP reinterpretation / law amendment / authority creation.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who can only say “it’s fully decided somewhere” is performing, not teaching. Speakable completion is care.

**Manifestations:**

- CDACQ-01…CDACQ-05 answers are producible for every material affirmed or refused close.
- Explanations never soft-amend law or narrate completion as EIP replacement, rule re-execution, or authority transfer.
- Audience projections differ in vocabulary, not in constitutional facts.
- Opaque “trust the complete badge” theatre is a defect.

---

### CDACO-05 — Preserve audit continuity

**Definition.** Ensure that every material completion judgement leaves a reconstructable trail: which completed execution outcomes were consumed, which decision lifecycle stages were completed, which decision rules were applied, which validations were completed, which constitutional decisions were published, and which boundaries remained intact.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later say *why* decision production was declared finished cannot be trusted. Silent “complete” badges destroy student and auditor trust.

**Manifestations:**

- Audit records preserve constitutional references, not only technical job logs or CI annotations.
- Explainability obligations in `COMPLETION_EXPLAINABILITY.md` apply to all material completion judgements.
- Missing CDAC identity against criteria, missing completed-outcome identity, missing decision-rule identity, missing validation identity, missing decision identity, missing stage identity, or missing boundary honesty is a defect.
- Student / developer / auditor projections share one truth with different vocabulary.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CDACO-01 conflicts with inventing unpublished stages so close can complete | Lifecycle honesty wins — refuse / defer / escalate |
| CDACO-02 conflicts with stack-specific non-reproducible close | Determinism wins |
| CDACO-03 conflicts with ephemeral “we remember it completed” theatre | Repeatability wins |
| CDACO-04 conflicts with unspeakable “trust us” completion | Explainability wins |
| CDACO-05 conflicts with opaque complete badges | Audit continuity wins |
| Any CDACO conflicts with executing rules / reinterpreting EIP / amending law / creating authority / inventing educational success | Non-implication honesty wins — refuse the overclaim |

No supporting objective authorises violating CDACO-01.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — Constitution / EIP integrity obligations, Programme VI–IX authority preservation, WS3 / MS001 CDAO duties, and WS3 / MS002 CDALO duties for the *completion* horizon.

## 5. Non-Objectives

Constitutional decision completion does **not** optimise for:

- constitutional rule execution or re-authoring of completed execution outcomes;
- reinterpretation or replacement of Educational Interpretation Principles;
- constitutional amendment, soft rewrite of specifications, or creation / replacement of constitutional authority;
- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- determining governance outside published constitutional authority;
- freezing a stack, vendor, or topology as permanent constitutional law;
- CI throughput, flaky-decision suppression, or demo continuity as educational or constitutional success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when required lifecycle stages, decision-rule applications, validations, publications, or records remain incomplete;
- collapsing CDAC criteria into one mega-flag that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics signals as substitutes for published completion criteria;
- treating documentation of a pipeline as optional commentary on whatever code already shipped;
- declaring rule re-execution, EIP replacement, law amendment, authority creation, or educational success solely because completion was affirmed;
- collapsing execution completion (CEEC) or context completion (CECC) into decision completion.

---

## 6. Closing Statement

> **A concern that becomes “decision complete” by re-executing constitutional rules — or by reinterpreting EIP, amending law, creating authority, or inventing educational success — is a failed educational system.  
> A concern that confirms only that the required published decision lifecycle concluded, decision rules were applied, validations completed, decisions published, and the process formally closed — with determinism, repeatability, explainability, and audit continuity intact — is doing its only completion job.**
