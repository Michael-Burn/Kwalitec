## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor needs bright conditions for saying “this constitutional decision-production work is finished” — and equally bright refusals when it is not. After WS3 / MS001 has named how completed outcomes become constitutional decisions and WS3 / MS002 has named how decision activities are organised, Programme X Workstream 3 still needs one fulfilment checklist:

> **What must be true before constitutional speech may affirm that the required decision lifecycle has concluded, published decision rules have been applied, constitutional decisions have been validated and published, and the decision process has been formally closed?**

These criteria answer that question. They are **normative constitutional conditions**, not database predicates, CI job statuses, ticket states, process exit codes, or API status codes.

## 3. Criterion Definitions

### CDAC-01 — Lifecycle completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every lifecycle stage required by the concern’s published WS3 / MS002 scope has been executed — or that a published refuse / defer / escalate disposition exhausted remaining *decision-production* duties for that stage set with honest progression through recording, publication (or refuse-publish), and closure of what occurred — so decision speech may treat the lifecycle as completed for the concern.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Identifiable decision concern opened under WS3 / MS001–MS002.
- Named completed-execution-outcome identities (WS2 / MS003) for the concern.
- Required CDAL-01…CDAL-07 stage identities for the concern scope.
- Stage traces / disposition records for each required stage (including lawful early stops).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed stage-fulfilment record naming which required stages completed.
- Or a lawful stop record naming which stages were refused / deferred / escalated and why remaining decision-production duties are exhausted.

**Completion conditions.**

- Required stages map to published CDAL-01…CDAL-07 only.
- Mandatory initiation, outcome intake, decision-rule evaluation, construction, validation, publication, and closure obligations for the concern are not silently omitted when complete decision production is claimed.
- Lawful early stops still leave honest stage outcomes; silent disappearance of a concern is a defect.
- Refusal of “pipeline skipped stages” or “we know it ran” without stage identity as completion.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That stage completion executes constitutional rules or re-authors completed execution outcomes.
- That stage completion reinterprets Educational Interpretation Principles.
- That stage completion amends constitutional specifications or creates constitutional authority.
- That stage completion means the learner succeeded or governance finished outside published authority.
- That skipped stages may be invented or implied from CI theatre.
- That CEEC execution completion alone equals CDAC decision completion (CEEC is the outcome-input horizon; CDAC is decision-production fulfilment).

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who declares decision production “finished” without confirming each required decision step occurred has not finished lawful care — even if the desk looks tidy.

---

### CDAC-02 — Decision rules applied

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every published decision rule required by the concern’s published scope was applied under CDAL-03 / CDA-02 — without inventing unpublished decision customs, re-executing WS2 execution rules under a “decision” badge, reinterpreting EIP, or treating rule application as constitutional amendment.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Named completed-execution-outcome identities admitted at CDAL-02.
- Published constitutional decision-rule identities under the concern (Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII / IX as published decision rules).
- Decision-rule evaluation records under CDAL-03 / CDA-02.
- Boundary confirmations that evaluation did not invent law or re-execute WS2 rules.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed decision-rule-application record naming which published decision rules were applied against which completed outcomes.
- Or a lawful refuse / defer / escalate record naming which required decision rules remain unapplied and why.

**Completion conditions.**

- Decision rules cited are published constitutional decision rules — not unpublished customs, tribal knowledge, or “how main decides today.”
- Application cites completed execution outcomes by identity; invented or incomplete outcomes are a hard stop.
- Application never silently becomes WS2 rule re-execution.
- Refusal of coverage percentages, lint scores, Twin confidence, or CI greens as substitutes for named decision-rule application.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That applying decision rules executes constitutional (WS2) rules.
- That applying decision rules reinterprets or replaces Educational Interpretation Principles.
- That applying decision rules amends constitutional specifications or transfers constitutional authority.
- That a particular stack, language, or framework is privileged as law.
- That product quality or educational quality was judged.
- That unpublished decision customs may be invented so close can succeed.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who claims “the decision rules were applied” without naming which published decision rules were applied against which completed outcomes has performed decision theatre, not rule application.

---

### CDAC-03 — Decision validation completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every constitutional validation required by the concern’s published scope has been completed under CDAL-05 / CDA-04 — confirming constructed decisions cite completed outcomes, published decision rules, and constitutional authority without boundary violation — without skipping validation, forging validity, or treating validation as new law.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Candidate constitutional decisions constructed under CDAL-04 / CDA-03.
- Published validation rules under WS3 / MS001–MS002 (especially CDA-04 / CDAL-05).
- Boundary confirmations that validation does not invent law, freeze stack authority, or skip required checks.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed validation records with lawful valid / invalid / refuse dispositions.
- Or a lawful stop record naming why required validations could not complete without invention.

**Completion conditions.**

- Validations cite published constitutional references, named completed outcomes, and applied decision rules.
- Invalid constructed decisions are not treated as validated-valid for publication.
- “Validation happened somewhere” without named validation identity is not completion.
- Lawful invalid / refuse dispositions still satisfy *validation completion* when they are the honest outcome — completeness of *validation* is not completeness of *valid disposition*, and neither is constitutional amendment.
- Prior lawful validation outcomes for the same published concern are not silently erased under close theatre.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That completing validation amends constitutional specifications or creates constitutional authority.
- That a valid disposition freezes the implementation, stack, vendor, or release as permanent constitutional law.
- That an invalid disposition may be silently erased or replaced so close can look green.
- That validation may be skipped because construction “looked right.”
- That Educational Validation Framework quality review is complete.
- That EIP was reinterpreted during validation.

**Tutor rationale.** Recording a hard validation disposition is not rewriting the syllabus — but omitting required validation while claiming the session finished is dishonest. Completeness of *validation* is not completeness of *constitutional change*.

---

### CDAC-04 — Constitutional decisions published

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every constitutional decision required by the concern’s published scope has been published under CDAL-06 / CDA-05 — for audit and explainability — without inventing validity, hiding failed validation, rewriting boundaries, or freezing a stack as law.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Validated constitutional decision identities (CDAC-03 outputs for valid dispositions; or honest refuse-publish records).
- Published publication rules under WS3 / MS001–MS002 (especially CDAL-06 / CDA-05).
- Boundary confirmations that publication does not invent validity or upgrade decisions into constitutional amendments.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Identifiable published constitutional decision identities with lawful dispositions.
- Or a lawful refuse-publish / stop record naming why required decisions could not be published without invention.

**Completion conditions.**

- Published decisions cite completed outcomes, applied decision rules, and validation records.
- Invalid decisions must not be published as constitutionally valid.
- “Decision happened somewhere” without named published decision identity is not completion.
- Lawful refuse-publish after failed validation still leaves honest publication posture — completeness of *publication duty* is not completeness of *affirmative disposition*, and neither is constitutional amendment.
- Continuity forbids silently replacing prior published decisions for the same concern under close theatre.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That publishing a decision executes constitutional rules or reinterprets EIP.
- That publishing a decision amends constitutional specifications or creates constitutional authority.
- That a published decision freezes the implementation, stack, vendor, or release as permanent constitutional law.
- That an invalid decision may be published as valid so close can look green.
- That MS001 decision-architecture meaning was replaced by publication theatre.
- That educational success or independent governance outside constitutional authority was achieved.

**Tutor rationale.** Publishing an honest decision disposition is care; publishing invented validity or hiding failed validation is harm dressed as completion.

---

### CDAC-05 — Decision process formally closed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that the decision concern was formally closed under published CDAL-07 obligations and boundary honesty — exhausting remaining *decision-production lifecycle* duties without inventing constitutional rule execution, reinterpreting EIP, amending constitutional specifications, or creating constitutional authority.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Published decisions and preserved decision records (CDAC-04 and audit trail).
- CDAL-07 closure rules and `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md` hard stops.
- Explicit non-claim confirmations (non-execution / non-EIP-reinterpretation / non-amendment / non-authority-creation / non-educational-success).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Formal close disposition: affirmed complete / not yet / refuse close / defer / escalate.
- Boundary preservation record confirming forbidden overclaims did not occur.

**Completion conditions.**

- Closure occurs only after required stages, decision-rule applications, validations, and publication obligations for the concern are satisfied — or after a published stop exhausts remaining decision-production duties with honest recording.
- Closure speech answers CDACQ-01…CDACQ-05 or explicitly refuses mute theatre.
- Closure never silently replaces decision dispositions or upgrades them into constitutional law.
- Refusal of ticket closure, PR merge, decision badges, or pipeline greens alone as formal constitutional close.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That formal close executes constitutional rules or reinterprets Educational Interpretation Principles.
- That formal close amends constitutional specifications or creates / replaces constitutional authority.
- That formal close freezes the implementation, release, stack, or vendor as law.
- That formal close silently replaces prior lawful constitutional decisions.
- That formal close means the student succeeded or is exam-ready.
- That formal close means governance outside published constitutional authority has finished as constitutional law.

**Tutor rationale.** Closing the decision folder is care only when the required steps, validations, publications, and records exist — and only when nobody pretends the close rewrote the syllabus, re-ran the marking engine, or finished the candidate’s education.

## 5. Lawful Stop Dispositions

Not every incomplete concern is a defect. Published WS3 / MS002 refuse / defer / escalate outcomes may **exhaust remaining decision-production duties** for a concern without affirming lifecycle fulfilment as “complete” in the celebratory sense — while still requiring honest recording, publication (or refuse-publish), and formal close of what occurred.

| Disposition | Constitutional meaning | May affirm CDAC completion? |
|-------------|------------------------|-----------------------------|
| **Affirm complete** | CDAC-01…CDAC-05 hold for the concern | Yes |
| **Not yet** | Required stages, decision-rule applications, validations, publications, or records remain outstanding | No |
| **Refuse close** | Affirmation would invent stages / rules / validations / publications, erase records, re-execute rules, or overclaim | No |
| **Defer** | Published wait for upstream corpus / completed outcomes / decision-rule identity | No (unless published law says remaining decision-production duties are exhausted) |
| **Escalate** | Published stop requiring human / corpus governance attention | No (unless published law says remaining decision-production duties are exhausted) |

A lawful stop that exhausts decision-production duties is **honest unfinished fulfilment or honest refusal** — never stealth constitutional rule execution, EIP reinterpretation, specification amendment, authority creation, educational success, or independent governance outside constitutional authority.

---

## 6. Non-Criteria

The following are **not** constitutional completion criteria:

- CI pipeline greens, flaky-decision suppression, or job exit codes;
- ticket closure, PR merge, or documentation checklist ticks alone;
- coverage percentages, lint scores, decision badges, or elapsed time;
- Twin / Adaptive / Experience / Mission confidence signals;
- UI celebration states or analytics milestones;
- “looks decided enough for the demo”;
- WS2 / MS003 execution completion (CEEC) alone (required outcome-input horizon — CEEC ≠ CDAC);
- WS1 / MS003 context completion (CECC) alone (orthogonal preparation fulfilment);
- Educational Validation Framework quality release (orthogonal);
- any implication that constitutional rules were re-executed because decision production closed;
- any implication that Educational Interpretation Principles were reinterpreted because decision production closed;
- any implication that constitutional specifications changed or authority was created because decision production closed;
- any implication that governance outside published constitutional authority finished because decision production closed.

> **Constitutional decision production is complete only when the required lifecycle is completed, published decision rules are applied, decision validation is completed, constitutional decisions are published, and the decision process is formally closed.  
> Satisfying CDAC-01…CDAC-05 never means constitutional rules were executed, Educational Interpretation Principles were reinterpreted, constitutional law was amended, or constitutional authority was created.**
