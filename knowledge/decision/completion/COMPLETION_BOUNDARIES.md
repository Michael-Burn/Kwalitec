## 1. Purpose

Decision machinery that silently turns “the decision lifecycle finished” into “constitutional rules were re-executed,” “EIP now means X,” “the Constitution now says Y,” “we are the new constitutional authority,” or “student succeeded” destroys student trust and educational integrity.

This document draws a bright line between **lawful constitutional decision completion** (Programme X / WS3 / MS003) and **constitutional rule execution / EIP reinterpretation / specification amendment / authority creation / educational judgement / independent governance outside constitutional authority** owned elsewhere.

---

## 2. Boundary Principles

1. **Confirm decision-production fulfilment, do not legislate.** Completion affirms fulfilment of published decision lifecycle duties; it does not create constitutional law.
2. **Decision production only.** Rule re-execution, EIP reinterpretation, specification amendment, authority creation, and educational success are never inferred from close.
3. **Not constitutional execution.** WS2 owns rule execution; completion never re-runs or rewrites completed execution outcomes.
4. **Not EIP reinterpretation.** Educational Interpretation Principles survive close exactly as published.
5. **Not constitutional amendment.** Close never patches law or redefines constitutional meaning.
6. **Not authority creation.** Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard after close; completion machinery never becomes that standard.
7. **Not independent governance outside constitutional authority.** Close may confirm published decision rules that *reference* Programme IX (or other) authority were applied; it never becomes an independent producer of those judgements outside published law.
8. **Exactly as published.** Close never redefines CDA / CDAL catalogues or soft-amends Programme VI–IX meaning.
9. **Published criteria only.** Pipelines, tickets, process exits, and elapsed time are not fulfilment law.
10. **Published records only.** Audit continuity is constitutional, not free-form log theatre.
11. **Reference decisions honestly.** Published constitutional decisions may be cited; publishing or closing never upgrades disposition into law change / EIP replacement / authority transfer.
12. **Meaning non-reinterpretation.** Programme VI questions and EIP claim classes survive close intact.
13. **Implementation neutrality.** No stack becomes law by proximity to a complete flag.
14. **Decision-complete ≠ quality.** Completion never grades learning, mastery, readiness, or product success.
15. **Decision-complete ≠ executed.** CEEC execution completion is a prerequisite outcome horizon; it is not CDAC fulfilment.
16. **Decision-complete ≠ prepared.** CECC preparation completion remains upstream and orthogonal.
17. **Explain the stop.** Students, developers, and auditors should hear when close must be refused.
18. **No emergency exemption.** Deadlines and demos never mint completion power, execution power, or amendment power.
19. **Determinism and repeatability survive close.** Close must not invent contradictory dispositions from identical published inputs.
20. **Validation honesty survives close.** Invalid decisions must not be laundered as published-valid under completion theatre.

## 3. What Decision Completion May Do (Lawful)

| Lawful action | Constitutional meaning |
|---------------|------------------------|
| **Confirm decision completion** | Affirm CDAC-01…CDAC-05 when published criteria hold for a lawful decision concern |
| **Confirm publication** | Cite that required constitutional decisions were published under CDAL-06 / CDA-05 — without upgrading publication into law amendment / EIP replacement / authority creation |
| **Confirm formal closure** | Cite that the concern was formally closed under CDAL-07 / boundary honesty — without creating constitutional authority |
| **Confirm decision-rule application** | Cite that required published decision rules were applied under CDAL-03 / CDA-02 — without inventing rules or re-executing WS2 rules |
| **Confirm decision validation** | Cite that required validations were completed under CDAL-05 / CDA-04 — without forging validity |
| **Preserve audit continuity** | Leave reconstructable constitutional records of outcomes, stages, decision rules, validations, decisions, and boundaries |
| **Refuse unlawful close** | Prefer honest not-yet / refuse / defer / escalate over improvisation |
| **Compose required stages and components** | Confirm fulfilment across published CDAL / CDA conjunctions without collapsing failed duties |
| **Remain implementation-neutral** | Honour completion law across lawful equivalent stacks |
| **Preserve determinism and repeatability** | Ensure same published inputs do not yield contradictory completion stories |
| **Explain completion** | Answer outcomes / decision rules / validations / decisions / boundaries without rewriting meaning |
| **Propose corpus amendments upstream** | Recommend (not enact) amendments when law itself must change before decision production can complete |

These actions **confirm constitutional decision-production fulfilment**. They do **not** publish a new Constitution, invent coach warrants, mint tips, author ownership maps, invent CDA / CDAL / CDAC types, author runtime behaviour, execute constitutional rules, reinterpret EIP, amend specifications, create constitutional authority, evaluate fidelity in place of Programme IX outside published decision-rule references, or judge educational quality.

## 4. What Decision Completion Must NEVER Do

| Forbidden action | Why | Lawful alternative |
|------------------|-----|--------------------|
| **Execute constitutional rules** | WS2 owns rule execution; decision completion consumes completed outcomes | Cite completed outcomes as published; never re-execute |
| **Reinterpret constitutional meaning / EIP** | EIP and Programme VI own meaning; completion confirms application of published decision rules only | Preserve identity of published EIP / meaning; never invent reinterpretation outcomes |
| **Amend constitutional specifications** | Only corpus governance may amend meaning | Leave specs unchanged; propose amendment upstream or refuse incomplete decision production |
| **Create / replace constitutional authority** | Published corpora remain the sole standard | Affirm fulfilment under published authority; never become that authority |
| **Invent unpublished decision rules or stages** | Only CDAL / CDA catalogues and published decision rules are lawful | Refuse / defer / escalate until published inputs exist |
| **Forge validation or hide invalidity** | Invalid decisions must not be published as valid | Record honest invalid / refuse-publish dispositions |
| **Upgrade decisions into law or stack freeze** | Published decisions are dispositions for audit / explainability, not new constitutions | Narrate decisions as decisions; never as law / stack canon |
| **Determine governance outside constitutional authority** | Programme IX (and peers) own their judgements under their catalogues | Confirm published decision rules that *reference* that authority were applied — never independently re-produce those judgements |
| **Imply educational success** | Learning / mastery / readiness are not completion products | Keep educational claims under Constitution / EIP / Programme VI |
| **Privilege a stack as completion law** | Implementation neutrality is mandatory | Apply CDAC criteria to constitutional identities, not vendor labels |
| **Erase audit trails** | Continuity (EIP-005) forbids tidy amnesia | Preserve reconstructable records |
| **Implement Runtime A / CI / Flask as constitutional law** | Technology is out of scope for this corpus | Keep completion technology-independent |

## 5. Boundary Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| Close would require re-executing WS2 rules | Refuse close — CDAC confirms decision fulfilment, never execution |
| Close would require soft-amending EIP or Programme VI meaning | Refuse close — propose corpus amendment upstream |
| Close would invent unpublished decision rules or CDAL stages | Refuse close — unpublished customs are not fulfilment law |
| Close would publish invalid decisions as valid | Refuse close — validation honesty wins |
| Close would declare completion machinery “the new constitution” | Refuse close — authority remains upstream |
| Close would treat CEEC complete as CDAC complete | Refuse collapse — CEEC is outcome input; CDAC is decision-production fulfilment |
| Close would grade student mastery from “decision complete” | Refuse educational overclaim |
| Demo deadline conflicts with unfinished CDAC-01…CDAC-05 | Prefer honest not-yet — demos never mint completion power |

---

## 6. Closing Statement

> **Decision completion may confirm that decision production completed, that constitutional decisions were published, and that the process was formally closed.  
> The moment it executes constitutional rules, reinterprets constitutional meaning, amends constitutional specifications, or creates constitutional authority, it has left constitutional education and entered product fiction.**
