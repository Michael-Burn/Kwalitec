## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which execution outcomes* were consumed — which decision rules were applied, which validations were completed, which constitutional decisions were published, and which limits remained intact — is not closing honestly; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–IX have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, runtime, and governance speech speakable, after WS1–WS2 have made preparation / execution / completion speech speakable, and after WS3 / MS001–MS002 have made decision-architecture and lifecycle speech speakable, Programme X still needs one decision-completion-speech answer:

> **“Which execution outcomes were consumed, which decision rules were applied, which validations were completed, which constitutional decisions were published, and which constitutional boundaries remained intact?”**

That answer must cover every material affirmed (or refused) completion without inventing educational success, implying constitutional rule execution, reinterpreting EIP, announcing constitutional amendment, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, freezing a stack as law, or modifying constitutional law.

## 2. What Decision Completion Explainability Is

**Constitutional Decision Completion Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **fulfilment (or lawful refusal of fulfilment) of the required constitutional decision lifecycle** — not to invent a second tutor voice from CI complete badges, and not to grade the student, re-execute constitutional rules, or freeze a stack as law.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Execution outcomes consumed** | Named completed execution outcomes (WS2 / MS003) admitted under CDAL-02 | Which execution outcomes were consumed? |
| **Decision rules applied** | Named published decision rules applied under CDAL-03 / CDA-02 | Which decision rules were applied? |
| **Validations completed** | Named constitutional validations completed under CDAL-05 / CDA-04 | Which validations were completed? |
| **Constitutional decisions published** | Named reconstructable decisions published under CDAL-06 / CDA-05 | Which constitutional decisions were published? |
| **Boundaries intact** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden overclaims / amendments / substitutions did not occur | Which constitutional boundaries remained intact? |

Decision Completion Explainability is:

- **fulfilment-centred** — it narrates why CDAC criteria hold (or why close is refused);
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CDA / CDAL / CDAC identities where applicable;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published specifications, completed outcomes, decision rules, validations, decisions, and named stage / publication records;
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to amend corpora;
- **non-executive / non-interpretive** — completion is never narrated as constitutional rule execution or EIP reinterpretation;
- **quality-honest** — complete is never narrated as mastery, readiness, or educational quality;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence / validation speech, WS1 context / resolution / completion speech, WS2 engine / lifecycle / completion speech, WS3 / MS001 decision speech, or WS3 / MS002 lifecycle speech;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match CDACO-05 reconstruction needs;
- **authority-honest** — explanations must not paper over authority inference or stack canonisation.

Decision Completion Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–IX / WS1 / WS2 / WS3 meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a claim that constitutional rules were re-executed;
- a claim that Educational Interpretation Principles were reinterpreted or replaced;
- a constitutional amendment instrument or authority-creation warrant;
- a substitute for WS3 / MS001 decision explainability or WS3 / MS002 lifecycle explainability (compose with; do not replace);
- a substitute for WS2 / MS003 execution-completion explainability (compose with; do not collapse);
- a rendering system, UI template, CI badge, decision visualiser, or analytics dashboard;
- a claim that clear completion speech guarantees learning, a pass, conformity, certification, approval, or new constitutional law;
- a licence to present process exits, ticket graphs, or pipeline greens as constitutional proof or educational quality.

## 3. Mandatory Explanation Questions

Every material completion judgement (affirm or refuse close) should answer all five questions:

### CDACQ-01 — Which execution outcomes were consumed?

Name the primary completed execution outcome identities (WS2 / MS003 CEEC-completed outcomes) admitted under CDAL-02 for the concern — or name which required completed outcomes remain unavailable / refused when refusing close. Never narrate invented, rewritten, incomplete, or re-executed outcomes as lawful consumption. “Outcomes happened somewhere” is not a constitutional completed-outcome identity.

### CDACQ-02 — Which decision rules were applied?

Name the primary published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII / IX *decision-rule* identities applied under CDAL-03 / CDA-02 for the concern — or name which required decision rules remain unapplied / refused when refusing close. Include lawful early-stop application outcomes honestly. Never narrate a skipped mandatory decision rule as applied because a later pipeline job glowed green, and never narrate WS2 *execution* rules as if they were re-run under a decision badge. “It ran in CI” is not a constitutional decision-rule identity.

### CDACQ-03 — Which validations were completed?

Name the primary constitutional validations completed under CDAL-05 / CDA-04 for the concern — or name which required validations remain incomplete / invalid / refused when refusing close. Never narrate skipped, forged, or soft-amended validations as successful completion. Distinguish:

- **valid dispositions** — candidate decisions that survived constitutional validation;
- **invalid / refuse dispositions** — honest validation outcomes that block publication as valid;
- **continuity honesty** — prior lawful validation history not erased for convenience (EIP-005).

“Validation assumed OK” is not a constitutional validation identity.

### CDACQ-04 — Which constitutional decisions were published?

Name the primary constitutional decision identities published under CDAL-06 / CDA-05 for the concern — or name which required decisions remain unpublished / refuse-published when refusing close. Never narrate log volume, ticket graphs, or CI annotations alone as constitutional decisions. Distinguish:

- **constitutional decisions** — reconstructable dispositions produced under published decision rules for downstream audit and explainability;
- **decision-process state** — speakable progress / closure of constitutional decision production (not educational state, not independent governance outside constitutional authority);
- **continuity honesty** — prior lawful decision history not erased for convenience (EIP-005).

Never narrate published decisions as “student is ready,” “mastery confirmed,” “rules re-executed,” “EIP rewritten,” “law updated,” “this stack is now constitutional,” or “authority transferred.”

### CDACQ-05 — Which constitutional boundaries remained intact?

Confirm the hard stops in `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md` that were checked — especially non-execution of constitutional rules, non-reinterpretation of Educational Interpretation Principles, non-amendment of constitutional specifications, non-creation / non-replacement of constitutional authority, implementation neutrality, determinism / repeatability honesty, validation honesty, and non-equation of completion with educational success or constitutional truth.

When refusing close, name which boundary would have been breached by affirming completion (for example: re-executing rules from outcomes; narrating “decided therefore EIP changed”; publishing invalid decisions as valid; erasing audit trails; freezing a stack as law; declaring completion machinery the new constitutional authority).

## 5. Explanation Defects

The following are constitutional explainability defects:

- mute “decision complete” badges without CDACQ-01…CDACQ-05 answers;
- narrating completion as constitutional rule execution or EIP reinterpretation;
- narrating completion as constitutional amendment or authority creation / replacement;
- narrating completion as educational success, mastery, or exam readiness;
- collapsing CEEC execution completion or CECC context completion into CDAC decision completion in speech;
- presenting CI greens, ticket graphs, coverage, or lint scores as constitutional proof;
- erasing prior lawful decision / validation / publication trails to make close look tidy;
- presenting a preferred stack, vendor, or Runtime A shape as constitutional law via completion speech;
- replacing WS3 / MS001 decision explainability or WS3 / MS002 lifecycle explainability instead of composing with them.
