## Purpose

This folder defines **when constitutional decision production has been lawfully completed**.

It answers *what decision completion must optimise*, *under which constitutional conditions decision production is complete*, *what completion may and must never do*, and *how decision completion is constitutionally explained* — without implementing Runtime A, executing constitutional rules, reinterpreting Educational Interpretation Principles, amending constitutional specifications, replacing constitutional authority, determining governance outside constitutional authority, or elevating completion into a constitutional authority.

It does **not** implement Runtime A, test runners, CI/CD pipelines, GitHub Actions, Python, Flask, SQLAlchemy, REST, OpenAPI, infrastructure, or application services.

> **The Constitutional Decision Completion Model answers:  
> “When has constitutional decision production lawfully completed?”  
> Decision completion confirms that the required decision lifecycle has concluded, published decision rules have been applied, constitutional decisions have been validated and published, and the decision process has been formally closed.  
> Completion concerns decision production only.  
> It never executes constitutional rules, interprets constitutional law independently, creates constitutional authority, or amends constitutional specifications.**

## Architectural requirement

Decision Completion confirms that **constitutional decision production has concluded according to the published decision lifecycle**.

| Lawful | Unlawful |
|--------|----------|
| Confirm lifecycle completion | Execute constitutional rules (WS2 engine responsibility) |
| Confirm decision-rule application completed | Reinterpret Educational Interpretation Principles |
| Confirm decision validation completed | Amend constitutional specifications |
| Confirm constitutional decisions published | Create or replace constitutional authority |
| Confirm decision process formally closed | Elevate completion machinery into a constitutional producer or executor |
| Preserve determinism, repeatability, explainability, audit continuity | Invent unpublished completion customs |
| Explain outcomes / decision rules / validations / decisions / boundaries | Freeze a stack as law via “decision complete” |

**Decision Completion confirms that constitutional decision production has concluded according to the published decision lifecycle.  
It never performs constitutional execution or creates constitutional authority.**

## How to use this corpus

1. Confirm a decision concern lawfully opened under WS3 / MS001–MS002 and consuming completed execution outcomes under WS2 / MS003 — refuse completion theatre for decision production that never lawfully began.
2. Read `CONSTITUTIONAL_DECISION_COMPLETION_MODEL.md` for stack position and integrity rules.
3. Optimise under `COMPLETION_OBJECTIVES.md`.
4. Evaluate fulfilment conditions under `COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md` (CDAC-01…CDAC-05).
5. Enforce hard stops under `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md`.
6. Require explainability contracts from `COMPLETION_EXPLAINABILITY.md` before student-, developer-, or auditor-facing completion narration.
7. Do not implement decision or completion machinery that contradicts this corpus without amending it first — and do not treat decision completion as constitutional execution, EIP replacement, or constitutional amendment.
