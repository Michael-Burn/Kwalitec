## 2. What Constitutional Decision Completion Is

**Constitutional Decision Completion** is confirmation that **constitutional decision production** for a published decision concern has **lawfully completed** — that the **required decision lifecycle has concluded**, **published decision rules have been applied**, **constitutional decisions have been validated**, **constitutional decisions have been published**, and the **decision process has been formally closed** — such that determinism, repeatability, explainability, and audit continuity hold for that concern under WS3 / MS001–MS002 — without claiming that any rule-execution, EIP-reinterpretation, specification-amendment, authority-creation, educational, or independent-governance question was resolved by the close itself.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Decision concern** | A bounded constitutional unit of decision-production work opened under published CDA / CDAL law | What decision work is being assessed for completion? |
| **Completion warrant** | Judgement that lifecycle fulfilment may be assessed | May we ask whether decision production is complete? |
| **Completion criteria** | Constitutional conditions that must hold (`COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md`) | What must be true for fulfilment? |
| **Completion judgement** | Affirmed / not yet / refused (no lawful close) | Is constitutional decision production complete for this concern? |
| **Lifecycle fulfilment** | Required CDAL-01…CDAL-07 obligations executed (or lawfully stopped with honest recording) | Did the published lifecycle conclude? |
| **Decision-rule application fulfilment** | Required published decision rules applied under CDAL-03 / CDA-02 | Were decision rules applied? |
| **Decision-validation fulfilment** | Required constitutional validations completed under CDAL-05 / CDA-04 | Were decisions validated? |
| **Decision-publication fulfilment** | Constitutional decisions published under CDAL-06 / CDA-05 | Were decisions published? |
| **Decision-process closure** | Formal close of decision production under CDAL-07 / boundary honesty | Was the decision process formally closed? |
| **Boundary preservation record** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden overclaims / amendments / substitutions did not occur | Did constitutional integrity survive close? |

Constitutional Decision Completion is:

- **decision-production-scoped** — it concerns fulfilment of the published decision lifecycle, not rule re-execution, EIP reinterpretation, law-making, or independent governance outside constitutional authority;
- **lifecycle-bound** — fulfilment references CDAL-01…CDAL-07 stages actually required for the concern under WS3 / MS002;
- **criteria-gated** — fulfilment requires stated constitutional conditions (`COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md` CDAC-01…CDAC-05);
- **outcome-consuming** — completion confirms completed execution outcomes were consumed; it never invents, rewrites, or re-executes them;
- **decision-honest** — completion confirms constitutional decisions were validated and published; it never upgrades them into constitutional amendments, EIP replacements, or stack freezes;
- **audit-capable** — fulfilment cites reconstructable constitutional records, not ticket theatre or CI greens;
- **deterministic** — same completed outcomes, same published decision rules, same validations, same stage sequence ⇒ same completion disposition for the same concern scope;
- **repeatable** — close is reproducible from reconstructable inputs and not race-dependent or operator-dependent;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by completion;
- **non-executive** — completion never performs constitutional rule execution;
- **non-interpretive** — completion never reinterprets Educational Interpretation Principles;
- **non-legislative** — completion never modifies constitutional specifications or replaces constitutional authority;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which outcomes were consumed, which decision rules were applied, which validations were completed, which decisions were published, and which boundaries remained intact;
- **corpus-bound** — every material completion cites identifiable published specifications, completed outcomes, decision rules, validations, decisions, and named stage / publication records;
- **non-substitutive** — a completed decision process never becomes the constitutional standard or a substitute for Constitution / EIP / Programme authority.

Constitutional Decision Completion is **not**:

- execution of constitutional rules or re-authoring of completed execution outcomes;
- reinterpretation or replacement of Educational Interpretation Principles;
- amendment of constitutional specifications or creation of constitutional authority;
- determination of conformance, verification, compliance, or certification outcomes outside published decision-rule references;
- educational success, learner mastery, progress, or exam readiness;
- a Programme VI / VII / VIII educational, orchestration, or runtime completion judgement;
- transfer of authority, minting of tips, invention of EST/CST postures, or soft rewrite of constitutional specifications under a “decision done” label;
- a claim that pipeline greens, ticket closure, elapsed time, or “decision badge green” equal fulfilment;
- a UI celebration, analytics milestone, or “decided forever” flag as educational or constitutional law;
- a licence to skip outstanding published lifecycle duties because a product surface looks finished;
- a substitute for WS3 / MS001 decision-architecture meaning or WS3 / MS002 lifecycle law — completion *judges fulfilment of* those corpora; it does not redefine CDA or CDAL catalogues;
- a substitute for WS2 / MS003 execution completion — CEEC confirms execution fulfilment and publishes completed outcomes; CDAC confirms decision-production fulfilment and *requires* those completed outcomes as input;
- a substitute for Programme VII educational / orchestration decision ownership — those corpora define educational decision ownership; CDAC confirms *constitutional decision-production* fulfilment;
- a substitute for Programme II Decision Simulation — that framework measures advisory divergence; it is not constitutional Decision Completion;
- a substitute for Programme IX conformance, verification, compliance, certification, or evolution — those corpora produce governance judgements under their own catalogues; CDAC may confirm published decision rules that *reference* that authority were applied and never becomes an independent producer outside it.

## 4. Completion Assessment (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material decision concern that claims lawful completion travels through constitutional assessment phases. These are **normative judgement obligations**, not an implementation architecture.

```
Identify published decision concern opened under CDA / CDAL law
        │
        ▼
Confirm required CDAL-01…CDAL-07 stages and CDA-01…CDA-06 applications for the concern
        │
        ├── refuse / defer / escalate (lawful stop — law unchanged; no invented “complete”)
        │
        └── assess → Apply CDAC-01…CDAC-05
                │
                ├── not yet / refuse close (outstanding duties or boundary breach)
                │
                └── affirm → Emit completion judgement; preserve audit continuity and repeatability
                        │
                        ▼
                Explain outcomes / decision rules / validations / decisions / boundaries
```

| Phase | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **Identify** | Name only published decision concerns opened under WS3 / MS001–MS002 | Concern is unpublished custom, tribal knowledge, or “how decisions work today” |
| **Confirm lifecycle obligations** | Verify required CDAL stages and CDA components for the concern | Stages / components would invent law, rewrite specs, reinterpret EIP, re-execute rules, or privilege a stack as law |
| **Assess criteria** | Apply CDAC-01…CDAC-05 without softening into product theatre | Any required criterion fails; or close would claim rule execution / EIP reinterpretation / law amendment / authority creation |
| **Preserve & explain** | Record audit continuity; answer CDACQ-01…CDACQ-05 | Path would erase decision trails, treat completion as constitutional amendment, or narrate “decided therefore law / EIP / authority changed” |

> **Decision completion never “best-effort invents” a sixth phase called constitutional rule execution, EIP reinterpretation, specification amendment, authority creation, or stack canonisation.**

## 7. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Decision Completion Model exists so that:

1. **Decision-production honesty remains intact** — “complete” means the published decision lifecycle concluded, not that a pipeline glowed green.
2. **Decisions remain decisions** — close confirms validation and publication of constitutional decisions; it never upgrades them into constitutional amendments, EIP replacements, or stack freezes.
3. **Determinism remains intact** — the same published inputs yield the same completion disposition.
4. **Repeatability remains intact** — close is reproducible from reconstructable constitutional records.
5. **Audit continuity survives close** — completion leaves reconstructable constitutional records.
6. **Authority remains upstream** — completed decision production never becomes the constitutional source for the next judgement.
7. **EIP remains intact** — decision completion never reinterprets or replaces Educational Interpretation Principles.
8. **Execution remains untouched** — decision completion never becomes a second execution engine.
9. **Implementations remain replaceable** — completion does not freeze Flask, SQLAlchemy, a particular API shape, or a CI vendor as law.
10. **Explainability remains intact** — completion speech describes decision-production fulfilment; it does not redefine what learning means or what constitutional truth is.
11. **Students remain protected** — product theatre cannot launder unfinished decision work as “decided therefore lawful forever / mastered / authority transferred.”
