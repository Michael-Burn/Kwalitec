## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “spell-checker found no typos” or “we redefined the word overnight so the report looks clean.” The tutor optimises **honest vocabulary integrity**: only terminology that remains semantically consistent, canonically recognised, definition-stable, unambiguous (or lawfully disambiguated), and readable may be called intact — without rewriting those terms so a readiness report can pass.

These objectives bind every constitutional vocabulary integrity assessment and every successor assessment machinery that claims constitutional vocabulary integrity meaning.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### CVIO-01 — Preserve semantic consistency

**Definition.** Ensure that every material vocabulary integrity assessment determines — for named published terms under recognised CVT-01…CVT-10 categories and CVI-01…CVI-07 categories — whether each term retains one published constitutional meaning within its lawful scope (or is lawfully disambiguated where published law separates usages), or honestly reports violated / deferred / escalated when semantic consistency cannot be confirmed.

**Includes:**

- Applying the closed CVT / CVI catalogues required by published law for the assessment scope.
- Binding every material assessment to identifiable published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–XI specifications and subordinate workstream / milestone corpora.
- Detecting the same term assigned incompatible constitutional meanings without published disambiguation.
- Preferring lawful violated / deferred / escalated findings over redefining terms so readiness “passes.”
- Keeping semantic consistency distinct from educational quality, mastery, readiness theatre, product success, or constitutional amendment.
- Preserving the Model’s posture: findings validate speech structure; they do not become law or rewrite definitions.

**Excludes:**

- Treating green spell-checkers, thesaurus scans, NLP similarity scores, Twin estimates, Adaptive scores, or UI ticks as constitutional semantic consistency.
- Soft-amending specifications, inventing unpublished glossaries, redefining terms, or privileging “how main speaks” to force intact status.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for vocabulary integrity.
- Declaring “close enough” semantics without documenting a corpus amendment.
- Treating published disambiguation tables as automatic proof that colliding usages are the same meaning — disambiguation separates; it does not merge.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software pretends contradictory constitutional speech is fine because integration needs a green path. Semantic honesty is care; vocabulary theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CVIO-02 — Preserve canonical terminology

**Definition.** Ensure that every material vocabulary assessment recognises only published constitutional terms with reconstructable owning corpora — so tribal jargon, invented glosses, and “preferred synonyms” never silently become constitutional law.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who invents private labels for official syllabus concepts cannot be audited. Canonical speech is institutional memory.

**Manifestations:**

- Findings cite owning corpus paths and published term identities, not only ticket IDs or commit SHAs.
- Canonical recognition under CVI-01 requires published ownership; missing ownership → deferred / escalated, never invent-in-place.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing prior lawful vocabulary assessment history to simplify the next report.
- Synonyms used as operational shorthand must map to published canonical terms or be reported as non-canonical.
- Programme IX CT lineage may support reconstruction; CVI never treats CT records as a licence to redefine term meaning.

---

### CVIO-03 — Preserve definition stability

**Definition.** Ensure that published meaning statements for constitutional terms remain as published across the assessment scope — so silent drift, soft redefinition, and dual glosses are impossible without a published amendment.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who changes what “mastered” means mid-course destroys trust. Definition drift is educational amnesia.

**Manifestations:**

- Definition stability checks compare published meaning statements for the same term across the assessment scope.
- Intact findings confirm that published definitions remain reconstructable and unchanged under validation.
- Violated / deferred / escalated findings block honest claims that drifted definitions are “ready.”
- Definition stability never authorises rewriting definitions under CVI pretext.
- Preserving stability never authorises inventing a “temporary gloss” for demos.

---

### CVIO-04 — Preserve ambiguity avoidance

**Definition.** Ensure that vocabulary assessments detect and report usages that permit incompatible constitutional readings without published disambiguation — so ambiguity is never silently treated as intact.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who uses “assessment” to mean both student exam and constitutional validation without saying which is which abandons clarity for convenience.

**Manifestations:**

- Ambiguous usages are assessed under CVI-04 and reported honestly as collisions, published disambiguations, or violated.
- Published “Catalogue disambiguation” / vocabulary disambiguation tables are evidence of lawful separation — not licences to collapse meanings.
- Forbidden actions in `VOCABULARY_BOUNDARIES.md` are hard stops.
- Ambiguity avoidance never authorises inventing a new term category outside CVT-01…CVT-10 without amendment.
- Cross-programme reuse of everyday English words (e.g. “validation”, “assessment”, “authority”) requires published ownership clarity when used as constitutional terms.

---

### CVIO-05 — Preserve constitutional readability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material vocabulary integrity assessment leaves a reconstructable, speakable trail that supports honest Programme XI integration and readiness judgements — which terms were assessed, which canonical definitions were applied, whether semantic inconsistencies were identified, which findings were produced, and which assessment limits were preserved — so the constitutional corpus remains readable across Programmes VI–XI.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later say *why* a term was accepted cannot be trusted. Silent green scans destroy student and auditor trust. Readiness that ignores broken vocabulary is readiness theatre.

**Manifestations:**

- Audit records preserve constitutional references, not only technical scanner logs.
- Explainability obligations in `VOCABULARY_EXPLAINABILITY.md` apply to all material assessments.
- Missing CVI identity, missing corpus refs, missing term / definition note, missing inconsistency note, or missing finding disposition is a defect.
- Intact findings may inform downstream readiness; they never themselves certify educational readiness, mastery, or product success.
- Violated / deferred / escalated findings block honest readiness claims that depend on the broken vocabulary structure.
- Student / developer / auditor projections share one truth with different vocabulary.
- Constitutional readability never authorises publishing vocabulary findings as new constitutional law.
- Repeatability: same published terms, same CVI criteria → same disposition, independently of language, framework, datastore, CI vendor, or preferred Runtime A shape.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CVIO-01 conflicts with “helping” dual meanings look ready | Semantic consistency wins — violated / deferred / escalated |
| CVIO-02 conflicts with inventing a preferred glossary for demos | Canonical terminology wins |
| CVIO-03 conflicts with soft-redefining a term overnight | Definition stability wins |
| CVIO-04 conflicts with collapsing ambiguous usages so demos pass | Ambiguity avoidance wins |
| CVIO-05 conflicts with opaque “trust us” vocabulary badges or false readiness | Constitutional readability wins |

No supporting objective authorises violating CVIO-01.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — Constitution / EIP integrity obligations and Programme VI–XI authority preservation for the vocabulary integrity horizon.

---

## 5. Non-Objectives

Constitutional vocabulary integrity does **not** optimise for:

- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- CI throughput, flaky-test suppression, or coverage theatre as educational success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when specifications or term definitions are incomplete;
- collapsing CVI categories into one mega-check that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics / spell-checker / NLP signals as substitutes for published constitutional terminology;
- creating, redefining, inventing, or deleting constitutional terms so reports look tidy;
- replacing Programme IX CT lineage preservation, Programme XI / WS1–WS3 assurance horizons, or Programme IX–X evaluative / execution horizons;
- treating documentation as optional commentary on whatever code already shipped.

## 6. Closing Statement

> **An assessment that becomes “intact” by rewriting constitutional terminology is a failed educational system.  
> An assessment that validates only published terms — with semantic consistency, canonical recognition, definition stability, ambiguity avoidance, and constitutional readability intact — is doing its only vocabulary-integrity job.**
