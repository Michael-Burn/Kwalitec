## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor needs bright conditions for saying “this constitutional vocabulary assessment work is finished” — and equally bright refusals when it is not. After WS4 / MS001 has named how terminology is validated and WS4 / MS002 has named how assessments are organised, Programme XI Workstream 4 still needs one fulfilment checklist:

> **What must be true before constitutional speech may affirm that the required vocabulary assessment lifecycle has concluded, canonical terminology has been assessed, semantic consistency and definition stability validated, findings published, and the assessment formally closed?**

These criteria answer that question. They are **normative constitutional conditions**, not database predicates, CI job statuses, ticket states, thesaurus-scan greens, or API status codes.

---

## 2. Criterion Catalogue

All material affirmed completions require **CVIC-01 through CVIC-05** to hold. Failure of any criterion ⇒ **not yet** or **refuse close**. Do not invent a partial “mostly complete” terminology-redefinition, meaning-reinterpretation, educational, or constitutional status.

| ID | Criterion | Primary question |
|----|-----------|------------------|
| **CVIC-01** | Lifecycle completed | Were required CVIL-01…CVIL-08 stages completed for the concern? |
| **CVIC-02** | Vocabulary assessment completed | Were required named constitutional terms assessed and canonical definitions validated under published CVI / CVT law? |
| **CVIC-03** | Semantic validation completed | Were required semantic consistency, ambiguity avoidance, and definition stability validations completed under published law? |
| **CVIC-04** | Findings publication completed | Were required vocabulary integrity findings lawfully constructed and published? |
| **CVIC-05** | Formal assessment closure | Was the vocabulary assessment concern formally closed under published boundary honesty? |

Optional supporting honesty checks (do not replace CVIC-01…CVIC-05):

| ID | Check | Primary question |
|----|-------|------------------|
| **CVIC-06** | Catalogue honesty | Were stages and evaluations typed under published CVIL-01…CVIL-08 and CVI-01…CVI-07 / CVT-01…CVT-10 only? |
| **CVIC-07** | Continuity honesty | Was prior lawful assessment history preserved (EIP-005)? |
| **CVIC-08** | Non-claim honesty | Does the judgement refuse terminology-redefinition / meaning-reinterpretation / specification-amendment / authority-creation / educational overclaim? |
| **CVIC-09** | Neutrality honesty | Does the judgement refuse stack / vendor / glossary-tool canonisation as completion law? |
| **CVIC-10** | Repeatability honesty | Would the same published inputs yield the same completion disposition? |

## 3. Criterion Definitions

### CVIC-01 — Lifecycle completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every lifecycle stage required by the concern’s published WS4 / MS002 scope has been executed — or that a published refuse / defer / escalate disposition exhausted remaining *vocabulary assessment* duties for that stage set with honest progression to finding construction / publication (or refuse-publish) and closure of what occurred.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Identifiable vocabulary assessment concern opened under WS4 / MS001–MS002.
- Required CVIL-01…CVIL-08 stage identities for the concern scope.
- Stage traces / disposition records for each required stage (including lawful early stops).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed stage-fulfilment record naming which required stages completed.
- Or a lawful stop record naming which stages were refused / deferred / escalated and why remaining assessment duties are exhausted.

**Completion conditions.**

- Required stages map to published CVIL-01…CVIL-08 only.
- Mandatory initiation, discovery, canonical definition validation, semantic consistency validation, ambiguity validation, definition stability validation, finding construction, and publication obligations for the concern are not silently omitted.
- Lawful early stops still leave honest stage outcomes; silent disappearance of a concern is a defect.
- Refusal of “pipeline skipped validation” or “we know it ran” without stage identity as completion.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That stage completion redefines constitutional terminology or amends constitutional specifications.
- That stage completion reinterprets constitutional meaning or freezes a stack as law.
- That stage completion means the learner succeeded or educational readiness was achieved.
- That skipped stages may be invented or implied from CI theatre.
- That Programme IX conformity / verification / compliance / certification was performed under stage progress.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who declares vocabulary assessment “finished” without confirming each required assessment step occurred has not finished lawful care — even if the desk looks tidy.

---

### CVIC-02 — Vocabulary assessment completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every named constitutional term required by the concern’s published scope was assessed under published CVT-01…CVT-10 categories and that canonical definitions were validated under published CVI-01 (and related CVIR rules) — or that a published refuse disposition exhausted remaining *vocabulary assessment* duties when required terms / definitions could not be lawfully assessed without inventing glossaries.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Named constitutional term identities in scope (discovered / lawfully classified under CVT law).
- Published canonical definition identities under assessment (owning corpus + meaning statement).
- Evaluation dispositions under applicable CVI / CVT types (especially CVIL-02 / CVIL-03 outcomes).
- Stage traces for CVIL-02 / CVIL-03 as required by concern scope.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed vocabulary-assessment record naming which terms were assessed under which published CVT categories and which canonical definitions were validated.
- Or a lawful refuse / defer / escalate record naming which required terms / definitions remain unassessed and why.

**Completion conditions.**

- Terms are named (not “the corpus,” “the glossary,” or vendor labels alone).
- Assessment cites published constitutional specifications and CVT / CVI categories, not unpublished customs.
- Discovery named only published terms — inventing terms or preferred glossaries to force assessment is a hard stop.
- Canonical definition validation confirms reconstructable ownership and published meaning statements; inventing glosses is a hard stop.
- Refusal of thesaurus scans, NLP confidence, Twin heuristics, or CI greens as substitutes for named vocabulary assessment.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That assessed terms were therefore redefined, invented, or deleted.
- That a particular stack, language, framework, or glossary tool is privileged as law.
- That product quality or educational quality was judged.
- That constitutional specifications were modified to match the assessment.
- That Programme IX CT lineage preservation equals vocabulary assessment fulfilment.
- That discovery alone equals whole-concern completion (discovery / definition outcomes feed CVIC-02; they do not replace CVIC-01…CVIC-05).

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who claims “the vocabulary was checked” without naming which terms were assessed against which published canonical definitions has performed scanner theatre, not vocabulary assessment fulfilment.

---

### CVIC-03 — Semantic validation completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every semantic validation required by the concern’s published scope has been lawfully completed under published CVI / CVIL law — confirming semantic consistency and terminology reuse honesty (CVI-02 / CVI-03 / CVIL-04), ambiguity avoidance (CVI-04 / CVIL-05), and definition stability (CVI-05 and related CVI-06 where material / CVIL-06) — without soft-redefining meaning, hiding ambiguity, or inventing stability.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Named terms and validated canonical definitions under assessment (CVIC-02 inputs / CVIL-02…CVIL-03 outputs).
- Published semantic-validation rules under WS4 / MS001–MS002 (especially CVIL-04…CVIL-06 and CVI-02…CVI-06 / CVIR).
- Boundary confirmations that validation does not redefine terms or privilege a preferred glossary as law.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed semantic-validation record naming which semantic consistency, ambiguity, and definition-stability validations were completed (or honestly failed validation) for which terms.
- Or a lawful refuse / defer / escalate record naming which required semantic validations remain incomplete and why.

**Completion conditions.**

- Semantic validations cite published constitutional references, not unpublished tribal glossaries.
- One-meaning honesty and reuse honesty remain as published; soft-redefinition to force intact status is a hard stop.
- Ambiguity dispositions report undisambiguated incompatible readings honestly; hiding ambiguity under close theatre is a hard stop.
- Definition stability confirms published definitions remain as published; inventing stability by erasing drift evidence is a hard stop.
- “Semantics were checked somewhere” without named validation identity is not completion.
- Lawful failed / deferred / escalated semantic outcomes still satisfy *validation completion* when they are the honest outcome — completeness of *validation* is not completeness of *intact disposition*, and neither is terminology redefinition.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That completing semantic validation redefines constitutional terminology or amends specifications.
- That a validated semantic outcome freezes the implementation, stack, vendor, or release as permanent constitutional law.
- That a failed / deferred / escalated semantic outcome may be silently erased so close can look green.
- That MS001 vocabulary meaning was replaced, skipped, or rewritten by semantic validation.
- That Educational Validation Framework quality review is complete.
- That educational success or exam readiness follows from semantic validation.

**Tutor rationale.** Recording that semantic consistency, ambiguity, and definition stability were checked — including when they fail — is care. Soft-redefining a term so the assessment can finish is fiction. Completeness of *semantic validation* is not redefinition of *constitutional meaning*.

---

### CVIC-04 — Findings publication completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every vocabulary integrity finding required by the concern’s published scope has been lawfully constructed and published under published CVI / CVIL rules — including intact, violated, deferred, and escalated dispositions — without rewriting those dispositions into terminology redefinition, meaning reinterpretation, specification amendment, or constitutional authority, and without inventing intact status or hiding inconsistencies.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Vocabulary assessment and semantic-validation dispositions under applicable CVI-01…CVI-07 types (CVIL-03…CVIL-06 outputs).
- Published finding-construction and publication rules under WS4 / MS001–MS002 (especially CVIL-07 / CVIL-08).
- Boundary confirmations that findings do not invent law or redefine terms.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Identifiable vocabulary integrity finding identities with lawful dispositions, published under honesty rules.
- Or a lawful stop record naming why required findings could not be constructed / published without invention.

**Completion conditions.**

- Findings cite published constitutional references, named terms, validated (or honestly failed) definitions, and semantic validations.
- Dispositions remain within published vocabulary (intact / violated / deferred / escalated).
- “Assessment happened somewhere” without named finding identity and publication record is not completion.
- Lawful violated / deferred / escalated findings still satisfy *publication* when they are the honest outcome — completeness of *publication* is not completeness of *intact disposition*, and neither is constitutional amendment.
- Publication never invents intact status, hides inconsistencies, or freezes a stack as law.
- Continuity forbids silently replacing prior lawful findings under close theatre.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That publishing a finding redefines constitutional terminology or amends constitutional specifications.
- That an intact finding freezes the implementation, stack, vendor, or release as permanent constitutional law.
- That a violated / deferred / escalated finding may be silently erased or upgraded so close can look green.
- That MS001 vocabulary evaluation was replaced, skipped, or rewritten by publication.
- That Programme IX conformity / verification / compliance / certification was achieved by publication.
- That the learner succeeded or is exam-ready.

**Tutor rationale.** Publishing a hard vocabulary disposition is not rewriting the syllabus — but omitting a required finding while claiming the assessment finished is dishonest. Completeness of *findings publication* is not completeness of *constitutional change*.

---

### CVIC-05 — Formal assessment closure

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that the vocabulary assessment concern was formally closed under published CVIL-08 / boundary honesty — exhausting remaining *vocabulary assessment lifecycle* duties without redefining constitutional terminology, reinterpreting constitutional meaning, amending constitutional specifications, creating constitutional authority, or implying educational success.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Published findings and assessment records (CVIC-01…CVIC-04).
- CVIL-08 closure / publication honesty rules and `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md` hard stops.
- Explicit non-claim confirmations (terminology redefinition / meaning reinterpretation / specification amendment / authority creation / educational success).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Formal close disposition: affirmed complete / not yet / refuse close / defer / escalate.
- Boundary preservation record confirming forbidden overclaims did not occur.

**Completion conditions.**

- Closure occurs only after required stages, vocabulary assessment, semantic validation, findings publication, and assessment-record obligations for the concern are satisfied — or after a published stop exhausts remaining assessment duties with honest recording.
- Closure speech answers CVICQ-01…CVICQ-05 or explicitly refuses mute theatre.
- Closure never silently upgrades findings dispositions or redefines terms.
- Refusal of ticket closure, PR merge, thesaurus-scan greens, or pipeline greens alone as formal constitutional close.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That formal close redefines constitutional terminology or invents preferred glossaries as law.
- That formal close reinterprets Programme VI–X / EIP meaning.
- That formal close amends constitutional specifications.
- That formal close creates constitutional authority.
- That formal close means the implementation is constitutionally conformant, verified, compliant, or certified.
- That formal close means the student succeeded or is exam-ready.
- That formal close freezes a stack, vendor, or release as permanent constitutional law.

**Tutor rationale.** Closing the assessment folder is care only when the required steps, vocabulary assessment, semantic validations, findings, and records exist — and only when nobody pretends the close is a new constitution, a rewritten glossary, or a pass certificate for the candidate.

## 4. Criterion Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CVIC-01 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CVIC-02 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CVIC-03 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CVIC-04 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — mute complete badges are defects |
| CVIC-05 fails | Affirmation is unlawful even if earlier stages appear present |
| Optional CVIC-06…CVIC-10 fail | Treat as honesty defects; do not soft-pass CVIC-01…CVIC-05 |
| Any criterion “passes” only by redefining terminology / reinterpreting meaning / amending specifications / creating authority / implying educational success | The implied overclaim voids lawful affirmation |

All of CVIC-01…CVIC-05 are **conjunctive** for material affirmed completion. There is no lawful “four of five” educational, fidelity, terminology-redefinition, or constitutional status.

Semantic validation is **not** a sixth catalogue criterion beyond CVIC-03. It aggregates required CVIL-04…CVIL-06 / CVI-02…CVI-06 obligations. Affirming close without those validations where the concern required them fails CVIC-01 and/or CVIC-03.

## 5. Lawful Stop Dispositions

Not every incomplete concern is a defect. Published WS4 / MS002 refuse / defer / escalate / violated outcomes may **exhaust remaining vocabulary assessment duties** for a concern without affirming lifecycle fulfilment as “complete” in the celebratory sense — while still requiring honest recording and formal close of what occurred.

| Disposition | Constitutional meaning | May affirm CVIC completion? |
|-------------|------------------------|----------------------------|
| **Affirm complete** | CVIC-01…CVIC-05 hold for the concern | Yes |
| **Not yet** | Required stages, vocabulary assessment, semantic validation, findings, or records remain outstanding | No |
| **Refuse close** | Affirmation would invent stages / findings, redefine terms, invent authority, or overclaim | No |
| **Defer** | Published wait for upstream corpus / term / definition identity | No (unless published law says remaining assessment duties are exhausted) |
| **Escalate** | Published stop requiring human / corpus governance attention | No (unless published law says remaining assessment duties are exhausted) |

A lawful stop that exhausts assessment duties is **honest unfinished fulfilment or honest refusal** — never stealth terminology redefinition, meaning reinterpretation, specification amendment, constitutional authority, educational success, or Programme IX evaluative disposition.

---

## 6. Non-Criteria

The following are **not** constitutional completion criteria:

- CI pipeline greens, flaky-scan suppression, or job exit codes;
- ticket closure, PR merge, or documentation checklist ticks alone;
- thesaurus scans, spell-checkers, NLP scores, or OpenAPI linkers alone;
- coverage percentages, lint scores, or elapsed time;
- Twin / Adaptive / Experience / Mission confidence signals;
- UI celebration states or analytics milestones;
- “looks consistent enough for the demo”;
- MS001 intact disposition alone (that is evaluation outcome, not whole-concern CVIC fulfilment);
- Programme XI / WS1–WS3 CDIC / CCIC / CBIC completion alone (orthogonal structure horizons);
- Programme IX CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC completion alone (orthogonal implementation horizons);
- Educational Validation Framework quality release (orthogonal);
- any implication that constitutional specifications changed, terms were redefined, or authority was created because assessment closed.

## 7. Closing Statement

> **Constitutional vocabulary integrity assessment is complete only when the required lifecycle is completed, vocabulary assessment is completed, semantic validation is completed, findings publication is completed, and the assessment is formally closed.  
> Satisfying CVIC-01…CVIC-05 never redefines constitutional terminology, reinterprets constitutional meaning, amends constitutional specifications, or creates constitutional authority.**
