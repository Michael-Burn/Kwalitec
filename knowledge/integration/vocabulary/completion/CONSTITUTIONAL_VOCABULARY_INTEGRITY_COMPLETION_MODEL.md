## 2. What Constitutional Vocabulary Integrity Completion Is

**Constitutional Vocabulary Integrity Completion** is confirmation that the **required constitutional vocabulary integrity assessment lifecycle** for a published assessment concern has **lawfully concluded** — such that canonical terminology assessment, semantic consistency validation, ambiguity and definition stability validation, findings publication, and formal assessment closure hold for that concern under WS4 / MS001–MS002 — without claiming that any terminology-redefinition, meaning-reinterpretation, specification-amendment, or constitutional-authority question was resolved by the close itself.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Assessment concern** | A bounded constitutional unit of vocabulary integrity work opened under published CVI / CVIL law | What vocabulary assessment is being assessed for completion? |
| **Completion warrant** | Judgement that assessment fulfilment may be assessed | May we ask whether the vocabulary assessment is complete? |
| **Completion criteria** | Constitutional conditions that must hold (`COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md`) | What must be true for fulfilment? |
| **Completion judgement** | Affirmed / not yet / refused (no lawful close) | Is constitutional vocabulary assessment complete for this concern? |
| **Lifecycle fulfilment** | Required CVIL-01…CVIL-08 obligations executed (or lawfully stopped with honest recording) | Did the published assessment lifecycle run? |
| **Vocabulary assessment** | Named constitutional terms assessed and canonical definitions validated under published CVI / CVT law | Were canonical terms assessed? |
| **Semantic validation** | Semantic consistency, terminology reuse, ambiguity avoidance, and definition stability validated | Were semantic validations completed? |
| **Findings publication** | Vocabulary integrity findings published under honesty rules | Were findings published? |
| **Formal assessment closure** | Assessment concern closed under published boundary honesty | Was the assessment formally closed? |
| **Boundary preservation record** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden overclaims / redefinitions / reinterpretations did not occur | Did constitutional integrity survive close? |

Constitutional Vocabulary Integrity Completion is:

- **assessment-scoped** — it concerns fulfilment of the published vocabulary assessment lifecycle, not constitutional authorship, terminology rewriting, educational outcomes, or stack canonisation;
- **lifecycle-bound** — fulfilment references CVIL-01…CVIL-08 stages actually required for the concern under WS4 / MS002;
- **criteria-gated** — fulfilment requires stated constitutional conditions (`COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md` CVIC-01…CVIC-05);
- **terminology-honest** — completion confirms canonical terms were assessed under published CVI / CVT law; it never invents or redefines terms;
- **semantics-honest** — completion confirms semantic consistency, ambiguity, and definition stability were validated; it never soft-redefines meaning;
- **publication-honest** — completion confirms findings were published; it never invents intact status or hides inconsistencies;
- **audit-capable** — fulfilment cites reconstructable constitutional records, not ticket theatre or CI greens;
- **repeatable** — same published corpora, same discovered terms / definitions, same stage sequence ⇒ same completion disposition for the same concern scope;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by completion;
- **non-legislative** — completion never creates constitutional authority or amends constitutional specifications;
- **non-mutating** — completion never redefines constitutional terminology under close pretext;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which terms / definitions / validations / findings / boundaries applied;
- **corpus-bound** — every material completion cites identifiable published specifications, terms, definitions, and named findings / records;
- **non-substitutive** — a completed vocabulary assessment never becomes the constitutional standard or a substitute for MS001 evaluation meaning.

Constitutional Vocabulary Integrity Completion is **not**:

- redefinition of constitutional terminology or invention of a preferred glossary as law;
- reinterpretation of constitutional meaning or Programme VI–X / EIP claim classes;
- amendment of constitutional specifications under Constitution / EIP / Programmes VI–X / WS1–WS3 / WS4 / MS001–MS002;
- creation of constitutional authority or a soft second constitution;
- educational success, learner mastery, progress, or exam readiness;
- implementation conformity, verification, compliance, or certification;
- a Programme VI / VII / VIII educational, orchestration, or runtime completion judgement;
- a claim that pipeline greens, ticket closure, elapsed time, or thesaurus-scan badges equal fulfilment;
- a UI celebration, analytics milestone, or “vocabulary forever intact” flag as educational or constitutional law;
- a licence to skip outstanding published lifecycle duties because a product surface looks finished;
- a substitute for WS4 / MS001 vocabulary integrity meaning or WS4 / MS002 lifecycle law — completion *judges fulfilment of* those corpora; it does not redefine CVI, CVT, or CVIL catalogues;

## 4. Completion Lifecycle (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material vocabulary integrity assessment concern that claims lawful completion travels through constitutional assessment phases. These are **normative judgement obligations**, not an implementation architecture.

```
Identify published vocabulary assessment concern opened under CVI / CVIL law
        │
        ▼
Confirm required CVIL-01…CVIL-08 stages and CVI-01…CVI-07 / CVT-01…CVT-10 applications for the concern
        │
        ├── refuse / defer / escalate (lawful stop — law unchanged; no invented “complete”)
        │
        └── assess → Apply CVIC-01…CVIC-05
                │
                ├── not yet / refuse close (outstanding duties or boundary breach)
                │
                └── affirm → Emit completion judgement; preserve audit continuity and repeatability
                        │
                        ▼
                Explain terms / definitions / semantic validations / findings / boundaries
```

| Phase | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **Identify** | Name only published vocabulary assessment concerns opened under WS4 / MS001–MS002 | Concern is unpublished custom, tribal glossary, or “how the thesaurus scanner works today” |
| **Confirm lifecycle** | Verify required CVIL stages and CVI / CVT applications for the concern | Stages would invent law, redefine terms, reinterpret meaning, invent authority, or privilege a stack as law |
| **Assess criteria** | Apply CVIC-01…CVIC-05 without softening into product theatre | Any required criterion fails; or close would claim terminology redefinition / meaning reinterpretation / specification amendment / authority creation |
| **Preserve & explain** | Record audit continuity; answer CVICQ-01…CVICQ-05 | Path would erase assessment trails, treat completion as constitutional authority, or narrate “complete therefore terms redefined / meaning changed / mastered” |

> **Vocabulary integrity completion never “best-effort invents” a fifth phase called terminology redefinition, meaning reinterpretation, specification amendment, or constitutional authority creation.**

## 7. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Vocabulary Integrity Completion Model exists so that:

1. **Lifecycle honesty remains intact** — “complete” means the published vocabulary assessment lifecycle ran, not that a thesaurus scanner glowed green.
2. **Canonical terminology remains honest** — close confirms published terms were assessed; it never invents or redefines them.
3. **Semantic consistency remains intact** — close confirms one-meaning honesty and reuse were validated; it never soft-redefines meaning.
4. **Ambiguity and definition stability remain intact** — close confirms ambiguity avoidance and definition stability were validated; it never hides drift.
5. **Publication remains honest** — close confirms findings were published under honesty rules; it never invents intact status.
6. **Formal closure remains speakable** — assessment close exhausts assessment duties without becoming law-making.
7. **Audit continuity survives close** — completion leaves reconstructable constitutional records.
8. **Repeatability remains intact** — the same published inputs yield the same completion disposition.
9. **Authority remains upstream** — completed vocabulary assessment never becomes the constitutional source for the next judgement.
10. **Implementations remain replaceable** — completion does not freeze Flask, SQLAlchemy, a particular API shape, or a CI vendor as law.
11. **Explainability remains intact** — completion speech describes assessment fulfilment; it does not redefine what learning means or what conformity is.
12. **Students remain protected** — product theatre cannot launder unfinished or inconsistent corpus terminology as “vocabulary assessment complete therefore lawful / ready / permanently frozen.”
