## 1. Purpose

Assessment machinery that silently turns “the vocabulary assessment finished” into “the Constitution now says X,” “yesterday’s terms mean something new,” “this glossary tool is now the superior authority,” “student succeeded,” or “specifications are amended” destroys student trust and educational integrity.

This document draws a bright line between **lawful constitutional vocabulary integrity completion** (Programme XI / WS4 / MS003) and **terminology authorship / meaning reinterpretation / specification amendment / constitutional authority creation / educational judgement** owned elsewhere.

---

## 2. Boundary Principles

1. **Confirm assessment fulfilment, do not legislate.** Completion affirms fulfilment of published vocabulary assessment lifecycle duties; it does not create constitutional law.
2. **Assessment only.** Terminology redefinition, meaning reinterpretation, specification amendment, and constitutional authority creation are never inferred from close.
3. **Not terminology rewriting.** Close never invents, redefines, soft-amends, or silently deletes published terms.
4. **Not MS001 meaning replacement.** Completion judges fulfilment of CVI evaluation; it never redefines CVI categories, CVT term categories, or CVIR rules.
5. **Not MS002 lifecycle rewrite.** Completion judges fulfilment of CVIL stages; it never invents a ninth stage called law-making.
6. **Not constitutional amendment.** Close never patches law or redefines constitutional meaning.
7. **Not meaning reinterpretation.** Programme VI questions and EIP claim classes survive close intact.
8. **Not authority creation.** Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard after close.
9. **Exactly as published.** Close never redefines CVI / CVT / CVIL catalogues or soft-amends Programme VI–X meaning.
10. **Published criteria only.** Pipelines, tickets, thesaurus scans, and elapsed time are not fulfilment law.
11. **Published records only.** Audit continuity is constitutional, not free-form log theatre.
12. **Reference findings honestly.** Published findings may be cited; closing never upgrades disposition into terminology redefinition or authority creation.
13. **Implementation neutrality.** No stack or glossary tool becomes law by proximity to a complete flag.
14. **Assessment-complete ≠ quality.** Completion never grades learning, mastery, readiness, or product success.
15. **Assessment-complete ≠ Programme IX evaluative close.** Completion never substitutes for CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC fulfilment.
16. **Assessment-complete ≠ WS1–WS3 completion.** Completion never substitutes for CDIC / CCIC / CBIC fulfilment.
17. **Explain the stop.** Students, developers, and auditors should hear when close must be refused.
18. **No emergency exemption.** Deadlines and demos never mint completion power or amendment power.
19. **Repeatability survives close.** Close must not invent contradictory dispositions from identical published inputs.
20. **Continuity survives close.** EIP-005 forbids erasing prior lawful assessment history to tidy the report.

## 3. What Vocabulary Integrity Completion May Do (Lawful)

| Lawful action | Constitutional meaning |
|---------------|------------------------|
| **Confirm assessment completion** | Affirm CVIC-01…CVIC-05 when published criteria hold for a lawful vocabulary assessment concern |
| **Confirm publication** | Cite that required vocabulary integrity findings were lawfully constructed and published under published CVI / CVIL rules — without rewriting or silently upgrading dispositions |
| **Confirm formal closure** | Affirm that the assessment concern was formally closed under published boundary honesty |
| **Preserve assessment records** | Leave reconstructable constitutional records of terms, definitions, semantic validations, stages, findings, and boundaries |
| **Preserve audit continuity** | Leave reconstructable constitutional records of affirmed or refused close |
| **Refuse unlawful close** | Prefer honest not-yet / refuse / defer / escalate over improvisation |
| **Compose required stages and types** | Confirm fulfilment across published CVIL / CVI / CVT conjunctions without collapsing failed duties |
| **Remain implementation-neutral** | Honour completion law across lawful equivalent stacks |
| **Preserve repeatability** | Ensure same published inputs do not yield contradictory completion stories |
| **Explain completion** | Answer terms / definitions / semantic validations / findings / boundaries without rewriting meaning |
| **Propose corpus amendments upstream** | Recommend (not enact) amendments when law itself must change before assessment can complete |

These actions **confirm vocabulary assessment fulfilment**. They do **not** publish a new Constitution, invent coach warrants, mint tips, author ownership maps, invent CVI / CVT / CVIL / CVIC types, author runtime behaviour, redefine terms, reinterpret constitutional meaning, amend constitutional specifications, create constitutional authority, finish Programme IX evaluative horizons as constitutional law, or judge educational quality.

## 4. What Vocabulary Integrity Completion Must NEVER Do

| Forbidden action | Why | Lawful alternative |
|------------------|-----|--------------------|
| **Redefine constitutional terminology** | Only published corpora define term meanings | Cite findings honestly; refuse silent rewrite under close theatre |
| **Reinterpret constitutional meaning** | Close never soft-amends Programme VI–X / EIP meaning | Preserve identity of published meaning; never invent it |
| **Amend constitutional specifications** | Only corpus governance may amend meaning | Leave specs unchanged; propose amendment upstream or refuse incomplete assessment |
| **Create constitutional authority** | Only published corpora define constitutional standards | Keep completion as assessment fulfilment; never as next-cycle law |
| **Invent constitutional terms** | Discovery / assessment name published terms only | Refuse invented glossaries; escalate to corpus governance if law is missing |
| **Invent preferred glossaries as law** | Canonical definitions remain in owning corpora | Cite published definitions; never elevate thesaurus / NLP products as law |
| **Hide ambiguity or invent intact status** | Ambiguity honesty is educational care | Publish violated / deferred / escalated findings honestly |
| **Erase definition drift under close** | Definition stability honesty is educational care | Report stability defects; never invent permanence |
| **Soft-merge incompatible meanings** | Semantic consistency is published one-meaning honesty | Report inconsistencies; never rewrite meaning under close |
| **Replace MS001 vocabulary evaluation** | MS001 owns CVI / CVT meaning | Confirm assessment support for evaluation honesty; never claim “complete therefore new CVI law” |
| **Replace MS002 lifecycle law** | MS002 owns CVIL stages | Confirm stage fulfilment; never invent stages |
| **Create educational meaning** | Programme VI owns coach / planner questions | Preserve identity of published meaning; never invent it |
| **Author runtime behaviour** | Programme VIII + permitted writers own execution law | Assess vocabulary against published law; do not mint behaviour |
| **Bypass audit / publication / closure requirements** | Requirements are educational care, not optional polish | Prefer understatement / refuse over invented green completes |
| **Privilege a technology as constitutional** | Implementation neutrality | Confirm assessment obligations, not Flask / SQLAlchemy / REST / OpenAPI / thesaurus fashion |
| **Erase assessment trails to tidy close** | EIP-005 continuity | Preserve prior lawful stage / finding / record artefacts; refuse destructive “cleanup” |

| **Present CI / tickets / thesaurus scans as fulfilment law** | Theatre is not constitution | Require CVIC-01…CVIC-05 content |
| **Imply educational success** | Learning outcomes are not assessment fulfilment | Keep student speech honest: assessment-complete ≠ ready |
| **Imply Programme IX evaluative completion** | Conformity / verification / compliance / certification are orthogonal | Compose with CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC; do not treat them as CVIC (or vice versa) |

| **Collapse with CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC / CDIC / CCIC / CBIC completion** | Those fulfilment horizons are orthogonal | Compose with sibling MS003 corpora; do not treat them as CVIC |

## 5. Boundary Tests

Before affirming completion, ask:

1. Would this close still be lawful if no CI job had run?
2. Would this close still be lawful if the preferred stack or glossary tool were replaced by another lawful equivalent?
3. Does the close require inventing an unpublished term, preferred glossary, or stage?
4. Does the close silently rewrite a violated / deferred / escalated finding into intact?
5. Does the close narrate terminology redefinition, meaning reinterpretation, specification amendment, or constitutional authority creation?
6. Can students / developers / auditors answer CVICQ-01…CVICQ-05 from the close record?
7. Would the same published inputs yield the same completion disposition tomorrow?

Any “no” that indicates invention, redefinition, reinterpretation, amendment, stack privilege, mute theatre, or non-repeatability ⇒ **refuse close**.

---

## 6. Emergency and Product Pressure

Deadlines, demos, investor reviews, and release trains **never** authorise:

- inventing CVIC criteria outside CVIC-01…CVIC-05;
- inventing terms, preferred glossaries, or intact findings so close can complete;
- amending Constitution / EIP / Programmes VI–X / WS1–WS3 / WS4 / MS001–MS002 via completion theatre;
- freezing Flask, SQLAlchemy, OpenAPI, a CI vendor, a thesaurus product, or Runtime A as constitutional law;
- narrating assessment-complete as educational success or Programme IX evaluative success.

Lawful response under pressure: **not yet**, **refuse close**, **defer**, or **escalate** — with speakable reasons.
