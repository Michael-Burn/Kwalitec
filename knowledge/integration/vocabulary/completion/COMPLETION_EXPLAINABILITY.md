## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which constitutional terms* were assessed — which canonical definitions were validated, which semantic validations were completed, which findings were published, and which limits remained intact — is not closing honestly; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–X have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, governance, and execution speech speakable, after Programme XI / WS1–WS3 have made dependency / catalogue / boundary speech speakable, and after WS4 / MS001–MS002 have made vocabulary and lifecycle speech speakable, Programme XI still needs one vocabulary-assessment-completion-speech answer:

> **“Which constitutional terms were assessed, which canonical definitions were validated, which semantic validations were completed, which findings were published, and which constitutional boundaries remained intact?”**

That answer must cover every material affirmed (or refused) completion without inventing educational success, redefining constitutional terminology, reinterpreting constitutional meaning, amending constitutional specifications, creating constitutional authority, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, or freezing a stack as law.

## 2. What Vocabulary Integrity Completion Explainability Is

**Constitutional Vocabulary Integrity Completion Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **fulfilment (or lawful refusal of fulfilment) of the required constitutional vocabulary integrity assessment lifecycle** — not to invent a second tutor voice from CI complete badges, and not to grade the student, redefine terminology, reinterpret meaning, amend specifications, or create constitutional authority.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Terms assessed** | Named constitutional terms evaluated under CVI / CVT law | Which constitutional terms were assessed? |
| **Canonical definitions validated** | Named published meaning statements and owning corpora | Which canonical definitions were validated? |
| **Semantic validations completed** | Named semantic consistency, ambiguity, and definition-stability outcomes | Which semantic validations were completed? |
| **Findings published** | Named vocabulary integrity findings with lawful dispositions | Which findings were published? |
| **Boundaries intact** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden overclaims / redefinitions / reinterpretations did not occur | Which constitutional boundaries remained intact? |

Vocabulary Integrity Completion Explainability is:

- **fulfilment-centred** — it narrates why CVIC criteria hold (or why close is refused);
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CVI / CVT / CVIL / CVIC identities where applicable;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published specifications, named terms, definitions, semantic validations, and named findings;
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to create authority or amend corpora;
- **non-mutating** — explanation never narrates completion as terminology redefinition or soft meaning change;
- **finding-honest** — explanation never silently upgrades published findings;
- **quality-honest** — complete is never narrated as mastery, readiness, or educational quality;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack or glossary-tool preference as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence / validation speech, Programme IX evaluative speech, Programme X execution speech, WS1–WS3 integrity speech, MS001 vocabulary speech, or MS002 lifecycle speech;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match CVIC reconstruction needs;
- **authority-honest** — explanations must not paper over authority inference or stack / glossary canonisation.

Vocabulary Integrity Completion Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–X / WS1–WS3 / WS4 meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a constitutional authority instrument or amendment warrant;
- a silent terminology-redefinition or finding-upgrade mechanism;
- a Programme IX evaluative-finish report as constitutional law;
- a substitute for WS4 / MS001 vocabulary explainability or WS4 / MS002 lifecycle explainability (compose with; do not replace);
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS1–WS3 CDIC / CCIC / CBIC completion explainability (compose with; do not collapse);
- a substitute for Programme IX CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC completion explainability (compose with; do not collapse);
- a rendering system, UI template, CI badge, glossary visualiser, or analytics dashboard;
- a claim that clear completion speech guarantees learning, a pass, conformity, constitutional amendment, or terminology redefinition;
- a licence to present thesaurus scans, lint scores, ticket graphs, or pipeline greens as constitutional proof or educational quality.

## 3. Mandatory Explanation Questions

Every material completion judgement (affirm or refuse close) should answer all five questions:

### CVICQ-01 — Which constitutional terms were assessed?

Name the primary constitutional term identities evaluated under the assessment concern — including applicable CVT-01…CVT-10 categories and CVI applications — or name which required terms remain unassessed when refusing close. Never narrate invented terms or preferred glossaries as lawful assessment subjects. “The corpus was scanned somewhere” is not a constitutional term identity.

### CVICQ-02 — Which canonical definitions were validated?

Name the primary published canonical definition identities and owning corpora validated under the concern — or name which required definitions remain unvalidated when refusing close. Include lawful failed / deferred / escalated definition outcomes honestly. Never narrate invented glosses as validated definitions. “Looks defined enough” is not a constitutional definition identity.

### CVICQ-03 — Which semantic validations were completed?

Name the primary semantic consistency, terminology reuse, ambiguity avoidance, and definition-stability validation identities and outcomes completed under the concern (especially CVIL-04…CVIL-06 / CVI-02…CVI-06) — or name which required semantic validations remain incomplete when refusing close. Never narrate soft-redefinition, hidden ambiguity, or invented stability as lawful validation. “Looks consistent enough” is not a constitutional semantic-validation identity.

### CVICQ-04 — Which findings were published?

Name the primary vocabulary integrity finding identities and dispositions published under the concern (intact / violated / deferred / escalated) — or name which required findings remain unpublished when refusing close. Never narrate a violated / deferred / escalated finding as upgraded by completion, and never narrate a silent replacement of a prior lawful finding. “Looks intact enough” is not a constitutional finding identity.

### CVICQ-05 — Which constitutional boundaries remained intact?

Confirm the hard stops in `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md` that were checked — especially non-redefinition of constitutional terminology, non-reinterpretation of constitutional meaning, non-amendment of constitutional specifications, non-creation of constitutional authority, implementation neutrality, repeatability honesty, and non-equation of completion with educational success, Programme IX evaluative completion, WS1–WS3 completion, or constitutional truth.

## 5. Audience Rules

| Audience | May hear | Must not hear as educational law |
|----------|----------|-----------------------------------|
| **Student** | Plain speech that a vocabulary check finished (or did not), without syllabus rewrite claims | “You’re exam-ready,” “the law changed,” “these words now mean something new,” “everything is permanently intact forever” |
| **Developer** | Named corpus identities, CVI / CVT / CVIL / CVIC IDs, term / definition / semantic-validation / finding refs, refuse reasons | “Ship because assessment-complete,” “rewrite terms in main to match the badge,” “freeze this glossary tool as constitutional” |
| **Auditor** | Reconstructable CVIC-01…CVIC-05 outcomes, CVICQ answers, boundary preservation, continuity honesty | Mute “complete” badges, invented terms, invented definitions, silent finding upgrades |

Student-facing and auditor-facing projections **must share one constitutional truth**. Vocabulary may differ; facts may not.

## 6. Forbidden Explanations

The following explanation patterns are constitutionally defective:

- “Assessment complete, therefore constitutional terminology now means X.”
- “Assessment complete, therefore Programme VI / EIP meaning now means Y.”
- “Assessment complete, therefore constitutional specifications are amended.”
- “Assessment complete, therefore constitutional authority now resides in the thesaurus / pipeline / completion badge.”
- “Assessment complete, therefore the student is exam-ready / mastered / succeeded.”
- “Assessment complete, therefore the implementation is conformant / verified / compliant / certified.”
- “Assessment complete because CI was green / the thesaurus looked tidy / the ticket closed.”
- “Terms were assessed somehow” without named term identity.
- “Definitions were validated somehow” without named definition identity.
- “Semantics were validated somehow” without named semantic-validation identity.
- “Findings were published somehow” without named finding identity and disposition.
- Silent upgrade of violated / deferred / escalated findings into intact under close narration.
- Narrating a preferred stack, language, framework, vendor, or glossary tool as constitutional law because assessment closed.
- Collapsing CVIC completion into CDIC / CCIC / CBIC / CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC completion (or vice versa) without distinction.
