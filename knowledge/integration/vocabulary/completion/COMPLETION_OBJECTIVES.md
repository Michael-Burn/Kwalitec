## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “thesaurus greens,” “tickets closed,” or “glossaries shown.” The tutor optimises **honest closing of constitutional vocabulary assessment work**: when the published lifecycle for a concern has lawfully concluded, canonical terminology has been assessed, semantic consistency and definition stability validated, findings published, and the assessment formally closed, the tutor can say so — without pretending terminology was redefined, without soft-reinterpreting meaning so the report looks tidy, without pretending the student has mastered the topic, and without amending the syllabus because the machinery stopped cleanly.

These objectives bind every constitutional vocabulary integrity completion judgement and every successor assessment machinery that claims vocabulary assessment fulfilment.

## 2. Primary Objective

### CVICO-01 — Confirm lawful assessment completion

**Definition.** Ensure that runtime / assessment / audit speech affirms vocabulary integrity completion only when a published assessment concern under WS4 / MS001–MS002 has concluded the required CVIL-01…CVIL-08 lifecycle obligations — or honestly refuses close when fulfilment criteria are not met.

**Includes:**

- Assessing only concerns that lawfully opened under WS4 / MS001–MS002 initiation → discovery → canonical definition validation → semantic consistency validation → ambiguity validation → definition stability validation → finding construction → publication law.
- Affirming completion only when `COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md` conditions (CVIC-01…CVIC-05) hold.
- Preferring lawful “not yet” / refuse-close over inventing a “done enough” status for product convenience.
- Distinguishing lawful stop dispositions (refuse / defer / escalate / violated as published outcomes that exhaust remaining *assessment* duties with honest recording) from abandoned lifecycle duties.
- Binding every material completion judgement to reconstructable constitutional references.

**Excludes:**

- Declaring complete because a CI job exited, a ticket closed, a thesaurus scan glowed, or a UI spinner stopped.
- Treating Twin / Adaptive / Experience / Mission heuristics as completion warrants.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for assessment honesty.
- Collapsing “vocabulary assessment complete” into “terminology redefined,” “meaning reinterpreted,” “specifications amended,” “constitutional authority created,” or “student succeeded.”

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software celebrates unfinished vocabulary assessment — or celebrates finished scanner machinery as finished glossary rewriting or law-making. Honest confirmation is care; completion theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CVICO-02 — Preserve semantic consistency

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming completion never invents, soft-redefines, or silently collapses published term meanings — and never upgrades a violated / deferred / escalated semantic finding into “intact” under close theatre.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who “helpfully” merges two incompatible uses of “assessment” so the report can finish has abandoned educational honesty even if the completion UX looks clean.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion leaves named term identities, semantic dispositions, and reuse outcomes reconstructable.
- Close never substitutes thesaurus scans, NLP confidence, or ticket states for published semantic findings.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing or silently rewriting prior lawful semantic dispositions to simplify the next report.
- Soft-redefining meaning under “completion cleanup” is a defect.

---

### CVICO-03 — Preserve canonical terminology

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming completion never invents unpublished terms, invents preferred glossaries as law, or replaces published canonical definitions — so every closed assessment remains reconstructable to published owning corpora and definitions.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later walk from yesterday’s finding to the published definition that bound the term cannot be trusted. Completion that invents glossaries is educational fiction dressed as done.

**Manifestations:**

- Affirmed closes leave term identities and canonical definition identities reconstructable with findings.
- Invented terms, invented glossaries, and stack-as-glossary substitutions are defects.
- Continuity forbids deleting prior lawful definition walks to “clean up” completion.
- Incomplete canonical assessment for a required concern scope blocks affirmation.

---

### CVICO-04 — Preserve constitutional readability

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming completion never leaves the corpus less speakable — never hides unresolved ambiguity as “complete enough,” never narrates silent definition drift as intact, and never substitutes opaque complete badges for speakable term / definition / finding identities.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who can only say “the vocabulary check finished somewhere” has abandoned care for theatre. Speakable constitutional language is educational integrity.

**Manifestations:**

- Affirmed closes leave term, definition, semantic-validation, and finding identities speakable under CVICQ-01…CVICQ-05.
- Mute “complete” badges without reconstructable vocabulary identities are defects.
- Unresolved ambiguity upgraded to intact under close theatre is a defect.
- Readability failures void lawful affirmation even if a scanner badge looks green.

---

### CVICO-05 — Preserve assessment repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that the same published corpora, same discovered terms / definitions, same CVI applications, and same CVIL stage sequence yield the same completion disposition for the same concern — so close never invents contradictory stories from identical constitutional inputs.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who marks the same syllabus terminology “complete” on Monday and “not yet” on Tuesday without a published corpus change has abandoned care for theatre.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion dispositions are deterministic under published inputs.
- Silent non-determinism, ambient “main branch as glossary,” and demo-only close rules are defects.
- Optional honesty checks (CVIC-06…CVIC-10) support, never replace, CVIC-01…CVIC-05.
- Repeatability failures void lawful affirmation even if a badge looks green.

---

### CVICO-06 — Preserve explainability and auditability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material completion judgement leaves a reconstructable, speakable trail: which constitutional terms were assessed, which canonical definitions were validated, which semantic validations were completed, which findings were published, and which boundaries remained intact.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who can only say “the vocabulary check is done somewhere” is performing, not teaching. Speakable completion is care.

**Manifestations:**

- Audit records preserve constitutional references, not only technical job logs or CI annotations.
- Explainability obligations in `COMPLETION_EXPLAINABILITY.md` apply to all material completion judgements (CVICQ-01…CVICQ-05).
- Missing CVIC identity against criteria, missing term refs, missing definition identity, missing semantic-validation identity, or missing finding identity is a defect.
- Student / developer / auditor projections share one truth with different vocabulary.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CVICO-01 conflicts with inventing unpublished terms so close can complete | Assessment honesty wins — refuse / defer / escalate |
| CVICO-02 conflicts with soft-redefining meaning to erase inconsistency for tidy close | Semantic consistency wins |
| CVICO-03 conflicts with inventing preferred glossaries so terminology looks complete | Canonical terminology wins |
| CVICO-04 conflicts with mute complete badges or hidden ambiguity | Constitutional readability wins |
| CVICO-05 conflicts with ambient non-determinism or demo-only close rules | Repeatability wins |
| CVICO-06 conflicts with opaque complete badges | Explainability / auditability wins |
| Any CVICO conflicts with redefining terminology / reinterpreting meaning / amending specifications / creating authority | Non-legislative honesty wins — refuse the overclaim |

No supporting objective authorises violating CVICO-01.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — Constitution / EIP integrity obligations, Programme VI–X authority preservation, MS001 CVIO duties, and MS002 CVILO duties for the *completion* horizon.

---

## 5. Non-Objectives

Constitutional vocabulary integrity completion does **not** optimise for:

- redefining constitutional terminology or inventing preferred glossaries via close;
- reinterpreting Programme VI–X / EIP meaning during close;
- amending constitutional specifications under “completion” pretext;
- creating constitutional authority or soft-amending corpora via close;
- constitutional conformance disposition, fidelity scoring, or “assessed therefore conformant” theatre;
- verification success, compliance disposition, certification seals, or implementation-approval judgement;
- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- CI throughput, flaky-scan suppression, or demo continuity as educational or constitutional success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when required assessment duties are incomplete;
- collapsing CVIC criteria into one mega-flag that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics signals as substitutes for published completion criteria;
- treating thesaurus greens as optional commentary that silently becomes law;
- declaring terminology redefinition, meaning reinterpretation, specification amendment, or authority creation solely because completion was affirmed.

---

## 6. Closing Statement

> **A concern that becomes “vocabulary assessment complete” by redefining terminology, reinterpreting meaning, amending specifications, or creating constitutional authority is a failed educational system.  
> A concern that confirms only lawful assessment conclusion — with semantic consistency, canonical terminology, constitutional readability, repeatability, and explainability intact — is doing its only completion job.**
