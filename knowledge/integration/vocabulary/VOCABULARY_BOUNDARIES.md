## 1. Purpose

Assessment that silently becomes a second constitution — rewriting definitions in “findings,” inventing preferred glossaries, elevating Programme XI into an authorship layer, or treating a thesaurus preference as the new standard — destroys student trust and educational integrity.

This document draws a bright line between **lawful constitutional vocabulary integrity** (Programme XI / WS4 / MS001) and **constitutional authorship / educational judgement / terminology mutation** (Constitution / EIP / Programmes VI–XI / permitted writers).

---

## 2. Boundary Principles

1. **Validate, do not legislate.** Vocabulary integrity measures semantic consistency; it does not create law.
2. **Published terms only.** Unpublished glossaries and “main as dictionary” are hard stops as standards.
3. **Published vocabulary categories only.** Unpublished CVI / CVT customs are unlawful.
4. **Named corpus identities only.** Assessments without identifiable constitutional subjects are theatre.
5. **Exactly as published.** Successful intact status never rewrites specification meaning or term definitions.
6. **Authority first.** Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard.
7. **No terminology mutation.** Findings never create, redefine, invent, delete, or soft-amend constitutional terms.
8. **No meaning rewrite.** Educational and constitutional meanings remain with their owning corpora.
9. **No new authority.** Programme XI never becomes foundational law by validating others.
10. **No specification amendment.** Soft-amendment via findings destroys integrity.
11. **No reinterpretation.** Programme VI–X meanings and EIP claim classes survive assessment intact.
12. **Ambiguity honesty.** Inconsistencies are reported, not “fixed” in situ by redefinition.
13. **Intact ≠ quality.** Vocabulary integrity never grades learning, mastery, readiness, or product success.
14. **Implementation independence.** No stack, vendor, or scanner becomes law by proximity.
15. **No bypass.** Product urgency never waives constitutional vocabulary integrity requirements.
16. **Repeatability.** Race-dependent dispositions are a defect.
17. **Explain the stop.** Students, developers, and auditors should hear when assessment must refuse intact status.
18. **No emergency exemption.** Deadlines and demos never mint constitutional amendment power via findings.

## 3. What Vocabulary Integrity May Do (Lawful)

| Lawful action | Constitutional meaning |
|---------------|------------------------|
| **Analyse constitutional terminology** | Inspect published corpus identities, published terms, and published definitions under CVI-01…CVI-07 and CVT-01…CVT-10 |
| **Validate canonical term recognition** | Confirm published ownership and reconstructable identity under CVI-01 / CVIR-01 |
| **Validate semantic consistency** | Confirm one published meaning per term within lawful scope under CVI-02 / CVIR-02 |
| **Validate terminology reuse** | Confirm reuse honesty under CVI-03 / CVIR-03 |
| **Validate ambiguity avoidance** | Detect and classify undisambiguated incompatible readings under CVI-04 / CVIR-04 |
| **Validate definition stability** | Confirm published definitions remain as published under CVI-05 / CVIR-05 |
| **Validate cross-corpus coherence** | Confirm speakable separation of related senses under CVI-06 / CVIR-06 |
| **Identify semantic inconsistencies** | Name dual meanings, silent drift, undisambiguated ambiguity, or non-canonical glosses |
| **Report vocabulary findings** | Emit intact / violated / deferred / escalated under published criteria |
| **Preserve audit records** | Keep reconstructable trails under CVI-07 |
| **Refuse unlawful intact claims** | Prefer honest violated / deferred / escalated over improvisation |
| **Compose required categories** | Apply published CVI / CVT conjunctions without collapsing failed checks |
| **Remain repeatable and neutral** | Same published inputs → same disposition; no stack privilege |
| **Explain vocabulary integrity** | Answer terms / definitions / inconsistencies / findings / assessment limits without rewriting meaning |
| **Propose corpus amendments upstream** | Recommend (not enact) amendments when published terminology itself must change |

These actions **validate vocabulary consistency**. They do **not** publish a new Constitution, invent coach warrants, mint tips, author ownership maps, invent CVI / CVT types, amend programme law, redefine terminology, alter meaning, or judge educational quality.

| **Privilege a technology as constitutional** | Implementation neutrality | Evaluate terminology, not Flask / npm / spell-checker fashion |

## 6. Boundary Checks (Pre-Finding)

Before material constitutional vocabulary integrity is treated as established, assessment requires affirmative answers:

| Check | Question |
|-------|----------|
| **CVIB-01 Corpus identity** | Are assessed subjects published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–XI corpus identities (not unpublished glossaries)? |
| **CVIB-02 Integrity class** | Are required CVI-01…CVI-07 categories being applied (not unpublished customs)? |
| **CVIB-03 Term category** | Are assessed terms classified under CVT-01…CVT-10 as required? |
| **CVIB-04 Term identity** | Which named published terms are under assessment? |
| **CVIB-05 Canonical definition** | Which owning-corpus published definitions were applied? |
| **CVIB-06 Semantic consistency** | Do usages retain one published meaning within lawful scope (or cite disambiguation)? |
| **CVIB-07 Reuse honesty** | Does repeated use preserve canonical meaning or disclose published disambiguation? |
| **CVIB-08 Ambiguity** | Have incompatible readings without published disambiguation been identified and reported? |
| **CVIB-09 Definition stability** | Do published definitions remain as published across the assessment scope? |
| **CVIB-10 Non-legislation** | Does the assessment avoid creating constitutional authority or amending specifications? |
| **CVIB-11 Non-mutation** | Does the assessment avoid redefining, inventing, or deleting constitutional terminology? |
| **CVIB-12 Non-reinterpretation** | Does the assessment avoid redefining Programme VI–X / EIP meaning? |
| **CVIB-13 Non-substitution** | Does the assessment avoid treating Programme XI findings or any assessed corpus as the next constitutional standard? |
| **CVIB-14 Neutrality / intact ≠ quality** | Does the assessment avoid stack privilege and quality / student-readiness conflation? |
| **CVIB-15 Repeatability / audit** | Is disposition reproducible; will CVI-07 / explainability records preserve terms, definitions, inconsistencies, findings, and assessment limits? |

Any failed check → violated / deferred / escalated. Do not “best-effort” invent glossary law, redefine terms, or soft-amend specifications in situ.
