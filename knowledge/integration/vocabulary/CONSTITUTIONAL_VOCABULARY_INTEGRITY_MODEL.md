## 2. What Constitutional Vocabulary Integrity Is

**Constitutional Vocabulary Integrity** is the binding mechanism by which **semantic consistency of constitutional terminology** throughout the published constitutional corpus may be **defined, validated, and governed**, yielding **vocabulary integrity findings** that preserve canonical recognition, semantic consistency, honest reuse, ambiguity avoidance, definition stability, and explainability — without becoming a source of constitutional law, a glossary rewriter, or a substitute for upstream programme meaning.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Published constitutional corpus** | Constitution, EIP, Programme VI–XI specifications and their subordinate workstream / milestone corpora | What corpus is under vocabulary assessment? |
| **Constitutional term** | A published word or phrase that carries constitutional meaning within an owning corpus | Which term is being assessed? |
| **Canonical definition** | The published meaning statement for a term in its owning corpus — never an invented gloss | Which definition binds this term? |
| **Vocabulary term category** | A recognised CVT-01…CVT-10 grouping of constitutional terminology roles for integrity assessment | Which term category contains this usage? |
| **Semantic consistency** | The same term retains one published meaning within its lawful scope; incompatible meanings without published disambiguation are defects | Does the term mean one thing as published? |
| **Terminology reuse** | Repeated use of a published term that preserves its canonical meaning (or cites published disambiguation) | Is reuse honest? |
| **Ambiguity** | A term usage that permits incompatible constitutional readings without published disambiguation | Was ambiguity avoided or disclosed? |
| **Definition stability** | Published definitions remain as published; silent drift or soft redefinition is a defect | Is the definition intact? |
| **Vocabulary integrity finding** | Lawful disposition: intact / violated / deferred / escalated — never “new law” and never a glossary rewrite | Does vocabulary remain lawful? |
| **Constitutional authority** | The exclusive right of published corpora to define educational and execution meaning | Who owns the standard? |
| **Implementation neutrality** | Vocabulary validation speaks to constitutional identities, not to a preferred stack as law | Remains the stack replaceable? |
| **Auditability** | Every material assessment leaves reconstructable constitutional traces | Can the judgement be reconstructed? |
| **Explainability** | Terms, definitions, inconsistencies, findings, and assessment limits are speakable | Can the judgement be narrated honestly? |

Constitutional Vocabulary Integrity is:

- **validation-centred** — it validates published terminology; it never authors or redefines it;
- **canonical-bound** — only published terms with reconstructable owning corpora may certify intact status;
- **semantics-preserving** — the same term retains one published meaning within its lawful scope;
- **reuse-honest** — repeated use preserves canonical meaning or cites published disambiguation;
- **ambiguity-resistant** — incompatible readings without published disambiguation are hard defects;
- **definition-stable** — published definitions remain as published; silent drift is forbidden;
- **catalogue-closed** — only CVI-01…CVI-07 may be applied as constitutional vocabulary integrity categories;
- **term-category-bound** — material assessments classify under recognised CVT-01…CVT-10 term categories;
- **corpus-bound** — every material assessment cites identifiable published specifications and term / definition identities;
- **finding-producing** — assessments emit intact / violated / deferred / escalated dispositions;
- **audit-capable** — every material assessment leaves reconstructable constitutional traces;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which terms were assessed under which definitions with which findings;
- **non-legislative** — a vocabulary finding never becomes constitutional law or a soft amendment;
- **non-mutating** — the model never modifies constitutional specifications or terminology under validation pretext;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by vocabulary law.

Constitutional Vocabulary Integrity is **not**:

- a second Educational Constitution, EIP, or Programme VI–X amendment path;
- a licence to reinterpret coach meaning, ownership maps, tip warrants, runtime contracts, conformance criteria, verification meaning, compliance meaning, certification meaning, evolution meaning, execution meaning, dependency meaning, catalogue meaning, or boundary meaning in vocabulary prose;
- a testing framework, CI/CD pipeline, GitHub Action, spell-checker, thesaurus, NLP product, or OpenAPI linker;
- a claim that “vocabulary intact” guarantees learning, mastery, exam readiness, product success, perpetual conformity, or frozen stack authority;
- a substitute for Programme IX MS002 constitutional traceability — CT preserves spec/artefact/finding lineage; CVI validates *constitutional vocabulary semantic integrity*;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS1 dependency integrity — CDI validates *corpus dependency structure*; CVI validates *terminology semantics*;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS2 catalogue integrity — CCI validates *catalogue uniqueness and namespace honesty*; CVI validates *term meaning consistency* (IDs and meanings compose; they do not conflate);
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS3 boundary integrity — CBI validates *boundary consistency*; CVI validates *vocabulary semantic stability*;
- a substitute for Programme IX conformance / verification / compliance / certification — those evaluate implementations and obligations; CVI validates *vocabulary corpus consistency*;
- a substitute for Programme X execution / decision / audit / explainability — those organise constitutional activity; CVI never executes, decides, audits, or explains educational outcomes in their place;
- a licence to create, redefine, invent, or delete constitutional terminology under “integrity” pretext.

## 3. Vocabulary Integrity Posture (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material constitutional vocabulary integrity assessment travels through three constitutional phases. These are **normative assessment obligations**, not an implementation architecture.

```
Identify published terms and term categories under assessment
        │
        ▼
Validate canonical recognition, semantic consistency, reuse,
ambiguity avoidance, and definition stability under CVI-01…CVI-07
        │
        ├── violated / deferred / escalated (lawful finding — terminology unchanged)
        │
        └── intact → Emit findings and preserve audit records
                │
                ▼
        Explain terms / definitions / inconsistencies / findings / assessment limits
```

| Phase | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **Identify** | Name only published constitutional corpus identities, published terms, and recognised CVT categories | Term is unpublished tribal jargon, invented gloss, or “how main speaks today” as law |
| **Validate** | Apply CVI-01…CVI-07 for canonical recognition, semantic consistency, reuse, ambiguity avoidance, and definition stability | Assessment would invent law, redefine terms, soft-amend definitions, or privilege a stack as law |
| **Record** | Produce findings and audit-speakable trails without amending corpora or mutating terminology | Path would treat findings as constitutional amendments or soft redefinitions of published terms |

> **Vocabulary integrity never “best-effort invents” a fourth phase called law-making, glossary rewriting, or stack canonisation.**

## 6. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Vocabulary Integrity Model exists so that:

1. **Canonical terminology remains recognised** — assessments speak only published terms with reconstructable owning corpora.
2. **Semantic consistency remains honest** — the same term continues to mean what its owning corpus published.
3. **Reuse remains faithful** — repeated use preserves canonical meaning or cites published disambiguation.
4. **Ambiguity remains avoided or disclosed** — incompatible readings are not silently tolerated.
5. **Definitions remain stable** — published meanings are not quietly drifted to paper over readiness theatre.
6. **Constitutional readability remains intact** — students, developers, and auditors can reconstruct what terms mean across Programmes VI–XI.
7. **Students remain protected** — product urgency cannot launder contradictory vocabulary as “approved architecture.”
