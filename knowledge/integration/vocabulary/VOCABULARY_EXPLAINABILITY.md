## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which terms* were checked — which canonical definitions were applied, whether semantic inconsistencies were found, what finding resulted, and which assessment limits were preserved — is not coaching; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–X have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, runtime, governance, and execution speech speakable, and after Programme XI / WS1–WS3 have made dependency, catalogue, and boundary speech speakable, Programme XI still needs one vocabulary-speech answer:

> **“Which constitutional terms were assessed, which canonical definitions were applied, were any semantic inconsistencies identified, which findings were produced, and which assessment limits were preserved?”**

That answer must cover every material vocabulary integrity assessment without inventing educational meaning, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, redefining terminology, freezing a stack as law, inventing unpublished CVI / CVT types, or presenting intact status as educational quality or student readiness.

## 2. What Vocabulary Explainability Is

**Constitutional Vocabulary Integrity Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **validation of named constitutional terms under published CVI categories and CVT classifications** — not to invent a second tutor voice from thesaurus scanners, and not to grade the student.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Terms assessed** | Named published terms and CVI-01…CVI-07 / CVT-01…CVT-10 classifications under validation | Which constitutional terms were assessed? |
| **Canonical definitions applied** | Named owning-corpus published meaning statements applied to those terms | Which canonical definitions were applied? |
| **Semantic inconsistencies identified** | Dual meanings, silent drift, undisambiguated ambiguity, or non-canonical glosses found (or confirmed absent) | Were any semantic inconsistencies identified? |
| **Integrity findings** | intact / violated / deferred / escalated (with composition notes) | Which findings were produced? |
| **Assessment limits preserved** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden terminology redefinition / meaning alteration / specification amendment / authority creation did not occur | Which assessment limits were preserved? |

Vocabulary Explainability is:

- **validation-centred** — it narrates terms → definitions → inconsistencies → findings under law;
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CVI / CVT identities;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published specifications and term / definition statements;
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to amend corpora, redefine terms, or invent glossaries;
- **quality-honest** — intact status is never narrated as mastery, readiness, or educational quality;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference or scanner green as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence speech, Programme IX governance speech, Programme X execution / decision / audit / explanation speech, Programme XI / WS1 dependency speech, Programme XI / WS2 catalogue speech, or Programme XI / WS3 boundary speech;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match CVI-07 reconstruction needs;
- **repeatability-honest** — explanations must not paper over race-dependent dispositions.

Vocabulary Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–XI meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a substitute for Programme IX CT explainability, Programme XI / WS1–WS3 assurance explainability, or Programme X explainability architecture corpora;
- a rendering system, UI template, CI badge, log shipper, thesaurus product, NLP dashboard, or analytics dashboard;
- a claim that clear vocabulary speech guarantees learning or a pass;
- a licence to present scanner greens, thesaurus consistency, or pipeline badges as constitutional proof or educational quality;
- a licence to narrate terminology redefinition as “integrity repairs.”

## 3. Mandatory Explanation Questions

Every material constitutional vocabulary integrity assessment should answer all five questions:

### CVIEQ-01 — Which constitutional terms were assessed?

Name the primary published terms and the CVI-01…CVI-07 categories / CVT-01…CVT-10 classifications under validation (for example “authority” under CVT-01 / CVI-02 across Programme VII ownership corpora; “validation” under CVT-06 / CVI-04 across EV vs CVI senses; “assessment” under CVT-10 / CVI-04 separating educational vs integrity assessment). Unpublished tribal jargon and “neighbouring folder labels” are not constitutional terms.

### CVIEQ-02 — Which canonical definitions were applied?

Name the reconstructable owning-corpus published meaning statements (and relevant CVIR-01…CVIR-10 rules) applied to the assessment. Missing definition identity is a defect to disclose — not a gap to invent. “Supported by ticket #1234,” “supported by thesaurus,” or “supported by NLP similarity” is not a canonical constitutional definition.

### CVIEQ-03 — Were any semantic inconsistencies identified?

Name inconsistencies under review and their disposition:

- **None identified (within scope)** — usages retain published meaning (or cite published disambiguation) for the assessment scope.
- **Dual meaning** — the same term carries incompatible constitutional senses without published disambiguation; name the usages and owners.
- **Silent drift** — published definition and later usages diverge without amendment; name the statements.
- **Undisambiguated ambiguity** — incompatible readings remain possible; name the collision.
- **Non-canonical gloss** — unpublished glossary treated as law; name the gloss and why it fails CVIR-01.
- **Incomplete** — definition or ownership missing; disposition must be deferred / escalated, not silently intact.

Peer composition that is published as lawful multi-sense disambiguation is not automatically an inconsistency; undeclared dual exclusive meanings are.

### CVIEQ-04 — Which findings were produced?

Distinguish:

- **intact** — published terminology satisfies applicable CVI categories and CVIR rules for the assessment scope;
- **violated** — canonical recognition, semantic consistency, reuse, ambiguity, definition stability, coherence, or catalogue rules failed;
- **deferred** — lawful pause pending identifiable missing published inputs;
- **escalated** — requires corpus governance / human constitutional review beyond ordinary assessment.

Composition notes must name which CVI-01…CVI-07 categories (and CVT classifications) bound the finding. “Vocabulary looks fine” is not a finding.

### CVIEQ-05 — Which assessment limits were preserved?

Confirm explicitly that the assessment did **not**:

- redefine constitutional terminology or create new constitutional law;
- alter constitutional meaning or invent preferred glossaries as law;
- amend constitutional specifications;
- substitute Programme XI or any assessed corpus for upstream authority;
- narrate intact status as educational quality, mastery, or student exam readiness;
- privilege a technology, spell-checker, NLP product, or CI vendor as constitutional law.

Failed assessment-limit confirmation → the explanation is constitutionally defective even if a disposition string exists.

## 4. Audience Projections (One Truth)

| Audience | Vocabulary focus | Must still include |
|----------|------------------|--------------------|
| **Student-facing** | Plain language that the study system’s rules still use shared, stable words for who-decides and what-was-checked, without secretly swapping meanings | CVIEQ-01…CVIEQ-05 truth; no mastery / pass claims from intact status |
| **Developer-facing** | Corpus paths, CVI / CVT IDs, definition citations, inconsistency dispositions, finding disposition | Same constitutional truth; no stack-as-law narration |
| **Auditor-facing** | CVIR rule IDs, CVI-07 record identity, vocabulary checklist (CVIB), assessment-limit confirmation | Reconstructability; continuity with prior findings |

Projections may simplify vocabulary. They may **not** simplify away definition identity, inconsistency status, or assessment-limit failures.

## 5. Explanation Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why defective | Lawful alternative |
|--------------|---------------|--------------------|
| “Vocabulary is fine because CI is green” | Proxy theatre | Cite CVI / CVT categories, definitions, and findings |
| “We fixed integrity by redefining the term” | Mutation under explanation pretext | Report inconsistency; propose upstream amendment |
| “Intact means the student is ready” | Quality conflation | Keep vocabulary integrity distinct from student readiness |
| “Definition is the Jira epic” | Non-constitutional origin | Cite Constitution / EIP / programme paths and CVIR IDs |
| “No conflicts in thesaurus / NLP output” | Wrong subject horizon | Map to published definitions or refuse |
| “Programme XI now defines Programme VI terms” | Authority / meaning substitution | Preserve VI meaning; XI validates only |
| “Temporary gloss for the demo” | Emergency exemption | Deferred / escalated / violated — never demo law |
| “Trust us — the words are obvious” | Opacity | Answer CVIEQ-01…CVIEQ-05 explicitly |
| “We merged those senses to make integration simpler” | Semantic rewrite | Report dual meaning as violated; propose upstream amendment |
