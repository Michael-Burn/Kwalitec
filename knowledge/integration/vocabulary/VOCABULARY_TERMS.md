## 1. Purpose

Vocabulary integrity without recognised term categories invents law by proximity: whichever everyday English word a document happens to use becomes the tutor’s “proof the constitution speaks one language.”

This catalogue names the only lawful constitutional vocabulary term categories an assessment may classify under — and the only vocabulary integrity categories an assessment may apply — and binds each to purpose, scope, recognition rules, inputs, outputs, validation rules, and permitted / prohibited behaviours.

**Hard constraint:** This document never redefines constitutional terminology, never reinterprets constitutional meaning, never amends constitutional specifications, and never creates constitutional authority.

## 2. Recognised Vocabulary Term Categories (CVT-01…CVT-10)

| ID | Term category | Constitutional role (short) | Illustrative published usages (non-exhaustive) |
|----|---------------|-----------------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| **CVT-01** | Authority | Who may define, own, or decide under published law | Constitutional authority; ownership; defining rights; authority chains |
| **CVT-02** | Responsibility | Duty allocation and non-absorption across horizons | Responsibility boundaries; duty owners; non-responsibility statements |
| **CVT-03** | Governance | How conformity, lineage, verification, compliance, certification, and evolution are spoken | Conformance; traceability; verification; compliance; certification; evolution |
| **CVT-04** | Interpretation | How published law may be read without inventing rival meaning | EIP interpretation principles; claim honesty; non-reinterpretation limits |
| **CVT-05** | Execution | How constitutional activity is prepared and run | Execution context; execution engine; runtime execution of published law |
| **CVT-06** | Validation | How published artefacts / structures are checked for lawful eligibility or integrity | Evidence validation (EV); integrity validation (CDI / CCI / CBI / CVI); never silent merge of these senses |
| **CVT-07** | Audit | How decisions and assessments leave reconstructable records | Constitutional audit; audit records; auditability obligations |
| **CVT-08** | Explainability | How constitutional judgements are narrated honestly | EIP-003; programme explainability corpora; EQ families |
| **CVT-09** | Completion | When a constitutional activity may be affirmed as lawfully finished | Completion models; completion criteria; completion boundaries |
| **CVT-10** | Assessment | Evaluative / integrity / quality judgements and their distinct scopes | Vocabulary / boundary / catalogue / dependency assessment; educational assessment; never silent merge |

Material assessments must classify every assessed term under one or more of these categories as published law requires. Cross-cutting situations may bind multiple categories; none may invent a category outside this catalogue.

**Relation to published definitions:** Owning corpora alone define what each term means. CVT membership *classifies term usages for integrity assessment*; it does not replace, redefine, or invent those meanings.

**Relation to educational quality:** No CVT category judges whether learning was good, whether a tip was wise, or whether the student is ready. Intact ≠ quality. Intact ≠ conformant. Intact ≠ certified.

### 2.1 How canonical terms are recognised

A term is **canonically recognised** for vocabulary integrity assessment only when all of the following hold:

1. **Published ownership** — an identifiable Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–XI corpus (or lawful subordinate workstream / milestone corpus) owns the term’s meaning statement.
2. **Named identity** — the assessment can cite the specification path (and, where applicable, catalogue ID) that publishes the term.
3. **CVT classification** — the usage is classified under one or more CVT-01…CVT-10 categories required by the assessment scope.
4. **No invent-in-place** — tribal jargon, ticket slang, and “preferred synonyms” without published ownership are non-canonical and may not certify intact status.

Recognition classifies; it never creates. Missing ownership → deferred / escalated (or violated if a non-canonical term is treated as law).

### 2.2 How semantic consistency is evaluated across the corpus

Semantic consistency evaluation compares **published meaning statements** for the same term (or lawfully equivalent published synonym declared as such) across the assessment scope:

| Evaluation step | Constitutional obligation |
|-----------------|---------------------------|
| **Scope naming** | Name the published corpora and terms under assessment |
| **Definition binding** | Cite the owning corpus’s published definition for each term |
| **Usage sampling** | Identify material usages of the term across the scoped corpus |
| **Consistency test** | Confirm usages retain one published meaning within lawful scope, or cite published disambiguation |
| **Inconsistency classification** | Report dual meanings, silent drift, or undisambiguated ambiguity as violated / deferred / escalated |
| **Finding emission** | Emit intact / violated / deferred / escalated under CVI-01…CVI-07 — never rewrite definitions |

Cross-programme reuse of ordinary English (e.g. “validation”, “assessment”, “authority”) is **not** automatically inconsistent when published ownership and disambiguation separate the senses. It **is** inconsistent when the same usage context permits incompatible constitutional readings without published separation.

## 4. Global Validation Rules

These rules apply to every CVT category and every CVI category:

| Rule ID | Validation rule |
|---------|-----------------|
| **CVIR-01 Canonical ownership** | Every material term under assessment must reconstruct to a published owning corpus. Unpublished glossaries used as law are defects. |
| **CVIR-02 Semantic consistency** | Within lawful scope, a term must retain one published meaning. Drift without amendment is a defect. |
| **CVIR-03 Reuse honesty** | Repeated use must preserve canonical meaning or cite published disambiguation. Silent dual meanings are defects. |
| **CVIR-04 Ambiguity honesty** | Detected incompatible readings must be reported as collisions, published disambiguations, or violated — never silently ignored or “fixed” by redefinition in situ. |
| **CVIR-05 Definition fidelity** | Published definitions remain as published. Silent soft-redefinition is a defect. |
| **CVIR-06 Cross-corpus coherence** | Related terms across Programmes VI–XI must remain speakably separated by ownership; silent merges of distinct senses are defects. |
| **CVIR-07 Catalogue / category closure** | Only CVT-01…CVT-10 may classify term categories and only CVI-01…CVI-07 may classify integrity assessments. Unpublished customs are unlawful. |
| **CVIR-08 Non-mutation** | Validation never creates, redefines, invents, deletes, or soft-amends constitutional terminology or specifications. Findings report; they do not rewrite. |
| **CVIR-09 Neutrality** | Validation criteria speak to constitutional identities and published terminology — never “must use Flask / SQLAlchemy / spell-checker / GitHub Actions.” |
| **CVIR-10 Finding honesty** | Dispositions are intact / violated / deferred / escalated only. “Close enough,” “temporary gloss,” or “demo exception” are not constitutional dispositions. |

## 5. Term Category Profiles

### 5.1 CVT-01 — Authority

**Purpose.** Classify terminology about who may define, own, or decide so authority speech remains reconstructable and unabsorbed by foreign horizons.

**Recognition expectations.** Authority terms cite Constitution / EIP / Programme VII ownership corpora (and related authority maps) as published. Programme XI may *validate* authority vocabulary; it may not *become* foundational authority by naming it.

**Permitted.** Confirm consistent use of “constitutional authority,” ownership, and defining-right speech; report dual meanings.

**Prohibited.** Redefine authority; elevate architecture / Version 2 docs to foundational authority peers as law via vocabulary findings.

---

### 5.2 CVT-02 — Responsibility

**Purpose.** Classify duty-allocation terminology so responsibility speech remains separated as published.

**Recognition expectations.** Responsibility terms reconstruct to published responsibility / non-responsibility statements (including `*_BOUNDARIES.md` duty maps).

**Permitted.** Validate consistent duty vocabulary; report absorption disguised as synonym theatre.

**Prohibited.** Rewrite duty owners; invent rival responsibility glosses under integrity pretext.

---

### 5.3 CVT-03 — Governance

**Purpose.** Classify conformance, traceability, verification, compliance, certification, and evolution terminology so governance senses remain separated from educational / runtime / assurance senses.

**Recognition expectations.** Governance terms reconstruct to Programme IX owning corpora. “Verification” under CVT-03 is not Programme XI vocabulary validation.

**Permitted.** Confirm consistent governance vocabulary; report collisions with assurance / educational senses.

**Prohibited.** Collapse Programme IX “verification” into CVI “validation”; renumber or redefine Programme IX catalogues via vocabulary findings.

---

### 5.4 CVT-04 — Interpretation

**Purpose.** Classify terminology about how published law may be read — so interpretation speech never becomes a back door to rival meaning.

**Recognition expectations.** Interpretation terms reconstruct to EIP and published non-reinterpretation limits.

**Permitted.** Validate that “interpretation” usages honour EIP claim honesty and non-reinterpretation.

**Prohibited.** Reinterpret constitutional meaning under “vocabulary clarification” pretext.

---

### 5.5 CVT-05 — Execution

**Purpose.** Classify terminology about preparing and running constitutional activity so execution speech remains distinct from educational meaning and from Programme XI assurance.

**Recognition expectations.** Execution terms reconstruct to Programme VIII / X owning corpora as published.

**Permitted.** Confirm consistent execution vocabulary; report silent merges with educational “execution of a study plan” where published law separates them.

**Prohibited.** Author runtime behaviour; treat vocabulary findings as execution law.

---

### 5.6 CVT-06 — Validation

**Purpose.** Classify “validation” speech across evidence eligibility, integrity assurance, and related checks — so distinct validation senses remain speakable.

**Recognition expectations.** Validation terms must name their owning sense (e.g. EV evidence validation vs CDI / CCI / CBI / CVI integrity validation). Undisambiguated “validated” as mega-proof is a defect when senses collide.

**Permitted.** Require disambiguation among EV / CDI / CCI / CBI / CVI and peers; report silent merges.

**Prohibited.** Treat CVI intact status as Programme VIII evidence eligibility or Programme IX verification.

---

### 5.7 CVT-07 — Audit

**Purpose.** Classify audit / auditability terminology so record-keeping speech remains distinct from educational quality and from CI log theatre.

**Recognition expectations.** Audit terms reconstruct to Programme X audit corpora and published auditability obligations across programmes.

**Permitted.** Confirm consistent audit vocabulary; report conflation of “CI log” with constitutional audit.

**Prohibited.** Invent rival audit models; present scanner logs as constitutional audits under vocabulary pretext.

---

### 5.8 CVT-08 — Explainability

**Purpose.** Classify explainability terminology so narration obligations remain honest and non-legislative.

**Recognition expectations.** Explainability terms reconstruct to EIP-003 and programme explainability corpora (including EQ families).

**Permitted.** Confirm consistent explainability vocabulary; report conflation with marketing copy.

**Prohibited.** Let explanation prose redefine terms; treat explainability speech as new constitutional law.

---

### 5.9 CVT-09 — Completion

**Purpose.** Classify completion terminology so “finished” senses remain bound to published completion models and criteria.

**Recognition expectations.** Completion terms reconstruct to published completion corpora (programme completion models, criteria, boundaries).

**Permitted.** Confirm consistent completion vocabulary; report silent merges of educational completion with constitutional completion.

**Prohibited.** Invent completion criteria via vocabulary findings; treat CVI intact as educational or product completion.

---

### 5.10 CVT-10 — Assessment

**Purpose.** Classify assessment terminology so evaluative / integrity / educational assessment senses remain separated.

**Recognition expectations.** Assessment terms must name their owning sense (e.g. constitutional vocabulary assessment vs student educational assessment vs Educational Validation Framework quality assessment).

**Permitted.** Require disambiguation; report undisambiguated “assessment” mega-usage as ambiguity under CVI-04.

**Prohibited.** Treat vocabulary assessment findings as student mastery judgements or quality release decisions.

## 6. Integrity Category Profiles

### 6.1 CVI-01 — Canonical Term Recognition

**Purpose.** Confirm that terms under assessment are published, owned, and reconstructable.

**Inputs.** Named terms; owning corpus paths; CVT classification.  
**Outputs.** Recognition findings (recognised / non-canonical / incomplete).  
**Prohibited.** Inventing ownership; minting new terms to fill gaps.

### 6.2 CVI-02 — Semantic Consistency

**Purpose.** Confirm one published meaning per term within lawful scope.

**Inputs.** Published definitions; material usages.  
**Outputs.** Consistency findings.  
**Prohibited.** Soft-merging incompatible meanings so reports pass.

### 6.3 CVI-03 — Terminology Reuse Integrity

**Purpose.** Confirm repeated use preserves canonical meaning or cites published disambiguation.

**Inputs.** Reuse instances; canonical definitions; disambiguation tables.  
**Outputs.** Reuse findings.  
**Prohibited.** Declaring reuse “fine” because the same string appears often.

### 6.4 CVI-04 — Ambiguity Avoidance

**Purpose.** Detect and classify undisambiguated incompatible readings.

**Inputs.** Ambiguous usages; published disambiguation.  
**Outputs.** Ambiguity / collision findings.  
**Prohibited.** Silent ignore; in-situ redefinition as a “fix.”

### 6.5 CVI-05 — Definition Stability

**Purpose.** Confirm published definitions remain as published across the assessment scope.

**Inputs.** Published meaning statements across scoped corpora / versions as published law requires.  
**Outputs.** Stability findings.  
**Prohibited.** Quiet gloss swaps; demo-only temporary definitions.

### 6.6 CVI-06 — Cross-corpus Vocabulary Coherence

**Purpose.** Confirm related terms remain speakably coherent across Programme VI–XI without silent merges of distinct senses.

**Inputs.** Related term sets; ownership maps; CVT membership.  
**Outputs.** Coherence findings.  
**Prohibited.** Collapsing programme horizons into one mega-vocabulary under “readability” pretext.

### 6.7 CVI-07 — Assessment Integrity

**Purpose.** Preserve reconstructable vocabulary assessment records for audit and explainability.

**Inputs.** Assessment records; term / definition identities; dispositions.  
**Outputs.** Assessment-lineage / integrity records.  
**Prohibited.** Opaque badges; race-dependent dispositions; erasing prior lawful findings.
