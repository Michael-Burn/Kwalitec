## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which constitutional terms* were assessed — which canonical definitions were applied, which semantic validations were completed, which findings were produced, and which limits remained intact — is not coaching; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–X have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, runtime, governance, and execution speech speakable, after Programme XI / WS1–WS3 have made dependency / catalogue / boundary integrity speech speakable, and after WS4 / MS001 has made vocabulary integrity speech speakable, Programme XI still needs one lifecycle-speech answer:

> **“Which constitutional terms were assessed, which canonical definitions were applied, which semantic validations were completed, which findings were produced, and which constitutional boundaries remained intact?”**

That answer must cover every material vocabulary integrity lifecycle without inventing educational meaning, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, redefining terminology, creating constitutional authority, freezing a stack as law, inventing unpublished CVI / CVT / CVIL types, replacing MS001 validation meaning, or presenting publication as educational quality.

## 2. What Lifecycle Explainability Is

**Constitutional Vocabulary Integrity Lifecycle Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **coordination of named vocabulary assessments through published CVIL stages** — discovering published terms under CVT categories, validating canonical definitions, validating semantic consistency and terminology reuse, validating ambiguity avoidance, validating definition stability, applying published CVI types, and producing / publishing findings — not to invent a second tutor voice from spell-checkers, not to grade the student, and not to replace constitutional authority.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Terms assessed** | Named published terms and CVT-01…CVT-10 classifications under assessment | Which constitutional terms were assessed? |
| **Canonical definitions applied** | Reconstructable owning-corpus published meaning statements applied at CVIL-03 | Which canonical definitions were applied? |
| **Semantic validations completed** | Canonical / consistency / reuse / ambiguity / stability validations under CVI-01…CVI-06 across CVIL-03…CVIL-06 | Which semantic validations were completed? |
| **Findings produced** | intact / violated / deferred / escalated (with stage binding and composition notes) | Which findings were produced? |
| **Boundaries remaining intact** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden terminology redefinition / meaning reinterpretation / legislation / authority-creation / substitution did not occur | Which constitutional boundaries remained intact? |

Lifecycle Explainability is:

- **coordination-centred** — it narrates terms → definitions → semantic validations → findings under law;
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CVIL, CVI, and CVT identities;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published corpora and terms;
- **discovery-honest** — every material justification cites identifiable published terms / categories assessed (or names the hard stop when none exist);
- **definition-honest** — every material justification cites applied canonical definitions (or names the hard stop when unmet);
- **semantics-honest** — every material justification cites consistency / reuse / ambiguity / stability validation without hiding or “fixing” defects by soft-redefinition;
- **finding-honest** — every material finding is bound to the producing lifecycle stages;
- **record-honest** — every material lifecycle cites which assessment records were preserved / published;
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to amend corpora;
- **non-authoritative** — publication is never narrated as a freeze of stack authority or a substitute for published corpora;
- **quality-honest** — intact status is never narrated as mastery, readiness theatre, or educational quality;
- **mutation-honest** — explanation never hides terminology redefinition performed “so the lifecycle could proceed”;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence / validation speech, Programme IX governance speech, Programme X activity speech, WS1–WS3 integrity speech, or WS4 / MS001 vocabulary integrity speech;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match CVILO-05 reconstruction needs;
- **repeatability-honest** — explanations must not paper over race-dependent dispositions or private stage shortcuts.

Lifecycle Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–X / WS1–WS3 / WS4 / MS001 meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a substitute for CVI findings speech, CT lineage speech, CDI / CCI / CBI / CDIL / CCIL / CBIL speech, or Programme IX–X evaluative / execution speech;
- a CI status page, coverage report, spell-checker summary, thesaurus summary, NLP similarity report, or linter summary presented as constitutional coordination;
- a freeze of implementations, stacks, vendors, or releases as constitutional law;
- a licence to narrate invented terms, invented definitions, or soft-redefined meanings as if they were lawful inputs.

## 3. Mandatory Explainability Questions

Every material constitutional vocabulary integrity lifecycle must be able to answer:

### CVILEQ-01 — Which constitutional terms were assessed?

**Required content**

- Named published term identities and CVT-01…CVT-10 category memberships discovered at CVIL-02
- Binding between term identities and intended / applied CVI types
- Confirmation that unpublished customs, invented glossaries, tribal jargon, or scanner noise were not used as constitutional terms

**Defect if**

- “Terms are fine” with no term or category references
- Anonymous “the docs” or “the glossary” without identifiable published terms / categories
- Terms invented, renamed, redefined, or deleted as assessment without disclosure and hard stop

---

### CVILEQ-02 — Which canonical definitions were applied?

**Required content**

- Named owning-corpus published meaning statements confirmed at CVIL-03 (and relevant CVIR-01…CVIR-10 rules)
- Explicit refuse-validate-definition disposition when ownership or definition identity was unmet
- Confirmation that definitions or ownership claims were not invented or silently skipped
- Specification path (and, where applicable, catalogue ID) for each material definition

**Defect if**

- “Definitions are obvious” with no owning-corpus references
- “Supported by ticket #1234,” “supported by thesaurus,” or “supported by NLP similarity” as the sole definition identity
- Definitions invented, soft-amended, or silently skipped to force later intact status

---

### CVILEQ-03 — Which semantic validations were completed?

**Required content**

- Named validations completed across CVIL-03…CVIL-06 under CVI-01…CVI-06 (canonical recognition, semantic consistency, terminology reuse, ambiguity avoidance, definition stability, and cross-corpus coherence where material)
- Explicit refuse / violated / deferred / escalated dispositions when any required validation was unmet
- Confirmation that consistency, reuse, ambiguity, or stability defects were not “fixed” by soft-redefining terms in situ
- Collision / ambiguity status where material (none detected / detected / inconclusive — with named conflicting usages when detected)

**Defect if**

- “Semantics look fine” with no CVI / CVIL stage binding
- Silent stage skips that claim findings without CVIL-03…CVIL-06 honesty
- Soft-redefinition or invented disambiguation presented as validation completion

---

### CVILEQ-04 — Which findings were produced?

**Required content**

- Explicit disposition(s): intact / violated / deferred / escalated
- Binding to the producing CVIL stages (especially CVIL-03…CVIL-07) and applied CVI-01…CVI-07 / CVT-01…CVT-10 identities
- Composition notes when multiple CVI types or CVT categories bound the finding
- Confirmation that findings were not rewritten to match product urgency

**Defect if**

- “Vocabulary looks fine” with no disposition vocabulary
- Collapse of multi-type failures into a single false “intact”
- Findings narrated as new law, glossary rewrites, mastery claims, or frozen stack authority

---

### CVILEQ-05 — Which constitutional boundaries remained intact?

**Required content**

- Explicit confirmation that forbidden terminology redefinition, meaning reinterpretation, specification amendment, and authority creation did not occur
- Confirmation of non-substitution (lifecycle machinery did not become the next constitutional standard)
- Confirmation of non-MS001-replacement (publication did not rewrite CVI / CVT meaning)
- Reference to relevant CVILB-01…CVILB-19 checks where material

**Defect if**

- Missing boundary confirmation when publication is claimed
- Publication that hides breached boundaries
- Narration that treats lifecycle progress as constitutional authority or soft amendment

## 4. Audience Projections

| Audience | Projection style | Must preserve |
|----------|------------------|---------------|
| **Student-facing** | Plain language that the study system’s rules still use shared, stable words for who-decides and what-was-checked, without secretly swapping meanings | CVILEQ-01…CVILEQ-05 truth; no mastery / pass claims from intact status |
| **Developer-facing** | Named terms, CVT / CVI / CVIL IDs, definition paths, stage dispositions, and refuse reasons | Same constitutional facts as auditor speech |
| **Auditor-facing** | Full reconstruction: CVILEQ-01…CVILEQ-05 answers, stage traces, boundary checks (CVILB), CVI-07 record identity | Continuity of prior lawful records |

Student speech may omit catalogue IDs; it may not omit truth. Developer and auditor speech share one reconstructable trail.

## 5. Forbidden Explanation Patterns

| Pattern | Defect | Lawful alternative |
|---------|--------|--------------------|
| “Trust us — the words are obvious” | Opacity | Answer CVILEQ-01…CVILEQ-05 explicitly |
| “Spell-check / thesaurus / NLP passed” | Proxy as law | Cite published terms, definitions, CVI types, and CVIL stages |
| “We redefined the term so the report is green” | Soft amendment | Report violated / propose upstream amendment |
| “Lifecycle complete = student ready” | Quality conflation | Keep coordination speech distinct from readiness speech |
| “Publication freezes this stack as law” | Authority creation | Publish findings; keep implementations replaceable |
| “Missing definition — invent a gloss” | Invent-in-place | Refuse-validate-definition / deferred / escalated |
| Silent stage skip with claimed findings | Skip discipline | Record refuse / fail dispositions honestly through CVIL-07 / CVIL-08 |

## 6. Reconstruction Contract

A material constitutional vocabulary integrity lifecycle is explainably reconstructable only when auditors can recover:

1. Terms assessed (CVILEQ-01)
2. Canonical definitions applied (CVILEQ-02)
3. Semantic validations completed (CVILEQ-03)
4. Findings produced and records / publication artefacts preserved (CVILEQ-04)
5. Constitutional boundaries / limits remaining intact (CVILEQ-05)

Missing any material element is an explainability defect under CVILO-05.
