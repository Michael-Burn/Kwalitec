## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “scanner finished,” “stages skipped for speed,” or “glossary badge shown.” The tutor optimises **honest organisation of vocabulary assessment work**: only a repeatable, consistent, implementation-neutral, explainable, and auditable sequence — discovering published terms under CVT-01…CVT-10 and validating canonical recognition, semantic consistency, reuse honesty, ambiguity avoidance, and definition stability under CVI-01…CVI-07 — may support lawful vocabulary integrity findings.

These objectives bind every constitutional vocabulary integrity lifecycle and every successor coordination machinery that claims constitutional vocabulary integrity lifecycle meaning.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### CVILO-01 — Preserve semantic consistency

**Definition.** Ensure that every material vocabulary integrity lifecycle organises assessments through recognised CVIL-01…CVIL-08 stages — initiating against identifiable published corpora, discovering named terms under CVT-01…CVT-10 categories, validating canonical definitions, validating semantic consistency and terminology reuse, validating ambiguity avoidance, validating definition stability, and constructing / publishing findings under published CVI-01…CVI-07 categories — so that findings occur only after lawful initiation and discovery, and that findings are published without becoming law, glossary rewrites, or replacements for constitutional authority.

**Includes:**

- Binding every material lifecycle to identifiable published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–XI specifications and subordinate workstream / milestone corpora.
- Discovering only published term identities and classifying them under published CVT-01…CVT-10 categories.
- Applying only published CVI-01…CVI-07 categories and CVIR validation rules across stages.
- Preferring lawful violated / deferred / escalated / refuse-progress outcomes over rewriting law, inventing glossaries, inventing semantic consistency, soft-redefining terms in situ, or mutating corpora so the lifecycle “completes.”
- Keeping lifecycle progress distinct from educational quality, mastery, readiness theatre, product success, thesaurus-scanner greenery, or constitutional amendment.
- Preserving the Model’s posture: stages coordinate assessment; they do not become law or constitutional authority.

**Excludes:**

- Treating green CI, coverage percentages, linter scores, Twin estimates, Adaptive scores, UI ticks, spell-checkers, or NLP similarity as lifecycle fulfilment.
- Soft-amending specifications, inventing unpublished customs, inventing or redefining terms, inventing disambiguation, or privileging “how main speaks” to force progress.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for lawful coordination.
- Declaring “close enough” publication without documenting a corpus amendment.
- Freezing an implementation as permanently lawful because the lifecycle published findings.
- Creating constitutional authority under a lifecycle badge.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software pretends unfinished or dishonest vocabulary assessment is fine because integration needs a finished green glossary report. Coordination honesty is care; lifecycle theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CVILO-02 — Preserve canonical terminology

**Definition.** Ensure that every material lifecycle validates — and leaves reconstructable — published ownership and reconstructable identity of assessed terms under CVI-01 / CVIR-01, so only canonically recognised terminology may support intact dispositions and unpublished glossaries never become law by proximity to stage progress.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who invents worksheet labels mid-lesson because “students will know what we mean” destroys institutional honesty for every future student.

**Manifestations:**

- CVIL-02 discovers only published terms under CVT-01…CVT-10; inventing terms or categories is a defect.
- CVIL-03 requires reconstructable owning-corpus definitions; inventing glosses is a hard stop.
- Findings cite term identities, owning corpora, and CVT classifications, not only ticket IDs or commit SHAs.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing prior lawful assessment history to simplify the next report.
- Programme IX CT lineage may support reconstruction; CVIL never treats CT records as a licence to invent terminology.
- Canonical-terminology preservation never authorises inventing missing definitions or preferred synonyms as law.

---

### CVILO-03 — Preserve definition stability

**Definition.** Ensure that lifecycle organisation confirms — under CVI-05 / CVIR-05 and related CVIR rules — that published meaning statements remain as published across the assessment scope, so silent drift and soft overnight redefinition are impossible without a published amendment.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who quietly changes what “assessment” means between Monday’s worksheet and Friday’s report has abandoned continuity for convenience.

**Manifestations:**

- CVIL-06 confirms definition stability under published CVI / CVIR law; silent drift is a hard defect.
- Soft redefinition without amendment is reported as violated — never “fixed” by rewriting definitions under lifecycle pretence.
- Forbidden actions in `LIFECYCLE_BOUNDARIES.md` are hard stops.
- Definition-stability preservation never authorises inventing new definitions or soft-amending existing ones.
- Where material, CVIL-06 also confirms cross-corpus coherence under CVI-06 without merging incompatible senses.

---

### CVILO-04 — Preserve assessment repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that the same published corpus identities, the same discovered terms and CVT classifications, the same published CVI / CVIR criteria, and the same CVIL stage sequence yield the same vocabulary disposition (intact / violated / deferred / escalated) and the same lawful stage outcomes — absent a published non-deterministic exception (none are granted by this Model for constitutional meaning or integrity status).

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who treats the same glossary map as lawful or not depending on which reviewer woke first — or skips stages randomly — is not coaching; they are gambling.

**Manifestations:**

- Only CVIL-01…CVIL-08 may organise constitutional vocabulary integrity lifecycles.
- Stage purpose, inputs, outputs, and permitted / prohibited activities remain as published in `LIFECYCLE_STAGES.md`.
- Stage transitions and outcomes are corpus-bound and reproducible.
- Parallel CI runners, sharding, and cache races must not change constitutional disposition or invent stage skips.
- Explicit multi-type CVI / multi-category CVT sets use published conjunction / published stop rules, not random reviewer races.
- “Eventually consistent lifecycle meaning” is a constitutional defect.
- Invented or unstable term inputs, and invented semantic-consistency claims, are hard stops — not soft variables.

---

### CVILO-05 — Preserve constitutional readability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material vocabulary integrity lifecycle can honestly answer the mandatory explainability questions in `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md` and leaves a reconstructable trail: which constitutional terms were assessed, which canonical definitions were applied, which semantic validations were completed, which findings were produced, and which constitutional boundaries remained intact — so the corpus remains speakable without opaque “trust us” vocabulary badges.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later say *why* a glossary was accepted — or *at which stage* a finding arose — cannot be trusted. Silent green vocabulary badges destroy student and auditor trust.

**Manifestations:**

- Explainability obligations apply to all material lifecycles.
- Student / developer / auditor projections share one truth with different vocabulary.
- Audit records preserve constitutional references, not only technical scanner logs.
- Consumed corpus identities, CVT / CVI identities, CVIL stage identities, definition identities, semantic-validation status, and finding dispositions are recorded when material.
- Missing CVIL identity, missing corpus / term / definition refs, missing semantic-validation status, missing finding disposition, or missing boundary confirmation is a defect.
- Lifecycle progress is never narrated as mastery, readiness theatre, educational quality, new law, a terminology redefinition, or a freeze of stack authority.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing prior lawful lifecycle history to simplify the next report.
- Readability here means *constitutional corpus speakability* — not student exam readiness and not Runtime A deployment theatre.

## 5. Non-Objectives

The constitutional vocabulary integrity lifecycle does **not** optimise for:

- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- CI throughput, flaky-test suppression, or coverage theatre as educational success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when specifications or declared terminology are incomplete;
- collapsing CVIL stages into one mega-step that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics / spell-checker / NLP signals as substitutes for published constitutional terminology;
- freezing implementations, releases, or stacks as permanently lawful;
- treating documentation as optional commentary on whatever code already shipped;
- replacing MS001 vocabulary integrity meaning, Programme XI / WS1–WS3 integrity / lifecycle, Programme IX CT lineage preservation, or Programme IX–X evaluative / execution horizons;
- creating, renaming, or soft-redefining constitutional terminology so glossaries look tidy;
- creating constitutional authority under a lifecycle badge.

## 6. Closing Statement

> **A lifecycle that becomes “complete” by redefining terminology, reinterpreting meaning, amending specifications, or creating constitutional authority is a failed educational system.
