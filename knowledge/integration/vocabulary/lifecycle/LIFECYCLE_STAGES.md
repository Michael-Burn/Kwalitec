## 1. Purpose

Vocabulary assessment without a closed stage catalogue invents law by proximity: whichever scanner step, ticket status, or reviewer habit happens to finish becomes the tutor’s “proof the system ran a constitutional vocabulary integrity assessment.”

This catalogue names the only lawful constitutional vocabulary integrity lifecycle stages — and binds each stage to purpose, inputs, outputs, and permitted / prohibited activities.

---

## 2. Stage Principles

1. **Ordered and skip-disciplined.** Stages may be brief under thin scope; mandatory initiation, discovery, canonical definition / semantic consistency / ambiguity / definition stability validation, finding construction, and publication obligations may not be silently omitted when a vocabulary integrity finding is claimed.
2. **Published terms before findings.** Identifiable published corpora and discovered terms / definitions are confirmed before intact / violated dispositions are constructed.
3. **Explainability accumulates.** Each stage leaves traceable artefacts for `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.
4. **Refusal is a stage outcome.** Violated, deferred, escalated, and refuse-progress are first-class conclusions.
5. **Deterministic transitions.** Same published inputs ⇒ same next stage outcomes.
6. **Stages coordinate; they do not legislate.** No stage creates, modifies, or redefines constitutional law.
7. **Stages do not become constitutional authority.** Publication never freezes a glossary topology as permanently lawful or replaces published corpora.
8. **Stages do not redefine terminology.** Findings report semantic structure; they never create, redefine, invent, or delete constitutional terms.
9. **Stages apply published CVT / CVI / CVIR law.** Term categories, integrity categories, and validation rules remain owned by WS4 / MS001.

## 3. Catalogue Overview

| ID | Stage | Constitutional purpose (short) | Primary inputs | Primary outputs |
|----|-------|--------------------------------|----------------|-----------------|
| **CVIL-01** | Assessment Initiation | Open a vocabulary assessment against named published corpora and scope | Published corpus identities + concern scope + optional CVI / CVT indication | Initiated concern record (or refuse-open) |
| **CVIL-02** | Vocabulary Discovery | Identify published terms and classify under CVT-01…CVT-10 | Initiated concern + candidate published term identities | Discovered term / category set bound to CVI types (or refuse-discover) |
| **CVIL-03** | Canonical Definition Validation | Validate published ownership and reconstructable definitions under CVI-01 | Discovered terms / categories + published meaning statements | Validated definition record (or refuse-validate-definition) |
| **CVIL-04** | Semantic Consistency Validation | Validate one-meaning honesty and terminology reuse under CVI-02 / CVI-03 | Definition-validated term set + published usages | Validated semantic / reuse record (or refuse-validate-semantics) |
| **CVIL-05** | Ambiguity Validation | Detect and classify undisambiguated incompatible readings under CVI-04 | Semantics-validated set + published disambiguation tables | Ambiguity disposition (or refuse-validate-ambiguity) |
| **CVIL-06** | Definition Stability Validation | Confirm published definitions remain as published under CVI-05 (and CVI-06 where material) | Prior stage bundle + published definition history / coherence statements | Stability / coherence disposition (or refuse-stability) |
| **CVIL-07** | Finding Construction | Emit intact / violated / deferred / escalated under published criteria | Stage dispositions + CVI / CVIR criteria | Vocabulary integrity findings |

Material vocabulary assessment claiming constitutional findings must map to this sequence as published law requires. Stages may be brief; none may invent a stage outside this catalogue.

**Relation to CVI / CVT / CVIR / CVIO / CT / CC / CDI / CCI / CBI / EV:** Those catalogues remain defined solely by their owners. CVIL stages *organise when* vocabulary assessment activities occur; they do not replace, reclassify, invent, amend, or rewrite those catalogues.

**Semantic Consistency stage scope:** CVIL-04 applies CVI-02 (Semantic Consistency) and CVI-03 (Terminology Reuse Integrity) under published CVIR rules — covering the lifecycle’s semantic-consistency and terminology-reuse validation duties within one recognised stage.

## 5. Stage Definitions

### CVIL-01 — Assessment Initiation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Open a material vocabulary integrity assessment concern by naming the published constitutional corpora under assessment and the scope of the concern — without inventing law, opening against unpublished glossaries, or treating initiation as an intact finding.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Identifiable published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–XI specification references for the concern scope
- Concern identity (what vocabulary-integrity question is under assessment)
- Optional published indication of required CVI-01…CVI-07 categories and CVT-01…CVT-10 term categories for the scope
- Optional identifiable supporting authority / lineage references (not rewritten)

**Constitutional outputs**

- An initiated assessment concern record naming corpora, specifications, scope, and intended CVI / CVT sets — **or**
- Lawful refuse-open when standards are unpublished, scope is theatrical, or initiation would invent law

**Permitted activities**

- Name published corpora and specifications as assessment standards
- Bound the concern scope without amending corpora
- Record intended CVI types and CVT categories from the published catalogues
- Refuse initiation when constitutional preconditions fail
- Preserve initiation audit identity for later stages

**Prohibited activities**

- Treat “how docs speak,” tribal glossaries, or stack preference as the standard
- Create or modify constitutional specifications by opening a concern
- Issue an intact / violated finding at initiation
- Invent unpublished vocabulary integrity categories or term categories
- Skip initiation when a material finding will later be claimed
- Redefine terminology, reinterpret meaning, or create constitutional authority at initiation

**Authority preserved**  
Published corpora remain the sole standard. Initiation organises; it does not legislate.

---

### CVIL-02 — Vocabulary Discovery

**Constitutional purpose**  
Identify **published constitutional terms** among the named corpora and classify them under recognised **CVT-01…CVT-10** term categories — so later stages assess real published terminology, not anonymous theatre — without inventing, renaming, or redefining term identities.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Initiated concern from CVIL-01
- Candidate published term identities (words / phrases as published by owning corpora)
- Published CVT-01…CVT-10 term-category definitions and CVI-01…CVI-07 category definitions from WS4 / MS001
- Optional supporting CT lineage identities already established under upstream law (unchanged)

**Constitutional outputs**

- A discovered term / category set bound to the concern and intended CVI types — **or**
- Lawful refuse-discover when terms are missing, forged, invented, unpublished, or would require unlawful mutation / redefinition to “discover”

**Permitted activities**

- Identify and name published terms
- Classify terms under published CVT-01…CVT-10 only
- Bind discovered terms to the initiated concern and intended CVI types
- Refuse discovery when terms cannot be honestly affirmed as published
- Preserve discovery records for audit and explainability

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent missing terms or invent category membership outside CVT-01…CVT-10
- Rename, redefine, or delete terminology to force later intact status
- Treat spell-checkers, thesauri, NLP similarity, ticket links, or CI noise as constitutional terms without published mapping
- Redefine educational or constitutional meaning while “discovering”
- Present discovery as itself an intact finding or a freeze of stack authority
- Create constitutional authority under discovery pretence

**Authority preserved**  
Published terminology remains upstream fact. Discovery organises identity and category classification; it does not author terms or educational truth.

---

### CVIL-03 — Canonical Definition Validation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Validate that discovered terms have **published ownership** and **reconstructable canonical definitions** under CVI-01 / CVIR-01 — without inventing missing glosses, inventing ownership, or soft-redefining definitions in situ.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Discovered term / category set from CVIL-02
- Published meaning statements from owning corpora
- Published canonical-recognition rules (especially CVIR-01 and related CVIR rules)
- Optional supporting lineage / authority references consumed as inputs (unchanged)

**Constitutional outputs**

- A validated definition record per assessed term (canonical ownership / definition identity confirmed) — **or**
- Lawful refuse-validate-definition when ownership or definition identity cannot be reconstructed, glosses would be invented, or validation would require soft-redefinition

**Permitted activities**

- Confirm published ownership for discovered terms
- Bind each term to its reconstructable owning-corpus meaning statement
- Prefer honest refuse-validate-definition / deferred / escalated over inventing glosses
- Remain repeatable and implementation-neutral
- Preserve definition-validation records for later stages

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent missing definitions, glosses, or ownership claims
- Soft-amend specifications so non-canonical terms become “canonical”
- Privilege a technology stack as constitutional
- Redefine Programme VI–X meaning or CVI / CVT meaning in validation prose
- Present definition validation as new constitutional law
- Create constitutional authority by declaring invented glosses “approved”

**Authority preserved**  
WS4 / MS001 owns canonical-recognition rules. This stage applies them; it does not amend terminology or become constitutional authority.

---

### CVIL-04 — Semantic Consistency Validation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Validate **semantic consistency** under CVI-02 and **terminology reuse honesty** under CVI-03 — confirming that each term retains one published meaning within its lawful scope (or cites published disambiguation), and that repeated use preserves canonical meaning — without inventing disambiguation, silently merging incompatible senses, or redefining terms in situ.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Definition-validated term set from CVIL-03
- Published usages across the assessment scope and meaning statements from owning corpora
- Published consistency / reuse rules (especially CVIR-02 / CVIR-03 / CVIR-08)
- Published disambiguation tables where they exist (unchanged)

**Constitutional outputs**

- Validated semantic / reuse record: consistency / reuse disposition (intact under rules / violated / deferred / escalated) — **or**
- Lawful refuse-validate-semantics when consistency or reuse honesty cannot be confirmed without inventing meanings, inventing disambiguation, or rewriting law

**Permitted activities**

- Confirm one published meaning per term within lawful scope under CVI-02 / CVIR-02
- Confirm reuse honesty under CVI-03 / CVIR-03
- Report incompatible dual meanings without disambiguation as hard defects
- Prefer honest violated / deferred / escalated over soft-redefinition
- Preserve semantic-validation records for finding construction

**Prohibited activities**

- Create, redefine, invent, or delete constitutional terminology
- Soft-amend specifications so dual meanings become “the same”
- Treat thesaurus / NLP similarity as constitutional semantic consistency without published mapping
- Hide consistency / reuse defects to manufacture later intact status
- Present semantic validation as licence to rewrite the corpus
- Create constitutional authority by declaring colliding senses “approved”
- Invent missing disambiguation tables during validation

**Authority preserved**  
Semantic validation reports meaning-structure defects. It never authors law or redefines terms.

---

### CVIL-05 — Ambiguity Validation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Detect and classify **undisambiguated incompatible readings** under CVI-04 / CVIR-04 — so ambiguity is either avoided by published law or honestly disclosed — without inventing disambiguation tables or hiding ambiguous usages.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Semantics-validated set from CVIL-04
- Published ambiguity / disambiguation rules (especially CVIR-04)
- Published disambiguation tables where they exist (unchanged)
- Collision / dual-meaning status from CVIL-04 where material

**Constitutional outputs**

- Ambiguity disposition for the concern (avoided / disclosed / violated / deferred / escalated) — **or**
- Lawful refuse-validate-ambiguity when ambiguity status cannot be honestly confirmed without inventing disambiguation or rewriting law

**Permitted activities**

- Detect incompatible readings that lack published disambiguation
- Classify ambiguities under published CVI-04 / CVIR-04 rules
- Prefer honest violated / deferred / escalated over inventing disambiguation theatre
- Preserve ambiguity-validation records for finding construction

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent missing disambiguation tables or ambiguity rules
- Hide ambiguous usages to manufacture later intact status
- Soft-amend specifications so ambiguity becomes “resolved”
- Modify constitutional specifications to force ambiguity pass
- Present ambiguity pass as educational quality or stack freeze
- Create constitutional authority by declaring ambiguous usages “approved”
- Redefine constitutional meaning under ambiguity-validation pretence

**Authority preserved**  
Published corpora own disambiguation. This stage confirms them; it does not become a glossary rewriter or constitutional authority.

---

### CVIL-06 — Definition Stability Validation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Confirm that assessed terms **preserve published definition stability** under CVI-05 / CVIR-05 — published meaning statements remain as published; silent drift or soft overnight redefinition is a defect — and, where material, confirm **cross-corpus coherence** under CVI-06 without merging incompatible senses.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Prior stage dispositions from CVIL-01…CVIL-05
- Published definition statements and lawful continuity expectations
- Published stability / coherence rules (especially CVIR-05 / CVIR-06)
- Workstream / milestone term identities as published

**Constitutional outputs**

- Stability / coherence disposition for the concern (preserved / violated / deferred / escalated) — **or**
- Lawful refuse-stability when stability or coherence cannot be honestly confirmed without inventing drift “fixes,” soft-redefining definitions, or rewriting law

**Permitted activities**

- Confirm published definitions remain as published under CVI-05 / CVIR-05
- Confirm speakable separation of related senses under CVI-06 / CVIR-06 where material
- Report silent drift or soft redefinition as violated
- Prefer honest violated / deferred / escalated over soft-amendment
- Preserve stability / coherence records for finding construction

**Prohibited activities**

- Soft-redefine constitutional terminology to “stabilise” reports
- Invent new definitions under stability pretence
- Soft-amend specifications so invent-in-place glosses become “growth”
- Modify constitutional specifications to force stability pass
- Present stability pass as educational quality or stack freeze
- Collapse distinct published senses into one mega-definition under “validation”
- Create constitutional authority by declaring redefined terms “approved”

**Authority preserved**  
Published corpora own definitions and continuity. This stage confirms them; it does not become a redefinition engine or constitutional authority.

---

### CVIL-07 — Finding Construction

**Constitutional purpose**  
Emit **vocabulary integrity findings** as lawful dispositions under published criteria — speaking to whether assessed terminology remains intact under canonical recognition, semantic consistency, reuse honesty, ambiguity avoidance, and definition stability — without converting findings into amendments, educational grades, glossary rewrites, or freezes of stack authority.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Stage dispositions from CVIL-01…CVIL-06
- Published CVI-01…CVI-07 criteria and CVIR validation rules from WS4 / MS001
- Published decision vocabulary (intact / violated / deferred / escalated)
- Bound references to corpora, terms, CVT categories, definitions, semantic validations, ambiguity / stability dispositions

**Constitutional outputs**

- Vocabulary integrity findings for the concern (including composition outcomes when multiple CVI types / CVT categories apply)
- Explicit non-authority framing (findings are integrity dispositions only — never new law and never glossary rewrites)
- Assessment records binding stages, terms, categories, definitions, semantic validations, ambiguity / stability, and findings

**Permitted activities**

- Produce findings under published dispositions only
- Bind each finding to the CVI types, CVT categories, terms, and stages that produced it
- Record composition / stop-rule application honestly
- Prefer understatement over invented green
- Propose (not enact) upstream corpus amendments when law itself must change
- Preserve audit-speakable assessment records under CVI-07 honesty

**Prohibited activities**

- Present findings as constitutional amendments or new educational meaning
- Narrate intact status as mastery, readiness theatre, educational quality, permanent conformity, or frozen stack authority
- Rewrite findings to match product urgency
- Collapse multi-type failures into a single false “intact”
- Create, redefine, invent, or delete constitutional terminology while “constructing findings”
- Invent unpublished CVI categories or CVT term categories during finding construction
- Erase stage history so findings look clean
- Create constitutional authority via finding text

**Authority preserved**  
Findings validate speech structure against law. They never become law, glossary rewrites, or constitutional authority.

---

### CVIL-08 — Finding Publication

**Constitutional purpose**  
Publish vocabulary integrity findings and assessment records under **published honesty rules** so audiences receive lawful assessment speech — without inventing intact status, hiding inconsistencies or ambiguities, rewriting boundaries, amending specifications, redefining terminology, or freezing a stack as law.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Findings and assessment records from CVIL-07
- Published publication / honesty rules from WS4 / MS001 and EIP explainability / continuity law
- Boundary confirmations (non-redefinition, non-reinterpretation, non-legislation, non-authority-creation, non-substitution)

**Constitutional outputs**

- Published assessment artefacts projecting one constitutional truth to authorised audiences — **or**
- Lawful refuse-publish when records are incomplete, boundaries were breached, or publication would invent intact status / hide inconsistencies / redefine terminology / freeze stack authority / replace constitutional authority

**Permitted activities**

- Publish findings and records under published honesty rules
- Project student / developer / auditor vocabularies without changing constitutional facts
- Refuse publication when audit or boundary preconditions fail
- Keep publication distinct from educational quality, terminology redefinition, and authority creation

**Prohibited activities**

- Publish invented intact status or incomplete-assessment theatre as intact
- Hide refuse / defer / escalate / violated dispositions to manufacture green
- Narrate publication as constitutional amendment, educational success, terminology redefinition, or frozen stack law
- Redefine constitutional terminology or create constitutional authority as a publication act
- Modify constitutional specifications as a publication act
- Present seals, badges, spell-checker greenery, or NLP scores as substitutes for published records
- Reinterpret constitutional meaning in publication speech

**Authority preserved**  
Publication communicates assessment findings. Published corpora remain the sole standard.

## 6. Stage Transition Rules

| From | To | Transition requires |
|------|----|---------------------|
| — | CVIL-01 | Material vocabulary assessment concern needs opening |
| CVIL-01 | CVIL-02 | Concern initiated (not refuse-open) |

| CVIL-02 | CVIL-07 / CVIL-08 path | Refuse-discover recorded honestly |
| CVIL-03 | CVIL-04 | Canonical definitions validated (not refuse-validate-definition) — or honest fail disposition carried forward |
| CVIL-03 | CVIL-07 / CVIL-08 path | Refuse-validate-definition recorded honestly |
| CVIL-04 | CVIL-05 | Semantic consistency / reuse validated (not refuse-validate-semantics) — or honest fail disposition carried forward |
| CVIL-04 | CVIL-07 / CVIL-08 path | Refuse-validate-semantics recorded honestly |
| CVIL-05 | CVIL-06 | Ambiguity disposition available (not refuse-validate-ambiguity) — or honest fail disposition carried forward |
| CVIL-05 | CVIL-07 / CVIL-08 path | Refuse-validate-ambiguity recorded honestly |
| CVIL-06 | CVIL-07 | Stability / coherence disposition available (not refuse-stability) — or honest fail disposition carried forward |
| CVIL-06 | CVIL-07 / CVIL-08 path | Refuse-stability recorded honestly |
| CVIL-07 | CVIL-08 | Findings constructed under published vocabulary |
| CVIL-08 | — | Publication completed or refuse-publish recorded |

Silent jumps that claim findings without CVIL-01…CVIL-06 honesty are constitutionally defective.

## 7. Composition with Vocabulary Integrity Categories

| During stage | Relationship to CVI-01…CVI-07 / CVT-01…CVT-10 |
|--------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| CVIL-01 | May *name* intended CVI types and CVT categories; does not apply full validation |
| CVIL-02 | Discovers terms those categories / types will assess |
| CVIL-03 | Applies canonical recognition rules (CVI-01) |
| CVIL-04 | Applies semantic consistency and terminology reuse rules (CVI-02 / CVI-03) |
| CVIL-05 | Applies ambiguity avoidance rules (CVI-04) |
| CVIL-06 | Applies definition stability (and CVI-06 cross-corpus coherence where material) |
| **CVIL-07** | **Constructs** findings under CVI-01…CVI-07 (including CVI-07 assessment-record honesty) |
| CVIL-08 | Publishes CVI-bound findings without inventing types or redefining terms |

Cross-cutting concerns may bind multiple CVI types and CVT categories across stages. Lifecycle stages remain CVIL-01…CVIL-08 only.
