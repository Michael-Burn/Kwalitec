## 1. Purpose

A lifecycle that silently becomes a second constitution — redefining terminology so stages “complete,” inventing semantic consistency in “findings,” licensing a preferred glossary under a published badge, inventing terms to force CVIL-07, treating a preferred implementation as the new standard, mutating corpora mid-lifecycle, or freezing a stack as law — destroys student trust and educational integrity.

This document draws a bright line between **lawful constitutional vocabulary integrity lifecycle coordination** (Programme XI / WS4 / MS002) and **constitutional authorship / educational judgement / terminology redefinition / meaning reinterpretation / authority creation / specification amendment** (Constitution / EIP / Programmes VI–X / WS1–WS3 / WS4 / MS001 / permitted writers).

## 2. Boundary Principles

1. **Coordinate, do not legislate.** The lifecycle organises vocabulary assessment; it does not create law.
2. **Published specifications only.** Unpublished customs and “main as vocabulary” are hard stops as standards.
3. **Published stages only.** Unpublished CVIL customs are unlawful.
4. **Published vocabulary integrity categories only.** Stages apply CVI-01…CVI-07 only.
5. **Published term categories only.** Stages classify under CVT-01…CVT-10 only.
6. **Published terms only.** Lifecycles without identifiable published terms are theatre.
7. **Exactly as published.** Successful progress never rewrites specification meaning or terminology definitions.
8. **Authority first.** Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard.
9. **Implementation independence.** No stack, vendor, or reference implementation becomes law by proximity to publication.
10. **Published ≠ quality.** Lifecycle publication never grades learning, mastery, readiness, or product success.
11. **Published ≠ redefined terminology.** The lifecycle never creates or soft-redefines terms so findings look intact.
12. **Published ≠ new authority.** The lifecycle never elevates Programme XI into foundational law by coordinating others.
13. **No meaning redefinition.** Programme VI–X meanings, EIP claim classes, and CVI / CVT criteria survive coordination intact.
14. **No authority substitution.** A published finding never replaces the corpus for the next judgement.
15. **No terminology mutation.** Coordination never creates, deletes, renames, soft-redefines, or invents constitutional terminology.
16. **No bypass.** Product urgency never waives constitutional lifecycle requirements.
17. **Repeatability / consistency.** Race-dependent or private stage shortcuts are defects.
18. **Explain the stop.** Students, developers, and auditors should hear when the lifecycle must refuse progress or publication.
19. **No emergency exemption.** Deadlines and demos never mint constitutional amendment power or authority creation via lifecycle publication.
20. **Ambiguity honesty.** Colliding senses that confuse vocabulary are reported, not “fixed” by soft-redefinition in situ.
21. **Definition stability honesty.** Published definitions remain as published; invent-overnight glosses are forbidden.

## 3. What the Lifecycle May Do (Lawful)

| Lawful action | Constitutional meaning |
|---------------|------------------------|
| **Coordinate vocabulary assessment** | Organise activities through CVIL-01…CVIL-08 |
| **Validate constitutional terminology** | Apply published CVT / CVI / CVIR law to discovered published terms |
| **Validate term categories** | Classify and confirm membership under CVT-01…CVT-10 at CVIL-02 |
| **Validate canonical definitions** | Confirm published ownership and reconstructable definitions at CVIL-03 |
| **Validate semantic consistency / terminology reuse** | Confirm CVI-02 / CVI-03 honesty at CVIL-04 |
| **Validate ambiguity avoidance** | Confirm CVI-04 honesty at CVIL-05 |
| **Validate definition stability** | Confirm published definitions remain as published at CVIL-06 |
| **Identify semantic findings** | Emit intact / violated / deferred / escalated under published criteria |
| **Publish assessment findings** | Communicate findings and records under published honesty rules |
| **Consume constitutional specifications** | Use published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–XI corpora as assessment standards |
| **Preserve established traceability in records** | Carry established CT-01…CT-07 relationships into assessment records; never invent them |
| **Apply vocabulary integrity categories** | Across stages, apply published CVI-01…CVI-07 only |
| **Refuse unlawful progress** | Prefer honest refuse / defer / escalate / violated over improvisation |
| **Remain repeatable and consistent** | Same published inputs → same stage outcomes and dispositions |
| **Remain implementation-neutral** | Honour lifecycle law across lawful equivalent stacks |
| **Support readiness honesty** | Strengthen vocabulary-structure honesty without becoming educational readiness or constitutional authority |
| **Explain lifecycle progress** | Answer terms / definitions / semantic validations / findings / boundaries without rewriting meaning |
| **Propose corpus amendments upstream** | Recommend (not enact) amendments when law itself must change |

These actions **coordinate vocabulary assessment**. They do **not** publish a new Constitution, invent coach warrants, mint tips, author ownership maps, invent CVI / CVT / CVIL types, invent CT links, author runtime behaviour, judge educational quality, redefine terminology, reinterpret meaning, create constitutional authority, or amend specifications under coordination pretext.

## 4. What the Lifecycle Must NEVER Do

| Forbidden action | Why | Lawful alternative |
|------------------|-----|--------------------|
| **Redefine constitutional terminology** | Terminology is published corpus fact, not a lifecycle output | Report semantics as found; refuse invent-in-place glosses |
| **Reinterpret constitutional meaning** | Owning corpora alone define what terms mean | Assess against published meanings; never reinterpret them |
| **Amend constitutional specifications** | Only corpus governance may amend law | Leave specs unchanged; propose amendment upstream or mark violated / refuse |
| **Create constitutional authority** | Published corpora alone own constitutional meaning | Cite published law only; refuse unpublished standards; never elevate lifecycle progress into authority |
| **Invent term identities** | Discovery organises published terms | Refuse-discover when terms are missing |
| **Invent term-category membership** | CVT catalogue is closed under WS4 / MS001 | Classify under CVT-01…CVT-10 only |
| **Invent semantic consistency / disambiguation** | Honesty requires real published structure | Refuse-validate when consistency or disambiguation cannot be reconstructed |
| **Erase ambiguities in situ** | Ambiguity honesty | Report ambiguities; never soft-redefine under assessment pretence |
| **Invent vocabulary integrity categories** | CVI catalogue is closed under WS4 / MS001 | Apply CVI-01…CVI-07 only |
| **Invent lifecycle stages** | CVIL catalogue is closed under this corpus | Apply CVIL-01…CVIL-08 only |
| **Invent traceability** | CT owns lawful lineage | Preserve established relationships in records; refuse invented links |
| **Bypass lifecycle requirements** | Requirements are educational care, not optional polish | Prefer understatement / refuse over invented green |
| **Privilege a technology as constitutional** | Implementation neutrality | Coordinate assessment, not Flask / SQLAlchemy / REST / OpenAPI fashion |
| **Determine educational quality** | No CVIL stage is a quality rubric | Keep coordination speech distinct from quality speech |
| **Determine constitutional truth** | Constitution / EIP own truth | Obey published law; do not adjudicate truth in lifecycle machinery |
| **Transfer authority** | Programme VII WS2 owns ownership, permission, and conflict disposition | Apply Authority corpora; refuse domain absorption via stages |
| **Invent educational state** | Programme VII WS4 owns EST/CST context law | Assess against published postures only |
| **Generate unpublished recommendations** | Programme VII WS3 + Programme VI owners own tip warrant | Surface integrity findings, not new tips |
| **Treat Twin / Adaptive / UI / CI / spell-checkers / NLP as constitutional producers** | They are subjects / delivery surfaces | Require published corpus mapping or refuse |
| **Present scores / coverage / acks / scanner greens as constitutional integrity or publication** | Operational proxies are not lifecycle law | Cite published corpora, CVT / CVI types, and CVIL stages |
| **Become irreplaceable by coordination topology** | Replaceability | Keep lifecycle law implementation-independent |
| **Freeze stack authority via publication** | Neutrality / authority preservation | Publish findings; never make the stack the next standard |
| **Replace MS001 vocabulary integrity meaning** | CVI / CVT own validation meaning | Sequence assessments; never rewrite CVI / CVT criteria |
| **Replace Programme XI / WS1–WS3 CDI / CCI / CBI / CDIL / CCIL / CBIL** | Sibling integrity owns structure assessment | Compose with sibling horizons; never treat “vocabulary intact” as “structure intact forever” |
| **Replace Programme IX CT** | CT owns lineage | Compose with CT; never treat “linked” as “vocabulary intact forever” |

## 6. Boundary Checks (Pre-Publication)

Before a material constitutional vocabulary integrity lifecycle is treated as lawfully published, coordination requires affirmative answers:

| Check | Question |
|-------|----------|
| **CVILB-01 Specification / corpus identity** | Are assessment standards published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–XI corpora (not unpublished customs)? |
| **CVILB-02 Stage class** | Are required CVIL-01…CVIL-08 stages being applied (not unpublished customs)? |
| **CVILB-03 Integrity class** | Are required CVI-01…CVI-07 categories applied (not unpublished customs)? |
| **CVILB-04 Term category class** | Are required CVT-01…CVT-10 categories applied (not unpublished customs)? |
| **CVILB-05 Term identity** | Which named constitutional terms were assessed? |
| **CVILB-06 Canonical-definition identity** | Which owning-corpus published definitions were applied (none invented)? |
| **CVILB-07 Semantic-validation status** | Which semantic consistency / reuse validations were performed (none hidden; none “fixed” by soft-redefinition)? |
| **CVILB-08 Ambiguity / stability status** | Was ambiguity avoidance and definition stability confirmed or honestly reported violated? |
| **CVILB-09 Finding binding** | Which lifecycle stages produced the vocabulary integrity findings (especially CVIL-03…CVIL-07)? |
| **CVILB-10 Record identity** | Which assessment records were preserved? |
| **CVILB-11 Non-redefinition** | Does the lifecycle avoid redefining constitutional terminology? |
| **CVILB-12 Non-reinterpretation** | Does the lifecycle avoid reinterpreting constitutional meaning or Programme VI–X educational / constitutional meaning? |
| **CVILB-13 Non-legislation** | Does the lifecycle avoid creating or amending constitutional specifications? |
| **CVILB-14 Non-authority-creation** | Does the lifecycle avoid creating constitutional authority or treating findings as new law? |
| **CVILB-15 Non-substitution** | Does the lifecycle avoid treating the implementation or lifecycle machinery as the next constitutional standard? |
| **CVILB-16 Non-MS001-replacement** | Is publication framed as assessment coordination complete — not a rewrite of CVI / CVT meaning? |
| **CVILB-17 Neutrality** | Does the lifecycle avoid privileging a language, framework, protocol, or CI vendor as law? |
| **CVILB-18 Publication ≠ quality** | Is the outcome framed as lifecycle coordination / vocabulary integrity — not educational quality? |
| **CVILB-19 Repeatability / audit** | Are stage outcomes reproducible; do explainability records preserve terms, definitions, semantic validations, findings, and boundaries? |

Any failed check → refuse-publish / deferred / escalated / violated as published. Do not “best-effort” invent lifecycle law, invent terminology, soft-amend specifications, redefine terms, create constitutional authority, or replace constitutional authority in situ.
