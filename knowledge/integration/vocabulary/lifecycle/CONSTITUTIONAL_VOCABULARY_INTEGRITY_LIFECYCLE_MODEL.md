## 2. What the Constitutional Vocabulary Integrity Lifecycle Is

The **constitutional vocabulary integrity lifecycle** is the binding organisation by which **vocabulary integrity assessments** may proceed through recognised stages — initiating against published corpus identities, discovering published terms and classifying them under CVT-01…CVT-10 categories, validating canonical definitions under CVI-01, validating semantic consistency and terminology reuse under CVI-02 / CVI-03, validating ambiguity avoidance under CVI-04, validating definition stability under CVI-05 (and related CVI-06 cross-corpus coherence where material), and constructing / publishing **vocabulary integrity findings** that preserve canonical recognition, semantic consistency, honest reuse, ambiguity avoidance, definition stability, repeatability, consistency, implementation neutrality, auditability, and explainability — without becoming a source of constitutional law, a glossary rewriter, a substitute for MS001 vocabulary integrity meaning, or a replacement for upstream programme authority.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Published constitutional corpus** | Constitution, EIP, Programme VI–XI specifications and their subordinate workstream / milestone corpora | Which corpus may the lifecycle organise assessment against? |
| **Constitutional term** | A published word or phrase that carries constitutional meaning within an owning corpus | Which term is under assessment? |
| **Canonical definition** | The published meaning statement for a term in its owning corpus — never an invented gloss | Which definition binds this term? |
| **Vocabulary term category** | A recognised CVT-01…CVT-10 grouping of constitutional terminology roles for integrity assessment | Which term category contains this usage? |
| **Semantic consistency** | The same term retains one published meaning within its lawful scope; incompatible meanings without published disambiguation are defects | Does the term mean one thing as published? |
| **Terminology reuse** | Repeated use of a published term that preserves its canonical meaning (or cites published disambiguation) | Is reuse honest? |
| **Ambiguity** | A term usage that permits incompatible constitutional readings without published disambiguation | Was ambiguity avoided or disclosed? |
| **Definition stability** | Published definitions remain as published; silent drift or soft redefinition is a defect | Is the definition intact? |
| **Lifecycle stage** | A recognised CVIL-01…CVIL-08 organisational step | Which coordination step is active? |
| **Vocabulary integrity finding** | Lawful disposition: intact / violated / deferred / escalated — never “new law” and never a glossary rewrite | Does vocabulary remain lawful? |
| **Assessment record / publication artefact** | Audit-speakable record of terms, definitions, semantic validations, stages, findings, and preserved boundaries | Can the assessment be reconstructed? |
| **Constitutional authority** | The exclusive right of published corpora to define educational and execution meaning | Who owns the standard? |
| **Implementation neutrality** | Lifecycle coordination is judged against law and published terminology, not against a preferred stack, vendor, or topology | Remains the stack replaceable? |
| **Repeatability** | Same published corpora, same discovered terms / definitions, same stage sequence → same disposition | Can the judgement be reproduced? |
| **Auditability** | Every material lifecycle leaves reconstructable constitutional traces | Can the judgement be reconstructed? |
| **Explainability** | Terms, definitions, semantic validations, findings, stages, and boundaries are speakable | Can the judgement be narrated honestly? |

The constitutional vocabulary integrity lifecycle is:

- **coordination-centred** — it organises vocabulary assessments; it never authors constitutional obligations or terminology;
- **discovery-bound** — it discovers published terms and classifies them under CVT categories; it never invents glossaries to force progress;
- **canonical-bound** — only published terms with reconstructable owning corpora may certify intact status;
- **semantics-preserving** — the same term retains one published meaning within its lawful scope;
- **reuse-honest** — repeated use preserves canonical meaning or cites published disambiguation;
- **ambiguity-resistant** — incompatible readings without published disambiguation are hard defects;
- **definition-stable** — published definitions remain as published; silent drift is forbidden;
- **catalogue-closed** — only CVIL-01…CVIL-08 may be applied as constitutional vocabulary integrity lifecycle stages;
- **type-faithful** — stages apply only published CVI-01…CVI-07 categories, CVT-01…CVT-10 term categories, and CVIR validation rules from WS4 / MS001; they never invent categories;
- **finding-producing** — assessments emit intact / violated / deferred / escalated dispositions;
- **publication-honest** — findings are published under honesty rules; publication never redefines terms or becomes law;
- **authority-preserving** — published corpora remain the sole source of constitutional meaning;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by lifecycle law;
- **corpus-bound** — every material lifecycle cites identifiable published specifications and term / definition identities;
- **audit-capable** — every material lifecycle leaves reconstructable constitutional traces;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which terms were assessed under which definitions with which semantic validations and findings;
- **non-legislative** — a vocabulary finding never becomes constitutional law or a soft amendment;
- **non-substitutive** — lifecycle machinery never becomes the constitutional standard for the next judgement;
- **non-mutating** — the lifecycle never modifies constitutional specifications or terminology under coordination pretext.

The constitutional vocabulary integrity lifecycle is **not**:

- a second Educational Constitution, EIP, Programme VI–X, WS1–WS3, or WS4 / MS001 amendment path;
- a licence to reinterpret coach meaning, ownership maps, tip warrants, runtime contracts, conformance criteria, traceability meaning, verification meaning, compliance meaning, certification meaning, evolution meaning, execution meaning, dependency meaning, catalogue meaning, boundary meaning, or CVI / CVT categories in lifecycle prose;
- a testing framework, CI/CD pipeline, GitHub Action, spell-checker, thesaurus, NLP product, or OpenAPI linker;
- a claim that “lifecycle complete” guarantees learning, mastery, exam readiness, product success, perpetual conformity, or frozen stack authority;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS4 / MS001 vocabulary integrity meaning — CVI / CVT define categories and term roles; CVIL sequences when assessment occurs;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS1 dependency integrity / lifecycle — CDI / CDIL validate and sequence *corpus dependency structure*; CVIL coordinates *terminology semantic integrity assessment*;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS2 catalogue integrity / lifecycle — CCI / CCIL validate and sequence *catalogue identifier integrity*; CVIL coordinates *term meaning consistency*;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS3 boundary integrity / lifecycle — CBI / CBIL validate and sequence *boundary consistency*; CVIL coordinates *vocabulary semantic stability*;
- a substitute for Programme IX MS002 constitutional traceability — CT preserves spec/artefact/finding lineage; CVIL coordinates *constitutional vocabulary integrity assessment*;
- a substitute for Programme IX conformance / verification / compliance / certification — those evaluate implementations and obligations; CVIL validates *vocabulary corpus sequencing*;

## 3. Lifecycle Posture (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material constitutional vocabulary integrity lifecycle travels through eight constitutional stages. These are **normative coordination obligations**, not an implementation architecture and not a CI job graph.

```
CVIL-01 Assessment Initiation
        │
        ▼
CVIL-02 Vocabulary Discovery
        │
        ▼
CVIL-03 Canonical Definition Validation
        │
        ▼
CVIL-04 Semantic Consistency Validation
        │
        ▼
CVIL-05 Ambiguity Validation
        │
        ▼
CVIL-06 Definition Stability Validation
        │
        ├── violated / deferred / escalated (lawful finding — terminology unchanged)
        │
        └── intact (or other lawful disposition) →
                │
                ▼
CVIL-07 Finding Construction
        │
        ▼
CVIL-08 Finding Publication
        │
        ▼
Explain terms / definitions / semantic validations / findings / boundaries
```

| Stage | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **CVIL-01 Assessment Initiation** | Open a vocabulary assessment concern against named published corpora and scope | Scope is unpublished custom, tribal glossary, or “how docs speak today” |
| **CVIL-02 Vocabulary Discovery** | Identify published terms and classify them under CVT-01…CVT-10 | Terms / categories are invented, unpublished, or would require redefinition to “discover” them |
| **CVIL-03 Canonical Definition Validation** | Validate published ownership and reconstructable canonical definitions under CVI-01 | Validation would invent glosses, invent ownership, or soft-redefine definitions |
| **CVIL-04 Semantic Consistency Validation** | Validate one-meaning honesty and terminology reuse under CVI-02 / CVI-03 | Validation would invent disambiguation, silently merge incompatible senses, or redefine terms in situ |
| **CVIL-05 Ambiguity Validation** | Detect and classify undisambiguated incompatible readings under CVI-04 | Validation would invent disambiguation tables or hide ambiguous usages |
| **CVIL-06 Definition Stability Validation** | Confirm published definitions remain as published under CVI-05 (and CVI-06 where material) | Validation would soft-redefine definitions, invent drift “fixes,” or rewrite cross-corpus senses |
| **CVIL-07 Finding Construction** | Emit intact / violated / deferred / escalated findings under published criteria | Findings would become amendments, educational quality grades, or glossary rewrites |
| **CVIL-08 Finding Publication** | Publish findings and assessment records under published honesty rules | Publication would invent intact status, hide inconsistencies, rewrite boundaries, or freeze a stack as law |

> **The lifecycle never “best-effort invents” a ninth stage called law-making, glossary rewriting, stack canonisation, or terminology mutation.**

**Semantic Consistency stage scope:** CVIL-04 applies CVI-02 (Semantic Consistency) and CVI-03 (Terminology Reuse Integrity) under published CVIR rules — covering the lifecycle’s semantic-consistency and terminology-reuse validation duties within one recognised stage.

## 6. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Vocabulary Integrity Lifecycle Model exists so that:

1. **Law remains upstream of coordination** — stages organise assessment against published corpora; progress does not quietly become the specifications.
2. **Discovery remains honest** — discovery names published terms and CVT categories; it does not invent glossaries to force intact status.
3. **Canonical definitions remain reconstructable** — definition validation confirms published ownership; it does not invent glosses.
4. **Semantics remain consistent** — semantic validation confirms one-meaning honesty and reuse honesty; it never soft-redefines terms in situ.
5. **Ambiguity remains disclosed** — ambiguity validation reports undisambiguated incompatible readings; it never invents disambiguation theatre.
6. **Definitions remain stable** — stability validation reports published definitions as found; it never soft-amends overnight.
7. **Findings remain sequenced** — intact / violated / deferred / escalated dispositions arise after lawful initiation, discovery, and validation — not as random green theatre.
8. **Authority remains honest** — no team may treat a published finding as the new constitutional source.
9. **Implementations remain replaceable** — lifecycle law does not freeze Flask, SQLAlchemy, a particular API shape, or a CI vendor as law.
10. **Repeatability remains honest** — the same published corpora, discovered terms / definitions, and stage sequence yield the same disposition.
11. **Audit remains possible** — every material lifecycle can be reconstructed against named terms, definitions, semantic validations, stages, and findings.
12. **Explainability remains intact** — lifecycle speech describes coordination of vocabulary assessment; it does not redefine what learning means.
13. **Students remain protected** — product theatre cannot launder colliding or redefined terminology as “lifecycle intact.”
