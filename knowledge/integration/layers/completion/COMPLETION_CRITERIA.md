## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor needs bright conditions for saying “this constitutional layer assessment work is finished” — and equally bright refusals when it is not. After WS5 / MS001 has named how architectural layers are validated and WS5 / MS002 has named how assessments are organised, Programme XI Workstream 5 still needs one fulfilment checklist:

> **What must be true before constitutional speech may affirm that the required layer assessment lifecycle has concluded, layer ordering / authority flow / dependency direction / bypass and cycle validations have completed, findings published, and the assessment formally closed?**

These criteria answer that question. They are **normative constitutional conditions**, not database predicates, CI job statuses, ticket states, import-scan greens, or API status codes.

All material affirmed completions require **CLIC-01 through CLIC-05** to hold. Failure of any criterion ⇒ **not yet** or **refuse close**. Do not invent a partial “mostly complete” layer-redefinition, authority-hierarchy-modification, meaning-reinterpretation, educational, or constitutional status.

| **CLIC-04** | Findings publication completed | Were required layer integrity findings lawfully constructed and published? |

## 3. Criterion Definitions

### CLIC-01 — Lifecycle completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every lifecycle stage required by the concern’s published WS5 / MS002 scope has been executed — or that a published refuse / defer / escalate disposition exhausted remaining *layer assessment* duties for that stage set with honest progression to finding construction / publication (or refuse-publish) and closure of what occurred.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Identifiable layer assessment concern opened under WS5 / MS001–MS002.
- Required CLIL-01…CLIL-08 stage identities for the concern scope.
- Stage traces / disposition records for each required stage (including lawful early stops).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed stage-fulfilment record naming which required stages completed.
- Or a lawful stop record naming which stages were refused / deferred / escalated and why remaining assessment duties are exhausted.

**Completion conditions.**

- Required stages map to published CLIL-01…CLIL-08 only.
- Mandatory initiation, discovery, ordering validation, authority-flow validation, dependency-direction validation, bypass and cycle validation, finding construction, and publication obligations for the concern are not silently omitted.
- Lawful early stops still leave honest stage outcomes; silent disappearance of a concern is a defect.
- Refusal of “pipeline skipped validation” or “we know it ran” without stage identity as completion.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That stage completion redefines constitutional layers or amends constitutional specifications.
- That stage completion modifies authority hierarchy, invents bypasses as intact, or freezes a stack as law.
- That stage completion means the learner succeeded or educational readiness was achieved.
- That skipped stages may be invented or implied from CI theatre.
- That Programme IX conformity / verification / compliance / certification was performed under stage progress.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who declares layer assessment “finished” without confirming each required assessment step occurred has not finished lawful care — even if the desk looks tidy.

---

### CLIC-02 — Layer assessment completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every named constitutional architectural layer required by the concern’s published scope was assessed under published CL-01…CL-04 identities and CLI-01…CLI-07 categories / CLIR rules — or that a published refuse disposition exhausted remaining *layer assessment* duties when required layers could not be lawfully assessed without inventing layer identities.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Named layer identities in scope (published under CL-01…CL-04).
- Published constitutional corpus identities under assessment.
- Assessment dispositions under applicable CLI types.
- Stage traces for CLIL-02 (Layer Discovery) as required by concern scope.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed layer-assessment record naming which layers were assessed under which published CLI categories.
- Or a lawful refuse / defer / escalate record naming which required layers remain unassessed and why.

**Completion conditions.**

- Layer subjects are named under CL-01…CL-04 (not “the corpus,” “the stack,” folder trees, or vendor labels alone).
- Assessment cites published constitutional specifications and CLI categories, not unpublished customs.
- Discovery named only published layers — inventing layers to force assessment is a hard stop.
- Layer outcomes report honestly; mutating or reordering layers to erase contradictions is a hard stop.
- Refusal of import scanners, Twin confidence, or CI greens as substitutes for named layer assessment.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That assessed layers were therefore rewritten, invented, reordered, or deleted.
- That a particular stack, language, or framework is privileged as law.
- That product quality or educational quality was judged.
- That constitutional specifications were modified to match the assessment.
- That Programme IX CT lineage preservation equals layer assessment fulfilment.
- That any single CLI type alone equals whole-concern completion (type outcomes feed CLIC-02 / CLIC-03; they do not replace CLIC-01…CLIC-05).

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who claims “the layers were checked” without naming which CL-01…CL-04 identities were assessed against which published categories has performed scanner theatre, not assessment fulfilment.

---

### CLIC-03 — Architectural validation completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every architectural validation required by the concern’s published scope has been lawfully completed under published CLI / CLIL law — confirming reconstructable layer ordering (CLIL-03 / CLI-01), authority flow (CLIL-04 / CLI-02), inter-layer dependency direction (CLIL-05 / CLI-03), and bypass and cycle absence (CLIL-06 / CLI-04 / CLI-05), with isolation honesty under CLI-06 confirmed as published composition requires — without inventing missing layers, inventing authority paths, inventing reverse authorship, or silently upgrading unvalidated / violated bypass or cycle findings into “intact.”

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Named layers under assessment (CLIC-02 inputs / CLIL-02 outputs).
- Published architectural-validation rules under WS5 / MS001–MS002 (especially CLIL-03…CLIL-06 and CLI-01…CLI-06 / CLIR fidelity rules).
- Boundary confirmations that validation does not redefine layers, modify authority hierarchy, or privilege a stack as law.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed architectural-validation record naming which ordering, authority-flow, dependency-direction, bypass, and cycle outcomes were validated (or honestly failed validation) for which layers / paths.
- Or a lawful refuse / defer / escalate record naming which required architectural validations remain incomplete and why.

**Completion conditions.**

- Ordering outcomes cite published CL-01 → CL-02 → CL-03 → CL-04 and lawful VI→…→XI order; layer inversion is a hard stop.
- Authority paths remain reconstructable to owning corpora; invented authority is a hard stop.
- Inter-layer dependencies respect superior → subordinate / consumer orientation; reverse authorship is a hard stop.
- Bypass and cycle outcomes report honestly; inventing intact status or hiding detected bypasses / cycles is a hard stop.
- “Architecture was checked somewhere” without named ordering / path / edge / bypass / cycle identity is not completion.
- Lawful failed / deferred / escalated architectural outcomes still satisfy *validation completion* when they are the honest outcome — completeness of *validation* is not completeness of *intact disposition*, and neither is constitutional authority creation or layer rewrite.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That validating architecture redefines layers, modifies authority hierarchy, or amends specifications.
- That a validated path freezes the implementation, stack, vendor, or release as permanent constitutional law.
- That a failed / deferred / escalated ordering, authority, dependency, bypass, or cycle outcome may be silently erased so close can look green.
- That MS001 layer meaning was replaced, skipped, or rewritten by architectural validation.
- That Educational Validation Framework quality review is complete.
- That educational success or exam readiness follows from architectural validation.
- That architectural validation alone equals whole-concern completion (architectural outcomes feed CLIC-03; they do not replace CLIC-01…CLIC-05).

**Tutor rationale.** Recording that ordering, authority flow, dependency direction, and bypass / cycle checks ran — including when they fail — is care. Inventing intact architecture so the assessment can finish is fiction. Completeness of *architectural validation* is not creation of *constitutional architecture*.

### CLIC-04 — Findings publication completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every layer integrity finding required by the concern’s published scope has been lawfully constructed and published under published CLI / CLIL rules — including intact, violated, deferred, and escalated dispositions — without rewriting those dispositions into layer redefinition, authority-hierarchy modification, meaning reinterpretation, specification amendment, or constitutional authority creation, and without inventing intact status or hiding violations.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Assessment and architectural-validation dispositions under applicable CLI-01…CLI-07 types (CLIL-03…CLIL-06 outputs).
- Published finding-construction and publication rules under WS5 / MS001–MS002 (especially CLIL-07 / CLIL-08).
- Boundary confirmations that findings do not invent law or rewrite layers / authority hierarchy / dependency direction.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Identifiable layer integrity finding identities with lawful dispositions, published under honesty rules.
- Or a lawful stop record naming why required findings could not be constructed / published without invention.

**Completion conditions.**

- Findings cite published constitutional references, named layers, and validated (or honestly failed) architectural outcomes.
- Dispositions remain within published vocabulary (intact / violated / deferred / escalated).
- “Assessment happened somewhere” without named finding identity and publication record is not completion.
- Lawful violated / deferred / escalated findings still satisfy *publication* when they are the honest outcome — completeness of *publication* is not completeness of *intact disposition*, and neither is constitutional amendment.
- Publication never invents intact status, hides violations, reorders layers, or freezes a stack as law.
- Continuity forbids silently replacing prior lawful findings under close theatre.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That publishing a finding redefines layers, modifies authority hierarchy, or amends constitutional specifications.
- That an intact finding freezes the implementation, stack, vendor, or release as permanent constitutional law.
- That a violated / deferred / escalated finding may be silently erased or upgraded so close can look green.
- That MS001 layer evaluation was replaced, skipped, or rewritten by publication.
- That Programme IX conformity / verification / compliance / certification was achieved by publication.
- That the learner succeeded or is exam-ready.

**Tutor rationale.** Publishing a hard layer disposition is not rewriting the syllabus — but omitting a required finding while claiming the assessment finished is dishonest. Completeness of *findings publication* is not completeness of *constitutional change*.

---

### CLIC-05 — Formal assessment closure

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that the layer assessment concern was formally closed under published CLIL-08 / layer honesty — exhausting remaining *layer assessment lifecycle* duties without redefining constitutional layers, modifying authority hierarchy, reinterpreting constitutional meaning, amending constitutional specifications, creating constitutional authority, or implying educational success.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Published findings and assessment records (CLIC-01…CLIC-04).
- CLIL-08 closure / publication honesty rules and `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md` hard stops.
- Explicit non-claim confirmations (layer redefinition / authority-hierarchy modification / meaning reinterpretation / specification amendment / authority creation / educational success).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Formal close disposition: affirmed complete / not yet / refuse close / defer / escalate.
- Layer preservation record confirming forbidden overclaims did not occur.

**Completion conditions.**

- Closure occurs only after required stages, layer assessment, architectural validation, findings publication, and assessment-record obligations for the concern are satisfied — or after a published stop exhausts remaining assessment duties with honest recording.
- Closure speech answers CLICQ-01…CLICQ-05 or explicitly refuses mute theatre.
- Closure never silently upgrades findings dispositions or rewrites layers / authority hierarchy / dependency direction.
- Refusal of ticket closure, PR merge, import-scan greens, or pipeline greens alone as formal constitutional close.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That formal close redefines, reorders, invents, or deletes constitutional layers.
- That formal close modifies authority hierarchy or invents authority paths.
- That formal close reinterprets Programme VI–X / EIP meaning.
- That formal close amends constitutional specifications.
- That formal close creates constitutional authority.
- That formal close means the implementation is constitutionally conformant, verified, compliant, or certified.
- That formal close means the student succeeded or is exam-ready.
- That formal close freezes a stack, vendor, or release as permanent constitutional law.

**Tutor rationale.** Closing the assessment folder is care only when the required steps, layer assessments, architectural checks, findings, and records exist — and only when nobody pretends the close is a new constitution, a rewritten architecture, or a pass certificate for the candidate.

## 4. Criterion Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CLIC-01 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CLIC-02 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CLIC-03 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CLIC-04 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — mute complete badges are defects |
| CLIC-05 fails | Affirmation is unlawful even if earlier stages appear present |
| Optional CLIC-06…CLIC-10 fail | Treat as honesty defects; do not soft-pass CLIC-01…CLIC-05 |
| Any criterion “passes” only by redefining layers / modifying authority hierarchy / reinterpreting meaning / amending specifications / creating authority / implying educational success | The implied overclaim voids lawful affirmation |

All of CLIC-01…CLIC-05 are **conjunctive** for material affirmed completion. There is no lawful “four of five” educational, fidelity, layer-redefinition, or constitutional status.

Layer ordering, authority flow, dependency direction, and bypass / cycle validations are **not** sixth-through-ninth catalogue criteria. They are required assessment obligations under CLIC-03 (and required stages under CLIC-01 via CLIL-03…CLIL-06), with named layer assessment specialised under CLIC-02. Affirming close without those validations where the concern required them fails CLIC-01 and/or CLIC-02 and/or CLIC-03.

## 5. Lawful Stop Dispositions

Not every incomplete concern is a defect. Published WS5 / MS002 refuse / defer / escalate / violated outcomes may **exhaust remaining layer assessment duties** for a concern without affirming lifecycle fulfilment as “complete” in the celebratory sense — while still requiring honest recording and formal close of what occurred.

| Disposition | Constitutional meaning | May affirm CLIC completion? |
|-------------|------------------------|----------------------------|
| **Affirm complete** | CLIC-01…CLIC-05 hold for the concern | Yes |
| **Not yet** | Required stages, layer assessment, architectural validation, findings, or records remain outstanding | No |
| **Refuse close** | Affirmation would invent stages / findings, redefine layers, modify hierarchy, invent authority, or overclaim | No |
| **Defer** | Published wait for upstream corpus / layer / path identity | No (unless published law says remaining assessment duties are exhausted) |
| **Escalate** | Published stop requiring human / corpus governance attention | No (unless published law says remaining assessment duties are exhausted) |

A lawful stop that exhausts assessment duties is **honest unfinished fulfilment or honest refusal** — never stealth layer redefinition, authority-hierarchy modification, meaning reinterpretation, specification amendment, constitutional authority creation, educational success, or Programme IX evaluative disposition.

---

## 6. Non-Criteria

The following are **not** constitutional completion criteria:

- CI pipeline greens, flaky-scan suppression, or job exit codes;
- ticket closure, PR merge, or documentation checklist ticks alone;
- import scanners, folder adjacency heuristics, or OpenAPI linkers alone;
- coverage percentages, lint scores, or elapsed time;
- Twin / Adaptive / Experience / Mission confidence signals;
- UI celebration states or analytics milestones;
- “looks layered enough for the demo”;
- MS001 intact disposition alone (that is evaluation outcome, not whole-concern CLIC fulfilment);
- Programme XI WS1–WS4 CDIC / CCIC / CBIC / CVIC completion alone (orthogonal corpus horizons);
- Programme IX CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC completion alone (orthogonal implementation horizons);
- Educational Validation Framework quality release (orthogonal);
- any implication that constitutional layers changed, authority hierarchy was modified, specifications were amended, or authority was created because assessment closed.
