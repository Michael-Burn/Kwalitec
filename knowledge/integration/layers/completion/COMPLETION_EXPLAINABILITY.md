## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which constitutional layers* were assessed — which authority paths were validated, which dependency relationships were validated, whether any bypasses or cycles were identified, and which findings were published — is not closing honestly; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–X have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, governance, and execution speech speakable, and after WS5 / MS001–MS002 have made layer and lifecycle speech speakable, Programme XI still needs one layer-assessment-completion-speech answer:

> **“Which constitutional layers were assessed, which authority paths were validated, which dependency relationships were validated, were any bypasses or cycles identified, and which findings were published?”**

That answer must cover every material affirmed (or refused) completion without inventing educational success, redefining constitutional layers, modifying authority hierarchy, reinterpreting constitutional meaning, amending constitutional specifications, creating constitutional authority, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, or freezing a stack as law.

## 2. What Layer Integrity Completion Explainability Is

**Constitutional Layer Integrity Completion Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **fulfilment (or lawful refusal of fulfilment) of the required constitutional layer integrity assessment lifecycle** — not to invent a second tutor voice from CI complete badges, and not to grade the student, redefine layers, modify authority hierarchy, or amend constitutional specifications.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Layers assessed** | Named CL-01…CL-04 constitutional architectural layers evaluated under CLI law | Which constitutional layers were assessed? |
| **Authority paths validated** | Named superior → subordinate authority paths and outcomes | Which authority paths were validated? |
| **Dependency relationships validated** | Named inter-layer dependency edges and direction outcomes | Which dependency relationships were validated? |
| **Bypasses / cycles identified** | Named bypass and cycle findings (including none / violated / deferred / escalated) | Were any bypasses or cycles identified? |
| **Findings published** | Named layer integrity findings with lawful dispositions | Which findings were published? |

Layer Integrity Completion Explainability is:

- **fulfilment-centred** — it narrates why CLIC criteria hold (or why close is refused);
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CLI / CL / CLIL / CLIC identities where applicable;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published specifications, named layers, authority paths, dependency relationships, bypasses / cycles, and named findings;
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to create authority or amend corpora;
- **non-mutating** — explanation never narrates completion as layer rewrite, hierarchy inversion, or dependency direction invention;
- **finding-honest** — explanation never silently upgrades published findings;
- **quality-honest** — complete is never narrated as mastery, readiness, or educational quality;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence / validation speech, Programme IX evaluative speech, Programme X execution speech, MS001 layer speech, or MS002 lifecycle speech;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match CLIC reconstruction needs;
- **authority-honest** — explanations must not paper over authority inference or stack canonisation.

Layer Integrity Completion Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–X / WS5 meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a constitutional authority instrument or amendment warrant;
- a silent layer-rewrite, hierarchy-modification, or finding-upgrade mechanism;
- a Programme IX evaluative-finish report as constitutional law;
- a substitute for WS5 / MS001 layer explainability or WS5 / MS002 lifecycle explainability (compose with; do not replace);
- a substitute for Programme XI WS1–WS4 CDIC / CCIC / CBIC / CVIC completion explainability (compose with; do not collapse);
- a substitute for Programme IX CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC completion explainability (compose with; do not collapse);
- a rendering system, UI template, CI badge, graph visualiser, or analytics dashboard;
- a claim that clear completion speech guarantees learning, a pass, conformity, constitutional amendment, or layer rewrite;
- a licence to present import scanners, lint scores, ticket graphs, or pipeline greens as constitutional proof or educational quality.

## 3. Mandatory Explanation Questions

Every material completion judgement (affirm or refuse close) should answer all five questions:

### CLICQ-01 — Which constitutional layers were assessed?

Name the primary CL-01…CL-04 layer identities evaluated under the assessment concern — including applicable CLI-01…CLI-07 categories — or name which required layers remain unassessed when refusing close. Never narrate invented layers, folder trees, or vendor labels as lawful assessment subjects. “The corpus was scanned somewhere” is not a constitutional layer identity.

### CLICQ-02 — Which authority paths were validated?

Name the primary superior → subordinate authority-path identities and outcomes validated under the concern — or name which required authority paths remain unvalidated when refusing close. Include lawful failed / deferred / escalated authority-flow outcomes honestly. Never narrate invented authority as validated flow, and never narrate absorbed superior rights as lawful hierarchy validation. “Looks authorised enough” is not a constitutional authority-path identity.

### CLICQ-03 — Which dependency relationships were validated?

Name the primary inter-layer dependency-edge identities and direction outcomes validated under the concern — or name which required dependency relationships remain unvalidated when refusing close. Include lawful failed / deferred / escalated direction outcomes honestly. Never narrate reverse authorship as lawful dependency validation. “Looks connected enough” is not a constitutional dependency-relationship identity.

### CLICQ-04 — Were any bypasses or cycles identified?

Name the primary bypass and cycle finding identities and dispositions under the concern (including explicit “none identified” when that is the honest published outcome) — or name which required bypass / cycle validations remain incomplete when refusing close. Never narrate a violated / deferred / escalated bypass or cycle finding as upgraded by completion, and never narrate silent replacement of a prior lawful finding. “Looks acyclic enough” is not a constitutional bypass / cycle identity.

### CLICQ-05 — Which findings were published?

Name the primary layer integrity finding identities and dispositions published under the concern (intact / violated / deferred / escalated) — or name which required findings remain unpublished when refusing close. Confirm the hard stops in `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md` that were checked — especially non-redefinition of layers, non-modification of authority hierarchy, non-reinterpretation of constitutional meaning, non-amendment of constitutional specifications, non-creation of constitutional authority, implementation neutrality, repeatability honesty, and non-equation of completion with educational success, Programme IX evaluative completion, WS1–WS4 assessment completion, or constitutional truth.

## 5. Audience Projections

| Audience | Lawful speech | Unlawful speech |
|----------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Student** | Plain-language statement that the architectural layer check finished (or why it could not), without claiming mastery or a pass | “You are ready because layers are complete”; “the system rewrote the syllabus architecture” |
| **Developer** | Named layers, stages, CLI / CLIL / CLIC identities, and finding dispositions sufficient to reconstruct the close | Mute complete badges; invented layers; stack canonisation as law |
| **Auditor** | Reconstructable CLIC-01…CLIC-05 outcomes, CLICQ answers, boundary preservation, continuity honesty | Mute “complete” badges, invented layers, invented authority paths, silent finding upgrades |

Student, developer, and auditor projections share **one truth** with different vocabulary. They never diverge into contradictory constitutional stories.

## 6. Explainability Hard Stops

Explanation is constitutionally defective when it:

- cannot name layers, authority paths, dependency relationships, bypass / cycle outcomes, or findings for a material close;
- narrates completion as layer redefinition, authority-hierarchy modification, meaning reinterpretation, specification amendment, or constitutional authority creation;
- upgrades violated / deferred / escalated findings into intact under close theatre;
- equates completion with educational success, Programme IX evaluative completion, or WS1–WS4 assessment completion;
- presents CI greens, import scanners, tickets, or elapsed time as constitutional proof;
- freezes a stack, vendor, or release as permanent constitutional law;
- erases prior lawful assessment history to tidy the narrative.
