## 2. What Constitutional Layer Integrity Is

**Constitutional Layer Integrity** is the binding mechanism by which **architectural layering** throughout the published constitutional corpus may be **defined, validated, and governed**, yielding **layer integrity findings** that preserve layer ordering, authority hierarchy, lawful inter-layer dependency direction, architectural isolation, bypass absence, cycle absence, and explainability — without becoming a source of constitutional law, a rewriter of layers, or a substitute for upstream programme meaning.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Published constitutional corpus** | Constitution, EIP, Programme VI–XI specifications and their subordinate workstream / milestone corpora | What corpus is under layer assessment? |
| **Constitutional architectural layer** | A recognised CL-01…CL-04 band of constitutional authority and responsibility | Which layer is being assessed? |
| **Layer ordering** | The published superior → subordinate sequence among CL-01…CL-04 (and lawful programme order within CL-03) | Is layer order intact? |
| **Authority flow** | Reconstructable path by which superior layers lawfully authorise subordinate layers | Which authority paths were validated? |
| **Inter-layer dependency direction** | Lawful orientation of consumption / subordination between layers (never reverse authorship) | Does dependency respect layer direction? |
| **Layer bypass** | A relationship or claim that skips a required superior layer to obtain authority or meaning unlawfully | Were any layer bypasses detected? |
| **Architectural cycle** | A closed path among layers that would circularise or invert constitutional authority | Were any architectural cycles detected? |
| **Architectural isolation** | Separation of concerns among layers so one layer does not absorb another’s authorship | Is isolation preserved? |
| **Layer integrity finding** | Lawful disposition: intact / violated / deferred / escalated — never “new law” and never a rewrite of published layers | Does layering remain lawful? |
| **Constitutional authority** | The exclusive right of published corpora to define educational and execution meaning | Who owns the standard? |
| **Implementation neutrality** | Layer validation speaks to constitutional identities, not to a preferred stack as law | Remains the stack replaceable? |
| **Auditability** | Every material assessment leaves reconstructable constitutional traces | Can the judgement be reconstructed? |
| **Explainability** | Layers, authority paths, bypasses, cycles, findings, and assessment limits are speakable | Can the judgement be narrated honestly? |

Constitutional Layer Integrity is:

- **validation-centred** — it validates published architectural layering; it never authors or rewrites layers;
- **order-bound** — recognised layers remain in published superior → subordinate sequence;
- **authority-preserving** — every material authority path remains reconstructable through lawful layers;
- **direction-bound** — inter-layer dependencies respect published superior → subordinate / consumer orientation;
- **bypass-resistant** — skipping required superior layers to obtain authority or meaning is a hard defect;
- **acyclic** — closed paths that circularise or invert layer authority are hard defects;
- **isolation-preserving** — architectural separation of concerns among layers remains intact;
- **catalogue-closed** — only CLI-01…CLI-07 may be applied as constitutional layer integrity categories; only CL-01…CL-04 may classify architectural layers;
- **corpus-bound** — every material assessment cites identifiable published specifications and layer identities;
- **finding-producing** — assessments emit intact / violated / deferred / escalated dispositions;
- **audit-capable** — every material assessment leaves reconstructable constitutional traces;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which layers were assessed under which authority paths with which findings;
- **non-legislative** — a layer finding never becomes constitutional law or a soft amendment;
- **non-mutating** — the model never modifies constitutional specifications, layers, or authority flow under validation pretext;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by layer law.

Constitutional Layer Integrity is **not**:

- a second Educational Constitution, EIP, or Programme VI–X amendment path;
- a licence to reinterpret coach meaning, ownership maps, tip warrants, runtime contracts, conformance criteria, verification meaning, compliance meaning, certification meaning, evolution meaning, execution meaning, dependency meaning, catalogue meaning, boundary meaning, or vocabulary meaning in layer prose;
- a testing framework, CI/CD pipeline, GitHub Action, package-dependency scanner, folder-adjacency rule, or OpenAPI linker;
- a claim that “layer intact” guarantees learning, mastery, exam readiness, product success, perpetual conformity, or frozen stack authority;
- a substitute for Programme IX MS002 constitutional traceability — CT preserves spec/artefact/finding lineage; CLI validates *constitutional architectural layer integrity*;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS1 dependency integrity — CDI validates *corpus dependency structure*; CLI validates *architectural layering*;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS2 catalogue integrity — CCI validates *catalogue uniqueness and namespace honesty*; CLI validates *layer architecture*;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS3 boundary integrity — CBI validates *boundary consistency* (CBI-06 reports layer-boundary concerns); CLI owns *architectural layer validation*;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS4 vocabulary integrity — CVI validates *terminology semantics*; CLI validates *layer architecture*;
- a substitute for Programme IX conformance / verification / compliance / certification — those evaluate implementations and obligations; CLI validates *layer corpus consistency*;
- a substitute for Programme X execution / decision / audit / explainability — those organise constitutional activity; CLI never executes, decides, audits, or explains educational outcomes in their place;
- a licence to create, reorder, invent, or delete constitutional layers under “integrity” pretext.

## 3. Layer Integrity Posture (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material constitutional layer integrity assessment travels through three constitutional phases. These are **normative assessment obligations**, not an implementation architecture.

```
Identify published layers and authority paths under assessment
        │
        ▼
Validate ordering, authority flow, dependency direction,
bypass absence, and cycle absence under CLI-01…CLI-07
        │
        ├── violated / deferred / escalated (lawful finding — layers unchanged)
        │
        └── intact → Emit findings and preserve audit records
                │
                ▼
        Explain layers / authority paths / bypasses / cycles / findings / assessment limits
```

| Phase | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **Identify** | Name only published constitutional corpus identities, recognised CL-01…CL-04 layers, and reconstructable authority paths | Layer map is unpublished tribal custom, invented stack diagram, or “how folders sit today” as law |
| **Validate** | Apply CLI-01…CLI-07 for ordering, authority flow, dependency direction, bypass absence, and cycle absence | Assessment would invent law, redefine layers, reverse authority, or privilege a stack as law |
| **Record** | Produce findings and audit-speakable trails without amending corpora or mutating layers | Path would treat findings as constitutional amendments or soft redefinitions of published layers |

> **Layer integrity never “best-effort invents” a fourth phase called law-making, layer rewriting, or stack canonisation.**

## 6. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Layer Integrity Model exists so that:

1. **Constitutional layering remains ordered** — assessments confirm CL-01…CL-04 (and lawful programme order within CL-03) as published.
2. **Authority hierarchy remains honest** — superior layers continue to authorise subordinate layers; subordinates never silently become superiors.
3. **Dependency direction remains lawful** — inter-layer edges respect superior → subordinate / consumer orientation.
4. **Architectural isolation remains intact** — layers do not absorb each other’s authorship under readiness pressure.
5. **Bypasses remain forbidden** — skipping required superior layers cannot launder authority or meaning.
6. **Cycles remain forbidden** — closed paths that circularise layer authority cannot pass as intact.
7. **Implementation readiness remains speakable** — intact layer structure may inform readiness; it never itself certifies learning, mastery, or product success.
8. **Students remain protected** — product urgency cannot launder inverted layering as “approved architecture.”
