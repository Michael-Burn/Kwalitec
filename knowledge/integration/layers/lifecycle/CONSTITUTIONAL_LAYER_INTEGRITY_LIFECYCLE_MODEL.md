## 2. What the Constitutional Layer Integrity Lifecycle Is

The **constitutional layer integrity lifecycle** is the binding organisation by which **layer integrity assessments** may proceed through recognised stages — initiating against published corpus identities, discovering recognised CL-01…CL-04 layers, validating ordering / authority flow / dependency direction / bypass absence / cycle absence under published CLI-01…CLI-07 categories, and constructing / publishing **layer integrity findings** that preserve layer ordering, authority hierarchy, lawful inter-layer dependency direction, architectural isolation, bypass absence, cycle absence, repeatability, consistency, implementation neutrality, auditability, and explainability — without becoming a source of constitutional law, a rewriter of layers or authority hierarchy, a substitute for MS001 layer meaning, or a replacement for upstream programme authority.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Published constitutional corpus** | Constitution, EIP, Programme VI–XI specifications and their subordinate workstream / milestone corpora | Which corpus may the lifecycle organise assessment against? |
| **Constitutional architectural layer** | A recognised CL-01…CL-04 band of constitutional authority and responsibility | Which layer is under assessment? |
| **Layer ordering** | The published superior → subordinate sequence among CL-01…CL-04 (and lawful programme order within CL-03) | Is layer order intact? |
| **Authority flow** | Reconstructable path by which superior layers lawfully authorise subordinate layers | Which authority paths were validated? |
| **Inter-layer dependency direction** | Lawful orientation of consumption / subordination between layers (never reverse authorship) | Which dependency paths were validated? |
| **Layer bypass** | A relationship or claim that skips a required superior layer to obtain authority or meaning unlawfully | Were any bypasses identified? |
| **Architectural cycle** | A closed path among layers that would circularise or invert constitutional authority | Were any cycles identified? |
| **Lifecycle stage** | A recognised CLIL-01…CLIL-08 organisational step | Which coordination step is active? |
| **Layer integrity finding** | Lawful disposition: intact / violated / deferred / escalated — never “new law” and never a rewrite of published layers | Does layering remain lawful? |
| **Assessment record / publication artefact** | Audit-speakable record of layers, authority paths, dependency paths, bypasses, cycles, stages, findings, and preserved limits | Can the assessment be reconstructed? |
| **Constitutional authority** | The exclusive right of published corpora to define educational and execution meaning | Who owns the standard? |
| **Implementation neutrality** | Lifecycle coordination is judged against law and published layers, not against a preferred stack, vendor, or topology | Remains the stack replaceable? |
| **Repeatability** | Same published corpora, same discovered layers, same stage sequence → same disposition | Can the judgement be reproduced? |
| **Auditability** | Every material lifecycle leaves reconstructable constitutional traces | Can the judgement be reconstructed? |
| **Explainability** | Layers, authority paths, dependency paths, bypasses, cycles, findings, stages, and limits are speakable | Can the judgement be narrated honestly? |

The constitutional layer integrity lifecycle is:

- **coordination-centred** — it organises layer assessments; it never authors constitutional obligations or layers;
- **discovery-bound** — it discovers recognised published layers; it never invents layers to force progress;
- **order-preserving** — published CL-01…CL-04 (and VI→…→XI) order remains reconstructable across stages;
- **authority-preserving** — published authority paths remain reconstructable and unabsorbed across stages;
- **direction-bound** — inter-layer dependencies respect published superior → subordinate / consumer orientation;
- **bypass-resistant** — skipping required superior layers to obtain authority or meaning is a hard defect;
- **acyclic** — closed paths that circularise or invert layer authority are hard defects;
- **isolation-preserving** — architectural separation of concerns among layers remains intact;
- **catalogue-closed** — only CLIL-01…CLIL-08 may be applied as constitutional layer integrity lifecycle stages;
- **type-faithful** — stages apply only published CL-01…CL-04 layers, CLI-01…CLI-07 categories, and CLIR validation rules from WS5 / MS001; they never invent layers or categories;
- **finding-producing** — assessments emit intact / violated / deferred / escalated dispositions;
- **publication-honest** — findings are published under honesty rules; publication never rewrites layers or becomes law;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by lifecycle law;
- **corpus-bound** — every material lifecycle cites identifiable published specifications and layer identities;
- **audit-capable** — every material lifecycle leaves reconstructable constitutional traces;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which layers were assessed under which authority and dependency paths with which findings;
- **non-legislative** — a layer finding never becomes constitutional law or a soft amendment;
- **non-substitutive** — lifecycle machinery never becomes the constitutional standard for the next judgement;
- **non-mutating** — the lifecycle never modifies constitutional specifications, layers, or authority hierarchy under coordination pretext.

The constitutional layer integrity lifecycle is **not**:

- a second Educational Constitution, EIP, Programme VI–X, or WS5 / MS001 amendment path;
- a licence to reinterpret coach meaning, ownership maps, tip warrants, runtime contracts, conformance criteria, traceability meaning, verification meaning, compliance meaning, certification meaning, evolution meaning, execution meaning, dependency meaning, catalogue meaning, boundary meaning, vocabulary meaning, or CLI / CL catalogues in lifecycle prose;
- a testing framework, CI/CD pipeline, GitHub Action, import-graph scanner, folder-adjacency rule, or OpenAPI linker;
- a claim that “lifecycle complete” guarantees learning, mastery, exam readiness, product success, perpetual conformity, or frozen stack authority;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS5 / MS001 layer integrity meaning — CLI defines categories and validation; CLIL sequences when assessment occurs;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS1 dependency integrity — CDI / CDIL validate and sequence *corpus dependency structure*; CLIL coordinates *architectural layer assessment*;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS2 catalogue integrity — CCI / CCIL validate and sequence *catalogue uniqueness*; CLIL coordinates *architectural layer assessment*;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS3 boundary integrity — CBI / CBIL validate and sequence *boundary consistency* (CBI-06 reports layer-boundary concerns); CLIL coordinates *architectural layer integrity assessment*;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS4 vocabulary integrity — CVI / CVIL validate and sequence *terminology semantics*; CLIL coordinates *architectural layer assessment*;
- a substitute for Programme IX MS002 constitutional traceability — CT preserves spec/artefact/finding lineage; CLIL coordinates *constitutional layer integrity assessment sequencing*;
- a substitute for Programme IX conformance / verification / compliance / certification — those evaluate implementations and obligations; CLIL validates *layer corpus consistency sequencing*;
- a substitute for Programme X execution / decision / audit / explainability — those organise constitutional activity; CLIL never executes, decides, audits, or explains educational outcomes in their place;

## 3. Lifecycle Posture (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material constitutional layer integrity lifecycle travels through eight constitutional stages. These are **normative coordination obligations**, not an implementation architecture and not a CI job graph.

```
CLIL-01 Assessment Initiation
        │
        ▼
CLIL-02 Layer Discovery
        │
        ▼
CLIL-03 Layer Ordering Validation
        │
        ▼
CLIL-04 Authority Flow Validation
        │
        ▼
CLIL-05 Dependency Direction Validation
        │
        ▼
CLIL-06 Bypass and Cycle Validation
        │
        ├── violated / deferred / escalated (lawful finding — layers unchanged)
        │
        └── intact (or other lawful disposition) →
                │
                ▼
CLIL-07 Finding Construction
        │
        ▼
CLIL-08 Finding Publication
        │
        ▼
Explain layers / authority paths / dependency paths /
bypasses / cycles / findings / intact limits
```

| Stage | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **CLIL-01 Assessment Initiation** | Open a layer assessment concern against named published corpora and scope | Scope is unpublished custom, tribal diagram, or “how main is organised today” |
| **CLIL-02 Layer Discovery** | Identify recognised published layers under CL-01…CL-04 | Layers are invented, unpublished, or would require rewriting layer maps to “discover” them |
| **CLIL-03 Layer Ordering Validation** | Validate published CL-01…CL-04 (and VI→…→XI) order | Validation would invent layers, invert order, privilege a stack as law, or rewrite maps |
| **CLIL-04 Authority Flow Validation** | Validate reconstructable superior → subordinate authority paths | Validation would invent authority paths, absorb superiors, or rewrite hierarchy |
| **CLIL-05 Dependency Direction Validation** | Validate lawful inter-layer dependency orientation | Validation would reverse authorship, invent edges, or mutate published direction |
| **CLIL-06 Bypass and Cycle Validation** | Detect unlawful superior skips and authority-circularising closed paths | Validation would invent “temporary bypasses,” hide cycles, or rewrite graphs to pass |
| **CLIL-07 Finding Construction** | Emit intact / violated / deferred / escalated findings under published criteria | Findings would become amendments, educational quality grades, or layer rewrites |
| **CLIL-08 Finding Publication** | Publish findings and assessment records under published honesty rules | Publication would invent intact status, hide bypasses/cycles, rewrite layers, or freeze a stack as law |

> **The lifecycle never “best-effort invents” a ninth stage called law-making, layer rewriting, authority-hierarchy mutation, stack canonisation, or layer map soft-amendment.**

## 6. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Layer Integrity Lifecycle Model exists so that:

1. **Law remains upstream of coordination** — stages organise assessment against published corpora; progress does not quietly become the specifications.
2. **Discovery remains honest** — discovery names recognised published layers; it does not invent layer maps to force intact status.
3. **Ordering remains reconstructable** — ordering validation confirms published CL-01…CL-04 sequence; it does not invent or invert layers.
4. **Authority remains hierarchical** — authority-flow validation confirms superior → subordinate paths; it never rewrites hierarchy for convenience.
5. **Dependency direction remains faithful** — direction validation reports lawful orientation; it never reverse-authors superiors from below.
6. **Bypasses and cycles remain speakable** — bypass and cycle validation reports defects honestly; it never hides them for greenery.
7. **Findings remain sequenced** — intact / violated / deferred / escalated dispositions arise after lawful initiation, discovery, and validation — not as random green theatre.
8. **Authority remains honest** — no team may treat a published finding as the new constitutional source.
9. **Implementations remain replaceable** — lifecycle law does not freeze Flask, SQLAlchemy, a particular API shape, or a CI vendor as law.
10. **Repeatability remains honest** — the same published corpora, discovered layers, and stage sequence yield the same disposition.
11. **Audit remains possible** — every material lifecycle can be reconstructed against named layers, stages, authority paths, dependency paths, bypasses, cycles, and findings.
12. **Explainability remains intact** — lifecycle speech describes coordination of layer assessment; it does not redefine what learning means.
13. **Students remain protected** — product theatre cannot launder inverted, bypassed, or cyclic layering as “lifecycle intact.”
