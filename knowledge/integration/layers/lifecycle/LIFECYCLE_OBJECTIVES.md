## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “scanner finished,” “stages skipped for speed,” or “layer badge shown.” The tutor optimises **honest organisation of layer assessment work**: only a repeatable, consistent, implementation-neutral, explainable, and auditable sequence — discovering recognised CL-01…CL-04 layers and validating ordering, authority flow, dependency direction, bypass absence, and cycle absence under CLI-01…CLI-07 — may support lawful layer integrity findings.

These objectives bind every constitutional layer integrity lifecycle and every successor coordination machinery that claims constitutional layer integrity lifecycle meaning.

## 2. Primary Objective

### CLILO-01 — Preserve constitutional layering

**Definition.** Ensure that every material layer integrity lifecycle organises assessments through recognised CLIL-01…CLIL-08 stages — initiating against identifiable published corpora, discovering named layers under CL-01…CL-04, validating layer ordering, validating authority flow, validating dependency direction, validating absence of bypasses and cycles, and constructing / publishing findings under published CLI-01…CLI-07 categories — so that findings occur only after lawful initiation and discovery, and that findings are published without becoming law, layer rewrites, authority-hierarchy mutations, or replacements for constitutional authority.

**Includes:**

- Binding every material lifecycle to identifiable published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–XI specifications and subordinate workstream / milestone corpora.
- Discovering only recognised published layers as inputs under CL-01…CL-04.
- Applying only published CLI-01…CLI-07 categories and CLIR validation rules across stages.
- Preferring lawful violated / deferred / escalated / refuse-progress outcomes over rewriting law, inventing layer maps, inventing authority paths, hiding bypasses / cycles, or mutating corpora so the lifecycle “completes.”
- Keeping lifecycle progress distinct from educational quality, mastery, readiness theatre, product success, import-graph greenery, or constitutional amendment.
- Preserving the Model’s posture: stages coordinate assessment; they do not become law or constitutional authority.

**Excludes:**

- Treating green CI, coverage percentages, linter scores, Twin estimates, Adaptive scores, UI ticks, import graphs, or folder-adjacency scans as lifecycle fulfilment.
- Soft-amending specifications, inventing unpublished customs, inventing or reordering layers, inventing authority paths, or privileging “how main is organised” to force progress.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for lawful coordination.
- Declaring “close enough” publication without documenting a corpus amendment.
- Freezing an implementation as permanently lawful because the lifecycle published findings.
- Creating constitutional authority under a lifecycle badge.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software pretends unfinished or dishonest layer assessment is fine because integration needs a finished green architecture report. Coordination honesty is care; lifecycle theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CLILO-02 — Preserve authority hierarchy

**Definition.** Ensure that every material lifecycle validates — and leaves reconstructable — published superior → subordinate authority paths so subordinates never silently become foundational authority, and superior meaning is never authored from below through stage progress.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who lets a worksheet quietly claim syllabus authorship has abandoned institutional order for convenience.

**Manifestations:**

- CLIL-04 requires reconstructable published authority paths; silent absorption of superiors or invented path identity is a defect.
- Findings cite owning layers and authority-path identities, not only ticket IDs or commit SHAs.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing prior lawful assessment history to simplify the next report.
- Authority-hierarchy preservation never authorises inventing missing authority paths or rewriting published hierarchy.

---

### CLILO-03 — Preserve dependency direction

**Definition.** Ensure that lifecycle organisation preserves published superior → subordinate / consumer orientation among inter-layer dependencies — so reverse authorship, invented edges, and silent direction flips are reported honestly rather than papered over for progress.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who lets a later chapter rewrite an earlier foundation so a progress chart stays green has abandoned care for theatre.

**Manifestations:**

- CLIL-05 applies published CLI-03 / CLIR-03 rules for inter-layer dependency direction.
- Reverse authorship without published warrant is a hard defect, not a soft variable.
- Forbidden actions in `LIFECYCLE_BOUNDARIES.md` are hard stops.
- Dependency-direction preservation never authorises inventing, reversing, or deleting published inter-layer edges.

---

### CLILO-04 — Preserve architectural isolation

**Definition.** Ensure that lifecycle organisation preserves separation of concerns among CL-01…CL-04 — and among Programme VI→…→XI within CL-03 — so silent layer absorption, bypass-assisted collapse, and mega-law mergers are reported honestly rather than celebrated as “simpler integration.”

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who collapses syllabus, marking scheme, and scanner into one blob so the study plan “looks unified” has abandoned institutional clarity.

**Manifestations:**

- CLIL-03…CLIL-06 apply published CLI / CLIR rules for ordering, authority, direction, bypass, cycle, and isolation honesty.
- Isolation breaches under CLI-06 are reported as absorption, bypass-assisted collapse, or violated — never soft-merged into intact.
- Architectural-isolation preservation never authorises inventing layers or absorbing superiors via lifecycle prose.
- Compose with CBI-06 / CDI layering rules; never treat them as automatic substitutes for CLI / CLIL honesty.

---

### CLILO-05 — Preserve assessment repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that the same published corpus identities, the same discovered layers, the same published CLI / CLIR criteria, and the same CLIL stage sequence yield the same layer disposition (intact / violated / deferred / escalated) and the same lawful stage outcomes — absent a published non-deterministic exception (none are granted by this Model for constitutional meaning or integrity status) — and that every material lifecycle remains explainable and auditable under `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who treats the same syllabus layering as lawful or not depending on which reviewer woke first — or skips stages randomly — is not coaching; they are gambling. A tutor who cannot later say *why* a layer map was accepted cannot be trusted.

**Manifestations:**

- Only CLIL-01…CLIL-08 may organise constitutional layer integrity lifecycles.
- Stage purpose, inputs, outputs, and permitted / prohibited activities remain as published in `LIFECYCLE_STAGES.md`.
- Stage transitions and outcomes are corpus-bound and reproducible.
- Parallel CI runners, sharding, and cache races must not change constitutional disposition or invent stage skips.
- Explicit multi-type CLI sets use published conjunction / published stop rules, not random reviewer races.
- “Eventually consistent lifecycle meaning” is a constitutional defect.
- Invented or unstable layer inputs, and invented authority / dependency paths, are hard stops — not soft variables.
- Intact findings published after CLIL-01…CLIL-08 may inform downstream readiness honesty; they never themselves certify educational readiness, mastery, or product success.
- Explainability obligations apply to all material lifecycles (layers assessed, authority paths, dependency paths, bypasses / cycles, findings produced, limits remaining intact).

## 5. Non-Objectives

The constitutional layer integrity lifecycle does **not** optimise for:

- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- CI throughput, flaky-test suppression, or coverage theatre as educational success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when specifications or published layers are incomplete;
- collapsing CLIL stages into one mega-step that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics / import-graph / folder-adjacency signals as substitutes for published constitutional layers;
- freezing implementations, releases, or stacks as permanently lawful;
- treating documentation as optional commentary on whatever code already shipped;
- replacing MS001 layer integrity meaning, Programme XI WS1–WS4 integrity horizons, Programme IX CT lineage preservation, or Programme IX–X evaluative / execution horizons;
- rewriting, inventing, reordering, or deleting constitutional layers so reports look tidy;
- modifying authority hierarchy under coordination pretence;
- creating constitutional authority under a lifecycle badge;
- reinterpreting constitutional meaning or amending constitutional specifications under coordination pretence.
