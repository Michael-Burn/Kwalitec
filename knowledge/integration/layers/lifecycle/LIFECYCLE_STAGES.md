## 1. Purpose

Layer assessment without a closed stage catalogue invents law by proximity: whichever scanner step, ticket status, or reviewer habit happens to finish becomes the tutor’s “proof the system ran a constitutional layer integrity assessment.”

This catalogue names the only lawful constitutional layer integrity lifecycle stages — and binds each stage to purpose, inputs, outputs, and permitted / prohibited activities.

---

## 2. Stage Principles

1. **Ordered and skip-disciplined.** Stages may be brief under thin scope; mandatory initiation, discovery, ordering / authority / dependency / bypass-cycle validation, finding construction, and publication obligations may not be silently omitted when a layer integrity finding is claimed.
2. **Published layers before findings.** Identifiable published corpora and discovered layers are confirmed before intact / violated dispositions are constructed.
3. **Explainability accumulates.** Each stage leaves traceable artefacts for `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.
4. **Refusal is a stage outcome.** Violated, deferred, escalated, and refuse-progress are first-class conclusions.
5. **Deterministic transitions.** Same published inputs ⇒ same next stage outcomes.
6. **Stages coordinate; they do not legislate.** No stage creates, modifies, or redefines constitutional law.
7. **Stages do not become constitutional authority.** Publication never freezes a corpus topology as permanently lawful or replaces published corpora.
8. **Stages do not rewrite layers.** Findings report consistency; they never create, reorder, redefine, or delete layers or authority hierarchy.
9. **Stages apply published CLI / CLIR / CL law.** Categories, validation rules, and layer identities remain owned by WS5 / MS001.

## 3. Catalogue Overview

| ID | Stage | Constitutional purpose (short) | Primary inputs | Primary outputs |
|----|-------|--------------------------------|----------------|-----------------|
| **CLIL-01** | Assessment Initiation | Open a layer assessment against named published corpora and scope | Published corpus identities + concern scope + optional CLI indication | Initiated concern record (or refuse-open) |
| **CLIL-02** | Layer Discovery | Identify recognised published layers under CL-01…CL-04 | Initiated concern + candidate published layer identities | Discovered layer set bound to CLI types (or refuse-discover) |

**Relation to CLI / CL / CLIR / CLIO / CT / CC / CDI / CCI / CBI / CVI / EV:** Those catalogues remain defined solely by their owners. CLIL stages *organise when* layer assessment activities occur; they do not replace, reclassify, invent, amend, or rewrite those catalogues.

**Bypass and cycle stage scope:** CLIL-06 applies CLI-04 (Layer Bypass Detection) and CLI-05 (Layer Cycle Detection) under published CLIR rules — covering the lifecycle’s bypass and cycle validation duties within one recognised stage. Isolation honesty under CLI-06 may be confirmed across CLIL-03…CLIL-06 as published composition requires.

## 5. Stage Definitions

### CLIL-01 — Assessment Initiation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Open a material layer integrity assessment concern by naming the published constitutional corpora under assessment and the scope of the concern — without inventing law, opening against unpublished customs, or treating initiation as an intact finding.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Identifiable published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–XI specification references for the concern scope
- Concern identity (what layer-integrity question is under assessment)
- Optional published indication of required CLI-01…CLI-07 categories for the scope
- Optional identifiable supporting authority / lineage references (not rewritten)

**Constitutional outputs**

- An initiated assessment concern record naming corpora, specifications, scope, and intended CLI type set — **or**
- Lawful refuse-open when standards are unpublished, scope is theatrical, or initiation would invent law

**Permitted activities**

- Name published corpora and specifications as assessment standards
- Bound the concern scope without amending corpora
- Record intended CLI types from the published catalogue
- Refuse initiation when constitutional preconditions fail
- Preserve initiation audit identity for later stages

**Prohibited activities**

- Treat “how main is organised,” tribal diagrams, or stack preference as the standard
- Create or modify constitutional specifications by opening a concern
- Issue an intact / violated finding at initiation
- Invent unpublished layer categories or layers
- Skip initiation when a material finding will later be claimed
- Create constitutional authority or rewrite layers at initiation

**Authority preserved**  
Published corpora remain the sole standard. Initiation organises; it does not legislate.

### CLIL-02 — Layer Discovery

**Constitutional purpose**  
Identify **recognised published architectural layers** among the named corpora under CL-01…CL-04 — so later stages assess real published layers, not anonymous theatre — without inventing, reordering, or deleting layers.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Initiated concern from CLIL-01
- Candidate published layer identities (CL-01 Constitution, CL-02 EIP, CL-03 Constitutional Programmes, CL-04 Constitutional Assurance, and lawful programme-band memberships within CL-03)
- Published CL-01…CL-04 and CLI-01…CLI-07 definitions from WS5 / MS001
- Optional supporting CT lineage identities already established under upstream law (unchanged)

**Constitutional outputs**

- A discovered layer set bound to the concern and intended CLI types — **or**
- Lawful refuse-discover when layers are missing, forged, invented, unpublished, or would require unlawful mutation to “discover”

**Permitted activities**

- Identify and name recognised published layers
- Classify corpora under published CL-01…CL-04 only
- Bind discovered layers to the initiated concern
- Refuse discovery when layers cannot be honestly affirmed as published
- Preserve discovery records for audit and explainability

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent missing layers or preferred architecture diagrams as law
- Reorder, redefine, or delete layers to force later intact status
- Treat folder trees, package namespaces, or CI noise as constitutional layers without published mapping
- Redefine educational or constitutional meaning while “discovering”
- Present discovery as itself an intact finding or a freeze of stack authority
- Create constitutional authority under discovery pretence

**Authority preserved**  
Published layers remain upstream facts. Discovery organises identity; it does not author layers or educational truth.

### CLIL-03 — Layer Ordering Validation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Validate that discovered layers remain in published **superior → subordinate order** — CL-01 → CL-02 → CL-03 → CL-04, and lawful Programme VI → … → XI order within CL-03 — without inventing layers, inverting order, or rewriting maps.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Discovered layer set from CLIL-02
- Published ordering rules (CLI-01 / CLIR-01) from WS5 / MS001
- Published programme order within CL-03 where material

**Constitutional outputs**

- Ordering disposition for the concern (preserved / violated / deferred / escalated) — **or**
- Lawful refuse-validate when ordering cannot be honestly confirmed without inventing or inverting layers

**Permitted activities**

- Confirm published CL-01…CL-04 order and lawful programme order within CL-03
- Identify inverted, collapsed, or invented layer maps that contradict published order
- Prefer honest refuse-validate / deferred / escalated over rewriting order
- Remain repeatable and implementation-neutral
- Preserve ordering records for later stages

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent missing layers or reorder published layers to force pass
- Collapse distinct horizons into one mega-layer under “ordering validation”
- Modify constitutional specifications to force ordering pass
- Privilege a technology stack as constitutional
- Redefine Programme VI–X meaning or CLI meaning in validation prose
- Present ordering validation as new constitutional law
- Mutate layer maps during validation
- Create constitutional authority under validation pretence

**Authority preserved**  
WS5 / MS001 owns ordering validation rules. This stage applies them; it does not amend the catalogue or become constitutional authority.

### CLIL-04 — Authority Flow Validation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Validate that each material **authority path** remains reconstructable from superior layers to subordinate layers — without inventing missing paths, absorbing superiors into subordinates, or rewriting authority hierarchy.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Ordering-validated bundle from CLIL-03
- Candidate authority-path identities (CLI-02 candidates)
- Published CLIR authority-path fidelity rules from WS5 / MS001
- Published constitutional authority order (Constitution / EIP → VI → VII → VIII → IX → X → XI and lawful workstream subordination)

**Constitutional outputs**

- A validated authority-flow record per assessed path — **or**
- Lawful refuse-validate when authority cannot be reconstructed, is invented, is absorbed into a subordinate, or validation would require rewriting hierarchy

**Permitted activities**

- Confirm reconstructable published superior → subordinate authority paths
- Detect authority absorption that would let a subordinate become practical superior of its own superior
- Prefer honest refuse-validate / deferred / escalated over inventing missing paths
- Preserve validation records for later stages

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent missing authority paths or ownership links
- Absorb superior authority into a subordinate via lifecycle prose
- Modify authority hierarchy to force validation
- Modify constitutional specifications to force validation
- Privilege a technology stack as constitutional
- Present authority-flow validation as new constitutional law
- Create constitutional authority under validation pretence

**Authority preserved**  
Published corpora own authority hierarchy. This stage confirms paths; it does not rewrite hierarchy or become constitutional authority.

### CLIL-05 — Dependency Direction Validation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Validate **inter-layer dependency direction** under CLI-03 — so consumption / subordination respects published superior → subordinate orientation and reverse authorship is reported honestly without inventing or flipping edges in situ.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Authority-validated bundle from CLIL-04
- Named inter-layer dependency edges under assessment (CLI-03 candidates)
- Published CLIR direction-fidelity rules from WS5 / MS001

**Constitutional outputs**

- Direction disposition for the concern (preserved / violated / deferred / escalated) — **or**
- Lawful refuse-validate when direction cannot be honestly confirmed without inventing edges or reverse authorship

**Permitted activities**

- Confirm published inter-layer orientations for the assessment scope
- Identify reverse authorship, invented edges, or silent direction flips
- Prefer honest violated / deferred / escalated over direction mutation
- Compose with CDI direction rules without absorbing CDI meaning
- Preserve direction records for finding construction

**Prohibited activities**

- Reverse published dependency direction under lifecycle pretence
- Invent inter-layer edges so stages can proceed
- Soft-amend specifications so reverse authorship becomes “composition”
- Treat import-graph warnings as constitutional direction status without published mapping
- Hide direction violations to manufacture later intact status
- Present direction validation as licence to rewrite the corpus
- Create constitutional authority by declaring reverse authorship “approved”

**Authority preserved**  
Direction validation reports fidelity to published orientation. It never authors law or rewrites dependency direction.

### CLIL-06 — Bypass and Cycle Validation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Validate **absence of layer bypasses** (CLI-04) and **absence of architectural cycles** (CLI-05) — reporting skipped required superiors and authority-circularising closed paths honestly without inventing temporary bypasses, hiding cycles, or rewriting graphs to pass.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Direction-validated bundle from prior stages
- Candidate paths / layer graphs for bypass and cycle detection
- Published CLIR bypass-refusal and acyclicity rules from WS5 / MS001
- Optional isolation claims under CLI-06 where composition requires confirmation across this stage

**Constitutional outputs**

- Bypass / cycle disposition for the concern (none detected / detected / deferred / escalated), including named bypasses and cycles when detected — **or**
- Lawful refuse-validate when bypass / cycle status cannot be honestly confirmed without inventing exceptions or rewriting graphs

**Permitted activities**

- Detect relationships that skip required superior layers to obtain authority or meaning
- Detect closed paths that circularise or invert layer authority
- Report isolation breaches assisted by bypass or cycle patterns
- Prefer honest violated / deferred / escalated over hiding defects
- Preserve bypass / cycle records for finding construction

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent “temporary bypass,” “demo exception,” or “close enough cycle” dispositions
- Hide detected bypasses or cycles to manufacture later intact status
- Soft-amend layer graphs so cycles become “composition”
- Treat folder-adjacency or CI greenery as constitutional acyclicity without published mapping
- Present bypass / cycle pass as educational quality or stack freeze
- Rewrite layers or authority hierarchy to manufacture bypass / cycle honesty
- Create constitutional authority by declaring bypasses “approved”

**Authority preserved**  
Bypass and cycle validation reports structural fidelity. It never authors law or rewrites layer graphs.

### CLIL-07 — Finding Construction

**Constitutional purpose**  
Emit **layer integrity findings** as lawful dispositions under published criteria — speaking to whether assessed layers remain intact under ordering, authority flow, dependency direction, bypass absence, cycle absence, and isolation — without converting findings into amendments, educational grades, layer rewrites, or freezes of stack authority.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Stage dispositions from CLIL-01…CLIL-06
- Published CLI-01…CLI-07 criteria and CLIR validation rules from WS5 / MS001
- Published decision vocabulary (intact / violated / deferred / escalated)
- Bound references to corpora, layers, authority paths, dependency paths, bypasses, cycles, and isolation disposition

**Constitutional outputs**

- Layer integrity findings for the concern (including composition outcomes when multiple CLI types apply)
- Explicit non-authority framing (findings are integrity dispositions only — never new law)
- Assessment records binding stages, layers, validations, bypasses / cycles, and findings

**Permitted activities**

- Produce findings under published dispositions only
- Bind each finding to the CLI types, layers, and stages that produced it
- Record composition / stop-rule application honestly
- Prefer understatement over invented green
- Propose (not enact) upstream corpus amendments when law itself must change
- Preserve audit-speakable assessment records under CLI-07 honesty

**Prohibited activities**

- Present findings as constitutional amendments or new educational meaning
- Narrate intact status as mastery, readiness theatre, educational quality, permanent conformity, or frozen stack authority
- Rewrite findings to match product urgency
- Collapse multi-type failures into a single false “intact”
- Mutate layers, authority hierarchy, or dependency direction while “constructing findings”
- Invent unpublished CLI categories or CL layers during finding construction
- Erase stage history so findings look clean
- Create constitutional authority via finding text
- Redefine constitutional layers in finding speech

**Authority preserved**  
Findings validate structure against law. They never become law or constitutional authority.

---

### CLIL-08 — Finding Publication

**Constitutional purpose**  
Publish layer integrity findings and assessment records under **published honesty rules** so audiences receive lawful assessment speech — without inventing intact status, hiding bypasses or cycles, rewriting layers, amending specifications, modifying authority hierarchy, or freezing a stack as law.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Findings and assessment records from CLIL-07
- Published publication / honesty rules from WS5 / MS001 and EIP explainability / continuity law
- Layer confirmations (non-legislation, non-reinterpretation, non-mutation, non-layer-redefinition, non-hierarchy-modification, non-authority-creation, non-substitution)

**Constitutional outputs**

- Published assessment artefacts projecting one constitutional truth to authorised audiences — **or**
- Lawful refuse-publish when records are incomplete, boundaries were breached, or publication would invent intact status / hide bypasses or cycles / freeze stack authority / replace constitutional authority

**Permitted activities**

- Publish findings and records under published honesty rules
- Project student / developer / auditor vocabularies without changing constitutional facts
- Refuse publication when audit or boundary preconditions fail
- Keep publication distinct from educational quality, layer rewrites, and authority creation

**Prohibited activities**

- Publish invented intact status or incomplete-assessment theatre as intact
- Hide refuse / defer / escalate / violated dispositions to manufacture green
- Narrate publication as constitutional amendment, educational success, or frozen stack law
- Create constitutional authority as a publication act
- Modify constitutional specifications, layers, or authority hierarchy as a publication act
- Present seals, badges, or scanner greenery as substitutes for published records
- Reinterpret constitutional meaning in publication speech

**Authority preserved**  
Publication communicates assessment findings. Published corpora remain the sole standard.
