## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which layers* were assessed — which authority paths and dependency paths were validated, whether any bypasses or cycles were identified, and which findings were produced — is not coaching; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–X have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, runtime, governance, and execution speech speakable, after Programme XI / WS1–WS4 have made dependency / catalogue / boundary / vocabulary integrity speech speakable, and after WS5 / MS001 has made layer integrity speech speakable, Programme XI still needs one lifecycle-speech answer:

> **“Which constitutional layers were assessed, which authority paths were validated, which dependency paths were validated, were any bypasses or cycles identified, and which findings were produced?”**

That answer must cover every material layer integrity lifecycle without inventing educational meaning, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, redefining layers, modifying authority hierarchy, creating constitutional authority, freezing a stack as law, inventing unpublished CLI or CLIL types, replacing MS001 validation meaning, or presenting publication as educational quality.

## 2. What Lifecycle Explainability Is

**Constitutional Layer Integrity Lifecycle Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **coordination of named layer assessments through published CLIL stages** — discovering recognised CL-01…CL-04 layers, validating ordering, authority flow, dependency direction, bypass absence, and cycle absence, applying published CLI types, and producing / publishing findings — not to invent a second tutor voice from import graphs, not to grade the student, and not to replace constitutional authority.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Layers assessed** | Named published CL-01…CL-04 layers and CLI-01…CLI-07 classifications under assessment | Which constitutional layers were assessed? |
| **Authority paths validated** | Reconstructable superior → subordinate paths confirmed at CLIL-04 | Which authority paths were validated? |
| **Dependency paths validated** | Named inter-layer edges and orientation confirmed at CLIL-05 | Which dependency paths were validated? |
| **Bypasses or cycles identified** | Skipped superiors and authority-circularising closed paths detected at CLIL-06 | Were any bypasses or cycles identified? |
| **Findings produced** | intact / violated / deferred / escalated (with stage binding and composition notes) | Which findings were produced? |
| **Assessment limits remaining intact** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden legislation / mutation / reinterpretation / layer redefinition / hierarchy modification / authority-creation / substitution did not occur | Which constitutional assessment limits remained intact? |

Lifecycle Explainability is:

- **coordination-centred** — it narrates layers → ordering → authority → direction → bypasses / cycles → findings under law;
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CLIL, CLI, and CL identities;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published corpora and layer identities;
- **discovery-honest** — every material justification cites identifiable published layers assessed (or names the hard stop when none exist);
- **authority-honest** — every material justification cites validated authority paths (or names the hard stop when unmet);
- **direction-honest** — every material justification cites validated dependency paths (or names the hard stop when unmet);
- **bypass-cycle-honest** — every material justification cites bypass / cycle status without hiding defects;
- **finding-honest** — every material finding is bound to the producing lifecycle stages;
- **record-honest** — every material lifecycle cites which assessment records were preserved / published;
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to amend corpora;
- **non-authoritative** — publication is never narrated as a freeze of stack authority or a substitute for published corpora;
- **quality-honest** — intact status is never narrated as mastery, readiness theatre, or educational quality;
- **mutation-honest** — explanation never hides layer / hierarchy changes performed “so the lifecycle could proceed”;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence / validation speech, Programme IX governance speech, Programme X activity speech, WS1–WS4 integrity speech, or WS5 / MS001 layer integrity speech;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match CLILO-05 reconstruction needs;
- **repeatability-honest** — explanations must not paper over race-dependent dispositions or private stage shortcuts.

Lifecycle Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–X / WS1–WS4 / WS5 / MS001 meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a substitute for CLI findings speech, CT lineage speech, CDI / CCI / CBI / CVI speech, or Programme IX–X evaluative / execution speech;
- a CI status page, coverage report, import-graph summary, or folder-adjacency summary presented as constitutional coordination;
- a freeze of implementations, stacks, vendors, or releases as constitutional law;
- a licence to narrate invented layers, rewritten hierarchy, or hidden bypasses / cycles as if they were lawful inputs.

## 3. Mandatory Explainability Questions

Every material constitutional layer integrity lifecycle must be able to answer:

### CLILEQ-01 — Which constitutional layers were assessed?

**Required content**

- Named published CL-01…CL-04 layer identities and corpus memberships discovered at CLIL-02
- Binding between layer identities and intended / applied CLI types
- Confirmation that unpublished customs, invented architecture diagrams, folder labels, or scanner noise were not used as constitutional layers

**Defect if**

- “Layers are fine” with no layer references
- Anonymous “the docs” or “the architecture” without identifiable published layers
- Layers invented, reordered, redefined, or deleted as assessment without disclosure and hard stop

---

### CLILEQ-02 — Which authority paths were validated?

**Required content**

- Named superior → subordinate authority paths confirmed at CLIL-04 (and relevant CLIR-01…CLIR-10 rules)
- Explicit refuse-validate disposition when authority paths were unmet
- Confirmation that authority paths were not invented or silently absorbed into subordinates

**Defect if**

- Assessment proceeds with “authority assumed”
- Authority paths invented mid-lifecycle to force intact status
- Silent omission of mandatory superior layer identities
- “Supported by ticket #1234,” “supported by architecture slide,” or “supported by import graph” as the sole authority-path identity

---

### CLILEQ-03 — Which dependency paths were validated?

**Required content**

- Named inter-layer dependency edges and orientations confirmed at CLIL-05 under CLI-03 / CLIR-03
- Explicit refuse-validate / violated / deferred / escalated disposition when direction was unmet
- Confirmation that edges were not invented or reverse-authored under lifecycle pretence

**Defect if**

- Assessment proceeds with “dependencies assumed”
- Dependency paths invented or flipped mid-lifecycle to force intact status
- Silent omission of material inter-layer edges when findings are claimed

---

### CLILEQ-04 — Were any bypasses or cycles identified?

**Required content**

- Bypass / cycle status from CLIL-06: none detected / detected / deferred / escalated (or refuse-validate)
- Named bypasses (skipped required superiors) and cycles (authority-circularising closed paths) when detected
- Confirmation that temporary-bypass or demo-exception theatre was not used
- Isolation notes where bypass / cycle patterns assisted layer absorption (CLI-06 composition)

**Defect if**

- Bypass / cycle status omitted when findings are claimed
- Detected bypasses or cycles quietly dropped from publication speech
- Narration that contradictions were “resolved” by rewriting layer graphs under lifecycle pretence

---

### CLILEQ-05 — Which findings were produced?

**Required content**

- Named layer integrity findings from CLIL-07 (intact / violated / deferred / escalated)
- Binding of each finding to producing CLIL stages (especially CLIL-03…CLIL-07) and applied CLI-01…CLI-07 / CL-01…CL-04 identities
- Publication artefacts from CLIL-08 where material (or refuse-publish identity)
- Continuity sufficient to reconstruct corpora, layers, authority paths, dependency paths, bypasses / cycles, CLI types, stages, findings, and intact limits

**Defect if**

- “Intact” with no reconstructable finding identity
- Findings that drop stage-to-finding binding or layer identity
- Silent erasure of prior lawful assessment / lifecycle history
- Publication that invents intact status or hides violated dispositions
- Collapse of multi-type failures into a single false “intact”

---

### CLILEQ-06 — Which constitutional assessment limits remained intact?

**Required content**

- Explicit confirmation against `LIFECYCLE_BOUNDARIES.md` checks material to the concern (especially non-legislation, non-reinterpretation, non-mutation, non-layer-redefinition, non-hierarchy-modification, non-authority-creation, non-substitution)
- Disclosure of any hard stop that prevented progress or publication
- Explicit confirmation that assessed published layers that remained lawful were not rewritten by coordination
- Confirmation of non-MS001-replacement (publication did not rewrite CLI / CL meaning)

**Defect if**

- Intact limits are implied, not speakable
- Publication speech smuggles authority creation, layer redefinition, hierarchy modification, meaning reinterpretation, or law rewrites
- Artefact / layer mutation or invented statements are hidden
