## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which assurance domains* were checked — which readiness dimensions were satisfied, whether readiness gaps were found, and which findings resulted — is not coaching; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–X have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, runtime, governance, and execution speech speakable, and after Programme XI / WS1–WS5 have made dependency, catalogue, boundary, vocabulary, and layer speech speakable, Programme XI still needs one readiness-speech answer:

> **“Which assurance domains were evaluated, which readiness dimensions were satisfied, were any readiness gaps identified, and which findings were produced?”**

That answer must cover every material runtime readiness assessment without inventing educational meaning, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, executing Runtime A, freezing a stack as law, inventing unpublished CRRD types, or presenting ready status as educational quality or student exam readiness.

## 2. What Runtime Readiness Explainability Is

**Constitutional Runtime Readiness Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **evaluation of named readiness dimensions under published CRRD categories from completed assurance** — not to invent a second tutor voice from deployment gates, and not to grade the student.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Assurance domains evaluated** | Named WS1–WS5 (and applicable VI–X) domains whose completed assurance / published guidance were consumed | Which assurance domains were evaluated? |
| **Readiness dimensions satisfied** | Named CRRD-01…CRRD-05 dispositions that held (or failed) for the concern | Which readiness dimensions were satisfied? |
| **Readiness gaps identified** | Named missing / incomplete inputs or dimension shortfalls (or confirmed absent) | Were any readiness gaps identified? |
| **Readiness findings** | ready / not-ready / deferred / escalated (with composition notes) | Which findings were produced? |

Runtime Readiness Explainability is:

- **evaluation-centred** — it narrates domains → dimensions → gaps → findings under law;
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CRRD identities;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published specifications and completed assurance identities;
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to amend corpora, redefine meaning, or invent maps;
- **non-executing** — explanation never narrates Runtime A execution as the meaning of ready;
- **quality-honest** — ready status is never narrated as mastery, student exam readiness, or educational quality;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference or CI green as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **horizon-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence speech, Programme IX governance speech, Programme X execution / decision / audit / explanation speech, or Programme XI / WS1–WS5 assurance speech;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match CRRI reconstruction needs;
- **repeatability-honest** — explanations must not paper over race-dependent dispositions.

Runtime Readiness Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–XI meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a substitute for Programme IX CT explainability, Programme XI / WS1–WS5 assurance explainability, or Programme X explainability architecture corpora;
- a rendering system, UI template, CI badge, log shipper, deployment dashboard, or analytics product;
- a claim that clear readiness speech guarantees learning or a pass;
- a licence to present scanner greens, deployment gates, or pipeline badges as constitutional proof or educational quality;
- a licence to narrate Runtime A execution or artefact mutation as “readiness repairs.”

## 3. Mandatory Explanation Questions

Every material constitutional runtime readiness assessment should answer all four questions:

### CRREQ-01 — Which assurance domains were evaluated?

Name the primary published assurance domains consumed for the concern (for example WS1 dependency completion under CDIC; WS2 catalogue completion under CCIC; WS3 boundary completion under CBIC; WS4 vocabulary completion under CVIC; WS5 layer completion under CLIC; and, where applicable, citeable Programme VI–X guidance presence for governance / semantic composition).

Unpublished tribal “everything looks fine” and “neighbouring folder labels” are not constitutional assurance domains.

**Defects to disclose:** missing domain identity; invented completion; silent assumption that unfinished WS1–WS5 work was complete.

---

### CRREQ-02 — Which readiness dimensions were satisfied?

Name the CRRD-01…CRRD-05 dimensions applied and their dispositions (satisfied / not satisfied / deferred / escalated / scoped out with reconstructable notes). For overall readiness (CRRD-05), name the required subordinate dimensions and how they composed.

“Supported by ticket #1234,” “supported by deployment gate,” or “supported by green main” is not a constitutional readiness dimension.

**Defects to disclose:** unpublished custom dimensions; majority-vote theatre that ignores a required failed dimension; silent scope-out of a required dimension.

---

### CRREQ-03 — Were any readiness gaps identified?

State whether readiness gaps were found (or confirmed absent). Name each material gap: unfinished prerequisite assurance, failed dimension, architectural / governance / semantic incoherence, missing citeable guidance, or composition conflict.

Absence of gaps must be an affirmative reconstructable claim when ready status is asserted — not silence.

**Defects to disclose:** erased gaps for tidy ready status; “known issues” spoken only in private chat; gaps renamed as “future enhancements” while ready is claimed.

---

### CRREQ-04 — Which findings were produced?

Name the ready / not-ready / deferred / escalated disposition(s), including dimension-level and overall findings where both exist, with enough constitutional reference for reconstruction.

Findings speech must preserve boundaries: ready is not Runtime A execution, not specification amendment, not student success, not Programme IX certification.

**Defects to disclose:** missing finding identity; contradictory findings from identical published inputs without published corpus change; narration that converts ready into authority creation or artefact mutation.

## 4. Audience Projections

| Audience | Vocabulary posture | Same truth |
|----------|-------------------|------------|
| **Student-facing** | Plain language about whether the *platform’s constitutional guidance* is ready to support building coaching behaviour — never implying the *student* is exam-ready | Domains, dimensions, gaps, findings remain honest |
| **Developer-facing** | Named CRRD / WS1–WS5 / Programme VI–X identities and gap references | Same dispositions; denser identifiers |
| **Auditor-facing** | Reconstructable trails: concern identity, assurance input IDs, dimension dispositions, gap list, finding IDs, boundary preservation notes | Same truth with audit continuity |

Audience adaptation never changes disposition. Softening “not-ready” into “ready for demo” for any audience is a defect.

## 6. Forbidden Explanation Patterns

1. **Ready-as-mastery** — narrating corpus readiness as student exam readiness or learning success.
2. **Ready-as-Runtime-A** — narrating ready as executed / shipped product behaviour.
3. **Ready-as-law** — narrating findings as constitutional amendments or new authority.
4. **Gap erasure** — omitting known gaps while claiming ready.
5. **Stack canonisation** — presenting Flask / SQLAlchemy / OpenAPI / a vendor as the meaning of ready.
6. **CI-as-constitution** — presenting pipeline greens as CRREQ answers.
7. **WS1–WS5 ventriloquism** — inventing integrity findings the assurance corpora did not publish.
8. **Horizon collapse** — answering CRREQ questions with Programme IX certification speech or Programme VIII contract speech alone.

---

## 7. Minimal Explanation Record

Every material readiness assessment should leave a reconstructable record containing at least:

1. Readiness concern identity  
2. Assurance domains evaluated (CRREQ-01)  
3. Dimensions applied and dispositions (CRREQ-02)  
4. Gaps identified or confirmed absent (CRREQ-03)  
5. Findings produced (CRREQ-04)  
6. Boundary preservation note (no runtime execution; no artefact mutation; no authority creation; no specification amendment)  
7. Repeatability / input identity notes sufficient for reconstruction  

Missing any of (1)–(6) for a material ready claim is a constitutional explainability defect.
