## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “deployment greens,” “tickets closed,” or “ready badges shown.” The tutor optimises **honest closing of constitutional runtime readiness assessment work**: when the published lifecycle for a concern has lawfully concluded, prerequisite assurance has been evaluated, readiness dimensions and overall readiness assessed, findings published, and the assessment formally closed, the tutor can say so — without pretending Runtime A may now execute under constitutional seal, without rewriting artefacts so readiness looks tidy, without pretending the student has mastered the topic, and without amending the syllabus because the machinery stopped cleanly.

These objectives bind every constitutional runtime readiness completion judgement and every successor assessment machinery that claims readiness assessment fulfilment.

## 2. Primary Objective

### CRRCO-01 — Confirm lawful assessment completion

**Definition.** Ensure that runtime / assessment / audit speech affirms runtime readiness completion only when a published assessment concern under WS6 / MS001–MS002 has concluded the required CRRL-01…CRRL-07 lifecycle obligations — or honestly refuses close when fulfilment criteria are not met.

**Includes:**

- Assessing only concerns that lawfully opened under WS6 / MS001–MS002 initiation → assurance collection → prerequisite validation → dimension evaluation → overall evaluation → finding construction → publication law.
- Affirming completion only when `COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md` conditions (CRRC-01…CRRC-05) hold.
- Preferring lawful “not yet” / refuse-close over inventing a “done enough” status for product convenience.
- Distinguishing lawful stop dispositions (refuse / defer / escalate / not-ready as published outcomes that exhaust remaining *assessment* duties with honest recording) from abandoned lifecycle duties.
- Binding every material completion judgement to reconstructable constitutional references.

**Excludes:**

- Declaring complete because a CI job exited, a ticket closed, a deployment gate glowed, or a UI spinner stopped.
- Treating Twin / Adaptive / Experience / Mission heuristics as completion warrants.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for assessment honesty.
- Collapsing “readiness assessment complete” into “Runtime A may execute,” “artefacts rewritten,” “specifications amended,” “constitutional authority created,” or “student succeeded.”

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software celebrates unfinished readiness assessment — or celebrates finished pipeline machinery as finished runtime execution or law-making. Honest confirmation is care; completion theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CRRCO-02 — Preserve implementation readiness

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming completion never invents ready status, silently upgrades not-ready / deferred / escalated findings, or treats assessment close as a Runtime A execution warrant — so implementation readiness honesty published under CRRD / CRRL law survives close intact.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who “helpfully” upgrades “not ready” to “ready” so the assessment can finish has abandoned educational honesty even if the completion UX looks clean. Completeness of *assessment* is not completeness of *ready disposition*, and neither is Runtime A permission.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion leaves named readiness dispositions reconstructable (ready / not-ready / deferred / escalated).
- Close never substitutes deployment gates, pipeline greens, or ticket states for published readiness findings.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing or silently rewriting prior lawful readiness dispositions to simplify the next report.
- Narrating assessment-complete as Runtime A execution warrant is a defect.

---

### CRRCO-03 — Preserve assurance traceability

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming completion never invents missing WS1–WS5 completion identities, re-authors integrity findings, or erases which assurance domains were consumed — so every closed assessment remains reconstructable to published completed assurance.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later walk from yesterday’s readiness close to the completed dependency / catalogue / boundary / vocabulary / layer assurance that fed it cannot be trusted. Completion that invents assurance is educational fiction dressed as done.

**Manifestations:**

- Affirmed closes leave completed-assurance identities reconstructable with readiness findings.
- Invented WS1–WS5 completion, re-authored integrity findings, and silent assurance erasure are defects.
- Continuity forbids deleting prior lawful assurance-consumption records to “clean up” completion.
- Incomplete prerequisite assurance evaluation for a required concern scope blocks affirmation.

---

### CRRCO-04 — Preserve constitutional confidence

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming completion never soft-amends published corpora, invents constitutional authority, or treats a preferred stack as the constitutional source — so closed assessments leave constitutional confidence intact rather than laundering urgency into law.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who treats “assessment complete” as licence to rewrite the syllabus or freeze a stack as superior authority has abandoned constitutional care for product theatre.

**Manifestations:**

- Close never creates constitutional authority or amends constitutional specifications.
- Close never modifies constitutional artefacts under “completion cleanup.”
- Stack / vendor / deployment-topology preferences never become constitutional standards via close.
- Boundary honesty (`COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md`) survives every affirmed close.

---

### CRRCO-05 — Preserve assessment repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that the same published corpora, same consumed completed assurance, same CRRD applications, and same CRRL stage sequence yield the same completion disposition for the same concern — so close never invents contradictory stories from identical constitutional inputs.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who marks the same readiness concern “complete” on Monday and “not yet” on Tuesday without a published corpus or assurance change has abandoned care for theatre.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion dispositions are deterministic under published inputs.
- Silent non-determinism, ambient “main branch as ready,” and demo-only close rules are defects.
- Optional honesty checks (CRRC-06…CRRC-10) support, never replace, CRRC-01…CRRC-05.
- Repeatability failures void lawful affirmation even if a badge looks green.

---

### CRRCO-06 — Preserve completion explainability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material completion judgement can answer which assurance domains were evaluated, which readiness dimensions were satisfied, whether overall readiness was achieved, and which findings were published — without narrating Runtime A execution, artefact mutation, specification amendment, or authority creation.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot say what finished — only that “it completed” — has not closed honestly; they have performed.

**Manifestations:**

- Affirmed and refused closes answer CRRCQ-01…CRRCQ-04.
- Explanation never invents ready status, hides gaps, or narrates stack canonisation.
- Student-plain and auditor vocabulary share one constitutional truth.
- Mute “complete” badges without CRRCQ answers are defects.

## 5. Non-Objectives

Runtime readiness completion must **not** optimise for:

- Runtime A execution, simulation, or shipping under “assessment complete”;
- Constitutional artefact mutation or soft-amendment under close theatre;
- Creation of constitutional authority or specification amendment via completion judgements;
- Educational success, mastery, exam readiness, or product quality scores;
- Programme IX conformity / verification / compliance / certification fulfilment as if they were CRRC;
- Throughput, latency, demo continuity, or investor-review greens as substitutes for assessment honesty;
- Freezing Flask, SQLAlchemy, OpenAPI, a CI vendor, or Runtime A as constitutional law.
