## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor needs bright conditions for saying “this constitutional runtime readiness assessment work is finished” — and equally bright refusals when it is not. After WS6 / MS001 has named how readiness is evaluated and WS6 / MS002 has named how assessments are organised, Programme XI Workstream 6 still needs one fulfilment checklist:

> **What must be true before constitutional speech may affirm that the required readiness assessment lifecycle has concluded, prerequisite assurance evaluated, readiness dimensions and overall readiness assessed, findings published, and the assessment formally closed?**

These criteria answer that question. They are **normative constitutional conditions**, not database predicates, CI job statuses, ticket states, deployment-gate greens, or API status codes.

---

## 2. Criterion Catalogue

All material affirmed completions require **CRRC-01 through CRRC-05** to hold. Failure of any criterion ⇒ **not yet** or **refuse close**. Do not invent a partial “mostly complete” runtime-execution, artefact-mutation, specification-amendment, educational, or constitutional status.

| ID | Criterion | Primary question |
|----|-----------|------------------|
| **CRRC-01** | Lifecycle completed | Were required CRRL-01…CRRL-07 stages completed for the concern? |
| **CRRC-02** | Readiness assessment completed | Were prerequisite assurance evaluation and required readiness dimension assessments completed under published CRR / CRRL law? |
| **CRRC-03** | Readiness evaluation completed | Was overall readiness evaluation completed under published CRRD-05 law? |
| **CRRC-04** | Findings publication completed | Were required runtime readiness findings lawfully constructed and published? |
| **CRRC-05** | Formal assessment closure | Was the readiness assessment concern formally closed under published boundary honesty? |

Optional supporting honesty checks (do not replace CRRC-01…CRRC-05):

| ID | Check | Primary question |
|----|-------|------------------|
| **CRRC-06** | Catalogue honesty | Were stages and evaluations typed under published CRRL-01…CRRL-07 and CRRD-01…CRRD-05 only? |
| **CRRC-07** | Continuity honesty | Was prior lawful assessment history preserved (EIP-005)? |
| **CRRC-08** | Non-claim honesty | Does the judgement refuse runtime-execution / Runtime-A-implementation / artefact-mutation / specification-amendment / authority-creation / educational overclaim? |
| **CRRC-09** | Neutrality honesty | Does the judgement refuse stack / vendor / deployment-topology canonisation as completion law? |
| **CRRC-10** | Repeatability honesty | Would the same published inputs yield the same completion disposition? |

## 3. Criterion Definitions

### CRRC-01 — Lifecycle completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every lifecycle stage required by the concern’s published WS6 / MS002 scope has been executed — or that a published refuse / defer / escalate disposition exhausted remaining *readiness assessment* duties for that stage set with honest progression to finding construction / publication (or refuse-publish) and closure of what occurred.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Identifiable readiness assessment concern opened under WS6 / MS001–MS002.
- Required CRRL-01…CRRL-07 stage identities for the concern scope.
- Stage traces / disposition records for each required stage (including lawful early stops).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed stage-fulfilment record naming which required stages completed.
- Or a lawful stop record naming which stages were refused / deferred / escalated and why remaining assessment duties are exhausted.

**Completion conditions.**

- Required stages map to published CRRL-01…CRRL-07 only.
- Mandatory initiation, assurance collection, prerequisite validation, dimension evaluation, overall evaluation, finding construction, and publication obligations for the concern are not silently omitted.
- Lawful early stops still leave honest stage outcomes; silent disappearance of a concern is a defect.
- Refusal of “pipeline skipped validation” or “we know it ran” without stage identity as completion.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That stage completion executes runtime behaviour or implements Runtime A.
- That stage completion modifies constitutional artefacts or amends constitutional specifications.
- That stage completion creates constitutional authority or freezes a stack as law.
- That stage completion means the learner succeeded or educational / exam readiness was achieved.
- That skipped stages may be invented or implied from CI theatre.
- That Programme IX conformity / verification / compliance / certification was performed under stage progress.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who declares readiness assessment “finished” without confirming each required assessment step occurred has not finished lawful care — even if the desk looks tidy.

---

### CRRC-02 — Readiness assessment completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that prerequisite assurance evaluation and every readiness dimension assessment required by the concern’s published scope were completed under published CRR / CRRL law — including gathering identifiable completed WS1–WS5 assurance (CRRL-02), validating readiness prerequisites (CRRL-03), and evaluating required CRRD-01…CRRD-04 dimensions (CRRL-04) — or that a published refuse disposition exhausted remaining *assessment* duties when required inputs could not be lawfully assessed without inventing assurance or dimensions.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Named completed WS1–WS5 assurance identities in scope (or honest gaps).
- Published readiness prerequisite identities for the concern.
- Required CRRD-01…CRRD-04 dimension identities for the concern scope.
- Stage traces for CRRL-02 / CRRL-03 / CRRL-04 as required by concern scope.
- Evaluation dispositions under applicable CRRD types (structural, governance, semantic, architectural).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed readiness-assessment record naming which assurance domains were evaluated, which prerequisites were validated, and which CRRD-01…CRRD-04 dimensions were assessed — including gap outcomes.
- Or a lawful refuse / defer / escalate record naming which required assessment duties remain incomplete and why.

**Completion conditions.**

- Assurance subjects are named completed WS1–WS5 outcomes (not “the corpus,” “the stack,” or vendor labels alone).
- Assessment cites published constitutional specifications and CRRD categories, not unpublished customs.
- Collection gathered only completed / identifiable WS1–WS5 outcomes — inventing assurance to force assessment is a hard stop.
- Prerequisite validation confirms reconstructable completeness; inventing completion or skipping unfinished assurance is a hard stop.
- Dimension evaluation applies only published CRRD-01…CRRD-04 as required; inventing dimensions or re-authoring WS1–WS5 findings is a hard stop.
- Refusal of deployment gates, Twin confidence, pipeline greens, or CI badges as substitutes for named readiness assessment.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That assessed dimensions were therefore rewritten, invented, or soft-amended.
- That unfinished WS1–WS5 assurance was completed by readiness assessment theatre.
- That a particular stack, language, or framework is privileged as law.
- That product quality or educational quality was judged.
- That constitutional artefacts or specifications were modified to match the assessment.
- That Runtime A executed or was authorised under dimension assessment.
- That overall readiness (CRRD-05) alone equals whole-concern completion (overall evaluation feeds CRRC-03; it does not replace CRRC-01…CRRC-05).

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who claims “readiness was assessed” without naming which assurance domains and CRRD dimensions were evaluated against which published categories has performed scanner theatre, not assessment fulfilment.

---

### CRRC-03 — Readiness evaluation completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that overall readiness evaluation required by the concern’s published scope has been lawfully completed under published CRRD-05 / CRRL-05 law — aggregating subordinate dimension outcomes honestly into ready / not-ready / deferred / escalated disposition — without inventing ready status while required subordinate dimensions remain unresolved without honest composition notes, and without narrating overall evaluation as Runtime A execution, artefact mutation, specification amendment, or authority creation.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Dimension assessment outcomes under applicable CRRD-01…CRRD-04 (CRRC-02 / CRRL-04 outputs).
- Published overall-readiness aggregation rules under WS6 / MS001–MS002 (especially CRRD-05 and CRRL-05).
- Boundary confirmations that aggregation does not invent ready status or privilege a stack as law.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed overall-readiness-evaluation record naming the CRRD-05 disposition and which subordinate dimension outcomes composed it (or honestly failed composition).
- Or a lawful refuse / defer / escalate record naming why overall evaluation could not be completed without invention.

**Completion conditions.**

- Overall evaluation cites published CRRD-05 rules, not unpublished “ready enough” customs.
- Affirming ready while required subordinate dimensions remain unresolved without composition notes is a hard stop.
- “Overall was checked somewhere” without named CRRD-05 disposition is not completion.
- Lawful not-ready / deferred / escalated overall outcomes still satisfy *evaluation completion* when they are the honest outcome — completeness of *evaluation* is not completeness of *ready disposition*, and neither is Runtime A permission or constitutional authority creation.
- Invented ready status to force close is a hard stop.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That completing overall evaluation executes runtime behaviour or implements Runtime A.
- That completing overall evaluation modifies constitutional artefacts or amends specifications.
- That a ready disposition freezes the implementation, stack, vendor, or release as permanent constitutional law.
- That a not-ready / deferred / escalated overall outcome may be silently erased so close can look green.
- That MS001 readiness meaning was replaced, skipped, or rewritten by overall evaluation.
- That Educational Validation Framework quality review is complete.
- That educational success or exam readiness follows from overall evaluation.

**Tutor rationale.** Recording that overall readiness was evaluated — including when the disposition is not-ready — is care. Inventing ready status so the assessment can finish is fiction. Completeness of *readiness evaluation* is not permission to *run Runtime A*.

---

### CRRC-04 — Findings publication completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every runtime readiness finding required by the concern’s published scope has been lawfully constructed and published under published CRR / CRRL rules — including ready, not-ready, deferred, and escalated dispositions — without rewriting those dispositions into Runtime A warrants, artefact mutations, specification amendments, or constitutional authority, and without inventing ready status or hiding readiness gaps.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Dimension and overall evaluation dispositions under applicable CRRD-01…CRRD-05 types (CRRL-04…CRRL-05 outputs).
- Published finding-construction and publication rules under WS6 / MS001–MS002 (especially CRRL-06 / CRRL-07).
- Boundary confirmations that findings do not invent law, execute runtime, or rewrite artefacts.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Identifiable runtime readiness finding identities with lawful dispositions, published under honesty rules.
- Or a lawful stop record naming why required findings could not be constructed / published without invention.

**Completion conditions.**

- Findings cite published CRRD criteria and named assessment subjects; unpublished readiness customs are hard stops.
- Ready / not-ready / deferred / escalated dispositions are published honestly; silent upgrade of not-ready into ready is a hard stop.
- Readiness gaps identified during assessment remain named in publication; gap erasure for tidy ready status is forbidden.
- Publication never executes Runtime A, modifies artefacts, amends specifications, or creates constitutional authority.
- “Looks published enough” without identifiable finding identity is not completion.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That published findings amend constitutional specifications or create constitutional authority.
- That published ready status authorises Runtime A execution or freezes a stack as law.
- That unpublished findings may be implied from CI theatre, tickets, or deployment gates.
- That finding publication equals educational success, conformity, verification, compliance, or certification.
- That publication may silently replace prior lawful findings (EIP-005 continuity forbids destructive cleanup).

**Tutor rationale.** Publishing that the corpus is not ready — with named gaps — is educational care. Inventing a ready finding so the pipeline can close is product fiction.

---

### CRRC-05 — Formal assessment closure

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that the readiness assessment concern was formally closed under published boundary honesty — exhausting remaining *assessment* duties for the concern without becoming a fifth phase of runtime execution, Runtime A implementation, artefact mutation, specification amendment, or constitutional authority creation.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Affirmed (or lawfully refused) CRRC-01…CRRC-04 outcomes for the concern.
- Published closure / boundary rules under WS6 / MS001–MS002 and `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md`.
- Assessment records sufficient for audit continuity and CRRCQ explainability.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Formal assessment-closure record for the concern (affirmed complete, or refused / deferred / escalated close with speakable reasons).
- Boundary preservation confirmation that forbidden acts did not occur under close theatre.

**Completion conditions.**

- Closure occurs only after required CRRC-01…CRRC-04 duties are fulfilled or lawfully exhausted with honest recording.
- Closure leaves reconstructable records of stages, assurance domains, dimensions, overall disposition, findings, and boundaries.
- Closure never executes runtime behaviour, implements Runtime A, modifies artefacts, amends specifications, or creates constitutional authority.
- Closure never upgrades published findings or invents ready status.
- Silent abandonment of an open concern without closure record is a defect.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That formal closure creates constitutional authority or amends constitutional specifications.
- That formal closure executes Runtime A or ships product behaviour.
- That formal closure modifies constitutional artefacts “as cleanup.”
- That formal closure equals student exam readiness, mastery, or educational quality.
- That formal closure equals Programme IX conformity / verification / compliance / certification completion.
- That ticket closure, sprint end, or deployment success equals constitutional assessment closure.

**Tutor rationale.** Closing the assessment book is care. Using the close stamp as a Runtime A launch button, a syllabus rewrite, or a “student ready” seal is fiction.

## 4. Application Rules

1. **All five required.** Material affirmed completion requires CRRC-01…CRRC-05 simultaneously.
2. **No invented sixth criterion.** Additional honesty checks (CRRC-06…CRRC-10) support judgement; they do not replace the catalogue.
3. **Not-ready may still complete.** Completeness of *assessment* is orthogonal to *ready disposition*. A lawfully published not-ready finding can support assessment completion when CRRC-01…CRRC-05 hold.
4. **Refuse over theatre.** Prefer not-yet / refuse-close when any required criterion fails.
5. **Compose, do not conflate.** WS1–WS5 integrity completion and Programme IX evaluative completion are orthogonal; none substitutes for CRRC.
6. **Repeatability.** Same published inputs must not yield contradictory CRRC dispositions for the same concern.
