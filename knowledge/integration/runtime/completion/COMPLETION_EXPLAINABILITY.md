## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which assurance domains* were evaluated — which readiness dimensions were satisfied, whether overall readiness was achieved, and which findings were published — is not closing honestly; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–X have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, governance, and execution speech speakable, after WS1–WS5 have made integrity speech speakable, and after WS6 / MS001–MS002 have made readiness and lifecycle speech speakable, Programme XI still needs one readiness-assessment-completion-speech answer:

> **“Which assurance domains were evaluated, which readiness dimensions were satisfied, was overall readiness achieved, and which findings were published?”**

That answer must cover every material affirmed (or refused) completion without inventing educational success, executing Runtime A, modifying artefacts, amending constitutional specifications, creating constitutional authority, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, or freezing a stack as law.

## 2. What Runtime Readiness Completion Explainability Is

**Constitutional Runtime Readiness Completion Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **fulfilment (or lawful refusal of fulfilment) of the required constitutional runtime readiness assessment lifecycle** — not to invent a second tutor voice from CI complete badges, and not to grade the student, execute Runtime A, rewrite artefacts, or amend constitutional specifications.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Assurance domains evaluated** | Named completed WS1–WS5 assurance domains / identities consumed under the assessment | Which assurance domains were evaluated? |
| **Readiness dimensions satisfied** | Named CRRD-01…CRRD-04 dimension outcomes under the concern | Which readiness dimensions were satisfied? |
| **Overall readiness achieved** | Named CRRD-05 disposition for the concern | Was overall readiness achieved? |
| **Findings published** | Named runtime readiness findings with lawful dispositions | Which findings were published? |

Runtime Readiness Completion Explainability is:

- **fulfilment-centred** — it narrates why CRRC criteria hold (or why close is refused);
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CRRD / CRRL / CRRC identities where applicable;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published specifications, completed assurance identities, named dimensions, and named findings;
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to create authority or amend corpora;
- **non-executing** — explanation never narrates completion as Runtime A warrant or runtime execution;
- **non-mutating** — explanation never narrates completion as artefact rewrite or specification amendment;
- **finding-honest** — explanation never silently upgrades published findings;
- **quality-honest** — complete is never narrated as mastery, exam readiness, or educational quality;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence / validation speech, Programme IX evaluative speech, Programme X execution speech, WS1–WS5 integrity speech, MS001 readiness speech, or MS002 lifecycle speech;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match CRRC reconstruction needs;
- **authority-honest** — explanations must not paper over authority inference or stack canonisation.

Runtime Readiness Completion Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–X / WS1–WS5 / WS6 meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a constitutional authority instrument or amendment warrant;
- a Runtime A execution warrant or silent artefact-rewrite mechanism;
- a Programme IX evaluative-finish report as constitutional law;
- a substitute for WS6 / MS001 readiness explainability or WS6 / MS002 lifecycle explainability (compose with; do not replace);
- a substitute for Programme XI WS1–WS5 completion explainability (compose with; do not collapse);
- a substitute for Programme IX CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC completion explainability (compose with; do not collapse);
- a rendering system, UI template, CI badge, deployment dashboard, or analytics dashboard;
- a claim that clear completion speech guarantees learning, a pass, conformity, Runtime A success, constitutional amendment, or artefact rewrite;
- a licence to present deployment gates, lint scores, ticket graphs, or pipeline greens as constitutional proof or educational quality.

## 3. Mandatory Explanation Questions

Every material completion judgement (affirm or refuse close) should answer all four questions:

### CRRCQ-01 — Which assurance domains were evaluated?

Name the primary completed WS1–WS5 assurance domain identities consumed under the assessment concern — dependency, catalogue, boundary, vocabulary, and/or layer — including which completion outcomes were cited and which required assurance remains missing when refusing close. Never narrate invented unfinished assurance as lawful evaluation subjects. “The corpus was scanned somewhere” is not a constitutional assurance-domain identity.

### CRRCQ-02 — Which readiness dimensions were satisfied?

Name the primary CRRD-01…CRRD-04 dimension identities assessed under the concern and their dispositions (satisfied / not satisfied / deferred / escalated / gap) — or name which required dimensions remain unassessed when refusing close. Include lawful shortfalls honestly. Never narrate invented dimensions as assessed subjects, and never narrate silent upgrade of unsatisfied dimensions into satisfied. “Looks ready enough” is not a constitutional readiness-dimension identity.

### CRRCQ-03 — Was overall readiness achieved?

Name the CRRD-05 overall readiness disposition for the concern (ready / not-ready / deferred / escalated) and which subordinate dimension outcomes composed it — or name why overall evaluation remains incomplete when refusing close. Never narrate ready while required subordinate dimensions remain unresolved without composition notes. Never narrate overall readiness as Runtime A permission, educational success, or constitutional amendment. “Looks green enough” is not a constitutional overall-readiness disposition.

### CRRCQ-04 — Which findings were published?

Name the primary runtime readiness finding identities and dispositions published under the concern (ready / not-ready / deferred / escalated) — or name which required findings remain unpublished when refusing close. Never narrate a not-ready / deferred / escalated finding as upgraded by completion, and never narrate a silent replacement of a prior lawful finding. “Looks published enough” is not a constitutional finding identity.

## 5. Audience Rules

| Audience | May hear | Must not hear as educational law |
|----------|----------|-----------------------------------|
| **Student** | Plain speech that a readiness check finished (or did not), without Runtime A launch claims or syllabus rewrite claims | “You’re exam-ready,” “the product may now run under constitutional seal,” “the law changed,” “this stack is now the syllabus,” “everything is permanently ready forever” |
| **Developer** | Named corpus identities, CRRD / CRRL / CRRC IDs, assurance / dimension / finding refs, refuse reasons | “Ship because assessment-complete,” “run Runtime A because the badge is green,” “rewrite artefacts in main to match the badge,” “freeze this framework as constitutional” |
| **Auditor** | Reconstructable CRRC-01…CRRC-05 outcomes, CRRCQ answers, boundary preservation, continuity honesty | Mute “complete” badges, invented assurance, invented ready findings, silent finding upgrades, Runtime A warrants disguised as close |

Student-facing and auditor-facing projections **must share one constitutional truth**. Vocabulary may differ; facts may not.

## 6. Forbidden Explanations

Explanations must **never**:

- narrate assessment-complete as Runtime A execution, simulation, or shipping authority;
- narrate assessment-complete as constitutional artefact mutation or soft-amendment;
- narrate assessment-complete as constitutional specification amendment or authority creation;
- narrate assessment-complete as student mastery, exam readiness, or educational quality;
- narrate assessment-complete as Programme IX conformity / verification / compliance / certification;
- invent assurance domains, readiness dimensions, overall ready status, or findings to make close speech tidy;
- silently upgrade not-ready / deferred / escalated findings under completion narration;
- present deployment gates, CI greens, tickets, Twin confidence, or elapsed time as constitutional fulfilment proof;
- replace MS001 readiness explainability or MS002 lifecycle explainability with mute “complete” theatre;
- freeze a stack, vendor, or Runtime A shape as constitutional law in completion speech.
