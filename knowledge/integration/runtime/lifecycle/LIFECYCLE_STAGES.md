## 1. Purpose

Readiness assessment without a closed stage catalogue invents law by proximity: whichever deployment gate, ticket status, or reviewer habit happens to finish becomes the tutor’s “proof the system ran a constitutional runtime readiness assessment.”

This catalogue names the only lawful constitutional runtime readiness lifecycle stages — and binds each stage to purpose, inputs, outputs, and permitted / prohibited activities.

---

## 2. Stage Principles

1. **Ordered and skip-disciplined.** Stages may be brief under thin scope; mandatory initiation, assurance collection, prerequisite validation, dimension evaluation, overall evaluation, finding construction, and publication obligations may not be silently omitted when a readiness finding is claimed.
2. **Completed assurance before findings.** Identifiable published corpora and completed WS1–WS5 assurance are confirmed before ready / not-ready dispositions are constructed.
3. **Explainability accumulates.** Each stage leaves traceable artefacts for `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.
4. **Refusal is a stage outcome.** Not-ready, deferred, escalated, and refuse-progress are first-class conclusions.
5. **Deterministic transitions.** Same published inputs ⇒ same next stage outcomes.
6. **Stages coordinate; they do not legislate.** No stage creates, modifies, or redefines constitutional law.
7. **Stages do not become constitutional authority.** Publication never freezes a corpus topology as permanently lawful or replaces published corpora.
8. **Stages do not execute runtime.** Findings report readiness; they never implement Runtime A or run product behaviour.
9. **Stages apply published CRRD law.** Dimensions and evaluation rules remain owned by WS6 / MS001.
10. **Stages consume completed assurance.** They never re-author WS1–WS5 findings under readiness pretext.

## 3. Catalogue Overview

| ID | Stage | Constitutional purpose (short) | Primary inputs | Primary outputs |
|----|-------|--------------------------------|----------------|-----------------|
| **CRRL-01** | Assessment Initiation | Open a readiness assessment against named published corpora and scope | Published corpus identities + concern scope + optional CRRD indication | Initiated concern record (or refuse-open) |
| **CRRL-02** | Assurance Evidence Collection | Gather completed WS1–WS5 assurance evidence for the concern | Initiated concern + candidate completion identities | Collected assurance evidence set (or refuse-collect) |
| **CRRL-03** | Prerequisite Validation | Validate readiness prerequisites and completeness | Collected assurance + published prerequisite rules | Prerequisite disposition (or refuse-validate) |
| **CRRL-04** | Readiness Dimension Evaluation | Evaluate required CRRD-01…CRRD-04 dimensions | Prerequisite-valid bundle + CRRD rules | Dimension dispositions + named gaps (or refuse-evaluate) |
| **CRRL-05** | Overall Readiness Evaluation | Aggregate under CRRD-05 overall constitutional readiness | Dimension dispositions + concern scope | Overall readiness disposition (or refuse-aggregate) |
| **CRRL-06** | Finding Construction | Emit ready / not-ready / deferred / escalated under published criteria | Stage dispositions + CRRD criteria | Runtime readiness findings |
| **CRRL-07** | Readiness Publication | Publish findings and assessment records under honesty rules | Findings + boundary confirmations | Published assessment artefacts (or refuse-publish) |

Material readiness assessment claiming constitutional findings must map to this sequence as published law requires. Stages may be brief; none may invent a stage outside this catalogue.

**Relation to CRRD / CRRO / CT / CC / EV / WS1–WS5:** Those catalogues remain defined solely by their owners. CRRL stages *organise when* readiness assessment activities occur; they do not replace, reclassify, invent, amend, or rewrite those catalogues.

```text
CRRL-01  Assessment Initiation
           ↓
CRRL-02  Assurance Evidence Collection
           ↓
CRRL-03  Prerequisite Validation
           ↓
CRRL-04  Readiness Dimension Evaluation
           ↓
CRRL-05  Overall Readiness Evaluation
           ↓
CRRL-06  Finding Construction
           ↓
```

Lawful early stop dispositions (refuse-open / refuse-collect / refuse-validate / refuse-evaluate / refuse-aggregate / deferred / escalated / not-ready) may exhaust remaining *evaluation* duties while still requiring honest finding construction, publication (or refuse-publish), of what occurred — never silent disappearance of the concern.

## 5. Stage Definitions

### CRRL-01 — Assessment Initiation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Open a material runtime readiness assessment concern by naming the published constitutional corpora under assessment, the readiness concern scope, and the intended CRRD dimension set — without inventing law, opening against unpublished customs, treating initiation as a ready finding, or executing runtime behaviour.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Identifiable published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–X specification references for the concern scope
- Concern identity (what corpus-readiness-for-implementation question is under assessment)
- Optional published indication of required CRRD-01…CRRD-05 dimensions for the scope
- Optional identifiable supporting authority / lineage / completion references (not rewritten)

**Constitutional outputs**

- An initiated assessment concern record naming corpora, specifications, scope, and intended CRRD type set — **or**
- Lawful refuse-open when standards are unpublished, scope is theatrical, or initiation would invent law

**Permitted activities**

- Name published corpora and specifications as assessment standards
- Bound the concern scope without amending corpora
- Record intended CRRD types from the published catalogue
- Refuse initiation when constitutional preconditions fail
- Preserve initiation audit identity for later stages

**Prohibited activities**

- Treat “how main deploys,” tribal knowledge, or stack preference as the standard
- Create or modify constitutional specifications by opening a concern
- Issue a ready / not-ready finding at initiation
- Invent unpublished readiness dimensions
- Skip initiation when a material finding will later be claimed
- Create constitutional authority, execute runtime, or implement Runtime A at initiation

**Authority preserved**  
Published corpora remain the sole standard. Initiation organises; it does not legislate or execute.

---

### CRRL-02 — Assurance Evidence Collection

**Constitutional purpose**  
Gather **completed WS1–WS5 assurance evidence** identifiable for the initiated concern — so later stages evaluate real completed integrity outcomes, not anonymous theatre — without inventing, rewriting, or re-authoring assurance findings.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Initiated concern from CRRL-01
- Candidate completed assurance identities from Programme XI / WS1–WS5 (dependency, catalogue, boundary, vocabulary, layer — including lawful completion identities)
- Published CRRD required-input rules from WS6 / MS001
- Optional supporting CT lineage identities already established under upstream law (unchanged)

**Constitutional outputs**

- A collected assurance evidence set bound to the concern and intended CRRD types — **or**
- Lawful refuse-collect when assurance is missing, unfinished, forged, invented, unpublished, or would require unlawful mutation / re-authorship to “collect”

**Permitted activities**

- Identify and name completed WS1–WS5 assurance outcomes
- Bind collected evidence to the initiated concern and intended CRRD types
- Record honest gaps where required assurance is incomplete or absent
- Refuse collection when evidence cannot be honestly affirmed as completed
- Preserve collection records for audit and explainability

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent missing completed assurance
- Re-author, soft-amend, or rewrite WS1–WS5 findings under collection pretence
- Treat CI inventory scans, ticket links, or deployment noise as constitutional assurance without published mapping
- Redefine educational or constitutional meaning while “collecting”
- Present collection as itself a ready finding or a freeze of stack authority
- Create constitutional authority or execute runtime under collection pretence

**Authority preserved**  
Completed assurance remains an upstream fact. Collection organises identity; it does not author assurance or educational truth.

---

### CRRL-03 — Prerequisite Validation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Validate that **published readiness prerequisites** — including required completed assurance completeness, CRRD scope honesty, corpus citeability, and boundary integrity material to the concern — hold before dimension evaluation proceeds — without inventing completion, skipping unfinished assurance, or privileging a stack as law.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Collected assurance evidence set from CRRL-02
- Published readiness prerequisite rules from WS6 / MS001
- Published CRRD required-input tables for the concern scope
- Optional supporting authority / lineage references consumed as inputs (unchanged)

**Constitutional outputs**

- A validated prerequisite record for the concern — **or**
- Lawful refuse-validate when prerequisites are unmet, completion is invented, unfinished assurance is silently skipped, or validation would require rewriting law or artefacts

**Permitted activities**

- Confirm reconstructable completeness for in-scope WS1–WS5 inputs
- Confirm intended CRRD scope remains published and honest for the concern
- Confirm required VI–X corpora remain citeable as published guidance (presence, not re-determination)
- Refuse validation when prerequisites cannot be honestly affirmed
- Preserve prerequisite records for audit and explainability

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent completion identities to force validation
- Silently skip unfinished prerequisite assurance so later stages can affirm ready
- Treat deployment gates or CI greens as prerequisite satisfaction
- Redefine CRRD meaning while validating
- Present prerequisite validation as itself a ready finding
- Create constitutional authority, execute runtime, or modify artefacts under validation pretence

**Authority preserved**  
Published prerequisites remain upstream law. Validation confirms; it does not invent readiness or rewrite corpora.

---

### CRRL-04 — Readiness Dimension Evaluation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Evaluate required **CRRD-01…CRRD-04** readiness dimensions (structural, governance, semantic, architectural) under published WS6 / MS001 rules from completed assurance and published guidance — without inventing dimensions, re-authoring assurance, or executing Runtime A.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Prerequisite-valid (or honestly refused) bundle from CRRL-03
- Published CRRD-01…CRRD-04 definitions and evaluation rules from [`../READINESS_MODEL.md`](../READINESS_MODEL.md)
- Collected completed assurance evidence bound to each required dimension
- Published VI–X guidance presence references where required by dimension

**Constitutional outputs**

- Per-dimension dispositions (ready / not-ready / deferred / escalated) with named readiness gaps — **or**
- Lawful refuse-evaluate when evaluation would invent dimensions, re-author assurance, execute runtime, or rewrite law

**Permitted activities**

- Apply published CRRD-01…CRRD-04 only as required by concern scope
- Consume completed assurance as dimension inputs
- Name readiness gaps honestly
- Refuse evaluation when required inputs are unavailable without inventing them
- Preserve dimension evaluation records for audit and explainability

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent unpublished readiness dimensions
- Re-author WS1–WS5 findings under dimension evaluation pretence
- Soft-omit a required dimension to force overall ready later
- Execute Runtime A, services, or adapters to “prove” a dimension
- Determine educational quality, mastery, or student exam readiness
- Create constitutional authority or amend specifications under evaluation pretence

**Authority preserved**  
CRRD catalogue remains owned by WS6 / MS001. Evaluation applies; it does not legislate or execute.

---

### CRRL-05 — Overall Readiness Evaluation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Aggregate required dimension dispositions under **CRRD-05 overall constitutional readiness** for the published concern — without inventing missing dimension results, silently overriding not-ready subordinates, or treating aggregation as new law.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Dimension dispositions and gap records from CRRL-04
- Published CRRD-05 aggregation rules from WS6 / MS001
- Concern scope and composition notes requirements

**Constitutional outputs**

- An overall readiness disposition for the concern — **or**
- Lawful refuse-aggregate when aggregation would invent missing dimensions, hide required not-ready subordinates without composition notes, or become legislation / runtime execution

**Permitted activities**

- Aggregate under published CRRD-05 rules only
- Require honest composition notes when lawful partial scopes omit optional dimensions
- Refuse aggregation that would invent ready status
- Preserve overall evaluation records for audit and explainability

**Prohibited activities**

- Affirm overall ready while any *required* subordinate dimension remains not-ready / deferred / escalated without honest composition notes
- Invent dimension results at aggregation time
- Collapse overall readiness into Runtime A execution warrant
- Treat overall ready as student exam readiness or product success
- Create constitutional authority or amend specifications under aggregation pretence

**Authority preserved**  
Overall readiness remains an aggregate disposition under published CRRD-05. Aggregation coordinates; it does not legislate.

---

### CRRL-06 — Finding Construction

**Constitutional purpose**  
Construct **runtime readiness findings** — ready / not-ready / deferred / escalated — bound to producing stages, consumed assurance, dimensions, and named gaps — without findings becoming amendments, educational quality grades, runtime execution warrants, or artefact mutations.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Stage dispositions from CRRL-01…CRRL-05
- Published CRRD evaluation criteria and disposition vocabulary from WS6 / MS001
- Named readiness gaps and boundary confirmations material to the concern

**Constitutional outputs**

- Runtime readiness findings with reconstructable binding to domains, dimensions, gaps, stages, and consumed assurance

**Permitted activities**

- Emit ready / not-ready / deferred / escalated under published criteria
- Bind findings to producing CRRL stages and CRRD identities
- Include named readiness gaps in finding records
- Prefer understatement over invented ready status
- Preserve finding identities for publication and audit

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent ready status without reconstructable stage / assurance / dimension binding
- Convert findings into constitutional amendments or soft law
- Narrate findings as mastery, student exam readiness, or educational quality
- Encode Runtime A execution, stack freezes, or authority creation in findings
- Drop gap disclosure to make findings look clean
- Re-author WS1–WS5 assurance inside finding prose

**Authority preserved**  
Findings report readiness dispositions. They never become the next constitutional standard.

---

### CRRL-07 — Readiness Publication

**Constitutional purpose**  
Publish readiness findings and assessment records under **published honesty rules** — so students, developers, and auditors can reconstruct the lifecycle — without inventing ready status, hiding gaps, rewriting boundaries, executing runtime, or freezing a stack as law.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Findings from CRRL-06
- Assessment records from prior stages
- Boundary confirmations against `LIFECYCLE_BOUNDARIES.md`
- Explainability answers required by `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md`

**Constitutional outputs**

- Published assessment artefacts (findings, gap records, stage traces, boundary confirmations) — **or**
- Lawful refuse-publish when publication would invent ready status, hide gaps, rewrite boundaries, execute runtime, create authority, or freeze a stack as law

**Permitted activities**

- Publish findings and records under honesty rules
- Disclose readiness gaps and refuse-* dispositions
- Confirm boundaries remained intact
- Refuse publication when honesty cannot be affirmed
- Preserve publication artefacts for continuity (EIP-005)

**Prohibited activities**

- Publish invented ready status or silently drop violated / not-ready dispositions
- Hide readiness gaps or unfinished prerequisite assurance
- Rewrite constitutional artefacts or specifications at publication time
- Execute Runtime A or implement services under publication pretence
- Present publication as new constitutional law or frozen stack authority
- Create constitutional authority by publishing

**Authority preserved**  
Publication communicates findings. Published corpora remain unchanged and remain the sole constitutional standard.

---

## 6. Stage Transition Rules

1. **Forward only under honesty.** A stage may advance only when its outputs are reconstructable or a lawful refuse-* / deferred / escalated disposition is recorded.
2. **No silent skips.** Claiming a material readiness finding without mappable CRRL-01…CRRL-07 coordination is defective.
3. **Early stop still publishes honesty.** Lawful early stops still require finding construction and publication (or refuse-publish) of what occurred.
4. **Same inputs ⇒ same outcomes.** Race-dependent stage outcomes are defects under CRRLO-02.
5. **No back-door legislation.** Returning to earlier stages to “fix” corpora, invent assurance, or execute runtime is prohibited; propose amendments upstream outside lifecycle pretence.
6. **CRRD fidelity.** Stages never invent readiness dimensions outside CRRD-01…CRRD-05.
7. **Assurance fidelity.** Stages never re-author WS1–WS5 findings to force ready status.

> Stages that invent law, invent assurance, execute runtime, modify artefacts, create constitutional authority, or amend specifications are not lifecycle stages — they are constitutional defects with progress bars.**
