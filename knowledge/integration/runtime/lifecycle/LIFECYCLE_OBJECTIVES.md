## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “scanner finished,” “stages skipped for speed,” or “ready badge shown.” The tutor optimises **honest organisation of readiness assessment work**: only a repeatable, assurance-traceable, architecturally coherent, constitutionally confident, and explainable sequence — gathering completed WS1–WS5 assurance and evaluating readiness under CRRD-01…CRRD-05 — may support lawful runtime readiness findings.

These objectives bind every constitutional runtime readiness lifecycle and every successor coordination machinery that claims constitutional runtime readiness lifecycle meaning.

## 2. Primary Objective

### CRRLO-01 — Preserve implementation readiness

**Definition.** Ensure that every material runtime readiness lifecycle organises assessments through recognised CRRL-01…CRRL-07 stages — initiating against identifiable published readiness concerns, gathering completed WS1–WS5 assurance evidence, validating prerequisites, evaluating readiness dimensions and overall readiness under published CRRD-01…CRRD-05, and constructing / publishing findings — so that ready dispositions occur only after lawful initiation, collection, validation, and evaluation, and that findings are published without becoming law, runtime execution, artefact mutation, or replacements for constitutional authority.

**Includes:**

- Binding every material lifecycle to identifiable published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–X specifications, WS1–WS5 completed assurance identities, and WS6 / MS001 CRRD catalogue law.
- Gathering only completed / identifiable assurance evidence as inputs.
- Applying only published CRRD-01…CRRD-05 dimensions across stages.
- Preferring lawful not-ready / deferred / escalated / refuse-progress outcomes over inventing assurance, inventing prerequisites, inventing dimensions, executing runtime, mutating corpora, or creating authority so the lifecycle “completes.”
- Keeping lifecycle progress distinct from educational quality, mastery, student exam readiness, product success, deployment-gate greenery, Runtime A execution, or constitutional amendment.
- Preserving the Model’s posture: stages coordinate assessment; they do not become law, runtime engines, or constitutional authority.

**Excludes:**

- Treating green CI, coverage percentages, linter scores, Twin estimates, Adaptive scores, UI ticks, or deployment gates as lifecycle fulfilment.
- Soft-amending specifications, inventing unpublished customs, inventing or rewriting assurance, inventing completion identities, mutating artefacts, executing Runtime A, or privileging “how main deploys” to force progress.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for lawful coordination.
- Declaring “close enough” publication without documenting a corpus amendment.
- Freezing an implementation as permanently lawful because the lifecycle published findings.
- Creating constitutional authority under a lifecycle badge.
- Collapsing “corpus ready to support implementation” into “Runtime A executed.”

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software pretends unfinished or dishonest readiness assessment is fine because integration needs a finished green gate. Coordination honesty is care; lifecycle theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CRRLO-02 — Preserve assessment repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that the same published readiness concern, the same consumed completed assurance evidence, the same published CRRD criteria, and the same CRRL stage sequence yield the same readiness disposition (ready / not-ready / deferred / escalated) and the same lawful stage outcomes — absent a published non-deterministic exception (none are granted by this Model for constitutional meaning or readiness status).

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who treats the same completed syllabus assurance as ready or not depending on which reviewer woke first — or skips stages randomly — is not coaching; they are gambling.

**Manifestations:**

- Only CRRL-01…CRRL-07 may organise constitutional runtime readiness lifecycles.
- Stage purpose, inputs, outputs, and permitted / prohibited activities remain as published in `LIFECYCLE_STAGES.md`.
- Stage transitions and outcomes are corpus-bound and reproducible.
- Parallel CI runners, sharding, and cache races must not change constitutional disposition or invent stage skips.
- Explicit multi-dimension CRRD sets use published conjunction / published stop rules, not random reviewer races.
- “Eventually consistent lifecycle meaning” is a constitutional defect.
- Invented or unstable assurance inputs, and invented prerequisites, are hard stops — not soft variables.

---

### CRRLO-03 — Preserve assurance traceability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material lifecycle gathers — and leaves reconstructable — completed WS1–WS5 assurance identities and published corpus references consumed as readiness inputs, so origin of every ready / not-ready disposition remains speakable across stages.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who cannot say *which completed integrity assessments* yesterday’s “ready” seal consumed cannot be trusted. Lifecycles without assurance-traceability are educational amnesia with a progress bar.

**Manifestations:**

- CRRL-02 requires identifiable completed assurance evidence; missing identities are defects, not soft variables.
- Findings cite corpus paths, completion identities, and CRRD identities, not only ticket IDs or commit SHAs.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing prior lawful assessment history to simplify the next report.
- Programme IX CT lineage may support reconstruction; CRRL never treats CT records as a licence to invent completed assurance.
- Assurance-traceability preservation never authorises inventing missing completion artefacts or re-authoring WS1–WS5 findings.

---

### CRRLO-04 — Preserve architectural coherence

**Definition.** Ensure that lifecycle organisation honours published architectural coherence obligations from WS6 / MS001 — structural, governance, semantic, and architectural readiness dimensions remain evaluated against completed layer / dependency / boundary / vocabulary assurance and published VI–X guidance — so no subordinate horizon silently absorbs a superior’s authority or invents coherence through stage progress.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who lets yesterday’s worksheet rewrite today’s syllabus architecture has abandoned institutional order for convenience.

**Manifestations:**

- CRRL-04 evaluates required CRRD-01…CRRD-04 dimensions under published MS001 rules; silent omission to force ready is defective.
- CRRL-05 aggregates under CRRD-05 without inventing missing dimension results.
- Workstream / milestone corpora remain subordinate to their published programme models.
- Sibling composition within a layer is permitted only as published; it never collapses distinct horizons into one mega-law.
- Forbidden actions in `LIFECYCLE_BOUNDARIES.md` are hard stops.
- Architectural coherence preservation never authorises reordering layers, softening boundaries, or inventing coherence outside published assurance.

---

### CRRLO-05 — Preserve constitutional confidence

**Definition.** Ensure that lifecycle findings support honest constitutional confidence — so ready claims consume completed assurance and published guidance through lawful stages rather than inventing confidence despite unfinished prerequisites, unresolved dimensions, or incompletely assessed corpora — and never elevate lifecycle machinery into constitutional authority.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who declares “constitutionally confident — ship it” while integrity assessments remain unfinished is performing confidence theatre, not care.

**Manifestations:**

- Ready findings published after CRRL-01…CRRL-07 may inform downstream implementation work; they never themselves certify educational readiness, mastery, product success, or freeze stack authority.
- Not-ready / deferred / escalated findings, and refuse-* dispositions, block honest confidence claims that depend on incomplete or broken assurance.
- Confidence proxies (CI greens, coverage, deployment gates) never redefine CRRLO-01…CRRLO-06.
- Constitutional confidence here means *honest corpus readiness for lawful implementation support* — not student exam readiness and not Runtime A deployment theatre.
- Preserving confidence never authorises rewriting artefacts, executing runtime, creating authority, or skipping stages so a release can ship.

---

### CRRLO-06 — Preserve explainability and auditability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material runtime readiness lifecycle can honestly answer the mandatory explainability questions in `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md` and leaves a reconstructable trail: which assurance domains were evaluated, which readiness dimensions were satisfied, whether readiness gaps were identified, and which findings were produced.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later say *why* a corpus was called ready — or *at which stage* a finding arose — cannot be trusted. Silent green readiness seals destroy student and auditor trust.

**Manifestations:**

- Explainability obligations apply to all material lifecycles.
- Student / developer / auditor projections share one truth with different vocabulary.
- Audit records preserve constitutional references, not only technical deployment logs.
- Consumed corpus identities, completed assurance identities, CRRD identities, CRRL stage identities, gap records, and finding dispositions are recorded when material.
- Missing CRRL identity, missing domain / assurance refs, missing dimension status, missing gap disclosure, missing finding disposition, or missing boundary confirmation is a defect.
- Lifecycle progress is never narrated as mastery, student exam readiness, educational quality, new law, runtime execution, or a freeze of stack authority.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing prior lawful lifecycle history to simplify the next report.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CRRLO-01 conflicts with “helping” unfinished assurance look lifecycle-complete | Lawful coordination honesty wins — not-ready / deferred / escalated / refuse-progress |
| CRRLO-02 conflicts with race-dependent reviewer outcomes or private stage shortcuts | Assessment repeatability wins |
| CRRLO-03 conflicts with log volume reduction that erases assurance identities | Assurance traceability wins |
| CRRLO-04 conflicts with soft-absorbing a superior programme into a subordinate or inventing coherence | Architectural coherence wins |
| CRRLO-05 conflicts with shipping despite incomplete or dishonest readiness assessment | Honest constitutional confidence wins — refuse false ready |
| CRRLO-06 conflicts with opaque “trust us” readiness badges | Explainability and auditability win |

No supporting objective authorises violating CRRLO-01.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — Constitution / EIP integrity obligations, Programme VI–X authority preservation, WS1–WS5 assurance honesty, and WS6 / MS001 readiness objectives for the lifecycle horizon.

## 5. Non-Objectives

The constitutional runtime readiness lifecycle does **not** optimise for:

- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- CI throughput, flaky-test suppression, or coverage theatre as educational success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when specifications or completed assurance are incomplete;
- collapsing CRRL stages into one mega-step that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics / deployment-gate signals as substitutes for published completed assurance;
- freezing implementations, releases, or stacks as permanently lawful;
- treating documentation as optional commentary on whatever code already shipped;
- replacing MS001 readiness meaning, Programme IX CT lineage preservation, or Programme IX–X evaluative / execution horizons;
- executing Runtime A or product behaviour so readiness “looks proven”;
- rewriting, inventing, or deleting constitutional artefacts so readiness looks tidy;
- creating constitutional authority under a lifecycle badge.
