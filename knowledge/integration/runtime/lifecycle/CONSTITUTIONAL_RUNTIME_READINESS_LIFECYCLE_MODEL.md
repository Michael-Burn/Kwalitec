Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

That answer must ensure assessments initiate against published readiness concerns; gather completed assurance evidence from Workstreams 1–5; validate prerequisite completeness; evaluate readiness dimensions; evaluate overall readiness; construct readiness findings; and publish those findings — while remaining fully repeatable, consistent, implementation-neutral, auditable, and explainable — without executing runtime behaviour, implementing Runtime A, modifying constitutional artefacts, creating constitutional authority, or amending constitutional specifications.

This document records that posture so every future runtime readiness lifecycle (and successor) has a single constitutional reference for *how readiness assessment work may be sequenced without becoming law, a runtime engine, or a soft constitution*.

> **The Constitutional Runtime Readiness Lifecycle Model describes how readiness assessment activities are lawfully coordinated from initiation through publication.

## 2. What the Constitutional Runtime Readiness Lifecycle Is

The **constitutional runtime readiness lifecycle** is the binding organisation by which **runtime readiness assessments** may proceed through recognised stages — initiating against published readiness concerns, gathering completed WS1–WS5 assurance evidence, validating prerequisite completeness, evaluating readiness under published CRRD-01…CRRD-05 dimensions, aggregating overall readiness, and constructing / publishing **runtime readiness findings** that preserve implementation readiness honesty, assessment repeatability, assurance traceability, architectural coherence, constitutional confidence, auditability, and explainability — without becoming a source of constitutional law, a runtime executor, a rewriter of artefacts, a substitute for MS001 readiness meaning, or a replacement for upstream programme authority.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Published constitutional corpus** | Constitution, EIP, Programme VI–XI specifications and their subordinate workstream / milestone corpora | Which corpus may the lifecycle organise readiness assessment against? |
| **Completed prerequisite assurance** | Lawful WS1–WS5 integrity completion outcomes available for honest consumption | Which completed assurance may the lifecycle gather? |
| **Readiness prerequisite** | Published precondition for lawful readiness evaluation (completed assurance identity, CRRD scope, corpus citeability, boundary integrity) | Which prerequisites must be satisfied before dimension evaluation? |
| **Readiness dimension** | A recognised CRRD-01…CRRD-05 category of readiness judgement from WS6 / MS001 | Which readiness kinds may stages apply? |
| **Lifecycle stage** | A recognised CRRL-01…CRRL-07 organisational step | Which coordination step is active? |
| **Readiness gap** | Named missing or incomplete assurance input, dimension shortfall, or coherence defect blocking ready status | Were any readiness gaps identified? |
| **Runtime readiness finding** | Lawful disposition: ready / not-ready / deferred / escalated — never “new law” and never executed runtime behaviour | May implementation proceed under published guidance? |
| **Assessment record / publication artefact** | Audit-speakable record of domains, completed assurance consumed, prerequisites, dimensions, gaps, stages, findings, and preserved boundaries | Can the assessment be reconstructed? |
| **Constitutional authority** | The exclusive right of published corpora to define educational and execution meaning | Who owns the standard? |
| **Implementation neutrality** | Lifecycle coordination is judged against law and completed assurance, not against a preferred stack, vendor, or topology | Remains the stack replaceable? |
| **Repeatability** | Same published concerns, same consumed completed assurance, same prerequisites, same stage sequence → same disposition | Can the judgement be reproduced? |
| **Auditability** | Every material lifecycle leaves reconstructable constitutional traces | Can the judgement be reconstructed? |
| **Explainability** | Domains, dimensions, gaps, findings, stages, and boundaries are speakable | Can the judgement be narrated honestly? |

The constitutional runtime readiness lifecycle is:

- **coordination-centred** — it organises readiness assessments; it never authors constitutional obligations;
- **assurance-consuming** — it gathers completed WS1–WS5 assurance findings; it never invents, rewrites, or re-authors them;
- **prerequisite-bound** — it validates readiness prerequisites before dimension evaluation; it never skips confirmation theatre;
- **catalogue-closed** — only CRRL-01…CRRL-07 may be applied as constitutional runtime readiness lifecycle stages;
- **type-faithful** — stages apply only published CRRD-01…CRRD-05 dimensions from WS6 / MS001; they never invent dimensions;
- **evaluation-honest** — it evaluates readiness; it never executes runtime behaviour or implements Runtime A;
- **gap-revealing** — assessments name readiness gaps rather than inventing readiness theatre;
- **finding-producing** — assessments emit ready / not-ready / deferred / escalated dispositions;
- **publication-honest** — findings are published under honesty rules; publication never becomes law or runtime execution;
- **authority-preserving** — published corpora remain the sole source of constitutional meaning;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by lifecycle law;
- **corpus-bound** — every material lifecycle cites identifiable published specifications and completed assurance identities;
- **audit-capable** — every material lifecycle leaves reconstructable constitutional traces;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which domains / dimensions / gaps / findings applied under which stages;
- **non-legislative** — a readiness finding never becomes constitutional law or a soft amendment;
- **non-substitutive** — lifecycle machinery never becomes the constitutional standard for the next judgement;
- **non-mutating** — the lifecycle never modifies constitutional artefacts or specifications under coordination pretext;
- **non-executing** — the lifecycle never runs Runtime A, services, adapters, or product behaviour.

The constitutional runtime readiness lifecycle is **not**:

- a second Educational Constitution, EIP, Programme VI–X, WS1–WS5, or WS6 / MS001 amendment path;
- a licence to reinterpret coach meaning, ownership maps, tip warrants, runtime contracts, conformance criteria, traceability meaning, verification meaning, compliance meaning, certification meaning, evolution meaning, execution meaning, WS1–WS5 integrity meaning, or CRRD dimensions in lifecycle prose;
- a testing framework, CI/CD pipeline, GitHub Action, deployment gate, feature flag, or OpenAPI readiness checker;
- a claim that “lifecycle complete” guarantees learning, mastery, exam readiness, product success, perpetual conformity, or frozen stack authority;
- Runtime A, Programme VIII runtime execution, or any product runtime behaviour;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS6 / MS001 readiness meaning — CRR / CRRD define readiness evaluation; CRRL sequences when that evaluation occurs;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS1–WS5 integrity / lifecycle / completion — those validate and close *corpus structure*; CRRL *consumes completed outcomes* and never re-runs them as a substitute;
- a substitute for Programme VIII runtime architecture — Programme VIII defines *how* software may execute law; CRRL coordinates *whether the corpus is ready to support that implementation*;
- a substitute for Programme IX conformance / verification / compliance / certification — those evaluate *implementations* against published obligations; CRRL coordinates *corpus readiness assessment sequencing*;
- a substitute for Programme X execution / decision / audit / explainability — those organise constitutional activity; CRRL never executes those activities;
- a licence to invent completed assurance, skip prerequisites, execute runtime, modify artefacts, create constitutional authority, or amend specifications under “lifecycle” pretext.

## 3. Lifecycle Posture (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material constitutional runtime readiness lifecycle travels through seven constitutional stages. These are **normative coordination obligations**, not an implementation architecture and not a CI job graph.

```
CRRL-01 Assessment Initiation
        │
        ▼
CRRL-02 Assurance Evidence Collection
        │
        ▼
CRRL-03 Prerequisite Validation
        │
        ▼
CRRL-04 Readiness Dimension Evaluation
        │
        ▼
CRRL-05 Overall Readiness Evaluation
        │
        ├── not-ready / deferred / escalated (lawful finding — corpus unchanged)
        │
        └── ready (or other lawful disposition) →
                │
                ▼
CRRL-06 Finding Construction
        │
        ▼
CRRL-07 Readiness Publication
        │
        ▼
Explain domains / dimensions / gaps / findings / boundaries
```

| Stage | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **CRRL-01 Assessment Initiation** | Open a readiness assessment concern against named published corpora, readiness scope, and intended CRRD set | Scope is unpublished custom, tribal knowledge, or “how main deploys today” |
| **CRRL-02 Assurance Evidence Collection** | Gather identifiable completed WS1–WS5 assurance evidence as inputs | Evidence is invented, unfinished, re-authored, or would require rewriting assurance to “collect” it |
| **CRRL-03 Prerequisite Validation** | Confirm published readiness prerequisites and completeness for the concern | Validation would invent completion, skip unfinished assurance, or privilege a stack as law |
| **CRRL-04 Readiness Dimension Evaluation** | Evaluate required CRRD-01…CRRD-04 dimensions under published MS001 rules | Evaluation would invent dimensions, re-author WS1–WS5 findings, or execute Runtime A |
| **CRRL-05 Overall Readiness Evaluation** | Aggregate under CRRD-05 overall constitutional readiness | Aggregation would affirm ready while required subordinate dimensions remain unresolved without honest composition notes |
| **CRRL-06 Finding Construction** | Emit ready / not-ready / deferred / escalated findings under published criteria | Findings would become amendments, educational quality grades, runtime execution warrants, or artefact mutations |
| **CRRL-07 Readiness Publication** | Publish findings and assessment records under published honesty rules | Publication would invent ready status, hide gaps, rewrite boundaries, execute runtime, or freeze a stack as law |

> **The lifecycle never “best-effort invents” an eighth stage called law-making, runtime execution, artefact mutation, stack canonisation, or constitutional authority creation.**

## 6. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Runtime Readiness Lifecycle Model exists so that:

1. **Law remains upstream of coordination** — stages organise assessment against published corpora and completed assurance; progress does not quietly become the specifications.
2. **Assurance gathering remains honest** — collection names completed WS1–WS5 outcomes; it does not invent unfinished assurance to force ready status.
3. **Prerequisites remain reconstructable** — prerequisite validation confirms completeness; it does not invent missing completion identities.
4. **Dimension evaluation remains faithful** — stages apply published CRRD-01…CRRD-05 only; they never invent dimensions or re-author assurance.
5. **Overall readiness remains honest** — aggregation under CRRD-05 never silently omits required not-ready subordinate dimensions.
6. **Gaps remain speakable** — readiness gaps are named; they are never erased so a badge can glow.
7. **Findings remain sequenced** — ready / not-ready / deferred / escalated dispositions arise after lawful initiation, collection, validation, and evaluation — not as random green theatre.
8. **Authority remains honest** — no team may treat a published finding as the new constitutional source.
9. **Implementations remain replaceable** — lifecycle law does not freeze Flask, SQLAlchemy, a particular API shape, or a CI vendor as law.
10. **Runtime remains unexecuted** — readiness lifecycle never runs Runtime A under assessment pretext.
11. **Repeatability remains honest** — the same published concerns, consumed assurance, and stage sequence yield the same disposition.
12. **Audit remains possible** — every material lifecycle can be reconstructed against named domains, dimensions, gaps, stages, and findings.
13. **Explainability remains intact** — lifecycle speech describes coordination of readiness assessment; it does not redefine what learning means.
14. **Students remain protected** — product theatre cannot launder unfinished constitutional guidance as “lifecycle ready.”
