## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which assurance domains* were evaluated — which readiness dimensions were satisfied, whether readiness gaps were identified, and which findings were produced — is not coaching; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–X have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, runtime, governance, and execution speech speakable, after WS1–WS5 have made integrity assurance speech speakable, and after WS6 / MS001 has made readiness evaluation speech speakable, Programme XI still needs one lifecycle-speech answer:

> **“Which assurance domains were evaluated, which readiness dimensions were satisfied, were any readiness gaps identified, and which findings were produced?”**

That answer must cover every material runtime readiness lifecycle without inventing educational meaning, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, mutating artefacts, creating constitutional authority, executing Runtime A, freezing a stack as law, inventing unpublished CRRD or CRRL types, replacing MS001 readiness meaning, or presenting publication as educational quality.

## 2. What Lifecycle Explainability Is

**Constitutional Runtime Readiness Lifecycle Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **coordination of named readiness assessments through published CRRL stages** — gathering completed assurance, validating prerequisites, evaluating published CRRD dimensions, aggregating overall readiness, and producing / publishing findings — not to invent a second tutor voice from deployment gates, not to grade the student, and not to replace constitutional authority.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Assurance domains evaluated** | Named WS1–WS5 / VI–X domains whose completed assurance or published guidance presence was consumed | Which assurance domains were evaluated? |
| **Readiness dimensions satisfied** | Named CRRD-01…CRRD-05 dispositions for the concern | Which readiness dimensions were satisfied? |
| **Readiness gaps identified** | Named missing / incomplete assurance, dimension shortfalls, or coherence defects | Were any readiness gaps identified? |
| **Findings produced** | ready / not-ready / deferred / escalated (with stage binding and composition notes) | Which findings were produced? |

Lifecycle Explainability is:

- **coordination-centred** — it narrates domains → dimensions → gaps → findings under law;
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CRRL and CRRD identities;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published corpora and completed assurance identities;
- **assurance-honest** — every material justification cites identifiable completed assurance consumed (or names the hard stop / gap when unmet);
- **dimension-honest** — every material justification cites CRRD dispositions without inventing dimensions;
- **gap-honest** — every material justification cites readiness gaps without hiding them;
- **finding-honest** — every material finding is bound to the producing lifecycle stages;
- **record-honest** — every material lifecycle cites which assessment records were preserved / published;
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to amend corpora;
- **non-authoritative** — publication is never narrated as a freeze of stack authority or a substitute for published corpora;
- **non-executing** — explanation never narrates Runtime A execution as readiness proof;
- **quality-honest** — ready status is never narrated as mastery, student exam readiness, or educational quality;
- **mutation-honest** — explanation never hides artefact changes performed “so the lifecycle could proceed”;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence / validation speech, Programme IX governance speech, Programme X activity speech, WS1–WS5 integrity speech, or WS6 / MS001 readiness evaluation speech;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match CRRLO-06 reconstruction needs;
- **repeatability-honest** — explanations must not paper over race-dependent dispositions or private stage shortcuts.

Lifecycle Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–X / WS1–WS5 / WS6 / MS001 meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a substitute for CRRD findings speech, CT lineage speech, or Programme IX–X evaluative / execution speech;
- a CI status page, coverage report, deployment-gate summary, or linter summary presented as constitutional coordination;
- a freeze of implementations, stacks, vendors, or releases as constitutional law;
- a licence to narrate invented assurance, invented dimensions, executed Runtime A, or mutated artefacts as if they were lawful inputs.

## 3. Mandatory Explainability Questions

Every material constitutional runtime readiness lifecycle must be able to answer:

### CRRLEQ-01 — Which assurance domains were evaluated?

**Required content**

- Named assurance domains consumed at CRRL-02 / validated at CRRL-03 (e.g. dependency, catalogue, boundary, vocabulary, layer completion identities; and VI–X guidance presence where required)
- Binding between domain identities and intended / applied CRRD types
- Confirmation that unpublished customs, invented completion, or deployment-gate noise were not used as constitutional assurance

**Defect if**

- “Assurance is fine” with no domain or completion references
- Anonymous “the docs” or “the pipelines” without identifiable completed assurance
- Assurance invented, re-authored, or silently assumed complete without disclosure and hard stop

---

### CRRLEQ-02 — Which readiness dimensions were satisfied?

**Required content**

- Named CRRD-01…CRRD-05 dispositions from CRRL-04 / CRRL-05 (ready / not-ready / deferred / escalated per required dimension)
- Binding between each dimension disposition and consumed assurance / prerequisite records
- Explicit composition notes when optional dimensions were lawfully scoped out
- Confirmation that unpublished readiness dimensions were not applied

**Defect if**

- “Ready” with no reconstructable CRRD identities
- Required dimensions silently omitted to force overall ready
- Dimension results invented at aggregation without stage binding

---

### CRRLEQ-03 — Were any readiness gaps identified?

**Required content**

- Gap status: none identified / gaps identified / inconclusive
- Named gaps (missing / incomplete assurance, dimension shortfalls, coherence defects) when identified
- Confirmation that gaps were not hidden or erased by inventing completion or mutating artefacts

**Defect if**

- Gap status omitted when findings are claimed
- Identified gaps quietly dropped from publication speech
- Narration that gaps were “resolved” by rewriting artefacts, inventing assurance, or executing runtime under lifecycle pretence

---

### CRRLEQ-04 — Which findings were produced?

**Required content**

- Named runtime readiness findings from CRRL-06 (ready / not-ready / deferred / escalated)
- Binding of each finding to producing CRRL stages (especially CRRL-03…CRRL-06)
- Publication artefacts from CRRL-07 where material (or refuse-publish identity)
- Continuity sufficient to reconstruct corpora, assurance domains, dimensions, gaps, CRRD types, stages, findings, and boundaries

**Defect if**

- “Ready” with no reconstructable finding identity
- Findings that drop stage-to-finding binding or assurance / dimension identity
- Silent erasure of prior lawful assessment / lifecycle history
- Publication that invents ready status or hides not-ready dispositions

Projections differ in vocabulary. They must not differ in constitutional facts.

## 5. Forbidden Explainability Moves

| Forbidden narration | Why | Lawful alternative |
|---------------------|-----|--------------------|
| “Lifecycle published ⇒ new constitutional law” | Legislation via publication | “Findings recorded; corpora unchanged” |
| “Lifecycle published ⇒ artefacts rewritten” | Artefact mutation | “Readiness assessed as found; artefacts unchanged” |
| “Lifecycle published ⇒ authority created” | Authority creation | “Assessment coordinated; published corpora remain the standard” |
| “Lifecycle published ⇒ Runtime A executed” | Runtime execution | “Corpus readiness evaluated; no runtime behaviour executed” |
| “Ready ⇒ student ready / mastered” | Quality / mastery out of scope | Keep corpus-readiness speech distinct from educational claim classes |
| “We invented the missing completion so prerequisites validated” | Invented assurance | Refuse-validate; propose lawful completion upstream |
| “We fixed the gap in the lifecycle” | Artefact / assurance mutation | Report gap-identified; refuse rewriting under assessment pretence |
| “Stage skipped because the deployment gate was green” | Unpublished stage customs | Apply CRRL-01…CRRL-07 or refuse |
| “We adjusted the docs so the lifecycle could finish” | Specification / artefact mutation | Refuse-collect / not-ready; propose lawful change outside lifecycle pretence |
| “This stack is now the standard” | Authority substitution / neutrality breach | Keep implementation as assessed subject |
| “Findings update the constitution” | Legislation via explanation | Propose amendment upstream; leave corpora unchanged |
| “Linked in CT ⇒ ready forever” | CT / CRR conflation | Compose with CT; keep readiness speech distinct |

## 7. Audit Alignment

Lifecycle explainability records must support reconstruction of:

1. Assurance domains evaluated (CRRLEQ-01)
2. Readiness dimensions satisfied (CRRLEQ-02)
3. Readiness gaps identified (CRRLEQ-03)
4. Findings produced and records / publication artefacts preserved (CRRLEQ-04)
5. Applied CRRD-01…CRRD-05 identities (from WS6 / MS001 speech)
6. Finding dispositions (ready / not-ready / deferred / escalated / refuse-*)
7. Constitutional boundaries remaining intact (`LIFECYCLE_BOUNDARIES.md` / CRRLB checks)
8. Continuity of prior lawful lifecycle history (EIP-005)

Missing any material element is an explainability defect under CRRLO-06.
