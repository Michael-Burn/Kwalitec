## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “pipeline greens,” “tickets closed,” or “we shipped something that looks like a runtime.” The tutor optimises **honest readiness**: when completed assurance shows the syllabus and constitutional corpus provide sufficient, coherent, validated guidance, the tutor can say the corpus is ready to support implementation work — without pretending unfinished chapters are ready, without rewriting the syllabus so today’s badge passes, without executing the product under a readiness seal, and without treating “ready” as student mastery or frozen stack law.

These objectives bind every constitutional runtime readiness assessment and every successor readiness machinery that claims corpus readiness for implementation.

## 2. Primary Objective

### CRRO-01 — Preserve implementation readiness

**Definition.** Ensure that runtime / assessment / audit speech affirms constitutional runtime readiness only when a published readiness concern under CRRD-01…CRRD-05 has been evaluated from identifiable completed WS1–WS5 assurance and published VI–X guidance — or honestly refuses ready status when criteria are not met — so implementation work may proceed only under demonstrably sufficient constitutional guidance.

**Includes:**

- Assessing only concerns that lawfully identify required completed assurance inputs and readiness dimensions.
- Affirming ready status only when `READINESS_MODEL.md` dimension conditions hold for the concern scope.
- Preferring lawful “not-ready” / deferred / escalated over inventing a “ready enough” status for product convenience.
- Distinguishing lawful stop dispositions (not-ready / deferred / escalated as published outcomes with honest gap recording) from abandoned evaluation duties.
- Binding every material readiness finding to reconstructable constitutional references and completed assurance identities.
- Keeping implementation readiness distinct from Runtime A execution, stack preference, educational quality, mastery, or student exam readiness.

**Excludes:**

- Declaring ready because a CI job exited, a ticket closed, a deployment gate glowed, or a UI spinner stopped.
- Treating Twin / Adaptive / Experience / Mission heuristics as readiness warrants.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for readiness honesty.
- Collapsing “corpus ready to support implementation” into “Runtime A executed,” “artefacts modified,” “meaning redefined,” “specifications amended,” “constitutional authority created,” or “student succeeded.”
- Privileging a reference stack, language, or vendor as the meaning of “implementation ready.”

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software celebrates unfinished constitutional guidance as ready to build — or celebrates finished readiness theatre as finished runtime execution or student success. Honest readiness is care; readiness theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CRRO-02 — Preserve constitutional completeness

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming readiness never invents, skips, or silently assumes unfinished prerequisite WS1–WS5 assurance — so every ready finding leaves prerequisite completeness reconstructable as published.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who “helpfully” treats unfinished integrity assessments as complete so the readiness report can pass has abandoned educational honesty even if the readiness UX looks clean.

**Manifestations:**

- Ready findings cite identifiable completed assurance artefacts (or lawful completion judgements) for required inputs.
- Missing CDIC / CCIC / CBIC / CVIC / CLIC (or equivalent published completion) for a required dimension blocks ready status for that dimension.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing or silently rewriting prior lawful “not yet complete” dispositions to simplify the next readiness report.
- Inventing completion under “readiness cleanup” is a defect.

---

### CRRO-03 — Preserve architectural coherence

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming readiness never papers over layer / dependency / boundary incoherence — so every ready finding leaves architectural coherence reconstructable from completed WS1 / WS3 / WS5 assurance.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later say which architectural relationships still hold — because readiness “tidied” layer or dependency findings — cannot be trusted. Readiness that invents coherence is educational fiction dressed as go.

**Manifestations:**

- Affirmed ready status for architectural dimensions leaves layer / dependency / boundary identities reconstructable with findings.
- Silent overwrite of violated layer / dependency / boundary findings to force ready status is a defect.
- Architectural readiness (CRRD-04) consumes completed CLI / CDI / CBI outcomes; it never rewrites them.
- Coherence here means *constitutional architectural coherence* — not deployment topology preference and not student exam readiness.

---

### CRRO-04 — Preserve governance confidence

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming readiness never softens authority, responsibility, or behavioural boundaries so a report looks green — so every ready finding leaves governance confidence reconstructable from completed boundary / layer assurance and published Programme IX–X composition.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who softens “must not” into “may” overnight so readiness can pass has abandoned care for theatre. Governance confidence that survives only until the next demo is not confidence.

**Manifestations:**

- Governance readiness (CRRD-02) cites completed boundary / layer outcomes and published governance guidance without rewriting them.
- Soft-amending authority or responsibility statements under readiness pretext is a defect.
- Intact governance confidence here means *corpus governance honesty* — not product release confidence theatre and not certification seals.
- Violated / deferred / escalated boundary or layer findings block honest governance-ready claims that depend on those intact structures.

---

### CRRO-05 — Preserve traceability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material readiness assessment leaves a reconstructable, speakable trail that supports honest Programme XI integration and implementation judgements — which assurance domains were evaluated, which readiness dimensions were satisfied, whether readiness gaps were identified, which findings were produced, and which assessment limits were preserved — so the constitutional corpus remains readable across Programmes VI–XI.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later say *why* the corpus was called ready cannot be trusted. Silent green readiness destroys student and auditor trust. Implementation that ignores broken lineage is implementation theatre.

**Manifestations:**

- Audit records preserve constitutional references and completed assurance identities, not only technical job logs or CI annotations.
- Explainability obligations in `RUNTIME_EXPLAINABILITY.md` apply to all material readiness assessments (CRREQ-01…CRREQ-04).
- Missing CRRD identity, missing assurance-domain identity, missing gap identity when gaps exist, or missing finding identity is a defect.
- Student / developer / auditor projections share one truth with different vocabulary.
- Ready findings may inform downstream implementation work; they never themselves certify educational readiness, mastery, conformity, or product success.

---

### CRRO-06 — Preserve semantic readiness honesty

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming readiness never invents terminology stability or silently drifts definitions so implementation speech looks tidy — so every ready finding leaves semantic readiness reconstructable from completed vocabulary assurance and published meaning corpora.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who quietly renames syllabus terms so the readiness report can claim “semantic ready” has abandoned educational honesty. Stable meaning is care; vocabulary theatre is harm.

**Manifestations:**

- Semantic readiness (CRRD-03) consumes completed CVI / CVIC outcomes and cites published meaning corpora without redefining them.
- Silent definition drift under “readiness alignment” is a defect.
- Semantic readiness here means *constitutional terminology / meaning sufficiency for implementation guidance* — not marketing copy readiness and not student comprehension scoring.
- Ambiguous or unstable published terms that block honest implementation guidance must appear as readiness gaps when in scope.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CRRO-01 conflicts with inventing unfinished assurance so ready can pass | Implementation readiness honesty wins — refuse / defer / escalate |
| CRRO-02 conflicts with skipping incomplete WS1–WS5 closes | Constitutional completeness wins |
| CRRO-03 conflicts with rewriting layer / dependency / boundary findings for tidy ready | Architectural coherence wins |
| CRRO-04 conflicts with softening boundaries so governance looks ready | Governance confidence wins |
| CRRO-05 conflicts with opaque “trust us” ready badges | Traceability / explainability wins |
| CRRO-06 conflicts with drifting definitions for semantic theatre | Semantic readiness honesty wins |
| Any CRRO conflicts with executing runtime / modifying artefacts / creating authority / amending specifications | Non-legislative / non-executing honesty wins — refuse the overclaim |

No supporting objective authorises violating CRRO-01.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — Constitution / EIP integrity obligations, Programme VI–X authority preservation, and Programme XI / WS1–WS5 assurance honesty for the *runtime readiness* horizon.

---

## 5. Non-Objectives

Constitutional runtime readiness does **not** optimise for:

- implementing runtime behaviour, Runtime A, services, adapters, or product execution;
- modifying, rewriting, or soft-amending constitutional artefacts;
- creating constitutional authority or soft-amending corpora via findings;
- reinterpreting Programme VI–X / EIP meaning during readiness;
- amending constitutional specifications under “readiness” pretext;
- constitutional conformance disposition, fidelity scoring, or “ready therefore conformant” theatre;
- verification success, compliance disposition, certification seals, or implementation-approval judgement as Programme IX substitutes;
- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or student exam-readiness judgement;
- CI throughput, deployment frequency, feature-flag theatre, or vendor lock-in as readiness meaning;
- re-authoring WS1–WS5 integrity findings under aggregation pretext;
- freezing a preferred stack, language, framework, or cloud topology as constitutional law.

---

## 6. Closing Statement

> **Runtime readiness objectives keep implementation timing honest.  
> Preserve implementation readiness, constitutional completeness, architectural coherence, governance confidence, semantic honesty, and traceability.  
> Never optimise for executing Runtime A, rewriting artefacts, inventing authority, or celebrating unfinished assurance as ready.**
