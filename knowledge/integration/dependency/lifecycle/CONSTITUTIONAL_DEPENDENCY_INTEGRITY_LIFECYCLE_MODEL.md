## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not declare a syllabus “coherent” because a pipeline skipped authority-chain validation, quietly reverse-engineered edges mid-assessment so graphs look acyclic, rewrite the syllabus so today’s scanner passes, or treat a green lifecycle badge as new constitutional law that freezes the stack. After the Constitution and EIP have defined *educational law*, after Programmes VI–X have published *meaning, orchestration, runtime, governance, and execution law*, and after WS1 / MS001 has defined *how dependencies are validated under CDI-01…CDI-07*, the platform still needs one lifecycle answer:

That answer must ensure assessments initiate against published corpora; discover named dependency relationships; validate authority chains; evaluate dependency direction; detect circular dependencies; validate constitutional layering; construct dependency integrity findings; and publish those findings — while remaining fully repeatable, consistent, implementation-neutral, auditable, and explainable — without creating constitutional authority, reinterpreting constitutional law, amending constitutional specifications, or modifying constitutional dependencies.

This document records that posture so every future dependency integrity lifecycle (and successor) has a single constitutional reference for *how dependency assessment work may be sequenced without becoming law, an edge rewriter, or a second constitution*.

> **The Constitutional Dependency Integrity Lifecycle Model describes how dependency assessment activities are lawfully coordinated from initiation through publication.  
> It does not create educational meaning, invent ownership, mint tips, invent runtime behaviour, amend law, rewrite dependencies, or become constitutional authority.**

## 2. What the Constitutional Dependency Integrity Lifecycle Is

The **constitutional dependency integrity lifecycle** is the binding organisation by which **dependency integrity assessments** may proceed through recognised stages — initiating against published corpus identities, discovering declared / lawfully implied relationships, validating authority chains and direction, detecting cycles, validating layering under published CDI-01…CDI-07 categories, and constructing / publishing **dependency integrity findings** that preserve acyclicity, authority traceability, constitutional layering, repeatability, consistency, implementation neutrality, auditability, and explainability — without becoming a source of constitutional law, a rewriter of dependencies, a substitute for MS001 dependency meaning, or a replacement for upstream programme authority.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Published constitutional corpus** | Constitution, EIP, Programme VI–X specifications and their subordinate workstream / milestone corpora | Which corpus may the lifecycle organise assessment against? |
| **Constitutional dependency** | A declared or implied lawful subordination / composition / consumption relationship between published corpus identities | Which relationship is under assessment? |
| **Authority chain** | Reconstructable path from Constitution / EIP through programme layers to the assessed dependency | Which authority supports this edge? |
| **Dependency direction** | The lawful orientation from superior authority toward subordinate consumer (never the reverse as authorship) | Does the edge point the right way? |
| **Cycle** | A closed path of dependencies that would make authority circular or layering ambiguous | Was circularity detected? |
| **Constitutional layering** | The published order: Constitution / EIP → VI → VII → VIII → IX → X → XI (and lawful subordinate workstreams within each) | Is layering preserved? |
| **Lifecycle stage** | A recognised CDIL-01…CDIL-07 organisational step | Which coordination step is active? |
| **Dependency integrity finding** | Lawful disposition: intact / violated / deferred / escalated — never “new law” and never a rewrite of edges | Does the dependency remain lawful? |
| **Assessment record / publication artefact** | Audit-speakable record of dependencies, authority chains, cycles, stages, findings, and preserved boundaries | Can the assessment be reconstructed? |
| **Constitutional authority** | The exclusive right of published corpora to define educational and execution meaning | Who owns the standard? |
| **Implementation neutrality** | Lifecycle coordination is judged against law and published relationships, not against a preferred stack, vendor, or topology | Remains the stack replaceable? |
| **Repeatability** | Same published corpora, same discovered relationships, same stage sequence → same disposition | Can the judgement be reproduced? |
| **Auditability** | Every material lifecycle leaves reconstructable constitutional traces | Can the judgement be reconstructed? |
| **Explainability** | Dependencies, authority chains, cycles, findings, stages, and boundaries are speakable | Can the judgement be narrated honestly? |

The constitutional dependency integrity lifecycle is:

- **coordination-centred** — it organises dependency assessments; it never authors constitutional obligations or dependencies;
- **discovery-bound** — it discovers published relationships; it never invents edges to force progress;
- **authority-preserving** — every material assessment remains reconstructable to a published authority chain;
- **direction-bound** — stages evaluate superior → subordinate / consumer orientation; they never reverse authorship;
- **acyclic-honest** — circular dependencies that invert or confuse constitutional authority are hard defects;
- **layering-preserving** — Programme and workstream layering remains as published;
- **catalogue-closed** — only CDIL-01…CDIL-07 may be applied as constitutional dependency integrity lifecycle stages;
- **type-faithful** — stages apply only published CDI-01…CDI-07 categories and CDIR validation rules from WS1 / MS001; they never invent categories;
- **finding-producing** — assessments emit intact / violated / deferred / escalated dispositions;
- **publication-honest** — findings are published under honesty rules; publication never rewrites edges or becomes law;
- **authority-preserving** — published corpora remain the sole source of constitutional meaning;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by lifecycle law;
- **corpus-bound** — every material lifecycle cites identifiable published specifications and relationships;
- **audit-capable** — every material lifecycle leaves reconstructable constitutional traces;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which dependencies were assessed under which authority chains with which findings;
- **non-legislative** — a dependency finding never becomes constitutional law or a soft amendment;
- **non-substitutive** — lifecycle machinery never becomes the constitutional standard for the next judgement;
- **non-mutating** — the lifecycle never modifies constitutional specifications or dependency relationships under coordination pretext.

The constitutional dependency integrity lifecycle is **not**:

- a second Educational Constitution, EIP, Programme VI–X, or WS1 / MS001 amendment path;
- a licence to reinterpret coach meaning, ownership maps, tip warrants, runtime contracts, conformance criteria, traceability meaning, verification meaning, compliance meaning, certification meaning, evolution meaning, execution meaning, or CDI categories in lifecycle prose;
- a testing framework, CI/CD pipeline, GitHub Action, package-dependency scanner, or OpenAPI linker;
- a claim that “lifecycle complete” guarantees learning, mastery, exam readiness, product success, perpetual conformity, or frozen stack authority;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS1 / MS001 dependency integrity meaning — CDI defines categories and validation; CDIL sequences when assessment occurs;
- a substitute for Programme IX MS002 constitutional traceability — CT preserves spec/artefact/finding lineage; CDIL coordinates *constitutional corpus dependency assessment*;
- a substitute for Programme IX conformance / verification / compliance / certification — those evaluate implementations and obligations; CDIL validates *corpus dependency structure sequencing*;
- a substitute for Programme X execution / decision / audit / explainability — those organise constitutional activity; CDIL never executes, decides, audits, or explains educational outcomes in their place;
- a licence to create, reverse, invent, or delete constitutional dependencies under “lifecycle” pretext.

## 3. Lifecycle Posture (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material constitutional dependency integrity lifecycle travels through seven constitutional stages. These are **normative coordination obligations**, not an implementation architecture and not a CI job graph.

```
CDIL-01 Assessment Initiation
        │
        ▼
CDIL-02 Dependency Discovery
        │
        ▼
CDIL-03 Authority Validation
        │
        ▼
CDIL-04 Cycle Detection
        │
        ▼
CDIL-05 Layer Validation
        │
        ├── violated / deferred / escalated (lawful finding — corpus edges unchanged)
        │
        └── intact (or other lawful disposition) →
                │
                ▼
CDIL-06 Finding Construction
        │
        ▼
CDIL-07 Finding Publication
        │
        ▼
Explain dependencies / authority chains / cycles / findings / boundaries
```

| Stage | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **CDIL-01 Assessment Initiation** | Open a dependency assessment concern against named published corpora and scope | Scope is unpublished custom, tribal knowledge, or “how main cites today” |
| **CDIL-02 Dependency Discovery** | Identify declared / lawfully implied dependency relationships under CDI-01…CDI-07 | Relationships are invented, unpublished, or would require rewriting edges to “discover” them |
| **CDIL-03 Authority Validation** | Validate reconstructable authority chains and dependency direction | Validation would invent chains, reverse authority, privilege a stack as law, or rewrite edges |
| **CDIL-04 Cycle Detection** | Detect closed dependency paths that invert or confuse constitutional authority | Detection would “fix” cycles in situ by mutating relationships |
| **CDIL-05 Layer Validation** | Confirm Constitution / EIP → VI–X → XI (and lawful workstream) layering | Validation would absorb superior authority into a subordinate or invent a new layer |
| **CDIL-06 Finding Construction** | Emit intact / violated / deferred / escalated findings under published criteria | Findings would become amendments, educational quality grades, or edge rewrites |
| **CDIL-07 Finding Publication** | Publish findings and assessment records under published honesty rules | Publication would invent intact status, hide cycles, rewrite boundaries, or freeze a stack as law |

> **The lifecycle never “best-effort invents” an eighth stage called law-making, reinterpretation, edge rewriting, stack canonisation, or dependency mutation.**

## 6. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Dependency Integrity Lifecycle Model exists so that:

1. **Law remains upstream of coordination** — stages organise assessment against published corpora; progress does not quietly become the specifications.
2. **Discovery remains honest** — discovery names published relationships; it does not invent edges to force intact status.
3. **Authority remains reconstructable** — authority validation confirms chains; it does not invent missing superior links.
4. **Direction remains honest** — stages evaluate published orientation; they never reverse authorship by citation theatre.
5. **Acyclicity remains honest** — cycle detection reports closed paths; it never mutates the graph in situ.
6. **Layering remains intact** — layer validation preserves Constitution / EIP and Programme order through Programme XI.
7. **Findings remain sequenced** — intact / violated / deferred / escalated dispositions arise after lawful initiation, discovery, and validation — not as random green theatre.
8. **Authority remains honest** — no team may treat a published finding as the new constitutional source.
9. **Implementations remain replaceable** — lifecycle law does not freeze Flask, SQLAlchemy, a particular API shape, or a CI vendor as law.
10. **Repeatability remains honest** — the same published corpora, discovered relationships, and stage sequence yield the same disposition.
11. **Audit remains possible** — every material lifecycle can be reconstructed against named dependencies, authority chains, cycles, stages, and findings.
12. **Explainability remains intact** — lifecycle speech describes coordination of dependency assessment; it does not redefine what learning means.
13. **Students remain protected** — product theatre cannot launder circular or inverted corpora as “lifecycle intact.”
