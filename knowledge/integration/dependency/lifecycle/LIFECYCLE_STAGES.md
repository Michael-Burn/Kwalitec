## 1. Purpose

Dependency assessment without a closed stage catalogue invents law by proximity: whichever scanner step, ticket status, or reviewer habit happens to finish becomes the tutor’s “proof the system ran a constitutional dependency integrity assessment.”

This catalogue names the only lawful constitutional dependency integrity lifecycle stages — and binds each stage to purpose, inputs, outputs, and permitted / prohibited activities.

---

## 2. Stage Principles

1. **Ordered and skip-disciplined.** Stages may be brief under thin scope; mandatory initiation, discovery, authority validation, cycle detection, layer validation, finding construction, and publication obligations may not be silently omitted when a dependency integrity finding is claimed.
2. **Published relationships before findings.** Identifiable published corpora and discovered relationships are confirmed before intact / violated dispositions are constructed.
3. **Explainability accumulates.** Each stage leaves traceable artefacts for `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.
4. **Refusal is a stage outcome.** Violated, deferred, escalated, and refuse-progress are first-class conclusions.
5. **Deterministic transitions.** Same published inputs ⇒ same next stage outcomes.
6. **Stages coordinate; they do not legislate.** No stage creates, modifies, or redefines constitutional law.
7. **Stages do not become constitutional authority.** Publication never freezes a corpus topology as permanently lawful or replaces published corpora.
8. **Stages do not rewrite dependencies.** Findings report structure; they never create, reverse, invent, or delete edges.
9. **Stages apply published CDI / CDIR law.** Categories and validation rules remain owned by WS1 / MS001.

## 3. Catalogue Overview

| ID | Stage | Constitutional purpose (short) | Primary inputs | Primary outputs |
|----|-------|--------------------------------|----------------|-----------------|
| **CDIL-01** | Assessment Initiation | Open a dependency assessment against named published corpora and scope | Published corpus identities + concern scope + optional CDI indication | Initiated concern record (or refuse-open) |
| **CDIL-02** | Dependency Discovery | Identify declared / lawfully implied dependency relationships under CDI-01…CDI-07 | Initiated concern + candidate published relationship identities | Discovered relationship set bound to CDI types (or refuse-discover) |
| **CDIL-03** | Authority Validation | Validate reconstructable authority chains and dependency direction | Discovered relationships + published authority order | Validated authority / direction record (or refuse-validate) |
| **CDIL-04** | Cycle Detection | Detect closed paths that invert or confuse constitutional authority | Validated relationship / authority bundle | Cycle status: none / detected / inconclusive (or refuse-detect) |
| **CDIL-05** | Layer Validation | Confirm published constitutional layering for the assessed relationships | Relationship / authority / cycle bundle + published layering order | Layering disposition (or refuse-layer) |
| **CDIL-06** | Finding Construction | Emit intact / violated / deferred / escalated under published criteria | Stage dispositions + CDI / CDIR criteria | Dependency integrity findings |
| **CDIL-07** | Finding Publication | Publish findings and assessment records under honesty rules | Findings + boundary confirmations | Published assessment artefacts (or refuse-publish) |

Material dependency assessment claiming constitutional findings must map to this sequence as published law requires. Stages may be brief; none may invent a stage outside this catalogue.

**Relation to CDI / CDIR / CDIO / CT / CC / EV:** Those catalogues remain defined solely by their owners. CDIL stages *organise when* dependency assessment activities occur; they do not replace, reclassify, invent, amend, or rewrite those catalogues.

```text
CDIL-01  Assessment Initiation
           ↓

           ↓
CDIL-03  Authority Validation
           ↓

           ↓

           ↓
```

Lawful early stop dispositions (refuse-open / refuse-discover / refuse-validate / refuse-detect / refuse-layer / deferred / escalated / violated) may exhaust remaining *validation* duties while still requiring honest finding construction, publication (or refuse-publish), of what occurred — never silent disappearance of the concern.

## 5. Stage Definitions

### CDIL-01 — Assessment Initiation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Open a material dependency integrity assessment concern by naming the published constitutional corpora under assessment and the scope of the concern — without inventing law, opening against unpublished customs, or treating initiation as an intact finding.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Identifiable published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–X specification references for the concern scope
- Concern identity (what dependency-integrity question is under assessment)
- Optional published indication of required CDI-01…CDI-07 categories for the scope
- Optional identifiable supporting authority / lineage references (not rewritten)

**Constitutional outputs**

- An initiated assessment concern record naming corpora, specifications, scope, and intended CDI type set — **or**
- Lawful refuse-open when standards are unpublished, scope is theatrical, or initiation would invent law

**Permitted activities**

- Name published corpora and specifications as assessment standards
- Bound the concern scope without amending corpora
- Record intended CDI types from the published catalogue
- Refuse initiation when constitutional preconditions fail
- Preserve initiation audit identity for later stages

**Prohibited activities**

- Treat “how main cites,” tribal knowledge, or stack preference as the standard
- Create or modify constitutional specifications by opening a concern
- Issue an intact / violated finding at initiation
- Invent unpublished dependency categories
- Skip initiation when a material finding will later be claimed
- Create constitutional authority or rewrite dependencies at initiation

**Authority preserved**  
Published corpora remain the sole standard. Initiation organises; it does not legislate.

---

### CDIL-02 — Dependency Discovery

**Constitutional purpose**  
Identify **declared or lawfully implied dependency relationships** among the named corpora under recognised CDI-01…CDI-07 categories — so later stages assess real published relationships, not anonymous theatre — without inventing, reversing, or deleting edges.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Initiated concern from CDIL-01
- Candidate published relationship identities (declared subordination / composition / consumption edges, or relationships lawfully implied by published corpora)
- Published CDI-01…CDI-07 category definitions from WS1 / MS001
- Optional supporting CT lineage identities already established under upstream law (unchanged)

**Constitutional outputs**

- A discovered relationship set bound to the concern and intended CDI types — **or**
- Lawful refuse-discover when relationships are missing, forged, invented, unpublished, or would require unlawful mutation to “discover”

**Permitted activities**

- Identify and name published dependency relationships
- Classify relationships under published CDI-01…CDI-07 only
- Bind discovered relationships to the initiated concern
- Refuse discovery when relationships cannot be honestly affirmed as published
- Preserve discovery records for audit and explainability

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent missing dependency relationships
- Rewrite, reverse, or delete edges to force later intact status
- Treat import graphs, package scanners, ticket links, or CI noise as constitutional relationships without published mapping
- Redefine educational or constitutional meaning while “discovering”
- Present discovery as itself an intact finding or a freeze of stack authority
- Create constitutional authority under discovery pretence

**Authority preserved**  
Published relationships remain upstream facts. Discovery organises identity; it does not author edges or educational truth.

---

### CDIL-03 — Authority Validation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Validate that each discovered dependency has a **reconstructable authority chain** and that **dependency direction** respects published superior → subordinate / consumer orientation — without inventing missing superior links, reversing authorship, or rewriting edges.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Discovered relationship set from CDIL-02
- Published constitutional authority order (Constitution / EIP → VI → VII → VIII → IX → X → XI and lawful workstream subordination)
- Published CDIR direction and authority-traceability rules from WS1 / MS001
- Optional supporting lineage / authority references consumed as inputs (unchanged)

**Constitutional outputs**

- A validated authority / direction record per assessed relationship — **or**
- Lawful refuse-validate when authority chains are missing, invented, direction is reversed, or validation would require rewriting law or edges

**Permitted activities**

- Confirm reconstructable authority chains for discovered relationships
- Evaluate dependency direction under published superior → subordinate / consumer orientation
- Prefer honest refuse-validate / deferred / escalated over inventing missing links
- Remain repeatable and implementation-neutral
- Preserve validation records for later stages

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent missing superior documents or authority-chain links
- Reverse dependency direction so a subordinate becomes practical author of its superior
- Modify constitutional specifications to force validation
- Privilege a technology stack as constitutional
- Redefine Programme VI–X meaning or CDI meaning in validation prose
- Present authority validation as new constitutional law
- Mutate dependency relationships during validation

**Authority preserved**  
WS1 / MS001 owns direction and authority-traceability rules. This stage applies them; it does not amend the catalogue or become constitutional authority.

---

### CDIL-04 — Cycle Detection

**Constitutional purpose**  
Detect **closed dependency paths** that would invert, confuse, or circularise constitutional authority — reporting cycle status honestly without “fixing” cycles by mutating the dependency graph in situ.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Validated relationship / authority bundle from CDIL-03
- Published acyclicity rules (especially CDIR cycle rules and CDI cycle categories from WS1 / MS001)
- Discovered relationship set and authority-chain identities

**Constitutional outputs**

- Cycle status for the concern: none detected / cycle detected / inconclusive — with named closed paths when detected — **or**
- Lawful refuse-detect when detection cannot proceed honestly without inventing structure or mutating edges

**Permitted activities**

- Analyse published dependency paths for closed loops that confuse authority
- Name detected cycles with corpus identities
- Report inconclusive status when published inputs are insufficient
- Prefer honest cycle-detected / deferred / escalated over graph rewriting
- Preserve cycle-status records for finding construction

**Prohibited activities**

- Mutate, reverse, invent, or delete dependencies to erase cycles
- Soft-amend specifications so circular citation becomes “composition”
- Treat package-scanner cycle warnings as constitutional cycle status without published mapping
- Hide detected cycles to manufacture later intact status
- Present cycle detection as licence to rewrite the corpus
- Create constitutional authority by declaring cycles “approved”

**Authority preserved**  
Cycle detection reports structural defects. It never authors law or rewrites edges.

---

### CDIL-05 — Layer Validation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Confirm that assessed relationships **preserve published constitutional layering** — Constitution / EIP above Programme VI, and onward through VII–XI with lawful workstream subordination — without absorbing superior authority into a subordinate horizon.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Relationship / authority / cycle bundle from prior stages
- Published layering order and CDIR / CDI layering rules from WS1 / MS001
- Workstream / milestone subordination identities as published

**Constitutional outputs**

- Layering disposition for the concern (preserved / violated / deferred / escalated) — **or**
- Lawful refuse-layer when layering cannot be honestly confirmed without inventing layers or rewriting authority

**Permitted activities**

- Confirm edges respect published programme and workstream order
- Identify layer inversions that place lower horizons as authors of higher meaning
- Compose sibling relationships within a layer only as published
- Prefer honest violated / deferred / escalated over soft-absorbing superiors
- Preserve layering records for finding construction

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent a new constitutional layer outside published programmes
- Absorb superior programme meaning into a subordinate via lifecycle prose
- Collapse distinct horizons into one mega-law under “layer validation”
- Modify constitutional specifications to force layering pass
- Present layering pass as educational quality or stack freeze
- Rewrite dependencies to manufacture layering honesty

**Authority preserved**  
Published programmes own layering. This stage confirms it; it does not become a new layer or constitutional authority.

---

### CDIL-06 — Finding Construction

**Constitutional purpose**  
Emit **dependency integrity findings** as lawful dispositions under published criteria — speaking to whether assessed relationships remain intact under direction, authority, acyclicity, and layering — without converting findings into amendments, educational grades, edge rewrites, or freezes of stack authority.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Stage dispositions from CDIL-01…CDIL-05
- Published CDI-01…CDI-07 criteria and CDIR validation rules from WS1 / MS001
- Published decision vocabulary (intact / violated / deferred / escalated)
- Bound references to corpora, relationships, authority chains, cycle status, and layering disposition

**Constitutional outputs**

- Dependency integrity findings for the concern (including composition outcomes when multiple CDI types apply)
- Explicit non-authority framing (findings are integrity dispositions only — never new law)
- Assessment records binding stages, relationships, authority chains, cycles, layering, and findings

**Permitted activities**

- Produce findings under published dispositions only
- Bind each finding to the CDI types, relationships, and stages that produced it
- Record composition / stop-rule application honestly
- Prefer understatement over invented green
- Propose (not enact) upstream corpus amendments when law itself must change
- Preserve audit-speakable assessment records

**Prohibited activities**

- Present findings as constitutional amendments or new educational meaning
- Narrate intact status as mastery, readiness theatre, educational quality, permanent conformity, or frozen stack authority
- Rewrite findings to match product urgency
- Collapse multi-type failures into a single false “intact”
- Mutate dependency relationships while “constructing findings”
- Invent unpublished CDI categories during finding construction
- Erase stage history so findings look clean
- Create constitutional authority via finding text

**Authority preserved**  
Findings validate structure against law. They never become law or constitutional authority.

---

### CDIL-07 — Finding Publication

**Constitutional purpose**  
Publish dependency integrity findings and assessment records under **published honesty rules** so audiences receive lawful assessment speech — without inventing intact status, hiding cycles or layer violations, rewriting boundaries, amending specifications, or freezing a stack as law.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Findings and assessment records from CDIL-06
- Published publication / honesty rules from WS1 / MS001 and EIP explainability / continuity law
- Boundary confirmations (non-legislation, non-reinterpretation, non-mutation, non-substitution, non-authority-creation)

**Constitutional outputs**

- Published assessment artefacts projecting one constitutional truth to authorised audiences — **or**
- Lawful refuse-publish when records are incomplete, boundaries were breached, or publication would invent intact status / hide cycles / freeze stack authority / replace constitutional authority

**Permitted activities**

- Publish findings and records under published honesty rules
- Project student / developer / auditor vocabularies without changing constitutional facts
- Refuse publication when audit or boundary preconditions fail
- Keep publication distinct from educational quality, edge rewrites, and authority creation

**Prohibited activities**

- Publish invented intact status or incomplete-assessment theatre as intact
- Hide refuse / defer / escalate / violated dispositions to manufacture green
- Narrate publication as constitutional amendment, educational success, or frozen stack law
- Create constitutional authority as a publication act
- Modify constitutional specifications or dependency relationships as a publication act
- Present seals, badges, or scanner greenery as substitutes for published records
- Reinterpret constitutional meaning in publication speech

**Authority preserved**  
Publication communicates assessment findings. Published corpora remain the sole standard.

## 6. Stage Transition Rules

| From | To | Transition requires |
|------|----|---------------------|
| — | CDIL-01 | Material dependency assessment concern needs opening |
| CDIL-01 | CDIL-02 | Concern initiated (not refuse-open) |
| CDIL-01 | CDIL-06 / CDIL-07 path | Refuse-open recorded honestly |
| CDIL-02 | CDIL-03 | Relationships discovered (not refuse-discover) |
| CDIL-02 | CDIL-06 / CDIL-07 path | Refuse-discover recorded honestly |
| CDIL-03 | CDIL-04 | Authority / direction validated (not refuse-validate) — or honest fail disposition carried forward |
| CDIL-03 | CDIL-06 / CDIL-07 path | Refuse-validate recorded honestly |
| CDIL-04 | CDIL-05 | Cycle status available (not refuse-detect) — or honest fail disposition carried forward |
| CDIL-04 | CDIL-06 / CDIL-07 path | Refuse-detect recorded honestly |
| CDIL-05 | CDIL-06 | Layering disposition available (not refuse-layer) — or honest fail disposition carried forward |
| CDIL-05 | CDIL-06 / CDIL-07 path | Refuse-layer recorded honestly |
| CDIL-06 | CDIL-07 | Findings constructed under published vocabulary |
| CDIL-07 | — | Publication completed or refuse-publish recorded |

Silent jumps that claim findings without CDIL-01…CDIL-05 honesty are constitutionally defective.

## 7. Composition with Dependency Categories

| During stage | Relationship to CDI-01…CDI-07 |
|--------------|-------------------------------|
| CDIL-01 | May *name* intended CDI types; does not apply full validation |
| CDIL-02 | Discovers relationships those types will assess |
| CDIL-03 | Applies direction / authority rules under those types |
| CDIL-04 | Applies cycle rules under those types |
| CDIL-05 | Applies layering rules under those types |
| **CDIL-06** | **Constructs** findings under CDI-01…CDI-07 |
| CDIL-07 | Publishes CDI-bound findings without inventing types |

Cross-cutting concerns may bind multiple CDI types across stages. Lifecycle stages remain CDIL-01…CDIL-07 only.
