## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “scanner finished,” “stages skipped for speed,” or “graph badge shown.” The tutor optimises **honest organisation of dependency assessment work**: only a repeatable, consistent, implementation-neutral, explainable, and auditable sequence — discovering published relationships and validating authority, direction, cycles, and layering under CDI-01…CDI-07 — may support lawful dependency integrity findings.

These objectives bind every constitutional dependency integrity lifecycle and every successor coordination machinery that claims constitutional dependency integrity lifecycle meaning.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### CDILO-01 — Preserve dependency integrity through lawful coordination

**Definition.** Ensure that every material dependency integrity lifecycle organises assessments through recognised CDIL-01…CDIL-07 stages — initiating against identifiable published corpora, discovering named relationships, validating authority chains and direction, detecting cycles, validating layering, and constructing / publishing findings under published CDI-01…CDI-07 categories — so that findings occur only after lawful initiation and discovery, and that findings are published without becoming law, edge rewrites, or replacements for constitutional authority.

**Includes:**

- Binding every material lifecycle to identifiable published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–X specifications and subordinate workstream / milestone corpora.
- Discovering only declared / lawfully implied dependency relationships as inputs.
- Applying only published CDI-01…CDI-07 categories and CDIR validation rules across stages.
- Preferring lawful violated / deferred / escalated / refuse-progress outcomes over rewriting law, inventing edges, inventing authority chains, erasing cycles in situ, or mutating corpora so the lifecycle “completes.”
- Keeping lifecycle progress distinct from educational quality, mastery, readiness theatre, product success, package-scanner greenery, or constitutional amendment.
- Preserving the Model’s posture: stages coordinate assessment; they do not become law or constitutional authority.

**Excludes:**

- Treating green CI, coverage percentages, linter scores, Twin estimates, Adaptive scores, UI ticks, or package scanners as lifecycle fulfilment.
- Soft-amending specifications, inventing unpublished customs, inventing or rewriting dependencies, inventing authority chains, mutating edges, or privileging “how main cites” to force progress.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for lawful coordination.
- Declaring “close enough” publication without documenting a corpus amendment.
- Freezing an implementation as permanently lawful because the lifecycle published findings.
- Creating constitutional authority under a lifecycle badge.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software pretends unfinished or dishonest dependency assessment is fine because integration needs a finished green graph. Coordination honesty is care; lifecycle theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CDILO-02 — Preserve authority traceability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material lifecycle validates — and leaves reconstructable — authority chains from Constitution / EIP through the relevant Programme layer(s) to each assessed dependency, so origin of the relationship remains speakable across stages.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who cannot say *which superior rule* yesterday’s note obeyed cannot be trusted. Lifecycles without authority-chain validation are educational amnesia with a progress bar.

**Manifestations:**

- CDIL-03 requires reconstructable authority chains; missing links are defects, not soft variables.
- Findings cite corpus paths and specification identities, not only ticket IDs or commit SHAs.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing prior lawful assessment history to simplify the next report.
- Programme IX CT lineage may support reconstruction; CDIL never treats CT records as a licence to invent superior law.
- Authority-traceability preservation never authorises inventing missing superior documents.

---

### CDILO-03 — Preserve constitutional layering

**Definition.** Ensure that lifecycle organisation honours the published constitutional order — Constitution / EIP above Programme VI, VI above VII, VII above VIII, VIII above IX, IX above X, X above XI, and lawful workstream / milestone subordination within each programme — so no subordinate horizon silently absorbs a superior’s authority through stage progress.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who lets yesterday’s worksheet rewrite today’s syllabus has abandoned institutional order for convenience.

**Manifestations:**

- CDIL-05 confirms layering under published order; edges that place a lower programme as author of a higher programme’s meaning are hard defects.
- Workstream / milestone corpora remain subordinate to their published programme models.
- Sibling composition within a layer is permitted only as published; it never collapses distinct horizons into one mega-law.
- Forbidden actions in `LIFECYCLE_BOUNDARIES.md` are hard stops.
- Layering preservation never authorises inventing a new layer outside published programmes.

---

### CDILO-04 — Preserve assessment repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that the same published corpus identities, the same discovered dependency relationships, the same published CDI / CDIR criteria, and the same CDIL stage sequence yield the same dependency disposition (intact / violated / deferred / escalated) and the same lawful stage outcomes — absent a published non-deterministic exception (none are granted by this Model for constitutional meaning or integrity status).

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who treats the same syllabus map as lawful or not depending on which reviewer woke first — or skips stages randomly — is not coaching; they are gambling.

**Manifestations:**

- Only CDIL-01…CDIL-07 may organise constitutional dependency integrity lifecycles.
- Stage purpose, inputs, outputs, and permitted / prohibited activities remain as published in `LIFECYCLE_STAGES.md`.
- Stage transitions and outcomes are corpus-bound and reproducible.
- Parallel CI runners, sharding, and cache races must not change constitutional disposition or invent stage skips.
- Explicit multi-type CDI sets use published conjunction / published stop rules, not random reviewer races.
- “Eventually consistent lifecycle meaning” is a constitutional defect.
- Invented or unstable dependency inputs, and invented authority chains, are hard stops — not soft variables.

---

### CDILO-05 — Preserve implementation readiness

**Definition.** Ensure that lifecycle findings support honest Programme XI integration and readiness judgements — so readiness claims consume intact dependency structure produced through lawful stages rather than inventing readiness despite circular, inverted, untraceable, or incompletely assessed corpora.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who declares a student “exam-ready” while the study plan cites itself in circles is performing readiness theatre, not care.

**Manifestations:**

- Intact findings published after CDIL-01…CDIL-07 may inform downstream readiness; they never themselves certify educational readiness, mastery, or product success.
- Violated / deferred / escalated findings, and refuse-* dispositions, block honest readiness claims that depend on the broken or incomplete structure.
- Readiness proxies (CI greens, coverage, scanner scores) never redefine CDILO-01…CDILO-06.
- Implementation readiness here means *constitutional corpus readiness for lawful integration* — not student exam readiness and not Runtime A deployment theatre.
- Preserving readiness never authorises rewriting dependencies or skipping stages so a release can ship.

---

### CDILO-06 — Preserve explainability and auditability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material dependency integrity lifecycle can honestly answer the mandatory explainability questions in `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md` and leaves a reconstructable trail: which dependency relationships were assessed, which authority chains were validated, whether cycles were detected, which findings were produced, and which constitutional boundaries remained intact.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later say *why* a relationship was accepted — or *at which stage* a finding arose — cannot be trusted. Silent green graphs destroy student and auditor trust.

**Manifestations:**

- Explainability obligations apply to all material lifecycles.
- Student / developer / auditor projections share one truth with different vocabulary.
- Audit records preserve constitutional references, not only technical scanner logs.
- Consumed corpus identities, CDI identities, CDIL stage identities, authority-chain identities, cycle status, and finding dispositions are recorded when material.
- Missing CDIL identity, missing corpus / relationship refs, missing authority-chain note, missing cycle status, missing finding disposition, or missing boundary confirmation is a defect.
- Lifecycle progress is never narrated as mastery, readiness theatre, educational quality, new law, an edge rewrite, or a freeze of stack authority.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing prior lawful lifecycle history to simplify the next report.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CDILO-01 conflicts with “helping” circular corpora look lifecycle-complete | Lawful coordination honesty wins — violated / deferred / escalated / refuse-progress |
| CDILO-02 conflicts with log volume reduction that erases authority chains | Authority traceability wins |
| CDILO-03 conflicts with soft-absorbing a superior programme into a subordinate | Constitutional layering wins |
| CDILO-04 conflicts with race-dependent reviewer outcomes or private stage shortcuts | Assessment repeatability wins |
| CDILO-05 conflicts with shipping despite broken or incompletely assessed dependency structure | Honest readiness wins — refuse false readiness |
| CDILO-06 conflicts with opaque “trust us” graph badges | Explainability and auditability win |

No supporting objective authorises violating CDILO-01.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — Constitution / EIP integrity obligations, Programme VI–X authority preservation, and WS1 / MS001 dependency integrity objectives for the lifecycle horizon.

## 5. Non-Objectives

The constitutional dependency integrity lifecycle does **not** optimise for:

- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- CI throughput, flaky-test suppression, or coverage theatre as educational success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when specifications or declared relationships are incomplete;
- collapsing CDIL stages into one mega-step that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics / package-scanner signals as substitutes for published constitutional relationships;
- freezing implementations, releases, or stacks as permanently lawful;
- treating documentation as optional commentary on whatever code already shipped;
- replacing MS001 dependency integrity meaning, Programme IX CT lineage preservation, or Programme IX–X evaluative / execution horizons;
- rewriting, inventing, or deleting constitutional dependencies so graphs look tidy;
- creating constitutional authority under a lifecycle badge.
