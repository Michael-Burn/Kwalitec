## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which dependency relationships* were assessed — which authority chains were validated, whether cycles were detected, which findings were produced, and which limits remained intact — is not coaching; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–X have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, runtime, governance, and execution speech speakable, and after WS1 / MS001 has made dependency integrity speech speakable, Programme XI still needs one lifecycle-speech answer:

> **“Which dependency relationships were assessed, which authority chains were validated, were any cycles detected, which findings were produced, and which constitutional boundaries remained intact?”**

That answer must cover every material dependency integrity lifecycle without inventing educational meaning, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, mutating edges, creating constitutional authority, freezing a stack as law, inventing unpublished CDI or CDIL types, replacing MS001 validation meaning, or presenting publication as educational quality.

## 2. What Lifecycle Explainability Is

**Constitutional Dependency Integrity Lifecycle Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **coordination of named dependency assessments through published CDIL stages** — discovering published relationships, validating authority chains and direction, detecting cycles, validating layering, applying published CDI types, and producing / publishing findings — not to invent a second tutor voice from package scanners, not to grade the student, and not to replace constitutional authority.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Dependency relationships assessed** | Named published corpus identities and relationships under assessment | Which dependency relationships were assessed? |
| **Authority chains validated** | Reconstructable paths from Constitution / EIP through programme layers to assessed edges | Which authority chains were validated? |
| **Cycle status** | none detected / cycle detected / inconclusive (with named paths when material) | Were any cycles detected? |
| **Findings produced** | intact / violated / deferred / escalated (with stage binding and composition notes) | Which findings were produced? |
| **Boundaries remaining intact** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden legislation / mutation / reinterpretation / authority-creation / substitution did not occur | Which constitutional boundaries remained intact? |

Lifecycle Explainability is:

- **coordination-centred** — it narrates relationships → authority chains → cycles → layering → findings under law;
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CDIL and CDI identities;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published corpora and relationships;
- **discovery-honest** — every material justification cites identifiable published relationships assessed (or names the hard stop when none exist);
- **authority-honest** — every material justification cites validated authority chains (or names the hard stop when unmet);
- **cycle-honest** — every material justification cites cycle status without hiding or “fixing” cycles;
- **finding-honest** — every material finding is bound to the producing lifecycle stages;
- **record-honest** — every material lifecycle cites which assessment records were preserved / published;
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to amend corpora;
- **non-authoritative** — publication is never narrated as a freeze of stack authority or a substitute for published corpora;
- **quality-honest** — intact status is never narrated as mastery, readiness theatre, or educational quality;
- **mutation-honest** — explanation never hides edge changes performed “so the lifecycle could proceed”;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence / validation speech, Programme IX governance speech, Programme X activity speech, or WS1 / MS001 dependency integrity speech;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match CDILO-06 reconstruction needs;
- **repeatability-honest** — explanations must not paper over race-dependent dispositions or private stage shortcuts.

Lifecycle Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–X / WS1 / MS001 meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a substitute for CDI findings speech, CT lineage speech, or Programme IX–X evaluative / execution speech;
- a CI status page, coverage report, package-scanner summary, or linter summary presented as constitutional coordination;
- a freeze of implementations, stacks, vendors, or releases as constitutional law;
- a licence to narrate invented relationships, invented authority chains, or mutated edges as if they were lawful inputs.

## 3. Mandatory Explainability Questions

Every material constitutional dependency integrity lifecycle must be able to answer:

### CDILEQ-01 — Which dependency relationships were assessed?

**Required content**

- Named published corpus identities and relationship identities discovered at CDIL-02
- Binding between relationship identities and intended / applied CDI types
- Confirmation that unpublished customs, invented edges, or scanner noise were not used as constitutional relationships

**Defect if**

- “Dependencies are fine” with no relationship references
- Anonymous “the docs” or “the imports” without identifiable published relationships
- Relationships invented, rewritten, reversed, or deleted as assessment without disclosure and hard stop

---

### CDILEQ-02 — Which authority chains were validated?

**Required content**

- Named authority chains confirmed at CDIL-03 (Constitution / EIP → programme layer(s) → assessed edge)
- Explicit refuse-validate disposition when chains were unmet
- Confirmation that authority-chain links were not invented or silently skipped

**Defect if**

- Assessment proceeds with “authority assumed”
- Authority chains invented mid-lifecycle to force intact status
- Silent omission of mandatory superior identities

---

### CDILEQ-03 — Were any cycles detected?

**Required content**

- Cycle status from CDIL-04: none detected / cycle detected / inconclusive
- Named closed paths when cycles were detected
- Confirmation that cycles were not hidden or erased by edge mutation

**Defect if**

- Cycle status omitted when findings are claimed
- Detected cycles quietly dropped from publication speech
- Narration that cycles were “resolved” by rewriting dependencies under lifecycle pretence

---

### CDILEQ-04 — Which findings were produced?

**Required content**

- Named dependency integrity findings from CDIL-06 (intact / violated / deferred / escalated)
- Binding of each finding to producing CDIL stages (especially CDIL-03…CDIL-06)
- Publication artefacts from CDIL-07 where material (or refuse-publish identity)
- Continuity sufficient to reconstruct corpora, relationships, authority chains, cycle status, CDI types, stages, findings, and boundaries

**Defect if**

- “Intact” with no reconstructable finding identity
- Findings that drop stage-to-finding binding or relationship identity
- Silent erasure of prior lawful assessment / lifecycle history
- Publication that invents intact status or hides violated dispositions

---

### CDILEQ-05 — Which constitutional boundaries remained intact?

**Required content**

- Explicit confirmation against `LIFECYCLE_BOUNDARIES.md` checks material to the concern (especially non-legislation, non-reinterpretation, non-mutation, non-authority-creation, non-substitution)
- Disclosure of any hard stop that prevented progress or publication

**Defect if**

- Boundaries are implied, not speakable
- Publication speech smuggles authority creation, edge mutation, meaning reinterpretation, or law rewrites
- Artefact / dependency mutation or invented relationships are hidden

## 4. Audience Projections

| Audience | Vocabulary emphasis | Same truth |
|----------|---------------------|------------|
| **Student-facing** | Plain speech that published learning materials were checked for coherent structure — not that the student mastered a topic or that the product is “intact forever as law” | Relationships / authority chains / cycles / findings / boundaries remain reconstructable |
| **Developer-facing** | Corpus paths, CDI / CDIL / CDIR / CT identities, finding codes, record identities | No private “trust the scanner” meaning |
| **Auditor-facing** | Full reconstruction: CDILEQ-01…CDILEQ-05 answers, stage traces, boundary checks | Continuity of prior lawful records |

Projections differ in vocabulary. They must not differ in constitutional facts.

## 5. Forbidden Explainability Moves

| Forbidden narration | Why | Lawful alternative |
|---------------------|-----|--------------------|
| “Lifecycle published ⇒ new constitutional law” | Legislation via publication | “Findings recorded; corpora unchanged” |
| “Lifecycle published ⇒ dependencies rewritten” | Dependency mutation | “Structure assessed as found; edges unchanged” |
| “Lifecycle published ⇒ authority created” | Authority creation | “Assessment coordinated; published corpora remain the standard” |
| “Intact ⇒ student ready / mastered” | Quality / mastery out of scope | Keep structure speech distinct from educational claim classes |
| “We invented the missing link so authority validated” | Invented authority chains | Refuse-validate; propose lawful amendment upstream |
| “We fixed the cycle in the lifecycle” | Edge mutation | Report cycle-detected; refuse graph rewriting under assessment pretence |
| “Stage skipped because the scanner was green” | Unpublished stage customs | Apply CDIL-01…CDIL-07 or refuse |
| “We adjusted the docs so the lifecycle could finish” | Specification / dependency mutation | Refuse-discover / violated; propose lawful change outside lifecycle pretence |
| “This stack is now the standard” | Authority substitution / neutrality breach | Keep implementation as assessed subject |
| “Findings update the constitution” | Legislation via explanation | Propose amendment upstream; leave corpora unchanged |
| “Linked in CT ⇒ dependency intact forever” | CT / CDI conflation | Compose with CT; keep dependency integrity speech distinct |

## 7. Audit Alignment

Lifecycle explainability records must support reconstruction of:

1. Dependency relationships assessed (CDILEQ-01)
2. Authority chains validated (CDILEQ-02)
3. Cycle detection status (CDILEQ-03)
4. Findings produced and records / publication artefacts preserved (CDILEQ-04)
5. Constitutional boundaries remaining intact (CDILEQ-05)
6. Applied CDI-01…CDI-07 identities (from WS1 / MS001 speech)
7. Finding dispositions (intact / violated / deferred / escalated / refuse-*)
8. Continuity of prior lawful lifecycle history (EIP-005)

Missing any material element is an explainability defect under CDILO-06.
