## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “scanner greens,” “tickets closed,” or “graphs shown.” The tutor optimises **honest closing of constitutional dependency assessment work**: when the published lifecycle for a concern has lawfully concluded, dependencies have been evaluated, authority chains and layering validated, findings published, and the assessment formally closed, the tutor can say so — without pretending constitutional authority was created, without rewriting edges so the graph looks tidy, without pretending the student has mastered the topic, and without amending the syllabus because the machinery stopped cleanly.

These objectives bind every constitutional dependency integrity completion judgement and every successor assessment machinery that claims dependency assessment fulfilment.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### CDICO-01 — Confirm lawful assessment completion

**Definition.** Ensure that runtime / assessment / audit speech affirms dependency integrity completion only when a published assessment concern under WS1 / MS001–MS002 has concluded the required CDIL-01…CDIL-07 lifecycle obligations — or honestly refuses close when fulfilment criteria are not met.

**Includes:**

- Assessing only concerns that lawfully opened under WS1 / MS001–MS002 initiation → discovery → authority validation → cycle detection → layer validation → finding construction → publication law.
- Affirming completion only when `COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md` conditions (CDIC-01…CDIC-05) hold.
- Preferring lawful “not yet” / refuse-close over inventing a “done enough” status for product convenience.
- Distinguishing lawful stop dispositions (refuse / defer / escalate / violated as published outcomes that exhaust remaining *assessment* duties with honest recording) from abandoned lifecycle duties.
- Binding every material completion judgement to reconstructable constitutional references.

**Excludes:**

- Declaring complete because a CI job exited, a ticket closed, a package graph glowed, or a UI spinner stopped.
- Treating Twin / Adaptive / Experience / Mission heuristics as completion warrants.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for assessment honesty.
- Collapsing “dependency assessment complete” into “constitutional authority created,” “edges rewritten,” “law reinterpreted,” “specifications amended,” or “student succeeded.”

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software celebrates unfinished dependency assessment — or celebrates finished scanner machinery as finished law-making or edge rewriting. Honest confirmation is care; completion theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CDICO-02 — Preserve dependency integrity

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming completion never invents, reverses, deletes, or silently rewrites published constitutional dependency relationships — and never upgrades a violated / deferred / escalated finding into “intact” under close theatre.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who “helpfully” erases a circular citation so the assessment can finish has abandoned educational honesty even if the completion UX looks clean.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion leaves named dependency identities and dispositions reconstructable.
- Close never substitutes package graphs, import scanners, or ticket states for published dependency findings.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing or silently rewriting prior lawful dependency dispositions to simplify the next report.
- Mutating edges under “completion cleanup” is a defect.

---

### CDICO-03 — Preserve authority traceability

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming completion never invents missing superior authority links, reverses authorship direction, or treats a preferred stack as the constitutional source — so every closed assessment remains reconstructable to published authority chains.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later walk from yesterday’s finding to the superior corpus that authorised the edge cannot be trusted. Completion that invents chains is educational fiction dressed as done.

**Manifestations:**

- Affirmed closes leave authority-chain identities reconstructable with dependency identities and findings.
- Invented superior links, reversed authorship, and stack-as-law substitutions are defects.
- Continuity forbids deleting prior lawful authority walks to “clean up” completion.
- Incomplete authority validation for a required concern scope blocks affirmation.

---

### CDICO-04 — Preserve repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that the same published corpora, same discovered relationships, same CDI applications, and same CDIL stage sequence yield the same completion disposition for the same concern — so close never invents contradictory stories from identical constitutional inputs.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who marks the same syllabus relationship “complete” on Monday and “not yet” on Tuesday without a published corpus change has abandoned care for theatre.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion dispositions are deterministic under published inputs.
- Silent non-determinism, ambient “main branch as graph,” and demo-only close rules are defects.
- Optional honesty checks (CDIC-06…CDIC-10) support, never replace, CDIC-01…CDIC-05.
- Repeatability failures void lawful affirmation even if a badge looks green.

---

### CDICO-05 — Preserve implementation readiness honesty

**Definition.** Ensure that completion judgements speak to assessment fulfilment independently of programming language, framework, datastore, CI vendor, cloud topology, or preferred Runtime A shape — and never narrate “assessment complete” as frozen stack readiness, product success, or educational readiness.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who declares only one notebook brand “dependency-ready forever” has abandoned educational law for vendor preference. Readiness for *implementation work under intact law* is not readiness as *law itself*.

**Manifestations:**

- CDIC criteria speak to constitutional identities and obligations, not “must use Flask / SQLAlchemy / OpenAPI.”
- Equivalent lawful implementations under the same published law may both reach lawful assessment completion.
- Replacing an assessed stack with another that still obeys published law does not itself create unlawful completion.
- “Our reference implementation completes this way” is never a substitute for published criteria.
- Assessment-complete never freezes a stack, vendor, or release as constitutional law or student exam readiness.

---

### CDICO-06 — Preserve explainability and auditability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material completion judgement leaves a reconstructable, speakable trail: which dependencies were assessed, which authority chains were validated, which findings were published, and which boundaries remained intact.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who can only say “the dependency check is done somewhere” is performing, not teaching. Speakable completion is care.

**Manifestations:**

- Audit records preserve constitutional references, not only technical job logs or CI annotations.
- Explainability obligations in `COMPLETION_EXPLAINABILITY.md` apply to all material completion judgements (CDICQ-01…CDICQ-04).
- Missing CDIC identity against criteria, missing dependency refs, missing authority-chain identity, or missing finding identity is a defect.
- Student / developer / auditor projections share one truth with different vocabulary.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CDICO-01 conflicts with inventing unpublished edges so close can complete | Assessment honesty wins — refuse / defer / escalate |
| CDICO-02 conflicts with rewriting edges to erase cycles for tidy close | Dependency integrity wins |
| CDICO-03 conflicts with inventing superior links so authority looks complete | Authority traceability wins |
| CDICO-04 conflicts with ambient non-determinism or demo-only close rules | Repeatability wins |
| CDICO-05 conflicts with privileging a reference stack as completion law or narrating exam readiness | Implementation readiness honesty wins |
| CDICO-06 conflicts with opaque complete badges | Explainability / auditability wins |
| Any CDICO conflicts with creating authority / altering dependencies / reinterpreting meaning / amending specifications | Non-legislative honesty wins — refuse the overclaim |

No supporting objective authorises violating CDICO-01.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — Constitution / EIP integrity obligations, Programme VI–X authority preservation, MS001 CDIO duties, and MS002 CDILO duties for the *completion* horizon.

---

## 5. Non-Objectives

Constitutional dependency integrity completion does **not** optimise for:

- creating constitutional authority or soft-amending corpora via close;
- inventing, reversing, deleting, or rewriting constitutional dependency relationships;
- reinterpreting Programme VI–X / EIP meaning during close;
- amending constitutional specifications under “completion” pretext;
- constitutional conformance disposition, fidelity scoring, or “assessed therefore conformant” theatre;
- verification success, compliance disposition, certification seals, or implementation-approval judgement;
- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- CI throughput, flaky-scan suppression, or demo continuity as educational or constitutional success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when required assessment duties are incomplete;
- collapsing CDIC criteria into one mega-flag that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics signals as substitutes for published completion criteria;
- treating scanner greens as optional commentary that silently becomes law;
- declaring authority creation, edge rewrite, meaning reinterpretation, or specification amendment solely because completion was affirmed.

## 6. Closing Statement

> **A concern that becomes “dependency assessment complete” by creating constitutional authority, rewriting edges, reinterpreting law, or amending specifications is a failed educational system.  
> A concern that confirms only lawful assessment conclusion — with dependency integrity, authority traceability, repeatability, readiness honesty, and explainability intact — is doing its only completion job.**
