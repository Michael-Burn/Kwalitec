## 1. Purpose

Assessment machinery that silently turns “the dependency assessment finished” into “the Constitution now says X,” “yesterday’s edges are rewritten,” “this stack is now the superior authority,” “student succeeded,” or “specifications are amended” destroys student trust and educational integrity.

This document draws a bright line between **lawful constitutional dependency integrity completion** (Programme XI / WS1 / MS003) and **constitutional authorship / dependency mutation / meaning reinterpretation / specification amendment / educational judgement** owned elsewhere.

---

## 2. Boundary Principles

1. **Confirm assessment fulfilment, do not legislate.** Completion affirms fulfilment of published dependency assessment lifecycle duties; it does not create constitutional law.
2. **Assessment only.** Constitutional authority creation, dependency mutation, meaning reinterpretation, and specification amendment are never inferred from close.
3. **Not dependency rewriting.** Close never invents, reverses, deletes, or silently rewrites published edges.
4. **Not MS001 meaning replacement.** Completion judges fulfilment of CDI evaluation; it never redefines CDI categories or CDIR rules.
5. **Not MS002 lifecycle rewrite.** Completion judges fulfilment of CDIL stages; it never invents an eighth stage called law-making.
6. **Not constitutional amendment.** Close never patches law or redefines constitutional meaning.
7. **Not meaning reinterpretation.** Programme VI questions and EIP claim classes survive close intact.
8. **Not authority creation.** Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard after close.
9. **Exactly as published.** Close never redefines CDI / CDIL catalogues or soft-amends Programme VI–X meaning.
10. **Published criteria only.** Pipelines, tickets, package graphs, and elapsed time are not fulfilment law.
11. **Published records only.** Audit continuity is constitutional, not free-form log theatre.
12. **Reference findings honestly.** Published findings may be cited; closing never upgrades disposition into authority creation or edge rewrite.
13. **Implementation neutrality.** No stack becomes law by proximity to a complete flag.
14. **Assessment-complete ≠ quality.** Completion never grades learning, mastery, readiness, or product success.
15. **Assessment-complete ≠ Programme IX evaluative close.** Completion never substitutes for CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC fulfilment.
16. **Explain the stop.** Students, developers, and auditors should hear when close must be refused.
17. **No emergency exemption.** Deadlines and demos never mint completion power or amendment power.
18. **Repeatability survives close.** Close must not invent contradictory dispositions from identical published inputs.
19. **Layering survives close.** Close never absorbs superior authority into a subordinate under “complete” theatre.
20. **Continuity survives close.** EIP-005 forbids erasing prior lawful assessment history to tidy the report.

## 3. What Dependency Integrity Completion May Do (Lawful)

| Lawful action | Constitutional meaning |
|---------------|------------------------|
| **Confirm assessment completion** | Affirm CDIC-01…CDIC-05 when published criteria hold for a lawful dependency assessment concern |
| **Confirm publication** | Cite that required dependency integrity findings were lawfully constructed and published under published CDI / CDIL rules — without rewriting or silently upgrading dispositions |
| **Confirm formal closure** | Affirm that the assessment concern was formally closed under published boundary honesty |
| **Preserve assessment records** | Leave reconstructable constitutional records of dependencies, authority chains, stages, findings, and boundaries |
| **Preserve audit continuity** | Leave reconstructable constitutional records of affirmed or refused close |
| **Refuse unlawful close** | Prefer honest not-yet / refuse / defer / escalate over improvisation |
| **Compose required stages and types** | Confirm fulfilment across published CDIL / CDI conjunctions without collapsing failed duties |
| **Remain implementation-neutral** | Honour completion law across lawful equivalent stacks |
| **Preserve repeatability** | Ensure same published inputs do not yield contradictory completion stories |
| **Explain completion** | Answer dependencies / authority chains / findings / boundaries without rewriting meaning |
| **Propose corpus amendments upstream** | Recommend (not enact) amendments when law itself must change before assessment can complete |

These actions **confirm dependency assessment fulfilment**. They do **not** publish a new Constitution, invent coach warrants, mint tips, author ownership maps, invent CDI / CDIL / CDIC types, author runtime behaviour, rewrite edges, create constitutional authority, reinterpret constitutional meaning, amend constitutional specifications, finish Programme IX evaluative horizons as constitutional law, or judge educational quality.

## 4. What Dependency Integrity Completion Must NEVER Do

| Forbidden action | Why | Lawful alternative |
|------------------|-----|--------------------|
| **Create constitutional authority** | Only published corpora define constitutional standards | Keep completion as assessment fulfilment; never as next-cycle law |
| **Alter dependency relationships** | Edges are published corpus relationships, not draft notes | Cite findings honestly; refuse silent rewrite under close theatre |
| **Reinterpret constitutional meaning** | Close never soft-amends Programme VI–X / EIP meaning | Preserve identity of published meaning; never invent it |
| **Amend constitutional specifications** | Only corpus governance may amend meaning | Leave specs unchanged; propose amendment upstream or refuse incomplete assessment |
| **Invent dependency relationships** | Discovery / evaluation name published relationships only | Refuse invented edges; escalate to corpus governance if law is missing |
| **Reverse authorship direction** | Superior → subordinate orientation is constitutional | Report reversed edges as violations; never “fix” by reversing again under close |
| **Invent authority chains** | Authority validation confirms reconstructable chains | Refuse invented superior links; prefer lawful failed / deferred / escalated outcomes |
| **Hide cycles or invent intact status** | Acyclicity honesty is educational care | Publish violated / deferred / escalated findings honestly |
| **Absorb superior authority into a subordinate** | Layering is published constitutional order | Report layering defects; never rewrite layers under close |
| **Replace MS001 dependency evaluation** | MS001 owns CDI meaning | Confirm assessment support for evaluation honesty; never claim “complete therefore new CDI law” |
| **Replace MS002 lifecycle law** | MS002 owns CDIL stages | Confirm stage fulfilment; never invent stages |
| **Create educational meaning** | Programme VI owns coach / planner questions | Preserve identity of published meaning; never invent it |
| **Author runtime behaviour** | Programme VIII + permitted writers own execution law | Assess corpus dependencies against published law; do not mint behaviour |
| **Bypass audit / publication / closure requirements** | Requirements are educational care, not optional polish | Prefer understatement / refuse over invented green completes |
| **Privilege a technology as constitutional** | Implementation neutrality | Confirm assessment obligations, not Flask / SQLAlchemy / REST / OpenAPI fashion |
| **Erase assessment trails to tidy close** | EIP-005 continuity | Preserve prior lawful stage / finding / record artefacts; refuse destructive “cleanup” |
| **Upgrade finding disposition by closing** | CDIC-04 preserves identity; close does not rewrite status | Record findings honestly including violated / deferred / escalated |
| **Present CI / tickets / package graphs as fulfilment law** | Theatre is not constitution | Require CDIC-01…CDIC-05 content |
| **Imply educational success** | Learning outcomes are not assessment fulfilment | Keep student speech honest: assessment-complete ≠ ready |
| **Imply Programme IX evaluative completion** | Conformity / verification / compliance / certification are orthogonal | Compose with CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC; do not treat them as CDIC (or vice versa) |
| **Emergency-amend via deadline** | Urgency never mints amendment power | Refuse incomplete close; escalate to corpus governance |
| **Collapse with CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC completion** | Implementation lineage / verification / compliance / certification fulfilment are orthogonal horizons | Compose with Programme IX MS003 corpora; do not treat them as CDIC |

## 5. Boundary Tests

Before affirming completion, ask:

1. Would this close still be lawful if no CI job had run?
2. Would this close still be lawful if the preferred stack were replaced by another lawful equivalent?
3. Does the close require inventing an unpublished dependency, authority chain, or stage?
4. Does the close silently rewrite a violated / deferred / escalated finding into intact?
5. Does the close narrate constitutional authority creation, edge mutation, meaning reinterpretation, or specification amendment?
6. Can students / developers / auditors answer CDICQ-01…CDICQ-04 from the close record?
7. Would the same published inputs yield the same completion disposition tomorrow?

Any “no” that indicates invention, mutation, reinterpretation, amendment, stack privilege, mute theatre, or non-repeatability ⇒ **refuse close**.

---

## 6. Emergency and Product Pressure

Deadlines, demos, investor reviews, and release trains **never** authorise:

- inventing CDIC criteria outside CDIC-01…CDIC-05;
- inventing edges, authority chains, or intact findings so close can complete;
- amending Constitution / EIP / Programmes VI–X / WS1 / MS001–MS002 via completion theatre;
- freezing Flask, SQLAlchemy, OpenAPI, a CI vendor, or Runtime A as constitutional law;
- narrating assessment-complete as educational success or Programme IX evaluative success.

Lawful response under pressure: **not yet**, **refuse close**, **defer**, or **escalate** — with speakable reasons.

## 7. Closing Statement

> **Dependency integrity completion may confirm assessment completion, publication, and formal closure.  
> The moment it creates constitutional authority, alters dependency relationships, reinterprets constitutional meaning, or amends constitutional specifications, it has ceased to be constitutional completion and become product fiction.**
