## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor needs bright conditions for saying “this constitutional dependency assessment work is finished” — and equally bright refusals when it is not. After WS1 / MS001 has named how dependencies are validated and WS1 / MS002 has named how assessments are organised, Programme XI Workstream 1 still needs one fulfilment checklist:

> **What must be true before constitutional speech may affirm that the required dependency assessment lifecycle has concluded, dependencies have been evaluated, authority chains validated, findings published, and the assessment formally closed?**

These criteria answer that question. They are **normative constitutional conditions**, not database predicates, CI job statuses, ticket states, package-graph greens, or API status codes.

---

## 2. Criterion Catalogue

All material affirmed completions require **CDIC-01 through CDIC-05** to hold. Failure of any criterion ⇒ **not yet** or **refuse close**. Do not invent a partial “mostly complete” authority, edge-rewrite, meaning-reinterpretation, educational, or constitutional status.

| ID | Criterion | Primary question |
|----|-----------|------------------|
| **CDIC-01** | Lifecycle completed | Were required CDIL-01…CDIL-07 stages completed for the concern? |
| **CDIC-02** | Dependency evaluation completed | Were required named dependency relationships evaluated under published CDI law (including direction, cycle detection, and layer validation)? |
| **CDIC-03** | Authority validation completed | Were required authority chains and lawful direction validated under published law? |
| **CDIC-04** | Findings publication completed | Were required dependency integrity findings lawfully constructed and published? |
| **CDIC-05** | Assessment formally closed | Was the dependency assessment concern formally closed under published boundary honesty? |

Optional supporting honesty checks (do not replace CDIC-01…CDIC-05):

| ID | Check | Primary question |
|----|-------|------------------|
| **CDIC-06** | Catalogue honesty | Were stages and evaluations typed under published CDIL-01…CDIL-07 and CDI-01…CDI-07 only? |
| **CDIC-07** | Continuity honesty | Was prior lawful assessment history preserved (EIP-005)? |
| **CDIC-08** | Non-claim honesty | Does the judgement refuse authority-creation / edge-mutation / meaning-reinterpretation / specification-amendment / educational overclaim? |
| **CDIC-09** | Neutrality honesty | Does the judgement refuse stack / vendor canonisation as completion law? |
| **CDIC-10** | Repeatability honesty | Would the same published inputs yield the same completion disposition? |

## 3. Criterion Definitions

### CDIC-01 — Lifecycle completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every lifecycle stage required by the concern’s published WS1 / MS002 scope has been executed — or that a published refuse / defer / escalate disposition exhausted remaining *dependency assessment* duties for that stage set with honest progression to finding construction / publication (or refuse-publish) and closure of what occurred.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Identifiable dependency assessment concern opened under WS1 / MS001–MS002.
- Required CDIL-01…CDIL-07 stage identities for the concern scope.
- Stage traces / disposition records for each required stage (including lawful early stops).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed stage-fulfilment record naming which required stages completed.
- Or a lawful stop record naming which stages were refused / deferred / escalated and why remaining assessment duties are exhausted.

**Completion conditions.**

- Required stages map to published CDIL-01…CDIL-07 only.
- Mandatory initiation, discovery, authority validation, cycle detection, layer validation, finding construction, and publication obligations for the concern are not silently omitted.
- Lawful early stops still leave honest stage outcomes; silent disappearance of a concern is a defect.
- Refusal of “pipeline skipped validation” or “we know it ran” without stage identity as completion.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That stage completion creates constitutional authority or amends constitutional specifications.
- That stage completion alters dependency relationships or freezes a stack as law.
- That stage completion means the learner succeeded or educational readiness was achieved.
- That skipped stages may be invented or implied from CI theatre.
- That Programme IX conformity / verification / compliance / certification was performed under stage progress.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who declares dependency assessment “finished” without confirming each required assessment step occurred has not finished lawful care — even if the desk looks tidy.

---

### CDIC-02 — Dependency evaluation completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every named constitutional dependency relationship required by the concern’s published scope was evaluated under published CDI-01…CDI-07 categories and CDIR rules — including dependency direction, cycle detection (CDIL-04), and layer validation (CDIL-05) — or that a published refuse disposition exhausted remaining *evaluation* duties when required relationships could not be lawfully assessed without inventing edges.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Named dependency relationship identities in scope (declared / lawfully implied under CDI law).
- Published constitutional corpus identities under assessment.
- Evaluation dispositions under applicable CDI types (direction, acyclicity, layering).
- Stage traces for CDIL-02 / CDIL-04 / CDIL-05 as required by concern scope.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed dependency-evaluation record naming which relationships were assessed under which published CDI categories — including direction, cycle, and layer outcomes.
- Or a lawful refuse / defer / escalate record naming which required relationships remain unevaluated and why.

**Completion conditions.**

- Dependency subjects are named (not “the corpus,” “the stack,” or vendor labels alone).
- Evaluation cites published constitutional specifications and CDI categories, not unpublished customs.
- Discovery named only declared / lawfully implied relationships — inventing edges to force evaluation is a hard stop.
- Cycle detection reports closed paths honestly; mutating relationships to erase cycles is a hard stop.
- Layer validation preserves published Constitution / EIP → VI–X → XI layering; absorbing superior authority into a subordinate is a hard stop.
- Refusal of package graphs, import scanners, Twin confidence, or CI greens as substitutes for named dependency evaluation.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That evaluated dependencies were therefore rewritten, invented, or deleted.
- That a particular stack, language, or framework is privileged as law.
- That product quality or educational quality was judged.
- That constitutional specifications were modified to match the evaluation.
- That Programme IX CT lineage preservation equals dependency evaluation fulfilment.
- That layer validation alone equals whole-concern completion (layer outcomes feed CDIC-02; they do not replace CDIC-01…CDIC-05).

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who claims “the dependencies were checked” without naming which relationships were assessed for direction, cycles, and layering against which published categories has performed scanner theatre, not evaluation fulfilment.

---

### CDIC-03 — Authority validation completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every authority-chain validation required by the concern’s published scope has been lawfully completed under published CDI / CDIL law — confirming reconstructable paths from Constitution / EIP through programme layers to the assessed dependency, and confirming lawful superior → subordinate / consumer orientation — without inventing missing superior links or reversing authorship.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Named dependency relationships under assessment (CDIC-02 inputs / CDIL-02 outputs).
- Published authority-validation rules under WS1 / MS001–MS002 (especially CDIL-03 and CDI authority-traceability rules).
- Boundary confirmations that validation does not invent chains or privilege a stack as law.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed authority-validation record naming which authority chains were validated (or honestly failed validation) for which dependencies.
- Or a lawful refuse / defer / escalate record naming which required chains remain unvalidated and why.

**Completion conditions.**

- Authority chains cite published constitutional references, not unpublished tribal knowledge.
- Direction remains superior → subordinate / consumer; reversed authorship is a hard stop.
- “Authority was checked somewhere” without named chain identity is not completion.
- Lawful failed / deferred / escalated authority outcomes still satisfy *validation completion* when they are the honest outcome — completeness of *validation* is not completeness of *intact disposition*, and neither is constitutional authority creation.
- Invented superior links to force intact status are hard stops.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That validating an authority chain creates constitutional authority or amends specifications.
- That a validated chain freezes the implementation, stack, vendor, or release as permanent constitutional law.
- That a failed / deferred / escalated authority outcome may be silently erased so close can look green.
- That MS001 dependency meaning was replaced, skipped, or rewritten by authority validation.
- That Educational Validation Framework quality review is complete.
- That educational success or exam readiness follows from authority validation.

**Tutor rationale.** Recording that an authority chain was checked — including when it fails — is care. Inventing a superior link so the assessment can finish is fiction. Completeness of *authority validation* is not creation of *constitutional authority*.

---

### CDIC-04 — Findings publication completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every dependency integrity finding required by the concern’s published scope has been lawfully constructed and published under published CDI / CDIL rules — including intact, violated, deferred, and escalated dispositions — without rewriting those dispositions into constitutional authority, edge mutation, meaning reinterpretation, or specification amendment, and without inventing intact status or hiding cycles.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Evaluation and authority-validation dispositions under applicable CDI-01…CDI-07 types (CDIL-03…CDIL-05 outputs).
- Published finding-construction and publication rules under WS1 / MS001–MS002 (especially CDIL-06 / CDIL-07).
- Boundary confirmations that findings do not invent law or rewrite edges.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Identifiable dependency integrity finding identities with lawful dispositions, published under honesty rules.
- Or a lawful stop record naming why required findings could not be constructed / published without invention.

**Completion conditions.**

- Findings cite published constitutional references, named dependencies, and validated (or honestly failed) authority chains.
- Dispositions remain within published vocabulary (intact / violated / deferred / escalated).
- “Assessment happened somewhere” without named finding identity and publication record is not completion.
- Lawful violated / deferred / escalated findings still satisfy *publication* when they are the honest outcome — completeness of *publication* is not completeness of *intact disposition*, and neither is constitutional amendment.
- Publication never invents intact status, hides cycles, or freezes a stack as law.
- Continuity forbids silently replacing prior lawful findings under close theatre.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That publishing a finding creates constitutional authority or amends constitutional specifications.
- That an intact finding freezes the implementation, stack, vendor, or release as permanent constitutional law.
- That a violated / deferred / escalated finding may be silently erased or upgraded so close can look green.
- That MS001 dependency evaluation was replaced, skipped, or rewritten by publication.
- That Programme IX conformity / verification / compliance / certification was achieved by publication.
- That the learner succeeded or is exam-ready.

**Tutor rationale.** Publishing a hard dependency disposition is not rewriting the syllabus — but omitting a required finding while claiming the assessment finished is dishonest. Completeness of *findings publication* is not completeness of *constitutional change*.

---

### CDIC-05 — Assessment formally closed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that the dependency assessment concern was formally closed under published CDIL-07 / boundary honesty — exhausting remaining *dependency assessment lifecycle* duties without inventing constitutional authority, altering dependency relationships, reinterpreting constitutional meaning, amending constitutional specifications, or implying educational success.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Published findings and assessment records (CDIC-01…CDIC-04).
- CDIL-07 closure / publication honesty rules and `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md` hard stops.
- Explicit non-claim confirmations (authority creation / edge mutation / meaning reinterpretation / specification amendment / educational success).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Formal close disposition: affirmed complete / not yet / refuse close / defer / escalate.
- Boundary preservation record confirming forbidden overclaims did not occur.

**Completion conditions.**

- Closure occurs only after required stages, dependency evaluation, authority validation, findings publication, and assessment-record obligations for the concern are satisfied — or after a published stop exhausts remaining assessment duties with honest recording.
- Closure speech answers CDICQ-01…CDICQ-04 or explicitly refuses mute theatre.
- Closure never silently upgrades findings dispositions or rewrites edges.
- Refusal of ticket closure, PR merge, package-graph greens, or pipeline greens alone as formal constitutional close.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That formal close creates constitutional authority or amends constitutional specifications.
- That formal close alters, invents, reverses, or deletes constitutional dependency relationships.
- That formal close reinterprets Programme VI–X / EIP meaning.
- That formal close means the implementation is constitutionally conformant, verified, compliant, or certified.
- That formal close means the student succeeded or is exam-ready.
- That formal close freezes a stack, vendor, or release as permanent constitutional law.

**Tutor rationale.** Closing the assessment folder is care only when the required steps, evaluations, authority checks, findings, and records exist — and only when nobody pretends the close is a new constitution, a rewritten dependency graph, or a pass certificate for the candidate.

## 4. Criterion Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CDIC-01 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CDIC-02 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CDIC-03 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CDIC-04 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — mute complete badges are defects |
| CDIC-05 fails | Affirmation is unlawful even if earlier stages appear present |
| Optional CDIC-06…CDIC-10 fail | Treat as honesty defects; do not soft-pass CDIC-01…CDIC-05 |
| Any criterion “passes” only by creating authority / altering dependencies / reinterpreting meaning / amending specifications / implying educational success | The implied overclaim voids lawful affirmation |

All of CDIC-01…CDIC-05 are **conjunctive** for material affirmed completion. There is no lawful “four of five” educational, fidelity, authority-creation, or constitutional status.

Layer validation is **not** a sixth catalogue criterion. It is a required evaluation obligation under CDIC-02 (and a required stage under CDIC-01 via CDIL-05). Affirming close without layer validation where the concern required it fails CDIC-01 and/or CDIC-02.

## 5. Lawful Stop Dispositions

Not every incomplete concern is a defect. Published WS1 / MS002 refuse / defer / escalate / violated outcomes may **exhaust remaining dependency assessment duties** for a concern without affirming lifecycle fulfilment as “complete” in the celebratory sense — while still requiring honest recording and formal close of what occurred.

| Disposition | Constitutional meaning | May affirm CDIC completion? |
|-------------|------------------------|----------------------------|
| **Affirm complete** | CDIC-01…CDIC-05 hold for the concern | Yes |
| **Not yet** | Required stages, dependency evaluation, authority validation, findings, or records remain outstanding | No |
| **Refuse close** | Affirmation would invent stages / findings, rewrite edges, invent authority, or overclaim | No |
| **Defer** | Published wait for upstream corpus / relationship / authority identity | No (unless published law says remaining assessment duties are exhausted) |
| **Escalate** | Published stop requiring human / corpus governance attention | No (unless published law says remaining assessment duties are exhausted) |

A lawful stop that exhausts assessment duties is **honest unfinished fulfilment or honest refusal** — never stealth constitutional authority, edge rewrite, meaning reinterpretation, specification amendment, educational success, or Programme IX evaluative disposition.

---

## 6. Non-Criteria

The following are **not** constitutional completion criteria:

- CI pipeline greens, flaky-scan suppression, or job exit codes;
- ticket closure, PR merge, or documentation checklist ticks alone;
- package graphs, import scanners, or OpenAPI linkers alone;
- coverage percentages, lint scores, or elapsed time;
- Twin / Adaptive / Experience / Mission confidence signals;
- UI celebration states or analytics milestones;
- “looks acyclic enough for the demo”;
- MS001 intact disposition alone (that is evaluation outcome, not whole-concern CDIC fulfilment);
- Programme IX CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC completion alone (orthogonal implementation horizons);
- Educational Validation Framework quality release (orthogonal);
- any implication that constitutional specifications changed, edges were rewritten, or authority was created because assessment closed.

## 7. Closing Statement

> **Constitutional dependency integrity assessment is complete only when the required lifecycle is completed, dependency evaluation is completed, authority validation is completed, findings publication is completed, and the assessment is formally closed.  
> Satisfying CDIC-01…CDIC-05 never creates constitutional authority, alters dependency relationships, reinterprets constitutional law, or amends constitutional specifications.**
