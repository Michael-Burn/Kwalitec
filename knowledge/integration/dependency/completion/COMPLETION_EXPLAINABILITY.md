## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which dependencies* were assessed — which authority chains were validated, which findings were published, and which limits remained intact — is not closing honestly; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–X have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, governance, and execution speech speakable, and after WS1 / MS001–MS002 have made dependency and lifecycle speech speakable, Programme XI still needs one dependency-assessment-completion-speech answer:

> **“Which dependencies were assessed, which authority chains were validated, which findings were published, and which constitutional boundaries remained intact?”**

That answer must cover every material affirmed (or refused) completion without inventing educational success, creating constitutional authority, altering dependency relationships, reinterpreting constitutional meaning, amending constitutional specifications, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, or freezing a stack as law.

## 2. What Dependency Integrity Completion Explainability Is

**Constitutional Dependency Integrity Completion Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **fulfilment (or lawful refusal of fulfilment) of the required constitutional dependency integrity assessment lifecycle** — not to invent a second tutor voice from CI complete badges, and not to grade the student, create constitutional authority, rewrite edges, or amend constitutional specifications.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Dependencies assessed** | Named constitutional dependency relationships evaluated under CDI law | Which dependencies were assessed? |
| **Authority chains validated** | Named reconstructable authority chains and direction outcomes | Which authority chains were validated? |
| **Findings published** | Named dependency integrity findings with lawful dispositions | Which findings were published? |
| **Boundaries intact** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden overclaims / mutations / reinterpretations did not occur | Which constitutional boundaries remained intact? |

Dependency Integrity Completion Explainability is:

- **fulfilment-centred** — it narrates why CDIC criteria hold (or why close is refused);
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CDI / CDIL / CDIC identities where applicable;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published specifications, named relationships, authority chains, and named findings;
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to create authority or amend corpora;
- **non-mutating** — explanation never narrates completion as edge rewrite or layer absorption;
- **finding-honest** — explanation never silently upgrades published findings;
- **quality-honest** — complete is never narrated as mastery, readiness, or educational quality;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence / validation speech, Programme IX evaluative speech, Programme X execution speech, MS001 dependency speech, or MS002 lifecycle speech;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match CDIC reconstruction needs;
- **authority-honest** — explanations must not paper over authority inference or stack canonisation.

Dependency Integrity Completion Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–X / WS1 meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a constitutional authority instrument or amendment warrant;
- a silent dependency-rewrite or finding-upgrade mechanism;
- a Programme IX evaluative-finish report as constitutional law;
- a substitute for WS1 / MS001 dependency explainability or WS1 / MS002 lifecycle explainability (compose with; do not replace);
- a substitute for Programme IX CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC completion explainability (compose with; do not collapse);
- a rendering system, UI template, CI badge, graph visualiser, or analytics dashboard;
- a claim that clear completion speech guarantees learning, a pass, conformity, constitutional amendment, or edge rewrite;
- a licence to present package graphs, lint scores, ticket graphs, or pipeline greens as constitutional proof or educational quality.

## 3. Mandatory Explanation Questions

Every material completion judgement (affirm or refuse close) should answer all four questions:

### CDICQ-01 — Which dependencies were assessed?

Name the primary constitutional dependency relationship identities evaluated under the assessment concern — including applicable CDI-01…CDI-07 categories and direction / cycle / layer outcomes — or name which required dependencies remain unevaluated when refusing close. Never narrate invented edges as lawful assessment subjects. “The corpus was scanned somewhere” is not a constitutional dependency identity.

### CDICQ-02 — Which authority chains were validated?

Name the primary authority-chain identities and direction outcomes validated under the concern — or name which required chains remain unvalidated when refusing close. Include lawful failed / deferred / escalated authority outcomes honestly. Never narrate invented superior links as validated chains, and never narrate reversed authorship as lawful direction. “Looks authoritative enough” is not a constitutional authority-chain identity.

### CDICQ-03 — Which findings were published?

Name the primary dependency integrity finding identities and dispositions published under the concern (intact / violated / deferred / escalated) — or name which required findings remain unpublished when refusing close. Never narrate a violated / deferred / escalated finding as upgraded by completion, and never narrate a silent replacement of a prior lawful finding. “Looks intact enough” is not a constitutional finding identity.

### CDICQ-04 — Which constitutional boundaries remained intact?

Confirm the hard stops in `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md` that were checked — especially non-creation of constitutional authority, non-alteration of dependency relationships, non-reinterpretation of constitutional meaning, non-amendment of constitutional specifications, implementation neutrality, repeatability honesty, and non-equation of completion with educational success, Programme IX evaluative completion, or constitutional truth.

## 5. Audience Rules

| Audience | May hear | Must not hear as educational law |
|----------|----------|-----------------------------------|
| **Student** | Plain speech that a dependency check finished (or did not), without syllabus rewrite claims | “You’re exam-ready,” “the law changed,” “this stack is now the syllabus,” “everything is permanently intact forever” |
| **Developer** | Named corpus identities, CDI / CDIL / CDIC IDs, dependency / authority-chain / finding refs, refuse reasons | “Ship because assessment-complete,” “rewrite edges in main to match the badge,” “freeze this framework as constitutional” |
| **Auditor** | Reconstructable CDIC-01…CDIC-05 outcomes, CDICQ answers, boundary preservation, continuity honesty | Mute “complete” badges, invented edges, invented authority chains, silent finding upgrades |

Student-facing and auditor-facing projections **must share one constitutional truth**. Vocabulary may differ; facts may not.

## 6. Forbidden Explanations

The following explanation patterns are constitutionally defective:

- “Assessment complete, therefore constitutional authority now resides in the scanner / pipeline / completion badge.”
- “Assessment complete, therefore we rewrote the dependency graph to remove cycles.”
- “Assessment complete, therefore Programme VI / EIP meaning now means X.”
- “Assessment complete, therefore constitutional specifications are amended.”
- “Assessment complete, therefore the student is exam-ready / mastered / succeeded.”
- “Assessment complete, therefore the implementation is conformant / verified / compliant / certified.”
- “Assessment complete because CI was green / the package graph looked acyclic / the ticket closed.”
- “Authority was validated somehow” without named chain identity.
- “Findings were published somehow” without named finding identity and disposition.
- Silent upgrade of violated / deferred / escalated findings into intact under close narration.
- Narrating a preferred stack, language, framework, or vendor as constitutional law because assessment closed.
- Collapsing CDIC completion into CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC completion (or vice versa) without distinction.
