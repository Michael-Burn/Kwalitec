Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

> **“How are constitutional dependencies defined, validated and constrained so that the constitutional corpus remains acyclic, traceable and authoritative?”**

That answer must validate dependency direction, validate authority traceability, detect circular dependencies, preserve constitutional layering, and produce dependency integrity findings — without creating constitutional authority, reinterpreting constitutional law, amending constitutional specifications, or modifying constitutional dependencies.

This document records that posture so every future dependency assessment (and successor) has a single constitutional reference for *how the corpus may remain lawfully related without becoming a second constitution*.

> **The Constitutional Dependency Integrity Model describes how constitutional dependencies are validated throughout the published corpus.  
> It does not create educational meaning, invent ownership, mint tips, invent runtime behaviour, amend law, or become constitutional authority.**

## 2. What Constitutional Dependency Integrity Is

**Constitutional Dependency Integrity** is the binding mechanism by which **lawful dependency relationships** throughout the published constitutional corpus may be **defined, validated, and constrained**, yielding **dependency integrity findings** that preserve acyclicity, authority traceability, constitutional layering, and explainability — without becoming a source of constitutional law, a rewriter of dependencies, or a substitute for upstream programme meaning.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Published constitutional corpus** | Constitution, EIP, Programme VI–X specifications and their subordinate workstream / milestone corpora | What corpus is under dependency assessment? |
| **Constitutional dependency** | A declared or implied lawful subordination / composition / consumption relationship between published corpus identities | Which relationship is being assessed? |
| **Dependency direction** | The lawful orientation from superior authority toward subordinate consumer (never the reverse as authorship) | Does the edge point the right way? |
| **Authority chain** | Reconstructable path from Constitution / EIP through programme layers to the assessed dependency | Which authority supports this edge? |
| **Cycle** | A closed path of dependencies that would make authority circular or layering ambiguous | Was circularity detected? |
| **Constitutional layering** | The published order: Constitution / EIP → VI → VII → VIII → IX → X → XI (and lawful subordinate workstreams within each) | Is layering preserved? |
| **Dependency integrity finding** | Lawful disposition: intact / violated / deferred / escalated — never “new law” and never a rewrite of edges | Does the dependency remain lawful? |
| **Constitutional authority** | The exclusive right of published corpora to define educational and execution meaning | Who owns the standard? |
| **Implementation neutrality** | Dependency validation speaks to constitutional identities, not to a preferred stack as law | Remains the stack replaceable? |
| **Auditability** | Every material assessment leaves reconstructable constitutional traces | Can the judgement be reconstructed? |
| **Explainability** | Dependencies, authority chains, cycles, findings, and boundaries are speakable | Can the judgement be narrated honestly? |

Constitutional Dependency Integrity is:

- **validation-centred** — it validates published dependency relationships; it never authors or rewires them;
- **direction-bound** — lawful edges respect published superior → subordinate orientation;
- **authority-preserving** — every material dependency remains reconstructable to a published authority chain;
- **acyclic** — circular dependencies that invert or confuse constitutional authority are hard defects;
- **layering-preserving** — Programme and workstream layering remains as published;
- **catalogue-closed** — only CDI-01…CDI-07 may be applied as constitutional dependency categories;
- **corpus-bound** — every material assessment cites identifiable published specifications;
- **finding-producing** — assessments emit intact / violated / deferred / escalated dispositions;
- **audit-capable** — every material assessment leaves reconstructable constitutional traces;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which dependency was assessed under which authority chain with which findings;
- **non-legislative** — a dependency finding never becomes constitutional law or a soft amendment;
- **non-mutating** — the model never modifies constitutional specifications or dependency relationships under validation pretext;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by dependency law.

Constitutional Dependency Integrity is **not**:

- a second Educational Constitution, EIP, or Programme VI–X amendment path;
- a licence to reinterpret coach meaning, ownership maps, tip warrants, runtime contracts, conformance criteria, verification meaning, compliance meaning, certification meaning, evolution meaning, or execution meaning in dependency prose;
- a testing framework, CI/CD pipeline, GitHub Action, package-dependency scanner, or OpenAPI linker;
- a claim that “dependency intact” guarantees learning, mastery, exam readiness, product success, perpetual conformity, or frozen stack authority;
- a substitute for Programme IX MS002 constitutional traceability — CT preserves spec/artefact/finding lineage; CDI validates *constitutional corpus dependency integrity*;
- a substitute for Programme IX conformance / verification / compliance / certification — those evaluate implementations and obligations; CDI validates *corpus dependency structure*;
- a substitute for Programme X execution / decision / audit / explainability — those organise constitutional activity; CDI never executes, decides, audits, or explains educational outcomes in their place;
- a licence to create, reverse, invent, or delete constitutional dependencies under “integrity” pretext.

## 3. Dependency Integrity Posture (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material constitutional dependency integrity assessment travels through three constitutional phases. These are **normative assessment obligations**, not an implementation architecture.

```
Identify published dependency relationships under assessment
        │
        ▼
Validate direction, authority chain, acyclicity, and layering under CDI-01…CDI-07
        │
        ├── violated / deferred / escalated (lawful finding — corpus edges unchanged)
        │
        └── intact → Emit findings and preserve audit records
                │
                ▼
        Explain dependency / authority chain / cycles / findings / boundaries
```

| Phase | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **Identify** | Name only published constitutional corpus identities and declared / lawfully implied dependency relationships | Relationship is unpublished tribal knowledge, invented edge, or “how main cites today” as law |
| **Validate** | Apply CDI-01…CDI-07 for direction, authority traceability, cycle detection, and layering | Assessment would invent law, rewrite edges, reverse authority, or privilege a stack as law |
| **Record** | Produce findings and audit-speakable trails without amending corpora or mutating dependencies | Path would treat findings as constitutional amendments or rewrite the dependency graph |

> **Dependency integrity never “best-effort invents” a fourth phase called law-making, edge rewriting, or stack canonisation.**

## 6. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Dependency Integrity Model exists so that:

1. **Authority remains acyclic** — no subordinate corpus may become the superior of its own superior through circular citation theatre.
2. **Direction remains honest** — dependency edges point from published authority toward lawful consumers, never invent authorship by reverse citation.
3. **Traceability of authority remains reconstructable** — every material dependency can name its supporting authority chain.
4. **Layering remains intact** — Constitution / EIP and Programmes VI–X retain published order through Programme XI validation.
5. **Readiness remains honest** — integration and readiness work cannot pretend circular or inverted corpora are “ready.”
6. **Transparency remains intact** — dependency assessments are speakable; silent green graphs are not constitutional proof.
7. **Students remain protected** — product urgency cannot launder unlawful corpus structure as “approved architecture.”
