## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “import graph green” or “docs cite each other somehow.” The tutor optimises **honest dependency integrity**: only relationships that respect published direction, reconstructable authority chains, acyclicity, and constitutional layering may be called intact — without rewriting those relationships so a readiness report can pass.

These objectives bind every constitutional dependency integrity assessment and every successor assessment machinery that claims constitutional dependency integrity meaning.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### CDIO-01 — Preserve acyclic dependencies

**Definition.** Ensure that every material dependency integrity assessment determines — for named published corpus identities under recognised CDI-01…CDI-07 categories — whether dependency relationships remain free of cycles that invert, confuse, or circularise constitutional authority, or honestly reports violated / deferred / escalated when acyclicity cannot be confirmed.

**Includes:**

- Applying the closed CDI catalogue required by published law for the assessment scope.
- Binding every material assessment to identifiable published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–X specifications and subordinate workstream / milestone corpora.
- Detecting closed dependency paths that would make a subordinate corpus the practical superior of its own superior.
- Preferring lawful violated / deferred / escalated findings over rewriting edges so readiness “passes.”
- Keeping acyclicity distinct from educational quality, mastery, readiness theatre, product success, or constitutional amendment.
- Preserving the Model’s posture: findings validate structure; they do not become law or rewire the corpus.

**Excludes:**

- Treating green package scanners, import graphs, ticket links, Twin estimates, Adaptive scores, or UI ticks as constitutional acyclicity.
- Soft-amending specifications, inventing unpublished customs, reversing edges, or privileging “how main cites” to force intact status.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for dependency integrity.
- Declaring “close enough” acyclicity without documenting a corpus amendment.
- Treating mutual citation between peers as automatic proof of lawful composition when authority direction is unclear — peer composition must remain as published, never as circular authorship.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software pretends circular constitutional authority is fine because integration needs a green path. Acyclicity honesty is care; dependency theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CDIO-02 — Preserve authority traceability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material dependency can be traced along a reconstructable authority chain from Constitution / EIP through the relevant Programme layer(s) to the assessed edge — so origin of the relationship is reconstructable.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who cannot say *which superior rule* yesterday’s note obeyed cannot be trusted. Dependencies without authority chains are educational amnesia.

**Manifestations:**

- Findings cite corpus paths and specification identities, not only ticket IDs or commit SHAs.
- Authority chains name published superior → intermediate → subordinate identities without inventing missing links.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing prior lawful dependency assessment history to simplify the next report.
- Missing specification identity, missing edge identity, or missing authority-chain link is a defect.
- Programme IX CT lineage may support reconstruction; CDI never treats CT records as a licence to invent superior law.

---

### CDIO-03 — Preserve constitutional layering

**Definition.** Ensure that dependency assessments honour the published constitutional order — Constitution / EIP above Programme VI, VI above VII, VII above VIII, VIII above IX, IX above X, X above XI, and lawful workstream / milestone subordination within each programme — so no subordinate horizon silently absorbs a superior’s authority.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who lets yesterday’s worksheet rewrite today’s syllabus has abandoned institutional order for convenience.

**Manifestations:**

- Edges that place a lower programme as author of a higher programme’s meaning are hard defects.
- Workstream / milestone corpora remain subordinate to their published programme models.
- Sibling composition within a layer is permitted only as published; it never collapses distinct horizons into one mega-law.
- Forbidden actions in `DEPENDENCY_BOUNDARIES.md` are hard stops.
- Layering preservation never authorises inventing a new layer outside published programmes.

---

### CDIO-04 — Preserve implementation readiness

**Definition.** Ensure that dependency integrity findings support honest Programme XI integration and readiness judgements — so readiness claims consume intact dependency structure rather than inventing readiness despite circular, inverted, or untraceable corpora.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who declares a student “exam-ready” while the study plan cites itself in circles is performing readiness theatre, not care.

**Manifestations:**

- Intact findings may inform downstream readiness; they never themselves certify educational readiness, mastery, or product success.
- Violated / deferred / escalated findings block honest readiness claims that depend on the broken structure.
- Readiness proxies (CI greens, coverage, scanner scores) never redefine CDIO-01…CDIO-05.
- Implementation readiness here means *constitutional corpus readiness for lawful integration* — not student exam readiness and not Runtime A deployment theatre.
- Preserving readiness never authorises rewriting dependencies so a release can ship.

---

### CDIO-05 — Preserve dependency transparency

**Definition.** Ensure that every material dependency integrity assessment leaves a reconstructable, speakable trail: which dependency was assessed, which authority chain supports it, whether cycles were detected, which findings were produced, and which boundaries were preserved.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later say *why* a relationship was accepted cannot be trusted. Silent green graphs destroy student and auditor trust.

**Manifestations:**

- Audit records preserve constitutional references, not only technical scanner logs.
- Explainability obligations in `DEPENDENCY_EXPLAINABILITY.md` apply to all material assessments.
- Missing CDI identity, missing corpus refs, missing authority-chain note, missing cycle status, or missing finding disposition is a defect.
- Student / developer / auditor projections share one truth with different vocabulary.
- Transparency never authorises publishing dependency findings as new constitutional law.

---

### CDIO-06 — Preserve repeatability and neutrality

**Definition.** Ensure that the same published corpus identities, the same declared dependency relationships, and the same published CDI criteria yield the same dependency disposition (intact / violated / deferred / escalated) — independently of programming language, framework, datastore, CI vendor, cloud topology, or preferred Runtime A shape.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who treats the same syllabus map as lawful or not depending on which reviewer woke first — or which notebook brand is fashionable — is not coaching; they are gambling.

**Manifestations:**

- Assessment classification and outcomes are corpus-bound and reproducible.
- Parallel CI runners, sharding, and cache races must not change constitutional disposition.
- Explicit multi-type CDI sets use published conjunction / published stop rules, not random reviewer races.
- “Eventually consistent dependency meaning” is a constitutional defect.
- Equivalent lawful documentation topologies under the same published law may both be intact; no stack becomes law by proximity.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CDIO-01 conflicts with “helping” circular corpora look ready | Acyclicity honesty wins — violated / deferred / escalated |
| CDIO-02 conflicts with log volume reduction that erases authority chains | Authority traceability wins |
| CDIO-03 conflicts with soft-absorbing a superior programme into a subordinate | Constitutional layering wins |
| CDIO-04 conflicts with shipping despite broken dependency structure | Honest readiness wins — refuse false readiness |
| CDIO-05 conflicts with opaque “trust us” graph badges | Dependency transparency wins |
| CDIO-06 conflicts with race-dependent reviewer outcomes or stack privilege | Repeatability and neutrality win |

No supporting objective authorises violating CDIO-01.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — Constitution / EIP integrity obligations and Programme VI–X authority preservation for the dependency integrity horizon.

---

## 5. Non-Objectives

Constitutional dependency integrity does **not** optimise for:

- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- CI throughput, flaky-test suppression, or coverage theatre as educational success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when specifications or declared relationships are incomplete;
- collapsing CDI categories into one mega-check that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics / package-scanner signals as substitutes for published constitutional relationships;
- rewriting, inventing, or deleting constitutional dependencies so graphs look tidy;
- replacing Programme IX CT lineage preservation or Programme IX–X evaluative / execution horizons;
- treating documentation as optional commentary on whatever code already shipped.

## 6. Closing Statement

> **An assessment that becomes “intact” by rewriting constitutional dependencies is a failed educational system.  
> An assessment that validates only published relationships — with acyclicity, authority traceability, layering, readiness honesty, transparency, and repeatability intact — is doing its only dependency-integrity job.**
