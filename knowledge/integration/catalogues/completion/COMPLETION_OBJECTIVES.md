## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “scanner greens,” “tickets closed,” or “prefix maps shown.” The tutor optimises **honest closing of constitutional catalogue assessment work**: when the published lifecycle for a concern has lawfully concluded, catalogue families have been assessed, namespaces and identifiers validated, numbering and extensibility verified, findings published, and the assessment formally closed, the tutor can say so — without pretending new catalogue identifiers were created, without renumbering yesterday’s catalogues so the report looks tidy, without pretending the student has mastered the topic, and without amending the syllabus because the machinery stopped cleanly.

These objectives bind every constitutional catalogue integrity completion judgement and every successor assessment machinery that claims catalogue assessment fulfilment.

## 2. Primary Objective

### CCICO-01 — Confirm lawful assessment completion

**Definition.** Ensure that runtime / assessment / audit speech affirms catalogue integrity completion only when a published assessment concern under WS2 / MS001–MS002 has concluded the required CCIL-01…CCIL-07 lifecycle obligations — or honestly refuses close when fulfilment criteria are not met.

**Includes:**

- Assessing only concerns that lawfully opened under WS2 / MS001–MS002 initiation → discovery → namespace validation → identifier validation → numbering validation → finding construction → publication law.
- Affirming completion only when `COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md` conditions (CCIC-01…CCIC-06) hold.
- Preferring lawful “not yet” / refuse-close over inventing a “done enough” status for product convenience.
- Distinguishing lawful stop dispositions (refuse / defer / escalate / violated as published outcomes that exhaust remaining *assessment* duties with honest recording) from abandoned lifecycle duties.
- Binding every material completion judgement to reconstructable constitutional references.

**Excludes:**

- Declaring complete because a CI job exited, a ticket closed, a string scan glowed, or a UI spinner stopped.
- Treating Twin / Adaptive / Experience / Mission heuristics as completion warrants.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for assessment honesty.
- Collapsing “catalogue assessment complete” into “identifiers created,” “catalogues renumbered,” “specifications amended,” “constitutional authority created,” or “student succeeded.”

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software celebrates unfinished catalogue assessment — or celebrates finished scanner machinery as finished identifier creation or renumbering. Honest confirmation is care; completion theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CCICO-02 — Preserve catalogue integrity

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming completion never invents, renames, renumbers, invents-in-place, or silently rewrites published constitutional catalogue identifiers — and never upgrades a violated / deferred / escalated finding into “intact” under close theatre.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who “helpfully” renumbers a colliding prefix so the assessment can finish has abandoned educational honesty even if the completion UX looks clean.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion leaves named catalogue / family identities and dispositions reconstructable.
- Close never substitutes string scanners, ticket states, or vocabulary maps for published catalogue findings.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing or silently rewriting prior lawful catalogue dispositions to simplify the next report.
- Creating or renumbering identifiers under “completion cleanup” is a defect.

---

### CCICO-03 — Preserve namespace consistency

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming completion never silently merges distinct catalogue families / prefixes, invents unpublished disambiguation, or treats overlapping labels as harmless cosmetics — so every closed assessment remains reconstructable to published namespace separation rules.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later say which family owned which prefix cannot be trusted. Completion that merges namespaces is educational amnesia dressed as done.

**Manifestations:**

- Affirmed closes leave namespace-validation identities reconstructable with family identities and findings.
- Silent merges, invented disambiguations, and prefix theatre as substitutes for CCIL-03 honesty are defects.
- Continuity forbids deleting prior lawful collision disclosures to “clean up” completion.
- Incomplete namespace walks for a required concern scope block affirmation.

---

### CCICO-04 — Preserve identifier uniqueness

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming completion never invents uniqueness, redefines dual meanings as “close enough,” or renumbers IDs in situ so collisions disappear — so every closed assessment remains honest about uniqueness and consistency under published CCI-01 / CCI-02 law.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who quietly assigns new codes so yesterday’s collision vanishes has rewritten the syllabus under completion pretext.

**Manifestations:**

- Affirmed closes leave uniqueness / consistency dispositions reconstructable.
- Invented uniqueness, silent dual meanings, and in-situ renumbering are defects.
- Continuity forbids erasing prior lawful uniqueness defects to tidy the close.
- Missing identifier validation after an affirmed close is a defect.

---

### CCICO-05 — Preserve assessment repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming completion preserves the property that the same published corpora, same discovered catalogues, and same stage sequence yield the same completion disposition for the same concern scope — so close never becomes a one-off theatre event.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who cannot reproduce yesterday’s “complete” from the same published inputs cannot be trusted. Non-repeatable close is luck, not care.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion dispositions cite published inputs, not ephemeral scanner state alone.
- Contradictory closes from identical published inputs are defects.
- Continuity forbids erasing prior lawful completion judgements to invent a cleaner story.
- Stack / vendor / CI differences alone must not flip disposition when constitutional inputs are identical.

---

### CCICO-06 — Preserve explainability and implementation neutrality

**Definition.** Ensure that every material completion judgement can answer the mandatory explainability questions — which families, which namespace validations, which identifier validations, which findings, which boundaries — without redefining constitutional meaning, creating identifiers, renumbering catalogues, amending specifications, or implying conformity / educational success; and that judgements speak independently of programming language, framework, datastore, CI vendor, or preferred Runtime A shape.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who can only say “it’s fully catalogued somewhere” — or who declares only one notebook brand “fully complete” — is performing, not teaching.

**Manifestations:**

- CCICQ-01…CCICQ-05 answers are producible for every material affirmed or refused close.
- Explanations never soft-amend law or narrate completion as identifier creation, renumbering, authority, conformity, or certification.
- CCIC criteria speak to constitutional identities and obligations, not “must use Flask / SQLAlchemy / OpenAPI.”
- Equivalent lawful implementations under the same published law may both reach lawful completion.
- Opaque “trust the complete badge” theatre is a defect.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CCICO-01 conflicts with inventing unpublished closes so assessment can complete | Lawful-assessment honesty wins — refuse / defer / escalate |
| CCICO-02 conflicts with renumbering IDs so close looks green | Catalogue integrity preservation wins |
| CCICO-03 conflicts with silently merging colliding prefixes | Namespace consistency wins |
| CCICO-04 conflicts with inventing uniqueness under close theatre | Identifier uniqueness wins |
| CCICO-05 conflicts with one-off demo closes | Assessment repeatability wins |
| CCICO-06 conflicts with unspeakable “trust us” completion or stack canonisation | Explainability and implementation neutrality win |
| Any CCICO conflicts with creating identifiers / renumbering catalogues / amending specifications / creating authority / implying educational success | Non-mutation / non-implication honesty wins — refuse the overclaim |

No supporting objective authorises violating CCICO-01.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — Constitution / EIP integrity obligations, Programme VI–X authority preservation, WS2 / MS001 CCIO-01…CCIO-05, and WS2 / MS002 CCILO duties for the *completion* horizon.

## 5. Non-Objectives

Constitutional catalogue integrity completion does **not** optimise for:

- creating, renaming, renumbering, inventing, or deleting constitutional catalogue identifiers;
- constitutional authority creation, soft amendment, or “complete therefore new law” theatre;
- verification success, test-pass rates, coverage theatre, or implementation-correctness judgement;
- certification of releases, stacks, vendors, or implementations;
- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- CI throughput, flaky-scan suppression, or demo continuity as educational or constitutional success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when required assessment duties are incomplete;
- collapsing CCIC criteria into one mega-flag that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics signals as substitutes for published completion criteria;
- treating documentation links as optional commentary on whatever vocabulary already shipped;
- declaring conformity, verification, certification, educational success, identifier creation, renumbering, or constitutional amendment solely because completion was affirmed;
- substituting for Programme XI / WS1 CDIC dependency assessment completion.

## 6. Closing Statement

> **A concern that becomes “catalogue assessment complete” by creating identifiers, renumbering catalogues, amending constitutional law, or creating constitutional authority is a failed educational system.  
> A concern that confirms only that the published catalogue assessment lifecycle concluded — with catalogue integrity, namespace consistency, identifier uniqueness, repeatability, explainability, and neutrality intact — is doing its only completion job.**
