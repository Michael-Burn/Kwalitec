## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which catalogue families* were assessed — which namespace validations were completed, which identifier validations were completed, which findings were published, and which limits remained intact — is not closing honestly; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–X have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, governance, and execution speech speakable, and after WS2 / MS001–MS002 have made catalogue and lifecycle speech speakable, Programme XI still needs one catalogue-assessment-completion-speech answer:

> **“Which catalogue families were assessed, which namespace validations were completed, which identifier validations were completed, which findings were published, and which constitutional boundaries remained intact?”**

That answer must cover every material affirmed (or refused) completion without inventing educational success, creating catalogue identifiers, renumbering constitutional catalogues, reinterpreting catalogue meanings, amending constitutional specifications, creating constitutional authority, transferring ownership, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, or freezing a stack as law.

## 2. What Catalogue Integrity Completion Explainability Is

**Constitutional Catalogue Integrity Completion Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **fulfilment (or lawful refusal of fulfilment) of the required constitutional catalogue integrity assessment lifecycle** — not to invent a second tutor voice from CI complete badges, and not to grade the student, create catalogue identifiers, renumber catalogues, amend constitutional specifications, or create constitutional authority.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Catalogue families assessed** | Named CCF-01…CCF-07 family memberships and published catalogue identities assessed under the concern | Which catalogue families were assessed? |
| **Namespace validations completed** | Named family / prefix / collision validations under CCI / CCIL namespace law | Which namespace validations were completed? |
| **Identifier validations completed** | Named uniqueness / consistency (and, where material, numbering / extensibility) validations | Which identifier validations were completed? |
| **Findings published** | Named catalogue integrity findings with lawful dispositions | Which findings were published? |
| **Boundaries intact** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden overclaims / mutations / renumberings did not occur | Which constitutional boundaries remained intact? |

Catalogue Integrity Completion Explainability is:

- **fulfilment-centred** — it narrates why CCIC criteria hold (or why close is refused);
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CCI / CCF / CCIL / CCIC identities where applicable;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published specifications, named catalogues / families, validations, and named findings;
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to create authority or amend corpora;
- **non-mutating** — explanation never narrates completion as identifier creation or catalogue renumbering;
- **finding-honest** — explanation never silently upgrades published findings;
- **quality-honest** — complete is never narrated as mastery, readiness, or educational quality;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence / validation speech, Programme IX evaluative speech, Programme X execution speech, WS1 dependency speech, MS001 catalogue speech, or MS002 lifecycle speech;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match CCIC reconstruction needs;
- **authority-honest** — explanations must not paper over authority inference, renumbering, or stack canonisation.

Catalogue Integrity Completion Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–X / WS1 / WS2 meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a constitutional authority instrument or amendment warrant;
- a silent identifier-creation, renumbering, or finding-upgrade mechanism;
- a Programme IX evaluative-finish report as constitutional law;
- a substitute for WS2 / MS001 catalogue explainability or WS2 / MS002 lifecycle explainability (compose with; do not replace);
- a substitute for Programme IX CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC completion explainability (compose with; do not collapse);
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS1 CDIC completion explainability (compose with; do not collapse);
- a rendering system, UI template, CI badge, prefix visualiser, or analytics dashboard;
- a claim that clear completion speech guarantees learning, a pass, conformity, constitutional amendment, identifier creation, or renumbering;
- a licence to present string scanners, lint scores, ticket graphs, or pipeline greens as constitutional proof or educational quality.

## 3. Mandatory Explanation Questions

Every material completion judgement (affirm or refuse close) should answer all five questions:

### CCICQ-01 — Which catalogue families were assessed?

Name the primary CCF-01…CCF-07 family memberships and published catalogue identities assessed under the assessment concern — including applicable discovery / classification outcomes from CCIL-02 and numbering / extensibility outcomes from CCIL-05 where material — or name which required families / catalogues remain unassessed when refusing close. Never narrate invented families or renumbered catalogues as lawful assessment subjects. “The corpus was scanned somewhere” is not a constitutional catalogue-family identity.

### CCICQ-02 — Which namespace validations were completed?

Name the primary namespace-validation identities and collision / separation outcomes completed under the concern (especially CCIL-03 / CCI-03 / CCI-04) — or name which required namespace validations remain incomplete when refusing close. Include lawful failed / deferred / escalated namespace outcomes honestly. Never narrate silent merges as validated separation, and never narrate invented disambiguations as published namespace law. “Looks namespaced enough” is not a constitutional namespace-validation identity.

### CCICQ-03 — Which identifier validations were completed?

Name the primary uniqueness / consistency validation identities and outcomes completed under the concern (especially CCIL-04 / CCI-01 / CCI-02) — and, where material for honest speech, numbering / extensibility validation outcomes from CCIL-05 / CCI-05 / CCI-06 — or name which required identifier validations remain incomplete when refusing close. Never narrate invented uniqueness, dual meanings redefined in situ, created identifiers, or renumbered IDs as lawful validation. “Looks unique enough” is not a constitutional identifier-validation identity.

### CCICQ-04 — Which findings were published?

Name the primary catalogue integrity finding identities and dispositions published under the concern (intact / violated / deferred / escalated) — or name which required findings remain unpublished when refusing close. Never narrate a violated / deferred / escalated finding as upgraded by completion, and never narrate a silent replacement of a prior lawful finding. “Looks intact enough” is not a constitutional finding identity.

### CCICQ-05 — Which constitutional boundaries remained intact?

Confirm the hard stops in `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md` that were checked — especially non-creation of catalogue identifiers, non-renumbering of constitutional catalogues, non-reinterpretation of catalogue meanings, non-amendment of constitutional specifications, non-creation of constitutional authority, implementation neutrality, repeatability honesty, and non-equation of completion with educational success, Programme IX evaluative completion, WS1 dependency completion, or constitutional truth.
