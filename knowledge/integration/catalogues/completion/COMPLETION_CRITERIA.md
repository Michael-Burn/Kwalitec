## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor needs bright conditions for saying “this constitutional catalogue assessment work is finished” — and equally bright refusals when it is not. After WS2 / MS001 has named how catalogues are validated and WS2 / MS002 has named how assessments are organised, Programme XI Workstream 2 still needs one fulfilment checklist:

> **What must be true before constitutional speech may affirm that the required catalogue assessment lifecycle has concluded, catalogue families have been assessed, namespace and identifier validations completed, numbering and extensibility verified, findings published, and the assessment formally closed?**

These criteria answer that question. They are **normative constitutional conditions**, not database predicates, CI job statuses, ticket states, string-scan greens, or API status codes.

---

## 2. Criterion Catalogue

All material affirmed completions require **CCIC-01 through CCIC-06** to hold. Failure of any criterion ⇒ **not yet** or **refuse close**. Do not invent a partial “mostly complete” identifier-creation, renumbering, meaning-reinterpretation, educational, or constitutional status.

| ID | Criterion | Primary question |
|----|-----------|------------------|
| **CCIC-01** | Lifecycle completed | Were required CCIL-01…CCIL-07 stages completed for the concern? |
| **CCIC-02** | Catalogue assessment completed | Were required catalogue families assessed under published CCF law, including numbering and extensibility validation where required? |
| **CCIC-03** | Namespace validation completed | Was required namespace separation / collision honesty validated? |
| **CCIC-04** | Identifier validation completed | Was required identifier uniqueness and consistency validated? |
| **CCIC-05** | Findings publication completed | Were required catalogue integrity findings lawfully constructed and published? |
| **CCIC-06** | Formal assessment closure | Was the catalogue assessment concern formally closed under published boundary honesty? |

Optional supporting honesty checks (do not replace CCIC-01…CCIC-06):

| ID | Check | Primary question |
|----|-------|------------------|
| **CCIC-07** | Catalogue honesty | Were stages and evaluations typed under published CCIL-01…CCIL-07, CCI-01…CCI-07, and CCF-01…CCF-07 only? |
| **CCIC-08** | Continuity honesty | Was prior lawful assessment history preserved (EIP-005)? |
| **CCIC-09** | Non-claim honesty | Does the judgement refuse identifier-creation / renumbering / meaning-reinterpretation / specification-amendment / authority-creation / educational overclaim? |
| **CCIC-10** | Neutrality honesty | Does the judgement refuse stack / vendor canonisation as completion law? |
| **CCIC-11** | Repeatability honesty | Would the same published inputs yield the same completion disposition? |

## 3. Criterion Definitions

### CCIC-01 — Lifecycle completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every lifecycle stage required by the concern’s published WS2 / MS002 scope has been executed — or that a published refuse / defer / escalate disposition exhausted remaining *catalogue assessment* duties for that stage set with honest progression to finding construction / publication (or refuse-publish) and closure of what occurred.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Identifiable catalogue assessment concern opened under WS2 / MS001–MS002.
- Required CCIL-01…CCIL-07 stage identities for the concern scope.
- Stage traces / disposition records for each required stage (including lawful early stops).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed stage-fulfilment record naming which required stages completed.
- Or a lawful stop record naming which stages were refused / deferred / escalated and why remaining assessment duties are exhausted.

**Completion conditions.**

- Required stages map to published CCIL-01…CCIL-07 only.
- Mandatory initiation, discovery, namespace validation, identifier validation, numbering validation, finding construction, and publication obligations for the concern are not silently omitted.
- Lawful early stops still leave honest stage outcomes; silent disappearance of a concern is a defect.
- Refusal of “pipeline skipped validation” or “we know it ran” without stage identity as completion.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That stage completion creates catalogue identifiers or renumbers constitutional catalogues.
- That stage completion amends constitutional specifications or creates constitutional authority.
- That stage completion freezes a stack as law.
- That stage completion means the learner succeeded or educational readiness was achieved.
- That skipped stages may be invented or implied from CI theatre.
- That Programme IX conformity / verification / compliance / certification was performed under stage progress.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who declares catalogue assessment “finished” without confirming each required assessment step occurred has not finished lawful care — even if the desk looks tidy.

---

### CCIC-02 — Catalogue assessment completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every catalogue family assessment required by the concern’s published scope was completed under published CCF-01…CCF-07 families — including discovery and classification of named catalogues (CCIL-02) and numbering integrity / extensibility posture validation (CCIL-05 / CCI-05 / CCI-06) where required — or that a published refuse disposition exhausted remaining *catalogue assessment* duties when required catalogues could not be lawfully assessed without inventing families, renumbering ranges, or inventing-in-place entries.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Named published catalogue identities in scope.
- Recognised CCF-01…CCF-07 family classifications for those catalogues.
- Stage traces for CCIL-02 and CCIL-05 as required by concern scope.
- Published numbering / closure / extensibility rules under WS2 / MS001.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed catalogue-assessment record naming which catalogues were assessed under which published CCF families — including numbering / extensibility outcomes where required.
- Or a lawful refuse / defer / escalate record naming which required catalogues / families remain unassessed and why.

**Completion conditions.**

- Catalogue subjects are named (not “the corpus,” “the docs,” or vendor labels alone).
- Family classification cites published CCF-01…CCF-07 only; inventing families to force assessment is a hard stop.
- Discovery named only published catalogues — inventing catalogues or prefixes to force assessment is a hard stop.
- Numbering validation preserves published ranges, gaps, and succession; silent renumbering is a hard stop.
- Extensibility posture confirms amendment-only growth; invent-in-place entries are a hard stop.
- Refusal of string scanners, Twin confidence, or CI greens as substitutes for named catalogue family assessment.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That assessed catalogues were therefore created, renumbered, renamed, or deleted.
- That a particular stack, language, or framework is privileged as law.
- That product quality or educational quality was judged.
- That constitutional specifications were modified to match the assessment.
- That Programme IX CT lineage preservation equals catalogue family assessment fulfilment.
- That numbering validation alone equals whole-concern completion (numbering outcomes feed CCIC-02; they do not replace CCIC-01…CCIC-06).

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who claims “the catalogues were checked” without naming which families were assessed — and whether numbering and extensibility remained as published — has performed scanner theatre, not assessment fulfilment.

### CCIC-03 — Namespace validation completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every namespace-validation obligation required by the concern’s published scope has been lawfully completed under published CCI / CCIL law — confirming reconstructable family / prefix separation and collision honesty under CCI-03 / CCI-04 and CCIL-03 — without silently merging families, inventing unpublished disambiguation, or renumbering prefixes under validation pretext.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Discovered catalogue / family set under assessment (CCIC-02 / CCIL-02 outputs).
- Published namespace / prefix / disambiguation rules under WS2 / MS001–MS002 (especially CCIL-03 and CCI-03 / CCI-04 / relevant CCIR rules).
- Boundary confirmations that validation does not invent disambiguation or renumber prefixes.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed namespace-validation record naming which namespace rules were applied and which collisions were disclosed (or honestly failed validation) for which families.
- Or a lawful refuse / defer / escalate record naming which required namespace validations remain incomplete and why.

**Completion conditions.**

- Namespace validations cite published constitutional references and family / prefix identities, not unpublished tribal vocabulary maps.
- Overlapping labels across families are disclosed as collisions or published disambiguations — never silently merged.
- “Namespaces were checked somewhere” without named validation identity is not completion.
- Lawful failed / deferred / escalated namespace outcomes still satisfy *validation completion* when they are the honest outcome — completeness of *validation* is not completeness of *intact disposition*, and neither is identifier creation or renumbering.
- Invented disambiguations to force intact status are hard stops.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That validating a namespace creates catalogue identifiers or renumbers catalogues.
- That a validated namespace freezes the implementation, stack, vendor, or release as permanent constitutional law.
- That a failed / deferred / escalated namespace outcome may be silently erased so close can look green.
- That MS001 catalogue meaning was replaced, skipped, or rewritten by namespace validation.
- That Educational Validation Framework quality review is complete.
- That educational success or exam readiness follows from namespace validation.

**Tutor rationale.** Recording that a namespace was checked — including when collisions remain — is care. Inventing a disambiguation so the assessment can finish is fiction. Completeness of *namespace validation* is not creation of *catalogue identifiers*.

---

### CCIC-04 — Identifier validation completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every identifier-validation obligation required by the concern’s published scope has been lawfully completed under published CCI / CCIL law — confirming uniqueness and consistency under CCI-01 / CCI-02 and CCIL-04 — without inventing uniqueness, redefining dual meanings, creating identifiers, or renumbering IDs in situ.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Namespace-validated catalogue set under assessment (CCIC-03 / CCIL-03 outputs).
- Published meaning statements and uniqueness / consistency rules under WS2 / MS001–MS002 (especially CCIL-04 and CCI-01 / CCI-02).
- Collision status from CCIL-03 where material.
- Boundary confirmations that validation does not create or renumber identifiers.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed identifier-validation record naming which uniqueness / consistency validations were completed (or honestly failed) for which catalogues / identifiers.
- Or a lawful refuse / defer / escalate record naming which required identifier validations remain incomplete and why.

**Completion conditions.**

- Identifier subjects are named (not “everything looked unique”).
- Validation cites published constitutional specifications and CCI categories, not unpublished customs.
- Duplicate identifiers for distinct meanings without published disambiguation are hard defects — never silently “fixed” by renumbering under close.
- The same identifier retains one published meaning within its namespace; inventing dual meanings or redefining meaning in situ is a hard stop.
- “Identifiers were checked somewhere” without named validation identity is not completion.
- Lawful failed / deferred / escalated uniqueness outcomes still satisfy *validation completion* when they are the honest outcome — completeness of *validation* is not completeness of *intact disposition*, and neither is catalogue renumbering.
- Creating or renumbering identifiers to force intact status is a hard stop.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That validating identifiers creates new catalogue identifiers or renumbers existing ones.
- That a validated identifier freezes the implementation, stack, vendor, or release as permanent constitutional law.
- That a failed / deferred / escalated uniqueness outcome may be silently erased or “fixed” by renumbering so close can look green.
- That MS001 catalogue meaning was replaced, skipped, or rewritten by identifier validation.
- That Programme IX conformity / verification / compliance / certification was achieved by identifier validation.
- That the learner succeeded or is exam-ready.

**Tutor rationale.** Recording that uniqueness and consistency were checked — including when they fail — is care. Quietly issuing new codes so collisions vanish is syllabus rewriting dressed as completion.

### CCIC-05 — Findings publication completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every catalogue integrity finding required by the concern’s published scope has been lawfully constructed and published under published CCI / CCIL rules — including intact, violated, deferred, and escalated dispositions — without rewriting those dispositions into identifier creation, renumbering orders, meaning reinterpretation, specification amendment, or constitutional authority, and without inventing intact status or hiding collisions.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Family, namespace, identifier, and numbering dispositions under applicable CCI-01…CCI-07 types (CCIL-02…CCIL-05 outputs).
- Published finding-construction and publication rules under WS2 / MS001–MS002 (especially CCIL-06 / CCIL-07).
- Boundary confirmations that findings do not invent law, create IDs, or renumber catalogues.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Identifiable catalogue integrity finding identities with lawful dispositions, published under honesty rules.
- Or a lawful stop record naming why required findings could not be constructed / published without invention.

**Completion conditions.**

- Findings cite published constitutional references, named catalogues / families, and validated (or honestly failed) namespace / identifier / numbering outcomes.
- Dispositions remain within published vocabulary (intact / violated / deferred / escalated).
- “Assessment happened somewhere” without named finding identity and publication record is not completion.
- Lawful violated / deferred / escalated findings still satisfy *publication* when they are the honest outcome — completeness of *publication* is not completeness of *intact disposition*, and neither is constitutional amendment or renumbering.
- Publication never invents intact status, hides collisions, creates identifiers, renumbers catalogues, or freezes a stack as law.
- Continuity forbids silently replacing prior lawful findings under close theatre.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That publishing a finding creates catalogue identifiers, renumbers catalogues, amends constitutional specifications, or creates constitutional authority.
- That an intact finding freezes the implementation, stack, vendor, or release as permanent constitutional law.
- That a violated / deferred / escalated finding may be silently erased or upgraded so close can look green.
- That MS001 catalogue evaluation was replaced, skipped, or rewritten by publication.
- That Programme IX conformity / verification / compliance / certification was achieved by publication.
- That the learner succeeded or is exam-ready.

**Tutor rationale.** Publishing a hard catalogue disposition is not rewriting the syllabus — but omitting a required finding while claiming the assessment finished is dishonest. Completeness of *findings publication* is not completeness of *catalogue change*.

---

### CCIC-06 — Formal assessment closure

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that the catalogue assessment concern was formally closed under published CCIL-07 / boundary honesty — exhausting remaining *catalogue assessment lifecycle* duties without inventing catalogue identifiers, renumbering constitutional catalogues, reinterpreting catalogue meanings, amending constitutional specifications, creating constitutional authority, or implying educational success.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Published findings and assessment records (CCIC-01…CCIC-05).
- CCIL-07 closure / publication honesty rules and `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md` hard stops.
- Explicit non-claim confirmations (identifier creation / renumbering / meaning reinterpretation / specification amendment / authority creation / educational success).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Formal close disposition: affirmed complete / not yet / refuse close / defer / escalate.
- Boundary preservation record confirming forbidden overclaims did not occur.

**Completion conditions.**

- Closure occurs only after required stages, catalogue family assessment, namespace validation, identifier validation, findings publication, and assessment-record obligations for the concern are satisfied — or after a published stop exhausts remaining assessment duties with honest recording.
- Closure speech answers CCICQ-01…CCICQ-05 or explicitly refuses mute theatre.
- Closure never silently upgrades findings dispositions, creates identifiers, or renumbers catalogues.
- Refusal of ticket closure, PR merge, string-scan greens, or pipeline greens alone as formal constitutional close.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That formal close creates catalogue identifiers or renumbers constitutional catalogues.
- That formal close reinterprets catalogue meanings or Programme VI–X / EIP meaning.
- That formal close amends constitutional specifications or creates constitutional authority.
- That formal close means the implementation is constitutionally conformant, verified, compliant, or certified.
- That formal close means the student succeeded or is exam-ready.
- That formal close freezes a stack, vendor, or release as permanent constitutional law.

**Tutor rationale.** Closing the assessment folder is care only when the required steps, family assessments, namespace and identifier validations, findings, and records exist — and only when nobody pretends the close is a new catalogue registry, a renumbering order, or a pass certificate for the candidate.

## 4. Criterion Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CCIC-01 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CCIC-02 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CCIC-03 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CCIC-04 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CCIC-05 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — mute complete badges are defects |
| CCIC-06 fails | Affirmation is unlawful even if earlier stages appear present |
| Optional CCIC-07…CCIC-11 fail | Treat as honesty defects; do not soft-pass CCIC-01…CCIC-06 |
| Any criterion “passes” only by creating identifiers / renumbering catalogues / reinterpreting meanings / amending specifications / creating authority / implying educational success | The implied overclaim voids lawful affirmation |

All of CCIC-01…CCIC-06 are **conjunctive** for material affirmed completion. There is no lawful “five of six” educational, fidelity, identifier-creation, or constitutional status.

Numbering and extensibility validation is **not** a seventh catalogue criterion. It is a required assessment obligation under CCIC-02 (and a required stage under CCIC-01 via CCIL-05). Affirming close without numbering / extensibility validation where the concern required it fails CCIC-01 and/or CCIC-02.

## 5. Lawful Stop Dispositions

Not every incomplete concern is a defect. Published WS2 / MS002 refuse / defer / escalate / violated outcomes may **exhaust remaining catalogue assessment duties** for a concern without affirming lifecycle fulfilment as “complete” in the celebratory sense — while still requiring honest recording and formal close of what occurred.

| Disposition | Constitutional meaning | May affirm CCIC completion? |
|-------------|------------------------|----------------------------|
| **Affirm complete** | CCIC-01…CCIC-06 hold for the concern | Yes |
| **Not yet** | Required stages, catalogue assessment, namespace / identifier validation, findings, or records remain outstanding | No |
| **Refuse close** | Affirmation would invent stages / findings, create IDs, renumber catalogues, invent authority, or overclaim | No |
| **Defer** | Published wait for upstream corpus / catalogue / family / namespace identity | No (unless published law says remaining assessment duties are exhausted) |
| **Escalate** | Published stop requiring human / corpus governance attention | No (unless published law says remaining assessment duties are exhausted) |

A lawful stop that exhausts assessment duties is **honest unfinished fulfilment or honest refusal** — never stealth identifier creation, catalogue renumbering, meaning reinterpretation, specification amendment, constitutional authority, educational success, or Programme IX evaluative disposition.

---

## 6. Non-Criteria

The following are **not** constitutional completion criteria:

- CI pipeline greens, flaky-scan suppression, or job exit codes;
- ticket closure, PR merge, or documentation checklist ticks alone;
- string scanners, prefix maps, or OpenAPI linkers alone;
- coverage percentages, lint scores, or elapsed time;
- Twin / Adaptive / Experience / Mission confidence signals;
- UI celebration states or analytics milestones;
- “looks unique enough for the demo”;
- MS001 intact disposition alone (that is evaluation outcome, not whole-concern CCIC fulfilment);
- Programme IX CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC completion alone (orthogonal implementation horizons);
- Programme XI / WS1 CDIC completion alone (orthogonal dependency assessment horizon);
- Educational Validation Framework quality release (orthogonal);
- any implication that constitutional specifications changed, identifiers were created, catalogues were renumbered, or authority was created because assessment closed.

---

## 7. Closing Statement

> **Constitutional catalogue integrity assessment is complete only when the required lifecycle is completed, catalogue assessment is completed, namespace validation is completed, identifier validation is completed, findings publication is completed, and the assessment is formally closed.  
> Satisfying CCIC-01…CCIC-06 never creates catalogue identifiers, renumbers constitutional catalogues, amends constitutional specifications, or creates constitutional authority.**
