## 1. Purpose

Assessment machinery that silently turns “the catalogue assessment finished” into “new identifiers now exist,” “yesterday’s catalogues are renumbered,” “this stack is now the superior vocabulary authority,” “student succeeded,” or “specifications are amended” destroys student trust and educational integrity.

This document draws a bright line between **lawful constitutional catalogue integrity completion** (Programme XI / WS2 / MS003) and **identifier authorship / catalogue renumbering / meaning reinterpretation / specification amendment / constitutional authority / educational judgement** owned elsewhere.

---

## 2. Boundary Principles

1. **Confirm assessment fulfilment, do not legislate.** Completion affirms fulfilment of published catalogue assessment lifecycle duties; it does not create constitutional law.
2. **Assessment only.** Identifier creation, catalogue renumbering, meaning reinterpretation, specification amendment, and constitutional authority creation are never inferred from close.
3. **Not identifier creation.** Close never creates, renames, invents, or deletes published catalogue identifiers.
4. **Not catalogue renumbering.** Close never renumbers ranges, prefixes, or catalogue entries under “cleanup” theatre.
5. **Not MS001 meaning replacement.** Completion judges fulfilment of CCI evaluation; it never redefines CCI categories, CCF families, or CCIR rules.
6. **Not MS002 lifecycle rewrite.** Completion judges fulfilment of CCIL stages; it never invents an eighth stage called law-making or renumbering.
7. **Not constitutional amendment.** Close never patches law or redefines constitutional meaning.
8. **Not meaning reinterpretation.** Programme VI questions, catalogue meanings, and EIP claim classes survive close intact.
9. **Not authority creation.** Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard after close.
10. **Exactly as published.** Close never redefines CCI / CCF / CCIL catalogues or soft-amends Programme VI–X meaning.
11. **Published criteria only.** Pipelines, tickets, string scanners, and elapsed time are not fulfilment law.
12. **Published records only.** Audit continuity is constitutional, not free-form log theatre.
13. **Reference findings honestly.** Published findings may be cited; closing never upgrades disposition into identifier creation or renumbering.
14. **Implementation neutrality.** No stack becomes law by proximity to a complete flag.
15. **Assessment-complete ≠ quality.** Completion never grades learning, mastery, readiness, or product success.
16. **Assessment-complete ≠ Programme IX evaluative close.** Completion never substitutes for CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC fulfilment.
17. **Assessment-complete ≠ WS1 dependency close.** Completion never substitutes for CDIC fulfilment.
18. **Explain the stop.** Students, developers, and auditors should hear when close must be refused.
19. **No emergency exemption.** Deadlines and demos never mint completion power, renumbering power, or amendment power.
20. **Repeatability survives close.** Close must not invent contradictory dispositions from identical published inputs.
21. **Continuity survives close.** EIP-005 forbids erasing prior lawful assessment history to tidy the report.

## 3. What Catalogue Integrity Completion May Do (Lawful)

| Lawful action | Constitutional meaning |
|---------------|------------------------|
| **Confirm assessment completion** | Affirm CCIC-01…CCIC-06 when published criteria hold for a lawful catalogue assessment concern |
| **Confirm publication** | Cite that required catalogue integrity findings were lawfully constructed and published under published CCI / CCIL rules — without rewriting or silently upgrading dispositions |
| **Confirm formal closure** | Affirm that the assessment concern was formally closed under published boundary honesty |
| **Preserve assessment records** | Leave reconstructable constitutional records of families, namespaces, identifiers, stages, findings, and boundaries |
| **Preserve audit continuity** | Leave reconstructable constitutional records of affirmed or refused close |
| **Refuse unlawful close** | Prefer honest not-yet / refuse / defer / escalate over improvisation |
| **Compose required stages and types** | Confirm fulfilment across published CCIL / CCI / CCF conjunctions without collapsing failed duties |
| **Remain implementation-neutral** | Honour completion law across lawful equivalent stacks |
| **Preserve repeatability** | Ensure same published inputs do not yield contradictory completion stories |
| **Explain completion** | Answer families / namespaces / identifiers / findings / boundaries without rewriting meaning |
| **Propose corpus amendments upstream** | Recommend (not enact) amendments when law itself must change before assessment can complete — including proposed new identifiers or renumbering via lawful amendment, never via close |

These actions **confirm catalogue assessment fulfilment**. They do **not** publish a new Constitution, invent coach warrants, mint tips, author ownership maps, invent CCI / CCF / CCIL / CCIC types, author runtime behaviour, create catalogue identifiers, renumber catalogues, create constitutional authority, reinterpret catalogue meanings, amend constitutional specifications, finish Programme IX evaluative horizons as constitutional law, or judge educational quality.

## 4. What Catalogue Integrity Completion Must NEVER Do

| Forbidden action | Why | Lawful alternative |
|------------------|-----|--------------------|
| **Create catalogue identifiers** | Only owning published catalogues may add entries by lawful amendment | Cite findings honestly; propose upstream amendment; never mint IDs under close |
| **Renumber constitutional catalogues** | Published numbering is constitutional identity, not draft notes | Report numbering defects; never “fix” by renumbering under close theatre |
| **Reinterpret catalogue meanings** | Close never soft-amends published identifier meanings | Preserve identity of published meaning; never invent it |
| **Amend constitutional specifications** | Only corpus governance may amend meaning | Leave specs unchanged; propose amendment upstream or refuse incomplete assessment |
| **Create constitutional authority** | Only published corpora define constitutional standards | Keep completion as assessment fulfilment; never as next-cycle law |
| **Invent catalogue families or prefixes** | Discovery / assessment name published catalogues under CCF only | Refuse invented families; escalate to corpus governance if law is missing |
| **Silently merge namespaces** | Namespace honesty is educational care | Disclose collisions; never merge under close |
| **Invent uniqueness or hide collisions** | Uniqueness honesty is educational care | Publish violated / deferred / escalated findings honestly |
| **Invent-in-place extensibility** | Growth requires lawful amendment of the owning catalogue | Report extensibility defects; never invent entries under close |
| **Replace MS001 catalogue evaluation** | MS001 owns CCI / CCF meaning | Confirm assessment support for evaluation honesty; never claim “complete therefore new CCI law” |
| **Replace MS002 lifecycle law** | MS002 owns CCIL stages | Confirm stage fulfilment; never invent stages |
| **Create educational meaning** | Programme VI owns coach / planner questions | Preserve identity of published meaning; never invent it |
| **Author runtime behaviour** | Programme VIII + permitted writers own execution law | Assess catalogues against published law; do not mint behaviour |
| **Bypass audit / publication / closure requirements** | Requirements are educational care, not optional polish | Prefer understatement / refuse over invented green completes |
| **Privilege a technology as constitutional** | Implementation neutrality | Confirm assessment obligations, not Flask / SQLAlchemy / REST / OpenAPI fashion |
| **Erase assessment trails to tidy close** | EIP-005 continuity | Preserve prior lawful stage / finding / record artefacts; refuse destructive “cleanup” |
| **Upgrade finding disposition by closing** | CCIC-05 preserves identity; close does not rewrite status | Record findings honestly including violated / deferred / escalated |
| **Present CI / tickets / string scanners as fulfilment law** | Theatre is not constitution | Require CCIC-01…CCIC-06 content |
| **Imply educational success** | Learning outcomes are not assessment fulfilment | Keep student speech honest: assessment-complete ≠ ready |
| **Imply Programme IX evaluative completion** | Conformity / verification / compliance / certification are orthogonal | Compose with CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC; do not treat them as CCIC (or vice versa) |
| **Imply WS1 dependency completion** | Dependency assessment fulfilment is orthogonal | Compose with CDIC; do not treat it as CCIC (or vice versa) |
| **Emergency-amend via deadline** | Urgency never mints amendment or renumbering power | Refuse incomplete close; escalate to corpus governance |
| **Collapse with CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC / CDIC completion** | Implementation and dependency fulfilment are orthogonal horizons | Compose with those corpora; do not treat them as CCIC |

## 5. Boundary Checks

Material completion judgements should be able to answer:

| ID | Check |
|----|-------|
| **CCICB-01 Assessment class** | Is this affirming *catalogue assessment fulfilment* (not identifier authorship, renumbering, law-making, or educational success)? |
| **CCICB-02 Criteria class** | Are required CCIC-01…CCIC-06 criteria being applied (not unpublished customs)? |
| **CCICB-03 Identifier non-creation** | Did close refuse to create, rename, invent, or delete catalogue identifiers? |
| **CCICB-04 Non-renumbering** | Did close refuse to renumber constitutional catalogues or ranges? |
| **CCICB-05 Meaning preservation** | Did close refuse to reinterpret published catalogue meanings? |
| **CCICB-06 Non-amendment** | Did close leave constitutional specifications unchanged? |
| **CCICB-07 Non-authority** | Did close refuse to become constitutional authority for the next judgement? |
| **CCICB-08 Publication honesty** | Were findings cited without silent upgrade or collision erasure? |
| **CCICB-09 Neutrality** | Did close refuse stack / vendor canonisation as completion law? |
| **CCICB-10 Continuity** | Was prior lawful assessment history preserved (EIP-005)? |
| **CCICB-11 Explainability** | Can CCICQ-01…CCICQ-05 be answered without mute theatre? |
| **CCICB-12 Orthogonality** | Did close refuse to stand in for CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC / CDIC fulfilment? |

Failure of any material check ⇒ refuse affirmation.

---

## 6. Closing Statement

> **Catalogue integrity completion may confirm that assessment finished, findings were published, and the assessment formally closed.  
> The moment completion creates catalogue identifiers, renumbers constitutional catalogues, reinterprets catalogue meanings, amends constitutional specifications, or creates constitutional authority, it has left constitutional education and entered product fiction.**
