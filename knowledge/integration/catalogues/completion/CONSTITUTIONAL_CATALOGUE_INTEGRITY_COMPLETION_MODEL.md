Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

## 2. What Constitutional Catalogue Integrity Completion Is

**Constitutional Catalogue Integrity Completion** is confirmation that the **required constitutional catalogue integrity assessment lifecycle** for a published assessment concern has **lawfully concluded** — such that catalogue family assessment, namespace validation, identifier validation, numbering and extensibility validation, findings publication, and formal assessment closure hold for that concern under WS2 / MS001–MS002 — without claiming that any identifier-creation, renumbering, specification-amendment, or constitutional-authority question was resolved by the close itself.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Assessment concern** | A bounded constitutional unit of catalogue integrity work opened under published CCI / CCIL law | What catalogue assessment is being assessed for completion? |
| **Completion warrant** | Judgement that assessment fulfilment may be assessed | May we ask whether the catalogue assessment is complete? |
| **Completion criteria** | Constitutional conditions that must hold (`COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md`) | What must be true for fulfilment? |
| **Completion judgement** | Affirmed / not yet / refused (no lawful close) | Is constitutional catalogue assessment complete for this concern? |
| **Lifecycle fulfilment** | Required CCIL-01…CCIL-07 obligations executed (or lawfully stopped with honest recording) | Did the published assessment lifecycle run? |
| **Catalogue family assessment** | Named catalogues classified and assessed under published CCF-01…CCF-07 families | Were catalogue families assessed? |
| **Namespace validation** | Family / prefix separation and collision honesty validated | Was namespace separation validated? |
| **Identifier validation** | Uniqueness and consistency validated under published CCI law | Was identifier integrity verified? |
| **Numbering / extensibility validation** | Published ranges, succession, and amendment-only growth posture confirmed | Was numbering integrity verified? |
| **Findings publication** | Catalogue integrity findings published under honesty rules | Were findings published? |
| **Formal assessment closure** | Assessment concern closed under published boundary honesty | Was the assessment formally closed? |
| **Boundary preservation record** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden overclaims / mutations / renumberings did not occur | Did constitutional integrity survive close? |

Constitutional Catalogue Integrity Completion is:

- **assessment-scoped** — it concerns fulfilment of the published catalogue assessment lifecycle, not constitutional authorship, identifier creation, educational outcomes, or stack canonisation;
- **lifecycle-bound** — fulfilment references CCIL-01…CCIL-07 stages actually required for the concern under WS2 / MS002;
- **criteria-gated** — fulfilment requires stated constitutional conditions (`COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md` CCIC-01…CCIC-06);
- **family-honest** — completion confirms catalogue families were assessed under published CCF law; it never invents families or renumbers members;
- **namespace-honest** — completion confirms namespace separation was validated; it never silently merges prefixes or invents disambiguation;
- **identifier-honest** — completion confirms uniqueness and consistency were validated; it never creates or renumbers identifiers under close pretext;
- **numbering-honest** — completion confirms numbering and extensibility were validated; it never renumbers ranges or invents-in-place entries;
- **publication-honest** — completion confirms findings were published; it never invents intact status or hides collisions;
- **audit-capable** — fulfilment cites reconstructable constitutional records, not ticket theatre or CI greens;
- **repeatable** — same published corpora, same discovered catalogues, same stage sequence ⇒ same completion disposition for the same concern scope;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by completion;
- **non-legislative** — completion never creates constitutional authority or amends constitutional specifications;
- **non-mutating** — completion never creates, renames, renumbers, invents, or deletes catalogue identifiers under close pretext;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which families / namespaces / identifiers / findings / boundaries applied;
- **corpus-bound** — every material completion cites identifiable published specifications, catalogues, and named findings / records;
- **non-substitutive** — a completed catalogue assessment never becomes the constitutional standard or a substitute for MS001 evaluation meaning.

Constitutional Catalogue Integrity Completion is **not**:

- creation of catalogue identifiers or a soft second catalogue registry;
- renumbering, renaming, invention, or deletion of constitutional catalogue identifiers;
- reinterpretation of catalogue meanings or Programme VI–X / EIP claim classes;
- amendment of constitutional specifications under Constitution / EIP / Programmes VI–X / WS1 / WS2 / MS001–MS002;
- creation of constitutional authority or a soft second constitution;
- educational success, learner mastery, progress, or exam readiness;
- implementation conformity, verification, compliance, or certification;
- a Programme VI / VII / VIII educational, orchestration, or runtime completion judgement;
- a claim that pipeline greens, ticket closure, elapsed time, or scanner badges equal fulfilment;
- a UI celebration, analytics milestone, or “catalogues forever intact” flag as educational or constitutional law;
- a licence to skip outstanding published lifecycle duties because a product surface looks finished;
- a substitute for WS2 / MS001 catalogue integrity meaning or WS2 / MS002 lifecycle law — completion *judges fulfilment of* those corpora; it does not redefine CCI / CCF / CCIL catalogues;
- a substitute for Programme IX CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC completion — those horizons evaluate implementation lineage / verification / compliance / certification; CCIC confirms *corpus catalogue assessment* fulfilment;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS1 CDIC completion — that horizon confirms *dependency assessment* fulfilment; CCIC confirms *catalogue assessment* fulfilment.

## 4. Completion Lifecycle (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material catalogue integrity assessment concern that claims lawful completion travels through constitutional assessment phases. These are **normative judgement obligations**, not an implementation architecture.

```
Identify published catalogue assessment concern opened under CCI / CCIL law
        │
        ▼
Confirm required CCIL-01…CCIL-07 stages and CCI / CCF applications for the concern
        │
        ├── refuse / defer / escalate (lawful stop — law unchanged; no invented “complete”)
        │
        └── assess → Apply CCIC-01…CCIC-06
                │
                ├── not yet / refuse close (outstanding duties or boundary breach)
                │
                └── affirm → Emit completion judgement; preserve audit continuity and repeatability
                        │
                        ▼
                Explain families / namespaces / identifiers / findings / boundaries
```

| Phase | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **Identify** | Name only published catalogue assessment concerns opened under WS2 / MS001–MS002 | Concern is unpublished custom, tribal knowledge, or “how the scanner works today” |
| **Confirm lifecycle** | Verify required CCIL stages and CCI / CCF applications for the concern | Stages would invent law, create IDs, renumber catalogues, reinterpret meanings, invent authority, or privilege a stack as law |
| **Assess criteria** | Apply CCIC-01…CCIC-06 without softening into product theatre | Any required criterion fails; or close would claim ID creation / renumbering / meaning reinterpretation / specification amendment / authority creation |
| **Preserve & explain** | Record audit continuity; answer CCICQ-01…CCICQ-05 | Path would erase assessment trails, treat completion as constitutional authority, or narrate “complete therefore IDs created / catalogues renumbered / mastered” |

> **Catalogue integrity completion never “best-effort invents” a fifth phase called identifier creation, catalogue renumbering, meaning reinterpretation, specification amendment, or constitutional authority creation.**

The Constitutional Catalogue Integrity Completion Model exists so that:

1. **Lifecycle honesty remains intact** — “complete” means the published catalogue assessment lifecycle ran, not that a scanner glowed green.
2. **Catalogue family assessment remains honest** — close confirms families were assessed; it never invents families or renumbers members.
3. **Namespace honesty remains intact** — close confirms namespace validation occurred; it never silently merges prefixes.
4. **Identifier uniqueness remains intact** — close confirms uniqueness and consistency were validated; it never creates or renumbers IDs.
5. **Numbering and extensibility remain intact** — close confirms numbering validation occurred; it never renumbers ranges or invents-in-place.
6. **Publication remains honest** — close confirms findings were published under honesty rules; it never invents intact status.
7. **Formal closure remains speakable** — assessment close exhausts assessment duties without becoming law-making or renumbering.
8. **Audit continuity survives close** — completion leaves reconstructable constitutional records.
9. **Repeatability remains intact** — the same published inputs yield the same completion disposition.
10. **Authority remains upstream** — completed catalogue assessment never becomes the constitutional source for the next judgement.

12. **Explainability remains intact** — completion speech describes assessment fulfilment; it does not redefine what learning means or what conformity is.
13. **Students remain protected** — product theatre cannot launder unfinished or collided catalogue work as “catalogue assessment complete therefore lawful / ready / permanently renumbered.”
