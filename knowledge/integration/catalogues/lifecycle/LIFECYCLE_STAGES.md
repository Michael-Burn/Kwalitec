## 1. Purpose

Catalogue assessment without a closed stage catalogue invents law by proximity: whichever scanner step, ticket status, or reviewer habit happens to finish becomes the tutor’s “proof the system ran a constitutional catalogue integrity assessment.”

This catalogue names the only lawful constitutional catalogue integrity lifecycle stages — and binds each stage to purpose, inputs, outputs, and permitted / prohibited activities.

---

## 2. Stage Principles

1. **Ordered and skip-disciplined.** Stages may be brief under thin scope; mandatory initiation, discovery, namespace validation, identifier validation, numbering validation, finding construction, and publication obligations may not be silently omitted when a catalogue integrity finding is claimed.
2. **Published catalogues before findings.** Identifiable published corpora and discovered catalogues / families are confirmed before intact / violated dispositions are constructed.
3. **Explainability accumulates.** Each stage leaves traceable artefacts for `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.
4. **Refusal is a stage outcome.** Violated, deferred, escalated, and refuse-progress are first-class conclusions.
5. **Deterministic transitions.** Same published inputs ⇒ same next stage outcomes.
6. **Stages coordinate; they do not legislate.** No stage creates, modifies, or redefines constitutional law.
7. **Stages do not become constitutional authority.** Publication never freezes a vocabulary topology as permanently lawful or replaces published corpora.
8. **Stages do not create or renumber identifiers.** Findings report structure; they never create, rename, renumber, invent, or delete catalogue IDs.
9. **Stages apply published CCF / CCI / CCIR law.** Families, categories, and validation rules remain owned by WS2 / MS001.

## 3. Catalogue Overview

| ID | Stage | Constitutional purpose (short) | Primary inputs | Primary outputs |
|----|-------|--------------------------------|----------------|-----------------|
| **CCIL-01** | Assessment Initiation | Open a catalogue assessment against named published corpora and scope | Published corpus identities + concern scope + optional CCI / CCF indication | Initiated concern record (or refuse-open) |
| **CCIL-02** | Catalogue Discovery | Identify published catalogues and classify under CCF-01…CCF-07 | Initiated concern + candidate published catalogue identities | Discovered catalogue / family set bound to CCI types (or refuse-discover) |
| **CCIL-03** | Namespace Validation | Validate family / prefix separation and collision honesty | Discovered catalogues / families + published namespace rules | Validated namespace / collision record (or refuse-validate-namespace) |
| **CCIL-04** | Identifier Validation | Validate uniqueness and consistency under CCI-01 / CCI-02 | Namespace-validated catalogue set + published meaning statements | Validated identifier record (or refuse-validate-identifier) |
| **CCIL-05** | Numbering Validation | Confirm numbering integrity and extensibility posture under CCI-05 / CCI-06 | Identifier-validated set + published numbering / closure rules | Numbering / extensibility disposition (or refuse-number) |
| **CCIL-06** | Finding Construction | Emit intact / violated / deferred / escalated under published criteria | Stage dispositions + CCI / CCIR criteria | Catalogue integrity findings |
| **CCIL-07** | Finding Publication | Publish findings and assessment records under honesty rules | Findings + boundary confirmations | Published assessment artefacts (or refuse-publish) |

Material catalogue assessment claiming constitutional findings must map to this sequence as published law requires. Stages may be brief; none may invent a stage outside this catalogue.

**Relation to CCI / CCF / CCIR / CCIO / CT / CC / CDI / CDIL / EV:** Those catalogues remain defined solely by their owners. CCIL stages *organise when* catalogue assessment activities occur; they do not replace, reclassify, invent, amend, renumber, or rewrite those catalogues.

## 5. Stage Definitions

### CCIL-01 — Assessment Initiation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Open a material catalogue integrity assessment concern by naming the published constitutional corpora under assessment and the scope of the concern — without inventing law, opening against unpublished customs, or treating initiation as an intact finding.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Identifiable published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–XI specification references for the concern scope
- Concern identity (what catalogue-integrity question is under assessment)
- Optional published indication of required CCI-01…CCI-07 categories and CCF-01…CCF-07 families for the scope
- Optional identifiable supporting authority / lineage references (not rewritten)

**Constitutional outputs**

- An initiated assessment concern record naming corpora, specifications, scope, and intended CCI / CCF sets — **or**
- Lawful refuse-open when standards are unpublished, scope is theatrical, or initiation would invent law

**Permitted activities**

- Name published corpora and specifications as assessment standards
- Bound the concern scope without amending corpora
- Record intended CCI types and CCF families from the published catalogues
- Refuse initiation when constitutional preconditions fail
- Preserve initiation audit identity for later stages

**Prohibited activities**

- Treat “how docs label,” tribal knowledge, or stack preference as the standard
- Create or modify constitutional specifications by opening a concern
- Issue an intact / violated finding at initiation
- Invent unpublished catalogue integrity categories or families
- Skip initiation when a material finding will later be claimed
- Create catalogue identifiers, renumber catalogues, or create constitutional authority at initiation

**Authority preserved**  
Published corpora remain the sole standard. Initiation organises; it does not legislate.

---

### CCIL-02 — Catalogue Discovery

**Constitutional purpose**  
Identify **published constitutional catalogues** among the named corpora and classify them under recognised **CCF-01…CCF-07** families — so later stages assess real published catalogues, not anonymous theatre — without inventing, renaming, or renumbering catalogue identities.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Initiated concern from CCIL-01
- Candidate published catalogue identities (closed identifier sets as published by owning corpora)
- Published CCF-01…CCF-07 family definitions and CCI-01…CCI-07 category definitions from WS2 / MS001
- Optional supporting CT lineage identities already established under upstream law (unchanged)

**Constitutional outputs**

- A discovered catalogue / family set bound to the concern and intended CCI types — **or**
- Lawful refuse-discover when catalogues are missing, forged, invented, unpublished, or would require unlawful mutation / renumbering to “discover”

**Permitted activities**

- Identify and name published catalogues
- Classify catalogues under published CCF-01…CCF-07 only
- Bind discovered catalogues to the initiated concern and intended CCI types
- Refuse discovery when catalogues cannot be honestly affirmed as published
- Preserve discovery records for audit and explainability

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent missing catalogues or invent family membership outside CCF-01…CCF-07
- Rename, renumber, or delete catalogue identifiers to force later intact status
- Treat string scanners, ticket links, or CI noise as constitutional catalogues without published mapping
- Redefine educational or constitutional meaning while “discovering”
- Present discovery as itself an intact finding or a freeze of stack authority
- Create catalogue identifiers or constitutional authority under discovery pretence

**Authority preserved**  
Published catalogues remain upstream facts. Discovery organises identity and family classification; it does not author catalogues or educational truth.

---

### CCIL-03 — Namespace Validation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Validate that discovered catalogues **preserve namespace separation** under published family / prefix expectations and that **collisions** are reported honestly under CCI-03 / CCI-04 — without inventing missing disambiguation, silently merging families, or renumbering prefixes.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Discovered catalogue / family set from CCIL-02
- Published namespace rules (CCIR-03 / CCIR-04 / CCIR-07 and family prefix expectations from `CATALOGUE_FAMILIES.md`)
- Published disambiguation tables where they exist (unchanged)
- Optional supporting lineage / authority references consumed as inputs (unchanged)

**Constitutional outputs**

- A validated namespace / collision record per assessed catalogue / family set — **or**
- Lawful refuse-validate-namespace when separation cannot be reconstructed, disambiguation would be invented, families would be silently merged, or validation would require renumbering

**Permitted activities**

- Confirm family / prefix separation for discovered catalogues
- Detect and classify identifier collisions across families under CCI-04
- Prefer honest refuse-validate-namespace / deferred / escalated over inventing disambiguation or merging families
- Remain repeatable and implementation-neutral
- Preserve validation records for later stages

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent missing disambiguation tables or namespace rules
- Silently merge distinct CCF families into one mega-vocabulary
- Modify constitutional specifications to force namespace pass
- Privilege a technology stack as constitutional
- Redefine Programme VI–X meaning or CCI / CCF meaning in validation prose
- Present namespace validation as new constitutional law
- Renumber prefixes or catalogue identifiers during validation
- Create constitutional authority by declaring collisions “approved”

**Authority preserved**  
WS2 / MS001 owns namespace and collision rules. This stage applies them; it does not amend the catalogue or become constitutional authority.

---

### CCIL-04 — Identifier Validation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Validate **identifier uniqueness** and **identifier consistency** under CCI-01 / CCI-02 — confirming that no two published entries share the same ID for incompatible meanings without published disambiguation, and that each ID retains one published meaning within its owning namespace — without renumbering IDs or redefining meanings in situ.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Namespace-validated catalogue set from CCIL-03
- Published catalogue entries and meaning statements from owning corpora
- Published uniqueness / consistency rules (especially CCIR-01 / CCIR-02 / CCIR-08)
- Collision status from CCIL-03 where material

**Constitutional outputs**

- Validated identifier record: uniqueness / consistency disposition (intact under rules / violated / deferred / escalated) — **or**
- Lawful refuse-validate-identifier when uniqueness or consistency cannot be honestly confirmed without inventing meanings, renumbering IDs, or rewriting law

**Permitted activities**

- Confirm uniqueness under CCI-01 / CCIR-01
- Confirm one published meaning per ID within namespace under CCI-02 / CCIR-02
- Report collisions already disclosed at CCIL-03 as uniqueness defects when disambiguation is absent
- Prefer honest violated / deferred / escalated over renumbering
- Preserve identifier-validation records for finding construction

**Prohibited activities**

- Create, rename, renumber, invent, or delete catalogue identifiers
- Soft-amend specifications so dual meanings become “the same”
- Treat string-scanner uniqueness as constitutional uniqueness without published mapping
- Hide uniqueness / consistency defects to manufacture later intact status
- Present identifier validation as licence to rewrite the corpus
- Create constitutional authority by declaring colliding IDs “approved”
- Redefine catalogue meanings under validation pretence

**Authority preserved**  
Identifier validation reports structural defects. It never authors law or renumbers catalogues.

---

### CCIL-05 — Numbering Validation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Confirm that assessed catalogues **preserve published numbering integrity** under CCI-05 and **lawful extensibility posture** under CCI-06 — published ranges, gaps, and succession remain as published; growth requires amendment of the owning catalogue — without renumbering ranges or inventing entries in place.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Identifier-validated set from prior stages
- Published numbering declarations, ranges, gaps, and succession rules
- Published closure / amendment-path statements for extensibility (CCI-06 / CCIR-06)
- Workstream / milestone catalogue identities as published

**Constitutional outputs**

- Numbering / extensibility disposition for the concern (preserved / violated / deferred / escalated) — **or**
- Lawful refuse-number when numbering or extensibility cannot be honestly confirmed without inventing ranges, renumbering, or invent-in-place growth

**Permitted activities**

- Confirm published ranges, gaps, and succession honesty under CCI-05 / CCIR-05
- Confirm amendment-only growth rules remain intact under CCI-06 / CCIR-06
- Report gaps as lawful when published; report silent renumbering as violated
- Prefer honest violated / deferred / escalated over soft-renumbering or invent-in-place
- Preserve numbering / extensibility records for finding construction

**Prohibited activities**

- Renumber constitutional catalogues to “make room” or tidy collisions
- Invent new catalogue identifiers under extensibility pretence
- Soft-amend specifications so invent-in-place becomes “growth”
- Modify constitutional specifications to force numbering pass
- Present numbering pass as educational quality or stack freeze
- Collapse distinct catalogues into one numbering space under “validation”
- Create constitutional authority by declaring renumbered IDs “approved”

**Authority preserved**  
Published catalogues own numbering and closure. This stage confirms them; it does not become a renumbering engine or constitutional authority.

---

### CCIL-06 — Finding Construction

**Constitutional purpose**  
Emit **catalogue integrity findings** as lawful dispositions under published criteria — speaking to whether assessed catalogues remain intact under uniqueness, consistency, namespace separation, numbering, and extensibility — without converting findings into amendments, educational grades, renumbering orders, or freezes of stack authority.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Stage dispositions from CCIL-01…CCIL-05
- Published CCI-01…CCI-07 criteria and CCIR validation rules from WS2 / MS001
- Published decision vocabulary (intact / violated / deferred / escalated)
- Bound references to corpora, catalogues, families, namespace rules, identifier validations, numbering / extensibility disposition

**Constitutional outputs**

- Catalogue integrity findings for the concern (including composition outcomes when multiple CCI types / CCF families apply)
- Explicit non-authority framing (findings are integrity dispositions only — never new law and never renumbering orders)
- Assessment records binding stages, catalogues, families, namespace rules, identifier validations, numbering / extensibility, and findings

**Permitted activities**

- Produce findings under published dispositions only
- Bind each finding to the CCI types, CCF families, catalogues, and stages that produced it
- Record composition / stop-rule application honestly
- Prefer understatement over invented green
- Propose (not enact) upstream corpus amendments when law itself must change
- Preserve audit-speakable assessment records under CCI-07 honesty

**Prohibited activities**

- Present findings as constitutional amendments or new educational meaning
- Narrate intact status as mastery, readiness theatre, educational quality, permanent conformity, or frozen stack authority
- Rewrite findings to match product urgency
- Collapse multi-type failures into a single false “intact”
- Create, rename, renumber, invent, or delete catalogue identifiers while “constructing findings”
- Invent unpublished CCI categories or CCF families during finding construction
- Erase stage history so findings look clean
- Create constitutional authority via finding text

**Authority preserved**  
Findings validate structure against law. They never become law, renumbering orders, or constitutional authority.

---

### CCIL-07 — Finding Publication

**Constitutional purpose**  
Publish catalogue integrity findings and assessment records under **published honesty rules** so audiences receive lawful assessment speech — without inventing intact status, hiding collisions or numbering defects, rewriting boundaries, amending specifications, renumbering catalogues, or freezing a stack as law.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Findings and assessment records from CCIL-06
- Published publication / honesty rules from WS2 / MS001 and EIP explainability / continuity law
- Boundary confirmations (non-identifier-creation, non-renumbering, non-legislation, non-reinterpretation, non-authority-creation, non-substitution)

**Constitutional outputs**

- Published assessment artefacts projecting one constitutional truth to authorised audiences — **or**
- Lawful refuse-publish when records are incomplete, boundaries were breached, or publication would invent intact status / hide collisions / renumber catalogues / freeze stack authority / replace constitutional authority

**Permitted activities**

- Publish findings and records under published honesty rules
- Project student / developer / auditor vocabularies without changing constitutional facts
- Refuse publication when audit or boundary preconditions fail
- Keep publication distinct from educational quality, identifier renumbering, and authority creation

**Prohibited activities**

- Publish invented intact status or incomplete-assessment theatre as intact
- Hide refuse / defer / escalate / violated dispositions to manufacture green
- Narrate publication as constitutional amendment, educational success, identifier renumbering, or frozen stack law
- Create catalogue identifiers or constitutional authority as a publication act
- Modify constitutional specifications or renumber catalogues as a publication act
- Present seals, badges, or scanner greenery as substitutes for published records
- Reinterpret constitutional meaning in publication speech

**Authority preserved**  
Publication communicates assessment findings. Published corpora remain the sole standard.

## 6. Stage Transition Rules

| From | To | Transition requires |
|------|----|---------------------|
| — | CCIL-01 | Material catalogue assessment concern needs opening |
| CCIL-01 | CCIL-02 | Concern initiated (not refuse-open) |
| CCIL-01 | CCIL-06 / CCIL-07 path | Refuse-open recorded honestly |
| CCIL-02 | CCIL-03 | Catalogues / families discovered (not refuse-discover) |
| CCIL-02 | CCIL-06 / CCIL-07 path | Refuse-discover recorded honestly |
| CCIL-03 | CCIL-04 | Namespace / collision validated (not refuse-validate-namespace) — or honest fail disposition carried forward |
| CCIL-03 | CCIL-06 / CCIL-07 path | Refuse-validate-namespace recorded honestly |
| CCIL-04 | CCIL-05 | Identifier uniqueness / consistency validated (not refuse-validate-identifier) — or honest fail disposition carried forward |
| CCIL-04 | CCIL-06 / CCIL-07 path | Refuse-validate-identifier recorded honestly |
| CCIL-05 | CCIL-06 | Numbering / extensibility disposition available (not refuse-number) — or honest fail disposition carried forward |
| CCIL-05 | CCIL-06 / CCIL-07 path | Refuse-number recorded honestly |
| CCIL-06 | CCIL-07 | Findings constructed under published vocabulary |
| CCIL-07 | — | Publication completed or refuse-publish recorded |

Silent jumps that claim findings without CCIL-01…CCIL-05 honesty are constitutionally defective.

## 7. Composition with Catalogue Integrity Categories

| During stage | Relationship to CCI-01…CCI-07 / CCF-01…CCF-07 |
|--------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| CCIL-01 | May *name* intended CCI types and CCF families; does not apply full validation |
| CCIL-02 | Discovers catalogues those families / types will assess |
| CCIL-03 | Applies namespace separation / collision rules (CCI-03 / CCI-04) |
| CCIL-04 | Applies uniqueness / consistency rules (CCI-01 / CCI-02) |
| CCIL-05 | Applies numbering / extensibility rules (CCI-05 / CCI-06) |
| **CCIL-06** | **Constructs** findings under CCI-01…CCI-07 (including CCI-07 assessment-record honesty) |
| CCIL-07 | Publishes CCI-bound findings without inventing types or renumbering catalogues |

Cross-cutting concerns may bind multiple CCI types and CCF families across stages. Lifecycle stages remain CCIL-01…CCIL-07 only.
