## 2. What the Constitutional Catalogue Integrity Lifecycle Is

The **constitutional catalogue integrity lifecycle** is the binding organisation by which **catalogue integrity assessments** may proceed through recognised stages — initiating against published corpus identities, discovering published catalogues and classifying them under CCF-01…CCF-07 families, validating namespace separation, validating identifier uniqueness and consistency, validating numbering integrity and extensibility under published CCI-01…CCI-07 categories, and constructing / publishing **catalogue integrity findings** that preserve uniqueness, identifier consistency, namespace separation, numbering integrity, extensibility, repeatability, consistency, implementation neutrality, auditability, and explainability — without becoming a source of constitutional law, a renumbering engine, a substitute for MS001 catalogue integrity meaning, or a replacement for upstream programme authority.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Published constitutional corpus** | Constitution, EIP, Programme VI–XI specifications and their subordinate workstream / milestone corpora | Which corpus may the lifecycle organise assessment against? |
| **Constitutional catalogue** | A published closed set of identifiers that classifies constitutional meaning, stages, criteria, objectives, boundaries, or related assessment artefacts | Which catalogue is under assessment? |
| **Catalogue family** | A recognised grouping of catalogues under CCF-01…CCF-07 for integrity assessment | Which family contains this catalogue? |
| **Namespace separation** | Distinct catalogue families / prefixes remain reconstructably separated; overlapping labels are disclosed collisions, not silent merges | Are namespaces honestly separated? |
| **Identifier uniqueness / consistency** | No two published entries share the same ID for incompatible meanings without disambiguation; the same ID retains one published meaning within its namespace | Are identifiers unique and consistent? |
| **Numbering integrity** | Published ranges, gaps, and succession rules remain as published; silent renumbering is a defect | Is numbering intact as published? |
| **Extensibility posture** | Future entries may be added only by lawful amendment of the owning catalogue — never by invent-in-place | Can the catalogue grow lawfully? |
| **Lifecycle stage** | A recognised CCIL-01…CCIL-07 organisational step | Which coordination step is active? |
| **Catalogue integrity finding** | Lawful disposition: intact / violated / deferred / escalated — never “new law” and never a renumbering order | Does the catalogue remain lawful? |
| **Assessment record / publication artefact** | Audit-speakable record of families, namespace rules, identifier validations, stages, findings, and preserved boundaries | Can the assessment be reconstructed? |
| **Constitutional authority** | The exclusive right of published corpora to define educational and execution meaning | Who owns the standard? |
| **Implementation neutrality** | Lifecycle coordination is judged against law and published catalogues, not against a preferred stack, vendor, or topology | Remains the stack replaceable? |
| **Repeatability** | Same published corpora, same discovered catalogues, same stage sequence → same disposition | Can the judgement be reproduced? |
| **Auditability** | Every material lifecycle leaves reconstructable constitutional traces | Can the judgement be reconstructed? |
| **Explainability** | Families, namespace rules, identifier validations, findings, stages, and boundaries are speakable | Can the judgement be narrated honestly? |

The constitutional catalogue integrity lifecycle is:

- **coordination-centred** — it organises catalogue assessments; it never authors constitutional obligations or catalogue identifiers;
- **discovery-bound** — it discovers published catalogues and classifies them under CCF families; it never invents prefixes to force progress;
- **namespace-honest** — overlapping prefixes across families are reported as collisions or published disambiguations — never silently merged;
- **identifier-preserving** — uniqueness and consistency remain as published; silent dual meanings are hard defects;
- **numbering-faithful** — published numbering remains as published; silent renumbering is forbidden;
- **extensibility-preserving** — growth requires lawful amendment of the owning catalogue;
- **catalogue-closed** — only CCIL-01…CCIL-07 may be applied as constitutional catalogue integrity lifecycle stages;
- **type-faithful** — stages apply only published CCI-01…CCI-07 categories, CCF-01…CCF-07 families, and CCIR validation rules from WS2 / MS001; they never invent categories or families;
- **finding-producing** — assessments emit intact / violated / deferred / escalated dispositions;
- **publication-honest** — findings are published under honesty rules; publication never renumbers IDs or becomes law;
- **authority-preserving** — published corpora remain the sole source of constitutional meaning;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by lifecycle law;
- **corpus-bound** — every material lifecycle cites identifiable published specifications and catalogue identities;
- **audit-capable** — every material lifecycle leaves reconstructable constitutional traces;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which families were assessed under which namespace rules with which identifier validations and findings;
- **non-legislative** — a catalogue finding never becomes constitutional law or a soft amendment;
- **non-substitutive** — lifecycle machinery never becomes the constitutional standard for the next judgement;
- **non-mutating** — the lifecycle never modifies constitutional specifications or catalogue identifiers under coordination pretext.

The constitutional catalogue integrity lifecycle is **not**:

- a second Educational Constitution, EIP, Programme VI–X, WS1, or WS2 / MS001 amendment path;
- a licence to reinterpret coach meaning, ownership maps, tip warrants, runtime contracts, conformance criteria, traceability meaning, verification meaning, compliance meaning, certification meaning, evolution meaning, execution meaning, dependency meaning, or CCI / CCF categories in lifecycle prose;
- a testing framework, CI/CD pipeline, GitHub Action, string scanner, or OpenAPI linker;
- a claim that “lifecycle complete” guarantees learning, mastery, exam readiness, product success, perpetual conformity, or frozen stack authority;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS2 / MS001 catalogue integrity meaning — CCI / CCF define categories and families; CCIL sequences when assessment occurs;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS1 dependency integrity / lifecycle — CDI / CDIL validate and sequence *corpus dependency structure*; CCIL coordinates *catalogue identifier integrity assessment*;
- a substitute for Programme IX MS002 constitutional traceability — CT preserves spec/artefact/finding lineage; CCIL coordinates *constitutional catalogue integrity assessment*;
- a substitute for Programme IX conformance / verification / compliance / certification — those evaluate implementations and obligations; CCIL validates *catalogue corpus structure sequencing*;
- a substitute for Programme X execution / decision / audit / explainability — those organise constitutional activity; CCIL never executes, decides, audits, or explains educational outcomes in their place;
- a licence to create, rename, renumber, invent, or delete constitutional catalogue identifiers under “lifecycle” pretext.

## 3. Lifecycle Posture (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material constitutional catalogue integrity lifecycle travels through seven constitutional stages. These are **normative coordination obligations**, not an implementation architecture and not a CI job graph.

```
CCIL-01 Assessment Initiation
        │
        ▼
CCIL-02 Catalogue Discovery
        │
        ▼
CCIL-03 Namespace Validation
        │
        ▼
CCIL-04 Identifier Validation
        │
        ▼
CCIL-05 Numbering Validation
        │
        ├── violated / deferred / escalated (lawful finding — catalogues unchanged)
        │
        └── intact (or other lawful disposition) →
                │
                ▼
CCIL-06 Finding Construction
        │
        ▼
CCIL-07 Finding Publication
        │
        ▼
Explain families / namespace rules / identifier validations / findings / boundaries
```

| Stage | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **CCIL-01 Assessment Initiation** | Open a catalogue assessment concern against named published corpora and scope | Scope is unpublished custom, tribal knowledge, or “how docs label today” |
| **CCIL-02 Catalogue Discovery** | Identify published catalogues and classify them under CCF-01…CCF-07 | Catalogues / families are invented, unpublished, or would require renumbering to “discover” them |
| **CCIL-03 Namespace Validation** | Validate family / prefix separation and collision honesty under CCI-03 / CCI-04 | Validation would invent disambiguation, silently merge families, or renumber prefixes |
| **CCIL-04 Identifier Validation** | Validate uniqueness and consistency under CCI-01 / CCI-02 | Validation would invent uniqueness, redefine meanings, or renumber IDs in situ |
| **CCIL-05 Numbering Validation** | Confirm numbering integrity and extensibility posture under CCI-05 / CCI-06 | Validation would renumber ranges, invent-in-place entries, or invent a new layer of IDs |
| **CCIL-06 Finding Construction** | Emit intact / violated / deferred / escalated findings under published criteria | Findings would become amendments, educational quality grades, or renumbering orders |
| **CCIL-07 Finding Publication** | Publish findings and assessment records under published honesty rules | Publication would invent intact status, hide collisions, rewrite boundaries, or freeze a stack as law |

> **The lifecycle never “best-effort invents” an eighth stage called law-making, renumbering, stack canonisation, or catalogue mutation.**

## 6. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Catalogue Integrity Lifecycle Model exists so that:

1. **Law remains upstream of coordination** — stages organise assessment against published corpora; progress does not quietly become the specifications.
2. **Discovery remains honest** — discovery names published catalogues and CCF families; it does not invent prefixes to force intact status.
3. **Namespaces remain separated** — namespace validation confirms family / prefix separation; it does not silently merge vocabularies.
4. **Identifiers remain unique and consistent** — identifier validation confirms uniqueness and one-meaning-per-ID honesty; it never renumbers in situ.
5. **Numbering remains intact** — numbering validation reports published ranges as found; it never renumbers to paper over collisions.
6. **Extensibility remains lawful** — stages confirm amendment-only growth; invent-in-place is a hard defect.
7. **Findings remain sequenced** — intact / violated / deferred / escalated dispositions arise after lawful initiation, discovery, and validation — not as random green theatre.
8. **Authority remains honest** — no team may treat a published finding as the new constitutional source.
9. **Implementations remain replaceable** — lifecycle law does not freeze Flask, SQLAlchemy, a particular API shape, or a CI vendor as law.
10. **Repeatability remains honest** — the same published corpora, discovered catalogues, and stage sequence yield the same disposition.
11. **Audit remains possible** — every material lifecycle can be reconstructed against named families, namespace rules, identifier validations, stages, and findings.
12. **Explainability remains intact** — lifecycle speech describes coordination of catalogue assessment; it does not redefine what learning means.
13. **Students remain protected** — product theatre cannot launder colliding or renumbered catalogues as “lifecycle intact.”
