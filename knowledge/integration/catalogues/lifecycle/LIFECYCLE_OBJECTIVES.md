## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “scanner finished,” “stages skipped for speed,” or “prefix badge shown.” The tutor optimises **honest organisation of catalogue assessment work**: only a repeatable, consistent, implementation-neutral, explainable, and auditable sequence — discovering published catalogues under CCF-01…CCF-07 and validating namespaces, identifiers, numbering, and extensibility under CCI-01…CCI-07 — may support lawful catalogue integrity findings.

These objectives bind every constitutional catalogue integrity lifecycle and every successor coordination machinery that claims constitutional catalogue integrity lifecycle meaning.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### CCILO-01 — Preserve catalogue integrity through lawful coordination

**Definition.** Ensure that every material catalogue integrity lifecycle organises assessments through recognised CCIL-01…CCIL-07 stages — initiating against identifiable published corpora, discovering named catalogues under CCF-01…CCF-07 families, validating namespace separation, validating identifier uniqueness and consistency, validating numbering integrity and extensibility, and constructing / publishing findings under published CCI-01…CCI-07 categories — so that findings occur only after lawful initiation and discovery, and that findings are published without becoming law, renumbering orders, or replacements for constitutional authority.

**Includes:**

- Binding every material lifecycle to identifiable published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–XI specifications and subordinate workstream / milestone corpora.
- Discovering only published catalogue identities and classifying them under published CCF-01…CCF-07 families.
- Applying only published CCI-01…CCI-07 categories and CCIR validation rules across stages.
- Preferring lawful violated / deferred / escalated / refuse-progress outcomes over rewriting law, inventing catalogues, inventing uniqueness, renumbering IDs in situ, or mutating corpora so the lifecycle “completes.”
- Keeping lifecycle progress distinct from educational quality, mastery, readiness theatre, product success, string-scanner greenery, or constitutional amendment.
- Preserving the Model’s posture: stages coordinate assessment; they do not become law or constitutional authority.

**Excludes:**

- Treating green CI, coverage percentages, linter scores, Twin estimates, Adaptive scores, UI ticks, or string scanners as lifecycle fulfilment.
- Soft-amending specifications, inventing unpublished customs, inventing or renumbering catalogues, inventing uniqueness, mutating identifiers, or privileging “how main labels” to force progress.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for lawful coordination.
- Declaring “close enough” publication without documenting a corpus amendment.
- Freezing an implementation as permanently lawful because the lifecycle published findings.
- Creating catalogue identifiers or constitutional authority under a lifecycle badge.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software pretends unfinished or dishonest catalogue assessment is fine because integration needs a finished green vocabulary report. Coordination honesty is care; lifecycle theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CCILO-02 — Preserve namespace consistency

**Definition.** Ensure that every material lifecycle validates — and leaves reconstructable — namespace separation among recognised CCF-01…CCF-07 families and published prefix conventions, so overlapping labels are disclosed as collisions or published disambiguations and never silently merged across stages.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who quietly treats two different worksheet codes as the same because the labels look similar cannot be trusted. Lifecycles without namespace validation are educational amnesia with a progress bar.

**Manifestations:**

- CCIL-03 requires reconstructable family / prefix separation; silent merges are defects, not soft variables.
- Findings cite CCF family identities, prefix maps, and disambiguation tables, not only ticket IDs or commit SHAs.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing prior lawful assessment history to simplify the next report.
- Programme IX CT lineage may support reconstruction; CCIL never treats CT records as a licence to invent namespace law.
- Namespace-consistency preservation never authorises inventing missing disambiguation tables or merging families in situ.

---

### CCILO-03 — Preserve identifier uniqueness

**Definition.** Ensure that lifecycle organisation confirms — under CCI-01 / CCI-02 and related CCIR rules — that identifiers remain unique for their published meanings (or are lawfully disambiguated) and retain one published meaning within their owning namespace, so dual meanings and silent reuse are impossible without a published amendment.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who uses the same item code for two different syllabus meanings has abandoned institutional honesty for convenience.

**Manifestations:**

- CCIL-04 confirms uniqueness and consistency under published CCI / CCIR law; collisions without disambiguation are hard defects.
- Identifier drift without amendment is reported as violated — never “fixed” by renumbering under lifecycle pretence.
- Forbidden actions in `LIFECYCLE_BOUNDARIES.md` are hard stops.
- Uniqueness preservation never authorises creating new identifiers or renumbering existing ones.

---

### CCILO-04 — Preserve assessment repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that the same published corpus identities, the same discovered catalogues and family classifications, the same published CCI / CCIR criteria, and the same CCIL stage sequence yield the same catalogue disposition (intact / violated / deferred / escalated) and the same lawful stage outcomes — absent a published non-deterministic exception (none are granted by this Model for constitutional meaning or integrity status).

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who treats the same vocabulary map as lawful or not depending on which reviewer woke first — or skips stages randomly — is not coaching; they are gambling.

**Manifestations:**

- Only CCIL-01…CCIL-07 may organise constitutional catalogue integrity lifecycles.
- Stage purpose, inputs, outputs, and permitted / prohibited activities remain as published in `LIFECYCLE_STAGES.md`.
- Stage transitions and outcomes are corpus-bound and reproducible.
- Parallel CI runners, sharding, and cache races must not change constitutional disposition or invent stage skips.
- Explicit multi-type CCI / multi-family CCF sets use published conjunction / published stop rules, not random reviewer races.
- “Eventually consistent lifecycle meaning” is a constitutional defect.
- Invented or unstable catalogue inputs, and invented uniqueness claims, are hard stops — not soft variables.

---

### CCILO-05 — Preserve long-term extensibility

**Definition.** Ensure that lifecycle organisation confirms amendment-only growth rules remain intact under CCI-06 / CCIR-06 — so future catalogue entries may be added only by lawful amendment of the owning catalogue, never by invent-in-place, silent renumbering “to make room,” or lifecycle theatre that pretends growth happened without amendment.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who invents new worksheet codes mid-lesson because “we needed more numbers” destroys the syllabus map for every future student.

**Manifestations:**

- CCIL-05 confirms numbering integrity and extensibility posture; invent-in-place and silent renumbering are hard defects.
- Intact findings after CCIL-01…CCIL-07 may inform downstream readiness; they never themselves authorise inventing new catalogue IDs.
- Extensibility proxies (CI greens, coverage, scanner scores) never redefine CCILO-01…CCILO-06.
- Long-term extensibility here means *constitutional catalogue growth honesty* — not product feature sprawl and not Runtime A deployment theatre.
- Preserving extensibility never authorises creating identifiers, renumbering catalogues, or skipping stages so a release can ship.

---

### CCILO-06 — Preserve explainability and auditability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material catalogue integrity lifecycle can honestly answer the mandatory explainability questions in `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md` and leaves a reconstructable trail: which catalogue families were assessed, which namespace rules were applied, which identifier validations were performed, which findings were produced, and which constitutional boundaries remained intact.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later say *why* a vocabulary was accepted — or *at which stage* a finding arose — cannot be trusted. Silent green vocabulary badges destroy student and auditor trust.

**Manifestations:**

- Explainability obligations apply to all material lifecycles.
- Student / developer / auditor projections share one truth with different vocabulary.
- Audit records preserve constitutional references, not only technical scanner logs.
- Consumed corpus identities, CCF / CCI identities, CCIL stage identities, namespace-rule identities, identifier-validation status, and finding dispositions are recorded when material.
- Missing CCIL identity, missing corpus / catalogue / family refs, missing namespace-rule note, missing identifier-validation status, missing finding disposition, or missing boundary confirmation is a defect.
- Lifecycle progress is never narrated as mastery, readiness theatre, educational quality, new law, an identifier renumbering, or a freeze of stack authority.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing prior lawful lifecycle history to simplify the next report.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CCILO-01 conflicts with “helping” colliding catalogues look lifecycle-complete | Lawful coordination honesty wins — violated / deferred / escalated / refuse-progress |
| CCILO-02 conflicts with soft-merging two families into one prefix | Namespace consistency wins |
| CCILO-03 conflicts with renumbering IDs so uniqueness “passes” | Identifier uniqueness honesty wins — refuse renumber |
| CCILO-04 conflicts with race-dependent reviewer outcomes or private stage shortcuts | Assessment repeatability wins |
| CCILO-05 conflicts with invent-in-place IDs or renumbering “to make room” | Long-term extensibility wins — refuse invent / renumber |
| CCILO-06 conflicts with opaque “trust us” vocabulary badges | Explainability and auditability win |

No supporting objective authorises violating CCILO-01.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — Constitution / EIP integrity obligations, Programme VI–X authority preservation, and WS2 / MS001 catalogue integrity objectives for the lifecycle horizon.

---

## 5. Non-Objectives

The constitutional catalogue integrity lifecycle does **not** optimise for:

- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- CI throughput, flaky-test suppression, or coverage theatre as educational success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when specifications or declared catalogues are incomplete;
- collapsing CCIL stages into one mega-step that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics / string-scanner signals as substitutes for published constitutional catalogues;
- freezing implementations, releases, or stacks as permanently lawful;
- treating documentation as optional commentary on whatever code already shipped;
- replacing MS001 catalogue integrity meaning, Programme XI / WS1 dependency integrity / lifecycle, Programme IX CT lineage preservation, or Programme IX–X evaluative / execution horizons;
- creating, renaming, or renumbering constitutional catalogue identifiers so vocabularies look tidy;
- creating constitutional authority under a lifecycle badge.

---

## 6. Closing Statement

> **A lifecycle that becomes “complete” by creating catalogue identifiers, renumbering catalogues, amending specifications, or creating constitutional authority is a failed educational system.  
> A lifecycle that coordinates only published catalogue assessment — with namespace consistency, identifier uniqueness, repeatability, extensibility honesty, explainability, and auditability intact — is doing its only coordination job.**
