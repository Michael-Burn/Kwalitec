## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “grep found no duplicates” or “we renumbered overnight so the report looks clean.” The tutor optimises **honest catalogue integrity**: only catalogues that remain unique, consistent, namespace-separated, numbering-intact, and lawfully extensible may be called intact — without rewriting those catalogues so a readiness report can pass.

These objectives bind every constitutional catalogue integrity assessment and every successor assessment machinery that claims constitutional catalogue integrity meaning.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### CCIO-01 — Preserve uniqueness

**Definition.** Ensure that every material catalogue integrity assessment determines — for named published catalogue identities under recognised CCF-01…CCF-07 families and CCI-01…CCI-07 categories — whether identifiers remain unique for their published meanings (or are lawfully disambiguated where prefixes overlap), or honestly reports violated / deferred / escalated when uniqueness cannot be confirmed.

**Includes:**

- Applying the closed CCF / CCI catalogues required by published law for the assessment scope.
- Binding every material assessment to identifiable published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–XI specifications and subordinate workstream / milestone corpora.
- Detecting identical identifiers assigned to incompatible constitutional meanings without published disambiguation.
- Preferring lawful violated / deferred / escalated findings over renumbering catalogues so readiness “passes.”
- Keeping uniqueness distinct from educational quality, mastery, readiness theatre, product success, or constitutional amendment.
- Preserving the Model’s posture: findings validate structure; they do not become law or rewrite identifiers.

**Excludes:**

- Treating green string scanners, ticket links, Twin estimates, Adaptive scores, or UI ticks as constitutional uniqueness.
- Soft-amending specifications, inventing unpublished customs, renumbering IDs, or privileging “how main labels” to force intact status.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for catalogue integrity.
- Declaring “close enough” uniqueness without documenting a corpus amendment.
- Treating published disambiguation tables as automatic proof that colliding labels are the same meaning — disambiguation separates; it does not merge.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software pretends colliding constitutional vocabularies are fine because integration needs a green path. Uniqueness honesty is care; catalogue theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CCIO-02 — Preserve consistency

**Definition.** Ensure that every material catalogue identifier continues to denote the same published constitutional meaning within its owning catalogue and namespace — so identity drift, silent redefinition, and dual meanings are impossible without a published amendment.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who uses the same worksheet code for two different syllabus items cannot be trusted. Identifier drift is educational amnesia.

**Manifestations:**

- Findings cite catalogue ownership paths and specification identities, not only ticket IDs or commit SHAs.
- Consistency checks compare published meaning statements for the same ID across the assessment scope.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing prior lawful catalogue assessment history to simplify the next report.
- Missing specification identity, missing catalogue identity, or contradictory meaning statements for one ID are defects.
- Programme IX CT lineage may support reconstruction; CCI never treats CT records as a licence to redefine identifier meaning.

---

### CCIO-03 — Preserve namespace integrity

**Definition.** Ensure that catalogue assessments honour published namespace separation among recognised families (CCF-01…CCF-07) and prefix conventions — so overlapping labels are disclosed as collisions or published disambiguations, never silently merged into one mega-vocabulary.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who lets “EC” mean three incompatible things depending on which folder the student opened has abandoned institutional order for convenience.

**Manifestations:**

- Family membership under CCF-01…CCF-07 is named for every material catalogue under assessment.
- Overlapping prefixes across families are assessed under CCI-03 / CCI-04 and reported honestly.
- Published “Catalogue disambiguation” tables are evidence of lawful separation — not licences to collapse meanings.
- Forbidden actions in `CATALOGUE_BOUNDARIES.md` are hard stops.
- Namespace preservation never authorises inventing a new family outside CCF-01…CCF-07 without amendment.

---

### CCIO-04 — Preserve extensibility

**Definition.** Ensure that catalogue integrity findings support honest future growth of published catalogues — so new identifiers may be added only by amending the owning catalogue under its published closure rules, never by invent-in-place, silent renumbering, or absorbing another family’s IDs.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who “just adds a new tip code” mid-lesson without updating the shared catalogue destroys tomorrow’s auditability for today’s convenience.

**Manifestations:**

- Intact findings confirm that published closure / amendment rules remain reconstructable for lawful extension.
- Violated / deferred / escalated findings block honest claims that invent-in-place catalogues are “ready.”
- Extensibility never authorises creating identifiers under CCI pretext.
- Extensibility here means *constitutional catalogue readiness for lawful amendment* — not student exam readiness and not Runtime A feature theatre.
- Preserving extensibility never authorises renumbering existing entries to make room.

---

### CCIO-05 — Preserve traceability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material catalogue integrity assessment leaves a reconstructable, speakable trail: which catalogue families were assessed, which namespace rules were applied, whether collisions were identified, which findings were produced, and which boundaries were preserved.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later say *why* a catalogue was accepted cannot be trusted. Silent green scans destroy student and auditor trust.

**Manifestations:**

- Audit records preserve constitutional references, not only technical scanner logs.
- Explainability obligations in `CATALOGUE_EXPLAINABILITY.md` apply to all material assessments.
- Missing CCI identity, missing CCF family, missing namespace-rule note, missing collision status, or missing finding disposition is a defect.
- Student / developer / auditor projections share one truth with different vocabulary.
- Traceability never authorises publishing catalogue findings as new constitutional law or soft renumbering orders.
- Repeatability: same published catalogues, same families, same CCI criteria → same disposition, independently of language, framework, datastore, CI vendor, or preferred Runtime A shape.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CCIO-01 conflicts with “helping” colliding catalogues look ready | Uniqueness honesty wins — violated / deferred / escalated |
| CCIO-02 conflicts with log volume reduction that erases meaning identity | Consistency wins |
| CCIO-03 conflicts with soft-merging two families into one prefix | Namespace integrity wins |
| CCIO-04 conflicts with invent-in-place IDs or renumbering to “make room” | Lawful extensibility wins — refuse invent / renumber |
| CCIO-05 conflicts with opaque “trust us” catalogue badges | Catalogue traceability wins |

No supporting objective authorises violating CCIO-01.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — Constitution / EIP integrity obligations and Programme VI–XI authority preservation for the catalogue integrity horizon.

---

## 5. Non-Objectives

Constitutional catalogue integrity does **not** optimise for:

- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- CI throughput, flaky-test suppression, or coverage theatre as educational success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when specifications or catalogue declarations are incomplete;
- collapsing CCI categories or CCF families into one mega-check that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics / string-scanner signals as substitutes for published constitutional catalogues;
- creating, renaming, renumbering, or deleting constitutional catalogue identifiers so registries look tidy;
- replacing Programme IX CT lineage preservation, Programme XI / WS1 dependency integrity, or Programme IX–X evaluative / execution horizons;
- treating documentation as optional commentary on whatever code already shipped.

## 6. Closing Statement

> **An assessment that becomes “intact” by renumbering constitutional catalogues is a failed educational system.  
> An assessment that validates only published catalogues — with uniqueness, consistency, namespace integrity, extensibility, and traceability intact — is doing its only catalogue-integrity job.**
