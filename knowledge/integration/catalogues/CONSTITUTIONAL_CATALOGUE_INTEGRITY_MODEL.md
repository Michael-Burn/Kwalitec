## 2. What Constitutional Catalogue Integrity Is

**Constitutional Catalogue Integrity** is the binding mechanism by which **catalogue consistency** throughout the published constitutional corpus may be **defined, validated, and governed**, yielding **catalogue integrity findings** that preserve uniqueness, identifier consistency, namespace separation, numbering integrity, extensibility, and explainability — without becoming a source of constitutional law, a renumbering engine, or a substitute for upstream programme meaning.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Published constitutional corpus** | Constitution, EIP, Programme VI–XI specifications and their subordinate workstream / milestone corpora | What corpus is under catalogue assessment? |
| **Constitutional catalogue** | A published closed set of identifiers that classifies constitutional meaning, stages, criteria, objectives, boundaries, or related assessment artefacts | Which catalogue is being assessed? |
| **Catalogue family** | A recognised grouping of catalogues under CCF-01…CCF-07 for integrity assessment | Which family contains this catalogue? |
| **Identifier uniqueness** | No two published catalogue entries share the same identifier for different constitutional meanings without published disambiguation | Is the ID unique as law requires? |
| **Identifier consistency** | The same identifier always denotes the same published meaning within its owning catalogue and namespace | Does the ID mean one thing? |
| **Namespace separation** | Distinct catalogue families / prefixes remain reconstructably separated; overlapping labels are disclosed collisions, not silent merges | Are namespaces honestly separated? |
| **Numbering integrity** | Published ranges, gaps, and succession rules remain as published; silent renumbering is a defect | Is numbering intact as published? |
| **Extensibility** | Future catalogue entries may be added only by lawful amendment of the owning catalogue — never by silent invent-in-place | Can the catalogue grow lawfully? |
| **Catalogue integrity finding** | Lawful disposition: intact / violated / deferred / escalated — never “new law” and never a renumbering order | Does the catalogue remain lawful? |
| **Constitutional authority** | The exclusive right of published corpora to define educational and execution meaning | Who owns the standard? |
| **Implementation neutrality** | Catalogue validation speaks to constitutional identities, not to a preferred stack as law | Remains the stack replaceable? |
| **Auditability** | Every material assessment leaves reconstructable constitutional traces | Can the judgement be reconstructed? |
| **Explainability** | Families, namespace rules, collisions, findings, and boundaries are speakable | Can the judgement be narrated honestly? |

Constitutional Catalogue Integrity is:

- **validation-centred** — it validates published catalogues; it never authors or renumbers them;
- **uniqueness-bound** — duplicate identifiers for distinct meanings without published disambiguation are hard defects;
- **consistency-preserving** — the same identifier retains one published meaning within its namespace;
- **namespace-honest** — overlapping prefixes across families are reported as collisions or published disambiguations — never silently merged;
- **numbering-faithful** — published numbering remains as published; silent renumbering is forbidden;
- **extensibility-preserving** — growth requires lawful amendment of the owning catalogue;
- **catalogue-closed** — only CCI-01…CCI-07 may be applied as constitutional catalogue integrity categories;
- **family-bound** — material assessments classify under recognised CCF-01…CCF-07 families;
- **corpus-bound** — every material assessment cites identifiable published specifications and catalogue identities;
- **finding-producing** — assessments emit intact / violated / deferred / escalated dispositions;
- **audit-capable** — every material assessment leaves reconstructable constitutional traces;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which families were assessed under which namespace rules with which collisions and findings;
- **non-legislative** — a catalogue finding never becomes constitutional law or a soft amendment;
- **non-mutating** — the model never modifies constitutional specifications or catalogue identifiers under validation pretext;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by catalogue law.

Constitutional Catalogue Integrity is **not**:

- a second Educational Constitution, EIP, or Programme VI–X amendment path;
- a licence to reinterpret coach meaning, ownership maps, tip warrants, runtime contracts, conformance criteria, verification meaning, compliance meaning, certification meaning, evolution meaning, execution meaning, or dependency meaning in catalogue prose;
- a testing framework, CI/CD pipeline, GitHub Action, string scanner, or OpenAPI linker;
- a claim that “catalogue intact” guarantees learning, mastery, exam readiness, product success, perpetual conformity, or frozen stack authority;
- a substitute for Programme IX MS002 constitutional traceability — CT preserves spec/artefact/finding lineage; CCI validates *constitutional catalogue identifier integrity*;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS1 dependency integrity — CDI validates *corpus dependency structure*; CCI validates *catalogue uniqueness and namespace honesty*;
- a substitute for Programme IX conformance / verification / compliance / certification — those evaluate implementations and obligations; CCI validates *catalogue corpus structure*;
- a substitute for Programme X execution / decision / audit / explainability — those organise constitutional activity; CCI never executes, decides, audits, or explains educational outcomes in their place;
- a licence to create, rename, renumber, invent, or delete constitutional catalogue identifiers under “integrity” pretext.

## 3. Catalogue Integrity Posture (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material constitutional catalogue integrity assessment travels through three constitutional phases. These are **normative assessment obligations**, not an implementation architecture.

```
Identify published catalogues and families under assessment
        │
        ▼
Validate uniqueness, consistency, namespaces, numbering, and extensibility under CCI-01…CCI-07
        │
        ├── violated / deferred / escalated (lawful finding — catalogues unchanged)
        │
        └── intact → Emit findings and preserve audit records
                │
                ▼
        Explain families / namespace rules / collisions / findings / boundaries
```

| Phase | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **Identify** | Name only published constitutional catalogue identities and recognised CCF families | Catalogue is unpublished tribal knowledge, invented prefix, or “how docs label today” as law |
| **Validate** | Apply CCI-01…CCI-07 for uniqueness, consistency, namespace separation, numbering, and extensibility | Assessment would invent law, renumber IDs, redefine meanings, or privilege a stack as law |
| **Record** | Produce findings and audit-speakable trails without amending corpora or mutating identifiers | Path would treat findings as constitutional amendments or soft renumbering orders |

> **Catalogue integrity never “best-effort invents” a fourth phase called law-making, renumbering, or stack canonisation.**

## 6. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Catalogue Integrity Model exists so that:

1. **Identifiers remain unique** — no silent reuse of the same ID for incompatible constitutional meanings.
2. **Consistency remains honest** — the same ID continues to mean what its owning catalogue published.
3. **Namespaces remain separated** — families and prefixes do not silently collapse into one mega-vocabulary.
4. **Numbering remains intact** — catalogues are not quietly renumbered to paper over collisions.
5. **Extensibility remains lawful** — growth happens by amending the owning catalogue, never by invent-in-place.
6. **Traceability remains reconstructable** — every material catalogue assessment can name families, rules, collisions, and findings.
7. **Students remain protected** — product urgency cannot launder colliding catalogues as “approved architecture.”
