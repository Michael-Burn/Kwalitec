## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which catalogue families* were checked — which namespace rules applied, whether collisions were found, what finding resulted, and which limits were preserved — is not coaching; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–X have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, runtime, governance, and execution speech speakable, and after Programme XI / WS1 has made dependency speech speakable, Programme XI still needs one catalogue-speech answer:

> **“Which catalogue families were assessed, which namespace rules were applied, were any collisions identified, which integrity findings were produced, and which constitutional boundaries were preserved?”**

That answer must cover every material catalogue integrity assessment without inventing educational meaning, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, renaming identifiers, renumbering catalogues, freezing a stack as law, inventing unpublished CCI / CCF types, or presenting intact status as educational quality or student readiness.

## 2. What Catalogue Explainability Is

**Constitutional Catalogue Integrity Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **validation of named constitutional catalogues under published CCF families and CCI categories** — not to invent a second tutor voice from string scanners, and not to grade the student.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Catalogue families assessed** | Named CCF-01…CCF-07 memberships and the catalogues under validation | Which catalogue families were assessed? |
| **Namespace rules applied** | Published CCIR / family prefix / disambiguation rules used | Which namespace rules were applied? |
| **Collision status** | none / disambiguated / collided / inconclusive | Were any collisions identified? |
| **Integrity findings** | intact / violated / deferred / escalated (with composition notes) | Which findings were produced? |
| **Boundaries preserved** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden rename / renumber / redefine / amend / authority-creation did not occur | Which constitutional boundaries were preserved? |

Catalogue Explainability is:

- **validation-centred** — it narrates families → namespace rules → collisions → findings under law;
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CCF / CCI identities;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published specifications and catalogues;
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to amend corpora or renumber IDs;
- **quality-honest** — intact status is never narrated as mastery, readiness, or educational quality;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference or scanner green as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence speech, Programme IX governance speech, Programme X execution / decision / audit / explanation speech, or Programme XI / WS1 dependency speech;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match CCI-07 reconstruction needs;
- **repeatability-honest** — explanations must not paper over race-dependent dispositions.

Catalogue Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–XI meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a substitute for Programme IX CT explainability, Programme XI / WS1 dependency explainability, or Programme X explainability architecture corpora;
- a rendering system, UI template, CI badge, log shipper, catalogue-visualisation product, or analytics dashboard;
- a claim that clear catalogue speech guarantees learning or a pass;
- a licence to present string-scan greens, grep uniqueness, or pipeline badges as constitutional proof or educational quality;
- a licence to narrate renumbering as “integrity repairs.”

## 3. Mandatory Explanation Questions

Every material constitutional catalogue integrity assessment should answer all five questions:

### CCIEQ-01 — Which catalogue families were assessed?

Name the primary CCF-01…CCF-07 families and the published catalogues under validation (for example CCF-04 runtime EC/EV alongside CCF-05 governance CC/CT; CCF-07 CDI alongside this CCI corpus; CCF-02 educational strategy catalogues). Unpublished tribal adjacency and “neighbouring folders” are not constitutional catalogue families.

### CCIEQ-02 — Which namespace rules were applied?

Name the reconstructable namespace rules applied: relevant CCIR-01…CCIR-10 rules, family prefix expectations from `CATALOGUE_FAMILIES.md`, and any published “Catalogue disambiguation” tables consulted. Missing rule identity is a defect to disclose — not a gap to invent. “Supported by ticket #1234” or “supported by grep” is not a namespace rule.

### CCIEQ-03 — Were any collisions identified?

Distinguish:

- **None** — no overlapping identifiers for incompatible meanings were found among the assessed catalogues under published criteria.
- **Disambiguated** — overlapping labels exist but published disambiguation separates meanings by owning namespace / corpus; disclose the disambiguation identity.
- **Collided** — name the colliding identifiers and owning corpora at constitutional identity granularity (not only file paths).
- **Inconclusive** — identities or ownership incomplete; disposition must be deferred / escalated, not silently intact.

Published disambiguation is not automatic proof that two labels mean the same thing; it proves lawful separation.

### CCIEQ-04 — Which findings were produced?

Distinguish:

- **intact** — published catalogues satisfy applicable CCF / CCI categories and CCIR rules for the assessment scope;
- **violated** — uniqueness, consistency, namespace separation, collision honesty, numbering, extensibility, or catalogue rules failed;
- **deferred** — lawful pause pending identifiable missing published inputs;
- **escalated** — requires corpus governance / human constitutional review beyond ordinary assessment.

Composition notes must name which CCI-01…CCI-07 categories and CCF-01…CCF-07 families bound the finding. “Catalogues look fine” is not a finding.

### CCIEQ-05 — Which constitutional boundaries were preserved?

Confirm explicitly that the assessment did **not**:

- create catalogue identifiers or renumber / rename existing catalogues;
- redefine catalogue meanings;
- amend constitutional specifications;
- create constitutional authority or new constitutional law;
- substitute Programme XI or any assessed corpus for upstream authority;
- narrate intact status as educational quality, mastery, or student exam readiness;
- privilege a technology, scanner, or CI vendor as constitutional law.

Failed boundary confirmation → the explanation is constitutionally defective even if a disposition string exists.

| **Auditor-facing** | Namespace rule IDs, disambiguation citations, CCI-07 record identity, boundary checklist | Reconstructability; continuity with prior findings |

Projections may simplify vocabulary. They may **not** simplify away collision status, namespace-rule gaps, or boundary failures.

## 5. Explanation Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why defective | Lawful alternative |
|--------------|---------------|--------------------|
| “Catalogues are fine because CI is green” | Proxy theatre | Cite CCF / CCI categories, catalogue identities, and findings |
| “We fixed integrity by renumbering CC to CCx” | Mutation under explanation pretext | Report collision; propose upstream amendment |
| “Intact means the student is ready” | Quality conflation | Keep catalogue integrity distinct from student readiness |
| “Namespace rule is the Jira epic” | Non-constitutional origin | Cite Constitution / EIP / programme paths and CCIR IDs |
| “No duplicates in grep output” | Wrong subject horizon | Map to published catalogue identities or refuse |
| “Programme XI now defines Programme VI IDs” | Authority substitution | Preserve VI meaning; XI validates only |
| “Temporary renumber for the demo” | Emergency exemption | Deferred / escalated / violated — never demo law |
| “EC means the same everywhere” | Silent merge | Disclose runtime EC vs EVF EC disambiguation or collision |
| “Trust us — the prefixes are obvious” | Opacity | Answer CCIEQ-01…CCIEQ-05 explicitly |
