## 1. Purpose

Assessment that silently becomes a second constitution — rewriting “must not” into “may,” inventing authority in “findings,” elevating Programme XI into an authorship layer, or treating a preferred operational limit as the new standard — destroys student trust and educational integrity.

This document draws a bright line between **lawful constitutional boundary integrity** (Programme XI / WS3 / MS001) and **constitutional authorship / educational judgement / boundary mutation** (Constitution / EIP / Programmes VI–XI / permitted writers).

---

## 2. Boundary Principles

1. **Validate, do not legislate.** Boundary integrity measures boundary consistency; it does not create law.
2. **Published boundaries only.** Unpublished customs and “main as limit” are hard stops as standards.
3. **Published boundary categories only.** Unpublished CBI customs are unlawful.
4. **Named corpus identities only.** Assessments without identifiable constitutional subjects are theatre.
5. **Exactly as published.** Successful intact status never rewrites specification meaning, responsibilities, or constraints.
6. **Authority first.** Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard.
7. **No boundary mutation.** Findings never create, soften, invent, delete, or soft-amend constitutional boundaries.
8. **No responsibility rewrite.** Duty maps remain with their owning corpora.
9. **No new authority.** Programme XI never becomes foundational law by validating others.
10. **No specification amendment.** Soft-amendment via findings destroys integrity.
11. **No reinterpretation.** Programme VI–X meanings and EIP claim classes survive assessment intact.
12. **Constraint honesty.** Contradictions are reported, not “fixed” in situ by softening.
13. **Intact ≠ quality.** Boundary integrity never grades learning, mastery, readiness, or product success.
14. **Implementation independence.** No stack, vendor, or scanner becomes law by proximity.
15. **No bypass.** Product urgency never waives constitutional boundary integrity requirements.
16. **Repeatability.** Race-dependent dispositions are a defect.
17. **Explain the stop.** Students, developers, and auditors should hear when assessment must refuse intact status.
18. **No emergency exemption.** Deadlines and demos never mint constitutional amendment power via findings.

## 3. What Boundary Integrity May Do (Lawful)

| Lawful action | Constitutional meaning |
|---------------|------------------------|
| **Analyse constitutional boundaries** | Inspect published corpus identities and published boundary statements under CBI-01…CBI-07 |
| **Validate authority boundaries** | Confirm defining / owning / deciding limits under CBI-01 / CBIR-01 |
| **Validate responsibility boundaries** | Confirm duty allocation and non-absorption under CBI-02 / CBIR-02 |
| **Validate behavioural constraints** | Confirm may / must / must-not consistency under CBI-03 / CBIR-03 |
| **Validate consumption boundaries** | Confirm lawful consumption under CBI-04 / CBIR-04 |
| **Validate production boundaries** | Confirm lawful production under CBI-05 / CBIR-05 |
| **Validate layer / isolation boundaries** | Confirm separation of concerns under CBI-06 / CBIR-06 |
| **Identify boundary findings** | Name authority absorption, duty collisions, constraint contradictions, consumption / production overreach, or layer inversions |
| **Report integrity observations** | Emit intact / violated / deferred / escalated under published criteria |
| **Preserve audit records** | Keep reconstructable trails under CBI-07 |
| **Refuse unlawful intact claims** | Prefer honest violated / deferred / escalated over improvisation |
| **Compose required categories** | Apply published CBI conjunctions without collapsing failed checks |
| **Remain repeatable and neutral** | Same published inputs → same disposition; no stack privilege |
| **Explain boundary integrity** | Answer boundaries / constraints / separations / findings / assessment limits without rewriting meaning |
| **Propose corpus amendments upstream** | Recommend (not enact) amendments when published boundaries themselves must change |

These actions **validate boundary consistency**. They do **not** publish a new Constitution, invent coach warrants, mint tips, author ownership maps, invent CBI types, amend programme law, redefine responsibilities, soften constraints, or judge educational quality.

## 4. What Boundary Integrity Must NEVER Do

| Forbidden action | Why | Lawful alternative |
|------------------|-----|--------------------|
| **Redefine constitutional authority** | Only corpus governance may change published authority | Leave authority unchanged; report absorption; propose amendment upstream |
| **Modify responsibilities** | Duty owners own responsibility maps | Leave duties unchanged; report collisions / absorption |
| **Alter constitutional constraints** | Silent softening destroys integrity | Leave may / must / must-not unchanged; report contradictions |
| **Amend constitutional specifications** | Soft-amendment via findings destroys integrity | Leave specs unchanged; mark violated / deferred / escalated |
| **Create constitutional authority** | Findings are not legislation; Programme XI is not foundational law | Cite published authority only; refuse unpublished standards |
| **Create constitutional law** | Boundary integrity is validation, not authorship | Cite published law only |
| **Reinterpret constitutional meaning** | EIP and programme owners own interpretation boundaries | Obey published law; do not reinterpret under boundary pretext |
| **Redefine constitutional responsibilities** | Meaning / ownership owners own duty allocation | Confirm published duties; never rewrite them in findings |
| **Substitute constitutional authority** | Published corpora remain the standard after any finding | Keep assessed corpora as subjects, never as next-cycle law |
| **Bypass boundary integrity requirements** | Requirements are educational care, not optional polish | Prefer understatement / violated over invented green |
| **Privilege a technology as constitutional** | Implementation neutrality | Evaluate boundaries, not Flask / npm / scanner fashion |
| **Determine educational quality** | No CBI is a quality rubric | Keep integrity speech distinct from quality speech |
| **Determine constitutional truth** | Constitution / EIP own truth | Obey published law; do not adjudicate truth in boundary machinery |
| **Replace Programme IX CT / CC / CV / CCM / CRT** | Distinct governance horizons | Compose; do not conflate or absorb |
| **Replace Programme XI / WS1 CDI** | Distinct assurance horizon | Validate boundaries; never rewrite dependency structure |
| **Replace Programme XI / WS2 CCI** | Distinct assurance horizon | Validate boundaries; never renumber catalogues |
| **Replace Programme X execution / decision / audit / explainability** | Distinct activity horizons | Validate boundaries; never execute those activities |
| **Transfer authority** | Programme VII WS2 owns ownership, permission, and conflict disposition | Apply Authority corpora; refuse domain absorption via findings |
| **Invent educational state** | Programme VII WS4 owns EST/CST context law | Evaluate against published postures only |
| **Generate unpublished recommendations** | Programme VII WS3 + Programme VI owners own tip warrant | Surface integrity findings, not new tips |
| **Treat Twin / Adaptive / UI / CI / boundary scans as constitutional producers** | They are subjects / delivery surfaces | Require published boundary mapping or refuse |
| **Present scanner greens / grep consistency / coverage as constitutional integrity** | Operational proxies are not boundary law | Cite published corpora and CBI types |
| **Become irreplaceable by assessment topology** | Replaceability | Keep boundary law implementation-independent |

## 6. Boundary Checks (Pre-Finding)

Before material constitutional boundary integrity is treated as established, assessment requires affirmative answers:

| Check | Question |
|-------|----------|
| **CBIB-01 Corpus identity** | Are assessed subjects published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–XI corpus identities (not unpublished customs)? |
| **CBIB-02 Boundary class** | Are required CBI-01…CBI-07 categories being applied (not unpublished customs)? |
| **CBIB-03 Boundary identity** | Which named published boundary statements are under assessment? |
| **CBIB-04 Authority** | Are authority boundaries confirmed intact or honestly reported absorbed / missing? |
| **CBIB-05 Responsibility** | Are responsibility separations confirmed intact or honestly reported collided / absorbed? |
| **CBIB-06 Constraints** | Have may / must / must-not constraints been evaluated for consistency? |
| **CBIB-07 Consumption** | Do consumption claims match published consumption rights? |
| **CBIB-08 Production** | Do production claims match published production rights? |
| **CBIB-09 Layer / isolation** | Does the assessment preserve published programme / workstream / milestone separation of concerns? |
| **CBIB-10 Non-legislation** | Does the assessment avoid creating constitutional authority or amending specifications? |
| **CBIB-11 Non-mutation** | Does the assessment avoid altering, inventing, softening, or deleting constitutional boundaries / responsibilities / constraints? |
| **CBIB-12 Non-reinterpretation** | Does the assessment avoid redefining Programme VI–X / EIP meaning? |
| **CBIB-13 Non-substitution** | Does the assessment avoid treating Programme XI findings or any assessed corpus as the next constitutional standard? |
| **CBIB-14 Neutrality / intact ≠ quality** | Does the assessment avoid stack privilege and quality / student-readiness conflation? |
| **CBIB-15 Repeatability / audit** | Is disposition reproducible; will CBI-07 / explainability records preserve boundaries, constraints, separations, findings, and assessment limits? |

Any failed check → violated / deferred / escalated. Do not “best-effort” invent boundary law, soften constraints, or soft-amend specifications in situ.
