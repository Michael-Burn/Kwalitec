Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not declare a syllabus “bounded” because a pipeline skipped authority-boundary validation, quietly softened “must not” into “may” mid-assessment so reports look clean, rewrite responsibilities so today’s scanner passes, or treat a green lifecycle badge as new constitutional law that freezes the stack. After the Constitution and EIP have defined *educational law*, after Programmes VI–X have published *meaning, orchestration, runtime, governance, and execution law* with published boundaries, after Programme XI / WS1–WS2 have validated *dependency structure* and *catalogue consistency*, and after WS3 / MS001 has defined *how boundaries are validated under CBI-01…CBI-07*, the platform still needs one lifecycle answer:

> **“How are constitutional boundary integrity assessments organised from initiation through publication?”**

That answer must ensure assessments initiate against published corpora; discover named boundary statements; validate authority boundaries; validate responsibility boundaries; validate behavioural constraints and consumption / production boundaries; validate constitutional layer separation; construct boundary integrity findings; and publish those findings — while remaining fully repeatable, consistent, implementation-neutral, auditable, and explainable — without creating constitutional authority, redefining constitutional responsibilities, reinterpreting constitutional meaning, or amending constitutional specifications.

This document records that posture so every future boundary integrity lifecycle (and successor) has a single constitutional reference for *how boundary assessment work may be sequenced without becoming law, a responsibility rewriter, or a second constitution*.

> **The Constitutional Boundary Integrity Lifecycle Model describes how boundary assessment activities are lawfully coordinated from initiation through publication.  
> It does not create educational meaning, invent ownership, mint tips, invent runtime behaviour, amend law, redefine responsibilities, or become constitutional authority.**

## 2. What the Constitutional Boundary Integrity Lifecycle Is

The **constitutional boundary integrity lifecycle** is the binding organisation by which **boundary integrity assessments** may proceed through recognised stages — initiating against published corpus identities, discovering published boundary statements, validating authority / responsibility / behavioural / consumption / production / layer boundaries under published CBI-01…CBI-07 categories, and constructing / publishing **boundary integrity findings** that preserve authority integrity, responsibility separation, constraint consistency, consumption / production honesty, layer isolation, repeatability, consistency, implementation neutrality, auditability, and explainability — without becoming a source of constitutional law, a rewriter of responsibilities or constraints, a substitute for MS001 boundary meaning, or a replacement for upstream programme authority.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Published constitutional corpus** | Constitution, EIP, Programme VI–XI specifications and their subordinate workstream / milestone corpora | Which corpus may the lifecycle organise assessment against? |
| **Constitutional boundary** | A published statement of authority, responsibility, behavioural constraint, consumption right, production right, or layer separation | Which boundary is under assessment? |
| **Authority boundary** | Published limit on who may define, own, or decide under constitutional law | Who may lawfully exercise this authority? |
| **Responsibility boundary** | Published allocation of duties that must not be absorbed by a foreign horizon | Which responsibilities remain separated? |
| **Behavioural constraint** | Published “may”, “must”, or “must not” obligation governing constitutional behaviour | Which constraints bind this corpus? |
| **Consumption / production boundary** | Published limits on what a corpus may lawfully consume or produce | What may be consumed or produced? |
| **Layer boundary** | Published separation of concerns among Constitution / EIP and Programmes VI–XI | Is separation of concerns intact? |
| **Lifecycle stage** | A recognised CBIL-01…CBIL-08 organisational step | Which coordination step is active? |
| **Boundary integrity finding** | Lawful disposition: intact / violated / deferred / escalated — never “new law” and never a rewrite of published boundaries | Does the boundary remain lawful? |
| **Assessment record / publication artefact** | Audit-speakable record of boundaries, constraints, stages, findings, and preserved limits | Can the assessment be reconstructed? |
| **Constitutional authority** | The exclusive right of published corpora to define educational and execution meaning | Who owns the standard? |
| **Implementation neutrality** | Lifecycle coordination is judged against law and published boundaries, not against a preferred stack, vendor, or topology | Remains the stack replaceable? |
| **Repeatability** | Same published corpora, same discovered boundaries, same stage sequence → same disposition | Can the judgement be reproduced? |
| **Auditability** | Every material lifecycle leaves reconstructable constitutional traces | Can the judgement be reconstructed? |
| **Explainability** | Boundaries, authority / responsibility validations, constraints, findings, stages, and limits are speakable | Can the judgement be narrated honestly? |

The constitutional boundary integrity lifecycle is:

- **coordination-centred** — it organises boundary assessments; it never authors constitutional obligations or boundaries;
- **discovery-bound** — it discovers published boundary statements; it never invents boundaries to force progress;
- **authority-preserving** — published authority limits remain reconstructable and unabsorbed across stages;
- **responsibility-preserving** — published duty allocations remain separated as published;
- **constraint-faithful** — “may”, “must”, and “must not” statements remain as published; silent softening is a defect;
- **consumption-honest** — corpora may consume only what published law permits;
- **production-honest** — corpora may produce only what published law permits;
- **layering-preserving** — constitutional separation of concerns remains intact;
- **catalogue-closed** — only CBIL-01…CBIL-08 may be applied as constitutional boundary integrity lifecycle stages;
- **type-faithful** — stages apply only published CBI-01…CBI-07 categories and CBIR validation rules from WS3 / MS001; they never invent categories;
- **finding-producing** — assessments emit intact / violated / deferred / escalated dispositions;
- **publication-honest** — findings are published under honesty rules; publication never rewrites boundaries or becomes law;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by lifecycle law;
- **corpus-bound** — every material lifecycle cites identifiable published specifications and boundary statements;
- **audit-capable** — every material lifecycle leaves reconstructable constitutional traces;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which boundaries were assessed under which constraints with which findings;
- **non-legislative** — a boundary finding never becomes constitutional law or a soft amendment;
- **non-substitutive** — lifecycle machinery never becomes the constitutional standard for the next judgement;
- **non-mutating** — the lifecycle never modifies constitutional specifications, responsibilities, or constraints under coordination pretext.

The constitutional boundary integrity lifecycle is **not**:

- a second Educational Constitution, EIP, Programme VI–X, or WS3 / MS001 amendment path;
- a licence to reinterpret coach meaning, ownership maps, tip warrants, runtime contracts, conformance criteria, traceability meaning, verification meaning, compliance meaning, certification meaning, evolution meaning, execution meaning, dependency meaning, catalogue meaning, or CBI categories in lifecycle prose;
- a testing framework, CI/CD pipeline, GitHub Action, string scanner, or OpenAPI linker;
- a claim that “lifecycle complete” guarantees learning, mastery, exam readiness, product success, perpetual conformity, or frozen stack authority;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS3 / MS001 boundary integrity meaning — CBI defines categories and validation; CBIL sequences when assessment occurs;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS1 dependency integrity — CDI / CDIL validate and sequence *corpus dependency structure*; CBIL coordinates *boundary consistency assessment*;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS2 catalogue integrity — CCI / CCIL validate and sequence *catalogue uniqueness*; CBIL coordinates *boundary consistency assessment*;
- a substitute for Programme IX MS002 constitutional traceability — CT preserves spec/artefact/finding lineage; CBIL coordinates *constitutional boundary integrity assessment sequencing*;
- a substitute for Programme IX conformance / verification / compliance / certification — those evaluate implementations and obligations; CBIL validates *boundary corpus consistency sequencing*;
- a substitute for Programme X execution / decision / audit / explainability — those organise constitutional activity; CBIL never executes, decides, audits, or explains educational outcomes in their place;
- a licence to create, soften, invent, redefine, or delete constitutional boundaries, responsibilities, or constraints under “lifecycle” pretext.

## 3. Lifecycle Posture (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material constitutional boundary integrity lifecycle travels through eight constitutional stages. These are **normative coordination obligations**, not an implementation architecture and not a CI job graph.

```
CBIL-01 Assessment Initiation
        │
        ▼
CBIL-02 Boundary Discovery
        │
        ▼
CBIL-03 Authority Validation
        │
        ▼
CBIL-04 Responsibility Validation
        │
        ▼
CBIL-05 Constraint Validation
        │  (behavioural + consumption + production)
        ▼
CBIL-06 Layer Validation
        │
        ├── violated / deferred / escalated (lawful finding — boundaries unchanged)
        │
        └── intact (or other lawful disposition) →
                │
                ▼
CBIL-07 Finding Construction
        │
        ▼
CBIL-08 Finding Publication
        │
        ▼
Explain boundaries / authority / responsibility / constraints / findings / intact limits
```

| Stage | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **CBIL-01 Assessment Initiation** | Open a boundary assessment concern against named published corpora and scope | Scope is unpublished custom, tribal knowledge, or “how main behaves today” |
| **CBIL-02 Boundary Discovery** | Identify published boundary statements under CBI-01…CBI-07 | Boundaries are invented, unpublished, or would require rewriting statements to “discover” them |
| **CBIL-03 Authority Validation** | Validate published authority boundaries (who may define, own, or decide) | Validation would invent authority, absorb superior authority, privilege a stack as law, or rewrite statements |
| **CBIL-04 Responsibility Validation** | Validate published responsibility boundaries and non-absorption | Validation would redefine duties, invent dual exclusive ownership, or rewrite responsibility statements |
| **CBIL-05 Constraint Validation** | Validate behavioural may / must / must-not and consumption / production boundaries | Validation would soften constraints, invent consumption / production rights, or mutate statements |
| **CBIL-06 Layer Validation** | Confirm Constitution / EIP → VI–XI (and lawful workstream) layer separation | Validation would absorb superior authority into a subordinate or invent a new layer |
| **CBIL-07 Finding Construction** | Emit intact / violated / deferred / escalated findings under published criteria | Findings would become amendments, educational quality grades, or boundary rewrites |
| **CBIL-08 Finding Publication** | Publish findings and assessment records under published honesty rules | Publication would invent intact status, hide constraint softening, rewrite boundaries, or freeze a stack as law |

> **The lifecycle never “best-effort invents” a ninth stage called law-making, responsibility rewriting, constraint softening, stack canonisation, or boundary mutation.**

## 6. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Boundary Integrity Lifecycle Model exists so that:

1. **Law remains upstream of coordination** — stages organise assessment against published corpora; progress does not quietly become the specifications.
2. **Discovery remains honest** — discovery names published boundary statements; it does not invent boundaries to force intact status.
3. **Authority remains reconstructable** — authority validation confirms published who-may-decide limits; it does not invent missing authority or absorb superiors.
4. **Responsibility remains separated** — responsibility validation confirms published duty allocations; it never redefines them for convenience.
5. **Constraints remain faithful** — constraint validation reports may / must / must-not and consumption / production honesty; it never softens constraints in situ.
6. **Layering remains intact** — layer validation preserves Constitution / EIP and Programme order through Programme XI.
7. **Findings remain sequenced** — intact / violated / deferred / escalated dispositions arise after lawful initiation, discovery, and validation — not as random green theatre.
8. **Authority remains honest** — no team may treat a published finding as the new constitutional source.
9. **Implementations remain replaceable** — lifecycle law does not freeze Flask, SQLAlchemy, a particular API shape, or a CI vendor as law.
10. **Repeatability remains honest** — the same published corpora, discovered boundaries, and stage sequence yield the same disposition.
11. **Audit remains possible** — every material lifecycle can be reconstructed against named boundaries, stages, constraints, and findings.
12. **Explainability remains intact** — lifecycle speech describes coordination of boundary assessment; it does not redefine what learning means.
13. **Students remain protected** — product theatre cannot launder contradicted or softened boundaries as “lifecycle intact.”
