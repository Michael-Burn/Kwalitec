## 1. Purpose

Boundary assessment without a closed stage catalogue invents law by proximity: whichever scanner step, ticket status, or reviewer habit happens to finish becomes the tutor’s “proof the system ran a constitutional boundary integrity assessment.”

This catalogue names the only lawful constitutional boundary integrity lifecycle stages — and binds each stage to purpose, inputs, outputs, and permitted / prohibited activities.

---

## 2. Stage Principles

1. **Ordered and skip-disciplined.** Stages may be brief under thin scope; mandatory initiation, discovery, authority / responsibility / constraint / layer validation, finding construction, and publication obligations may not be silently omitted when a boundary integrity finding is claimed.
2. **Published boundaries before findings.** Identifiable published corpora and discovered boundary statements are confirmed before intact / violated dispositions are constructed.
3. **Explainability accumulates.** Each stage leaves traceable artefacts for `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.
4. **Refusal is a stage outcome.** Violated, deferred, escalated, and refuse-progress are first-class conclusions.
5. **Deterministic transitions.** Same published inputs ⇒ same next stage outcomes.
6. **Stages coordinate; they do not legislate.** No stage creates, modifies, or redefines constitutional law.
7. **Stages do not become constitutional authority.** Publication never freezes a corpus topology as permanently lawful or replaces published corpora.
8. **Stages do not rewrite boundaries.** Findings report consistency; they never create, soften, redefine, or delete boundary statements.
9. **Stages apply published CBI / CBIR law.** Categories and validation rules remain owned by WS3 / MS001.

## 3. Catalogue Overview

| ID | Stage | Constitutional purpose (short) | Primary inputs | Primary outputs |
|----|-------|--------------------------------|----------------|-----------------|
| **CBIL-01** | Assessment Initiation | Open a boundary assessment against named published corpora and scope | Published corpus identities + concern scope + optional CBI indication | Initiated concern record (or refuse-open) |

| **CBIL-03** | Authority Validation | Validate published authority boundaries | Discovered boundaries + published authority order | Validated authority-boundary record (or refuse-validate) |
| **CBIL-04** | Responsibility Validation | Validate published responsibility boundaries and non-absorption | Authority-validated bundle + published responsibility statements | Validated responsibility-boundary record (or refuse-validate) |

Material boundary assessment claiming constitutional findings must map to this sequence as published law requires. Stages may be brief; none may invent a stage outside this catalogue.

**Relation to CBI / CBIR / CBIO / CT / CC / CDI / CCI / EV:** Those catalogues remain defined solely by their owners. CBIL stages *organise when* boundary assessment activities occur; they do not replace, reclassify, invent, amend, or rewrite those catalogues.

**Constraint stage scope:** CBIL-05 applies CBI-03 (Behavioural Constraints), CBI-04 (Consumption Boundaries), and CBI-05 (Production Boundaries) under published CBIR rules — covering the lifecycle’s behavioural, consumption, and production validation duties within one recognised stage.

## 5. Stage Definitions

### CBIL-01 — Assessment Initiation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Open a material boundary integrity assessment concern by naming the published constitutional corpora under assessment and the scope of the concern — without inventing law, opening against unpublished customs, or treating initiation as an intact finding.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Identifiable published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–XI specification references for the concern scope
- Concern identity (what boundary-integrity question is under assessment)
- Optional published indication of required CBI-01…CBI-07 categories for the scope
- Optional identifiable supporting authority / lineage references (not rewritten)

**Constitutional outputs**

- An initiated assessment concern record naming corpora, specifications, scope, and intended CBI type set — **or**
- Lawful refuse-open when standards are unpublished, scope is theatrical, or initiation would invent law

**Permitted activities**

- Name published corpora and specifications as assessment standards
- Bound the concern scope without amending corpora
- Record intended CBI types from the published catalogue
- Refuse initiation when constitutional preconditions fail
- Preserve initiation audit identity for later stages

**Prohibited activities**

- Treat “how main behaves,” tribal knowledge, or stack preference as the standard
- Create or modify constitutional specifications by opening a concern
- Issue an intact / violated finding at initiation
- Invent unpublished boundary categories
- Skip initiation when a material finding will later be claimed
- Create constitutional authority or rewrite boundaries at initiation

**Authority preserved**  
Published corpora remain the sole standard. Initiation organises; it does not legislate.

---

### CBIL-02 — Boundary Discovery

**Constitutional purpose**  
Identify **published boundary statements** among the named corpora under recognised CBI-01…CBI-07 categories — so later stages assess real published boundaries, not anonymous theatre — without inventing, softening, or deleting statements.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Initiated concern from CBIL-01
- Candidate published boundary identities (authority, responsibility, behavioural, consumption, production, layer statements, or assessment-integrity records as published)
- Published CBI-01…CBI-07 category definitions from WS3 / MS001
- Optional supporting CT lineage identities already established under upstream law (unchanged)

**Constitutional outputs**

- A discovered boundary set bound to the concern and intended CBI types — **or**
- Lawful refuse-discover when boundaries are missing, forged, invented, unpublished, or would require unlawful mutation to “discover”

**Permitted activities**

- Identify and name published boundary statements
- Classify boundaries under published CBI-01…CBI-07 only
- Bind discovered boundaries to the initiated concern
- Refuse discovery when boundaries cannot be honestly affirmed as published
- Preserve discovery records for audit and explainability

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent missing boundary statements
- Soften, redefine, or delete boundaries to force later intact status
- Treat string scanners, ticket links, or CI noise as constitutional boundaries without published mapping
- Redefine educational or constitutional meaning while “discovering”
- Present discovery as itself an intact finding or a freeze of stack authority
- Create constitutional authority under discovery pretence

**Authority preserved**  
Published boundaries remain upstream facts. Discovery organises identity; it does not author boundaries or educational truth.

---

### CBIL-03 — Authority Validation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Validate that each discovered **authority boundary** remains reconstructable to its published owning corpus and that no subordinate horizon silently absorbs superior authority — without inventing missing authority, rewriting statements, or privileging a stack as law.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Discovered boundary set from CBIL-02 (especially CBI-01 candidates)
- Published constitutional authority order (Constitution / EIP → VI → VII → VIII → IX → X → XI and lawful workstream subordination)
- Published CBIR authority-fidelity rules from WS3 / MS001
- Optional supporting lineage / authority references consumed as inputs (unchanged)

**Constitutional outputs**

- A validated authority-boundary record per assessed authority statement — **or**
- Lawful refuse-validate when authority cannot be reconstructed, is invented, is absorbed into a subordinate, or validation would require rewriting law

**Permitted activities**

- Confirm reconstructable published authority boundaries
- Detect authority absorption that would let a subordinate become practical superior of its own superior
- Prefer honest refuse-validate / deferred / escalated over inventing missing authority
- Remain repeatable and implementation-neutral
- Preserve validation records for later stages

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent missing authority statements or ownership links
- Absorb superior authority into a subordinate via lifecycle prose
- Modify constitutional specifications to force validation
- Privilege a technology stack as constitutional
- Redefine Programme VI–X meaning or CBI meaning in validation prose
- Present authority validation as new constitutional law
- Mutate boundary statements during validation
- Create constitutional authority under validation pretence

**Authority preserved**  
WS3 / MS001 owns authority-boundary validation rules. This stage applies them; it does not amend the catalogue or become constitutional authority.

---

### CBIL-04 — Responsibility Validation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Validate that each discovered **responsibility boundary** retains its published duty owner and that exclusive duties are not silently transferred or dual-claimed without published warrant — without redefining responsibilities or rewriting statements.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Authority-validated bundle from CBIL-03
- Discovered responsibility-boundary identities (CBI-02 candidates)
- Published CBIR responsibility-fidelity rules from WS3 / MS001
- Published horizon ownership / duty allocations as stated in corpora

**Constitutional outputs**

- A validated responsibility-boundary record per assessed duty statement — **or**
- Lawful refuse-validate when responsibilities cannot be honestly confirmed, are redefined in situ, or would require inventing dual exclusive ownership

**Permitted activities**

- Confirm published responsibility allocations and non-absorption
- Identify silent duty transfer or dual exclusive ownership without published warrant
- Prefer honest refuse-validate / deferred / escalated over redefining duties
- Preserve responsibility-validation records for later stages

**Prohibited activities**

- Redefine constitutional responsibilities under validation pretence
- Invent missing duty statements to force intact status
- Soft-transfer exclusive duties between horizons via lifecycle prose
- Modify constitutional specifications to force responsibility pass
- Present responsibility validation as educational quality or stack freeze
- Create constitutional authority by declaring redefined duties “approved”

**Authority preserved**  
Published corpora own responsibility allocations. This stage confirms them; it does not rewrite duties or become constitutional authority.

---

### CBIL-05 — Constraint Validation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Validate **behavioural constraints** (published “may”, “must”, and “must not”) and **consumption / production boundaries** under CBI-03…CBI-05 — reporting contradictions and invented rights honestly without softening constraints or inventing consumption / production rights in situ.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Responsibility-validated bundle from CBIL-04
- Discovered behavioural, consumption, and production boundary identities (CBI-03…CBI-05 candidates)
- Published CBIR constraint-, consumption-, and production-fidelity rules from WS3 / MS001

**Constitutional outputs**

- Constraint disposition for the concern (preserved / violated / deferred / escalated), covering behavioural and consumption / production honesty — **or**
- Lawful refuse-validate when constraints cannot be honestly confirmed without softening, inventing rights, or rewriting statements

**Permitted activities**

- Confirm published may / must / must-not statements remain as published
- Confirm published consumption and production rights remain as published
- Identify silent softening, contradiction without published precedence, or invented rights
- Prefer honest violated / deferred / escalated over constraint mutation
- Preserve constraint records for finding construction

**Prohibited activities**

- Soften “must not” into “may” (or equivalent) under lifecycle pretence
- Invent consumption or production rights so stages can proceed
- Soft-amend specifications so contradictory constraints become “composition”
- Treat string-scanner warnings as constitutional constraint status without published mapping
- Hide constraint contradictions to manufacture later intact status
- Present constraint validation as licence to rewrite the corpus
- Create constitutional authority by declaring softened constraints “approved”

**Authority preserved**  
Constraint validation reports fidelity to published statements. It never authors law or rewrites constraints.

---

### CBIL-06 — Layer Validation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Confirm that assessed boundaries **preserve published constitutional layer separation** — Constitution / EIP above Programme VI, and onward through VII–XI with lawful workstream subordination — without absorbing superior authority into a subordinate horizon.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Constraint-validated bundle from prior stages
- Published layering order and CBIR / CBI layering rules from WS3 / MS001
- Workstream / milestone subordination identities as published

**Constitutional outputs**

- Layering disposition for the concern (preserved / violated / deferred / escalated) — **or**
- Lawful refuse-layer when layering cannot be honestly confirmed without inventing layers or rewriting authority

**Permitted activities**

- Confirm boundaries respect published programme and workstream order
- Identify layer inversions that place lower horizons as authors of higher meaning
- Compose sibling relationships within a layer only as published
- Prefer honest violated / deferred / escalated over soft-absorbing superiors
- Preserve layering records for finding construction

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent a new constitutional layer outside published programmes
- Absorb superior programme meaning into a subordinate via lifecycle prose
- Collapse distinct horizons into one mega-law under “layer validation”
- Modify constitutional specifications to force layering pass
- Present layering pass as educational quality or stack freeze
- Rewrite boundaries to manufacture layering honesty

**Authority preserved**  
Published programmes own layering. This stage confirms it; it does not become a new layer or constitutional authority.

---

### CBIL-07 — Finding Construction

**Constitutional purpose**  
Emit **boundary integrity findings** as lawful dispositions under published criteria — speaking to whether assessed boundaries remain intact under authority, responsibility, constraint, consumption, production, and layering — without converting findings into amendments, educational grades, boundary rewrites, or freezes of stack authority.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Stage dispositions from CBIL-01…CBIL-06
- Published CBI-01…CBI-07 criteria and CBIR validation rules from WS3 / MS001
- Published decision vocabulary (intact / violated / deferred / escalated)
- Bound references to corpora, boundaries, authority / responsibility validations, constraints, and layering disposition

**Constitutional outputs**

- Boundary integrity findings for the concern (including composition outcomes when multiple CBI types apply)
- Explicit non-authority framing (findings are integrity dispositions only — never new law)
- Assessment records binding stages, boundaries, validations, constraints, layering, and findings

**Permitted activities**

- Produce findings under published dispositions only
- Bind each finding to the CBI types, boundaries, and stages that produced it
- Record composition / stop-rule application honestly
- Prefer understatement over invented green
- Propose (not enact) upstream corpus amendments when law itself must change
- Preserve audit-speakable assessment records

**Prohibited activities**

- Present findings as constitutional amendments or new educational meaning
- Narrate intact status as mastery, readiness theatre, educational quality, permanent conformity, or frozen stack authority
- Rewrite findings to match product urgency
- Collapse multi-type failures into a single false “intact”
- Mutate boundary statements, responsibilities, or constraints while “constructing findings”
- Invent unpublished CBI categories during finding construction
- Erase stage history so findings look clean
- Create constitutional authority via finding text
- Redefine constitutional responsibilities in finding speech

**Authority preserved**  
Findings validate structure against law. They never become law or constitutional authority.

---

### CBIL-08 — Finding Publication

**Constitutional purpose**  
Publish boundary integrity findings and assessment records under **published honesty rules** so audiences receive lawful assessment speech — without inventing intact status, hiding constraint softening or responsibility absorption, rewriting boundaries, amending specifications, or freezing a stack as law.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Findings and assessment records from CBIL-07
- Published publication / honesty rules from WS3 / MS001 and EIP explainability / continuity law
- Boundary confirmations (non-legislation, non-reinterpretation, non-mutation, non-responsibility-redefinition, non-authority-creation, non-substitution)

**Constitutional outputs**

- Published assessment artefacts projecting one constitutional truth to authorised audiences — **or**
- Lawful refuse-publish when records are incomplete, boundaries were breached, or publication would invent intact status / hide constraint violations / freeze stack authority / replace constitutional authority

**Permitted activities**

- Publish findings and records under published honesty rules
- Project student / developer / auditor vocabularies without changing constitutional facts
- Refuse publication when audit or boundary preconditions fail
- Keep publication distinct from educational quality, boundary rewrites, and authority creation

**Prohibited activities**

- Publish invented intact status or incomplete-assessment theatre as intact
- Hide refuse / defer / escalate / violated dispositions to manufacture green
- Narrate publication as constitutional amendment, educational success, or frozen stack law
- Create constitutional authority as a publication act
- Modify constitutional specifications, responsibilities, or constraints as a publication act
- Present seals, badges, or scanner greenery as substitutes for published records
- Reinterpret constitutional meaning in publication speech

**Authority preserved**  
Publication communicates assessment findings. Published corpora remain the sole standard.

Silent jumps that claim findings without CBIL-01…CBIL-06 honesty are constitutionally defective.
