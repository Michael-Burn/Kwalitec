## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor needs bright conditions for saying “this constitutional boundary assessment work is finished” — and equally bright refusals when it is not. After WS3 / MS001 has named how boundaries are validated and WS3 / MS002 has named how assessments are organised, Programme XI Workstream 3 still needs one fulfilment checklist:

> **What must be true before constitutional speech may affirm that the required boundary assessment lifecycle has concluded, authority / responsibility / behavioural / consumption / production / layer validations have completed, findings published, and the assessment formally closed?**

These criteria answer that question. They are **normative constitutional conditions**, not database predicates, CI job statuses, ticket states, string-scan greens, or API status codes.

---

## 2. Criterion Catalogue

All material affirmed completions require **CBIC-01 through CBIC-05** to hold. Failure of any criterion ⇒ **not yet** or **refuse close**. Do not invent a partial “mostly complete” authority-creation, responsibility-redefinition, meaning-reinterpretation, educational, or constitutional status.

| ID | Criterion | Primary question |
|----|-----------|------------------|
| **CBIC-01** | Lifecycle completed | Were required CBIL-01…CBIL-08 stages completed for the concern? |
| **CBIC-02** | Boundary assessment completed | Were required named boundaries assessed under published CBI law (including authority, responsibility, behavioural, consumption, production, and layer validations)? |
| **CBIC-03** | Governance validation completed | Were required governance constraints — authority limits, responsibility separations, and behavioural may / must / must-not obligations — validated under published law? |
| **CBIC-04** | Findings publication completed | Were required boundary integrity findings lawfully constructed and published? |
| **CBIC-05** | Formal assessment closure | Was the boundary assessment concern formally closed under published boundary honesty? |

Optional supporting honesty checks (do not replace CBIC-01…CBIC-05):

| ID | Check | Primary question |
|----|-------|------------------|
| **CBIC-06** | Catalogue honesty | Were stages and evaluations typed under published CBIL-01…CBIL-08 and CBI-01…CBI-07 only? |
| **CBIC-07** | Continuity honesty | Was prior lawful assessment history preserved (EIP-005)? |
| **CBIC-08** | Non-claim honesty | Does the judgement refuse authority-creation / responsibility-redefinition / meaning-reinterpretation / specification-amendment / educational overclaim? |
| **CBIC-09** | Neutrality honesty | Does the judgement refuse stack / vendor canonisation as completion law? |
| **CBIC-10** | Repeatability honesty | Would the same published inputs yield the same completion disposition? |

## 3. Criterion Definitions

### CBIC-01 — Lifecycle completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every lifecycle stage required by the concern’s published WS3 / MS002 scope has been executed — or that a published refuse / defer / escalate disposition exhausted remaining *boundary assessment* duties for that stage set with honest progression to finding construction / publication (or refuse-publish) and closure of what occurred.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Identifiable boundary assessment concern opened under WS3 / MS001–MS002.
- Required CBIL-01…CBIL-08 stage identities for the concern scope.
- Stage traces / disposition records for each required stage (including lawful early stops).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed stage-fulfilment record naming which required stages completed.
- Or a lawful stop record naming which stages were refused / deferred / escalated and why remaining assessment duties are exhausted.

**Completion conditions.**

- Required stages map to published CBIL-01…CBIL-08 only.
- Mandatory initiation, discovery, authority validation, responsibility validation, constraint validation (behavioural / consumption / production), layer validation, finding construction, and publication obligations for the concern are not silently omitted.
- Lawful early stops still leave honest stage outcomes; silent disappearance of a concern is a defect.
- Refusal of “pipeline skipped validation” or “we know it ran” without stage identity as completion.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That stage completion creates constitutional authority or amends constitutional specifications.
- That stage completion redefines responsibilities, softens constraints, or freezes a stack as law.
- That stage completion means the learner succeeded or educational readiness was achieved.
- That skipped stages may be invented or implied from CI theatre.
- That Programme IX conformity / verification / compliance / certification was performed under stage progress.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who declares boundary assessment “finished” without confirming each required assessment step occurred has not finished lawful care — even if the desk looks tidy.

---

### CBIC-02 — Boundary assessment completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every named constitutional boundary statement required by the concern’s published scope was assessed under published CBI-01…CBI-07 categories and CBIR rules — including authority boundary validation (CBIL-03 / CBI-01), responsibility boundary validation (CBIL-04 / CBI-02), behavioural constraint validation (CBIL-05 / CBI-03), consumption and production boundary validation (CBIL-05 / CBI-04 / CBI-05), and layer validation (CBIL-06 / CBI-06) — or that a published refuse disposition exhausted remaining *boundary assessment* duties when required boundaries could not be lawfully assessed without inventing statements.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Named boundary statement identities in scope (published under CBI law).
- Published constitutional corpus identities under assessment.
- Assessment dispositions under applicable CBI types (authority, responsibility, behavioural, consumption, production, layer).
- Stage traces for CBIL-02…CBIL-06 as required by concern scope.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed boundary-assessment record naming which boundaries were assessed under which published CBI categories — including authority, responsibility, behavioural, consumption, production, and layer outcomes.
- Or a lawful refuse / defer / escalate record naming which required boundaries remain unassessed and why.

**Completion conditions.**

- Boundary subjects are named (not “the corpus,” “the stack,” or vendor labels alone).
- Assessment cites published constitutional specifications and CBI categories, not unpublished customs.
- Discovery named only published boundary statements — inventing boundaries to force assessment is a hard stop.
- Authority / responsibility / constraint / consumption / production / layer outcomes report honestly; mutating boundaries to erase contradictions is a hard stop.
- Layer validation preserves published Constitution / EIP → VI–X → XI layering; absorbing superior authority into a subordinate is a hard stop.
- Refusal of string scanners, Twin confidence, or CI greens as substitutes for named boundary assessment.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That assessed boundaries were therefore rewritten, invented, softened, or deleted.
- That a particular stack, language, or framework is privileged as law.
- That product quality or educational quality was judged.
- That constitutional specifications were modified to match the assessment.
- That Programme IX CT lineage preservation equals boundary assessment fulfilment.
- That any single CBI type alone equals whole-concern completion (type outcomes feed CBIC-02; they do not replace CBIC-01…CBIC-05).

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who claims “the boundaries were checked” without naming which statements were assessed for authority, responsibility, behavioural, consumption, production, and layering against which published categories has performed scanner theatre, not assessment fulfilment.

---

### CBIC-03 — Governance validation completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every governance-constraint validation required by the concern’s published scope has been lawfully completed under published CBI / CBIL law — confirming reconstructable authority limits (who may define, own, or decide), responsibility separations (which duties remain with which horizons), and behavioural may / must / must-not obligations — without inventing missing authority, absorbing foreign responsibilities, or silently softening constraints.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Named boundary statements under assessment (CBIC-02 inputs / CBIL-02…CBIL-05 outputs).
- Published governance-validation rules under WS3 / MS001–MS002 (especially CBIL-03…CBIL-05 and CBI-01…CBI-03 / CBIR fidelity rules).
- Boundary confirmations that validation does not invent authority, redefine responsibilities, or privilege a stack as law.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed governance-validation record naming which authority limits, responsibility separations, and behavioural constraints were validated (or honestly failed validation) for which boundaries.
- Or a lawful refuse / defer / escalate record naming which required governance constraints remain unvalidated and why.

**Completion conditions.**

- Governance constraints cite published constitutional references, not unpublished tribal knowledge.
- Authority limits remain reconstructable to owning corpora; invented authority is a hard stop.
- Responsibility separations remain as published; silent duty transfer or absorption is a hard stop.
- Behavioural may / must / must-not statements remain as published; silent softening is a hard stop.
- “Governance was checked somewhere” without named constraint identity is not completion.
- Lawful failed / deferred / escalated governance outcomes still satisfy *validation completion* when they are the honest outcome — completeness of *validation* is not completeness of *intact disposition*, and neither is constitutional authority creation.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That validating a governance constraint creates constitutional authority or amends specifications.
- That a validated constraint freezes the implementation, stack, vendor, or release as permanent constitutional law.
- That a failed / deferred / escalated governance outcome may be silently erased so close can look green.
- That MS001 boundary meaning was replaced, skipped, or rewritten by governance validation.
- That Educational Validation Framework quality review is complete.
- That educational success or exam readiness follows from governance validation.
- That governance validation alone equals whole-concern completion (governance outcomes feed CBIC-03; they do not replace CBIC-01…CBIC-05).

**Tutor rationale.** Recording that authority limits, responsibility separations, and behavioural constraints were checked — including when they fail — is care. Inventing authority or absorbing foreign duties so the assessment can finish is fiction. Completeness of *governance validation* is not creation of *constitutional authority*.

---

### CBIC-04 — Findings publication completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every boundary integrity finding required by the concern’s published scope has been lawfully constructed and published under published CBI / CBIL rules — including intact, violated, deferred, and escalated dispositions — without rewriting those dispositions into constitutional authority, responsibility redefinition, meaning reinterpretation, or specification amendment, and without inventing intact status or hiding violations.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Assessment and governance-validation dispositions under applicable CBI-01…CBI-07 types (CBIL-03…CBIL-06 outputs).
- Published finding-construction and publication rules under WS3 / MS001–MS002 (especially CBIL-07 / CBIL-08).
- Boundary confirmations that findings do not invent law or rewrite boundaries / responsibilities / constraints.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Identifiable boundary integrity finding identities with lawful dispositions, published under honesty rules.
- Or a lawful stop record naming why required findings could not be constructed / published without invention.

**Completion conditions.**

- Findings cite published constitutional references, named boundaries, and validated (or honestly failed) governance constraints.
- Dispositions remain within published vocabulary (intact / violated / deferred / escalated).
- “Assessment happened somewhere” without named finding identity and publication record is not completion.
- Lawful violated / deferred / escalated findings still satisfy *publication* when they are the honest outcome — completeness of *publication* is not completeness of *intact disposition*, and neither is constitutional amendment.
- Publication never invents intact status, hides violations, softens constraints, or freezes a stack as law.
- Continuity forbids silently replacing prior lawful findings under close theatre.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That publishing a finding creates constitutional authority or amends constitutional specifications.
- That an intact finding freezes the implementation, stack, vendor, or release as permanent constitutional law.
- That a violated / deferred / escalated finding may be silently erased or upgraded so close can look green.
- That MS001 boundary evaluation was replaced, skipped, or rewritten by publication.
- That Programme IX conformity / verification / compliance / certification was achieved by publication.
- That the learner succeeded or is exam-ready.

**Tutor rationale.** Publishing a hard boundary disposition is not rewriting the syllabus — but omitting a required finding while claiming the assessment finished is dishonest. Completeness of *findings publication* is not completeness of *constitutional change*.

---

### CBIC-05 — Formal assessment closure

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that the boundary assessment concern was formally closed under published CBIL-08 / boundary honesty — exhausting remaining *boundary assessment lifecycle* duties without inventing constitutional authority, redefining constitutional responsibilities, reinterpreting constitutional meaning, amending constitutional specifications, altering constitutional constraints, or implying educational success.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Published findings and assessment records (CBIC-01…CBIC-04).
- CBIL-08 closure / publication honesty rules and `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md` hard stops.
- Explicit non-claim confirmations (authority creation / responsibility redefinition / meaning reinterpretation / specification amendment / constraint alteration / educational success).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Formal close disposition: affirmed complete / not yet / refuse close / defer / escalate.
- Boundary preservation record confirming forbidden overclaims did not occur.

**Completion conditions.**

- Closure occurs only after required stages, boundary assessment, governance validation, findings publication, and assessment-record obligations for the concern are satisfied — or after a published stop exhausts remaining assessment duties with honest recording.
- Closure speech answers CBICQ-01…CBICQ-05 or explicitly refuses mute theatre.
- Closure never silently upgrades findings dispositions or rewrites boundaries / responsibilities / constraints.
- Refusal of ticket closure, PR merge, string-scan greens, or pipeline greens alone as formal constitutional close.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That formal close creates constitutional authority or amends constitutional specifications.
- That formal close redefines, transfers, absorbs, or deletes constitutional responsibilities.
- That formal close alters, softens, invents, or deletes constitutional constraints.
- That formal close reinterprets Programme VI–X / EIP meaning.
- That formal close means the implementation is constitutionally conformant, verified, compliant, or certified.
- That formal close means the student succeeded or is exam-ready.
- That formal close freezes a stack, vendor, or release as permanent constitutional law.

**Tutor rationale.** Closing the assessment folder is care only when the required steps, boundary assessments, governance checks, findings, and records exist — and only when nobody pretends the close is a new constitution, a rewritten responsibility map, or a pass certificate for the candidate.

## 4. Criterion Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CBIC-01 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CBIC-02 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CBIC-03 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CBIC-04 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — mute complete badges are defects |
| CBIC-05 fails | Affirmation is unlawful even if earlier stages appear present |
| Optional CBIC-06…CBIC-10 fail | Treat as honesty defects; do not soft-pass CBIC-01…CBIC-05 |
| Any criterion “passes” only by creating authority / redefining responsibilities / reinterpreting meaning / amending specifications / altering constraints / implying educational success | The implied overclaim voids lawful affirmation |

All of CBIC-01…CBIC-05 are **conjunctive** for material affirmed completion. There is no lawful “four of five” educational, fidelity, authority-creation, or constitutional status.

Authority, responsibility, behavioural, consumption, production, and layer validations are **not** sixth-through-eleventh catalogue criteria. They are required assessment obligations under CBIC-02 (and required stages under CBIC-01 via CBIL-03…CBIL-06), with governance-constraint honesty specialised under CBIC-03. Affirming close without those validations where the concern required them fails CBIC-01 and/or CBIC-02 and/or CBIC-03.

## 5. Lawful Stop Dispositions

Not every incomplete concern is a defect. Published WS3 / MS002 refuse / defer / escalate / violated outcomes may **exhaust remaining boundary assessment duties** for a concern without affirming lifecycle fulfilment as “complete” in the celebratory sense — while still requiring honest recording and formal close of what occurred.

| Disposition | Constitutional meaning | May affirm CBIC completion? |
|-------------|------------------------|----------------------------|
| **Affirm complete** | CBIC-01…CBIC-05 hold for the concern | Yes |
| **Not yet** | Required stages, boundary assessment, governance validation, findings, or records remain outstanding | No |
| **Refuse close** | Affirmation would invent stages / findings, rewrite responsibilities, invent authority, or overclaim | No |
| **Defer** | Published wait for upstream corpus / boundary / governance identity | No (unless published law says remaining assessment duties are exhausted) |
| **Escalate** | Published stop requiring human / corpus governance attention | No (unless published law says remaining assessment duties are exhausted) |

A lawful stop that exhausts assessment duties is **honest unfinished fulfilment or honest refusal** — never stealth constitutional authority, responsibility rewrite, meaning reinterpretation, specification amendment, educational success, or Programme IX evaluative disposition.

---

## 6. Non-Criteria

The following are **not** constitutional completion criteria:

- CI pipeline greens, flaky-scan suppression, or job exit codes;
- ticket closure, PR merge, or documentation checklist ticks alone;
- string scanners, folder adjacency heuristics, or OpenAPI linkers alone;
- coverage percentages, lint scores, or elapsed time;
- Twin / Adaptive / Experience / Mission confidence signals;
- UI celebration states or analytics milestones;
- “looks bounded enough for the demo”;
- MS001 intact disposition alone (that is evaluation outcome, not whole-concern CBIC fulfilment);
- Programme XI WS1 CDIC or WS2 CCIC completion alone (orthogonal corpus horizons);
- Programme IX CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC completion alone (orthogonal implementation horizons);
- Educational Validation Framework quality release (orthogonal);
- any implication that constitutional specifications changed, responsibilities were redefined, constraints were softened, or authority was created because assessment closed.

## 7. Closing Statement

> **Constitutional boundary integrity assessment is complete only when the required lifecycle is completed, boundary assessment is completed, governance validation is completed, findings publication is completed, and the assessment is formally closed.  
> Satisfying CBIC-01…CBIC-05 never creates constitutional authority, redefines constitutional responsibilities, reinterprets constitutional meaning, or amends constitutional specifications.**
