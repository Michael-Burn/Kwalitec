## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “scanner greens,” “tickets closed,” or “boundary graphs shown.” The tutor optimises **honest closing of constitutional boundary assessment work**: when the published lifecycle for a concern has lawfully concluded, authority / responsibility / behavioural / consumption / production / layer validations have completed, findings published, and the assessment formally closed, the tutor can say so — without pretending constitutional authority was created, without rewriting responsibilities so the report looks tidy, without pretending the student has mastered the topic, and without amending the syllabus because the machinery stopped cleanly.

These objectives bind every constitutional boundary integrity completion judgement and every successor assessment machinery that claims boundary assessment fulfilment.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### CBICO-01 — Confirm lawful assessment completion

**Definition.** Ensure that runtime / assessment / audit speech affirms boundary integrity completion only when a published assessment concern under WS3 / MS001–MS002 has concluded the required CBIL-01…CBIL-08 lifecycle obligations — or honestly refuses close when fulfilment criteria are not met.

**Includes:**

- Assessing only concerns that lawfully opened under WS3 / MS001–MS002 initiation → discovery → authority validation → responsibility validation → constraint validation → layer validation → finding construction → publication law.
- Affirming completion only when `COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md` conditions (CBIC-01…CBIC-05) hold.
- Preferring lawful “not yet” / refuse-close over inventing a “done enough” status for product convenience.
- Distinguishing lawful stop dispositions (refuse / defer / escalate / violated as published outcomes that exhaust remaining *assessment* duties with honest recording) from abandoned lifecycle duties.
- Binding every material completion judgement to reconstructable constitutional references.

**Excludes:**

- Declaring complete because a CI job exited, a ticket closed, a string scanner glowed, or a UI spinner stopped.
- Treating Twin / Adaptive / Experience / Mission heuristics as completion warrants.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for assessment honesty.
- Collapsing “boundary assessment complete” into “constitutional authority created,” “responsibilities redefined,” “meaning reinterpreted,” “specifications amended,” or “student succeeded.”

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software celebrates unfinished boundary assessment — or celebrates finished scanner machinery as finished law-making or responsibility rewriting. Honest confirmation is care; completion theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CBICO-02 — Preserve governance integrity

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming completion never invents missing authority, softens published governance constraints, or upgrades a violated / deferred / escalated governance finding into “intact” under close theatre — so authority limits, ownership maps, and may / must / must-not obligations remain as published after close.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who “helpfully” erases an authority collision so the assessment can finish has abandoned educational honesty even if the completion UX looks clean.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion leaves named authority-boundary and constraint identities and dispositions reconstructable.
- Close never substitutes string scanners, ownership heuristics, or ticket states for published governance findings.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing or silently rewriting prior lawful governance dispositions to simplify the next report.
- Inventing authority or softening constraints under “completion cleanup” is a defect.

---

### CBICO-03 — Preserve responsibility separation

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming completion never transfers, absorbs, dual-claims, or silently rewrites published responsibility allocations — so every closed assessment leaves duty owners reconstructable as published.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later say which horizon still owns yesterday’s duty — because close “tidied” ownership — cannot be trusted. Completion that absorbs foreign responsibilities is educational fiction dressed as done.

**Manifestations:**

- Affirmed closes leave responsibility-boundary identities reconstructable with findings.
- Silent duty transfer, dual exclusive ownership without published warrant, and horizon absorption are defects.
- Continuity forbids deleting prior lawful responsibility walks to “clean up” completion.
- Incomplete responsibility validation for a required concern scope blocks affirmation.

---

### CBICO-04 — Preserve constitutional consistency

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming completion never invents, softens, deletes, or silently rewrites published boundary statements — and never upgrades a violated / deferred / escalated finding into “intact” — so authority, responsibility, behavioural, consumption, production, and layer boundaries remain consistent with published law after close.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who “helpfully” softens a “must not” so the assessment can finish has abandoned educational honesty even if the completion UX looks clean.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion leaves named boundary identities and dispositions reconstructable across CBI-01…CBI-07 applications in scope.
- Close never substitutes package adjacency, import scanners, or ticket states for published boundary findings.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing or silently rewriting prior lawful boundary dispositions to simplify the next report.
- Mutating boundaries under “completion cleanup” is a defect.

---

### CBICO-05 — Preserve assessment repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that the same published corpora, same discovered boundaries, same CBI applications, and same CBIL stage sequence yield the same completion disposition for the same concern — so close never invents contradictory stories from identical constitutional inputs.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who marks the same syllabus boundary “complete” on Monday and “not yet” on Tuesday without a published corpus change has abandoned care for theatre.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion dispositions are deterministic under published inputs.
- Silent non-determinism, ambient “main branch as boundary,” and demo-only close rules are defects.
- Optional honesty checks (CBIC-06…CBIC-10) support, never replace, CBIC-01…CBIC-05.
- Repeatability failures void lawful affirmation even if a badge looks green.

---

### CBICO-06 — Preserve explainability and auditability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material completion judgement leaves a reconstructable, speakable trail: which boundaries were assessed, which governance constraints were validated, which responsibility separations were confirmed, which findings were published, and which constitutional boundaries remained intact.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who can only say “the boundary check is done somewhere” is performing, not teaching. Speakable completion is care.

**Manifestations:**

- Audit records preserve constitutional references, not only technical job logs or CI annotations.
- Explainability obligations in `COMPLETION_EXPLAINABILITY.md` apply to all material completion judgements (CBICQ-01…CBICQ-05).
- Missing CBIC identity against criteria, missing boundary refs, missing governance-constraint identity, missing responsibility-separation identity, or missing finding identity is a defect.
- Student / developer / auditor projections share one truth with different vocabulary.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CBICO-01 conflicts with inventing unpublished boundaries so close can complete | Assessment honesty wins — refuse / defer / escalate |
| CBICO-02 conflicts with inventing authority or softening governance constraints for tidy close | Governance integrity wins |
| CBICO-03 conflicts with absorbing foreign responsibilities so ownership looks complete | Responsibility separation wins |
| CBICO-04 conflicts with rewriting boundaries to erase contradictions for tidy close | Constitutional consistency wins |
| CBICO-05 conflicts with ambient non-determinism or demo-only close rules | Repeatability wins |
| CBICO-06 conflicts with opaque complete badges | Explainability / auditability wins |
| Any CBICO conflicts with creating authority / redefining responsibilities / reinterpreting meaning / amending specifications | Non-legislative honesty wins — refuse the overclaim |

No supporting objective authorises violating CBICO-01.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — Constitution / EIP integrity obligations, Programme VI–X authority preservation, MS001 CBIO duties, and MS002 CBILO duties for the *completion* horizon.

---

## 5. Non-Objectives

Constitutional boundary integrity completion does **not** optimise for:

- creating constitutional authority or soft-amending corpora via close;
- inventing, softening, deleting, or rewriting constitutional boundaries, responsibilities, or constraints;
- reinterpreting Programme VI–X / EIP meaning during close;
- amending constitutional specifications under “completion” pretext;
- constitutional conformance disposition, fidelity scoring, or “assessed therefore conformant” theatre;
- verification success, compliance disposition, certification seals, or implementation-approval judgement;
- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- CI throughput, flaky-scan suppression, or demo continuity as educational or constitutional success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when required assessment duties are incomplete;
- collapsing CBIC criteria into one mega-flag that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics signals as substitutes for published completion criteria;
- treating scanner greens as optional commentary that silently becomes law;
- declaring authority creation, responsibility redefinition, meaning reinterpretation, or specification amendment solely because completion was affirmed.

---

## 6. Closing Statement

> **A concern that becomes “boundary assessment complete” by creating constitutional authority, redefining responsibilities, reinterpreting meaning, or amending specifications is a failed educational system.  
> A concern that confirms only lawful assessment conclusion — with governance integrity, responsibility separation, constitutional consistency, repeatability, and explainability intact — is doing its only completion job.**
