## 2. What Constitutional Boundary Integrity Completion Is

**Constitutional Boundary Integrity Completion** is confirmation that the **required constitutional boundary integrity assessment lifecycle** for a published assessment concern has **lawfully concluded** — such that authority boundary validation, responsibility boundary validation, behavioural constraint validation, consumption and production boundary validation, layer validation, findings publication, and formal assessment closure hold for that concern under WS3 / MS001–MS002 — without claiming that any constitutional-authority, responsibility-redefinition, meaning-reinterpretation, or specification-amendment question was resolved by the close itself.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Assessment concern** | A bounded constitutional unit of boundary integrity work opened under published CBI / CBIL law | What boundary assessment is being assessed for completion? |
| **Completion warrant** | Judgement that assessment fulfilment may be assessed | May we ask whether the boundary assessment is complete? |
| **Completion criteria** | Constitutional conditions that must hold (`COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md`) | What must be true for fulfilment? |
| **Completion judgement** | Affirmed / not yet / refused (no lawful close) | Is constitutional boundary assessment complete for this concern? |
| **Lifecycle fulfilment** | Required CBIL-01…CBIL-08 obligations executed (or lawfully stopped with honest recording) | Did the published assessment lifecycle run? |
| **Boundary assessment** | Named boundary statements assessed under published CBI categories (authority, responsibility, behavioural, consumption, production, layer) | Were boundaries assessed? |
| **Governance validation** | Reconstructable authority limits, responsibility separations, and behavioural constraints validated as published | Were governance constraints validated? |
| **Findings publication** | Boundary integrity findings published under honesty rules | Were findings published? |
| **Formal assessment closure** | Assessment concern closed under published boundary honesty | Was the assessment formally closed? |
| **Boundary preservation record** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden overclaims / mutations / reinterpretations did not occur | Did constitutional integrity survive close? |

Constitutional Boundary Integrity Completion is:

- **assessment-scoped** — it concerns fulfilment of the published boundary assessment lifecycle, not constitutional authorship, responsibility rewriting, educational outcomes, or stack canonisation;
- **lifecycle-bound** — fulfilment references CBIL-01…CBIL-08 stages actually required for the concern under WS3 / MS002;
- **criteria-gated** — fulfilment requires stated constitutional conditions (`COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md` CBIC-01…CBIC-05);
- **boundary-honest** — completion confirms boundaries were assessed under published CBI law; it never invents, softens, or rewrites boundary statements;
- **authority-honest** — completion confirms authority boundaries were validated; it never invents authority or absorbs superior rights into a subordinate;
- **responsibility-honest** — completion confirms responsibility separations were validated; it never redefines or transfers published duties;
- **constraint-honest** — completion confirms behavioural, consumption, and production constraints were validated; it never softens “must not” into “may”;
- **layering-honest** — completion confirms layer validation occurred; it never absorbs superior authority into a subordinate;
- **publication-honest** — completion confirms findings were published; it never invents intact status or hides violations;
- **audit-capable** — fulfilment cites reconstructable constitutional records, not ticket theatre or CI greens;
- **repeatable** — same published corpora, same discovered boundaries, same stage sequence ⇒ same completion disposition for the same concern scope;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by completion;
- **non-legislative** — completion never creates constitutional authority or amends constitutional specifications;
- **non-mutating** — completion never redefines responsibilities or alters constraints under close pretext;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which boundaries / governance constraints / responsibility separations / findings / intact limits applied;
- **corpus-bound** — every material completion cites identifiable published specifications, boundary statements, and named findings / records;
- **non-substitutive** — a completed boundary assessment never becomes the constitutional standard or a substitute for MS001 evaluation meaning.

Constitutional Boundary Integrity Completion is **not**:

- creation of constitutional authority or a soft second constitution;
- redefinition, transfer, or absorption of constitutional responsibilities;
- reinterpretation of constitutional meaning or Programme VI–X / EIP claim classes;
- amendment of constitutional specifications under Constitution / EIP / Programmes VI–X / WS3 / MS001–MS002;
- alteration, softening, invention, or deletion of published behavioural, consumption, or production constraints;
- educational success, learner mastery, progress, or exam readiness;
- implementation conformity, verification, compliance, or certification;
- a Programme VI / VII / VIII educational, orchestration, or runtime completion judgement;
- a claim that pipeline greens, ticket closure, elapsed time, or scanner badges equal fulfilment;
- a UI celebration, analytics milestone, or “boundaries forever intact” flag as educational or constitutional law;
- a licence to skip outstanding published lifecycle duties because a product surface looks finished;
- a substitute for WS3 / MS001 boundary integrity meaning or WS3 / MS002 lifecycle law — completion *judges fulfilment of* those corpora; it does not redefine CBI or CBIL catalogues;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS1 CDIC or WS2 CCIC completion — those horizons evaluate dependency / catalogue assessment fulfilment; CBIC confirms *boundary consistency assessment* fulfilment;
- a substitute for Programme IX CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC completion — those horizons evaluate implementation lineage / verification / compliance / certification; CBIC confirms *corpus boundary assessment* fulfilment.

## 4. Completion Lifecycle (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material boundary integrity assessment concern that claims lawful completion travels through constitutional assessment phases. These are **normative judgement obligations**, not an implementation architecture.

```
Identify published boundary assessment concern opened under CBI / CBIL law
        │
        ▼
Confirm required CBIL-01…CBIL-08 stages and CBI-01…CBI-07 applications for the concern
        │
        ├── refuse / defer / escalate (lawful stop — law unchanged; no invented “complete”)
        │
        └── assess → Apply CBIC-01…CBIC-05
                │
                ├── not yet / refuse close (outstanding duties or boundary breach)
                │
                └── affirm → Emit completion judgement; preserve audit continuity and repeatability
                        │
                        ▼
                Explain boundaries / governance constraints / responsibility separations /
                findings / intact limits
```

| Phase | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **Identify** | Name only published boundary assessment concerns opened under WS3 / MS001–MS002 | Concern is unpublished custom, tribal knowledge, or “how the scanner works today” |
| **Confirm lifecycle** | Verify required CBIL stages and CBI applications for the concern | Stages would invent law, rewrite responsibilities, soften constraints, invent authority, or privilege a stack as law |
| **Assess criteria** | Apply CBIC-01…CBIC-05 without softening into product theatre | Any required criterion fails; or close would claim authority creation / responsibility redefinition / meaning reinterpretation / specification amendment |
| **Preserve & explain** | Record audit continuity; answer CBICQ-01…CBICQ-05 | Path would erase assessment trails, treat completion as constitutional authority, or narrate “complete therefore law changed / responsibilities rewritten / mastered” |

> **Boundary integrity completion never “best-effort invents” a fifth phase called authority creation, responsibility redefinition, meaning reinterpretation, or specification amendment.**

3. **Authority remains reconstructable** — close confirms authority boundaries were validated; it never invents missing authority.
