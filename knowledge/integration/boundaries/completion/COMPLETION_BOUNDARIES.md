## 1. Purpose

Assessment machinery that silently turns “the boundary assessment finished” into “the Constitution now says X,” “yesterday’s responsibilities are rewritten,” “this stack is now the superior authority,” “must not became may,” “student succeeded,” or “specifications are amended” destroys student trust and educational integrity.

This document draws a bright line between **lawful constitutional boundary integrity completion** (Programme XI / WS3 / MS003) and **constitutional authorship / responsibility redefinition / constraint alteration / meaning reinterpretation / specification amendment / educational judgement** owned elsewhere.

---

## 2. Boundary Principles

1. **Confirm assessment fulfilment, do not legislate.** Completion affirms fulfilment of published boundary assessment lifecycle duties; it does not create constitutional law.
2. **Assessment only.** Constitutional authority creation, responsibility redefinition, constraint alteration, meaning reinterpretation, and specification amendment are never inferred from close.
3. **Not responsibility rewriting.** Close never transfers, absorbs, dual-claims, or silently rewrites published duties.
4. **Not constraint alteration.** Close never softens, invents, or deletes published may / must / must-not or consumption / production limits.
5. **Not MS001 meaning replacement.** Completion judges fulfilment of CBI evaluation; it never redefines CBI categories or CBIR rules.
6. **Not MS002 lifecycle rewrite.** Completion judges fulfilment of CBIL stages; it never invents a ninth stage called law-making.
7. **Not constitutional amendment.** Close never patches law or redefines constitutional meaning.
8. **Not meaning reinterpretation.** Programme VI questions and EIP claim classes survive close intact.
9. **Not authority creation.** Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard after close.
10. **Exactly as published.** Close never redefines CBI / CBIL catalogues or soft-amends Programme VI–X meaning.
11. **Published criteria only.** Pipelines, tickets, string scanners, and elapsed time are not fulfilment law.
12. **Published records only.** Audit continuity is constitutional, not free-form log theatre.
13. **Reference findings honestly.** Published findings may be cited; closing never upgrades disposition into authority creation or responsibility rewrite.
14. **Implementation neutrality.** No stack becomes law by proximity to a complete flag.
15. **Assessment-complete ≠ quality.** Completion never grades learning, mastery, readiness, or product success.
16. **Assessment-complete ≠ Programme IX evaluative close.** Completion never substitutes for CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC fulfilment.
17. **Assessment-complete ≠ WS1 / WS2 assessment close.** Completion never substitutes for CDIC / CCIC fulfilment.
18. **Explain the stop.** Students, developers, and auditors should hear when close must be refused.
19. **No emergency exemption.** Deadlines and demos never mint completion power or amendment power.
20. **Repeatability survives close.** Close must not invent contradictory dispositions from identical published inputs.
21. **Layering survives close.** Close never absorbs superior authority into a subordinate under “complete” theatre.
22. **Continuity survives close.** EIP-005 forbids erasing prior lawful assessment history to tidy the report.

## 3. What Boundary Integrity Completion May Do (Lawful)

| Lawful action | Constitutional meaning |
|---------------|------------------------|
| **Confirm assessment completion** | Affirm CBIC-01…CBIC-05 when published criteria hold for a lawful boundary assessment concern |
| **Confirm publication** | Cite that required boundary integrity findings were lawfully constructed and published under published CBI / CBIL rules — without rewriting or silently upgrading dispositions |
| **Confirm formal closure** | Affirm that the assessment concern was formally closed under published boundary honesty |
| **Preserve assessment records** | Leave reconstructable constitutional records of boundaries, governance constraints, responsibility separations, stages, findings, and intact limits |
| **Preserve audit continuity** | Leave reconstructable constitutional records of affirmed or refused close |
| **Refuse unlawful close** | Prefer honest not-yet / refuse / defer / escalate over improvisation |
| **Compose required stages and types** | Confirm fulfilment across published CBIL / CBI conjunctions without collapsing failed duties |
| **Remain implementation-neutral** | Honour completion law across lawful equivalent stacks |
| **Preserve repeatability** | Ensure same published inputs do not yield contradictory completion stories |
| **Explain completion** | Answer boundaries / governance constraints / responsibility separations / findings / intact limits without rewriting meaning |
| **Propose corpus amendments upstream** | Recommend (not enact) amendments when law itself must change before assessment can complete |

These actions **confirm boundary assessment fulfilment**. They do **not** publish a new Constitution, invent coach warrants, mint tips, author ownership maps, invent CBI / CBIL / CBIC types, author runtime behaviour, rewrite responsibilities, create constitutional authority, alter constraints, reinterpret constitutional meaning, amend constitutional specifications, finish Programme IX evaluative horizons as constitutional law, or judge educational quality.

## 4. What Boundary Integrity Completion Must NEVER Do

| Forbidden action | Why | Lawful alternative |
|------------------|-----|--------------------|
| **Create constitutional authority** | Only published corpora define constitutional standards | Keep completion as assessment fulfilment; never as next-cycle law |
| **Redefine constitutional responsibilities** | Duties are published corpus allocations, not draft notes | Cite findings honestly; refuse silent rewrite under close theatre |
| **Alter constitutional constraints** | May / must / must-not and consumption / production limits are published law | Report constraint defects; never soften under close |
| **Amend constitutional specifications** | Only corpus governance may amend meaning | Leave specs unchanged; propose amendment upstream or refuse incomplete assessment |
| **Reinterpret constitutional meaning** | Close never soft-amends Programme VI–X / EIP meaning | Preserve identity of published meaning; never invent it |
| **Invent boundary statements** | Discovery / assessment name published boundaries only | Refuse invented boundaries; escalate to corpus governance if law is missing |
| **Invent authority** | Authority validation confirms reconstructable limits | Refuse invented superior rights; prefer lawful failed / deferred / escalated outcomes |
| **Absorb foreign responsibilities** | Responsibility separation is constitutional | Report absorption defects; never “fix” by rewriting owners under close |
| **Hide violations or invent intact status** | Finding honesty is educational care | Publish violated / deferred / escalated findings honestly |
| **Absorb superior authority into a subordinate** | Layering is published constitutional order | Report layering defects; never rewrite layers under close |
| **Replace MS001 boundary evaluation** | MS001 owns CBI meaning | Confirm assessment support for evaluation honesty; never claim “complete therefore new CBI law” |
| **Replace MS002 lifecycle law** | MS002 owns CBIL stages | Confirm stage fulfilment; never invent stages |
| **Create educational meaning** | Programme VI owns coach / planner questions | Preserve identity of published meaning; never invent it |
| **Author runtime behaviour** | Programme VIII + permitted writers own execution law | Assess corpus boundaries against published law; do not mint behaviour |
| **Bypass audit / publication / closure requirements** | Requirements are educational care, not optional polish | Prefer understatement / refuse over invented green completes |
| **Privilege a technology as constitutional** | Implementation neutrality | Confirm assessment obligations, not Flask / SQLAlchemy / REST / OpenAPI fashion |
| **Erase assessment trails to tidy close** | EIP-005 continuity | Preserve prior lawful stage / finding / record artefacts; refuse destructive “cleanup” |
| **Upgrade finding disposition by closing** | CBIC-04 preserves identity; close does not rewrite status | Record findings honestly including violated / deferred / escalated |
| **Present CI / tickets / string scanners as fulfilment law** | Theatre is not constitution | Require CBIC-01…CBIC-05 content |
| **Imply educational success** | Learning outcomes are not assessment fulfilment | Keep student speech honest: assessment-complete ≠ ready |
| **Imply Programme IX evaluative completion** | Conformity / verification / compliance / certification are orthogonal | Compose with CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC; do not treat them as CBIC (or vice versa) |
| **Imply WS1 / WS2 assessment completion** | Dependency / catalogue fulfilment are orthogonal | Compose with CDIC / CCIC; do not treat them as CBIC (or vice versa) |
| **Emergency-amend via deadline** | Urgency never mints amendment power | Refuse incomplete close; escalate to corpus governance |
| **Collapse with CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC / CDIC / CCIC completion** | Those fulfilment horizons are orthogonal | Compose with sibling MS003 corpora; do not treat them as CBIC |

## 5. Boundary Tests

Before affirming completion, ask:

1. Would this close still be lawful if no CI job had run?
2. Would this close still be lawful if the preferred stack were replaced by another lawful equivalent?
3. Does the close require inventing an unpublished boundary, authority, responsibility, or stage?
4. Does the close silently rewrite a violated / deferred / escalated finding into intact?
5. Does the close narrate constitutional authority creation, responsibility redefinition, constraint alteration, meaning reinterpretation, or specification amendment?
6. Can students / developers / auditors answer CBICQ-01…CBICQ-05 from the close record?
7. Would the same published inputs yield the same completion disposition tomorrow?

Any “no” that indicates invention, mutation, reinterpretation, amendment, stack privilege, mute theatre, or non-repeatability ⇒ **refuse close**.

---

## 6. Emergency and Product Pressure

Deadlines, demos, investor reviews, and release trains **never** authorise:

- inventing CBIC criteria outside CBIC-01…CBIC-05;
- inventing boundaries, authority, responsibility rewrites, or intact findings so close can complete;
- amending Constitution / EIP / Programmes VI–X / WS3 / MS001–MS002 via completion theatre;
- freezing Flask, SQLAlchemy, OpenAPI, a CI vendor, or Runtime A as constitutional law;
- narrating assessment-complete as educational success or Programme IX evaluative success.

Lawful response under pressure: **not yet**, **refuse close**, **defer**, or **escalate** — with speakable reasons.
