## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “grep found no must-not clashes” or “we softened the boundary overnight so the report looks clean.” The tutor optimises **honest boundary integrity**: only boundaries that preserve published authority, responsibility separation, constraint consistency, constitutional isolation, and governance integrity may be called intact — without rewriting those boundaries so a readiness report can pass.

These objectives bind every constitutional boundary integrity assessment and every successor assessment machinery that claims constitutional boundary integrity meaning.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### CBIO-01 — Preserve constitutional authority

**Definition.** Ensure that every material boundary integrity assessment determines — for named published corpus identities under recognised CBI-01…CBI-07 categories — whether published authority boundaries remain intact (who may define, own, or decide), or honestly reports violated / deferred / escalated when authority preservation cannot be confirmed.

**Includes:**

- Applying the closed CBI catalogue required by published law for the assessment scope.
- Binding every material assessment to identifiable published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–XI specifications and subordinate workstream / milestone corpora.
- Detecting authority absorption that would let a subordinate corpus become the practical superior of its own superior.
- Preferring lawful violated / deferred / escalated findings over rewriting authority statements so readiness “passes.”
- Keeping authority preservation distinct from educational quality, mastery, readiness theatre, product success, or constitutional amendment.
- Preserving the Model’s posture: findings validate structure; they do not become law or rewrite authority.

**Excludes:**

- Treating green boundary scanners, ticket links, Twin estimates, Adaptive scores, or UI ticks as constitutional authority integrity.
- Soft-amending specifications, inventing unpublished customs, elevating Programme XI findings to foundational law, or privileging “how main decides today” to force intact status.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for authority integrity.
- Declaring “close enough” authority without documenting a corpus amendment.
- Treating mutual citation between peers as automatic proof of shared authority when exclusive defining rights remain published separately.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software pretends a worksheet can rewrite the syllabus because integration needs a green path. Authority honesty is care; boundary theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CBIO-02 — Preserve responsibility separation

**Definition.** Ensure that every material responsibility boundary continues to allocate published duties to their owning horizons — so silent duty absorption, dual ownership without published warrant, and foreign-horizon takeover remain impossible without a published amendment.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who lets yesterday’s marker also invent tomorrow’s syllabus has abandoned institutional order for convenience.

**Manifestations:**

- Findings cite owning corpus paths and published responsibility statements, not only ticket IDs or commit SHAs.
- Separation checks compare published duty allocations across the assessment scope.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing prior lawful boundary assessment history to simplify the next report.
- Missing specification identity, missing responsibility statement, or contradictory exclusive-duty claims are defects.
- Programme IX CT lineage may support reconstruction; CBI never treats CT records as a licence to reassign responsibilities.

---

### CBIO-03 — Preserve constraint consistency

**Definition.** Ensure that published “may”, “must”, and “must not” constraints remain internally consistent across the assessed corpus — so silent softening, contradiction, or selective ignoring of constraints cannot produce an intact finding.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who says “must not hint the answer” in one note and “may hint for demos” in another without amending the shared rule cannot be trusted.

**Manifestations:**

- Material assessments name the constraint statements under evaluation (may / must / must not) and their owning corpora.
- Contradictory constraints on the same constitutional behaviour without published precedence or amendment are hard defects.
- Softening “must not” into “may” under integrity pretext is forbidden.
- Forbidden actions in `BOUNDARY_BOUNDARIES.md` are hard stops.
- Constraint consistency never authorises inventing new constraints outside published corpora.

---

### CBIO-04 — Preserve constitutional isolation

**Definition.** Ensure that boundary assessments honour published separation of concerns among Constitution / EIP and Programmes VI–XI (and lawful workstream / milestone subordination) — so no subordinate horizon silently absorbs a superior’s meaning, ownership, execution, or governance role.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who lets a marking sheet rewrite institutional exam rules has collapsed isolation for convenience.

**Manifestations:**

- Layer and horizon boundaries under CBI-06 remain reconstructable.
- Consumption and production boundaries under CBI-04 / CBI-05 confirm that cross-horizon use remains consumption / production as published — never authorship by proximity.
- Sibling composition within a layer is permitted only as published; it never collapses distinct horizons into one mega-law.
- Isolation never authorises inventing a new constitutional layer outside published programmes.
- Intact isolation here means *constitutional corpus isolation* — not student exam readiness and not Runtime A deployment theatre.

---

### CBIO-05 — Preserve governance integrity

**Definition.** Ensure that every material boundary integrity assessment leaves a reconstructable, speakable trail that supports honest Programme XI integration and readiness judgements — which boundaries were assessed, which constraints were evaluated, which responsibility separations were validated, which findings were produced, and which assessment limits were preserved.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later say *why* a boundary was accepted cannot be trusted. Silent green scans destroy student and auditor trust. Readiness that ignores broken boundaries is readiness theatre.

**Manifestations:**

- Audit records preserve constitutional references, not only technical scanner logs.
- Explainability obligations in `BOUNDARY_EXPLAINABILITY.md` apply to all material assessments.
- Missing CBI identity, missing corpus refs, missing constraint note, missing separation note, or missing finding disposition is a defect.
- Intact findings may inform downstream readiness; they never themselves certify educational readiness, mastery, or product success.
- Violated / deferred / escalated findings block honest readiness claims that depend on the broken boundary structure.
- Student / developer / auditor projections share one truth with different vocabulary.
- Governance integrity never authorises publishing boundary findings as new constitutional law.
- Repeatability: same published boundaries, same CBI criteria → same disposition, independently of language, framework, datastore, CI vendor, or preferred Runtime A shape.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CBIO-01 conflicts with “helping” absorbed authority look ready | Authority honesty wins — violated / deferred / escalated |
| CBIO-02 conflicts with log volume reduction that erases duty ownership | Responsibility separation wins |
| CBIO-03 conflicts with soft-amending “must not” into “may” | Constraint consistency wins |
| CBIO-04 conflicts with collapsing horizons so demos pass | Constitutional isolation wins |
| CBIO-05 conflicts with opaque “trust us” boundary badges or false readiness | Governance integrity wins |

No supporting objective authorises violating CBIO-01.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — Constitution / EIP integrity obligations and Programme VI–XI authority preservation for the boundary integrity horizon.

---

## 5. Non-Objectives

Constitutional boundary integrity does **not** optimise for:

- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- CI throughput, flaky-test suppression, or coverage theatre as educational success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when specifications or boundary declarations are incomplete;
- collapsing CBI categories into one mega-check that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics / boundary-scanner signals as substitutes for published constitutional boundaries;
- creating, softening, inventing, or deleting constitutional boundaries so reports look tidy;
- replacing Programme IX CT lineage preservation, Programme XI / WS1 dependency integrity, Programme XI / WS2 catalogue integrity, or Programme IX–X evaluative / execution horizons;
- treating documentation as optional commentary on whatever code already shipped.
