## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which boundaries* were checked — which constraints were evaluated, which responsibility separations were validated, what finding resulted, and which assessment limits were preserved — is not coaching; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–X have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, runtime, governance, and execution speech speakable, and after Programme XI / WS1–WS2 have made dependency and catalogue speech speakable, Programme XI still needs one boundary-speech answer:

> **“Which boundaries were assessed, which constitutional constraints were evaluated, which responsibility separations were validated, which findings were produced, and which assessment limits were preserved?”**

That answer must cover every material boundary integrity assessment without inventing educational meaning, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, modifying responsibilities, softening constraints, freezing a stack as law, inventing unpublished CBI types, or presenting intact status as educational quality or student readiness.

## 2. What Boundary Explainability Is

**Constitutional Boundary Integrity Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **validation of named constitutional boundaries under published CBI categories** — not to invent a second tutor voice from boundary scanners, and not to grade the student.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Boundaries assessed** | Named published boundary statements and CBI-01…CBI-07 categories under validation | Which boundaries were assessed? |
| **Constraints evaluated** | Named may / must / must-not statements (and related CBIR rules) applied | Which constitutional constraints were evaluated? |
| **Responsibility separations validated** | Named duty allocations confirmed intact, collided, absorbed, or incomplete | Which responsibility separations were validated? |
| **Integrity findings** | intact / violated / deferred / escalated (with composition notes) | Which findings were produced? |
| **Assessment limits preserved** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden authority creation / responsibility rewrite / constraint alteration / specification amendment did not occur | Which assessment limits were preserved? |

Boundary Explainability is:

- **validation-centred** — it narrates boundaries → constraints → separations → findings under law;
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CBI identities;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published specifications and boundary statements;
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to amend corpora, rewrite duties, or soften constraints;
- **quality-honest** — intact status is never narrated as mastery, readiness, or educational quality;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference or scanner green as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence speech, Programme IX governance speech, Programme X execution / decision / audit / explanation speech, Programme XI / WS1 dependency speech, or Programme XI / WS2 catalogue speech;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match CBI-07 reconstruction needs;
- **repeatability-honest** — explanations must not paper over race-dependent dispositions.

Boundary Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–XI meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a substitute for Programme IX CT explainability, Programme XI / WS1 dependency explainability, Programme XI / WS2 catalogue explainability, or Programme X explainability architecture corpora;
- a rendering system, UI template, CI badge, log shipper, boundary-visualisation product, or analytics dashboard;
- a claim that clear boundary speech guarantees learning or a pass;
- a licence to present scanner greens, grep consistency, or pipeline badges as constitutional proof or educational quality;
- a licence to narrate constraint softening as “integrity repairs.”

## 3. Mandatory Explanation Questions

Every material constitutional boundary integrity assessment should answer all five questions:

### CBIEQ-01 — Which boundaries were assessed?

Name the primary published boundary statements and the CBI-01…CBI-07 categories under validation (for example Programme VIII “must not invent educational meaning” under CBI-03; Programme VII ownership exclusivity under CBI-01 / CBI-02; Programme XI / WS3 production-of-findings-only under CBI-05; Constitution / EIP → VI–X layer isolation under CBI-06). Unpublished tribal limits and “neighbouring folders” are not constitutional boundaries.

### CBIEQ-02 — Which constitutional constraints were evaluated?

Name the reconstructable may / must / must-not statements (and relevant CBIR-01…CBIR-10 rules) applied to the assessment. Missing constraint identity is a defect to disclose — not a gap to invent. “Supported by ticket #1234” or “supported by grep” is not a constitutional constraint.

### CBIEQ-03 — Which responsibility separations were validated?

Name the duty allocations under review and their disposition:

- **Intact** — published exclusive / composed responsibilities remain as published.
- **Collided** — two or more corpora claim exclusive responsibility for the same duty without published composition warrant; name the owners.
- **Absorbed** — a foreign horizon has taken a published duty without warrant; name the absorber and the rightful owner.
- **Incomplete** — ownership statements missing; disposition must be deferred / escalated, not silently intact.

Peer composition that is published as composition is not automatically a collision; undeclared dual exclusive ownership is.

### CBIEQ-04 — Which findings were produced?

Distinguish:

- **intact** — published boundaries satisfy applicable CBI categories and CBIR rules for the assessment scope;
- **violated** — authority, responsibility, constraint, consumption, production, layer, or catalogue rules failed;
- **deferred** — lawful pause pending identifiable missing published inputs;
- **escalated** — requires corpus governance / human constitutional review beyond ordinary assessment.

Composition notes must name which CBI-01…CBI-07 categories bound the finding. “Boundaries look fine” is not a finding.

### CBIEQ-05 — Which assessment limits were preserved?

Confirm explicitly that the assessment did **not**:

- redefine constitutional authority or create new constitutional law;
- modify or redefine constitutional responsibilities;
- alter published may / must / must-not constraints;
- amend constitutional specifications;
- substitute Programme XI or any assessed corpus for upstream authority;
- narrate intact status as educational quality, mastery, or student exam readiness;
- privilege a technology, scanner, or CI vendor as constitutional law.

Failed assessment-limit confirmation → the explanation is constitutionally defective even if a disposition string exists.

## 4. Audience Projections (One Truth)

| Audience | Vocabulary focus | Must still include |
|----------|------------------|--------------------|
| **Student-facing** | Plain language that the study system’s rules still keep who-decides and who-must-not clear, without secretly swapping duties | CBIEQ-01…CBIEQ-05 truth; no mastery / pass claims from intact status |
| **Developer-facing** | Corpus paths, CBI IDs, constraint citations, separation dispositions, finding disposition | Same constitutional truth; no stack-as-law narration |
| **Auditor-facing** | CBIR rule IDs, CBI-07 record identity, boundary checklist (CBIB), assessment-limit confirmation | Reconstructability; continuity with prior findings |

Projections may simplify vocabulary. They may **not** simplify away constraint evaluation, responsibility-separation status, or assessment-limit failures.

## 5. Explanation Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why defective | Lawful alternative |
|--------------|---------------|--------------------|
| “Boundaries are fine because CI is green” | Proxy theatre | Cite CBI categories, boundary statements, and findings |
| “We fixed integrity by changing must-not to may” | Mutation under explanation pretext | Report contradiction; propose upstream amendment |
| “Intact means the student is ready” | Quality conflation | Keep boundary integrity distinct from student readiness |
| “Constraint is the Jira epic” | Non-constitutional origin | Cite Constitution / EIP / programme paths and CBIR IDs |
| “No conflicts in grep output” | Wrong subject horizon | Map to published boundary statements or refuse |
| “Programme XI now defines Programme VI duties” | Authority / responsibility substitution | Preserve VI meaning; XI validates only |
| “Temporary may for the demo” | Emergency exemption | Deferred / escalated / violated — never demo law |
| “Trust us — the limits are obvious” | Opacity | Answer CBIEQ-01…CBIEQ-05 explicitly |
| “We absorbed that duty to make integration simpler” | Responsibility rewrite | Report absorption as violated; propose upstream amendment |
