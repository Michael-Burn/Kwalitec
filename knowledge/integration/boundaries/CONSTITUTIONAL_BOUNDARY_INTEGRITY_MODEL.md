## 2. What Constitutional Boundary Integrity Is

**Constitutional Boundary Integrity** is the binding mechanism by which **constitutional boundaries** throughout the published constitutional corpus may be **defined, validated, and governed**, yielding **boundary integrity findings** that preserve authority integrity, responsibility separation, constraint consistency, consumption / production honesty, layer isolation, and explainability — without becoming a source of constitutional law, a rewriter of responsibilities, or a substitute for upstream programme meaning.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Published constitutional corpus** | Constitution, EIP, Programme VI–XI specifications and their subordinate workstream / milestone corpora | What corpus is under boundary assessment? |
| **Constitutional boundary** | A published statement of authority, responsibility, behavioural constraint, consumption right, production right, or layer separation that limits what a corpus may lawfully do | Which boundary is being assessed? |
| **Authority boundary** | Published limit on who may define, own, or decide under constitutional law | Who may lawfully exercise this authority? |
| **Responsibility boundary** | Published allocation of duties that must not be absorbed by a foreign horizon | Which responsibilities remain separated? |
| **Behavioural constraint** | Published “may”, “must”, or “must not” obligation governing constitutional behaviour | Which constraints bind this corpus? |
| **Consumption boundary** | Published limit on which artefacts / meanings a corpus may lawfully consume | What may be consumed? |
| **Production boundary** | Published limit on which artefacts / findings a corpus may lawfully produce | What may be produced? |
| **Layer boundary** | Published separation of concerns among Constitution / EIP and Programmes VI–XI (and lawful workstream / milestone subordination) | Is separation of concerns intact? |
| **Boundary integrity finding** | Lawful disposition: intact / violated / deferred / escalated — never “new law” and never a rewrite of published boundaries | Does the boundary remain lawful? |
| **Constitutional authority** | The exclusive right of published corpora to define educational and execution meaning | Who owns the standard? |
| **Implementation neutrality** | Boundary validation speaks to constitutional identities, not to a preferred stack as law | Remains the stack replaceable? |
| **Auditability** | Every material assessment leaves reconstructable constitutional traces | Can the judgement be reconstructed? |
| **Explainability** | Boundaries, constraints, separations, findings, and assessment limits are speakable | Can the judgement be narrated honestly? |

Constitutional Boundary Integrity is:

- **validation-centred** — it validates published boundaries; it never authors or rewrites them;
- **authority-preserving** — published authority limits remain reconstructable and unabsorbed;
- **responsibility-preserving** — published duty allocations remain separated as published;
- **constraint-faithful** — “may”, “must”, and “must not” statements remain as published; silent softening is a defect;
- **consumption-honest** — corpora may consume only what published law permits;
- **production-honest** — corpora may produce only what published law permits;
- **layer-preserving** — constitutional separation of concerns remains intact;
- **catalogue-closed** — only CBI-01…CBI-07 may be applied as constitutional boundary integrity categories;
- **corpus-bound** — every material assessment cites identifiable published specifications and boundary statements;
- **finding-producing** — assessments emit intact / violated / deferred / escalated dispositions;
- **audit-capable** — every material assessment leaves reconstructable constitutional traces;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which boundaries were assessed under which constraints with which findings;
- **non-legislative** — a boundary finding never becomes constitutional law or a soft amendment;
- **non-mutating** — the model never modifies constitutional specifications, responsibilities, or constraints under validation pretext;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by boundary law.

Constitutional Boundary Integrity is **not**:

- a second Educational Constitution, EIP, or Programme VI–X amendment path;
- a licence to reinterpret coach meaning, ownership maps, tip warrants, runtime contracts, conformance criteria, verification meaning, compliance meaning, certification meaning, evolution meaning, execution meaning, dependency meaning, or catalogue meaning in boundary prose;
- a testing framework, CI/CD pipeline, GitHub Action, string scanner, or OpenAPI linker;
- a claim that “boundary intact” guarantees learning, mastery, exam readiness, product success, perpetual conformity, or frozen stack authority;
- a substitute for Programme IX MS002 constitutional traceability — CT preserves spec/artefact/finding lineage; CBI validates *constitutional boundary integrity*;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS1 dependency integrity — CDI validates *corpus dependency structure*; CBI validates *boundary consistency*;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS2 catalogue integrity — CCI validates *catalogue uniqueness and namespace honesty*; CBI validates *boundary consistency*;
- a substitute for Programme IX conformance / verification / compliance / certification — those evaluate implementations and obligations; CBI validates *boundary corpus consistency*;
- a substitute for Programme X execution / decision / audit / explainability — those organise constitutional activity; CBI never executes, decides, audits, or explains educational outcomes in their place;
- a licence to create, soften, invent, or delete constitutional boundaries under “integrity” pretext.

## 3. Boundary Integrity Posture (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material constitutional boundary integrity assessment travels through three constitutional phases. These are **normative assessment obligations**, not an implementation architecture.

```
Identify published boundaries under assessment
        │
        ▼
Validate authority, responsibility, constraints, consumption,
production, and layer separation under CBI-01…CBI-07
        │
        ├── violated / deferred / escalated (lawful finding — boundaries unchanged)
        │
        └── intact → Emit findings and preserve audit records
                │
                ▼
        Explain boundaries / constraints / separations / findings / assessment limits
```

| Phase | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **Identify** | Name only published constitutional corpus identities and published boundary statements | Boundary is unpublished tribal knowledge, invented “must not”, or “how main behaves today” as law |
| **Validate** | Apply CBI-01…CBI-07 for authority, responsibility, constraints, consumption, production, and layer integrity | Assessment would invent law, redefine responsibilities, soften constraints, or privilege a stack as law |
| **Record** | Produce findings and audit-speakable trails without amending corpora or mutating boundaries | Path would treat findings as constitutional amendments or soft rewrites of published boundaries |

> **Boundary integrity never “best-effort invents” a fourth phase called law-making, responsibility rewriting, or stack canonisation.**

## 6. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Boundary Integrity Model exists so that:

1. **Authority remains preserved** — no subordinate corpus may silently absorb a superior’s exclusive right to define or decide.
2. **Responsibility separation remains honest** — published duties are not absorbed by foreign horizons under “integration” pretext.
3. **Constraints remain consistent** — “may”, “must”, and “must not” statements are not silently softened or contradicted across the corpus.
4. **Constitutional isolation remains intact** — layer and horizon separations survive product urgency.
5. **Governance integrity remains reconstructable** — boundary assessments support honest Programme XI readiness and integration judgements.
6. **Transparency remains intact** — boundary assessments are speakable; silent green scans are not constitutional proof.
7. **Students remain protected** — product urgency cannot launder contradictory boundaries as “approved architecture.”
