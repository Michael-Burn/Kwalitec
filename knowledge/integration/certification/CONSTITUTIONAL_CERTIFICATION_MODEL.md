## 2. What Constitutional Certification Is

**Constitutional Certification** (Programme XI / WS7) is the binding mechanism by which the **certification status of the published constitutional corpus** may be **determined** from **completed constitutional assurance** (WS1–WS5) and **runtime readiness** (WS6), yielding **certification findings** and **certification decisions** that preserve assurance completeness, readiness honesty, architectural coherence, constitutional consistency, evidence traceability, governance transparency, and repeatability — without becoming a source of constitutional law, a runtime executor, a rewriter of artefacts, a Programme IX implementation certificate, or a substitute for upstream programme meaning.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Published constitutional corpus** | Constitution, EIP, Programme VI–XI specifications and their subordinate workstream / milestone corpora | What corpus is under certification assessment? |
| **Completed assurance (WS1–WS5)** | Lawful dependency / catalogue / boundary / vocabulary / layer integrity completion outcomes available for honest consumption | Which integrity assurance may certification consume? |
| **Runtime readiness (WS6)** | Lawful CRR / CRRD readiness findings available for honest consumption | Was runtime readiness achieved? |
| **Certification dimension** | A recognised CXCD-01…CXCD-05 category of certification judgement | Which certification kind is being evaluated? |
| **Certification prerequisite** | Published precondition for lawful corpus certification (completed assurance identities, readiness identity, dimension evidence, authority references, boundary integrity) | Which prerequisites must be satisfied? |
| **Assurance completeness** | Required WS1–WS6 assurance concerns lawfully complete for the certification scope | Is prerequisite assurance complete? |
| **Architectural coherence** | Layer / dependency / boundary coherence reconstructable from completed assurance | Does architectural coherence hold? |
| **Constitutional consistency** | Corpus meaning, authority, vocabulary, and boundary consistency reconstructable without contradiction | Does constitutional consistency hold? |
| **Overall certification status** | Aggregate disposition across required dimensions for a published certification concern | Is the corpus formally certified? |
| **Certification finding** | Lawful disposition: certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated — never “new law” and never executed runtime behaviour | What status was determined? |
| **Certification decision** | Published disposition of corpus certification status with reconstructable evidence trail | What decision was published? |
| **Constitutional authority** | The exclusive right of published corpora to define educational and execution meaning | Who owns the standard? |
| **Implementation neutrality** | Certification speaks to constitutional evidence sufficiency, not to a preferred stack as law | Remains the stack replaceable? |
| **Auditability** | Every material assessment leaves reconstructable constitutional traces | Can the judgement be reconstructed? |
| **Explainability** | Workstreams, readiness, prerequisites, status, and evidence are speakable | Can the judgement be narrated honestly? |

Constitutional Certification is:

- **evidence-only** — it evaluates completed assurance and readiness evidence; it never invents, rewrites, or re-authors that evidence;
- **assurance-consuming** — it aggregates completed WS1–WS6 assurance findings; it never re-runs those assessments as a substitute for consuming their outcomes;
- **evaluation-centred** — it determines certification status; it never implements runtime behaviour;
- **dimension-bound** — only CXCD-01…CXCD-05 may classify certification judgements;
- **prerequisite-bound** — unfinished prerequisite assurance or unreadiness blocks certified status for dependent dimensions;
- **authority-preserving** — published corpora remain the sole source of constitutional meaning;
- **non-legislative** — a certification finding never becomes constitutional law or a soft amendment;
- **non-mutating** — the model never modifies constitutional artefacts or specifications under certification pretext;
- **non-executing** — certification never runs Runtime A, services, adapters, or product behaviour;
- **corpus-bound** — every material assessment cites identifiable published specifications and completed assurance identities;
- **finding-producing** — assessments emit certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated dispositions;
- **decision-publishing** — assessments publish certification decisions with reconstructable evidence;
- **audit-capable** — every material assessment leaves reconstructable constitutional traces;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which workstreams / readiness / prerequisites / status / evidence applied;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by certification law;
- **non-substitutive** — certification machinery never becomes the constitutional standard for the next judgement;
- **horizon-honest** — it never pretends to be Programme IX CRT implementation certification.

Constitutional Certification is **not**:

- a second Educational Constitution, EIP, or Programme VI–X amendment path;
- a licence to reinterpret coach meaning, ownership maps, tip warrants, runtime contracts, conformance criteria, verification meaning, compliance meaning, Programme IX certification meaning, evolution meaning, execution meaning, or WS1–WS6 integrity / readiness meaning in certification prose;
- a testing framework, CI/CD pipeline, GitHub Action, deployment gate, feature flag, or OpenAPI certification checker;
- a claim that “certified” guarantees learning, mastery, exam readiness, product success, perpetual conformity, or frozen stack authority;
- Runtime A, Programme VIII runtime execution, or any product runtime behaviour;
- a substitute for Programme VIII runtime architecture — Programme VIII defines *how* software may execute law; CXC never executes that law;
- a substitute for Programme IX CRT — CRT certifies *implementations* from *completed compliance*; CXC certifies the *corpus* from *completed Programme XI assurance and runtime readiness*;
- a substitute for Programme IX conformance / verification / compliance — those evaluate *implementations* against published obligations; CXC evaluates *corpus certification status*;
- a substitute for Programme X execution / decision / audit / explainability — those organise constitutional activity; CXC never executes those activities;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS1–WS6 integrity, readiness, or completion — those validate, close, and evaluate corpus structure and readiness; CXC *consumes* their completed outcomes;
- a substitute for Educational Validation Framework quality review — quality release remains a distinct horizon;
- a licence to implement, ship, or “temporarily run” runtime behaviour under certification pretext;
- a licence to modify constitutional artefacts “so certification can pass.”

## 3. Certification Posture (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material constitutional corpus certification assessment travels through four constitutional phases. These are **normative assessment obligations**, not an implementation architecture.

```
Identify published certification concern and required completed WS1–WS6 evidence
        │
        ▼
Evaluate certification prerequisites and CXCD-01…CXCD-05 dimensions
from completed assurance and runtime readiness
        │
        ├── not-certified / deferred / escalated (lawful finding — corpus unchanged;
        │                                         no runtime execution; gaps named)
        │
        └── certified → Record findings and publish certification decision
                │
                ▼
        Explain workstreams considered / readiness achieved /
        prerequisites satisfied / status determined / evidence supporting the decision
```

| Phase | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **Identify** | Name only published certification concerns, required CXCD dimensions, and identifiable completed WS1–WS6 assurance artefacts | Assurance is unfinished and silently assumed complete; certification map is unpublished tribal custom; “main looks certified” is treated as law |
| **Evaluate** | Apply CXCD-01…CXCD-05 for assurance completeness, runtime readiness, architectural coherence, constitutional consistency, and overall status | Assessment would invent law, execute runtime, modify artefacts, privilege a stack as law, invent missing assurance, or collapse into Programme IX CRT |
| **Record** | Produce findings and keep audit-speakable trails without amending corpora or implementing behaviour | Path would treat findings as constitutional amendments, soft redefinitions, or Runtime A execution warrants |
| **Publish** | Publish certification decisions with reconstructable evidence identity | Path would publish seals without evidence, erase prerequisites, or invent certified status for unfinished assurance |

> **Constitutional Certification never “best-effort invents” a fifth phase called law-making, artefact rewriting, or runtime execution.**

## 6. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Certification Model exists so that:

1. **Constitutional confidence remains honest** — the corpus is certified only when completed assurance and readiness evidence support that claim.
2. **Evidence remains traceable** — every certification decision cites identifiable WS1–WS6 evidence.
3. **Implementation confidence remains speakable** — teams may rely on certified corpus status without confusing it for Runtime A execution or stack freeze.
4. **Governance remains transparent** — certification prerequisites, dimensions, and refusals are reconstructable.
5. **Certification remains repeatable** — same published corpora, same completed assurance, same concern scope ⇒ same disposition.
6. **Students remain protected** — product urgency cannot launder incomplete constitutional guidance as “certified to build.”
7. **Stacks remain replaceable** — certified status never freezes a particular Runtime A shape as law.
8. **Horizons remain distinct** — corpus certification never pretends to be Programme IX implementation certification.
