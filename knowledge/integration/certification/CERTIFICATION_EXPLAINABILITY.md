## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which assurance workstreams* were considered — whether runtime readiness was achieved, which certification prerequisites were satisfied, what certification status was determined, and which evidence supported the decision — is not coaching; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–X have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, runtime, governance, and execution speech speakable, and after Programme XI / WS1–WS6 have made dependency, catalogue, boundary, vocabulary, layer, and readiness speech speakable, Programme XI still needs one corpus-certification-speech answer:

> **“Which assurance workstreams were considered, was runtime readiness achieved, which certification prerequisites were satisfied, what certification status was determined, and which evidence supported the decision?”**

That answer must cover every material corpus certification assessment without inventing educational meaning, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, executing Runtime A, freezing a stack as law, inventing unpublished CXCD types, collapsing into Programme IX CRT seals, or presenting certified status as educational quality or student exam readiness.

## 2. What Certification Explainability Is

**Constitutional Certification Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **evaluation of named certification dimensions under published CXCD categories from completed assurance and runtime readiness** — not to invent a second tutor voice from deployment seals, and not to grade the student.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Assurance workstreams considered** | Named WS1–WS6 workstreams whose completed assurance / readiness were consumed | Which assurance workstreams were considered? |
| **Runtime readiness achieved** | Named CRR / CRRD disposition(s) confirming readiness achievement (or honest failure / deferral / escalation) | Was runtime readiness achieved? |
| **Certification prerequisites satisfied** | Named prerequisites that held (or failed) for the concern | Which certification prerequisites were satisfied? |
| **Certification status determined** | certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated (with composition notes) | What certification status was determined? |
| **Evidence supporting the decision** | Named completed assurance / readiness identities and CXCD dispositions that support the published decision | Which evidence supported the decision? |

Certification Explainability is:

- **evaluation-centred** — it narrates workstreams → readiness → prerequisites → status → evidence under law;
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CXCD identities;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published specifications and completed assurance / readiness identities;
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to amend corpora, redefine meaning, or invent maps;
- **non-executing** — explanation never narrates Runtime A execution as the meaning of certified;
- **quality-honest** — certified status is never narrated as mastery, student exam readiness, or educational quality;
- **horizon-honest** — certified status is never narrated as Programme IX CRT implementation certification;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference or CI green as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **horizon-preserving** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence speech, Programme IX governance / CRT speech, Programme X execution / decision / audit / explanation speech, or Programme XI / WS1–WS6 assurance / readiness speech;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match CXCI reconstruction needs;
- **repeatability-honest** — explanations must not paper over race-dependent dispositions.

Certification Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–XI meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a substitute for Programme IX CT / CRT explainability, Programme XI / WS1–WS6 assurance / readiness explainability, or Programme X explainability architecture corpora;
- a rendering system, UI template, CI badge, log shipper, deployment dashboard, or analytics product;
- a claim that clear certification speech guarantees learning or a pass;
- a licence to present scanner greens, deployment gates, or pipeline badges as constitutional proof or educational quality;
- a licence to narrate Runtime A execution or artefact mutation as “certification repairs.”

## 3. Mandatory Explanation Questions

Every material constitutional corpus certification assessment should answer all five questions:

### CXCEQ-01 — Which assurance workstreams were considered?

Name the primary published assurance workstreams consumed for the concern (for example WS1 dependency completion under CDIC; WS2 catalogue completion under CCIC; WS3 boundary completion under CBIC; WS4 vocabulary completion under CVIC; WS5 layer completion under CLIC; WS6 runtime readiness under CRR / CRRD; and, where applicable, citeable Programme VI–X guidance presence for consistency composition).

Unpublished tribal “everything looks fine” and “neighbouring folder labels” are not constitutional assurance workstreams.

**Defects to disclose:** missing workstream identity; invented completion; silent assumption that unfinished WS1–WS6 work was complete.

---

### CXCEQ-02 — Was runtime readiness achieved?

State whether Programme XI / WS6 runtime readiness was achieved for the concern scope. Name the CRR / CRRD dispositions consumed (ready / not-ready / deferred / escalated) and any material readiness gaps.

“Supported by deployment gate,” “supported by green main,” or “we feel ready” is not constitutional runtime readiness achievement.

**Defects to disclose:** invented ready status; silent omission of required readiness when overall certified is claimed; narrating readiness achievement as Runtime A execution.

---

### CXCEQ-03 — Which certification prerequisites were satisfied?

Name the certification prerequisites applied and their dispositions (satisfied / not satisfied / deferred / escalated / scoped out with reconstructable notes). Prerequisites include completed assurance identities, readiness identity, required CXCD evidence, authority references, and boundary integrity as published for the concern.

“Supported by ticket #1234” or “supported by demo urgency” is not a constitutional certification prerequisite.

**Defects to disclose:** unpublished custom prerequisites; majority-vote theatre that ignores a required failed prerequisite; silent scope-out of a required prerequisite.

---

### CXCEQ-04 — What certification status was determined?

Name the certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated disposition(s), including dimension-level and overall findings where both exist, with enough constitutional reference for reconstruction. Name the published certification decision identity when a decision was published.

Status speech must preserve boundaries: certified is not Runtime A execution, not specification amendment, not student success, not Programme IX CRT success.

**Defects to disclose:** missing finding / decision identity; contradictory dispositions from identical published inputs without published corpus change; narration that converts certified into authority creation or artefact mutation.

---

### CXCEQ-05 — Which evidence supported the decision?

Name the identifiable completed assurance / readiness artefacts, CXCD dimension dispositions, and supporting authority references that support the published certification decision (or that support a not-certified / deferred / escalated refusal).

Absence of supporting evidence for a certified claim is a defect — silence is not evidence.

**Defects to disclose:** missing evidence identity; CI greens presented as constitutional evidence; WS1–WS6 ventriloquism inventing findings those corpora did not publish; evidence that contradicts the narrated status without disclosure.

## 6. Forbidden Explanation Patterns

1. **Certified-as-mastery** — narrating corpus certification as student exam readiness or learning success.
2. **Certified-as-Runtime-A** — narrating certified as executed / shipped product behaviour.
3. **Certified-as-law** — narrating findings as constitutional amendments or new authority.
4. **Certified-as-CRT** — narrating corpus certification as Programme IX implementation certification.
5. **Gap erasure** — omitting known gaps while claiming certified.
6. **Stack canonisation** — presenting Flask / SQLAlchemy / OpenAPI / a vendor as the meaning of certified.
7. **CI-as-constitution** — presenting pipeline greens as CXCEQ answers.
8. **WS1–WS6 ventriloquism** — inventing integrity or readiness findings those corpora did not publish.
9. **Horizon collapse** — answering CXCEQ questions with Programme IX CRT speech or Programme VIII contract speech alone.

## 7. Minimal Explanation Record

Every material certification assessment should leave a reconstructable record containing at least:

1. Certification concern identity  
2. Assurance workstreams considered (CXCEQ-01)  
3. Runtime readiness achievement (CXCEQ-02)  
4. Prerequisites applied and dispositions (CXCEQ-03)  
5. Certification status / decision determined (CXCEQ-04)  
6. Evidence supporting the decision (CXCEQ-05)  
7. Boundary preservation note (no authority creation; no artefact mutation; no meaning redefinition; no runtime execution; no Programme IX CRT conflation)  
8. Repeatability / input identity notes sufficient for reconstruction  

Missing any of (1)–(7) for a material certified claim is a constitutional explainability defect.
