## 2. What Constitutional Certification Completion Is

**Constitutional Certification Completion** (Programme XI / WS7) is confirmation that the **required constitutional certification assessment lifecycle** for a published certification concern has **lawfully concluded** — such that certification prerequisite evaluation, certification dimension evaluation, certification status determination, findings publication, and formal assessment closure hold for that concern under WS7 / MS001–MS002 — without claiming that any constitutional-authority, artefact-mutation, runtime-execution, or specification-amendment question was resolved by the close itself.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Certification concern** | A bounded constitutional unit of corpus certification work opened under published CXC / CXCL law | What certification assessment is being assessed for completion? |
| **Completion warrant** | Judgement that assessment fulfilment may be assessed | May we ask whether the certification assessment is complete? |
| **Completion criteria** | Constitutional conditions that must hold (`COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md`) | What must be true for fulfilment? |
| **Completion judgement** | Affirmed / not yet / refused (no lawful close) | Is constitutional certification assessment complete for this concern? |
| **Lifecycle fulfilment** | Required CXCL-01…CXCL-07 obligations executed (or lawfully stopped with honest recording) | Did the published assessment lifecycle run? |
| **Certification prerequisite evaluation** | Completed WS1–WS6 assurance / readiness inputs gathered and prerequisites validated under published law | Were certification prerequisites evaluated? |
| **Certification dimension evaluation** | Required CXCD-01…CXCD-04 dimensions assessed under published MS001 rules | Were certification dimensions assessed? |
| **Certification status determination** | Aggregate disposition under CXCD-05 determined honestly | Was certification status determined? |
| **Findings publication** | Certification findings / decisions published under honesty rules | Were findings published? |
| **Formal assessment closure** | Assessment concern closed under published boundary honesty | Was the assessment formally closed? |
| **Boundary preservation record** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden overclaims / mutations / executions / amendments did not occur | Did constitutional integrity survive close? |

Constitutional Certification Completion is:

- **assessment-scoped** — it concerns fulfilment of the published certification assessment lifecycle, not constitutional authority creation, artefact rewriting, runtime execution, educational outcomes, or stack canonisation;
- **lifecycle-bound** — fulfilment references CXCL-01…CXCL-07 stages actually required for the concern under WS7 / MS002;
- **criteria-gated** — fulfilment requires stated constitutional conditions (`COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md` CXCC-01…CXCC-05);
- **assurance-honest** — completion confirms WS1–WS6 assurance and readiness were evaluated as prerequisites; it never invents unfinished assurance;
- **dimension-honest** — completion confirms certification dimensions were assessed under published CXCD law; it never invents dimensions or re-authors assurance;
- **status-honest** — completion confirms overall certification status was determined under CXCD-05; it never silently upgrades not-certified subordinate dimensions;
- **publication-honest** — completion confirms findings were published; it never invents certified status or hides gaps;
- **audit-capable** — fulfilment cites reconstructable constitutional records, not ticket theatre or CI greens;
- **repeatable** — same published corpora, same consumed assurance, same stage sequence ⇒ same completion disposition for the same concern scope;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by completion;
- **non-legislative** — completion never creates constitutional authority or amends constitutional specifications;
- **non-mutating** — completion never modifies constitutional artefacts under close pretext;
- **non-executing** — completion never runs Runtime A, services, adapters, or product behaviour;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which workstreams / readiness / dimensions / status / evidence applied;
- **corpus-bound** — every material completion cites identifiable published specifications, completed assurance identities, and named findings / records;
- **horizon-honest** — it never pretends to be Programme IX CRTC implementation certification completion;
- **non-substitutive** — a completed certification assessment never becomes the constitutional standard or a substitute for MS001 evaluation meaning.

Constitutional Certification Completion is **not**:

- creation of constitutional authority or a soft second constitution;
- modification, soft-amendment, rewrite, or deletion of constitutional artefacts;
- amendment of constitutional specifications under Constitution / EIP / Programmes VI–X / WS1–WS6 / WS7 / MS001–MS002;
- execution of runtime behaviour or a soft Runtime A warrant;
- educational success, learner mastery, progress, or exam readiness;
- Programme IX implementation conformity, verification, compliance, or certification;
- a Programme VI / VII / VIII educational, orchestration, or runtime completion judgement;
- a claim that pipeline greens, ticket closure, elapsed time, or seal badges equal fulfilment;
- a UI celebration, analytics milestone, or “certified forever” flag as educational or constitutional law;
- a licence to skip outstanding published lifecycle duties because a product surface looks finished;
- a substitute for WS7 / MS001 certification meaning or WS7 / MS002 lifecycle law — completion *judges fulfilment of* those corpora; it does not redefine CXCD or CXCL catalogues;
- a substitute for Programme XI WS1–WS6 CDIC / CCIC / CBIC / CVIC / CLIC / CRRC completion — those horizons close *integrity / readiness assessments*; CXCC confirms *certification assessment* fulfilment after consuming those outcomes;
- a substitute for Programme IX CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC completion — those horizons evaluate implementation lineage / verification / compliance / certification; CXCC confirms *corpus certification assessment* fulfilment.

## 4. Completion Lifecycle (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material certification assessment concern that claims lawful completion travels through constitutional assessment phases. These are **normative judgement obligations**, not an implementation architecture.

```
Identify published certification assessment concern opened under CXC / CXCL law
        │
        ▼
Confirm required CXCL-01…CXCL-07 stages and CXCD-01…CXCD-05 applications for the concern
        │
        ├── refuse / defer / escalate (lawful stop — law unchanged; no invented “complete”;
        │                              no Runtime A execution; no artefact rewrite)
        │
        └── assess → Apply CXCC-01…CXCC-05
                │
                ├── not yet / refuse close (outstanding duties or boundary breach)
                │
                └── affirm → Emit completion judgement; preserve audit continuity and repeatability
                        │
                        ▼
                Explain workstreams / readiness / dimensions / status / evidence
```

| Phase | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **Identify** | Name only published certification assessment concerns opened under WS7 / MS001–MS002 | Concern is unpublished custom, tribal knowledge, or “how main seals today” |
| **Confirm lifecycle** | Verify required CXCL stages and CXCD applications for the concern | Stages would invent law, execute runtime, modify artefacts, invent assurance, or privilege a stack as law |
| **Assess criteria** | Apply CXCC-01…CXCC-05 without softening into product theatre | Any required criterion fails; or close would claim authority creation / artefact mutation / runtime execution / specification amendment |
| **Preserve & explain** | Record audit continuity; answer CXCCQ-01…CXCCQ-05 | Path would erase assessment trails, treat completion as new law, or narrate “complete therefore certified forever / Runtime A authorised / mastered” |

> **Certification completion never “best-effort invents” a fifth phase called constitutional authority creation, artefact mutation, runtime execution, or specification amendment.**

## 7. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Certification Completion Model exists so that:

1. **Lifecycle honesty remains intact** — “complete” means the published certification assessment lifecycle ran, not that a seal badge glowed green.
2. **Prerequisite evaluation remains honest** — close confirms completed WS1–WS6 assurance and readiness were evaluated; it never invents unfinished assurance.
3. **Dimension evaluation remains honest** — close confirms CXCD dimensions were assessed; it never invents dimensions or re-authors assurance.
4. **Status determination remains honest** — close confirms CXCD-05 aggregation occurred; it never silently upgrades not-certified subordinate dimensions.
5. **Publication remains honest** — close confirms findings were published under honesty rules; it never invents certified status or hides gaps.
6. **Formal closure remains speakable** — assessment close exhausts assessment duties without becoming law-making or Runtime A execution.
7. **Constitutional confidence survives close** — completion never creates authority or soft-amends corpora.
8. **Evidence traceability survives close** — completion leaves reconstructable constitutional records of consumed assurance and supporting evidence.
9. **Governance transparency survives close** — completion leaves speakable status, findings, and boundary records.
10. **Repeatability remains intact** — the same published inputs yield the same completion disposition.
11. **Authority remains upstream** — completed certification assessment never becomes the constitutional source for the next judgement.
12. **Implementations remain replaceable** — completion does not freeze Flask, SQLAlchemy, a particular API shape, or a CI vendor as law.
13. **Runtime remains unexecuted** — completion never runs Runtime A under close pretext.
14. **Explainability remains intact** — completion speech describes assessment fulfilment; it does not redefine what learning means or what conformity is.
15. **Students remain protected** — product theatre cannot launder unfinished certification work as “assessment complete therefore lawful / permanently frozen / exam-ready.”
