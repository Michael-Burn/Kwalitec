## 1. Purpose

Assessment machinery that silently turns “the certification assessment finished” into “the Constitution is now rewritten,” “yesterday’s artefacts are amended,” “this stack is now the superior authority,” “Runtime A may now run,” “student succeeded,” or “Programme IX CRT sealed” destroys student trust and educational integrity.

This document draws a bright line between **lawful constitutional certification completion** (Programme XI / WS7 / MS003) and **constitutional authority creation / artefact mutation / meaning redefinition / runtime execution / specification amendment / educational judgement** owned elsewhere.

---

## 2. Boundary Principles

1. **Confirm assessment fulfilment, do not legislate.** Completion affirms fulfilment of published certification assessment lifecycle duties; it does not create constitutional law.
2. **Assessment only.** Authority creation, artefact mutation, meaning redefinition, runtime execution, and specification amendment are never inferred from close.
3. **Not constitutional authority.** Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard after close.
4. **Not artefact rewriting.** Close never invents, soft-amends, deletes, or silently rewrites published constitutional artefacts.
5. **Not meaning redefinition.** Close never reinterprets Programme VI–X meaning, EIP claim classes, or WS1–WS6 catalogues.
6. **Not runtime execution.** Close never runs services, adapters, APIs, UI, or product behaviour under “complete” or “certified.”
7. **Not constitutional amendment.** Close never patches law or soft-amends constitutional specifications.
8. **Not MS001 meaning replacement.** Completion judges fulfilment of CXCD evaluation; it never redefines CXCD dimensions.
9. **Not MS002 lifecycle rewrite.** Completion judges fulfilment of CXCL stages; it never invents an eighth stage called law-making or runtime execution.
10. **Not Programme IX CRT.** Close never performs or pretends to be implementation certification (CRT / CRTL / CRTC).
11. **Exactly as published.** Close never redefines CXCD / CXCL catalogues or soft-amends Programme VI–X meaning.
12. **Published criteria only.** Pipelines, tickets, seal badges, and elapsed time are not fulfilment law.
13. **Published records only.** Audit continuity is constitutional, not free-form log theatre.
14. **Reference findings honestly.** Published findings may be cited; closing never upgrades disposition into certified fiction or authority.
15. **Implementation neutrality.** No stack becomes law by proximity to a complete flag.
16. **Assessment-complete ≠ quality.** Completion never grades learning, mastery, exam readiness, or product success.
17. **Assessment-complete ≠ Programme IX evaluative close.** Completion never substitutes for CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC fulfilment.
18. **Assessment-complete ≠ WS1–WS6 integrity / readiness close.** Completion never substitutes for CDIC / CCIC / CBIC / CVIC / CLIC / CRRC fulfilment (it may confirm those outcomes were consumed).
19. **Explain the stop.** Students, developers, and auditors should hear when close must be refused.
20. **No emergency exemption.** Deadlines and demos never mint completion power, authority power, or amendment power.
21. **Repeatability survives close.** Close must not invent contradictory dispositions from identical published inputs.
22. **Continuity survives close.** EIP-005 forbids erasing prior lawful assessment history to tidy the report.

## 3. What Certification Completion May Do (Lawful)

| Lawful action | Constitutional meaning |
|---------------|------------------------|
| **Confirm certification completion** | Affirm CXCC-01…CXCC-05 when published criteria hold for a lawful certification assessment concern |
| **Confirm publication** | Cite that required certification findings were lawfully constructed and published under published CXC / CXCL rules — without rewriting or silently upgrading dispositions |
| **Confirm formal closure** | Affirm that the assessment concern was formally closed under published boundary honesty |
| **Preserve assessment records** | Leave reconstructable constitutional records of workstreams, readiness, dimensions, status, stages, findings, evidence, and boundaries |
| **Preserve audit continuity** | Leave reconstructable constitutional records of affirmed or refused close |
| **Refuse unlawful close** | Prefer honest not-yet / refuse / defer / escalate over improvisation |
| **Compose required stages and types** | Confirm fulfilment across published CXCL / CXCD conjunctions without collapsing failed duties |
| **Remain implementation-neutral** | Honour completion law across lawful equivalent stacks |
| **Preserve repeatability** | Ensure same published inputs do not yield contradictory completion stories |
| **Explain completion** | Answer workstreams / readiness / dimensions / status / evidence without rewriting meaning |
| **Propose corpus amendments upstream** | Recommend (not enact) amendments when law itself must change before assessment can complete |

These actions **confirm certification assessment fulfilment**. They do **not** publish a new Constitution, invent coach warrants, mint tips, author ownership maps, invent CXCD / CXCL / CXCC types, author runtime behaviour, execute Runtime A, modify artefacts, create constitutional authority, amend constitutional specifications, finish Programme IX evaluative horizons as constitutional law, or judge educational quality.

## 4. What Certification Completion Must NEVER Do

| Forbidden action | Why | Lawful alternative |
|------------------|-----|--------------------|
| **Create constitutional authority** | Only published corpora define constitutional standards | Keep completion as assessment fulfilment; never as next-cycle law |
| **Modify constitutional artefacts** | Artefacts are published corpus truth, not draft notes | Cite findings honestly; refuse silent rewrite under close theatre |
| **Redefine constitutional meaning** | Programme VI–X / EIP / WS1–WS6 own published meaning | Preserve identity of published meaning; never invent or drift it under close |
| **Execute runtime behaviour** | Programme VIII + permitted writers own execution law; certification close is assessment only | Confirm assessment fulfilment; never run services, APIs, UI, or product behaviour under “complete” |
| **Amend constitutional specifications** | Only corpus governance may amend meaning | Leave specs unchanged; propose amendment upstream or refuse incomplete assessment |
| **Invent completed assurance** | CXCL-02 gathers completed WS1–WS6 outcomes only | Refuse invented assurance; escalate unfinished prerequisite work |
| **Invent certification dimensions** | CXCD-01…CXCD-05 are owned by WS7 / MS001 | Apply published dimensions only; never invent under close |
| **Upgrade finding disposition by closing** | CXCC-04 preserves identity; close does not rewrite status | Record findings honestly including not-certified / deferred / escalated |
| **Hide certification gaps** | Gap honesty is educational care | Publish gaps; never erase them so close can look green |
| **Replace MS001 certification evaluation** | MS001 owns CXCD meaning | Confirm assessment support for evaluation honesty; never claim “complete therefore new CXCD law” |
| **Replace MS002 lifecycle law** | MS002 owns CXCL stages | Confirm stage fulfilment; never invent stages |
| **Perform Programme IX CRT / CRTL / CRTC** | Implementation certification is a distinct horizon | Compose with CRTC; never treat CXCC as CRT seal (or vice versa) |
| **Create educational meaning** | Programme VI owns coach / planner questions | Preserve identity of published meaning; never invent it |
| **Author runtime behaviour** | Programme VIII + permitted writers own execution law | Assess corpus certification against published law; do not mint behaviour |
| **Bypass audit / publication / closure requirements** | Requirements are educational care, not optional polish | Prefer understatement / refuse over invented green completes |
| **Privilege a technology as constitutional** | Implementation neutrality | Confirm assessment obligations, not Flask / SQLAlchemy / REST / OpenAPI fashion |
| **Erase assessment trails to tidy close** | EIP-005 continuity | Preserve prior lawful stage / finding / record artefacts; refuse destructive “cleanup” |
| **Present CI / tickets / seal badges as fulfilment law** | Theatre is not constitution | Require CXCC-01…CXCC-05 content |
| **Imply educational success** | Learning outcomes are not assessment fulfilment | Keep student speech honest: assessment-complete ≠ exam-ready |
| **Imply Programme IX evaluative completion** | Conformity / verification / compliance / CRT are orthogonal | Compose with CTC / CVC / CCC / CRTC; do not treat them as CXCC (or vice versa) |

## 5. Boundary Checks (Pre-Affirmation)

Before affirming CXCC completion, confirm:

| ID | Check |
|----|-------|
| **CXCCB-01** | Concern lawfully opened under WS7 / MS001–MS002 |
| **CXCCB-02** | Required CXCL stages and CXCD applications identified without invention |
| **CXCCB-03** | CXCC-01…CXCC-05 assessed against reconstructable records |
| **CXCCB-04** | No authority-creation / artefact-mutation / meaning-redefinition claim in the completion speech |
| **CXCCB-05** | No runtime-execution / Runtime-A / specification-amendment claim in the completion speech |
| **CXCCB-06** | No educational-success / Programme-IX-CRT claim in the completion speech |
| **CXCCB-07** | No stack / vendor canonisation as completion law |
| **CXCCB-08** | CXCCQ-01…CXCCQ-05 answers producible for the judgement |
| **CXCCB-09** | Continuity preserved — prior lawful assessment history not erased |
| **CXCCB-10** | Repeatability preserved — identical published inputs would not contradict this disposition |

Failure of any check ⇒ refuse affirmation.

## 6. Closing Statement

> **Certification completion may confirm that certification assessment has lawfully concluded, that findings were published, and that the assessment was formally closed.  
> It must never create constitutional authority, modify constitutional artefacts, redefine constitutional meaning, execute runtime behaviour, or amend constitutional specifications.**
