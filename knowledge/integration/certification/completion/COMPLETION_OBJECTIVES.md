## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “seal greens,” “tickets closed,” or “certified badges shown.” The tutor optimises **honest closing of constitutional certification assessment work**: when the published lifecycle for a concern has lawfully concluded, certification prerequisites have been evaluated, certification dimensions and status determined, findings published, and the assessment formally closed, the tutor can say so — without pretending constitutional authority was created, without rewriting artefacts so certification looks tidy, without pretending Runtime A may now execute under seal, without pretending the student has mastered the topic, and without amending the syllabus because the machinery stopped cleanly.

These objectives bind every constitutional certification completion judgement and every successor assessment machinery that claims certification assessment fulfilment.

## 2. Primary Objective

### CXCCO-01 — Confirm lawful certification completion

**Definition.** Ensure that runtime / assessment / audit speech affirms certification completion only when a published assessment concern under WS7 / MS001–MS002 has concluded the required CXCL-01…CXCL-07 lifecycle obligations — or honestly refuses close when fulfilment criteria are not met.

**Includes:**

- Assessing only concerns that lawfully opened under WS7 / MS001–MS002 initiation → evidence collection → prerequisite validation → dimension evaluation → status determination → finding construction → publication law.
- Affirming completion only when `COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md` conditions (CXCC-01…CXCC-05) hold.
- Preferring lawful “not yet” / refuse-close over inventing a “done enough” status for product convenience.
- Distinguishing lawful stop dispositions (refuse / defer / escalate / not-certified as published outcomes that exhaust remaining *assessment* duties with honest recording) from abandoned lifecycle duties.
- Binding every material completion judgement to reconstructable constitutional references.

**Excludes:**

- Declaring complete because a CI job exited, a ticket closed, a seal badge glowed, or a UI spinner stopped.
- Treating Twin / Adaptive / Experience / Mission heuristics as completion warrants.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for assessment honesty.
- Collapsing “certification assessment complete” into “constitutional authority created,” “artefacts rewritten,” “specifications amended,” “Runtime A may execute,” “Programme IX CRT sealed,” or “student succeeded.”

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software celebrates unfinished certification assessment — or celebrates finished pipeline machinery as finished law-making or Runtime A permission. Honest confirmation is care; completion theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CXCCO-02 — Preserve constitutional confidence

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming completion never soft-amends published corpora, invents constitutional authority, modifies constitutional artefacts, or treats a preferred stack as the constitutional source — so closed assessments leave constitutional confidence intact rather than laundering urgency into law.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who treats “assessment complete” as licence to rewrite the syllabus or freeze a stack as superior authority has abandoned constitutional care for product theatre.

**Manifestations:**

- Close never creates constitutional authority or amends constitutional specifications.
- Close never modifies constitutional artefacts under “completion cleanup.”
- Stack / vendor / deployment-topology preferences never become constitutional standards via close.
- Boundary honesty (`COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md`) survives every affirmed close.

---

### CXCCO-03 — Preserve evidence traceability

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming completion never invents missing WS1–WS6 completion identities, re-authors integrity / readiness findings, or erases which assurance workstreams and evidence supported the certification decision — so every closed assessment remains reconstructable to published completed assurance and named supporting evidence.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later walk from yesterday’s certification close to the completed dependency / catalogue / boundary / vocabulary / layer / readiness assurance that fed it cannot be trusted. Completion that invents evidence is educational fiction dressed as done.

**Manifestations:**

- Affirmed closes leave completed-assurance identities and supporting evidence reconstructable with certification findings.
- Invented WS1–WS6 completion, re-authored integrity / readiness findings, and silent evidence erasure are defects.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids deleting prior lawful assurance-consumption records to “clean up” completion.
- Incomplete prerequisite assurance evaluation for a required concern scope blocks affirmation.

---

### CXCCO-04 — Preserve governance transparency

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming completion never hides certification status, silently upgrades not-certified / deferred / escalated findings, or erases which dimensions and decisions were published — so closed assessments remain speakable to students, developers, and auditors under published honesty rules.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who “helpfully” upgrades “not certified” to “certified” so the assessment can finish has abandoned educational honesty even if the completion UX looks clean. Completeness of *assessment* is not completeness of *certified disposition*, and neither is constitutional authority.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion leaves named certification dispositions reconstructable (certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated).
- Close never substitutes seal badges, pipeline greens, or ticket states for published certification findings.
- Continuity forbids erasing or silently rewriting prior lawful certification dispositions to simplify the next report.
- Narrating assessment-complete as constitutional authority, Runtime A warrant, or Programme IX CRT seal is a defect.

---

### CXCCO-05 — Preserve certification repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that the same published corpora, same consumed completed assurance / readiness, same CXCD applications, and same CXCL stage sequence yield the same completion disposition for the same concern — so close never invents contradictory stories from identical constitutional inputs.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who marks the same certification concern “complete” on Monday and “not yet” on Tuesday without a published corpus or assurance change has abandoned care for theatre.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion dispositions are deterministic under published inputs.
- Silent non-determinism, ambient “main branch as certified,” and demo-only close rules are defects.
- Optional honesty checks (CXCC-06…CXCC-10) support, never replace, CXCC-01…CXCC-05.
- Repeatability failures void lawful affirmation even if a badge looks green.

---

### CXCCO-06 — Preserve completion explainability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material completion judgement can answer which assurance workstreams were considered, whether runtime readiness was confirmed, which certification dimensions were satisfied, what certification status was determined, and which evidence supported the certification decision — without narrating authority creation, artefact mutation, runtime execution, or specification amendment.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot say what finished — only that “it completed” — has not closed honestly; they have performed.

**Manifestations:**

- Affirmed and refused closes answer CXCCQ-01…CXCCQ-05.
- Explanation never invents certified status, hides gaps, or narrates stack canonisation.
- Student-plain and auditor vocabulary share one constitutional truth.
- Mute “complete” badges without CXCCQ answers are defects.

## 5. Non-Objectives

Constitutional certification completion must **not** optimise for:

- Creation of constitutional authority or specification amendment via completion judgements;
- Constitutional artefact mutation or soft-amendment under close theatre;
- Runtime A execution, simulation, or shipping under “assessment complete”;
- Educational success, mastery, exam readiness, or product quality scores;
- Programme IX conformity / verification / compliance / CRT / CRTC fulfilment as if they were CXCC;
- Throughput, latency, demo continuity, or investor-review greens as substitutes for assessment honesty;
- Freezing Flask, SQLAlchemy, OpenAPI, a CI vendor, or Runtime A as constitutional law.
