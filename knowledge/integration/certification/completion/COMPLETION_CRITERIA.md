## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor needs bright conditions for saying “this constitutional certification assessment work is finished” — and equally bright refusals when it is not. After WS7 / MS001 has named how corpus certification is evaluated and WS7 / MS002 has named how assessments are organised, Programme XI Workstream 7 still needs one fulfilment checklist:

> **What must be true before constitutional speech may affirm that the required certification assessment lifecycle has concluded, certification prerequisites evaluated, certification dimensions and status determined, findings published, and the assessment formally closed?**

These criteria answer that question. They are **normative constitutional conditions**, not database predicates, CI job statuses, ticket states, seal-badge greens, or API status codes.

---

## 2. Criterion Catalogue

All material affirmed completions require **CXCC-01 through CXCC-05** to hold. Failure of any criterion ⇒ **not yet** or **refuse close**. Do not invent a partial “mostly complete” authority-creation, artefact-mutation, runtime-execution, educational, or constitutional status.

| ID | Criterion | Primary question |
|----|-----------|------------------|
| **CXCC-01** | Lifecycle completed | Were required CXCL-01…CXCL-07 stages completed for the concern? |
| **CXCC-02** | Certification assessment completed | Were certification prerequisite evaluation and required certification dimension assessments completed under published CXC / CXCL law? |
| **CXCC-03** | Certification decision completed | Was certification status determination completed under published CXCD-05 law? |
| **CXCC-04** | Findings publication completed | Were required certification findings lawfully constructed and published? |
| **CXCC-05** | Formal assessment closure | Was the certification assessment concern formally closed under published boundary honesty? |

Optional supporting honesty checks (do not replace CXCC-01…CXCC-05):

| ID | Check | Primary question |
|----|-------|------------------|
| **CXCC-06** | Catalogue honesty | Were stages and evaluations typed under published CXCL-01…CXCL-07 and CXCD-01…CXCD-05 only? |
| **CXCC-07** | Continuity honesty | Was prior lawful assessment history preserved (EIP-005)? |
| **CXCC-08** | Non-claim honesty | Does the judgement refuse authority-creation / artefact-mutation / runtime-execution / specification-amendment / educational / Programme-IX-CRT overclaim? |
| **CXCC-09** | Neutrality honesty | Does the judgement refuse stack / vendor / deployment-topology canonisation as completion law? |
| **CXCC-10** | Repeatability honesty | Would the same published inputs yield the same completion disposition? |

## 3. Criterion Definitions

### CXCC-01 — Lifecycle completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every lifecycle stage required by the concern’s published WS7 / MS002 scope has been executed — or that a published refuse / defer / escalate disposition exhausted remaining *certification assessment* duties for that stage set with honest progression to finding construction / publication (or refuse-publish) and closure of what occurred.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Identifiable certification assessment concern opened under WS7 / MS001–MS002.
- Required CXCL-01…CXCL-07 stage identities for the concern scope.
- Stage traces / disposition records for each required stage (including lawful early stops).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed stage-fulfilment record naming which required stages completed.
- Or a lawful stop record naming which stages were refused / deferred / escalated and why remaining assessment duties are exhausted.

**Completion conditions.**

- Required stages map to published CXCL-01…CXCL-07 only.
- Mandatory initiation, evidence collection, prerequisite validation, dimension evaluation, status determination, finding construction, and publication obligations for the concern are not silently omitted.
- Lawful early stops still leave honest stage outcomes; silent disappearance of a concern is a defect.
- Refusal of “pipeline skipped validation” or “we know it ran” without stage identity as completion.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That stage completion creates constitutional authority or freezes a stack as law.
- That stage completion modifies constitutional artefacts or amends constitutional specifications.
- That stage completion executes runtime behaviour or implements Runtime A.
- That stage completion means the learner succeeded or educational / exam readiness was achieved.
- That skipped stages may be invented or implied from CI theatre.
- That Programme IX conformity / verification / compliance / CRT was performed under stage progress.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who declares certification assessment “finished” without confirming each required assessment step occurred has not finished lawful care — even if the desk looks tidy.

---

### CXCC-02 — Certification assessment completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that certification prerequisite evaluation and every certification dimension assessment required by the concern’s published scope were completed under published CXC / CXCL law — including gathering identifiable completed WS1–WS6 assurance and readiness (CXCL-02), validating certification prerequisites (CXCL-03), and evaluating required CXCD-01…CXCD-04 dimensions (CXCL-04) — or that a published refuse disposition exhausted remaining *assessment* duties when required inputs could not be lawfully assessed without inventing assurance or dimensions.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Named completed WS1–WS6 assurance / readiness identities in scope (or honest gaps).
- Published certification prerequisite identities for the concern.
- Required CXCD-01…CXCD-04 dimension identities for the concern scope.
- Stage traces for CXCL-02 / CXCL-03 / CXCL-04 as required by concern scope.
- Evaluation dispositions under applicable CXCD types (assurance completeness, runtime readiness, architectural coherence, constitutional consistency).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed certification-assessment record naming which assurance workstreams were considered, whether runtime readiness was confirmed, which prerequisites were validated, and which CXCD-01…CXCD-04 dimensions were assessed — including gap outcomes.
- Or a lawful refuse / defer / escalate record naming which required assessment duties remain incomplete and why.

**Completion conditions.**

- Assurance subjects are named completed WS1–WS6 outcomes (not “the corpus,” “the stack,” or vendor labels alone).
- Assessment cites published constitutional specifications and CXCD categories, not unpublished customs.
- Collection gathered only completed / identifiable WS1–WS6 outcomes — inventing assurance to force assessment is a hard stop.
- Prerequisite validation confirms reconstructable completeness; inventing completion or skipping unfinished assurance is a hard stop.
- Dimension evaluation applies only published CXCD-01…CXCD-04 as required; inventing dimensions or re-authoring WS1–WS6 findings is a hard stop.
- Refusal of seal badges, Twin confidence, pipeline greens, or CI badges as substitutes for named certification assessment.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That assessed dimensions were therefore rewritten, invented, or soft-amended.
- That unfinished WS1–WS6 assurance was completed by certification assessment theatre.
- That a particular stack, language, or framework is privileged as law.
- That product quality or educational quality was judged.
- That constitutional artefacts or specifications were modified to match the assessment.
- That Runtime A executed or was authorised under dimension assessment.
- That overall certification status (CXCD-05) alone equals whole-concern completion (status determination feeds CXCC-03; it does not replace CXCC-01…CXCC-05).
- That Programme IX CRT implementation certification was performed.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who claims “certification was assessed” without naming which assurance workstreams and CXCD dimensions were evaluated against which published categories has performed scanner theatre, not assessment fulfilment.

### CXCC-03 — Certification decision completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that certification status determination required by the concern’s published scope has been lawfully completed under published CXCD-05 / CXCL-05 law — aggregating subordinate dimension outcomes honestly into certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated disposition — without inventing certified status while required subordinate dimensions remain unresolved without honest composition notes, and without narrating status determination as constitutional authority creation, artefact mutation, runtime execution, or specification amendment.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Dimension assessment outcomes under applicable CXCD-01…CXCD-04 (CXCC-02 / CXCL-04 outputs).
- Published overall-certification aggregation rules under WS7 / MS001–MS002 (especially CXCD-05 and CXCL-05).
- Boundary confirmations that aggregation does not invent certified status or privilege a stack as law.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed certification-decision record naming the CXCD-05 disposition and which subordinate dimension outcomes composed it (or honestly failed composition).
- Or a lawful refuse / defer / escalate record naming why status determination could not be completed without invention.

**Completion conditions.**

- Status determination cites published CXCD-05 rules, not unpublished “certified enough” customs.
- Affirming certified while required subordinate dimensions remain unresolved without composition notes is a hard stop.
- “Status was checked somewhere” without named CXCD-05 disposition is not completion.
- Lawful not-certified / deferred / escalated status outcomes still satisfy *decision completion* when they are the honest outcome — completeness of *decision* is not completeness of *certified disposition*, and neither is Runtime A permission or constitutional authority creation.
- Invented certified status to force close is a hard stop.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That completing status determination creates constitutional authority or amends constitutional specifications.
- That completing status determination modifies constitutional artefacts or executes runtime behaviour.
- That a certified disposition freezes the implementation, stack, vendor, or release as permanent constitutional law.
- That a not-certified / deferred / escalated status outcome may be silently erased so close can look green.
- That MS001 certification meaning was replaced, skipped, or rewritten by status determination.
- That Educational Validation Framework quality review is complete.
- That educational success or exam readiness follows from status determination.
- That Programme IX CRT implementation certification was issued.

**Tutor rationale.** Recording that certification status was determined — including when the disposition is not-certified — is care. Inventing certified status so the assessment can finish is fiction. Completeness of *certification decision* is not permission to *create law* or *run Runtime A*.

### CXCC-04 — Findings publication completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every certification finding required by the concern’s published scope has been lawfully constructed and published under published CXC / CXCL rules — including certified, not-certified, deferred, and escalated dispositions — without rewriting those dispositions into constitutional authority, artefact mutations, specification amendments, or Runtime A warrants, and without inventing certified status or hiding certification gaps.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Dimension and status determination dispositions under applicable CXCD-01…CXCD-05 types (CXCL-04…CXCL-05 outputs).
- Published finding-construction and publication rules under WS7 / MS001–MS002 (especially CXCL-06 / CXCL-07).
- Boundary confirmations that findings do not invent law, execute runtime, or rewrite artefacts.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Identifiable certification finding identities with lawful dispositions, published under honesty rules.
- Or a lawful stop record naming why required findings could not be constructed / published without invention.

**Completion conditions.**

- Findings cite published CXCD criteria and named assessment subjects; unpublished certification customs are hard stops.
- Certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated dispositions are published honestly; silent upgrade of not-certified into certified is a hard stop.
- Certification gaps identified during assessment remain named in publication; gap erasure for tidy certified status is forbidden.
- Publication never creates constitutional authority, modifies artefacts, amends specifications, or executes Runtime A.
- “Looks published enough” without identifiable finding identity is not completion.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That published findings amend constitutional specifications or create constitutional authority.
- That published certified status authorises Runtime A execution or freezes a stack as law.
- That unpublished findings may be implied from CI theatre, tickets, or seal badges.
- That finding publication equals educational success, conformity, verification, compliance, or Programme IX CRT certification.
- That publication may silently replace prior lawful findings (EIP-005 continuity forbids destructive cleanup).

**Tutor rationale.** Publishing that the corpus is not certified — with named gaps — is educational care. Inventing a certified finding so the pipeline can close is product fiction.

---

### CXCC-05 — Formal assessment closure

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that the certification assessment concern was formally closed under published boundary honesty — exhausting remaining *assessment* duties for the concern without becoming a fifth phase of constitutional authority creation, artefact mutation, runtime execution, or specification amendment.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Affirmed (or lawfully refused) CXCC-01…CXCC-04 outcomes for the concern.
- Published closure / boundary rules under WS7 / MS001–MS002 and `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md`.
- Assessment records sufficient for audit continuity and CXCCQ explainability.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Formal assessment-closure record for the concern (affirmed complete, or refused / deferred / escalated close with speakable reasons).
- Boundary preservation confirmation that forbidden acts did not occur under close theatre.

**Completion conditions.**

- Closure occurs only after required CXCC-01…CXCC-04 duties are fulfilled or lawfully exhausted with honest recording.
- Closure leaves reconstructable records of stages, assurance workstreams, readiness confirmation, dimensions, status, findings, evidence, and boundaries.
- Closure never creates constitutional authority, modifies artefacts, amends specifications, or executes runtime behaviour.
- Closure never upgrades published findings or invents certified status.
- Silent abandonment of an open concern without closure record is a defect.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That formal closure creates constitutional authority or amends constitutional specifications.
- That formal closure executes Runtime A or ships product behaviour.
- That formal closure modifies constitutional artefacts “as cleanup.”
- That formal closure equals student exam readiness, mastery, or educational quality.
- That formal closure equals Programme IX conformity / verification / compliance / CRT / CRTC completion.
- That ticket closure, sprint end, or seal success equals constitutional assessment closure.

**Tutor rationale.** Closing the assessment book is care. Using the close stamp as a law-making button, a syllabus rewrite, a Runtime A launch, or a “student ready” seal is fiction.

## 4. Application Rules

1. **All five required.** Material affirmed completion requires CXCC-01…CXCC-05 simultaneously.
2. **No invented sixth criterion.** Additional honesty checks (CXCC-06…CXCC-10) support judgement; they do not replace the catalogue.
3. **Not-certified may still complete.** Completeness of *assessment* is orthogonal to *certified disposition*. A lawfully published not-certified finding can support assessment completion when CXCC-01…CXCC-05 hold.
4. **Refuse over theatre.** Prefer not-yet / refuse-close when any required criterion fails.
5. **Compose, do not conflate.** WS1–WS6 integrity / readiness completion and Programme IX evaluative / CRT completion are orthogonal; none substitutes for CXCC.
6. **Repeatability.** Same published inputs must not yield contradictory CXCC dispositions for the same concern.
