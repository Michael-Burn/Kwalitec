## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “scanner finished,” “stages skipped for speed,” or “certified badge shown.” The tutor optimises **honest organisation of certification assessment work**: only a repeatable, evidence-traceable, constitutionally confident, governance-transparent, and consistent sequence — gathering completed WS1–WS6 assurance and readiness and evaluating certification under CXCD-01…CXCD-05 — may support lawful certification findings and decisions.

These objectives bind every constitutional certification lifecycle and every successor coordination machinery that claims constitutional certification lifecycle meaning.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### CXCLO-01 — Preserve lawful certification coordination

**Definition.** Ensure that every material certification lifecycle organises assessments through recognised CXCL-01…CXCL-07 stages — initiating against identifiable published certification concerns, gathering completed WS1–WS6 assurance and readiness evidence, validating prerequisites, evaluating certification dimensions and overall status under published CXCD-01…CXCD-05, and constructing / publishing findings and decisions — so that certified dispositions occur only after lawful initiation, collection, validation, and evaluation, and that findings are published without becoming law, runtime execution, artefact mutation, Programme IX CRT seals, or replacements for constitutional authority.

**Includes:**

- Binding every material lifecycle to identifiable published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–X specifications, WS1–WS6 completed assurance / readiness identities, and WS7 / MS001 CXCD catalogue law.
- Gathering only completed / identifiable assurance and readiness evidence as inputs.
- Applying only published CXCD-01…CXCD-05 dimensions across stages.
- Preferring lawful not-certified / deferred / escalated / refuse-progress outcomes over inventing assurance, inventing prerequisites, inventing dimensions, executing runtime, mutating corpora, creating authority, or collapsing into Programme IX CRT so the lifecycle “completes.”
- Keeping lifecycle progress distinct from educational quality, mastery, student exam readiness, product success, deployment-gate greenery, Runtime A execution, Programme IX CRT success, or constitutional amendment.
- Preserving the Model’s posture: stages coordinate assessment; they do not become law, runtime engines, or constitutional authority.

**Excludes:**

- Treating green CI, coverage percentages, linter scores, Twin estimates, Adaptive scores, UI ticks, or deployment gates as lifecycle fulfilment.
- Soft-amending specifications, inventing unpublished customs, inventing or rewriting assurance, inventing completion identities, mutating artefacts, executing Runtime A, or privileging “how main ships seals” to force progress.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for lawful coordination.
- Declaring “close enough” publication without documenting a corpus amendment.
- Freezing an implementation as permanently lawful because the lifecycle published findings.
- Creating constitutional authority under a lifecycle badge.
- Collapsing “corpus certified from evidence” into “Runtime A executed” or “Programme IX CRT sealed.”

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software pretends unfinished or dishonest certification assessment is fine because integration needs a finished green seal. Coordination honesty is care; lifecycle theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CXCLO-02 — Preserve certification repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that the same published certification concern, the same consumed completed assurance and readiness evidence, the same published CXCD criteria, and the same CXCL stage sequence yield the same certification disposition (certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated) and the same lawful stage outcomes — absent a published non-deterministic exception (none are granted by this Model for constitutional meaning or certification status).

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who treats the same completed syllabus assurance as certified or not depending on which reviewer woke first — or skips stages randomly — is not coaching; they are gambling.

**Manifestations:**

- Only CXCL-01…CXCL-07 may organise constitutional certification lifecycles.
- Stage purpose, inputs, outputs, and permitted / prohibited activities remain as published in `LIFECYCLE_STAGES.md`.
- Stage transitions and outcomes are corpus-bound and reproducible.
- Parallel CI runners, sharding, and cache races must not change constitutional disposition or invent stage skips.
- Explicit multi-dimension CXCD sets use published conjunction / published stop rules, not random reviewer races.
- “Eventually consistent lifecycle meaning” is a constitutional defect.
- Invented or unstable assurance inputs, and invented prerequisites, are hard stops — not soft variables.

---

### CXCLO-03 — Preserve evidence traceability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material lifecycle gathers — and leaves reconstructable — completed WS1–WS6 assurance and readiness identities and published corpus references consumed as certification inputs, so origin of every certified / not-certified disposition remains speakable across stages.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who cannot say *which completed integrity and readiness assessments* yesterday’s “certified” seal consumed cannot be trusted. Lifecycles without evidence-traceability are educational amnesia with a progress bar.

**Manifestations:**

- CXCL-02 requires identifiable completed assurance and readiness evidence; missing identities are defects, not soft variables.
- Findings cite corpus paths, completion identities, readiness identities, and CXCD identities, not only ticket IDs or commit SHAs.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing prior lawful assessment history to simplify the next report.
- Programme IX CT lineage may support reconstruction; CXCL never treats CT records as a licence to invent completed assurance.
- Evidence-traceability preservation never authorises inventing missing completion artefacts or re-authoring WS1–WS6 findings.

---

### CXCLO-04 — Preserve constitutional confidence

**Definition.** Ensure that lifecycle findings support honest constitutional confidence — so certified claims consume completed assurance, readiness, and published guidance through lawful stages rather than inventing confidence despite unfinished prerequisites, unresolved dimensions, or incompletely assessed corpora — and never elevate lifecycle machinery into constitutional authority.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who declares “constitutionally confident — ship it” while integrity or readiness assessments remain unfinished is performing confidence theatre, not care.

**Manifestations:**

- Certified findings published after CXCL-01…CXCL-07 may inform downstream integration work; they never themselves certify educational readiness, mastery, product success, Programme IX CRT success, or freeze stack authority.
- Not-certified / deferred / escalated findings, and refuse-* dispositions, block honest confidence claims that depend on incomplete or broken assurance.
- Confidence proxies (CI greens, coverage, deployment gates) never redefine CXCLO-01…CXCLO-06.
- Constitutional confidence here means *honest corpus certification from lawful assessment coordination* — not student exam readiness and not Runtime A deployment theatre.
- Preserving confidence never authorises rewriting artefacts, executing runtime, creating authority, or skipping stages so a release can ship.

---

### CXCLO-05 — Preserve governance transparency

**Definition.** Ensure that every material certification lifecycle leaves reconstructable, speakable governance trails — which stages ran, which prerequisites held or failed, which refusals occurred, and which boundaries remained intact — so certification coordination remains auditable and never hides behind opaque seals.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later say *at which stage* certification stopped — or *why* a prerequisite failed — cannot be trusted. Silent green seals destroy student and auditor trust.

**Manifestations:**

- Explainability obligations in `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md` apply to all material lifecycles.
- Student / developer / auditor projections share one truth with different vocabulary.
- Audit records preserve constitutional references, not only technical deployment logs.
- Consumed corpus identities, completed assurance / readiness identities, CXCD identities, CXCL stage identities, gap records, finding dispositions, and boundary confirmations are recorded when material.
- Missing CXCL identity, missing workstream / readiness refs, missing dimension status, missing status disposition, missing evidence identity, or missing boundary confirmation is a defect.
- Lifecycle progress is never narrated as mastery, student exam readiness, educational quality, new law, runtime execution, Programme IX CRT success, or a freeze of stack authority.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing prior lawful lifecycle history to simplify the next report.

---

### CXCLO-06 — Preserve assessment consistency

**Definition.** Ensure that lifecycle organisation honours published assessment consistency — required CXCD-01…CXCD-05 dimensions remain evaluated against completed WS1–WS6 assurance and published MS001 rules under the same CXCL sequence — so no stage silently invents dimensions, skips prerequisites, or invents certified status through inconsistent application.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who applies different certification standards to the same completed assurance depending on which gate is running has abandoned institutional order for convenience.

**Manifestations:**

- CXCL-04 evaluates required CXCD-01…CXCD-04 dimensions under published MS001 rules; silent omission to force certified is defective.
- CXCL-05 aggregates under CXCD-05 without inventing missing dimension results.
- Workstream / milestone corpora remain subordinate to their published programme models.
- Sibling composition within a layer is permitted only as published; it never collapses distinct horizons into one mega-law (especially CXC vs Programme IX CRT).
- Forbidden actions in `LIFECYCLE_BOUNDARIES.md` are hard stops.
- Assessment consistency preservation never authorises reordering stages, softening prerequisites, or inventing certified status outside published CXCD criteria.

## 5. Non-Objectives

The constitutional certification lifecycle does **not** optimise for:

- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- CI throughput, flaky-test suppression, or coverage theatre as educational success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when specifications or completed assurance are incomplete;
- collapsing CXCL stages into one mega-step that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics / deployment-gate signals as substitutes for published completed assurance;
- freezing implementations, releases, or stacks as permanently lawful;
- treating documentation as optional commentary on whatever code already shipped;
- replacing MS001 certification meaning, Programme IX CRT / CRTL, Programme IX CT lineage preservation, or Programme IX–X evaluative / execution horizons;
- executing Runtime A or product behaviour so certification “looks proven”;
- rewriting, inventing, or deleting constitutional artefacts so certification looks tidy;
- creating constitutional authority under a lifecycle badge.
