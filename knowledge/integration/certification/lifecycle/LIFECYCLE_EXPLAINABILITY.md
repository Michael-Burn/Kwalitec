## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which assurance workstreams* were evaluated — which readiness evidence was considered, which certification dimensions were satisfied, what certification status was determined, and which evidence supported the decision — is not coaching; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–X have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, runtime, governance, and execution speech speakable, after WS1–WS6 have made integrity and readiness speech speakable, and after WS7 / MS001 has made certification evaluation speech speakable, Programme XI still needs one lifecycle-speech answer:

> **“Which assurance workstreams were evaluated, which readiness evidence was considered, which certification dimensions were satisfied, what certification status was determined, and which evidence supported the decision?”**

That answer must cover every material certification lifecycle without inventing educational meaning, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, mutating artefacts, creating constitutional authority, executing Runtime A, freezing a stack as law, inventing unpublished CXCD or CXCL types, replacing MS001 certification meaning, collapsing into Programme IX CRT seals, or presenting publication as educational quality.

## 2. What Lifecycle Explainability Is

**Constitutional Certification Lifecycle Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **coordination of named certification assessments through published CXCL stages** — gathering completed assurance and readiness, validating prerequisites, evaluating published CXCD dimensions, determining overall certification status, and producing / publishing findings and decisions — not to invent a second tutor voice from deployment gates, not to grade the student, and not to replace constitutional authority.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Assurance workstreams evaluated** | Named WS1–WS5 workstreams whose completed assurance was consumed | Which assurance workstreams were evaluated? |
| **Readiness evidence considered** | Named WS6 CRR / CRRD readiness identities consumed (or honest gaps) | Which readiness evidence was considered? |
| **Certification dimensions satisfied** | Named CXCD-01…CXCD-05 dispositions for the concern | Which certification dimensions were satisfied? |
| **Certification status determined** | certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated (with stage binding and composition notes) | What certification status was determined? |
| **Evidence supporting the decision** | Named completed assurance / readiness identities and CXCD dispositions that support the published decision | Which evidence supported the decision? |

Lifecycle Explainability is:

- **coordination-centred** — it narrates workstreams → readiness → dimensions → status → evidence under law;
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CXCL and CXCD identities;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published corpora and completed assurance / readiness identities;
- **assurance-honest** — every material justification cites identifiable completed assurance consumed (or names the hard stop / gap when unmet);
- **readiness-honest** — every material justification cites identifiable readiness evidence consumed (or names the hard stop / gap when unmet);
- **dimension-honest** — every material justification cites CXCD dispositions without inventing dimensions;
- **status-honest** — every material justification cites overall status without inventing certified outcomes;
- **evidence-honest** — every material decision cites supporting evidence without hiding gaps;
- **finding-honest** — every material finding is bound to the producing lifecycle stages;
- **record-honest** — every material lifecycle cites which assessment records were preserved / published;
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to amend corpora;
- **non-authoritative** — publication is never narrated as a freeze of stack authority or a substitute for published corpora;
- **non-executing** — explanation never narrates Runtime A execution as certification proof;
- **horizon-honest** — certified status is never narrated as Programme IX CRT implementation certification;
- **quality-honest** — certified status is never narrated as mastery, student exam readiness, or educational quality;
- **mutation-honest** — explanation never hides artefact changes performed “so the lifecycle could proceed”;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence / validation speech, Programme IX governance / CRT speech, Programme X activity speech, WS1–WS6 integrity / readiness speech, or WS7 / MS001 certification evaluation speech;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match CXCLO-05 reconstruction needs;
- **repeatability-honest** — explanations must not paper over race-dependent dispositions or private stage shortcuts.

Lifecycle Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–X / WS1–WS6 / WS7 / MS001 meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a substitute for CXCD findings speech, CT lineage speech, Programme IX CRT / CRTL speech, or Programme IX–X evaluative / execution speech;
- a CI status page, coverage report, deployment-gate summary, or linter summary presented as constitutional coordination;
- a freeze of implementations, stacks, vendors, or releases as constitutional law;
- a licence to narrate invented assurance, invented dimensions, executed Runtime A, or mutated artefacts as if they were lawful inputs.

## 3. Mandatory Explainability Questions

Every material constitutional certification lifecycle must be able to answer:

### CXCLEQ-01 — Which assurance workstreams were evaluated?

**Required content**

- Named assurance workstreams consumed at CXCL-02 / validated at CXCL-03 (e.g. dependency, catalogue, boundary, vocabulary, layer completion identities; and VI–X guidance presence where required)
- Binding between workstream identities and intended / applied CXCD types
- Confirmation that unpublished customs, invented completion, or deployment-gate noise were not used as constitutional assurance

**Defect if**

- “Assurance is fine” with no workstream or completion references
- Anonymous “the docs” or “the pipelines” without identifiable completed assurance
- Assurance invented, re-authored, or silently assumed complete without disclosure and hard stop

---

### CXCLEQ-02 — Which readiness evidence was considered?

**Required content**

- Named WS6 CRR / CRRD readiness identities consumed at CXCL-02 / validated at CXCL-03 (or honest gaps when readiness evidence is incomplete / absent)
- Binding between readiness identities and CXCD-02 (runtime readiness) / overall status composition
- Confirmation that unpublished readiness customs or deployment-gate greens were not used as constitutional readiness evidence

**Defect if**

- “Ready enough” with no reconstructable readiness identity
- Readiness invented, re-authored, or silently assumed achieved without disclosure and hard stop
- Narration that unreadiness was “resolved” by executing Runtime A under lifecycle pretence

---

### CXCLEQ-03 — Which certification dimensions were satisfied?

**Required content**

- Named CXCD-01…CXCD-05 dispositions from CXCL-04 / CXCL-05 (certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated per required dimension)
- Binding between each dimension disposition and consumed assurance / readiness / prerequisite records
- Explicit composition notes when optional dimensions were lawfully scoped out
- Confirmation that unpublished certification dimensions were not applied

**Defect if**

- “Certified” with no reconstructable CXCD identities
- Required dimensions silently omitted to force overall certified
- Dimension results invented at aggregation without stage binding

---

### CXCLEQ-04 — What certification status was determined?

**Required content**

- Named overall certification status from CXCL-05 (certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated)
- Binding of status to producing CXCL stages (especially CXCL-03…CXCL-06)
- Finding construction identity from CXCL-06 and publication artefacts from CXCL-07 where material (or refuse-publish identity)
- Continuity sufficient to reconstruct corpora, workstreams, readiness, dimensions, status, CXCD types, stages, findings, and boundaries

**Defect if**

- “Certified” with no reconstructable status or finding identity
- Findings that drop stage-to-status binding or assurance / dimension identity
- Silent erasure of prior lawful assessment / lifecycle history
- Publication that invents certified status or hides not-certified dispositions

---

### CXCLEQ-05 — Which evidence supported the decision?

**Required content**

- Named completed assurance / readiness identities and CXCD dispositions that support the published decision
- Binding between evidence identities and the certification decision / finding
- Gap disclosure when supporting evidence is incomplete or when not-certified / deferred / escalated rests on named gaps
- Confirmation that evidence was not invented, re-authored, or mutated under lifecycle pretence

**Defect if**

- “Evidence supports certified” with no reconstructable evidence identities
- Supporting evidence silently swapped, invented, or erased between stages
- Narration that gaps were “resolved” by rewriting artefacts, inventing assurance, executing runtime, or creating authority under lifecycle pretence

Projections differ in vocabulary. They must not differ in constitutional facts.

## 5. Forbidden Explainability Moves

| Forbidden narration | Why | Lawful alternative |
|---------------------|-----|--------------------|
| “Lifecycle published ⇒ new constitutional law” | Legislation via publication | “Findings recorded; corpora unchanged” |
| “Lifecycle published ⇒ artefacts rewritten” | Artefact mutation | “Certification assessed as found; artefacts unchanged” |
| “Lifecycle published ⇒ authority created” | Authority creation | “Assessment coordinated; published corpora remain the standard” |
| “Lifecycle published ⇒ Runtime A executed” | Runtime execution | “Corpus certification evaluated; no runtime behaviour executed” |
| “Certified ⇒ Programme IX CRT sealed” | Horizon conflation | Keep corpus certification distinct from implementation certification |
| “Certified ⇒ student ready / mastered” | Quality / mastery out of scope | Keep corpus-certification speech distinct from educational claim classes |
| “We invented the missing completion so prerequisites validated” | Invented assurance | Refuse-validate; propose lawful completion upstream |
| “We fixed the gap in the lifecycle” | Artefact / assurance mutation | Report gap-identified; refuse rewriting under assessment pretence |
| “Stage skipped because the deployment gate was green” | Unpublished stage customs | Apply CXCL-01…CXCL-07 or refuse |
| “We adjusted the docs so the lifecycle could finish” | Specification / artefact mutation | Refuse-collect / not-certified; propose lawful change outside lifecycle pretence |
| “This stack is now the standard” | Authority substitution / neutrality breach | Keep implementation as assessed subject |
| “Findings update the constitution” | Legislation via explanation | Propose amendment upstream; leave corpora unchanged |
| “Linked in CT ⇒ certified forever” | CT / CXC conflation | Compose with CT; keep certification speech distinct |

## 7. Audit Alignment

Lifecycle explainability records must support reconstruction of:

1. Assurance workstreams evaluated (CXCLEQ-01)
2. Readiness evidence considered (CXCLEQ-02)
3. Certification dimensions satisfied (CXCLEQ-03)
4. Certification status determined and findings / publication artefacts preserved (CXCLEQ-04)
5. Evidence supporting the decision (CXCLEQ-05)
6. Applied CXCD-01…CXCD-05 identities (from WS7 / MS001 speech)
7. Finding dispositions (certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated / refuse-*)
8. Constitutional boundaries remaining intact (`LIFECYCLE_BOUNDARIES.md` / CXCLB checks)
9. Continuity of prior lawful lifecycle history (EIP-005)

Missing any material element is an explainability defect under CXCLO-05.
