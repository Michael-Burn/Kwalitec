## 1. Purpose

Certification assessment without a closed stage catalogue invents law by proximity: whichever deployment gate, ticket status, or reviewer habit happens to finish becomes the tutor’s “proof the system ran a constitutional certification assessment.”

This catalogue names the only lawful constitutional certification lifecycle stages — and binds each stage to purpose, inputs, outputs, and permitted / prohibited activities.

---

## 2. Stage Principles

1. **Ordered and skip-disciplined.** Stages may be brief under thin scope; mandatory initiation, evidence collection, prerequisite validation, dimension evaluation, status determination, finding construction, and publication obligations may not be silently omitted when a certification finding is claimed.
2. **Completed assurance before findings.** Identifiable published corpora and completed WS1–WS6 assurance / readiness are confirmed before certified / not-certified dispositions are constructed.
3. **Explainability accumulates.** Each stage leaves traceable artefacts for `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.
4. **Refusal is a stage outcome.** Not-certified, deferred, escalated, and refuse-progress are first-class conclusions.
5. **Deterministic transitions.** Same published inputs ⇒ same next stage outcomes.
6. **Stages coordinate; they do not legislate.** No stage creates, modifies, or redefines constitutional law.
7. **Stages do not become constitutional authority.** Publication never freezes a corpus topology as permanently lawful or replaces published corpora.
8. **Stages do not execute runtime.** Findings report certification status; they never implement Runtime A or run product behaviour.
9. **Stages apply published CXCD law.** Dimensions and evaluation rules remain owned by WS7 / MS001.
10. **Stages consume completed assurance.** They never re-author WS1–WS6 findings under certification pretext.
11. **Stages remain horizon-honest.** They never collapse into Programme IX CRT / CRTL implementation certification.

## 3. Catalogue Overview

| ID | Stage | Constitutional purpose (short) | Primary inputs | Primary outputs |
|----|-------|--------------------------------|----------------|-----------------|
| **CXCL-01** | Assessment Initiation | Open a certification assessment against named published corpora and scope | Published corpus identities + concern scope + optional CXCD indication | Initiated concern record (or refuse-open) |
| **CXCL-02** | Evidence Collection | Gather completed WS1–WS6 assurance and readiness evidence for the concern | Initiated concern + candidate completion / readiness identities | Collected evidence set (or refuse-collect) |
| **CXCL-03** | Certification Prerequisite Validation | Validate certification prerequisites and completeness | Collected evidence + published prerequisite rules | Prerequisite disposition (or refuse-validate) |
| **CXCL-04** | Certification Dimension Evaluation | Evaluate required CXCD-01…CXCD-04 dimensions | Prerequisite-valid bundle + CXCD rules | Dimension dispositions + named gaps (or refuse-evaluate) |
| **CXCL-05** | Certification Status Determination | Aggregate under CXCD-05 overall certification status | Dimension dispositions + concern scope | Overall certification status (or refuse-aggregate) |
| **CXCL-06** | Finding Construction | Emit certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated under published criteria | Stage dispositions + CXCD criteria | Certification findings |
| **CXCL-07** | Certification Publication | Publish findings and decisions under honesty rules | Findings + boundary confirmations | Published assessment artefacts (or refuse-publish) |

Material certification assessment claiming constitutional findings must map to this sequence as published law requires. Stages may be brief; none may invent a stage outside this catalogue.

**Relation to CXCD / CXCO / CT / CRT / CRTL / WS1–WS6:** Those catalogues remain defined solely by their owners. CXCL stages *organise when* certification assessment activities occur; they do not replace, reclassify, invent, amend, or rewrite those catalogues.

## 5. Stage Definitions

### CXCL-01 — Assessment Initiation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Open a material certification assessment concern by naming the published constitutional corpora under assessment, the certification concern scope, and the intended CXCD dimension set — without inventing law, opening against unpublished customs, treating initiation as a certified finding, or executing runtime behaviour.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Identifiable published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–X specification references for the concern scope
- Concern identity (what corpus-certification-from-assurance-and-readiness question is under assessment)
- Optional published indication of required CXCD-01…CXCD-05 dimensions for the scope
- Optional identifiable supporting authority / lineage / completion / readiness references (not rewritten)

**Constitutional outputs**

- An initiated assessment concern record naming corpora, specifications, scope, and intended CXCD type set — **or**
- Lawful refuse-open when standards are unpublished, scope is theatrical, or initiation would invent law

**Permitted activities**

- Name published corpora and specifications as assessment standards
- Bound the concern scope without amending corpora
- Record intended CXCD types from the published catalogue
- Refuse initiation when constitutional preconditions fail
- Preserve initiation audit identity for later stages

**Prohibited activities**

- Treat “how main ships seals,” tribal knowledge, or stack preference as the standard
- Create or modify constitutional specifications by opening a concern
- Issue a certified / not-certified finding at initiation
- Invent unpublished certification dimensions
- Skip initiation when a material finding will later be claimed
- Create constitutional authority, execute runtime, or implement Runtime A at initiation
- Open a Programme IX CRT concern under CXCL pretence

**Authority preserved**  
Published corpora remain the sole standard. Initiation organises; it does not legislate or execute.

---

### CXCL-02 — Evidence Collection

**Constitutional purpose**  
Gather **completed WS1–WS6 assurance and readiness evidence** identifiable for the initiated concern — so later stages evaluate real completed integrity and readiness outcomes, not anonymous theatre — without inventing, rewriting, or re-authoring assurance or readiness findings.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Initiated concern from CXCL-01
- Candidate completed assurance identities from Programme XI / WS1–WS5 (dependency, catalogue, boundary, vocabulary, layer — including lawful completion identities)
- Candidate readiness identities from Programme XI / WS6 (CRR / CRRD findings — including lawful readiness lifecycle / completion identities where published)
- Published CXCD required-input rules from WS7 / MS001
- Optional supporting CT lineage identities already established under upstream law (unchanged)

**Constitutional outputs**

- A collected evidence set bound to the concern and intended CXCD types — **or**
- Lawful refuse-collect when evidence is missing, unfinished, forged, invented, unpublished, or would require unlawful mutation / re-authorship to “collect”

**Permitted activities**

- Identify and name completed WS1–WS6 assurance and readiness outcomes
- Bind collected evidence to the initiated concern and intended CXCD types
- Record honest gaps where required assurance or readiness is incomplete or absent
- Refuse collection when evidence cannot be honestly affirmed as completed
- Preserve collection records for audit and explainability

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent missing completed assurance or readiness
- Re-author, soft-amend, or rewrite WS1–WS6 findings under collection pretence
- Treat CI inventory scans, ticket links, or deployment noise as constitutional assurance without published mapping
- Redefine educational or constitutional meaning while “collecting”
- Present collection as itself a certified finding or a freeze of stack authority
- Create constitutional authority or execute runtime under collection pretence

**Authority preserved**  
Completed assurance and readiness remain upstream facts. Collection organises identity; it does not author assurance, readiness, or educational truth.

---

### CXCL-03 — Certification Prerequisite Validation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Validate that **published certification prerequisites** — including required completed assurance completeness, readiness identity honesty, CXCD scope honesty, corpus citeability, and boundary integrity material to the concern — hold before dimension evaluation proceeds — without inventing completion, skipping unfinished assurance, or privileging a stack as law.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Collected evidence set from CXCL-02
- Published certification prerequisite rules from WS7 / MS001
- Published CXCD required-input tables for the concern scope
- Optional supporting authority / lineage references consumed as inputs (unchanged)

**Constitutional outputs**

- A validated prerequisite record for the concern — **or**
- Lawful refuse-validate when prerequisites are unmet, completion is invented, unfinished assurance is silently skipped, or validation would require rewriting law or artefacts

**Permitted activities**

- Confirm reconstructable completeness for in-scope WS1–WS6 inputs
- Confirm intended CXCD scope remains published and honest for the concern
- Confirm required VI–X corpora remain citeable as published guidance (presence, not re-determination)
- Refuse validation when prerequisites cannot be honestly affirmed
- Preserve prerequisite records for audit and explainability

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent completion or readiness identities to force validation
- Silently skip unfinished prerequisite assurance so later stages can affirm certified
- Treat deployment gates or CI greens as prerequisite satisfaction
- Redefine CXCD meaning while validating
- Present prerequisite validation as itself a certified finding
- Create constitutional authority, execute runtime, or modify artefacts under validation pretence

**Authority preserved**  
Published prerequisites remain upstream law. Validation confirms; it does not invent certification or rewrite corpora.

---

### CXCL-04 — Certification Dimension Evaluation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Evaluate required **CXCD-01…CXCD-04** certification dimensions (assurance completeness, runtime readiness, architectural coherence, constitutional consistency) under published WS7 / MS001 rules from completed assurance and readiness — without inventing dimensions, re-authoring assurance, or executing Runtime A.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Prerequisite-valid (or honestly refused) bundle from CXCL-03
- Published CXCD-01…CXCD-04 definitions and evaluation rules from [`../CERTIFICATION_MODEL.md`](../CERTIFICATION_MODEL.md)
- Collected completed assurance and readiness evidence bound to each required dimension
- Published VI–X guidance presence references where required by dimension

**Constitutional outputs**

- Per-dimension dispositions (certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated) with named certification gaps — **or**
- Lawful refuse-evaluate when evaluation would invent dimensions, re-author assurance, execute runtime, or rewrite law

**Permitted activities**

- Apply published CXCD-01…CXCD-04 only as required by concern scope
- Consume completed assurance and readiness as dimension inputs
- Name certification gaps honestly
- Refuse evaluation when required inputs are unavailable without inventing them
- Preserve dimension evaluation records for audit and explainability

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent unpublished certification dimensions
- Re-author WS1–WS6 findings under dimension evaluation pretence
- Soft-omit a required dimension to force overall certified later
- Execute Runtime A, services, or adapters to “prove” a dimension
- Determine educational quality, mastery, or student exam readiness
- Issue Programme IX CRT seals under CXCD evaluation pretence
- Create constitutional authority or amend specifications under evaluation pretence

**Authority preserved**  
CXCD catalogue remains owned by WS7 / MS001. Evaluation applies; it does not legislate or execute.

---

### CXCL-05 — Certification Status Determination

**Constitutional purpose**  
Aggregate required dimension dispositions under **CXCD-05 overall certification status** for the published concern — without inventing missing dimension results, silently overriding not-certified subordinates, or treating aggregation as new law.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Dimension dispositions and gap records from CXCL-04
- Published CXCD-05 aggregation rules from WS7 / MS001
- Concern scope and composition notes requirements

**Constitutional outputs**

- An overall certification status for the concern — **or**
- Lawful refuse-aggregate when aggregation would invent missing dimensions, hide required not-certified subordinates without composition notes, or become legislation / runtime execution / Programme IX CRT substitution

**Permitted activities**

- Aggregate under published CXCD-05 rules only
- Require honest composition notes when lawful partial scopes omit optional dimensions
- Refuse aggregation that would invent certified status
- Preserve overall status records for audit and explainability

**Prohibited activities**

- Affirm overall certified while any *required* subordinate dimension remains not-certified / deferred / escalated without honest composition notes
- Invent dimension results at aggregation time
- Collapse overall certification into Runtime A execution warrant or Programme IX CRT seal
- Treat overall certified as student exam readiness or product success
- Create constitutional authority or amend specifications under aggregation pretence

**Authority preserved**  
Overall certification status remains an aggregate disposition under published CXCD-05. Aggregation coordinates; it does not legislate.

---

### CXCL-06 — Finding Construction

**Constitutional purpose**  
Construct **certification findings** — certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated — bound to producing stages, consumed assurance and readiness, dimensions, and named gaps — without findings becoming amendments, educational quality grades, runtime execution warrants, Programme IX CRT seals, or artefact mutations.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Stage dispositions from CXCL-01…CXCL-05
- Published CXCD evaluation criteria and disposition vocabulary from WS7 / MS001
- Named certification gaps and boundary confirmations material to the concern

**Constitutional outputs**

- Certification findings with reconstructable binding to workstreams, readiness, dimensions, status, evidence, stages, and consumed assurance

**Permitted activities**

- Emit certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated under published criteria
- Bind findings to producing CXCL stages and CXCD identities
- Include named certification gaps in finding records
- Prefer understatement over invented certified status
- Preserve finding identities for publication and audit

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent certified status without reconstructable stage / assurance / dimension binding
- Convert findings into constitutional amendments or soft law
- Narrate findings as mastery, student exam readiness, educational quality, or Programme IX CRT success
- Encode Runtime A execution, stack freezes, or authority creation in findings
- Drop gap disclosure to make findings look clean
- Re-author WS1–WS6 assurance inside finding prose

**Authority preserved**  
Findings report certification dispositions. They never become the next constitutional standard.

---

### CXCL-07 — Certification Publication

**Constitutional purpose**  
Publish certification findings and decisions under **published honesty rules** — so students, developers, and auditors can reconstruct the lifecycle — without inventing certified status, hiding gaps, rewriting boundaries, executing runtime, or freezing a stack as law.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Findings from CXCL-06
- Assessment records from prior stages
- Boundary confirmations against `LIFECYCLE_BOUNDARIES.md`
- Explainability answers required by `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md`

**Constitutional outputs**

- Published assessment artefacts (findings, decisions, gap records, stage traces, boundary confirmations) — **or**
- Lawful refuse-publish when publication would invent certified status, hide gaps, rewrite boundaries, execute runtime, create authority, collapse into Programme IX CRT, or freeze a stack as law

**Permitted activities**

- Publish findings and decisions under honesty rules
- Disclose certification gaps and refuse-* dispositions
- Confirm boundaries remained intact
- Refuse publication when honesty cannot be affirmed
- Preserve publication artefacts for continuity (EIP-005)

**Prohibited activities**

- Publish invented certified status or silently drop violated / not-certified dispositions
- Hide certification gaps or unfinished prerequisite assurance
- Rewrite constitutional artefacts or specifications at publication time
- Execute Runtime A or implement services under publication pretence
- Present publication as new constitutional law, frozen stack authority, or Programme IX CRT recognition
- Create constitutional authority by publishing

**Authority preserved**  
Publication communicates findings and decisions. Published corpora remain unchanged and remain the sole constitutional standard.

## 6. Stage Transition Rules

1. **Forward only under honesty.** A stage may advance only when its outputs are reconstructable or a lawful refuse-* / deferred / escalated disposition is recorded.
2. **No silent skips.** Claiming a material certification finding without mappable CXCL-01…CXCL-07 coordination is defective.
3. **Early stop still publishes honesty.** Lawful early stops still require finding construction and publication (or refuse-publish) of what occurred.
4. **Same inputs ⇒ same outcomes.** Race-dependent stage outcomes are defects under CXCLO-02.
5. **No back-door legislation.** Returning to earlier stages to “fix” corpora, invent assurance, or execute runtime is prohibited; propose amendments upstream outside lifecycle pretence.
6. **CXCD fidelity.** Stages never invent certification dimensions outside CXCD-01…CXCD-05.
7. **Assurance fidelity.** Stages never re-author WS1–WS6 findings to force certified status.
8. **Horizon fidelity.** Stages never substitute Programme IX CRT / CRTL for corpus certification.
