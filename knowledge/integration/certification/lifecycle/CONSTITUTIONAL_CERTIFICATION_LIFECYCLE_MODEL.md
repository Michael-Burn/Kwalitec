## 2. What the Constitutional Certification Lifecycle Is

The **constitutional certification lifecycle** is the binding organisation by which **corpus certification assessments** may proceed through recognised stages — initiating against published certification concerns, gathering completed WS1–WS6 assurance and readiness evidence, validating certification prerequisites, evaluating certification under published CXCD-01…CXCD-05 dimensions, determining overall certification status, and constructing / publishing **certification findings and decisions** that preserve certification repeatability, evidence traceability, constitutional confidence, governance transparency, assessment consistency, auditability, and explainability — without becoming a source of constitutional law, a runtime executor, a rewriter of artefacts, a Programme IX implementation certificate, a substitute for MS001 certification meaning, or a replacement for upstream programme authority.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Published constitutional corpus** | Constitution, EIP, Programme VI–XI specifications and their subordinate workstream / milestone corpora | Which corpus may the lifecycle organise certification assessment against? |
| **Completed prerequisite assurance** | Lawful WS1–WS6 integrity completion and readiness outcomes available for honest consumption | Which completed assurance may the lifecycle gather? |
| **Certification prerequisite** | Published precondition for lawful certification evaluation (completed assurance identity, readiness identity, CXCD scope, corpus citeability, boundary integrity) | Which prerequisites must be satisfied before dimension evaluation? |
| **Certification dimension** | A recognised CXCD-01…CXCD-05 category of certification judgement from WS7 / MS001 | Which certification kinds may stages apply? |
| **Lifecycle stage** | A recognised CXCL-01…CXCL-07 organisational step | Which coordination step is active? |
| **Certification gap** | Named missing or incomplete assurance input, readiness shortfall, dimension shortfall, or coherence defect blocking certified status | Were any certification gaps identified? |
| **Certification finding / decision** | Lawful disposition: certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated — never “new law” and never executed runtime behaviour | What status was determined and published? |
| **Assessment record / publication artefact** | Audit-speakable record of workstreams, readiness, prerequisites, dimensions, status, evidence, stages, findings, and preserved boundaries | Can the assessment be reconstructed? |
| **Constitutional authority** | The exclusive right of published corpora to define educational and execution meaning | Who owns the standard? |
| **Implementation neutrality** | Lifecycle coordination is judged against law and completed assurance, not against a preferred stack, vendor, or topology | Remains the stack replaceable? |
| **Repeatability** | Same published concerns, same consumed completed assurance, same prerequisites, same stage sequence → same disposition | Can the judgement be reproduced? |
| **Auditability** | Every material lifecycle leaves reconstructable constitutional traces | Can the judgement be reconstructed? |
| **Explainability** | Workstreams, readiness, dimensions, status, evidence, stages, and boundaries are speakable | Can the judgement be narrated honestly? |

The constitutional certification lifecycle is:

- **coordination-centred** — it organises certification assessments; it never authors constitutional obligations;
- **assurance-consuming** — it gathers completed WS1–WS6 assurance and readiness findings; it never invents, rewrites, or re-authors them;
- **prerequisite-bound** — it validates certification prerequisites before dimension evaluation; it never skips confirmation theatre;
- **catalogue-closed** — only CXCL-01…CXCL-07 may be applied as constitutional certification lifecycle stages;
- **type-faithful** — stages apply only published CXCD-01…CXCD-05 dimensions from WS7 / MS001; they never invent dimensions;
- **evaluation-honest** — it evaluates certification status; it never executes runtime behaviour or implements Runtime A;
- **gap-revealing** — assessments name certification gaps rather than inventing certification theatre;
- **finding-producing** — assessments emit certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated dispositions;
- **publication-honest** — findings and decisions are published under honesty rules; publication never becomes law or runtime execution;
- **authority-preserving** — published corpora remain the sole source of constitutional meaning;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by lifecycle law;
- **corpus-bound** — every material lifecycle cites identifiable published specifications and completed assurance identities;
- **audit-capable** — every material lifecycle leaves reconstructable constitutional traces;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which workstreams / readiness / dimensions / status / evidence applied under which stages;
- **non-legislative** — a certification finding never becomes constitutional law or a soft amendment;
- **non-substitutive** — lifecycle machinery never becomes the constitutional standard for the next judgement;
- **non-mutating** — the lifecycle never modifies constitutional artefacts or specifications under coordination pretext;
- **non-executing** — the lifecycle never runs Runtime A, services, adapters, or product behaviour;
- **horizon-honest** — it never pretends to be Programme IX CRT / CRTL implementation certification.

The constitutional certification lifecycle is **not**:

- a second Educational Constitution, EIP, Programme VI–X, WS1–WS6, or WS7 / MS001 amendment path;
- a licence to reinterpret coach meaning, ownership maps, tip warrants, runtime contracts, conformance criteria, traceability meaning, verification meaning, compliance meaning, Programme IX certification meaning, evolution meaning, execution meaning, WS1–WS6 integrity / readiness meaning, or CXCD dimensions in lifecycle prose;
- a testing framework, CI/CD pipeline, GitHub Action, deployment gate, feature flag, or OpenAPI certification checker;
- a claim that “lifecycle complete” or “certified” guarantees learning, mastery, exam readiness, product success, perpetual conformity, Programme IX CRT success, or frozen stack authority;

- a substitute for Programme XI / WS7 / MS001 certification meaning — CXC / CXCD define certification evaluation; CXCL sequences when that evaluation occurs;
- a substitute for Programme XI / WS1–WS6 integrity / readiness / lifecycle / completion — those validate, close, and evaluate corpus structure and readiness; CXCL *consumes completed outcomes* and never re-runs them as a substitute;
- a substitute for Programme VIII runtime architecture — Programme VIII defines *how* software may execute law; CXCL coordinates *whether corpus certification assessment is sequenced*;
- a substitute for Programme IX CRT / CRTL — those certify *implementations* from *completed compliance*; CXCL coordinates *corpus certification assessment sequencing* from *completed Programme XI assurance and readiness*;
- a substitute for Programme IX conformance / verification / compliance — those evaluate *implementations* against published obligations; CXCL coordinates *corpus certification assessment sequencing*;

## 3. Lifecycle Posture (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material constitutional certification lifecycle travels through seven constitutional stages. These are **normative coordination obligations**, not an implementation architecture and not a CI job graph.

```
CXCL-01 Assessment Initiation
        │
        ▼
CXCL-02 Evidence Collection
        │
        ▼
CXCL-03 Certification Prerequisite Validation
        │
        ▼
CXCL-04 Certification Dimension Evaluation
        │
        ▼
CXCL-05 Certification Status Determination
        │
        ├── not-certified / deferred / escalated (lawful finding — corpus unchanged)
        │
        └── certified (or other lawful disposition) →
                │
                ▼
CXCL-06 Finding Construction
        │
        ▼
CXCL-07 Certification Publication
        │
        ▼
Explain workstreams / readiness / dimensions / status / evidence / boundaries
```

| Stage | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **CXCL-01 Assessment Initiation** | Open a certification assessment concern against named published corpora, certification scope, and intended CXCD set | Scope is unpublished custom, tribal knowledge, or “how main ships seals today” |
| **CXCL-02 Evidence Collection** | Gather identifiable completed WS1–WS6 assurance and readiness evidence as inputs | Evidence is invented, unfinished, re-authored, or would require rewriting assurance to “collect” it |
| **CXCL-03 Certification Prerequisite Validation** | Confirm published certification prerequisites and completeness for the concern | Validation would invent completion, skip unfinished assurance, or privilege a stack as law |
| **CXCL-04 Certification Dimension Evaluation** | Evaluate required CXCD-01…CXCD-04 dimensions under published MS001 rules | Evaluation would invent dimensions, re-author WS1–WS6 findings, or execute Runtime A |
| **CXCL-05 Certification Status Determination** | Aggregate under CXCD-05 overall certification status | Aggregation would affirm certified while required subordinate dimensions remain unresolved without honest composition notes |
| **CXCL-06 Finding Construction** | Emit certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated findings under published criteria | Findings would become amendments, educational quality grades, runtime execution warrants, Programme IX CRT seals, or artefact mutations |
| **CXCL-07 Certification Publication** | Publish findings and certification decisions under published honesty rules | Publication would invent certified status, hide gaps, rewrite boundaries, execute runtime, or freeze a stack as law |

> **The lifecycle never “best-effort invents” an eighth stage called law-making, runtime execution, artefact mutation, stack canonisation, Programme IX CRT substitution, or constitutional authority creation.**

## 6. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Certification Lifecycle Model exists so that:

1. **Law remains upstream of coordination** — stages organise assessment against published corpora and completed assurance; progress does not quietly become the specifications.
2. **Assurance gathering remains honest** — collection names completed WS1–WS6 outcomes; it does not invent unfinished assurance to force certified status.
3. **Prerequisites remain reconstructable** — prerequisite validation confirms completeness; it does not invent missing completion identities.
4. **Dimension evaluation remains faithful** — stages apply published CXCD-01…CXCD-05 only; they never invent dimensions or re-author assurance.
5. **Status determination remains honest** — aggregation under CXCD-05 never silently omits required not-certified subordinate dimensions.
6. **Gaps remain speakable** — certification gaps are named; they are never erased so a seal can glow.
7. **Findings remain sequenced** — certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated dispositions arise after lawful initiation, collection, validation, and evaluation — not as random green theatre.
8. **Authority remains honest** — no team may treat a published finding as the new constitutional source.
9. **Implementations remain replaceable** — lifecycle law does not freeze Flask, SQLAlchemy, a particular API shape, or a CI vendor as law.
10. **Runtime remains unexecuted** — certification lifecycle never runs Runtime A under assessment pretext.
11. **Horizons remain distinct** — corpus certification lifecycle never pretends to be Programme IX CRT / CRTL.
12. **Repeatability remains honest** — the same published concerns, consumed assurance, and stage sequence yield the same disposition.
13. **Audit remains possible** — every material lifecycle can be reconstructed against named workstreams, dimensions, status, stages, and findings.
14. **Explainability remains intact** — lifecycle speech describes coordination of certification assessment; it does not redefine what learning means.
15. **Students remain protected** — product theatre cannot launder unfinished constitutional guidance as “lifecycle certified.”
