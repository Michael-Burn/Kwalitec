# Educational Dimensions

**Framework ID:** EVF-003  
**Programme:** Programme V — Educational Validation Framework  
**Classification:** Layer 3 — Educational Quality Assessment  
**Status:** APPROVED — governing catalogue  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective:** July 2026  

---

## 1. Purpose

Educational Dimensions are the permanent quality axes used to assess every educational capability.

Layer 3 answers:

> Against durable educational dimensions, how strong is this capability?

Dimensions may evolve over time (additive catalogue changes). Removing or redefining a dimension requires a version bump of this document and a note in the next Version Approval Report.

---

## 2. Permanent dimension catalogue (Version 1.0)

| ID | Dimension | Definition | Typical question |
|---|---|---|---|
| ED-01 | **Educational Soundness** | Guidance respects syllabus reality, learning sequence, and honest claim strength | Would a careful tutor recognise this as educationally sensible? |
| ED-02 | **Exam Readiness** | Behaviour contributes to genuine exam preparation rather than busywork or false readiness | Does this help the student become more exam-ready within stated bounds? |
| ED-03 | **Practicality** | Fits scarce time, interrupted evenings, and real candidate constraints | Can a weeknight candidate actually execute this? |
| ED-04 | **Personalisation** | Adapts to the student’s state without inventing unsupported individuality | Is adaptation visible, bounded, and evidence-linked? |
| ED-05 | **Explainability** | Student can see what to do, why, and what happens next | Can the student inspect the educational reason? |
| ED-06 | **Motivation** | Supports sustained productive study without manipulation or empty pep | Does this help the student continue learning honestly? |
| ED-07 | **Consistency** | Same educational story across Plan, Mission, Recovery, Revision, Readiness, and explanations | Do surfaces agree on the educational truth? |
| ED-08 | **Confidence** | Produces warranted student confidence — neither overclaim nor paralysing underclaim | Is confidence calibrated to evidence? |

## 3. Applicability

Not every dimension applies equally to every capability. Reviewers mark:

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Applicable** | Must be scored |
| **Partially applicable** | Score with reduced weight note |
| **Not applicable** | Omit from that capability’s Dimension Quality contribution; state why |

### Default applicability (Version 1.0)

| Capability | ED-01 | ED-02 | ED-03 | ED-04 | ED-05 | ED-06 | ED-07 | ED-08 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| EC-01 Master Planner | A | A | A | A | A | P | A | A |
| EC-02 Daily Coach | A | A | A | A | A | A | A | A |
| EC-03 Learning Coach | A | P | A | A | A | A | A | A |
| EC-04 Recovery Coach | A | P | A | A | A | A | A | A |
| EC-05 Revision Coach | A | A | A | A | A | P | A | A |
| EC-06 Exam Coach | A | A | A | A | A | P | A | A |

A = Applicable · P = Partially applicable

## 4. Rating scale

Each applicable dimension receives one rating:

| Rating | Numeric (for Gate) | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| **Strong** | 90 | Clear educational strength with evidence |
| **Adequate** | 75 | Meets bar with minor reservations |
| **Mixed** | 55 | Material strengths and weaknesses |
| **Weak** | 30 | Does not meet educational quality bar |
| **Failing** | 0 | Harmful, dishonest, or unusable |
| **Insufficient Evidence** | 0 | Cannot rate; counts as Gate integrity issue |

Reviewers must cite evidence (transcript excerpt, capability observation, baseline screen, comparative note). Uncited Strong ratings are non-compliant.

---

## 5. Dimension Quality Index

For Gate computation:

1. Convert each Applicable / Partially applicable rating to numeric score.  
2. Partially applicable scores may be weighted ×0.5 in the capability mean (declare in report).  
3. Capability dimension mean feeds the version Dimension Quality Index (mean across required capabilities).

---

## 6. Evolution rules

Allowed without Constitution amendment:

- Add a new ED-ID with definition, applicability defaults, and scoring guidance  
- Clarify definitions without changing meaning  
- Adjust default applicability tables with rationale  

Requires Constitution / Standard review:

- Removing a dimension relied on by an APPROVED version without replacement  
- Changing numeric bands so that prior APPROVED outcomes become incomparable without migration notes
