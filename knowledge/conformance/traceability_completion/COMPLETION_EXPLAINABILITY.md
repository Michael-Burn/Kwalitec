## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which constitutional specifications* were linked — which implementation artefacts were linked, which conformance findings were linked, which provenance was preserved, and which limits remained intact — is not closing honestly; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–VIII have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, and runtime speech speakable, after MS001 has made conformance speech speakable, and after MS002 has made relationship speech speakable, Programme IX still needs one traceability-completion-speech answer:

> **“Which constitutional specifications were linked, which implementation artefacts were linked, which conformance findings were linked, which provenance was preserved, and which constitutional boundaries remained intact?”**

That answer must cover every material affirmed (or refused) completion without inventing educational success, implying conformity, implying verification, implying certification, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, freezing a stack as law, or modifying constitutional law.

## 2. What Traceability Completion Explainability Is

**Constitutional Traceability Completion Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **fulfilment (or lawful refusal of fulfilment) of required constitutional traceability relationships** — not to invent a second tutor voice from CI complete badges, and not to grade the student, certify conformity, or certify a release.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Specifications linked** | Named Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII documents (and MS001 identities where finding linkage applies) | Which constitutional specifications were linked? |
| **Artefacts linked** | Named implementation subjects related under the concern | Which implementation artefacts were linked? |
| **Findings linked** | Named MS001 assessment records related where required | Which conformance findings were linked? |
| **Provenance preserved** | Reconstructable origin from published identity → related subject surviving close | Which provenance was preserved? |
| **Boundaries intact** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden overclaims / amendments / substitutions did not occur | Which constitutional boundaries remained intact? |

Traceability Completion Explainability is:

- **fulfilment-centred** — it narrates why CTC criteria hold (or why close is refused);
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CT / CTC identities where applicable;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published specifications and named subjects;
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to amend corpora;
- **non-evaluative-substitute** — completion is never narrated as conformity, verification, or certification;
- **quality-honest** — complete is never narrated as mastery, readiness, or educational quality;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence / validation speech, MS001 conformance speech, or MS002 relationship speech;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match CTC-05 reconstruction needs;
- **authority-honest** — explanations must not paper over authority inference or stack canonisation.

Traceability Completion Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–VIII / MS001 / MS002 meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a conformance certificate or substitute for CC findings;
- a verification report or certification instrument;
- a substitute for MS002 CTEQ relationship explainability (compose with; do not replace);
- a rendering system, UI template, CI badge, dependency visualiser, or analytics dashboard;
- a claim that clear completion speech guarantees learning, a pass, conformity, verification, or certification;
- a licence to present coverage, lint scores, ticket graphs, or pipeline greens as constitutional proof or educational quality.

## 3. Mandatory Explanation Questions

Every material completion judgement (affirm or refuse close) should answer all five questions:

### CTCQ-01 — Which constitutional specifications were linked?

Name the primary published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII specifications (paths and identities) whose required linkages were confirmed — or name which required specifications remain unlinked when refusing close. When finding linkage applies, also name the MS001 assessment / corpus identities grounding those findings. When a clause or catalogue ID is material, name it (for example EIP-002 claim class; RC-02; EC-01 as *subject*). Unpublished tribal norms and “how main works” are not constitutional specifications.

### CTCQ-02 — Which implementation artefacts were linked?

Name the primary artefacts under completed (or refused) relationship (for example structural module boundaries, behavioural disposition set, evidence-consumption path, runtime contract binding map, interface composition map, API operation set, audit trail). Protocol or vendor names alone are not sufficient without constitutional mapping. If the concern’s scope includes no implementation artefacts (rare; specification-to-specification only under CT-01), say so explicitly and do not invent artefact theatre.

### CTCQ-03 — Which conformance findings were linked?

Name the primary MS001 assessment / finding identities linked under the concern (especially via CT-07) — or state that findings were out of scope for this concern, or name which required findings remain unlinked when refusing close. Never narrate a linked non-conformant / deferred / escalated finding as upgraded by completion. “Assessment happened somewhere” is not a constitutional finding identity.

### CTCQ-04 — Which provenance was preserved?

Distinguish:

- **specification provenance** — which published identity anchors the completed relationships;
- **subject provenance** — which named artefact / finding remains related and under what claimed obligation;
- **lineage walkability** — whether published specification → CT type → named subject can still be reconstructed without invented hops after close;
- **continuity honesty** — prior lawful lineage not erased for convenience (EIP-005).

Never narrate provenance as “student is ready,” “mastery confirmed,” “law updated,” “this stack is now constitutional,” “linked therefore conformant,” “verified,” or “certified.”

### CTCQ-05 — Which constitutional boundaries remained intact?

Confirm the hard stops in `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md` that were checked — especially non-implication of constitutional conformance, non-implication of verification completion, non-implication of certification, non-redefinition of constitutional specifications, non-modification of constitutional authority, implementation neutrality, and non-equation of completion with educational success or constitutional truth.

## 4. Explanation Components

| ID | Component | Obligation |
|----|-----------|------------|
| **CTCC-01** | Specification identity | Named Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII refs (+ MS001 where findings apply) linked or refused |
| **CTCC-02** | Artefact identity | Named implementation subjects linked or refused / declared out of scope |
| **CTCC-03** | Finding identity | Named conformance findings linked or refused / declared out of scope |
| **CTCC-04** | Criteria identity | Named CTC-01…CTC-05 outcomes for the judgement |
| **CTCC-05** | Provenance record | Published identity → related subject walkability after close |
| **CTCC-06** | Boundary preservation record | Confirmation that forbidden acts / overclaims did not occur |
| **CTCC-07** | Authority preservation note | Confirmation that published corpora remain the standard |
| **CTCC-08** | Neutrality note | Confirmation that no stack / vendor was privileged as law |
| **CTCC-09** | Non-conformance-implication note | Confirmation that completion was not narrated as conformity |
| **CTCC-10** | Non-verification / non-certification note | Confirmation that completion was not narrated as verified or certified |
| **CTCC-11** | Refusal / deferral honesty | When no lawful fulfilment existed, say so |
| **CTCC-12** | Continuity note | Prior lawful lineage history not erased (EIP-005) |
| **CTCC-13** | Non-claim honesty | Explicit refusal of mastery / pass / quality / meaning-rewrite / law-amendment / stack-canonisation / conformity-from-complete / verification-from-complete / certification-from-complete overclaim |
| **CTCC-14** | Audience projection | Student-plain vs developer/auditor vocabulary over one truth |

Components are **constitutional content obligations**, not database columns, API fields, CI annotations, or UI widgets. Persistence and rendering are out of scope for this milestone.

## 5. Audience Rules

| Audience | May hear | Must not hear as educational law |
|----------|----------|----------------------------------|
| **Student** | That the learning system’s behaviour remains connected to published educational rules, why a stop occurred when lineage is incomplete, and what limits apply — in plain educational language | Internal CTC IDs as theatre; CI traces; ticket graphs as “proof you are ready”; complete as mastery, as conformity, as verified, or as certified |
| **Developer / auditor** | Full CTCQ-01…05 answers, corpus paths, CT / CTC IDs, provenance walks, boundary checks, neutrality and non-implication notes | Product success metrics, pipeline greens, or coverage counts substituted for constitutional refs; completion framed as educational quality, new law, conformity, verification, or certification |

Student and developer traces must describe the **same constitutional truth**. Vocabulary may differ; facts may not.
