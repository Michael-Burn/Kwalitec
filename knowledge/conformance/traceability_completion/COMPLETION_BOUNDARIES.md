## 1. Purpose

Linkage machinery that silently turns “relationships are complete” into “implementation is conformant,” “tests verified correctness,” “release is certified,” “student succeeded,” or “the Constitution now says X” destroys student trust and educational integrity.

This document draws a bright line between **lawful constitutional traceability completion** (Programme IX / WS1 / MS003) and **conformance evaluation / verification / certification / educational judgement / constitutional authorship** owned elsewhere.

---

## 2. Boundary Principles

1. **Confirm relationships, do not legislate.** Completion affirms fulfilment of published lineage duties; it does not create constitutional law.
2. **Traceability only.** Conformance, verification, certification, and educational success are never inferred from close.
3. **Not conformance evaluation.** MS001 owns fidelity disposition.
4. **Not verification.** Testing frameworks and correctness proofs are out of this corpus’s authority.
5. **Not certification.** Completion never certifies implementations, releases, stacks, or vendors.
6. **Not constitutional amendment.** Close never patches law or modifies constitutional authority.
7. **Exactly as published.** Close never redefines constitutional specifications or meaning.
8. **Published criteria only.** Tickets, graphs, coverage, and pipeline greens are not fulfilment law.
9. **Published records only.** Audit continuity is constitutional, not free-form log theatre.
10. **Reference provenance honestly.** Provenance may be cited; it is not rewritten by citing it.
11. **Reference findings honestly.** Linked findings may be cited; linking never upgrades disposition to conformant.
12. **Authority non-transfer.** Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard after close.
13. **Meaning non-reinterpretation.** Programme VI questions and EIP claim classes survive close intact.
14. **Implementation neutrality.** No stack becomes law by proximity to a complete flag.
15. **Traced-complete ≠ quality.** Completion never grades learning, mastery, readiness, or product success.
16. **Traced-complete ≠ conformant.** Completion never replaces MS001 evaluation.
17. **Explain the stop.** Students, developers, and auditors should hear when close must be refused.
18. **No emergency exemption.** Deadlines and demos never mint completion power or amendment power.

## 3. What Traceability Completion May Do (Lawful)

| Lawful action | Constitutional meaning |
|---------------|------------------------|
| **Confirm relationships** | Affirm CTC-01…CTC-05 when published criteria hold for a lawful traceability concern |
| **Reference provenance** | Cite published specification → CT type → named subject walks preserved under MS002 — without rewriting provenance or inventing anchors |
| **Preserve audit continuity** | Leave reconstructable constitutional records of affirmed or refused close |
| **Refuse unlawful close** | Prefer honest not-yet / refuse / defer / escalate over improvisation |
| **Compose required types** | Confirm fulfilment across published CT conjunctions without collapsing failed links |
| **Remain implementation-neutral** | Honour completion law across lawful equivalent stacks |
| **Explain completion** | Answer specs / artefacts / findings / provenance / boundaries without rewriting meaning |
| **Propose corpus amendments upstream** | Recommend (not enact) amendments when law itself must change before lineage can complete |

These actions **confirm relationship fulfilment**. They do **not** publish a new Constitution, invent coach warrants, mint tips, author ownership maps, invent CT or CTC types, author runtime behaviour, evaluate fidelity in place of MS001, verify correctness, certify releases, or judge educational quality.

## 4. What Traceability Completion Must NEVER Do

| Forbidden action | Why | Lawful alternative |
|------------------|-----|--------------------|
| **Imply constitutional conformance** | MS001 owns fidelity disposition | Confirm links only; never narrate “complete therefore conformant” |
| **Imply verification completion** | Verification / testing is out of this corpus | Refuse to speak test-pass or correctness as CTC fulfilment |
| **Imply certification** | Completion is not a certificate authority | Affirm relationship fulfilment only; never certify releases or stacks |
| **Redefine constitutional specifications** | Only corpus governance may amend meaning | Leave specs unchanged; propose amendment upstream or refuse incomplete lineage |
| **Modify constitutional authority** | Published corpora remain the standard after any close | Keep artefacts / findings as related subjects, never as next-cycle law |
| **Create constitutional law** | Completion judgements are not legislation | Cite published law only; refuse unpublished anchors |
| **Replace conformance evaluation** | MS001 owns CC disposition | Supply / confirm the map assessment walks; never claim conformity from completion |
| **Create educational meaning** | Programme VI owns coach / planner questions | Preserve identity of published meaning; never invent it |
| **Author runtime behaviour** | Programme VIII + permitted writers own execution law | Relate behaviour to published law; do not mint behaviour |
| **Bypass provenance / audit requirements** | Requirements are educational care, not optional polish | Prefer understatement / refuse over invented green completes |
| **Privilege a technology as constitutional** | Implementation neutrality | Confirm obligations, not Flask / SQLAlchemy / REST / OpenAPI fashion |
| **Erase lineage to tidy close** | EIP-005 continuity | Preserve prior lawful walks; refuse destructive “cleanup” |
| **Upgrade finding disposition by linking** | CT-07 / CTC-03 preserve identity; they do not rewrite fidelity | Link findings honestly including non-conformant / deferred / escalated |
| **Present CI / tickets / coverage as fulfilment law** | Theatre is not constitution | Require CTC-01…CTC-05 content |
| **Imply educational success** | Learning outcomes are not lineage fulfilment | Keep student speech honest: related ≠ ready |
| **Emergency-amend via deadline** | Urgency never mints amendment power | Refuse incomplete close; escalate to corpus governance |

## 5. Boundary Tests (Pre-Affirmation)

Before material constitutional traceability completion is treated as affirmed, close requires affirmative answers:

| # | Test | Fail ⇒ |
|---|------|--------|
| 1 | Are all required published specifications identifiable and linked (CTC-01)? | not yet / refuse |
| 2 | Are all required named artefacts linked (CTC-02)? | not yet / refuse |
| 3 | If findings are in scope, are required findings linked without disposition rewrite (CTC-03)? | not yet / refuse |
| 4 | Does provenance survive reconstructably (CTC-04)? | refuse |
| 5 | Do audit records support CTCQ-01…CTCQ-05 (CTC-05)? | refuse |
| 6 | Would affirmation imply conformance, verification, certification, educational success, or law change? | refuse overclaim |
| 7 | Would affirmation redefine specifications or modify constitutional authority? | refuse |
| 8 | Would affirmation privilege a stack / vendor as law? | refuse |
| 9 | Would affirmation erase prior lawful lineage for convenience? | refuse |
| 10 | Can the stop or affirmation be explained honestly to student and auditor? | refuse mute theatre |

Any single fail blocks lawful affirmation.

## 7. Closing Statement

> **Traceability completion may confirm relationships, reference provenance, and preserve audit continuity.  
> It must never imply constitutional conformance, imply verification completion, imply certification, redefine constitutional specifications, or modify constitutional authority.**

This document does not replace sibling boundaries. It binds **constitutional traceability completion** so those boundaries survive linkage machinery, CI, demos, and replacements.
