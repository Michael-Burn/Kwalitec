## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “tickets linked,” “deps mapped,” or “green checkmarks shown.” The tutor optimises **honest closing of constitutional lineage work**: when the required published relationships for a concern are established and preserved, the tutor can say so — without pretending the implementation is conformant, without pretending tests passed, without pretending the student has mastered the topic, and without rewriting the syllabus because the machinery stopped cleanly.

These objectives bind every constitutional traceability completion judgement and every successor linkage machinery that claims constitutional lineage fulfilment.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### CTCO-01 — Confirm required relationships exist

**Definition.** Ensure that runtime / assessment / audit speech affirms traceability completion only when a published traceability concern under MS002 has the required CT-01…CT-07 relationships among named constitutional specifications, implementation artefacts, and/or conformance findings — or honestly refuses close when fulfilment criteria are not met.

**Includes:**

- Assessing only concerns that lawfully opened under MS002 identify → relate → preserve law and applicable CT-01…CT-07 bindings.
- Affirming completion only when `COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md` conditions (CTC-01…CTC-05) hold.
- Preferring lawful “not yet” / refuse-close over inventing a “done enough” status for product convenience.
- Distinguishing lawful stop dispositions (refuse / defer / escalate as published outcomes that exhaust remaining *lineage* duties) from abandoned relationship duties.
- Binding every material completion judgement to reconstructable constitutional references.

**Excludes:**

- Declaring complete because a ticket was closed, a dependency appeared in a graph, a coverage map looked full, or a UI spinner stopped.
- Treating Twin / Adaptive / Experience / Mission heuristics as completion warrants.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for relationship honesty.
- Collapsing “traceability complete” into “conformant,” “verified,” “certified,” “student succeeded,” or “constitution updated.”

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software celebrates unfinished lineage work — or celebrates finished linkage machinery as finished fidelity, verification, or learning. Honest confirmation is care; completion theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CTCO-02 — Confirm provenance preservation

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming traceability completion never erases, forges, truncates, or replaces published constitutional identity, subject identity, or succession history required to reconstruct where the relationship came from.

**Tutor rationale.** A close that “helpfully” drops which published rule was cited — or invents a prettier origin so the audit looks tidy — destroys educational honesty even if the completion UX looks clean.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion leaves specification provenance and subject provenance intact.
- Close never substitutes ticket IDs, SHAs, or stack names for published corpus identity.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing prior lawful provenance to simplify the next report.
- Missing provenance after an affirmed close is a defect.

---

### CTCO-03 — Confirm lineage continuity

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming completion preserves walkability of published constitutional specification → applied CT type → named implementation artefact and/or conformance finding — so the chain of relation remains reconstructable after close.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who cannot later walk from yesterday’s finding to the syllabus clause it cited cannot be trusted. Completion that breaks the walk is educational amnesia dressed as done.

**Manifestations:**

- Affirmed closes leave corpus paths, CT identities, and subject identities reconstructable together.
- Invented hops, collapsed mega-links that hide missing subjects, and erased CT identities are defects.
- Continuity forbids deleting prior lawful lineage walks to “clean up” completion.
- Incomplete walks for a required concern scope block affirmation.

---

### CTCO-04 — Preserve auditability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material completion judgement leaves a reconstructable trail: which specifications were linked, which artefacts were linked, which findings were linked, which provenance was preserved, and which boundaries remained intact.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later say *why* lineage was declared finished cannot be trusted. Silent “complete” badges destroy student and auditor trust.

**Manifestations:**

- Audit records preserve constitutional references, not only technical link graphs or CI annotations.
- Explainability obligations in `COMPLETION_EXPLAINABILITY.md` apply to all material completion judgements.
- Missing CTC identity against criteria, missing specification refs, or missing subject identity is a defect.
- Student / developer / auditor projections share one truth with different vocabulary.

---

### CTCO-05 — Preserve explainability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material completion judgement can answer the mandatory explainability questions — which specifications, which artefacts, which findings, which provenance, which boundaries — without redefining constitutional meaning or implying conformity / verification / certification / educational success.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who can only say “it’s fully documented somewhere” is performing, not teaching. Speakable completion is care.

**Manifestations:**

- CTCQ-01…CTCQ-05 answers are producible for every material affirmed or refused close.
- Explanations never soft-amend law or narrate completion as authority, conformity, verification, or certification.
- Audience projections differ in vocabulary, not in constitutional facts.
- Opaque “trust the complete badge” theatre is a defect.

---

### CTCO-06 — Preserve implementation neutrality

**Definition.** Ensure that completion judgements speak to constitutional relationship fulfilment independently of programming language, framework, datastore, CI vendor, cloud topology, or preferred Runtime A shape — so no stack becomes constitutional by proximity to a “complete” flag.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who declares only one notebook brand “fully traced” has abandoned educational law for vendor preference.

**Manifestations:**

- CTC criteria speak to constitutional identities and obligations, not “must use Flask / SQLAlchemy / OpenAPI.”
- Equivalent lawful implementations under the same published law may both reach lawful completion.
- Replacing a linked stack with another that still obeys published law does not itself create unlawful completion.
- “Our reference implementation completes this way” is never a substitute for published criteria.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CTCO-01 conflicts with inventing unpublished links so close can complete | Required-relationship honesty wins — refuse / defer / escalate |
| CTCO-02 conflicts with log volume reduction that erases corpus refs | Provenance preservation wins |
| CTCO-03 conflicts with collapsing walks into unspeakable mega-links | Lineage continuity wins |
| CTCO-04 conflicts with opaque complete badges | Auditability wins |
| CTCO-05 conflicts with unspeakable “trust us” completion | Explainability wins |
| CTCO-06 conflicts with privileging a reference stack as completion law | Implementation neutrality wins |
| Any CTCO conflicts with implying conformance / verification / certification / educational success / law change | Non-implication honesty wins — refuse the overclaim |

No supporting objective authorises violating CTCO-01.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — Constitution / EIP integrity obligations, Programme VI–VIII authority preservation, MS001 CCO-02, and MS002 CTO-01…CTO-06 for the *completion* horizon.

---

## 5. Non-Objectives

Constitutional traceability completion does **not** optimise for:

- constitutional conformance disposition, fidelity scoring, or “linked therefore conformant” theatre;
- verification success, test-pass rates, coverage theatre, or implementation-correctness judgement;
- certification of releases, stacks, vendors, or implementations;
- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- CI throughput, flaky-link suppression, or demo continuity as educational or constitutional success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when required relationships are incomplete;
- collapsing CTC criteria into one mega-flag that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics signals as substitutes for published completion criteria;
- treating documentation links as optional commentary on whatever code already shipped;
- declaring conformity, verification, certification, educational success, or constitutional amendment solely because completion was affirmed.

---

## 6. Closing Statement

> **A concern that becomes “traceability complete” by rewriting constitutional law — or by implying conformance, verification, certification, or educational success — is a failed educational system.  
> A concern that confirms only required published relationships — with provenance, lineage continuity, auditability, explainability, and neutrality intact — is doing its only completion job.**
