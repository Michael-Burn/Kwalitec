Authority order for constitutional traceability completion:

> The Constitutional Traceability Model (Programme IX / WS1 / MS002) defines how published constitutional specifications, implementation artefacts, and conformance findings remain lawfully related.  
> **This Constitutional Traceability Completion Model (Programme IX / Workstream 1 / MS003) defines when required constitutional traceability relationships have been lawfully established and preserved.**

## 2. What Constitutional Traceability Completion Is

**Constitutional Traceability Completion** is confirmation that **required constitutional traceability relationships** among published constitutional specifications, implementation artefacts, and conformance findings have been **lawfully established and preserved** — such that provenance, lineage continuity, and audit continuity hold for that published concern under MS002 — without claiming that any conformance, verification, certification, educational, or constitutional-authorship question was resolved by the close itself.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Traceability concern** | A bounded constitutional unit of lineage work opened under published CT / MS002 law (identify → relate → preserve) | What lineage work is being assessed for completion? |
| **Completion warrant** | Judgement that relationship fulfilment may be assessed | May we ask whether required relationships are complete? |
| **Completion criteria** | Constitutional conditions that must hold (`COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md`) | What must be true for fulfilment? |
| **Completion judgement** | Affirmed / not yet / refused (no lawful close) | Is constitutional traceability complete for this concern? |
| **Provenance preservation** | Reconstructable origin from published identity → related subject survives close | Did provenance remain intact? |
| **Audit continuity** | Reconstructable constitutional records of the completed relationships | Can the close be audited? |
| **Boundary preservation record** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden overclaims / amendments / substitutions did not occur | Did constitutional integrity survive close? |

Constitutional Traceability Completion is:

- **relationship-scoped** — it concerns fulfilment of required lineage duties, not fidelity, correctness, or educational outcomes;
- **catalogue-bound** — fulfilment references CT-01…CT-07 relationships actually required for the concern under MS002;
- **criteria-gated** — fulfilment requires stated constitutional conditions (`COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md` CTC-01…CTC-05);
- **provenance-preserving** — published identities remain the sole constitutional origin through close;
- **lineage-continuous** — published specification → CT type → named subject remains walkable after close;
- **audit-capable** — fulfilment cites reconstructable constitutional records, not ticket theatre or CI greens;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by completion;
- **non-certifying** — completion never certifies conformity, correctness, verification success, educational success, or law change;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which specs / artefacts / findings / provenance / boundaries applied;
- **corpus-bound** — every material completion cites identifiable published specifications and named related subjects;
- **non-substitutive** — a completed lineage map never becomes the constitutional standard or a substitute for MS001 evaluation.

Constitutional Traceability Completion is **not**:

- a determination of constitutional conformance (CC disposition remains MS001’s job);
- verification of implementation correctness, test success, or runtime behaviour truth;
- certification of implementations, releases, stacks, or vendors;
- educational success, learner mastery, progress, or exam readiness;
- a Programme VI / VII / VIII educational, orchestration, or runtime completion judgement;
- reinterpretation or modification of constitutional specifications under a “lineage done” label;
- transfer of authority, minting of tips, invention of EST/CST postures, or amendment of constitutional specifications;
- a claim that ticket links, dependency graphs, coverage maps, pipeline greens, or elapsed time equal fulfilment;
- a UI celebration, analytics milestone, or “documented” flag as educational or constitutional law;
- a licence to skip outstanding published lineage duties because a product surface looks finished;
- a substitute for MS002 relationship law — completion *judges fulfilment of* MS002 relationships; it does not redefine CT types.

## 4. Completion Lifecycle (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material traceability concern that claims lawful completion travels through constitutional assessment phases. These are **normative judgement obligations**, not an implementation architecture.

```
Identify published constitutional specifications, artefacts, and findings in scope
        │
        ▼
Confirm required CT-01…CT-07 relationships under MS002 for the concern
        │
        ├── refuse / defer / escalate (lawful stop — law unchanged; no invented “complete”)
        │
        └── assess → Apply CTC-01…CTC-05
                │
                ├── not yet / refuse close (outstanding duties or boundary breach)
                │
                └── affirm → Emit completion judgement; preserve provenance and audit continuity
                        │
                        ▼
                Explain specs / artefacts / findings / provenance / boundaries
```

| Phase | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **Identify** | Name only published constitutional specifications and named subjects in scope | Scope is unpublished custom, tribal knowledge, or “how main works today” |
| **Confirm relationships** | Verify required CT-01…CT-07 links under MS002 for the concern | Relationships would invent law, rewrite specs, reinterpret meaning, infer authority, or privilege a stack as law |
| **Assess criteria** | Apply CTC-01…CTC-05 without softening into product theatre | Any required criterion fails; or close would claim conformity / verification / certification / educational success / law change |
| **Preserve & explain** | Record provenance and audit continuity; answer CTCQ-01…CTCQ-05 | Path would erase lineage, treat completion as constitutional amendment, or narrate “linked therefore conformant” |

> **Traceability completion never “best-effort invents” a fifth phase called conformance, verification, certification, educational success, or law-making.**

## 7. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Traceability Completion Model exists so that:

1. **Relationship honesty remains intact** — “complete” means required published links exist, not that tickets were closed.
2. **Provenance remains honest** — close never erases or forges published constitutional origin.
3. **Lineage remains reconstructable** — auditors can walk from finding → artefact → specification after close without invented hops.
4. **Audit continuity survives close** — completion leaves reconstructable constitutional records.
5. **Authority remains upstream** — completed lineage never becomes the constitutional source for the next judgement.
6. **Implementations remain replaceable** — completion does not freeze Flask, SQLAlchemy, a particular API shape, or a CI vendor as law.
7. **Explainability remains intact** — completion speech describes relationship fulfilment; it does not redefine what learning means or what conformity is.
8. **Students remain protected** — product theatre cannot launder non-conformant or unverified behaviour as “fully traced therefore lawful / ready / certified.”
