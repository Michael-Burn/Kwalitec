## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor needs bright conditions for saying “this constitutional lineage work is finished” — and equally bright refusals when it is not. After MS001 has named when conformity may be claimed and MS002 has named how specs, artefacts, and findings remain lawfully related, Programme IX Workstream 1 still needs one fulfilment checklist:

> **What must be true before constitutional speech may affirm that required traceability relationships among published specifications, implementation artefacts, and conformance findings have been lawfully established and preserved?**

These criteria answer that question. They are **normative constitutional conditions**, not database predicates, CI job statuses, ticket states, dependency-graph completeness scores, or API status codes.

---

## 2. Criterion Catalogue

All material affirmed completions require **CTC-01 through CTC-05** to hold. Failure of any criterion ⇒ **not yet** or **refuse close**. Do not invent a partial “mostly complete” conformance, verification, certification, educational, or constitutional status.

| ID | Criterion | Primary question |
|----|-----------|------------------|
| **CTC-01** | Required constitutional specifications linked | Were required published specifications linked under MS002? |
| **CTC-02** | Required implementation artefacts linked | Were required named implementation artefacts linked under MS002? |
| **CTC-03** | Required conformance findings linked | Were required conformance findings linked where the concern demands them? |
| **CTC-04** | Provenance preserved | Did published origin → related subject survive intact? |
| **CTC-05** | Audit records complete | Do reconstructable constitutional audit records exist for the completed relationships? |

Optional supporting honesty checks (do not replace CTC-01…CTC-05):

| ID | Check | Primary question |
|----|-------|------------------|
| **CTC-06** | Catalogue honesty | Were required relationships typed under published CT-01…CT-07 only? |
| **CTC-07** | Continuity honesty | Was prior lawful lineage history preserved (EIP-005)? |
| **CTC-08** | Non-claim honesty | Does the judgement refuse conformance / verification / certification / educational / amendment overclaim? |
| **CTC-09** | Neutrality honesty | Does the judgement refuse stack / vendor canonisation as completion law? |

## 3. Criterion Definitions

### CTC-01 — Required constitutional specifications linked

**Definition.** Every published constitutional specification required by the concern’s MS002 scope has been lawfully linked under applicable CT-01…CT-07 relationships — citing identifiable Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII identities (and MS001 identities where CT-07 / finding linkage applies) — or the concern lawfully stopped under published refusal rules when required specification linkage was impossible.

**Requires:**

- Identifiable published corpus paths / specification identities in scope for the concern.
- Evidence that required specification linkages were established under MS002, or that a published refuse / defer / escalate disposition exhausted remaining *lineage* duties for that specification set.
- Refusal of “ticket mentioned a doc” or “we know which file” without published identity as completion.

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- That the linked specifications were amended, reinterpreted, or newly authored.
- That the implementation conforms to those specifications (MS001 / CC remains distinct).
- That verification, tests, or certification succeeded.
- That the learner understood, progressed, or mastered anything.
- That constitutional law was updated.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who declares notes “fully related to the syllabus” without naming which syllabus clauses were cited has not finished lawful lineage care — even if the desk looks tidy.

---

### CTC-02 — Required implementation artefacts linked

**Definition.** Every named implementation artefact required by the concern’s MS002 scope has been lawfully linked under applicable CT-02…CT-06 (and composed types as published) — or the concern lawfully stopped under published refusal rules when required artefact linkage was impossible.

**Requires:**

- Named implementation subjects in scope (for example structural modules, behavioural disposition sets, evidence-handling paths, runtime bindings, interface composition maps, API surfaces, audit artefacts).
- Evidence that required artefact linkages were established under MS002, or that a published stop exhausted remaining *lineage* duties for that artefact set.
- Refusal of unnamed “the backend,” “the service,” or vendor labels alone as completed subjects.

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- That the artefacts are correct, tested, verified, or conformant.
- That a particular stack, language, or framework is privileged as law.
- That product quality or educational quality was judged.
- That constitutional specifications were modified to match the artefacts.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who claims “the plan is documented against the code” without naming which artefacts were related has performed documentation theatre, not lineage fulfilment.

---

### CTC-03 — Required conformance findings linked

**Definition.** Where the concern’s published MS002 scope requires assessment lineage (especially CT-07), every required conformance finding / assessment record has been lawfully linked to its grounding published specifications and named subjects — or the concern lawfully stopped under published refusal rules when required finding linkage was impossible.

**Requires:**

- Identifiable MS001 assessment / finding identities where the concern demands them.
- Evidence that required finding linkages were established under MS002, or that a published stop exhausted remaining *lineage* duties for that finding set.
- Refusal of “assessment happened somewhere” without named finding identity as completion.

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- That the finding’s disposition was “conformant.”
- That linking a non-conformant, deferred, or escalated finding somehow upgrades fidelity.
- That MS001 evaluation was replaced, skipped, or certified by this close.
- That verification frameworks or Educational Validation Framework quality reviews are complete.

**Tutor rationale.** Linking a finding is not endorsing its educational or fidelity meaning — but omitting a required finding while claiming lineage complete is dishonest. Completeness of *linkage* is not completeness of *conformity*.

---

### CTC-04 — Provenance preserved

**Definition.** Affirming completion leaves reconstructable constitutional provenance intact: published specification identity → applied CT type(s) → named related subject(s), without erasure, forgery, truncation, or substitution of unpublished customs for published origin.

**Requires:**

- Specification provenance and subject provenance remain reconstructable after close.
- Lineage walkability (CTO-02 / CTCO-03) survives the completion judgement.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing prior lawful provenance to make close look tidy.
- Refusal of ticket IDs, SHAs, coverage maps, or stack names as substitutes for published provenance.

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- That provenance proves conformity, correctness, certification, or educational success.
- That Twin / Adaptive / analytics origin stories may replace published constitutional identity.
- That law was amended so provenance could look cleaner.

**Tutor rationale.** A finished folder that no longer shows *which rule* was cited is not finished care; it is amnesia with a green badge.

---

### CTC-05 — Audit records complete

**Definition.** Reconstructable constitutional audit records exist for the completed relationships: which specifications were linked, which artefacts were linked, which findings were linked (where required), which provenance was preserved, and which boundaries remained intact — sufficient for student / developer / auditor reconstruction under `COMPLETION_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.

**Requires:**

- Material completion judgements leave audit-speakable records aligned with CTCQ-01…CTCQ-05.
- Records cite published constitutional references, not only technical link graphs or CI annotations.
- Missing CT identity, missing specification refs, missing subject identity, or missing boundary honesty for a material affirmed close is a defect.
- Lawful refuse / defer / escalate closes also leave honest records of *why* fulfilment was not affirmed.

**Does not require / does not mean:**

- A particular database schema, log shipper, CI annotation format, or UI widget.
- That audit completeness equals conformance, verification, certification, or educational success.
- That volume of logs substitutes for constitutional content.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later reconstruct why lineage was declared finished cannot be trusted — and neither can a platform that celebrates mute “complete” flags.

## 4. Criterion Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CTC-01 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CTC-02 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CTC-03 fails where findings are in scope | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CTC-03 is out of scope (concern requires no finding linkage) | CTC-03 does not block; do not invent finding theatre to force a green criterion |
| CTC-04 fails | Affirmation is unlawful even if links appear present |
| CTC-05 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — mute complete badges are defects |
| Optional CTC-06…CTC-09 fail | Treat as honesty defects; do not soft-pass CTC-01…CTC-05 |
| Any criterion “passes” only by implying conformance / verification / certification / educational success / law change | The implied overclaim voids lawful affirmation |

All of CTC-01…CTC-05 are **conjunctive** for material affirmed completion when each is in scope. There is no lawful “four of five” educational, fidelity, or constitutional status.

---

## 5. Lawful Stop Dispositions

Not every incomplete concern is a defect. Published MS002 refuse / defer / escalate outcomes may **exhaust remaining lineage duties** for a concern without affirming relationship fulfilment as “complete” in the celebratory sense.

| Disposition | Constitutional meaning | May affirm CTC completion? |
|-------------|------------------------|----------------------------|
| **Affirm complete** | CTC-01…CTC-05 hold for the concern | Yes |
| **Not yet** | Required relationships or records remain outstanding | No |
| **Refuse close** | Affirmation would invent links, erase provenance, or overclaim | No |
| **Defer** | Published wait for upstream corpus / assessment / artefact identity | No (unless published law says remaining lineage duties are exhausted) |
| **Escalate** | Published stop requiring human / corpus governance attention | No (unless published law says remaining lineage duties are exhausted) |

A lawful stop that exhausts lineage duties is **honest unfinished fulfilment or honest refusal** — never a stealth conformity certificate, verification pass, product certification, educational success, or constitutional amendment.

---

## 6. Non-Criteria

The following are **not** constitutional completion criteria:

- CI pipeline greens, flaky-link suppression, or job exit codes;
- ticket closure, PR merge, or documentation checklist ticks alone;
- dependency-graph density, coverage percentages, or lint scores;
- Twin / Adaptive / Experience / Mission confidence signals;
- elapsed time, ack counts, or UI celebration states;
- “looks documented enough for the demo”;
- MS001 conformant disposition (that is fidelity law, not CTC);
- test-suite success or verification framework pass (out of scope for this corpus);
- Educational Validation Framework quality release (orthogonal);
- any implication that constitutional specifications changed because lineage closed.

---

## 7. Closing Statement

> **Constitutional traceability is complete only when required published specifications, implementation artefacts, and (where required) conformance findings are linked — with provenance preserved and audit records complete.  
> Satisfying CTC-01…CTC-05 never means the implementation is conformant, verified, certified, educationally successful, or that constitutional law has changed.**
