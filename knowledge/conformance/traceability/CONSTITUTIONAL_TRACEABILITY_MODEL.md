## Authority

This document is the constitutional overview of the **Constitutional Traceability Model** for Kwalitec.

It is subordinate to the Educational Constitution, Educational Interpretation Principles (EIP), Programme VI educational meaning corpora, Programme VII orchestration corpora, Programme VIII runtime constitutional corpora, and the Constitutional Conformance Model (Programme IX / WS1 / MS001). It governs **how constitutional specifications and implementation artefacts are lawfully traced to one another** — its objectives, recognised traceability types, hard boundaries, and explainability. It does not authorise implementation shortcuts that contradict the Constitution, and it does not create constitutional law, modify constitutional specifications, reinterpret constitutional meaning, establish constitutional authority, replace conformance evaluation, mint recommendations, invent educational state, or substitute any implementation for constitutional authority.

Authority order for constitutional traceability:

> Constitution defines educational truth and curriculum primacy.  
> EIP defines evidence classification, continuity, explainability, claim honesty, and mutation rights.  
> Programme VI defines educational meaning and may publish learning / assessment warrants.  
> Programme VII defines orchestration flow, ownership, recommendations, context, and orchestration evidence artefacts.  
> Programme VIII defines how runtime implementations may lawfully execute published law (contracts, events, evidence, services, interfaces).  
> The Constitutional Conformance Model (Programme IX / WS1 / MS001) defines when an implementation may be considered constitutionally conformant to that published law.  
> **This Constitutional Traceability Model (Programme IX / Workstream 1 / MS002) defines how published constitutional specifications, implementation artefacts, and conformance findings remain lawfully related.**  
> Downstream linkage machinery, CI theatre, product surfaces, Twin, Adaptive, and narration must preserve relationships under these rules — never become constitutional producers by proximity to lineage records.

## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not declare yesterday’s plan “lawful” because a ticket mentioned a doc, silently rewrite the syllabus so today’s code looks linked, or treat a dependency graph as educational truth. After the Constitution and EIP have defined *educational law*, after Programmes VI–VIII have published *meaning, orchestration, and runtime law*, and after MS001 has defined *when conformity may be claimed*, the platform still needs one traceability answer:

> **“How are constitutional specifications and implementation artefacts lawfully traced to one another?”**

That answer must preserve constitutional provenance, preserve implementation neutrality, support repeatable conformance, and remain fully auditable and explainable — without creating constitutional law, reinterpreting constitutional meaning, modifying constitutional specifications, or establishing constitutional authority.

This document records that posture so every future lineage record (and successor) has a single constitutional reference for *how software, specs, and assessments may remain related without becoming law*.

> **The Constitutional Traceability Model describes the constitutional relationship linking published specifications to implementation artefacts and conformance findings.  
> It does not create educational meaning, invent ownership, mint tips, invent runtime behaviour, or become constitutional law.**

## 2. What Constitutional Traceability Is

**Constitutional traceability** is the binding relationship by which **published constitutional specifications**, **implementation artefacts**, and **conformance findings** remain **lawfully related**, preserving constitutional provenance, implementation neutrality, repeatable conformance support, and auditability — without becoming a source of constitutional law, educational truth, or a substitute for conformance evaluation.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Published constitutional corpus** | Constitution, EIP, Programme VI / VII / VIII specifications that publish educational and execution law | Which law is referenced? |
| **Implementation artefact** | Code, behaviour, evidence handling, runtime posture, interfaces, APIs, and audit artefacts claimed to relate to that law | Which subject is related? |
| **Conformance finding** | MS001 disposition (conformant / non-conformant / deferred / escalated) and assessment record | Which assessment outcome is linked? |
| **Traceability relationship** | A recognised CT-01…CT-07 link among specs, artefacts, and/or findings | Which lineage category applies? |
| **Constitutional provenance** | Reconstructable origin of the link from published identity → related subject | Can origin be reconstructed? |
| **Implementation neutrality** | Lineage speaks to constitutional identities, not to a preferred stack as law | Remains the stack replaceable? |
| **Repeatable conformance support** | Stable relationships that MS001 assessments can rely on without rewriting law | Can fidelity be re-checked? |
| **Auditability** | Every material relationship leaves reconstructable constitutional traces | Can the link be audited? |
| **Explainability** | Specs, artefacts, category, provenance, and boundaries are speakable | Can the link be narrated honestly? |

Constitutional traceability is:

- **relationship-preserving** — it records lawful links; it never authors the linked specifications;
- **provenance-preserving** — published identities remain the sole constitutional origin of meaning;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by lineage;
- **catalogue-closed** — only CT-01…CT-07 may be applied as constitutional traceability types;
- **corpus-bound** — every material relationship cites identifiable published specifications;
- **conformance-supporting** — relationships enable repeatable MS001 evaluation; they do not replace it;
- **audit-capable** — every material relationship leaves reconstructable constitutional traces;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which law was linked to which artefacts under which category;
- **non-substitutive** — a linked implementation never becomes the constitutional standard for the next judgement.

Constitutional traceability is **not**:

- a second Educational Constitution, EIP, Programme VI / VII / VIII, or MS001 amendment path;
- a licence to reinterpret coach meaning, ownership maps, tip warrants, runtime contracts, or conformance criteria in lineage prose;
- a testing framework, CI/CD pipeline, GitHub Action, dependency scanner, or OpenAPI linker;
- a claim that “traced” guarantees learning, mastery, exam readiness, product success, or conformity;
- a substitute for MS001 conformance evaluation — CT preserves relationships; CC evaluates fidelity;
- a substitute for Programme VIII evidence validation (EV-xx) — EV validates evidence eligibility; CT preserves lineage;
- a substitute for Educational Validation Framework quality review — quality release remains a distinct horizon.

## 3. Traceability Posture (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material constitutional traceability relationship travels through three constitutional phases. These are **normative lineage obligations**, not an implementation architecture.

```
Identify published constitutional specifications to be related
        │
        ▼
Relate named implementation artefacts and/or conformance findings under CT-01…CT-07
        │
        ├── refuse / defer / escalate (lawful stop — law unchanged; no invented link)
        │
        └── preserve → Emit relationship and preserve provenance records
                │
                ▼
        Explain specs / artefacts / category / provenance / boundaries
```

| Phase | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **Identify** | Name only published constitutional specifications as relationship anchors | Anchor is unpublished custom, tribal knowledge, or “how main works today” |
| **Relate** | Apply CT-01…CT-07 among named artefacts and/or findings | Relationship would invent law, rewrite specs, reinterpret meaning, infer authority, or privilege a stack as law |
| **Preserve** | Record provenance without amending corpora or replacing conformance | Path would treat links as constitutional amendments, freeze an implementation as authority, or claim conformity solely from linkage |

> **Traceability never “best-effort invents” a fourth phase called law-making, reinterpretation, authority inference, or stack canonisation.**

The Constitutional Traceability Model is constitutionally responsible for:

| **Require explainability** | Make specs, artefacts, category, provenance, and boundaries speakable (`TRACEABILITY_EXPLAINABILITY.md`) |

### 5.1 Binding non-responsibility

The Constitutional Traceability Model must **not**:

- invent new constitutional traceability types outside CT-01…CT-07 without a Programme IX amendment;
- create, modify, or deprecate constitutional specifications under Programmes VI–VIII (or Constitution / EIP / MS001) via lineage records;
- reinterpret Programme VI educational meaning or EIP claim classes during linkage;
- establish constitutional authority by declaring a linked artefact “the new standard”;
- replace MS001 conformance evaluation with “linked therefore conformant”;
- implement Runtime A, testing frameworks, CI/CD, GitHub Actions, Python, Flask, SQLAlchemy, REST, OpenAPI, or infrastructure;
- present dependency graphs, ticket links, coverage maps, or pipeline greens as constitutional law or educational truth.

## 6. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Traceability Model exists so that:

1. **Provenance remains honest** — every material link cites published constitutional identity, not tribal memory.
2. **Lineage remains reconstructable** — auditors can walk from finding → artefact → specification without inventing steps.
3. **Conformance remains repeatable** — stable relationships support MS001 re-evaluation without rewriting law to match code.
4. **Authority remains upstream** — linked implementations never become the constitutional source for the next judgement.
5. **Implementations remain replaceable** — lineage does not freeze Flask, SQLAlchemy, a particular API shape, or a CI vendor as law.
6. **Explainability remains intact** — traceability speech describes relationships; it does not redefine what learning means.
7. **Students remain protected** — product theatre cannot launder non-conformant behaviour as “documented therefore lawful.”
