## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which obligation* was judged — which findings were consumed, which specifications were referenced, what determination resulted, and which limits were preserved — is not coaching; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–VIII have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, and runtime speech speakable, after WS1 has made conformance and lineage speech speakable, and after WS2 has made verification speech speakable, Programme IX still needs one compliance-speech answer:

> **“Which constitutional obligations were evaluated, which verification findings were consumed, which constitutional specifications were referenced, which compliance determination was reached, and which constitutional boundaries were preserved?”**

That answer must cover every material compliance determination without inventing educational meaning, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, inventing or rewriting findings, inventing lineage, freezing a stack as law, inventing unpublished CCM types, issuing certificates, replacing MS001 / WS2 evaluation, or presenting satisfaction as educational quality.

## 2. What Compliance Explainability Is

**Constitutional Compliance Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **determination of whether named published constitutional obligations have been satisfied under published CCM types, consuming identifiable verification findings, referencing published specifications, and using established traceability** — not to invent a second tutor voice from seal machinery, not to grade the student, and not to certify an implementation.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Obligations evaluated** | Named published constitutional duties under Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII (and related WS1 / WS2 assessment law as applicable) | Which constitutional obligations were evaluated? |
| **Verification findings consumed** | Named CV findings (and related lawful lifecycle / completion artefacts) used as inputs | Which verification findings were consumed? |
| **Specifications referenced** | Named Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII documents (and clauses as applicable) | Which constitutional specifications were referenced? |
| **Determination reached** | satisfied / not-satisfied / deferred / escalated (with composition notes; never certificates) | Which compliance determination was reached? |
| **Boundaries preserved** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden legislation / redefinition / substitution / certification did not occur | Which constitutional boundaries were preserved? |

Compliance Explainability is:

- **obligation-centred** — it narrates obligations → findings → specifications → determination under law;
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CCM identities;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published specifications and obligations;
- **finding-honest** — every material justification cites identifiable verification findings consumed (or names the hard stop when required findings are missing);
- **lineage-honest** — every material justification cites established CT relationships consumed (or names the hard stop when none exist);
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to amend corpora;
- **non-certifying** — satisfaction is never narrated as a certificate;
- **quality-honest** — satisfaction is never narrated as mastery, readiness, or educational quality;
- **neighbour-honest** — satisfaction is never narrated as permanent conformity, permanent verification, or a rewrite of CC / CV meaning;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence / validation speech, MS001 conformance speech, MS002 lineage speech, or WS2 verification speech;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match CCMO-04 reconstruction needs;
- **consistency-honest** — explanations must not paper over race-dependent or contradictory dispositions.

Compliance Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–VIII / MS001 / MS002 / WS2 meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a conformity certificate, verification certificate, or substitute for CC / CV findings;
- a substitute for CT lineage speech;
- a substitute for Programme VIII evidence-validation or contract explainability corpora;
- a rendering system, UI template, CI badge, seal registry, log shipper, or analytics dashboard;
- a claim that clear compliance speech guarantees learning, a pass, permanent conformity, permanent verification, or a certificate;
- a licence to present coverage, lint scores, pipeline greens, or seals as constitutional proof or educational quality.

## 3. Mandatory Explanation Questions

Every material constitutional compliance determination should answer all five questions:

### CCMEQ-01 — Which constitutional obligations were evaluated?

Name the primary published constitutional obligations (corpus paths, catalogue IDs, and clauses as applicable). Unpublished tribal norms and “how main works” are not constitutional obligations.

### CCMEQ-02 — Which verification findings were consumed?

Name the primary identifiable verification findings (CV type, finding identity, disposition) consumed as inputs. If required findings are missing, say so and record the hard stop — do not invent or rewrite findings in explanation prose. “Pipeline was green” is not a verification finding.

### CCMEQ-03 — Which constitutional specifications were referenced?

Name the primary published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII specifications (paths and identities) that publish the obligations under determination. When a clause or catalogue ID is material, name it (for example EIP-002 claim class; RC-02; EC-01 as *subject*). Unpublished tribal norms and “how main works” are not constitutional specifications.

### CCMEQ-04 — Which compliance determination was reached?

Distinguish:

- **satisfied** — required obligations passed under published law for the named scope given consumed findings; satisfaction affirmed without certificate;
- **not-satisfied** — required obligations failed; satisfaction must not be claimed for the contemplated scope;
- **deferred** — published law requires waiting (for example incomplete finding identity or incomplete established lineage still expected under continuity);
- **escalated** — published law requires human / corpus / authority escalation paths.

Never narrate determinations as “student is ready,” “mastery confirmed,” “law updated,” “this stack is now constitutional,” “certified,” “permanently verified,” or “permanently conformant.”

### CCMEQ-05 — Which constitutional boundaries were preserved?

Confirm the hard stops in `COMPLIANCE_BOUNDARIES.md` that were checked — especially non-certification of implementations, non-modification of specifications, non-replacement of constitutional authority, non-redefinition of educational / constitutional meaning, implementation neutrality, and non-equation of satisfaction with educational quality, permanent conformity, or permanent verification.

|----|-----------|------------|

| **CCMEC-02** | Finding identity | Named verification findings consumed (or explicit hard-stop for missing findings) |
| **CCMEC-03** | Specification identity | Named Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII refs |
| **CCMEC-04** | Traceability identity | Named established CT relationships consumed (or explicit hard-stop for missing lineage) |
| **CCMEC-05** | Compliance type identity | Named CCM-01…CCM-07 binding(s) |
| **CCMEC-06** | Criteria identity | Published permitted / prohibited determination criteria applied |
| **CCMEC-07** | Determination disposition | satisfied / not-satisfied / deferred / escalated with composition |
| **CCMEC-08** | Boundary preservation record | Confirmation that forbidden acts did not occur |
| **CCMEC-09** | Authority preservation note | Confirmation that published corpora remain the standard |
| **CCMEC-10** | Non-certification note | Confirmation that no implementation / stack / vendor / release certificate was issued |
| **CCMEC-11** | Neutrality note | Confirmation that no stack / vendor was privileged as law |
| **CCMEC-12** | Consistency note | Disposition reproducibility / non-contradiction from published inputs |
| **CCMEC-13** | Neighbour-support note | Confirmation that CCM consumes CC / CT / CV without rewriting their meaning |
| **CCMEC-14** | Refusal / deferral honesty | When no lawful satisfaction existed, say so |
| **CCMEC-15** | Continuity note | Prior lawful compliance history not erased (EIP-005) |
| **CCMEC-16** | Non-claim honesty | Explicit refusal of mastery / pass / quality / meaning-redefinition / law-amendment / stack-canonisation / certification / permanent-conformity / permanent-verification overclaim |
| **CCMEC-17** | Audience projection | Student-plain vs developer/auditor vocabulary over one truth |

Components are **constitutional content obligations**, not database columns, API fields, CI annotations, seal fields, or UI widgets. Persistence and rendering are out of scope for this milestone.

## 5. Audience Rules

| Audience | May hear | Must not hear as educational law |
|----------|----------|----------------------------------|
| **Student** | Whether the learning system’s obligations under published educational rules have been satisfied, why a stop occurred, and what limits apply — in plain educational language | Internal CCM IDs as theatre; CI traces; seals as “proof you are ready”; satisfaction as mastery or a certificate |
| **Developer / auditor** | Full CCMEQ-01…05 answers, corpus paths, CCM IDs, finding identities consumed, CT identities consumed, determination composition, boundary checks, neutrality / consistency / non-certification notes | Product success metrics, pipeline greens, coverage counts, or seals substituted for constitutional refs; satisfaction framed as educational quality, new law, or a certificate |

Student and developer traces must describe the **same constitutional truth**. Vocabulary may differ; facts may not.
