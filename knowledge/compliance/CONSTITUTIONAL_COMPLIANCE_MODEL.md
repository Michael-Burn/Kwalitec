## Authority

This document is the constitutional overview of the **Constitutional Compliance Model** for Kwalitec.

It is subordinate to the Educational Constitution, Educational Interpretation Principles (EIP), Programme VI educational meaning corpora, Programme VII orchestration corpora, Programme VIII runtime constitutional corpora, the Constitutional Conformance Model (Programme IX / WS1 / MS001), the Constitutional Traceability Model (Programme IX / WS1 / MS002), and the Constitutional Verification Model (Programme IX / WS2 / MS001 and successor lifecycle / completion corpora). It governs **when an implementation may be considered constitutionally compliant** — its objectives, recognised compliance types, hard boundaries, and explainability. It does not authorise implementation shortcuts that contradict the Constitution, and it does not create constitutional law, modify constitutional specifications, redefine constitutional meaning, replace constitutional authority, certify implementations, mint recommendations, invent educational state, re-author verification findings, or substitute any compliance machinery for constitutional authority.

Authority order for constitutional compliance:

> Constitution defines educational truth and curriculum primacy.  
> EIP defines evidence classification, continuity, explainability, claim honesty, and mutation rights.  
> Programme VI defines educational meaning and may publish learning / assessment warrants.  
> Programme VII defines orchestration flow, ownership, recommendations, context, and orchestration evidence artefacts.  
> Programme VIII defines how runtime implementations may lawfully execute published law (contracts, events, evidence, services, interfaces).  
> The Constitutional Conformance Model (Programme IX / WS1 / MS001) defines when an implementation may be considered constitutionally conformant to that published law.  
> The Constitutional Traceability Model (Programme IX / WS1 / MS002) defines how published constitutional specifications, implementation artefacts, and conformance findings remain lawfully related.  
> The Constitutional Verification Model (Programme IX / WS2 / MS001) defines when and how an implementation may be constitutionally verified against published specifications using established traceability.  
> **This Constitutional Compliance Model (Programme IX / Workstream 3 / MS001) defines when an implementation may be considered constitutionally compliant — whether published constitutional obligations have been satisfied using verification findings, published specifications, and established traceability.**  
> Downstream compliance machinery, CI theatre, product surfaces, Twin, Adaptive, and narration must determine under these rules — never become constitutional producers by proximity to compliance determinations.

## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not declare a study system “compliant” because a badge glowed green, quietly rewrite yesterday’s syllabus obligation so today’s report looks finished, or treat a seal as educational truth. After the Constitution and EIP have defined *educational law*, after Programmes VI–VIII have published *meaning, orchestration, and runtime law*, after WS1 has defined *when conformity may be claimed* and *how lineage is preserved*, and after WS2 has produced *verification findings*, the platform still needs one compliance answer:

> **“When may an implementation be considered constitutionally compliant?”**

That answer must ensure determinations consume **verification findings**, **published constitutional specifications**, and **established traceability relationships**; determine **constitutional obligation status**; preserve consistency, auditability, explainability, and implementation neutrality — without creating constitutional law, modifying constitutional specifications, redefining constitutional meaning, replacing constitutional authority, or certifying implementations.

This document records that posture so every future compliance determination (and successor) has a single constitutional reference for *how obligation satisfaction may be judged without becoming law or a certificate*.

> **The Constitutional Compliance Model describes the constitutional determination of whether published constitutional obligations have been satisfied using constitutional verification findings.  
> It does not create educational meaning, invent ownership, mint tips, invent runtime behaviour, certify implementations, or become constitutional law.**

## 2. What Constitutional Compliance Is

**Constitutional compliance** is the binding determination by which **published constitutional obligations** may be judged **satisfied or not satisfied** using **constitutional verification findings**, **published constitutional specifications**, and **established traceability relationships** (with identifiable conformance relationships as inputs where applicable) — yielding **compliance determinations** that preserve constitutional authority, implementation neutrality, consistency, auditability, and explainability — without becoming a source of constitutional law, educational truth, a substitute for conformance or verification meaning, or a certification of any implementation.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Published constitutional obligation** | A duty published by Constitution, EIP, or Programme VI / VII / VIII (and related WS1 / WS2 law that binds assessment) that an implementation must satisfy | Which obligation is under determination? |
| **Published constitutional corpus** | Constitution, EIP, Programme VI / VII / VIII specifications that publish educational and execution law | Which specifications were referenced? |
| **Verification finding** | Lawful CV disposition (satisfied / not-satisfied / deferred / escalated) consumed as input — never rewritten by compliance | Which verification findings were consumed? |
| **Traceability relationship** | Established CT-01…CT-07 link among specs, artefacts, and/or findings | Which lineage was consumed? |
| **Conformance relationship / artefact** | MS001 relationship or assessment record consumed as input — never rewritten by compliance | Which conformance relationship was consumed? |
| **Compliance determination** | A recognised CCM-01…CCM-07 judgement of obligation status: satisfied / not-satisfied / deferred / escalated — never “new law” and never a certificate | Have the obligations been satisfied? |
| **Constitutional authority** | The exclusive right of published corpora to define educational and execution meaning | Who owns the standard? |
| **Implementation neutrality** | Compliance is judged against law and findings, not against a preferred stack, vendor, or topology | Remains the stack replaceable? |
| **Consistency** | Same published obligations, same consumed findings / relationships / specifications → same determination | Can the judgement be reproduced without contradiction? |
| **Auditability** | Every material compliance determination leaves reconstructable constitutional traces | Can the judgement be reconstructed? |
| **Explainability** | Obligations, findings, specs, determinations, and boundaries are speakable | Can the judgement be narrated honestly? |

Constitutional compliance is:

- **obligation-determining** — it judges whether published obligations have been satisfied; it never authors those obligations;
- **finding-consuming** — it uses verification findings as inputs; it never invents or rewrites findings to force satisfaction;
- **specification-consuming** — it references published constitutional specifications; it never amends them;
- **traceability-consuming** — it uses established CT relationships; it never invents lineage to force a pass;
- **conformance-honest** — it may consume CC relationships / artefacts; it never replaces conformance meaning or certifies conformity;
- **verification-honest** — it may consume CV findings; it never becomes a second verification engine or a certificate mill;
- **authority-preserving** — published corpora remain the sole source of constitutional meaning;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by compliance;
- **catalogue-closed** — only CCM-01…CCM-07 may be applied as constitutional compliance types;
- **corpus-bound** — every material determination cites identifiable published specifications and obligations;
- **audit-capable** — every material determination leaves reconstructable constitutional traces;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which obligations were evaluated from which findings under which specifications;
- **non-certifying** — a satisfied determination never becomes a certificate that freezes an implementation as lawfully permanent;
- **non-substitutive** — compliance machinery never becomes the constitutional standard for the next judgement.

Constitutional compliance is **not**:

- a second Educational Constitution, EIP, Programme VI / VII / VIII, MS001, MS002, or WS2 amendment path;
- a licence to reinterpret coach meaning, ownership maps, tip warrants, runtime contracts, conformance criteria, traceability meaning, or verification meaning in compliance prose;
- a testing framework, CI/CD pipeline, GitHub Action, linter policy, OpenAPI checker, or seal registry;
- a claim that “compliant” guarantees learning, mastery, exam readiness, product success, perpetual conformity, or permanent verification;
- a substitute for MS001 conformance evaluation — CCM determines obligation status; CC defines when conformity may be claimed;
- a substitute for MS002 traceability — CT preserves relationships; CCM consumes them;
- a substitute for WS2 verification — CV evaluates evidence against requirements; CCM consumes findings to determine obligation status;
- a substitute for Programme VIII evidence validation (EV-xx) — EV validates educational evidence eligibility; CCM determines *obligation satisfaction*;
- a substitute for Educational Validation Framework quality review — quality release remains a distinct horizon;
- a certification authority for implementations, stacks, vendors, or releases.

## 3. Compliance Posture (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material constitutional compliance determination travels through four constitutional phases. These are **normative compliance obligations**, not an implementation architecture.

```
Identify published constitutional obligations and referenced specifications
        │
        ▼
Consume verification findings, established traceability, and conformance relationships
        │
        ▼
Determine obligation status under CCM-01…CCM-07
        │
        ├── not-satisfied / deferred / escalated (lawful determination — law unchanged; no certificate)
        │
        └── satisfied → Emit determination and preserve audit records
                │
                ▼
        Explain obligations / findings / specs / determination / boundaries
```

| Phase | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **Identify** | Name only published constitutional obligations and specifications as the determination standard | Standard is unpublished custom, tribal knowledge, or “how main works today” |
| **Consume** | Use identifiable verification findings, established CT relationships, and identifiable conformance relationships as inputs | Findings would be invented or rewritten, lineage invented, or authority inferred from a badge |
| **Determine** | Apply CCM-01…CCM-07 to judge obligation status | Determination would invent law, rewrite specs, redefine meaning, privilege a stack as law, or certify an implementation |
| **Record** | Produce determinations and audit-speakable trails without amending corpora | Path would treat determinations as constitutional amendments, certificates, or freeze an implementation as authority |

> **Compliance never “best-effort invents” a fifth phase called law-making, reinterpretation, certification, or stack canonisation.**

## 5. Core Responsibilities

The Constitutional Compliance Model is constitutionally responsible for:

| Responsibility | Meaning |
|----------------|---------|
| **Define compliance as obligation determination** | Bind determinations as judgements of whether published obligations have been satisfied (`CONSTITUTIONAL_COMPLIANCE_MODEL.md`) |
| **Bind objectives** | Enforce obligation-status honesty, consistency, neutrality, auditability, and explainability (`COMPLIANCE_OBJECTIVES.md`) |
| **Close the compliance catalogue** | Permit only recognised CCM-01…CCM-07 types (`COMPLIANCE_TYPES.md`) |
| **Draw hard boundaries** | Forbid law creation, law modification, meaning redefinition, authority substitution, and certification (`COMPLIANCE_BOUNDARIES.md`) |
| **Require explainability** | Make obligations, findings, specs, determinations, and boundaries speakable (`COMPLIANCE_EXPLAINABILITY.md`) |
| **Preserve layering** | Keep compliance subordinate to Constitution, EIP, Programmes VI–VIII, WS1 conformance / traceability, and WS2 verification |

### 5.1 Binding non-responsibility

The Constitutional Compliance Model must **not**:

- invent new constitutional compliance types outside CCM-01…CCM-07 without a Programme IX amendment;
- create, modify, or deprecate constitutional specifications under Programmes VI–VIII (or Constitution / EIP / WS1 / WS2) via determinations;
- redefine Programme VI educational meaning, EIP claim classes, CC criteria, CT lineage meaning, or CV verification meaning during determination;
- author runtime behaviour, services, APIs, or UI under the label “compliant”;
- certify implementations, stacks, vendors, releases, or pipelines as constitutionally permanent;
- substitute a preferred implementation, stack, or vendor as the constitutional standard;
- invent verification findings or rewrite findings to force a satisfied determination;
- invent traceability relationships to force a satisfied determination;
- rewrite conformance findings to force a satisfied determination;
- implement Runtime A, testing frameworks, CI/CD, GitHub Actions, Python, Flask, SQLAlchemy, REST, OpenAPI, or infrastructure;
- present green pipelines, coverage percentages, linter scores, or seals as constitutional law, educational truth, or certificates.

## 6. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Compliance Model exists so that:

1. **Law remains upstream of determination** — obligation status is judged by published specifications and consumed findings; determinations do not quietly become the specifications.
2. **Verification remains honest** — compliance consumes findings; it does not invent or rewrite them so a report “passes.”
3. **Traceability remains honest** — compliance consumes established lineage; it does not invent links so obligations look satisfied.
4. **Conformance and verification remain supported, not replaced** — compliance speaks to obligation status without becoming a second conformity engine, a second verification engine, or a certificate mill.
5. **Authority remains honest** — no team may treat a compliant artefact as the new constitutional source.
6. **Implementations remain replaceable** — compliance does not freeze Flask, SQLAlchemy, a particular API shape, or a CI vendor as law.
7. **Consistency remains honest** — the same published obligations and consumed inputs yield the same determination.
8. **Audit remains possible** — every material determination can be reconstructed against named obligations, findings, specifications, and boundaries.
9. **Explainability remains intact** — compliance speech describes obligation status; it does not redefine what learning means.
10. **Students remain protected** — product theatre cannot launder unsatisfied educational obligations as “compliant approved.”
