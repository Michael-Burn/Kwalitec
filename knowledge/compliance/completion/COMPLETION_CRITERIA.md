## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor needs bright conditions for saying “this constitutional compliance work is finished” — and equally bright refusals when it is not. After WS3 / MS001 has named when and how compliance determination may occur and WS3 / MS002 has named how compliance activities are organised, Programme IX Workstream 3 still needs one fulfilment checklist:

> **What must be true before constitutional speech may affirm that the required compliance lifecycle has been fully executed and compliance determinations have been lawfully produced, recorded, and preserved?**

These criteria answer that question. They are **normative constitutional conditions**, not database predicates, CI job statuses, ticket states, seal badges, or API status codes.

---

## 2. Criterion Catalogue

All material affirmed completions require **CCC-01 through CCC-05** to hold. Failure of any criterion ⇒ **not yet** or **refuse close**. Do not invent a partial “mostly complete” certification, approval, educational, governance, or constitutional status.

| ID | Criterion | Primary question |
|----|-----------|------------------|
| **CCC-01** | Required lifecycle stages completed | Were required CCL-01…CCL-07 stages completed for the concern? |
| **CCC-02** | Required verification findings consumed | Were required named verification findings consumed under published law? |
| **CCC-03** | Required compliance determinations produced | Were required compliance determinations produced under published CCM criteria? |
| **CCC-04** | Audit records preserved | Do reconstructable constitutional audit records exist for the completed compliance? |
| **CCC-05** | Compliance formally closed | Was the compliance concern formally closed under published CCL-07 / boundary honesty? |

Optional supporting honesty checks (do not replace CCC-01…CCC-05):

| ID | Check | Primary question |
|----|-------|------------------|
| **CCC-06** | Catalogue honesty | Were stages and determinations typed under published CCL-01…CCL-07 and CCM-01…CCM-07 only? |
| **CCC-07** | Continuity honesty | Was prior lawful compliance history preserved (EIP-005)? |
| **CCC-08** | Non-claim honesty | Does the judgement refuse certification / approval / educational / governance / amendment overclaim? |
| **CCC-09** | Neutrality honesty | Does the judgement refuse stack / vendor canonisation as completion law? |
| **CCC-10** | Repeatability honesty | Would the same published inputs yield the same completion disposition? |

## 3. Criterion Definitions

### CCC-01 — Required lifecycle stages completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every lifecycle stage required by the concern’s published WS3 / MS002 scope has been executed — or that a published refuse / defer / escalate disposition exhausted remaining *compliance* duties for that stage set with honest progression to recording and closure of what occurred.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Identifiable compliance concern opened under WS3 / MS001–MS002.
- Required CCL-01…CCL-07 stage identities for the concern scope.
- Stage traces / disposition records for each required stage (including lawful early stops).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed stage-fulfilment record naming which required stages completed.
- Or a lawful stop record naming which stages were refused / deferred / escalated and why remaining determination duties are exhausted.

**Completion conditions.**

- Required stages map to published CCL-01…CCL-07 only.
- Mandatory initiation, findings preparation, lineage confirmation, assessment, determination production, recording, and closure obligations for the concern are not silently omitted.
- Lawful early stops still leave honest stage outcomes; silent disappearance of a concern is a defect.
- Refusal of “pipeline skipped prep” or “we know it ran” without stage identity as completion.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That stage completion certifies or approves the implementation.
- That stage completion means the learner succeeded or governance finished.
- That skipped stages may be invented or implied from CI theatre.
- That constitutional specifications were amended by progressing stages.
- That verification was re-run or findings were rewritten under stage progress.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who declares obligation review “finished” without confirming each required review step occurred has not finished lawful care — even if the desk looks tidy.

---

### CCC-02 — Required verification findings consumed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every named verification finding required by the concern’s published scope was identified, prepared, and consumed as input under published WS3 / MS001–MS002 law — without inventing, rewriting, or re-authoring those findings as a second verification engine — or that a published refuse disposition exhausted remaining *consumption* duties when required findings could not be lawfully consumed.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Named verification finding identities in scope (lawful CV dispositions).
- Published constitutional specification and obligation identities under determination.
- Established CT relationships consumed (never invented) for the concern.
- Findings-preparation and consumption records under CCL-02 (and related stages).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed findings-consumption record naming which verification findings were consumed against which published obligations.
- Or a lawful refuse / defer / escalate record naming which required findings remain unconsumed and why.

**Completion conditions.**

- Findings subjects are named (not “the tests,” “the pipeline,” or vendor labels alone).
- Consumption cites published constitutional specifications and obligations, not unpublished customs.
- Findings consumed were produced under WS2 law — invented or rewritten findings are a hard stop.
- Traceability consumed for determination was established under WS1 / MS002 — invented lineage is a hard stop.
- Refusal of coverage percentages, lint scores, Twin confidence, or CI greens as substitutes for named findings consumption.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That consumed findings are therefore certified, approved, or educationally successful.
- That consuming findings re-runs or replaces WS2 verification.
- That a particular stack, language, or framework is privileged as law.
- That product quality or educational quality was judged.
- That constitutional specifications were modified to match the findings.
- That Programme VIII EV eligibility equals findings-consumption fulfilment.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who claims “the marks were used” without naming which marks were consumed against which syllabus obligations has performed review theatre, not consumption fulfilment.

---

### CCC-03 — Required compliance determinations produced

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every compliance determination required by the concern’s published scope has been lawfully produced under published CCM criteria — including satisfied, not-satisfied, deferred, and escalated dispositions — without rewriting those dispositions into certification, approval, educational success, or governance completion.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Assessment dispositions under applicable CCM-01…CCM-07 types (CCL-04 outputs).
- Published determination-production rules under WS3 / MS001–MS002 (especially CCL-05).
- Boundary confirmations that determinations do not invent law or certificates.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Identifiable compliance determination identities with lawful dispositions.
- Or a lawful stop record naming why required determinations could not be produced without invention.

**Completion conditions.**

- Determinations cite published constitutional references, named obligations, and consumed findings.
- Dispositions remain within published vocabulary (satisfied / not-satisfied / deferred / escalated).
- “Assessment happened somewhere” without named determination identity is not completion.
- Lawful not-satisfied / deferred / escalated determinations still satisfy *production* when they are the honest outcome — completeness of *production* is not completeness of *satisfaction*, and neither is certification.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That producing a determination upgrades the implementation to certified or approved.
- That a satisfied determination is a certificate or approval seal.
- That a not-satisfied determination may be silently erased so close can look green.
- That MS001 conformance evaluation was replaced, skipped, or certified by determination production.
- That Educational Validation Framework quality review is complete.
- That governance, programme, or release completion is finished as constitutional law.

**Tutor rationale.** Recording a hard obligation-status is not endorsing the product — but omitting a required determination while claiming the session finished is dishonest. Completeness of *determination production* is not completeness of *certification*.

---

### CCC-04 — Audit records preserved

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that reconstructable constitutional audit records exist for the completed compliance: which obligations were assessed, which verification findings were consumed, which lifecycle stages were completed, which compliance determinations were produced, and which boundaries remained intact — sufficient for student / developer / auditor reconstruction under `COMPLETION_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Stage traces (CCL-01…CCL-07).
- Findings-consumption and determination records.
- Boundary preservation confirmations.
- Continuity obligations under EIP-005.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Audit-speakable records aligned with CCCQ-01…CCCQ-05.
- Honest refuse / defer / escalate records when fulfilment was not affirmed.

**Completion conditions.**

- Material completion judgements leave records that cite published constitutional references, not only technical job logs or CI annotations.
- Missing CCM / CCL identity, missing obligation refs, missing finding identity, missing determination identity, or missing boundary honesty for a material affirmed close is a defect.
- Continuity forbids erasing prior lawful compliance trails to make close look tidy.
- Refusal of log volume alone as a substitute for constitutional content.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That a particular database schema, log shipper, CI annotation format, or UI widget is required.
- That audit completeness equals certification, approval, educational success, or governance completion.
- That volume of logs substitutes for constitutional content.
- That Twin / Adaptive / analytics origin stories may replace published constitutional identity.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later reconstruct why compliance was declared finished cannot be trusted — and neither can a platform that celebrates mute “complete” flags.

---

### CCC-05 — Compliance formally closed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that the compliance concern was formally closed under published CCL-07 obligations and boundary honesty — exhausting remaining *compliance lifecycle* duties without inventing a certificate, implementation approval, educational success, governance completion, or constitutional amendment.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Recorded determinations and audit records (CCC-03 / CCC-04).
- CCL-07 closure rules and `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md` hard stops.
- Explicit non-claim confirmations (certification / approval / educational success / governance completion / law change).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Formal close disposition: affirmed complete / not yet / refuse close / defer / escalate.
- Boundary preservation record confirming forbidden overclaims did not occur.

**Completion conditions.**

- Closure occurs only after required stages, findings consumption, determinations, and audit obligations for the concern are satisfied — or after a published stop exhausts remaining compliance duties with honest recording.
- Closure speech answers CCCQ-01…CCCQ-05 or explicitly refuses mute theatre.
- Closure never silently upgrades determination dispositions.
- Refusal of ticket closure, PR merge, seal badges, or pipeline greens alone as formal constitutional close.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That formal close certifies the implementation, release, stack, or vendor.
- That formal close approves the implementation for production or educational use as constitutional law.
- That formal close amends constitutional specifications or transfers constitutional authority.
- That formal close means the student succeeded or is exam-ready.
- That formal close means governance, programme, or release governance has finished as constitutional law.

**Tutor rationale.** Closing the obligation-review folder is care only when the required steps, determinations, and records exist — and only when nobody pretends the close is a certificate for the syllabus, the product, or the candidate.

## 4. Criterion Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CCC-01 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CCC-02 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CCC-03 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CCC-04 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — mute complete badges are defects |
| CCC-05 fails | Affirmation is unlawful even if earlier stages appear present |
| Optional CCC-06…CCC-10 fail | Treat as honesty defects; do not soft-pass CCC-01…CCC-05 |
| Any criterion “passes” only by implying certification / approval / educational success / governance completion / law change | The implied overclaim voids lawful affirmation |

All of CCC-01…CCC-05 are **conjunctive** for material affirmed completion. There is no lawful “four of five” educational, certification, approval, governance, or constitutional status.

---

## 5. Lawful Stop Dispositions

Not every incomplete concern is a defect. Published WS3 / MS002 refuse / defer / escalate outcomes may **exhaust remaining compliance duties** for a concern without affirming lifecycle fulfilment as “complete” in the celebratory sense — while still requiring honest recording and formal close of what occurred.

| Disposition | Constitutional meaning | May affirm CCC completion? |
|-------------|------------------------|----------------------------|
| **Affirm complete** | CCC-01…CCC-05 hold for the concern | Yes |
| **Not yet** | Required stages, findings consumption, determinations, or records remain outstanding | No |
| **Refuse close** | Affirmation would invent stages / findings / determinations, erase audit trails, or overclaim | No |
| **Defer** | Published wait for upstream corpus / findings / lineage identity | No (unless published law says remaining compliance duties are exhausted) |
| **Escalate** | Published stop requiring human / corpus governance attention | No (unless published law says remaining compliance duties are exhausted) |

A lawful stop that exhausts compliance duties is **honest unfinished fulfilment or honest refusal** — never a stealth certificate, product approval, educational success, governance completion, or constitutional amendment.

---

## 6. Non-Criteria

The following are **not** constitutional completion criteria:

- CI pipeline greens, flaky-test suppression, or job exit codes;
- ticket closure, PR merge, or documentation checklist ticks alone;
- coverage percentages, lint scores, seal badges, or elapsed time;
- Twin / Adaptive / Experience / Mission confidence signals;
- UI celebration states or analytics milestones;
- “looks compliant enough for the demo”;
- MS001 conformant disposition (that is fidelity law, not CCC);
- WS1 / MS003 traceability completion (CTC) alone (orthogonal lineage fulfilment);
- WS2 / MS003 verification completion (CVC) alone (orthogonal verification lifecycle fulfilment — findings may be inputs after CVC, but CVC ≠ CCC);
- Educational Validation Framework quality release (orthogonal);
- any implication that constitutional specifications changed because compliance closed;
- any implication that governance or programme work is finished because compliance closed.

---

## 7. Closing Statement

> **Constitutional compliance is complete only when required lifecycle stages are completed, required verification findings are consumed, required compliance determinations are produced, audit records are preserved, and compliance is formally closed.  
> Satisfying CCC-01…CCC-05 never means the implementation is certified, approved, educationally successful, that governance is finished, or that constitutional law has changed.**
