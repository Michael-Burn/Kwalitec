## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which constitutional obligations* were assessed — which verification findings were consumed, which compliance lifecycle stages were completed, which compliance determinations were produced, and which limits remained intact — is not closing honestly; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–VIII have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, and runtime speech speakable, after WS1 has made conformance and traceability speech speakable, after WS2 has made verification speech speakable, and after WS3 / MS001–MS002 have made compliance and lifecycle speech speakable, Programme IX still needs one compliance-completion-speech answer:

> **“Which constitutional obligations were assessed, which verification findings were consumed, which compliance lifecycle stages were completed, which compliance determinations were produced, and which constitutional boundaries remained intact?”**

That answer must cover every material affirmed (or refused) completion without inventing educational success, implying certification, approving implementations, implying governance completion, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, freezing a stack as law, or modifying constitutional law.

## 2. What Compliance Completion Explainability Is

**Constitutional Compliance Completion Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **fulfilment (or lawful refusal of fulfilment) of the required constitutional compliance lifecycle** — not to invent a second tutor voice from CI complete badges, and not to grade the student, certify an implementation, approve a release, or finish governance as constitutional law.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Obligations assessed** | Named published constitutional obligations under determination | Which constitutional obligations were assessed? |
| **Verification findings consumed** | Named verification findings consumed as inputs under the concern | Which verification findings were consumed? |
| **Lifecycle stages completed** | Named CCL-01…CCL-07 stages executed (or lawfully stopped) for the concern | Which compliance lifecycle stages were completed? |
| **Determinations produced** | Named compliance determinations with lawful dispositions | Which compliance determinations were produced? |
| **Boundaries intact** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden overclaims / amendments / substitutions did not occur | Which constitutional boundaries remained intact? |

Compliance Completion Explainability is:

- **fulfilment-centred** — it narrates why CCC criteria hold (or why close is refused);
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CCM / CCL / CCC identities where applicable;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published specifications, obligations, and named findings / determinations;
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to amend corpora;
- **non-evaluative-substitute** — completion is never narrated as certification, approval, educational success, or governance finish;
- **quality-honest** — complete is never narrated as mastery, readiness, or educational quality;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence / validation speech, MS001 conformance speech, MS002 relationship speech, WS2 verification speech, WS3 / MS001 compliance speech, or WS3 / MS002 lifecycle speech;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match CCC-04 reconstruction needs;
- **authority-honest** — explanations must not paper over authority inference or stack canonisation.

Compliance Completion Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–VIII / WS1 / WS2 / WS3 meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a certification instrument or implementation approval;
- a governance-finish report as constitutional law;
- a substitute for WS3 / MS001 compliance explainability or WS3 / MS002 lifecycle explainability (compose with; do not replace);
- a substitute for WS2 / MS003 verification completion explainability or WS1 / MS003 traceability completion explainability (compose with; do not collapse);
- a rendering system, UI template, CI badge, seal visualiser, or analytics dashboard;
- a claim that clear completion speech guarantees learning, a pass, conformity, certification, approval, or governance finish;
- a licence to present coverage, lint scores, ticket graphs, or pipeline greens as constitutional proof or educational quality.

## 3. Mandatory Explanation Questions

Every material completion judgement (affirm or refuse close) should answer all five questions:

### CCCQ-01 — Which constitutional obligations were assessed?

Name the primary published constitutional obligations (and the Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII specifications that publish them) whose status was determined under the compliance concern — or name which required obligations remain unassessed when refusing close. When a clause or catalogue ID is material, name it (for example EIP-002 claim class; RC-02; CCM-02 as *type applied*). Unpublished tribal norms and “how main works” are not constitutional obligations.

### CCCQ-02 — Which verification findings were consumed?

Name the primary verification finding identities and dispositions consumed as inputs under the concern (satisfied / not-satisfied / deferred / escalated) — or name which required findings remain unconsumed when refusing close. Never narrate invented or rewritten findings as lawful consumption. “Tests passed somewhere” is not a constitutional finding identity.

### CCCQ-03 — Which compliance lifecycle stages were completed?

Name the primary CCL-01…CCL-07 stages completed for the concern — or name which required stages remain incomplete / refused when refusing close. Include lawful early-stop stage outcomes honestly. Never narrate a skipped mandatory stage as completed because a later pipeline job glowed green. “It ran in CI” is not a constitutional stage identity.

### CCCQ-04 — Which compliance determinations were produced?

Name the primary compliance determination identities and dispositions produced under the concern (satisfied / not-satisfied / deferred / escalated) — or name which required determinations remain unproduced when refusing close. Never narrate a not-satisfied / deferred / escalated determination as upgraded by completion. “Looks compliant enough” is not a constitutional determination identity.

### CCCQ-05 — Which constitutional boundaries remained intact?

Confirm the hard stops in `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md` that were checked — especially non-implication of certification, non-implication of implementation approval, non-modification of constitutional specifications, non-replacement of constitutional authority, implementation neutrality, repeatability honesty, and non-equation of completion with educational success, governance completion, or constitutional truth.

## 5. Audience Rules

| Audience | May hear | Must not hear as educational law |
|----------|----------|----------------------------------|
| **Student** | That the learning system’s behaviour was checked against published educational rules, why a stop occurred when compliance is incomplete, and what limits apply — in plain educational language | Internal CCC IDs as theatre; CI traces; seal badges as “proof you are ready”; complete as mastery, as certified, as approved, or as governance finished |
| **Developer / auditor** | Full CCCQ-01…05 answers, corpus paths, CCM / CCL / CCC IDs, stage traces, finding and determination dispositions, boundary checks, neutrality and non-implication notes | Product success metrics, pipeline greens, or seal counts substituted for constitutional refs; completion framed as educational quality, new law, certification, approval, or governance finish |

Student and developer traces must describe the **same constitutional truth**. Vocabulary may differ; facts may not.
