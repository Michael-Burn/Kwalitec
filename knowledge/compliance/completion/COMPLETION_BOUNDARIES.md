## 1. Purpose

Compliance machinery that silently turns “the compliance lifecycle finished” into “implementation is certified,” “implementation is approved,” “student succeeded,” “governance is finished,” or “the Constitution now says X” destroys student trust and educational integrity.

This document draws a bright line between **lawful constitutional compliance completion** (Programme IX / WS3 / MS003) and **certification / approval / educational judgement / governance finish / constitutional authorship** owned elsewhere.

---

## 2. Boundary Principles

1. **Confirm lifecycle fulfilment, do not legislate.** Completion affirms fulfilment of published compliance lifecycle duties; it does not create constitutional law.
2. **Compliance only.** Certification, approval, educational success, and governance completion are never inferred from close.
3. **Not conformance evaluation.** WS1 / MS001 owns fidelity disposition.
4. **Not certification.** Completion never certifies implementations, releases, stacks, or vendors.
5. **Not approval.** Completion never approves implementations for production, delivery, or educational use as constitutional law.
6. **Not constitutional amendment.** Close never patches law or modifies constitutional authority.
7. **Exactly as published.** Close never redefines constitutional specifications or meaning.
8. **Published criteria only.** Pipelines, tickets, seals, and elapsed time are not fulfilment law.
9. **Published records only.** Audit continuity is constitutional, not free-form log theatre.
10. **Reference determinations honestly.** Produced determinations may be cited; producing them never upgrades disposition to certified / approved / mastered.
11. **Authority non-transfer.** Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard after close.
12. **Meaning non-reinterpretation.** Programme VI questions and EIP claim classes survive close intact.
13. **Implementation neutrality.** No stack becomes law by proximity to a complete flag.
14. **Compliance-complete ≠ quality.** Completion never grades learning, mastery, readiness, or product success.
15. **Compliance-complete ≠ certified / approved.** Completion never becomes a certificate or approval authority.
16. **Compliance-complete ≠ governance finished.** Completion never finishes programme or release governance as constitutional law.
17. **Explain the stop.** Students, developers, and auditors should hear when close must be refused.
18. **No emergency exemption.** Deadlines and demos never mint completion power or amendment power.
19. **Repeatability survives close.** Close must not invent contradictory dispositions from identical published inputs.

## 3. What Compliance Completion May Do (Lawful)

| Lawful action | Constitutional meaning |
|---------------|------------------------|
| **Confirm lifecycle completion** | Affirm CCC-01…CCC-05 when published criteria hold for a lawful compliance concern |
| **Confirm determination recording** | Cite that required compliance determinations were produced and recorded under published CCM criteria — without rewriting dispositions |
| **Preserve audit continuity** | Leave reconstructable constitutional records of affirmed or refused close |
| **Refuse unlawful close** | Prefer honest not-yet / refuse / defer / escalate over improvisation |
| **Compose required stages and types** | Confirm fulfilment across published CCL / CCM conjunctions without collapsing failed duties |
| **Remain implementation-neutral** | Honour completion law across lawful equivalent stacks |
| **Preserve repeatability** | Ensure same published inputs do not yield contradictory completion stories |
| **Explain completion** | Answer obligations / findings / stages / determinations / boundaries without rewriting meaning |
| **Propose corpus amendments upstream** | Recommend (not enact) amendments when law itself must change before compliance can complete |

These actions **confirm compliance lifecycle fulfilment**. They do **not** publish a new Constitution, invent coach warrants, mint tips, author ownership maps, invent CCM / CCL / CCC types, author runtime behaviour, evaluate fidelity in place of MS001, certify releases, approve implementations, finish governance as constitutional law, or judge educational quality.

## 4. What Compliance Completion Must NEVER Do

| Forbidden action | Why | Lawful alternative |
|------------------|-----|--------------------|
| **Imply certification** | Completion is not a certificate authority | Affirm lifecycle fulfilment only; never certify releases or stacks |
| **Imply implementation approval** | Completion is not an approval authority | Affirm lifecycle fulfilment only; never approve production or delivery as constitutional law |
| **Modify constitutional specifications** | Only corpus governance may amend meaning | Leave specs unchanged; propose amendment upstream or refuse incomplete compliance |
| **Replace constitutional authority** | Published corpora remain the standard after any close | Keep determinations as compliance outcomes, never as next-cycle law |
| **Create constitutional law** | Completion judgements are not legislation | Cite published law only; refuse unpublished anchors |
| **Redefine constitutional meaning** | Close never soft-amends Programme VI–VIII / EIP meaning | Preserve identity of published meaning; never invent it |
| **Replace conformance evaluation** | MS001 owns CC disposition | Supply / confirm compliance support for obligation honesty; never claim conformity-from-complete as a certificate |
| **Re-author verification findings** | WS2 owns verification; compliance consumes findings | Cite consumed findings honestly; never rewrite them under close |
| **Create educational meaning** | Programme VI owns coach / planner questions | Preserve identity of published meaning; never invent it |
| **Author runtime behaviour** | Programme VIII + permitted writers own execution law | Determine obligation status against published law; do not mint behaviour |
| **Bypass audit / determination requirements** | Requirements are educational care, not optional polish | Prefer understatement / refuse over invented green completes |
| **Privilege a technology as constitutional** | Implementation neutrality | Confirm obligations, not Flask / SQLAlchemy / REST / OpenAPI fashion |
| **Erase compliance trails to tidy close** | EIP-005 continuity | Preserve prior lawful stage / determination records; refuse destructive “cleanup” |
| **Upgrade determination disposition by closing** | CCC-03 preserves identity; close does not rewrite status | Record determinations honestly including not-satisfied / deferred / escalated |
| **Present CI / tickets / seals as fulfilment law** | Theatre is not constitution | Require CCC-01…CCC-05 content |
| **Imply educational success** | Learning outcomes are not lifecycle fulfilment | Keep student speech honest: compliance-complete ≠ ready |
| **Imply governance completion** | Programme / release governance is not CCC fulfilment | Keep speech honest: compliance-complete ≠ governance finished |
| **Emergency-amend via deadline** | Urgency never mints amendment power | Refuse incomplete close; escalate to corpus governance |
| **Collapse with CVC / CTC completion** | Verification and lineage fulfilment are orthogonal | Compose with WS2 / MS003 and WS1 / MS003; do not treat CVC or CTC as CCC (or vice versa) |

## 5. Boundary Tests (Pre-Affirmation)

Before material constitutional compliance completion is treated as affirmed, close requires affirmative answers:

| # | Test | Fail ⇒ |
|---|------|--------|
| 1 | Were required lifecycle stages completed (CCC-01)? | not yet / refuse |
| 2 | Were required verification findings consumed without rewrite (CCC-02)? | not yet / refuse |
| 3 | Were required compliance determinations produced without disposition rewrite (CCC-03)? | not yet / refuse |
| 4 | Do audit records support CCCQ-01…CCCQ-05 (CCC-04)? | refuse |
| 5 | Was compliance formally closed under boundary honesty (CCC-05)? | refuse |
| 6 | Would affirmation imply certification, approval, educational success, governance completion, or law change? | refuse overclaim |
| 7 | Would affirmation redefine specifications or replace constitutional authority? | refuse |
| 8 | Would affirmation privilege a stack / vendor as law? | refuse |
| 9 | Would affirmation erase prior lawful compliance trails for convenience? | refuse |
| 10 | Can the stop or affirmation be explained honestly to student and auditor? | refuse mute theatre |

Any single fail blocks lawful affirmation.

## 6. Collisions with Neighbour Corpora

| Neighbour claim | Lawful coexistence | Unlawful collapse |
|-----------------|--------------------|-------------------|
| MS001 “conformant” | May coexist with completed compliance for the same subjects | Treating CCC complete as CC certified / approved (or vice versa as automatic) |
| CCM “determination produced” | Completion judges *lifecycle fulfilment including determinations* | Treating any single determination as whole-concern completion without stages / audit / close |
| CCL “stage closed” | Completion judges *required stages complete together* | Treating any single CCL stage as whole-concern completion |
| CVC “verification complete” | Orthogonal fulfilment; findings may be consumed after CVC | Treating verification complete as compliance complete (or vice versa) |
| CTC “lineage complete” | Orthogonal fulfilment horizons | Treating lineage complete as compliance complete (or vice versa) |
| Programme VIII EV eligible | Orthogonal eligibility | Treating eligibility as compliance lifecycle complete |
| Workflow / execution / evidence completion | Orthogonal fulfilment horizons | Treating compliance close as orchestration / runtime / evidence-handling success |
| Educational Validation Framework release | Quality lens | Treating CCC complete as quality certified |
| CI green / seal badge | Engineering signals | Treating signals as CCC criteria |
| “Governance finished” | Delivery / programme process | Treating CCC complete as governance completion |

## 7. Closing Statement

> **Compliance completion may confirm lifecycle completion, confirm determination recording, and preserve audit continuity.  
> It must never imply certification, imply implementation approval, modify constitutional specifications, or replace constitutional authority.**

This document does not replace sibling boundaries. It binds **constitutional compliance completion** so those boundaries survive compliance machinery, CI, demos, and replacements.
