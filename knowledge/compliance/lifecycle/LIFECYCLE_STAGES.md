## 1. Purpose

Compliance without a closed stage catalogue invents law by proximity: whichever pipeline step, ticket status, or reviewer habit happens to finish becomes the tutor’s “proof the system ran a constitutional compliance determination.”

This catalogue names the only lawful constitutional compliance lifecycle stages — and binds each stage to purpose, inputs, outputs, and permitted / prohibited activities.

---

## 2. Stage Principles

1. **Ordered and skip-disciplined.** Stages may be brief under thin scope; mandatory initiation, findings identity, lineage confirmation, assessment, determination, recording, and closure obligations may not be silently omitted when a compliance determination is claimed.
2. **Consumption before assessment.** Published obligations, named verification findings, and established CT relationships are identified before CCM determination.
3. **Explainability accumulates.** Each stage leaves traceable artefacts for `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.
4. **Refusal is a stage outcome.** Not-satisfied, deferred, escalated, and refuse-progress are first-class conclusions.
5. **Deterministic transitions.** Same published inputs ⇒ same next stage outcomes.
6. **Stages coordinate; they do not legislate.** No stage creates, modifies, or redefines constitutional law.
7. **Stages do not certify.** Closure never freezes an implementation as permanently lawful.
8. **Stages do not replace verification.** Findings are consumed; they are never re-authored as a second verification engine.

## 3. Catalogue Overview

| ID | Stage | Constitutional purpose (short) | Primary inputs | Primary outputs |
|----|-------|--------------------------------|----------------|-----------------|
| **CCL-01** | Compliance Initiation | Open a compliance concern against named published obligations and scope | Published corpus / obligation identities + concern scope + optional CC artefacts | Initiated concern record (or refuse-open) |
| **CCL-02** | Findings Preparation | Identify and prepare named verification findings for determination | Initiated concern + candidate finding identities | Prepared findings bundle (or refuse-prepare) |
| **CCL-03** | Traceability Confirmation | Confirm established CT relationships required for the concern | Prepared findings + required CT identities | Confirmed lineage set (or refuse-confirm) |
| **CCL-04** | Constitutional Assessment | Apply CCM-01…CCM-07 against prepared findings under published obligations | Confirmed lineage + published CCM criteria + findings | Assessment dispositions under CCM types |
| **CCL-05** | Determination Production | Emit compliance determinations under published criteria | Assessment dispositions | Compliance determinations (satisfied / not-satisfied / deferred / escalated) |
| **CCL-06** | Determination Recording | Preserve audit-speakable trails of the lifecycle | Determinations + stage traces | Audit records bound to obligations / findings / relationships / stages |
| **CCL-07** | Compliance Closure | Close the lifecycle honestly when duties are exhausted | Recorded determinations + boundary confirmations | Closed lifecycle (or refuse-close) — never a certificate |

Material compliance claiming constitutional determinations must map to this sequence as published law requires. Stages may be brief; none may invent a stage outside this catalogue.

**Relation to CCM / CC / CT / CV / EV:** Those catalogues remain defined solely by their owners. CCL stages *organise when* compliance activities occur; they do not replace, reclassify, certify, re-verify, or invent those catalogues.

## 4. Canonical Stage Sequence

```text
CCL-01  Compliance Initiation
           ↓
CCL-02  Findings Preparation
           ↓
CCL-03  Traceability Confirmation
           ↓
CCL-04  Constitutional Assessment
           ↓
CCL-05  Determination Production
           ↓
CCL-06  Determination Recording
           ↓
CCL-07  Compliance Closure
```

Lawful early stop dispositions (refuse-open / refuse-prepare / refuse-confirm / deferred / escalated / not-satisfied) may exhaust remaining *assessment* duties while still requiring honest recording and closure of what occurred — never silent disappearance of the concern.

## 5. Stage Definitions

### CCL-01 — Compliance Initiation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Open a material compliance concern by naming the published constitutional obligations under determination and the scope of the concern — without inventing law, opening against unpublished customs, or treating initiation as a certificate.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Identifiable published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII obligation and specification references for the concern scope
- Concern identity (what obligation-status question is under compliance determination)
- Optional identifiable WS1 / MS001 conformance artefacts to be consumed (not rewritten)
- Optional published indication of required CCM-01…CCM-07 types for the scope

**Constitutional outputs**

- An initiated compliance concern record naming obligations, specifications, scope, and intended CCM type set — **or**
- Lawful refuse-open when standards are unpublished, scope is theatrical, or initiation would invent law

**Permitted activities**

- Name published obligations and specifications as determination standards
- Bound the concern scope without amending corpora
- Record intended CCM types from the published catalogue
- Refuse initiation when constitutional preconditions fail
- Preserve initiation audit identity for later stages

**Prohibited activities**

- Treat “how main works,” tribal knowledge, or stack preference as the standard
- Create or modify constitutional specifications by opening a concern
- Certify an implementation at initiation
- Invent unpublished compliance types
- Skip initiation when a material determination will later be claimed

**Authority preserved**  
Published corpora remain the sole standard. Initiation organises; it does not legislate.

---

### CCL-02 — Findings Preparation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Identify and prepare **named verification findings** for determination — so assessment judges obligation status from real verification outputs, not anonymous theatre — without inventing, rewriting, or re-running verification under a compliance badge.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Initiated concern from CCL-01
- Candidate verification finding identities (structural, behavioural, evidence, runtime, interface, API, governance findings as applicable)
- Published findings-identity expectations from WS2 and relevant WS3 / MS001 corpora

**Constitutional outputs**

- A prepared findings bundle with named verification findings bound to the concern — **or**
- Lawful refuse-prepare when findings identity is missing, forged, invented, rewritten, or would require unlawful re-verification as compliance

**Permitted activities**

- Collect and name identifiable verification findings
- Bind findings to the initiated concern and intended CCM types
- Refuse preparation when findings cannot be honestly identified
- Preserve findings-identity records for audit and explainability

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent missing verification findings
- Rewrite findings to force a later satisfied determination
- Re-run verification under the label “compliance preparation”
- Treat CI noise, coverage percentages, UI ticks, or seals as constitutional findings without published mapping
- Redefine educational or constitutional meaning while “preparing”
- Present preparation as satisfaction or certification

**Authority preserved**  
Verification findings remain upstream inputs. Preparation organises identity; it does not author verification or educational truth.

---

### CCL-03 — Traceability Confirmation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Confirm that **established** CT-01…CT-07 relationships required for the concern exist and are consumable — so assessment does not invent lineage mid-flight.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Prepared findings bundle from CCL-02
- Required published CT relationship identities for the concern scope
- Established CT provenance / relationship records from WS1 / MS002
- Optional conformance artefact identities already linked under CT law

**Constitutional outputs**

- A confirmed lineage set naming the established relationships to be consumed — **or**
- Lawful refuse-confirm when required relationships are missing, invented, unstable, or forged

**Permitted activities**

- Confirm existence and identity of established CT relationships
- Bind confirmed lineage to the concern and prepared findings
- Refuse progress when lineage is incomplete for the required scope
- Preserve lineage-consumption records for audit

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent CT relationships to force progress
- Treat “somehow linked in a ticket” as established constitutional traceability
- Rewrite CT meaning or forge provenance
- Treat confirmed lineage as itself “compliant,” “verified,” or “conformant”
- Skip lineage confirmation when material determinations will be claimed

**Authority preserved**  
WS1 / MS002 owns relationship law. This stage consumes and confirms presence; it does not author lineage.

---

### CCL-04 — Constitutional Assessment

**Constitutional purpose**  
Apply published **CCM-01…CCM-07** compliance types against prepared verification findings under published constitutional obligations and confirmed lineage — yielding assessment dispositions without rewriting law, re-authoring findings, or certifying implementations.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Confirmed lineage set from CCL-03
- Prepared findings bundle from CCL-02
- Published constitutional obligations and specifications named at CCL-01
- Published CCM-01…CCM-07 criteria from WS3 / MS001
- Optional conformance artefacts consumed as inputs (unchanged)

**Constitutional outputs**

- Assessment dispositions per applied CCM type (satisfied / not-satisfied / deferred / escalated under published criteria)
- Composition notes when multiple CCM types bind the concern

**Permitted activities**

- Apply only published CCM-01…CCM-07 types required by the concern
- Assess named findings against published obligations using confirmed lineage
- Prefer honest not-satisfied / deferred / escalated over improvisation
- Compose published CCM conjunctions without collapsing failed checks
- Remain repeatable and implementation-neutral

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent unpublished compliance types or customs
- Modify constitutional specifications to force satisfaction
- Redefine Programme VI educational meaning, CC criteria, CT meaning, or CV meaning in assessment prose
- Privilege a technology stack as constitutional
- Certify implementations, stacks, vendors, or releases
- Invent lineage during assessment
- Invent or rewrite verification findings during assessment
- Rewrite conformance findings to force a pass
- Determine educational quality, mastery, or readiness as a CCM outcome
- Become a second verification engine

**Authority preserved**  
WS3 / MS001 owns compliance types. This stage applies them; it does not amend the catalogue or become constitutional authority.

---

### CCL-05 — Determination Production

**Constitutional purpose**  
Emit **compliance determinations** as lawful dispositions under published criteria — speaking to whether published obligations have been satisfied — without converting determinations into amendments, certificates, or educational grades.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Assessment dispositions from CCL-04
- Published determination vocabulary (satisfied / not-satisfied / deferred / escalated)
- Bound references to obligations, specifications, findings, relationships, and CCM types

**Constitutional outputs**

- Compliance determinations for the concern (including composition outcomes when multiple CCM types apply)
- Explicit non-certificate framing (determinations are obligation-status dispositions only)

**Permitted activities**

- Produce determinations under published dispositions only
- Bind each determination to the CCM types and obligations that produced it
- Record composition / stop-rule application honestly
- Prefer understatement over invented green
- Propose (not enact) upstream corpus amendments when law itself must change

**Prohibited activities**

- Issue certificates for implementations, stacks, vendors, releases, or pipelines
- Present determinations as constitutional amendments or new educational meaning
- Narrate satisfaction as mastery, readiness, educational quality, permanent conformity, or permanent verification
- Rewrite determinations to match product urgency
- Collapse multi-type failures into a single false “satisfied”

**Authority preserved**  
Determinations judge obligation status against law using findings. They never become law or certificates.

---

### CCL-06 — Determination Recording

**Constitutional purpose**  
Preserve **audit-speakable records** of the lifecycle so reconstruction can answer which obligations, findings, relationships, stages, and determinations applied — without erasing history or forging provenance.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Compliance determinations from CCL-05
- Stage traces from CCL-01…CCL-05
- Boundary confirmations required by `LIFECYCLE_BOUNDARIES.md`
- Continuity obligations from EIP-005

**Constitutional outputs**

- Audit records binding obligations, specifications, verification finding identities, CT relationships, CCM types, CCL stages, determinations, and preserved boundaries
- Reconstructable explainability substrate for `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md`

**Permitted activities**

- Record constitutional references (not only technical CI logs)
- Bind determinations to the producing stage (especially CCL-04 / CCL-05)
- Preserve prior lawful history; refuse silent erasure
- Project one truth to student / developer / auditor vocabularies later

**Prohibited activities**

- Erase prior lawful compliance or lifecycle history to simplify the next report
- Forge provenance or invent missing corpus references
- Treat audit records as constitutional amendments
- Drop stage-to-determination binding
- Substitute ticket IDs, SHAs, or stack names for published corpus identity as the sole record

**Authority preserved**  
Recording preserves reconstructability. It does not author law or certify subjects.

---

### CCL-07 — Compliance Closure

**Constitutional purpose**  
Close the compliance lifecycle **honestly** when coordination duties for the concern are exhausted — including lawful early stops — without certifying implementations, freezing stacks, replacing verification, or pretending incomplete work is complete.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Recorded determinations and audit records from CCL-06
- Confirmation that remaining coordination duties are exhausted or lawfully stopped
- Boundary confirmations (non-certification, non-legislation, non-redefinition, non-substitution, non-verification-replacement)

**Constitutional outputs**

- Closed lifecycle disposition for the concern — **or**
- Lawful refuse-close when recording is incomplete, boundaries were breached, or closure would imply certification / permanent conformity / new law / verification replacement

**Permitted activities**

- Affirm closure when published duties are exhausted
- Close after lawful refuse / defer / escalate / not-satisfied paths with honest records
- Refuse closure when audit or boundary preconditions fail
- Keep closure distinct from conformance certificates, verification completion, and educational success

**Prohibited activities**

- Certify implementations, stacks, vendors, or releases by closing
- Replace MS001 conformance evaluation with “lifecycle closed”
- Replace WS2 verification with “lifecycle closed”
- Redefine constitutional meaning in closure speech
- Modify constitutional specifications or implementation artefacts as a closure act
- Erase inconvenient stage history so closure looks clean
- Treat closure as licence for the next cycle to use the implementation as constitutional authority

**Authority preserved**  
Closure ends coordination for the concern. Published corpora remain the sole standard afterward.

## 6. Stage Transition Rules

| From | To | Transition requires |
|------|----|---------------------|
| — | CCL-01 | Material compliance concern needs opening |
| CCL-01 | CCL-02 | Concern initiated (not refuse-open) |
| CCL-01 | CCL-06 / CCL-07 path | Refuse-open recorded honestly |
| CCL-02 | CCL-03 | Findings prepared (not refuse-prepare) |
| CCL-02 | CCL-06 / CCL-07 path | Refuse-prepare recorded honestly |
| CCL-03 | CCL-04 | Lineage confirmed (not refuse-confirm) |
| CCL-03 | CCL-06 / CCL-07 path | Refuse-confirm recorded honestly |
| CCL-04 | CCL-05 | Assessment dispositions available |
| CCL-05 | CCL-06 | Determinations produced under published vocabulary |
| CCL-06 | CCL-07 | Audit records preserved |
| CCL-07 | — | Closed or refuse-close recorded |

Silent jumps that claim determinations without CCL-01…CCL-03 honesty are constitutionally defective.

---

## 7. Composition with Compliance Types

| During stage | Relationship to CCM-01…CCM-07 |
|--------------|-------------------------------|
| CCL-01 | May *name* intended CCM types; does not apply them |
| CCL-02 | Prepares verification findings those types will consume |
| CCL-03 | Confirms lineage those types will consume |
| **CCL-04** | **Applies** CCM-01…CCM-07 |
| CCL-05 | Emits determinations produced by those applications |
| CCL-06 | Records CCM identities with determinations |
| CCL-07 | Closes without inventing new CCM types |

Cross-cutting concerns may bind multiple CCM types at CCL-04. Lifecycle stages remain CCL-01…CCL-07 only.

## 8. Closing Statement

> **Stages sequence compliance.  
> Stages do not redefine constitutional meaning, certify implementations, replace verification, or replace constitutional authority.**
