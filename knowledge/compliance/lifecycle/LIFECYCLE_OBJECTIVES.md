## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “seal finished,” “stages skipped for speed,” or “badge shown.” The tutor optimises **honest organisation of compliance work**: only a repeatable, consistent, implementation-neutral, explainable, and auditable sequence — consuming published obligations, identifiable verification findings, and established lineage — may support lawful compliance determinations.

These objectives bind every constitutional compliance lifecycle and every successor coordination machinery that claims constitutional compliance lifecycle meaning.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### CCLO-01 — Coordinate compliance lawfully

**Definition.** Ensure that every material compliance lifecycle organises activities through recognised CCL-01…CCL-07 stages — consuming published constitutional specifications, identifiable verification findings, and established traceability relationships — so that CCM-01…CCM-07 determinations occur only after lawful initiation, findings preparation, and lineage confirmation, and that determinations are produced and recorded without becoming law or certificates.

**Includes:**

- Binding every material lifecycle to identifiable published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII obligations and specifications.
- Consuming identifiable verification findings and established CT relationships (and identifiable conformance artefacts where material) as inputs.
- Applying only published CCM-01…CCM-07 types during CCL-04.
- Preferring lawful not-satisfied / deferred / escalated / refuse-progress outcomes over rewriting law, inventing or rewriting findings, inventing lineage, or mutating artefacts so the lifecycle “completes.”
- Keeping lifecycle progress distinct from educational quality, mastery, readiness, product success, conformity certificates, verification re-runs, or constitutional amendment.
- Preserving the Model’s posture: stages coordinate determination; they do not become law or certificates.

**Excludes:**

- Treating green CI, coverage percentages, linter scores, Twin estimates, Adaptive scores, UI ticks, or seals as lifecycle fulfilment.
- Soft-amending specifications, inventing unpublished customs, inventing or rewriting verification findings, inventing lineage, mutating artefacts, or privileging “how main works” to force progress.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for lawful coordination.
- Declaring “close enough” closure without documenting a corpus amendment.
- Issuing certificates that freeze an implementation as permanently compliant because the lifecycle closed.
- Re-running or replacing verification under a compliance badge.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software pretends unfinished or dishonest compliance work is fine because the product needs a finished seal. Coordination honesty is care; lifecycle theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CCLO-02 — Preserve repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that the same published constitutional obligations, the same consumed verification findings, the same established traceability relationships, the same published CCM criteria, and the same CCL stage sequence yield the same compliance disposition (satisfied / not-satisfied / deferred / escalated) and the same lawful stage outcomes — absent a published non-deterministic exception (none are granted by this Model for constitutional meaning or obligation status).

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who treats the same findings as satisfying or not depending on which reviewer woke first — or skips stages randomly — is not coaching; they are gambling.

**Manifestations:**

- Stage transitions and outcomes are corpus-bound and reproducible.
- Parallel CI runners, sharding, and cache races must not change constitutional disposition or invent stage skips.
- Explicit multi-type CCM sets at CCL-04 use published conjunction / published stop rules, not random reviewer races.
- “Eventually consistent lifecycle meaning” is a constitutional defect.
- Invented or unstable lineage inputs, and rewritten findings, are hard stops — not soft variables.

---

### CCLO-03 — Preserve consistency

**Definition.** Ensure that lifecycle organisation applies the same published stage meanings, stop rules, and determination dispositions across concerns, reviewers, and successors — so “compliance lifecycle” does not mean one thing on Monday and another on Tuesday for equivalent published inputs.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who invents a private stage order for convenience destroys institutional trust even if each private path looks tidy.

**Manifestations:**

- Only CCL-01…CCL-07 may organise constitutional compliance lifecycles.
- Stage purpose, inputs, outputs, and permitted / prohibited activities remain as published in `LIFECYCLE_STAGES.md`.
- Equivalent concerns under the same published law receive equivalent stage treatment.
- Ad hoc stage insertion, silent omission of mandatory lineage confirmation, or private “fast path” certificates are defects.
- Consistency never authorises inventing unpublished stages to “harmonise” product processes.

---

### CCLO-04 — Preserve implementation neutrality

**Definition.** Ensure that lifecycle coordination organises determination against published obligations independently of programming language, framework, datastore, CI vendor, cloud topology, or preferred Runtime A shape — so no stack becomes constitutional by proximity to lifecycle progress or closure.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who declares only one notebook brand “correct” has abandoned educational law for vendor preference.

**Manifestations:**

- CCL criteria speak to constitutional coordination obligations, not “must use Flask / SQLAlchemy / OpenAPI / GitHub Actions.”
- Equivalent lawful implementations under the same published law may both complete lawful lifecycles.
- Replacing a stack that previously yielded satisfying findings with another that still obeys published law does not itself create not-satisfaction — and does not create a certificate for either stack.
- “Our reference implementation does it this way” is never a substitute for a published specification or a published stage.

---

### CCLO-05 — Preserve explainability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material compliance lifecycle can honestly answer the mandatory explainability questions in `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md` — so students, developers, and auditors hear the same constitutional truth in appropriate vocabulary.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who cannot say *which obligation* was judged from *which findings* under *which lineage* at *which stage* is performing, not determining compliance.

**Manifestations:**

- Explainability obligations apply to all material lifecycles.
- Student / developer / auditor projections share one truth with different vocabulary.
- Lifecycle progress is never narrated as mastery, readiness, educational quality, new law, a verification re-run, or a certificate.
- Stack preference and race-dependent outcomes are defects to disclose, not mysteries to hide.

---

### CCLO-06 — Preserve auditability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material compliance lifecycle leaves a reconstructable trail: which constitutional obligations were evaluated, which verification findings were consumed, which traceability relationships were used, which lifecycle stage produced each determination, and which constitutional boundaries were preserved.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later say *why* an obligation was judged satisfied — or *at which stage* a determination arose — cannot be trusted. Silent green seals destroy student and auditor trust.

**Manifestations:**

- Audit records preserve constitutional references, not only technical CI logs.
- Consumed CT identities, CCM identities, CCL stage identities, verification finding identities, and conformance artefact identities are recorded when material.
- Missing CCL identity, missing obligation / specification refs, missing findings identity, missing stage-to-determination binding, or missing determination disposition is a defect.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing prior lawful lifecycle history to simplify the next report.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CCLO-01 conflicts with “helping” incomplete findings look lifecycle-complete | Lawful coordination honesty wins — not-satisfied / deferred / escalated / refuse-progress |
| CCLO-02 conflicts with race-dependent reviewer outcomes | Repeatability wins |
| CCLO-03 conflicts with private stage shortcuts | Consistency wins |
| CCLO-04 conflicts with privileging a reference stack as law | Implementation neutrality wins |
| CCLO-05 conflicts with opaque “trust us” seals | Explainability wins |
| CCLO-06 conflicts with log volume reduction that erases corpus refs or stage binding | Auditability wins |

No supporting objective authorises violating CCLO-01.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — Constitution / EIP integrity obligations, Programme VI–VIII authority preservation, WS1 conformance honesty, WS1 traceability provenance, WS2 verification honesty, and WS3 / MS001 compliance objectives for the lifecycle horizon.

---

## 5. Non-Objectives

The constitutional compliance lifecycle does **not** optimise for:

- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- CI throughput, flaky-test suppression, or coverage theatre as educational success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when specifications, findings, or lineage are incomplete;
- collapsing CCL stages into one mega-step that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics signals as substitutes for published constitutional obligations;
- certifying implementations, releases, or stacks as permanently lawful;
- treating documentation as optional commentary on whatever code already shipped;
- replacing MS001 conformance evaluation, MS002 lineage preservation, WS2 verification evaluation, or WS3 / MS001 compliance meaning;
- modifying implementation artefacts to manufacture passable obligation status;
- re-authoring verification findings under a compliance seal.

---

## 6. Closing Statement

> **A lifecycle that becomes “complete” by rewriting constitutional law, inventing lineage, rewriting findings, mutating artefacts, or issuing certificates is a failed educational system.  
> A lifecycle that coordinates only published compliance — with repeatability, consistency, neutrality, explainability, and auditability intact — is doing its only coordination job.**
