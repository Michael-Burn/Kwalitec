## Authority

This document is the constitutional overview of the **Constitutional Compliance Lifecycle Model** for Kwalitec.

It is subordinate to the Educational Constitution, Educational Interpretation Principles (EIP), Programme VI educational meaning corpora, Programme VII orchestration corpora, Programme VIII runtime constitutional corpora, the Constitutional Conformance Model (Programme IX / WS1 / MS001), the Constitutional Traceability Model (Programme IX / WS1 / MS002), the Constitutional Verification Model (Programme IX / WS2 / MS001 and successor lifecycle / completion corpora), and the Constitutional Compliance Model (Programme IX / WS3 / MS001). It governs **how constitutional compliance activities are lawfully organised from initiation through production of compliance determinations** — its objectives, recognised stages, hard boundaries, and explainability. It does not authorise implementation shortcuts that contradict the Constitution, and it does not create constitutional law, modify constitutional specifications, redefine constitutional meaning, replace constitutional authority, certify implementations, mint recommendations, invent educational state, rewrite WS3 / MS001 compliance types, re-author verification findings, replace verification, or substitute any lifecycle machinery for constitutional authority.

Authority order for constitutional compliance lifecycle:

> Constitution defines educational truth and curriculum primacy.  
> EIP defines evidence classification, continuity, explainability, claim honesty, and mutation rights.  
> Programme VI defines educational meaning and may publish learning / assessment warrants.  
> Programme VII defines orchestration flow, ownership, recommendations, context, and orchestration evidence artefacts.  
> Programme VIII defines how runtime implementations may lawfully execute published law (contracts, events, evidence, services, interfaces).  
> The Constitutional Conformance Model (Programme IX / WS1 / MS001) defines when an implementation may be considered constitutionally conformant to that published law.  
> The Constitutional Traceability Model (Programme IX / WS1 / MS002) defines how published constitutional specifications, implementation artefacts, and conformance findings remain lawfully related.  
> The Constitutional Verification Model (Programme IX / WS2) defines when and how an implementation may be constitutionally verified and organises verification through to findings.  
> The Constitutional Compliance Model (Programme IX / WS3 / MS001) defines when an implementation may be considered constitutionally compliant — whether published constitutional obligations have been satisfied.  
> **This Constitutional Compliance Lifecycle Model (Programme IX / Workstream 3 / MS002) defines how those compliance activities are lawfully organised from initiation through production of compliance determinations.**  
> Downstream compliance machinery, CI theatre, product surfaces, Twin, Adaptive, and narration must organise under these stages — never become constitutional producers by proximity to lifecycle progress.

## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not declare work “compliant” because a pipeline skipped findings preparation, invent lineage mid-assessment so obligations look linked, rewrite the syllabus so today’s seal passes, re-run verification under a compliance badge, or treat a green seal as a certificate that freezes the stack as law. After the Constitution and EIP have defined *educational law*, after Programmes VI–VIII have published *meaning, orchestration, and runtime law*, after WS1 has defined *conformance and traceability*, after WS2 has produced *verification findings*, and after WS3 / MS001 has defined *when and how compliance determination may occur*, the platform still needs one lifecycle answer:

> **“How are constitutional compliance activities lawfully organised from initiation through production of compliance determinations?”**

That answer must ensure compliance consumes **published constitutional specifications**, **verification findings**, and **established traceability**; coordinates determinations under recognised CCM-01…CCM-07 types; produces **compliance determinations**; and remains fully repeatable, consistent, implementation-neutral, auditable, and explainable — without certifying implementations, modifying constitutional specifications, redefining constitutional meaning, replacing verification, or becoming constitutional authority.

This document records that posture so every future compliance lifecycle (and successor) has a single constitutional reference for *how compliance work may be sequenced without becoming law or a certificate bureau*.

> **The Constitutional Compliance Lifecycle Model describes how compliance activities are lawfully coordinated from initiation through determinations.  
> It does not create educational meaning, invent ownership, mint tips, invent runtime behaviour, certify implementations, or become constitutional law.**

## 2. What the Constitutional Compliance Lifecycle Is

The **constitutional compliance lifecycle** is the binding organisation by which **compliance activities** may proceed through recognised stages — consuming published constitutional specifications, identifiable verification findings, and established traceability relationships — yielding **compliance determinations** and **audit records** that preserve constitutional authority, implementation neutrality, repeatability, consistency, auditability, and explainability — without becoming a source of constitutional law, a substitute for conformance or verification meaning, a rewrite of compliance types, or a certification of any implementation.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Published constitutional corpus** | Constitution, EIP, Programme VI / VII / VIII specifications that publish educational and execution law | Which obligations may the lifecycle organise determination against? |
| **Verification finding** | Lawful CV disposition (satisfied / not-satisfied / deferred / escalated) consumed as input — never rewritten by the lifecycle | Which verification findings may the lifecycle consume? |
| **Established traceability** | CT-01…CT-07 relationships among specs, artefacts, and/or findings already lawfully established | Which lineage may the lifecycle consume? |
| **Compliance type** | A recognised CCM-01…CCM-07 determination category from WS3 / MS001 | Which determination kinds may stages apply? |
| **Lifecycle stage** | A recognised CCL-01…CCL-07 organisational step | Which coordination step is active? |
| **Compliance determination** | Lawful disposition: satisfied / not-satisfied / deferred / escalated — never “new law” and never a certificate | Have the obligations been satisfied? |
| **Constitutional authority** | The exclusive right of published corpora to define educational and execution meaning | Who owns the standard? |
| **Implementation neutrality** | Lifecycle coordination is judged against law and findings, not against a preferred stack, vendor, or topology | Remains the stack replaceable? |
| **Repeatability** | Same published obligations, same consumed findings, same consumed relationships, same stage sequence → same disposition | Can the judgement be reproduced? |
| **Auditability** | Every material lifecycle leaves reconstructable constitutional traces | Can the judgement be reconstructed? |
| **Explainability** | Obligations, findings, relationships, stages, determinations, and boundaries are speakable | Can the judgement be narrated honestly? |

The constitutional compliance lifecycle is:

- **coordination-centred** — it organises compliance activities; it never authors constitutional obligations;
- **consumption-bound** — it consumes published specs, identifiable verification findings, and established CT relationships; it never invents them;
- **catalogue-closed** — only CCL-01…CCL-07 may be applied as constitutional compliance lifecycle stages;
- **type-faithful** — stages apply only published CCM-01…CCM-07 compliance types from WS3 / MS001; they never invent types;
- **verification-honest** — it consumes CV findings; it never becomes a second verification engine or rewrites findings to force satisfaction;
- **conformance-supporting** — it may consume CC relationships; it never replaces conformance meaning or certifies conformity;
- **authority-preserving** — published corpora remain the sole source of constitutional meaning;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by lifecycle law;
- **corpus-bound** — every material lifecycle cites identifiable published specifications and obligations;
- **audit-capable** — every material lifecycle leaves reconstructable constitutional traces;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which obligations were evaluated from which findings under which relationships and stages;
- **non-certifying** — a satisfied determination never becomes a certificate that freezes an implementation as lawfully permanent;
- **non-substitutive** — lifecycle machinery never becomes the constitutional standard for the next judgement;
- **non-mutating** — the lifecycle never modifies constitutional specifications or implementation artefacts under coordination pretext.

The constitutional compliance lifecycle is **not**:

- a second Educational Constitution, EIP, Programme VI / VII / VIII, WS1, WS2, or WS3 / MS001 amendment path;
- a licence to reinterpret coach meaning, ownership maps, tip warrants, runtime contracts, conformance criteria, traceability meaning, verification meaning, or compliance types in lifecycle prose;
- a testing framework, CI/CD pipeline, GitHub Action, linter policy, or OpenAPI checker;
- a claim that “lifecycle complete” guarantees learning, mastery, exam readiness, product success, perpetual conformity, perpetual verification, or certification;
- a substitute for MS001 conformance evaluation — CCL coordinates compliance; CC defines when conformity may be claimed;
- a substitute for MS002 traceability — CT preserves relationships; CCL consumes them;
- a substitute for WS2 verification — CV evaluates evidence against requirements; CCL consumes findings and never re-runs verification;
- a substitute for WS3 / MS001 compliance meaning — CCM defines determination kinds; CCL sequences when they occur;
- a substitute for Programme VIII evidence validation (EV-xx) — EV validates educational evidence eligibility; CCL coordinates *obligation-status* determination;
- a substitute for Educational Validation Framework quality review — quality release remains a distinct horizon;
- a certification authority for implementations, stacks, vendors, or releases;
- a licence to modify implementation artefacts “so compliance can proceed.”

## 3. Lifecycle Posture (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material constitutional compliance lifecycle travels through seven constitutional stages. These are **normative coordination obligations**, not an implementation architecture and not a CI job graph.

```
CCL-01 Compliance Initiation
        │
        ▼
CCL-02 Findings Preparation
        │
        ▼
CCL-03 Traceability Confirmation
        │
        ▼
CCL-04 Constitutional Assessment
        │
        ├── not-satisfied / deferred / escalated (lawful determination — law unchanged; no certificate)
        │
        └── satisfied (or other lawful disposition) →
                │
                ▼
CCL-05 Determination Production
        │
        ▼
CCL-06 Determination Recording
        │
        ▼
CCL-07 Compliance Closure
        │
        ▼
Explain obligations / findings / relationships / stages / determinations / boundaries
```

| Stage | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **CCL-01 Initiation** | Open a compliance concern against named published obligations and scope | Scope is unpublished custom, tribal knowledge, or “how main works today” |
| **CCL-02 Findings preparation** | Identify and prepare named verification findings for determination | Findings identity is missing, invented, rewritten, or would require re-running verification as compliance |
| **CCL-03 Traceability confirmation** | Confirm established CT relationships required for the concern | Lineage would be invented, forged, or treated as optional theatre |
| **CCL-04 Constitutional assessment** | Apply CCM-01…CCM-07 against prepared findings under published obligations | Assessment would invent law, rewrite specs, redefine meaning, privilege a stack as law, re-author findings, or certify an implementation |
| **CCL-05 Determination production** | Emit satisfied / not-satisfied / deferred / escalated under published criteria | Determinations would become amendments, certificates, or educational quality grades |
| **CCL-06 Determination recording** | Preserve audit-speakable trails of obligations, findings, relationships, stages, and determinations | Path would erase history, forge provenance, or treat records as new law |
| **CCL-07 Closure** | Close the lifecycle honestly when duties are exhausted | Closure would certify an implementation, freeze a stack, replace verification, or pretend incomplete work is done |

> **The lifecycle never “best-effort invents” an eighth stage called law-making, reinterpretation, certification, stack canonisation, artefact mutation, or verification re-authorship.**

## 6. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Compliance Lifecycle Model exists so that:

1. **Law remains upstream of coordination** — stages organise determination against published obligations; progress does not quietly become the specifications.
2. **Verification remains honest** — preparation identifies named findings; it does not invent or rewrite them to force satisfaction.
3. **Traceability remains honest** — the lifecycle consumes established lineage; it does not invent links so assessment can proceed.
4. **Compliance remains sequenced** — CCM determinations occur under CCL-04 after lawful initiation, findings preparation, and lineage confirmation — not as random green theatre.
5. **Verification remains supported, not replaced** — lifecycle determinations consume findings without becoming a second verification engine or a certificate mill.
6. **Conformance remains supported, not replaced** — lifecycle determinations speak to obligation status without becoming a second conformity engine.
7. **Authority remains honest** — no team may treat a closed lifecycle as the new constitutional source.
8. **Implementations remain replaceable** — lifecycle law does not freeze Flask, SQLAlchemy, a particular API shape, or a CI vendor as law.
9. **Repeatability remains honest** — the same published obligations, consumed findings, consumed relationships, and stage sequence yield the same disposition.
10. **Audit remains possible** — every material lifecycle can be reconstructed against named obligations, findings, relationships, stages, and determinations.
11. **Explainability remains intact** — lifecycle speech describes coordination of obligation determination; it does not redefine what learning means.
12. **Students remain protected** — product theatre cannot launder unsatisfied educational obligations as “lifecycle approved.”
