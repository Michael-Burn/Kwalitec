## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “pipeline finished,” “stages skipped for speed,” or “badge shown.” The tutor optimises **honest organisation of verification work**: only a repeatable, consistent, implementation-neutral, explainable, and auditable sequence — consuming published law, named evidence, and established lineage — may support lawful verification findings.

These objectives bind every constitutional verification lifecycle and every successor coordination machinery that claims constitutional verification lifecycle meaning.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### CVLO-01 — Coordinate verification lawfully

**Definition.** Ensure that every material verification lifecycle organises activities through recognised CVL-01…CVL-07 stages — consuming published constitutional specifications, named implementation evidence, and established traceability relationships — so that CV-01…CV-07 evaluations occur only after lawful initiation, evidence preparation, and lineage validation, and that findings are produced and recorded without becoming law or certificates.

**Includes:**

- Binding every material lifecycle to identifiable published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII specifications.
- Consuming established CT relationships and identifiable conformance artefacts as inputs where material.
- Applying only published CV-01…CV-07 types during CVL-04.
- Preferring lawful not-satisfied / deferred / escalated / refuse-progress outcomes over rewriting law, inventing lineage, or mutating artefacts so the lifecycle “completes.”
- Keeping lifecycle progress distinct from educational quality, mastery, readiness, product success, conformity certificates, or constitutional amendment.
- Preserving the Model’s posture: stages coordinate evaluation; they do not become law or certificates.

**Excludes:**

- Treating green CI, coverage percentages, linter scores, Twin estimates, Adaptive scores, or UI ticks as lifecycle fulfilment.
- Soft-amending specifications, inventing unpublished customs, inventing lineage, mutating artefacts, or privileging “how main works” to force progress.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for lawful coordination.
- Declaring “close enough” closure without documenting a corpus amendment.
- Issuing certificates that freeze an implementation as permanently verified because the lifecycle closed.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software pretends unfinished or dishonest verification work is fine because the product needs a finished badge. Coordination honesty is care; lifecycle theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CVLO-02 — Preserve repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that the same published constitutional specifications, the same implementation evidence facts, the same established traceability relationships, the same published CV criteria, and the same CVL stage sequence yield the same verification disposition (satisfied / not-satisfied / deferred / escalated) and the same lawful stage outcomes — absent a published non-deterministic exception (none are granted by this Model for constitutional meaning or requirement satisfaction).

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who treats the same evidence as satisfying or not depending on which reviewer woke first — or skips stages randomly — is not coaching; they are gambling.

**Manifestations:**

- Stage transitions and outcomes are corpus-bound and reproducible.
- Parallel CI runners, sharding, and cache races must not change constitutional disposition or invent stage skips.
- Explicit multi-type CV sets at CVL-04 use published conjunction / published stop rules, not random reviewer races.
- “Eventually consistent lifecycle meaning” is a constitutional defect.
- Invented or unstable lineage inputs are hard stops, not soft variables.

---

### CVLO-03 — Preserve consistency

**Definition.** Ensure that lifecycle organisation applies the same published stage meanings, stop rules, and finding dispositions across concerns, reviewers, and successors — so “verification lifecycle” does not mean one thing on Monday and another on Tuesday for equivalent published inputs.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who invents a private stage order for convenience destroys institutional trust even if each private path looks tidy.

**Manifestations:**

- Only CVL-01…CVL-07 may organise constitutional verification lifecycles.
- Stage purpose, inputs, outputs, and permitted / prohibited activities remain as published in `LIFECYCLE_STAGES.md`.
- Equivalent concerns under the same published law receive equivalent stage treatment.
- Ad hoc stage insertion, silent omission of mandatory lineage validation, or private “fast path” certificates are defects.
- Consistency never authorises inventing unpublished stages to “harmonise” product processes.

---

### CVLO-04 — Preserve implementation neutrality

**Definition.** Ensure that lifecycle coordination organises evaluation against published law independently of programming language, framework, datastore, CI vendor, cloud topology, or preferred Runtime A shape — so no stack becomes constitutional by proximity to lifecycle progress or closure.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who declares only one notebook brand “correct” has abandoned educational law for vendor preference.

**Manifestations:**

- CVL criteria speak to constitutional coordination obligations, not “must use Flask / SQLAlchemy / OpenAPI / GitHub Actions.”
- Equivalent lawful implementations under the same published law may both complete lawful lifecycles.
- Replacing a stack that previously yielded satisfying evidence with another that still obeys published law does not itself create not-satisfaction — and does not create a certificate for either stack.
- “Our reference implementation does it this way” is never a substitute for a published specification or a published stage.

---

### CVLO-05 — Preserve explainability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material verification lifecycle can honestly answer the mandatory explainability questions in `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md` — so students, developers, and auditors hear the same constitutional truth in appropriate vocabulary.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who cannot say *which rule* was checked against *which evidence* under *which lineage* at *which stage* is performing, not verifying.

**Manifestations:**

- Explainability obligations apply to all material lifecycles.
- Student / developer / auditor projections share one truth with different vocabulary.
- Lifecycle progress is never narrated as mastery, readiness, educational quality, new law, or a certificate.
- Stack preference and race-dependent outcomes are defects to disclose, not mysteries to hide.

---

### CVLO-06 — Preserve auditability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material verification lifecycle leaves a reconstructable trail: which specifications were evaluated, which implementation evidence was consumed, which traceability relationships were used, which lifecycle stage produced each finding, and which constitutional boundaries were preserved.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later say *why* evidence was accepted — or *at which stage* a finding arose — cannot be trusted. Silent green badges destroy student and auditor trust.

**Manifestations:**

- Audit records preserve constitutional references, not only technical CI logs.
- Consumed CT identities, CV identities, CVL stage identities, and conformance artefact identities are recorded when material.
- Missing CVL identity, missing specification refs, missing evidence identity, missing stage-to-finding binding, or missing finding disposition is a defect.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing prior lawful lifecycle history to simplify the next report.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CVLO-01 conflicts with “helping” incomplete evidence look lifecycle-complete | Lawful coordination honesty wins — not-satisfied / deferred / escalated / refuse-progress |
| CVLO-02 conflicts with race-dependent reviewer outcomes | Repeatability wins |
| CVLO-03 conflicts with private stage shortcuts | Consistency wins |
| CVLO-04 conflicts with privileging a reference stack as law | Implementation neutrality wins |
| CVLO-05 conflicts with opaque “trust us” badges | Explainability wins |
| CVLO-06 conflicts with log volume reduction that erases corpus refs or stage binding | Auditability wins |

No supporting objective authorises violating CVLO-01.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — Constitution / EIP integrity obligations, Programme VI–VIII authority preservation, WS1 conformance honesty, WS1 traceability provenance, and WS2 / MS001 verification objectives for the lifecycle horizon.

---

## 5. Non-Objectives

The constitutional verification lifecycle does **not** optimise for:

- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- CI throughput, flaky-test suppression, or coverage theatre as educational success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when specifications or lineage are incomplete;
- collapsing CVL stages into one mega-step that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics signals as substitutes for published constitutional specifications;
- certifying implementations, releases, or stacks as permanently lawful;
- treating documentation as optional commentary on whatever code already shipped;
- replacing MS001 conformance evaluation, MS002 lineage preservation, or WS2 / MS001 verification meaning;
- modifying implementation artefacts to manufacture passable evidence.

---

## 6. Closing Statement

> **A lifecycle that becomes “complete” by rewriting constitutional law, inventing lineage, mutating artefacts, or issuing certificates is a failed educational system.  
> A lifecycle that coordinates only published verification — with repeatability, consistency, neutrality, explainability, and auditability intact — is doing its only coordination job.**
