## Authority

This document is the constitutional overview of the **Constitutional Verification Lifecycle Model** for Kwalitec.

It is subordinate to the Educational Constitution, Educational Interpretation Principles (EIP), Programme VI educational meaning corpora, Programme VII orchestration corpora, Programme VIII runtime constitutional corpora, the Constitutional Conformance Model (Programme IX / WS1 / MS001), the Constitutional Traceability Model (Programme IX / WS1 / MS002), and the Constitutional Verification Model (Programme IX / WS2 / MS001). It governs **how constitutional verification activities are lawfully organised from initiation through production of verification findings** — its objectives, recognised stages, hard boundaries, and explainability. It does not authorise implementation shortcuts that contradict the Constitution, and it does not create constitutional law, modify constitutional specifications, redefine constitutional meaning, replace constitutional authority, certify implementations, mint recommendations, invent educational state, rewrite WS2 / MS001 verification types, or substitute any lifecycle machinery for constitutional authority.

Authority order for constitutional verification lifecycle:

> Constitution defines educational truth and curriculum primacy.  
> EIP defines evidence classification, continuity, explainability, claim honesty, and mutation rights.  
> Programme VI defines educational meaning and may publish learning / assessment warrants.  
> Programme VII defines orchestration flow, ownership, recommendations, context, and orchestration evidence artefacts.  
> Programme VIII defines how runtime implementations may lawfully execute published law (contracts, events, evidence, services, interfaces).  
> The Constitutional Conformance Model (Programme IX / WS1 / MS001) defines when an implementation may be considered constitutionally conformant to that published law.  
> The Constitutional Traceability Model (Programme IX / WS1 / MS002) defines how published constitutional specifications, implementation artefacts, and conformance findings remain lawfully related.  
> The Constitutional Verification Model (Programme IX / WS2 / MS001) defines when and how an implementation may be constitutionally verified against published specifications using established traceability.  
> **This Constitutional Verification Lifecycle Model (Programme IX / Workstream 2 / MS002) defines how those verification activities are lawfully organised from initiation through production of verification findings.**  
> Downstream verification machinery, CI theatre, product surfaces, Twin, Adaptive, and narration must organise under these stages — never become constitutional producers by proximity to lifecycle progress.

## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not declare work “verified” because a pipeline skipped preparation, invent lineage mid-check so evidence looks linked, rewrite the syllabus so today’s artefact passes, or treat a green badge as a certificate that freezes the stack as law. After the Constitution and EIP have defined *educational law*, after Programmes VI–VIII have published *meaning, orchestration, and runtime law*, after WS1 has defined *conformance and traceability*, and after WS2 / MS001 has defined *when and how verification may occur*, the platform still needs one lifecycle answer:

> **“How are constitutional verification activities lawfully organised from initiation through production of verification findings?”**

That answer must ensure verification consumes **published constitutional specifications**, **implementation evidence**, and **established traceability**; coordinates assessments under recognised CV-01…CV-07 types; produces **verification findings**; and remains fully repeatable, consistent, implementation-neutral, auditable, and explainable — without certifying implementations, modifying constitutional specifications, redefining constitutional meaning, or becoming constitutional authority.

This document records that posture so every future verification lifecycle (and successor) has a single constitutional reference for *how verification work may be sequenced without becoming law or a certificate bureau*.

> **The Constitutional Verification Lifecycle Model describes how verification activities are lawfully coordinated from initiation through findings.  
> It does not create educational meaning, invent ownership, mint tips, invent runtime behaviour, certify implementations, or become constitutional law.**

## 2. What the Constitutional Verification Lifecycle Is

The **constitutional verification lifecycle** is the binding organisation by which **verification activities** may proceed through recognised stages — consuming published constitutional specifications, named implementation evidence, and established traceability relationships — yielding **verification findings** and **audit records** that preserve constitutional authority, implementation neutrality, repeatability, consistency, auditability, and explainability — without becoming a source of constitutional law, a substitute for conformance meaning, a rewrite of verification types, or a certification of any implementation.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Published constitutional corpus** | Constitution, EIP, Programme VI / VII / VIII specifications that publish educational and execution law | Which requirements may the lifecycle organise evaluation against? |
| **Implementation evidence** | Named artefacts, behaviours, trails, and records claimed to show that an implementation satisfies those requirements | Which evidence may the lifecycle consume? |
| **Established traceability** | CT-01…CT-07 relationships among specs, artefacts, and/or findings already lawfully established | Which lineage may the lifecycle consume? |
| **Verification type** | A recognised CV-01…CV-07 evaluation category from WS2 / MS001 | Which assessment kinds may stages apply? |
| **Lifecycle stage** | A recognised CVL-01…CVL-07 organisational step | Which coordination step is active? |
| **Verification finding** | Lawful disposition: satisfied / not-satisfied / deferred / escalated — never “new law” and never a certificate | Did the evidence satisfy published requirements? |
| **Constitutional authority** | The exclusive right of published corpora to define educational and execution meaning | Who owns the standard? |
| **Implementation neutrality** | Lifecycle coordination is judged against law and evidence, not against a preferred stack, vendor, or topology | Remains the stack replaceable? |
| **Repeatability** | Same published requirements, same evidence facts, same consumed relationships, same stage sequence → same disposition | Can the judgement be reproduced? |
| **Auditability** | Every material lifecycle leaves reconstructable constitutional traces | Can the judgement be reconstructed? |
| **Explainability** | Specs, evidence, relationships, stages, findings, and boundaries are speakable | Can the judgement be narrated honestly? |

The constitutional verification lifecycle is:

- **coordination-centred** — it organises verification activities; it never authors constitutional requirements;
- **consumption-bound** — it consumes published specs, named evidence, and established CT relationships; it never invents them;
- **catalogue-closed** — only CVL-01…CVL-07 may be applied as constitutional verification lifecycle stages;
- **type-faithful** — stages apply only published CV-01…CV-07 verification types from WS2 / MS001; they never invent types;
- **conformance-supporting** — it supports WS1 / MS001 fidelity honesty; it never replaces conformance meaning or certifies conformity;
- **authority-preserving** — published corpora remain the sole source of constitutional meaning;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by lifecycle law;
- **corpus-bound** — every material lifecycle cites identifiable published specifications;
- **audit-capable** — every material lifecycle leaves reconstructable constitutional traces;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which law was evaluated against which evidence under which relationships and stages;
- **non-certifying** — a satisfied finding never becomes a certificate that freezes an implementation as lawfully permanent;
- **non-substitutive** — lifecycle machinery never becomes the constitutional standard for the next judgement;
- **non-mutating** — the lifecycle never modifies constitutional specifications or implementation artefacts under coordination pretext.

The constitutional verification lifecycle is **not**:

- a second Educational Constitution, EIP, Programme VI / VII / VIII, WS1, or WS2 / MS001 amendment path;
- a licence to reinterpret coach meaning, ownership maps, tip warrants, runtime contracts, conformance criteria, traceability meaning, or verification types in lifecycle prose;
- a testing framework, CI/CD pipeline, GitHub Action, linter policy, or OpenAPI checker;
- a claim that “lifecycle complete” guarantees learning, mastery, exam readiness, product success, perpetual conformity, or certification;
- a substitute for MS001 conformance evaluation — CVL coordinates verification; CC defines when conformity may be claimed;
- a substitute for MS002 traceability — CT preserves relationships; CVL consumes them;
- a substitute for WS2 / MS001 verification meaning — CV defines assessment kinds; CVL sequences when they occur;
- a substitute for Programme VIII evidence validation (EV-xx) — EV validates educational evidence eligibility; CVL coordinates *implementation* evidence verification;
- a substitute for Educational Validation Framework quality review — quality release remains a distinct horizon;
- a certification authority for implementations, stacks, vendors, or releases;
- a licence to modify implementation artefacts “so verification can proceed.”

## 3. Lifecycle Posture (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material constitutional verification lifecycle travels through seven constitutional stages. These are **normative coordination obligations**, not an implementation architecture and not a CI job graph.

```
CVL-01 Verification Initiation
        │
        ▼
CVL-02 Evidence Preparation
        │
        ▼
CVL-03 Traceability Validation
        │
        ▼
CVL-04 Constitutional Evaluation
        │
        ├── not-satisfied / deferred / escalated (lawful finding — law unchanged; no certificate)
        │
        └── satisfied (or other lawful disposition) →
                │
                ▼
CVL-05 Findings Production
        │
        ▼
CVL-06 Findings Recording
        │
        ▼
CVL-07 Verification Closure
        │
        ▼
Explain specs / evidence / relationships / stages / findings / boundaries
```

| Stage | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **CVL-01 Initiation** | Open a verification concern against named published specifications and scope | Scope is unpublished custom, tribal knowledge, or “how main works today” |
| **CVL-02 Evidence preparation** | Identify and prepare named implementation evidence for evaluation | Evidence identity is missing, invented, or would require mutating artefacts |
| **CVL-03 Traceability validation** | Confirm established CT relationships required for the concern | Lineage would be invented, forged, or treated as optional theatre |
| **CVL-04 Constitutional evaluation** | Apply CV-01…CV-07 against prepared evidence under published requirements | Evaluation would invent law, rewrite specs, redefine meaning, privilege a stack as law, or certify an implementation |
| **CVL-05 Findings production** | Emit satisfied / not-satisfied / deferred / escalated under published criteria | Findings would become amendments, certificates, or educational quality grades |
| **CVL-06 Findings recording** | Preserve audit-speakable trails of specs, evidence, relationships, stages, and findings | Path would erase history, forge provenance, or treat records as new law |
| **CVL-07 Closure** | Close the lifecycle honestly when duties are exhausted | Closure would certify an implementation, freeze a stack, or pretend incomplete work is done |

> **The lifecycle never “best-effort invents” an eighth stage called law-making, reinterpretation, certification, stack canonisation, or artefact mutation.**

- invent new constitutional verification types outside CV-01…CV-07 (owned by WS2 / MS001);

## 6. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Verification Lifecycle Model exists so that:

1. **Law remains upstream of coordination** — stages organise evaluation against published specifications; progress does not quietly become the specifications.
2. **Evidence remains honest** — preparation identifies named evidence; it does not mutate artefacts to force satisfaction.
3. **Traceability remains honest** — the lifecycle consumes established lineage; it does not invent links so evaluation can proceed.
4. **Verification remains sequenced** — CV assessments occur under CVL-04 after lawful initiation, evidence preparation, and lineage validation — not as random green theatre.
5. **Conformance remains supported, not replaced** — lifecycle findings strengthen fidelity honesty without becoming a second conformity engine or a certificate mill.
6. **Authority remains honest** — no team may treat a closed lifecycle as the new constitutional source.
7. **Implementations remain replaceable** — lifecycle law does not freeze Flask, SQLAlchemy, a particular API shape, or a CI vendor as law.
8. **Repeatability remains honest** — the same published requirements, evidence facts, consumed relationships, and stage sequence yield the same disposition.
9. **Audit remains possible** — every material lifecycle can be reconstructed against named specs, evidence, relationships, stages, and findings.
10. **Explainability remains intact** — lifecycle speech describes coordination of evidence evaluation; it does not redefine what learning means.
11. **Students remain protected** — product theatre cannot launder non-satisfying educational behaviour as “lifecycle approved.”
