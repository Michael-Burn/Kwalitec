## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor needs bright conditions for saying “this constitutional verification work is finished” — and equally bright refusals when it is not. After WS2 / MS001 has named when and how verification may occur and WS2 / MS002 has named how verification activities are organised, Programme IX Workstream 2 still needs one fulfilment checklist:

> **What must be true before constitutional speech may affirm that the required verification lifecycle has been fully executed and verification findings have been lawfully produced and recorded?**

These criteria answer that question. They are **normative constitutional conditions**, not database predicates, CI job statuses, ticket states, coverage scores, or API status codes.

---

## 2. Criterion Catalogue

All material affirmed completions require **CVC-01 through CVC-05** to hold. Failure of any criterion ⇒ **not yet** or **refuse close**. Do not invent a partial “mostly complete” compliance, certification, approval, educational, or constitutional status.

| ID | Criterion | Primary question |
|----|-----------|------------------|
| **CVC-01** | Required lifecycle stages completed | Were required CVL-01…CVL-07 stages completed for the concern? |
| **CVC-02** | Required evidence evaluated | Was required named implementation evidence evaluated under published requirements? |
| **CVC-03** | Required findings produced | Were required verification findings produced under published CV criteria? |
| **CVC-04** | Audit records preserved | Do reconstructable constitutional audit records exist for the completed verification? |
| **CVC-05** | Verification formally closed | Was the verification concern formally closed under published CVL-07 / boundary honesty? |

Optional supporting honesty checks (do not replace CVC-01…CVC-05):

| ID | Check | Primary question |
|----|-------|------------------|
| **CVC-06** | Catalogue honesty | Were stages and assessments typed under published CVL-01…CVL-07 and CV-01…CV-07 only? |
| **CVC-07** | Continuity honesty | Was prior lawful verification history preserved (EIP-005)? |
| **CVC-08** | Non-claim honesty | Does the judgement refuse compliance / certification / approval / educational / amendment overclaim? |
| **CVC-09** | Neutrality honesty | Does the judgement refuse stack / vendor canonisation as completion law? |
| **CVC-10** | Repeatability honesty | Would the same published inputs yield the same completion disposition? |

## 3. Criterion Definitions

### CVC-01 — Required lifecycle stages completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every lifecycle stage required by the concern’s published WS2 / MS002 scope has been executed — or that a published refuse / defer / escalate disposition exhausted remaining *verification* duties for that stage set with honest progression to recording and closure of what occurred.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Identifiable verification concern opened under WS2 / MS001–MS002.
- Required CVL-01…CVL-07 stage identities for the concern scope.
- Stage traces / disposition records for each required stage (including lawful early stops).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed stage-fulfilment record naming which required stages completed.
- Or a lawful stop record naming which stages were refused / deferred / escalated and why remaining evaluation duties are exhausted.

**Completion conditions.**

- Required stages map to published CVL-01…CVL-07 only.
- Mandatory initiation, evidence identity, lineage validation, evaluation, findings, recording, and closure obligations for the concern are not silently omitted.
- Lawful early stops still leave honest stage outcomes; silent disappearance of a concern is a defect.
- Refusal of “pipeline skipped prep” or “we know it ran” without stage identity as completion.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That stage completion means the implementation is constitutionally compliant.
- That stage completion certifies or approves the implementation.
- That skipped stages may be invented or implied from CI theatre.
- That constitutional specifications were amended by progressing stages.
- That the learner understood, progressed, or mastered anything.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who declares marking “finished” without confirming each required marking step occurred has not finished lawful care — even if the desk looks tidy.

---

### CVC-02 — Required evidence evaluated

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every named implementation evidence subject required by the concern’s published scope was prepared and evaluated against identifiable published constitutional specifications under applicable CV-01…CV-07 types — or that a published refuse disposition exhausted remaining *evaluation* duties when required evidence could not be lawfully assessed.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Named implementation evidence identities in scope.
- Published constitutional specification identities under evaluation.
- Established CT relationships consumed (never invented) for the concern.
- Evaluation dispositions under applicable CV types.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed evidence-evaluation record naming which evidence was assessed against which published requirements.
- Or a lawful refuse / defer / escalate record naming which required evidence remains unevaluated and why.

**Completion conditions.**

- Evidence subjects are named (not “the backend,” “the service,” or vendor labels alone).
- Evaluation cites published constitutional specifications, not unpublished customs.
- Traceability consumed for evaluation was established under WS1 / MS002 — invented lineage is a hard stop.
- Refusal of coverage percentages, lint scores, or Twin confidence as substitutes for named evidence evaluation.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That evaluated evidence is therefore correct, compliant, certified, or approved.
- That a particular stack, language, or framework is privileged as law.
- That product quality or educational quality was judged.
- That constitutional specifications were modified to match the evidence.
- That Programme VIII EV eligibility equals verification evaluation fulfilment.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who claims “the script was marked” without naming which answers were assessed against which syllabus clauses has performed marking theatre, not evaluation fulfilment.

---

### CVC-03 — Required findings produced

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every verification finding required by the concern’s published scope has been lawfully produced under published CV criteria — including satisfied, not-satisfied, deferred, and escalated dispositions — without rewriting those dispositions into compliance, certification, or approval.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Evaluation dispositions under applicable CV-01…CV-07 types (CVL-04 outputs).
- Published findings-production rules under WS2 / MS001–MS002 (especially CVL-05).
- Boundary confirmations that findings do not invent law or certificates.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Identifiable verification finding identities with lawful dispositions.
- Or a lawful stop record naming why required findings could not be produced without invention.

**Completion conditions.**

- Findings cite published constitutional references and named evidence.
- Dispositions remain within published vocabulary (satisfied / not-satisfied / deferred / escalated).
- “Assessment happened somewhere” without named finding identity is not completion.
- Lawful not-satisfied / deferred / escalated findings still satisfy *production* when they are the honest outcome — completeness of *production* is not completeness of *satisfaction*.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That producing a finding upgrades the implementation to compliant.
- That a satisfied finding is a certificate or approval.
- That a not-satisfied finding may be silently erased so close can look green.
- That MS001 conformance evaluation was replaced, skipped, or certified by findings production.
- That Educational Validation Framework quality review is complete.

**Tutor rationale.** Recording a hard mark is not endorsing the candidate — but omitting a required mark while claiming the session finished is dishonest. Completeness of *findings production* is not completeness of *compliance*.

---

### CVC-04 — Audit records preserved

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that reconstructable constitutional audit records exist for the completed verification: which specifications were evaluated, which evidence was assessed, which lifecycle stages were completed, which findings were produced, and which boundaries remained intact — sufficient for student / developer / auditor reconstruction under `COMPLETION_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Stage traces (CVL-01…CVL-07).
- Evaluation and findings records.
- Boundary preservation confirmations.
- Continuity obligations under EIP-005.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Audit-speakable records aligned with CVCQ-01…CVCQ-05.
- Honest refuse / defer / escalate records when fulfilment was not affirmed.

**Completion conditions.**

- Material completion judgements leave records that cite published constitutional references, not only technical job logs or CI annotations.
- Missing CV / CVL identity, missing specification refs, missing evidence identity, missing finding identity, or missing boundary honesty for a material affirmed close is a defect.
- Continuity forbids erasing prior lawful verification trails to make close look tidy.
- Refusal of log volume alone as a substitute for constitutional content.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That a particular database schema, log shipper, CI annotation format, or UI widget is required.
- That audit completeness equals compliance, certification, approval, or educational success.
- That volume of logs substitutes for constitutional content.
- That Twin / Adaptive / analytics origin stories may replace published constitutional identity.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later reconstruct why verification was declared finished cannot be trusted — and neither can a platform that celebrates mute “complete” flags.

---

### CVC-05 — Verification formally closed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that the verification concern was formally closed under published CVL-07 obligations and boundary honesty — exhausting remaining *verification lifecycle* duties without inventing a certificate, compliance disposition, approval, educational success, or constitutional amendment.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Recorded findings and audit records (CVC-03 / CVC-04).
- CVL-07 closure rules and `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md` hard stops.
- Explicit non-claim confirmations (compliance / certification / approval / educational success / law change).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Formal close disposition: affirmed complete / not yet / refuse close / defer / escalate.
- Boundary preservation record confirming forbidden overclaims did not occur.

**Completion conditions.**

- Closure occurs only after required stages, evidence evaluation, findings, and audit obligations for the concern are satisfied — or after a published stop exhausts remaining verification duties with honest recording.
- Closure speech answers CVCQ-01…CVCQ-05 or explicitly refuses mute theatre.
- Closure never silently upgrades findings dispositions.
- Refusal of ticket closure, PR merge, or pipeline greens alone as formal constitutional close.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That formal close means the implementation is constitutionally compliant.
- That formal close certifies the implementation, release, stack, or vendor.
- That formal close approves the implementation for production or educational use as constitutional law.
- That formal close amends constitutional specifications or transfers constitutional authority.
- That formal close means the student succeeded or is exam-ready.

**Tutor rationale.** Closing the marking folder is care only when the required steps, marks, and records exist — and only when nobody pretends the close is a pass certificate for the syllabus or the candidate.

## 4. Criterion Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CVC-01 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CVC-02 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CVC-03 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CVC-04 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — mute complete badges are defects |
| CVC-05 fails | Affirmation is unlawful even if earlier stages appear present |
| Optional CVC-06…CVC-10 fail | Treat as honesty defects; do not soft-pass CVC-01…CVC-05 |
| Any criterion “passes” only by implying compliance / certification / approval / educational success / law change | The implied overclaim voids lawful affirmation |

All of CVC-01…CVC-05 are **conjunctive** for material affirmed completion. There is no lawful “four of five” educational, fidelity, certification, or constitutional status.

---

## 5. Lawful Stop Dispositions

Not every incomplete concern is a defect. Published WS2 / MS002 refuse / defer / escalate outcomes may **exhaust remaining verification duties** for a concern without affirming lifecycle fulfilment as “complete” in the celebratory sense — while still requiring honest recording and formal close of what occurred.

| Disposition | Constitutional meaning | May affirm CVC completion? |
|-------------|------------------------|----------------------------|
| **Affirm complete** | CVC-01…CVC-05 hold for the concern | Yes |
| **Not yet** | Required stages, evidence evaluation, findings, or records remain outstanding | No |
| **Refuse close** | Affirmation would invent stages / findings, erase audit trails, or overclaim | No |
| **Defer** | Published wait for upstream corpus / evidence / lineage identity | No (unless published law says remaining verification duties are exhausted) |
| **Escalate** | Published stop requiring human / corpus governance attention | No (unless published law says remaining verification duties are exhausted) |

A lawful stop that exhausts verification duties is **honest unfinished fulfilment or honest refusal** — never a stealth compliance certificate, product certification, implementation approval, educational success, or constitutional amendment.

---

## 6. Non-Criteria

The following are **not** constitutional completion criteria:

- CI pipeline greens, flaky-test suppression, or job exit codes;
- ticket closure, PR merge, or documentation checklist ticks alone;
- coverage percentages, lint scores, or elapsed time;
- Twin / Adaptive / Experience / Mission confidence signals;
- UI celebration states or analytics milestones;
- “looks verified enough for the demo”;
- MS001 conformant disposition (that is fidelity law, not CVC);
- WS1 / MS003 traceability completion (CTC) alone (orthogonal lineage fulfilment);
- Educational Validation Framework quality release (orthogonal);
- any implication that constitutional specifications changed because verification closed.

---

## 7. Closing Statement

> **Constitutional verification is complete only when required lifecycle stages are completed, required evidence is evaluated, required findings are produced, audit records are preserved, and verification is formally closed.  
> Satisfying CVC-01…CVC-05 never means the implementation is compliant, certified, approved, educationally successful, or that constitutional law has changed.**
