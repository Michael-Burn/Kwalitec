## 1. Purpose

Verification machinery that silently turns “the verification lifecycle finished” into “implementation is compliant,” “release is certified,” “implementation is approved,” “student succeeded,” or “the Constitution now says X” destroys student trust and educational integrity.

This document draws a bright line between **lawful constitutional verification completion** (Programme IX / WS2 / MS003) and **compliance evaluation / certification / approval / educational judgement / constitutional authorship** owned elsewhere.

---

## 2. Boundary Principles

1. **Confirm lifecycle fulfilment, do not legislate.** Completion affirms fulfilment of published verification lifecycle duties; it does not create constitutional law.
2. **Verification only.** Compliance, certification, approval, and educational success are never inferred from close.
3. **Not conformance evaluation.** WS1 / MS001 owns fidelity disposition.
4. **Not certification.** Completion never certifies implementations, releases, stacks, or vendors.
5. **Not approval.** Completion never approves implementations for production, delivery, or educational use as constitutional law.
6. **Not constitutional amendment.** Close never patches law or modifies constitutional authority.
7. **Exactly as published.** Close never redefines constitutional specifications or meaning.
8. **Published criteria only.** Pipelines, tickets, coverage, and elapsed time are not fulfilment law.
9. **Published records only.** Audit continuity is constitutional, not free-form log theatre.
10. **Reference findings honestly.** Produced findings may be cited; producing them never upgrades disposition to compliant / certified / approved.
11. **Authority non-transfer.** Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard after close.
12. **Meaning non-reinterpretation.** Programme VI questions and EIP claim classes survive close intact.
13. **Implementation neutrality.** No stack becomes law by proximity to a complete flag.
14. **Verified-complete ≠ quality.** Completion never grades learning, mastery, readiness, or product success.
15. **Verified-complete ≠ compliant.** Completion never replaces MS001 evaluation.
16. **Explain the stop.** Students, developers, and auditors should hear when close must be refused.
17. **No emergency exemption.** Deadlines and demos never mint completion power or amendment power.
18. **Repeatability survives close.** Close must not invent contradictory dispositions from identical published inputs.

## 3. What Verification Completion May Do (Lawful)

| Lawful action | Constitutional meaning |
|---------------|------------------------|
| **Confirm lifecycle completion** | Affirm CVC-01…CVC-05 when published criteria hold for a lawful verification concern |
| **Confirm findings production** | Cite that required verification findings were produced under published CV criteria — without rewriting dispositions |
| **Preserve audit records** | Leave reconstructable constitutional records of affirmed or refused close |
| **Refuse unlawful close** | Prefer honest not-yet / refuse / defer / escalate over improvisation |
| **Compose required stages and types** | Confirm fulfilment across published CVL / CV conjunctions without collapsing failed duties |
| **Remain implementation-neutral** | Honour completion law across lawful equivalent stacks |
| **Preserve repeatability** | Ensure same published inputs do not yield contradictory completion stories |
| **Explain completion** | Answer specs / evidence / stages / findings / boundaries without rewriting meaning |
| **Propose corpus amendments upstream** | Recommend (not enact) amendments when law itself must change before verification can complete |

These actions **confirm verification lifecycle fulfilment**. They do **not** publish a new Constitution, invent coach warrants, mint tips, author ownership maps, invent CV / CVL / CVC types, author runtime behaviour, evaluate fidelity in place of MS001, certify releases, approve implementations, or judge educational quality.

## 4. What Verification Completion Must NEVER Do

| Forbidden action | Why | Lawful alternative |
|------------------|-----|--------------------|
| **Imply constitutional compliance** | WS1 / MS001 owns fidelity disposition | Confirm lifecycle fulfilment only; never narrate “complete therefore compliant” |
| **Imply certification** | Completion is not a certificate authority | Affirm lifecycle fulfilment only; never certify releases or stacks |
| **Approve implementations** | Completion is not an approval authority | Affirm lifecycle fulfilment only; never approve production or delivery as constitutional law |
| **Redefine constitutional law** | Only corpus governance may amend meaning | Leave specs unchanged; propose amendment upstream or refuse incomplete verification |
| **Replace constitutional authority** | Published corpora remain the standard after any close | Keep findings as verification outcomes, never as next-cycle law |
| **Create constitutional law** | Completion judgements are not legislation | Cite published law only; refuse unpublished anchors |
| **Replace conformance evaluation** | MS001 owns CC disposition | Supply / confirm verification support for fidelity honesty; never claim conformity from completion |
| **Create educational meaning** | Programme VI owns coach / planner questions | Preserve identity of published meaning; never invent it |
| **Author runtime behaviour** | Programme VIII + permitted writers own execution law | Evaluate evidence against published law; do not mint behaviour |
| **Bypass audit / findings requirements** | Requirements are educational care, not optional polish | Prefer understatement / refuse over invented green completes |
| **Privilege a technology as constitutional** | Implementation neutrality | Confirm obligations, not Flask / SQLAlchemy / REST / OpenAPI fashion |
| **Erase verification trails to tidy close** | EIP-005 continuity | Preserve prior lawful stage / finding records; refuse destructive “cleanup” |
| **Upgrade finding disposition by closing** | CVC-03 preserves identity; close does not rewrite fidelity | Record findings honestly including not-satisfied / deferred / escalated |
| **Present CI / tickets / coverage as fulfilment law** | Theatre is not constitution | Require CVC-01…CVC-05 content |
| **Imply educational success** | Learning outcomes are not lifecycle fulfilment | Keep student speech honest: verified-complete ≠ ready |
| **Emergency-amend via deadline** | Urgency never mints amendment power | Refuse incomplete close; escalate to corpus governance |
| **Collapse with CTC completion** | Lineage fulfilment is orthogonal | Compose with WS1 / MS003; do not treat CTC as CVC (or vice versa) |

## 5. Boundary Tests (Pre-Affirmation)

Before material constitutional verification completion is treated as affirmed, close requires affirmative answers:

| # | Test | Fail ⇒ |
|---|------|--------|
| 1 | Were required lifecycle stages completed (CVC-01)? | not yet / refuse |
| 2 | Was required evidence evaluated (CVC-02)? | not yet / refuse |
| 3 | Were required findings produced without disposition rewrite (CVC-03)? | not yet / refuse |
| 4 | Do audit records support CVCQ-01…CVCQ-05 (CVC-04)? | refuse |
| 5 | Was verification formally closed under boundary honesty (CVC-05)? | refuse |
| 6 | Would affirmation imply compliance, certification, approval, educational success, or law change? | refuse overclaim |
| 7 | Would affirmation redefine specifications or replace constitutional authority? | refuse |
| 8 | Would affirmation privilege a stack / vendor as law? | refuse |
| 9 | Would affirmation erase prior lawful verification trails for convenience? | refuse |
| 10 | Can the stop or affirmation be explained honestly to student and auditor? | refuse mute theatre |

Any single fail blocks lawful affirmation.

## 6. Collisions with Neighbour Corpora

| Neighbour claim | Lawful coexistence | Unlawful collapse |
|-----------------|--------------------|-------------------|
| MS001 “conformant” | May coexist with completed verification for the same subjects | Treating CVC complete as CC compliant (or vice versa as automatic) |
| CV “finding produced” | Completion judges *lifecycle fulfilment including findings* | Treating any single finding as whole-concern completion without stages / audit / close |
| CVL “stage closed” | Completion judges *required stages complete together* | Treating any single CVL stage as whole-concern completion |
| CTC “lineage complete” | Orthogonal fulfilment horizons | Treating lineage complete as verification complete (or vice versa) |
| Programme VIII EV eligible | Orthogonal eligibility | Treating eligibility as verification lifecycle complete |
| Workflow / execution / evidence completion | Orthogonal fulfilment horizons | Treating verification close as orchestration / runtime / evidence-handling success |
| Educational Validation Framework release | Quality lens | Treating CVC complete as quality certified |
| CI green / coverage high | Engineering signals | Treating signals as CVC criteria |

---

## 7. Closing Statement

> **Verification completion may confirm lifecycle completion, confirm findings production, and preserve audit records.  
> It must never imply constitutional compliance, imply certification, approve implementations, redefine constitutional law, or replace constitutional authority.**

This document does not replace sibling boundaries. It binds **constitutional verification completion** so those boundaries survive verification machinery, CI, demos, and replacements.
