## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which constitutional specifications* were evaluated — which evidence was assessed, which lifecycle stages were completed, which findings were produced, and which limits remained intact — is not closing honestly; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–VIII have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, and runtime speech speakable, after WS1 has made conformance and traceability speech speakable, and after WS2 / MS001–MS002 have made verification and lifecycle speech speakable, Programme IX still needs one verification-completion-speech answer:

> **“Which constitutional specifications were evaluated, which evidence was assessed, which lifecycle stages were completed, which findings were produced, and which constitutional boundaries remained intact?”**

That answer must cover every material affirmed (or refused) completion without inventing educational success, implying compliance, implying certification, approving implementations, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, freezing a stack as law, or modifying constitutional law.

---

## 2. What Verification Completion Explainability Is

**Constitutional Verification Completion Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **fulfilment (or lawful refusal of fulfilment) of the required constitutional verification lifecycle** — not to invent a second tutor voice from CI complete badges, and not to grade the student, certify conformity, or approve a release.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Specifications evaluated** | Named Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII documents under evaluation | Which constitutional specifications were evaluated? |
| **Evidence assessed** | Named implementation evidence subjects evaluated under the concern | Which evidence was assessed? |
| **Lifecycle stages completed** | Named CVL-01…CVL-07 stages executed (or lawfully stopped) for the concern | Which lifecycle stages were completed? |
| **Findings produced** | Named verification findings with lawful dispositions | Which findings were produced? |
| **Boundaries intact** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden overclaims / amendments / substitutions did not occur | Which constitutional boundaries remained intact? |

Verification Completion Explainability is:

- **fulfilment-centred** — it narrates why CVC criteria hold (or why close is refused);
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CV / CVL / CVC identities where applicable;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published specifications and named evidence / findings;
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to amend corpora;
- **non-evaluative-substitute** — completion is never narrated as compliance, certification, or approval;
- **quality-honest** — complete is never narrated as mastery, readiness, or educational quality;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence / validation speech, MS001 conformance speech, MS002 relationship speech, WS2 / MS001 verification speech, or WS2 / MS002 lifecycle speech;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match CVC-04 reconstruction needs;
- **authority-honest** — explanations must not paper over authority inference or stack canonisation.

Verification Completion Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–VIII / WS1 / WS2 meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a compliance certificate or substitute for CC findings;
- a certification instrument or implementation approval;
- a substitute for WS2 / MS001 CVEQ verification explainability or WS2 / MS002 CVLEQ lifecycle explainability (compose with; do not replace);
- a rendering system, UI template, CI badge, coverage visualiser, or analytics dashboard;
- a claim that clear completion speech guarantees learning, a pass, conformity, certification, or approval;
- a licence to present coverage, lint scores, ticket graphs, or pipeline greens as constitutional proof or educational quality.

## 3. Mandatory Explanation Questions

Every material completion judgement (affirm or refuse close) should answer all five questions:

### CVCQ-01 — Which constitutional specifications were evaluated?

Name the primary published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII specifications (paths and identities) whose requirements were evaluated under the verification concern — or name which required specifications remain unevaluated when refusing close. When a clause or catalogue ID is material, name it (for example EIP-002 claim class; RC-02; EC-01 as *subject*). Unpublished tribal norms and “how main works” are not constitutional specifications.

### CVCQ-02 — Which evidence was assessed?

Name the primary implementation evidence subjects assessed (for example structural module boundaries, behavioural disposition set, evidence-consumption path, runtime contract binding map, interface composition map, API operation set, audit trail) — or name which required evidence remains unassessed when refusing close. Protocol or vendor names alone are not sufficient without constitutional mapping. If a published refuse-prepare / refuse-evaluate disposition applied, say so explicitly and do not invent evidence theatre.

### CVCQ-03 — Which lifecycle stages were completed?

Name the primary CVL-01…CVL-07 stages completed for the concern — or name which required stages remain incomplete / refused when refusing close. Include lawful early-stop stage outcomes honestly. Never narrate a skipped mandatory stage as completed because a later pipeline job glowed green. “It ran in CI” is not a constitutional stage identity.

### CVCQ-04 — Which findings were produced?

Name the primary verification finding identities and dispositions produced under the concern (satisfied / not-satisfied / deferred / escalated) — or name which required findings remain unproduced when refusing close. Never narrate a not-satisfied / deferred / escalated finding as upgraded by completion. “Tests passed somewhere” is not a constitutional finding identity.

### CVCQ-05 — Which constitutional boundaries remained intact?

Confirm the hard stops in `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md` that were checked — especially non-implication of constitutional compliance, non-implication of certification, non-approval of implementations, non-redefinition of constitutional law, non-replacement of constitutional authority, implementation neutrality, repeatability honesty, and non-equation of completion with educational success or constitutional truth.

| **CVCC-08** | Authority preservation note | Confirmation that published corpora remain the standard |
| **CVCC-09** | Neutrality note | Confirmation that no stack / vendor was privileged as law |
| **CVCC-10** | Non-compliance-implication note | Confirmation that completion was not narrated as conformity |
| **CVCC-11** | Non-certification / non-approval note | Confirmation that completion was not narrated as certified or approved |
| **CVCC-12** | Refusal / deferral honesty | When no lawful fulfilment existed, say so |
| **CVCC-13** | Continuity note | Prior lawful verification history not erased (EIP-005) |
| **CVCC-14** | Repeatability note | Same published inputs would not yield a contradictory close |
| **CVCC-15** | Non-claim honesty | Explicit refusal of mastery / pass / quality / meaning-rewrite / law-amendment / stack-canonisation / compliance-from-complete / certification-from-complete / approval-from-complete overclaim |
| **CVCC-16** | Audience projection | Student-plain vs developer/auditor vocabulary over one truth |

Components are **constitutional content obligations**, not database columns, API fields, CI annotations, or UI widgets. Persistence and rendering are out of scope for this milestone.

## 5. Audience Rules

| Audience | May hear | Must not hear as educational law |
|----------|----------|----------------------------------|
| **Student** | That the learning system’s behaviour was checked against published educational rules, why a stop occurred when verification is incomplete, and what limits apply — in plain educational language | Internal CVC IDs as theatre; CI traces; coverage maps as “proof you are ready”; complete as mastery, as compliance, as certified, or as approved |
| **Developer / auditor** | Full CVCQ-01…05 answers, corpus paths, CV / CVL / CVC IDs, stage traces, finding dispositions, boundary checks, neutrality and non-implication notes | Product success metrics, pipeline greens, or coverage counts substituted for constitutional refs; completion framed as educational quality, new law, compliance, certification, or approval |

Student and developer traces must describe the **same constitutional truth**. Vocabulary may differ; facts may not.

A complete material journey may carry every layer that applied. Completion speech without upstream specification identity is hollow. Verification speech without completion honesty invites silent “verified forever” drift. Conformance speech without distinct CVC honesty invites “verified therefore compliant” fiction.

## 9. Out of Scope

This milestone does **not** implement:

- explanation renderers or UI copy systems;
- CI badge systems, coverage visualisers, logging pipelines, or analytics products;
- Runtime A services or testing frameworks;
- GitHub Actions, Python, Flask, SQLAlchemy, REST, OpenAPI, or infrastructure;
- prompt templates or LLM narration layers;
- certification authorities, approval engines, or conformance evaluators.

Those may later *obey* this explainability law. They do not *define* it.
