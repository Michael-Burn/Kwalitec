## Authority

This document is the constitutional overview of the **Constitutional Verification Completion Model** for Kwalitec.

It is subordinate to the Educational Constitution, Educational Interpretation Principles (EIP), Programme VI educational meaning corpora, Programme VII orchestration corpora, Programme VIII runtime constitutional corpora, the Constitutional Conformance Model (Programme IX / WS1 / MS001), the Constitutional Traceability Model (Programme IX / WS1 / MS002), the Constitutional Verification Model (Programme IX / WS2 / MS001), and the Constitutional Verification Lifecycle Model (Programme IX / WS2 / MS002). It governs **when a constitutional verification activity has lawfully completed** — its objectives, fulfilment criteria, hard boundaries, and explainability. It does not authorise implementation shortcuts that contradict the Constitution, and it does not determine constitutional compliance, certify implementations, approve implementations, invent educational success, modify constitutional specifications, reinterpret constitutional meaning, establish constitutional authority, replace MS001 evaluation, rewrite CV or CVL catalogues, or substitute any implementation for constitutional authority.

Authority order for constitutional verification completion:

> Constitution defines educational truth and curriculum primacy.  
> EIP defines evidence classification, continuity, explainability, claim honesty, and mutation rights.  
> Programme VI defines educational meaning and may publish learning / assessment warrants.  
> Programme VII defines orchestration flow, ownership, recommendations, context, and orchestration evidence artefacts.  
> Programme VIII defines how runtime implementations may lawfully execute published law (contracts, events, evidence, services, interfaces).  
> The Constitutional Conformance Model (Programme IX / WS1 / MS001) defines when an implementation may be considered constitutionally conformant to that published law.  
> The Constitutional Traceability Model (Programme IX / WS1 / MS002) defines how published constitutional specifications, implementation artefacts, and conformance findings remain lawfully related.  
> The Constitutional Verification Model (Programme IX / WS2 / MS001) defines when and how an implementation may be constitutionally verified against published specifications using established traceability.  
> The Constitutional Verification Lifecycle Model (Programme IX / WS2 / MS002) defines how verification activities are lawfully organised from initiation through production of verification findings.  
> **This Constitutional Verification Completion Model (Programme IX / Workstream 2 / MS003) defines when a constitutional verification activity has lawfully completed — that the required lifecycle has been executed and findings have been lawfully produced and recorded.**  
> Downstream verification machinery, CI theatre, product surfaces, Twin, Adaptive, and narration must consume this completion law — never become constitutional producers by declaring “verification done,” and never collapse lifecycle fulfilment into compliance, certification, approval, educational success, or law change.

## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not confuse “the marking session finished” with “the candidate passed,” nor treat a closed pipeline as a certificate that freezes the syllabus, nor rewrite the syllabus because a verification job exited cleanly. After the Constitution and EIP have defined *educational law*, after Programmes VI–VIII have published *meaning, orchestration, and runtime law*, after WS1 has defined *conformance and traceability*, after WS2 / MS001 has defined *when and how verification may occur*, and after WS2 / MS002 has defined *how verification activities are organised*, the platform still needs one verification-completion answer:

> **“When has a constitutional verification activity completed?”**

That answer must confirm that the required verification lifecycle has been executed, verification findings have been lawfully produced, and audit records have been preserved — while remaining fully explainable, auditable, repeatable, and implementation-neutral — without implying constitutional compliance, certification, implementation approval, educational success, or constitutional modification.

This document records that posture so every future verification completion judgement (and successor) has a single constitutional reference for *when constitutional verification may be affirmed as complete*.

> **The Constitutional Verification Completion Model describes confirmation that the required verification *lifecycle* has been executed and verification *findings* have been lawfully produced and recorded.  
> It does not determine compliance, certify implementations, approve implementations, invent educational success, or amend constitutional law.  
> It does not implement Runtime A, testing frameworks, CI/CD, Python, Flask, SQLAlchemy, REST, OpenAPI, or infrastructure.**

## 2. What Constitutional Verification Completion Is

**Constitutional Verification Completion** is confirmation that the **required constitutional verification lifecycle** for a published verification concern has been **fully executed** and that **verification findings** have been **lawfully produced and recorded** — such that audit continuity, repeatability, and implementation neutrality hold for that concern under WS2 / MS001–MS002 — without claiming that any compliance, certification, approval, educational, or constitutional-authorship question was resolved by the close itself.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Verification concern** | A bounded constitutional unit of verification work opened under published CV / CVL law | What verification work is being assessed for completion? |
| **Completion warrant** | Judgement that lifecycle fulfilment may be assessed | May we ask whether verification is complete? |
| **Completion criteria** | Constitutional conditions that must hold (`COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md`) | What must be true for fulfilment? |
| **Completion judgement** | Affirmed / not yet / refused (no lawful close) | Is constitutional verification complete for this concern? |
| **Lifecycle fulfilment** | Required CVL-01…CVL-07 obligations executed (or lawfully stopped with honest recording) | Did the published lifecycle run? |
| **Findings production** | Lawful verification dispositions produced under CV criteria | Were findings produced? |
| **Audit continuity** | Reconstructable constitutional records of the completed verification | Can the close be audited? |
| **Boundary preservation record** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden overclaims / amendments / substitutions did not occur | Did constitutional integrity survive close? |

Constitutional Verification Completion is:

- **verification-scoped** — it concerns fulfilment of the published verification lifecycle, not fidelity disposition, certification, or educational outcomes;
- **lifecycle-bound** — fulfilment references CVL-01…CVL-07 stages actually required for the concern under WS2 / MS002;
- **criteria-gated** — fulfilment requires stated constitutional conditions (`COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md` CVC-01…CVC-05);
- **findings-honest** — completion confirms findings were produced; it never upgrades disposition to “compliant,” “certified,” or “approved”;
- **audit-capable** — fulfilment cites reconstructable constitutional records, not ticket theatre or CI greens;
- **repeatable** — same published requirements, same evidence facts, same consumed relationships, same stage sequence ⇒ same completion disposition for the same concern scope;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by completion;
- **non-certifying** — completion never certifies conformity, correctness, educational success, or law change;
- **non-approving** — completion never approves implementations, releases, stacks, or vendors;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which specs / evidence / stages / findings / boundaries applied;
- **corpus-bound** — every material completion cites identifiable published specifications and named evidence / findings;
- **non-substitutive** — a completed verification never becomes the constitutional standard or a substitute for MS001 evaluation.

Constitutional Verification Completion is **not**:

- a determination of constitutional compliance or conformity (CC disposition remains MS001’s job);
- certification of implementations, releases, stacks, or vendors;
- approval of implementations, product surfaces, or delivery milestones;
- educational success, learner mastery, progress, or exam readiness;
- a Programme VI / VII / VIII educational, orchestration, or runtime completion judgement;
- reinterpretation or modification of constitutional specifications under a “verification done” label;
- transfer of authority, minting of tips, invention of EST/CST postures, or amendment of constitutional specifications;
- a claim that pipeline greens, ticket closure, elapsed time, or coverage percentages equal fulfilment;
- a UI celebration, analytics milestone, or “tested” flag as educational or constitutional law;
- a licence to skip outstanding published lifecycle duties because a product surface looks finished;
- a substitute for WS2 / MS001 verification meaning or WS2 / MS002 lifecycle law — completion *judges fulfilment of* those corpora; it does not redefine CV or CVL catalogues;
- a substitute for WS1 / MS003 traceability completion — CTC confirms lineage fulfilment; CVC confirms verification lifecycle fulfilment.

## 4. Completion Lifecycle (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material verification concern that claims lawful completion travels through constitutional assessment phases. These are **normative judgement obligations**, not an implementation architecture.

```
Identify published verification concern opened under CV / CVL law
        │
        ▼
Confirm required CVL-01…CVL-07 stages and CV-01…CV-07 applications for the concern
        │
        ├── refuse / defer / escalate (lawful stop — law unchanged; no invented “complete”)
        │
        └── assess → Apply CVC-01…CVC-05
                │
                ├── not yet / refuse close (outstanding duties or boundary breach)
                │
                └── affirm → Emit completion judgement; preserve audit continuity and repeatability
                        │
                        ▼
                Explain specs / evidence / stages / findings / boundaries
```

| Phase | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **Identify** | Name only published verification concerns opened under WS2 / MS001–MS002 | Concern is unpublished custom, tribal knowledge, or “how CI works today” |
| **Confirm lifecycle** | Verify required CVL stages and CV applications for the concern | Stages would invent law, rewrite specs, reinterpret meaning, infer authority, or privilege a stack as law |
| **Assess criteria** | Apply CVC-01…CVC-05 without softening into product theatre | Any required criterion fails; or close would claim compliance / certification / approval / educational success / law change |
| **Preserve & explain** | Record audit continuity; answer CVCQ-01…CVCQ-05 | Path would erase verification trails, treat completion as constitutional amendment, or narrate “verified therefore compliant / certified / approved” |

> **Verification completion never “best-effort invents” a fifth phase called compliance, certification, approval, educational success, or law-making.**

## 7. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Verification Completion Model exists so that:

1. **Lifecycle honesty remains intact** — “complete” means the published verification lifecycle ran, not that a pipeline glowed green.
2. **Findings remain honest** — close confirms findings were produced; it never upgrades disposition into compliance, certification, or approval.
3. **Audit continuity survives close** — completion leaves reconstructable constitutional records.
4. **Repeatability remains intact** — the same published inputs yield the same completion disposition.
5. **Authority remains upstream** — completed verification never becomes the constitutional source for the next judgement.
6. **Implementations remain replaceable** — completion does not freeze Flask, SQLAlchemy, a particular API shape, or a CI vendor as law.
7. **Explainability remains intact** — completion speech describes lifecycle fulfilment; it does not redefine what learning means or what conformity is.
8. **Students remain protected** — product theatre cannot launder non-compliant or unapproved behaviour as “verification complete therefore lawful / ready / certified.”
