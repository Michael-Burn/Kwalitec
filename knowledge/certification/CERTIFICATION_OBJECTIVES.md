## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “seal green” or “certificate issued.” The tutor optimises **honest recognition of constitutional satisfaction**: only published constitutional obligations that can be recognised from identifiable completed compliance activities — without rewriting those specifications, inventing compliance, re-running verification, or freezing a stack as law — may support a certification recognition.

These objectives bind every constitutional certification recognition and every successor certification machinery that claims constitutional certification.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### CRTO-01 — Recognise constitutional satisfaction

**Definition.** Ensure that every material certification recognition formally recognises — for named published constitutional obligations, consuming identifiable completed compliance activities under recognised CRT-01…CRT-07 types — that those obligations have been satisfied, or honestly reports not-certified / deferred / escalated when completed satisfaction cannot be recognised.

**Includes:**

- Applying the closed CRT catalogue required by published law for the recognition scope.
- Binding every material recognition to identifiable published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII obligations and specifications.
- Consuming identifiable completed compliance activities (CCM determinations + lawful CCC completion, with supporting lineage / authority references intact) as inputs.
- Preferring lawful not-certified / deferred / escalated recognitions over rewriting law or inventing completed compliance so a seal “passes.”
- Keeping certification recognition distinct from educational quality, mastery, readiness, product success, verification re-runs, compliance re-runs, conformity re-evaluation, or constitutional amendment.
- Preserving the Model’s posture: recognitions formally recognise satisfaction; they do not become law or upstream evaluation engines.

**Excludes:**

- Treating green CI, coverage percentages, linter scores, Twin estimates, Adaptive scores, UI ticks, or seals as constitutional satisfaction.
- Soft-amending specifications, inventing unpublished customs, inventing or rewriting completed compliance, inventing lineage, or privileging “how main works” to force recognition.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for constitutional certification.
- Declaring “close enough” satisfaction without documenting a corpus amendment and completed compliance.
- Freezing an implementation as permanently constitutional law because a recognition was issued.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software pretends unsatisfied educational obligations are fine because the product needs a certificate. Recognition honesty is care; certification theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CRTO-02 — Preserve governance integrity

**Definition.** Ensure that certification recognition never becomes a back door to amend constitutional corpora, absorb constitutional authority, launder incomplete compliance, invent completion, or present product urgency as governance override.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who stamps “certified” to silence unfinished obligation work has abandoned educational governance.

**Manifestations:**

- Incomplete compliance activities remain hard stops, not soft variables for seals.
- Certification recognitions never enact, soft-amend, or deprecate constitutional specifications.
- Authority maps remain owned by published corpora; certification does not transfer ownership.
- “Ship anyway / certify later” improvisation is a constitutional defect.
- Governance notes under CRT-07 compose with other CRT types without replacing them.

---

### CRTO-03 — Preserve explainability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material certification recognition can honestly answer the mandatory explainability questions in `CERTIFICATION_EXPLAINABILITY.md` — so students, developers, and auditors hear the same constitutional truth in appropriate vocabulary.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who cannot say *which obligations* were recognised from *which completed compliance* under *which category* and *which authority* is performing, not recognising.

**Manifestations:**

- Explainability obligations apply to all material recognitions.
- Student / developer / auditor projections share one truth with different vocabulary.
- Certification recognition is never narrated as mastery, readiness, educational quality, new law, permanent verification, permanent compliance, or frozen stack authority.
- Stack preference and race-dependent outcomes are defects to disclose, not mysteries to hide.

---

### CRTO-04 — Preserve auditability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material certification recognition leaves a reconstructable trail: which constitutional obligations were satisfied, which completed compliance activities were consumed, which certification category applies, which constitutional authority supports certification, and which constitutional boundaries were preserved.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later say *why* an implementation was certified cannot be trusted. Silent seals destroy student and auditor trust.

**Manifestations:**

- Certification records preserve constitutional references, not only technical CI logs or badge images.
- Consumed compliance determination identities, completion identities, and supporting CT / authority identities are recorded when material.
- Missing CRT identity, missing obligation / authority refs, missing completed-compliance identity, or missing recognition disposition is a defect.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing prior lawful certification history to simplify the next seal.

---

### CRTO-05 — Preserve repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that the same published constitutional obligations, the same consumed completed compliance activities, the same supporting constitutional authority references, and the same published CRT criteria yield the same certification recognition (certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated) — absent a published non-deterministic exception (none are granted by this Model for constitutional meaning or satisfaction recognition).

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who treats the same completed compliance as certified or not depending on which reviewer woke first is not coaching; they are gambling.

**Manifestations:**

- Recognition classification and outcomes are corpus-bound and reproducible.
- Parallel CI runners, sharding, and cache races must not change constitutional disposition.
- Explicit multi-type CRT sets use published conjunction / published stop rules, not random reviewer races.
- “Eventually consistent certification meaning” is a constitutional defect.
- Invented or unstable completed-compliance inputs are hard stops, not soft variables.
- Contradictory recognitions for equivalent published inputs without published justification are defects.

---

### CRTO-06 — Preserve constitutional authority and implementation neutrality

**Definition.** Ensure that certification recognitions preserve published corpora as the sole constitutional standard and determine recognition independently of programming language, framework, datastore, CI vendor, cloud topology, or preferred Runtime A shape — so no stack becomes constitutional by proximity to a certification recognition.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who declares only one notebook brand “certified forever as law” has abandoned educational law for vendor preference.

**Manifestations:**

- CRT criteria speak to constitutional satisfaction recognition, not “must use Flask / SQLAlchemy / OpenAPI.”
- Equivalent lawful implementations under the same published law and equivalent completed compliance may both be recognised.
- Replacing a previously certified stack with another that still obeys published law and completes compliance does not itself create constitutional law for either stack.
- “Our reference implementation does it this way” is never a substitute for a published specification.
- A certified artefact never becomes the evaluation standard for the next cycle.

---

### CRTO-07 — Consume completed compliance without replacing upstream evaluation

**Definition.** Ensure that certification strengthens recognition honesty by consuming WS3 completed compliance activities (and preserving WS1 / WS2 lineage and findings in records) — without rewriting CCM / CCC / CV / CC meaning, inventing satisfaction, re-running verification or compliance as silent second engines, or presenting certification as a substitute for those horizons.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who stamps “certified = forever verified = forever compliant = new law” has abandoned honesty and authority.

**Manifestations:**

- Certification may consume completed CCM determinations and CCC completion as inputs; it may not amend them.
- Certification may preserve CV findings and CC / CT relationships in records; it may not re-author them.
- “Certified” must never be narrated as “new constitutional law,” “permanently verified by certification,” or “compliance re-run skipped.”
- Missing lawful completion or missing required satisfied determinations remain hard stops even when a team wants a fast certificate story.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CRTO-01 conflicts with “helping” incomplete compliance look certified | Constitutional satisfaction honesty wins — not-certified / deferred / escalated |
| CRTO-02 conflicts with urgency that soft-amends law via seals | Governance integrity wins |
| CRTO-03 conflicts with opaque “trust us” certificates | Explainability wins |
| CRTO-04 conflicts with log volume reduction that erases corpus refs | Auditability wins |
| CRTO-05 conflicts with race-dependent reviewer outcomes | Repeatability wins |
| CRTO-06 conflicts with privileging a reference stack as law | Authority preservation and implementation neutrality win |
| CRTO-07 conflicts with treating certification as a re-run of verification or compliance | Consume-without-replacement wins |

No supporting objective authorises violating CRTO-01.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — Constitution / EIP integrity obligations, Programme VI–VIII authority preservation, WS1 conformance honesty, WS1 traceability provenance, WS2 verification finding honesty, and WS3 compliance / completion honesty for the certification horizon.

---

## 5. Non-Objectives

Constitutional certification does **not** optimise for:

- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- CI throughput, flaky-test suppression, coverage theatre, or seal theatre as educational success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when specifications or completed compliance are incomplete;
- collapsing CRT types into one mega-seal that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics signals as substitutes for published constitutional obligations and completed compliance;
- creating, modifying, or superseding constitutional law via recognitions;
- performing verification or compliance under the label “certification”;
- treating documentation as optional commentary on whatever code already shipped;
- replacing MS001 conformance evaluation, MS002 lineage preservation, WS2 verification evaluation, or WS3 compliance determination / completion.

---

## 6. Closing Statement

> **A recognition that becomes “certified” by rewriting constitutional law, inventing completed compliance, or re-running verification / compliance in secret is a failed educational system.  
> A recognition that formally recognises only published obligations satisfied through completed compliance — with governance integrity, explainability, auditability, and repeatability intact — is doing its only certification job.**
