## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “seal finished,” “stages skipped for speed,” or “badge shown.” The tutor optimises **honest organisation of certification work**: only a repeatable, consistent, implementation-neutral, explainable, and auditable sequence — consuming completed compliance activities and validated prerequisites — may support lawful certification decisions.

These objectives bind every constitutional certification lifecycle and every successor coordination machinery that claims constitutional certification lifecycle meaning.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### CRTLO-01 — Coordinate certification lawfully

**Definition.** Ensure that every material certification lifecycle organises activities through recognised CRTL-01…CRTL-07 stages — consuming identifiable completed compliance activities, validating certification prerequisites, and applying published CRT-01…CRT-07 types — so that certification decisions occur only after lawful initiation and compliance confirmation, and that decisions are recorded and published without becoming law, verification substitutes, compliance substitutes, or replacements for constitutional authority.

**Includes:**

- Binding every material lifecycle to identifiable published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII obligations and specifications.
- Consuming identifiable completed compliance activities (CCM determinations + lawful CCC completion, with supporting lineage / authority references intact) as inputs.
- Validating published certification prerequisites before CRTL-03 assessment.
- Applying only published CRT-01…CRT-07 types during CRTL-03.
- Preferring lawful not-certified / deferred / escalated / refuse-progress outcomes over rewriting law, inventing or rewriting completed compliance, inventing prerequisites, performing verification, re-determining compliance, or mutating artefacts so the lifecycle “completes.”
- Keeping lifecycle progress distinct from educational quality, mastery, readiness, product success, verification re-runs, compliance re-runs, conformity re-evaluation, or constitutional amendment.
- Preserving the Model’s posture: stages coordinate recognition; they do not become law or constitutional authority.

**Excludes:**

- Treating green CI, coverage percentages, linter scores, Twin estimates, Adaptive scores, UI ticks, or seals as lifecycle fulfilment.
- Soft-amending specifications, inventing unpublished customs, inventing or rewriting completed compliance, inventing prerequisites, mutating artefacts, performing verification, re-determining compliance, or privileging “how main works” to force progress.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for lawful coordination.
- Declaring “close enough” closure without documenting a corpus amendment.
- Freezing an implementation as permanently lawful because the lifecycle closed.
- Re-running or replacing verification or compliance under a certification badge.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software pretends unfinished or dishonest certification work is fine because the product needs a finished seal. Coordination honesty is care; lifecycle theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CRTLO-02 — Preserve consistency

**Definition.** Ensure that lifecycle organisation applies the same published stage meanings, stop rules, and decision dispositions across concerns, reviewers, and successors — so “certification lifecycle” does not mean one thing on Monday and another on Tuesday for equivalent published inputs.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who invents a private stage order for convenience destroys institutional trust even if each private path looks tidy.

**Manifestations:**

- Only CRTL-01…CRTL-07 may organise constitutional certification lifecycles.
- Stage purpose, inputs, outputs, and permitted / prohibited activities remain as published in `LIFECYCLE_STAGES.md`.
- Equivalent concerns under the same published law receive equivalent stage treatment.
- Ad hoc stage insertion, silent omission of mandatory compliance confirmation, or private “fast path” seals are defects.
- Consistency never authorises inventing unpublished stages to “harmonise” product processes.

---

### CRTLO-03 — Preserve repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that the same published constitutional obligations, the same consumed completed compliance activities, the same validated prerequisites, the same published CRT criteria, and the same CRTL stage sequence yield the same certification disposition (certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated) and the same lawful stage outcomes — absent a published non-deterministic exception (none are granted by this Model for constitutional meaning or recognition status).

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who treats the same completed compliance as certified or not depending on which reviewer woke first — or skips stages randomly — is not coaching; they are gambling.

**Manifestations:**

- Stage transitions and outcomes are corpus-bound and reproducible.
- Parallel CI runners, sharding, and cache races must not change constitutional disposition or invent stage skips.
- Explicit multi-type CRT sets at CRTL-03 use published conjunction / published stop rules, not random reviewer races.
- “Eventually consistent lifecycle meaning” is a constitutional defect.
- Invented or unstable completed-compliance inputs, and invented prerequisites, are hard stops — not soft variables.

---

### CRTLO-04 — Preserve implementation neutrality

**Definition.** Ensure that lifecycle coordination organises recognition against published obligations independently of programming language, framework, datastore, CI vendor, cloud topology, or preferred Runtime A shape — so no stack becomes constitutional by proximity to lifecycle progress or closure.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who declares only one notebook brand “correct” has abandoned educational law for vendor preference.

**Manifestations:**

- CRTL criteria speak to constitutional coordination obligations, not “must use Flask / SQLAlchemy / OpenAPI / GitHub Actions.”
- Equivalent lawful implementations under the same published law may both complete lawful lifecycles.
- Replacing a stack that previously yielded completed compliance with another that still obeys published law does not itself create not-certification — and does not freeze either stack as constitutional authority.
- “Our reference implementation does it this way” is never a substitute for a published specification or a published stage.

---

### CRTLO-05 — Preserve explainability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material certification lifecycle can honestly answer the mandatory explainability questions in `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md` — so students, developers, and auditors hear the same constitutional truth in appropriate vocabulary.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who cannot say *which completed compliance* was consumed under *which prerequisites* at *which stage* that produced the decision — and *which records* were preserved — is performing, not coordinating certification.

**Manifestations:**

- Explainability obligations apply to all material lifecycles.
- Student / developer / auditor projections share one truth with different vocabulary.
- Lifecycle progress is never narrated as mastery, readiness, educational quality, new law, a verification re-run, a compliance re-run, or a freeze of stack authority.
- Stack preference and race-dependent outcomes are defects to disclose, not mysteries to hide.

---

### CRTLO-06 — Preserve auditability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material certification lifecycle leaves a reconstructable trail: which completed compliance activities were consumed, which certification prerequisites were satisfied, which lifecycle stage produced the certification decision, which certification records were preserved, and which constitutional boundaries remained intact.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later say *why* satisfaction was recognised — or *at which stage* a decision arose — cannot be trusted. Silent green seals destroy student and auditor trust.

**Manifestations:**

- Audit records preserve constitutional references, not only technical CI logs.
- Consumed completed-compliance identities, CRT identities, CRTL stage identities, prerequisite identities, and supporting authority / lineage identities are recorded when material.
- Missing CRTL identity, missing obligation / specification refs, missing completed-compliance identity, missing stage-to-decision binding, missing record identity, or missing decision disposition is a defect.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing prior lawful lifecycle history to simplify the next report.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CRTLO-01 conflicts with “helping” incomplete compliance look lifecycle-complete | Lawful coordination honesty wins — not-certified / deferred / escalated / refuse-progress |
| CRTLO-02 conflicts with private stage shortcuts | Consistency wins |
| CRTLO-03 conflicts with race-dependent reviewer outcomes | Repeatability wins |
| CRTLO-04 conflicts with privileging a reference stack as law | Implementation neutrality wins |
| CRTLO-05 conflicts with opaque “trust us” seals | Explainability wins |
| CRTLO-06 conflicts with log volume reduction that erases corpus refs or stage binding | Auditability wins |

No supporting objective authorises violating CRTLO-01.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — Constitution / EIP integrity obligations, Programme VI–VIII authority preservation, WS1 conformance honesty, WS1 traceability provenance, WS2 verification honesty, WS3 compliance honesty, and WS4 / MS001 certification objectives for the lifecycle horizon.

---

## 5. Non-Objectives

The constitutional certification lifecycle does **not** optimise for:

- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- CI throughput, flaky-test suppression, or coverage theatre as educational success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when specifications, completed compliance, or prerequisites are incomplete;
- collapsing CRTL stages into one mega-step that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics signals as substitutes for published constitutional obligations or completed compliance;
- freezing implementations, releases, or stacks as permanently lawful;
- treating documentation as optional commentary on whatever code already shipped;
- replacing MS001 conformance evaluation, MS002 lineage preservation, WS2 verification evaluation, WS3 compliance determination, or WS4 / MS001 certification meaning;
- modifying implementation artefacts to manufacture passable recognition;
- performing verification or re-determining compliance under a certification seal.

---

## 6. Closing Statement

> **A lifecycle that becomes “complete” by rewriting constitutional law, inventing completed compliance, performing verification, re-determining compliance, mutating artefacts, or replacing constitutional authority is a failed educational system.  
> A lifecycle that coordinates only published certification — with consistency, repeatability, neutrality, explainability, and auditability intact — is doing its only coordination job.**
