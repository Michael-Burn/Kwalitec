## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which completed compliance* was consumed — which prerequisites were satisfied, which stage produced the certification decision, which records were preserved, and which limits remained intact — is not coaching; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–VIII have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, and runtime speech speakable, after WS1 has made conformance and lineage speech speakable, after WS2 has made verification speech speakable, after WS3 has made compliance speech speakable, and after WS4 / MS001 has made certification speech speakable, Programme IX still needs one lifecycle-speech answer:

> **“Which completed compliance activities were consumed, which certification prerequisites were satisfied, which lifecycle stage produced the certification decision, which certification records were preserved, and which constitutional boundaries remained intact?”**

That answer must cover every material certification lifecycle without inventing educational meaning, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, inventing completed compliance, performing verification, re-determining compliance, freezing a stack as law, inventing unpublished CRT or CRTL types, replacing MS001 evaluation, or presenting closure as educational quality.

## 2. What Lifecycle Explainability Is

**Constitutional Certification Lifecycle Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **coordination of named certification activities through published CRTL stages** — consuming completed compliance activities, validating certification prerequisites, applying published CRT types, and producing decisions and records — not to invent a second tutor voice from CI machinery, not to grade the student, and not to replace constitutional authority.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Completed compliance activities consumed** | Named CCM determinations under lawful CCC completion (and supporting lineage / authority references) used as inputs | Which completed compliance activities were consumed? |
| **Certification prerequisites satisfied** | Named published preconditions confirmed at CRTL-02 | Which certification prerequisites were satisfied? |
| **Lifecycle stage producing the certification decision** | Named CRTL stage (especially CRTL-03 / CRTL-04) bound to each decision | Which lifecycle stage produced the certification decision? |
| **Certification records preserved** | Named certification records from CRTL-05 (and publication artefacts from CRTL-06 where material) | Which certification records were preserved? |
| **Boundaries remaining intact** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden verification / compliance-redetermination / redefinition / mutation / substitution did not occur | Which constitutional boundaries remained intact? |

Lifecycle Explainability is:

- **coordination-centred** — it narrates completed compliance → prerequisites → stages → decisions → records under law;
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CRTL and CRT identities;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published obligations and specifications;
- **compliance-honest** — every material justification cites identifiable completed compliance consumed (or names the hard stop when none exist);
- **prerequisite-honest** — every material justification cites validated prerequisites (or names the hard stop when unmet);
- **stage-honest** — every material decision is bound to the producing lifecycle stage;
- **record-honest** — every material lifecycle cites which certification records were preserved;
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to amend corpora;
- **non-authoritative** — recognition or closure is never narrated as a freeze of stack authority or a substitute for published corpora;
- **quality-honest** — recognition or closure is never narrated as mastery, readiness, or educational quality;
- **verification-honest** — closure is never narrated as a verification re-run or substitute for CV / CVL / CVC;
- **compliance-honest** — closure is never narrated as a second CCM determination or substitute for WS3;
- **conformance-honest** — closure is never narrated as permanent conformity or a rewrite of CC meaning;
- **mutation-honest** — explanation never hides artefact changes performed “so certification could proceed”;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence / validation speech, MS001 conformance speech, MS002 lineage speech, WS2 verification speech, WS3 compliance speech, or WS4 / MS001 certification speech;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match CRTLO-06 reconstruction needs;
- **repeatability-honest** — explanations must not paper over race-dependent dispositions or private stage shortcuts.

Lifecycle Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–VIII / WS1 / WS2 / WS3 / WS4 MS001 meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a substitute for CC findings, CT lineage speech, CV verification speech, CCM compliance-determination speech, or CRT certification-recognition speech;
- a CI status page, coverage report, or linter summary presented as constitutional coordination;
- a freeze of implementations, stacks, vendors, or releases as constitutional law;
- a licence to narrate invented completed compliance, performed verification, or re-determined compliance as if they were lawful inputs.

## 3. Mandatory Explainability Questions

Every material constitutional certification lifecycle must be able to answer:

### Q1 — Which completed compliance activities were consumed?

**Required content**

- Named completed compliance identities (CCM determinations + lawful CCC completion, with supporting lineage / authority references as applicable)
- Binding between completed-compliance identities and intended / applied CRT types
- Confirmation that unpublished customs, invented determinations, or incomplete lifecycles were not used as completed compliance

**Defect if**

- “The system was certified” with no completed-compliance references
- Anonymous “the build” or “the tests” without identifiable completed compliance
- Completed compliance invented, rewritten, or re-determined as certification without disclosure and hard stop

---

### Q2 — Which certification prerequisites were satisfied?

**Required content**

- Named published prerequisites confirmed at CRTL-02
- Explicit refuse-confirm disposition when prerequisites were unmet
- Confirmation that prerequisites were not invented or silently skipped

**Defect if**

- Assessment proceeds with “prerequisites assumed”
- Prerequisites invented mid-lifecycle to force CRTL-03
- Silent omission of mandatory completion / obligation / authority prerequisites

---

### Q3 — Which lifecycle stage produced the certification decision?

**Required content**

- Binding of each certification decision to the producing CRTL stage (normally CRTL-03 assessment dispositions crystallised at CRTL-04)
- Honest recording of refuse-open / refuse-confirm / refuse-publish / refuse-close dispositions to the stages that produced them

**Defect if**

- Decisions appear without stage binding
- Closure or publication narrated as the producing stage for substantive recognition claims that actually arose earlier
- Silent stage skips when decisions are claimed

---

### Q4 — Which certification records were preserved?

**Required content**

- Named certification records produced at CRTL-05
- Publication artefacts from CRTL-06 where material (or refuse-publish identity)
- Continuity sufficient to reconstruct obligations, completed compliance, prerequisites, CRT types, stages, decisions, and boundaries

**Defect if**

- “Certified” with no reconstructable record identity
- Records that drop stage-to-decision binding or completed-compliance identity
- Silent erasure of prior lawful certification / lifecycle history

---

### Q5 — Which constitutional boundaries remained intact?

**Required content**

- Explicit confirmation against `LIFECYCLE_BOUNDARIES.md` checks material to the concern (especially non-verification, non-compliance-redetermination, non-redefinition, non-mutation, non-substitution)
- Disclosure of any hard stop that prevented progress or closure

**Defect if**

- Boundaries are implied, not speakable
- Closure speech smuggles verification performance, compliance re-determination, conformity freezes, or law rewrites
- Artefact mutation or invented completed compliance is hidden

## 4. Audience Projections

| Audience | Vocabulary emphasis | Same truth |
|----------|---------------------|------------|
| **Student-facing** | Plain speech that the product was recognised against published learning obligations from completed checks — not that the student mastered a topic or that the product is “certified forever as law” | Completed compliance / prerequisites / stage / records / boundaries remain reconstructable |
| **Developer-facing** | Corpus paths, CRT / CRTL / CCM / CCC / CT / CV identities, decision codes, record identities | No private “trust the seal” meaning |
| **Auditor-facing** | Full reconstruction: Q1–Q5 answers, stage traces, boundary checks | Continuity of prior lawful records |

Projections differ in vocabulary. They must not differ in constitutional facts.

## 5. Forbidden Explainability Moves

| Forbidden narration | Why | Lawful alternative |
|---------------------|-----|--------------------|
| “Lifecycle closed ⇒ verified again” | Performs / replaces WS2 verification | “Certification coordinated; verification remains WS2” |
| “Lifecycle closed ⇒ compliance re-run” | Replaces WS3 compliance | “Completed compliance consumed; compliance remains WS3” |
| “Lifecycle closed ⇒ conformant forever” | Replaces CC meaning | “Certification coordinated; conformance remains WS1 / MS001” |
| “Lifecycle closed ⇒ new constitutional law” | Legislation via closure | “Recognition recorded; corpora unchanged” |
| “Certified ⇒ student ready / mastered” | Quality / mastery out of scope | Keep recognition speech distinct from educational claim classes |
| “We confirmed compliance ourselves in certification” | Compliance re-determination | Consume published completed compliance; refuse invented re-runs |
| “We rechecked the tests in certification” | Verification performance | Never perform verification; refuse invented re-runs |
| “Stage skipped because CI was green” | Unpublished stage customs | Apply CRTL-01…CRTL-07 or refuse |
| “We adjusted the code so the lifecycle could finish” | Artefact mutation | Refuse-confirm / not-certified; propose lawful change outside lifecycle pretence |
| “This stack is now the standard” | Authority substitution / neutrality breach | Keep implementation as recognised subject |
| “Decisions update the constitution” | Legislation via explanation | Propose amendment upstream; leave corpora unchanged |

## 7. Audit Alignment

Lifecycle explainability records must support reconstruction of:

1. Completed compliance activities consumed (Q1)
2. Certification prerequisites satisfied (Q2)
3. Lifecycle stage producing each certification decision (Q3)
4. Certification records preserved (Q4)
5. Constitutional boundaries remaining intact (Q5)
6. Applied CRT-01…CRT-07 identities (from WS4 / MS001 speech)
7. Decision dispositions (certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated / refuse-*)
8. Continuity of prior lawful lifecycle history (EIP-005)

Missing any material element is an explainability defect under CRTLO-05 / CRTLO-06.
