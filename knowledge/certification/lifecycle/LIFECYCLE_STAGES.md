## 1. Purpose

Certification without a closed stage catalogue invents law by proximity: whichever pipeline step, ticket status, or reviewer habit happens to finish becomes the tutor’s “proof the system ran a constitutional certification recognition.”

This catalogue names the only lawful constitutional certification lifecycle stages — and binds each stage to purpose, inputs, outputs, and permitted / prohibited activities.

---

## 2. Stage Principles

1. **Ordered and skip-disciplined.** Stages may be brief under thin scope; mandatory initiation, compliance confirmation, assessment, decision, recording, publication, and closure obligations may not be silently omitted when a certification decision is claimed.
2. **Completed compliance before assessment.** Identifiable completed compliance activities and validated prerequisites are confirmed before CRT recognition.
3. **Explainability accumulates.** Each stage leaves traceable artefacts for `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.
4. **Refusal is a stage outcome.** Not-certified, deferred, escalated, and refuse-progress are first-class conclusions.
5. **Deterministic transitions.** Same published inputs ⇒ same next stage outcomes.
6. **Stages coordinate; they do not legislate.** No stage creates, modifies, or redefines constitutional law.
7. **Stages do not become constitutional authority.** Closure never freezes an implementation as permanently lawful or replaces published corpora.
8. **Stages do not perform verification.** Verification findings are never re-authored as a second verification engine.
9. **Stages do not determine compliance.** Completed compliance is consumed; it is never re-determined under a certification badge.

## 3. Catalogue Overview

| ID | Stage | Constitutional purpose (short) | Primary inputs | Primary outputs |
|----|-------|--------------------------------|----------------|-----------------|
| **CRTL-01** | Certification Initiation | Open a certification concern against named published obligations and scope | Published corpus / obligation identities + concern scope + optional CRT indication | Initiated concern record (or refuse-open) |
| **CRTL-02** | Compliance Confirmation | Confirm completed compliance activities and certification prerequisites | Initiated concern + candidate completed-compliance identities + published prerequisites | Confirmed completed-compliance bundle + satisfied prerequisites (or refuse-confirm) |
| **CRTL-03** | Certification Assessment | Apply CRT-01…CRT-07 against confirmed completed compliance under published obligations | Confirmed completed compliance + published CRT criteria + obligations | Assessment dispositions under CRT types |
| **CRTL-04** | Certification Decision | Emit certification decisions under published criteria | Assessment dispositions | Certification decisions (certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated) |
| **CRTL-05** | Certification Recording | Preserve audit-speakable trails of the lifecycle | Decisions + stage traces | Certification records bound to obligations / completed compliance / prerequisites / stages |
| **CRTL-06** | Certification Publication | Publish decisions and records under published honesty rules | Certification records + boundary confirmations | Published certification artefacts (or refuse-publish) |
| **CRTL-07** | Certification Closure | Close the lifecycle honestly when duties are exhausted | Published artefacts + boundary confirmations | Closed lifecycle (or refuse-close) — never a freeze of constitutional authority |

Material certification claiming constitutional decisions must map to this sequence as published law requires. Stages may be brief; none may invent a stage outside this catalogue.

**Relation to CRT / CCM / CCC / CC / CT / CV / EV:** Those catalogues remain defined solely by their owners. CRTL stages *organise when* certification activities occur; they do not replace, reclassify, re-verify, re-determine compliance, invent, or amend those catalogues.

## 4. Canonical Stage Sequence

```text
CRTL-01  Certification Initiation
           ↓
CRTL-02  Compliance Confirmation
           ↓
CRTL-03  Certification Assessment
           ↓
CRTL-04  Certification Decision
           ↓
CRTL-05  Certification Recording
           ↓
CRTL-06  Certification Publication
           ↓
CRTL-07  Certification Closure
```

Lawful early stop dispositions (refuse-open / refuse-confirm / deferred / escalated / not-certified) may exhaust remaining *recognition* duties while still requiring honest recording, publication (or refuse-publish), and closure of what occurred — never silent disappearance of the concern.

## 5. Stage Definitions

### CRTL-01 — Certification Initiation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Open a material certification concern by naming the published constitutional obligations under recognition and the scope of the concern — without inventing law, opening against unpublished customs, or treating initiation as formal certification.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Identifiable published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII obligation and specification references for the concern scope
- Concern identity (what constitutional-satisfaction recognition question is under certification)
- Optional published indication of required CRT-01…CRT-07 types for the scope
- Optional identifiable supporting authority / lineage references (not rewritten)

**Constitutional outputs**

- An initiated certification concern record naming obligations, specifications, scope, and intended CRT type set — **or**
- Lawful refuse-open when standards are unpublished, scope is theatrical, or initiation would invent law

**Permitted activities**

- Name published obligations and specifications as recognition standards
- Bound the concern scope without amending corpora
- Record intended CRT types from the published catalogue
- Refuse initiation when constitutional preconditions fail
- Preserve initiation audit identity for later stages

**Prohibited activities**

- Treat “how main works,” tribal knowledge, or stack preference as the standard
- Create or modify constitutional specifications by opening a concern
- Issue a formal certification decision at initiation
- Invent unpublished certification types
- Skip initiation when a material decision will later be claimed
- Perform verification or determine compliance at initiation

**Authority preserved**  
Published corpora remain the sole standard. Initiation organises; it does not legislate.

---

### CRTL-02 — Compliance Confirmation

**Constitutional purpose**  
Confirm that **identifiable completed compliance activities** exist for the concern and that **published certification prerequisites** are satisfied — so assessment recognises satisfaction from real completed compliance, not anonymous theatre — without inventing, rewriting, or re-determining compliance, and without performing verification.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Initiated concern from CRTL-01
- Candidate completed compliance identities (CCM determinations under lawful CCC completion, with supporting lineage / authority references as applicable)
- Published certification prerequisites from WS4 / MS001 and related WS3 completion law
- Optional supporting CT / CC artefact identities already established under upstream law

**Constitutional outputs**

- A confirmed completed-compliance bundle bound to the concern, plus an explicit prerequisite-satisfaction record — **or**
- Lawful refuse-confirm when completed compliance is missing, forged, invented, rewritten, incomplete, or would require unlawful re-determination of compliance or performance of verification

**Permitted activities**

- Identify and name identifiable completed compliance activities
- Validate published certification prerequisites for the concern
- Bind completed compliance and prerequisites to the initiated concern and intended CRT types
- Refuse confirmation when completed compliance or prerequisites cannot be honestly affirmed
- Preserve confirmation records for audit and explainability

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent missing completed compliance
- Rewrite compliance determinations or completion judgements to force later certification
- Re-run compliance determination under the label “certification confirmation”
- Perform verification or re-author verification findings
- Treat CI noise, coverage percentages, UI ticks, or seals as completed compliance without published mapping
- Redefine educational or constitutional meaning while “confirming”
- Present confirmation as itself formal certification or a freeze of stack authority

**Authority preserved**  
Completed compliance remains an upstream input. Confirmation organises identity and prerequisite honesty; it does not author compliance, verification, or educational truth.

---

### CRTL-03 — Certification Assessment

**Constitutional purpose**  
Apply published **CRT-01…CRT-07** certification types against confirmed completed compliance under published constitutional obligations and satisfied prerequisites — yielding assessment dispositions without rewriting law, re-determining compliance, performing verification, or replacing constitutional authority.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Confirmed completed-compliance bundle and prerequisite record from CRTL-02
- Published constitutional obligations and specifications named at CRTL-01
- Published CRT-01…CRT-07 criteria from WS4 / MS001
- Optional supporting lineage / conformance artefacts consumed as inputs (unchanged)

**Constitutional outputs**

- Assessment dispositions per applied CRT type (certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated under published criteria)
- Composition notes when multiple CRT types bind the concern

**Permitted activities**

- Apply only published CRT-01…CRT-07 types required by the concern
- Assess confirmed completed compliance against published obligations using satisfied prerequisites
- Prefer honest not-certified / deferred / escalated over improvisation
- Compose published CRT conjunctions without collapsing failed checks
- Remain repeatable and implementation-neutral

**Prohibited activities**

- Invent unpublished certification types or customs
- Modify constitutional specifications to force certification
- Redefine Programme VI educational meaning, CC criteria, CT meaning, CV meaning, CCM meaning, or CRT meaning in assessment prose
- Privilege a technology stack as constitutional
- Freeze implementations, stacks, vendors, or releases as constitutional law
- Invent completed compliance during assessment
- Re-determine compliance during assessment
- Perform verification or rewrite verification findings during assessment
- Rewrite conformance findings to force a pass
- Determine educational quality, mastery, or readiness as a CRT outcome
- Become a second compliance or verification engine

**Authority preserved**  
WS4 / MS001 owns certification types. This stage applies them; it does not amend the catalogue or become constitutional authority.

---

### CRTL-04 — Certification Decision

**Constitutional purpose**  
Emit **certification decisions** as lawful dispositions under published criteria — speaking to whether constitutional satisfaction has been formally recognised — without converting decisions into amendments, educational grades, verification substitutes, compliance substitutes, or freezes of stack authority.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Assessment dispositions from CRTL-03
- Published decision vocabulary (certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated)
- Bound references to obligations, specifications, completed compliance, prerequisites, and CRT types

**Constitutional outputs**

- Certification decisions for the concern (including composition outcomes when multiple CRT types apply)
- Explicit non-authority framing (decisions are formal recognition dispositions only — never new law)

**Permitted activities**

- Produce decisions under published dispositions only
- Bind each decision to the CRT types and obligations that produced it
- Record composition / stop-rule application honestly
- Prefer understatement over invented green
- Propose (not enact) upstream corpus amendments when law itself must change

**Prohibited activities**

- Present decisions as constitutional amendments or new educational meaning
- Narrate certification as mastery, readiness, educational quality, permanent conformity, permanent verification, permanent compliance without completed compliance, or frozen stack authority
- Rewrite decisions to match product urgency
- Collapse multi-type failures into a single false “certified”
- Perform verification or re-determine compliance while “deciding”

**Authority preserved**  
Decisions recognise satisfaction against law using completed compliance. They never become law or constitutional authority.

---

### CRTL-05 — Certification Recording

**Constitutional purpose**  
Preserve **audit-speakable certification records** of the lifecycle so reconstruction can answer which completed compliance, prerequisites, stages, and decisions applied — without erasing history or forging provenance.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Certification decisions from CRTL-04
- Stage traces from CRTL-01…CRTL-04
- Boundary confirmations required by `LIFECYCLE_BOUNDARIES.md`
- Continuity obligations from EIP-005

**Constitutional outputs**

- Certification records binding obligations, specifications, completed compliance identities, prerequisites, CRT types, CRTL stages, decisions, and preserved boundaries
- Reconstructable explainability substrate for `LIFECYCLE_EXPLAINABILITY.md`

**Permitted activities**

- Record constitutional references (not only technical CI logs)
- Bind decisions to the producing stage (especially CRTL-03 / CRTL-04)
- Preserve prior lawful history; refuse silent erasure
- Project one truth to student / developer / auditor vocabularies later

**Prohibited activities**

- Erase prior lawful certification or lifecycle history to simplify the next report
- Forge provenance or invent missing corpus references
- Treat certification records as constitutional amendments
- Drop stage-to-decision binding
- Substitute ticket IDs, SHAs, or stack names for published corpus identity as the sole record
- Hide incomplete compliance or failed prerequisites in the record

**Authority preserved**  
Recording preserves reconstructability. It does not author law or replace constitutional authority.

---

### CRTL-06 — Certification Publication

**Constitutional purpose**  
Publish certification decisions and records under **published honesty rules** so audiences receive lawful recognition speech — without inventing satisfaction, hiding incomplete compliance, rewriting boundaries, or freezing a stack as law.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Certification records from CRTL-05
- Published publication / honesty rules from WS4 / MS001 and EIP explainability / continuity law
- Boundary confirmations (non-verification, non-compliance-redetermination, non-legislation, non-redefinition, non-substitution)

**Constitutional outputs**

- Published certification artefacts projecting one constitutional truth to authorised audiences — **or**
- Lawful refuse-publish when records are incomplete, boundaries were breached, or publication would invent satisfaction / hide incomplete compliance / freeze stack authority / replace constitutional authority

**Permitted activities**

- Publish decisions and records under published honesty rules
- Project student / developer / auditor vocabularies without changing constitutional facts
- Refuse publication when audit or boundary preconditions fail
- Keep publication distinct from educational quality, verification re-runs, and compliance re-runs

**Prohibited activities**

- Publish invented satisfaction or incomplete-compliance theatre as certified
- Hide refuse / defer / escalate / not-certified dispositions to manufacture green
- Narrate publication as constitutional amendment, educational success, or frozen stack law
- Perform verification or re-determine compliance as a publication act
- Modify constitutional specifications or implementation artefacts as a publication act
- Present seals, badges, or CI greenery as substitutes for published records

**Authority preserved**  
Publication communicates recognition. Published corpora remain the sole standard.

---

### CRTL-07 — Certification Closure

**Constitutional purpose**  
Close the certification lifecycle **honestly** when coordination duties for the concern are exhausted — including lawful early stops — without performing verification, re-determining compliance, replacing constitutional authority, freezing stacks, or pretending incomplete work is complete.

**Constitutional inputs**

- Published artefacts (or refuse-publish records) from CRTL-06
- Confirmation that remaining coordination duties are exhausted or lawfully stopped
- Boundary confirmations (non-verification, non-compliance-redetermination, non-legislation, non-redefinition, non-substitution)

**Constitutional outputs**

- Closed lifecycle disposition for the concern — **or**
- Lawful refuse-close when recording / publication is incomplete, boundaries were breached, or closure would imply authority replacement / permanent conformity / new law / verification or compliance substitution

**Permitted activities**

- Affirm closure when published duties are exhausted
- Close after lawful refuse / defer / escalate / not-certified paths with honest records
- Refuse closure when audit or boundary preconditions fail
- Keep closure distinct from conformance re-evaluation, verification completion, compliance re-determination, and educational success

**Prohibited activities**

- Replace constitutional authority by closing
- Replace MS001 conformance evaluation with “lifecycle closed”
- Replace WS2 verification with “lifecycle closed”
- Replace WS3 compliance with “lifecycle closed”
- Redefine constitutional meaning in closure speech
- Modify constitutional specifications or implementation artefacts as a closure act
- Erase inconvenient stage history so closure looks clean
- Treat closure as licence for the next cycle to use the implementation as constitutional authority
- Freeze implementations, stacks, vendors, or releases as constitutional law by closing

**Authority preserved**  
Closure ends coordination for the concern. Published corpora remain the sole standard afterward.

## 6. Stage Transition Rules

| From | To | Transition requires |
|------|----|---------------------|
| — | CRTL-01 | Material certification concern needs opening |
| CRTL-01 | CRTL-02 | Concern initiated (not refuse-open) |
| CRTL-01 | CRTL-05 / CRTL-06 / CRTL-07 path | Refuse-open recorded honestly |
| CRTL-02 | CRTL-03 | Completed compliance confirmed and prerequisites satisfied (not refuse-confirm) |
| CRTL-02 | CRTL-05 / CRTL-06 / CRTL-07 path | Refuse-confirm recorded honestly |
| CRTL-03 | CRTL-04 | Assessment dispositions available |
| CRTL-04 | CRTL-05 | Decisions produced under published vocabulary |
| CRTL-05 | CRTL-06 | Certification records preserved |
| CRTL-06 | CRTL-07 | Publication completed or refuse-publish recorded |
| CRTL-07 | — | Closed or refuse-close recorded |

Silent jumps that claim decisions without CRTL-01…CRTL-02 honesty are constitutionally defective.

## 7. Composition with Certification Types

| During stage | Relationship to CRT-01…CRT-07 |
|--------------|-------------------------------|
| CRTL-01 | May *name* intended CRT types; does not apply them |
| CRTL-02 | Confirms completed compliance those types will consume |
| **CRTL-03** | **Applies** CRT-01…CRT-07 |
| CRTL-04 | Emits decisions produced by those applications |
| CRTL-05 | Records CRT identities with decisions |
| CRTL-06 | Publishes CRT-bound decisions without inventing types |
| CRTL-07 | Closes without inventing new CRT types |

Cross-cutting concerns may bind multiple CRT types at CRTL-03. Lifecycle stages remain CRTL-01…CRTL-07 only.
