It governs **how constitutional certification activities are lawfully organised from initiation through formal certification** — its objectives, recognised stages, hard boundaries, and explainability. It does not authorise implementation shortcuts that contradict the Constitution, and it does not create constitutional law, modify constitutional specifications, redefine constitutional meaning, replace constitutional authority, perform verification, determine compliance, mint recommendations, invent educational state, rewrite WS4 / MS001 certification types, or substitute any lifecycle machinery for constitutional authority.

> The Constitutional Verification Model (Programme IX / WS2) defines when and how an implementation may be constitutionally verified and organises verification through to findings.  
> The Constitutional Compliance Model (Programme IX / WS3) defines when an implementation may be considered constitutionally compliant — whether published constitutional obligations have been satisfied — and when that compliance work has lawfully completed.  
> The Constitutional Certification Model (Programme IX / WS4 / MS001) defines when an implementation may be constitutionally certified — formal recognition that published constitutional obligations have been satisfied based on completed compliance activities.  
> **This Constitutional Certification Lifecycle Model (Programme IX / Workstream 4 / MS002) defines how those certification activities are lawfully organised from initiation through formal certification.**

## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not declare work “certified” because a pipeline skipped compliance confirmation, invent incomplete compliance mid-assessment so seals look finished, rewrite the syllabus so today’s badge passes, re-run verification or compliance under a certification badge, or treat a green seal as new constitutional law that freezes the stack. After the Constitution and EIP have defined *educational law*, after Programmes VI–VIII have published *meaning, orchestration, and runtime law*, after WS1 has defined *conformance and traceability*, after WS2 has produced *verification findings*, after WS3 has produced *completed compliance*, and after WS4 / MS001 has defined *when and how certification recognition may occur*, the platform still needs one lifecycle answer:

> **“How are constitutional certification activities lawfully organised from initiation through formal certification?”**

That answer must ensure certification consumes **completed compliance activities**; validates **certification prerequisites**; coordinates recognition under recognised CRT-01…CRT-07 types; produces **certification decisions**; preserves **certification records**; and remains fully repeatable, consistent, implementation-neutral, auditable, and explainable — without performing verification, determining compliance, modifying constitutional specifications, redefining constitutional meaning, or becoming constitutional authority.

This document records that posture so every future certification lifecycle (and successor) has a single constitutional reference for *how certification work may be sequenced without becoming law, a verification engine, or a compliance substitute*.

> **The Constitutional Certification Lifecycle Model describes how certification activities are lawfully coordinated from initiation through formal certification.  
> It does not create educational meaning, invent ownership, mint tips, invent runtime behaviour, perform verification, determine compliance, or become constitutional law.**

## 2. What the Constitutional Certification Lifecycle Is

The **constitutional certification lifecycle** is the binding organisation by which **certification activities** may proceed through recognised stages — consuming completed compliance activities (identifiable CCM determinations under lawful CCC completion), validating certification prerequisites, and applying published CRT-01…CRT-07 types — yielding **certification decisions** and **certification records** that preserve constitutional authority, implementation neutrality, repeatability, consistency, auditability, and explainability — without becoming a source of constitutional law, a substitute for conformance / verification / compliance meaning, a rewrite of certification types, a verification engine, or a second compliance determination engine.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Published constitutional corpus** | Constitution, EIP, Programme VI / VII / VIII specifications that publish educational and execution law | Which obligations may the lifecycle organise recognition against? |
| **Completed compliance activity** | Lawful CCM determination(s) whose required compliance lifecycle has completed under CCC criteria — never invented or re-determined by the lifecycle | Which completed compliance activities may the lifecycle consume? |
| **Certification prerequisite** | Published precondition for lawful recognition (completed compliance identity, obligation identity, CRT scope, authority references, boundary integrity) | Which prerequisites must be satisfied before assessment? |
| **Certification type** | A recognised CRT-01…CRT-07 recognition category from WS4 / MS001 | Which recognition kinds may stages apply? |
| **Lifecycle stage** | A recognised CRTL-01…CRTL-07 organisational step | Which coordination step is active? |
| **Certification decision** | Lawful disposition: certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated — never “new law” and never a freeze of stack authority | Has satisfaction been formally recognised? |
| **Certification record** | Audit-speakable record of obligations, completed compliance consumed, prerequisites, category, decision, and preserved boundaries | Can the decision be reconstructed? |
| **Constitutional authority** | The exclusive right of published corpora to define educational and execution meaning | Who owns the standard? |
| **Implementation neutrality** | Lifecycle coordination is judged against law and completed compliance, not against a preferred stack, vendor, or topology | Remains the stack replaceable? |
| **Repeatability** | Same published obligations, same consumed completed compliance, same prerequisites, same stage sequence → same disposition | Can the judgement be reproduced? |
| **Auditability** | Every material lifecycle leaves reconstructable constitutional traces | Can the judgement be reconstructed? |
| **Explainability** | Completed compliance, prerequisites, stages, decisions, records, and boundaries are speakable | Can the judgement be narrated honestly? |

The constitutional certification lifecycle is:

- **coordination-centred** — it organises certification activities; it never authors constitutional obligations;
- **compliance-consuming** — it consumes completed compliance activities; it never invents, rewrites, or re-determines them;
- **prerequisite-bound** — it validates certification prerequisites before assessment; it never skips confirmation theatre;
- **catalogue-closed** — only CRTL-01…CRTL-07 may be applied as constitutional certification lifecycle stages;
- **type-faithful** — stages apply only published CRT-01…CRT-07 types from WS4 / MS001; they never invent types;
- **verification-honest** — it never performs verification and never re-authors CV findings;
- **compliance-honest** — it never determines compliance and never becomes a second CCM engine;
- **authority-preserving** — published corpora remain the sole source of constitutional meaning;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by lifecycle law;
- **corpus-bound** — every material lifecycle cites identifiable published specifications and obligations;
- **audit-capable** — every material lifecycle leaves reconstructable constitutional traces;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which completed compliance was consumed under which prerequisites and stages;
- **non-legislative** — a certification decision never becomes constitutional law or a soft amendment;
- **non-substitutive** — lifecycle machinery never becomes the constitutional standard for the next judgement;
- **non-mutating** — the lifecycle never modifies constitutional specifications or implementation artefacts under coordination pretext.

The constitutional certification lifecycle is **not**:

- a second Educational Constitution, EIP, Programme VI / VII / VIII, WS1, WS2, WS3, or WS4 / MS001 amendment path;
- a licence to reinterpret coach meaning, ownership maps, tip warrants, runtime contracts, conformance criteria, traceability meaning, verification meaning, compliance meaning, or certification types in lifecycle prose;
- a testing framework, CI/CD pipeline, GitHub Action, linter policy, or OpenAPI checker;
- a claim that “lifecycle complete” guarantees learning, mastery, exam readiness, product success, perpetual conformity, perpetual verification, perpetual compliance, or frozen stack authority;
- a substitute for MS001 conformance evaluation — CRTL coordinates certification; CC defines when conformity may be claimed;
- a substitute for MS002 traceability — CT preserves relationships; CRTL preserves them in records;
- a substitute for WS2 verification — CV evaluates evidence against requirements; CRTL never performs verification;
- a substitute for WS3 compliance — CCM determines obligation status and CCC confirms completion; CRTL consumes those completed activities and never re-determines them;
- a substitute for WS4 / MS001 certification meaning — CRT defines recognition kinds; CRTL sequences when they occur;
- a substitute for Programme VIII evidence validation (EV-xx) — EV validates educational evidence eligibility; CRTL coordinates *certification recognition*;
- a substitute for Educational Validation Framework quality review — quality release remains a distinct horizon;
- a licence to modify implementation artefacts “so certification can proceed.”

## 3. Lifecycle Posture (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material constitutional certification lifecycle travels through seven constitutional stages. These are **normative coordination obligations**, not an implementation architecture and not a CI job graph.

```
CRTL-01 Certification Initiation
        │
        ▼
CRTL-02 Compliance Confirmation
        │
        ▼
CRTL-03 Certification Assessment
        │
        ├── not-certified / deferred / escalated (lawful decision — law unchanged; no authority freeze)
        │
        └── certified (or other lawful disposition) →
                │
                ▼
CRTL-04 Certification Decision
        │
        ▼
CRTL-05 Certification Recording
        │
        ▼
CRTL-06 Certification Publication
        │
        ▼
CRTL-07 Certification Closure
        │
        ▼
Explain completed compliance / prerequisites / stages / records / boundaries
```

| Stage | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **CRTL-01 Initiation** | Open a certification concern against named published obligations and scope | Scope is unpublished custom, tribal knowledge, or “how main works today” |
| **CRTL-02 Compliance confirmation** | Confirm identifiable completed compliance activities and certification prerequisites | Completed compliance is missing, invented, rewritten, incomplete, or would require re-running compliance / verification |
| **CRTL-03 Certification assessment** | Apply CRT-01…CRT-07 against confirmed completed compliance under published obligations | Assessment would invent law, rewrite specs, redefine meaning, privilege a stack as law, re-determine compliance, perform verification, or freeze stack authority |
| **CRTL-04 Certification decision** | Emit certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated under published criteria | Decisions would become amendments, educational quality grades, or substitutes for constitutional authority |
| **CRTL-05 Certification recording** | Preserve audit-speakable trails of completed compliance, prerequisites, stages, and decisions | Path would erase history, forge provenance, or treat records as new law |
| **CRTL-06 Certification publication** | Publish certification decisions and records under published honesty rules | Publication would invent satisfaction, hide incomplete compliance, rewrite boundaries, or freeze a stack as law |
| **CRTL-07 Closure** | Close the lifecycle honestly when duties are exhausted | Closure would perform verification, re-determine compliance, replace constitutional authority, or pretend incomplete work is done |

> **The lifecycle never “best-effort invents” an eighth stage called law-making, reinterpretation, verification, compliance re-determination, stack canonisation, or artefact mutation.**

## 6. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Certification Lifecycle Model exists so that:

1. **Law remains upstream of coordination** — stages organise recognition against published obligations; progress does not quietly become the specifications.
2. **Compliance remains honest** — confirmation identifies completed compliance; it does not invent or re-determine it to force certification.
3. **Prerequisites remain honest** — the lifecycle validates published prerequisites; it does not skip confirmation so assessment can proceed.
4. **Certification remains sequenced** — CRT recognitions occur under CRTL-03 after lawful initiation and compliance confirmation — not as random green theatre.
5. **Verification remains untouched** — lifecycle decisions never become a second verification engine.
6. **Compliance remains supported, not replaced** — lifecycle decisions consume completed compliance without becoming a second determination engine.
7. **Authority remains honest** — no team may treat a closed lifecycle as the new constitutional source.
8. **Implementations remain replaceable** — lifecycle law does not freeze Flask, SQLAlchemy, a particular API shape, or a CI vendor as law.
9. **Repeatability remains honest** — the same published obligations, consumed completed compliance, prerequisites, and stage sequence yield the same disposition.
10. **Audit remains possible** — every material lifecycle can be reconstructed against named completed compliance, prerequisites, stages, decisions, and records.
11. **Explainability remains intact** — lifecycle speech describes coordination of certification recognition; it does not redefine what learning means.
12. **Students remain protected** — product theatre cannot launder incomplete compliance as “lifecycle certified.”
