## 1. Purpose

Recognition without a closed certification catalogue invents law by proximity: whichever badge, report, or pipeline happens to be green becomes the tutor’s “proof the system is constitutionally certified.”

This catalogue names the only lawful constitutional certification types a recognition may apply — and binds each type to purpose, scope, inputs, outputs, and permitted / prohibited recognitions.

## 2. Catalogue Overview

| ID | Certification type | Constitutional purpose (short) | Primary inputs | Primary outputs |
|----|--------------------|--------------------------------|----------------|-----------------|
| **CRT-01** | Structural Certification | Recognise structural / layering obligation satisfaction | Completed CCM-01 (+ related completion) + published structural obligations | Structural satisfaction recognised / refused |
| **CRT-02** | Behavioural Certification | Recognise behavioural obligation satisfaction | Completed CCM-02 (+ related completion) + published behavioural obligations | Behavioural satisfaction recognised / refused |
| **CRT-03** | Evidence Certification | Recognise evidence-handling obligation satisfaction | Completed CCM-03 (+ related completion) + EIP / Programme VIII evidence obligations | Evidence satisfaction recognised / refused |
| **CRT-04** | Runtime Certification | Recognise runtime execution obligation satisfaction | Completed CCM-04 (+ related completion) + Programme VIII runtime obligations | Runtime satisfaction recognised / refused |
| **CRT-05** | Interface Certification | Recognise interface composition obligation satisfaction | Completed CCM-05 (+ related completion) + Programme VIII interface obligations | Interface satisfaction recognised / refused |
| **CRT-06** | API Certification | Recognise API-facing obligation satisfaction | Completed CCM-06 (+ related completion) + published callable-act obligations | API satisfaction recognised / refused |
| **CRT-07** | Governance Certification | Recognise governance / authority-preservation obligation satisfaction | Completed CCM-07 (+ related completion) + governance / authority obligations | Governance satisfaction recognised / refused |

Material recognition must map to one or more of these types as published law requires. Cross-cutting situations may bind multiple CRT types; none may invent a type outside this catalogue.

**Relation to CC / CT / CV / CCM / CCC / RC / EC / EV:** Those catalogues remain defined solely by their owners. CRT types *formally recognise constitutional satisfaction* given consumed completed compliance; they do not replace, reclassify, re-verify, re-determine, invent, or amend those catalogues.

**Relation to educational quality:** No CRT type judges whether learning was good, whether a tip was wise, or whether the student is ready. Certified ≠ quality. Certified ≠ new law. Certified ≠ permanently verified by certification. Certified ≠ permanently compliant without completed compliance. Certified ≠ frozen stack authority.

## 3. CRT-01 — Structural Certification

### Constitutional purpose

Formally recognise that published **structural and layering obligations** have been **satisfied** — so educational meaning, orchestration, runtime execution, and delivery surfaces do not collapse into a single unlawful authority — using completed structural compliance activities.

### Constitutional scope

Recognition of structural placement of responsibilities, ownership boundaries, and layering duties published by Constitution / EIP / Programmes VI–VIII (and related authority matrices), after lawful structural compliance completion. Does **not** require a particular package layout, language module system, or repository topology beyond what published law states. Does **not** freeze structure as constitutional law.

### Constitutional inputs

- Published structural / layering / authority obligations and specifications (Constitution, EIP-001, Programme VI / VII / VIII boundaries and stack positions).
- Identifiable completed structural compliance activities (typically CCM-01 determinations + lawful CCC completion for that scope).
- Supporting CT relationships and CV / CC artefacts preserved in the compliance trail (consumed as record lineage, never re-authored).
- Published CRTO-01 / CRTO-02 / CRTO-06 / CRTO-07 expectations.

### Constitutional outputs

- Structural certification recognition: **certified** or **not-certified**.
- On not-certified: lawful **deferred / escalated** when published law requires waiting or human / corpus escalation.
- Certification record note (structural obligations recognised as satisfied: true/false).
- Explicit non-legislation: no structural constitutional amendment is emitted.

### Permitted recognitions

- Recognise that completed compliance established satisfaction that educational meaning authorship remains with Programme VI producers.
- Recognise that completed compliance established satisfaction that orchestration ownership / tip / state authorship remains with Programme VII producers.
- Recognise that completed compliance established satisfaction that runtime executes published law rather than inventing parallel authority maps.
- Preserve CRT-07-compatible governance notes when authority preservation is material to the structural recognition.
- Refuse recognition when structural compliance is incomplete or unsatisfied.

### Prohibited recognitions

- Require a particular directory tree, framework, or ORM as constitutional structure.
- Rewrite published layering diagrams to match the code under recognition.
- Invent completed compliance or rewrite CCM-01 determinations so structural obligations “pass.”
- Treat architectural preference docs as Constitution / EIP amendments.
- Declare educational meaning “owned by whichever service holds the data.”
- Freeze a package layout or service topology as constitutional law.
- Perform verification or compliance under the CRT-01 label.
- Use CRT-01 to create new constitutional layering law.

## 4. CRT-02 — Behavioural Certification

### Constitutional purpose

Formally recognise that published **behavioural obligations** have been **satisfied** — so students experience only lawful refusal, deferral, escalation, progression, and explanation postures — using completed behavioural compliance activities.

### Constitutional scope

Recognition of behaviours whose constitutional meaning is published (for example refuse when evidence is missing, defer when continuity requires waiting, escalate when ownership conflicts), after lawful behavioural compliance completion. Does **not** grade pedagogy quality, invent new behavioural law, or freeze behaviour as constitutional law.

### Constitutional inputs

- Published behavioural obligations and specifications (Programme VI / VII / VIII behavioural boundaries; EIP continuity / explainability postures as published).
- Identifiable completed behavioural compliance activities (typically CCM-02 + lawful completion).
- Supporting lineage / finding trail preserved by compliance (consumed as record lineage).
- Published CRTO-01 / CRTO-03 / CRTO-07 expectations.

### Constitutional outputs

- Behavioural certification recognition: **certified** or **not-certified** (+ deferred / escalated).
- Named behavioural obligations recognised as satisfied or refused.
- Certification record note (lawful behavioural postures preserved: true/false).
- Explicit non-legislation note.

### Permitted recognitions

- Recognise completed-compliance satisfaction that refuse / defer / escalate postures remain expressible where law requires them.
- Recognise completed-compliance satisfaction that progression and explanation behaviours remain bound to published warrants.
- Detect seal theatre that would certify behaviour without completed behavioural compliance — and refuse.
- Compose with CRT-07 when behavioural recognition would otherwise hide authority failure.

### Prohibited recognitions

- Grade tip quality, coaching style, or pedagogical elegance as constitutional certification.
- Invent new behavioural obligations in recognition prose.
- Treat UX polish, A/B winners, or engagement metrics as constitutional behavioural law.
- Rewrite published behavioural meaning so a convenient path “passes.”
- Re-run behavioural verification or compliance under the CRT-02 label.
- Freeze a particular UI flow as constitutional law.
- Use CRT-02 to create new constitutional behavioural law.

## 9. CRT-07 — Governance Certification

### Constitutional purpose

Formally recognise that published **governance and authority-preservation obligations** have been **satisfied** — so constitutional authority, conformance honesty, traceability provenance, verification integrity, compliance honesty, continuity, and explainability survive operational pressure without certification becoming a second constitution or a soft-amendment bureau.

### Constitutional scope

Recognition of fidelity to published governance / authority / continuity / explainability / conformance / traceability / verification / compliance obligations as they bind how implementations and assessment machinery preserve constitutional authority, after lawful governance compliance completion. Includes reconstructability of certification recognitions under this Model. Does **not** mandate a particular log shipper, SIEM, governance tool, or certification vendor. Does **not** freeze an organisation, release, or stack as constitutional law.

### Constitutional inputs

- Published governance-relevant obligations (Constitution / EIP authority and continuity / explainability standards; Programme VII authority corpora; WS1 conformance and traceability boundaries; WS2 verification boundaries; WS3 compliance / completion boundaries; this Model’s boundaries and explainability corpus).
- Identifiable completed governance compliance activities (typically CCM-07 + lawful completion) and related assessment trails.
- Supporting CT relationships (including CT-07 assessment lineage where applicable).
- Published CRTO-02 / CRTO-03 / CRTO-04 / CRTO-05 / CRTO-06 / CRTO-07 expectations.

### Constitutional outputs

- Governance certification recognition: **certified** or **not-certified** (+ deferred / escalated).
- Confirmation that authority preservation, non-legislation, non-substitution, and reconstructability obligations are recognised as satisfied per completed compliance.
- Certification record meta-note (obligations / completed compliance / category / authority / boundaries preserved: true/false).
- Explicit non-legislation note (governance certification is not a constitutional amendment of the product).

### Permitted recognitions

- Recognise completed-compliance satisfaction that material educational / runtime acts leave constitutional refs, not only technical logs.
- Recognise completed-compliance satisfaction that material CRT recognitions answer the mandatory explainability questions.
- Recognise completed-compliance satisfaction that verification / conformance / lineage / compliance / certification machinery does not invent law, rewrite specs, or replace constitutional authority.
- Detect erasure of prior lawful history for UX or log-cost convenience — and refuse recognition when findings / completed compliance so require.
- Confirm student / developer / auditor projections can share one truth.

### Prohibited recognitions

- Require a particular logging vendor, GRC product, or metrics backend as constitutional.
- Treat log volume, retention dashboards, SOC2 theatre, or “observability scores” as constitutional governance law.
- Fabricate governance trails after the fact to force recognition.
- Erase not-certified recognitions to present a green history.
- Soft-amend Constitution / EIP / Programmes VI–VIII / WS1–WS3 under the label CRT-07.
- Perform verification or compliance under the CRT-07 label.
- Freeze organisational process or product seals as constitutional law.
- Use CRT-07 to invent educational meaning or amend constitutional specifications.

## 10. Catalogue Discipline

| Rule | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Closed set** | Only CRT-01…CRT-07 are constitutional certification types |
| **Multi-bind allowed** | A single recognition may bind multiple CRT types when published law requires |
| **No silent skip** | Required types for a scope may not be omitted for convenience |
| **Subjects ≠ types** | CC / CT / CV / CCM / CCC / RC / EC / EV / Programme VI–VII IDs may appear as *inputs*; they are not CRT types |
| **Recognitions ≠ amendments** | Not-certified results may motivate corpus proposals; they do not enact law |
| **Recognitions ≠ upstream evaluation** | Certified results never re-run or replace CC / CV / CCM evaluation |
| **Consume completed compliance only** | Incomplete, invented, or rewritten compliance is a hard stop |
| **Technology-neutral** | No CRT type privileges a language, framework, protocol, or CI vendor |

---

## 11. Closing Statement

> **Only published certification types may speak to formal recognition of constitutional satisfaction.  
> Unpublished “implied certification,” green seals, preferred stacks, and soft amendments are not constitutional law — and never become it through certification theatre.**
