## 1. Purpose

Certification that silently becomes a second constitution — rewriting specs so a seal passes, inventing educational meaning in “recognitions,” licensing runtime behaviour under a green certificate, inventing or rewriting completed compliance to force recognition, inventing lineage to force a pass, treating a preferred implementation as the new standard, re-running verification or compliance under a certification badge, or freezing a stack as law — destroys student trust and educational integrity.

This document draws a bright line between **lawful constitutional certification** (Programme IX / WS4 / MS001) and **constitutional authorship / educational judgement / runtime authorship / conformance meaning / lineage authorship / verification authorship / compliance authorship** (Constitution / EIP / Programmes VI–VIII / WS1 / WS2 / WS3 / permitted writers).

---

## 2. Boundary Principles

1. **Recognise, do not legislate.** Certification formally recognises satisfaction; it does not create law.
2. **Published obligations only.** Unpublished customs and “main as law” are hard stops as standards.
3. **Published certification types only.** Unpublished CRT customs are unlawful.
4. **Completed compliance only.** Recognitions without identifiable completed compliance activities (when required) are theatre.
5. **Established lineage preserved.** Invented CT relationships are hard stops as certification inputs.
6. **Exactly as published.** Successful recognition never rewrites specification meaning.
7. **Authority first.** Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard supporting certification.
8. **Implementation independence.** No stack, vendor, or reference implementation becomes law by proximity to a recognition.
9. **Certified ≠ quality.** Certification never grades learning, mastery, readiness, or product success.
10. **Certified ≠ new law.** Certification never creates, modifies, or supersedes constitutional specifications.
11. **Certified ≠ verification / compliance re-run.** Certification never replaces WS2 or WS3 meaning or silently re-executes those horizons.
12. **No redefinition.** Programme VI questions, EIP claim classes, CC criteria, CT lineage meaning, CV finding meaning, and CCM / CCC meaning survive recognition intact.
13. **No authority substitution.** A certified artefact never replaces the corpus for the next judgement.
14. **No bypass.** Product urgency never waives constitutional certification requirements or invents completed compliance.
15. **Repeatability.** Race-dependent or contradictory dispositions for equivalent inputs are a defect.
16. **Explain the stop.** Students, developers, and auditors should hear when certification must refuse recognition.
17. **No emergency exemption.** Deadlines and demos never mint constitutional amendment power via recognitions.

## 3. What Certification May Do (Lawful)

| Lawful action | Constitutional meaning |
|---------------|------------------------|
| **Recognise completed compliance** | Apply CRT-01…CRT-07 to formally recognise constitutional satisfaction from identifiable completed CCM / CCC activities |
| **Preserve certification records** | Keep reconstructable trails of obligations, completed compliance, category, supporting authority, recognition disposition, and boundaries |
| **Preserve audit continuity** | Leave prior lawful certification history intact under EIP-005; do not erase not-certified history for seal convenience |
| **Consume constitutional specifications as supporting authority** | Reference published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII corpora as the sole recognition standard |
| **Preserve constitutional traceability in records** | Carry established CT-01…CT-07 relationships into certification records; never invent them |
| **Refuse unlawful recognition claims** | Prefer honest not-certified / deferred / escalated over improvisation |
| **Compose required types** | Apply published CRT conjunctions without collapsing failed checks |
| **Remain repeatable** | Same published inputs → same recognition |
| **Remain implementation-neutral** | Honour certification law across lawful equivalent stacks |
| **Support without replacing neighbours** | Strengthen recognition honesty without replacing CC / CT / CV / CCM / CCC meaning |
| **Explain certification** | Answer obligations / completed compliance / category / authority / boundaries without rewriting meaning |
| **Propose corpus amendments upstream** | Recommend (not enact) amendments when law itself must change |

These actions **recognise constitutional satisfaction**. They do **not** publish a new Constitution, invent coach warrants, mint tips, author ownership maps, invent CRT types, invent CT links, invent or rewrite CV findings, invent or rewrite CCM / CCC artefacts, author runtime behaviour, judge educational quality, or freeze implementations as constitutional law.

## 4. What Certification Must NEVER Do

| Forbidden action | Why | Lawful alternative |
|------------------|-----|--------------------|
| **Modify constitutional specifications** | Only corpus governance may amend law | Leave specs unchanged; propose amendment upstream or mark not-certified |
| **Replace compliance** | WS3 owns obligation determination and completion | Consume completed compliance; never re-determine or invent completion |
| **Replace verification** | WS2 owns evidence-satisfaction evaluation | Preserve CV findings in records; never re-run or rewrite verification |
| **Redefine constitutional authority** | Published corpora remain the standard after any recognition | Keep artefacts as recognised subjects, never as next-cycle law |
| **Create constitutional law** | Recognitions are not legislation | Cite published law only; refuse unpublished standards |
| **Redefine constitutional / educational meaning** | Published corpora own meaning | Confirm published meaning; never rewrite it in recognitions |
| **Invent or rewrite completed compliance** | CCM / CCC own determination and completion | Consume identifiable completed activities; refuse invented or altered inputs |
| **Invent traceability** | CT owns lawful lineage | Preserve established relationships in records; refuse invented links |
| **Rewrite conformance / verification findings** | CC / CV own their meanings | Preserve as record lineage; never amend them to force recognition |
| **Bypass constitutional certification requirements** | Requirements are educational care, not optional polish | Prefer understatement / not-certified over invented green |
| **Privilege a technology as constitutional** | Implementation neutrality | Recognise satisfaction, not Flask / SQLAlchemy / REST / OpenAPI fashion |
| **Determine educational quality** | No CRT is a quality rubric | Keep recognition speech distinct from quality speech |
| **Determine constitutional truth** | Constitution / EIP own truth | Obey published law; do not adjudicate truth in certification machinery |
| **Transfer authority** | Programme VII WS2 owns ownership, permission, and conflict disposition | Apply Authority corpora; refuse domain absorption via recognitions |
| **Invent educational state** | Programme VII WS4 owns EST/CST context law | Recognise against published postures only |
| **Generate unpublished recommendations** | Programme VII WS3 + Programme VI owners own tip warrant | Surface recognition findings, not new tips |
| **Treat Twin / Adaptive / UI / CI noise as constitutional producers** | They are subjects / delivery surfaces | Require published corpus mapping and completed compliance or refuse |
| **Present scores / coverage / acks / cache hits / seals as constitutional satisfaction** | Operational proxies are not certification law | Cite published corpora, CRT types, and completed compliance |
| **Become irreplaceable by recognition topology** | Replaceability | Keep certification law implementation-independent |

## 6. Hard-Stop Catalogue

Recognition **must stop** (not-certified / deferred / escalated as published law requires) when any of the following hold:

1. Required published constitutional obligations or supporting authority are unidentified or unpublished.
2. Required completed compliance activities are missing, incomplete, invented, or rewritten.
3. Required CRT type(s) for the scope cannot be applied without inventing a type.
4. Recognition would require modifying constitutional specifications.
5. Recognition would require redefining constitutional or educational meaning.
6. Recognition would replace verification or compliance.
7. Recognition would privilege a stack, vendor, or reference implementation as constitutional law.
8. Recognition would erase prior lawful certification or compliance history for seal convenience.
9. Recognition would treat educational quality, mastery, readiness, or product success as constitutional satisfaction.
10. Recognition would treat green CI, coverage, lint, Twin / Adaptive / UI proxies, or seals as substitutes for completed compliance.

---

## 7. Closing Statement

> **Certification may recognise completed compliance, preserve certification records, and preserve audit continuity.  
> Certification must never modify constitutional specifications, replace compliance, replace verification, or redefine constitutional authority.  
> A seal that becomes law, invents completion, or re-runs upstream evaluation has left constitutional education.**
