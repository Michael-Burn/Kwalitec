## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which obligations* were recognised — which completed compliance activities were consumed, which certification category applied, which constitutional authority supported recognition, and which limits were preserved — is not coaching; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–VIII have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, and runtime speech speakable, after WS1 has made conformance and lineage speech speakable, after WS2 has made verification speech speakable, and after WS3 has made compliance and completion speech speakable, Programme IX still needs one certification-speech answer:

> **“Which constitutional obligations were satisfied, which completed compliance activities were consumed, which certification category applies, which constitutional authority supports certification, and which constitutional boundaries were preserved?”**

That answer must cover every material certification recognition without inventing educational meaning, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, inventing or rewriting completed compliance, inventing lineage, freezing a stack as law, inventing unpublished CRT types, replacing MS001 / WS2 / WS3 evaluation, or presenting recognition as educational quality.

## 2. What Certification Explainability Is

**Constitutional Certification Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **formal recognition that named published constitutional obligations have been satisfied under published CRT types, consuming identifiable completed compliance activities, and citing supporting constitutional authority** — not to invent a second tutor voice from seal machinery, not to grade the student, and not to freeze an implementation as constitutional law.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Obligations satisfied** | Named published constitutional duties under Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII (and related WS1 / WS2 / WS3 assessment law as applicable) | Which constitutional obligations were satisfied? |
| **Completed compliance consumed** | Named CCM determinations and lawful CCC completion artefacts used as inputs | Which completed compliance activities were consumed? |
| **Certification category** | Named CRT-01…CRT-07 binding(s) | Which certification category applies? |
| **Constitutional authority supporting certification** | Named Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII corpora (and clauses as applicable) that remain the standard | Which constitutional authority supports certification? |
| **Boundaries preserved** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden legislation / redefinition / substitution / verification-or-compliance replacement did not occur | Which constitutional boundaries were preserved? |

Certification Explainability is:

- **recognition-centred** — it narrates obligations → completed compliance → category → authority → boundaries under law;
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CRT identities;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published specifications and obligations;
- **completion-honest** — every material justification cites identifiable completed compliance consumed (or names the hard stop when required completion is missing);
- **lineage-honest** — every material justification preserves established CT relationships in the record (or names the hard stop when none exist);
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to amend corpora;
- **non-substitutive** — recognition is never narrated as replacement of verification or compliance;
- **quality-honest** — recognition is never narrated as mastery, readiness, or educational quality;
- **neighbour-honest** — recognition is never narrated as permanent conformity by certification alone, permanent verification by certification alone, or a rewrite of CC / CV / CCM meaning;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence / validation speech, MS001 conformance speech, MS002 lineage speech, WS2 verification speech, or WS3 compliance / completion speech;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match CRTO-04 reconstruction needs;
- **repeatability-honest** — explanations must not paper over race-dependent or contradictory dispositions.

Certification Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–VIII / MS001 / MS002 / WS2 / WS3 meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a substitute for CC / CV / CCM findings or CCC completion judgements;
- a substitute for CT lineage speech;
- a substitute for Programme VIII evidence-validation or contract explainability corpora;
- a rendering system, UI template, CI badge, seal registry, log shipper, or analytics dashboard;
- a claim that clear certification speech guarantees learning, a pass, permanent conformity, permanent verification, permanent compliance, or new constitutional law;
- a licence to present coverage, lint scores, pipeline greens, or seals as constitutional proof or educational quality.

## 3. Mandatory Explanation Questions

Every material constitutional certification recognition should answer all five questions:

### CRTEQ-01 — Which constitutional obligations were satisfied?

Name the primary published constitutional obligations (corpus paths, catalogue IDs, and clauses as applicable) whose satisfaction is being recognised. Unpublished tribal norms and “how main works” are not constitutional obligations.

### CRTEQ-02 — Which completed compliance activities were consumed?

Name the primary identifiable completed compliance activities (CCM type / determination identity / disposition, and CCC completion identity / disposition) consumed as inputs. If required completed compliance is missing, say so and record the hard stop — do not invent or rewrite compliance in explanation prose. “Pipeline was green” is not completed compliance.

### CRTEQ-03 — Which certification category applies?

Name the primary CRT-01…CRT-07 type(s) and the published permitted / prohibited recognition criteria applied. Cross-cutting recognition names all bound types (for example CRT-03 + CRT-04 + CRT-07). “Seal green” is not a constitutional category.

### CRTEQ-04 — Which constitutional authority supports certification?

Name the primary published Constitution / EIP / Programme VI / VII / VIII documents (and clauses as applicable) that remain the exclusive constitutional standard supporting the recognition. Do not narrate the certified artefact, a preferred stack, or a CI badge as constitutional authority.

### CRTEQ-05 — Which constitutional boundaries were preserved?

Confirm the hard stops in `CERTIFICATION_BOUNDARIES.md` that were checked — especially non-modification of specifications, non-replacement of compliance, non-replacement of verification, non-redefinition of constitutional authority, implementation neutrality, and non-equation of certification with educational quality or constitutional amendment.

## 5. Audience Rules

| Audience | May hear | Must not hear as educational law |
|----------|----------|----------------------------------|
| **Student** | Plain recognition that the study system’s constitutional obligations for a named scope were formally recognised (or honestly refused) after completed compliance checks | “You have mastered the topic,” “you are exam-ready,” “the syllabus changed,” “this app is now the law” |
| **Developer** | CRT identities, completed compliance identities, obligation / authority refs, disposition, boundary notes | Soft amendments, invented completion, “certify from green CI,” stack-as-law |
| **Auditor** | Full reconstructable trail answering CRTEQ-01…CRTEQ-05 with CRTEC components | Missing CRT / obligation / completed-compliance / authority identities; erased not-certified history |

Vocabulary may differ; truth must not.

## 6. Refusal and Deferral Speech

When certification must refuse recognition, explanation remains mandatory.

Lawful refusal speech:

- names the missing or unsatisfied obligations and/or incomplete compliance;
- names the CRT type(s) that could not lawfully recognise;
- names the boundary that stopped recognition (for example incomplete compliance, unpublished standard, authority substitution risk);
- refuses to invent completed compliance or rewrite specifications in prose.

Lawful deferral / escalation speech:

- names what published law is waiting for (for example completion still in progress under continuity);
- names the escalation owner when published law requires human / corpus escalation;
- does not narrate deferral as “certified enough for demo.”

## 7. Non-Explanation Catalogue

The following are **not** constitutional certification explanations:

- badge images, certificate PDFs, or seal screenshots without CRTEQ answers;
- coverage percentages, lint scores, or pipeline greens presented as constitutional satisfaction;
- Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics narratives presented as certification authority;
- “trusted because Runtime A shipped” or “trusted because main looks like this”;
- quality-release speech from Educational Validation Framework presented as CRT recognition;
- conformance, verification, or compliance speech silently re-labelled as certification;
- any prose that soft-amends Constitution / EIP / Programmes VI–VIII / WS1–WS3.
