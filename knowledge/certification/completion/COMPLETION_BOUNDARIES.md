## 1. Purpose

Certification machinery that silently turns “the certification lifecycle finished” into “the Constitution now says X,” “yesterday’s decision is rewritten,” “the stack has permanently evolved into law,” “student succeeded,” or “governance is finished” destroys student trust and educational integrity.

This document draws a bright line between **lawful constitutional certification completion** (Programme IX / WS4 / MS003) and **constitutional authorship / decision replacement / evolution initiation / authority redefinition / educational judgement / governance finish** owned elsewhere.

---

## 2. Boundary Principles

1. **Confirm lifecycle fulfilment, do not legislate.** Completion affirms fulfilment of published certification lifecycle duties; it does not create constitutional law.
2. **Certification only.** Constitutional amendment, implementation evolution, educational success, and governance completion are never inferred from close.
3. **Not conformance evaluation.** WS1 / MS001 owns fidelity disposition.
4. **Not verification.** Completion never performs verification or re-authors CV findings.
5. **Not compliance re-determination.** Completion consumes completed compliance; it never becomes a second CCM engine.
6. **Not constitutional amendment.** Close never patches law or redefines constitutional meaning.
7. **Not constitutional evolution.** Close never initiates constitutional evolution or freezes a stack as the next constitution.
8. **Not decision replacement.** Close never silently replaces certification decisions already lawfully issued.
9. **Not authority redefinition.** Published corpora remain the sole constitutional standard after close.
10. **Exactly as published.** Close never redefines CRT / CRTL catalogues or soft-amends Programme VI–VIII meaning.
11. **Published criteria only.** Pipelines, tickets, seals, and elapsed time are not fulfilment law.
12. **Published records only.** Audit continuity is constitutional, not free-form log theatre.
13. **Reference decisions honestly.** Issued decisions may be cited; issuing or closing never upgrades disposition into amendment / evolution / authority transfer.
14. **Meaning non-reinterpretation.** Programme VI questions and EIP claim classes survive close intact.
15. **Implementation neutrality.** No stack becomes law by proximity to a complete flag.
16. **Certification-complete ≠ quality.** Completion never grades learning, mastery, readiness, or product success.
17. **Certification-complete ≠ governance finished.** Completion never finishes programme or release governance as constitutional law.
18. **Explain the stop.** Students, developers, and auditors should hear when close must be refused.
19. **No emergency exemption.** Deadlines and demos never mint completion power or amendment power.
20. **Repeatability survives close.** Close must not invent contradictory dispositions from identical published inputs.

## 3. What Certification Completion May Do (Lawful)

| Lawful action | Constitutional meaning |
|---------------|------------------------|
| **Confirm lifecycle completion** | Affirm CRTC-01…CRTC-05 when published criteria hold for a lawful certification concern |
| **Confirm certification issuance** | Cite that required certification decisions were lawfully issued under published CRT criteria — without rewriting or silently replacing dispositions |
| **Preserve certification records** | Leave reconstructable constitutional records of decisions, stages, completed compliance consumed, and boundaries |
| **Preserve audit continuity** | Leave reconstructable constitutional records of affirmed or refused close |
| **Refuse unlawful close** | Prefer honest not-yet / refuse / defer / escalate over improvisation |
| **Compose required stages and types** | Confirm fulfilment across published CRTL / CRT conjunctions without collapsing failed duties |
| **Remain implementation-neutral** | Honour completion law across lawful equivalent stacks |
| **Preserve repeatability** | Ensure same published inputs do not yield contradictory completion stories |
| **Explain completion** | Answer completed compliance / stages / decisions / records / boundaries without rewriting meaning |
| **Propose corpus amendments upstream** | Recommend (not enact) amendments when law itself must change before certification can complete |

These actions **confirm certification lifecycle fulfilment**. They do **not** publish a new Constitution, invent coach warrants, mint tips, author ownership maps, invent CRT / CRTL / CRTC types, author runtime behaviour, evaluate fidelity in place of MS001, amend constitutional law, replace prior decisions, initiate constitutional evolution, redefine constitutional authority, finish governance as constitutional law, or judge educational quality.

## 4. What Certification Completion Must NEVER Do

| Forbidden action | Why | Lawful alternative |
|------------------|-----|--------------------|
| **Amend constitutional law** | Only corpus governance may amend meaning | Leave specs unchanged; propose amendment upstream or refuse incomplete certification |
| **Replace certification decisions** | Issued decisions are constitutional artefacts of recognition, not draft notes | Cite decisions honestly; refuse silent rewrite under close theatre |
| **Initiate constitutional evolution** | Completion is not an evolution authority | Affirm lifecycle fulfilment only; never freeze stacks as next-cycle law |
| **Redefine constitutional authority** | Published corpora remain the standard after any close | Keep decisions as recognition outcomes, never as next-cycle law |
| **Create constitutional law** | Completion judgements are not legislation | Cite published law only; refuse unpublished anchors |
| **Redefine constitutional meaning** | Close never soft-amends Programme VI–VIII / EIP meaning | Preserve identity of published meaning; never invent it |
| **Replace conformance evaluation** | MS001 owns CC disposition | Supply / confirm certification support for satisfaction honesty; never claim conformity-from-complete as new law |
| **Perform verification** | WS2 owns verification | Never re-author CV findings under close |
| **Re-determine compliance** | WS3 owns compliance; certification consumes completed compliance | Cite completed compliance honestly; never rewrite CCC / CCM under close |
| **Create educational meaning** | Programme VI owns coach / planner questions | Preserve identity of published meaning; never invent it |
| **Author runtime behaviour** | Programme VIII + permitted writers own execution law | Recognise satisfaction against published law; do not mint behaviour |
| **Bypass audit / decision / record requirements** | Requirements are educational care, not optional polish | Prefer understatement / refuse over invented green completes |
| **Privilege a technology as constitutional** | Implementation neutrality | Confirm obligations, not Flask / SQLAlchemy / REST / OpenAPI fashion |
| **Erase certification trails to tidy close** | EIP-005 continuity | Preserve prior lawful stage / decision / record artefacts; refuse destructive “cleanup” |
| **Upgrade decision disposition by closing** | CRTC-03 preserves identity; close does not rewrite status | Record decisions honestly including not-certified / deferred / escalated |
| **Present CI / tickets / seals as fulfilment law** | Theatre is not constitution | Require CRTC-01…CRTC-05 content |
| **Imply educational success** | Learning outcomes are not lifecycle fulfilment | Keep student speech honest: certification-complete ≠ ready |
| **Imply governance completion** | Programme / release governance is not CRTC fulfilment | Keep speech honest: certification-complete ≠ governance finished |
| **Emergency-amend via deadline** | Urgency never mints amendment power | Refuse incomplete close; escalate to corpus governance |
| **Collapse with CCC / CVC / CTC completion** | Compliance, verification, and lineage fulfilment are orthogonal or prerequisite horizons | Compose with WS3 / MS003, WS2 / MS003, and WS1 / MS003; do not treat CCC, CVC, or CTC as CRTC (or vice versa) |

## 5. Boundary Tests (Pre-Affirmation)

Before material constitutional certification completion is treated as affirmed, close requires affirmative answers:

| # | Test | Fail ⇒ |
|---|------|--------|
| 1 | Are all required published CRTL stages completed (CRTC-01)? | not yet / refuse |
| 2 | Are all required certification prerequisites satisfied, including named completed compliance (CRTC-02)? | not yet / refuse |
| 3 | Was a required certification decision lawfully issued without silent replacement (CRTC-03)? | not yet / refuse |
| 4 | Do certification records support CRTCQ-01…CRTCQ-05 (CRTC-04)? | refuse |
| 5 | Was certification formally closed under CRTL-07 / boundary honesty (CRTC-05)? | refuse |
| 6 | Would affirmation amend constitutional law, replace decisions, initiate evolution, or redefine authority? | refuse overclaim |
| 7 | Would affirmation imply educational success or governance completion? | refuse |
| 8 | Would affirmation privilege a stack / vendor as law? | refuse |
| 9 | Would affirmation erase prior lawful certification history for convenience? | refuse |
| 10 | Can the stop or affirmation be explained honestly to student and auditor? | refuse mute theatre |

Any single fail blocks lawful affirmation.
