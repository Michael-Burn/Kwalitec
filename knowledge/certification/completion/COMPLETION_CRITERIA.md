## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor needs bright conditions for saying “this constitutional certification work is finished” — and equally bright refusals when it is not. After WS4 / MS001 has named when and how certification recognition may occur and WS4 / MS002 has named how certification activities are organised, Programme IX Workstream 4 still needs one fulfilment checklist:

> **What must be true before constitutional speech may affirm that the required certification lifecycle has been fully executed and certification decisions have been lawfully issued, recorded, and preserved?**

These criteria answer that question. They are **normative constitutional conditions**, not database predicates, CI job statuses, ticket states, seal badges, or API status codes.

---

## 2. Criterion Catalogue

All material affirmed completions require **CRTC-01 through CRTC-05** to hold. Failure of any criterion ⇒ **not yet** or **refuse close**. Do not invent a partial “mostly complete” amendment, evolution, educational, governance, or constitutional status.

| ID | Criterion | Primary question |
|----|-----------|------------------|
| **CRTC-01** | Required lifecycle stages completed | Were required CRTL-01…CRTL-07 stages completed for the concern? |
| **CRTC-02** | Certification prerequisites satisfied | Were required certification prerequisites satisfied under published law? |
| **CRTC-03** | Certification decision issued | Was a required certification decision lawfully issued under published CRT criteria? |
| **CRTC-04** | Certification records preserved | Do reconstructable constitutional certification records exist for the completed recognition? |
| **CRTC-05** | Certification formally closed | Was the certification concern formally closed under published CRTL-07 / boundary honesty? |

Optional supporting honesty checks (do not replace CRTC-01…CRTC-05):

| ID | Check | Primary question |
|----|-------|------------------|
| **CRTC-06** | Catalogue honesty | Were stages and decisions typed under published CRTL-01…CRTL-07 and CRT-01…CRT-07 only? |
| **CRTC-07** | Continuity honesty | Was prior lawful certification history preserved (EIP-005)? |
| **CRTC-08** | Non-claim honesty | Does the judgement refuse amendment / evolution / authority-replacement / educational / governance overclaim? |
| **CRTC-09** | Neutrality honesty | Does the judgement refuse stack / vendor canonisation as completion law? |
| **CRTC-10** | Repeatability honesty | Would the same published inputs yield the same completion disposition? |

## 3. Criterion Definitions

### CRTC-01 — Required lifecycle stages completed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every lifecycle stage required by the concern’s published WS4 / MS002 scope has been executed — or that a published refuse / defer / escalate / not-certified disposition exhausted remaining *certification* duties for that stage set with honest progression to recording, publication (or refuse-publish), and closure of what occurred.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Identifiable certification concern opened under WS4 / MS001–MS002.
- Required CRTL-01…CRTL-07 stage identities for the concern scope.
- Stage traces / disposition records for each required stage (including lawful early stops).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed stage-fulfilment record naming which required stages completed.
- Or a lawful stop record naming which stages were refused / deferred / escalated and why remaining recognition duties are exhausted.

**Completion conditions.**

- Required stages map to published CRTL-01…CRTL-07 only.
- Mandatory initiation, compliance confirmation, assessment, decision, recording, publication, and closure obligations for the concern are not silently omitted.
- Lawful early stops still leave honest stage outcomes; silent disappearance of a concern is a defect.
- Refusal of “pipeline skipped confirmation” or “we know it ran” without stage identity as completion.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That stage completion amends constitutional specifications or initiates constitutional evolution.
- That stage completion replaces constitutional authority or freezes a stack as law.
- That stage completion means the learner succeeded or governance finished.
- That skipped stages may be invented or implied from CI theatre.
- That verification was performed or compliance was re-determined under stage progress.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who declares recognition “finished” without confirming each required recognition step occurred has not finished lawful care — even if the desk looks tidy.

---

### CRTC-02 — Certification prerequisites satisfied

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every certification prerequisite required by the concern’s published scope was validated under published WS4 / MS001–MS002 law — especially identifiable completed compliance activities under lawful CCC completion, published obligation identity, CRT scope, authority references, and boundary integrity — without inventing completed compliance, skipping confirmation theatre, or treating prerequisite satisfaction as constitutional amendment.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Named completed-compliance identities (CCM determinations + lawful CCC completion) in scope.
- Published constitutional obligation and specification identities under recognition.
- Published prerequisite catalogue for the concern (completed compliance identity, obligation identity, CRT scope, authority references, boundary integrity).
- Compliance-confirmation records under CRTL-02 (and related stages).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Affirmed prerequisite-satisfaction record naming which completed compliance activities and other prerequisites were validated against which published obligations.
- Or a lawful refuse / defer / escalate record naming which required prerequisites remain unsatisfied and why.

**Completion conditions.**

- Completed compliance subjects are named (not “compliance happened somewhere,” “the pipeline,” or vendor labels alone).
- Prerequisite satisfaction cites published constitutional specifications and obligations, not unpublished customs.
- Completed compliance consumed was produced under WS3 law with lawful CCC completion — invented or incomplete compliance is a hard stop.
- Traceability / authority references preserved for recognition were established under published law — invented lineage is a hard stop.
- Refusal of coverage percentages, lint scores, Twin confidence, or CI greens as substitutes for named prerequisite satisfaction.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That satisfied prerequisites amend constitutional law or transfer constitutional authority.
- That validating prerequisites re-runs verification or re-determines compliance.
- That a particular stack, language, or framework is privileged as law.
- That product quality or educational quality was judged.
- That constitutional specifications were modified to match the prerequisites.
- That CCC completion alone equals CRTC completion (CCC is a prerequisite horizon; CRTC is certification lifecycle fulfilment).

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who claims “the prerequisites were met” without naming which completed compliance activities supported recognition against which syllabus obligations has performed recognition theatre, not prerequisite fulfilment.

---

### CRTC-03 — Certification decision issued

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that every certification decision required by the concern’s published scope has been lawfully issued under published CRT criteria — including certified, not-certified, deferred, and escalated dispositions — without rewriting those dispositions into constitutional amendment, authority transfer, educational success, or governance completion, and without silently replacing prior lawful decisions.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Assessment dispositions under applicable CRT-01…CRT-07 types (CRTL-03 outputs).
- Published decision-issuance rules under WS4 / MS001–MS002 (especially CRTL-04).
- Boundary confirmations that decisions do not invent law or freeze stack authority.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Identifiable certification decision identities with lawful dispositions.
- Or a lawful stop record naming why required decisions could not be issued without invention.

**Completion conditions.**

- Decisions cite published constitutional references, named obligations, and consumed completed compliance.
- Dispositions remain within published vocabulary (certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated).
- “Assessment happened somewhere” without named decision identity is not completion.
- Lawful not-certified / deferred / escalated decisions still satisfy *issuance* when they are the honest outcome — completeness of *issuance* is not completeness of *certified disposition*, and neither is constitutional amendment.
- Prior lawful decisions for the same published concern are not silently replaced under close theatre.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That issuing a decision amends constitutional specifications or initiates constitutional evolution.
- That a certified decision freezes the implementation, stack, vendor, or release as permanent constitutional law.
- That a not-certified decision may be silently erased or replaced so close can look green.
- That MS001 conformance evaluation was replaced, skipped, or rewritten by decision issuance.
- That Educational Validation Framework quality review is complete.
- That governance, programme, or release completion is finished as constitutional law.

**Tutor rationale.** Recording a hard recognition disposition is not rewriting the syllabus — but omitting a required decision while claiming the session finished is dishonest. Completeness of *decision issuance* is not completeness of *constitutional change*.

---

### CRTC-04 — Certification records preserved

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that reconstructable constitutional certification records exist for the completed recognition: which completed compliance activities supported certification, which certification lifecycle stages were completed, which certification decision was issued, which records were preserved, and which boundaries remained intact — sufficient for student / developer / auditor reconstruction under `COMPLETION_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Stage traces (CRTL-01…CRTL-07).
- Prerequisite-satisfaction and decision records.
- Certification recording / publication artefacts under CRTL-05 / CRTL-06.
- Boundary preservation confirmations.
- Continuity obligations under EIP-005.

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Audit-speakable certification records aligned with CRTCQ-01…CRTCQ-05.
- Honest refuse / defer / escalate records when fulfilment was not affirmed.

**Completion conditions.**

- Material completion judgements leave records that cite published constitutional references, not only technical job logs or CI annotations.
- Missing CRT / CRTL identity, missing completed-compliance identity, missing decision identity, missing obligation refs, or missing boundary honesty for a material affirmed close is a defect.
- Continuity forbids erasing prior lawful certification trails to make close look tidy.
- Refusal of log volume alone as a substitute for constitutional content.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That a particular database schema, log shipper, CI annotation format, or UI widget is required.
- That record preservation equals constitutional amendment, implementation evolution, educational success, or governance completion.
- That volume of logs substitutes for constitutional content.
- That Twin / Adaptive / analytics origin stories may replace published constitutional identity.
- That preserving records silently replaces prior lawful decisions.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later reconstruct why certification was declared finished cannot be trusted — and neither can a platform that celebrates mute “complete” flags.

---

### CRTC-05 — Certification formally closed

**Constitutional purpose.** Confirm that the certification concern was formally closed under published CRTL-07 obligations and boundary honesty — exhausting remaining *certification lifecycle* duties without inventing a constitutional amendment, initiating constitutional evolution, replacing constitutional authority, inventing educational success, or implying governance completion.

**Constitutional inputs.**

- Issued decisions and preserved certification records (CRTC-03 / CRTC-04).
- CRTL-07 closure rules and `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md` hard stops.
- Explicit non-claim confirmations (amendment / evolution / authority replacement / educational success / governance completion).

**Constitutional outputs.**

- Formal close disposition: affirmed complete / not yet / refuse close / defer / escalate.
- Boundary preservation record confirming forbidden overclaims did not occur.

**Completion conditions.**

- Closure occurs only after required stages, prerequisites, decisions, and record-preservation obligations for the concern are satisfied — or after a published stop exhausts remaining certification duties with honest recording.
- Closure speech answers CRTCQ-01…CRTCQ-05 or explicitly refuses mute theatre.
- Closure never silently replaces decision dispositions or upgrades them into constitutional law.
- Refusal of ticket closure, PR merge, seal badges, or pipeline greens alone as formal constitutional close.

**Prohibited interpretations.**

- That formal close amends constitutional specifications or initiates constitutional evolution.
- That formal close replaces constitutional authority or freezes the implementation, release, stack, or vendor as law.
- That formal close silently replaces prior lawful certification decisions.
- That formal close means the student succeeded or is exam-ready.
- That formal close means governance, programme, or release governance has finished as constitutional law.

**Tutor rationale.** Closing the recognition folder is care only when the required steps, decisions, and records exist — and only when nobody pretends the close rewrote the syllabus, froze the product, or finished the candidate’s education.

## 4. Criterion Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CRTC-01 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CRTC-02 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CRTC-03 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — not yet / refuse close |
| CRTC-04 fails | Affirmation is unlawful — mute complete badges are defects |
| CRTC-05 fails | Affirmation is unlawful even if earlier stages appear present |
| Optional CRTC-06…CRTC-10 fail | Treat as honesty defects; do not soft-pass CRTC-01…CRTC-05 |
| Any criterion “passes” only by amending law / replacing decisions / initiating evolution / redefining authority / implying educational success / implying governance completion | The implied overclaim voids lawful affirmation |

All of CRTC-01…CRTC-05 are **conjunctive** for material affirmed completion. There is no lawful “four of five” educational, amendment, evolution, governance, or constitutional status.

## 5. Lawful Stop Dispositions

Not every incomplete concern is a defect. Published WS4 / MS002 refuse / defer / escalate / not-certified outcomes may **exhaust remaining certification duties** for a concern without affirming lifecycle fulfilment as “complete” in the celebratory sense — while still requiring honest recording, publication (or refuse-publish), and formal close of what occurred.

| Disposition | Constitutional meaning | May affirm CRTC completion? |
|-------------|------------------------|-----------------------------|
| **Affirm complete** | CRTC-01…CRTC-05 hold for the concern | Yes |
| **Not yet** | Required stages, prerequisites, decisions, or records remain outstanding | No |
| **Refuse close** | Affirmation would invent stages / prerequisites / decisions, erase records, replace decisions, or overclaim | No |
| **Defer** | Published wait for upstream corpus / completed compliance / prerequisite identity | No (unless published law says remaining certification duties are exhausted) |
| **Escalate** | Published stop requiring human / corpus governance attention | No (unless published law says remaining certification duties are exhausted) |

A lawful stop that exhausts certification duties is **honest unfinished fulfilment or honest refusal** — never a stealth constitutional amendment, implementation-evolution warrant, educational success, governance completion, or authority transfer.

---

## 6. Non-Criteria

The following are **not** constitutional completion criteria:

- CI pipeline greens, flaky-seal suppression, or job exit codes;
- ticket closure, PR merge, or documentation checklist ticks alone;
- coverage percentages, lint scores, seal badges, or elapsed time;
- Twin / Adaptive / Experience / Mission confidence signals;
- UI celebration states or analytics milestones;
- “looks certified enough for the demo”;
- MS001 conformant disposition (that is fidelity law, not CRTC);
- WS1 / MS003 traceability completion (CTC) alone (orthogonal lineage fulfilment);
- WS2 / MS003 verification completion (CVC) alone (orthogonal verification lifecycle fulfilment);
- WS3 / MS003 compliance completion (CCC) alone (required prerequisite — CCC ≠ CRTC);
- Educational Validation Framework quality release (orthogonal);
- any implication that constitutional specifications changed because certification closed;
- any implication that governance or programme work is finished because certification closed;
- any implication that an implementation has permanently evolved into constitutional law because certification closed.

---

## 7. Closing Statement

> **Constitutional certification is complete only when required lifecycle stages are completed, certification prerequisites are satisfied, a certification decision is issued, certification records are preserved, and certification is formally closed.  
> Satisfying CRTC-01…CRTC-05 never means constitutional law has been amended, constitutional evolution has begun, constitutional authority has been replaced, educational success has occurred, or governance is finished.**
