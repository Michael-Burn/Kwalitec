## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “pipeline greens,” “tickets closed,” or “seals shown.” The tutor optimises **honest closing of constitutional certification work**: when the published lifecycle for a concern has been fully executed and decisions lawfully issued, recorded, and preserved, the tutor can say so — without pretending the Constitution was amended, without pretending the stack is permanently frozen, without pretending the student has mastered the topic, without pretending governance is finished, and without rewriting recognition meaning because the machinery stopped cleanly.

These objectives bind every constitutional certification completion judgement and every successor certification machinery that claims constitutional certification fulfilment.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### CRTCO-01 — Confirm lifecycle completion

**Definition.** Ensure that runtime / assessment / audit speech affirms certification completion only when a published certification concern under WS4 / MS001–MS002 has executed the required CRTL-01…CRTL-07 lifecycle obligations — or honestly refuses close when fulfilment criteria are not met.

**Includes:**

- Assessing only concerns that lawfully opened under WS4 / MS001–MS002 initiation → compliance confirmation → assessment → decision → recording → publication → closure law.
- Affirming completion only when `COMPLETION_CRITERIA.md` conditions (CRTC-01…CRTC-05) hold.
- Preferring lawful “not yet” / refuse-close over inventing a “done enough” status for product convenience.
- Distinguishing lawful stop dispositions (refuse / defer / escalate / not-certified as published outcomes that exhaust remaining *certification* duties with honest recording) from abandoned lifecycle duties.
- Binding every material completion judgement to reconstructable constitutional references.

**Excludes:**

- Declaring complete because a CI job exited, a ticket closed, a seal badge glowed, or a UI spinner stopped.
- Treating Twin / Adaptive / Experience / Mission heuristics as completion warrants.
- Optimising for throughput, latency, or demo continuity as a substitute for lifecycle honesty.
- Collapsing “certification complete” into “constitution amended,” “stack frozen,” “student succeeded,” “governance finished,” or “authority transferred.”

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation fails when software celebrates unfinished certification work — or celebrates finished pipeline machinery as finished law-making, evolution, or learning. Honest confirmation is care; completion theatre is harm.

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### CRTCO-02 — Confirm certification issued

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming certification completion never occurs without lawful issuance and recording of certification decisions under published CRT criteria — including certified, not-certified, deferred, and escalated dispositions — without silently replacing those decisions or upgrading them into constitutional amendment, authority transfer, educational success, or governance completion.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who declares recognition “finished” without recording what was decided has not finished care — and a coach who quietly swaps yesterday’s not-certified decision for today’s green seal by closing the folder has abandoned honesty.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion leaves decision identities and dispositions reconstructable.
- Close never substitutes pipeline exit codes or ticket states for published decision dispositions.
- Continuity (EIP-005) forbids erasing or silently replacing prior lawful decisions to simplify the next report.
- Missing required decisions after an affirmed close is a defect.

---

### CRTCO-03 — Preserve audit continuity

**Definition.** Ensure that every material completion judgement leaves a reconstructable trail: which completed compliance activities supported certification, which certification lifecycle stages were completed, which certification decision was issued, which certification records were preserved, and which boundaries remained intact.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who cannot later say *why* certification was declared finished cannot be trusted. Silent “complete” badges destroy student and auditor trust.

**Manifestations:**

- Audit records preserve constitutional references, not only technical job logs or CI annotations.
- Explainability obligations in `COMPLETION_EXPLAINABILITY.md` apply to all material completion judgements.
- Missing CRTC identity against criteria, missing completed-compliance identity, missing decision identity, missing stage identity, or missing record identity is a defect.
- Student / developer / auditor projections share one truth with different vocabulary.

---

### CRTCO-04 — Preserve explainability

**Definition.** Ensure that every material completion judgement can answer the mandatory explainability questions — which completed compliance activities, which stages, which decision, which records, which boundaries — without redefining constitutional meaning or implying amendment / evolution / authority replacement / educational success / governance completion.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who can only say “it’s fully certified somewhere” is performing, not teaching. Speakable completion is care.

**Manifestations:**

- CRTCQ-01…CRTCQ-05 answers are producible for every material affirmed or refused close.
- Explanations never soft-amend law or narrate completion as authority transfer, constitutional evolution, or educational success.
- Audience projections differ in vocabulary, not in constitutional facts.
- Opaque “trust the complete badge” theatre is a defect.

---

### CRTCO-05 — Preserve repeatability

**Definition.** Ensure that affirming completion preserves the constitutional repeatability obligation: same published obligations, same consumed completed compliance, same prerequisites, same stage sequence ⇒ same completion disposition for the same concern scope — so close cannot invent contradictory “done” and “not done” stories from identical published inputs.

**Tutor rationale.** A recognition session that finishes differently for the same published inputs under the same rules is not care; it is lottery dressed as law.

**Manifestations:**

- Completion judgements remain reproducible from published constitutional inputs.
- Hidden stack-specific shortcuts that flip completion for one vendor only are defects.
- Continuity forbids deleting prior lawful stage / decision / record artefacts to force a different close next time.
- Non-deterministic “best effort complete” theatre is a defect.

---

### CRTCO-06 — Preserve implementation neutrality

**Definition.** Ensure that completion judgements speak to constitutional lifecycle fulfilment independently of programming language, framework, datastore, CI vendor, cloud topology, or preferred Runtime A shape — so no stack becomes constitutional by proximity to a “complete” flag.

**Tutor rationale.** A coach who declares only one notebook brand “fully certification-complete” has abandoned educational law for vendor preference.

**Manifestations:**

- CRTC criteria speak to constitutional identities and obligations, not “must use Flask / SQLAlchemy / OpenAPI.”
- Equivalent lawful implementations under the same published law may both reach lawful completion.
- Replacing a stack that still obeys published law does not itself create unlawful completion.
- “Our reference implementation completes this way” is never a substitute for published criteria.

## 4. Objective Interactions

| If … | Then … |
|------|--------|
| CRTCO-01 conflicts with inventing unpublished stages so close can complete | Lifecycle honesty wins — refuse / defer / escalate |
| CRTCO-02 conflicts with mute close that skips or silently replaces decisions | Decision issuance honesty wins |
| CRTCO-03 conflicts with opaque complete badges | Audit continuity wins |
| CRTCO-04 conflicts with unspeakable “trust us” completion | Explainability wins |
| CRTCO-05 conflicts with stack-specific non-reproducible close | Repeatability wins |
| CRTCO-06 conflicts with privileging a reference stack as completion law | Implementation neutrality wins |
| Any CRTCO conflicts with amending law / replacing decisions / initiating evolution / redefining authority / implying educational success / implying governance completion | Non-implication honesty wins — refuse the overclaim |

No supporting objective authorises violating CRTCO-01.

These objectives specialise — and never weaken — Constitution / EIP integrity obligations, Programme VI–VIII authority preservation, WS4 / MS001 CRTO duties, and WS4 / MS002 CRTLO duties for the *completion* horizon.

---

## 5. Non-Objectives

Constitutional certification completion does **not** optimise for:

- constitutional amendment, soft rewrite of specifications, or initiation of constitutional evolution;
- silent replacement of certification decisions already lawfully issued;
- redefinition or transfer of constitutional authority;
- educational quality scoring, mastery grading, or exam-readiness judgement;
- governance completion, programme-finish theatre, or release-governance seals as constitutional law;
- freezing a stack, vendor, or topology as permanent constitutional law;
- CI throughput, flaky-seal suppression, or demo continuity as educational or constitutional success;
- inventing constitutional certainty when required lifecycle stages, prerequisites, decisions, or records remain incomplete;
- collapsing CRTC criteria into one mega-flag that owns meaning;
- making a particular language, framework, store, or CI vendor irreplaceable;
- using Twin / Adaptive / UI / analytics signals as substitutes for published completion criteria;
- treating documentation of a pipeline as optional commentary on whatever code already shipped;
- declaring constitutional amendment, implementation evolution, educational success, governance completion, or authority replacement solely because completion was affirmed;
- collapsing compliance completion (CCC), verification completion (CVC), or traceability completion (CTC) into certification completion.

---

## 6. Closing Statement

> **A concern that becomes “certification complete” by rewriting constitutional law — or by replacing decisions, initiating evolution, redefining authority, inventing educational success, or implying governance completion — is a failed educational system.  
> A concern that confirms only that the required published lifecycle ran and decisions were lawfully issued, recorded, and preserved — with audit continuity, explainability, repeatability, and neutrality intact — is doing its only completion job.**
