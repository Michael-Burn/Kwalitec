## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor who cannot say *which completed compliance activities* supported certification — which certification lifecycle stages were completed, which certification decision was issued, which certification records were preserved, and which limits remained intact — is not closing honestly; they are performing.

After Programmes VI–VIII have made meaning, ownership, tips, context, contracts, evidence, and runtime speech speakable, after WS1–WS3 have made conformance, verification, and compliance speech speakable, and after WS4 / MS001–MS002 have made certification and lifecycle speech speakable, Programme IX still needs one certification-completion-speech answer:

> **“Which completed compliance activities supported certification, which certification lifecycle stages were completed, which certification decision was issued, which certification records were preserved, and which constitutional boundaries remained intact?”**

That answer must cover every material affirmed (or refused) completion without inventing educational success, amending constitutional law, replacing decisions, initiating constitutional evolution, redefining constitutional authority, implying governance completion, transferring authority, minting tips, inventing state, rewriting specifications, freezing a stack as law, or modifying constitutional law.

## 2. What Certification Completion Explainability Is

**Constitutional Certification Completion Explainability** is the constitutional obligation to narrate **fulfilment (or lawful refusal of fulfilment) of the required constitutional certification lifecycle** — not to invent a second tutor voice from CI complete badges, and not to grade the student, amend constitutional law, freeze a stack, or finish governance as constitutional law.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Completed compliance activities** | Named completed compliance activities (CCM + CCC) consumed as certification inputs | Which completed compliance activities supported certification? |
| **Lifecycle stages completed** | Named CRTL-01…CRTL-07 stages executed (or lawfully stopped) for the concern | Which certification lifecycle stages were completed? |
| **Certification decision issued** | Named certification decision with lawful disposition | Which certification decision was issued? |
| **Certification records preserved** | Named reconstructable certification records surviving close | Which certification records were preserved? |
| **Boundaries intact** | Explicit confirmation that forbidden overclaims / amendments / substitutions did not occur | Which constitutional boundaries remained intact? |

Certification Completion Explainability is:

- **fulfilment-centred** — it narrates why CRTC criteria hold (or why close is refused);
- **catalogue-bound** — every justification cites published CRT / CRTL / CRTC identities where applicable;
- **corpus-bound** — every justification cites published specifications, obligations, completed compliance, and named decisions / records;
- **non-legislative** — explanation never becomes a back door to amend corpora;
- **non-evolutionary** — explanation never narrates completion as constitutional evolution or stack freeze;
- **decision-honest** — explanation never silently replaces issued decisions;
- **quality-honest** — complete is never narrated as mastery, readiness, or educational quality;
- **technology-honest** — explanation never presents a stack preference as constitutional law;
- **audience-aware** — student speech and developer/auditor traces differ in vocabulary, not in truth;
- **layer-honest** — it does not replace Programme VI tip warrant speech, Programme VII ownership / workflow / state speech, EIP-002 evidence meaning speech, Programme VIII contract / evidence / validation speech, MS001 conformance speech, MS002 relationship speech, WS2 verification speech, WS3 compliance / completion speech, WS4 / MS001 certification speech, or WS4 / MS002 lifecycle speech;
- **audit-aligned** — explanation components match CRTC-04 reconstruction needs;
- **authority-honest** — explanations must not paper over authority inference or stack canonisation.

Certification Completion Explainability is **not**:

- a rewrite of Constitution / EIP / Programme VI–VIII / WS1 / WS2 / WS3 / WS4 meaning;
- a quality report on learning, coaching, or exam readiness;
- a constitutional amendment instrument or evolution warrant;
- a silent decision-replacement mechanism;
- a governance-finish report as constitutional law;
- a substitute for WS4 / MS001 certification explainability or WS4 / MS002 lifecycle explainability (compose with; do not replace);
- a substitute for WS3 / MS003 compliance completion explainability, WS2 / MS003 verification completion explainability, or WS1 / MS003 traceability completion explainability (compose with; do not collapse);
- a rendering system, UI template, CI badge, seal visualiser, or analytics dashboard;
- a claim that clear completion speech guarantees learning, a pass, conformity, constitutional amendment, or governance finish;
- a licence to present coverage, lint scores, ticket graphs, or pipeline greens as constitutional proof or educational quality.

## 3. Mandatory Explanation Questions

Every material completion judgement (affirm or refuse close) should answer all five questions:

### CRTCQ-01 — Which completed compliance activities supported certification?

Name the primary completed compliance activity identities (CCM determinations + lawful CCC completion references) consumed as inputs under the certification concern — or name which required completed compliance remains unavailable when refusing close. Never narrate invented or incomplete compliance as lawful support. “Compliance happened somewhere” is not a constitutional completed-compliance identity.

### CRTCQ-02 — Which certification lifecycle stages were completed?

Name the primary CRTL-01…CRTL-07 stages completed for the concern — or name which required stages remain incomplete / refused when refusing close. Include lawful early-stop stage outcomes honestly. Never narrate a skipped mandatory stage as completed because a later pipeline job glowed green. “It ran in CI” is not a constitutional stage identity.

### CRTCQ-03 — Which certification decision was issued?

Name the primary certification decision identities and dispositions issued under the concern (certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated) — or name which required decisions remain unissued when refusing close. Never narrate a not-certified / deferred / escalated decision as upgraded by completion, and never narrate a silent replacement of a prior lawful decision. “Looks certified enough” is not a constitutional decision identity.

### CRTCQ-04 — Which certification records were preserved?

Name the primary certification records preserved under the concern (obligation refs, completed-compliance refs, prerequisites, CRT category, decision disposition, stage traces, boundary confirmations) — or name which required records remain missing when refusing close. Never narrate log volume, ticket graphs, or CI annotations alone as constitutional records. Distinguish:

- **decision records** — which disposition was issued and under which CRT type;
- **lifecycle records** — which CRTL stages ran and with what outcomes;
- **continuity honesty** — prior lawful certification history not erased for convenience (EIP-005).

Never narrate preserved records as “student is ready,” “mastery confirmed,” “law updated,” “this stack is now constitutional,” or “governance finished.”

### CRTCQ-05 — Which constitutional boundaries remained intact?

Confirm the hard stops in `COMPLETION_BOUNDARIES.md` that were checked — especially non-amendment of constitutional law, non-replacement of certification decisions, non-initiation of constitutional evolution, non-redefinition of constitutional authority, implementation neutrality, repeatability honesty, and non-equation of completion with educational success, governance completion, or constitutional truth.

## 5. Audience Rules

| Audience | May hear | Must not hear as educational law |
|----------|----------|----------------------------------|
| **Student** | That formal recognition work against published educational rules has finished (or honestly stopped), why a stop occurred when certification is incomplete, and what limits apply — in plain educational language | Internal CRTC IDs as theatre; CI traces; seal badges as “proof you are ready”; complete as mastery, as constitutional amendment, as stack freeze, or as governance finished |
| **Developer / auditor** | Full CRTCQ-01…05 answers, corpus paths, CRT / CRTL / CRTC IDs, completed-compliance refs, decision identities, record inventories, boundary checks, neutrality and non-amendment notes | Product success metrics, pipeline greens, or coverage counts substituted for constitutional refs; completion framed as educational quality, new law, evolution, or authority replacement |

Student and developer traces must describe the **same constitutional truth**. Vocabulary may differ; facts may not.
