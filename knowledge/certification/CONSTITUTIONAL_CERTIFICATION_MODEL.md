## Authority

This document is the constitutional overview of the **Constitutional Certification Model** for Kwalitec.

It is subordinate to the Educational Constitution, Educational Interpretation Principles (EIP), Programme VI educational meaning corpora, Programme VII orchestration corpora, Programme VIII runtime constitutional corpora, the Constitutional Conformance Model (Programme IX / WS1 / MS001), the Constitutional Traceability Model (Programme IX / WS1 / MS002), the Constitutional Verification Model (Programme IX / WS2 and successor lifecycle / completion corpora), and the Constitutional Compliance Model (Programme IX / WS3 / MS001 and successor lifecycle / completion corpora). It governs **when an implementation may be constitutionally certified** — its objectives, recognised certification types, hard boundaries, and explainability. It does not authorise implementation shortcuts that contradict the Constitution, and it does not create constitutional law, modify constitutional specifications, redefine constitutional meaning, replace constitutional authority, perform verification, perform compliance, mint recommendations, invent educational state, or substitute any certification machinery for constitutional authority.

Authority order for constitutional certification:

> Constitution defines educational truth and curriculum primacy.  
> EIP defines evidence classification, continuity, explainability, claim honesty, and mutation rights.  
> Programme VI defines educational meaning and may publish learning / assessment warrants.  
> Programme VII defines orchestration flow, ownership, recommendations, context, and orchestration evidence artefacts.  
> Programme VIII defines how runtime implementations may lawfully execute published law (contracts, events, evidence, services, interfaces).  
> The Constitutional Conformance Model (Programme IX / WS1 / MS001) defines when an implementation may be considered constitutionally conformant to that published law.  
> The Constitutional Traceability Model (Programme IX / WS1 / MS002) defines how published constitutional specifications, implementation artefacts, and conformance findings remain lawfully related.  
> The Constitutional Verification Model (Programme IX / WS2) defines when and how an implementation may be constitutionally verified against published specifications using established traceability.  
> The Constitutional Compliance Model (Programme IX / WS3) defines when an implementation may be considered constitutionally compliant — whether published constitutional obligations have been satisfied — and when that compliance work has lawfully completed.  
> **This Constitutional Certification Model (Programme IX / Workstream 4 / MS001) defines when an implementation may be constitutionally certified — formal recognition that published constitutional obligations have been satisfied based on completed compliance activities.**  
> Downstream certification machinery, CI theatre, product surfaces, Twin, Adaptive, and narration must recognise under these rules — never become constitutional producers by proximity to certification recognitions.

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

> **The Constitutional Certification Model describes the constitutional recognition that an implementation has satisfied published constitutional obligations based on completed compliance activities.  
> It does not create educational meaning, invent ownership, mint tips, invent runtime behaviour, perform verification, perform compliance, or become constitutional law.**

## 2. What Constitutional Certification Is

**Constitutional certification** is the binding recognition by which an **implementation** may be **formally recognised as having satisfied published constitutional obligations**, using **completed compliance activities** (identifiable compliance determinations under CCM-01…CCM-07 and lawful compliance completion under CCC-01…CCC-05, with supporting lineage and authority references intact) — yielding **certification recognitions** and **certification records** that preserve constitutional authority, implementation neutrality, governance integrity, explainability, auditability, and repeatability — without becoming a source of constitutional law, educational truth, a substitute for conformance / verification / compliance meaning, or a freeze of any stack as permanent constitutional authority.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Published constitutional obligation** | A duty published by Constitution, EIP, or Programme VI / VII / VIII (and related WS1 / WS2 / WS3 law that binds assessment) that an implementation must satisfy | Which obligations were satisfied? |
| **Completed compliance activity** | Lawful CCM determination(s) whose required compliance lifecycle has completed under CCC criteria — never invented by certification | Which completed compliance activities were consumed? |
| **Certification category** | A recognised CRT-01…CRT-07 recognition class | Which certification category applies? |
| **Certification recognition** | Formal disposition: certified / not-certified / deferred / escalated — never “new law” and never a freeze of stack authority | Has satisfaction been formally recognised? |
| **Certification record** | Audit-speakable record of obligations, completed compliance consumed, category, supporting authority, and preserved boundaries | Can the recognition be reconstructed? |
| **Constitutional authority** | The exclusive right of published corpora to define educational and execution meaning | Who owns the standard that supports certification? |
| **Implementation neutrality** | Recognition is based on law and completed compliance, not on a preferred stack, vendor, or topology | Remains the stack replaceable? |
| **Governance integrity** | Certification never becomes a back door to amend corpora, absorb authority, or launder incomplete compliance | Was governance preserved? |
| **Explainability** | Obligations, completed compliance, category, authority, and boundaries are speakable | Can the recognition be narrated honestly? |
| **Auditability** | Every material recognition leaves reconstructable constitutional traces | Can the recognition be reconstructed? |
| **Repeatability** | Same published obligations, same completed compliance inputs, same published CRT criteria → same recognition | Can the recognition be reproduced without contradiction? |

Constitutional certification is:

- **recognition-centred** — it formally recognises constitutional satisfaction; it never authors the obligations being recognised;
- **compliance-consuming** — it uses completed compliance activities as inputs; it never invents or rewrites compliance determinations or completion judgements;
- **verification-honest** — it never performs verification and never re-authors CV findings;
- **compliance-honest** — it never performs compliance and never becomes a second determination engine;
- **authority-preserving** — published corpora remain the sole source of constitutional meaning supporting recognition;
- **implementation-neutral** — no particular language, framework, store, or CI tool is required or privileged by certification;
- **catalogue-closed** — only CRT-01…CRT-07 may be applied as constitutional certification types;
- **corpus-bound** — every material recognition cites identifiable published specifications and obligations;
- **completion-bound** — every material recognition requires lawful compliance completion for the recognised scope;
- **audit-capable** — every material recognition leaves reconstructable constitutional traces;
- **explainable** — students, developers, and auditors can answer which obligations were recognised from which completed compliance under which category and authority;
- **non-legislative** — a certification recognition never becomes constitutional law or a soft amendment;
- **non-substitutive** — certification machinery never becomes the constitutional standard for the next judgement.

Constitutional certification is **not**:

- a second Educational Constitution, EIP, Programme VI / VII / VIII, WS1, WS2, or WS3 amendment path;
- a licence to reinterpret coach meaning, ownership maps, tip warrants, runtime contracts, conformance criteria, traceability meaning, verification meaning, or compliance meaning in certification prose;
- a testing framework, CI/CD pipeline, GitHub Action, linter policy, OpenAPI checker, or seal registry that invents satisfaction;
- a claim that “certified” guarantees learning, mastery, exam readiness, product success, perpetual conformity, permanent verification, or permanent compliance;
- a substitute for MS001 conformance evaluation — CRT recognises satisfaction from completed compliance; CC defines when conformity may be claimed;
- a substitute for MS002 traceability — CT preserves relationships; CRT preserves them in records;
- a substitute for WS2 verification — CV evaluates evidence against requirements; CRT never performs verification;
- a substitute for WS3 compliance — CCM determines obligation status and CCC confirms completion; CRT consumes those completed activities;
- a substitute for Programme VIII evidence validation (EV-xx) — EV validates educational evidence eligibility; CRT recognises *constitutional satisfaction*;
- a substitute for Educational Validation Framework quality review — quality release remains a distinct horizon;
- a freeze of implementations, stacks, vendors, or releases as constitutional law.

## 3. Certification Posture (Constitutional, Not Technical)

Every material constitutional certification recognition travels through four constitutional phases. These are **normative certification obligations**, not an implementation architecture.

```
Identify published constitutional obligations and supporting constitutional authority
        │
        ▼
Consume completed compliance activities (determinations + lawful completion)
        │
        ▼
Recognise constitutional satisfaction under CRT-01…CRT-07
        │
        ├── not-certified / deferred / escalated (lawful recognition — law unchanged)
        │
        └── certified → Emit recognition and preserve certification records
                │
                ▼
        Explain obligations / completed compliance / category / authority / boundaries
```

| Phase | Constitutional meaning | Hard stop if … |
|-------|------------------------|----------------|
| **Identify** | Name only published constitutional obligations and the constitutional authority that supports recognition | Standard is unpublished custom, tribal knowledge, or “how main works today” |
| **Consume** | Use identifiable completed compliance activities as inputs | Compliance would be invented, incomplete, rewritten, or treated as optional theatre |
| **Recognise** | Apply CRT-01…CRT-07 to formally recognise (or refuse) constitutional satisfaction | Recognition would invent law, rewrite specs, redefine meaning, privilege a stack as law, re-run verification, or re-run compliance |
| **Record** | Produce certification records and audit-speakable trails without amending corpora | Path would treat recognitions as constitutional amendments or freeze an implementation as authority |

> **Certification never “best-effort invents” a fifth phase called law-making, reinterpretation, verification, compliance, or stack canonisation.**

## 5. Core Responsibilities

The Constitutional Certification Model is constitutionally responsible for:

| Responsibility | Meaning |
|----------------|---------|
| **Define certification as recognition** | Bind recognitions as formal recognition of constitutional satisfaction from completed compliance (`CONSTITUTIONAL_CERTIFICATION_MODEL.md`) |
| **Bind objectives** | Enforce satisfaction recognition, governance integrity, explainability, auditability, and repeatability (`CERTIFICATION_OBJECTIVES.md`) |
| **Close the certification catalogue** | Permit only recognised CRT-01…CRT-07 types (`CERTIFICATION_TYPES.md`) |
| **Draw hard boundaries** | Forbid law creation, law modification, meaning redefinition, authority substitution, verification performance, and compliance performance (`CERTIFICATION_BOUNDARIES.md`) |
| **Require explainability** | Make obligations, completed compliance, category, authority, and boundaries speakable (`CERTIFICATION_EXPLAINABILITY.md`) |
| **Preserve layering** | Keep certification subordinate to Constitution, EIP, Programmes VI–VIII, WS1 conformance / traceability, WS2 verification, and WS3 compliance |

### 5.1 Binding non-responsibility

The Constitutional Certification Model must **not**:

- invent new constitutional certification types outside CRT-01…CRT-07 without a Programme IX amendment;
- create, modify, or deprecate constitutional specifications under Programmes VI–VIII (or Constitution / EIP / WS1 / WS2 / WS3) via recognitions;
- redefine Programme VI educational meaning, EIP claim classes, CC criteria, CT lineage meaning, CV verification meaning, or CCM compliance meaning during recognition;
- author runtime behaviour, services, APIs, or UI under the label “certified”;
- perform verification or invent / rewrite verification findings;
- perform compliance or invent / rewrite compliance determinations or completion judgements;
- freeze implementations, stacks, vendors, releases, or pipelines as constitutional law;
- substitute a preferred implementation, stack, or vendor as the constitutional standard;
- invent completed compliance activities to force recognition;
- invent traceability relationships to force recognition;
- implement Runtime A, testing frameworks, CI/CD, GitHub Actions, Python, Flask, SQLAlchemy, REST, OpenAPI, or infrastructure;
- present green pipelines, coverage percentages, linter scores, or seals as constitutional law, educational truth, or substitutes for completed compliance.

## 6. Educational Purpose

The Constitutional Certification Model exists so that:

1. **Law remains upstream of recognition** — satisfaction is recognised against published specifications and completed compliance; recognitions do not quietly become the specifications.
2. **Compliance remains honest** — certification consumes completed compliance; it does not invent or rewrite determinations / completion so a seal “passes.”
3. **Verification remains untouched** — certification never becomes a second verification engine.
4. **Compliance remains untouched** — certification never becomes a second determination engine.
5. **Conformance, verification, and compliance remain supported, not replaced** — certification speaks to formal recognition without becoming a second conformity engine, a second verification engine, or a second compliance bureau.
6. **Authority remains honest** — no team may treat a certified artefact as the new constitutional source.
7. **Implementations remain replaceable** — certification does not freeze Flask, SQLAlchemy, a particular API shape, or a CI vendor as law.
8. **Governance integrity remains honest** — certification cannot launder incomplete compliance or soft-amend corpora.
9. **Audit remains possible** — every material recognition can be reconstructed against named obligations, completed compliance, category, authority, and boundaries.
10. **Explainability remains intact** — certification speech describes recognition of satisfaction; it does not redefine what learning means.
11. **Students remain protected** — product theatre cannot launder unsatisfied educational obligations as “constitutionally certified.”
