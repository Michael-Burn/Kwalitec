## 1. Purpose

Without transition law, recovery becomes a permanent mode, a silent plan rewrite, or a confusing sequence of unexplained “modes.”

With transition law, an expert tutor can:

- end recovery when sustainable progress returns;
- change pathway when the disruption profile evolves;
- escalate when local restorative journeys cannot restore a feasible plan;
- keep Daily Coach, Learning Coach, and Master Planner authorities intact across those moves.

---

## 2. Transition Principles

1. **Continuity of learner history.** Pathway change does not wipe educational identity (EIP-005).  
2. **Explain before switch.** Material transitions are speakable under `PATHWAY_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.  
3. **One primary pathway at a time** in student speech.  
4. **Success ends recovery posture** — recovery is not a lifestyle brand.  
5. **Insufficient recovery is educational information** — not moral failure.  
6. **Escalate rather than fake local success** when envelopes break.  
7. **Do not independently replan** during transition — Master Planner / Scheduling own structural change.  
8. **Daily Coach remains day authority** under the current authorised contract before and after transition.  
9. **Learning Coach evidence persists** across pathway change — restoring attendance mid-pathway is not proof learning progressed.  
10. **No streak or score gates.** Transitions are educational judgements, not numeric unlocks.

## 3. Transition Classes

| Class | Meaning | Typical destination |
|-------|---------|---------------------|
| **T1 — Resume normal coaching** | Pathway success; recovery episode ends | Ordinary Daily Coach under Active-class (or successor) plan rhythm |
| **T2 — Pathway change** | Same recovery episode; different RP better fits evolving profile | Another RP-XX as primary |
| **T3 — Escalation for structural restoration** | Recovery alone inadequate inside current envelopes | Rescheduling / Master Planner / Daily Coach escalation pathways (RS-08 family) |
| **T4 — Coordinated Learning Coach handoff** | Remaining problem is learning obstacle / intervention, not disruption continuity | Learning Coach diagnosis / LI ownership; recovery posture may end or become supporting |
| **T5 — Hold / gather** | Warrant or success unclear | Stay on gentle pathway or continuity repair; gather evidence — do not invent crisis or false completion |

## 4. T1 — Successful Recovery Returning to Normal Coaching

### 4.1 Educational meaning

The restorative journey has restored sustainable progress enough that ordinary plan-faithful Daily Coach rhythm is again the right educational posture.

### 4.2 Resume criteria (qualitative)

Resume normal coaching when **all** of the following can be honestly affirmed:

1. Sustainable momentum and consistency have returned relative to Profile capacity (RO-01, RO-02).  
2. Confidence no longer blocks lawful restart (RO-05), if that was in focus.  
3. The Canonical Study Plan (or Adapted / Recovered successor still within envelopes) can again be coached as ordinary Active-class rhythm.  
4. No active RT-04 regression warrant remains unaddressed — **or** Learning Coach has taken ownership of remaining learning response (T4).  
5. Explainability can honestly say normal progression has resumed.

### 4.3 Lawful effects

- End recovery episode / primary pathway posture.  
- Daily Coach returns to ordinary day-priority selection under the authorised plan.  
- Retain learner history; do not narrate the recovery as wiped identity or as mastery earned.

### 4.4 Unlawful effects

- Declaring success because the student “completed a pathway checklist.”  
- Declaring success because a streak returned.  
- Declaring mastery / exam readiness from resume.  
- Silently keeping punishment catch-up after claiming resume.

## 5. T2 — Insufficient Recovery Requiring a Different Pathway

### 5.1 Educational meaning

The current primary pathway is no longer the best educational match — either because it was provisionally wrong, or because recovery progress changed the leading disruption character.

### 5.2 When pathway change is warranted

Change primary pathway when:

1. **Success signs for the current pathway are not emerging** after a fair restorative attempt, and another RP better matches the observed profile; or  
2. **Profile evolved** (e.g. confidence restored → prolonged return logistics remain; return motion restored → evidence now shows foundation risk); or  
3. **Evidence clarified** a different leading problem (thin warrant → supported slip reading).

### 5.3 Lawful pathway-change patterns (illustrative)

| From | Toward | Educational reason |
|------|--------|--------------------|
| RP-03 | RP-01 / RP-02 / RP-06 | Freeze lifted; continuity journey can lead |
| RP-01 | RP-02 | Gap proved longer / restart needs prolonged care |
| RP-01 / RP-02 | RP-05 | Capacity infeasibility becomes the leading break |
| RP-01 / RP-02 / RP-06 | RP-04 / RP-07 | Motion returned; evidence now warrants knowledge / foundation work |
| RP-02 / RP-06 | RP-08 | Plan emphasis is exam-facing and must lead the restorative story |
| Any local RP | same family with different emphasis | Supporting → primary swap when speech needs a clearer story |

### 5.4 Discipline

- Re-run selection steps in `PATHWAY_SELECTION.md` (do not leap by habit).  
- Keep one primary in speech; explain why the recovery approach is changing.  
- Preserve continuity: “same destination; clearer restorative route.”  
- If change is needed because **envelopes** cannot hold any local pathway, use **T3** — do not rotate pathways as fake progress.

### 5.5 Insufficient recovery without a better local pathway

If no local RP can honestly restore progress:

> Prefer **T3 escalation** over endless T2 rotation.

## 6. T3 — Escalation Where Recovery Alone Is Inadequate

### 6.1 Educational meaning

Local restorative pathways cannot restore a feasible, plan-faithful journey inside current envelopes. Structural change is required through established planning pathways.

### 6.2 Escalation triggers (must escalate)

Escalate when any of the following hold:

1. Backlog or absence such that any local pathway would require catch-up that breaches intensity envelopes.  
2. Ongoing capacity reduction that invalidates remaining plan feasibility (RT-05) despite RP-05 attempts.  
3. Desire or need to consume protected revision for unfinished first-pass.  
4. Sitting / exam date change or strategy / blueprint supersession.  
5. Recovery needed but no recovery capacity remains and lighter load still infeasible.  
6. Persistent disruption despite repeated lawful local pathways / strategies.  
7. Exam-facing remaining journey infeasible under RP-08 local emphasis.  
8. Plan invalid, draft-only, or superseded while surfaces still request recovery coaching under the old contract.

### 6.3 Authority-preserving escalation path

```
Recovery pathway judges local restoration inadequate
        │
        ▼
RS-08-class escalation signal (Recovery Coach)
        │
        ├─→ Scheduling / Rescheduling (allocation, recovery capacity placement)
        │
        └─→ Master Planner (educational envelopes / strategy / new Canonical Study Plan)
                │
                ▼
        Adapted / Recovered / new authorised contract published
                │
                ▼
        Daily Coach coaches under the updated contract
        Recovery Coach may open a new warrant/pathway under the new contract if still needed
```

Hard rules:

> **Recovery Coach identifies the need; it does not absorb Scheduling or Master Planner authority.**  
> **Daily Coach escalation classes (E1/E2 family) remain the day-level structural handoff vocabulary — Recovery Coach aligns with them; it does not replace them.**

### 6.4 Continuity during escalation

- Continue supportive RO-05 speech — escalation is not abandonment.  
- Do not invent interim unofficial plans while waiting for structural authority.  
- Learner history persists (EIP-005).  
- After structural update, reassess warrant and pathway under the new contract (may be T1 if the new contract already embodies restoration).

---

## 7. T4 — Coordinated Learning Coach Handoff

### 7.1 Educational meaning

Disruption continuity is sufficiently restored (or was never the primary barrier), and the remaining educational problem is genuine learning progression / obstacle / intervention.

### 7.2 Lawful handoff cues

- Student attends sustainably, but Learning Coach evidence shows stalled progression.  
- RP-04 / RP-07 surface foundation or retrieval concerns that resolve into an explicit LOB diagnosis.  
- Exam-technique issues under continuous study require LI ownership rather than recovery framing.

### 7.3 Discipline

- End or demote recovery pathway posture when disruption warrant ends.  
- Do not keep recovery speech as a soft substitute for diagnosis.  
- Do not claim Learning Coach success from recovery pathway completion.

---

## 8. T5 — Hold / Gather

When warrant or pathway match is unclear:

- Prefer gentle RP-01 / continuity repair over dramatic pathway switches.  
- Gather Educational Evidence rather than inventing RT-04 / RP-04 certainty.  
- Correct uncertainty beats false crisis and false completion alike.

## 10. Continuity and Authority Checklist (Every Material Transition)

Before enacting T1–T4, confirm:

| Check | Fail action |
|-------|-------------|
| Is learner history preserved in speech and meaning? | Rewrite narration; never invent wipe |
| Does Daily Coach still own today’s priority under the current contract? | Do not commandeer the day via pathway speech |
| Is long-term intent change required? | Use T3 — do not mutate plan in Recovery Coach |
| Is mastery being claimed from transition? | Refuse; keep EIP-006 honesty |
| Is a learning obstacle now primary? | Use T4 |
| Can the student hear why the approach is changing? | Produce explainability first |

---

## 11. Anti-Transition Patterns (Forbidden)

- Permanent recovery mode with no resume criteria  
- Silent pathway oscillation without educational reason  
- Escalation theatre that still packs calendars inside Recovery Coach  
- “Pathway completed” mastery speech  
- Returning to normal coaching while still demanding punishment catch-up  
- Using transition to bypass Learning Coach diagnosis
