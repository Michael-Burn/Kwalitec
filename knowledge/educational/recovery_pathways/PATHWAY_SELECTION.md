## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor faced with meaningful disruption does not grab a familiar “catch-up mode.” The tutor asks what broke educationally, which restorative goods must lead, whether the journey can stay inside plan envelopes, what evidence supports the reading, what success would look like, and when another pathway — or escalation — would be wiser.

This document records that selection discipline so Recovery Coach pathway recommendations remain educationally honest.

---

## 2. Selection Principles

1. **Warrant first.** No RP without an explicit recovery warrant (`RECOVERY_TRIGGERS.md`). Temporary fluctuation → ordinary Daily Coach continuity, not a pathway.
2. **Match disruption character, not calendar guilt.** Missed cells alone never select RP-04, RP-07, or RP-08.
3. **One primary in speech.** Supporting pathways may exist; students hear one primary recovery story.
4. **Smallest sufficient journey.** Prefer RP-01 when a short restorative restart suffices.
5. **Confidence before content when freeze blocks return.** If shame/freeze prevents any restart, prefer RP-03 before denser knowledge pathways.
6. **Objective specificity.** Name which RO-XX goods the pathway advances.
7. **Envelope awareness.** If restoration cannot fit Canonical Study Plan / Profile capacity honestly, prefer escalation posture over fake local pathway success.
8. **Evidence honesty.** Regression / foundation claims require Educational Evidence warrant — never absence inference alone.
9. **Sibling humility.** If the primary problem is a learning obstacle under continuous study, defer to Learning Coach rather than forcing a recovery pathway.
10. **No numerical scoring.** No weights, ranks, fitness scores, or priority maths as educational law.

---

## 3. Selection Procedure (Educational)

The tutor follows this order. Implementations may later encode equivalents; they may not skip educational steps.

| Step | Action | Output |
|------|--------|--------|
| 1 | Confirm **recovery warrant** | RT-XX + fluctuation rejected |
| 2 | If temporary fluctuation | **No pathway** — Daily Coach continuity repair |
| 3 | Name **disruption profile** in educational language | Continuity length / confidence / knowledge / capacity / cadence / foundations / exam-phase timing |
| 4 | Name **RO-XX goods** that must lead | Priority among RO-01…RO-05 |
| 5 | List **candidate pathways** from Catalogue matched to the profile | Shortlist of RP-XX |
| 6 | Eliminate candidates that **must not be used** given evidence, Profile, envelopes, Learning Coach posture | Reduced shortlist |
| 7 | Prefer **smallest sufficient** primary pathway | Primary RP |
| 8 | Name **emphasised strategies** (RS-XX) inside MS001 boundaries | Tactic set |
| 9 | If no local pathway can restore honesty | Transition to **escalation** (`PATHWAY_TRANSITIONS.md`) — do not invent a pathway that silently replans |
| 10 | State **expected signs of successful recovery** | Success cues |
| 11 | Produce **explainability** under `PATHWAY_EXPLAINABILITY.md` | Student-facing account |

Skipping from missed ticks directly to a pathway is unlawful.

---

## 4. Preferential Reading Order (Qualitative)

When multiple pathways fit, apply this qualitative order — **not** a numeric rank:

1. **RP-03** if confidence/shame freeze would block any return  
2. **RP-01** if the disruption is short and foundations/confidence largely intact  
3. **RP-02** if the absence is prolonged relative to rhythm  
4. **RP-05** if capacity/intensity infeasibility is the leading break  
5. **RP-06** if clustered misses / broken cadence lead (without long single absence)  
6. **RP-07** if foundations are unsafe for further first-pass ambition  
7. **RP-04** if evidence warrants knowledge slip / retention risk as the leading educational problem  
8. **RP-08** when disruption lands under authorised exam-facing plan emphasis — may combine with the above as primary when exam-phase constraints dominate the restorative story  

Envelope breakage at any point → escalate rather than force a local pathway.

## 5. Per-Pathway Selection Cards

For every catalogue pathway: suitable disruption profile, educational objective, evidence required, expected signs of successful recovery, and situations where another pathway is preferable.

---

### RP-01 — Short Disruption Recovery

| Field | Provision |
|-------|-----------|
| **Suitable disruption profile** | Brief meaningful interruption; plan still feasible; confidence and foundations largely intact; ordinary resume is slightly insufficient |
| **Educational objective** | Restore safe momentum quickly (RO-01, RO-05) and re-enter ordinary rhythm without a long recovery arc |
| **Evidence required** | Observed short gap relative to Profile rhythm; absence of significant regression warrant; plan envelopes still honest; fluctuation rejected for a documented educational reason (e.g. clustered short misses or a short break that still needs intentional restart) |
| **Expected signs of successful recovery** | Student restarts lawful sittings; shame speech absent; ordinary Daily Coach plan jobs become workable again within a short restorative window |
| **Prefer another pathway when** | Absence is prolonged (RP-02); freeze dominates (RP-03); capacity broken (RP-05); cadence chronically broken (RP-06); foundations / slip evidence leads (RP-07 / RP-04); exam-facing constraints dominate (RP-08); envelopes break (escalate) |

---

### RP-02 — Prolonged Absence Recovery

| Field | Provision |
|-------|-----------|
| **Suitable disruption profile** | Extended break from study; immediate full-intensity resume unsafe or unrealistic |
| **Educational objective** | Rebuild sustainable return and confidence (RO-01, RO-05), then consistency (RO-02), protecting understanding as evidence warrants (RO-03/RO-04) |
| **Evidence required** | Multi-day / multi-week gap relative to planned rhythm; optional explicit illness/leave signal; Profile capacity reading for safe restart intensity; thin history after return may justify gentle restart **without** claiming RT-04 regression |
| **Expected signs of successful recovery** | Sustainable sittings resume; intensity can step toward ordinary plan rhythm without collapse; confidence speech improves; any later knowledge/foundation concerns are handled by transition rather than denial |
| **Prefer another pathway when** | Gap is short and gentle restart suffices (RP-01); freeze alone blocks even light restart (lead with RP-03); capacity permanently invalidates envelopes (RP-05 then escalate); exam-facing phase constraints dominate the restorative story (RP-08) |

---

### RP-03 — Confidence Rebuilding

| Field | Provision |
|-------|-----------|
| **Suitable disruption profile** | Disruption left shame, abandonment risk, or freeze; belief that return is possible is the primary blocker |
| **Educational objective** | Preserve and rebuild learner confidence (RO-05) so momentum (RO-01) can restart |
| **Evidence required** | Student-facing freeze / shame signals in reflection or coaching dialogue; failed restart attempts despite capacity being physically available; disruption warrant still present — confidence pathway is not a pep-talk substitute for no disruption |
| **Expected signs of successful recovery** | Student engages a lawful next job; recovery speech loses shame framing; motion becomes possible enough to transition to RP-01 / RP-02 / RP-06 as needed |
| **Prefer another pathway when** | Confidence is intact and the issue is prolonged absence logistics (RP-02), capacity (RP-05), or evidenced knowledge slip (RP-04); a Learning Coach confidence-calibration obstacle exists under continuous study without disruption warrant — then Learning Coach owns the response |

---

### RP-04 — Knowledge Reinforcement

| Field | Provision |
|-------|-----------|
| **Suitable disruption profile** | After disruption, evidence suggests meaningful slip or retention risk on previously progressing scope; attendance alone is no longer the leading problem |
| **Educational objective** | Minimise regression and protect long-term understanding (RO-04, RO-03) via targeted reinforcement |
| **Evidence required** | Accumulated Educational Evidence of slip (retrieval / application / reflection trail) — **not** calendar absence alone; optional Learning Coach decay / inconsistency posture in disruption context; named at-risk scope preferred over vague “everything” |
| **Expected signs of successful recovery** | Improved unaided retrieval / application on named at-risk scope; RT-04 warrant softens under honest evidence; student can resume broader plan jobs without pretending slip never happened |
| **Prefer another pathway when** | Evidence is thin after return (stay on RP-01/RP-02 and gather warrant); foundations are the structural issue (RP-07); Learning Coach diagnoses an obstacle requiring intervention meaning (coordinate — do not bypass); no disruption warrant (not a recovery pathway) |

---

### RP-05 — Workload Rebalancing

| Field | Provision |
|-------|-----------|
| **Suitable disruption profile** | Sustained availability / capacity change or overload such that ordinary intensity is infeasible |
| **Educational objective** | Restore feasible intensity so momentum and consistency can return (RO-01, RO-02, RO-05) |
| **Evidence required** | Profile capacity mismatch vs remaining sitting ambition; repeated infeasible Daily Coach days after local scope reduction; lasting availability change (not a single busy day) |
| **Expected signs of successful recovery** | Sustainable sittings under rebalanced load; step-up toward ordinary intensity becomes thinkable; or honest escalation produces an updated authorised plan under which coaching resumes |
| **Prefer another pathway when** | Capacity is fine and the issue is short absence (RP-01), prolonged absence logistics with recoverable capacity (RP-02), or cadence without intensity mismatch (RP-06); remaining journey cannot fit after temporary rebalance (escalate — do not fake RP-05 success) |

---

### RP-06 — Consistency Restoration

| Field | Provision |
|-------|-----------|
| **Suitable disruption profile** | Clustered missed sessions or stop–start cadence threatening consistency; not primarily one long absence block |
| **Educational objective** | Rebuild reliable continuity (RO-02) and workable momentum (RO-01) |
| **Evidence required** | Pattern of repeated misses across successive days/weeks; backlog growth that one-day catch-up cannot honestly clear; Profile capacity still potentially workable if cadence returns |
| **Expected signs of successful recovery** | Missed-session clusters reduce; study feels predictable enough to trust; Daily Coach can select ordinary plan jobs without perpetual crisis mode |
| **Prefer another pathway when** | One prolonged absence dominates (RP-02); intensity envelopes are broken (RP-05 / escalate); freeze blocks restart (RP-03); single miss only (no pathway — temporary fluctuation) |

---

### RP-07 — Prerequisite Reconstruction

| Field | Provision |
|-------|-----------|
| **Suitable disruption profile** | Disruption left earlier foundations unsafe for further first-pass ambition |
| **Educational objective** | Protect long-term understanding and limit regression (RO-03, RO-04) by reconstructing prerequisites within lawful repair windows |
| **Evidence required** | Foundation weakness on named earlier scope (practice / reflection / Learning Coach prerequisite-related diagnosis in disruption context); plan-allowed repair windows exist or escalation path is clear; syllabus-faithful prerequisite talk only |
| **Expected signs of successful recovery** | Stronger retrieval / application on foundation scope; Learning Coach prerequisite-related trail improves when coordinated; later topics can be attempted without coverage theatre on brittle bases |
| **Prefer another pathway when** | Foundations are intact and slip is local retention (RP-04); problem is continuity not structure (RP-01/RP-02/RP-06); obstacle exists without disruption continuity need (Learning Coach / LI); desire to reorder phases (escalate — not a pathway privilege) |

---

### RP-08 — Exam-Phase Recovery

| Field | Provision |
|-------|-----------|
| **Suitable disruption profile** | Meaningful disruption while Canonical Study Plan emphasis is exam-facing (revision / exam technique / late consolidation) |
| **Educational objective** | Restore continuity and protect understanding under exam-facing intent (RO-03, RO-04, RO-01) without panic cram or stolen revision |
| **Evidence required** | Plan/blueprint-authorised exam-facing phase emphasis; disruption warrant; honesty about remaining calendar feasibility; refuse mastery/readiness speech from recovery alone |
| **Expected signs of successful recovery** | Student resumes lawful exam-facing jobs sustainably; protected revision intent preserved; either ordinary exam-facing coaching resumes or escalation yields an honest updated plan |
| **Prefer another pathway when** | Plan is not in exam-facing emphasis (use RP-01…RP-07 as profile dictates); remaining exam journey is infeasible under any local restorative emphasis (escalate immediately); Learning Coach owns exam-technique obstacle under continuous study without disruption warrant |

## 6. Stacked Disruption

When disruption profiles stack (e.g. prolonged absence + confidence freeze + foundation risk):

1. Name the **primary** pathway using §4 preferential reading.  
2. Optionally note a **supporting** pathway for tutor/audit clarity.  
3. Speak **one** primary journey to the student.  
4. Plan transitions (`PATHWAY_TRANSITIONS.md`) rather than running parallel contradictory stories (e.g. do not simultaneously demand full knowledge reinforcement and confidence-only freeze care without sequencing).

Example sequencing (illustrative):

> RP-03 (unfreeze) → RP-02 (prolonged return) → RP-04 or RP-07 (if evidence warrants) → resume normal coaching — or escalate if envelopes break.

---

## 7. Interaction with Learning Coach Selection

| If Learning Coach posture is… | Pathway selection must… |
|-------------------------------|--------------------------|
| No obstacle; disruption continuity problem | Select recovery pathway normally |
| Obstacle diagnosed in disruption context | May select RP-04 / RP-07 / RP-03 as appropriate **and** consume LI meaning — never replace diagnosis |
| Obstacle under continuous study, no disruption warrant | **Do not** open a recovery pathway as substitute |
| Insufficient warrant / conflicting learning evidence | Do not invent RP-04/RP-07 certainty; prefer gentle continuity pathways + evidence gathering |

---

## 8. Anti-Selection Patterns (Forbidden)

- Selecting a pathway because it maps neatly to a UI card or feature flag  
- Selecting RP-04 / RP-07 from missed calendar cells alone  
- Selecting RP-08 to justify panic cramming outside authorised exam-facing intent  
- Selecting any pathway to “motivate” without warrant  
- Using numeric scores, ranks, or ensemble weights as educational law  
- Selecting a pathway that requires plan rewrite while pretending it is local recovery
