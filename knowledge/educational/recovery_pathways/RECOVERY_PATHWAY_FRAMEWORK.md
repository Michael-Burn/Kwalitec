## Authority

This document is the constitutional overview of **recovery pathways** for Kwalitec.

It is subordinate to the Educational Constitution, specialised educational models, and the Educational Recovery Model (MS001). It governs **how Kwalitec classifies the type of restorative journey appropriate after meaningful disruption** while remaining faithful to the long-term educational plan. It does not authorise implementation shortcuts that contradict the Constitution, and it does not invent disruption, evidence, strategy, or mastery meaning absent from upstream models.

Authority order for recovery pathways:

> Constitution defines educational truth and curriculum primacy.  
> Knowledge & Mastery defines coverage ≠ understanding ≠ mastery.  
> Evidence Model defines what may warrant educational claims.  
> Continuity Standard preserves rightful educational history across plan change.  
> Master Planner publishes the Canonical Study Plan and owns long-term educational envelopes.  
> Daily Coach owns *what is most educationally valuable today* under that contract.  
> Learning Coach owns longitudinal progression, obstacle diagnosis, and learning interventions.  
> Educational Recovery Model (MS001) defines *whether* recovery is warranted and *which* restorative objectives / strategies apply.  
> **This Recovery Pathway Framework (Workstream 4 / MS002) defines what type of recovery journey is educationally appropriate for the disruption.**  
> Downstream coaching and narration surfaces must consume this educational reasoning — never replace planning with catch-up theatre.

## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not apply one generic “catch-up mode” to every disruption. A short interruption after an otherwise healthy week, a multi-week illness, a confidence collapse on return, fragile foundations after a gap, an unsustainable workload, broken study cadence, missing prerequisites, and disruption during exam-facing phases each call for a **different restorative journey** — while the long-term destination remains the Master Planner’s authorised intent.

After a lawful recovery warrant, the tutor answers one living educational question:

> **“What type of recovery is most educationally appropriate for this disruption?”**

That answer must fit the disruption profile, pursue MS001 recovery objectives, remain faithful to Canonical Study Plan educational intent, preserve Daily Coach and Learning Coach authorities, and escalate honestly when recovery alone cannot restore a feasible journey.

This document records that tutor posture so every future Recovery Coach subsystem has a single educational reference for *pathway meaning*.

> **The Recovery Pathway Framework describes educational pathway reasoning.  
> It does not implement Runtime A logic, algorithms, analytics, or Twin writers.**

## 2. What a Recovery Pathway Is

A **recovery pathway** is a named **educational journey archetype** that describes the *kind* of restorative coaching appropriate after meaningful disruption — not a calendar packing algorithm, not a score band, and not a second study plan.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Disruption profile** | Educational reading of what broke continuity / capacity / confidence / knowledge safety | What kind of disruption is this? |
| **Recovery pathway** | Named restorative journey type matched to that profile | What type of recovery fits? |
| **Pathway objective** | Educational goods this pathway primarily restores (RO-XX) | What are we restoring by this route? |
| **Pathway tactics** | Lawful MS001 strategies emphasised inside the pathway | How do we walk this route? |
| **Pathway success** | Educational signs that this pathway has done its job | When may we transition or resume? |
| **Pathway limit** | Situations where another pathway — or escalation — is preferable | When is this the wrong route? |

A pathway is:

- **profile-matched** — selected from disruption character, not operational convenience;
- **objective-bound** — advances RO-01…RO-05 rather than calendar theatre;
- **plan-faithful** — preserves Canonical Study Plan educational intent wherever possible;
- **execution-adaptive** — may change near-term emphasis and restorative framing within envelopes;
- **strategy-consuming** — uses MS001 RS-XX tactics; does not invent a parallel strategy law;
- **transition-aware** — may move to another pathway, resume normal coaching, or escalate;
- **explainable** — students understand why this pathway, how it helps, and when ordinary progression resumes;
- **non-scoring** — qualitative educational judgement only.

A pathway is **not**:

- a substitute for Master Planner planning or strategy selection;
- a licence to invent overload or rewrite envelopes “to finish the pathway”;
- inference of mastery from pathway compliance, rest, or resumed ticks;
- a bypass of Daily Coach day authority or Learning Coach evidence/diagnosis layers;
- a duplicate of MS001 triggers or strategies under new labels without educational distinction;
- a claim that completing a pathway guarantees a pass.

## 3. Binding Distinction: Pathway vs Neighbours

| Layer | Educational job | Unlawful collapse |
|-------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Trigger (RT)** | Warrant that recovery is needed | Selecting a pathway without a warrant |
| **Objective (RO)** | Name goods to restore | Treating backlog clearance as an objective |
| **Pathway (RP)** | Name the *type* of restorative journey | Equating pathway with a new long-term plan |
| **Strategy (RS)** | Name restorative tactics inside the journey | Using strategies to rewrite strategy/plan authority |
| **Daily Coach** | Own today’s educational priority under the contract | Letting pathway speech commandeer the day |
| **Learning Coach** | Own progression / obstacle / intervention meaning | Using a recovery pathway to skip diagnosis |
| **Master Planner / Scheduling** | Own structural envelope and allocation change | Absorbing replan inside pathway “completion” |

Hard rule:

> **Pathways classify restorative journeys.  
> They do not mint plans, strategies, mastery, or day priorities.**

## 4. What the Pathway Framework Produces

Given a **lawful recovery warrant** and **accumulated educational evidence**, the Pathway Framework produces:

| Output family | Role |
|---------------|------|
| **Primary pathway** | Named RP-XX journey type |
| **Supporting pathway (optional)** | Secondary archetype when disruption is stacked — speech still names one primary |
| **Pathway rationale** | Educational link from disruption profile → pathway |
| **Emphasised strategies** | MS001 RS-XX tactics that serve this pathway |
| **Success signs** | Educational cues for pathway completion / transition |
| **Transition posture** | Stay / change pathway / resume normal coaching / escalate |
| **Pathway explainability** | Plain-language account for the student |

It answers:

| Question | Educational answer |
|----------|-------------------|
| What type of recovery? | Named pathway from the catalogue |
| Why this type? | Disruption profile + evidence + objectives |
| How does it restore progress? | Pathway objective + emphasised strategies |
| May Recovery Coach enact this alone? | Only within MS001 boundaries; else escalate |
| When does this pathway end? | Success signs, transition rules, or escalation |

## 5. Educational Purpose

The Pathway Framework exists so that:

1. **Different disruptions receive different restorative journeys** — not one catch-up mode.
2. **Pathway choice remains educational** — derived from tutoring principles, not UI or optimiser convenience.
3. **Long-term educational intent survives interruption** — pathways repair how the journey is walked; they do not invent a new destination.
4. **Confidence and continuity are protected** — students hear a coherent recovery story, not opaque mode switches.
5. **Authority stays layered** — pathway adaptation of execution never silently becomes replanning or mastery rewrite.
6. **Transitions are honest** — successful resume, pathway change, and escalation are named educational events.

## 6. Core Responsibilities

The Pathway Framework is educationally responsible for:

| Responsibility | Meaning |
|----------------|---------|
| **Catalogue lawful pathways** | Name IFoA-appropriate recovery journey types |
| **Select a pathway after warrant** | Match disruption profile without numerical scoring |
| **Bind pathways to objectives** | Keep RO-XX goods primary |
| **Consume strategies honestly** | Emphasise RS-XX within boundaries |
| **Govern transitions** | Resume, pathway change, or escalate with continuity |
| **Preserve authority boundaries** | Adapt execution; never independently replan / restrike strategy / redefine mastery |
| **Explain pathway decisions** | Supportive, evidence-traceable student speech |

It is **not** educationally responsible for:

- rewriting Canonical Study Plans or educational strategy;
- deciding today’s Daily Coach primary objective;
- diagnosing learning obstacles or selecting Learning Interventions;
- inferring or redefining mastery;
- packing calendars or inventing intensity;
- producing opaque pathway scores as educational proof.

## 7. Binding Educational Doctrine

1. **Warrant before pathway.** No pathway without a recovery warrant (`RECOVERY_TRIGGERS.md`). Temporary fluctuation receives ordinary Daily Coach continuity — not pathway theatre.
2. **Pathway answers type; strategy answers tactic.** Do not rename RS-XX as RP-XX without a genuine journey-level distinction.
3. **Educational first.** Catalogue entries come from IFoA tutoring practice and recovery principles — not from which feature is easiest to ship.
4. **Execution may adapt; intent must not silently change.** Pathways may reshape near-term emphasis within envelopes. Permanent alteration of long-term intent escalates to Master Planner / rescheduling.
5. **One primary pathway in speech.** Supporting pathways may exist; students hear one primary recovery story.
6. **Smallest sufficient journey.** Prefer shorter / gentler pathways when the disruption profile supports them.
7. **Sustainable progress beats heroic catch-up.** Pathway success is restored durable rhythm and understanding protection — not calendar clearance.
8. **Mastery is never inferred from pathway progress.** Rest, resumed ticks, and “pathway completion” do not mint understanding or exam readiness (EIP-006).
9. **Sibling authorities preserved.** Inform Daily Coach; consume Learning Coach evidence; escalate structural need through established pathways.
10. **Explainability is mandatory.** Silent pathway switches and shaming language are forbidden (EIP-003).

## 8. Position Relative to Sibling Authorities

| Authority | Owns | Pathway Framework relationship |
|-----------|------|--------------------------------|
| **Educational Recovery Model (MS001)** | Warrant, objectives, strategies, boundaries | Consumes; specialises into pathway types |
| **Master Planner / Canonical Study Plan** | Long-term educational envelopes, strategy, published contract | Consumes; escalates for structural change; never rewrites independently |
| **Scheduling / Rescheduling** | Allocation and authorised recovery capacity placement | Escalates when pathway cannot restore feasibility inside envelopes |
| **Study Plan Lifecycle (Recovered)** | Plan-state meaning when recovery capacity is engaged | Aligns pathway posture with published Recovered / Adapted states |
| **Daily Coach** | Today’s educational priority under the plan | Informs emphasis; never bypasses |
| **Learning Coach** | Progression, obstacles, learning interventions | Consumes evidence; coordinates when knowledge / prerequisite pathways meet obstacle diagnosis; never bypasses |
| **Knowledge & Mastery (EIP-006)** | Ladder meanings | Never collapses pathway compliance into mastery speech |

```
Recovery warrant (MS001)
        │
        ▼
Pathway selection (this Framework)
        │  primary RP-XX + emphasised RS-XX
        │
        ├─ local restorative journey (within envelopes)
        │     → informs Daily Coach emphasis & session design
        │     → may transition between pathways
        │     → may resume normal coaching on success
        │
        └─ recovery alone inadequate
              → Rescheduling / Master Planner / Daily Coach escalation pathways
              → may yield Adapted / Recovered / new Canonical Study Plan
              → Daily Coach then coaches under the updated contract
```

Architectural requirement restated:

> **Recovery pathways may adapt educational execution but must not independently modify long-term planning, educational strategy, or mastery judgements.  
> Where recovery is insufficient, escalation must follow the established Master Planner and Daily Coach pathways.**

## 9. Relationship to Learning Coach

| Situation | Primary authority | Pathway role |
|-----------|-------------------|--------------|
| Continuity / capacity / confidence broken by disruption | Recovery pathway | Own restorative journey type |
| Learning stall despite continuous study | Learning Coach (obstacle → intervention) | Do not invent a recovery pathway as a substitute diagnosis |
| Return after absence plus fragile foundations | Both, layered | Pathway may be prerequisite reconstruction / knowledge reinforcement; Learning Coach owns obstacle diagnosis when warranted |
| Exam-phase disruption with technique issues | Both, layered | Exam-phase recovery pathway restores continuity under exam-facing plan emphasis; Learning Coach owns exam-technique obstacle / intervention meaning |

Hard rule:

> **A recovery pathway must not be used to bypass Learning Coach diagnosis when the educational problem is a learning obstacle rather than disruption continuity.**
