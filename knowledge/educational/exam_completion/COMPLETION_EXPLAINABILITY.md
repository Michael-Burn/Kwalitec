> why preparation is considered complete,  
> what evidence supports that conclusion,

> Explanation is supportive — never judgement.  
> Explainability never invents educational certainty.  
> Completion never implies mastery or guaranteed examination performance.**

## 1. Purpose

Students should never have to guess whether examination preparation is over — or feel that “you’re exam ready” was invented from a mock counter, checklist, or countdown.

Completion explainability specialises MS001 examination explainability and MS002 strategy explainability so every material completion judgement answers — in plain educational language — why complete (or not), what evidence, what changes next, and under what circumstances preparation may resume before the examination.

Without completion explainability:

- ending intensive exam prep feels arbitrary or premature;
- continuing exam prep feels like punishment or busywork;
- students confuse Exam Coach duty fulfilment with mastery or a guaranteed pass;
- monitoring / maintenance feels unexplained or like distrust;
- upstream handoff feels like failure;
- resume before the sitting feels arbitrary.

With completion explainability:

- the student trusts the assessment-facing tutor posture;
- warrant → strategy → completion → next step stays visible;
- mastery and pass honesty are preserved;
- confidence is supported without false certainty.

## 3. Traceability Obligation (Architectural)

Every material completion judgement must be traceable through:

| Trace link | Student-facing role |
|------------|---------------------|
| **Prior preparation / assessment-facing context** | “We’ve been preparing how you show what you’ve already learned when…” |
| **Why complete / not yet** | “We can treat preparation as complete for this sitting because…” / “We’re not ready to call this complete because…” |
| **Evidence basis (brief)** | “Looking at how you demonstrate known material / your sitting plan / timed practice / how confidence matches the evidence…” |
| **Explicit non-grounds (when useful)** | “This isn’t just because the exam is soon / sessions were ticked / you finished a checklist / you sat N mocks…” |
| **What happens next** | “You can proceed to the examination…” / “We’ll keep a light watch until the sitting…” / “Light keep-alive of your approach…” / “Revision / recovery / learning coaching takes the lead…” / “After the sitting we’ll reflect…” |
| **Resume conditions** | “We’d reopen focused exam preparation before the sitting if…” |
| **Mastery / guarantee honesty** | “This means Exam Coach preparation duties are fulfilled for now — not that everything is mastered, and not that the exam is assured.” |

Internal IDs (ExCC-XX, ExCE-XX, ExCT-XX, EXS-XX, EST-XX) may exist for algorithms and audits. They must not appear as student-facing jargon.

Architectural requirement restated:

> **Examination preparation completion must be justified by accumulated educational evidence.  
> It confirms Exam Coach responsibilities fulfilled and must never be interpreted as mastery, guaranteed examination performance, or replacement for upstream coaching.**

A completion message with no evidence link — or one that cites only attendance, ticks, checklists, elapsed time, mock counts, or proximity — is invalid.

## 4. Four-Question Framework (Completion Specialisation)

Every material completion judgement must answer EIP-003’s four questions, specialised for completion:

| EIP-003 question | Completion specialisation |
|------------------|---------------------------|
| **What is known?** | What assessment-facing demonstration / planning / conditions / confidence / decision observations accumulated on prepared material |
| **What is estimated?** | Provisional judgements (e.g. residual readiness risk; provisional confidence) — labelled as provisional |
| **Why this guidance?** | Why completion is affirmed or withheld; why this ExCT transition |
| **What next?** | Concrete next coaching posture + monitoring / maintenance / resume / reflection statement |

Students must hear all four — especially **why complete / not yet**, **what happens next**, and **when preparation may resume** — not only “you’re done.”

---

## 5. Required Student Understandings

Material completion speech must make the following understandable:

### 5.1 Why preparation is considered complete (or not yet)

**When complete (ExCT-01 / ExCT-02 / ExCT-03):**

- Name fulfilled educational conditions in plain language (objectives addressed, strategy outcomes, appropriate readiness, evidence-calibrated confidence, cleared Exam Coach actions as relevant).
- Tie success to Exam Coach duties for *prepared* material — never to inventing new coverage.
- State clearly that intensive exam prep can lighten, monitoring / maintenance can begin, or the sitting may proceed.

**When not yet:**

- Name which assessment-facing conditions are still missing, without moral framing.
- Prefer concrete educational gaps (“timed practice on known topics is still fragile”) over vague “not ready.”
- If upstream escalation is required, say that examination preparation alone is not enough — without shame.

### 5.2 What evidence supports that conclusion

- Brief, plain account of the trail (demonstration of known material, sitting plan, timed practice, confidence matching evidence).
- Distinguish known observations from provisional estimates.
- Where useful, say what was *not* treated as proof (time alone, proximity alone, checklist alone, mock count alone).
- Never invent observations to make a tidy story.

### 5.3 What happens next

| Transition | Student-facing cue (illustrative posture, not copy-deck) |
|------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| **ExCT-01** | You can proceed to the examination — preparation duties for this sitting are fulfilled |
| **ExCT-02** | We’ll keep a light watch until the sitting / keep gathering a clearer picture |
| **ExCT-03** | Light keep-alive of your examination approach so it stays steady |
| **ExCT-04** | Consolidating revision takes the lead on known material that still needs strengthening |
| **ExCT-05** | Recovery coaching takes the lead until study continuity is steady again |
| **ExCT-06** | Examination tips alone aren’t enough — learning-focused coaching takes the lead |
| **ExCT-07** | After the sitting we’ll reflect on what it revealed and what comes next educationally |

### 5.4 Under what circumstances preparation may resume before the examination

Material success / monitoring speech must make resume conditions understandable when relevant:

- If new evidence shows assessment-facing fragility returning on prepared material, focused exam preparation may reopen.
- If Revision / Recovery / Learning Coach finishes an upstream episode and Exam Coach work is again lawful, assessment-facing emphasis may return.
- If the authorised sitting context changes in a way that creates a new lawful preparation need, preparation may resume with explainability.
- Resume is **not** triggered by countdown anxiety, comparison to others, or checklist guilt alone.

### 5.5 What completion does *not* mean

Material success speech must not allow the student to reasonably conclude that:

- topics are mastered because exam prep ended;
- the examination is now assured or a mark is promised;
- new syllabus content was learned under an exam-prep label;
- fragility or disruption history was erased;
- intensive exam prep is still owed “to be safe” after declaring success;
- Learning / Recovery / Revision problems were solved by finishing mocks;
- Exam Coach replaced upstream educational coaching.

## 6. Tone and Language Rules

1. **Supportive, never judgement.** Completion is coaching wisdom, not a moral verdict.  
2. **Hopeful and specific.** “Your sitting plan matches what you can already do” beats “good job surviving exam mode.”  
3. **Confirm duty fulfilment without inventing certainty.** Affirm Exam Coach progress without inventing mastery or a guaranteed pass.  
4. **No mastery / pass ceremony.** Forbid “fully mastered,” “exam ready for sure,” “you will pass,” or “learned this for the first time via exam prep.”  
5. **No timer, proximity, or quota speech as proof.** Forbid “your ten mocks are up,” “your exam week expired,” or “the exam is tomorrow so you’re done.”  
6. **No streak / checklist speech as proof.** Streaks and checklists may be UX chrome; they are not educational evidence in student explanation.  
7. **Honest upstream escalation.** ExCT-04…ExCT-06 are care for genuine educational problems, not abandonment or failure theatre.  
8. **Honest monitoring / maintenance.** ExCT-02 / ExCT-03 are protective vigilance or keep-alive — not distrust or unfinished punishment.  
9. **Close with next step and resume conditions when useful.** Every completion message names what happens next educationally.  
10. **Protect assessment-facing honesty** especially when withholding ExCT-01.

## 7. Worked Explainability Sketches (Non-Normative Copy)

These illustrate posture. Product copy may differ; educational obligations may not.

### 7.1 ExCT-01 proceed to examination

> We’ve been preparing how you show what you’ve already learned — your sitting plan is clear, timed practice on known topics has steadied, and your confidence matches the evidence rather than false certainty. For this sitting, examination preparation has done what it needed to do. That doesn’t mean everything is mastered or that the exam is assured — it means you can proceed with an honest preparation story behind you.

### 7.2 ExCT-03 maintenance with light ExCT-02 monitoring

> The intensive examination-approach work has done enough. We’ll keep a light keep-alive of your sitting plan and known strengths until the paper — not more grinding for its own sake. If timed practice on a known topic becomes fragile again, we can reopen focused preparation; we won’t invent that from the countdown alone.

### 7.3 ExCT-02 not yet — gather

> You’re preparing for assessment, which matters. We’re not calling this complete yet because the trail after recent rehearsal is still thin — especially for how you use the clock on material you already know. Next we keep the current approach and gather clearer evidence before we change posture.

### 7.4 ExCT-04 Revision Coach handback

> Timed rehearsal showed that a chapter you already studied is still fragile on return. The honest next step is consolidating revision of that known material — not another loop of exam tips. Assessment approach waits on that strengthen.

### 7.5 ExCT-05 Recovery Coach handoff

> After the recent disruption, the honest priority is restoring study continuity — not treating this as ordinary final exam prep. Recovery coaching leads until continuity is steady enough to re-enter examination preparation carefully.

### 7.6 ExCT-06 Learning Coach escalation

> We’ve given examination preparation a fair attempt on this covered topic, and the pattern still looks like a deeper learning problem rather than ordinary exam technique. The honest next step is learning-focused coaching — not another loop of the same exam approach. Finishing more mocks would not honestly fix this.

### 7.7 ExCT-07 post-examination reflection

> The sitting has happened. Examination preparation is no longer the lead story. Next we’ll reflect on what the paper revealed — time use, method selection, where known strengths held — and route what comes next honestly. One sitting doesn’t invent mastery or erase your educational history.

### 7.8 Forbidden sketches

> ~~Your exam countdown finished — preparation complete.~~  
> ~~You completed eight mocks — objective achieved.~~  
> ~~You’re fully exam ready and will pass.~~  
> ~~You learned this chapter for the first time through exam prep — well done.~~  
> ~~We’ll keep intensive exam grinding indefinitely to be safe.~~  
> ~~Others have practised more, so you’re not done.~~  
> ~~Checklist finished — mastery confirmed.~~

## 8. Surface Obligations

Any student-facing surface that presents examination preparation completion must:

1. Answer the four EIP-003 questions as specialised above.  
2. Make §5 understandings available without requiring the student to infer them — including resume conditions when relevant.  
3. Keep internal IDs invisible.  
4. Refuse mastery / guaranteed-performance / pass-certainty speech from completion.  
5. Refuse time-only / attendance-only / checklist-only / proximity-only / mock-count-only justifications.  
6. Align with Daily Coach explainability after ExCT-01…ExCT-03 — do not keep a parallel intensive Exam Coach narrator.  
7. Remain consistent with MS001 / MS002 prior-preparation, warrant, and strategy stories already told.  
8. When recommending monitoring, maintenance, or resume conditions, make those recommendations explicit and supportive.

---

## 9. Anti-Patterns (Forbidden)

- Silent completion with no evidence account  
- Completion celebrated as mastery graduation or guaranteed pass  
- “Monitoring” framed as probation or distrust  
- Upstream escalation framed as personal failure  
- Dual messaging: “complete” while still demanding intensive crisis mock volume  
- Maintenance spoken as unfinished punishment  
- Resume spoken as arbitrary panic without evidence  
- Jargon dumps (ExCC, ExCE, ExCT, EXS, EST, Twin, scores)
