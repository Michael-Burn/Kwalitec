## Authority

This document is the constitutional overview of **examination preparation completion** for Kwalitec.

It is subordinate to the Educational Constitution, specialised educational models, the Educational Examination Model (MS001), and the Examination Strategy Framework (MS002). It governs **how Kwalitec judges whether assessment-facing preparation has fulfilled Exam Coach educational responsibilities**, and **what educational transitions follow**, while remaining faithful to the Canonical Study Plan and sibling coach authorities. It does not authorise implementation shortcuts that contradict the Constitution, and it does not invent prior learning, evidence, mastery, strategy, or planning meaning absent from upstream models.

Authority order for examination preparation completion:

> Constitution defines educational truth and curriculum primacy.  
> Knowledge & Mastery defines coverage ≠ understanding ≠ mastery.  
> Evidence Model defines what may warrant educational claims.  
> Continuity Standard preserves rightful educational history across plan change.  
> Master Planner publishes the Canonical Study Plan and owns long-term educational envelopes, including examination-facing windows.  
> Daily Coach owns *what is most educationally valuable today* under that contract.  
> Learning Coach owns longitudinal progression, obstacle diagnosis, and learning interventions.  
> Recovery Coach owns restorative coaching after meaningful disruption.  
> Revision Coach owns consolidating warrant, strategy, and completion for previously learned material.  
> Educational Examination Model (MS001) defines *whether* examination preparation is warranted and *which* assessment-facing objectives / priorities apply.  
> Examination Strategy Framework (MS002) defines *what kind* of examination preparation approach is appropriate and strategy-level transitions.  
> **This Examination Preparation Completion Model (Workstream 6 / MS003) defines whether examination preparation has fulfilled its educational purpose and what educational transition follows.**  
> Downstream coaching and narration surfaces must consume this educational reasoning — never treat completion as mastery, guaranteed examination performance, or a silent plan rewrite.

## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not keep a student in intensive examination preparation forever — nor declare preparation complete because a countdown finished, a mock checklist closed, or the exam date grew near. After a lawful examination preparation emphasis, the tutor answers one living educational question:

> **“Has this learner completed examination preparation to the extent justified by the available educational evidence?”**

That answer must rest on accumulated educational evidence and constitutional Exam Coach authority, confirm that assessment-facing responsibilities have been fulfilled, lighten or redirect preparation posture honestly, proceed to the sitting or maintain calm vigilance when that is educationally right, escalate to Revision / Recovery / Learning Coach when unresolved educational weaknesses appear, and refuse to mint mastery or examination certainty from preparation completion.

This document records that tutor posture so every future Exam Coach subsystem has a single educational reference for *completion meaning*.

> **The Examination Preparation Completion Model describes educational completion reasoning.  
> It does not implement Runtime A logic, algorithms, analytics, or Twin writers.**

## 2. What Examination Preparation Completion Is

**Examination preparation completion** is the educational judgement that a lawful assessment-facing preparation emphasis has **fulfilled the educational responsibilities of the Exam Coach** — preparation objectives addressed, examination strategy outcomes achieved, appropriate provisional readiness for the current sitting, confidence supported by educational evidence, and no outstanding preparation actions within Exam Coach authority — for the student to **proceed to examination, continue monitoring until the sitting, enter maintenance preparation, hand unresolved weaknesses to an upstream coach, or transition to post-examination reflection**, without claiming that mastery was earned or that examination success is guaranteed.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Examination preparation emphasis / episode** | Active assessment-facing coaching under a lawful warrant and strategy | Is Exam Coach still the lead assessment-facing story? |
| **Completion criteria** | Educational conditions that indicate Exam Coach purpose fulfilled | What must be true educationally? |
| **Completion evidence** | Accumulated observations that may support those conditions | What trail justifies the claim? |
| **Completion judgement** | Achieved / not yet / incomplete destination | Has preparation fulfilled Exam Coach duties enough? |
| **Completion transition** | Educational move that follows the judgement | What changes next? |
| **Post-completion monitoring / maintenance** | Residual watchfulness or light keep-alive without intensive exam theatre | What vigilance remains until or after the sitting? |

Completion is:

- **evidence-justified** — rests on accumulated Educational Evidence, not elapsed time, checklists, or proximity alone;
- **assessment-facing** — success means Exam Coach responsibilities for this sitting are fulfilled, not that first learning or consolidating revision occurred under an exam label;
- **non-mastery / non-guarantee** — completion ≠ mastery ≠ guaranteed examination performance ≠ pass certainty;
- **authority-preserving** — successful close ends intensive Exam Coach identity as the lead story when duties are done; upstream coaches retain ownership of learning, revision, and recovery problems;
- **transition-aware** — may proceed to examination, monitor, maintain, hand upstream, or move to post-examination reflection;
- **explainable** — students understand why completion is (or is not) affirmed;
- **non-scoring** — qualitative educational judgement only.

Completion is **not**:

- a calendar milestone, countdown unlock, or examination-checklist finish line;
- inference from attendance, mock volume, or session completion alone;
- a licence to invent mastery or guaranteed examination success from preparation activity;
- a substitute for Master Planner planning or strategy selection at the long-term layer;
- a bypass of Daily Coach day authority or Learning / Recovery / Revision Coach layers;
- an excuse to extend intensive examination preparation indefinitely “to be safe”;
- a claim that Exam Coach replaced upstream educational coaching.

## 3. Binding Distinction: Completion vs Neighbours

| Layer | Educational job | Unlawful collapse |
|-------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Trigger (EPT)** | Warrant that examination preparation is needed | Declaring completion without a lawful assessment-facing emphasis ever existing |
| **Objective (EXO)** | Name assessment-facing goods to advance | Treating checklist clearance as the completion condition |
| **Strategy (EXS)** | Name the *kind* of examination preparation approach | Equating “strategy finished” with educational completion |
| **Strategy transition (EST1–EST8)** | Strategy-level final prep / handoff / reflection vocabulary | Inventing a rival transition law that ignores MS002 |
| **Completion (this corpus)** | Judge whether Exam Coach purpose was sufficiently fulfilled | Treating time elapsed, proximity, mocks, or ticks as proof |
| **Daily Coach** | Own today’s priority under the current contract | Letting completion speech commandeer the day or keep intensive exam theatre after duties are done |
| **Learning / Recovery / Revision Coach** | Own genuine learning, restorative, and consolidating meaning | Using completion to mint learning success or skip upstream ownership; extending exam prep instead of ExCT-04…ExCT-06 |
| **Master Planner / Scheduling** | Own structural envelope and allocation change | Absorbing replan inside “completion” theatre |

Hard rule:

> **Strategy compliance is not completion.  
> Attendance is not completion.  
> Elapsed time is not completion.  
> Examination proximity is not completion.  
> Checklist or mock volume is not completion.  
> Completion confirms Exam Coach responsibilities fulfilled; it does not mint mastery or guaranteed examination performance.**

## 4. Educational Purpose

The Examination Preparation Completion Model exists so that:

1. **Examination preparation emphases end for educational reasons** — not because a timer expired, a checklist finished, or the exam date arrived.
2. **Students are not trapped in permanent intensive exam theatre** once Exam Coach duties are honestly fulfilled.
3. **False completion is refused** when only attendance, mock counts, session ticks, checklists, or proximity exist.
4. **Proceed / monitor / maintenance become speakable** when the sitting is the next honest educational move, or light keep-alive is enough.
5. **Unresolved educational weaknesses return upstream** — examination preparation does not extend indefinitely as a soft substitute for Learning, Recovery, or Revision Coach.
6. **Mastery and pass honesty are preserved** — completion never pretends the examination is assured or that mastery was earned by exam tips alone.
7. **Students understand the close of the arc** — why success (or not), what evidence, what changes next, under what circumstances preparation may resume before the examination.

## 5. Core Responsibilities

The Examination Preparation Completion Model is educationally responsible for:

| Responsibility | Meaning |
|----------------|---------|
| **State completion criteria** | Name educational conditions that indicate Exam Coach purpose fulfilled (ExCC-XX) |
| **Bound completion evidence** | Require EIP-002-honest accumulation; forbid attendance-only / checklist-only / proximity-only / mock-volume-only proofs |
| **Produce a completion judgement** | Achieved, not yet, or incomplete destination requiring another transition |
| **Select completion transitions** | Proceed to examination, monitoring, maintenance, upstream handoff, or post-examination reflection |
| **Confirm Exam Coach authority cleared** | On success, no outstanding preparation actions remain *within Exam Coach authority* |
| **Preserve sibling authorities** | Never rewrite plan, strategy, mastery, Learning, Recovery, or Revision meanings via completion |
| **Require post-completion honesty** | Name residual monitoring / maintenance / resume conditions without keeping crisis exam speech |
| **Explain supportively** | Make completion speakable without false certainty, shame, or mastery / pass ceremony |

It is **not** educationally responsible for:

- rewriting Canonical Study Plans or educational strategy;
- deciding today’s Daily Coach primary objective after resume (Daily Coach owns that);
- diagnosing learning obstacles or selecting Learning Interventions;
- owning consolidating revision or restorative recovery;
- inferring or redefining mastery;
- guaranteeing examination performance;
- writing Twin estimates by itself;
- producing opaque “completion scores” as educational proof;
- inventing numerical mock-count, day-count, or proximity thresholds as educational law.

## 6. Binding Educational Doctrine

1. **Completion is an educational judgement, not a time-based, checklist, or proximity milestone.** Calendar elapsed, mock quotas, and “exam next week” do not complete preparation.
2. **Completion must be justified by accumulated educational evidence.** Thin or activity-only trails understate; they do not certify success.
3. **Completion confirms Exam Coach educational responsibilities fulfilled.** It must never be interpreted as evidence of mastery, guaranteed examination performance, or replacement for upstream educational coaching (EIP-006).
4. **Where preparation evidence reveals unresolved educational weaknesses, authority must transition back to the appropriate upstream coach** rather than extending examination preparation indefinitely.
5. **Attendance, checklist completion, mock volume, and session completion alone are insufficient.** Completing prep sittings may be activity; completion requires assessment-facing educational meaning from the evidence trail.
6. **MS001 ending posture and MS002 EST1–EST8 remain binding.** This corpus specialises them; it does not weaken them with softer theatre.
7. **Daily Coach authority is preserved.** After successful close of intensive Exam Coach emphasis, ordinary day-priority selection resumes under the authorised plan; Exam Coach does not retain crisis commandeering.
8. **Sibling coach evidence is preserved.** Completion does not invent learning success, dissolve Recovery ownership, or override Revision Coach recommendations.
9. **Canonical Study Plan remains the contract.** Completion may end intensive assessment-facing posture; it may not independently publish a new plan.
10. **Explainability is mandatory.** Silent “you’re exam ready” and silent permanent exam theatre are both forbidden (EIP-003).

## 8. Relationship to MS002 Strategy Transitions

MS002 `STRATEGY_TRANSITIONS.md` already states that strategies may move toward final preparation (EST1), hand back to Revision (EST2), Recovery (EST3), or Learning Coach (EST4), change within Exam Coach (EST5), escalate structurally (EST6), enter post-examination reflection (EST7), or hold/gather (EST8).

This Completion Model:

- **does not replace** those strategy transition laws;
- **specialises** them into named criteria (ExCC-XX), evidence classes (ExCE-XX), and post-judgement transitions (ExCT-XX);
- **tightens** the evidence obligation so attendance-only, checklist-only, proximity-only, mock-volume-only, and time-based completions are explicitly unlawful;
- **separates** Exam Coach duty fulfilment from mastery and guaranteed-performance speech more sharply for student-facing completion narration;
- **hardens** the upstream-coach escalation line so unresolved weaknesses do not become endless examination preparation.

Strategy change within Exam Coach (EST5), structural escalation (EST6), and hold/gather when warrant is unclear (EST8) remain MS002-owned when those are the primary moves; this corpus aligns ExCT-02 monitoring with residual-risk / thin-history cases without absorbing EST5–EST6–EST8 into false “completion success.”
