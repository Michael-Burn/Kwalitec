## 1. Purpose

Without evidence law, “exam prep complete” becomes either optimistic theatre (a few mocks, a finished checklist, a countdown) or opaque optimiser speech.

With evidence law, completion claims remain epistemically honest: Exam Coach duty fulfilment may be affirmed when the trail supports it; mastery and guaranteed-performance speech stay forbidden; thin history yields understatement.

---

## 2. EIP-002 Principles Applied to Completion

All EIP-002 principles bind completion judgements. Specialised readings:

| EIP-002 principle | Completion application |
|-------------------|------------------------|
| Evidence is observed | Completion cites educational happenings, not desired conclusions written backwards |
| Evidence accumulates | One favourable mock does not complete an examination preparation emphasis |
| Evidence has quality | Soft self-report ≠ high-certainty readiness or confidence calibration |
| Activity alone is not knowledge | Attendance / ticks / mock counts / proximity are not completion proof |
| Inference may not invent evidence | “Looks exam ready” without trail is unlawful |
| Absence of evidence is uncertainty | Thin assessment-facing history → continue gathering; do not certify |
| Coverage ≠ understanding | Cleared exam checklist / Study Progress ≠ Exam Coach completion or mastery |

Architectural requirement restated:

> **Examination preparation completion must be justified by accumulated educational evidence.**

Hard non-inference rule (milestone binding):

> **Do not infer completion solely from:  
> • elapsed time  
> • attendance  
> • checklist completion  
> • proximity to the examination**

## 3. What Completion Evidence Is For

Completion evidence supports claims about **fulfilled Exam Coach educational responsibilities and assessment-facing goods** (ExCC-XX).

It does **not** by itself authorise:

- Estimated Mastery uplift;
- first-learning of new syllabus content;
- examination performance guarantees or pass certainty;
- erasure of prior fragility / disruption history;
- rewriting of Canonical Study Plan envelopes;
- override of Revision Coach, Recovery Coach, or Learning Coach meanings.

Where completion speech touches readiness or confidence (ExCC-03 / ExCC-04), it may only claim **provisional appropriateness / calibration** for the next authorised posture — never high mastery language or guaranteed marks from examination preparation alone.

## 4. Evidence Class Catalogue

IDs (`ExCE-XX`) exist for audit and cross-reference. They must not appear as student-facing jargon.

### ExCE-01 — Preparation objectives advancement trail

**Definition.** Accumulated observations that the active EXO goods advanced under lawful examination preparation — demonstration of prepared understanding, examination planning, conditions management, confidence calibration, and/or sitting decision-making — without treating mere presence or mock volume as learning success.

**May support:** ExCC-01, ExCC-06; contributes to ExCC-03 when readiness depends on those goods.

**May include (educationally meaningful):**

- Pattern of improved demonstration of *already prepared* material under exam-facing practice across returns;
- Speakable examination plan / sitting rules that match accumulated strengths and risks;
- Conditions / decision rehearsal outcomes on prepared scope showing honest improvement;
- Alignment with Learning / Revision meaning: demonstration claims do not contradict sibling evidence.

**Must not treat as sufficient alone:**

- Login streaks or attendance;
- Mock / question counts without assessment-facing meaning;
- A single same-day success after intensive prompting;
- Session completed flags;
- Elapsed prep days or exam proximity.

**Quality note:** Objectives trail is primarily evidence about **assessment-facing advancement of prepared material**, not about first learning or mastery.

---

### ExCE-02 — Strategy outcome trail

**Definition.** Accumulated observations that the expected educational outcome of the active EXS-XX is present — readiness consolidation, authentic rehearsal familiarity, time discipline, question-selection judgement, confidence calibration, final consolidation posture, or careful post-disruption re-entry — provisional and evidence-honest.

**May support:** ExCC-02, ExCC-06; supports ExCT-01 / ExCT-03 when strategy job is done.

**May include:**

- Success signs for the active EXS emerging across fair assessment-facing attempts;
- Reduced leading gap that justified the strategy (e.g. conditions fragility easing after EXS-02);
- Honest understatement when outcomes are not emerging — prefer EST5 / ExCT-04…ExCT-06 over fake ExCE-02.

**Must not treat as sufficient alone:**

- Strategy catalogue item marked done;
- Days spent under an EXS label;
- Checklist of “strategy activities” finished;
- Proximity-triggered EXS-06 label without outcome trail.

---

### ExCE-03 — Sitting readiness trail

**Definition.** Accumulated signs that readiness for the *current* sitting is **appropriate given evidence** — provisional, scope-honest, confined to prepared material — never absolute “exam ready” certainty.

**May support:** ExCC-03, ExCC-06 when sitting-facing close is claimed.

**May include:**

- Exam-facing practice on *known* scope showing honest improvement or maintained strength relative to warrant open;
- Method selection / application / planning under more authentic conditions than at warrant open;
- Alignment with authorised examination-window / final-period intent for prepared scope;
- Honest naming of remaining fragile known topics without collapsing the whole story.

**Must not:**

- Infer readiness from past-paper volume alone;
- Infer readiness of unlearned syllabus from exam completion speech;
- Claim guaranteed pass or high mastery from readiness trail;
- Equate calendar “exam phase” or proximity with evidence of readiness.

---

### ExCE-04 — Evidence-calibrated confidence trail

**Definition.** Observations that examination confidence is aligning with accumulated educational evidence — reduced unjustified panic or unjustified certainty — without pep-talk theatre or pass promises.

**May support:** ExCC-04; contributes honesty to ExCC-06 and ExCT-01 proceed speech.

**May include:**

- Confidence speech / affect that better matches named strengths and fragile known topics;
- Reduced confidence misalignment that justified EXS-05, with corroborating ExCE-01 / ExCE-03 where those goods matter;
- Supportive, evidence-linked calm without inventing coverage.

**Must not:**

- Infer ExCC-04 from a single self-report “I feel ready”;
- Infer pass certainty from calmed nerves;
- Clear Learning / Revision / Recovery ownership by confidence theatre;
- Treat ordinary exam nerves as ExCE-06 blockers by themselves.

**Quality note:** Soft self-report may contribute but is insufficient alone for ExCC-04 when confidence was a primary EXS-05 job.

---

### ExCE-05 — Exam Coach authority clearance trail

**Definition.** Educational signs that **no outstanding assessment-facing actions remain within Exam Coach authority** for this sitting — the lawful EXO / EXS jobs Exam Coach opened are sufficiently advanced or honestly handed upstream.

**May support:** ExCC-05, ExCC-06 for ExCT-01 / ExCT-03 (and ExCT-02 when intensive Exam Coach ends).

**May include:**

- Explicit mapping of remaining work to monitoring / maintenance / Daily Coach ordinary guidance / upstream coaches — not unfinished Exam Coach duties;
- Absence of lawful Exam Coach actions still owed for demonstration / plan / conditions / decisions / confidence as scoped;
- Envelope conflicts escalated (EST6) rather than left as fake local leftovers.

**Must not:**

- Infer clearance from UI checklist completion;
- Treat unfinished Revision / Recovery / Learning work as Exam Coach debt to keep grinding;
- Reopen infinite Exam Coach actions from anxiety alone after criteria are met;
- Claim clearance while intensive EXS remains the lead coaching frame without educational reason.

---

### ExCE-06 — Negative / incompleteness evidence

**Definition.** Observations that **block** Exam Coach–success completion even if some positive signals exist.

**Must block or delay ExCT-01 / ExCT-03 when present:**

- Prior preparation missing for the target scope (first learning — refuse Exam Coach completion);
- Meaningful disruption primary (Recovery Coach — ExCT-05 / EST3);
- Consolidating revision primary or Revision Coach recommendations still dominant (ExCT-04 / EST2);
- Unresolved learning obstacle / thin genuine progression / misconception that exam approaches have not repaired (Learning Coach — ExCT-06 / EST4);
- Repeated same-pattern failure after fair attempts at appropriate EXS strategies;
- Only attendance / checklist / mock-count / elapsed-time / proximity signals available;
- Envelope breach requiring structural change (completion must not fake local success — MS002 EST6);
- Learning / Revision / Recovery evidence contradicts the Exam Coach–success claim.

Correct uncertainty, strategy change, or upstream escalation beat false completion and endless examination preparation.

## 5. Forbidden Inference Patterns

The following inferences are **educationally unlawful** for completion:

| Observed | Unlawful inference |
|----------|-------------------|
| Student attended / logged in | Examination preparation complete |
| Session or mission marked complete | Preparation complete / mastery / guaranteed pass |
| N mocks / past papers completed | Exam Coach purpose fulfilled |
| N days elapsed since warrant | Preparation complete |
| Examination is next week / countdown fired | Appropriate readiness achieved |
| Prep checklist finished | ExCC-06 purpose fulfilled |
| Strategy catalogue item marked done | Educational completion achieved |
| Student felt calm after encouragement | Confidence evidence-calibrated (alone) |
| Student rested near the exam | Readiness appropriate |
| One good mock script | Mastery / guaranteed examination performance |
| Confidence self-report alone | All ExCC criteria met |
| Comparison “others practised more” | Student has or has not completed preparation |

## 6. Accumulation and Sufficiency

### 6.1 Accumulation

Evidence for completion **accumulates**. Interpreters revise the completion judgement as history grows. Early assessment-facing returns typically support gentler demonstration / planning claims before stronger sitting-readiness or proceed-to-examination claims.

### 6.2 Sufficiency (qualitative)

A completion judgement is evidence-sufficient when:

1. Applicable ExCC criteria each have supporting ExCE classes (not merely hoped);
2. ExCE-06 blockers are absent or honestly redirected to ExCT-02 / ExCT-04…ExCT-06 / MS002 EST5–EST6 / EST8;
3. Claims that touch readiness or confidence use evidence quality appropriate to the claim;
4. Explainability can name the trail in plain language without inventing certainty, mastery, or guaranteed performance.

This Model does **not** define numerical sample sizes, scores, mock quotas, proximity thresholds, or half-lives. Sufficiency is educational judgement under EIP-002 quality discipline.

### 6.3 Thin history rule

After opening an examination preparation emphasis, history may be thin. Lawful responses:

- Continue the strategy and gather evidence;
- Affirm only weaker assessment-facing claims if clearly supported;
- Prefer ExCT-02 continued monitoring over ExCT-01 celebration when residual risk remains;
- Never fill thin history with mastery, guaranteed pass, or “fully exam ready” speech.

## 7. Relationship to Sibling Coach Evidence

| Situation | Evidence reading | Completion implication |
|-----------|------------------|------------------------|
| Assessment-facing practice improved; Revision Coach still owns fragile known hubs | ExCE-01/03 may support approach; consolidating problem remains | Prefer **ExCT-04** — do not override Revision Coach |
| Disruption primary near the sitting | Recovery evidence dominates | Prefer **ExCT-05** — do not complete as ordinary final prep |
| Exam rehearsal shows understanding never formed | Learning problem dominates | Prefer **ExCT-06** — do not extend exam tips indefinitely |
| Revision completion meaning already affirms consolidating success on known scope | Consume without redefinition | May support ExCE-03 honesty for prepared material |
| No Learning Coach trail yet | Do not invent progression success | Exam Coach–only completion possible; withhold mastery-adjacent speech |
| Sibling evidence contradicts Exam Coach–success claim | Consume / defer | Do not override with exam-completion fiat |

Hard rule:

> **Exam Coach must not bypass Learning, Recovery, or Revision Coach evidence.  
> Completing examination preparation sittings is not proof that learning progressed, revision finished, recovery succeeded, or mastery exists.  
> Where preparation evidence reveals unresolved educational weaknesses, authority must transition back to the appropriate upstream coach rather than extending examination preparation indefinitely.**

---

## 8. Traceability Obligation

Every material completion judgement must be traceable through:

| Trace link | Role |
|------------|------|
| Active warrant / strategy context | What assessment-facing emphasis is being closed or redirected |
| Prior-preparation confirmation | Material was previously learned / revised — not first-pass disguised as completion |
| Applicable ExCC criteria | Which educational conditions were evaluated |
| Supporting ExCE classes | What accumulated evidence was cited |
| Explicit non-reliance statement (when relevant) | What was *not* used (time alone, attendance alone, checklist alone, proximity alone) |
| Resulting ExCT transition | What educational move follows |
| Mastery / guarantee honesty | Explicit non-claim of mastery, guaranteed examination performance, or pass certainty |

A completion declaration with only attendance, checklist ticks, mock counts, elapsed time, or proximity in the trail is invalid.

---

## 9. Anti-Patterns (Forbidden)

- Completion dashboards powered by login, streak, mock-count, or countdown metrics as educational truth
- Silent Twin mastery writes triggered by exam-prep-complete events
- “Evidence” that is only a recommendation or desired outcome renamed as observation
- Selective citation of one good mock while ignoring ExCE-06 collapse / upstream patterns
- Using completion evidence vocabulary to invent a second Evidence Model
- Inferring first learning or mastery from examination preparation completion speech
