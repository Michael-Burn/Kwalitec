> **Completion criteria are educational conditions — not scoring dimensions, mock-count thresholds, checklists, or proximity gates.  
> Success is judged from accumulated educational evidence, not elapsed time or examination nearness alone.  
> Completion confirms Exam Coach responsibilities fulfilled; it never certifies mastery or guaranteed examination success.**

---

## 1. Purpose

Without explicit completion criteria, examination preparation either never ends (permanent paper-grinding mode) or ends falsely (a finished checklist, a mock quota, a countdown, or “the exam is next week”).

With explicit criteria, an expert tutor can affirm that assessment-facing coaching has fulfilled Exam Coach educational responsibilities enough to proceed to the sitting, lighten to monitoring / maintenance, or hand unresolved weaknesses upstream — while refusing mastery theatre, pass promises, and endless exam loops over Learning / Recovery / Revision problems.

---

## 2. Criteria Principles

1. **Educational first.** Criteria describe fulfilled Exam Coach purpose, not operational convenience.
2. **Evidence-bound.** Each criterion requires supporting trail under `COMPLETION_EVIDENCE.md`.
3. **Objective-aligned.** Criteria map to EXO-01…EXO-05 goods that preparation was pursuing.
4. **Strategy-sensitive emphasis.** Different EXS-XX approaches emphasise different criteria; successful closes still require the applicable assessment-facing conditions for the active goods.
5. **Prior-preparation gated.** Criteria presuppose lawful prior learning and revision substance — they never complete “exam prep” of unlearned material.
6. **Non-mastery / non-guarantee.** Meeting criteria confirms Exam Coach duties fulfilled; it does not certify mastery or guarantee examination performance.
7. **Non-temporal / non-proximity / non-volumetric.** No criterion is satisfied merely because time passed, the exam drew near, or many mocks occurred.
8. **Speakable.** Criteria must be explainable in plain educational language (`COMPLETION_EXPLAINABILITY.md`).

---

## 3. Criterion Catalogue

IDs (`ExCC-XX`) exist for audit and cross-reference. They must not appear as student-facing jargon.

### ExCC-01 — Preparation objectives addressed

**Definition.** The active EXO-01…EXO-05 goods that examination preparation was pursuing have been sufficiently advanced for the current sitting — demonstration of accumulated understanding, examination planning, conditions management, evidence-calibrated confidence, and/or sitting decision-making as applicable — without inventing coverage of unlearned material.

**Maps primarily to:** EXO-01…EXO-05 (whichever were in focus); typical across EXS-01…EXS-07 according to strategy emphasis.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional examination preparation succeeds when the assessment-facing goods the tutor set out to advance are honestly addressed — not when a prep checklist is ticked or a calendar phase ends.

**Success looks like:**

- Applicable EXO goods show honest advancement relative to the opening of the examination warrant.
- Speech names which assessment-facing goods are now sufficiently addressed for this sitting’s Exam Coach job.
- Claims remain provisional and evidence-honest (EIP-002 / EIP-006).

**Must not:**

- Equate finishing mocks or a prep checklist with addressed objectives.
- Claim objectives addressed for unlearned syllabus under an exam-prep label.
- Treat proximity or elapsed prep weeks as proof that EXO goods advanced.

**Evidence posture:** Requires ExCE-01 — never attendance, checklist, or proximity alone.

---

### ExCC-02 — Examination strategy outcomes achieved

**Definition.** The expected educational outcome of the active EXS-XX strategy has been sufficiently achieved for the current episode — readiness consolidation, rehearsal under conditions, time discipline, question-selection judgement, confidence calibration, final consolidation posture, or careful post-disruption exam re-entry as applicable — without treating strategy compliance as educational completion.

**Maps primarily to:** MS002 strategy expected outcomes; aligns with EST success posture for the active EXS.

**Tutor rationale.** An expert tutor chooses a *kind* of preparation for a reason. Completion requires that the strategy’s educational outcome is honestly present — not that the strategy label was applied for N days.

**Success looks like:**

- Success signs for the active EXS (as in `STRATEGY_SELECTION.md` / catalogue expected outcomes) are affirmable from accumulated evidence.
- Explainability can say the chosen approach has done enough for its purpose — or that another move (upstream / maintenance / monitoring) is now honest.
- Strategy change (EST5) is preferred when outcomes are not emerging but another EXS still fits — not fake ExCC-02 success.

**Must not:**

- Equate “we finished the strategy module” with achieved strategy outcomes.
- Claim EXS-06 final consolidation outcomes while inventing late coverage.
- Keep looping the same EXS after outcomes are met solely “to be safe.”

**Evidence posture:** Requires ExCE-02 when this criterion is in focus.

---

### ExCC-03 — Appropriate readiness for the current sitting

**Definition.** For the *current* authorised sitting, the learner’s assessment-facing readiness is **appropriate given accumulated evidence** — provisional, scope-honest, and consistent with prior preparation — without claiming the student is “exam ready” as mastery or pass certainty.

**Maps primarily to:** EXO-01 / EXO-02 / EXO-03 synthesis for the sitting; LO-07 honesty consumed without redefinition; typical for EXS-01 / EXS-02 / EXS-06.

**Tutor rationale.** Completion asks whether readiness is *appropriate for this sitting given the evidence*, not whether readiness is absolute. An expert tutor affirms provisional readiness strength when the trail supports it; the tutor does not invent readiness from proximity or volume.

**Success looks like:**

- Readiness speech is provisional, evidence-linked, and confined to prepared scope.
- Named fragile known topics remain honest without collapsing the whole preparation story.
- Intensive readiness work can honestly lighten toward proceed / monitor / maintenance.

**Must not:**

- Claim “exam ready” because a phase label, countdown, or completion event exists.
- Convert readiness into a numeric score in this corpus.
- Use readiness completion to excuse introducing new content as exam prep.
- Equate mock volume with readiness of unlearned scope.

**Evidence posture:** Requires ExCE-03 when this criterion is in focus; never sole proof from paper count or proximity.

**Applicability rule:**

> **ExCC-03 is binding for successful ExCT-01 / ExCT-02 / ExCT-03 closes that affirm sitting-facing readiness.  
> It is not a universal “exam ready” certificate for every strategy change or upstream handoff.**

---

### ExCC-04 — Confidence supported by educational evidence

**Definition.** The student’s examination confidence is **calibrated to accumulated educational evidence** — calm and purposeful where the trail supports strength; honest about remaining fragility — without manufacturing false certainty or pass promises.

**Maps primarily to:** EXO-04; typical for EXS-05 and as a supporting condition for EXS-06 / ExCT-01.

**Tutor rationale.** Unjustified panic and unjustified certainty both harm performance. Completion requires confidence that matches evidence — not pep-talk theatre or panic suppression that hides missing learning or unfinished revision.

**Success looks like:**

- Confidence speech cites what the evidence shows and what remains fragile.
- Affective misalignment that was the leading EXS-05 job has eased enough for the next honest posture.
- Confidence remains provisional where evidence is incomplete.

**Must not:**

- Treat “I feel ready” alone as ExCC-04.
- Promise a pass or mark as confidence completion.
- Use confidence completion to hide upstream Learning / Revision / Recovery ownership.
- Moralise ordinary exam nerves as failure to complete.

**Evidence posture:** Requires ExCE-04 when this criterion is in focus; soft self-report alone never substitutes for the full applicable ExCC set.

**Applicability rule:**

> **ExCC-04 is binding when EXO-04 / EXS-05 (or equivalent confidence calibration) was in focus, and as a supporting honesty condition for ExCT-01 proceed speech.  
> It is not a hidden universal gate that demands perfect calm before any monitoring close.**

---

### ExCC-05 — No outstanding preparation actions within Exam Coach authority

**Definition.** Within Exam Coach authority boundaries, there are **no outstanding assessment-facing preparation actions** that the Exam Coach still owes for this sitting — demonstration rehearsal, sitting plan, conditions practice, decision rules, or confidence calibration as scoped — without absorbing Learning / Recovery / Revision / Planning actions that Exam Coach must not own.

**Maps primarily to:** MS001 `EXAM_BOUNDARIES.md` lawful Exam Coach actions; architectural close of Exam Coach identity for the episode.

**Tutor rationale.** Completion means the Exam Coach has done what it may lawfully do. Outstanding first learning, consolidating revision, recovery, or structural plan change are **not** “outstanding Exam Coach actions” — they are upstream / planner handoffs (ExCT-04…ExCT-06 or MS002 EST6).

**Success looks like:**

- Remaining work, if any, is monitoring / maintenance (ExCT-02 / ExCT-03), ordinary Daily Coach under the plan, or explicit upstream ownership — not unfinished Exam Coach duties.
- Explainability can name that Exam Coach responsibilities for this sitting are cleared.
- Envelope-breaking needs are escalated honestly rather than left as fake local Exam Coach leftovers.

**Must not:**

- Declare ExCC-05 while still demanding intensive EXS loops as the lead story.
- Treat unfinished Revision / Recovery / Learning work as Exam Coach “outstanding actions” to keep grinding papers.
- Claim ExCC-05 from clearing a UI checklist of mocks.
- Invent new Exam Coach actions after declaring clearance solely from anxiety.

**Evidence posture:** Requires ExCE-05 for successful ExCT-01 / ExCT-03 closes; soft anxiety alone does not reopen infinite Exam Coach debt.

---

### ExCC-06 — Examination preparation purpose fulfilled (synthesis)

**Definition.** The active examination preparation emphasis has honestly fulfilled its educational purpose for the current sitting — applicable ExCC-01…ExCC-05 conditions are sufficiently advanced that intensive Exam Coach identity is no longer the lead educational story (or the honest next move is monitoring / maintenance / upstream handoff / post-examination reflection rather than more of the same).

**Maps primarily to:** MS002 EST1 / EST7 success postures; architectural close of intensive Exam Coach identity.

**Tutor rationale.** Completion is not only that one mock improved — it is that the tutor can honestly say Exam Coach educational responsibilities for this emphasis have been fulfilled, or that continuing exam theatre would be dishonest.

**Success looks like:**

- Applicable ExCC-01…ExCC-05 set for the active goods is honestly affirmable — **or** an upstream coach has taken ownership of remaining educational response (then prefer ExCT-04…ExCT-06 rather than pure Exam Coach success speech).
- Explainability can honestly say preparation has done enough for now, monitoring / maintenance is appropriate, the sitting may proceed, or upstream coaching leads.
- Prior-preparation and Exam Coach boundary honesty remain intact — completion does not rebrand first learning, revision, or recovery.

**Must not:**

- Declare purpose fulfilled while still demanding intensive crisis exam theatre indefinitely.
- Use ExCC-06 to mint mastery or guaranteed examination performance.
- Claim purpose fulfilled from checklist finish, mock count, elapsed time, or proximity alone.

**Evidence posture:** Synthesis criterion — affirmed only when the applicable ExCC-01…ExCC-05 set is honestly met and evidence under `COMPLETION_EVIDENCE.md` supports the claim — **or** when blockers force ExCT-04…ExCT-06 rather than fake success.

## 4. How Criteria Combine for Successful Completion

### 4.1 Core synthesis (always required for Exam Coach–success closes)

Successful proceed to examination (ExCT-01), monitoring until sitting (ExCT-02), or maintenance preparation (ExCT-03) requires honest affirmation of:

1. **ExCC-06** Examination preparation purpose fulfilled  
2. The **applicable objective-/strategy-aligned subset** of ExCC-01…ExCC-05 for the active EXO / EXS emphasis  
3. Supporting ExCE trail without ExCE-06 blockers that force Revision / Recovery / Learning / structural moves

### 4.2 Conditional criteria

| Criterion | When required |
|-----------|---------------|
| **ExCC-01** Preparation objectives addressed | Always for ExCT-01 / ExCT-02 / ExCT-03 — scoped to active EXO goods |
| **ExCC-02** Examination strategy outcomes achieved | When a primary EXS was in force for the episode being closed |
| **ExCC-03** Appropriate readiness for the current sitting | For ExCT-01 / ExCT-02 / ExCT-03 sitting-facing closes |
| **ExCC-04** Confidence supported by educational evidence | When EXO-04 / EXS-05 was in focus; supporting honesty for ExCT-01 proceed speech |
| **ExCC-05** No outstanding Exam Coach actions | For ExCT-01 / ExCT-03 (and ExCT-02 when intensive Exam Coach identity ends) |

### 4.3 Strategy emphasis (illustrative)

| Strategy emphasis | Criteria that typically lead |
|-------------------|------------------------------|
| EXS-01 Readiness Consolidation | ExCC-01 (EXO-01/02) + ExCC-03, then ExCC-06 |
| EXS-02 Examination Rehearsal | ExCC-01 (EXO-03/01) + ExCC-02 + ExCC-03, then ExCC-06 |
| EXS-03 Time-Management Preparation | ExCC-01 (EXO-02/05) + ExCC-02, then ExCC-06 |
| EXS-04 Question-Selection Practice | ExCC-01 (EXO-05/01) + ExCC-02, then ExCC-06 |
| EXS-05 Confidence Stabilisation | ExCC-04 + ExCC-01 honesty, then ExCC-06 |
| EXS-06 Final Consolidation | ExCC-01…ExCC-05 as scoped + ExCC-03, then ExCC-06 → often ExCT-01 / ExCT-02 / ExCT-03 |
| EXS-07 Post-Disruption Exam Prep | ExCC-01 / ExCC-02 for careful re-entry — refuse ExCT-01 if Recovery still primary (ExCT-05) |

### 4.4 Binding combination rule

> **All applicable criteria must be honestly affirmable from accumulated evidence.  
> Missing an applicable criterion means examination preparation completion is not yet achieved — use ExCT-02 monitoring language only if gathering / residual vigilance is honest, otherwise continue strategy, change strategy (MS002 EST5), escalate upstream (ExCT-04…ExCT-06), or apply MS002 EST6 when structure owns the problem.**

## 5. Explicit Non-Criteria (Forbidden Completion Grounds)

The following **never** complete examination preparation by themselves:

| Non-criterion | Why forbidden |
|---------------|---------------|
| Elapsed calendar days / weeks | Time is not educational evidence of Exam Coach purpose fulfilled |
| Examination proximity / countdown alone | Nearness ≠ readiness or addressed EXO goods |
| Mock / past-paper / question count alone | Volume ≠ assessment-facing educational meaning (EIP-002) |
| Attendance or login frequency alone | Presence ≠ demonstration / planning / conditions outcomes |
| Session / mission completion ticks alone | Completing sittings ≠ examination preparation completion |
| Examination prep checklist finished | Compliance ≠ Exam Coach purpose fulfilled |
| Strategy catalogue item “done” | Strategy compliance ≠ educational completion |
| Student declaration “I’m exam ready” alone | Soft signal; may support monitoring posture only with corroboration |
| Calendar “exam phase” label cleared | Phase theatre ≠ assessment-facing evidence |
| Comparison to other candidates’ mock volume | Shame theatre, not educational criterion |
| Feeling calm after a pep talk alone | Confidence without evidence trail ≠ ExCC-04 |

## 6. Relationship to Examination Objectives

| Objective | Completion reading |
|-----------|-------------------|
| EXO-01 Demonstrate accumulated understanding | Advanced when ExCC-01 / ExCC-03 hold for demonstration goods |
| EXO-02 Effective examination planning | Advanced when ExCC-01 / ExCC-02 hold for planning goods |
| EXO-03 Manage assessment conditions | Advanced when ExCC-01 / ExCC-02 hold for conditions goods |
| EXO-04 Reinforce examination confidence | Advanced provisionally when ExCC-04 holds — never as pass certainty |
| EXO-05 Sound decision-making during assessment | Advanced when ExCC-01 / ExCC-02 hold for sitting-judgement goods |

Hard rule:

> **Meeting completion criteria means Exam Coach educational responsibilities are sufficiently fulfilled for the next educational posture.  
> It does not mean mastery was earned, Learning Objectives were attained as mastery, or the examination is assured.**

---

## 7. Anti-Patterns (Forbidden)

- Declaring success because “the exam is next week”
- Declaring success because the student completed N mocks or a prep checklist
- Requiring universal perfect calm (ExCC-04 absolute) for every monitoring close
- Keeping intensive exam theatre after all applicable criteria are met
- Extending examination preparation indefinitely when upstream coach ownership is the honest move
- Using criteria as numeric scores or dashboards presented as educational truth
- Narrating criteria IDs to students
- Narrating completion as mastery graduation or guaranteed pass
