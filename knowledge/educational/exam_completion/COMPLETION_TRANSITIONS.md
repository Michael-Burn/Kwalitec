> **Transitions preserve educational continuity and authority boundaries.  
> Successful examination preparation may proceed to the sitting, continue monitoring until the sitting, or enter maintenance preparation.  
> Where unresolved educational weaknesses appear, authority returns to Revision, Recovery, or Learning Coach.  
> After the sitting, transition to post-examination educational reflection — not endless exam theatre.**

---

## 1. Purpose

Without transition law, completion becomes a badge with no coaching consequence — or a silent plan rewrite — or an endless intensive examination preparation loop.

With transition law, an expert tutor can:

- proceed to examination when Exam Coach duties are honestly fulfilled;
- continue monitoring until the sitting when residual vigilance is warranted;
- enter maintenance preparation when light keep-alive of examination approach is the honest next posture;
- hand back to Revision Coach when consolidating evidence becomes dominant;
- hand off to Recovery Coach when disruption is primary;
- escalate to Learning Coach when unresolved learning weaknesses appear;
- transition to post-examination educational reflection after the sitting;
- preserve Daily Coach and Canonical Study Plan authorities across those moves.

## 3. Transition Principles

1. **Continuity of learner history.** Completion does not wipe educational identity (EIP-005).  
2. **Explain before switch.** Material transitions are speakable under `COMPLETION_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.  
3. **Success lightens intensity.** ExCT-01 / ExCT-02 / ExCT-03 end intensive Exam Coach identity as the lead story when criteria are met.  
4. **Long-term intent unchanged by completion itself.** Structural change uses MS002 EST6 / Master Planner — never completion fiat.  
5. **Incomplete preparation is educational information** — not moral failure.  
6. **Escalate upstream rather than fake Exam Coach success** when Learning / Recovery / Revision owns the problem (architectural hard line).  
7. **Monitoring ≠ permanent intensive exam theatre.** ExCT-02 watchfulness must not keep crisis Exam Coach identity.  
8. **Sibling coach evidence persists** across completion transitions.  
9. **No streak, mock-count, proximity, or score gates.** Transitions are educational judgements.  
10. **One primary story in student speech** after the judgement.  
11. **Prior-preparation honesty.** Transitions never reinterpret completion as first learning or mastery.  
12. **Preserve Programme VI authority boundaries** across Daily Coach, Learning Coach, Recovery Coach, Revision Coach, and Master Planner.  
13. **Preparation may resume before the examination** when new evidence re-opens a lawful warrant — speak resume conditions honestly (see §8).

## 4. Transition Catalogue

### ExCT-01 — Proceed to examination

**Educational meaning.** Applicable completion criteria are honestly met on accumulated evidence. Exam Coach educational responsibilities for this sitting are fulfilled. The educationally appropriate next posture is to **proceed to the examination** — without claiming mastery or guaranteed performance.

**Aligns with:** Post-EST1 success posture when final preparation has done enough; architectural close of intensive Exam Coach identity before the sitting.

**Requires:**

- Applicable ExCC set affirmed (including ExCC-03 / ExCC-05 / ExCC-06 as scoped);
- Supporting ExCE trail without ExCE-06 blockers that force upstream / structural moves;
- Explainability can honestly say preparation has done enough for this sitting and what evidence supports that — without pass promises.

**Lawful effects:**

- End intensive Exam Coach episode as the lead coaching frame for this sitting;
- Affirm proceed-to-examination posture under authorised sitting context;
- Retain learner history; do not narrate success as mastery earned or guaranteed pass;
- Optional light ExCT-02 watch until the sitting if residual mild risk remains — speech must not keep two lead stories.

**Unlawful effects:**

- Declaring ExCT-01 because a countdown, checklist, mock quota, or timer finished;
- Narrating “proceed” while ExCE-06 blockers (Revision / Recovery / Learning / missing preparation) remain primary;
- Claiming mastery / guaranteed examination performance from ExCT-01;
- Silently rewriting plan envelopes “now that prep is done.”

**Authority boundary:** Exam Coach ends intensive assessment-facing lead story; Daily Coach still owns today’s priority under the authorised plan until the sitting; Master Planner intent unchanged.

---

### ExCT-02 — Continued monitoring until the sitting

**Educational meaning.** Either (a) completion is nearly supportable / supported but residual assessment-facing risk warrants watchfulness until the sitting without full intensive Exam Coach identity, or (b) evidence is still gathering and false ExCT-01 would be dishonest.

**Aligns with:** MS002 EST8 hold/gather specialised for the pre-sitting window; soft residual vigilance after near-success.

**Typical uses:**

- Applicable ExCC nearly met; mild residual readiness / confidence risk remains;
- Thin history: assessment-facing signs emerging, ExCC-03 not yet affirmable for ExCT-01;
- Post-ExCT-01 / ExCT-03 soft watch until the sitting without reopening intensive crisis unless warrants return.

**Lawful effects:**

- Reduce or end heavy intensive Exam Coach speech;
- Keep supportive assessment-facing tone and honest vigilance until the sitting;
- Continue gathering ExCE trail;
- Re-open intensive warrant/strategy if new ExCE-06 patterns or new lawful EPT warrants appear;
- Prefer ExCT-04…ExCT-06 if monitoring reveals upstream ownership rather than mere thin history.

**Unlawful effects:**

- Using “monitoring” as permanent unofficial intensive exam theatre without educational warrant;
- Using monitoring to avoid admitting ExCT-04…ExCT-06 or MS002 EST5–EST6 when those are the honest moves;
- Narrating monitoring as distrust, probation, or shame;
- Inventing mastery clearance or “exam ready” certainty during monitoring;
- Treating proximity alone as the reason monitoring ends in ExCT-01.

**Authority boundary:** Does not absorb Daily Coach day authority; does not invent structural window change.

---

### ExCT-03 — Maintenance preparation

**Educational meaning.** Applicable completion criteria are honestly met for the intensive assessment-facing job. **Light keep-alive of examination approach** — protecting known strengths, light sitting-plan rehearsal, evidence-calibrated confidence — is now the right educational posture until the sitting.

**Aligns with:** Lightened EST1 / EXS-06 keep-alive form after intensive EXS success.

**Requires:**

- Applicable ExCC set for the intensive strategy affirmed;
- ExCC-06 examination preparation purpose fulfilled for the intensive job;
- Supporting ExCE trail without blockers forcing upstream ownership;
- Explainability can honestly say the intensive job has done enough and light maintenance is appropriate.

**Lawful effects:**

- Primary posture becomes light maintenance of examination approach (often light EXS-06 form) inside authorised windows;
- Retain learner history; do not narrate maintenance as mastery earned or guaranteed pass;
- Keep light ExCT-02-class watch available if residual mild risk remains;
- Daily Coach still owns today’s priority under the authorised plan.

**Unlawful effects:**

- Declaring ExCT-03 because a checklist, streak, mock quota, or timer finished;
- Narrating “maintenance” while acute upstream problems remain the leading story;
- Claiming mastery / guaranteed performance from ExCT-03;
- Silently continuing intensive EXS-01…EXS-04 loops after claiming maintenance.

**Authority boundary:** Exam Coach ends intensive consolidating-exam identity for that emphasis; may supply light maintenance notes only; Master Planner intent unchanged.

**Relationship to ExCT-01 / ExCT-02:**

| Situation | Prefer |
|-----------|--------|
| Intensive job done; sitting may proceed with no special keep-alive story | **ExCT-01** (+ optional light ExCT-02) |
| Intensive job done; light approach keep-alive still warranted | **ExCT-03** |
| Nearly done / thin history / residual risk without full success speech | **ExCT-02** |

---

### ExCT-04 — Transition back to Revision Coach

**Educational meaning.** Accumulated examination preparation evidence indicates that **consolidating revision of previously learned material** is (or remains) the primary honest educational job. Authority must transition back to the **Revision Coach** rather than overriding Revision recommendations with more exam tips.

**Aligns with:** MS002 EST2.

**Architectural requirement restated:**

> **Where preparation evidence reveals unresolved educational weaknesses,  
> authority must transition back to the appropriate upstream coach  
> instead of extending examination preparation indefinitely.**

**When warranted (must hand back):**

- Revision warrant / incomplete consolidating completion becomes dominant for fragile known hubs blocking assessment preparation;
- Exam rehearsal shows durability / retrieval / fragile-knowledge problems that Revision Strategies own;
- Continuing EXS would require redefining or ignoring Revision Coach meaning;
- MS001 wrong-mode check fails: consolidation is the sole honest job;
- ExCE-06 revision-primary blockers dominate the trail.

**Lawful effects:**

- End or demote Exam Coach strategy ownership for that problem;
- Transfer consolidating ownership to Revision Coach (warrant → strategy → completion as applicable);
- Explain honestly: assessment approach waits on consolidating strength of known material;
- Exam Coach may remain layered only for approach framing that does not absorb revision authority.

**Unlawful effects:**

- Opening further EXS-02 / EXS-06 loops as a substitute for Revision Coach;
- Narrating handback as student failure rather than educational honesty;
- Keeping Exam Coach primary while contradicting Revision Coach recommendations;
- Using “one more mock week” to delay necessary Revision ownership.

**Authority boundary:** Revision Coach owns remaining consolidating response; Exam Coach may later supply supporting assessment-facing emphasis only if Revision meaning allows.

---

### ExCT-05 — Transition back to Recovery Coach

**Educational meaning.** **Significant disruption** is (or becomes) the primary educational problem. Examination preparation must not disguise catch-up as assessment-facing completion.

**Aligns with:** MS002 EST3.

**When warranted:**

- Active Recovery warrant is primary;
- Absence / capacity break makes examination completion speech dishonest;
- Student returns from disruption needing restorative continuity before ordinary ExCT-01 / ExCT-03;
- Attempting ExCT-01 / ExCT-03 would treat disruption as ordinary final prep success;
- ExCE-06 disruption-primary blockers dominate.

**Lawful effects:**

- Defer to Recovery Coach; examination strategies pause or become layered only when Recovery allows;
- After Recovery posture ends, prefer EXS-07 for careful exam re-entry before ordinary proceed / maintenance;
- Preserve continuity history; do not narrate as if disruption never occurred.

**Unlawful effects:**

- Declaring ExCT-01 as if illness / absence were a quiet prep week;
- Using examination rehearsal to bypass Recovery responsibilities;
- Inventing catch-up volume under an exam-completion label.

**Authority boundary:** Recovery Coach owns restorative response; Exam Coach does not complete catch-up as Exam Coach success.

---

### ExCT-06 — Transition back to Learning Coach

**Educational meaning.** Accumulated examination preparation evidence indicates that assessment-facing approaches are **insufficient** — a deeper learning problem exists (obstacle, thin genuine progression, misconception, or false readiness that exam tips cannot honestly repair). Authority must transition back to the **Learning Coach** rather than extending examination preparation indefinitely.

**Aligns with:** MS002 EST4.

**When warranted (must escalate):**

- Fair attempts at appropriate EXS strategies show no meaningful assessment-facing improvement because understanding never formed;
- Learning Coach posture already indicates obstacle / intervention ownership;
- “Prepared” material behaves as if understanding never formed, and demonstration speech has become dishonest;
- Exam Coach would need to override Learning Coach evidence to continue;
- Student needs diagnosis / Learning Intervention, not another examination approach label;
- ExCE-06 learning-problem blockers dominate the trail.

**Lawful effects:**

- End or demote Exam Coach strategy ownership for that problem;
- Transfer diagnostic / intervention ownership to Learning Coach;
- Explain honestly: examination preparation alone is not enough;
- Do **not** claim Learning Coach success from exam completion;
- Do **not** redefine mastery or invent first learning as “more exam prep.”

**Unlawful effects:**

- Opening further EXS-02 / EXS-04 loops as a substitute for diagnosis;
- Narrating escalation as student failure rather than educational honesty;
- Keeping Exam Coach as primary while contradicting Learning Coach evidence;
- Using “one more exam prep week” to delay necessary Learning Coach ownership.

**Authority boundary:** Learning Coach owns remaining educational response; Exam Coach may later supply supporting assessment-facing emphasis only if Learning Coach allows after diagnosis / intervention meaning.

---

### ExCT-07 — Transition to post-examination educational reflection

**Educational meaning.** The sitting has occurred (or been formally abandoned / deferred under plan honesty). Examination preparation is **no longer the primary educational job**. Authority transitions to **post-examination educational reflection** — reviewing what the sitting revealed, preserving educational continuity, and routing onward learning / revision / recovery / planning needs honestly.

**Aligns with:** MS002 EST7.

**Requires:**

- The examination sitting has taken place (or formal deferral / abandonment under plan honesty);
- Continuing EXS / ExCT-01…ExCT-03 speech would be assessment theatre without a sitting to prepare for;
- Reflection / review pathways can own the educational next step.

**Lawful effects:**

- End primary Exam Coach strategy / completion ownership for that sitting;
- Open post-examination reflection in supportive, evidence-honest language;
- Route new warrants: revision of known fragile hubs, Learning Coach diagnosis, Recovery if disruption followed, or Master Planner if next-sitting envelopes change;
- Preserve continuity — sitting outcome does not wipe educational identity.

**Unlawful effects:**

- Endless EXS-02 / ExCT-03 loops after the paper as if preparation were still primary;
- Minting mastery or failure identity from a single sitting without reflection honesty;
- Using post-exam speech to invent a new Canonical Study Plan silently;
- Treating ExCT-07 as automatic Exam Coach–success for the previous preparation (reflection may reveal that ExCT-04…ExCT-06 were the honest pre-sitting moves).

**Authority boundary:** Exam Coach ends primary for that sitting; Reflection and onward coaches / planner own next educational meaning.

## 5. Judgement → Transition Map

| Completion judgement | Primary transition |
|----------------------|-------------------|
| Applicable ExCC met; Exam Coach duties fulfilled; sitting may proceed | **ExCT-01** (+ optional light ExCT-02) |
| Nearly met / thin history / residual risk until sitting | **ExCT-02** |
| Applicable ExCC met; light approach keep-alive warranted | **ExCT-03** |
| Not met; consolidating revision primary | **ExCT-04** |
| Not met; disruption primary | **ExCT-05** |
| Not met; deeper learning problem / exam prep insufficient | **ExCT-06** |
| Sitting occurred / formally closed | **ExCT-07** |
| Not met; another local EXS fits better | **MS002 EST5** — strategy change within Exam Coach |
| Envelopes / feasibility broken | **MS002 EST6** — Rescheduling / Master Planner |
| Warrant unclear | Prefer gather under ExCT-02 / EST8 — do not invent ExCT-01 or EST6 theatre |

## 6. Post-Completion Authority Return (ExCT-01 / ExCT-02 / ExCT-03 Detail)

On lawful ExCT-01, ExCT-02, or ExCT-03:

| Authority | Posture after completion |
|-----------|--------------------------|
| **Exam Coach** | Intensive episode closed; may supply monitoring (ExCT-02) or maintenance (ExCT-03) notes only |
| **Daily Coach** | Owns ordinary day-priority under authorised plan |
| **Learning Coach** | Continues longitudinal meaning; unaffected inventively by completion |
| **Recovery Coach** | Unchanged — completion never absorbs restorative authority |
| **Revision Coach** | Unchanged — completion never overrides consolidating recommendations |
| **Master Planner** | Long-term intent unchanged by the completion act |
| **Scheduling** | No Exam-Coach-invented packing changes |
| **Evidence / Twin** | No mastery or guaranteed-performance write from completion event alone |

Architectural requirement restated:

> **Examination preparation completion confirms that the educational responsibilities of the Exam Coach have been fulfilled.  
> It must never be interpreted as evidence of mastery, guaranteed examination performance, or replacement for upstream educational coaching.  
> Successful close returns ordinary coaching posture without modifying long-term educational intent.**

## 8. Resume Conditions Before the Examination

Preparation may **resume** before the examination after ExCT-01 / ExCT-02 / ExCT-03 when new educational evidence re-opens a lawful examination preparation warrant — without treating resume as failure theatre.

**Lawful resume triggers (illustrative):**

- New ExCE-06 patterns appear (e.g. conditions fragility returns on prepared material);
- Revision / Recovery / Learning Coach returns ownership after resolving an upstream episode and Exam Coach layering is again lawful;
- Authorised plan / sitting context changes in a way that creates a new lawful EPT warrant;
- Monitoring (ExCT-02) accumulates evidence that intensive EXS is again the honest lead story.

**Unlawful resume:**

- Resuming intensive exam theatre solely from anxiety, countdown pressure, or “others are still grinding”;
- Resuming to compensate for absent first learning or unfinished Revision / Recovery ownership;
- Silent resume without explainability.

Students must hear **under what circumstances preparation may resume** (`COMPLETION_EXPLAINABILITY.md` §5.4).

## 9. Continuity and Authority Checklist (Every Material Transition)

Before enacting ExCT-01…ExCT-07, confirm:

| Check | Fail action |
|-------|-------------|
| Is learner history preserved in speech and meaning? | Rewrite narration; never invent wipe |
| Does prior preparation still hold for completed “exam prep”? | Refuse; route to first learning |
| Does evidence (not time/ticks/proximity/mocks alone) support the move? | Gather more / choose a different ExCT |
| Does Daily Coach still own today’s priority under the current contract? | Do not commandeer the day via completion speech |
| Is long-term intent change required? | Use MS002 EST6 — do not mutate plan in Exam Coach |
| Is mastery or guaranteed performance being claimed from the transition? | Refuse; keep EIP-006 honesty |
| Is consolidating revision now primary? | Use ExCT-04 — do not extend exam prep indefinitely |
| Is disruption primary? | Use ExCT-05 Recovery Coach |
| Is a learning obstacle now primary? | Use ExCT-06 Learning Coach |
| Can the student hear why the approach is changing? | Produce explainability first |

## 10. Anti-Transition Patterns (Forbidden)

- Permanent intensive examination preparation with no completion criteria  
- ExCT-01 / ExCT-03 from elapsed time, attendance, checklist ticks, mock counts, or proximity alone  
- Silent strategy oscillation without educational reason  
- Endless exam prep loops that avoid Learning / Recovery / Revision escalation  
- “Exam prep completed” mastery or guaranteed-pass speech  
- Returning to normal coaching while still demanding intensive crisis mock volume  
- Using transition to bypass Learning Coach diagnosis or Revision Coach recommendations  
- Using maintenance as euphemism for unresolved upstream weakness  
- Monitoring as shame probation  
- Completing catch-up disruption as Exam Coach success  
- Endless ExCT-03 after the sitting instead of ExCT-07
