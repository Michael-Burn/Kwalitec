## Authority

This document is the constitutional overview of the **Revision Coach** for Kwalitec.

It is subordinate to the Educational Constitution and specialised educational models. It governs **how Kwalitec consolidates previously learned knowledge into durable, retrievable understanding** while remaining faithful to the Canonical Study Plan and sibling coach authorities. It does not authorise implementation shortcuts that contradict the Constitution, and it does not invent prior learning, evidence, or planning meaning absent from upstream models.

Authority order for educational revision:

> Constitution defines educational truth and curriculum primacy.  
> Knowledge & Mastery defines coverage ≠ understanding ≠ mastery.  
> Evidence Model defines what may warrant educational claims.  
> Continuity Standard preserves rightful educational history across plan change.  
> Master Planner publishes the Canonical Study Plan and owns long-term educational envelopes, including protected revision windows.  
> Daily Coach owns *what is most educationally valuable today* under that contract.  
> Learning Coach owns longitudinal progression, obstacle diagnosis, and learning interventions.  
> Recovery Coach owns restorative coaching after meaningful disruption.  
> **This Educational Revision Model (Workstream 5 / MS001) defines how previously learned material is consolidated for durable exam preparation.**  
> Downstream coaching and narration surfaces must consume this educational reasoning — never replace first learning, recovery, or planning with revision theatre.

---

## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not treat revision as a second first-pass, nor as a catch-up disguise after absence. After material has been studied, the tutor answers one living educational question:

> **“What should this student revise now, and why?”**

That answer must strengthen retrieval and durability, reinforce understanding, integrate related concepts where warranted, maintain exam readiness, remain faithful to the Canonical Study Plan’s educational intent, and refuse to pretend that unlearned material is being “revised.”

This document records that tutor posture so every future Revision Coach subsystem has a single educational reference for *how revision is reasoned*.

> **The Revision Coach describes educational revision reasoning.  
> It does not implement Runtime A logic, algorithms, analytics, or Twin writers.**

---

## 2. What Educational Revision Is

**Educational revision** is the consolidating process by which Kwalitec helps a student **strengthen, retrieve, and integrate previously learned knowledge** so that understanding becomes more durable and exam-usable — without treating revision as first-time instruction, recovery from disruption, or long-term replanning.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Prior exposure** | Material the student has already studied or practised under lawful learning / plan authority | Has this been learned before? |
| **Revision warrant** | Judgement that consolidation of previously learned material is educationally justified now | Is revision needed — or first learning / recovery? |
| **Revision objectives** | Educational goods revision must pursue | What are we trying to strengthen? |
| **Revision priority** | Qualitative tutor ordering among lawful revision candidates | What should be revised first, and why? |
| **Revision emphasis** | Recommended focus within plan and day envelopes | How should today’s revision attention be directed? |

Revision is:

- **consolidating** — it strengthens existing learning rather than introducing new syllabus content as “revision”;
- **evidence-traceable** — recommendations cite accumulated educational evidence of prior learning and current need;
- **durability-seeking** — it prefers lasting retrieval and understanding over cram theatre;
- **plan-faithful** — it respects Canonical Study Plan revision windows and protections;
- **coach-coordinated** — it informs Daily Coach; consumes Learning Coach evidence; never replaces Recovery Coach;
- **exam-aware** — it serves professional examination readiness without inventing mastery from revision volume;
- **explainable** — students understand why this topic, what evidence supports it, and what outcome is expected.

Revision is **not**:

- a substitute for first-pass Learning Mode or Learning Coach progression of unlearned material;
- a licence to label new syllabus content as revision;
- a bypass of Recovery Coach when meaningful disruption is the true problem;
- inference of mastery from revision ticks, question volume, or calendar “revision phase” labels alone;
- a rewrite of the Canonical Study Plan or educational strategy;
- a claim that revision guarantees a pass.

## 3. What the Revision Coach Is

The **Revision Coach** is the educational capability that, given **prior exposure**, **accumulated educational evidence**, and **authorised plan / day context**, produces a **revision warrant**, selects **revision objectives**, states **qualitative priority emphasis**, states **boundary posture**, and produces **revision explainability**.

| Input family | Role |
|--------------|------|
| **Prior exposure trail** | Evidence that material was previously studied / practised — prerequisite for revision speech |
| **Accumulated Educational Evidence** | Trail that supports forgetting risk, durability, exam proximity, interdependence (EIP-002) |
| **Canonical Study Plan posture** | Including protected revision windows — consumed, not rewritten |
| **Student Educational Profile** | Strengths / weaknesses, decay risk, revision maturity, capacity |
| **Learning Coach meaning** | Progression / evidence / obstacle / intervention meaning — consumed honestly, never overridden |
| **Daily Coach context** | Today’s lawful day-priority authority — coordinated, never bypassed |
| **Recovery Coach posture** | Whether restorative disruption coaching is active — respected, never replaced |

into:

| Output family | Role |
|---------------|------|
| **Revision warrant** | Whether revision is educationally justified now |
| **Revision objectives in focus** | Which RVO-XX goods this emphasis prioritises |
| **Priority emphasis** | Qualitative ordering among lawful revision candidates (RVP factors) |
| **Boundary posture** | Local revision emphasis vs handoff to first learning / recovery / planning |
| **Expected educational outcome** | What consolidation should achieve if the advice is followed |
| **Revision explainability** | Plain-language account for the student |

It answers:

| Question | Educational answer |
|----------|-------------------|
| What should be revised? | Previously learned material with a lawful revision warrant |
| Why now? | Trigger class + evidence (forgetting, spacing, exam approach, fragility, prerequisites, interdependence) |
| What should revision achieve? | Retrieval, durability, understanding, integration, exam readiness |
| May Revision Coach enact this alone? | Only within boundaries; else hand off or escalate |
| How does this serve long-term retention? | Explicit link from emphasis to durable exam-usable knowledge |

The Revision Coach is:

- **prior-exposure-gated** — refuses revision theatre for unlearned syllabus content;
- **objective-bound** — pursues defined revision educational goods;
- **priority-aware** — ranks emphasis qualitatively using tutor factors, never opaque scores;
- **boundary-faithful** — protects plan, Daily Coach, Learning Coach, Recovery Coach, and mastery authorities;
- **evidence-honest** — never mints durability or readiness from revision volume alone;
- **explainable** — supportive, evidence-linked language;
- **non-scoring** — this Model defines meaning, not numeric revision metrics.

The Revision Coach is **not**:

- a second Master Planner, Scheduling Engine, Strategy selector, or Profile diagnostician;
- a second Daily Coach, Learning Coach, or Recovery Coach;
- ownership of Educational Evidence recording or Twin write authority;
- a UI surface, analytics dashboard, or scoring engine;
- a licence to disguise first-pass backlog or recovery catch-up as revision.

---

## 4. Educational Purpose

The Revision Coach exists so that:

1. **Previously learned material is consolidated deliberately** — not left to chance decay until panic before the sitting.
2. **Revision remains revision** — new content stays first learning; disruption stays recovery; long-term intent stays planning.
3. **Exam readiness is built from durable retrieval** — not from calendar labels or last-minute volume theatre.
4. **Evidence stays visible** — students see why this topic, not an opaque optimiser choice.
5. **Authority stays layered** — consolidating coaching does not silently become replanning, diagnosis, or restorative catch-up.
6. **Students understand the arc** — why revise, what evidence, what outcome, how it serves long-term retention.

---

## 5. Core Responsibilities

The Revision Coach is educationally responsible for:

| Responsibility | Meaning |
|----------------|---------|
| **Confirm prior exposure** | Refuse revision speech without prior learning warrant |
| **State revision warrant** | Explicit educational justification for consolidation now |
| **Pursue revision objectives** | Optimise consolidating goods (RVO-01…RVO-05) |
| **Prioritise revision emphasis** | Apply qualitative tutor factors (RVP) without numeric scoring |
| **Bound Revision Coach authority** | Separate consolidating emphasis from first learning, recovery, and planning |
| **Respect sibling coaches** | Coordinate with Daily Coach; consume Learning Coach evidence; never replace Recovery Coach |
| **Protect plan revision intent** | Honour protected revision windows as educational meaning, not residual scraps |
| **Explain consolidating purpose** | Make revision speakable with evidence and expected outcomes |

It is **not** educationally responsible for:

- introducing new syllabus content under a revision label;
- rewriting Canonical Study Plans or educational strategy;
- deciding today’s Daily Coach primary objective by commandeering day authority;
- diagnosing learning obstacles or selecting Learning Interventions (Learning Coach owns those meanings);
- restoring progress after meaningful disruption (Recovery Coach owns that meaning);
- inferring or redefining mastery;
- writing Twin estimates by itself;
- packing calendars or inventing intensity (Scheduling / Master Planner ownership);
- producing opaque “revision scores” as educational proof.

---

## 6. Binding Educational Doctrine

1. **Revision strengthens existing learning.** It does not replace first learning, recovery, or long-term planning.
2. **Prior exposure is mandatory.** Material without prior study / practice trail is not revision — it is first learning (or Learning Coach response).
3. **Traceability is mandatory.** Every material revision recommendation must cite accumulated educational evidence and a lawful warrant.
4. **Revision ≠ recovery.** Meaningful disruption is Recovery Coach territory; revision must not compensate for absent continuity by inventing consolidation theatre.
5. **Revision ≠ first learning.** New syllabus content must not be introduced as revision.
6. **Mastery is never redefined by revision.** Revision volume, ticks, and phase labels do not mint understanding or exam readiness (EIP-006).
7. **Daily Coach authority is preserved.** Revision informs today’s emphasis; it does not commandeer day-priority selection.
8. **Learning Coach evidence is preserved.** Revision does not override progression, obstacle, or intervention meaning.
9. **Canonical Study Plan remains the contract.** Revision may recommend emphasis within authorised revision windows; it may not independently rewrite the plan.
10. **Explainability is mandatory.** Silent revision theatre and opaque priority speech are forbidden (EIP-003).

## 7. Position Relative to Sibling Authorities

| Authority | Owns | Revision Coach relationship |
|-----------|------|-----------------------------|
| **Master Planner / Canonical Study Plan** | Long-term educational envelopes, protected revision windows, published contract | Consumes; never rewrites independently |
| **Scheduling / Rescheduling** | Allocation of revision windows and protections | Escalates when allocation change is required; does not steal protections |
| **Daily Coach** | Today’s educational priority under the plan | Coordinates emphasis; never bypasses |
| **Learning Coach** | Progression, obstacles, learning interventions | Consumes evidence; never overrides |
| **Recovery Coach** | Restorative coaching after meaningful disruption | Defers when disruption is primary; never replaces |
| **Educational Continuity (EIP-005)** | Persistence of learner educational history | Respects continuity; revision speech must not invent prior learning |
| **Knowledge & Mastery (EIP-006)** | Ladder meanings; Revision Mode must be labelled as revision | Never collapses revision into mastery speech or silent Learning Mode replacement |

```
Prior exposure + revision warrant
        │
        ▼
Revision Coach (this Model)
        │  warrant + objectives + priority emphasis + boundaries
        │
        ├─ lawful consolidating emphasis (within envelopes)
        │     → informs Daily Coach emphasis & session design
        │     → respects protected revision windows already published
        │
        └─ wrong-mode detection
              → first learning / Learning Coach (no prior exposure or learning barrier)
              → Recovery Coach (meaningful disruption is primary)
              → Master Planner / Scheduling (structural plan / window change)
```

## 8. Relationship to Daily Coach and Protected Revision

Daily Coach already recognises **authorised plan work**, including protected revision when the Canonical Study Plan has placed revision windows (see Daily Coach decision / protection gates).

| Layer | Question | Owner |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Plan / scheduling revision windows** | Has protected revision been placed as educational intent? | Master Planner / Scheduling |
| **Daily revision coaching** | What is today’s objective under that posture? | Daily Coach |
| **Educational Revision Model** | What previously learned material should be consolidated now — which objectives, priorities, boundaries, and explanations? | **Revision Coach (this corpus)** |

The Revision Coach supplies the **constitutional educational meaning** of revision as consolidating coaching.  
The Daily Coach **executes today’s priority** under the plan, including when that priority is revision work.  
Neither layer may invent first-pass content as revision or steal protected revision for unfinished first learning without lawful escalation.

---

## 9. Relationship to Learning Coach

| Situation | Primary coach | Revision Coach role |
|-----------|---------------|---------------------|
| Material never studied | Learning Coach / Daily first-pass | **Refuse revision warrant** |
| Progression thin or obstacle diagnosed on previously covered material | Learning Coach (obstacle → intervention) | May support retrieval / reinforcement emphasis **after** consuming diagnosis; must not invent competing cures |
| Spaced return shows durable retrieval risk | Revision Coach (often with Learning Coach decay posture) | Recommend consolidating emphasis with evidence-honest speech |
| False readiness / exam theatre | Learning Coach owns honesty of readiness claims | Revision Coach must not mint readiness from revision volume |

Hard rule:

> **Revision Coach must not override Learning Coach evidence.  
> Revisiting a topic is not proof that learning progressed or that mastery exists.**

---

## 10. Relationship to Recovery Coach

| Situation | Primary coach | Revision Coach role |
|-----------|---------------|---------------------|
| Prolonged absence / meaningful disruption | Recovery Coach | Defer restorative continuity; do not disguise catch-up as revision |
| Student returns and needs retrieval of at-risk previously learned scope | Layered | Recovery may emphasise focused retrieval as restorative strategy; Revision Coach may later own ordinary consolidation once recovery posture ends |
| Fragile durability without disruption | Revision Coach | Ordinary consolidating warrant — not a recovery episode |

Hard rule:

> **Revision must never be used to compensate for absent first learning or bypass Recovery Coach responsibilities.**
