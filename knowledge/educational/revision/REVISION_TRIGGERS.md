> **Revision requires prior exposure.  
> Not every quiet week is forgetting.  
> Not every missed session is a revision warrant.  
> Revision recommendations must be traceable to accumulated educational evidence.**

Identifier note: Revision Triggers use **RVT-XX** so they never collide with Recovery Triggers (**RT-XX**).

---

## 1. Purpose

If revision fires without prior learning, students experience first-pass content falsely labelled as consolidation.  
If revision never fires, previously learned material decays until panic before the sitting.  
If revision steals Recovery Coach’s job, disruption is papered over with “review this chapter” theatre.

This document draws bright lines between:

- **first learning** (no prior exposure / Learning Mode),
- **recovery** (meaningful disruption — Recovery Coach),
- **revision** (consolidating previously learned material — Revision Coach).

---

## 2. Core Distinctions

| Class | Meaning | Lawful owner |
|-------|---------|--------------|
| **First learning** | Student has not yet had meaningful educational exposure to the syllabus unit | Daily Coach / Learning Mode / Learning Coach — **not Revision Coach** |
| **Recovery** | Meaningful disruption to continuity / capacity / learning trail requiring restorative coaching | Recovery Coach — **not Revision Coach as primary** |
| **Revision** | Prior exposure exists and consolidating return is educationally warranted now | **Revision Coach** (this corpus), informing Daily Coach |

Binding principle:

> **Revision is prior-exposure-gated and evidence-gated.  
> Calendar “revision phase” labels alone are not educational proof of warrant.**

---

## 3. Preconditions for Any Revision Warrant

Before any RVT-XX trigger may open a Revision Coach recommendation, **all** of the following must hold:

1. **Prior exposure** — the target material has been studied or practised under lawful learning / plan authority (not merely appeared on a future calendar cell).
2. **Evidence honesty** — the warrant cites accumulated educational evidence or an authorised plan revision window with educational substance (EIP-002).
3. **Wrong-mode check** — the primary need is not absent first learning and not Recovery Coach restorative continuity.
4. **Plan / day envelope** — emphasis can be recommended within Canonical Study Plan and Daily Coach authority (or escalated honestly if not).

If any precondition fails, refuse revision theatre and hand off appropriately.

---

## 4. Not Revision (Refuse Warrant)

Treat as **not revision** — do **not** open a Revision Coach consolidating recommendation as primary — when any of the following hold:

| Situation | Why not revision | Lawful alternative |
|-----------|------------------|--------------------|
| Syllabus unit never studied | No prior exposure | First learning / Daily Coach Learning Mode |
| “Revision” request is actually unfinished first-pass backlog | Would steal consolidating meaning | First-pass under plan; escalate if protections conflict |
| Prolonged absence / meaningful disruption is the primary problem | Restorative continuity owns the moment | Recovery Coach (RT-* family) |
| Temporary fluctuation only (one quiet evening, short variation) | No consolidating crisis | Ordinary Daily Coach continuity — optional light return only if already authorised |
| Learning obstacle blocks progress on material that was “covered” but never understood | Diagnosis before consolidation theatre | Learning Coach obstacle → intervention |
| Thin or absent evidence of need, with no authorised revision window | Avoid invented revision pressure | Gather warrant / follow plan rhythm |

## 5. Revision Trigger Catalogue

Each trigger class below may warrant revision when preconditions and evidence support it. Triggers may co-occur.

### RVT-01 — Evidence of forgetting

**Definition.** Accumulated educational evidence indicates that previously demonstrated retrieval or application has weakened on return — enough to justify consolidating practice.

**Typical cues:**

- Spaced return weaker than earlier demonstrated performance
- Learning Coach decay / inconsistent progression posture on previously covered scope
- Student can no longer retrieve methods that were previously workable

**Revision emphasis:** RVO-01, RVO-02 primarily; RVO-03 if understanding also brittle.

**Must not:** Infer total forgetting from one missed week without return evidence; shame the student for ordinary decay.

---

### RVT-02 — Planned spaced review

**Definition.** Authorised plan design (protected revision window, consolidation phase, or scheduled return) calls for return to previously learned material as educational intent — not leftover calendar scraps.

**Typical cues:**

- Canonical Study Plan / blueprint places protected revision or consolidation return
- Profile / strategy already treats consolidation as the lawful next educational story for known material
- Spacing interval designed into the journey has matured

**Revision emphasis:** RVO-02, RVO-01; RVO-05 as exam proximity warrants.

**Must not:** Invent a revision window because packing left empty weeks; treat a phase label without return substance as warrant.

---

### RVT-03 — Approaching examination

**Definition.** Examination proximity makes consolidating previously learned material educationally urgent — maintaining exam-usable strength of what is already known.

**Typical cues:**

- Sitting date near enough that plan intent shifts toward exam-facing consolidation
- Profile revision maturity / mode authority indicates revision substance (not premature cram of unlearned content)
- Authorised mock / checkpoint preparation on previously covered syllabus

**Revision emphasis:** RVO-05 with RVO-01/RVO-02; RVO-04 where integration serves paper performance.

**Must not:** Use proximity to introduce new syllabus as “last-minute revision”; claim readiness from proximity alone.

---

### RVT-04 — Fragile durability

**Definition.** Evidence or Profile posture indicates that understanding of previously learned material is brittle — likely to fail under spacing, variation, or exam pressure — even if first-pass ticks look complete.

**Typical cues:**

- Same-day success without durable return
- Thin practice depth relative to coverage on previously studied units
- Learning Coach thin / falsely ready postures on consolidating candidates
- Profile decay risk elevated on covered strengths that have not been revisited

**Revision emphasis:** RVO-02, RVO-03, RVO-01.

**Must not:** Equate “covered on calendar” with durable knowledge; invent fragility without any evidence trail when plan already schedules ordinary return soon.

---

### RVT-05 — Prerequisite reinforcement

**Definition.** Later work (current or imminent) depends on previously learned foundations that need consolidating reinforcement so that subsequent learning remains safe.

**Typical cues:**

- Upcoming authorised topic depends on earlier units with weak return evidence
- Learning Coach / Profile flags prerequisite fragility on studied foundations
- Curriculum dependency makes reinforcement of prior learning the honest next consolidating job

**Revision emphasis:** RVO-03, RVO-01; supports later first learning without replacing it.

**Must not:** Teach the prerequisite for the first time under a revision label; expand into unrelated new content.

---

### RVT-06 — Topic interdependence

**Definition.** Previously learned related topics need consolidating together because exam use or curriculum structure requires them as a connected set.

**Typical cues:**

- Cross-topic methods commonly examined together after both parts were studied
- Integration gaps on return (student knows pieces in isolation but not together)
- Plan / blueprint interdependence that makes joint revision educationally coherent

**Revision emphasis:** RVO-04, with RVO-01/RVO-05 as warranted.

**Must not:** Force joint revision when one of the topics lacks prior exposure; use interdependence as breadth-first new learning.

---

## 6. Trigger Co-occurrence and Precedence

When triggers co-occur:

1. **Wrong-mode first** — if Recovery RT-* meaningful disruption or absent first learning applies, those own the primary response.
2. **Evidence of forgetting / fragile durability (RVT-01, RVT-04)** often outrank pure calendar spacing when return evidence is poor.
3. **Approaching examination (RVT-03)** intensifies exam-facing form of already warranted consolidation — it does not invent prior exposure.
4. **Planned spaced review (RVT-02)** supplies lawful window; other triggers supply *what* to emphasise inside it.
5. **Prerequisite (RVT-05)** and **interdependence (RVT-06)** shape *which previously learned set* enters focus.

Topic-level ordering within a warrant uses `REVISION_PRIORITIES.md`.

---

## 7. Relationship to Recovery Triggers

| Recovery trigger family | Revision Coach posture |
|-------------------------|------------------------|
| RT-01 / RT-02 absence / missed sessions | Prefer Recovery Coach; revision of at-risk known material may appear later as layered retrieval — not as primary “revision episode” to hide disruption |
| RT-03 / RT-04 learning / regression during disruption | Consume Learning Coach + Recovery meanings; do not open competing revision theatre that skips restorative continuity |
| No recovery warrant; ordinary decay on known material | Revision Coach RVT-01 / RVT-04 lawful |

Hard rule from architectural requirement:

> **Revision must never compensate for absent first learning or bypass Recovery Coach responsibilities.**

---

## 8. Lawful vs Unlawful Examples

| Situation | Lawful | Unlawful |
|-----------|--------|----------|
| Spaced return weak on CM1 chapter studied six weeks ago | RVT-01 / RVT-04; revise that chapter for retrieval | Label an unread later chapter as “revision” |
| Plan opens protected revision window | RVT-02; prioritise fragile previously learned units | Fill window with unfinished first-pass topics |
| Exam in three weeks; two topics never studied | First learning for unlearned; revision only for known | “Revise the whole syllabus” including unlearned units |
| Student ill for two weeks | Recovery Coach primary | Open Revision Coach episode as if disruption were ordinary spacing |
| Prerequisite of next topic was studied but brittle | RVT-05 reinforcement | Teach prerequisite first time as revision |
