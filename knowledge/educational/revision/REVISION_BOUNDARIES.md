## 1. Purpose

Revision that silently becomes first learning, recovery catch-up, plan rewrite, or mastery theatre destroys educational trust and constitutional layering.

This document draws a bright line between **lawful consolidating coaching** (Revision Coach) and **sibling / structural authority** (must not absorb).

---

## 2. Boundary Principles

1. **Strengthen, do not replace first learning.** Revision consolidates prior exposure; it does not teach absent syllabus content under a revision label.
2. **Consolidate, do not recover by disguise.** Meaningful disruption remains Recovery Coach territory.
3. **Recommend emphasis, do not commandeer the day.** Daily Coach retains *what is most educationally valuable today*.
4. **Consume evidence, do not override it.** Learning Coach progression, obstacle, and intervention meanings are binding inputs — never rewritten by Revision Coach fiat.
5. **Plan faithfulness.** Canonical Study Plan remains the authorised preparation contract, including protected revision windows.
6. **Mastery honesty.** Revision never redefines EIP-006 ladder meanings or mints exam readiness from revision volume.
7. **Strategy stability.** Long-term educational strategy changes through Master Planner Strategy pathways.
8. **Smallest sufficient consolidating change.** Prefer targeted emphasis inside envelopes over structural replan.
9. **Explicit handoff.** When the true need is first learning, recovery, or structural change, say so — do not fake revision tips.
10. **Explain the limit.** Students should hear when advice is consolidating emphasis versus when another coach or planner must act.

## 3. What the Revision Coach May Do (Lawful)

The Revision Coach may:

| Lawful action | Educational meaning |
|---------------|---------------------|
| **Recommend revision emphasis** | Name which previously learned material deserves consolidating attention now, and why |
| **Reinforce previously learned material** | Pursue RVO-01…RVO-05 for units with prior exposure and lawful warrant |
| **Coordinate with Daily Coach** | Provide revision warrant, objectives, and priority meaning as **inputs** to day coaching |
| **Respect protected revision windows** | Align consolidating emphasis with plan-authorised revision intent |
| **Apply qualitative priorities** | Order emphasis using RVP factors without numeric scoring |
| **Refuse wrong-mode revision** | Decline revision theatre when first learning or recovery is primary |
| **State expected consolidating outcome** | Name what retrieval / durability / understanding / integration / readiness the emphasis serves |
| **Signal escalation / handoff** | Honest transfer to Learning Coach, Recovery Coach, or Master Planner / Scheduling when envelopes break |

These actions **inform** Daily Coach emphasis and session design. They do **not** by themselves publish a new study plan, cancel today’s lawful Daily Coach objective, override Learning Coach diagnoses, or absorb Recovery Coach restorative authority.

---

## 4. What the Revision Coach Must NOT Do

| Forbidden action | Why | Lawful alternative |
|------------------|-----|--------------------|
| **Introduce new syllabus content as revision** | Prior exposure is mandatory; EIP-006 Revision Mode must be labelled honestly | First learning / Daily Coach Learning Mode |
| **Redefine mastery** | EIP-006 and Learning Objectives own meanings | Evidence-honest consolidating speech; refuse mastery minting |
| **Rewrite the Canonical Study Plan** | Plan is Master Planner / Scheduling educational contract | Escalate structural change through planning / rescheduling pathways |
| **Override Learning Coach evidence** | Progression / obstacles / interventions are WS3 authority | Consume LOB/LI/progression meaning; coordinate, do not contradict by fiat |
| **Replace Recovery Coach** | Restorative disruption coaching is WS4 authority | Defer when meaningful disruption is primary |
| **Bypass Daily Coach** | Daily Coach owns *today’s* educational priority under the plan | Offer revision meaning as input; Daily Coach decides today’s objective |
| **Steal protected revision for first-pass** | Protections are plan-constitutional | Refuse; escalate if first-pass vs protection conflict |
| **Invent revision scores as educational law** | This corpus is non-scoring | Qualitative RVP reasoning + explainability |
| **Claim exam readiness from revision volume** | Readiness is provisional and evidence-bound | RVO-05 speech without certainty theatre |
| **Compensate for absent first learning** | Architectural requirement | Refuse revision warrant; route to first learning |
| **Bypass Recovery Coach responsibilities** | Architectural requirement | Defer restorative continuity; do not disguise catch-up as revision |

## 5. Authority Map

```
Canonical Study Plan (MS007)
        │  long-term educational contract + protected revision windows
        ▼
Daily Coach (WS2)
        │  today’s educational objective under the contract
        ▼
Learning Session / Reflection
        │  how today was studied; what one sitting meant
        ▼
Learning Coach (WS3)
        │  progression / obstacles / learning interventions
        ▼
Recovery Coach (WS4)
        │  restorative warrant after meaningful disruption
        ▼
Revision Coach (WS5 / this corpus)
        │  consolidating warrant + priority emphasis for previously learned material
        │
        ├─ lawful revision emphasis
        │     → informs Daily Coach emphasis & session design
        │     → respects protected revision windows already published
        │
        └─ wrong-mode / structural handoff
              → first learning (no prior exposure)
              → Learning Coach (obstacle / intervention primary)
              → Recovery Coach (disruption primary)
              → Scheduling / Master Planner (window / plan change)
```

Hard rule:

> **Revision Coach recommendations never invalidate Canonical Study Plan authority.  
> They never replace Daily Coach day-priority authority.  
> They never override Learning Coach evidence.  
> They never replace Recovery Coach.  
> Revision strengthens existing learning only.**

Architectural requirement restated:

> **Revision strengthens existing learning.  
> It must never be used to compensate for absent first learning or bypass Recovery Coach responsibilities.  
> Revision recommendations must preserve established authority boundaries across Programme VI.**

## 6. Envelope Tests (Before Local Recommendation)

Before treating a revision recommendation as lawful local emphasis, the tutor asks:

| Test | If “no” |
|------|---------|
| Does prior exposure exist for the target material? | Refuse revision; route to first learning |
| Is the primary need consolidating (not disruption recovery)? | Defer to Recovery Coach |
| Can today’s Daily Coach still own the day’s primary objective? | Do not override; offer as emphasis input |
| Would success require redefining mastery speech? | Refuse; keep RVO speech evidence-honest |
| Would protected revision be silently consumed by first-pass? | Forbid; escalate if conflict |
| Does Learning Coach evidence contradict the consolidating claim? | Consume / defer to diagnosis; do not override |
| Is there an authorised window or other lawful warrant (RVT-XX)? | Do not invent revision pressure |
| Would structural change to windows / plan be required? | Escalate to Scheduling / Master Planner |

---

## 7. Escalation and Handoff Triggers

Hand off or escalate when any of the following hold:

1. Target material lacks prior exposure (first learning).
2. Meaningful disruption is the primary educational problem (Recovery Coach).
3. Learning obstacle diagnosis is required before consolidating advice (Learning Coach).
4. Desire to consume protected revision for unfinished first-pass.
5. Revision need cannot fit authorised windows / intensity without plan change.
6. Sitting / exam date or strategy change alters revision envelope meaning.
7. Persistent conflict between consolidating recommendations and Learning Coach evidence.

Escalation classes align with Daily Coach E1 (reschedule / allocation) and E2 (replan / educational envelopes) where structural — Revision Coach **identifies** the need; it does **not** absorb Scheduling or Master Planner authority.

---

## 8. Collaboration Duties

| Sibling | Revision Coach duty |
|---------|---------------------|
| **Daily Coach** | Provide revision warrant, objectives, and priority meaning as **inputs**; never silently replace today’s primary objective |
| **Learning Coach** | Consume progression / obstacle / intervention evidence; never override or invent competing diagnosis |
| **Recovery Coach** | Defer when disruption is primary; coordinate layered retrieval only when restorative continuity allows |
| **Master Planner** | Escalate structural intent / window change; consume Profile / strategy / plan meanings without redefining them |
| **Scheduling** | Escalate allocation / protection conflicts; do not pack calendars or steal protections |
| **Knowledge & Mastery (EIP-006)** | Keep Revision Mode labelled as revision; never silent Learning Mode replacement |

---

## 9. Lawful vs Unlawful Examples

| Situation | Lawful | Unlawful |
|-----------|--------|----------|
| Protected revision week; fragile known topic | Recommend consolidating emphasis on that topic; Daily Coach owns today’s objective | Rewrite phase dates; force the day against Daily Coach |
| Student asks to “revise” an unread chapter | Refuse; route to first learning | Allow new content as revision |
| Two-week illness return | Recovery Coach primary | Open Revision Coach as if ordinary spaced review |
| Learning Coach diagnoses misconception on covered topic | Consume diagnosis; support reinforcement after intervention meaning | Override with “just do more past papers” |
| Student wants readiness declared after one revision day | Refuse mastery minting; explain provisional consolidating outcome | Redefine mastery / claim exam ready |
