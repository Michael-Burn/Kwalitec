## 1. Purpose

Without explicit revision objectives, “revision” collapses into cramming new content, recycling calendar labels, or pretending volume equals durability.

With explicit objectives, an expert tutor can choose consolidating emphasis that strengthens retrieval, durability, understanding, integration, and exam readiness — while remaining honest that the student has already met the material before.

---

## 2. Objective Catalogue

### RVO-01 — Strengthen retrieval

**Definition.** Improve the student’s ability to recall and produce previously learned knowledge and methods without undue prompting — so that knowledge is usable under exam conditions.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional examinations reward what can be retrieved under time pressure. An expert IFoA tutor revises so that known material becomes findable again — not so that notes are merely re-read.

**Success looks like:**

- The student can retrieve key results, definitions, and methods with less scaffolding than at first exposure.
- Revision sittings emphasise active recall / practice of previously learned scope.
- Speech names retrieval as the educational good, not “covering the topic again.”

**Must not:**

- Equate passive re-reading with strengthened retrieval.
- Claim retrieval success from a single same-day success without any return warrant (Learning Coach durability reading still applies).

---

### RVO-02 — Improve durability

**Definition.** Increase the likelihood that previously demonstrated understanding persists across spacing — so that learning survives the weeks between study and examination.

**Tutor rationale.** IFoA journeys are long. Material learned once and never returned to becomes fragile. Revision exists so that knowledge becomes durable, not merely recently completed.

**Success looks like:**

- Spaced return appears when evidence or plan windows warrant it.
- Durability claims remain provisional and evidence-honest (EIP-002 / EIP-006).
- Revision protects against known decay risk without inventing catastrophic forgetting from thin evidence.

**Must not:**

- Treat calendar proximity alone as proof of durability.
- Narrate revision ticks as permanent retention.

---

### RVO-03 — Reinforce understanding

**Definition.** Deepen conceptual grasp of previously studied material so that the student can explain why methods work — not only reproduce steps from memory of a worked example.

**Tutor rationale.** Fragile familiarity often masquerades as understanding after first pass. Revision that reinforces understanding reduces brittle “pattern matching” under exam novelty.

**Success looks like:**

- Revision asks for explanation, variation, and sense-checking of previously covered ideas.
- Misconceptions identified on return are repaired as consolidation — not narrated as brand-new first learning when prior exposure exists.
- Understanding claims stay evidence-honest and never redefine mastery.

**Must not:**

- Confuse finishing a revision checklist with reinforced understanding.
- Use revision to silently teach an entirely new syllabus unit.

---

### RVO-04 — Integrate related concepts

**Definition.** Help the student connect previously learned topics that depend on, reinforce, or commonly appear together — so that knowledge forms a usable network rather than isolated ticks.

**Tutor rationale.** IFoA papers reward recognising when ideas travel together (prerequisites, dual methods, cross-topic applications). Integration is a consolidating good after the parts have been studied.

**Success looks like:**

- Revision emphasis links related previously learned units when interdependence warrants it.
- The student can see why Topic A and Topic B belong in the same consolidating pass.
- Integration speech cites curriculum dependency / exam relevance without inventing new coverage.

**Must not:**

- Force integration of topics the student has never studied.
- Use “integration” as a label for first-pass breadth theatre.

---

### RVO-05 — Maintain exam readiness

**Definition.** Keep previously learned material in a condition that supports authentic exam performance — timed retrieval, method selection, and application under sitting conditions — without claiming guaranteed success.

**Tutor rationale.** As the examination approaches, revision must serve readiness of what is already known. An expert tutor maintains exam-usable strength; the tutor does not invent readiness from unlearned syllabus or from panic volume alone.

**Success looks like:**

- Revision emphasis becomes more exam-facing as lawful proximity and prior exposure warrant.
- Readiness speech remains provisional and consistent with Learning Objectives LO-07 / EIP-006 honesty.
- Protected revision windows are used for consolidating exam-usable strength, not first-pass salvage theatre.

**Must not:**

- Claim the student is “exam ready” because a revision phase label exists.
- Convert exam readiness into a numeric score in this corpus.
- Use exam proximity to excuse introducing new content as revision.

## 3. Prior Exposure Prerequisite (Binding)

Every RVO-XX objective **presupposes prior exposure**.

| If… | Then… |
|-----|-------|
| Material has no prior study / practice trail | **Do not** pursue RVO-XX under Revision Coach — use first learning / Daily Coach / Learning Coach |
| Material was studied but understanding never formed | Learning Coach diagnosis / intervention may be primary; revision reinforcement (RVO-03) only when prior exposure still holds and consolidation is the honest frame |
| Material was studied and disruption broke continuity | Recovery Coach may be primary until restorative continuity returns |

Hard rule:

> **Revision objectives never authorise first-time instruction disguised as consolidation.**

---

## 4. Priority Ordering Among Objectives

When revision objectives compete within a lawful revision window, apply this binding educational order:

1. **RVO-01 — Retrieval** and **RVO-02 — Durability** when evidence shows forgetting risk or fragile return on previously learned scope  
2. **RVO-03 — Understanding** when return shows brittle familiarity without conceptual grip  
3. **RVO-04 — Integration** when related previously learned topics must cohere for exam use  
4. **RVO-05 — Exam readiness** as examination approaches — intensifying exam-facing consolidation of *already learned* material, never inventing coverage  

Within that order, qualitative priority among *topics* follows `REVISION_PRIORITIES.md` (durability, prerequisite importance, curriculum dependency, examination relevance).

Hard rule:

> **Exam panic does not outrank honesty about prior exposure.**

## 5. Relationship to Sibling Objectives

| Sibling corpus | Relationship |
|----------------|--------------|
| Learning Objectives LO-04 (Retrieval), LO-06 (Durable Retention), LO-07 (Exam Readiness) | Revision RVO-* pursue consolidating advancement of those goods for previously learned material; they do not redefine LO meanings |
| Learning Objectives LO-01 (Coverage) | Coverage of new syllabus is **not** a revision objective |
| Daily Coach day objectives | Day-level execution of today’s job; Revision Coach RVO-* define consolidating goods that may inform that job when revision is authorised |
| Recovery Objectives RO-* | Restorative goods after disruption; distinct from consolidating RVO-*; do not merge ID spaces or meanings |

---

## 6. Anti-Objectives (Forbidden Optimisations)

Revision must **not** optimise for:

- maximising new syllabus coverage under a revision label;
- maximising question volume without consolidating purpose;
- appearing “revised” via calendar phase theatre while durability remains untested;
- inventing mastery or exam-certainty from revision activity;
- stealing protected revision to clear unfinished first-pass without lawful escalation;
- bypassing Recovery Coach by calling catch-up “revision.”

---

## 7. How Objectives Bind Priorities and Speech

Every material revision recommendation must declare which RVO-XX goods it primarily advances.

A recommendation that advances calendar theatre or new-content coverage without advancing at least one of RVO-01…RVO-05 under prior exposure is educationally invalid for Revision Coach use.
