## 1. Purpose

Without evidence law, “recovery complete” becomes either optimistic theatre (a few ticks) or opaque optimiser speech.

With evidence law, completion claims remain epistemically honest: continuity and restorative goods may be affirmed when the trail supports them; mastery speech stays forbidden; thin history yields understatement.

---

## 2. EIP-002 Principles Applied to Completion

All EIP-002 principles bind completion judgements. Specialised readings:

| EIP-002 principle | Completion application |
|-------------------|------------------------|
| Evidence is observed | Completion cites educational happenings, not desired conclusions written backwards |
| Evidence accumulates | One favourable sitting does not complete an episode |
| Evidence has quality | Soft confidence ≠ high-certainty competence |
| Activity alone is not knowledge | Attendance / ticks are not completion proof |
| Inference may not invent evidence | “Looks recovered” without trail is unlawful |
| Absence of evidence is uncertainty | Thin return history → continue gathering; do not certify |
| Coverage ≠ understanding | Cleared backlog / Study Progress ≠ recovery completion or mastery |

Architectural requirement restated:

> **Recovery completion must be justified by accumulated educational evidence.**

---

## 3. What Completion Evidence Is For

Completion evidence supports claims about **restored educational continuity and restorative goods** (RCC-XX).

It does **not** by itself authorise:

- Estimated Mastery uplift;
- exam-readiness certainty;
- erasure of disruption history;
- rewriting of Canonical Study Plan envelopes.

Where completion speech touches understanding or prerequisite safety (RCC-03), it may only claim **sufficient safety for continuation**, using Learning Coach / evidence-honest observations — never high mastery language from recovery alone.

## 4. Evidence Class Catalogue

IDs (`RCE-XX`) exist for audit and cross-reference. They must not appear as student-facing jargon.

### RCE-01 — Continuity trail

**Definition.** Accumulated observations that study continuity has returned — regular lawful study opportunities engaged across time relative to Profile capacity — without treating mere presence as learning success.

**May support:** RCC-01, RCC-05, RCC-06.

**May include (educationally meaningful):**

- Pattern of resumed plan-faithful sittings after the disruption window;
- Reduction in clustered missed opportunities under a sustainable pattern;
- Persistence of return across successive days/weeks (qualitative accumulation — not a numeric unlock).

**Must not treat as sufficient alone:**

- Login streaks;
- Calendar cells marked present;
- A single resumed evening after a long gap.

**Quality note:** Continuity trail is primarily evidence about **study-life continuity**, not about mastery. It may be strong for RCC-01 while remaining silent on understanding.

---

### RCE-02 — Engagement quality trail

**Definition.** Observations that engagement with authorised educational work is stable and meaningful — starting, sustaining, and completing educationally relevant work — beyond opening the product.

**May support:** RCC-02, RCC-01, RCC-05.

**May include:**

- Repeated ability to name and start the next lawful study job;
- Sustained engagement within sittings (not only start-and-abandon as the dominant pattern);
- Session outcomes that show educational participation (practice attempted, reflection recorded, authorised work advanced) — still without minting mastery from completion events alone (EL-004).

**Must not treat as sufficient alone:**

- Session completed flags;
- Time-on-platform;
- Mission completion without educational meaning attached.

**Hard rule:**

> **Do not infer recovery from attendance or session completion alone.**

---

### RCE-03 — Prerequisite / learning-safety evidence

**Definition.** Educational Evidence (EIP-002) and Learning Coach meaning indicating that prerequisite or at-risk scope is **safe enough** for the next authorised work — when RCC-03 is in focus.

**May support:** RCC-03; contributes honesty to RCC-06 when regression warrants were active.

**May include:**

- Practice / retrieval / attempt observations on the emphasised prerequisite or at-risk scope;
- Learning Coach progression or obstacle evidence that foundation risk has eased or is now owned as an LOB/LI problem (then prefer CT-05);
- Honest understatement when observations conflict or remain thin.

**Must not:**

- Infer prerequisite competence from Study Progress coverage alone;
- Infer competence from rest during absence;
- Upgrade Estimated Mastery solely because recovery completion is declared;
- Invent RT-04 regression clearance without evidence.

**Quality note:** Prefer higher-quality educational observations for RCC-03. Soft self-report alone is insufficient for prerequisite safety claims.

---

### RCE-04 — Confidence and return-belief signals

**Definition.** Soft educational signals that shame, freeze, or abandonment risk has eased enough for lawful restart — always low-to-moderate epistemic weight unless corroborated.

**May support:** RCC-04; never sole support for RCC-03 or mastery-adjacent claims.

**May include:**

- Student reflection that return feels workable again;
- Observed unblocking: student starts lawful work after prior freeze;
- Reduction in abandonment-at-start patterns after confidence-focused recovery.

**Must not:**

- Treat repeated optimistic wording as high certainty;
- Use confidence signals to clear knowledge-safety warrants;
- Lead completion speech with personality praise instead of educational conditions.

---

### RCE-05 — Sustainable rhythm evidence

**Definition.** Accumulated signs that study load and frequency fit Profile capacity and authorised envelopes again — restorative temporary adjustments can be released without collapse.

**May support:** RCC-05, RCC-01, RCC-06.

**May include:**

- Sustainable frequency of lawful sittings relative to Profile;
- Ability to take ordinary plan jobs (not only crisis-light fragments) without immediate re-collapse;
- Alignment with plan posture: ordinary Active-class coaching becomes realistic again under the authorised contract.

**Must not:**

- Equate maximum packing density with sustainability;
- Treat authorised recovery-light work itself as proof that normal rhythm has returned;
- Ignore Profile capacity constraints when reading “success.”

---

### RCE-06 — Negative / incompleteness evidence

**Definition.** Observations that **block** completion even if some positive signals exist.

**Must block or delay CT-01 when present:**

- Active meaningful disruption still dominating the trail (warrant still live without restorative progress);
- Unaddressed RT-04 regression warrant without Learning Coach handoff;
- Repeated collapse immediately after attempted resume;
- Envelope breach requiring structural change (completion must not fake local success — use CT-04);
- Learning obstacle now primary while recovery speech would hide diagnosis (use CT-05);
- Only attendance / session-completion / elapsed-time signals available.

Correct uncertainty and continued pathway work beat false completion.

## 5. Forbidden Inference Patterns

The following inferences are **educationally unlawful** for completion:

| Observed | Unlawful inference |
|----------|-------------------|
| Student attended / logged in | Recovery complete |
| Session or mission marked complete | Recovery complete / mastery restored |
| N days elapsed since warrant | Recovery complete |
| Backlog cells cleared | Continuity and understanding restored |
| Pathway checklist finished | Educational completion achieved |
| Student rested during recovery-light posture | Knowledge recovered |
| One good practice set after absence | Mastery / full prerequisite reconstruction |
| Confidence self-report alone | All RCC criteria met |
| Comparison “others are further ahead” | Student has or has not recovered |

## 6. Accumulation and Sufficiency

### 6.1 Accumulation

Evidence for completion **accumulates**. Interpreters revise the completion judgement as history grows. Early return after prolonged absence typically supports gentle continuity claims before stronger RCC-03 safety claims.

### 6.2 Sufficiency (qualitative)

A completion judgement is evidence-sufficient when:

1. Applicable RCC criteria each have supporting RCE classes (not merely hoped);
2. RCE-06 blockers are absent or honestly redirected to CT-03 / CT-04 / CT-05;
3. Claims that touch understanding use evidence quality appropriate to the claim;
4. Explainability can name the trail in plain language without inventing certainty.

This Model does **not** define numerical sample sizes, scores, or half-lives. Sufficiency is educational judgement under EIP-002 quality discipline.

### 6.3 Thin history rule

After return from disruption, history may be thin. Lawful responses:

- Continue the pathway and gather evidence;
- Affirm only weaker continuity claims if clearly supported;
- Prefer CT-02 continued monitoring over CT-01 celebration when residual risk remains;
- Never fill thin history with mastery or “fully recovered knowledge” speech.

## 7. Relationship to Learning Coach Evidence

| Situation | Evidence reading | Completion implication |
|-----------|------------------|------------------------|
| Continuity restored; Learning Coach shows stalled progression | RCE-01/02 may support continuity; learning problem remains | Prefer **CT-05** — do not claim learning success from recovery completion |
| Foundation pathway with LOB diagnosis emerging | RCE-03 merges into obstacle evidence | Handoff; do not keep recovery as soft diagnosis |
| Regression warrant active | Requires evidence-honest treatment | Block CT-01 until addressed or handed off |
| No Learning Coach trail yet | Do not invent progression success | Continuity-only completion possible; withhold mastery-adjacent speech |

Hard rule:

> **Recovery Coach must not bypass Learning Coach evidence.  
> Restoring attendance is not proof that learning progressed.**

---

## 8. Traceability Obligation

Every material completion judgement must be traceable through:

| Trace link | Role |
|------------|------|
| Active warrant / pathway context | What recovery episode is being closed or redirected |
| Applicable RCC criteria | Which educational conditions were evaluated |
| Supporting RCE classes | What accumulated evidence was cited |
| Explicit non-reliance statement (when relevant) | What was *not* used (time alone, ticks alone) |
| Resulting CT transition | What educational move follows |
| Mastery honesty | Explicit non-claim of mastery / exam readiness |

A completion declaration with only attendance, session ticks, or elapsed time in the trail is invalid.

---

## 9. Anti-Patterns (Forbidden)

- Completion dashboards powered by login or streak metrics as educational truth
- Silent Twin mastery writes triggered by recovery-complete events
- “Evidence” that is only a recommendation or desired outcome renamed as observation
- Selective citation of one good day while ignoring RCE-06 collapse patterns
- Using completion evidence vocabulary to invent a second Evidence Model
