## 1. Purpose

Without transition law, completion becomes a badge with no coaching consequence — or a silent plan rewrite.

With transition law, an expert tutor can:

- return the student to ordinary Daily Coach guidance when criteria are met;
- keep honest monitoring without trapping the student in recovery-mode speech;
- change pathway when completion cannot yet be affirmed but another restorative route fits;
- escalate to replanning when local recovery cannot restore a feasible journey;
- hand learning problems to Learning Coach once continuity is restored.

| **T3 Escalation** | **CT-04** | Escalation to replanning / rescheduling |
| **T4 Learning Coach handoff** | **CT-05** | Continuity restored; learning response remains |

MS002 remains the pathway-level vocabulary. CT-XX are completion-judgement transitions that **align with** T1–T5 and add criteria/evidence discipline. They do not author a rival stack.

---

## 3. Transition Principles

1. **Continuity of learner history.** Completion does not wipe educational identity (EIP-005).  
2. **Explain before switch.** Material transitions are speakable under `COMPLETION_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.  
3. **Success returns Daily Coach authority.** CT-01 ends recovery-episode commandeering of day narrative.  
4. **Long-term intent unchanged by completion itself.** Structural change uses CT-04 / Master Planner — never completion fiat.  
5. **Incomplete recovery is educational information** — not moral failure.  
6. **Escalate rather than fake local success** when envelopes break.  
7. **Monitoring ≠ permanent recovery mode.** CT-02 watchfulness must not keep punishment catch-up or crisis identity.  
8. **Learning Coach evidence persists** across completion transitions.  
9. **No streak or score gates.** Transitions are educational judgements.  
10. **One primary story in student speech** after the judgement.

## 4. Transition Catalogue

### CT-01 — Return to normal Daily Coach guidance

**Educational meaning.** Applicable completion criteria are honestly met on accumulated evidence. The recovery episode ends. Ordinary plan-faithful Daily Coach day-priority selection under the authorised contract is again the right posture.

**Requires:**

- Core RCC set (RCC-01, RCC-02, RCC-05, RCC-06) affirmed;
- Conditional RCC-03 / RCC-04 affirmed when applicable;
- Supporting RCE trail without RCE-06 blockers;
- Explainability can honestly say normal progression has resumed.

**Lawful effects:**

- End recovery episode / primary pathway posture as the lead coaching frame;
- Return day-priority authority to ordinary Daily Coach under the authorised plan;
- Retain learner history; do not narrate wiped identity;
- Optional light CT-02-class monitoring *without* recovery-mode speech if residual fragility remains mild.

**Unlawful effects:**

- Declaring CT-01 because a checklist, streak, or timer finished;
- Keeping punishment catch-up after claiming resume;
- Claiming mastery / exam readiness from CT-01;
- Silently rewriting plan envelopes “now that recovery is done.”

**Authority boundary:** Recovery Coach ends restorative episode meaning; Daily Coach owns today thereafter; Master Planner intent unchanged.

---

### CT-02 — Continued monitoring

**Educational meaning.** Either (a) completion is nearly supportable but residual risk warrants watchfulness without full recovery-mode identity, or (b) evidence is still gathering and false CT-01 would be dishonest.

**Typical uses:**

- Core continuity returning after prolonged absence, with mild residual confidence fragility;
- Thin history: continuity signs emerging, RCC-03 not yet affirmable where required;
- Post-CT-01 soft watch for re-collapse without reopening a full episode unless warrants return.

**Lawful effects:**

- Reduce or end heavy recovery-mode speech;
- Keep supportive RO-05 tone and honest vigilance;
- Continue gathering RCE trail;
- Re-open full warrant/pathway if meaningful disruption returns.

**Unlawful effects:**

- Using “monitoring” as permanent unofficial lighter plan without Scheduling / Master Planner authority;
- Using monitoring to avoid admitting CT-03 / CT-04 / CT-05 when those are the honest moves;
- Narrating monitoring as distrust or probation.

**Authority boundary:** Does not absorb Daily Coach day authority; does not invent structural load change.

---

### CT-03 — Movement to an alternative recovery pathway

**Educational meaning.** Completion cannot yet be honestly affirmed under the current primary pathway; another RP-XX better fits the evolving disruption / recovery profile. The recovery episode continues under a different journey type.

**Aligns with:** MS002 T2.

**When warranted:**

- Success signs for the current pathway are not emerging after a fair restorative attempt;
- Profile evolved (e.g. freeze lifted → prolonged return logistics remain; motion returned → foundation risk now leading);
- Evidence clarified a different leading problem.

**Lawful effects:**

- Re-select pathway under `PATHWAY_SELECTION.md`;
- Keep one primary pathway in speech; explain why the approach is changing;
- Preserve continuity: “same destination; clearer restorative route.”

**Unlawful effects:**

- Rotating pathways as fake progress when envelopes cannot hold any local route (use CT-04);
- Pathway change used to bypass Learning Coach diagnosis (use CT-05 when learning is primary);
- Declaring “completion” of the old pathway as mastery.

**Authority boundary:** Still local Recovery Coach / pathway authority inside envelopes; structural need → CT-04.

---

### CT-04 — Escalation to replanning

**Educational meaning.** Local recovery cannot restore a feasible, plan-faithful journey inside current envelopes. Structural change is required through established Master Planner / Rescheduling / Daily Coach escalation pathways.

**Aligns with:** MS002 T3; MS001 RS-08 family; Daily Coach E1/E2-class structural handoffs.

**When warranted (must escalate):**

- Any local completion would require catch-up that breaches intensity envelopes;
- Ongoing capacity reduction invalidates remaining plan feasibility despite workload-rebalancing attempts;
- Protected revision would need to be consumed for unfinished first-pass;
- Sitting / exam date or strategy / blueprint change required;
- Persistent disruption despite repeated lawful local pathways;
- Exam-facing remaining journey infeasible under local RP-08 emphasis;
- Plan invalid, draft-only, or superseded while surfaces still request recovery completion under the old contract.

**Authority-preserving path:**

```
Completion judgement: local success impossible / dishonest
        │
        ▼
RS-08-class escalation signal (Recovery Coach)
        │
        ├─→ Scheduling / Rescheduling (allocation, recovery capacity placement)
        │
        └─→ Master Planner (educational envelopes / strategy / new Canonical Study Plan)
                │
                ▼
        Adapted / Recovered / new authorised contract published
                │
                ▼
        Daily Coach coaches under the updated contract
        Recovery Coach may reassess warrant / pathway / completion under the new contract
```

Hard rules:

> **Recovery Coach identifies the need; it does not absorb Scheduling or Master Planner authority.**  
> **Completion must never fake local success to avoid replanning.**  
> **Long-term educational intent changes only through Master Planner pathways — never through a completion event.**

**Continuity during escalation:**

- Supportive speech continues — escalation is not abandonment;
- No interim unofficial plans invented while waiting for structural authority;
- Learner history persists (EIP-005).

---

### CT-05 — Learning Coach handoff (recovery continuity restored; learning response remains)

**Educational meaning.** Disruption continuity is sufficiently restored (or was never the remaining barrier), and the primary remaining educational problem is genuine learning progression / obstacle / intervention.

**Aligns with:** MS002 T4.

**Lawful handoff cues:**

- RCC continuity criteria largely met, but Learning Coach evidence shows stalled progression;
- Foundation / retrieval concerns resolve into an explicit LOB diagnosis;
- Exam-technique issues under continuous study require LI ownership rather than recovery framing.

**Lawful effects:**

- End or demote recovery episode posture as the lead story;
- Transfer diagnostic / intervention ownership to Learning Coach;
- Do not claim Learning Coach success from recovery completion.

**Unlawful effects:**

- Keeping recovery speech as a soft substitute for diagnosis;
- Using handoff to dump unexplained “you’re behind on understanding” without Learning Coach meaning;
- Minting mastery clearance as part of the handoff.

## 5. Judgement → Transition Map

| Completion judgement | Primary transition |
|----------------------|-------------------|
| Applicable RCC met; evidence sufficient; no blockers | **CT-01** (+ optional light CT-02 watch) |
| Nearly met / thin history / residual fragility | **CT-02** |
| Not met; another local pathway fits better | **CT-03** |
| Not met; envelopes / feasibility broken | **CT-04** |
| Continuity OK; learning obstacle / progression primary | **CT-05** |
| Warrant unclear | Prefer gather under CT-02 / MS002 T5 — do not invent CT-01 or CT-04 theatre |

---

## 6. Post-Completion Authority Return (CT-01 Detail)

On lawful CT-01:

| Authority | Posture after completion |
|-----------|--------------------------|
| **Recovery Coach** | Episode closed; may supply residual monitoring notes only |
| **Daily Coach** | Owns ordinary day-priority under authorised plan |
| **Learning Coach** | Continues longitudinal meaning; unaffected inventively by completion |
| **Master Planner** | Long-term intent unchanged by the completion act |
| **Scheduling** | No Recovery-Coach-invented packing changes |
| **Evidence / Twin** | No mastery write from completion event alone |

Architectural requirement restated:

> **Successful recovery restores educational continuity and returns authority to the normal coaching pipeline without modifying long-term educational intent.**

## 8. Continuity and Authority Checklist (Every Material Transition)

Before enacting CT-01…CT-05, confirm:

| Check | Fail action |
|-------|-------------|
| Is learner history preserved in speech and meaning? | Rewrite narration; never invent wipe |
| Does evidence (not time/ticks alone) support the move? | Gather more / choose a different CT |
| Does Daily Coach still own today’s priority under the current contract? | Do not commandeer the day via completion speech |
| Is long-term intent change required? | Use CT-04 — do not mutate plan in Recovery Coach |
| Is mastery being claimed from the transition? | Refuse; keep EIP-006 honesty |
| Is a learning obstacle now primary? | Use CT-05 |
| Can the student hear why the approach is changing? | Produce explainability first |

---

## 9. Anti-Transition Patterns (Forbidden)

- Permanent recovery mode with no completion criteria  
- CT-01 from elapsed time, attendance, or session ticks alone  
- Silent pathway oscillation without educational reason  
- Escalation theatre that still packs calendars inside Recovery Coach  
- “Recovery completed” mastery speech  
- Returning to normal coaching while still demanding punishment catch-up  
- Using transition to bypass Learning Coach diagnosis  
- Monitoring as shame probation
