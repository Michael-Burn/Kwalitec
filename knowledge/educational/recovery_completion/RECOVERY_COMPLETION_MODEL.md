## Authority

This document is the constitutional overview of **recovery completion** for Kwalitec.

It is subordinate to the Educational Constitution, specialised educational models, the Educational Recovery Model (MS001), and the Recovery Pathway Framework (MS002). It governs **how Kwalitec judges whether educational recovery has restored continuity enough to resume normal progression**, and **what educational transitions follow**, while remaining faithful to the long-term educational plan. It does not authorise implementation shortcuts that contradict the Constitution, and it does not invent disruption, evidence, mastery, pathway, or planning meaning absent from upstream models.

Authority order for recovery completion:

> Constitution defines educational truth and curriculum primacy.  
> Knowledge & Mastery defines coverage ≠ understanding ≠ mastery.  
> Evidence Model defines what may warrant educational claims.  
> Continuity Standard preserves rightful educational history across plan change.  
> Master Planner publishes the Canonical Study Plan and owns long-term educational envelopes.  
> Daily Coach owns *what is most educationally valuable today* under that contract.  
> Learning Coach owns longitudinal progression, obstacle diagnosis, and learning interventions.  
> Educational Recovery Model (MS001) defines *whether* recovery is warranted and *which* restorative objectives / strategies apply.  
> Recovery Pathway Framework (MS002) defines *what type* of recovery journey is appropriate and pathway-level transitions.  
> **This Recovery Completion Model (Workstream 4 / MS003) defines whether recovery has been achieved and what educational transition follows.**  
> Downstream coaching and narration surfaces must consume this educational reasoning — never treat completion as mastery or as a silent plan rewrite.

## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not keep a student in “recovery mode” forever — nor declare the disruption cured because a calendar week elapsed or a few sessions were ticked. After a lawful recovery journey, the tutor answers one living educational question:

> **“Has this learner recovered sufficiently to resume normal educational progression?”**

That answer must rest on accumulated educational evidence, restore sustainable continuity under the authorised plan, return day-priority authority to the ordinary Daily Coach pipeline, preserve long-term educational intent unchanged by the completion act itself, and escalate or redirect honestly when completion cannot be affirmed.

This document records that tutor posture so every future Recovery Coach subsystem has a single educational reference for *completion meaning*.

> **The Recovery Completion Model describes educational completion reasoning.  
> It does not implement Runtime A logic, algorithms, analytics, or Twin writers.**

---

## 2. What Recovery Completion Is

**Recovery completion** is the educational judgement that a lawful recovery episode has **restored sufficient educational continuity** — momentum, consistency, confidence, and (where warranted) prerequisite safety — for the student to **resume ordinary plan-faithful coaching**, without claiming that understanding, mastery, or exam readiness has been achieved by the restorative journey alone.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Recovery episode** | Active restorative coaching under a lawful warrant and pathway | Is recovery still in force? |
| **Completion criteria** | Educational conditions that indicate recovery success | What must be true educationally? |
| **Completion evidence** | Accumulated observations that may support those conditions | What trail justifies the claim? |
| **Completion judgement** | Achieved / not yet / incomplete destination | Has recovery succeeded? |
| **Completion transition** | Educational move that follows the judgement | What changes next? |
| **Post-completion monitoring** | Residual watchfulness without recovery-mode speech | What vigilance remains? |

Completion is:

- **evidence-justified** — rests on accumulated Educational Evidence, not elapsed time;
- **continuity-restoring** — success means sustainable progress can resume under the authorised plan;
- **non-mastery** — completion ≠ understanding restored as fact ≠ mastery ≠ exam readiness;
- **authority-returning** — successful completion returns day-priority authority to ordinary Daily Coach;
- **plan-faithful** — completion itself does not modify long-term educational intent;
- **transition-aware** — may resume normal coaching, continue monitoring, change pathway, escalate, or hand off to Learning Coach;
- **explainable** — students understand why completion is (or is not) affirmed;
- **non-scoring** — qualitative educational judgement only.

Completion is **not**:

- a calendar milestone, streak unlock, or pathway checklist finish line;
- inference from attendance or session completion alone;
- a licence to invent mastery from rest, resumed ticks, or backlog clearance;
- a substitute for Master Planner planning or strategy selection;
- a bypass of Daily Coach day authority or Learning Coach evidence/diagnosis layers;
- a claim that disruption never happened or that learner history was wiped;
- a permanent softer plan invented by Recovery Coach fiat.

## 3. Binding Distinction: Completion vs Neighbours

| Layer | Educational job | Unlawful collapse |
|-------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Trigger (RT)** | Warrant that recovery is needed | Declaring completion without a lawful episode ever existing |
| **Objective (RO)** | Name goods to restore | Treating backlog clearance as the completion condition |
| **Pathway (RP)** | Name the *type* of restorative journey | Equating “pathway finished” with educational completion |
| **Pathway transition (T1–T5)** | Pathway-level resume / change / escalate vocabulary | Inventing a rival transition law that ignores MS002 |
| **Completion (this corpus)** | Judge whether continuity is restored enough to resume normal progression | Treating time elapsed or ticks as proof |
| **Daily Coach** | Own today’s priority under the current contract | Letting completion speech commandeer the day or keep crisis mode after resume |
| **Learning Coach** | Own progression / obstacle / intervention meaning | Using completion to mint learning success or skip diagnosis |
| **Master Planner / Scheduling** | Own structural envelope and allocation change | Absorbing replan inside “completion” theatre |

Hard rule:

> **Pathway compliance is not completion.  
> Attendance is not completion.  
> Elapsed time is not completion.  
> Completion restores continuity; it does not mint mastery.**

## 4. Educational Purpose

The Recovery Completion Model exists so that:

1. **Recovery episodes end for educational reasons** — not because a timer expired.
2. **Students are not trapped in permanent recovery mode** once sustainable continuity returns.
3. **False completion is refused** when only attendance, session ticks, or calendar clearance exist.
4. **Authority returns cleanly** to the ordinary Daily Coach pipeline under the authorised plan.
5. **Long-term educational intent survives completion** — completion repairs continuity; it does not invent a new philosophy.
6. **Incomplete recovery remains speakable** — alternative pathway, monitoring, Learning Coach handoff, or escalation are honest outcomes.
7. **Students understand the close of the arc** — why success (or not), what evidence, what changes next, what watchfulness remains.

## 5. Core Responsibilities

The Recovery Completion Model is educationally responsible for:

| Responsibility | Meaning |
|----------------|---------|
| **State completion criteria** | Name educational conditions that indicate recovery success (RCC-XX) |
| **Bound completion evidence** | Require EIP-002-honest accumulation; forbid attendance-only / tick-only proofs |
| **Produce a completion judgement** | Achieved, not yet, or incomplete destination requiring another transition |
| **Select completion transitions** | Resume normal coaching, monitor, change pathway, escalate, or hand off |
| **Return coaching authority lawfully** | On success, restore ordinary Daily Coach day-priority under the authorised plan |
| **Preserve sibling authorities** | Never rewrite plan, strategy, mastery, or Learning Coach meanings via completion |
| **Require post-completion honesty** | Name residual monitoring without keeping punishment catch-up |
| **Explain supportively** | Make completion speakable without false certainty or shame |

It is **not** educationally responsible for:

- rewriting Canonical Study Plans or educational strategy;
- deciding today’s Daily Coach primary objective after resume (Daily Coach owns that);
- diagnosing learning obstacles or selecting Learning Interventions;
- inferring or redefining mastery;
- writing Twin estimates by itself;
- producing opaque “completion scores” as educational proof;
- inventing numerical day-count thresholds as educational law.

## 6. Binding Educational Doctrine

1. **Completion is an educational judgement, not a time-based milestone.** Calendar elapsed does not complete recovery.
2. **Completion must be justified by accumulated educational evidence.** Thin or activity-only trails understate; they do not certify success.
3. **Completion does not imply mastery.** Restoring continuity ≠ restoring understanding as fact ≠ exam readiness (EIP-006).
4. **Successful recovery restores educational continuity** and returns authority to the normal coaching pipeline **without modifying long-term educational intent**.
5. **Attendance and session completion alone are insufficient.** Completing sittings may be activity; completion requires restorative educational meaning from the evidence trail.
6. **MS001 ending-trigger posture and MS002 T1 remain binding.** This corpus specialises them; it does not weaken them with softer theatre.
7. **Daily Coach authority is preserved.** After successful completion, ordinary day-priority selection resumes under the authorised plan; Recovery Coach does not retain crisis commandeering.
8. **Learning Coach evidence is preserved.** Completion does not invent progression success or dissolve an active obstacle diagnosis.
9. **Canonical Study Plan remains the contract.** Completion may end recovery posture; it may not independently publish a new plan.
10. **Explainability is mandatory.** Silent “you’re fine now” and silent permanent recovery mode are both forbidden (EIP-003).

## 8. Relationship to MS001 Ending Triggers and MS002 T1

MS001 `RECOVERY_TRIGGERS.md` §8 and MS002 `PATHWAY_TRANSITIONS.md` T1 already state that recovery ends when sustainable momentum and consistency return, the plan is again coachable as ordinary rhythm, regression warrants are addressed or handed to Learning Coach, and explainability can honestly say normal progression has resumed.

This Completion Model:

- **does not replace** those resume laws;
- **specialises** them into named criteria (RCC-XX), evidence classes (RCE-XX), and post-judgement transitions (CT-XX);
- **tightens** the evidence obligation so attendance-only and time-based completions are explicitly unlawful;
- **separates** successful resume from mastery speech more sharply for student-facing completion narration.
