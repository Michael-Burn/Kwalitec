## 1. Purpose

Without explicit completion criteria, recovery either never ends (permanent crisis mode) or ends falsely (a few ticks, a cleared calendar cell, or a week on the clock).

With explicit criteria, an expert tutor can affirm that restorative coaching has restored continuity enough for ordinary progression — while refusing mastery theatre and unfinished disruption warrants.

---

## 2. Criteria Principles

1. **Educational first.** Criteria describe restored learning conditions, not operational convenience.
2. **Evidence-bound.** Each criterion requires supporting trail under `COMPLETION_EVIDENCE.md`.
3. **Objective-aligned.** Criteria map to RO-01…RO-05 goods that recovery was pursuing.
4. **Pathway-sensitive emphasis.** Different RP-XX journeys emphasise different criteria; all successful resumes still need core continuity conditions.
5. **Profile-relative.** “Restored” is judged relative to Student Educational Profile capacity and reliability — not against an impossible ideal candidate.
6. **Non-mastery.** Meeting criteria restores continuity; it does not certify understanding or exam readiness.
7. **Non-temporal.** No criterion is satisfied merely because time passed.
8. **Speakable.** Criteria must be explainable in plain educational language (`COMPLETION_EXPLAINABILITY.md`).

## 3. Criterion Catalogue

IDs (`RCC-XX`) exist for audit and cross-reference. They must not appear as student-facing jargon.

### RCC-01 — Restored consistency

**Definition.** Reliable, plan-faithful study continuity has returned after interruption — clustered disruption no longer dominates the recent educational trail.

**Maps primarily to:** RO-02 (rebuild consistency); supports RO-01.

**Tutor rationale.** IFoA preparation compounds through steady cadence. Recovery succeeds when the student can again sustain regular sittings without perpetual restart crisis — not when a single heroic day appears.

**Success looks like:**

- Missed-session clusters have reduced under a sustainable pattern relative to Profile capacity.
- Successive lawful study opportunities are taken often enough that Daily Coach is not trapped in endless disruption repair speech.
- The student experiences study as predictable enough to trust again.

**Must not:**

- Treat a streak counter, badge, or single perfect week as proof by itself.
- Force intensity that Profile capacity cannot sustain in order to “look consistent.”

**Evidence posture:** Requires RCE-01 and typically RCE-02 / RCE-05 — never attendance counts alone.

---

### RCC-02 — Stable educational engagement

**Definition.** The student re-enters and sustains educationally meaningful engagement with authorised study work — starting and staying with lawful jobs — rather than only opening the product or marking empty presence.

**Maps primarily to:** RO-01 (restore momentum); supports RO-02 and RO-05.

**Tutor rationale.** Returning to motion is necessary but not sufficient. Completion needs engagement that is educationally real: the student can name, start, and sustain plan-faithful work without freeze or abandonment mid-sitting as the dominant pattern.

**Success looks like:**

- The student can name and start the next lawful study job without shame-driven overload.
- Engagement persists across more than an isolated restart attempt.
- Engagement quality is readable from the educational trail — not from login alone.

**Must not:**

- Infer stable engagement from app opens, page views, or “session completed” ticks without educational meaning.
- Equate overload catch-up bursts with stable engagement.

**Evidence posture:** Requires RCE-02; may be supported by RCE-01. Activity-only signals are insufficient (see `COMPLETION_EVIDENCE.md` §5).

---

### RCC-03 — Demonstrated prerequisite competence (when in focus)

**Definition.** Where the recovery warrant or pathway required foundation / prerequisite safety (typically RP-07, and often RP-04 / RP-08 when foundations were at risk), the student has shown **sufficient prerequisite competence for safe continuation** of the authorised next educational work — not full mastery of those prerequisites.

**Maps primarily to:** RO-03, RO-04; consumes Learning Coach meaning when present.

**Tutor rationale.** After disruption, fragile foundations can make ordinary progression unsafe. Completion of a foundation-focused recovery episode requires educational signs that prerequisite work is safe enough to continue — without inventing mastery from a short retrieval set.

**Success looks like:**

- Prerequisite or at-risk scope emphasised by the pathway shows honest educational improvement or safety sufficient for the next authorised plan job.
- Learning Coach evidence, where present, does not contradict the safety claim.
- Thin history yields understatement: gather more evidence rather than declare competence from hope.

**Must not:**

- Apply RCC-03 as a universal gate when foundations were never in focus (e.g. pure RP-03 confidence rebuild with no foundation warrant).
- Narrate prerequisite “mastered” or “fully recovered knowledge” from recovery completion.
- Infer competence from coverage ticks or calendar clearance of foundation topics.

**Evidence posture:** Requires RCE-03 when this criterion is in focus; soft confidence alone never substitutes.

**Applicability rule:**

> **RCC-03 is binding when the active pathway / warrant put prerequisite or knowledge-safety restoration in focus.  
> It is not a hidden universal mastery gate for every recovery episode.**

---

### RCC-04 — Recovered confidence

**Definition.** Learner confidence no longer blocks lawful restart and sustainable continuation — shame, freeze, or abandonment risk that warranted recovery has sufficiently eased for ordinary coaching to be workable again.

**Maps primarily to:** RO-05; often critical after RP-03 and after prolonged absence (RP-02).

**Tutor rationale.** Candidates who still cannot believe return is possible will not sustain RCC-01 / RCC-02. Confidence recovery is an educational condition for completion when it was part of the restorative job — not a personality score.

**Success looks like:**

- The student can re-enter study without freeze as the dominant barrier.
- Explanations are received as coaching, not as moral repair.
- Confidence no longer requires recovery-mode framing as the primary speech.

**Must not:**

- Treat one optimistic self-report as high-certainty proof.
- Declare confidence recovered while still using shame-based “you fell behind” framing.
- Ignore residual fragility that still warrants CT-02 monitoring.

**Evidence posture:** RCE-04 is typically low-to-moderate quality (EIP-002 soft signals) and must be corroborated by behavioural continuity (RCE-01 / RCE-02) when used as a completion pillar.

---

### RCC-05 — Sustainable study rhythm

**Definition.** A workable study rhythm has returned that fits Profile capacity and the authorised plan envelopes — progress can compound without heroic catch-up or perpetual lighter-load crisis framing.

**Maps primarily to:** RO-01, RO-02; aligns with MS001 ending-trigger posture.

**Tutor rationale.** Recovery succeeds when ordinary plan-faithful rhythm is again educationally realistic. A rhythm that only works under punishment intensity or permanent recovery-light theatre is not completion.

**Success looks like:**

- Sessions resume at sustainable frequency and load relative to Profile capacity.
- The Canonical Study Plan (or Adapted / Recovered successor still within envelopes) can again be coached as ordinary Active-class rhythm.
- Temporary restorative load adjustments can be released without collapse.

**Must not:**

- Equate calendar density with sustainable rhythm.
- Keep permanent unofficial lighter load after declaring completion without plan/scheduling authority.
- Treat “caught up on the calendar” as rhythm restored.

**Evidence posture:** Requires RCE-05 with RCE-01 / RCE-02 support.

---

### RCC-06 — Plan-coachable continuity restored

**Definition.** Ordinary Daily Coach day-priority selection under the authorised contract is again the right educational posture — recovery episode framing is no longer required as the primary coaching mode.

**Maps primarily to:** MS001 ending triggers; MS002 T1; architectural return of authority.

**Tutor rationale.** Completion is not only that the student feels better — it is that the educational system can honestly return to normal coaching without pretending the disruption is still the lead story.

**Success looks like:**

- No active RT-04 regression warrant remains unaddressed — **or** Learning Coach has taken ownership of remaining learning response (then prefer CT-05 rather than pure CT-01 mastery-of-recovery speech).
- Explainability can honestly say normal progression has resumed (or that monitoring continues without recovery-mode).
- Long-term educational intent remains the Master Planner’s authorised intent — unchanged by the completion judgement itself.

**Must not:**

- Declare plan-coachable continuity while still demanding punishment catch-up.
- Use RCC-06 to silently keep Recovered crisis speech after claiming resume.
- Claim the disruption history was erased (EIP-005 continuity honesty).

**Evidence posture:** Synthesis criterion — affirmed only when the applicable RCC-01…RCC-05 set is honestly met and evidence under `COMPLETION_EVIDENCE.md` supports the claim.

## 4. How Criteria Combine for Successful Completion

### 4.1 Core set (always required for CT-01 success)

Successful return to normal Daily Coach guidance (CT-01) requires honest affirmation of:

1. **RCC-01** Restored consistency  
2. **RCC-02** Stable educational engagement  
3. **RCC-05** Sustainable study rhythm  
4. **RCC-06** Plan-coachable continuity restored  

### 4.2 Conditional criteria

| Criterion | When required |
|-----------|---------------|
| **RCC-04** Recovered confidence | When RO-05 / RP-03 (or equivalent confidence freeze) was in focus for the episode |
| **RCC-03** Demonstrated prerequisite competence | When RP-07 / foundation-focused RP-04 / RP-08 knowledge-safety restoration was in focus |

### 4.3 Pathway emphasis (illustrative)

| Pathway emphasis | Criteria that typically lead |
|------------------|------------------------------|
| RP-01 Short disruption | RCC-01, RCC-02, RCC-05, RCC-06 |
| RP-02 Prolonged absence | RCC-01, RCC-02, RCC-04, RCC-05, RCC-06 |
| RP-03 Confidence rebuilding | RCC-04 first, then RCC-01 / RCC-02 / RCC-05 / RCC-06 |
| RP-04 Knowledge reinforcement | RCC-03 (as safety, not mastery), plus core set |
| RP-05 Workload rebalancing | RCC-05 and RCC-01 relative to feasible load; else CT-04 |
| RP-06 Consistency restoration | RCC-01 and RCC-05 lead |
| RP-07 Prerequisite reconstruction | RCC-03 + core set |
| RP-08 Exam-phase recovery | Core set + RCC-03 when foundations/retrieval were in focus; escalate early if infeasible |

### 4.4 Binding combination rule

> **All applicable criteria must be honestly affirmable from accumulated evidence.  
> Missing an applicable criterion means completion is not yet achieved — use CT-02 hold/monitor language only if gathering evidence, otherwise continue pathway, change pathway (CT-03), hand off (CT-05), or escalate (CT-04).**

## 5. Explicit Non-Criteria (Forbidden Completion Grounds)

The following **never** complete recovery by themselves:

| Non-criterion | Why forbidden |
|---------------|---------------|
| Elapsed calendar days / weeks | Time is not educational evidence of restored continuity |
| Streak length or engagement metrics | Theatre, not educational judgement |
| Pathway checklist finished | Compliance ≠ restored continuity |
| Attendance or login frequency alone | Activity without educational meaning (EIP-002) |
| Session / mission completion ticks alone | Completion of sittings ≠ recovery completion (EL-004 honesty) |
| Backlog / calendar clearance | Anti-objective under MS001; never a recovery success good |
| Student declaration “I’m fine now” alone | Soft signal; may support RCC-04 only with corroboration |
| Rest, lighter load, or recovery capacity used | Rest is not understanding; recovery posture ≠ success |
| Comparison to other candidates’ pace | Shame theatre, not educational criterion |

---

## 6. Relationship to Recovery Objectives

| Objective | Completion reading |
|-----------|-------------------|
| RO-01 Momentum | Advanced when RCC-02 and RCC-05 hold |
| RO-02 Consistency | Advanced when RCC-01 holds |
| RO-03 Long-term understanding | Protected during recovery; **not** certified as restored mastery by completion |
| RO-04 Regression minimisation | Where in focus, RCC-03 / evidence-honest safety — not “forgotten everything cured” speech |
| RO-05 Confidence | Advanced when RCC-04 holds (when applicable) |

Hard rule:

> **Meeting completion criteria means restorative goods are sufficiently restored for normal progression.  
> It does not mean Learning Objectives (LO-XX) were attained or mastery was earned.**

---

## 7. Anti-Patterns (Forbidden)

- Declaring success because “two weeks of recovery elapsed”
- Declaring success because the student completed three sessions
- Requiring universal mastery-gated RCC-03 for every short disruption
- Keeping recovery mode after all applicable criteria are met (refusal to return authority)
- Using criteria as numeric scores or dashboards presented as educational truth
- Narrating criteria IDs to students
