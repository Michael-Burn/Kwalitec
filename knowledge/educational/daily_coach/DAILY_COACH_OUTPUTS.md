## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not only *think* about today — the tutor *tells* the student, clearly:

- what matters most today;
- how to spend the available time;
- what kind of practice (if any) belongs;
- how to close the day honestly;
- why this advice exists;
- when the plan itself needs to change.

This catalogue names those educational outputs so downstream surfaces consume a shared coaching contract.

Identifiers (DCO-XX) exist for traceability. Educational meaning is binding; widgets and schemas are out of scope.

---

## 2. Output Principles

1. **One primary.** Exactly one primary educational objective for a coaching day (or an explicit rest / escalation interim).
2. **Plan-traceable.** Material outputs cite Canonical Study Plan / mode warrants.
3. **Capacity-bound.** Recommended blocks fit today’s available time and intensity envelopes.
4. **Claim-typed.** Facts, estimates, and advice remain distinguishable.
5. **Non-mastery-minting.** Completion guidance never implies understanding proof.
6. **Ranked.** Secondary outputs never compete as equal primaries.
7. **Explainable.** Every material recommendation carries educational rationale (DCO-06).
8. **Non-rewriting.** Outputs must not silently redefine Study Plan commitments.
9. **Field-free.** This catalogue defines meaning — not serialisation.

## 4. Output Specifications

### DCO-01 — Today’s primary objective

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **What** | One named educational job for today |
| **Must include** | Work type (learn / practise / consolidate / revise / checkpoint / mock prep / recover / rest); syllabus or plan warrant; realistic scope for today’s capacity |
| **Educational meaning** | Answers “What is the most educationally valuable thing to do today?” |
| **Must not** | Be a list of equal priorities; invent work absent from plan/mode authority; claim guaranteed readiness |
| **Relationship to Mission** | On Learning Mode first-pass days, aligns with Today’s Mission topic authority; on revision/recovery days, states the plan job explicitly |

### DCO-02 — Recommended study blocks

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **What** | Ordered educational time blocks that realise DCO-01 within today’s capacity |
| **Must include** | Intended activity per block; approximate duration band inside available time; relationship to plan session / intensity envelope |
| **Educational meaning** | Translates the primary objective into a livable evening/day structure |
| **Must not** | Exceed declared capacity; pack into leave; prescribe UI timers as educational law |
| **Note** | Blocks are educational concepts — not calendar packing algorithms (MS006) |

### DCO-03 — Practice focus

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **What** | The application / question-practice emphasis belonging to today, if authorised |
| **Must include** | Topic or skill focus warranted by plan practice commitment and/or today’s primary objective; honesty about evidence thinness |
| **Educational meaning** | Prevents coverage-only days from being mistaken for complete preparation when practice is due |
| **Must not** | Mint mastery from attempted questions; displace protected revision/recovery; become an undeclared second mission |

### DCO-04 — Reflection prompts

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **What** | Short educational prompts for end-of-day or end-of-session reflection |
| **Must include** | Prompts that separate what was studied, what felt difficult, and what remains uncertain — without forcing false confidence |
| **Educational meaning** | Supports honest close and soft evidence capture pathways already governed elsewhere |
| **Must not** | Trick the student into declaring mastery; introduce Twin jargon |

### DCO-05 — Confidence messages

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **What** | Supportive educational messages that sustain consistency without lying |
| **Must include** | Language consistent with claim types; recovery-safe tone after disruption |
| **Educational meaning** | Emotional support is part of tutoring — fabricated competence is not |
| **Must not** | Promise passes; equate completion with understanding; shame missed days |

### DCO-06 — Educational rationale

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **What** | Plain-language explanation of why DCO-01 (and material secondaries) were chosen |
| **Must include** | Link to Study Plan context + current educational context (see Explainability doc) |
| **Educational meaning** | Makes the coach trustworthy and inspectable |
| **Must not** | Dump internal IDs, scores, or multi-factor jargon |

### DCO-07 — Continuity / progress notice

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **What** | Honest statement of what planned work or Study Progress coverage recently moved — or did not |
| **Educational meaning** | Keeps journey continuity visible without readiness theatre |
| **Must not** | Narrate coverage as mastery; hide missed planned work when escalation depends on it |

### DCO-08 — Escalation notice

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **What** | Explicit educational notice that rescheduling (E1) or replanning (E2) is needed |
| **Must include** | Trigger facts; what Daily Coach refuses to invent; interim primary objective for today; next process |
| **Educational meaning** | Preserves plan integrity when today cannot lawfully absorb the conflict |
| **Must not** | Be replaced by silent plan rewrite or motivational denial |

### DCO-09 — Secondary recommendations

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **What** | Optional ranked educational advice beneath the primary (0–2 typical) |
| **Educational meaning** | Enrichment without decision overload |
| **Must not** | Compete with DCO-01; silently redefine Learning Mode Mission |

### DCO-10 — Mode / authority disclosure

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **What** | Disclosure when today’s primary job is revision, recovery, practice emphasis, rest, or otherwise not ordinary Learning Mode first-pass |
| **Educational meaning** | Prevents students from experiencing conflicting “mission” stories |
| **Must not** | Hide advisory vs mission differences |

## 5. Minimum Output Set

For a lawful **ordinary plan-based coaching day**, the Daily Coach must be able to produce at least:

1. **DCO-01** Today’s primary objective  
2. **DCO-02** Recommended study blocks (may be a single block)  
3. **DCO-06** Educational rationale  
4. **DCO-10** when today’s job is not ordinary Learning Mode first-pass  

And should ordinarily be able to produce:

5. **DCO-03** when practice is authorised today  
6. **DCO-04** reflection prompts for honest close  
7. **DCO-05** confidence message consistent with evidence honesty  
8. **DCO-07** when recent completion / miss facts are material  

When escalation triggers fire, **DCO-08** is mandatory and may reshape DCO-01 into an interim lawful objective.

---

## 6. Output Forbidden Forms

The Daily Coach must not emit educational outputs that:

- rewrite Canonical Study Plan phases, protections, or sitting ambition;
- present advisory topics as Today’s Mission under Learning Mode without disclosure;
- claim “Mastered,” exam marks, or readiness certainty from today’s plan adherence alone;
- prescribe punishment overload after missed work;
- require engineering or Twin vocabulary for the student to understand the day;
- equalise five “top priorities” as if the tutor could not decide.

## 7. Relationship to Downstream Experience

| Downstream surface (examples) | May consume | Must not invent beyond |
|-------------------------------|-------------|------------------------|
| Today’s Mission / session flows | DCO-01, DCO-02, DCO-06, DCO-10 | New educational priority law |
| Practice capture / feedback | DCO-03, DCO-07 | Mastery from ticks |
| Reflection experience | DCO-04, DCO-05 | Forced confidence theatre |
| Plan / home narration | DCO-06, DCO-07, DCO-08 | Conflicting plan stories |
| Notifications (future) | Summaries of DCO-01 / DCO-08 | New decision authority |

Mission generation algorithms, UI, and notifications remain **out of scope** for this milestone; they are future consumers of these educational outputs.
