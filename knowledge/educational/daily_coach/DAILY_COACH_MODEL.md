# Daily Coach Model

**Programme:** VI — Workstream 2 — Daily Coach  
**Milestone:** MS001 — Daily Coaching Model  
**Classification:** Highest educational authority for day-to-day coaching meaning within Programme VI Workstream 2  
**Status:** APPROVED — governing for Daily Coach educational contract  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-07-25  

---

## Authority

This document is the constitutional overview of the **Daily Coach** for Kwalitec.

It is subordinate to the Educational Constitution and specialised educational models. It governs **how a Canonical Study Plan becomes day-to-day educational guidance**. It does not authorise implementation shortcuts that contradict the Constitution, and it does not invent long-term educational meaning absent from the Canonical Study Plan.

Authority order for daily coaching:

> Constitution defines educational truth and curriculum primacy.  
> Knowledge & Mastery defines coverage ≠ understanding ≠ mastery.  
> Evidence Model defines what may warrant understanding claims.  
> Student Educational Profile diagnoses who the student is educationally now.  
> Master Planner (MS001–MS007) designs, decides, blueprints, schedules, and publishes the Canonical Study Plan.  
> **This Daily Coach Model (Workstream 2 / MS001) defines how that plan is interpreted for today.**  
> Downstream mission, session, and narration surfaces must consume this educational reasoning — never redesign the Study Plan while serving the day.

---

## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not leave the student alone with a multi-month plan and a blank evening. After the Canonical Study Plan exists, the tutor answers one living question every day:

> **“What is the most educationally valuable thing for this student to do today?”**

That answer must remain faithful to the long-term plan while adapting to the student’s current educational state and recent evidence.

This document records that tutor posture so every future daily coaching subsystem has a single educational reference for *how today’s guidance is formed*.

> **The Daily Coach describes educational reasoning.  
> It does not implement Runtime A logic, mission generation, or scheduling.**

---

## 2. What the Daily Coach Is

The **Daily Coach** is the educational capability that interprets an **Active-class Canonical Study Plan** (Active, Adapted, or Recovered under `STUDY_PLAN_LIFECYCLE.md`) together with the student’s current educational context, and produces **today’s educational guidance**.

| Input family | Role |
|--------------|------|
| **Canonical Study Plan** | Authorised long-term preparation contract — phases, sessions, protections, commitments |
| **Student Educational Profile** | Current educational diagnosis (not a re-invention of strategy) |
| **Recent learning evidence & session history** | What recently happened educationally |
| **Today’s capacity & interruptions** | What study life permits today |
| **Recovery / continuity posture** | Whether lighter load or continuity repair is already warranted |

into:

| Output family | Role |
|---------------|------|
| **Today’s educational guidance** | Primary objective, recommended study blocks, practice focus, reflection prompts, confidence messages, and educational rationale |

It answers:

| Question | Educational answer |
|----------|-------------------|
| What should I do today? | The single most educationally valuable primary objective under the plan |
| How should I study it? | Learning / practice / revision / recovery emphasis already authorised by the plan |
| How much is enough? | Study blocks inside today’s capacity and plan intensity envelopes |
| Why this, not that? | Plain educational rationale tied to plan + current context |
| When is the plan wrong for today? | Escalation signal — reschedule or replan — not silent rewrite |

The Daily Coach is:

- **plan-faithful** — every material recommendation traces to Canonical Study Plan authority;
- **context-sensitive** — adapts to Profile, evidence, capacity, interruptions, and recovery;
- **single-priority** — reduces decision burden to one primary educational objective for the day;
- **mode-respectful** — does not silently commandeer Learning Mode topic authority (EL-009 / EL-003);
- **evidence-honest** — completion and coverage are not narrated as mastery;
- **explainable** — students can understand why today’s advice exists;
- **non-redesigning** — never invalidates or silently rewrites the Canonical Study Plan;
- **escalation-aware** — identifies when upstream rescheduling or replanning is required.

The Daily Coach is **not**:

- a second Master Planner, Scheduling Engine, Strategy selector, or Profile diagnostician;
- a licence to invent phases, steal protected revision, or invent catch-up intensity;
- ownership of Study Progress, Educational Evidence, or Twin understanding;
- a UI surface, notification system, or mission-generation algorithm;
- a claim that following today’s guidance guarantees a pass.

---

## 3. Educational Purpose

The Daily Coach exists so that:

1. **The student always has one clear educational next step** grounded in the authorised Study Plan — not a competing dashboard of unrelated tasks.
2. **Long-term educational intent survives daily life** — missed evenings, short windows, and recovery days do not silently erase the plan’s purpose.
3. **Learning, practice, revision, and recovery stay balanced** according to plan commitments — not according to whichever surface shouts loudest.
4. **Consistency compounds** — sustainable daily rhythm is preferred over heroic one-day theatre.
5. **Trust is preserved** — explanations speak educational language; claims stay typed; escalation is honest when the plan no longer fits.

---

## 4. Core Responsibilities

The Daily Coach is educationally responsible for:

| Responsibility | Meaning |
|----------------|---------|
| **Select today’s educational objective** | Choose one primary educational job for today from authorised plan work and lawful mode authority |
| **Balance learning, practice, and revision** | Honour the plan’s phase emphasis and practice / consolidation / revision commitments without collapsing them into coverage theatre |
| **Maintain consistency** | Prefer sustainable continuity with the plan’s rhythm over boom–bust “catch-up” |
| **Protect recovery** | Treat authorised recovery / rest / freshness capacity as first-class coaching truth — not leftover scraps |
| **Respond to recent progress** | Adjust today’s emphasis when recent evidence and session history warrant — without inventing mastery |
| **Preserve long-term educational intent** | Keep the Canonical Study Plan’s commitments, protections, and phase meanings intact |

### 4.1 Binding non-responsibility

The Daily Coach must **not**:

- redesign the Canonical Study Plan;
- invent new educational envelopes, phases, or sitting ambition;
- re-select Educational Strategy;
- re-diagnose the Student Educational Profile as a substitute for consulting it;
- generate or pack calendar cells (Scheduling Engine / rescheduling policy);
- override Learning Mode topic authority without disclosed, constitutionally authorised mode change;
- mint Estimated Knowledge / Mastery from completion alone.

---

## 5. Educational Guarantees

A lawful Daily Coach posture **guarantees** the following educational properties — not exam outcomes.

| Guarantee | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Plan fidelity** | Material daily guidance is warranted by an Active-class Canonical Study Plan (or honestly refuses coaching when no such plan exists) |
| **Single primary objective** | The student receives one primary educational objective for today — not an unordered pile of equal priorities |
| **Envelope respect** | Recommended load stays inside plan intensity envelopes and today’s available capacity |
| **Protection honesty** | Protected revision, buffers, recovery, and rest remain visible coaching commitments |
| **Mode humility** | Learning Mode topic authority is not silently commandeered by advisory preference |
| **Evidence honesty** | Understanding and readiness claims remain claim-typed under Evidence Model / Explainability Standard |
| **Explainability** | Every material recommendation can answer the four explainability questions (see `DAILY_COACH_EXPLAINABILITY.md`) |
| **Continuity** | Daily guidance changes do not erase learner-owned educational history (EIP-005) |
| **Non-rewrite** | The Daily Coach never silently invalidates or rewrites Canonical Study Plan educational intent |
| **Honest escalation** | When significant deviation from plan commitments is required, the coach identifies rescheduling / replanning need |

### 5.1 What the Daily Coach does *not* guarantee

- That the student will pass the examination.
- That every recommended block will be completed.
- That Estimated Knowledge / Mastery equals Study Progress.
- That advisory recommendations may override Learning Mode Mission topic authority.
- That a dense study day equals readiness.

## 6. Tutor Posture (Binding Metaphor)

When forming today’s guidance, the system must behave as an expert IFoA tutor would:

1. **Read the plan before inventing the day.** No daily ambition exists without Canonical Study Plan warrant (when a plan is the preparation frame).
2. **One priority.** Tell the student the most valuable thing to do today — then optional secondary advice, clearly ranked.
3. **Honour today’s real life.** Short windows, leave, and interruptions are capacity truth — not moral failure.
4. **Protect what was reserved.** Revision windows and recovery capacity are coaching commitments, not optional leftovers.
5. **Separate coverage from competence.** Completing today’s work advances honest study; it does not declare mastery.
6. **Respond, don’t punish.** After missed days or weak practice, prefer lawful recovery / continuity repair over shame catch-up.
7. **Stay consistent.** Prefer the plan’s sustainable rhythm over inventing heroic intensity for one evening.
8. **Escalate honestly.** When the plan’s educational envelopes no longer fit remaining capacity or evidence, say that rescheduling or replanning is needed — do not silently rewrite the plan.
9. **Explain in educational language.** Students hear why *this* matters *today* in relation to the broader journey.
10. **Leave internal machinery invisible.** No Twin facets, optimiser scores, or registry IDs in student speech.

## 8. Relationship to Learning Mode and Today’s Mission

Under Learning Mode Version 1.0 (EL-009 / EL-003):

- **Today’s Mission topic authority** remains Learning Mode → Current Learning Topic for first-pass learning days.
- The Daily Coach **selects the educational job of the day** (learn / practise / consolidate / revise / recover / rest) from Canonical Study Plan emphasis and today’s context.
- When the plan’s authorised job for today is first-pass learning, Daily Coach primary objective and Learning Mode Mission must **align** — not compete.
- When the plan’s authorised job for today is revision, recovery, practice emphasis, checkpoint, or rest, Daily Coach must **disclose** that posture and must not present an advisory first-pass topic as if it were Learning Mode Mission replacement without lawful mode / plan warrant.
- Recommendations (EL-008) may advise optional secondary work; they must not silently redefine the primary objective or Mission topic.

The Daily Coach therefore **coordinates educational priority** without becoming a second Learning Mode authority.

---

## 9. Educational Integrity

Educational integrity for the Daily Coach means:

1. **One story with the Study Plan.** Daily advice and plan narration must not contradict each other without disclosed adaptation or escalation.
2. **Claim-type honesty.** Observed Facts, Derived Facts, Estimates, and Advice remain distinguishable (EIP-003).
3. **No mastery minting.** Session completion advances Study Progress where lawful; it does not create understanding claims.
4. **No punishment pedagogy.** Missed work triggers continuity repair and lawful recovery — not invented overload.
5. **No silent plan rewrite.** Material change to long-term educational intent requires Master Planner / Scheduling pathways — not Daily Coach improvisation.
6. **Continuity of history.** Changing today’s guidance never erases rightful learner history.

---

## 10. Determinism (Educational)

Given the same:

- Active-class Canonical Study Plan posture,
- Student Educational Profile posture,
- recent evidence / session history summary,
- today’s capacity and interruption declarations,
- recovery posture,

the Daily Coach must present the same educational priority posture (same primary objective class, same balance emphasis, same escalation posture).

Variation is allowed only when those inputs change, or when upstream plan lifecycle / rescheduling changes the contract being interpreted.

Numeric scoring schemes are out of scope for this milestone. Educational determinism here means **reproducible educational posture**, not a mandated formula.
