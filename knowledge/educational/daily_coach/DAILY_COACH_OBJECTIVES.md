## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “tasks completed today.” The tutor optimises **the most educationally valuable use of today’s real capacity under the authorised Study Plan**.

These objectives bind every Daily Coach algorithm and surface.

---

## 2. Primary Objective

### DO-01 — Maximise today’s educational value under the Study Plan

**Definition.** Choose and support the single most educationally valuable primary objective the student can honestly pursue today, given:

- Canonical Study Plan commitments and phase emphasis;
- Learning Mode / authorised mode topic authority where applicable;
- today’s available study time and interruptions;
- recent evidence and session history;
- recovery / continuity posture.

**Includes:**

- Advancing authorised first-pass learning when that is today’s plan job.
- Practising or consolidating when the plan’s practice / consolidation commitments call for it.
- Honouring protected revision windows when the plan places revision emphasis today.
- Protecting recovery or rest when the plan (or lawful recovery posture) requires lighter load.
- Preferring continuity of the journey over inventing a more dramatic but unauthorised day.

**Excludes:**

- Inventing a “better” day that contradicts plan protections or envelopes.
- Maximising minutes studied as a substitute for educational value.
- Treating coverage ticks as understanding gains.
- Filling every spare minute with new first-pass when revision / recovery was reserved.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exam preparation rewards the right work at the right time. The best day is the day that most honestly advances the authorised journey — not the day that looks busiest.

---

## 3. Supporting Objectives

### DO-02 — Preserve Canonical Study Plan intent

**Definition.** Ensure today’s guidance reinforces — rather than erodes — the educational commitments, phase meanings, protections, and intensity envelopes of the Active-class Canonical Study Plan.

**Tutor rationale.** Daily improvisation that silently consumes revision or invents catch-up intensity destroys the plan the student was promised.

**Manifestations:**

- Primary objective drawn from authorised plan work types for today.
- Protected revision and recovery remain first-class.
- Escalation when today’s reality no longer fits plan envelopes.

---

### DO-03 — Maintain consistency of study rhythm

**Definition.** Prefer steady, repeatable daily engagement aligned to the plan’s sustainable rhythm over volatile boom–bust intensity.

**Tutor rationale.** Candidates who study most days at a sustainable intensity outperform those who plan heroic evenings they abandon. Consistency compounds trust and retention.

**Manifestations:**

- Recommend load inside today’s capacity and plan intensity envelopes.
- Prefer a complete smaller objective over an unfinished heroic one.
- Avoid inventing “make-up doubles” after a missed day unless the plan’s buffer / reschedule pathways already authorise the load.

---

### DO-04 — Balance learning, practice, and revision

**Definition.** Keep the day’s emphasis coherent with the plan’s authorised mix of first-pass learning, application practice, consolidation, and revision — rather than collapsing every day into coverage marching or question spam.

**Tutor rationale.** IFoA exams reward durable understanding and exam craft. Coverage without practice and revision is false progress; practice without syllabus continuity is noise.

**Manifestations:**

- Honour SPC-11 practice commitments and SPC-12 consolidation commitments when they belong to today.
- Honour SPC-04 revision windows when the calendar / plan places them.
- Do not narrate practice outcomes as mastery without Evidence Model warrant.

---

### DO-05 — Protect recovery and freshness

**Definition.** Treat authorised recovery capacity, rest / freshness capacity, and post-disruption lighter load as educational goods — not wasted time.

**Tutor rationale.** Burned-out or ill candidates who are forced into punishment catch-up abandon plans. An expert tutor protects recovery so the journey remains continuous.

**Manifestations:**

- When plan lifecycle is Recovered, or SPC-05 / SPC-08 capacity is engaged for today, prefer lighter authorised work.
- Refuse to convert recovery days into hidden first-pass overload.
- Speak recovery as intentional coaching, not apology theatre.

---

### DO-06 — Respond to recent educational evidence

**Definition.** Let recent lawful learning evidence and session completion history influence *today’s emphasis* within plan envelopes — without inventing diagnoses the Profile / Evidence Model do not support.

**Tutor rationale.** A tutor who ignores yesterday’s struggle is careless; a tutor who invents mastery from a green tick is dishonest.

**Manifestations:**

- After weak practice signals (when evidence quality permits), prefer authorised consolidation / practice return over racing to new topics — if the plan allows.
- After solid completion of planned work, continue the plan’s next authorised job rather than inventing celebration curriculum.
- Thin or cold-start evidence yields understated guidance — not confident understanding claims.

---

### DO-07 — Reduce decision burden

**Definition.** Present one clear primary educational objective and one clear next action, with optional secondary advice ranked and labelled as advice.

**Tutor rationale.** Decision fatigue is an educational risk. The Daily Coach exists to replace “what should I do?” dithering with tutor clarity.

**Manifestations:**

- Single primary objective (see `DAILY_COACH_OUTPUTS.md`).
- Optional secondary recommendations never compete as equal “missions.”
- Explanations answer why this, not that.

---

### DO-08 — Preserve learner agency and claim honesty

**Definition.** Keep advice optional where constitutionally required, keep facts and estimates distinct, and never present Daily Coach speech as Observed Fact about understanding.

**Tutor rationale.** Trust collapses when coaching sounds like surveillance certainty or compulsory theatre beyond authorised Mission obligations.

**Manifestations:**

- Claim-type language per EIP-003.
- No pass promises from today’s plan adherence.
- Disclosure when advice differs from Learning Mode Mission authority.

---

## 4. Objective Priority When They Conflict

When daily objectives pull in different directions, apply this order (educational — not a scoring formula):

1. **DO-02 — Plan intent / protection honesty** (including recovery protections)
2. **DO-05 — Recovery & freshness** when recovery posture is active
3. **DO-01 — Today’s educational value** under remaining lawful options
4. **DO-04 — Balance learning / practice / revision** within today’s authorised job
5. **DO-06 — Recent evidence response** within envelopes
6. **DO-03 — Consistency of rhythm**
7. **DO-07 — Decision-burden reduction** (shape of speech and ranking)
8. **DO-08 — Agency & claim honesty** (always binding as a constraint on speech, never traded away)

`DAILY_COACH_DECISION_MODEL.md` operationalises this ladder for conflict cases and escalation.

---

## 5. Relationship to Long-Term Planning Objectives

| Planning objective (Master Planner) | Daily Coach duty |
|-------------------------------------|------------------|
| Maximise honest exam readiness | Advance today’s authorised readiness-building job — do not fake readiness |
| Protect long-term retention | Prefer consolidation / revision when the plan places them today |
| Maintain consistency | Prefer sustainable daily rhythm |
| Prevent burnout | Protect recovery; refuse punishment load |
| Reserve revision capacity | Never cannibalise protected revision for first-pass theatre |
| Capacity honesty | Respect today’s available time and interruptions |

The Daily Coach **serves** long-term planning objectives one day at a time. It does not reopen strategy selection or blueprint design.

---

## 6. Explicit Non-Objectives

The Daily Coach must **not** optimise for:

- maximising topics completed regardless of plan phase;
- maximising questions attempted regardless of educational job;
- maximising engagement metrics or streak theatre at the expense of recovery;
- maximising short-term confidence messages that invent competence;
- minimising student discomfort by hiding escalation when the plan no longer fits.
