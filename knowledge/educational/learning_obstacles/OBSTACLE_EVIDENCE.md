## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not “feel” an obstacle into existence. Belief that a prerequisite gap, weak retrieval, or revision neglect is present rises and falls with the **trail of observations**.

Educational Reflection may hypothesise after one sitting. Obstacle diagnosis reads **many sittings** — what confirms a cause, what weakens it, and what was never observed.

This document records that evidence discipline so diagnoses remain lawful under EIP-002.

---

## 2. Binding Distinctions (Preserved)

Obstacle evidence reading must never collapse:

| Concept | Obstacle use |
|---------|--------------|
| **Completion / coverage** | May support LOB-12 or activity context; never alone proves understanding barriers as fact |
| **Understanding** | Relevant to LOB-01, LOB-03, LOB-12 readings |
| **Retrieval** | Central to LOB-02; contributes to LOB-11 |
| **Application** | Central to LOB-08, LOB-03, LOB-01 differentiation |
| **Durable knowledge** | Central to LOB-09, LOB-11 |
| **Exam readiness** | Central to LOB-10 and false-readiness risks (LOB-07, LOB-12) |

Constitutional corollary for obstacles:

> Completing sessions never diagnoses an understanding obstacle by itself.  
> Familiarity never diagnoses weak retrieval without production attempts.  
> One practice failure never Supports a misconception.  
> One spaced return never Supports permanent fragility as eternal fact.  
> Calendar proximity never diagnoses exam technique without exam-like observations.

| EIP-002 / Learning Evidence principle | Obstacle reading |

| Evidence is observed | Diagnoses cite observations; they do not mint them |
| Evidence has quality | Supported content/structure LOBs need higher-quality performance warrant than soft signals alone |

## 4. How Evidence Classes Feed Diagnosis

Learning Evidence contribution classes (EC-A…EC-F) colour obstacle likelihood. They do not automatically assign LOBs.

| Class | Supports diagnosis when… | Weakens / blocks diagnosis when… |
|-------|--------------------------|----------------------------------|
| **EC-A Coverage / contact** | Rapid EC-A with empty deeper classes → LOB-12 / LOB-08 candidates; missing earlier EC-A on prerequisites → LOB-01 candidate | EC-A alone used to claim LOB-03/10/11 Supported |
| **EC-B Soft calibration** | Persistent confidence-vs-outcome conflict → LOB-07; “too much at once” → LOB-05 colouring; method self-report → LOB-06 colouring | Soft narrative alone Supports content LOBs; soft despair Supports incapacity-as-fact |
| **EC-C Retrieval / practice outcomes** | Patterned errors → LOB-03/01; thin EC-C despite coverage → LOB-08; prompt-vs-production gap → LOB-02; gains absent despite volume → LOB-06 | Single failed set Supports any LOB; success same-day used to deny LOB-11 without EC-E |
| **EC-D Exam-like performance** | Timed/paper process faults with fair isolated EC-C → LOB-10; integrated gaps → may reveal LOB-01/03 | One difficult mock brands permanent LOB-10; EC-D absence “proves” technique is fine |
| **EC-E Spacing / return** | Weak return after strong near-study → LOB-11; absent returns by design → LOB-09 | Claiming LOB-11 without any return; claiming LOB-09 when returns occurred and failed (that is LOB-11) |
| **EC-F Continuity / interruption** | Unplanned burst/gap cycles → LOB-04; post-break decay → recovery vs LOB-11 differentiation | Treating Continuity-governed recovery as shameful LOB-04 |

## 5. Accumulation Patterns → Diagnostic Effect

Aligned to Learning Evidence Model §5 patterns:

### 5.1 Confirming Accumulation

Multiple agreeing EC-C/EC-D (and EC-E where relevant) observations point the same way.

**Diagnostic effect:** May raise confidence toward **Supported** for the matching LOB — still an estimate.

### 5.2 Thin Accumulation

Many EC-A/EC-B; little EC-C/EC-E.

**Diagnostic effect:** May support **Provisional** LOB-08 or LOB-12; must **not** Support LOB-03, LOB-10, or LOB-11. Often **Insufficient warrant** for structural causes.

### 5.3 Conflicting Accumulation

Immediate success vs weak return; high soft confidence vs weak outcomes; fair untimed vs weak timed.

**Diagnostic effect:** Prefer **Conflicting** posture; shortlist rivals (e.g. LOB-02 vs LOB-11; LOB-07 vs content LOBs; LOB-10 vs LOB-01). Do not force a single Supported LOB for coaching ease.

### 5.4 Decaying Accumulation

Earlier EC-C warrant followed by absence and weaker return.

**Diagnostic effect:** Opens LOB-11 and Continuity/recovery readings; weakens overstated understanding claims; does not erase prior coverage facts.

### 5.5 Stall Accumulation

Continued sessions without objective advancement.

**Diagnostic effect:** Invites diagnosis (LPS-S → investigate LOB-01/03/06/08/12). Stall is a **symptom posture**, not a LOB.

### 5.6 Accelerating Accumulation

Confirming gains with warrant.

**Diagnostic effect:** Weakens stall-type LOBs on that scope; does not itself invent obstacles elsewhere. Watch LOB-07/12 if acceleration is coverage-only.

## 6. Support and Weakening by Obstacle

For each LOB: what strengthens the diagnosis, what weakens it, and which ladder distinctions matter.

---

### LOB-01 — Prerequisite Gap

| Direction | Evidence reading |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Supports** | Patterned foundation errors (EC-C); weak checks on earlier scope; curriculum-order jump; advanced stall after thin earlier trail |
| **Weakens** | Solid earlier-scope EC-C/EC-E; errors confined to a current false rule (LOB-03); practice never attempted on current scope (LOB-08 first) |
| **Ladder note** | Missing coverage on prerequisites (LO-01 earlier) differs from failed understanding (LO-03) on prerequisites — both can yield LOB-01, but speech must say which |

---

### LOB-02 — Weak Retrieval

| Direction | Evidence reading |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Supports** | Prompted/open success vs unaided failure (EC-C); recognition ≫ production across sessions |
| **Weakens** | Strong unaided production; no production attempts exist (cannot diagnose); spaced failure without cue contrast (LOB-11) |
| **Ladder note** | Familiarity (LO-02) intact does not contradict LOB-02 — it often coexists |

---

### LOB-03 — Misconception

| Direction | Evidence reading |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Supports** | Stable wrong method across item forms (EC-C); student-stated false rule (EC-B as colouring + EC-C); post-repair flip (later confirming) |
| **Weakens** | Heterogeneous errors; single failure; high variance under overload (LOB-05); thin sampling |
| **Ladder note** | Misconception blocks understanding *quality* (LO-03) even when coverage (LO-01) looks complete |

---

### LOB-04 — Inconsistent Study

| Direction | Evidence reading |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Supports** | EC-F burst/gap cycles; Profile reliability concerns; progression stall despite occasional intensity |
| **Weakens** | Steady cadence with other LOBs explaining stall; Continuity-named recovery plan; single life interruption |
| **Ladder note** | Cadence problems delay many LOs; they do not by themselves prove weak understanding |

---

### LOB-05 — Cognitive Overload

| Direction | Evidence reading |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Supports** | In-session decline as complexity stacks; unfinished multi-goal days; quality restores when scope narrows |
| **Weakens** | Failures persist under narrowed, calm conditions (content LOBs); soft preference for easy work alone |
| **Ladder note** | Overload can suppress application evidence without proving LO-03 absence |

---

### LOB-06 — Ineffective Study Strategies

| Direction | Evidence reading |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Supports** | High engagement time with thin EC-C gains; method descriptions of passive study; production rarely attempted |
| **Weakens** | Production-shaped practice already dense with patterned errors (LOB-03/01); absolute practice near zero (LOB-08) |
| **Ladder note** | Strategy affects conversion from coverage/familiarity into retrieval/application |

---

### LOB-07 — Confidence Mismatch

| Direction | Evidence reading |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Supports** | Repeated EC-B vs EC-C conflict after feedback; false readiness speech vs thin LO-07 warrant |
| **Weakens** | Soft and hard signals aligned; one emotional outlier; no performance trail (cannot claim overconfidence about untested skill) |
| **Ladder note** | Soft signals never author durable knowledge or exam readiness; they calibrate speech and LOB-07 |

---

### LOB-08 — Insufficient Practice

| Direction | Evidence reading |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Supports** | EC-A present, EC-C sparse; avoidance of questions; LPS-1 when practice expected |
| **Weakens** | Dense EC-C already; practice failed for structural reasons; unrecorded practice (observation gap ≠ proven sufficiency) |
| **Ladder note** | Distinguishes “covered” (LO-01) from “applied” (LO-05) |

---

### LOB-09 — Revision Neglect

| Direction | Evidence reading |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Supports** | Absent EC-E by design across earlier scopes; skipped revision windows; spacing-light pre-sitting trail |
| **Weakens** | Returns scheduled and executed (then read LOB-11 outcomes); early first-pass with lawful later revision still ahead |
| **Ladder note** | Neglect blocks durable knowledge (LO-06) claims and endangers exam readiness speech |

---

### LOB-10 — Exam Technique Deficiencies

| Direction | Evidence reading |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Supports** | EC-D process faults with comparatively fair isolated EC-C; repeated timing/structure/notation issues |
| **Weakens** | No EC-D exists; content failures dominate even untimed; single anomalous mock |
| **Ladder note** | Exam readiness (LO-07) is synthesis — technique is one contributor, never a prophesied pass/fail |

---

### LOB-11 — Fragile Durability

| Direction | Evidence reading |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Supports** | Near-study EC-C encouraging; spaced EC-E materially weaker on same scope |
| **Weakens** | No return observed (LOB-09 or insufficient warrant); near-study “success” was recognition-only; Continuity recovery still in progress |
| **Ladder note** | Application once (LO-05) never implies durable knowledge (LO-06) |

---

### LOB-12 — Coverage Ahead of Understanding

| Direction | Evidence reading |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Supports** | Rapid EC-A; empty/thin EC-C; soft coverage confidence; accelerating LPS-1 without performance |
| **Weakens** | Parallel confirming EC-C; plan-lawful first-pass with imminent practice; mis-logged coverage |
| **Ladder note** | Coverage advancing is not understanding progressing |

## 7. Evidence That Must Not Author Diagnosis Alone

| Observation family | May colour | Must not alone Support |
|--------------------|------------|------------------------|
| Mission / session completion | Activity, possible LOB-04 context | LOB-03, LOB-10, LOB-11, strong LOB-01 |
| Streak / minutes studied | Effort context for LOB-06 vs LOB-08 | Any understanding or exam-readiness obstacle as proven |
| Single Reflection sentence | Provisional hypothesis | Supported structural LOB |
| Felt confidence alone | LOB-07 colouring | Content incapacity or mastery obstacles |
| Sitting date proximity | Urgency for LOB-09/12 risk speech | LOB-10 without exam-like evidence |
| Recommendation accepted | Activity | Evidence that the obstacle existed or cleared |

## 8. Clearing or Revising a Diagnosis

Obstacle diagnoses are **reversible** as evidence evolves.

| Event | Educational effect |
|-------|--------------------|
| Confirming contrary EC-C/EC-E | Weaken or retire the LOB |
| Successful prerequisite rebuild then current gains | LOB-01 may clear on that scope |
| Conceptual repair then diversified success | LOB-03 may clear |
| Steady cadence restored | LOB-04 may clear |
| Spaced successful returns | LOB-11 weakens; LOB-09 may clear if returns now designed |
| New conflicting class appears | Move to Conflicting; do not silently keep Supported |

Clearing a diagnosis is **not** automatic mastery speech. Progression Model and Evidence Model still govern what may be claimed about understanding and readiness.

Intervention success is **not** itself evidence until qualifying observations are recorded (EIP-002: inference may not invent evidence).

## 9. Integrity Boundaries

| Boundary | Lawful | Unlawful |
|----------|--------|----------|
| Authorship | Interpret recorded observations | Invent EV records by naming a LOB |
| Scoring | Refuse numeric support weights here | Publish obstacle evidence scores as educational law |
| Ladder | Keep completion…exam readiness distinct | Collapse into “struggling” as sole reading |
| Soft signals | Level-1 calibration | Author Supported content LOBs |
| Twin | Consume estimates where they exist | Obstacle diagnosis writes Estimated Mastery |
| Intervention | Respond after diagnosis | Treat LI completion as proof the LOB cleared without new evidence |
