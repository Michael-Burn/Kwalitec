## What changed in MS003

Programme VI / Learning Coach **MS003 — Learning Intervention Framework** elevates interventions from a progression adjunct into a full educational specification:

| Concern | Location under MS003 |
|---------|----------------------|
| Constitutional overview | `LEARNING_INTERVENTION_FRAMEWORK.md` |
| Catalogue (incl. evidence gathering, deliberate practice, revision restructuring) | `INTERVENTION_CATALOGUE.md` |
| Selection after diagnosis (non-numeric) | `INTERVENTION_SELECTION.md` |
| Authority boundaries vs plan / Daily Coach / mastery / strategy | `INTERVENTION_BOUNDARIES.md` |
| Supportive explainability | `INTERVENTION_EXPLAINABILITY.md` |

Architectural chain (binding):

```
Evidence → Diagnosis → Intervention → Future evidence
```

No intervention may bypass diagnosis. No intervention may silently rewrite the Canonical Study Plan, override Daily Coach authority, redefine mastery, or change long-term educational strategy.

## Historical seed (MS001)

The original MS001 catalogue defined LI-01…LI-12 as tutor responses to progression triggers (stall, inconsistency, decay, acceleration, thin warrant, false readiness risk), with a diagnosis gate added when MS002 landed.

Those identifiers remain continuous under MS003. New governing entries include **LI-00 Evidence Gathering**, **LI-13 Deliberate Practice**, and **LI-14 Revision Restructuring**, with worked-example emphasis as **LI-08a**.

For selection tables, obstacle maps, boundaries, and speech contracts, use the MS003 corpus only.
