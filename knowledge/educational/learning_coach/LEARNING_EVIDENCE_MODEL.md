## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor forms beliefs about learning from a **trail of observations**, not from a single evening’s checkbox.

Educational Reflection interprets one sitting. The Learning Coach reads **many sittings**: what strengthened, what conflicted, what never appeared, and what faded after spacing.

This document records how that accumulation works educationally — qualitatively, traceably, and without inventing scoring mathematics.

---

## 2. Relationship to the Educational Evidence Model

| EIP-002 concept | Learning Coach use |
|-----------------|-------------------|
| Evidence is observed | Progression never invents observations |
| Evidence has quality | Higher claims need higher Quality Level warrant |
| Evidence accumulates | Strong progression speech requires density over time |
| Inference may not invent Evidence | Progression is inference over history, not new evidence minting |
| Activity alone is not knowledge | Session / mission completion alone never warrants understanding progression |
| Absence is uncertainty | Missing evidence → cautious or silent progression claims |

**Hard rule:**

> The Learning Coach may interpret Educational Evidence.  
> It may not mint Educational Evidence by renaming completions, recommendations, or coaching desires as observations.

Architectural Evidence Model artefacts under `knowledge/architecture/` remain implementation/architecture companions. Educational claim law for this corpus is EIP-002.

## 3. Accumulation Principles

### 3.1 Sessions Contribute; Sessions Do Not Certify

Each completed study session may contribute:

- activity / coverage observations (when lawful);
- performance observations (when outcomes exist);
- soft calibration (reflection, felt confidence);
- interruption / recovery context.

No single session **certifies** understanding, durable retention, or exam readiness.

### 3.2 Multiple Pieces Build Educational Understanding

Progression judgement emerges when several observations **cohere**:

- same syllabus scope revisited across days;
- practice outcomes that confirm or challenge earlier readings;
- spaced returns that test retention;
- Reflection conclusions that remain consistent (or honestly conflict).

Coherence is qualitative: patterns of confirmation, conflict, sparsity, and decay — not a weighted sum defined here.

### 3.3 Conflict Requires Understatement

When observations conflict (strong same-day recall, weak spaced return; high felt confidence, weak application), the Learning Coach:

1. names the conflict;
2. understates the stronger claim;
3. prefers the objective class of warrant when soft and hard disagree;
4. never averages conflict into false calm.

### 3.4 Recency Informs; History Constrains

Recent sessions may change near-term emphasis (Daily Coach / Reflection).  
Longitudinal progression still requires history:

- recent success after long failure → provisional recovery, not instant durable knowledge;
- recent failure after strong history → investigate (rust, exam form, fatigue) before erasing prior warrant.

### 3.5 Quality Before Quantity

Many Level-1 engagement ticks do not outrank fewer Level-2/3 performance observations for understanding claims.  
Volume of completion never compensates for absence of performance evidence.

### 3.6 No Numerical Scoring in This Model

This Model forbids defining:

- progression percentages;
- mastery scalars;
- evidence point systems;
- streak scores as learning proof.

Future implementation may use internal proxies elsewhere. Those proxies **must not redefine** the educational meanings here, and student-facing Learning Coach speech must remain qualitative and claim-typed (EIP-003).

## 4. Evidence Contribution Classes (Across Sessions)

The Learning Coach reads contributions by **educational class**, mapped to EIP-002 sources without inventing new claim law.

### EC-A — Coverage / Contact Contributions

| Aspect | Provision |
|--------|-----------|
| **What accumulates** | Lawful Study Progress advances; reading / study-completion observations for syllabus units |
| **Supports** | LO-01 Syllabus coverage; weakly LO-02 Familiarity |
| **Does not support alone** | LO-03–LO-07 strong claims |
| **Typical EIP-002 anchors** | EV-01, EV-02 (coverage path); Level 0–1 for understanding |

**Longitudinal reading:** Rising coverage across weeks shows journey movement. It never becomes “genuine learning” of understanding by density alone.

---

### EC-B — Engagement / Soft Calibration Contributions

| Aspect | Provision |
|--------|-----------|
| **What accumulates** | Reflections, felt difficulty, motivation, self-assessed confidence |
| **Supports** | Coaching tone; flags for struggle or overconfidence; weak LO-02 colouring |
| **Does not support alone** | Strong LO-03–LO-07 claims; mastery language |
| **Typical EIP-002 anchors** | Soft / Level 1 signals; Reflection Model soft inputs |

**Longitudinal reading:** Persistent “I don’t get this” alongside weak practice is educationally meaningful. Persistent “I know this” without performance remains soft.

---

### EC-C — Retrieval / Practice Outcome Contributions

| Aspect | Provision |
|--------|-----------|
| **What accumulates** | Question attempts with outcomes; quizzes; reconstruction under reduced prompts |
| **Supports** | LO-03 Understanding (provisional); LO-04 Retrieval; LO-05 Application |
| **Does not support alone** | LO-06 Durable retention (needs spacing); LO-07 Exam readiness (needs synthesis) |
| **Typical EIP-002 anchors** | EV-03, EV-04, EV-05; Levels 2–3 |

**Longitudinal reading:** Improving application across multiple sessions on the same scope is core “genuine learning” warrant. One lucky set is a stir, not a trajectory.

---

### EC-D — Exam-like / Integrated Performance Contributions

| Aspect | Provision |
|--------|-----------|
| **What accumulates** | Mocks, timed sets, multi-topic papers, exam-condition practice |
| **Supports** | Stronger LO-05; contributes heavily to LO-07 synthesis; may reveal integrated gaps |
| **Does not support alone** | Instant LO-06 on every micro-topic; prophesied pass/fail |
| **Typical EIP-002 anchors** | EV-06; Levels 3–4 when conditions warrant |

**Longitudinal reading:** Mocks are high-value progression evidence. One difficult mock is diagnostic, not a permanent brand of failure.

---

### EC-E — Spacing / Return Contributions

| Aspect | Provision |
|--------|-----------|
| **What accumulates** | Outcomes after deliberate delay; revision returns; post-interruption recovery performance |
| **Supports** | LO-06 Durable retention; strengthens or weakens prior LO-03–LO-05 readings |
| **Does not support alone** | Coverage for never-studied topics; LO-07 without broader synthesis |
| **Typical EIP-002 anchors** | Spaced practice / revision observations authorised under Evidence & Continuity rules |

**Longitudinal reading:** Spacing is the Learning Coach’s primary durability test. Same-day success without later return cannot claim durable knowledge.

---

### EC-F — Continuity / Interruption Context

| Aspect | Provision |
|--------|-----------|
| **What accumulates** | Missed cadence, breaks, recovery posture, planning reliability signals (often via Profile) |
| **Supports** | Interpretation of stalls; distinguishes rust from never-learned; informs intervention urgency |
| **Does not support alone** | Understanding or mastery claims |
| **Typical anchors** | Profile evolution events; Continuity Standard; Reflection interruption notes |

**Longitudinal reading:** Gaps change how prior evidence is read (possible decay), not whether prior coverage existed.

## 5. Accumulation Patterns the Tutor Recognises

### 5.1 Confirming Accumulation

Multiple EC-C/EC-D observations on the same scope agree in direction over time.

**Tutor reading:** Progression on understanding / application is becoming warrantable.  
**Speech:** “Across recent practice, evidence consistently suggests…”

### 5.2 Thin Accumulation

Many EC-A/EC-B observations; little or no EC-C/EC-E.

**Tutor reading:** Coverage or familiarity may be rising; genuine understanding progression is not yet claimable.  
**Speech:** “You’ve covered more, but we don’t yet have enough practice evidence to judge understanding.”

### 5.3 Conflicting Accumulation

Strong immediate performance (EC-C) with weak spaced return (EC-E), or high soft confidence (EC-B) with weak outcomes (EC-C).

**Tutor reading:** Inconsistent progression — intervene; do not average into mastery.  
**Speech:** “You can do this soon after study, but it weakens on return — so we’ll emphasise retrieval and spacing.”

### 5.4 Decaying Accumulation

Earlier EC-C warrant followed by long absence and weaker return.

**Tutor reading:** Estimated retention risk; coverage remains; understanding posture may need caution.  
**Speech:** “Earlier practice was encouraging; after the break we should rebuild retrieval before claiming the same strength.”

### 5.5 Accelerating Accumulation

Rapid confirming EC-C/EC-D with stable return signals, without soft-signal-only inflation.

**Tutor reading:** Acceleration may warrant increased challenge — still provisional; still not completion theatre.  
**Speech:** “Evidence is accumulating quickly and holding — we can raise challenge carefully.”

### 5.6 Stall Accumulation

Continued sessions (activity) without improvement — or with repeated failure patterns — on the same educational objective.

**Tutor reading:** Progression stall; intervention required (prerequisites, reinforcement, method change).  
**Speech:** “You’re putting in sessions, but the evidence isn’t showing growth yet — so we’ll change the educational approach.”

## 6. How Pieces Contribute Without Scoring

The Learning Coach builds understanding through **qualitative composition**:

| Composition question | Educational use |
|----------------------|-----------------|
| What objective is each observation about? | Map to LO-01…LO-07 |
| What quality class is it? | Honour EIP-002 Levels |
| Does it confirm, conflict, or stay sparse relative to prior history? | Pattern class (§5) |
| Has spacing been tested? | Gate LO-06 speech |
| Is warrant dense enough for the claim strength desired? | Silence or understatement if not |
| Does this stay consistent with the Student Educational Profile? | Re-consult Profile if materially inconsistent |

**Forbidden composition:** summing ticks into a mastery number; treating streak length as understanding; equating % syllabus covered with % learned.

**Required composition output:** a provisional progression reading that names (a) which objectives advanced, (b) which evidence classes support that, (c) what remains uncertain.

## 7. Session Series as Evidence Narrative

A minimal lawful narrative for progression:

1. **Scope** — which syllabus units / domains the trail concerns.
2. **Coverage posture** — what has been honestly studied (LO-01).
3. **Performance trail** — what application / retrieval observations exist (EC-C/EC-D).
4. **Spacing trail** — what returns exist (EC-E).
5. **Conflicts & gaps** — where warrant is thin or contradictory.
6. **Profile alignment** — consistency with current educational diagnosis.
7. **Claim** — progression posture + next educational priority (see States, Interventions, Explainability).

If steps 3–4 are empty, steps 6–7 may only claim coverage/familiarity progress or explicit uncertainty.

---

## 8. Binding Inference Rules (Learning Coach)

1. **Completion alone → no understanding progression claim.**  
2. **Familiarity / soft signals alone → no strong understanding, application, retention, or exam-readiness claim.**  
3. **One practice success → provisional stir only; not durable progression.**  
4. **Confirming multi-session practice → may support understanding / application progression language (provisional).**  
5. **Spaced successful return → may support durable retention language (provisional).**  
6. **Exam readiness → synthesis only; never from coverage density or calendar proximity alone.**  
7. **Conflict → understate; name both sides.**  
8. **Absence → uncertainty narrative, not invented weakness-as-fact or invented strength.**

These refine Constitution Article V and EIP-002 inference posture for longitudinal coaching. They do not amend them.

## 9. Integrity Boundaries

| Boundary | Lawful | Unlawful |
|----------|--------|----------|
| Evidence authorship | Interpret recorded observations | Invent evidence from coaching goals |
| Scoring | Refuse numeric progression definitions here | Publish Learning Coach scores as educational law |
| Twin writes | Consume Twin estimates where they exist | Learning Coach writes Estimated Mastery by fiat |
| Reflection | Use session conclusions as inputs to the trail | Treat one Reflection as long-term certification |
| Profile | Stay consistent or trigger re-diagnosis | Silently override Profile with progression theatre |
