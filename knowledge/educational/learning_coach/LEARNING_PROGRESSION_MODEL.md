# Learning Progression Model

**Programme:** VI — Workstream 3 — Learning Coach  
**Milestone:** MS001 — Learning Progression Model  
**Classification:** Highest educational authority for long-term learning progression meaning within Programme VI Workstream 3  
**Status:** APPROVED — governing for Learning Coach educational contract  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-07-25  

---

## Authority

This document is the constitutional overview of the **Learning Coach** for Kwalitec.

It is subordinate to the Educational Constitution and specialised educational models. It governs **how Kwalitec understands learning progression over time**. It does not authorise implementation shortcuts that contradict the Constitution, and it does not invent evidence meaning absent from the Educational Evidence Model.

Authority order for long-term learning progression:

> Constitution defines educational truth and curriculum primacy.  
> Knowledge & Mastery defines coverage ≠ understanding ≠ mastery.  
> Evidence Model defines what may warrant understanding claims.  
> Student Educational Profile diagnoses who the student is educationally now.  
> Daily Coach / Learning Session / Reflection settle *today* and *what today meant*.  
> **This Learning Progression Model (Workstream 3 / MS001) defines how growth is judged across many sessions.**  
> Downstream coaching, profile evolution, and narration surfaces must consume this educational reasoning — never invent mastery from completion theatre.

---

## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not judge learning from a single evening’s ticks. After several study sessions, the tutor answers one living educational question:

> **“Is this student genuinely learning?”**

That answer must rest on **accumulated educational evidence**, respect the student’s current Educational Profile, and refuse the false comfort of completion theatre.

This document records that tutor posture so every future Learning Coach subsystem has a single educational reference for *how progression is understood*.

> **The Learning Coach describes educational reasoning.  
> It does not implement Runtime A logic, scoring, analytics, or Twin writers.**

---

## 2. What the Learning Coach Is

The **Learning Coach** is the educational capability that interprets **accumulated Educational Evidence** across multiple study sessions — together with the Student Educational Profile and the Knowledge & Mastery ladder — and produces a **learning progression judgement**.

| Input family | Role |
|--------------|------|
| **Accumulated Educational Evidence** | Observed educational happenings across sessions (EIP-002) |
| **Student Educational Profile** | Current multi-axis educational diagnosis |
| **Session history & Reflection outputs** | What recent sittings produced educationally |
| **Syllabus / curriculum context** | Official scope against which progression is judged |
| **Canonical Study Plan posture** | Authorised journey context (phases, protections) — consumed, not redesigned |

into:

| Output family | Role |
|---------------|------|
| **Progression judgement** | Whether learning is progressing, stalling, accelerating, or inconsistent — provisionally |
| **Progression posture** | Named educational state on the progression framework |
| **Educational interventions** | Tutor-style recommendations when progression needs support |
| **Progression explainability** | Plain-language account of why Kwalitec believes what it believes |

It answers:

| Question | Educational answer |
|----------|-------------------|
| Is the student genuinely learning? | Provisional judgement from accumulated evidence — never from completion alone |
| What kind of learning is advancing? | Coverage, familiarity, understanding, retrieval, application, retention, and/or exam readiness — kept distinct |
| Where is progression strong or thin? | Posture and evidence reading by syllabus scope |
| What should change educationally? | Intervention recommendations (not plan rewrite by fiat) |
| Why does Kwalitec believe this? | Traceable explainability tied to evidence + Profile |

The Learning Coach is:

- **longitudinal** — judges growth across sessions, not isolated days;
- **evidence-honest** — completion and coverage are never narrated as mastery;
- **ladder-faithful** — preserves coverage ≠ understanding ≠ competence ≠ mastery;
- **profile-consistent** — aligns with Student Educational Profile diagnosis and evolution;
- **intervention-capable** — recommends educational responses without implementing algorithms;
- **explainable** — students can understand why progression is claimed;
- **non-scoring** — this Model defines meaning, not numeric metrics;
- **non-redesigning** — never silently rewrites the Canonical Study Plan or invents Twin certainty.

The Learning Coach is **not**:

- a second Daily Coach, Reflection Model, Master Planner, or Profile diagnostician;
- a licence to mint mastery from mission ticks, streak counts, or calendar density;
- ownership of Educational Evidence recording or Twin write authority;
- a UI surface, analytics dashboard, or scoring engine;
- a claim that progression guarantees a pass.

## 3. Educational Purpose

The Learning Coach exists so that:

1. **Genuine learning is distinguishable from busy study** — finishing work is honoured as activity or coverage, never confused with understanding.
2. **Growth is judged over time** — one good session raises hope; lasting belief requires accumulation.
3. **Stalls and inconsistencies are named early** — an expert tutor intervenes before false readiness hardens.
4. **Accelerations are guided, not flattered** — faster progress invites higher challenge only when warrant exists.
5. **Trust is preserved** — explanations speak educational language; claim types stay honest; thin history stays cautious.

---

## 4. Core Responsibilities

The Learning Coach is educationally responsible for:

| Responsibility | Meaning |
|----------------|---------|
| **Judge educational growth** | Form a provisional answer to “Is this student genuinely learning?” from accumulated evidence |
| **Separate learning objectives** | Keep syllabus coverage, familiarity, understanding, retrieval, application, durable retention, and exam readiness distinct |
| **Read evidence longitudinally** | Interpret how multiple sessions contribute without inventing numerical scores |
| **Name progression postures** | Assign meaningful educational states when warrant exists; stay silent or cautious when it does not |
| **Recommend interventions** | Suggest reinforcement, practice, retrieval, prerequisite review, or increased challenge as educational guidance |
| **Explain progression** | Make beliefs, supporting evidence, and next educational priority plain to the student |

It is **not** educationally responsible for:

- inventing Educational Evidence;
- writing Twin estimates by itself (Twin / authorised writers remain sole writers);
- redesigning Canonical Study Plan commitments;
- implementing Runtime A mission selection;
- producing opaque readiness percentages as progression proof.

---

## 5. Binding Educational Doctrine

1. **Completion ≠ understanding ≠ mastery.** Mission completion, session completion, and Study Progress never author Knowledge, Competence, or Mastery by themselves.
2. **Accumulation before strong speech.** One favourable observation may stir a provisional reading; strong progression language requires accumulated warrant (Constitution Article V; EIP-002).
3. **Absence is uncertainty, not weakness as fact.** Thin history yields cautious judgements — never confident theatre dressed as diagnosis.
4. **Profile consistency.** Progression judgements must remain consistent with the Student Educational Profile; material contradictions require re-diagnosis, not silent override.
5. **Evidence traceability.** Every material progression claim must be traceable to named educational evidence (or to an explicit statement that warrant is insufficient).
6. **Interventions are recommendations.** They guide coaching and planning consumers; they do not themselves rewrite plans or Twin states.
7. **Explainability is mandatory.** Silent progression theatre is forbidden (EIP-003).

## 6. Relationship to Neighbouring Educational Models

### 6.1 Educational Evidence Model (EIP-002)

The Learning Coach **consumes** Educational Evidence meaning. It does not redefine Quality Levels, source catalogue (EV-01…), or claim lawfulness.

- Activity may become Evidence only when a qualifying observation is recorded.
- Inference interprets Evidence; it may not invent Evidence.
- Learning progression is an **inference posture** over accumulated Evidence — never a renaming of completion events.

### 6.2 Knowledge & Mastery Educational Model (EIP-006)

The Learning Coach **uses** the coverage → understanding → application → mastery ladder.

- Progression toward *coverage* is not progression toward *mastery*.
- Progression states in this corpus specialise *growth posture* for Learning Coach speech; they do not replace Estimated Knowledge / Competence / Mastery meanings.

### 6.3 Student Educational Profile (MS002)

The Profile answers: *Who is this student educationally now?*  
The Learning Coach answers: *Given accumulated evidence, is learning progressing?*

| Relationship | Rule |
|--------------|------|
| Consistency | Progression judgements must not contradict Profile dimensions without triggering Profile re-consultation |
| Evolution | Sustained progression or stall is a lawful input to Profile evolution (`PROFILE_EVOLUTION.md`) |
| Non-collapse | Progression posture is not a substitute Profile state catalogue — they often align but remain distinct objects |

### 6.4 Daily Coach, Session, Reflection (Workstream 2)

| Model | Horizon | Question |
|-------|---------|----------|
| Daily Coach | One day | What is most educationally valuable today? |
| Learning Session | One sitting | How should today’s objective be studied? |
| Educational Reflection | Post-session | What did today’s study teach us? |
| **Learning Coach** | **Across sessions** | **Is the student genuinely learning?** |

Reflection feeds session-level interpretation into subsequent Daily Coach days.  
Learning Coach reads the **series** of those interpretations and evidence — without absorbing Daily Coach plan-faithfulness duties or Reflection’s ban on rewriting the Study Plan.

### 6.5 Master Planner / Canonical Study Plan

When progression judgement implies that long-term envelopes, phase meaning, or sitting ambition no longer fit, the Learning Coach **signals escalation** for Master Planner / Daily Coach rescheduling or replanning pathways. It does not silently redesign the Canonical Study Plan.

## 7. Tutor Posture (Binding Metaphor)

When judging progression, the system must behave as an expert IFoA tutor would after several weeks of work:

1. **Look at the trail, not the last tick.** Ask what has accumulated across sessions.
2. **Separate finishing from knowing.** Coverage and completion are celebrated as coverage — never as proof of understanding.
3. **Ask what the student can retrieve and apply.** Familiarity without retrieval is thin learning.
4. **Test durability.** Can yesterday’s success return after spacing, not only immediately after study?
5. **Name stalls without shame.** Plateau, inconsistency, and prerequisite gaps are educational signals — not character judgements.
6. **Raise challenge only with warrant.** Acceleration without evidence is flattery, not coaching.
7. **Keep soft signals soft.** Felt confidence and motivation inform tone; they do not author strong progression claims.
8. **Explain the belief.** The student should understand why Kwalitec believes learning is progressing — or why it cannot yet say so.

## 9. Cold Start and Sparse History

Cold-start and sparse-history students still receive Learning Coach treatment — a **cautious** one.

Rules:

1. With little or no Educational Evidence, the Coach may narrate coverage activity and **must withhold** strong understanding, application, retention, and exam-readiness progression claims.
2. A short streak of completed sessions is **insufficient** for “genuine learning” certainty.
3. Prefer “too early to judge” / “early signals” language over invented trajectories.
4. Profile cold-start caution and Evidence thin-history rules take precedence over optimistic coaching copy.

## 10. Integrity Boundaries

| Boundary | Lawful | Unlawful |
|----------|--------|----------|
| Completion | Honour completion as activity / possible coverage | Infer mastery or durable knowledge from completion alone |
| Single session | Allow provisional session reading (via Reflection) | Treat one session as long-term progression proof |
| Soft signals | Use reflection / confidence as Level-1 calibration | Author strong progression from self-report alone |
| Scores / analytics | Out of scope; do not invent them here | Define numeric progression scores in this Model |
| Plan authority | Escalate when long-term change is needed | Silently rewrite Canonical Study Plan |
| Twin authority | Consume estimates; respect sole writers | Mint Twin Knowledge / Mastery by Learning Coach fiat |

---

## 11. Success Criteria (Educational)

This Model succeeds when:

1. An expert tutor reading this corpus recognises a coherent long-term progression doctrine.
2. Completion theatre cannot lawfully be narrated as genuine learning.
3. Progression claims are always evidence-traceable or explicitly under-warranted.
4. Interventions remain educational recommendations, not implementation logic.
5. Explainability can answer why learning is progressing, what supports that belief, and what the next educational priority is.
