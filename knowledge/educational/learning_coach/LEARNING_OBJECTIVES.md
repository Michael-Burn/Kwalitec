## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not treat “topics finished” as the definition of learning success.

Professional actuarial exams reward **understanding that survives retrieval, application under exam-like conditions, and durable retention across time**. Syllabus coverage is necessary scaffolding — not the destination.

This document distinguishes seven educational objectives so the Learning Coach can judge *what kind of learning* is progressing — and refuse to collapse them.

---

## 2. Binding Separation Doctrine

The following must never be treated as synonyms:

| Objective | Primary question |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Syllabus coverage** | Has the student honestly studied this scope? |
| **Familiarity** | Does the material feel recognisable after contact? |
| **Understanding** | Does the student currently comprehend the ideas? |
| **Retrieval ability** | Can the student recall and reconstruct without prompts? |
| **Application** | Can the student use the ideas when asked to perform? |
| **Durable retention** | Does command survive spacing and return? |
| **Exam readiness** | Is preparation credible for the sitting under honest warrant? |

Constitutional grounding:

> Completing studying never implies Knowledge.  
> Having Knowledge never implies Competence.  
> Having Competence never implies Mastery.  
> Coverage theatre never implies exam readiness.

Learning Coach corollary:

> Completing a session never implies understanding.  
> Familiarity never implies retrieval.  
> Retrieval never implies application.  
> Application once never implies durable retention.  
> Retention on parts never implies whole-paper exam readiness.

## 3. Objective Catalogue

Each objective records: educational meaning, significance for IFoA preparation, typical supporting evidence class, what it does **not** prove, and student-facing speech cues.

---

### LO-01 — Syllabus Coverage

| Aspect | Definition |
|--------|------------|
| **Educational meaning** | Honest record that the student has studied (or lawfully completed study obligations for) a syllabus unit or ordered scope. Answers: **“Have I covered this?”** |
| **IFoA significance** | Official syllabi (CMP / course notes) define lawful learning order. Coverage is the spine of first-pass learning and the prerequisite for later practice and revision. Without coverage, later claims about understanding are often premature. |
| **Typical warrant** | Study Progress / lawful study-completion declarations; reading or mission completion when those *are* the defined study obligation (EIP-002 EV-01 / EV-02 coverage path). |
| **Does not prove** | Understanding, retrieval, application, durable retention, or exam readiness. |
| **Speech cue** | “You’ve covered this part of the syllabus” — never “You know this.” |

**Tutor note.** Coverage is celebrated as coverage. It is the start of the progression story, not the end.

---

### LO-02 — Familiarity

| Aspect | Definition |
|--------|------------|
| **Educational meaning** | Early recognisability after contact with material — the student has seen terms, structures, and worked examples enough that the topic no longer feels entirely foreign. Answers: **“Does this feel familiar?”** |
| **IFoA significance** | Familiarity reduces cognitive shock on return and supports motivation early in a topic. Actuarial subjects often feel alien on first reading; familiarity is a legitimate early milestone. |
| **Typical warrant** | Repeated engagement observations; soft reflection that material “looks familiar”; light recognition performance. Usually Level 1–2 at best for understanding claims. |
| **Does not prove** | Independent retrieval, correct application, or lasting command. Recognition under heavy prompting is not exam skill. |
| **Speech cue** | “This is becoming familiar” — never “You’re ready to sit this.” |

**Tutor note.** Familiarity is educationally real and educationally thin. The Coach must not dress familiarity as understanding.

---

### LO-03 — Understanding

| Aspect | Definition |
|--------|------------|
| **Educational meaning** | Provisional current comprehension of syllabus ideas — grasp of definitions, relationships, and reasoning, not merely having read them. Aligns with Estimated Knowledge / Knowledge posture (EIP-006). Answers: **“Do I currently understand this?”** |
| **IFoA significance** | IFoA assessments reward reasoned method, not rote decoration. Without understanding, practice becomes pattern-matching that collapses under novel wording. |
| **Typical warrant** | Accumulated practice/assessment observations that support comprehension (EIP-002 Levels 2–3+); lawful Twin estimates where authorised. Soft reflection may calibrate but must not author strong understanding claims alone. |
| **Does not prove** | Reliable unaided retrieval under pressure, transferable application, durable retention, or exam readiness. |
| **Speech cue** | “Evidence suggests you understand…” / “Your estimated understanding…” — never absolute “You know this” from thin history. |

**Tutor note.** Understanding is necessary and still provisional. Near-term grasp after study can fade without retrieval and return.

---

### LO-04 — Retrieval Ability

| Aspect | Definition |
|--------|------------|
| **Educational meaning** | Capacity to recall, reconstruct, and bring forward syllabus knowledge with reduced or removed prompts — closed-book, spaced, or cue-light conditions. Answers: **“Can I bring this back when I need it?”** |
| **IFoA significance** | Exams are retrieval environments. Students who only recognise answers in open notes fail when the page is blank. Retrieval practice is a core tutor tool for actuarial preparation. |
| **Typical warrant** | Performance under retrieval-emphasised conditions; spaced return success; reconstruction of methods without full worked-example scaffolding. |
| **Does not prove** | Correct application to novel problem forms, durability across long horizons, or whole-syllabus exam readiness. |
| **Speech cue** | “You can retrieve this more reliably…” — never “You’ve mastered this forever.” |

**Tutor note.** Retrieval is the bridge from “I understood it yesterday” to “I can still produce it.” It is distinct from both familiarity and application.

---

### LO-05 — Application

| Aspect | Definition |
|--------|------------|
| **Educational meaning** | Demonstrated ability to use understanding when asked to perform: solve, select, explain, or construct within attributed syllabus scope. Aligns with Competence semantic layer (EIP-006). Answers: **“Can I apply this when asked?”** |
| **IFoA significance** | Exam marks come from applying principles to questions — often multi-step, under time, with unfamiliar packaging. Application is where preparation becomes examinable skill. |
| **Typical warrant** | Question attempts with outcomes, quizzes, mocks, past-paper practice (EIP-002 EV-03–EV-06 class). Self-report alone is insufficient. Completion of a practice set without outcomes is insufficient. |
| **Does not prove** | That application will survive long spacing; that one successful set generalises to all forms; that the student is exam ready overall. |
| **Speech cue** | “Practice shows you can apply…” — never “Mission complete means you can apply.” |

**Tutor note.** Application once is hopeful. Application across forms and conditions is competence emerging. Neither is Mastery by itself.

---

### LO-06 — Durable Retention

| Aspect | Definition |
|--------|------------|
| **Educational meaning** | Sustained command that survives spacing, interruption, and return — not only immediate post-study success. Closely related to Mastery’s durability demand (EIP-006) without claiming full Estimated Mastery by label alone. Answers: **“Does this still hold after time away?”** |
| **IFoA significance** | Preparation spans months. Topics learned in week three must still be usable in week twenty and in the exam hall. Durable retention is the difference between temporary performance and lasting preparation. |
| **Typical warrant** | Accumulated competence-quality evidence over time; successful spaced returns; revision outcomes that confirm retained command. Single same-day success is never enough. |
| **Does not prove** | Whole-paper stamina, exam strategy under pressure, or readiness across unpractised neighbouring topics. |
| **Speech cue** | “This is holding up on return…” / “Estimated lasting command…” — never “Mastered” from one week’s streak. |

**Tutor note.** Durable retention is rare early. The Coach must prefer understatement until spacing evidence exists.

---

### LO-07 — Exam Readiness

| Aspect | Definition |
|--------|------------|
| **Educational meaning** | Provisional educational judgement that preparation for the named sitting is credible under honest warrant — integrating coverage posture, demonstrated application, retention, revision maturity, and remaining calendar — without prophesying pass/fail. Answers: **“Are we honestly ready for this sitting?”** |
| **IFoA significance** | Students and tutors must decide when to emphasise consolidation, mocks, and rest versus new first-pass. False exam-readiness theatre is the highest-trust failure mode in professional exam coaching. |
| **Typical warrant** | Dense accumulated evidence across LO-01–LO-06 for examinable scope; mock / exam-like performance where available; Profile feasibility and revision posture. Never calendar proximity alone. Never completion density alone. |
| **Does not prove** | A guaranteed pass. Does not erase remaining topic-level gaps that still require honesty. |
| **Speech cue** | “Preparation looks credible for…” / “We’re not yet ready to claim exam readiness because…” — never “You’re ready” from coverage percentage or days studied. |

**Tutor note.** Exam readiness is a **synthesis judgement**, not a seventh checkbox earned by finishing LO-01–LO-06 in isolation. Thin warrant yields “not yet claimable.”

## 4. Objective Relationships

```
Syllabus coverage (LO-01)
     ↓  enables but does not imply
Familiarity (LO-02)
     ↓  enables but does not imply
Understanding (LO-03)
     ↓  enables but does not imply
Retrieval ability (LO-04)
     ↓  enables but does not imply
Application (LO-05)
     ↓  enables but does not imply
Durable retention (LO-06)
     ↓  contributes to but does not alone decide
Exam readiness (LO-07)
```

Rules:

1. Later objectives may **depend on evidence that often accompanies** earlier ones; they must never be **inferred by fiat** from earlier ones.
2. A student may lawfully hold uneven profiles (e.g. strong coverage, thin retrieval; strong application on recent topics, weak retention after breaks).
3. Learning Coach progression judgements must name **which objective(s)** are advancing — never a single opaque “learning %”.
4. Profile dimensions and Knowledge/Competence/Mastery estimates remain the diagnostic and Twin-facing counterparts; these LOs are the Learning Coach’s **success vocabulary**.

---

## 5. What “Genuine Learning” Means

For Learning Coach purposes, the student is **genuinely learning** when accumulated educational evidence supports advancement on one or more of LO-03–LO-06 (understanding through durable retention), or honest progress toward LO-07, **without** mistaking LO-01/LO-02 alone for that advancement.

| Pattern | Learning Coach reading |
|---------|------------------------|
| Coverage rising, no performance evidence | Coverage progress — not yet genuine understanding progress |
| Familiarity rising after reading | Early contact progress — still thin |
| Understanding estimates improving on practice evidence | Genuine learning beginning |
| Retrieval / application improving across sessions | Genuine learning consolidating |
| Retention holding on spaced return | Genuine learning deepening |
| Exam-readiness claim with dense multi-objective warrant | Preparation maturity — still provisional |
| Many completions, flat or worsening application | Activity without genuine learning — intervention warranted |

---

## 6. Optimisation Targets for the Learning Coach

When the Learning Coach influences coaching consumers, it optimises educationally for:

1. **Honest advancement along the objective ladder** for authorised syllabus scope.
2. **Repair of the weakest material rung** that blocks further genuine learning (e.g. retrieval before more first-pass theatre).
3. **Protection against false readiness** — refuse LO-07 speech without warrant.
4. **Respect for student capacity and Profile** — genuine learning is sustainable, not heroic one-week theatre.

It does **not** optimise for:

- maximising missions completed;
- maximising minutes studied as a proxy for learning;
- maximising confidence language;
- maximising coverage ticks near the exam date as readiness theatre.
