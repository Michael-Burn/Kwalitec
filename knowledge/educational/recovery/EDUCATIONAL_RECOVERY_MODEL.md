## Authority

This document is the constitutional overview of the **Recovery Coach** for Kwalitec.

It is subordinate to the Educational Constitution and specialised educational models. It governs **how Kwalitec restores educational progress after meaningful disruption while remaining faithful to the long-term educational plan**. It does not authorise implementation shortcuts that contradict the Constitution, and it does not invent disruption, evidence, or planning meaning absent from upstream models.

Authority order for educational recovery:

> Constitution defines educational truth and curriculum primacy.  
> Knowledge & Mastery defines coverage ≠ understanding ≠ mastery.  
> Evidence Model defines what may warrant educational claims.  
> Continuity Standard preserves rightful educational history across plan change.  
> Master Planner publishes the Canonical Study Plan and owns long-term educational envelopes.  
> Daily Coach owns *what is most educationally valuable today* under that contract.  
> Learning Coach owns longitudinal progression, obstacle diagnosis, and learning interventions.  
> **This Educational Recovery Model (Workstream 4 / MS001) defines how progress is restored after meaningful disruption.**  
> Downstream coaching and narration surfaces must consume this educational reasoning — never replace planning with catch-up theatre.

## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not treat every quiet week as catastrophe — nor does the tutor pretend that prolonged absence, repeated missed sessions, or clear regression leave the journey unchanged. After meaningful disruption, the tutor answers one living educational question:

> **“How should this student recover educationally after meaningful disruption?”**

That answer must restore sustainable progress, protect long-term understanding and confidence, remain faithful to the Canonical Study Plan’s educational intent, and escalate honestly when structural replanning is required.

This document records that tutor posture so every future Recovery Coach subsystem has a single educational reference for *how recovery is reasoned*.

> **The Recovery Coach describes educational recovery reasoning.  
> It does not implement Runtime A logic, algorithms, analytics, or Twin writers.**

---

## 2. What Educational Recovery Is

**Educational recovery** is the restorative process by which Kwalitec helps a student **resume sustainable educational progress** after a **meaningful disruption** to study continuity, capacity, or demonstrated learning trail — without silently rewriting long-term educational intent.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Disruption** | Observed break or degradation of educational continuity or progress | What changed educationally? |
| **Recovery warrant** | Judgement that disruption is meaningful enough to require restorative coaching | Is recovery needed, or is this temporary fluctuation? |
| **Recovery objectives** | Educational goods recovery must pursue | What are we trying to restore? |
| **Recovery strategy** | Named restorative approach within boundaries | How should progress be restored? |
| **Resume of normal progression** | Return to ordinary Daily Coach / plan rhythm | When does recovery end? |

Recovery is:

- **restorative** — it rebuilds momentum, consistency, confidence, and safe continuity;
- **plan-faithful** — it preserves Canonical Study Plan educational intent wherever possible;
- **evidence-traceable** — recommendations cite observed disruption and accumulated evidence;
- **sustainable** — it prefers durable progress over heroic catch-up theatre;
- **non-punitive** — disruption is not moral failure;
- **escalation-aware** — structural change goes through Master Planner / rescheduling pathways;
- **explainable** — students understand why recovery exists and when it should end.

Recovery is **not**:

- a substitute for Master Planner planning or strategy selection;
- a licence to invent overload to “catch up” in one burst;
- inference of mastery from rest, resumed ticks, or backlog clearance;
- a bypass of Daily Coach day authority or Learning Coach evidence/diagnosis layers;
- a second Educational Continuity Standard (EIP-005 owns history persistence; this Model owns restorative coaching meaning);
- a claim that recovery guarantees a pass.

## 3. What the Recovery Coach Is

The **Recovery Coach** is the educational capability that, given **observed educational disruption** and **accumulated educational evidence**, produces a **recovery warrant**, selects **restorative strategies** aligned to **recovery objectives**, states **boundary posture** (local restoration vs escalation), and produces **recovery explainability**.

| Input family | Role |
|--------------|------|
| **Observed disruption** | Absence, missed sessions, availability shock, interrupted plan, regression signal, or related trigger class |
| **Accumulated Educational Evidence** | Trail that distinguishes fluctuation from meaningful disruption (EIP-002) |
| **Canonical Study Plan posture** | Active / Adapted / Recovered contract — consumed, not rewritten |
| **Student Educational Profile** | Capacity, reliability, recovery posture, planning constraints |
| **Daily Coach context** | Today’s lawful day-priority authority — informed, never bypassed |
| **Learning Coach meaning** | Progression / obstacle / intervention evidence — consumed honestly, never invented |

into:

| Output family | Role |
|---------------|------|
| **Recovery warrant** | Whether recovery is educationally justified |
| **Recovery objectives in focus** | Which RO-XX goods this episode prioritises |
| **Named recovery strategy(ies)** | Restorative approach(es) from the strategy catalogue |
| **Boundary posture** | Local restoration vs escalation to reschedule / replan |
| **Resume criterion** | Educational cues for ending recovery posture |
| **Recovery explainability** | Plain-language account for the student |

It answers:

| Question | Educational answer |
|----------|-------------------|
| Is recovery needed? | Warrant from meaningful disruption — not from every missed tick |
| What should recovery restore? | Momentum, consistency, understanding protection, regression minimisation, confidence |
| How should we restore it? | Named restorative strategy within plan envelopes |
| May Recovery Coach enact this alone? | Only within boundaries; else escalate |
| When does normal progression resume? | When continuity and sustainable rhythm return under the authorised plan |

The Recovery Coach is:

- **disruption-gated** — refuses recovery theatre for temporary fluctuation;
- **objective-bound** — pursues defined recovery educational goods;
- **strategy-specific** — names restorative approaches rather than vague “catch up”;
- **boundary-faithful** — protects plan, strategy, Daily Coach, Learning Coach, and mastery authorities;
- **evidence-honest** — never mints understanding from rest or backlog clearance;
- **continuity-respectful** — works with EIP-005 history persistence and Recovered plan posture;
- **explainable** — supportive, non-shaming language;
- **non-scoring** — this Model defines meaning, not numeric recovery metrics.

The Recovery Coach is **not**:

- a second Master Planner, Scheduling Engine, Strategy selector, or Profile diagnostician;
- a second Daily Coach or Learning Coach;
- ownership of Educational Evidence recording or Twin write authority;
- a UI surface, analytics dashboard, or scoring engine;
- a licence to punish absence with invented intensity.

## 4. Educational Purpose

The Recovery Coach exists so that:

1. **Disruption is met with educational wisdom** — neither denial nor panic catch-up.
2. **Sustainable progress is preferred** over theatrical backlog clearance that burns the student out.
3. **Long-term educational intent survives interruption** — recovery repairs continuity; it does not invent a new philosophy.
4. **Confidence is protected** — students who return after absence are coached, not shamed.
5. **Authority stays layered** — restorative coaching does not silently become replanning.
6. **Students understand the arc** — why recovery, what disruption, how it helps long-term success, when normal progression resumes.

---

## 5. Core Responsibilities

The Recovery Coach is educationally responsible for:

| Responsibility | Meaning |
|----------------|---------|
| **Recognise meaningful disruption** | Separate temporary fluctuation from recovery-worthy breaks |
| **State recovery warrant** | Explicit educational justification for restorative posture |
| **Pursue recovery objectives** | Optimise sustainable restoration goods (RO-01…RO-05) |
| **Select restorative strategies** | Name approaches that preserve educational intent |
| **Bound Recovery Coach authority** | Separate local restoration from Master Planner escalation |
| **Respect sibling coaches** | Inform Daily Coach; consume Learning Coach evidence; never bypass either |
| **Define resume of normal progression** | Say when recovery posture should end |
| **Explain supportively** | Make recovery speakable without shame or false certainty |

It is **not** educationally responsible for:

- rewriting Canonical Study Plans or educational strategy;
- deciding today’s Daily Coach primary objective;
- diagnosing learning obstacles or selecting Learning Interventions (Learning Coach owns those meanings);
- inferring or redefining mastery;
- writing Twin estimates by itself;
- packing calendars or inventing intensity (Scheduling / Master Planner ownership);
- producing opaque “recovery scores” as educational proof.

---

## 6. Binding Educational Doctrine

1. **Recovery restores progress; it does not replace planning.** Catch-up theatre that rewrites long-term intent is unlawful Recovery Coach behaviour.
2. **Recovery is restorative, not corrective planning.** Local restoration repairs continuity inside envelopes; structural change escalates.
3. **Meaningful disruption ≠ temporary fluctuation.** Missed evenings and quiet weeks do not automatically warrant a recovery episode.
4. **Traceability is mandatory.** Every material recovery recommendation must cite observed disruption and accumulated evidence.
5. **Sustainable progress beats heroic catch-up.** Prefer gradual reintegration and protected understanding over punishment volume.
6. **Mastery is never inferred from recovery.** Rest, resumed ticks, and backlog clearance do not mint understanding or exam readiness (EIP-006).
7. **Daily Coach authority is preserved.** Recovery informs today’s emphasis; it does not commandeer day-priority selection.
8. **Learning Coach evidence is preserved.** Recovery does not invent progression, obstacle, or intervention meaning.
9. **Canonical Study Plan remains the contract.** Recovery may recommend adaptations that fit Recovered / Adapted posture; it may not independently rewrite the plan.
10. **Explainability is mandatory.** Silent recovery theatre and shaming language are forbidden (EIP-003).

## 7. Position Relative to Sibling Authorities

| Authority | Owns | Recovery Coach relationship |
|-----------|------|-----------------------------|
| **Master Planner / Canonical Study Plan** | Long-term educational envelopes, strategy, published contract | Consumes; escalates for structural change; never rewrites independently |
| **Scheduling / Rescheduling** | Allocation and authorised recovery capacity placement | Escalates when disruption requires placement change |
| **Study Plan Lifecycle (Recovered)** | Plan-state meaning when recovery capacity is engaged | Aligns recovery posture with SPL-05; does not invent Recovered by fiat |
| **Daily Coach** | Today’s educational priority under the plan | Informs emphasis; never bypasses |
| **Learning Coach** | Progression, obstacles, learning interventions | Consumes evidence and diagnoses; does not replace obstacle/intervention layers |
| **Educational Continuity (EIP-005)** | Persistence of learner educational history | Respects continuity; recovery coaching ≠ history inventing |
| **Knowledge & Mastery (EIP-006)** | Ladder meanings | Never collapses recovery into mastery speech |

```
Meaningful disruption observed
        │
        ▼
Recovery Coach (this Model)
        │  warrant + objectives + strategies + boundaries
        │
        ├─ local restoration (within envelopes)
        │     → informs Daily Coach emphasis & session design
        │     → may align with plan Recovered posture already published
        │
        └─ structural need
              → Rescheduling (MS006) / Master Planner replan pathways
              → may yield Adapted / Recovered / new Canonical Study Plan
              → Daily Coach then coaches under the updated contract
```

## 8. Relationship to Daily Coach “Recovery”

Daily Coach already recognises **authorised lighter-load coaching** when the Canonical Study Plan / rescheduling posture has engaged recovery capacity (see `DAILY_COACH_DECISION_MODEL.md` §6).

| Layer | Question | Owner |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Plan / scheduling recovery capacity** | Has authorised lighter load been placed? | Master Planner / Scheduling → lifecycle Recovered |
| **Daily recovery coaching** | What is today’s objective under that posture? | Daily Coach |
| **Educational Recovery Model** | How should progress be restored after meaningful disruption — which objectives, strategies, boundaries, and explanations? | **Recovery Coach (this corpus)** |

The Recovery Coach supplies the **constitutional educational meaning** of recovery as a restorative journey.  
The Daily Coach **executes today’s priority** under the plan, including when that priority is recovery-light work.  
Neither layer may invent punishment catch-up or rewrite long-term envelopes alone.

---

## 9. Relationship to Learning Coach

| Situation | Primary coach | Recovery Coach role |
|-----------|---------------|---------------------|
| Learning stalls despite continuous study | Learning Coach (obstacle → intervention) | May note confidence / momentum effects; does not replace diagnosis |
| Prolonged absence then return | Recovery Coach | Restores continuity; Learning Coach later judges whether resumed sittings produce growth |
| Regression after disruption | Both, layered | Recovery addresses restorative continuity; Learning Coach addresses whether evidence shows learning decay vs disruption artefact |
| Obstacle diagnosed during recovery | Learning Coach owns diagnosis / LI | Recovery Coach must not invent a competing “cure” that bypasses diagnosis |

Hard rule:

> **Recovery Coach must not bypass Learning Coach evidence.  
> Restoring attendance is not proof that learning progressed.**
