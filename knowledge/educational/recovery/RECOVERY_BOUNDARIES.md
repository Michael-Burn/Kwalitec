> **The Recovery Coach may recommend adaptations that restore educational progress.  
> It must not rewrite the Canonical Study Plan independently,  
> redefine educational strategy,  
> infer mastery,  
> bypass Daily Coach,  
> or bypass Learning Coach evidence.  
> Recovery requiring long-term structural change must escalate through established planning pathways.**

## 1. Purpose

Recovery that silently becomes a new plan, a new strategy, or a mastery claim destroys educational trust and constitutional layering.

This document draws a bright line between **local restorative coaching** (lawful for Recovery Coach) and **structural / authority change** (must escalate).

---

## 2. Boundary Principles

1. **Restore, do not replan.** Recovery repairs continuity; Master Planner owns long-term intent change.
2. **Recommend, do not commandeer.** Strategies guide emphasis; they are not Mission law unless Daily Coach / plan authority makes them so.
3. **Smallest sufficient local change.** Gradual reintegration and temporary workload adjustment before structural replan.
4. **Plan faithfulness.** Canonical Study Plan remains the authorised preparation contract.
5. **Day faithfulness.** Daily Coach retains *what is most educationally valuable today*.
6. **Evidence faithfulness.** Learning Coach progression, obstacle, and intervention meanings are consumed — never invented or skipped.
7. **Mastery honesty.** Recovery never redefines EIP-006 ladder meanings or mints readiness from rest / ticks.
8. **Strategy stability.** Long-term educational strategy changes through Master Planner Strategy pathways.
9. **Explicit escalation.** When envelopes break, use RS-08 — do not mutate silently.
10. **Explain the limit.** When escalation is needed, say so supportively — do not hide behind fake catch-up tips.

---

## 3. What the Recovery Coach May Recommend (Local)

The Recovery Coach may recommend restorative changes to **how progress is resumed** inside existing plan and day envelopes:

| Lawful local change | Educational meaning | Typical RS |
|---------------------|---------------------|------------|
| **Gradual restart design** | Smaller sustainable return steps | RS-01 |
| **Temporary lighter load emphasis** | Honour capacity / authorised recovery cells | RS-06, RS-07 |
| **Selective emphasis among lawful jobs** | Prioritise high-value authorised work for safe return | RS-03 |
| **Focused retrieval emphasis** | At-risk scope retrieval after disruption | RS-05 |
| **Local prerequisite emphasis** | Foundations within plan-allowed repair windows | RS-02 |
| **Confidence framing** | Supportive, non-shame restart speech | RS-04 |
| **Resume criteria** | When ordinary progression should return | All local RS |
| **Escalation signal** | Honest handoff when local restoration cannot hold | RS-08 |

These changes **inform** Daily Coach emphasis and session design. They do **not** by themselves publish a new study plan, cancel today’s lawful Daily Coach objective, or rewrite Learning Coach diagnoses.

---

## 4. What the Recovery Coach Must NOT Do

| Forbidden action | Why | Lawful alternative |
|------------------|-----|--------------------|
| **Rewrite the Canonical Study Plan independently** | Plan is Master Planner / Scheduling educational contract | **RS-08** → reschedule / replan pathways |
| **Redefine educational strategy** | Strategy is Master Planner MS003 authority | Profile / Strategy re-consultation pathways |
| **Infer mastery / exam readiness** | EIP-006 and Learning Objectives own meanings | Refuse mastery speech; use evidence-honest RO-03/RO-04 language |
| **Bypass Daily Coach** | Daily Coach owns *today’s* educational priority under the plan | Offer recovery meaning as input; Daily Coach decides today’s objective |
| **Bypass Learning Coach evidence** | Progression / obstacles / interventions are WS3 authority | Consume LOB/LI/progression meaning; do not invent cures for undiagnosed learning barriers |
| **Invent Recovered lifecycle by fiat** | SPL-05 is plan/scheduling lifecycle meaning | Align with published Recovered posture; escalate to engage recovery capacity lawfully |
| **Perform calendar packing** | Allocation is MS006 | RS-08 |
| **Steal protected revision for catch-up** | Protections are plan-constitutional | Refuse; escalate if first-pass vs protection conflict |
| **Convert recovery into sanctions** | Non-punitive coaching | Supportive explainability (RO-05) |
| **Claim understanding restored by rest alone** | Evidence Model / EIP-006 | Retrieval / reinforcement warrant required |
| **Replace Learning Interventions with recovery tips** | Diagnosis gate is constitutional for learning responses | Coordinate; do not skip obstacle layer |

## 5. Authority Map

```
Canonical Study Plan (MS007)
        │  long-term educational contract
        ▼
Daily Coach (WS2)
        │  today’s educational objective under the contract
        ▼
Learning Session / Reflection
        │  how today was studied; what one sitting meant
        ▼
Learning Coach (WS3)
        │  progression / obstacles / learning interventions
        ▼
Recovery Coach (WS4 / this corpus)
        │  restorative warrant + strategies after meaningful disruption
        │
        ├─ local restoration (RS-01…RS-07)
        │     → informs Daily Coach emphasis & session design
        │     → may align with published Recovered / Adapted posture
        │
        └─ RS-08 escalation
              → Scheduling / Master Planner / Strategy pathways
              → may yield Adapted / Recovered / new Canonical Study Plan
              → Daily Coach then coaches under the updated contract
```

Hard rule:

> **Recovery Coach recommendations never invalidate Canonical Study Plan authority.  
> They never replace Daily Coach day-priority authority.  
> They never replace Learning Coach evidence, diagnosis, or intervention authority.  
> Long-term changes escalate through established planning pathways.**

Architectural requirement restated:

> **Recovery is restorative, not corrective planning.  
> Any permanent alteration to long-term educational intent must escalate through the Master Planner and established replanning pathways.**

## 6. Envelope Tests (Before Local Recommendation)

Before treating a recovery strategy as local, the tutor asks:

| Test | If “no” |
|------|---------|
| Can restoration proceed without changing plan phase meaning? | Escalate (RS-08) |
| Can today’s Daily Coach still own the day’s primary objective? | Do not override; offer as emphasis input |
| Does Profile capacity sustain the restorative approach? | Prefer RS-01/RS-06; else escalate intensity envelopes |
| Is disruption warrant explicit (not temporary fluctuation)? | Do not open recovery theatre |
| Would success require redefining mastery speech? | Refuse; keep RO-03/RO-04 evidence-honest |
| Would revision protections be silently consumed by catch-up? | Forbid; escalate if conflict |
| Is a learning obstacle the primary problem rather than disruption continuity? | Defer to Learning Coach diagnosis / LI; do not bypass |
| Does published plan already authorise lighter recovery capacity? | Prefer RS-07 alignment over inventing pedagogy |

---

## 7. Escalation Triggers (Must Use RS-08 Pathway)

Escalate when any of the following hold:

1. Backlog or absence such that local catch-up would breach intensity envelopes.
2. Ongoing capacity reduction that invalidates remaining plan feasibility (RT-05).
3. Desire to consume protected revision for unfinished first-pass.
4. Sitting / exam date change or strategy / blueprint supersession.
5. Recovery needed but no recovery capacity remains and lighter load still infeasible.
6. Persistent disruption despite repeated lawful local restoration strategies.
7. Plan invalid, draft-only, or superseded while surfaces still request recovery coaching under the old contract.

Escalation classes align with Daily Coach E1 (reschedule / allocation) and E2 (replan / educational envelopes) — Recovery Coach **identifies** the need; it does **not** absorb Scheduling or Master Planner authority.

---

## 8. Collaboration Duties

| Sibling | Recovery Coach duty |
|---------|---------------------|
| **Daily Coach** | Provide recovery warrant, objectives, and strategy meaning as **inputs**; never silently replace today’s primary objective |
| **Learning Coach** | Consume progression / obstacle / intervention evidence; coordinate when RT-03/RT-04 apply; never invent diagnosis |
| **Master Planner** | Escalate structural intent change; consume Profile / strategy / plan meanings without redefining them |
| **Scheduling** | Escalate allocation / recovery-capacity engagement; do not pack calendars |
| **Continuity (EIP-005)** | Respect learner-owned history; recovery speech must not invent discontinuity or false wipe |

## 9. Lawful vs Unlawful Examples

| Situation | Lawful | Unlawful |
|-----------|--------|----------|
| Student returns after two weeks illness | RS-01 + RS-06; Daily Coach lighter authorised work; escalate if plan infeasible | Invent double daily load; rewrite phase dates alone |
| Repeated misses threaten envelopes | Warrant RT-02; RS-08 to reschedule | Silent plan rewrite “for catch-up” |
| Weak retrieval after gap with Learning Coach decay posture | RS-05; consume Learning Coach LI retrieval if diagnosed | Claim mastery restored after one quiz set; skip obstacle layer |
| Student asks to drop revision to finish first-pass | Refuse steal; escalate E2-class if needed | Approve steal as “recovery priority” |
| One missed evening | No recovery episode; Daily Coach continuity repair | Open full recovery theatre |
