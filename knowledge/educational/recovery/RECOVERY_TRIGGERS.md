## 1. Purpose

If recovery fires too early, students experience panic coaching and invented catch-up.  
If recovery fires too late, continuity and confidence erode without support.

This document draws a bright line between **temporary fluctuation** (ordinary life noise under an intact journey) and **meaningful disruption** (warrant for Recovery Coach restorative posture).

---

## 2. Core Distinction

| Class | Meaning | Recovery Coach posture |
|-------|---------|------------------------|
| **Temporary fluctuation** | Short, explainable variation that does not threaten sustainable progress or plan integrity | **No recovery episode** — Daily Coach continuity repair / ordinary adaptation |
| **Meaningful disruption** | Break or degradation that threatens momentum, consistency, understanding protection, or plan feasibility | **Recovery warrant** — select objectives and strategies; escalate if structural |

Binding principle:

> **Recovery is disruption-gated.  
> Absence of ticks alone is not educational proof of disruption severity.**

---

## 3. Temporary Fluctuation (Not Recovery)

Treat as temporary fluctuation — **do not** open a Recovery Coach episode — when all of the following hold:

1. Gap or variation is short relative to the student’s established rhythm and Profile capacity.
2. The Canonical Study Plan remains feasible without stealing protections or inventing intensity.
3. There is no significant evidence of learning regression on at-risk scope.
4. The student can resume the next lawful plan job without shame-driven overload.
5. No major lasting change in study availability has invalidated remaining envelopes.

Examples (illustrative, not exhaustive):

- One missed evening in an otherwise consistent week
- A short session due to a late meeting
- A planned leave day already reflected in the plan
- Slightly lower energy on a single day with capacity still workable
- Preference to swap emphasis within today’s Daily Coach envelopes

Lawful response without Recovery Coach episode:

- Daily Coach continuity repair (resume next lawful job)
- Local scope reduction within envelopes
- Ordinary encouragement without recovery theatre

## 4. Meaningful Disruption Trigger Catalogue

Each trigger class below may warrant recovery when evidence and context support it. Triggers may co-occur.

### RT-01 — Prolonged absence

**Definition.** An extended break from study (illness, emergency, travel, life event) such that ordinary “resume next session” continuity is insufficient to restore sustainable progress.

**Typical cues:**

- Multi-day or multi-week gap relative to the student’s planned rhythm
- Explicit absence signal (illness / leave) already recognised in planning / rescheduling families where present
- Student returning after a gap that would make immediate full-intensity resume unsafe or unrealistic

**Recovery emphasis:** RO-01, RO-05 first; then RO-02; RO-03/RO-04 as evidence warrants.

**Must not:** Treat a single planned rest day as prolonged absence.

---

### RT-02 — Repeated missed sessions

**Definition.** A pattern of missed authorised sessions that clusters enough to threaten consistency and plan continuity — not a single miss.

**Typical cues:**

- Recurring gaps across successive days or weeks
- Backlog growing such that one-day catch-up would breach intensity envelopes
- Consistency (RO-02) clearly degraded relative to Profile / plan expectations

**Recovery emphasis:** RO-02, RO-01; escalate if backlog cannot fit envelopes honestly.

**Must not:** Open recovery for two non-consecutive misses in an otherwise healthy month without further warrant.

---

### RT-03 — Persistent learning obstacles during / after disruption

**Definition.** Learning Coach–recognised obstacles that persist or intensify in the context of disrupted study life — where restorative continuity is part of making learning responses workable again.

**Typical cues:**

- Explicit obstacle diagnosis (or insufficient-warrant gather posture) from Learning Coach
- Recovery needed so the student can actually attempt lawful interventions
- Disruption and obstacle co-occur (e.g. return after absence plus fragile foundations)

**Recovery emphasis:** Coordinate with Learning Coach — Recovery restores conditions; Learning Coach owns diagnosis / intervention meaning.

**Must not:** Invent an obstacle to justify recovery; bypass Learning Coach evidence.

---

### RT-04 — Significant evidence of regression

**Definition.** Accumulated educational evidence indicates meaningful slip on previously progressing scope — attributable in whole or part to disruption — not thin single-session noise.

**Typical cues:**

- Repeated weak retrieval / application on recently strong areas after a gap
- Reflection and session trail consistently show decay patterns
- Learning Coach progression posture indicates decay / inconsistency with disruption context

**Recovery emphasis:** RO-04, RO-03; strategies favour focused retrieval and prerequisite reinforcement.

**Must not:** Infer regression from missed calendar cells alone; claim mastery loss from absence without evidence.

---

### RT-05 — Major changes in study availability

**Definition.** Sustained shift in available study time, energy, or life constraints that makes the remaining Canonical Study Plan intensity or placement unrealistic under honest Profile capacity.

**Typical cues:**

- Job change, caring responsibilities, health capacity change lasting beyond a brief shock
- Profile capacity no longer matches remaining sitting ambition under current envelopes
- Daily Coach repeatedly finds today’s plan job infeasible even after local scope reduction

**Recovery emphasis:** Local temporary workload adjustment if envelopes allow; **escalate** when feasibility is broken (structural).

**Must not:** Treat a single busy day as “major availability change.”

---

### RT-06 — Interrupted study plans

**Definition.** The authorised preparation journey is interrupted — plan paused, superseded mid-journey, recovery capacity engaged, or lived divergence such that continuity coaching alone cannot hold educational intent.

**Typical cues:**

- Lifecycle movement toward Adapted / Recovered for disruption reasons
- Plan interruption requiring continuity repair beyond ordinary Daily Coach adaptation
- Rescheduling / replan pathways already signalled by Daily Coach escalation classes

**Recovery emphasis:** Align with published plan posture; escalate rather than invent a parallel plan.

**Must not:** Independently mark a plan Recovered or rewrite envelopes to create interruption theatre.

---

## 5. Warrant Composition

A lawful **recovery warrant** states:

| Element | Requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| **Trigger class(es)** | One or more RT-01…RT-06 with educational justification |
| **Observed disruption** | What changed in study life / plan continuity |
| **Evidence basis** | Accumulated evidence or explicit thin-warrant honesty |
| **Fluctuation rejected** | Why this is not temporary fluctuation (§3) |
| **Objectives in focus** | Which RO-XX goods this episode prioritises |
| **Boundary hint** | Likely local restoration vs escalation |

A recovery recommendation without a warrant is educationally invalid.

---

## 6. Evidence Honesty Rules

1. **Disruption observations** (missed sessions, absence, availability change) are facts about study life — not mastery claims.
2. **Regression claims** require Educational Evidence warrant (EIP-002) — never calendar inference alone.
3. **Thin history** after return may justify gentle recovery (RO-01/RO-05) without claiming RT-04 regression.
4. **Learning Coach evidence** must be consumed when present; Recovery Coach must not invent progression or obstacle meaning.
5. **Correct uncertainty beats false crisis** — if warrant is unclear, prefer continuity repair and evidence gathering over dramatic recovery speech.

---

## 7. Interaction with Daily Coach Escalation

| Daily Coach situation | Recovery Coach reading |
|-----------------------|------------------------|
| Single miss; resume next job | Temporary fluctuation — no episode |
| Plan already **Recovered** / SPC-05 day | Recovery posture may already be authorised at plan level; Recovery Coach supplies episode meaning if disruption warrant exists |
| Backlog threatens envelopes → E1/E2 | Meaningful disruption likely; Recovery Coach may warrant + **must escalate** for structural placement / envelope change |
| Student depleted; no recovery capacity left | Trigger family RT-01/RT-05 may apply; escalate rather than invent pedagogy |

---

## 8. Ending Triggers (Resume of Normal Progression)

Recovery posture should **end** when:

1. Sustainable momentum and consistency have returned relative to Profile capacity (RO-01, RO-02).
2. The Canonical Study Plan (or Adapted / Recovered successor still within envelopes) can again be coached as ordinary Active-class rhythm.
3. No active RT-04 regression warrant remains unaddressed — or Learning Coach has taken ownership of remaining learning response.
4. Explainability can honestly say normal progression has resumed.

Ending recovery is an educational judgement — not a streak counter reset.
