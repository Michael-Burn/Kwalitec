## 1. Purpose

Without named strategies, recovery collapses into vague “catch up” advice.  
With named strategies, Recovery Coach recommendations remain educationally specific, objective-bound, and boundary-aware.

---

## 2. Strategy Principles

1. **Warrant first.** No strategy without a recovery warrant (`RECOVERY_TRIGGERS.md`).
2. **Objective-bound.** Each strategy declares primary RO-XX goods.
3. **Smallest sufficient restoration.** Prefer gentle reintegration before structural replan.
4. **Plan faithfulness.** Preserve phase meaning, protections, and sitting intent where envelopes allow.
5. **Non-punitive.** Strategies rebuild; they do not sanction.
6. **Evidence-honest.** Strategies never mint mastery.
7. **Sibling-respectful.** Inform Daily Coach; consume Learning Coach meaning; escalate when envelopes break.
8. **Explainable.** Every strategy is speakable under `RECOVERY_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.

## 3. Strategy Catalogue

### RS-01 — Gradual reintegration

**Educational meaning.** Resume study in deliberately smaller, sustainable steps so momentum returns without shock overload.

**Primary objectives:** RO-01, RO-02, RO-05  
**Typical triggers:** RT-01, RT-02, RT-06  

**Tutor posture:**

- Start with lawful lighter or shorter work already compatible with plan envelopes / Recovered posture.
- Increase toward ordinary plan rhythm as consistency returns.
- Prefer several sustainable sittings over one heroic day.

**Preserves intent by:** Keeping the student on the authorised journey’s educational direction without inventing a parallel syllabus path.

**Must not:**

- Double daily intensity to “make up” the absence window.
- Skip protected revision permanently “until caught up.”

**Expected improvement cues:**

- Sessions resume and stick.
- Student reports (and trail shows) workable restart without collapse.

---

### RS-02 — Prerequisite reinforcement

**Educational meaning.** Strengthen earlier foundations that disruption may have left fragile, before pushing new first-pass ambition.

**Primary objectives:** RO-03, RO-04  
**Typical triggers:** RT-01, RT-04, RT-03 (with Learning Coach diagnosis)  

**Tutor posture:**

- Emphasise foundations *within* plan-allowed repair windows.
- Coordinate with Learning Coach prerequisite / reinforcement interventions when an obstacle is diagnosed.
- Keep coverage theatre from advancing while foundations are unsafe.

**Preserves intent by:** Protecting the plan’s assumption that later topics rest on earlier understanding.

**Must not:**

- Invent unofficial syllabus shortcuts.
- Rewrite phase order by Recovery Coach fiat.

**Expected improvement cues:**

- Stronger retrieval / application on named foundation scope.
- Learning Coach evidence trail improves on prerequisite-related obstacles.

---

### RS-03 — Selective reprioritisation

**Educational meaning.** Temporarily emphasise the highest-value authorised educational jobs for safe return — without claiming authority to redesign the whole journey.

**Primary objectives:** RO-01, RO-03  
**Typical triggers:** RT-02, RT-05, RT-06  

**Tutor posture:**

- Prefer plan-authorised sessions that restore continuity and understanding protection.
- Defer lower-urgency optional enrichment until rhythm returns.
- If true educational envelopes must change, **escalate** — do not silently reprioritise the contract.

**Preserves intent by:** Choosing among lawful jobs already present in the plan / Daily Coach authority space.

**Must not:**

- Invent a new strategy (e.g. switch to exam-only cramming) as “reprioritisation.”
- Steal protected revision to clear first-pass backlog under the guise of priority.

**Expected improvement cues:**

- Student works high-value lawful jobs consistently.
- Escalation occurs promptly when selective emphasis is insufficient.

---

### RS-04 — Confidence rebuilding

**Educational meaning.** Reconstruct the student’s belief that return is possible through supportive framing, achievable next steps, and honest non-shame speech.

**Primary objectives:** RO-05, RO-01  
**Typical triggers:** RT-01, RT-02, RT-04 (when confidence is the blocker)  

**Tutor posture:**

- Lead with intentional recovery, not moral deficit.
- Pair encouragement with a concrete lawful next job.
- Align with Learning Coach confidence-calibration interventions when over/under-confidence is diagnosed — without replacing that layer.

**Preserves intent by:** Keeping the student engaged with the authorised journey instead of abandoning it.

**Must not:**

- Empty pep talk without educational next step.
- Comparison theatre or “you’re behind everyone.”

**Expected improvement cues:**

- Student re-engages with a lawful session.
- Shame language disappears from the coaching dialogue.

---

### RS-05 — Focused retrieval

**Educational meaning.** Use cue-light production practice on at-risk scope to minimise regression and re-anchor durable knowledge after a gap.

**Primary objectives:** RO-04, RO-03  
**Typical triggers:** RT-04, RT-01 (after meaningful absence)  

**Tutor posture:**

- Target retrieval where evidence suggests slip — not random quiz theatre across the whole syllabus.
- Prefer quality of retrieval design over volume of questions.
- Consume Learning Coach retrieval interventions when obstacle diagnosis warrants them.

**Preserves intent by:** Protecting retention commitments the plan already treats as educational goods.

**Must not:**

- Claim mastery restored because a retrieval set was completed.
- Replace all plan jobs with endless quiz mode absent warrant.

**Expected improvement cues:**

- Improved unaided retrieval / application on named at-risk scope.
- Regression warrant (RT-04) softens under honest evidence.

---

### RS-06 — Temporary workload adjustment

**Educational meaning.** Reduce near-term study load intensity or session ambition temporarily so recovery is sustainable — inside authorised envelopes or via escalation when envelopes must change.

**Primary objectives:** RO-01, RO-02, RO-05  
**Typical triggers:** RT-01, RT-05, RT-06  

**Tutor posture:**

- Honour Profile capacity and any Recovered / SPC-05 lighter-load cells already published.
- Prefer temporary adjustment with an explicit resume criterion.
- If remaining journey cannot fit after temporary adjustment, escalate to rescheduling / replan.

**Preserves intent by:** Keeping the student on a feasible path toward the same educational destination rather than burning out and quitting.

**Must not:**

- Permanently shrink sitting ambition by Recovery Coach fiat.
- Present temporary lighter load as proof the student “can’t handle” the syllabus.

**Expected improvement cues:**

- Sustainable sittings return.
- Workload can step back toward ordinary plan intensity without collapse.

---

### RS-07 — Continuity alignment with authorised recovery capacity

**Educational meaning.** Align coaching with recovery capacity already placed by Scheduling / plan lifecycle (Recovered posture), ensuring Daily Coach and Recovery Coach speak the same restorative story.

**Primary objectives:** RO-01, RO-05  
**Typical triggers:** RT-06, RT-01 (when RD / recovery capacity engaged)  

**Tutor posture:**

- Treat published recovery cells / Recovered posture as first-class educational truth.
- Refuse catch-up doubles on recovery-authorised days.
- Explain recovery as intentional plan behaviour.

**Preserves intent by:** Executing the plan’s own recovery design rather than inventing a rival pedagogy.

**Must not:**

- Invent recovery capacity the plan does not authorise.
- Convert recovery cells into hidden first-pass overload.

**Expected improvement cues:**

- Student completes authorised lighter work without shame.
- Journey remains continuous under the same plan identity where lifecycle allows.

---

### RS-08 — Escalation for structural restoration

**Educational meaning.** When local strategies cannot restore progress inside current envelopes, escalate to Rescheduling / Master Planner pathways so long-term intent can be re-authorised honestly.

**Primary objectives:** RO-02, RO-03 (via lawful structural repair)  
**Typical triggers:** RT-02, RT-05, RT-06 when envelopes break  

**Tutor posture:**

- State clearly that local recovery is insufficient.
- Hand structural change to Scheduling (allocation) or Master Planner (educational envelopes / strategy).
- Continue supportive RO-05 speech during escalation — escalation is not abandonment.

**Preserves intent by:** Refusing fake local “fixes” that would silently rewrite the contract.

**Must not:**

- Perform calendar packing or strategy redesign inside Recovery Coach.
- Hide the need for replan behind temporary tips.

**Expected improvement cues:**

- Adapted / Recovered / new Canonical Study Plan becomes coaching authority.
- Daily Coach resumes under the updated contract.

## 4. Selection Guidance

When multiple strategies fit, prefer this order:

1. **RS-04** if confidence blocks any return  
2. **RS-01** / **RS-06** / **RS-07** to restore safe motion under capacity  
3. **RS-05** / **RS-02** when evidence warrants regression or foundation risk  
4. **RS-03** for temporary emphasis among lawful jobs  
5. **RS-08** as soon as envelopes cannot honestly hold restoration  

Strategies may combine (e.g. RS-01 + RS-05) when warrant supports both — provided boundaries remain intact.

---

## 5. Relationship to Learning Interventions

| Recovery strategy | Learning Intervention relationship |
|-------------------|-----------------------------------|
| RS-02, RS-05 | Often **complement** LI prerequisite / retrieval / reinforcement families — Recovery owns restorative continuity; Learning Coach owns obstacle-gated intervention meaning |
| RS-04 | May align with confidence-calibration LIs without replacing diagnosis |
| RS-01, RS-06, RS-07 | Primarily continuity / capacity restoration — not substitutes for obstacle diagnosis |
| RS-08 | Parallel to LI-12-style escalation — structural pathways, not tip theatre |

Hard rule:

> **A recovery strategy must not be used to bypass an explicit Learning Coach diagnosis gate when the educational problem is a learning obstacle rather than disruption continuity.**

---

## 6. Anti-Strategies (Forbidden)

- Punishment catch-up / double-speed backlog clearance as the primary “strategy”
- Silent consumption of protected revision for first-pass theatre
- Strategy redesign (e.g. sudden mock-only path) without Master Planner authority
- Mastery speech from rest or resumed ticks
- Shame-first restart protocols
- Ignoring published Recovered posture to invent a harsher local plan
