## 1. Purpose

Examination preparation that silently becomes first learning, consolidating revision, recovery catch-up, plan rewrite, or mastery theatre destroys educational trust and constitutional layering.

This document draws a bright line between **lawful assessment-facing coaching** (Exam Coach) and **sibling / structural authority** (must not absorb).

---

## 2. Boundary Principles

1. **Prepare for assessment, do not replace first learning.** Exam Coach orients accumulated understanding toward the sitting; it does not teach absent syllabus content under an exam-prep label.
2. **Prepare for assessment, do not replace revision.** Consolidating previously learned material remains Revision Coach territory.
3. **Prepare for assessment, do not recover by disguise.** Meaningful disruption remains Recovery Coach territory.
4. **Recommend emphasis, do not commandeer the day.** Daily Coach retains *what is most educationally valuable today*.
5. **Consume evidence, do not override it.** Learning Coach progression, obstacle, and intervention meanings are binding inputs — never rewritten by Exam Coach fiat.
6. **Consume sibling coach outputs, do not redefine them.** Learning, Recovery, and Revision meanings are consumed honestly.
7. **Plan faithfulness.** Canonical Study Plan remains the authorised preparation contract, including examination-facing windows.
8. **Mastery honesty.** Exam Coach never redefines EIP-006 ladder meanings or mints exam certainty from mocks or volume.
9. **Strategy reinforcement, not strategy rewrite.** Exam Coach may reinforce examination approach consistent with authorised educational strategy; long-term strategy changes through Master Planner Strategy pathways.
10. **Smallest sufficient assessment-facing change.** Prefer targeted emphasis inside envelopes over structural replan.
11. **Explicit handoff.** When the true need is first learning, revision, recovery, or structural change, say so — do not fake exam tips.
12. **Explain the limit.** Students should hear when advice is examination preparation versus when another coach or planner must act.

## 3. What the Exam Coach May Do (Lawful)

The Exam Coach may:

| Lawful action | Educational meaning |
|---------------|---------------------|
| **Recommend examination preparation** | Name how the learner should prepare for and approach the examination now, and why |
| **Coordinate with Daily Coach** | Provide examination warrant, objectives, and priority meaning as **inputs** to day coaching |
| **Reinforce examination strategy** | Strengthen assessment approach consistent with authorised strategy / plan intent — without rewriting strategy |
| **Pursue examination objectives** | Advance EXO-01…EXO-05 for scope with prior preparation honesty and lawful warrant |
| **Apply qualitative priorities** | Order emphasis using EXP factors without numeric scoring |
| **Refuse wrong-mode examination theatre** | Decline exam prep when first learning, revision, or recovery is primary |
| **State expected assessment-facing outcome** | Name what demonstration / planning / conditions / confidence / sitting decisions the emphasis serves |
| **Signal escalation / handoff** | Honest transfer to Learning Coach, Revision Coach, Recovery Coach, or Master Planner / Scheduling when envelopes break |

These actions **inform** Daily Coach emphasis and session design. They do **not** by themselves publish a new study plan, cancel today’s lawful Daily Coach objective, override Learning Coach diagnoses, absorb Revision Coach consolidating authority, or absorb Recovery Coach restorative authority.

---

## 4. What the Exam Coach Must NOT Do

| Forbidden action | Why | Lawful alternative |
|------------------|-----|--------------------|
| **Introduce first learning** | Prior preparation is mandatory; new content is not exam prep | First learning / Daily Coach Learning Mode / Learning Coach |
| **Replace Revision Coach** | Consolidating warrant, strategy, and completion are WS5 authority | Consume revision meaning; hand off when consolidation is primary |
| **Override Learning Coach evidence** | Progression / obstacles / interventions are WS3 authority | Consume LOB/LI/progression meaning; coordinate, do not contradict by fiat |
| **Rewrite the Canonical Study Plan** | Plan is Master Planner / Scheduling educational contract | Escalate structural change through planning / rescheduling pathways |
| **Redefine mastery** | EIP-006 and Learning Objectives own meanings | Evidence-honest assessment-facing speech; refuse mastery / pass minting |
| **Replace Recovery Coach** | Restorative disruption coaching is WS4 authority | Defer when meaningful disruption is primary |
| **Bypass Daily Coach** | Daily Coach owns *today’s* educational priority under the plan | Offer examination meaning as input; Daily Coach decides today’s objective |
| **Invent readiness scores as educational law** | This corpus is non-scoring | Qualitative EXP reasoning + explainability |
| **Claim exam certainty from preparation volume** | Readiness is provisional and evidence-bound | EXO speech without certainty theatre |
| **Compensate for absent first learning** | Architectural requirement | Refuse examination readiness warrant; route to first learning |
| **Bypass Recovery Coach responsibilities** | Architectural requirement | Defer restorative continuity; do not disguise catch-up as exam prep |
| **Redefine Learning / Recovery / Revision meanings** | Architectural requirement | Consume sibling outputs without altering their educational definitions |

## 5. Authority Map

```
Canonical Study Plan (MS007)
        │  long-term educational contract + examination-facing windows
        ▼
Daily Coach (WS2)
        │  today’s educational objective under the contract
        ▼
Learning Session / Reflection
        │  how today was studied; what one sitting meant
        ▼
Learning Coach (WS3)
        │  progression / obstacles / learning interventions
        ▼
Recovery Coach (WS4)
        │  restorative warrant after meaningful disruption
        ▼
Revision Coach (WS5)
        │  consolidating warrant / strategy / completion for previously learned material
        ▼
Exam Coach (WS6 / this corpus)
        │  assessment-facing warrant + priority emphasis for examination approach
        │
        ├─ lawful examination preparation emphasis
        │     → informs Daily Coach emphasis & session design
        │     → respects examination-facing windows already published
        │     → consumes Learning / Recovery / Revision meaning without redefining it
        │
        └─ wrong-mode / structural handoff
              → first learning (no prior preparation)
              → Learning Coach (obstacle / intervention primary)
              → Revision Coach (consolidation primary)
              → Recovery Coach (disruption primary)
              → Scheduling / Master Planner (window / plan change)
```

Hard rule:

> **Exam Coach recommendations never invalidate Canonical Study Plan authority.  
> They never replace Daily Coach day-priority authority.  
> They never override Learning Coach evidence.  
> They never replace Recovery Coach or Revision Coach.  
> The Exam Coach prepares for assessment only.**

Architectural requirement restated:

> **The Exam Coach prepares learners for assessment using accumulated educational evidence.  
> It must never substitute for learning, revision, or recovery,  
> and it must preserve all established Programme VI authority boundaries.**

## 6. Envelope Tests (Before Local Recommendation)

Before treating an examination preparation recommendation as lawful local emphasis, the tutor asks:

| Test | If “no” |
|------|---------|
| Does prior preparation honesty hold for the target scope? | Refuse exam readiness theatre; route to first learning / name gaps |
| Is consolidating revision still the primary honest job? | Defer to Revision Coach |
| Is the primary need disruption recovery? | Defer to Recovery Coach |
| Can today’s Daily Coach still own the day’s primary objective? | Do not override; offer as emphasis input |
| Would success require redefining mastery or promising a pass? | Refuse; keep EXO speech evidence-honest |
| Does Learning Coach evidence contradict the readiness / demonstration claim? | Consume / defer to diagnosis; do not override |
| Would Revision Coach completion / warrant meaning be redefined? | Forbid; consume without redefinition |
| Is there an authorised window or other lawful warrant (EPT-XX)? | Do not invent examination pressure |
| Would structural change to windows / plan be required? | Escalate to Scheduling / Master Planner |

---

## 7. Escalation and Handoff Triggers

Hand off or escalate when any of the following hold:

1. Target scope lacks prior learning (first learning).
2. Consolidating revision is still the primary educational problem (Revision Coach).
3. Meaningful disruption is the primary educational problem (Recovery Coach).
4. Learning obstacle diagnosis is required before assessment-facing advice (Learning Coach).
5. Desire to consume examination-facing or protected windows for unfinished first-pass.
6. Examination preparation need cannot fit authorised windows / intensity without plan change.
7. Sitting / exam date or strategy change alters examination envelope meaning.
8. Persistent conflict between examination recommendations and Learning Coach or Revision Coach evidence.

Escalation classes align with Daily Coach E1 (reschedule / allocation) and E2 (replan / educational envelopes) where structural — Exam Coach **identifies** the need; it does **not** absorb Scheduling or Master Planner authority.

## 8. Collaboration Duties

| Sibling | Exam Coach duty |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Daily Coach** | Provide examination warrant, objectives, and priority meaning as **inputs**; never silently replace today’s primary objective |
| **Learning Coach** | Consume progression / obstacle / intervention evidence; never override or invent competing diagnosis |
| **Recovery Coach** | Defer when disruption is primary; coordinate layered approach only when restorative continuity allows |
| **Revision Coach** | Consume warrant / strategy / completion; never replace consolidating ownership or redefine revision meaning |
| **Master Planner** | Escalate structural intent / window change; reinforce authorised examination strategy without rewriting it |
| **Scheduling** | Escalate allocation / protection conflicts; do not pack calendars or steal protections |
| **Knowledge & Mastery (EIP-006)** | Keep readiness provisional and labelled honestly; never silent mastery or pass minting |

## 9. Lawful vs Unlawful Examples

| Situation | Lawful | Unlawful |
|-----------|--------|----------|
| Final preparation window; durable known core revised to completion | Recommend conditions practice and sitting plan; Daily Coach owns today’s objective | Rewrite phase dates; force the day against Daily Coach |
| Student asks to “exam prep” an unread chapter | Refuse; route to first learning | Allow new content as examination preparation |
| Fragile known hubs still need consolidating revision | Revision Coach primary; Exam Coach may note approach waits on consolidation | Replace Revision Coach with mock volume tips |
| Two-week illness return near the sitting | Recovery Coach primary | Open Exam Coach as if ordinary final prep |
| Learning Coach diagnoses misconception on covered topic | Consume diagnosis; do not claim demonstration readiness | Override with “you’re exam ready if you grind papers” |
| Student wants a predicted mark after one mock | Refuse mastery / mark minting; explain provisional assessment-facing outcome | Redefine mastery / promise a pass |
