> Not every quiet week is an exam warrant.  
> Not every mock request is Exam Coach primary.  
> Examination preparation recommendations must be traceable to accumulated educational evidence.**

Identifier note: Exam Preparation Triggers use **EPT-XX** so they never collide with Revision Triggers (**RVT-XX**) or Recovery Triggers (**RT-XX**).

---

## 1. Purpose

If Exam Coach fires without prior learning, students experience first-pass content falsely labelled as examination preparation.  
If Exam Coach absorbs Revision Coach’s job, consolidating work is papered over with “exam tips” theatre.  
If Exam Coach never fires, students reach the sitting without coherent assessment approach.  
If Exam Coach steals Recovery Coach’s job, disruption is disguised as last-minute exam sprint.

This document draws bright lines between:

- **first learning** (no prior exposure / Learning Mode),
- **revision** (consolidating previously learned material — Revision Coach),
- **recovery** (meaningful disruption — Recovery Coach),
- **examination preparation** (assessment-facing approach using accumulated evidence — Exam Coach).

---

## 2. Core Distinctions

| Class | Meaning | Lawful owner |
|-------|---------|--------------|
| **First learning** | Student has not yet had meaningful educational exposure to the syllabus unit | Daily Coach / Learning Mode / Learning Coach — **not Exam Coach** |
| **Revision** | Prior exposure exists and consolidating return is educationally warranted now | Revision Coach — **not Exam Coach as consolidating owner** |
| **Recovery** | Meaningful disruption to continuity / capacity / learning trail requiring restorative coaching | Recovery Coach — **not Exam Coach as primary** |
| **Examination preparation** | Accumulated learning and revision meaning support assessment-facing preparation now | **Exam Coach** (this corpus), informing Daily Coach |

Binding principle:

> **Examination preparation is prior-preparation-gated and evidence-gated.  
> Calendar “exam phase” labels alone are not educational proof of warrant.**

---

## 3. Preconditions for Any Examination Preparation Warrant

Before any EPT-XX trigger may open an Exam Coach recommendation, **all** of the following must hold:

1. **Prior preparation honesty** — the warrant is grounded in accumulated educational evidence of learning and/or revision for the scope being prepared toward assessment (not merely a future calendar cell labelled “exam”).
2. **Evidence honesty** — the warrant cites accumulated educational evidence or an authorised examination-facing plan window with educational substance (EIP-002).
3. **Wrong-mode check** — the primary need is not absent first learning, not unfinished consolidating revision as the sole honest job, and not Recovery Coach restorative continuity.
4. **Plan / day envelope** — emphasis can be recommended within Canonical Study Plan and Daily Coach authority (or escalated honestly if not).

If any precondition fails, refuse examination theatre and hand off appropriately.

---

## 4. Not Examination Preparation (Refuse Warrant)

Treat as **not examination preparation** — do **not** open an Exam Coach assessment-facing recommendation as primary — when any of the following hold:

| Situation | Why not Exam Coach primary | Lawful alternative |
|-----------|----------------------------|--------------------|
| Syllabus unit never studied | No prior learning | First learning / Daily Coach Learning Mode |
| “Exam prep” request is unfinished first-pass backlog | Would steal assessment-facing meaning | First-pass under plan; escalate if protections conflict |
| Previously learned material needs consolidating revision as the primary job | Revision owns consolidating substance | Revision Coach (RVT-* / strategy / completion) |
| Prolonged absence / meaningful disruption is the primary problem | Restorative continuity owns the moment | Recovery Coach (RT-* family) |
| Temporary fluctuation only (one quiet evening, short variation) | No assessment-facing crisis | Ordinary Daily Coach continuity — optional light return only if already authorised |
| Learning obstacle blocks progress on material that was “covered” but never understood | Diagnosis before exam theatre | Learning Coach obstacle → intervention |
| Thin or absent evidence of need, with no authorised examination-facing window | Avoid invented exam pressure | Gather warrant / follow plan rhythm |
| Desire for a pass guarantee or numeric readiness score | Outside educational honesty | Refuse certainty theatre; keep provisional speech |

## 5. Examination Preparation Trigger Catalogue

Each trigger class below may warrant Exam Coach when preconditions and evidence support it. Triggers may co-occur.

### EPT-01 — Approaching examination window

**Definition.** Examination proximity and/or an authorised examination-facing plan window make assessment approach educationally primary — preparing how to perform and sit using what has already been learned and revised.

**Typical cues:**

- Sitting date near enough that plan intent shifts toward examination-facing preparation
- Canonical Study Plan / blueprint places an examination preparation or final approach window
- Profile / strategy already treats assessment approach as the lawful next educational story for prepared material

**Examination emphasis:** EXO-02, EXO-01, EXO-03; EXO-05 as sitting judgement warrants; EXO-04 as evidence-grounded support.

**Must not:** Use proximity to introduce new syllabus as “last-minute exam prep”; claim readiness from proximity alone; absorb unfinished revision as Exam Coach ownership.

---

### EPT-02 — Completion of planned revision

**Definition.** Revision Coach completion meaning (or authorised plan completion of consolidating substance) indicates that consolidating revision of relevant known material has strengthened enough that assessment-facing preparation becomes the honest next educational job.

**Typical cues:**

- Revision Completion Model judges consolidating revision sufficiently strengthened for the relevant scope
- Protected revision / consolidation windows have delivered their educational substance for known material
- Remaining work is approach, conditions, demonstration, and sitting decision-making rather than further first consolidation

**Examination emphasis:** EXO-01, EXO-03, EXO-05; EXO-02 for structuring the remaining examination period.

**Must not:** Treat a calendar “revision done” label without Revision Coach completion meaning as warrant; use completion theatre to skip fragile known topics that still need consolidating return; redefine revision completion inside Exam Coach.

---

### EPT-03 — Examination registration

**Definition.** Formal commitment to a named sitting (registration, entry confirmation, or equivalent authorised exam-context binding) makes examination approach a living educational question for that sitting’s prepared scope.

**Typical cues:**

- Student Educational Profile / study context binds to a named examination sitting
- Registration or entry confirmation changes lawful timing and examination-facing intent
- Plan / strategy already assumes that sitting as the preparation target

**Examination emphasis:** EXO-02 primarily (examination planning); EXO-01/EXO-04 as evidence supports; coordinate with plan windows.

**Must not:** Treat registration alone as proof of readiness; invent full-syllabus exam prep for unlearned units because “I’m entered”; rewrite plan intensity solely from registration anxiety.

---

### EPT-04 — Readiness review

**Definition.** An authorised educational readiness review — tutor- or plan-triggered — asks how prepared the learner is to approach the examination, requiring honest assessment-facing judgement without minting mastery or marks.

**Typical cues:**

- Plan / Daily Coach / Profile opens a readiness review checkpoint
- Learning Coach honesty and Revision Coach completion meaning are available to consume
- Student or authorised coach requests a review of examination approach (not a first-learning diagnosis)

**Examination emphasis:** EXO-01 honesty; EXO-02 adjustment of approach; EXO-04 confidence calibrated to evidence; hand off where gaps are learning / revision / recovery owned.

**Must not:** Convert readiness review into a numeric score as educational law; declare “exam ready” from thin evidence; hide Learning Coach or Revision Coach gaps behind confidence speech.

---

### EPT-05 — Final preparation period

**Definition.** The last authorised stretch before the sitting makes assessment conditions, demonstration of known material, sitting decision-making, and evidence-grounded confidence the primary educational goods — distinct from ordinary spaced revision earlier in the journey.

**Typical cues:**

- Plan enters a final preparation / pre-sitting phase with educational substance
- Revision of core known material is largely complete or concurrently framed under Revision Coach while Exam Coach owns approach
- Emphasis shifts to timed conditions, paper approach, and protecting demonstrated strength

**Examination emphasis:** EXO-03, EXO-05, EXO-01; EXO-02 for final sitting plan; EXO-04 without certainty inflation.

**Must not:** Use final period panic to expand into unlearned syllabus as exam prep; replace last consolidating needs with mock volume theatre; override Recovery Coach if disruption erupts in the final period.

## 6. Trigger Co-occurrence and Precedence

When triggers co-occur:

1. **Wrong-mode first** — if Recovery RT-* meaningful disruption, absent first learning, or Revision Coach consolidating primary need applies, those own the primary response.
2. **Completion of planned revision (EPT-02)** often supplies the honest gate into assessment-facing work when consolidation was the prior job.
3. **Approaching examination window (EPT-01)** and **final preparation period (EPT-05)** intensify assessment-facing form — they do not invent prior learning or redefine revision completion.
4. **Examination registration (EPT-03)** binds sitting context; other triggers supply *what* preparation emphasis is warranted inside that context.
5. **Readiness review (EPT-04)** is a judgement event — it may activate or refine Exam Coach emphasis, or honestly hand off.

Emphasis-level ordering within a warrant uses `EXAM_PRIORITIES.md`.

## 7. Differentiating Examination Preparation from Learning and Revision

| Question | If yes… | Owner |
|----------|---------|-------|
| Has this material never been studied? | First learning | Learning Mode / Learning Coach |
| Has it been studied but needs consolidating return as the primary job? | Revision | Revision Coach |
| Is the primary problem disruption to continuity? | Recovery | Recovery Coach |
| Is the primary question how to prepare for and approach the sitting using accumulated evidence? | Examination preparation | **Exam Coach** |

Binding differentiators:

| Domain | Primary question | Exam Coach may… | Exam Coach must not… |
|--------|------------------|------------------|----------------------|
| **Learning** | Is the student genuinely learning unlearned / blocked material? | Name gaps honestly; hand off | Teach new content as exam prep |
| **Revision** | What known material should be consolidated now, how, and whether enough? | Consume completion / warrant meaning | Replace Revision Coach ownership |
| **Examination** | How should the learner prepare for and approach the examination? | Own assessment-facing warrant and emphasis | Mint readiness from volume or phase labels |

Hard rule from architectural requirement:

> **The Exam Coach prepares learners for assessment.  
> It must never substitute for learning, revision, or recovery.**

---

## 8. Relationship to Recovery and Revision Triggers

| Sibling trigger family | Exam Coach posture |
|------------------------|--------------------|
| Recovery RT-01 / RT-02 absence / missed sessions | Prefer Recovery Coach; examination approach may appear later once continuity is stable |
| Recovery RT-03 / RT-04 learning / regression during disruption | Consume Learning Coach + Recovery meanings; do not open competing exam theatre |
| Revision RVT-* consolidating warrants | Prefer Revision Coach for consolidating substance; Exam Coach may layer conditions / approach without absorbing revision |
| Revision completion indicates consolidating substance sufficient | EPT-02 may open lawful Exam Coach warrant |
| No recovery warrant; revision complete or not primary; exam proximity / registration / review / final period | Exam Coach EPT-* lawful |

---

## 9. Lawful vs Unlawful Examples

| Situation | Lawful | Unlawful |
|-----------|--------|----------|
| Exam in three weeks; core known topics revised to completion meaning | EPT-01 / EPT-02 / EPT-05; coach approach, conditions, demonstration | “Exam prep” that teaches unread chapters |
| Student enters for the sitting | EPT-03; refine examination planning inside the plan | Declare readiness because registration exists |
| Protected revision still consolidating fragile known hubs | Revision Coach primary | Open Exam Coach as if consolidation were finished |
| Student ill for two weeks near the exam | Recovery Coach primary | Open Exam Coach sprint as if disruption were ordinary final prep |
| Readiness review shows thin Learning Coach evidence on a “covered” topic | Hand off to Learning Coach / refuse readiness minting | Score “72% exam ready” and proceed |
