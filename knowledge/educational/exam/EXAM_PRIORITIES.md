## 1. Purpose

Students often face more examination preparation work than one sitting can absorb. An expert IFoA tutor does not assign opaque points. The tutor asks which assessment-facing job most protects honest examination performance *now* — given readiness posture, coverage honesty, durable understanding, revision evidence, and assessment timing.

This document records that qualitative priority reasoning so Exam Coach emphasis remains explainable and consistent with Programme VI authorities.

---

## 2. Binding Non-Scoring Rule

Examination priority **must not** be expressed as:

- a numeric readiness score, urgency index, predicted mark, or pass probability presented as educational truth;
- a black-box ranking that students cannot understand in educational language;
- a formula that redefines mastery, Learning Coach evidence, Revision Coach completion, or Canonical Study Plan protections.

Internal implementation may later use proxies. Those proxies **must not redefine** the educational meanings here, and student-facing speech must remain qualitative and claim-typed (EIP-003).

---

## 3. Eligibility Before Priority

An emphasis enters the priority field only if:

1. Prior preparation honesty holds (`EXAM_PREPARATION_TRIGGERS.md` preconditions).
2. A lawful examination preparation warrant (one or more EPT-XX) applies — or an authorised examination-facing window is being filled with assessment-facing substance.
3. Exam Coach is the correct primary mode (not first learning; not Revision Coach consolidating primary; not Recovery Coach as primary).

Ineligible emphases are not “low priority exam prep” — they are **out of examination preparation field**.

---

## 4. Priority Factor Catalogue

An expert tutor weighs the following educational factors. Factors are **influences on judgement**, not additive points.

### EXP-01 — Educational readiness

**Meaning.** How honestly prepared is the learner — based on accumulated Learning Coach and related evidence — to demonstrate understanding under assessment conditions for the scope in view?

**Raises priority when:**

- Accumulated evidence supports assessment-facing demonstration of known material (EXO-01)
- Readiness review (EPT-04) shows a coherent prepared core that needs approach coaching
- Conditions / sitting decision-making would unlock performance of what is already known

**Lowers / forbids as Exam Coach priority when:**

- Learning Coach evidence shows thin progression, obstacle, or false readiness on the claimed scope
- “Readiness” would be invented from calendar proximity or mock volume alone

**Must not:** Convert readiness into a single scalar as educational law; override Learning Coach evidence to force examination theatre.

---

### EXP-02 — Syllabus coverage honesty

**Meaning.** How complete and honest is coverage of the authorised examination syllabus — distinguishing studied scope from unlearned gaps — so that examination preparation does not pretend unlearned units are preparable as assessment performance?

**Raises priority when:**

- Covered, learned, and revised scope needs assessment-facing demonstration and approach
- Coverage gaps are named so examination planning (EXO-02) can protect known strength without inventing whole-syllabus readiness

**Lowers / forbids when:**

- The proposed emphasis targets unlearned units under an exam-prep label
- Coverage theatre would hide gaps that require first learning

**Must not:** Equate Study Progress ticks with exam-usable understanding; use coverage language to smuggle first-time teaching into Exam Coach priority.

---

### EXP-03 — Durable understanding

**Meaning.** How durable is demonstrated understanding of the prepared scope — based on return evidence, spacing, and Learning Coach durability reading — such that examination preparation can honestly rehearse performance rather than paper over fragility?

**Raises priority when:**

- Durable known material needs conditions practice and sitting decision-making
- Fragility is limited and named; Exam Coach can protect durable cores while handing fragile consolidating needs to Revision Coach

**Lowers / pauses Exam Coach priority when:**

- Understanding is so fragile that consolidating revision is still the primary honest job
- Same-day success without durable return is being mistaken for examination readiness

**Must not:** Invent catastrophic fragility or permanent retention from thin evidence; treat durability as a numeric retention percentage as educational law.

---

### EXP-04 — Revision evidence

**Meaning.** What do Revision Coach warrant, strategy, and completion meanings say about whether consolidating revision has strengthened existing knowledge enough for assessment-facing work?

**Raises priority when:**

- Revision completion (EPT-02) supports moving to examination approach
- Revision evidence shows exam-usable strength on hubs that now need demonstration under conditions

**Lowers / defers when:**

- Revision Coach still owns consolidating primary need on the critical known scope
- Revision evidence contradicts claims that the student can demonstrate the material under sitting conditions

**Must not:** Redefine Revision Coach completion inside Exam Coach; ignore revision evidence to force final-prep theatre; replace Revision Coach priority with Exam Coach tips.

---

### EXP-05 — Assessment timing

**Meaning.** How does proximity to the sitting, registration binding, and authorised final preparation windows change the *form* of already eligible examination preparation — without expanding the field to unlearned content?

**Raises priority when:**

- Examination window / final period (EPT-01, EPT-05) makes conditions management and sitting decision-making urgent for known material
- Registration (EPT-03) binds lawful timing so examination planning must be made explicit

**Lowers when:**

- Timing pressure would be used as an excuse to skip honesty about learning or revision gaps
- Earlier journey stages still own first learning or consolidating revision as primary

**Must not:** Let proximity invent prior preparation; equate “exam next week” with highest priority for every topic including unlearned ones.

---

### EXP-06 — Evidence strength and plan-window honesty

**Meaning.** How well-supported is the assessment-facing need by Educational Evidence quality, sibling coach meanings, and Canonical Study Plan examination-facing intent?

**Raises priority when:**

- Multiple congruent observations support assessment-facing emphasis
- An authorised examination-facing window exists with educational substance

**Lowers / pauses when:**

- Evidence is thin — prefer gathering warrant or following plan rhythm over invented urgency
- Selecting the emphasis would consume protected windows unlawfully or contradict Learning / Revision / Recovery meaning

**Must not:** Override sibling coach evidence; treat leftover empty cells as automatic highest examination priority; invent readiness review outcomes without warrant.

## 5. How an Expert Tutor Combines Factors

There is **no numerical weighting scheme** in this Model. The tutor combines factors through educational narrative:

1. **Exclude** ineligible emphases (no prior preparation; wrong mode — learning / revision / recovery primary).
2. **Name the warrant** (which EPT-XX apply).
3. **Ask what fails first at the sitting if we do nothing assessment-facing today** — usually failure to demonstrate durable known material under conditions, or absence of a coherent examination plan.
4. **Prefer protecting durable known cores** over inventing coverage of unlearned gaps.
5. **Defer consolidating fragility** to Revision Coach; **defer disruption** to Recovery Coach; **defer unlearned gaps** to first learning / Learning Coach.
6. **Intensify conditions and sitting decision-making** as assessment timing warrants — without expanding the field.
7. **Explain** the ordering in plain language (see `EXAM_EXPLAINABILITY.md`).

Illustrative combination patterns (not scores):

| Pattern | Typical priority posture |
|---------|--------------------------|
| Strong revision completion + durable understanding + approaching window | Strong Exam Coach priority: demonstration, conditions, sitting decisions |
| High assessment timing but weak revision evidence on critical hubs | **Defer** consolidating primary to Revision Coach; Exam Coach may only frame approach around honesty |
| Coverage gaps on unlearned units near the exam | **Out of field** for those units — first learning / plan escalation; Exam Coach protects known scope |
| Readiness review with Learning Coach thin / falsely ready posture | Pause Exam Coach readiness speech; consume Learning Coach honesty |
| Any pattern during meaningful disruption primary | **Defer** to Recovery Coach |

## 6. Relationship to Daily Coach Selection

Examination priority produces **emphasis meaning** among assessment-facing candidates.  
Daily Coach still decides **whether examination preparation is today’s primary objective** under the Canonical Study Plan and day gates.

| Layer | Question |
|-------|----------|
| Exam Coach priorities | Among lawful examination preparation emphases, what deserves assessment-facing attention and why? |
| Daily Coach | Given plan, capacity, protections, and sibling inputs, what is most educationally valuable *today*? |

Exam Coach must not silently replace Daily Coach’s day-priority selection with an internal priority list.

---

## 7. Relationship to Revision Priorities

Revision priorities (RVP-*) order consolidating candidates among previously learned material.  
Exam priorities (EXP-*) order assessment-facing emphases once examination preparation is the lawful primary (or layered) job.

| If the honest job is… | Use |
|-----------------------|-----|
| What known topic to consolidate now | `REVISION_PRIORITIES.md` |
| How to prepare for and approach the examination | This document |
| Both concurrently (layered) | Revision owns consolidating substance; Exam Coach may own conditions / approach / sitting decisions without absorbing RVP ordering |

---

## 8. Anti-Patterns

Forbidden prioritisation behaviours:

- Ranking by predicted marks, pass probabilities, or opaque readiness indices
- Always prioritising the hardest unlearned topic as “exam prep”
- Always prioritising the easiest mock wins that invent false readiness
- Preferring question volume over demonstration of durable known material
- Ignoring revision completion meaning when consolidating work is incomplete
- Using assessment timing to override Learning Coach or replace Recovery Coach
- Numeric leaderboards of “exam urgency”
