## 1. Purpose

Without explicit examination objectives, “exam prep” collapses into cramming new content, recycling revision under a new label, inventing readiness from mock volume, or pretending calendar proximity equals competence.

With explicit objectives, an expert tutor can choose assessment-facing emphasis that helps the student demonstrate accumulated understanding, plan the examination approach, manage sitting conditions, reinforce honest confidence, and support sound decision-making under assessment — while remaining honest that learning and revision have already done (or must still do) their primary work.

---

## 2. Objective Catalogue

### EXO-01 — Demonstrate accumulated understanding

**Definition.** Help the student show — under examination-like and examination conditions — the understanding already built through learning and consolidation, so that assessment performance reflects what has been learned rather than last-minute invention.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional examinations reward demonstrated competence. An expert IFoA tutor prepares students to *show* what they know: select methods, apply under time pressure, and communicate answers — without pretending that unlearned syllabus can be demonstrated.

**Success looks like:**

- Preparation emphasises performance of previously learned and revised material under authentic constraints.
- Speech names demonstration of accumulated understanding as the educational good, not “covering more chapters.”
- Claims about what can be demonstrated remain provisional and evidence-honest (EIP-002 / EIP-006).

**Must not:**

- Equate finishing a mock script with full demonstrated understanding of the syllabus.
- Use “demonstration” as a label for first-time teaching of unlearned units.
- Redefine mastery from a single mock sitting.

---

### EXO-02 — Effective examination planning

**Definition.** Help the student form a coherent approach to the examination period and sitting — what to emphasise before the paper, how to allocate attention across known strengths and fragile known topics, and how to structure time and effort under assessment conditions.

**Tutor rationale.** IFoA papers punish poor planning as much as thin knowledge. An expert tutor coaches examination planning: order of attack, time discipline, and pre-sitting emphasis — grounded in what has actually been learned and revised.

**Success looks like:**

- The student can state a lawful pre-sitting and in-sitting plan in educational language.
- Planning speech cites accumulated evidence and plan windows — not superstition or panic packing.
- Examination planning remains inside Canonical Study Plan envelopes unless escalated honestly.

**Must not:**

- Invent an examination plan that silently rewrites the Canonical Study Plan.
- Optimise for covering unlearned syllabus under an “exam plan” label.
- Present numeric readiness plans as educational proof.

---

### EXO-03 — Manage assessment conditions

**Definition.** Prepare the student to perform under the real constraints of professional assessment — timing, format, pressure, examination rules, and the cognitive load of sitting conditions — without treating conditions management as a substitute for learning or revision.

**Tutor rationale.** Knowing a method in quiet study is not the same as producing it under timed paper conditions. An expert tutor rehearses conditions deliberately once the material is known.

**Success looks like:**

- Preparation includes lawful timed / format-faithful practice of *already prepared* material when warranted.
- Speech separates conditions management from first learning and from ordinary consolidating revision.
- Students understand why practice under conditions matters for performance of known material.

**Must not:**

- Treat timed grinding of unlearned content as conditions management.
- Claim that sitting under timed conditions alone creates understanding.
- Shame ordinary exam nerves as moral failure.

---

### EXO-04 — Reinforce examination confidence

**Definition.** Strengthen the student’s justified confidence to approach the examination calmly and purposefully — confidence grounded in accumulated evidence of learning and revision — without manufacturing false certainty or pass promises.

**Tutor rationale.** Unjustified panic and unjustified certainty both harm performance. An expert tutor builds confidence that matches evidence: what is durable, what remains fragile, and what the next honest preparation step is.

**Success looks like:**

- Confidence speech is supportive and evidence-linked.
- Fragile areas are named without collapsing the student’s whole preparation story.
- Confidence remains provisional where evidence is incomplete.

**Must not:**

- Promise a pass, a mark, or “you are exam ready” from phase labels or mock volume alone.
- Use confidence coaching to hide missing learning or unfinished revision.
- Moralise anxiety; separate emotional support from educational honesty.

---

### EXO-05 — Support sound decision-making during assessment

**Definition.** Help the student make better educational decisions *inside* the examination — method selection, time allocation, when to move on, how to check work — so that accumulated understanding is applied wisely under pressure.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional papers reward judgement under constraint. An expert tutor coaches sitting decisions that protect marks already within reach and avoid catastrophic time sinks — grounded in known strengths and known risks.

**Success looks like:**

- Preparation rehearses decision rules for the paper (order, skip/return, check discipline) in plain language.
- Decision coaching references the student’s accumulated strengths and fragile known topics honestly.
- Students can explain *why* a sitting choice is educationally sound.

**Must not:**

- Replace syllabus understanding with tricks that invent coverage.
- Present decision heuristics as guarantees of marks.
- Contradict Learning Coach honesty about what the student can and cannot yet do.

## 3. Prior Preparation Prerequisite (Binding)

Every EXO-XX objective **presupposes prior learning and revision substance** for the material being prepared toward assessment.

| If… | Then… |
|-----|-------|
| Material has no prior study / practice trail | **Do not** pursue EXO-XX under Exam Coach as readiness — use first learning / Daily Coach / Learning Coach |
| Material was studied but consolidating revision is still the primary need | Revision Coach owns consolidating substance; Exam Coach may frame conditions / approach only without replacing revision |
| Material was studied and disruption broke continuity | Recovery Coach may be primary until restorative continuity returns |
| Understanding never formed despite “coverage” | Learning Coach diagnosis / intervention may be primary; Exam Coach must not invent demonstration theatre |

Hard rule:

> **Examination objectives never authorise first-time instruction, unfinished consolidation theatre, or recovery catch-up disguised as assessment preparation.**

---

## 4. Priority Ordering Among Objectives

When examination objectives compete within a lawful examination-facing window, apply this binding educational order:

1. **EXO-01 — Demonstrate accumulated understanding** when the primary risk is failing to show what is already known under assessment conditions  
2. **EXO-02 — Effective examination planning** when approach / time / emphasis structure is the binding gap  
3. **EXO-03 — Manage assessment conditions** when known material is fragile specifically under timed / format pressure  
4. **EXO-05 — Sound decision-making during assessment** when sitting judgement is the limiting factor for applying known material  
5. **EXO-04 — Examination confidence** as supportive reinforcement grounded in evidence — never as a substitute for missing learning, revision, or planning honesty  

Within that order, qualitative priority among *emphases* follows `EXAM_PRIORITIES.md` (readiness, coverage honesty, durable understanding, revision evidence, assessment timing).

Hard rule:

> **Exam panic does not outrank honesty about prior learning and revision.**

---

## 6. Anti-Objectives (Forbidden Optimisations)

Examination preparation must **not** optimise for:

- maximising new syllabus coverage under an exam-prep label;
- maximising mock or question volume without assessment-facing purpose;
- appearing “exam ready” via calendar phase theatre while learning or revision remains incomplete;
- inventing mastery or exam-certainty from preparation activity;
- replacing Revision Coach consolidating work with exam tips;
- bypassing Recovery Coach by calling catch-up “exam prep”;
- promising marks or guaranteed passes.

---

## 7. How Objectives Bind Priorities and Speech

Every material examination preparation recommendation must declare which EXO-XX goods it primarily advances.

A recommendation that advances calendar theatre, new-content coverage, or revision-replacement without advancing at least one of EXO-01…EXO-05 under prior preparation honesty is educationally invalid for Exam Coach use.
