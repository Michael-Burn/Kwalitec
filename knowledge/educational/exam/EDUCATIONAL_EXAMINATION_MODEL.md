# Educational Examination Model

**Programme:** VI — Workstream 6 — Exam Coach  
**Milestone:** MS001 — Educational Examination Model  
**Classification:** Highest educational authority for examination preparation meaning within Programme VI Workstream 6  
**Status:** APPROVED — governing for Exam Coach educational contract  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-07-25  

---

## Authority

This document is the constitutional overview of the **Exam Coach** for Kwalitec.

It is subordinate to the Educational Constitution and specialised educational models. It governs **how Kwalitec prepares learners for professional assessment** while remaining faithful to the Canonical Study Plan and sibling coach authorities. It does not authorise implementation shortcuts that contradict the Constitution, and it does not invent readiness, evidence, or planning meaning absent from upstream models.

Authority order for educational examination preparation:

> Constitution defines educational truth and curriculum primacy.  
> Knowledge & Mastery defines coverage ≠ understanding ≠ mastery.  
> Evidence Model defines what may warrant educational claims.  
> Continuity Standard preserves rightful educational history across plan change.  
> Master Planner publishes the Canonical Study Plan and owns long-term educational envelopes, including examination-facing windows.  
> Daily Coach owns *what is most educationally valuable today* under that contract.  
> Learning Coach owns longitudinal progression, obstacle diagnosis, and learning interventions.  
> Recovery Coach owns restorative coaching after meaningful disruption.  
> Revision Coach owns consolidating previously learned material for durable exam-usable strength.  
> **This Educational Examination Model (Workstream 6 / MS001) defines how the learner prepares for and approaches the examination using that accumulated educational understanding.**  
> Downstream coaching and narration surfaces must consume this educational reasoning — never replace learning, revision, recovery, or planning with examination theatre.

---

## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not treat examination preparation as a second first-pass, nor as a substitute for consolidating revision, nor as heroic catch-up after disruption. After learning, recovery (when needed), and revision have done their work — or when lawful examination proximity makes assessment approach the primary educational question — the tutor answers one living educational question:

> **“How should this learner prepare for and approach the examination?”**

That answer must help the student demonstrate accumulated understanding, plan the examination approach, manage assessment conditions, reinforce examination confidence without false certainty, and support sound decision-making under sitting conditions — while remaining faithful to the Canonical Study Plan’s educational intent and refusing to pretend that unlearned material, unfinished consolidation, or unresolved disruption is “exam ready.”

This document records that tutor posture so every future Exam Coach subsystem has a single educational reference for *how examination preparation is reasoned*.

> **The Exam Coach describes educational examination preparation reasoning.  
> It does not implement Runtime A logic, algorithms, analytics, or Twin writers.**

---

## 2. What Educational Examination Preparation Is

**Educational examination preparation** is the assessment-facing process by which Kwalitec helps a student **prepare for and approach a professional examination** using **accumulated educational evidence** of learning, recovery posture, and revision — without treating examination preparation as first-time instruction, consolidating revision, restorative recovery, or long-term replanning.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Accumulated educational evidence** | Trail of learning, revision, and continuity meaning that may support assessment-facing claims | What do we honestly know about this learner’s preparation? |
| **Examination preparation warrant** | Judgement that assessment-facing preparation is educationally justified now | Is Exam Coach primary — or learning / revision / recovery? |
| **Examination objectives** | Educational goods examination preparation must pursue | What are we trying to prepare for? |
| **Examination priority** | Qualitative tutor ordering among lawful preparation emphases | What examination preparation should come first, and why? |
| **Examination approach** | Recommended posture for preparing toward and sitting the assessment | How should the learner prepare for and approach the exam? |

Examination preparation is:

- **assessment-facing** — it orients accumulated understanding toward sitting conditions and examination performance;
- **evidence-consuming** — it uses Learning Coach, Recovery Coach, and Revision Coach meaning without redefining them;
- **prior-preparation-gated** — it assumes prior learning and revision have already done their primary work (or honestly names when they have not);
- **plan-faithful** — it respects Canonical Study Plan examination-facing windows and protections;
- **coach-coordinated** — it informs Daily Coach; never replaces sibling coaches;
- **honesty-bound** — it refuses false readiness and pass guarantees;
- **explainable** — students understand why preparation is changing, what evidence supports it, and what outcome is expected.

Examination preparation is **not**:

- a substitute for first-pass Learning Mode or Learning Coach progression of unlearned material;
- a licence to replace Revision Coach consolidating responsibilities with “exam prep” theatre;
- a bypass of Recovery Coach when meaningful disruption is the true problem;
- inference of mastery or exam certainty from mocks, question volume, or calendar “exam phase” labels alone;
- a rewrite of the Canonical Study Plan or educational strategy;
- a claim that examination preparation guarantees a pass.

## 3. What the Exam Coach Is

The **Exam Coach** is the educational capability that, given **accumulated educational evidence**, **authorised plan / day context**, and **sibling coach meanings**, produces an **examination preparation warrant**, selects **examination objectives**, states **qualitative priority emphasis**, states **boundary posture**, and produces **examination explainability**.

| Input family | Role |
|--------------|------|
| **Accumulated Educational Evidence** | Trail that supports readiness posture, coverage honesty, durability, timing (EIP-002) |
| **Canonical Study Plan posture** | Including examination-facing windows — consumed, not rewritten |
| **Student Educational Profile** | Exam context, capacity, revision maturity, readiness posture |
| **Learning Coach meaning** | Progression / evidence / obstacle / intervention meaning — consumed honestly, never overridden |
| **Revision Coach meaning** | Revision warrant, strategy, completion — consumed, never redefined |
| **Recovery Coach posture** | Whether restorative disruption coaching is active — respected, never replaced |
| **Daily Coach context** | Today’s lawful day-priority authority — coordinated, never bypassed |

into:

| Output family | Role |
|---------------|------|
| **Examination preparation warrant** | Whether assessment-facing preparation is educationally justified now |
| **Examination objectives in focus** | Which EXO-XX goods this emphasis prioritises |
| **Priority emphasis** | Qualitative ordering among lawful preparation emphases (EXP factors) |
| **Boundary posture** | Local examination preparation vs handoff to learning / revision / recovery / planning |
| **Expected educational outcome** | What examination preparation should achieve if the advice is followed |
| **Examination explainability** | Plain-language account for the student |

It answers:

| Question | Educational answer |
|----------|-------------------|
| How should the learner prepare? | Assessment-facing posture grounded in accumulated evidence |
| Why now? | Trigger class + evidence (exam window, revision completion, registration, readiness review, final period) |
| What should preparation achieve? | Demonstrated understanding, exam planning, conditions management, confidence, sitting decision-making |
| May Exam Coach enact this alone? | Only within boundaries; else hand off or escalate |
| How does this serve examination performance? | Explicit link from preparation emphasis to honest sitting performance |

The Exam Coach is:

- **assessment-gated** — refuses examination theatre when first learning, revision, or recovery is the true primary need;
- **objective-bound** — pursues defined examination educational goods;
- **priority-aware** — ranks emphasis qualitatively using tutor factors, never opaque scores;
- **boundary-faithful** — protects plan, Daily Coach, Learning Coach, Recovery Coach, Revision Coach, and mastery authorities;
- **evidence-honest** — never mints readiness or pass certainty from volume alone;
- **explainable** — supportive, evidence-linked language;
- **non-scoring** — this Model defines meaning, not numeric readiness metrics.

The Exam Coach is **not**:

- a second Master Planner, Scheduling Engine, Strategy selector, or Profile diagnostician;
- a second Daily Coach, Learning Coach, Recovery Coach, or Revision Coach;
- ownership of Educational Evidence recording or Twin write authority;
- a UI surface, analytics dashboard, or scoring engine;
- a licence to disguise first-pass backlog, unfinished consolidation, or recovery catch-up as examination preparation.

---

## 4. Educational Purpose

The Exam Coach exists so that:

1. **Examination approach is coached deliberately** — not left to panic, superstition, or last-minute volume theatre.
2. **Examination preparation remains examination preparation** — new content stays first learning; consolidation stays revision; disruption stays recovery; long-term intent stays planning.
3. **Assessment performance is built from accumulated understanding** — not from calendar labels or invented readiness.
4. **Evidence stays visible** — students see why preparation is changing, not an opaque optimiser choice.
5. **Authority stays layered** — assessment-facing coaching does not silently become replanning, diagnosis, consolidating revision, or restorative catch-up.
6. **Students understand the arc** — why prepare this way, what evidence, what outcome, how it contributes to examination performance.

---

## 5. Core Responsibilities

The Exam Coach is educationally responsible for:

| Responsibility | Meaning |
|----------------|---------|
| **Confirm preparation gate** | Refuse examination theatre without prior learning / revision substance (or honest naming of gaps) |
| **State examination preparation warrant** | Explicit educational justification for assessment-facing preparation now |
| **Pursue examination objectives** | Optimise assessment-facing goods (EXO-01…EXO-05) |
| **Prioritise examination emphasis** | Apply qualitative tutor factors (EXP) without numeric scoring |
| **Bound Exam Coach authority** | Separate examination preparation from first learning, revision, recovery, and planning |
| **Respect sibling coaches** | Coordinate with Daily Coach; consume Learning / Recovery / Revision meanings; never replace them |
| **Protect plan examination intent** | Honour examination-facing windows as educational meaning, not residual scraps |
| **Explain assessment-facing purpose** | Make examination preparation speakable with evidence and expected outcomes |

It is **not** educationally responsible for:

- introducing new syllabus content under an examination-preparation label;
- rewriting Canonical Study Plans or educational strategy;
- deciding today’s Daily Coach primary objective by commandeering day authority;
- diagnosing learning obstacles or selecting Learning Interventions (Learning Coach owns those meanings);
- restoring progress after meaningful disruption (Recovery Coach owns that meaning);
- deciding what to revise, which revision strategy fits, or whether revision is complete (Revision Coach owns those meanings);
- inferring or redefining mastery;
- writing Twin estimates by itself;
- packing calendars or inventing intensity (Scheduling / Master Planner ownership);
- producing opaque “readiness scores” as educational proof;
- promising examination marks or guaranteed passes.

## 6. Binding Educational Doctrine

1. **The Exam Coach prepares for assessment.** It does not replace learning, revision, recovery, or long-term planning.
2. **Prior preparation is assumed.** Examination preparation presupposes prior learning and revision; gaps must be named honestly, not disguised as exam tips.
3. **Traceability is mandatory.** Every material examination preparation recommendation must cite accumulated educational evidence and a lawful warrant.
4. **Examination ≠ revision.** Consolidating previously learned material remains Revision Coach territory; Exam Coach may consume revision completion / emphasis without absorbing revision authority.
5. **Examination ≠ first learning.** New syllabus content must not be introduced as examination preparation.
6. **Examination ≠ recovery.** Meaningful disruption is Recovery Coach territory; Exam Coach must not compensate for absent continuity by inventing assessment theatre.
7. **Mastery is never redefined by examination preparation.** Mocks, question volume, and phase labels do not mint understanding or exam certainty (EIP-006).
8. **Daily Coach authority is preserved.** Examination preparation informs today’s emphasis; it does not commandeer day-priority selection.
9. **Learning Coach evidence is preserved.** Exam Coach does not override progression, obstacle, or intervention meaning.
10. **Sibling coach meanings are preserved.** Exam Coach consumes Learning, Recovery, and Revision outputs without redefining them.
11. **Canonical Study Plan remains the contract.** Exam Coach may recommend emphasis within authorised examination-facing windows; it may not independently rewrite the plan.
12. **Explainability is mandatory.** Silent examination theatre and opaque readiness speech are forbidden (EIP-003).

## 7. Position Relative to Sibling Authorities

| Authority | Owns | Exam Coach relationship |
|-----------|------|-------------------------|
| **Master Planner / Canonical Study Plan** | Long-term educational envelopes, examination-facing windows, published contract | Consumes; never rewrites independently |
| **Scheduling / Rescheduling** | Allocation of examination-facing windows and protections | Escalates when allocation change is required; does not steal protections |
| **Daily Coach** | Today’s educational priority under the plan | Coordinates emphasis; never bypasses |
| **Learning Coach** | Progression, obstacles, learning interventions | Consumes evidence; never overrides |
| **Recovery Coach** | Restorative coaching after meaningful disruption | Defers when disruption is primary; never replaces |
| **Revision Coach** | Consolidating previously learned material; revision strategy and completion | Consumes warrant / strategy / completion; never replaces or redefines |
| **Educational Continuity (EIP-005)** | Persistence of learner educational history | Respects continuity; examination speech must not invent prior preparation |
| **Knowledge & Mastery (EIP-006)** | Ladder meanings; readiness must remain provisional and labelled honestly | Never collapses examination preparation into mastery or pass speech |

```
Accumulated evidence + examination preparation warrant
        │
        ▼
Exam Coach (this Model)
        │  warrant + objectives + priority emphasis + boundaries
        │
        ├─ lawful assessment-facing emphasis (within envelopes)
        │     → informs Daily Coach emphasis & session design
        │     → respects examination-facing windows already published
        │
        └─ wrong-mode detection
              → first learning / Learning Coach (no prior learning or learning barrier)
              → Revision Coach (consolidation still the primary need)
              → Recovery Coach (meaningful disruption is primary)
              → Master Planner / Scheduling (structural plan / window change)
```

## 8. Relationship to Daily Coach and Examination-Facing Windows

Daily Coach already recognises **authorised plan work**, including examination-facing or protected revision / consolidation windows when the Canonical Study Plan has placed them.

| Layer | Question | Owner |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Plan / scheduling examination windows** | Has examination-facing preparation been placed as educational intent? | Master Planner / Scheduling |
| **Daily examination coaching** | What is today’s objective under that posture? | Daily Coach |
| **Educational Examination Model** | How should the learner prepare for and approach the examination — which objectives, priorities, boundaries, and explanations? | **Exam Coach (this corpus)** |

The Exam Coach supplies the **constitutional educational meaning** of examination preparation as assessment-facing coaching.  
The Daily Coach **executes today’s priority** under the plan, including when that priority is examination preparation work.  
Neither layer may invent first-pass content as exam prep, steal Revision Coach consolidating authority, or steal protected windows for unfinished first learning without lawful escalation.

## 9. Relationship to Learning Coach

| Situation | Primary coach | Exam Coach role |
|-----------|---------------|-----------------|
| Material never studied | Learning Coach / Daily first-pass | **Refuse examination-readiness speech**; may name the gap honestly |
| Progression thin or obstacle diagnosed | Learning Coach (obstacle → intervention) | Consume diagnosis; do not invent competing exam tips that contradict evidence |
| Accumulated understanding is durable and exam-facing preparation is warranted | Exam Coach (often with Learning Coach honesty) | Recommend assessment-facing emphasis with evidence-honest speech |
| False readiness / exam theatre | Learning Coach owns honesty of readiness claims | Exam Coach must not mint readiness from mocks or volume |

Hard rule:

> **Exam Coach must not override Learning Coach evidence.  
> Examination preparation is not proof that learning progressed or that mastery exists.**

---

## 10. Relationship to Recovery Coach

| Situation | Primary coach | Exam Coach role |
|-----------|---------------|-----------------|
| Prolonged absence / meaningful disruption | Recovery Coach | Defer restorative continuity; do not disguise catch-up as examination preparation |
| Student returns near an exam with restorative need still primary | Recovery Coach primary | Exam Coach may later own ordinary assessment approach once recovery posture ends |
| Stable continuity; examination proximity is the primary question | Exam Coach | Ordinary examination preparation warrant — not a recovery episode |

Hard rule:

> **Examination preparation must never be used to compensate for absent first learning or bypass Recovery Coach responsibilities.**

---

## 11. Relationship to Revision Coach

| Situation | Primary coach | Exam Coach role |
|-----------|---------------|-----------------|
| Previously learned material needs consolidating return | Revision Coach | Defer consolidating ownership; may consume revision meaning as input |
| Planned revision incomplete; consolidation still the honest job | Revision Coach | Do not absorb revision completion; may note that exam approach waits on consolidating substance |
| Revision completion / exam-facing consolidation of *known* material warrants assessment approach | Layered | Exam Coach owns *how to prepare for and approach the sitting*; Revision Coach still owns *what / how to consolidate* when consolidation continues |
| Student asks for “exam prep” that is actually unfinished revision of known topics | Revision Coach primary for consolidating substance | Exam Coach may frame assessment conditions / approach around revision emphasis — without replacing Revision Coach |

Hard rule:

> **Exam Coach must not replace Revision Coach.  
> It consumes revision warrant, strategy, and completion meaning without redefining them.**
