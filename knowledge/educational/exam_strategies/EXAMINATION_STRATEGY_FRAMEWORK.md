## Authority

This document is the constitutional overview of **examination preparation strategies** for Kwalitec.

It is subordinate to the Educational Constitution, specialised educational models, and the Educational Examination Model (MS001). It governs **how Kwalitec classifies the kind of assessment preparation approach appropriate for this learner at this stage** while remaining faithful to Learning, Recovery, and Revision Coach evidence and the Canonical Study Plan. It does not authorise implementation shortcuts that contradict the Constitution, and it does not invent readiness, evidence, mastery, or planning meaning absent from upstream models.

Authority order for examination strategies:

> Constitution defines educational truth and curriculum primacy.  
> Knowledge & Mastery defines coverage ≠ understanding ≠ mastery.  
> Evidence Model defines what may warrant educational claims.  
> Continuity Standard preserves rightful educational history across plan change.  
> Master Planner publishes the Canonical Study Plan and owns long-term educational envelopes, including examination-facing windows.  
> Daily Coach owns *what is most educationally valuable today* under that contract.  
> Learning Coach owns longitudinal progression, obstacle diagnosis, and learning interventions.  
> Recovery Coach owns restorative coaching after meaningful disruption.  
> Revision Coach owns consolidating previously learned material for durable exam-usable strength.  
> Educational Examination Model (MS001) defines *whether* examination preparation is warranted, *which* assessment-facing goods, and *qualitative priority*.  
> **This Examination Strategy Framework (Workstream 6 / MS002) defines what kind of examination preparation is educationally appropriate for this learner at this stage.**  
> Downstream coaching and narration surfaces must consume this educational reasoning — never replace learning, revision, recovery, or planning with strategy theatre.

## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not apply one generic “do more past papers” mode to every lawful examination-preparation need. Steadying demonstration of already-prepared material, authentic rehearsal under sitting conditions, time discipline, question-selection judgement, confidence calibrated to evidence, final pre-sitting consolidation, and careful return to assessment-facing work after disruption each call for a **different preparation approach** — while remaining honest that learning and revision have already done (or must still do) their primary work, and while never pretending that exam tips cure absent understanding, unfinished consolidation, or unresolved disruption.

After a lawful examination preparation warrant and priority emphasis, the tutor answers one living educational question:

> **“What kind of examination preparation is educationally appropriate for this learner at this stage?”**

That answer must fit the educational evidence profile, pursue MS001 examination objectives, remain faithful to Canonical Study Plan educational intent, preserve Daily Coach and sibling coach authorities, refuse compensation for absent learning, refuse override of Revision Coach recommendations, and escalate honestly upstream when examination preparation reveals unresolved educational weaknesses.

This document records that tutor posture so every future Exam Coach subsystem has a single educational reference for *strategy meaning*.

> **The Examination Strategy Framework describes educational strategy reasoning.  
> It does not implement Runtime A logic, algorithms, analytics, or Twin writers.**

## 2. What an Examination Strategy Is

An **examination strategy** is a named **educational approach archetype** that describes the *kind* of assessment-facing coaching appropriate for this learner at this stage — not a calendar packing algorithm, not a score band, not a second study plan, and not a substitute for consolidating revision.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Evidence profile** | Educational reading of what assessment-facing need the trail shows for this prepared learner | What kind of exam-preparation need is this? |
| **Examination strategy** | Named assessment-preparation approach matched to that profile | What kind of preparation fits? |
| **Strategy purpose** | Educational job this approach primarily does | What are we trying to prepare by this route? |
| **Strategy objectives** | MS001 EXO-XX goods this strategy primarily advances | Which assessment-facing goods lead? |
| **Strategy success** | Educational signs that this approach has done its job | When may we transition or lighten? |
| **Strategy limit** | Situations where another strategy — or Learning / Recovery / Revision handoff — is preferable | When is this the wrong approach? |

A strategy is:

- **evidence-matched** — selected from educational need, not operational convenience;
- **objective-bound** — advances EXO-01…EXO-05 rather than exam theatre;
- **prior-preparation-gated** — never applies where first learning, unfinished consolidating revision as primary job, or Recovery-primary disruption is the honest frame;
- **plan-faithful** — respects Canonical Study Plan examination-facing windows and protections;
- **coach-coordinated** — informs Daily Coach; consumes Learning / Recovery / Revision meanings; never replaces them;
- **transition-aware** — may move to another strategy, toward final preparation, or hand off upstream;
- **explainable** — students understand why this approach, what evidence, what outcome, what would change the choice;
- **non-scoring** — qualitative educational judgement only.

A strategy is **not**:

- a substitute for first-pass Learning Mode or Learning Coach progression of unlearned material;
- a licence to override or absorb Revision Coach consolidating recommendations;
- a bypass of Recovery Coach when meaningful disruption is the true problem;
- inference of mastery or exam certainty from mocks, question volume, or strategy compliance;
- a rewrite of the Canonical Study Plan or Master Planner educational strategy;
- a duplicate of MS001 triggers or priorities under new labels without educational distinction;
- a claim that completing a strategy guarantees a pass;
- a cure for unresolved learning, revision, or recovery problems through more assessment theatre.

## 3. Binding Distinction: Strategy vs Neighbours

| Layer | Educational job | Unlawful collapse |
|-------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Trigger (EPT)** | Warrant that examination preparation is needed | Selecting a strategy without a warrant |
| **Objective (EXO)** | Name assessment-facing goods to advance | Treating mock volume as an objective |
| **Priority (EXP)** | Order which emphases lead | Treating priority as a numeric score |
| **Strategy (EXS)** | Name the *kind* of examination preparation approach | Equating strategy with a new long-term plan, with revision, or with first learning |
| **Daily Coach** | Own today’s educational priority under the contract | Letting strategy speech commandeer the day |
| **Learning Coach** | Own progression / obstacle / intervention meaning | Using exam tips to skip diagnosis or override evidence |
| **Recovery Coach** | Own restorative continuity after disruption | Disguising catch-up as an examination strategy |
| **Revision Coach** | Own consolidating warrant / strategy / completion | Overriding unfinished consolidation with “exam prep” |
| **Master Planner / Scheduling** | Own structural envelope and allocation change | Absorbing replan inside strategy “completion” |

Hard rule:

> **Strategies classify assessment-preparation approaches.  
> They do not mint plans, mastery, day priorities, first learning, revision completion, or recovery episodes.**

## 4. What the Strategy Framework Produces

Given a **lawful examination preparation warrant**, **priority emphasis**, and **accumulated educational evidence**, the Strategy Framework produces:

| Output family | Role |
|---------------|------|
| **Primary strategy** | Named EXS-XX approach type |
| **Supporting strategy (optional)** | Secondary archetype when needs stack — speech still names one primary |
| **Strategy rationale** | Educational link from evidence profile → strategy |
| **Objectives in focus** | MS001 EXO-XX goods this strategy advances |
| **Expected educational outcome** | What preparation should achieve if the approach is followed |
| **Success recognition** | Educational cues that the strategy is working |
| **Transition posture** | Stay / change strategy / move toward final preparation / hand off upstream / post-sitting reflection |
| **Strategy explainability** | Plain-language account for the student |

It answers:

| Question | Educational answer |
|----------|-------------------|
| What kind of examination preparation? | Named strategy from the catalogue |
| Why this kind? | Evidence profile + objectives + plan posture + sibling coach meanings |
| What outcome is expected? | Expected assessment-facing outcome |
| How will success be recognised? | Success signs under the strategy |
| May Exam Coach enact this alone? | Only within MS001 boundaries; else escalate / hand off |
| When does this strategy end or change? | Transition rules |

## 5. Educational Purpose

The Strategy Framework exists so that:

1. **Different assessment-facing needs receive different approaches** — not one “more past papers” mode.
2. **Strategy choice remains educational** — derived from tutoring principles, not UI or optimiser convenience.
3. **Sibling coach meanings stay binding** — strategies consume Learning / Recovery / Revision meaning; they never override it.
4. **Examination preparation stays examination preparation** — strategies never invent first learning, absorb revision, or bypass recovery.
5. **Readiness stays honest** — strategies prepare demonstration of accumulated understanding; they do not mint coverage or certainty.
6. **Transitions are honest** — final preparation, strategy change, Revision / Recovery / Learning handoffs, and post-sitting reflection are named educational events.
7. **Students understand the approach** — why this kind of preparation, what evidence, what outcome, what would select a different strategy.

## 6. Core Responsibilities

The Strategy Framework is educationally responsible for:

| Responsibility | Meaning |
|----------------|---------|
| **Name lawful strategies** | Catalogue assessment-preparation approaches from educational principles |
| **Select after warrant** | Match evidence profile → EXS-XX without inventing warrant |
| **Bind to objectives** | Declare which EXO-XX goods the strategy advances |
| **Govern transitions** | Final preparation, strategy change, upstream-coach handoffs, post-sitting reflection |
| **Protect authority boundaries** | Never absorb first learning, Revision Coach, Recovery Coach, mastery, or plan rewrite |
| **Explain strategy choice** | Make approach selection speakable with evidence and expected outcomes |

It is **not** educationally responsible for:

- inventing examination preparation warrant or prior preparation honesty;
- deciding which assessment-facing goods lead when MS001 priority is unsettled;
- diagnosing learning obstacles or selecting Learning Interventions;
- restoring progress after meaningful disruption;
- deciding what to revise, which revision strategy fits, or whether revision is complete;
- rewriting Canonical Study Plans or educational strategy;
- deciding today’s Daily Coach primary objective by commandeering day authority;
- producing opaque “strategy scores” or readiness metrics as educational proof.

## 7. Binding Educational Doctrine

1. **Strategies optimise assessment preparation.** They do not compensate for absent learning, override Revision Coach recommendations, redefine mastery, or modify the Canonical Study Plan.
2. **Warrant first.** No EXS without an explicit examination preparation warrant and prior-preparation honesty (`EXAM_PREPARATION_TRIGGERS.md`).
3. **Evidence honesty.** Strategy selection cites accumulated educational evidence — never calendar guilt or mock volume alone.
4. **One primary in speech.** Supporting strategies may exist; students hear one primary assessment-preparation approach.
5. **Learning Coach evidence is preserved.** Strategies consume, never override, progression / obstacle / intervention meaning.
6. **Revision Coach recommendations are preserved.** Strategies consume revision warrant / strategy / completion; they never override unfinished consolidating ownership.
7. **Unresolved weaknesses escalate upstream.** Where examination preparation reveals unresolved educational weaknesses, authority returns to Learning, Recovery, or Revision Coach rather than compensating inside Exam Coach.
8. **Daily Coach authority is preserved.** Strategy informs how today’s assessment-facing work is shaped; it does not commandeer day-priority selection.
9. **Canonical Study Plan remains the contract.** Strategies may shape emphasis within authorised examination-facing windows; they may not independently rewrite the plan.
10. **Mastery is never redefined by strategy.** Strategy compliance does not mint understanding or exam readiness (EIP-006).
11. **Explainability is mandatory.** Silent strategy switches and opaque approach speech are forbidden (EIP-003).

## 10. Relationship to Learning, Recovery, and Revision Coaches (Architectural Hard Line)

| Situation | Primary authority | Strategy Framework role |
|-----------|-------------------|-------------------------|
| Lawful assessment-facing need on prepared material | Exam Coach strategy | Select EXS; pursue EXO goods |
| Consolidating revision becomes / remains the dominant honest job | Revision Coach | Do **not** override; hand back (EST2) |
| Learning obstacle / thin genuine progression revealed under exam practice | Learning Coach | Do **not** compensate with more exam tips; escalate |
| Meaningful disruption is / becomes primary | Recovery Coach | Defer; use EXS-07 only after Recovery allows assessment-facing return |
| False readiness theatre | Learning / Revision honesty | Strategies must not mint readiness |

Architectural requirement restated:

> **Examination strategies optimise assessment preparation.  
> Where examination preparation reveals unresolved educational weaknesses,  
> authority must transition back to the appropriate upstream coach  
> rather than attempting to solve the problem within the Exam Coach.**

## 11. Distinction from Revision Strategy RVS-06

**RVS-06 — Examination Consolidation** (Revision Coach MS002) consolidates *already learned* material into exam-usable strength as a **revision** approach.

**Examination Strategies (EXS-XX)** shape **assessment approach** once Exam Coach is lawfully primary: rehearsal, time management, question selection, confidence, final consolidation of *preparation posture*, and careful post-disruption exam return.

They may coordinate. They must not collapse:

| If the honest job is… | Owner |
|-----------------------|-------|
| Strengthening durability / retrieval / fragile known material | Revision Coach (RVS-*) |
| Coaching sitting approach, conditions, selection, confidence, final prep posture | Exam Coach (EXS-*) |
| Both layered | Name both authorities; one primary in speech |
