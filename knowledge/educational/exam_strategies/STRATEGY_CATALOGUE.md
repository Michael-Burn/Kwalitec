## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor carries a mental catalogue of *kinds* of examination preparation: consolidating readiness to demonstrate what is already prepared; rehearsing under authentic sitting conditions; building time-management discipline; practising question-selection judgement; stabilising confidence calibrated to evidence; final consolidation of preparation posture as the sitting approaches; and carefully restoring assessment-facing preparation after Recovery has made continuity safe again.

This catalogue records those educational approaches so Exam Coach selection, transitions, and explainability share one vocabulary — while remaining consistent with the Educational Examination Model and sibling coaches.

---

## 2. Catalogue Principles

1. **Educational first.** Strategies come from IFoA tutoring practice and assessment-preparation principles — not from which feature flag or calendar trick is easiest.
2. **Approaches, not syllabus lists.** Strategies shape *how* examination preparation is approached; MS001 priority still decides *which* emphases lead.
3. **Evidence-profile linked.** Each strategy declares the educational need profile it is built for.
4. **Objective-aware.** Each strategy primarily advances specific EXO-01…EXO-05 goods.
5. **Non-exclusive.** Needs may stack; prefer a **primary** strategy in speech.
6. **Reversible.** Strategies are provisional; they change as evidence and preparation progress evolve.
7. **Non-punitive.** Names describe preparation approaches — never character or moral deficit (“you panic because you are weak”).
8. **Plan-faithful.** Strategies preserve authorised examination-window meaning; they do not invent unofficial syllabus routes.
9. **Sibling-coach-humble.** Strategies refuse to compensate for absent learning, unfinished revision, or Recovery-owned disruption.
10. **No fitness scores.** Qualitative matching only — never numeric strategy ranks as educational law.

## 3. Framework Logic (Before Labels)

Before naming a strategy, the tutor asks, in order:

| Order | Tutor question | If unclear |
|-------|----------------|------------|
| 1 | Is there a lawful examination preparation warrant with prior-preparation honesty (not first learning / not Revision-primary / not Recovery-primary)? | Do not open a strategy |
| 2 | What primarily needs assessment-facing work — demonstration readiness, rehearsal under conditions, time discipline, question selection, confidence calibration, final pre-sitting consolidation, or careful post-disruption exam return? | Gather evidence; prefer gentler readiness consolidation when the trail is already steady |
| 3 | Which EXO-XX goods must lead? | Do not select a strategy that optimises volume theatre |
| 4 | Does Learning / Revision / Recovery evidence show an unresolved weakness that Exam Coach cannot honestly own? | Hand off upstream; do not invent a strategy as compensation |
| 5 | Can the approach stay inside current plan / day envelopes? | Prefer local strategy; escalate structural change honestly |

Examples such as “Examination Rehearsal” are **illustrative names** for approach types produced by this logic. Binding meanings are the definitions below.

## 4. Strategy Catalogue

Each entry records: educational meaning, suitable evidence profile, primary examination objectives, typical triggers, expected educational outcome, what it is not, and a constructive speech cue.

IDs (`EXS-XX`) exist for audit and cross-reference. They must not appear as student-facing jargon.

Detailed selection cards (evidence required, success signs, when another strategy is preferable) live in `STRATEGY_SELECTION.md`. Transition behaviour lives in `STRATEGY_TRANSITIONS.md`.

### EXS-01 — Readiness Consolidation

| Aspect | Provision |
|--------|-----------|
| **Educational meaning** | An assessment-preparation approach that steadies the learner’s ability to **demonstrate already-prepared understanding** under examination-facing conditions — bringing accumulated learning and revision into coherent demonstration readiness without inventing new coverage |
| **Suitable evidence profile** | Lawful examination warrant; prior learning and revision substance exists for the scope; readiness posture is usable but demonstration under assessment-facing practice still needs consolidating; Revision Coach does not currently own consolidating primary for the same problem |
| **Primary objectives** | EXO-01, then EXO-02 as structure warrants |
| **Typical triggers** | EPT examination-window / revision-completion / readiness-review families when demonstration readiness (not only timed grinding) is the leading story |
| **Expected educational outcome** | The student can more reliably show what has already been learned and revised under exam-facing practice; readiness speech remains provisional and evidence-honest |
| **What it is not** | First-pass cramming; overriding unfinished Revision Coach work; minting “exam ready” from volume; absorbing RVS consolidating ownership under a new label |
| **Speech cue** | “You’ve built the understanding — we’ll consolidate how you show it under exam-facing practice, not invent chapters you haven’t studied.” |

---

### EXS-02 — Examination Rehearsal

| Aspect | Provision |
|--------|-----------|
| **Educational meaning** | An assessment-preparation approach that rehearses **authentic sitting conditions** — timed, format-faithful practice of *already prepared* material — so performance under assessment constraints becomes familiar without pretending rehearsal creates understanding |
| **Suitable evidence profile** | Prior preparation honesty holds; known material is fragile specifically under timed / format pressure, or authorised final / exam-window posture makes authentic rehearsal the honest job; Learning Coach evidence does not contradict demonstration claims for the rehearsed scope |
| **Primary objectives** | EXO-03, EXO-01; EXO-05 as sitting judgement appears under rehearsal |
| **Typical triggers** | Final-period / exam-window EPTs when conditions management leads; readiness review showing conditions fragility on prepared material |
| **Expected educational outcome** | Steadier performance of known material under timed / format constraints; clearer sense of sitting rhythm without pass promises |
| **What it is not** | Timed grinding of unlearned content; a substitute for Revision Coach when consolidating substance is still primary; certainty minted from one mock script |
| **Speech cue** | “We’ll rehearse under real sitting conditions with material you already know — so timing and format stop being a surprise.” |

---

### EXS-03 — Time-Management Preparation

| Aspect | Provision |
|--------|-----------|
| **Educational meaning** | An assessment-preparation approach that builds **time discipline** for the examination period and sitting — allocating attention across known strengths and fragile known topics, pacing sections, and protecting marks already within reach |
| **Suitable evidence profile** | Prior preparation honesty holds; evidence or rehearsal shows time sinks, uneven pacing, or weak examination planning around time; approach structure (EXO-02) is the binding gap more than raw knowledge gaps |
| **Primary objectives** | EXO-02, EXO-05; EXO-03 as timing under conditions intertwines |
| **Typical triggers** | Exam-window / registration / final-period EPTs when planning and pacing lead; rehearsal evidence of time mismanagement |
| **Expected educational outcome** | A speakable sitting time plan grounded in accumulated strengths and risks; fewer catastrophic time sinks on prepared material |
| **What it is not** | A plan that silently rewrites the Canonical Study Plan; packing unlearned syllabus into “time management”; numeric readiness schedules as educational proof |
| **Speech cue** | “We’ll practise how you use the clock — protecting what you can already do, and avoiding time sinks that cost marks you could earn.” |

---

### EXS-04 — Question-Selection Practice

| Aspect | Provision |
|--------|-----------|
| **Educational meaning** | An assessment-preparation approach that strengthens **method and question selection judgement** under assessment pressure — choosing approaches, deciding when to move on, and applying known techniques wisely inside the paper |
| **Suitable evidence profile** | Prior preparation honesty holds; sitting judgement (not primarily missing syllabus content) limits application of known material; Learning Coach / revision evidence supports that methods exist but selection under pressure is weak |
| **Primary objectives** | EXO-05, EXO-01; EXO-02 as selection sits inside an examination plan |
| **Typical triggers** | Readiness review / rehearsal / final-period EPTs when decision-making inside the paper leads |
| **Expected educational outcome** | Clearer, speakable decision rules for selecting methods and questions; better use of accumulated strengths under pressure |
| **What it is not** | Trick lists that invent coverage; replacing understanding with heuristics; contradicting Learning Coach honesty about what cannot yet be done |
| **Speech cue** | “We’ll practise choosing the right approach inside the paper — so your judgement protects the marks your understanding already supports.” |

---

### EXS-05 — Confidence Stabilisation

| Aspect | Provision |
|--------|-----------|
| **Educational meaning** | An assessment-preparation approach that **calibrates examination confidence to evidence** — reducing unjustified panic and unjustified certainty so the student approaches the sitting calmly and purposefully without false readiness claims |
| **Suitable evidence profile** | Prior preparation honesty holds; confidence is misaligned with accumulated evidence (panic despite durable prepared material, or overconfidence despite named fragile known topics); Learning / Revision honesty can ground confidence speech |
| **Primary objectives** | EXO-04, with EXO-01 honesty; never as substitute for missing learning or unfinished revision |
| **Typical triggers** | Readiness review / final-period EPTs when affective calibration is the leading assessment-facing need; may support other EXS as secondary |
| **Expected educational outcome** | Confidence speech matches evidence; fragile areas named without collapsing the whole preparation story; calmer purposeful approach |
| **What it is not** | Pass promises; hiding gaps with pep talks; moralising ordinary exam nerves; using confidence to override Learning / Revision / Recovery ownership |
| **Speech cue** | “We’ll steady your confidence to match what the evidence actually shows — calm and purposeful, not false certainty.” |

---

### EXS-06 — Final Consolidation

| Aspect | Provision |
|--------|-----------|
| **Educational meaning** | An assessment-preparation approach for the **final pre-sitting period** that lightens breadth, protects known strengths, rehearses sitting posture lightly, and consolidates examination approach without inventing late coverage or panic theatre |
| **Suitable evidence profile** | Authorised final examination period / proximity; prior preparation honesty for the sitting scope; intensive new learning or heavy consolidating revision is no longer the honest primary story (or those needs are already named as handoffs); plan posture supports final assessment-facing emphasis |
| **Primary objectives** | EXO-02, EXO-03, EXO-01; EXO-04 without certainty inflation; EXO-05 for final sitting rules |
| **Typical triggers** | Final-period EPT family; may follow successful EXS-01 / EXS-02 / EXS-03 / EXS-04 as the sitting approaches (see transitions) |
| **Expected educational outcome** | Coherent final preparation posture; protected known strengths; sitting plan and conditions familiarity without late-syllabus invention |
| **What it is not** | Last-minute first-pass of unread units; overriding Revision Coach when consolidation suddenly becomes primary again; “you are exam ready” declarations; whole-syllabus panic packing |
| **Speech cue** | “In this final stretch we’ll consolidate your examination approach and protect what you already know — not invent coverage at the last minute.” |

---

### EXS-07 — Post-Disruption Examination Preparation

| Aspect | Provision |
|--------|-----------|
| **Educational meaning** | An assessment-preparation approach that carefully **restores lawful Exam Coach work after Recovery Coach** has made educational continuity safe enough — re-entering assessment-facing preparation without treating disruption as ordinary final prep or inventing catch-up as exam tips |
| **Suitable evidence profile** | Meaningful disruption occurred; Recovery Coach posture now allows assessment-facing return (or Recovery completion / handoff explicitly permits Exam Coach layering); prior preparation honesty still holds for the scope being prepared; the student needs gentle re-entry to examination approach rather than Recovery catch-up disguise |
| **Primary objectives** | EXO-02 and EXO-04 first (re-anchor approach and evidence-calibrated confidence); then EXO-01 / EXO-03 as safety allows |
| **Typical triggers** | Resume after Recovery; exam-window still active but disruption trail exists; never while Recovery remains primary |
| **Expected educational outcome** | Honest re-entry to examination preparation; approach and confidence rebuilt on restored continuity; no false claim that disruption never mattered |
| **What it is not** | Opening ordinary EXS-06 as if illness / absence were a quiet week; Recovery catch-up labelled as exam prep; bypassing Recovery while disruption is still primary |
| **Speech cue** | “You’ve restored study continuity — we’ll re-enter examination preparation carefully, starting from your evidence, not from panic catch-up.” |

## 6. Explicit Non-Strategies (Refuse)

The following are **not** lawful entries in this catalogue:

| Unlawful pseudo-strategy | Why refused | Lawful alternative |
|--------------------------|-------------|--------------------|
| First-pass under exam-prep label | No prior preparation honesty | First learning / Daily Coach Learning Mode |
| Revision override as exam strategy | Consolidating ownership is Revision Coach | Hand back to Revision Coach (EST2) |
| Recovery catch-up as exam strategy | Disruption owns the moment | Recovery Coach; EXS-07 only after Recovery allows |
| Obstacle cure by more mocks | Deeper learning problem | Learning Coach diagnosis → intervention |
| Whole-syllabus late cram including unlearned units | Invents coverage | Split: first learning / revision for gaps; Exam Coach only for prepared scope |
| Mastery minting / “exam ready” declaration | EIP-006 honesty | Provisional assessment-facing outcome speech |
| Numeric strategy optimiser as educational law | Non-scoring corpus | Qualitative selection in `STRATEGY_SELECTION.md` |
| Relabelled RVS-06 without Exam Coach warrant | Collapses revision into Exam Coach | Keep RVS-06 under Revision Coach; open EXS only after EPT warrant |
