## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor faced with lawful examination preparation does not grab a familiar “do more past papers” mode. The tutor asks what assessment-facing need the evidence shows, which examination goods must lead, whether sibling coaches still own the true problem, what success would look like, and when another strategy — or upstream handoff — would be wiser.

This document records that selection discipline so Exam Coach strategy recommendations remain educationally honest.

For each strategy, selection cards state:

- educational objective,
- suitable evidence profile,
- appropriate examination context,
- expected educational outcome,
- situations where another strategy (or handoff) is preferable.

---

## 2. Selection Principles

1. **Warrant first.** No EXS without an explicit examination preparation warrant and prior-preparation honesty (`EXAM_PREPARATION_TRIGGERS.md`).
2. **Match assessment-facing need, not calendar guilt.** Phase labels alone never select EXS-06 or intensive EXS-02.
3. **One primary in speech.** Supporting strategies may exist; students hear one primary examination approach.
4. **Smallest sufficient approach.** Prefer EXS-01 when steadying demonstration readiness suffices before intensive rehearsal.
5. **Sibling-coach humility.** If revision, recovery, or learning evidence shows those authorities own the problem, hand off rather than force a strategy.
6. **Objective specificity.** Name which EXO-XX goods the strategy advances.
7. **Envelope awareness.** Strategy informs emphasis within Canonical Study Plan / Daily Coach authority; escalate structural change honestly.
8. **Evidence honesty.** Conditions fragility / confidence misalignment / selection weakness claims require Educational Evidence warrant — never absence inference alone.
9. **Revision humility.** Do not override Revision Coach recommendations; if consolidating revision is primary, refuse EXS as primary.
10. **No numerical scoring.** No weights, ranks, fitness scores, or readiness maths as educational law.

---

## 3. Selection Procedure (Educational)

The tutor follows this order. Implementations may later encode equivalents; they may not skip educational steps.

| Step | Action | Output |
|------|--------|--------|
| 1 | Confirm **prior-preparation honesty** and **examination preparation warrant** | EPT-XX + wrong-mode rejected |
| 2 | If first learning, Revision-primary, or Recovery-primary | **No strategy** — hand off |
| 3 | Confirm **priority emphasis** under MS001 (EXP) | Leading assessment-facing emphasis |
| 4 | Name **evidence profile** in educational language | Demonstration readiness / conditions / time / selection / confidence / final posture / post-disruption return |
| 5 | Consult **Learning / Revision / Recovery posture** | Proceed / escalate if unresolved weakness |
| 6 | Name **EXO-XX goods** that must lead | Priority among EXO-01…EXO-05 |
| 7 | List **candidate strategies** from Catalogue matched to the profile | Shortlist of EXS-XX |
| 8 | Eliminate candidates that **must not be used** given evidence, Profile, envelopes, sibling coach posture | Reduced shortlist |
| 9 | Prefer **smallest sufficient** primary strategy | Primary EXS |
| 10 | State **expected educational outcome** and **success recognition** | Outcome + success cues |
| 11 | If Exam Coach alone cannot honestly help | Transition to **upstream handoff** (`STRATEGY_TRANSITIONS.md`) — do not invent exam theatre |
| 12 | Produce **explainability** under `STRATEGY_EXPLAINABILITY.md` | Student-facing account |

Skipping from “exam phase” directly to a strategy is unlawful.

## 4. Preferential Reading Order (Qualitative)

When multiple strategies fit, apply this qualitative order — **not** a numeric rank:

1. **Hand off upstream** if Learning / Recovery / Revision owns the true problem (no EXS as compensation)  
2. **EXS-07** if Recovery has just restored continuity and careful exam re-entry is required (do not jump to ordinary final prep)  
3. **EXS-05** if confidence misalignment is the leading assessment-facing problem *and* learning / revision substance already supports honest confidence speech  
4. **EXS-04** if sitting judgement / question selection on known material leads  
5. **EXS-03** if examination planning / time discipline is the binding gap  
6. **EXS-02** if authentic conditions rehearsal of prepared material leads  
7. **EXS-01** if demonstration readiness consolidation is the leading (or default sufficient) need  
8. **EXS-06** when authorised final period makes final consolidation of examination approach the leading story — may lighten form of the above without inventing coverage  

Envelope breakage or sibling-coach primary ownership at any point → hand off rather than force a local strategy.

## 5. Per-Strategy Selection Cards

Each card below is binding educational selection law for that EXS.

---

### EXS-01 — Readiness Consolidation

| Aspect | Provision |
|--------|-----------|
| **Educational objective** | Steady demonstration of already-prepared understanding under examination-facing practice (EXO-01) |
| **Suitable evidence profile** | Prior learning and revision substance exists; readiness is usable but demonstration still needs consolidating; no Recovery-primary disruption; Revision Coach does not require consolidating primary for the same weakness |
| **Appropriate examination context** | Exam-window or readiness-review posture; not necessarily last week before the paper |
| **Expected educational outcome** | More reliable demonstration of accumulated understanding; provisional readiness speech grounded in evidence |
| **Prefer another strategy / handoff when** | Conditions fragility leads → EXS-02; time planning leads → EXS-03; sitting judgement leads → EXS-04; confidence misalignment leads → EXS-05; final period leads → EXS-06; Recovery just ended → EXS-07; consolidating revision primary → Revision Coach; understanding never formed → Learning Coach |

---

### EXS-02 — Examination Rehearsal

| Aspect | Provision |
|--------|-----------|
| **Educational objective** | Manage assessment conditions by authentic rehearsal of prepared material (EXO-03, EXO-01) |
| **Suitable evidence profile** | Known material weakens specifically under timed / format pressure, or authorised proximity makes format-faithful rehearsal the honest job |
| **Appropriate examination context** | Exam-window / final period with enough prepared scope to rehearse honestly |
| **Expected educational outcome** | Steadier timed / format performance of known material; familiar sitting rhythm without pass promises |
| **Prefer another strategy / handoff when** | Demonstration readiness alone is enough → EXS-01; time sinks dominate without needing full paper rehearsal → EXS-03; unlearned content would be ground under timer → first learning / refuse; fragile known hubs need consolidating revision → Revision Coach; thin genuine understanding → Learning Coach |

---

### EXS-03 — Time-Management Preparation

| Aspect | Provision |
|--------|-----------|
| **Educational objective** | Build effective examination planning and pacing (EXO-02, EXO-05) |
| **Suitable evidence profile** | Rehearsal or approach evidence shows time mismanagement, uneven section pacing, or weak pre-sitting time structure on prepared material |
| **Appropriate examination context** | After some sense of prepared strengths/risks exists; often after or alongside light EXS-01 / EXS-02 |
| **Expected educational outcome** | Speakable sitting time plan; fewer catastrophic time sinks |
| **Prefer another strategy / handoff when** | Conditions unfamiliarity is primary → EXS-02; question selection judgement leads → EXS-04; plan rewrite is required to fit time → Master Planner / Scheduling (EST6); missing content is the real time problem → first learning / Revision honesty |

---

### EXS-04 — Question-Selection Practice

| Aspect | Provision |
|--------|-----------|
| **Educational objective** | Support sound decision-making during assessment (EXO-05) on known material |
| **Suitable evidence profile** | Methods exist but selection under pressure is weak; Learning / Revision evidence supports that the limiting factor is judgement, not absent syllabus |
| **Appropriate examination context** | Readiness review, rehearsal feedback, or final period when sitting decisions limit performance |
| **Expected educational outcome** | Clear decision rules for method / question selection; better use of accumulated strengths |
| **Prefer another strategy / handoff when** | Time allocation alone is the gap → EXS-03; confidence panic drives poor selection → EXS-05 (may support); methods never formed → Learning Coach; consolidating retrieval of known methods is primary → Revision Coach |

---

### EXS-05 — Confidence Stabilisation

| Aspect | Provision |
|--------|-----------|
| **Educational objective** | Reinforce examination confidence calibrated to evidence (EXO-04) |
| **Suitable evidence profile** | Panic despite durable prepared material, or overconfidence despite named fragile known topics; evidence trail can ground confidence speech |
| **Appropriate examination context** | Any lawful exam-window where affective misalignment harms approach — especially near the sitting |
| **Expected educational outcome** | Confidence matches evidence; calmer purposeful approach; fragile areas named without whole-story collapse |
| **Prefer another strategy / handoff when** | Confidence issues are really missing learning → Learning Coach; unfinished consolidating revision → Revision Coach; Recovery-owned disruption → Recovery Coach; final approach consolidation needed with steady confidence → EXS-06; pep talk would invent readiness → refuse |

---

### EXS-06 — Final Consolidation

| Aspect | Provision |
|--------|-----------|
| **Educational objective** | Consolidate examination approach in the final pre-sitting period (EXO-02, EXO-03, EXO-01) without late coverage invention |
| **Suitable evidence profile** | Authorised final period; prior preparation honesty for sitting scope; intensive first-pass or heavy consolidating revision is not the honest primary (or already named as handoff) |
| **Appropriate examination context** | Final stretch before the sitting under Canonical Study Plan examination-facing intent |
| **Expected educational outcome** | Coherent final posture; protected known strengths; sitting plan and light conditions familiarity |
| **Prefer another strategy / handoff when** | Final period not yet reached and demonstration readiness still leads → EXS-01; heavy conditions unfamiliarity remains → EXS-02; post-disruption return not yet careful → EXS-07; revision evidence becomes dominant again → Revision Coach (EST2); disruption returns → Recovery Coach (EST3) |

---

### EXS-07 — Post-Disruption Examination Preparation

| Aspect | Provision |
|--------|-----------|
| **Educational objective** | Re-enter assessment-facing preparation after Recovery restores continuity (EXO-02, EXO-04 → EXO-01/03) |
| **Suitable evidence profile** | Disruption trail exists; Recovery Coach no longer primary (or explicitly permits Exam Coach layering); prior preparation honesty still holds for target scope |
| **Appropriate examination context** | Resume into an exam-window after restorative continuity — never while Recovery owns the moment |
| **Expected educational outcome** | Honest, gentle re-entry to examination preparation without catch-up theatre |
| **Prefer another strategy / handoff when** | Recovery still primary → Recovery Coach; continuity restored and ordinary readiness consolidation fits → transition toward EXS-01; final period with stable continuity → EXS-06; learning / revision weaknesses dominate → Learning / Revision Coach |

---

## 6. Cross-Cutting Refusal Rules

Refuse any EXS selection when:

1. Prior-preparation honesty fails for the scope claimed.  
2. Revision Coach consolidating recommendations would be overridden.  
3. Recovery Coach still owns restorative continuity.  
4. Learning Coach evidence contradicts demonstration / readiness claims the strategy would require.  
5. The strategy would redefine mastery or promise a pass.  
6. The strategy would independently modify the Canonical Study Plan.  
7. The only “evidence” is calendar proximity or desired mock volume.

Architectural requirement restated:

> **Examination strategies optimise assessment preparation.  
> They must never compensate for absent learning, override Revision Coach recommendations,  
> redefine mastery, or modify the Canonical Study Plan.**
