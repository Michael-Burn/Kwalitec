> **Transitions preserve educational continuity and authority boundaries.  
> Strategies may evolve toward final preparation.  
> Where revision evidence becomes dominant, authority returns to the Revision Coach.  
> Where significant disruption occurs, authority returns to the Recovery Coach.  
> Where unresolved learning weaknesses appear, authority returns to the Learning Coach.  
> After the sitting, transition to post-examination reflection — not endless exam theatre.**

Identifier note: Examination Strategy Transitions use **EST-XX** so they never collide with Revision Strategy Transitions (**ST-XX**) or Recovery Pathway Transitions (**T-XX**).

---

## 1. Purpose

Without transition law, examination preparation becomes a permanent paper-grinding mode, a confusing sequence of unexplained “approaches,” or a loop that papers over Learning, Recovery, or Revision problems with more assessment theatre.

With transition law, an expert tutor can:

- move toward final preparation as the sitting approaches;
- change strategy when the assessment-facing need evolves;
- hand back to Revision Coach when consolidating evidence becomes dominant;
- hand off to Recovery Coach if significant disruption occurs;
- escalate to Learning Coach when unresolved learning weaknesses appear;
- transition to post-examination reflection after the sitting;
- keep Daily Coach and Canonical Study Plan authorities intact across those moves.

---

## 2. Transition Principles

1. **Continuity of learner history.** Strategy change does not wipe educational identity (EIP-005).  
2. **Explain before switch.** Material transitions are speakable under `STRATEGY_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.  
3. **One primary strategy at a time** in student speech.  
4. **Proximity may lighten breadth** — final consolidation protects known strengths; it does not invent late coverage.  
5. **Unresolved weaknesses are educational information** — not moral failure, and not a licence for infinite exam tips.  
6. **Escalate upstream rather than fake Exam Coach success** when Learning / Recovery / Revision owns the problem.  
7. **Do not independently replan** during transition — Master Planner / Scheduling own structural change.  
8. **Daily Coach remains day authority** under the current authorised contract before and after transition.  
9. **Sibling coach meanings persist** across strategy change — completing a strategy is not proof of mastery or pass certainty.  
10. **Recovery and Revision boundaries remain sacred** — strategy transitions never bypass them.  
11. **No streak or score gates.** Transitions are educational judgements, not numeric unlocks.

## 3. Transition Classes

| Class | Meaning | Typical destination |
|-------|---------|---------------------|
| **EST1 — Toward final preparation** | Assessment-facing work shifts into authorised final consolidation posture | EXS-06 (or lightened form of current EXS) |
| **EST2 — Revision Coach handback** | Revision evidence becomes / remains dominant; consolidating ownership returns | Revision Coach (warrant / strategy / completion) |
| **EST3 — Recovery Coach handoff** | Significant disruption becomes / remains primary | Recovery Coach restorative ownership |
| **EST4 — Learning Coach escalation** | Examination preparation reveals unresolved learning weakness | Learning Coach diagnosis / intervention |
| **EST5 — Strategy change within Exam Coach** | Same examination episode; different EXS better fits evolving profile | Another EXS-XX as primary |
| **EST6 — Structural escalation** | Strategy need cannot fit authorised windows / envelopes | Rescheduling / Master Planner pathways |
| **EST7 — Post-examination reflection** | The sitting has occurred; assessment-facing preparation ends as primary | Reflection / review pathways — not endless EXS |
| **EST8 — Hold / gather** | Warrant or success unclear | Stay on gentle EXS-01 or authorised return; gather evidence — do not invent crisis or false readiness |

## 4. EST1 — Transition Toward Final Preparation

### 4.1 Educational meaning

The learner is in (or entering) an authorised final examination period. Intensive breadth of examination preparation gives way to **final consolidation of examination approach** — protecting known strengths, light rehearsal of sitting posture, and calm confidence — without late-syllabus invention.

### 4.2 Criteria (qualitative)

Move toward **EXS-06** (or lighten the current EXS into final form) when **all** of the following can be honestly affirmed:

1. Canonical Study Plan / Profile / sitting context supports final-period examination-facing posture.  
2. Prior-preparation honesty still holds for the sitting scope (gaps named, not disguised).  
3. Primary EXO goods for earlier intensive strategies show enough improvement — **or** remaining issues are owned by upstream coaches (EST2–EST4).  
4. Explainability can honestly say the final stretch is about approach consolidation, not invented coverage.  
5. Daily Coach can absorb final-prep emphasis without inventing panic packing.

### 4.3 Lawful effects

- Primary strategy becomes EXS-06, or current EXS is narrated in final-period form.  
- Retain learner history; do not narrate final consolidation as mastery earned or guaranteed pass.  
- Keep Revision / Learning / Recovery handoffs available if evidence flips.

### 4.4 Unlawful effects

- Declaring final prep because a countdown widget fired, regardless of prior-preparation honesty.  
- Packing unlearned units into “final consolidation.”  
- Using EXS-06 to override unfinished Revision Coach recommendations.  
- Opening ordinary EXS-06 immediately after disruption without EXS-07 / Recovery honesty.

### 4.5 Worked example

> After readiness consolidation and one conditions rehearsal, the student enters the plan’s final examination window. Transition **EST1** to **EXS-06** — protect known CM1 strengths, light sitting plan rehearsal, evidence-calibrated confidence — not a new first-pass of unread chapters.

## 5. EST2 — Transition Back to Revision Coach

### 5.1 Educational meaning

Examination practice or readiness review reveals that **consolidating revision of previously learned material** is again the primary honest educational job. Exam Coach must not override Revision Coach recommendations by relabelling consolidating need as exam tips.

### 5.2 When Revision handback is warranted

- Revision warrant / incomplete consolidating completion becomes dominant for the fragile known hubs blocking assessment preparation.  
- Exam rehearsal shows durability / retrieval / fragile-knowledge problems that Revision Strategies (RVS-*) own.  
- Continuing EXS would require redefining or ignoring Revision Coach meaning.  
- MS001 wrong-mode check fails: consolidation is the sole honest job.

### 5.3 Lawful effects

- End or demote Exam Coach strategy ownership for that problem.  
- Hand consolidating ownership to Revision Coach (MS001 warrant → MS002 strategy → MS003 completion as applicable).  
- Explain honestly: assessment approach waits on consolidating strength of known material.  
- Exam Coach may remain layered only for approach framing that does not absorb revision authority.

### 5.4 Unlawful effects

- Opening EXS-02 / EXS-06 loops as a substitute for Revision Coach.  
- Narrating handback as student failure rather than educational honesty.  
- Keeping Exam Coach primary while contradicting Revision Coach recommendations.

### 5.5 Worked example

> Timed rehearsal exposes brittle return on a “covered” contingent-assurance chapter. Transition **EST2** — Revision Coach owns fragile-knowledge stabilisation; Exam Coach pauses demonstration claims for that hub.

Architectural requirement restated:

> **Examination strategies must never override Revision Coach recommendations.  
> Where revision evidence becomes dominant, authority returns to the Revision Coach.**

---

## 6. EST3 — Transition to Recovery Coach

### 6.1 Educational meaning

**Significant disruption** is (or becomes) the primary educational problem. Examination strategies must not disguise catch-up as assessment preparation.

### 6.2 When Recovery handoff is warranted

- Active Recovery warrant is primary.  
- Absence / capacity break makes examination strategy speech dishonest.  
- Student returns from disruption needing restorative continuity before ordinary EXS-01…EXS-06.  
- Attempting EXS-06 / EXS-02 would treat disruption as ordinary final prep.

### 6.3 Lawful effects

- Defer to Recovery Coach; examination strategies pause or become layered only when Recovery allows.  
- After Recovery posture ends, prefer **EXS-07** for careful exam re-entry before ordinary final prep.  
- Preserve continuity history; do not narrate as if disruption never occurred.

### 6.4 Unlawful effects

- Opening EXS-06 as if illness / absence were a quiet week.  
- Using examination rehearsal to bypass Recovery responsibilities.  
- Inventing catch-up volume under an exam-strategy label.

### 6.5 Worked example

> Two-week illness near the sitting. Transition **EST3** — Recovery Coach primary. Only after restorative continuity returns may **EXS-07** reopen assessment-facing preparation.

## 7. EST4 — Escalation to Learning Coach

### 7.1 Educational meaning

Examination preparation reveals an **unresolved learning weakness** — obstacle, thin genuine progression, misconception, or false readiness that exam tips cannot honestly repair. Authority must transition to the **Learning Coach** rather than compensating inside Exam Coach.

### 7.2 Escalation criteria (qualitative)

Escalate to Learning Coach when any of the following hold:

1. Fair attempts at appropriate EXS strategies show **no meaningful assessment-facing improvement** because understanding never formed.  
2. Learning Coach posture already indicates **obstacle / intervention** ownership.  
3. “Prepared” material behaves as if **understanding never formed**, and demonstration speech has become dishonest.  
4. Exam Coach would need to **override Learning Coach evidence** to continue.  
5. Student needs **diagnosis**, not another examination approach label.

### 7.3 Lawful effects

- End primary Exam Coach strategy ownership for that problem (or demote to explicitly supporting emphasis only if Learning Coach allows).  
- Hand educational response to Learning Coach progression / obstacle / intervention pathways.  
- Explain honestly: examination preparation alone is not enough; we need a learning diagnosis / response.  
- Do **not** redefine mastery or invent first learning as “more exam prep.”

### 7.4 Unlawful effects

- Opening EXS-02 / EXS-04 loops as a substitute for diagnosis.  
- Narrating escalation as student failure rather than educational honesty.  
- Keeping Exam Coach as primary while contradicting Learning Coach evidence.

Architectural requirement restated:

> **Where examination preparation reveals unresolved educational weaknesses,  
> authority must transition back to the appropriate upstream coach  
> rather than attempting to solve the problem within the Exam Coach.**

### 7.5 Worked example

> Question-selection practice repeatedly fails because the underlying method was never understood. Transition **EST4** — Learning Coach owns diagnosis; stop promising that more exam strategy will fix it.

## 8. EST5 — Strategy Change Within Exam Coach

### 8.1 Educational meaning

The current primary strategy is no longer the best educational match — either because it was provisionally wrong, or because preparation progress changed the leading need.

### 8.2 When strategy change is warranted

Change primary strategy when:

1. **Success signs for the current strategy are not emerging** after a fair assessment-facing attempt, and another EXS better matches the observed profile; or  
2. **Profile evolved** (e.g. demonstration readiness steadied → conditions rehearsal now leads; time plan clear → question selection now leads); or  
3. **Trigger / priority co-occurrence shifted** (e.g. readiness review resolved enough that final-period EXS-06 becomes primary).

### 8.3 Illustrative lawful evolutions

| From | Toward | Educational story |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| EXS-01 Readiness Consolidation | EXS-02 Examination Rehearsal | Demonstration steadier; authentic conditions now the leading gap |
| EXS-02 Examination Rehearsal | EXS-03 Time-Management Preparation | Conditions familiar; pacing / clock discipline now leads |
| EXS-03 / EXS-04 | EXS-06 Final Consolidation | Approach elements in place; authorised final period leads |
| EXS-07 Post-Disruption Exam Prep | EXS-01 Readiness Consolidation | Continuity restored; ordinary demonstration readiness now fits |
| EXS-07 Post-Disruption Exam Prep | EXS-06 Final Consolidation | Continuity restored and final window already active — only if Recovery honesty allows |
| Any intensive EXS | EXS-05 Confidence Stabilisation | Affective misalignment becomes the leading assessment-facing need |
| EXS-01…EXS-06 | EST2 / EST3 / EST4 destinations | Unresolved revision / disruption / learning weakness appears |

### 8.4 Lawful effects

- Rename primary EXS with explainability.  
- Re-state EXO focus and expected outcome.  
- Preserve prior-preparation and warrant honesty.

### 8.5 Unlawful effects

- Silent mode switches without educational reason.  
- Changing to EXS-06 to invent coverage of unlearned material.  
- Using strategy change to avoid EST2–EST4 when those apply.

---

## 9. EST6 — Structural Escalation (Plan / Scheduling)

### 9.1 Educational meaning

The assessment-facing need cannot be met honestly inside authorised windows, intensity, or envelopes without structural change.

### 9.2 Lawful effects

- Identify need for Rescheduling / Master Planner pathways.  
- Do not invent unofficial examination windows or steal protections.  
- Daily Coach / plan authorities remain owners of structural change.  
- Do not absorb replan inside strategy “completion.”

### 9.3 Unlawful effects

- Claiming strategy success while envelopes are broken.  
- Silently rewriting Canonical Study Plan examination intent through Exam Coach speech.

## 10. EST7 — Post-Examination Reflection

### 10.1 Educational meaning

The sitting has occurred. Examination preparation strategies are **no longer the primary educational job**. Authority transitions to **post-examination reflection** — reviewing what the sitting revealed, preserving educational continuity, and routing onward learning / revision / recovery / planning needs honestly.

### 10.2 Criteria

Enter EST7 when:

1. The examination sitting has taken place (or been formally abandoned / deferred under plan honesty); and  
2. Continuing EXS speech would be assessment theatre without a sitting to prepare for; and  
3. Reflection / review pathways can own the educational next step.

### 10.3 Lawful effects

- End primary Exam Coach strategy ownership for that sitting.  
- Open post-examination reflection in supportive, evidence-honest language.  
- Route new warrants: revision of known fragile hubs, Learning Coach diagnosis, Recovery if disruption followed, or Master Planner if next-sitting envelopes change.  
- Preserve continuity — sitting outcome does not wipe educational identity.

### 10.4 Unlawful effects

- Endless EXS-02 loops after the paper as if preparation were still primary.  
- Minting mastery or failure identity from a single sitting without reflection honesty.  
- Using post-exam speech to invent a new Canonical Study Plan silently.

### 10.5 Worked example

> After the CM1 sitting, transition **EST7** — reflect on time management and method selection under pressure; route fragile known topics to Revision Coach; do not keep “final consolidation” speech running.

---

## 11. EST8 — Hold / Gather

### 11.1 Educational meaning

Warrant, success, or sibling coach posture is unclear. Prefer gentler authorised assessment-facing return and evidence gathering over invented crisis or false readiness.

### 11.2 Lawful effects

- Stay on EXS-01 or light authorised emphasis when safe.  
- Gather Educational Evidence; re-enter selection when clear.  
- Do not mint strategies from calendar guilt.

## 12. Authority Boundary Summary Across Transitions

| Transition | May Exam Coach complete alone? | Must respect |
|------------|--------------------------------|--------------|
| EST1 Toward final preparation | Yes, as assessment-facing emphasis change | Plan final-window honesty; no coverage invention |
| EST2 Revision handback | **No** — hand off consolidating ownership | Revision Coach boundaries / recommendations |
| EST3 Recovery handoff | **No** — hand off ownership | Recovery Coach boundaries |
| EST4 Learning Coach | **No** — hand off ownership | Learning Coach diagnosis / intervention law |
| EST5 Strategy change | Yes, within Exam Coach field | MS001 warrant honesty; explainability |
| EST6 Structural | **No** — escalate | Master Planner / Scheduling |
| EST7 Post-examination reflection | Ends Exam Coach primary for that sitting | Reflection honesty; onward routing |
| EST8 Hold | Yes, lightly | Evidence honesty; no crisis theatre |

Hard rule:

> **Strategy transitions never rewrite the Canonical Study Plan,  
> never redefine mastery,  
> never override Revision Coach recommendations,  
> never bypass Recovery Coach,  
> and never use examination tips to compensate for Learning Coach problems.**
