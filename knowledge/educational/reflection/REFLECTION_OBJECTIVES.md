## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “reflection length” or “survey completion.” The tutor establishes **educational truth about the sitting** — what was achieved against today’s objective, what evidence appeared, what went wrong or remains thin, and whether tomorrow’s coaching needs a different emphasis.

These objectives bind every Educational Reflection interpretation and output decision.

Identifiers (ERO-XX) exist for traceability. Educational meaning is binding; implementation field names are out of scope.

---

## 2. Governing Constraint — Plan and Day Fidelity

### ERO-00 — Reflect against today’s authorised job

**Definition.** Every reflection judgement must evaluate the session relative to today’s Daily Coach primary objective (DCO-01) and disclosed day posture (including DCO-10 when present), as lived through the Learning Session aims.

**Includes:**

- Asking whether the authorised work type (learn / practise / consolidate / revise / checkpoint / recover / rest) was served.
- Judging partial attainment when capacity or interruption truncated the sitting.
- Escalating when the day’s job was impossible to serve honestly — without inventing a successful alternative narrative.

**Excludes:**

- Retroactively changing today’s objective to match what the student happened to do.
- Treating off-plan exploration as fulfilment of a different authorised job without disclosure.
- Using reflection to invent a new long-term phase or syllabus ambition.

**Tutor rationale.** Honest review requires a fixed warrant. Moving the goalposts after the sitting destroys trust.

**Priority.** ERO-00 is a **hard constraint** on all other reflection objectives. It is never traded away.

## 3. Primary Reflection Objective Family

A complete Educational Reflection typically settles **all primary objectives below** to the depth evidence allows — not an unordered motivational wishlist. When evidence is thin, objectives still apply as *questions*, answered with explicit uncertainty.

### ERO-01 — Was today’s objective achieved?

**Definition.** Establish the attainment posture of today’s Daily Coach objective: served, partially served, deferred, or not served.

**Includes:**

- Distinguishing *attempted*, *completed as activity*, and *educationally served*.
- Honouring lawful local session adaptations that still served DCO-01.
- Recording when escalation during the session already declared the day unsustainable.

**Must not.** Equate UI completion with educational achievement; claim full attainment when key phases were skipped without educational warrant.

**Tutor rationale.** Tomorrow’s coaching depends on whether yesterday’s job landed.

---

### ERO-02 — What evidence of learning was produced?

**Definition.** Identify which observations from the sitting qualify as Educational Evidence of learning (retrieval, practice performance, demonstrated method use, corrected misconceptions) versus mere activity or coverage.

**Includes:**

- Naming evidence quality band in educational terms (strong / moderate / soft / absent) without introducing scoring systems in this document.
- Separating Study Progress facts from understanding-supporting observations.

**Must not.** Invent evidence; promote soft self-report to high-certainty knowledge; collapse Activity → Knowledge without Evidence succession.

**Tutor rationale.** IFoA preparation rewards observed competence, not theatre.

---

### ERO-03 — What misconceptions emerged?

**Definition.** Surface conceptual, method, notation, or condition errors that appeared during the session and warrant future attention.

**Includes:**

- Misconceptions revealed by retrieval failure, wrong practice paths, or student self-report of confusion.
- Distinguishing *fleeting slip* from *recurring pattern* when history supports that distinction.

**Must not.** Pathologise normal struggle on first-pass material; invent weakness from missing soft signals alone; shame the student in narration.

**Tutor rationale.** Expert tutors treat misconceptions as teaching targets, not moral failures.

---

### ERO-04 — What remains uncertain?

**Definition.** State residual educational uncertainty: thin evidence, conflicting observations, interrupted work, incomplete activities, or topics touched without qualifying performance.

**Includes:**

- Explicit “we do not yet know” postures.
- Naming which parts of today’s objective lack supporting evidence even if the session was “completed.”

**Must not.** Fill uncertainty with optimistic mastery language; treat absence of evidence as evidence of weakness stated as fact.

**Tutor rationale.** Understatement preserves trust for later confident claims.

---

### ERO-05 — How confident should we be in today’s progress?

**Definition.** Calibrate claim strength for today’s progress narrative and for any provisional understanding update — bounded by evidence quality and history.

**Includes:**

- Aligning encouragement with honesty (compatible with Daily Coach DCO-05 spirit).
- Lowering confidence when interruptions, incomplete activities, or soft-only signals dominate.

**Must not.** Inflate confidence for motivation; mint readiness or pass predictions from a single sitting.

**Tutor rationale.** Confidence without calibration becomes false coaching.

---

### ERO-06 — Does tomorrow’s coaching need adjustment?

**Definition.** Decide the educational implication for subsequent Daily Coach days: continue as planned, reinforce within envelopes, adjust confidence/emphasis, or escalate for rescheduling / replanning.

**Includes:**

- Reinforcement recommendations when misconceptions or weak retrieval warrant return practice *inside* plan authority.
- Continuation when the day’s job was cleanly served and the plan’s next job remains appropriate.
- Escalation when capacity collapse, repeated miss, or envelope breach requires structural change.

**Must not.** Directly rewrite the Canonical Study Plan; invent a new primary tomorrow outside Daily Coach decision law; punish with overload catch-up.

**Tutor rationale.** Reflection exists to improve the next coaching judgement — not to seize Master Planner authority.

---

## 4. Supporting Reflection Objectives

These deepen honesty; they do not displace ERO-01…ERO-06.

### ERO-07 — Honour interruptions and incomplete work

**Definition.** Account for interruptions and incomplete activities as educational context that limits attainment and evidence strength — not as moral failure.

### ERO-08 — Preserve continuity of educational memory

**Definition.** Produce coaching notes that help subsequent Daily Coach days remember what mattered today without erasing history or inventing Twin certainty.

### ERO-09 — Keep product feedback out of educational conclusions

**Definition.** Ensure product research signals (RIP-001 and peers) do not satisfy ERO-01…ERO-06.

### ERO-10 — Explain the reflection

**Definition.** Ensure material conclusions are explainable under `REFLECTION_EXPLAINABILITY.md` before student-facing close narration.

---

## 5. Priority When Objectives Compete

When reflection conclusions conflict, resolve in this order:

1. **ERO-00** — day/job fidelity  
2. **ERO-02 / Evidence honesty** — do not invent observations  
3. **ERO-04** — uncertainty before false certainty  
4. **ERO-01** — attainment posture given honest evidence  
5. **ERO-03** — misconceptions as teaching targets  
6. **ERO-05** — confidence calibration  
7. **ERO-06** — tomorrow implication (continuation / reinforcement / escalation)  
8. Supporting objectives (ERO-07…ERO-10)

If ERO-06 would require changing educational envelopes, protections, or sitting ambition, **escalation wins** over silent local invention.

---

## 6. Relationship to Session and Daily Coach Objectives

| Layer | Optimises |
|-------|-----------|
| Daily Coach objectives | What is most valuable *to do today* |
| Session objectives (LSO-XX) | How to pursue that job *in the sitting* |
| Reflection objectives (ERO-XX) | What the sitting *meant educationally* afterward |

Reflection may report that a session objective was served while understanding evidence remains thin. That combination is lawful and must be narrated honestly.

## 7. Out of Scope

- Scoring rubrics, point systems, or weighted objective functions
- UI checklists for reflection forms
- Analytics KPIs for “reflection completion rate”
- Product satisfaction objectives
