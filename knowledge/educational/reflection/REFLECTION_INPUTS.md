## 1. Purpose

After a sitting, an expert IFoA tutor reviews what actually happened: what was finished, what was retrieved or practised, what the student said about their own understanding, where they stumbled, and what interrupted the work.

This catalogue names those educational inputs so reflection never depends on silent assumptions or product metrics mistaken for learning.

Identifiers (ERI-XX) exist for traceability. Educational meaning is binding; field names are out of scope.

---

## 2. Input Principles

1. **Observation before conclusion.** Reflection consumes what happened; it does not mint history.
2. **Warrant first.** Today’s Daily Coach objective (and session aims) frame which inputs matter.
3. **Quality ranked.** Soft self-report does not outrank qualifying performance when both exist.
4. **Activity ≠ evidence.** Completion and time-on-task are recorded honestly as journey facts; they do not automatically become understanding evidence.
5. **Interruptions are context.** They limit attainment and evidence strength; they are not moral data.
6. **Thin is allowed.** Cold start and sparse sittings require understatement, not filler.
7. **Product boundary.** Product research answers are not Educational Reflection inputs.
8. **No collection mandate here.** This document defines meanings required educationally — not how Runtime A gathers them.

## 3. Input Catalogue

| ID | Input | Educational job | Required? |
|----|-------|-----------------|-----------|
| ERI-01 | Today’s Daily Coach primary objective | Reflection warrant — what the day asked for | Yes |
| ERI-02 | Session aims & phase posture | What the sitting was designed to pursue / adapt | Yes |
| ERI-03 | Session completion posture | Whether the sitting completed, partial, deferred, or abandoned | Yes |
| ERI-04 | Retrieval performance | Observed recall / recognition outcomes during the session | Preferred when retrieval was part of the session |
| ERI-05 | Question / practice performance | Observed application outcomes on authorised practice | Preferred when practice was part of the session |
| ERI-06 | Self-reflection statements | Student’s own account of difficulty, clarity, and remaining questions | Preferred when captured; soft |
| ERI-07 | Identified misconceptions | Named conceptual / method errors observed or reported | Preferred when present |
| ERI-08 | Confidence statements | Student-expressed confidence about today’s material | Preferred when captured; soft |
| ERI-09 | Interruptions | Breaks, context switches, capacity shocks during the sitting | Yes when known |
| ERI-10 | Incomplete activities | Authorised work started but not finished | Yes when applicable |
| ERI-11 | Local adaptation / escalation record | Whether the session adapted locally or escalated to Daily Coach | Yes when present |
| ERI-12 | Prior evidence / estimate posture | Thin history and prior lawful estimates for the topic | Preferred; thin must be named |
| ERI-13 | Plan envelope context (read-only) | Intensity / protection / next-job context for lawful recommendations | Yes for ERO-06 honesty |

## 4. Input Specifications

### ERI-01 — Today’s Daily Coach primary objective

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **What** | DCO-01 as authorised for the day (work type, syllabus/plan warrant, scope) |
| **Educational use** | Anchor attainment judgement (ERO-01) |
| **Must not** | Be replaced after the fact by what the student preferred to study |
| **If missing** | Refuse Educational Reflection theatre that pretends a day-job existed |

### ERI-02 — Session aims & phase posture

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **What** | Session aim cluster and which educational phases were intended / entered |
| **Educational use** | Interpret whether method served the day; contextualise incomplete phases |
| **Must not** | Be treated as a second Daily Coach objective |

### ERI-03 — Session completion posture

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **What** | Completed / partially completed / interrupted close / abandoned / escalated-away |
| **Claim type** | Observed / derived journey fact |
| **Educational use** | Continuity and attainment context |
| **Must not** | Be narrated as mastery or readiness proof |

### ERI-04 — Retrieval performance

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **What** | Educationally meaningful retrieval outcomes (success, struggle, failure patterns) during the sitting |
| **Educational use** | Primary candidate for learning evidence when retrieval was authorised |
| **Quality** | Generally stronger than unaided self-report for recall claims |
| **Collection** | Out of scope |
| **Must not** | Be invented from “student felt they remembered” |

### ERI-05 — Question / practice performance

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **What** | Outcomes of authorised application / question practice in the session |
| **Educational use** | Evidence of method use, condition sensitivity, and exam craft under today’s practice aim |
| **Must not** | Mint Estimated Mastery from a small item set; ignore misconception patterns |

### ERI-06 — Self-reflection statements

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **What** | Student answers to educational reflection prompts (e.g. what was hard, what remains unclear) — including DCO-04-guided prompts when used |
| **Educational use** | Soft evidence / calibration; misconception leads; uncertainty naming |
| **Quality** | Soft — may support coaching notes and confidence adjustment; must not alone establish high-certainty understanding |
| **Must not** | Be coerced into false mastery declarations |

### ERI-07 — Identified misconceptions

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **What** | Specific educational errors identified during the sitting (by performance, tutor-method detection, or clear student articulation) |
| **Educational use** | Drive reinforcement recommendations and coaching notes |
| **Must not** | Be vague character labels (“weak student”); invent misconceptions from missing data |

### ERI-08 — Confidence statements

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **What** | Expressed confidence about today’s material or readiness |
| **Educational use** | Calibrate narration; detect over/under-confidence relative to performance when both exist |
| **Quality** | Soft |
| **Must not** | Override conflicting retrieval/practice evidence; become Twin mastery |

### ERI-09 — Interruptions

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **What** | Material interruptions that fragmented attention or capacity mid-session |
| **Educational use** | Bound attainment and evidence strength; explain incomplete work |
| **Must not** | Be treated as evidence of low ability |

### ERI-10 — Incomplete activities

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **What** | Authorised activities started but not finished (including skipped phases without educational warrant) |
| **Educational use** | Partial attainment; residual uncertainty; possible reinforcement need |
| **Must not** | Be silently ignored to preserve a “completed day” story |

### ERI-11 — Local adaptation / escalation record

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **What** | Whether Learning Session adaptation stayed local or escalated to Daily Coach (per MS002) |
| **Educational use** | Interpret whether the day’s job remained intact; carry forward escalation honesty |
| **Must not** | Relabel escalation as successful local completion |

### ERI-12 — Prior evidence / estimate posture

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **What** | Existing Educational Evidence and lawful estimates for the relevant topic/skills before today’s sitting |
| **Educational use** | Prevent single-sitting overclaim; support “recurring misconception” vs “first struggle” judgement |
| **If thin** | Name cold start / thin history explicitly |

### ERI-13 — Plan envelope context (read-only)

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **What** | Read-only view of intensity / protection envelopes and near-term authorised plan work |
| **Educational use** | Decide whether tomorrow implications stay inside envelopes or require escalation |
| **Must not** | Be mutated by Reflection; serve only as constraint awareness |

## 5. Input Completeness Rules

| Situation | Lawful Reflection posture |
|-----------|---------------------------|
| ERI-01 present; performance evidence present | Full attainment + evidence interpretation with calibrated confidence |
| ERI-01 present; only completion + soft self-report | Attainment as journey fact; understanding claims understated; soft inputs named as soft |
| Interruptions / incompletes dominate | Partial attainment; high residual uncertainty; reinforcement or reschedule consideration |
| Escalation already fired in-session | Reflection must not overwrite escalation with success theatre |
| Prior evidence thin | Single-sitting estimates remain provisional |
| Product check-in only | Not Educational Reflection — refuse educational conclusions from product research alone |
| DCO-01 missing | Refuse reflection that claims a day’s educational job |

**Binding rule:** Educational Reflection must not invent missing educational inputs to appear complete.

---

## 6. Inputs Reflection Must Not Treat as Educational Authority

The following may exist in product systems but are **not** educational authority for understanding claims:

- Streaks, badges, or engagement scores as proof of learning  
- Product satisfaction / RIP-001 answers as Educational Evidence  
- Raw optimiser scores or Twin facet dumps as student-facing reflection drivers  
- Time-on-page alone as mastery  
- Marketing readiness language  
- Another student’s anonymised averages as personal attainment proof  

---

## 7. Relationship to Daily Coach Inputs

Reflection **produces** educational meanings that subsequent Daily Coach days may consume as:

| Daily Coach input | How reflection contributes |
|-------------------|----------------------------|
| **DCI-06 Recent learning evidence** | Lawful observations and interpreted learning signals from today’s sitting |
| **DCI-07 Session completion history** | Completion / partial / missed posture for continuity |
| **DCI-12 Escalation flags** | When reflection emits escalation for structural change |

Reflection does not redefine those Daily Coach input meanings; it supplies educational content into them.
