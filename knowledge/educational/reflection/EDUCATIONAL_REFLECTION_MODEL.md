## Authority

This document is the constitutional overview of **Educational Reflection** for Kwalitec.

It is subordinate to the Educational Constitution, the Educational Evidence Model, the Daily Coach Model (MS001), and the Learning Session Model (MS002). It governs **how a completed study session is interpreted educationally** so that tomorrow’s coaching can respond honestly. It does not authorise implementation shortcuts that contradict the Constitution, and it does not rewrite the Canonical Study Plan.

Authority order for educational reflection:

> Constitution defines educational truth and curriculum primacy.  
> Knowledge & Mastery defines coverage ≠ understanding ≠ mastery.  
> Evidence Model defines what may warrant understanding claims.  
> Canonical Study Plan publishes the long-term preparation contract.  
> Daily Coach (MS001) selects *what is most educationally valuable to do today*.  
> Learning Session (MS002) defines *how that day’s objective is studied*.  
> **This Educational Reflection Model (Workstream 2 / MS003) defines how today’s session is interpreted after it closes.**  
> Downstream Twin, coaching, mission, runtime, and narration surfaces must consume this educational reasoning — never invent mastery from completion, and never rewrite the Study Plan from reflection convenience.

## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor, after a coaching sitting, does not merely tick “done.” The tutor asks a living educational question:

> **“What did we learn from today’s study?”**

That answer must:

- honour what today’s Daily Coach objective asked for;
- respect what the Learning Session actually produced;
- separate coverage, performance, confidence, and understanding;
- name misconceptions and remaining uncertainty;
- decide whether tomorrow needs continuation, reinforcement, or escalation;
- leave the Canonical Study Plan intact unless lawful upstream pathways change it.

This document records that tutor posture so every future reflection, coaching hand-off, and narration surface has a single educational reference for *how a completed session is interpreted*.

> **The Educational Reflection Model describes educational reasoning.  
> It does not implement Runtime A logic, scoring engines, analytics, or UI.**

## 2. What Educational Reflection Is

**Educational Reflection** is the educational unit in which Kwalitec interprets a **completed (or materially concluded) Learning Session** against **today’s Daily Coach primary objective**, producing an honest educational reading of progress, uncertainty, and coaching implications.

| Input family | Role |
|--------------|------|
| **Today’s Daily Coach primary objective (DCO-01)** | The day’s educational job — reflection warrant |
| **Session structure & aims (MS002)** | What the sitting was designed to pursue |
| **Post-session educational evidence** | Observations produced by today’s study (see `REFLECTION_INPUTS.md`) |
| **Prior understanding / evidence posture** | Thin history and prior estimates that bound honesty |
| **Plan / capacity context (read-only)** | Whether recommendations stay inside envelopes or must escalate |

into:

| Output family | Role |
|---------------|------|
| **Updated educational understanding posture** | What may provisionally be believed about today’s learning |
| **Reinforcement / continuation recommendations** | What tomorrow’s coaching should emphasise *inside* plan law |
| **Confidence calibration** | How strongly claims may be stated |
| **Escalation signal (when needed)** | When rescheduling or replanning is required |
| **Coaching notes** | Tutor-facing educational memory for subsequent Daily Coach days |
| **Reflection explainability** | Why these conclusions follow from today’s evidence |

It answers:

| Question | Educational answer |
|----------|-------------------|
| Was today’s objective achieved? | Relative to DCO-01 — not relative to an invented second day |
| What evidence of learning appeared? | Qualifying observations, ranked by Evidence Model quality |
| What misconceptions emerged? | Named educational difficulties — not personality theatre |
| What remains uncertain? | Explicit uncertainty where evidence is thin or conflicting |
| How confident should we be? | Claim strength bounded by evidence quality |
| Does tomorrow need adjustment? | Continuation, reinforcement, or escalation — never silent plan rewrite |

Educational Reflection is:

- **objective-anchored** — interprets the day against DCO-01;
- **evidence-honest** — completion is not mastery;
- **tutor-shaped** — mirrors how an expert IFoA tutor reviews a sitting before planning tomorrow;
- **loop-closing** — feeds subsequent Daily Coach decisions (DCI-06 / DCI-07 meanings);
- **claim-typed** — separates facts, estimates, and advice;
- **non-rewriting** — never silently redesigns Canonical Study Plan commitments;
- **escalation-aware** — returns structural change to Daily Coach / Master Planner pathways;
- **explainable** — students can understand what today achieved and what remains.

Educational Reflection is **not**:

- a second Daily Coach that invents tomorrow’s primary objective outside plan law;
- a Master Planner that rewrites journey envelopes;
- a scoring engine, analytics dashboard, or leaderboard;
- ownership of Canonical Study Plan publication;
- product research / RIP-001 check-in (product experience ≠ educational understanding);
- a claim that finishing the session guarantees a pass.

## 3. Educational Purpose

The Educational Reflection Model exists so that:

1. **The daily loop closes honestly** — today’s study informs tomorrow without fiction.
2. **IFoA-grade review habits are encoded** — achievement, difficulty, misconception, and residual uncertainty are named explicitly.
3. **Evidence principles survive the close** — soft self-report and hard retrieval stay correctly ranked.
4. **Confidence is calibrated** — encouragement never invents certainty.
5. **Authority stays layered** — Reflection interprets; Daily Coach chooses tomorrow within plan law; Master Planner owns the contract; rescheduling/replanning own structural change.

---

## 4. Core Responsibilities

Educational Reflection is educationally responsible for:

| Responsibility | Meaning |
|----------------|---------|
| **Judge objective attainment** | Assess whether today’s DCO-01 was served, partially served, or not served — without inventing a different objective |
| **Catalogue learning evidence** | Identify which post-session observations qualify as educational evidence vs mere activity |
| **Surface misconceptions** | Name emerging conceptual or method errors warranting future attention |
| **State remaining uncertainty** | Keep thin or conflicting history provisional |
| **Calibrate confidence** | Bound how strongly progress may be narrated |
| **Recommend next coaching posture** | Continuation, reinforcement, confidence adjustment, or escalation — as educational concepts |
| **Protect the Study Plan** | Refuse direct Canonical Study Plan rewrite; escalate when long-term change is required |
| **Explain the close** | Attach plain-language rationale for achievement, gaps, and coaching influence |

Educational Reflection is **not** educationally responsible for:

- publishing or mutating Canonical Study Plan components;
- reallocating calendar cells (Scheduling / Rescheduling Policy);
- selecting tomorrow’s primary objective in isolation from Daily Coach decision law;
- minting Estimated Mastery from ticks, minutes, or felt confidence alone;
- collecting product NPS / research survey answers as learning proof.

## 5. Integrity Invariants

These invariants bind all subordinate reflection documents and all future implementations that claim educational reflection meaning.

### ERM-01 — Objective fidelity

Reflection evaluates the day against the authorised Daily Coach primary objective (and disclosed day posture). It does not retroactively invent a “better” day to make outcomes look successful.

### ERM-02 — Completion ≠ understanding

Session completion, coverage ticks, and time spent are journey / activity facts. They do not, by themselves, establish Estimated Knowledge or Estimated Mastery.

### ERM-03 — Evidence Model obedience

All understanding claims must respect Educational Evidence principles: observation before inference; quality before certainty; absence is uncertainty; inference may not invent evidence.

### ERM-04 — Soft signals stay soft

Self-reflection and confidence statements may inform calibration and coaching notes. They do not become high-certainty understanding by repetition of wording alone.

### ERM-05 — No silent Study Plan rewrite

Reflection outputs may recommend reinforcement or escalation. They must never directly rewrite Canonical Study Plan phases, protections, intensity envelopes, or sitting ambition.

### ERM-06 — Escalation for structural change

Recommendations that require long-term allocation or educational-envelope change must escalate through Daily Coach rescheduling / replanning pathways (and Master Planner authority as established).

### ERM-07 — Claim typing

Student-facing and coach-facing reflection speech must distinguish Observed Fact, Derived Fact, Estimate, and Educational Advice.

### ERM-08 — Loop honesty

Reflection exists to improve subsequent Daily Coach decisions. It must not manufacture “recent evidence” theatre to justify unlawful tomorrow priorities.

### ERM-09 — Product boundary

Product research reflections must not write educational understanding states or be presented as Educational Reflection outcomes.

### ERM-10 — Explainability

Material reflection conclusions require educational rationale under `REFLECTION_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.

## 6. Position in the Daily Coaching Loop

```
Canonical Study Plan
        ↓
Daily Coach (MS001)  →  today’s primary objective (DCO-01)
        ↓
Learning Session (MS002)  →  study anatomy for that objective
        ↓
Educational Reflection (MS003)  →  “What did we learn today?”
        ↓
Updated understanding posture + coaching notes
+ reinforcement / continuation recommendations
(+ escalation when required)
        ↓
Subsequent Daily Coach day(s)
  (consume as recent evidence / session history —
   DCD-05 modulation within envelopes)
```

Reflection is the **educational hinge** between *today’s lived study* and *tomorrow’s coaching judgement*.
