## 1. Purpose

Students should never finish a sitting wondering what it meant — or believing that a tick equals a pass.

Reflection explainability exists so that every material attainment judgement, uncertainty statement, reinforcement suggestion, and escalation notice answers — in plain educational language — what is known, what is estimated, what remains open, and how tomorrow’s coaching may respond.

Without reflection explainability:

- completion feels like mastery;
- reinforcement feels like arbitrary repetition;
- escalation feels like punishment;
- tomorrow’s coaching feels disconnected from today’s work.

With reflection explainability:

- the daily loop is intelligible;
- claim types stay honest;
- Canonical Study Plan integrity remains visible;
- trust compounds across days.

## 2. Traceability Obligation (Architectural)

Every material Educational Reflection conclusion must be traceable through:

| Trace link | Student-facing role |
|------------|---------------------|
| **Today’s Daily Coach objective** | “Today we set out to…” |
| **Session contribution** | “In the sitting we…” |
| **Observed evidence** | “What we saw was…” / “We mainly have your notes that…” |
| **Attainment posture** | “Against today’s goal, we…” |
| **Remaining improvement** | “Still to strengthen…” / “Still uncertain…” |
| **Future coaching influence** | “So next time we will…” / “We may need to adjust the timetable because…” |
| **Plan integrity (when relevant)** | “This does not change your long-term plan by itself…” / “This needs a formal reschedule / replan…” |

Internal IDs (ERO-XX, ERI-XX, ERIJ-XX, EROut-XX, DCO-XX) may exist for algorithms and audits. They must not appear as student-facing jargon.

A reflection explanation with no Daily Coach objective warrant is invalid — even if it sounds motivating.

## 3. Explainability Principles

1. **Day first.** Name today’s objective before narrating techniques or feelings.
2. **Evidence before praise.** Ground achievement claims in observations (or explicitly soft signals).
3. **Separate coverage from competence.** “You completed the sitting” ≠ “you have mastered this.”
4. **Name uncertainty.** Thin or soft-only history must be audible.
5. **Misconceptions as targets.** Specific, respectful, syllabus-tied language.
6. **Tomorrow is advice under plan law.** Continuation/reinforcement are advice; primary tomorrow remains Daily Coach + plan.
7. **Escalation is honesty.** Structural change requests must say why envelopes cannot hold.
8. **No scores as speech.** Do not explain with opaque percentages unless a separate authorised surface defines them — and even then educational meaning leads.
9. **Claim typing.** Keep Observed Fact / Estimate / Advice distinguishable in narration.
10. **Align with Daily Coach & Session speech.** Reflection must not contradict DCO-06 / session rationale already given for the day.

## 4. Required Explanation Families

### 4.1 What today’s study achieved (ERE-01)

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **Must answer** | What educational job was attempted; what attainment class applies; what evidence supports that reading |
| **Must include** | Work type language (learn / practise / consolidate / revise / recover / rest) |
| **Must not** | Claim mastery from completion; hide incompletes |

**Example postures (illustrative, not templates):**

- “Today’s goal was practice on Topic T. You completed the authorised practice set with solid method on most items — so today’s practice job was served.”
- “Today’s goal was first-pass learning on Topic T. You covered the material, but we did not yet check retrieval — so we should treat understanding as still provisional.”

### 4.2 What remains to improve (ERE-02)

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **Must answer** | Misconceptions, weak retrieval/practice areas, incompletes, or residual uncertainty |
| **Must include** | Specific educational targets when known; explicit “not yet evidenced” when unknown |
| **Must not** | Vague deficit shaming; invent weakness from absent soft signals alone |

**Example postures:**

- “One method step still trips you: applying condition C before formula F. That is the next repair target.”
- “We do not yet have retrieval evidence for today’s ideas, so confidence should stay cautious.”

### 4.3 How today’s evidence influences future coaching (ERE-03)

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **Must answer** | Continuation, reinforcement, confidence adjustment, or escalation — and why |
| **Must include** | Plan-envelope honesty; statement that long-term plan changes go through reschedule/replan pathways |
| **Must not** | Promise a rewritten Study Plan from reflection alone; invent tomorrow’s primary outside Daily Coach law |

**Example postures:**

- “Because practice was solid, tomorrow can continue with the plan’s next authorised job — your Daily Coach will confirm that against today’s capacity.”
- “Because retrieval was weak on the key definitions, tomorrow’s coaching may emphasise authorised reinforcement inside your plan — not a new topic binge.”
- “Because several planned sittings were interrupted and the remaining work no longer fits safely, we need a reschedule rather than pretending the diary is fine.”

## 5. Claim-Type Speech Rules

| Claim type | Reflection use | Speech cue |
|------------|----------------|------------|
| **Observed Fact** | Completion posture, interruptions, specific practice/retrieval outcomes | “You completed… / You missed… / On these questions…” |
| **Derived Fact** | Attainment class derived from warrant + observations | “Against today’s goal, that means…” |
| **Estimate** | Understanding posture, residual uncertainty, confidence band | “Provisionally… / We estimate… / We still don’t know…” |
| **Educational Advice** | Reinforcement, continuation, confidence calibration | “Next, it would help to… / Keep confidence calibrated by…” |
| **Escalation disclosure** | Reschedule / replan need | “This needs a timetable / plan change because…” |

Mixing types in one paragraph is allowed only if the student can still tell which is which.

## 6. Special Cases

### 6.1 Soft-only reflection

Explain that self-report informs coaching notes and calibration, and that it does **not** by itself prove understanding.

### 6.2 Rest or recovery days

Achievement means honouring rest/recovery — explain that “doing nothing heavy” can be the educational success.

### 6.3 In-session escalation already raised

Reflection explanations should **confirm** the escalation story, not overwrite it with completion theatre.

### 6.4 Product check-in adjacency

If a product research prompt appears nearby in the product, educational reflection speech must remain separate and must not cite product satisfaction as learning evidence.

### 6.5 Conflict with earlier Daily Coach rationale

If today’s evidence revises emphasis for tomorrow, say what changed in evidence terms — do not silently contradict today’s DCO-06 as if it never existed.

## 7. Minimum Explainability Checklist

Before student-facing reflection narration ships educationally:

| ID | Check |
|----|-------|
| ERE-C1 | Today’s objective named |
| ERE-C2 | Achievement framed as attainment vs warrant — not as mastery-from-tick |
| ERE-C3 | Evidence basis stated (including soft/thin when applicable) |
| ERE-C4 | Remaining improvement / uncertainty stated |
| ERE-C5 | Future coaching influence stated (continue / reinforce / escalate) |
| ERE-C6 | Plan integrity respected (no silent rewrite implication) |
| ERE-C7 | Claim types distinguishable |
| ERE-C8 | No product-research-as-learning confusion |

Failure on ERE-C1, ERE-C2, ERE-C6, or ERE-C8 invalidates the explanation.

---

## 8. Relationship to Adjacent Explainability

| Layer | Explains |
|-------|----------|
| **Daily Coach explainability** | Why *this* is today’s educational priority under the Study Plan |
| **Session explainability** | Why *this sitting* is structured to serve that priority |
| **Reflection explainability** | What *the sitting produced*, what remains, and how that feeds *future coaching* |

Reflection speech must not contradict Daily Coach or Session rationale. It **closes** them into an honest educational after-action reading.
