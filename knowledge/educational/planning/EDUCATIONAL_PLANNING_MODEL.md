## Authority

This document is the constitutional overview of long-term educational planning for Kwalitec.

It is subordinate to the Educational Constitution and specialised educational models. It governs **educational reasoning** for Master Planner work. It does not authorise implementation shortcuts that contradict the Constitution.

Authority order for long-term planning:

> Constitution defines educational truth and curriculum primacy.  
> Knowledge & Mastery defines coverage ≠ understanding ≠ mastery.  
> Student Educational Profile (MS002) diagnoses who the student is educationally now.  
> Educational Strategy Framework (MS003) chooses which overall educational approach to adopt.  
> **This Model defines how an expert tutor designs the journey from registration to examination under that strategy.**  
> Planning Decision Engine (MS004) transforms Profile + Strategy + this Model into a Planning Decision Package before any timetable.  
> Planning Blueprint Model (MS005) organises that package into a date-independent study journey before any calendar.  
> Future planning algorithms must determine strategy, produce planning decisions, and assemble a blueprint before scheduling — and must implement this Model — never redefine it in code.

## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec exists to reduce daily decision burden while increasing productive learning for professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabuses such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not merely “spread topics until the exam date.” The tutor designs a complete educational journey that:

1. Honours official syllabus structure and prerequisites.
2. Fits inside the student’s real life (time, work, leave, energy).
3. Builds coverage honestly before claiming readiness.
4. Protects retention through spaced return and a genuine revision window.
5. Leaves capacity for mocks, recovery, and uncertainty.
6. Remains explainable in plain educational language.

This Model records that tutor posture so every future planning algorithm has a single educational reference.

---

## 2. What Educational Planning Is

**Educational planning** is the disciplined design of a student’s long-horizon study journey for a named examination sitting.

It answers:

| Question | Educational answer |
|----------|-------------------|
| Where is the student going? | A specific official examination and sitting date |
| Where are they now? | Student Educational Profile (Programme VI / MS002) — coverage, attempts, estimates, capacity, educational state |
| What approach do we take? | Educational Strategy (Programme VI / MS003) — overall educational approach before plan construction |
| What can they sustain? | Available weekly time, working schedule, leave, interruptions |
| What must the journey achieve? | Exam readiness under honest educational meaning |
| What must never be sacrificed? | Prerequisite order, revision capacity, sustainable load, truthfulness |

Educational planning is **not**:

- a calendar filled with arbitrary tasks;
- a content-generation syllabus;
- a black-box optimiser of opaque scores;
- a claim that finishing the plan equals mastery;
- a substitute for official CMP / course materials.

Kwalitec remains a **bring-your-own-materials** educational coach. Planning decides *when and in what educational posture* to study which syllabus units — not what textbook paragraphs to invent.

---

## 3. Tutor Posture (Binding Metaphor)

When designing a plan, the system must behave as an expert IFoA tutor would:

1. **Start from the exam.** The sitting date and syllabus are fixed educational anchors.
2. **Work backwards.** Revision and mock capacity are reserved before first-pass learning is packed.
3. **Respect prerequisites.** Later topics are not scheduled as if earlier foundations were optional.
4. **Prefer sustainable intensity.** A plan the student can keep beats an heroic schedule they abandon.
5. **Separate coverage from competence.** Studying a topic advances Study Progress; practice evidence informs estimates; neither invents mastery by fiat.
6. **Name uncertainty.** Thin history, previous fails, or unrealistic time must produce cautious plans — not confident theatre.
7. **Explain every material choice.** The student should understand *why this week looks like this*.
8. **Protect recovery.** Life interruptions are expected; plans must leave educational room to restart without shame or false diagnosis.

## 4. Journey Phases

An expert tutor designs the journey as ordered educational phases. Algorithms may name phases differently, but must preserve this educational meaning.

### 4.1 Registration & Intake

**Educational purpose:** Establish lawful planning inputs before committing the student to a trajectory.

**Tutor actions:** Confirm examination and sitting; understand available time and life constraints; capture prior progress and previous attempts; surface obvious feasibility risks early.

**Exit condition:** Minimum required inputs are known (see §5). Planning may begin.

### 4.2 Foundation & First-Pass Learning

**Educational purpose:** Honest sequential coverage of the official syllabus (Learning Mode posture).

**Tutor actions:** Sequence topics by official order / prerequisites; allocate study intensity the student can sustain; advance Current Learning Topic only through lawful Study Progress; keep practice honest without converting completion into mastery claims.

**Exit condition:** Syllabus first-pass coverage is complete *or* remaining first-pass work fits inside protected learning capacity before revision.

### 4.3 Consolidation Windows

**Educational purpose:** Interleaved return to recently studied material so retention does not collapse during a long first pass.

**Tutor actions:** Place light consolidation / spaced return without abandoning prerequisite order; avoid turning the whole plan into permanent revision too early.

### 4.4 Protected Revision

**Educational purpose:** Consolidate previously studied material under Revision Mode authority as the examination approaches.

**Tutor actions:** Reserve calendar capacity that first-pass learning must not consume; prioritise weak areas using evidence-backed estimates where available; deepen application under exam-like conditions.

**Invariant:** Revision capacity is planned *before* packing first-pass density. Compressing revision to “whatever is left” is educationally unlawful under this Model.

### 4.5 Mock & Exam Simulation

**Educational purpose:** Calibrate timing, stamina, and exam behaviour — not invent false pass guarantees.

**Tutor actions:** Place mocks when enough syllabus coverage exists to make the exercise educationally meaningful; allow recovery after mocks; treat mock outcomes as evidence inputs, not destiny.

### 4.6 Final Approach

**Educational purpose:** Stabilise confidence and focus; avoid last-minute syllabus expansion that destroys revision.

**Tutor actions:** Freeze or tightly constrain new first-pass topics; emphasise high-value revision; protect sleep and cognitive freshness before the sitting.

### 4.7 Recovery / Replan (as needed)

**Educational purpose:** Restart that still counts after interruption, illness, leave, or abandoned intensity.

**Tutor actions:** Reassess remaining time; protect what still counts educationally; reduce load before inventing heroic catch-up; never shame the student for life events.

## 5. Educational Inputs Required Before Planning

Planning must not invent missing truth. Collection UX is out of scope for MS001; the **educational requirement** remains.

### 5.1 Mandatory inputs (planning must not begin without them)

| Input | Why required |
|-------|----------------|
| **Examination / subject** | Official syllabus spine and supported-subject integrity |
| **Examination sitting / target date** | Horizon that all phase capacity derives from |
| **Available weekly study time** | Feasibility and intensity envelope |
| **Current syllabus progress** | Starting coverage position (Study Progress / Learning Progress context) |

Without these, an expert tutor would refuse to publish a complete plan — they would only discuss options or gather facts.

### 5.2 Strongly expected inputs (material quality degrades without them)

| Input | Why educationally important |
|-------|-----------------------------|
| **Working schedule / study-day pattern** | Determines which days can carry load |
| **Previous attempts** (pass/fail/history) | Changes risk posture and revision emphasis |
| **Planned leave / known interruptions** | Protects capacity and prevents impossible weeks |
| **Study preferences** (pace, morning/evening, session length bands) | Improves adherence without overriding educational law |
| **Revision expectations** | Student intent for Revision Mode timing and depth |
| **Strengths and weaknesses** (declared and/or evidence-backed estimates) | Guides adaptive emphasis *after* mandatory sequencing |

### 5.3 Optional enriching inputs

| Input | Educational use |
|-------|-----------------|
| Preferred mock timing windows | Refines simulation placement |
| Energy / burnout history | Tightens intensity ceilings |
| Concurrent subjects / competing exams | Forces explicit trade-offs |
| Material access notes (CMP edition, course notes) | Reality-checks resource assumptions — does not generate content |

### 5.4 Input integrity rules

1. Prefer **Observed Facts** and student declarations for coverage and calendar constraints.
2. Treat strengths/weaknesses as **estimates** unless evidence warrant exists.
3. Do not invent exam dates, progress, or available hours.
4. When inputs conflict (e.g. declared “full syllabus remaining” vs evidence of prior coverage), prefer continuity of lawful educational history and disclose uncertainty.
5. Cold-start students receive cautious default assumptions (see `PLANNING_ASSUMPTIONS.md`) — never confident overfit.

## 6. Planning Decision Classes

Every planning behaviour belongs to exactly one class:

| Class | Meaning | Examples |
|-------|---------|----------|
| **Mandatory** | Educationally required whenever a complete plan is produced | Prerequisite-respecting sequence; protected revision window; feasible weekly load; explainability |
| **Adaptive** | May vary with evidence, time pressure, and student context | Intensity within safe bands; consolidation density; mock timing within lawful windows; recovery buffers |
| **Forbidden** | Must never be produced by a planning algorithm | Impossible daily workloads; mastery-by-coverage claims; silent abandonment of syllabus order; consuming revision for first-pass; opaque black-box schedules |

Detail: [`PLANNING_DECISION_MODEL.md`](PLANNING_DECISION_MODEL.md).

## 7. What the Plan Must Optimise

Primary and supporting educational objectives are defined in [`PLANNING_OBJECTIVES.md`](PLANNING_OBJECTIVES.md).

In one sentence:

> Maximise honest exam readiness under sustainable load, while protecting retention, revision capacity, recovery, confidence, and educational truth.

When objectives conflict, the conflict-resolution order in `PLANNING_OBJECTIVES.md` applies. **Truth and feasibility outrank optimistic coverage theatre.**

---

## 8. Permanent Constraints

Permanent constraints are defined in [`PLANNING_CONSTRAINTS.md`](PLANNING_CONSTRAINTS.md).

In one sentence:

> No plan may demand impossible load, violate prerequisite order, erase revision capacity, conflate coverage with mastery, or ignore the student’s real available time.

Constraints are not soft preferences. A plan that violates them is educationally invalid even if it “covers more topics on paper.”

## 9. Relationship to Daily Experience

Long-term planning sets the **journey skeleton**. Daily missions and sessions execute within that skeleton.

| Horizon | Educational job |
|---------|-----------------|
| Long-term plan | Phases, sequencing policy, capacity envelopes, revision/mock placement |
| Weekly structure | Intensity and topic windows consistent with the plan |
| Daily mission | Reduce tonight’s decision burden within authorised Learning / Revision posture |
| Session | Completable study shell with honest practice close |

Master Planner algorithms must not silently redefine Learning Mode authority or Current Learning Topic rules. They allocate capacity and sequence policy; Runtime educational modes remain constitutionally governed.

---

## 10. Determinism and Reproducibility

Core planning judgements that shape the student’s journey must be reproducible from the same educational inputs.

Binding rules:

1. Same syllabus, dates, progress, and capacity inputs → same educational plan skeleton.
2. Random “variety shuffles” of syllabus order are forbidden.
3. Adaptive adjustments must be driven by named educational evidence or declared constraints — not opaque noise.
4. When the algorithm cannot decide safely, it must understate and request inputs rather than invent certainty.

---

## 11. Explainability Obligation

Every material planning decision must be explainable in plain educational language per [`PLANNING_EXPLAINABILITY.md`](PLANNING_EXPLAINABILITY.md) and the Educational Explainability Standard.

Minimum student-facing answers for material plan features:

1. What does this part of the plan ask me to do?
2. Why is this educationally justified?
3. What happens if I follow it?
4. What is known vs estimated?

Internal machinery (scores, twin facets, optimiser labels) must remain invisible.

## 12. Non-Goals of This Milestone

MS001 deliberately does **not**:

- implement study plan generation;
- define SQL schemas or feature flags;
- specify optimisation mathematics;
- redesign missions, recommendations, or Digital Twin;
- collect intake data in product UI.

Those belong to later Master Planner milestones, which must cite this Model as educational law.

---

## 13. Success Test for Future Algorithms

A planning algorithm is educationally compliant when:

1. It consumes only lawful educational inputs (or declared safe defaults), consulting the Student Educational Profile (MS002) for diagnosis and determining educational strategy (MS003) before generating any study plan.
2. It optimises the documented objectives under the documented conflict order.
3. It never violates permanent constraints.
4. Its decisions classify cleanly as mandatory or adaptive — never forbidden.
5. Every material output is explainable without revealing internal machinery.
6. It preserves Study Progress ≠ Estimated Knowledge ≠ Estimated Mastery.
7. It preserves curriculum primacy for both V1 flat and V2 hierarchical syllabuses.

If any test fails, the algorithm is out of compliance — even if coverage charts look complete.
