## 1. Purpose

Without transition law, completion becomes a badge with no coaching consequence — or a silent plan rewrite — or an endless intensive revision loop.

With transition law, an expert tutor can:

- move to maintenance revision when light keep-alive is the honest next posture;
- return the student to ordinary Daily Coach guidance when intensive consolidating emphasis has done enough;
- change revision strategy when completion cannot yet be affirmed but another consolidating route fits;
- escalate to Learning Coach when consolidating approaches have not resolved the underlying educational issue;
- keep honest monitoring without trapping the student in crisis revision speech;
- preserve Recovery Coach, first-learning, and Master Planner / Scheduling authorities when those own the problem (MS002 ST4–ST6).

## 2. Relationship to MS002 Strategy Transitions

| MS002 class | MS003 completion transition | Relationship |
|-------------|----------------------------|--------------|
| **ST1 Maintenance after successful reinforcement** | **RvCT-01** | Specialised success path with RvCC/RvCE gates → RVS-07 (or equivalent keep-alive) |
| **ST1 / ordinary plan absorption** | **RvCT-02** | Successful close → ordinary Daily Coach guidance under authorised plan |
| **ST2 Strategy change within revision** | **RvCT-03** | Alternative revision strategy when completion not yet honest under current RVS |
| **ST3 Escalation to Learning Coach** | **RvCT-04** | Revision insufficient; Learning Coach owns remaining response |
| **ST7 Hold / gather** | **RvCT-05** | Continued monitoring / soft close without false completion |
| **ST4 Recovery Coach handoff** | *(MS002 retains)* | Disruption primary — not a consolidating-success completion |
| **ST5 First-learning handoff** | *(MS002 retains)* | No prior exposure — refuse revision completion |
| **ST6 Structural escalation** | *(MS002 retains)* | Envelopes break — escalate; do not fake local completion |

MS002 remains the strategy-level vocabulary. RvCT-XX are completion-judgement transitions that **align with** ST1–ST7 and add criteria/evidence discipline. They do not author a rival stack.

## 3. Transition Principles

1. **Continuity of learner history.** Completion does not wipe educational identity (EIP-005).  
2. **Explain before switch.** Material transitions are speakable under `COMPLETION_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.  
3. **Success lightens intensity.** RvCT-01 / RvCT-02 end intensive consolidating identity as the lead story when criteria are met.  
4. **Long-term intent unchanged by completion itself.** Structural change uses MS002 ST6 / Master Planner — never completion fiat.  
5. **Incomplete revision is educational information** — not moral failure.  
6. **Escalate to Learning Coach rather than fake consolidating success** when obstacles remain (architectural hard line).  
7. **Monitoring ≠ permanent intensive revision.** RvCT-05 watchfulness must not keep crisis revision identity.  
8. **Learning Coach evidence persists** across completion transitions.  
9. **No streak, session-count, or score gates.** Transitions are educational judgements.  
10. **One primary story in student speech** after the judgement.  
11. **Prior-exposure honesty.** Transitions never reinterpret completion as first learning.  
12. **Preserve Programme VI authority boundaries** across Daily Coach, Learning Coach, Recovery Coach, and Master Planner.

## 4. Transition Catalogue

### RvCT-01 — Maintenance revision

**Educational meaning.** Applicable completion criteria are honestly met on accumulated evidence for the intensive consolidating job. Light protective return — typically **RVS-07 Maintenance Revision** — is now the right educational posture.

**Aligns with:** MS002 ST1.

**Requires:**

- Applicable RvCC set for the intensive strategy affirmed;
- RvCC-06 consolidating purpose fulfilled;
- Supporting RvCE trail without RvCE-06 blockers that force Learning Coach / Recovery / first-learning / structure;
- Explainability can honestly say the intensive job has done enough and light keep-alive is appropriate.

**Lawful effects:**

- Primary strategy becomes RVS-07 (or equivalent light keep-alive inside authorised windows);
- Retain learner history; do not narrate success as mastery earned or exam-certainty declared;
- Keep spaced return available when plan windows warrant;
- Optional light RvCT-05-class watch without intensive revision speech if residual fragility remains mild.

**Unlawful effects:**

- Declaring RvCT-01 because a checklist, streak, session quota, or timer finished;
- Narrating “maintenance” while acute forgetting / fragility remains the leading story;
- Claiming mastery / first learning / exam readiness from RvCT-01;
- Silently continuing intensive RVS-01–RVS-03 loops after claiming maintenance.

**Authority boundary:** Revision Coach ends intensive consolidating identity for that emphasis; Daily Coach still owns today’s priority under the authorised plan; Master Planner intent unchanged.

---

### RvCT-02 — Return to normal Daily Coach guidance

**Educational meaning.** Applicable completion criteria are honestly met. Intensive revision emphasis (and even special maintenance framing) is no longer required as the lead coaching story. Ordinary plan-faithful Daily Coach day-priority selection under the authorised contract is again the right posture — including ordinary authorised revision windows when the plan places them, without Revision Coach crisis identity.

**Aligns with:** MS002 ST1 ordinary-plan absorption; architectural return of day narrative.

**Requires:**

- Applicable RvCC set affirmed (or mild residual risk owned only as ordinary coaching vigilance);
- Supporting RvCE trail without blockers;
- Explainability can honestly say ordinary daily guidance resumes.

**Lawful effects:**

- End intensive revision episode / primary strategy posture as the lead coaching frame;
- Return day-priority authority to ordinary Daily Coach under the authorised plan;
- Retain learner history; do not narrate wiped fragility history as if it never existed;
- Protected revision windows remain plan meaning — Daily Coach may still schedule ordinary consolidating work without opening a new intensive Revision Coach crisis.

**Unlawful effects:**

- Declaring RvCT-02 from elapsed time, attendance, or session ticks alone;
- Keeping intensive revision or punishment volume after claiming resume;
- Claiming mastery / first learning from RvCT-02;
- Silently rewriting plan envelopes “now that revision is done.”

**Authority boundary:** Revision Coach ends intensive consolidating lead story; Daily Coach owns today thereafter; Master Planner intent unchanged.

**Relationship to RvCT-01:**

| Situation | Prefer |
|-----------|--------|
| Intensive job done; light keep-alive still warranted | **RvCT-01** |
| Intensive job done; ordinary plan rhythm sufficient (no special keep-alive story needed) | **RvCT-02** |
| Both mild keep-alive and ordinary guidance | RvCT-01 as strategy posture inside ordinary Daily Coach days — speech must not keep two lead stories |

---

### RvCT-03 — Transition to another revision strategy

**Educational meaning.** Completion cannot yet be honestly affirmed under the current primary strategy; another RVS-XX better fits the evolving consolidating profile. The revision emphasis continues under a different consolidating approach.

**Aligns with:** MS002 ST2.

**When warranted:**

- Success signs for the current strategy are not emerging after a fair consolidating attempt;
- Profile evolved (e.g. retrieval restored → durability across longer spacing remains; foundations refreshed → exam-facing consolidation of known material now leads);
- Evidence clarified a different leading consolidating need — still within prior-exposure honesty.

**Lawful effects:**

- Re-select strategy under `STRATEGY_SELECTION.md`;
- Keep one primary strategy in speech; explain why the approach is changing;
- Preserve continuity: “same consolidating destination; clearer approach.”

**Unlawful effects:**

- Rotating strategies as fake progress when Learning Coach ownership is required (use RvCT-04);
- Strategy change used to invent coverage of unlearned material;
- Declaring “completion” of the old strategy as mastery;
- Endless ST2 oscillation to avoid Learning Coach escalation.

**Authority boundary:** Still local Revision Coach / strategy authority inside envelopes; Learning Coach primary → RvCT-04; structural need → MS002 ST6.

---

### RvCT-04 — Escalation to Learning Coach

**Educational meaning.** Accumulated revision evidence indicates that consolidating approaches are **insufficient** — a deeper learning problem exists (obstacle, thin genuine progression, misconception, false readiness that revision cannot honestly repair). Authority must transition back to the **Learning Coach** rather than extending revision indefinitely.

**Aligns with:** MS002 ST3.

**Architectural requirement restated:**

> **Where revision evidence reveals unresolved learning obstacles,  
> authority must transition back to the Learning Coach  
> rather than extending revision indefinitely.**

**When warranted (must escalate):**

- Fair attempts at appropriate RVS strategies show no meaningful consolidating improvement;
- Learning Coach posture already indicates obstacle / intervention ownership;
- “Covered” material behaves as if understanding never formed, and consolidating speech has become dishonest;
- Revision would need to override Learning Coach evidence to continue;
- Student needs diagnosis / Learning Intervention, not another consolidating approach label;
- RvCE-06 learning-problem blockers dominate the trail.

**Lawful effects:**

- End or demote Revision Coach strategy ownership for that problem;
- Transfer diagnostic / intervention ownership to Learning Coach;
- Explain honestly: revision alone is not enough;
- Do **not** claim Learning Coach success from revision completion;
- Do **not** redefine mastery or invent first learning as “more revision.”

**Unlawful effects:**

- Opening further RVS-02 / RVS-03 loops as a substitute for diagnosis;
- Narrating escalation as student failure rather than educational honesty;
- Keeping Revision Coach as primary while contradicting Learning Coach evidence;
- Using “one more revision week” to delay necessary Learning Coach ownership.

**Authority boundary:** Learning Coach owns remaining educational response; Revision Coach may later supply supporting consolidating emphasis only if Learning Coach allows after diagnosis / intervention meaning.

---

### RvCT-05 — Continued monitoring

**Educational meaning.** Either (a) completion is nearly supportable but residual consolidating risk warrants watchfulness without full intensive revision identity, or (b) evidence is still gathering and false RvCT-01 / RvCT-02 would be dishonest.

**Aligns with:** MS002 ST7.

**Typical uses:**

- Retrieval improving after fragile return, with mild residual durability risk;
- Thin history: consolidating signs emerging, RvCC-04 / RvCC-05 not yet affirmable where required;
- Post-RvCT-01 / RvCT-02 soft watch for re-fragility without reopening intensive crisis unless warrants return.

**Lawful effects:**

- Reduce or end heavy intensive revision speech;
- Keep supportive consolidating tone and honest vigilance;
- Continue gathering RvCE trail;
- Re-open intensive warrant/strategy if forgetting / fragility evidence returns;
- Prefer Learning Coach (RvCT-04) if monitoring reveals unresolved obstacles rather than mere thin history.

**Unlawful effects:**

- Using “monitoring” as permanent unofficial intensive revision without educational warrant;
- Using monitoring to avoid admitting RvCT-03 / RvCT-04 or MS002 ST4–ST6 when those are the honest moves;
- Narrating monitoring as distrust, probation, or shame;
- Inventing mastery clearance during monitoring.

**Authority boundary:** Does not absorb Daily Coach day authority; does not invent structural window change.

## 5. Judgement → Transition Map

| Completion judgement | Primary transition |
|----------------------|-------------------|
| Applicable RvCC met; intensive job done; light keep-alive warranted | **RvCT-01** (+ optional light RvCT-05 watch) |
| Applicable RvCC met; ordinary plan rhythm sufficient | **RvCT-02** |
| Not met; another local consolidating strategy fits better | **RvCT-03** |
| Not met; deeper learning problem / revision insufficient | **RvCT-04** |
| Nearly met / thin history / residual fragility | **RvCT-05** |
| Disruption primary | **MS002 ST4** — Recovery Coach (not consolidating completion) |
| No prior exposure | **MS002 ST5** — first learning (refuse revision completion) |
| Envelopes / feasibility broken | **MS002 ST6** — Rescheduling / Master Planner |
| Warrant unclear | Prefer gather under RvCT-05 / ST7 — do not invent RvCT-01 or ST6 theatre |

## 6. Post-Completion Authority Return (RvCT-01 / RvCT-02 Detail)

On lawful RvCT-01 or RvCT-02:

| Authority | Posture after completion |
|-----------|--------------------------|
| **Revision Coach** | Intensive episode closed; may supply maintenance (RvCT-01) or residual monitoring notes only |
| **Daily Coach** | Owns ordinary day-priority under authorised plan |
| **Learning Coach** | Continues longitudinal meaning; unaffected inventively by completion |
| **Recovery Coach** | Unchanged — completion never absorbs restorative authority |
| **Master Planner** | Long-term intent unchanged by the completion act |
| **Scheduling** | No Revision-Coach-invented packing changes |
| **Evidence / Twin** | No mastery or first-learning write from completion event alone |

Architectural requirement restated:

> **Revision completion strengthens confidence in existing learning.  
> It must never be interpreted as evidence of first learning or mastery.  
> Successful close returns ordinary coaching posture without modifying long-term educational intent.**

```
        Active revision warrant + strategy
                    │
                    ▼

                    │
        ┌───────────┼───────────┬────────────┬────────────┐
        │           │           │            │            │
        ▼           ▼           ▼            ▼            ▼
     RvCT-01     RvCT-02     RvCT-03      RvCT-04      RvCT-05
   Maintenance  Ordinary DC  Alt. RVS    Learning     Monitor /
   (RVS-07)     guidance     (ST2)       Coach        gather
        │           │           │         (ST3)        (ST7)
        │           │           │            │            │
        ▼           ▼           ▼            ▼            ▼
  Keep-alive   Ordinary DC   New primary  LC owns     Soft watch
  + history    + history     RVS-XX       learning    or continue
  preserved    preserved                  response    strategy
```

Sibling exits (not consolidating-success completion):

```
RvCE-06 disruption primary     → MS002 ST4 Recovery Coach
RvCE-06 no prior exposure      → MS002 ST5 first learning
RvCE-06 envelope breach        → MS002 ST6 structural escalation
```

## 8. Continuity and Authority Checklist (Every Material Transition)

Before enacting RvCT-01…RvCT-05, confirm:

| Check | Fail action |
|-------|-------------|
| Is learner history preserved in speech and meaning? | Rewrite narration; never invent wipe |
| Does prior exposure still hold for completed “revision”? | Refuse; use ST5 first learning |
| Does evidence (not time/ticks/repetitions alone) support the move? | Gather more / choose a different RvCT |
| Does Daily Coach still own today’s priority under the current contract? | Do not commandeer the day via completion speech |
| Is long-term intent change required? | Use MS002 ST6 — do not mutate plan in Revision Coach |
| Is mastery or first learning being claimed from the transition? | Refuse; keep EIP-006 honesty |
| Is a learning obstacle now primary? | Use RvCT-04 — do not extend revision indefinitely |
| Is disruption primary? | Use MS002 ST4 Recovery Coach |
| Can the student hear why the approach is changing? | Produce explainability first |

---

## 9. Anti-Transition Patterns (Forbidden)

- Permanent intensive revision with no completion criteria  
- RvCT-01 / RvCT-02 from elapsed time, attendance, session ticks, or repetition counts alone  
- Silent strategy oscillation without educational reason  
- Endless revision loops that avoid Learning Coach escalation  
- “Revision completed” mastery or first-learning speech  
- Returning to normal coaching while still demanding intensive crisis volume  
- Using transition to bypass Learning Coach diagnosis  
- Using maintenance as euphemism for unresolved fragility  
- Monitoring as shame probation  
- Completing catch-up disruption as consolidating success
