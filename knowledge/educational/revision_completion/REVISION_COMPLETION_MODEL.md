## Authority

This document is the constitutional overview of **revision completion** for Kwalitec.

It is subordinate to the Educational Constitution, specialised educational models, the Educational Revision Model (MS001), and the Revision Strategy Framework (MS002). It governs **how Kwalitec judges whether consolidating revision has strengthened previously learned knowledge enough**, and **what educational transitions follow**, while remaining faithful to the Canonical Study Plan and sibling coach authorities. It does not authorise implementation shortcuts that contradict the Constitution, and it does not invent prior learning, evidence, mastery, strategy, or planning meaning absent from upstream models.

Authority order for revision completion:

> Constitution defines educational truth and curriculum primacy.  
> Knowledge & Mastery defines coverage ≠ understanding ≠ mastery.  
> Evidence Model defines what may warrant educational claims.  
> Continuity Standard preserves rightful educational history across plan change.  
> Master Planner publishes the Canonical Study Plan and owns long-term educational envelopes, including protected revision windows.  
> Daily Coach owns *what is most educationally valuable today* under that contract.  
> Learning Coach owns longitudinal progression, obstacle diagnosis, and learning interventions.  
> Recovery Coach owns restorative coaching after meaningful disruption.  
> Educational Revision Model (MS001) defines *whether* revision is warranted and *which* consolidating objectives / priorities apply.  
> Revision Strategy Framework (MS002) defines *what kind* of consolidating approach is appropriate and strategy-level transitions.  
> **This Revision Completion Model (Workstream 5 / MS003) defines whether revision has achieved its consolidating purpose and what educational transition follows.**  
> Downstream coaching and narration surfaces must consume this educational reasoning — never treat completion as first learning, mastery, or a silent plan rewrite.

## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not keep a student in intensive revision forever — nor declare consolidating success because a calendar week elapsed, a checklist finished, or a few revision sessions were ticked. After a lawful revision emphasis, the tutor answers one living educational question:

> **“Has this revision successfully strengthened the learner’s existing knowledge?”**

That answer must rest on accumulated educational evidence, strengthen confidence in *already learned* material, lighten or redirect consolidating posture honestly, return day-priority authority to ordinary Daily Coach guidance when intensive revision has done enough, escalate to Learning Coach when consolidating approaches have not resolved the underlying educational issue, and refuse to mint first learning or mastery from revision completion.

This document records that tutor posture so every future Revision Coach subsystem has a single educational reference for *completion meaning*.

> **The Revision Completion Model describes educational completion reasoning.  
> It does not implement Runtime A logic, algorithms, analytics, or Twin writers.**

## 2. What Revision Completion Is

**Revision completion** is the educational judgement that a lawful consolidating revision emphasis has **sufficiently strengthened previously learned knowledge** — retrieval, durability, understanding reinforcement, concept integration, prerequisite stability, and/or exam-usable readiness as the active RVO goods require — for the student to **move to maintenance, resume ordinary plan-faithful coaching, change consolidating strategy, or hand unresolved learning problems to the Learning Coach**, without claiming that first learning occurred or that mastery was earned by the revision journey alone.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Revision emphasis / episode** | Active consolidating coaching under a lawful warrant and strategy | Is revision still the lead consolidating story? |
| **Completion criteria** | Educational conditions that indicate revision success | What must be true educationally? |
| **Completion evidence** | Accumulated observations that may support those conditions | What trail justifies the claim? |
| **Completion judgement** | Achieved / not yet / incomplete destination | Has revision strengthened existing knowledge enough? |
| **Completion transition** | Educational move that follows the judgement | What changes next? |
| **Post-completion monitoring** | Residual watchfulness without intensive revision speech | What vigilance or maintenance remains? |

Completion is:

- **evidence-justified** — rests on accumulated Educational Evidence, not elapsed time or session count;
- **consolidating** — success means existing learning is stronger, not that new syllabus content was acquired;
- **non-mastery** — completion ≠ first learning ≠ understanding as new fact ≠ mastery ≠ exam-certainty;
- **confidence-strengthening** — affirms that known material is more durable / retrievable / integrated where evidence supports it;
- **authority-preserving** — successful close returns ordinary Daily Coach guidance under the authorised plan when intensive revision is no longer the lead story;
- **transition-aware** — may move to maintenance, ordinary coaching, another strategy, Learning Coach escalation, or continued monitoring;
- **explainable** — students understand why completion is (or is not) affirmed;
- **non-scoring** — qualitative educational judgement only.

Completion is **not**:

- a calendar milestone, streak unlock, or revision-checklist finish line;
- inference from attendance, repetition count, or session completion alone;
- a licence to invent mastery or first learning from revision volume;
- a substitute for Master Planner planning or strategy selection at the long-term layer;
- a bypass of Daily Coach day authority or Learning Coach evidence/diagnosis layers;
- a claim that revision guarantees a pass;
- an excuse to extend intensive revision indefinitely “to be safe.”

## 3. Binding Distinction: Completion vs Neighbours

| Layer | Educational job | Unlawful collapse |
|-------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Trigger (RVT)** | Warrant that revision is needed | Declaring completion without a lawful consolidating emphasis ever existing |
| **Objective (RVO)** | Name consolidating goods to strengthen | Treating checklist clearance as the completion condition |
| **Strategy (RVS)** | Name the *kind* of consolidating approach | Equating “strategy finished” with educational completion |
| **Strategy transition (ST1–ST7)** | Strategy-level maintenance / change / escalate vocabulary | Inventing a rival transition law that ignores MS002 |
| **Completion (this corpus)** | Judge whether existing knowledge was sufficiently strengthened | Treating time elapsed, repetitions, or ticks as proof |
| **Daily Coach** | Own today’s priority under the current contract | Letting completion speech commandeer the day or keep intensive revision after resume |
| **Learning Coach** | Own progression / obstacle / intervention meaning | Using completion to mint learning success or skip diagnosis; extending revision instead of ST3/RvCT-04 |
| **Master Planner / Scheduling** | Own structural envelope and allocation change | Absorbing replan inside “completion” theatre |
| **Recovery Coach** | Own restorative disruption coaching | Disguising catch-up completion as consolidating success |

Hard rule:

> **Strategy compliance is not completion.  
> Attendance is not completion.  
> Elapsed time is not completion.  
> Session or repetition count is not completion.  
> Completion strengthens confidence in existing learning; it does not mint first learning or mastery.**

## 4. Educational Purpose

The Revision Completion Model exists so that:

1. **Revision emphases end for educational reasons** — not because a timer expired or a quota of sittings finished.
2. **Students are not trapped in permanent intensive revision** once existing knowledge is honestly stronger.
3. **False completion is refused** when only attendance, repetition counts, session ticks, or calendar clearance exist.
4. **Maintenance and ordinary coaching become speakable** when light keep-alive or plan-faithful rhythm is again the right posture.
5. **Unresolved learning obstacles return to Learning Coach** — revision does not extend indefinitely as a soft diagnosis substitute.
6. **First-learning honesty is preserved** — completion never pretends unlearned material became known through revision theatre.
7. **Students understand the close of the arc** — why success (or not), what evidence, what changes next, what maintenance or monitoring remains.

## 5. Core Responsibilities

The Revision Completion Model is educationally responsible for:

| Responsibility | Meaning |
|----------------|---------|
| **State completion criteria** | Name educational conditions that indicate consolidating success (RvCC-XX) |
| **Bound completion evidence** | Require EIP-002-honest accumulation; forbid attendance-only / tick-only / repetition-count-only proofs |
| **Produce a completion judgement** | Achieved, not yet, or incomplete destination requiring another transition |
| **Select completion transitions** | Maintenance, ordinary Daily Coach, strategy change, Learning Coach escalation, or continued monitoring |
| **Return coaching authority lawfully** | On success, restore ordinary Daily Coach day-priority under the authorised plan when intensive revision is no longer primary |
| **Preserve sibling authorities** | Never rewrite plan, strategy, mastery, Learning Coach, or Recovery Coach meanings via completion |
| **Require post-completion honesty** | Name residual maintenance or monitoring without keeping crisis revision speech |
| **Explain supportively** | Make completion speakable without false certainty, shame, or mastery ceremony |

It is **not** educationally responsible for:

- rewriting Canonical Study Plans or educational strategy;
- deciding today’s Daily Coach primary objective after resume (Daily Coach owns that);
- diagnosing learning obstacles or selecting Learning Interventions;
- inferring or redefining mastery;
- introducing new syllabus content as completed “revision”;
- writing Twin estimates by itself;
- producing opaque “completion scores” as educational proof;
- inventing numerical session-count or day-count thresholds as educational law.

## 6. Binding Educational Doctrine

1. **Completion is an educational judgement, not a time-based or session-count milestone.** Calendar elapsed and revision sitting quotas do not complete revision.
2. **Completion must be justified by accumulated educational evidence.** Thin or activity-only trails understate; they do not certify success.
3. **Completion strengthens confidence in existing learning.** It must never be interpreted as evidence of first learning or mastery (EIP-006).
4. **Where revision evidence reveals unresolved learning obstacles, authority must transition back to the Learning Coach** rather than extending revision indefinitely.
5. **Attendance, repetition count, and session completion alone are insufficient.** Completing revision sittings may be activity; completion requires consolidating educational meaning from the evidence trail.
6. **MS001 ending posture and MS002 ST1–ST7 remain binding.** This corpus specialises them; it does not weaken them with softer theatre.
7. **Daily Coach authority is preserved.** After successful close of intensive revision emphasis, ordinary day-priority selection resumes under the authorised plan; Revision Coach does not retain crisis commandeering.
8. **Learning Coach evidence is preserved.** Completion does not invent progression success or dissolve an active obstacle diagnosis.
9. **Canonical Study Plan remains the contract.** Completion may end intensive consolidating posture; it may not independently publish a new plan.
10. **Explainability is mandatory.** Silent “you’re done revising” and silent permanent intensive revision are both forbidden (EIP-003).

## 8. Relationship to MS002 Strategy Transitions

MS002 `STRATEGY_TRANSITIONS.md` already states that successful reinforcement may move to maintenance (ST1), strategies may change within revision (ST2), deeper learning problems escalate to Learning Coach (ST3), and hold/gather applies when success is unclear (ST7).

This Completion Model:

- **does not replace** those strategy transition laws;
- **specialises** them into named criteria (RvCC-XX), evidence classes (RvCE-XX), and post-judgement transitions (RvCT-XX);
- **tightens** the evidence obligation so attendance-only, repetition-count-only, session-completion-only, and time-based completions are explicitly unlawful;
- **separates** successful consolidating strengthen from first-learning and mastery speech more sharply for student-facing completion narration;
- **hardens** the Learning Coach escalation line so unresolved obstacles do not become endless revision.

Recovery handoff (ST4), first-learning handoff (ST5), and structural escalation (ST6) remain MS002-owned when those authorities are primary; this corpus does not absorb them into “revision completion success.”
