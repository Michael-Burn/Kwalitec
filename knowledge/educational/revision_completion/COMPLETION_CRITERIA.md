> **Completion criteria are educational conditions — not scoring dimensions, session-count thresholds, or revision checklists.  
> Success is judged from accumulated educational evidence, not elapsed time or the amount of revision completed.  
> Completion strengthens confidence in existing learning; it never certifies first learning or mastery.**

---

## 1. Purpose

Without explicit completion criteria, revision either never ends (permanent intensive consolidating mode) or ends falsely (a few ticks, a finished checklist, a repetition quota, or a week on the clock).

With explicit criteria, an expert tutor can affirm that consolidating coaching has strengthened previously learned knowledge enough for maintenance, ordinary Daily Coach guidance, or another honest consolidating move — while refusing mastery theatre and endless revision loops over unresolved Learning Coach problems.

---

## 2. Criteria Principles

1. **Educational first.** Criteria describe strengthened existing learning, not operational convenience.
2. **Evidence-bound.** Each criterion requires supporting trail under `COMPLETION_EVIDENCE.md`.
3. **Objective-aligned.** Criteria map to RVO-01…RVO-05 goods that revision was pursuing.
4. **Strategy-sensitive emphasis.** Different RVS-XX approaches emphasise different criteria; successful closes still require the applicable consolidating conditions for the active goods.
5. **Prior-exposure gated.** Criteria presuppose lawful prior exposure — they never complete “revision” of unlearned material.
6. **Non-mastery / non-first-learning.** Meeting criteria strengthens confidence in existing knowledge; it does not certify mastery or invent first learning.
7. **Non-temporal / non-volumetric.** No criterion is satisfied merely because time passed or many revision sittings occurred.
8. **Speakable.** Criteria must be explainable in plain educational language (`COMPLETION_EXPLAINABILITY.md`).

## 3. Criterion Catalogue

IDs (`RvCC-XX`) exist for audit and cross-reference. They must not appear as student-facing jargon.

### RvCC-01 — Strengthened retrieval

**Definition.** The student’s ability to recall and produce previously learned knowledge and methods has improved under consolidating work — knowledge is more findable without undue scaffolding than when the revision warrant opened.

**Maps primarily to:** RVO-01; typical for RVS-02 and often RVS-01 / RVS-03 / RVS-06.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional examinations reward what can be retrieved under time pressure. Revision succeeds when known material becomes more usable again — not when notes were merely re-read or a quota of questions was finished.

**Success looks like:**

- Active recall / production on previously learned scope shows honest improvement across returns (not only same-day success).
- Less scaffolding is needed than at the opening of the consolidating emphasis.
- Speech names retrieval strength as the educational good, not “covering the topic again.”

**Must not:**

- Equate passive re-reading or checklist ticks with strengthened retrieval.
- Claim retrieval success from a single sitting without any return warrant.
- Treat question volume as proof of retrieval strength.

**Evidence posture:** Requires RvCE-01 — never attendance or repetition count alone.

---

### RvCC-02 — Improved durability

**Definition.** Previously demonstrated understanding shows improved likelihood of persisting across spacing — learning is less fragile across the weeks between study and examination than when the consolidating warrant opened.

**Maps primarily to:** RVO-02; typical for RVS-01 and post-success RVS-07 readiness.

**Tutor rationale.** IFoA journeys are long. Revision succeeds when known material holds better across returns — not when calendar proximity or recent activity creates a temporary familiarity theatre.

**Success looks like:**

- Spaced return shows less acute forgetting / fragility than the warrant that opened revision.
- Durability claims remain provisional and evidence-honest (EIP-002 / EIP-006).
- Intensive durability reinforcement can honestly lighten toward maintenance.

**Must not:**

- Treat calendar proximity alone as proof of durability.
- Narrate revision ticks or elapsed revision weeks as permanent retention.
- Declare durability while Learning Coach evidence still shows thin genuine progression on the same material.

**Evidence posture:** Requires RvCE-02 with RvCE-01 support where retrieval was also in focus.

---

### RvCC-03 — Successful concept integration

**Definition.** Previously learned related topics that were in consolidating focus now cohere more usable as a network — the student can connect interdependent ideas that had been studied separately — without inventing coverage of unlearned parts.

**Maps primarily to:** RVO-04; typical for RVS-05; may support RVS-06 when exam-facing integration of *known* material was the job.

**Tutor rationale.** IFoA papers reward recognising when ideas travel together. Integration completion means known parts work together better — not that new syllabus breadth was acquired under an integration label.

**Success looks like:**

- Linked previously learned units emphasised by the strategy show honest joint use / transfer improvement.
- The student can see why Topic A and Topic B belong together under exam / curriculum use.
- Integration speech remains confined to units with prior exposure.

**Must not:**

- Force “integration complete” when one linked topic still lacks prior exposure.
- Treat finishing a multi-topic checklist as integration success.
- Use integration completion to invent first-pass breadth.

**Evidence posture:** Requires RvCE-03 when this criterion is in focus.

**Applicability rule:**

> **RvCC-03 is binding when RVO-04 / RVS-05 (or equivalent integration emphasis) was in focus.  
> It is not a hidden universal gate for every retrieval-only revision episode.**

---

### RvCC-04 — Stable prerequisite understanding

**Definition.** Where revision emphasised studied foundations that onward work depends on (typically RVS-04 / RVT-05), those previously learned prerequisites are **stable enough for safe continuation** of the authorised next educational work — not fully mastered.

**Maps primarily to:** RVO-03 (and supporting RVO-01); consumes Learning Coach meaning when present.

**Tutor rationale.** After fragile foundation return, ordinary progression can be unsafe. Completion of prerequisite-focused revision requires educational signs that known foundations hold well enough to continue — without inventing mastery from a short refresh set.

**Success looks like:**

- Emphasised prerequisite / foundation scope shows honest consolidating improvement or safety sufficient for the next authorised plan job.
- Learning Coach evidence, where present, does not contradict the stability claim.
- Thin history yields understatement: gather more evidence rather than declare stability from hope.

**Must not:**

- Apply RvCC-04 as a universal mastery gate when foundations were never in focus.
- Narrate prerequisites “mastered” or “fully learned for the first time” from revision completion.
- Infer stability from coverage ticks or calendar clearance of foundation topics.
- Keep looping RVS-04 when evidence shows understanding never formed — escalate to Learning Coach (RvCT-04).

**Evidence posture:** Requires RvCE-04 when this criterion is in focus; soft confidence alone never substitutes.

**Applicability rule:**

> **RvCC-04 is binding when the active strategy / warrant put prerequisite refresh of studied foundations in focus.  
> It is not a hidden universal mastery gate for every revision episode.**

---

### RvCC-05 — Maintained exam readiness

**Definition.** Previously learned material emphasised for exam-facing consolidation remains in a condition that supports authentic exam performance — timed retrieval, method selection, application under sitting conditions — without claiming guaranteed success.

**Maps primarily to:** RVO-05; typical for RVS-06; may be a light reading under protected revision windows when exam proximity warrants.

**Tutor rationale.** As the examination approaches, revision succeeds when *already known* material stays exam-usable. An expert tutor maintains provisional readiness strength; the tutor does not invent readiness from unlearned syllabus or from panic volume alone.

**Success looks like:**

- Exam-facing consolidating emphasis on known material shows honest improvement or maintained strength relative to the warrant.
- Readiness speech remains provisional and consistent with Learning Objectives LO-07 / EIP-006 honesty.
- Protected revision windows used for consolidating exam-usable strength can honestly lighten or return to ordinary coaching when the consolidating job is done.

**Must not:**

- Claim the student is “exam ready” because a revision phase label or completion event exists.
- Convert exam readiness into a numeric score in this corpus.
- Use exam-readiness completion to excuse introducing new content as revision.
- Equate completed past-paper volume with maintained readiness of unlearned scope.

**Evidence posture:** Requires RvCE-05 when this criterion is in focus; never sole proof from paper count.

**Applicability rule:**

> **RvCC-05 is binding when RVO-05 / RVS-06 (or equivalent exam-facing consolidation of known material) was in focus.  
> It is not a universal “exam ready” certificate for every revision close.**

---

### RvCC-06 — Consolidating purpose fulfilled (synthesis)

**Definition.** The active revision emphasis has honestly fulfilled its consolidating purpose for the target material — applicable RVO goods are sufficiently advanced that intensive revision is no longer the lead educational story (or the honest next move is maintenance / ordinary coaching / Learning Coach handoff rather than more of the same).

**Maps primarily to:** MS002 ST1 success posture; architectural close of intensive consolidating identity.

**Tutor rationale.** Completion is not only that one metric improved — it is that the tutor can honestly say the consolidating job for this emphasis has done enough, or that continuing revision would be dishonest.

**Success looks like:**

- Applicable RvCC-01…RvCC-05 set for the active goods is honestly affirmable — **or** Learning Coach has taken ownership of remaining learning response (then prefer RvCT-04 rather than pure consolidating-success speech).
- Explainability can honestly say intensive revision has done enough for now, maintenance is appropriate, or ordinary guidance resumes.
- Prior exposure and Revision Mode honesty remain intact — completion does not rebrand first learning.

**Must not:**

- Declare consolidating purpose fulfilled while still demanding intensive crisis revision indefinitely.
- Use RvCC-06 to mint mastery or first learning.
- Claim purpose fulfilled from checklist finish, session count, or elapsed time alone.

**Evidence posture:** Synthesis criterion — affirmed only when the applicable RvCC-01…RvCC-05 set is honestly met and evidence under `COMPLETION_EVIDENCE.md` supports the claim — **or** when blockers force RvCT-04 rather than fake success.

## 4. How Criteria Combine for Successful Completion

### 4.1 Core synthesis (always required for consolidating-success closes)

Successful maintenance (RvCT-01) or return to ordinary Daily Coach guidance (RvCT-02) requires honest affirmation of:

1. **RvCC-06** Consolidating purpose fulfilled  
2. The **applicable objective-aligned subset** of RvCC-01…RvCC-05 for the active RVO / RVS emphasis  
3. Supporting RvCE trail without RvCE-06 blockers that force Learning Coach / Recovery / first-learning / structural moves

### 4.2 Conditional criteria

| Criterion | When required |
|-----------|---------------|
| **RvCC-01** Strengthened retrieval | When RVO-01 / RVS-02 (or retrieval-leading RVS-01 / RVS-03 / RVS-06) was in focus |
| **RvCC-02** Improved durability | When RVO-02 / RVS-01 (or durability-leading emphasis) was in focus |
| **RvCC-03** Successful concept integration | When RVO-04 / RVS-05 was in focus |
| **RvCC-04** Stable prerequisite understanding | When RVS-04 / RVT-05 foundation refresh was in focus |
| **RvCC-05** Maintained exam readiness | When RVO-05 / RVS-06 exam-facing consolidation of known material was in focus |

### 4.3 Strategy emphasis (illustrative)

| Strategy emphasis | Criteria that typically lead |
|-------------------|------------------------------|
| RVS-01 Durability Reinforcement | RvCC-02 (+ RvCC-01 support), then RvCC-06 |
| RVS-02 Retrieval Strengthening | RvCC-01, then RvCC-06 |
| RVS-03 Fragile Knowledge Stabilisation | RvCC-01 + RvCC-02 (understanding reinforcement reading), then RvCC-06 — else RvCT-04 if understanding never formed |
| RVS-04 Prerequisite Refresh | RvCC-04 (+ RvCC-01), then RvCC-06 |
| RVS-05 Concept Integration | RvCC-03, then RvCC-06 |
| RVS-06 Examination Consolidation | RvCC-05 (+ retrieval/durability as scoped), then RvCC-06 |
| RVS-07 Maintenance Revision | Light RvCC-02 / RvCC-01 keep-alive — completion often means “remain on maintenance” or RvCT-02; do not invent intensive success theatre |

### 4.4 Binding combination rule

> **All applicable criteria must be honestly affirmable from accumulated evidence.  
> Missing an applicable criterion means consolidating completion is not yet achieved — use RvCT-05 hold/monitor language only if gathering evidence, otherwise continue strategy, change strategy (RvCT-03), escalate to Learning Coach (RvCT-04), or apply MS002 ST4–ST6 when Recovery / first learning / structure owns the problem.**

## 5. Explicit Non-Criteria (Forbidden Completion Grounds)

The following **never** complete revision by themselves:

| Non-criterion | Why forbidden |
|---------------|---------------|
| Elapsed calendar days / weeks | Time is not educational evidence of strengthened knowledge |
| Session or revision sitting count | Volume ≠ consolidating success |
| Repetition / question count alone | Activity without educational meaning (EIP-002) |
| Attendance or login frequency alone | Presence ≠ retrieval / durability / integration |
| Session / mission completion ticks alone | Completing sittings ≠ revision completion |
| Revision checklist finished | Compliance ≠ strengthened existing learning |
| Strategy catalogue item “done” | Strategy compliance ≠ educational completion |
| Student declaration “I’ve revised enough” alone | Soft signal; may support monitoring posture only with corroboration |
| Calendar “revision phase” label cleared | Phase theatre ≠ consolidating evidence |
| Comparison to other candidates’ revision volume | Shame theatre, not educational criterion |
| Passive re-reading completed | Not strengthened retrieval |

## 6. Relationship to Revision Objectives

| Objective | Completion reading |
|-----------|-------------------|
| RVO-01 Retrieval | Advanced when RvCC-01 holds |
| RVO-02 Durability | Advanced when RvCC-02 holds |
| RVO-03 Understanding | Where in focus (often via RVS-03 / RVS-04), evidence-honest reinforcement — **not** certified as mastery by completion |
| RVO-04 Integration | Advanced when RvCC-03 holds |
| RVO-05 Exam readiness | Advanced provisionally when RvCC-05 holds — never as exam-certainty |

Hard rule:

> **Meeting completion criteria means consolidating goods are sufficiently advanced for the next educational posture.  
> It does not mean first learning occurred, Learning Objectives (LO-XX) were attained as mastery, or the exam is assured.**

---

## 7. Anti-Patterns (Forbidden)

- Declaring success because “two weeks of revision elapsed”
- Declaring success because the student completed N revision sessions or N past papers
- Requiring universal exam-readiness (RvCC-05) or integration (RvCC-03) for every retrieval-only episode
- Keeping intensive revision after all applicable criteria are met (refusal to return authority / move to maintenance)
- Extending revision indefinitely when Learning Coach ownership is the honest move
- Using criteria as numeric scores or dashboards presented as educational truth
- Narrating criteria IDs to students
- Narrating completion as first learning or mastery graduation
