## 1. Purpose

Without evidence law, “revision complete” becomes either optimistic theatre (a few ticks, a finished checklist) or opaque optimiser speech.

With evidence law, completion claims remain epistemically honest: consolidating strengthenings of *existing* knowledge may be affirmed when the trail supports them; first-learning and mastery speech stay forbidden; thin history yields understatement.

---

## 2. EIP-002 Principles Applied to Completion

All EIP-002 principles bind completion judgements. Specialised readings:

| EIP-002 principle | Completion application |
|-------------------|------------------------|
| Evidence is observed | Completion cites educational happenings, not desired conclusions written backwards |
| Evidence accumulates | One favourable revision sitting does not complete an emphasis |
| Evidence has quality | Soft self-report ≠ high-certainty durability or exam readiness |
| Activity alone is not knowledge | Attendance / ticks / repetition counts are not completion proof |
| Inference may not invent evidence | “Looks revised enough” without trail is unlawful |
| Absence of evidence is uncertainty | Thin consolidating history → continue gathering; do not certify |
| Coverage ≠ understanding | Cleared revision checklist / Study Progress ≠ consolidating success or mastery |

Architectural requirement restated:

> **Revision completion must be justified by accumulated educational evidence.**

---

## 3. What Completion Evidence Is For

Completion evidence supports claims about **strengthened existing learning and consolidating goods** (RvCC-XX).

It does **not** by itself authorise:

- Estimated Mastery uplift;
- first-learning of new syllabus content;
- exam-readiness certainty;
- erasure of prior fragility history;
- rewriting of Canonical Study Plan envelopes.

Where completion speech touches understanding, prerequisite stability, or exam readiness (RvCC-04 / RvCC-05), it may only claim **sufficient consolidating strengthen / safety / provisional readiness** for the next authorised posture — never high mastery language from revision alone.

## 4. Evidence Class Catalogue

IDs (`RvCE-XX`) exist for audit and cross-reference. They must not appear as student-facing jargon.

### RvCE-01 — Retrieval strengthen trail

**Definition.** Accumulated observations that recall and production of previously learned material improved under consolidating work — across returns, not only within a single sitting — without treating mere presence or question volume as learning success.

**May support:** RvCC-01, RvCC-06; contributes to RvCC-02 / RvCC-05 when those depend on usable retrieval.

**May include (educationally meaningful):**

- Pattern of improved active recall / production on previously learned scope across spaced returns;
- Reduced scaffolding need relative to the opening of the revision warrant;
- Practice outcomes that show consolidating participation on *known* material — still without minting mastery from completion events alone (EL-004).

**Must not treat as sufficient alone:**

- Login streaks or attendance;
- Question / repetition counts without retrieval meaning;
- A single same-day success after intensive prompting;
- Session completed flags.

**Quality note:** Retrieval trail is primarily evidence about **consolidating production strength**, not about first learning or mastery.

---

### RvCE-02 — Durability trail

**Definition.** Accumulated observations that previously demonstrated understanding holds better across spacing than when the consolidating warrant opened — provisional, evidence-honest, never permanent retention theatre.

**May support:** RvCC-02, RvCC-06; supports light RVS-07 maintenance posture.

**May include:**

- Spaced return showing reduced acute forgetting / fragility relative to the warrant;
- Persistence of workable retrieval across successive authorised returns (qualitative accumulation — not a numeric unlock);
- Alignment with Profile decay risk: durability claims remain modest when history is thin.

**Must not treat as sufficient alone:**

- Elapsed weeks since the warrant opened;
- Calendar proximity to the exam;
- Revision phase labels cleared;
- One good return after a long gap with no further trail.

**Hard rule:**

> **Do not infer successful revision from attendance, repetition count, or session completion alone.**

---

### RvCE-03 — Integration trail

**Definition.** Observations that previously learned related topics in consolidating focus cohere more usable together — joint use, transfer, interdependence recognition — without inventing coverage of unlearned units.

**May support:** RvCC-03; contributes honesty to RvCC-05 / RvCC-06 when exam-facing integration of known material was in focus.

**May include:**

- Practice / explanation / application that links previously studied interdependent units;
- Reduced isolated-topic brittleness when joint use was the consolidating job;
- Honest understatement when one linked unit still lacks prior exposure (then refuse integration completion; route that unit to first learning).

**Must not:**

- Infer integration success from multi-topic checklist finish;
- Infer integration from studying one known topic and one unlearned topic under an “integration” label;
- Upgrade mastery of either topic solely because integration revision is declared complete.

---

### RvCE-04 — Prerequisite stability evidence

**Definition.** Educational Evidence (EIP-002) and Learning Coach meaning indicating that studied foundations emphasised by RVS-04 / RVT-05 are **stable enough** for the next authorised work — when RvCC-04 is in focus.

**May support:** RvCC-04; contributes honesty to RvCC-06 when foundation refresh was active.

**May include:**

- Practice / retrieval / attempt observations on the emphasised previously learned prerequisite scope;
- Learning Coach progression or obstacle evidence that foundation risk has eased **or** is now owned as an LOB/LI problem (then prefer RvCT-04);
- Honest understatement when observations conflict or remain thin.

**Must not:**

- Infer prerequisite stability from Study Progress coverage alone;
- Infer first learning of foundations from “revision refresh” speech;
- Upgrade Estimated Mastery solely because revision completion is declared;
- Clear Learning Coach foundation diagnoses by Revision Coach fiat.

**Quality note:** Prefer higher-quality educational observations for RvCC-04. Soft self-report alone is insufficient for prerequisite stability claims.

---

### RvCE-05 — Exam-usable readiness trail

**Definition.** Accumulated signs that previously learned material emphasised for exam-facing consolidation supports authentic exam performance conditions — timed retrieval, method selection, application — always provisional.

**May support:** RvCC-05, RvCC-06 when RVS-06 / RVO-05 was in focus.

**May include:**

- Exam-facing consolidating practice on *known* scope showing honest improvement or maintained strength;
- Method selection / application under more authentic conditions than at warrant open;
- Alignment with protected revision intent for consolidating exam-usable strength of already learned material.

**Must not:**

- Infer exam readiness from past-paper volume alone;
- Infer readiness of unlearned syllabus from revision completion;
- Claim guaranteed pass or high mastery from readiness trail;
- Equate calendar “exam revision phase” with evidence of readiness.

---

### RvCE-06 — Negative / incompleteness evidence

**Definition.** Observations that **block** consolidating-success completion even if some positive signals exist.

**Must block or delay RvCT-01 / RvCT-02 when present:**

- Prior exposure missing for the target material (first learning — MS002 ST5; never revision completion);
- Meaningful disruption primary (Recovery Coach — MS002 ST4);
- Unresolved learning obstacle / thin genuine progression / misconception that consolidating approaches have not repaired (Learning Coach — RvCT-04);
- Repeated same-pattern failure after fair attempts at appropriate RVS strategies;
- Only attendance / repetition counts / session-completion / elapsed-time signals available;
- Envelope breach requiring structural change (completion must not fake local success — MS002 ST6);
- Learning Coach evidence contradicts the consolidating-success claim.

Correct uncertainty, strategy change, or Learning Coach escalation beat false completion and endless revision.

## 5. Forbidden Inference Patterns

The following inferences are **educationally unlawful** for completion:

| Observed | Unlawful inference |
|----------|-------------------|
| Student attended / logged in | Revision complete |
| Session or mission marked complete | Revision complete / mastery / first learning |
| N revision sessions completed | Existing knowledge strengthened enough |
| N questions / repetitions finished | Retrieval / durability / readiness achieved |
| N days elapsed since warrant | Revision complete |
| Revision checklist finished | Consolidating purpose fulfilled |
| Strategy catalogue item marked done | Educational completion achieved |
| Student re-read notes | Strengthened retrieval |
| Student rested during a revision window | Knowledge durable |
| One good practice set | Mastery / full prerequisite reconstruction / exam ready |
| Confidence self-report alone | All RvCC criteria met |
| Comparison “others revised more” | Student has or has not completed revision |

## 6. Accumulation and Sufficiency

### 6.1 Accumulation

Evidence for completion **accumulates**. Interpreters revise the completion judgement as history grows. Early consolidating returns typically support gentle retrieval claims before stronger durability or exam-readiness claims.

### 6.2 Sufficiency (qualitative)

A completion judgement is evidence-sufficient when:

1. Applicable RvCC criteria each have supporting RvCE classes (not merely hoped);
2. RvCE-06 blockers are absent or honestly redirected to RvCT-03 / RvCT-04 / RvCT-05 or MS002 ST4–ST6;
3. Claims that touch understanding, prerequisite stability, or exam readiness use evidence quality appropriate to the claim;
4. Explainability can name the trail in plain language without inventing certainty or mastery.

This Model does **not** define numerical sample sizes, scores, session quotas, or half-lives. Sufficiency is educational judgement under EIP-002 quality discipline.

### 6.3 Thin history rule

After opening a consolidating emphasis, history may be thin. Lawful responses:

- Continue the strategy and gather evidence;
- Affirm only weaker consolidating claims if clearly supported;
- Prefer RvCT-05 continued monitoring over RvCT-01 / RvCT-02 celebration when residual fragility remains;
- Never fill thin history with mastery, first-learning, or “fully revised” speech.

## 7. Relationship to Learning Coach Evidence

| Situation | Evidence reading | Completion implication |
|-----------|------------------|------------------------|
| Retrieval/durability improved; Learning Coach shows stalled genuine progression | RvCE-01/02 may support consolidating strengthen; learning problem remains | Prefer **RvCT-04** — do not claim learning success from revision completion |
| Fragile knowledge strategy with LOB diagnosis emerging | RvCE trail merges into obstacle evidence | Handoff; do not keep revision as soft diagnosis |
| “Covered” material behaves as never understood | Revision insufficient | Block RvCT-01 / RvCT-02; escalate to Learning Coach |
| No Learning Coach trail yet | Do not invent progression success | Consolidating-only completion possible; withhold mastery-adjacent speech |
| Learning Coach evidence contradicts consolidating claim | Consume / defer | Do not override with revision-completion fiat |

Hard rule:

> **Revision Coach must not bypass Learning Coach evidence.  
> Completing revision sittings is not proof that learning progressed or that mastery exists.  
> Where revision evidence reveals unresolved learning obstacles, authority must transition back to the Learning Coach rather than extending revision indefinitely.**

---

## 8. Traceability Obligation

Every material completion judgement must be traceable through:

| Trace link | Role |
|------------|------|
| Active warrant / strategy context | What consolidating emphasis is being closed or redirected |
| Prior-exposure confirmation | Material was previously learned — not first-pass disguised as completion |
| Applicable RvCC criteria | Which educational conditions were evaluated |
| Supporting RvCE classes | What accumulated evidence was cited |
| Explicit non-reliance statement (when relevant) | What was *not* used (time alone, ticks alone, repetition count alone) |
| Resulting RvCT transition | What educational move follows |
| First-learning / mastery honesty | Explicit non-claim of first learning, mastery, or exam-certainty |

A completion declaration with only attendance, repetition counts, session ticks, or elapsed time in the trail is invalid.

---

## 9. Anti-Patterns (Forbidden)

- Completion dashboards powered by login, streak, or question-count metrics as educational truth
- Silent Twin mastery writes triggered by revision-complete events
- “Evidence” that is only a recommendation or desired outcome renamed as observation
- Selective citation of one good revision day while ignoring RvCE-06 collapse / obstacle patterns
- Using completion evidence vocabulary to invent a second Evidence Model
- Inferring first learning from consolidating completion speech
