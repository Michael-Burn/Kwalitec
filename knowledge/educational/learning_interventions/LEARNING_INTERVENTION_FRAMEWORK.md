## Authority

This document is the constitutional overview of the **Learning Intervention Framework** for Kwalitec.

It is subordinate to the Educational Constitution and specialised educational models. It governs **how Kwalitec selects and explains educational responses after an explicit Learning Obstacle diagnosis**. It does not authorise implementation shortcuts that contradict the Constitution, and it does not invent evidence or diagnosis meaning absent from upstream models.

Authority order for intervention:

> Constitution defines educational truth and curriculum primacy.  
> Knowledge & Mastery defines coverage ≠ understanding ≠ mastery.  
> Evidence Model defines what may warrant educational claims.  
> Learning Progression Model asks whether the student is genuinely learning.  
> Learning Obstacle Model asks why progression is blocked — and gates intervention.  
> **This Learning Intervention Framework asks what educational response is warranted by that diagnosis.**  
> Downstream coaching, profile evolution, and narration surfaces must consume this educational reasoning — never invent a cure from a symptom, and never silently rewrite long-term planning.

---

## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor who has named a learning barrier does not stop at diagnosis. The tutor answers one further educational question:

> **“What is the most educationally appropriate response to this diagnosed obstacle?”**

That answer must rest on an **explicit diagnosis** (or an explicit insufficient-warrant evidence-gathering posture), pursue a **defined educational objective**, preserve **claim honesty**, respect **Daily Coach and Canonical Study Plan authority**, and remain **explainable** in supportive language.

This document records that tutor posture so every future Learning Coach subsystem has a single educational reference for *how interventions are selected and bounded*.

> **The Learning Intervention Framework describes educational response.  
> It does not implement Runtime A logic, scoring, analytics, or Twin writers.**

---

## 2. What a Learning Intervention Is

A **learning intervention** is a provisional educational recommendation that changes **how the student studies** — method, emphasis, retrieval design, practice focus, spacing, confidence calibration, or local consolidation — in response to a **diagnosed learning obstacle**.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Evidence** | Observed educational happenings and accumulation patterns | What was observed? |
| **Diagnosis (obstacle)** | Best educational explanation of why progression is blocked | What barrier best explains the trail? |
| **Intervention** | Educational response warranted by that diagnosis | What should change educationally? |
| **Future evidence** | New observations after the response is tried | Did the change improve learning? |

Interventions are:

- **diagnostic responses** — they answer a named obstacle, not a mood or a UI default;
- **objective-bound** — they aim to advance specific Learning Objectives (LO-01…LO-07);
- **provisional** — they remain open to revision as future evidence arrives;
- **guidance, not law** — they inform coaching emphasis; they are not Mission obligations unless Daily Coach / plan authority makes them so;
- **non-scoring** — selection uses educational reasoning, never priority maths as educational truth;
- **explainable** — every recommendation is speakable under the Explainability document.

Interventions are **not**:

- evidence (following advice does not mint understanding);
- diagnoses (a tip does not prove a cause);
- silent Canonical Study Plan rewrites;
- Daily Coach day-priority decisions;
- mastery redefinitions or opaque “learning scores”;
- long-term educational strategy changes (those escalate to Master Planner pathways);
- character judgements or sanctions for missed ticks.

## 3. What the Learning Intervention Framework Is

The **Learning Intervention Framework** is the educational capability that, given an **explicit obstacle diagnosis** (or explicit insufficient warrant), selects a **named educational intervention**, states its **educational objective**, defines **expected evidence of improvement**, and produces **supportive explainability**.

| Input family | Role |
|--------------|------|
| **Explicit obstacle diagnosis** | Named LOB (or insufficient-warrant / conflicting posture) from MS002 |
| **Diagnostic confidence posture** | Qualitative strength — colours how firmly to recommend |
| **Accumulated Educational Evidence** | Trail that justified the diagnosis (consumed, not redefined) |
| **Progression posture** | LPS colouring that invited diagnosis |
| **Student Educational Profile** | Capacity, recovery, planning reliability constraints |
| **Canonical Study Plan / Daily Coach envelopes** | What local change is lawful without escalation |

into:

| Output family | Role |
|---------------|------|
| **Named intervention** | Catalogue entry (LI-XX) matched to the diagnosis |
| **Educational objective** | Which LO(s) the response aims to advance |
| **Expected improvement evidence** | What future trail would indicate success |
| **Boundary posture** | Local coaching change vs escalation required |
| **Intervention explainability** | Supportive student-facing account |

It answers:

| Question | Educational answer |
|----------|-------------------|
| What should change educationally? | Named intervention when diagnosis warrants |
| Why this response? | Trace to obstacle + evidence trail |
| What improvement is expected? | Defined objective + future-evidence cues |
| May the Learning Coach enact this alone? | Only within boundaries; else escalate |
| Is warrant too thin to intervene? | Evidence gathering — not a pretended cure |

The Framework is:

- **response-oriented** — selects educational change after diagnosis;
- **diagnosis-gated** — refuses tip-first coaching;
- **boundary-faithful** — protects plan, Daily Coach, mastery, and strategy authorities;
- **evidence-honest** — never “fixes” thin trails by speaking stronger attainment;
- **smallest-sufficient** — prefers local method/emphasis change before structural replan;
- **explainable** — supportive, evidence-based language;
- **layer-faithful** — never collapses evidence, diagnosis, intervention, and future evidence.

The Framework is **not**:

- a second Obstacle Model, Progression Model, Evidence Model, or Profile diagnostician;
- ownership of Educational Evidence recording or Twin write authority;
- a UI surface, analytics dashboard, or scoring engine;
- a licence to commandeer Daily Coach or rewrite the Canonical Study Plan;
- a claim that following an intervention guarantees a pass.

## 4. Educational Purpose

The Learning Intervention Framework exists so that:

1. **Responses follow causes** — generic advice without diagnosis is forbidden coaching theatre.
2. **IFoA preparation interventions are educationally specific** — retrieval, prerequisites, misconception repair, spacing, deliberate practice, and exam form are distinct responses.
3. **Students understand the why** — supportive explanations link diagnosis → response → expected improvement.
4. **Authority stays layered** — local coaching changes do not silently become long-term planning.
5. **Future evidence closes the loop** — interventions are judged by later educational trail, not by compliance theatre.
6. **Insufficient warrant is respected** — when diagnosis cannot yet be named honestly, the lawful “intervention” is evidence gathering.

## 5. Core Responsibilities

The Learning Intervention Framework is educationally responsible for:

| Responsibility | Meaning |
|----------------|---------|
| **Select educational responses** | Match catalogue interventions to explicit diagnoses |
| **Require diagnosis gate** | Refuse recommendations without LOB (or explicit gather-evidence posture) |
| **Define educational objectives** | State which Learning Objectives the response advances |
| **Define expected improvement evidence** | Say what future observations would indicate success |
| **Bound Learning Coach authority** | Separate local change from escalation |
| **Explain supportively** | Make the recommendation speakable as improvement guidance |

It is **not** educationally responsible for:

- inventing Educational Evidence or diagnoses;
- writing Twin estimates by itself;
- selecting or executing Runtime A missions;
- publishing or mutating Canonical Study Plans;
- deciding today’s Daily Coach primary objective;
- producing opaque “intervention priority” scores as educational proof.

## 6. Binding Educational Doctrine

1. **Evidence ≠ diagnosis ≠ intervention ≠ future evidence.** These four layers remain separate. No document, product surface, or future algorithm may collapse them into a single opaque label.
2. **No intervention without diagnosis.** A Learning Intervention recommendation is unlawful unless it cites an explicit obstacle diagnosis — or cites an explicit *insufficient warrant* / *conflicting* posture whose only lawful next step is further evidence gathering.
3. **Intervention does not rewrite diagnosis.** Coaching copy may not invent a cause to justify a preferred tip.
4. **Intervention does not mint evidence.** Following advice is not proof that understanding or mastery advanced.
5. **Completion ≠ understanding ≠ mastery.** Intervention success reading must preserve the Learning Objectives ladder (LO-01…LO-07) and EIP-006 separations.
6. **Smallest sufficient change.** Prefer study-method / practice-emphasis / retrieval / spacing / confidence adjustments before structural replan.
7. **Escalate when envelopes break.** Long-term strategy, Canonical Study Plan structure, intensity envelopes, and sitting ambition change through existing planning pathways — never by Learning Coach fiat.
8. **Daily Coach authority preserved.** Interventions inform emphasis; they do not override today’s lawful Daily Coach objective selection.
9. **Mastery not redefined.** Interventions never author LPS-7 / exam-readiness speech from compliance or coverage alone.
10. **Explainability is mandatory.** Silent intervention theatre and shaming language are forbidden (EIP-003).
11. **Non-punitive.** Interventions are support — never sanctions for missed ticks.

## 7. Four Constitutional Layers (Binding)

```
Layer 1 — EVIDENCE
  Observed educational happenings + accumulation patterns
  (EIP-002; Learning Evidence Model EC-A…EC-F)
        ↓  interpret; never invent
Layer 2 — DIAGNOSIS
  Named learning obstacle(s) + qualitative confidence posture
  (Learning Obstacle Model; Catalogue; Diagnosis; Obstacle Evidence)
        ↓  respond; never rewrite evidence or invent cause
Layer 3 — INTERVENTION
  Educational recommendation matched to the diagnosis
  (this Framework; Catalogue; Selection; Boundaries; Explainability)
        ↓  observe again; never declare success from compliance alone
Layer 4 — FUTURE EVIDENCE
  New observations after the response is attempted
  (feeds Evidence Model; may clear, revise, or escalate diagnosis)
```

| Lawful flow | Unlawful flow |
|-------------|----------------|
| Evidence → Diagnosis → Intervention → Future evidence | Evidence → Intervention (skip diagnosis) |
| Diagnosis cites evidence trail | Diagnosis invented to fit a tip |
| Intervention cites diagnosis meaning | Intervention invents a cause post hoc |
| Insufficient warrant → evidence gathering | Insufficient warrant → pretended cure |
| Future evidence re-reads success honestly | “You followed advice, therefore you improved” |

### 8.1 Learning Obstacle Model (MS002)

| Model | Question |
|-------|----------|
| Obstacles | Why is learning not progressing as expected? |
| **Interventions** | **What educational response is warranted?** |

Diagnosis **gates** intervention. This Framework does not redefine LOB-01…LOB-12 meanings.

### 8.2 Learning Progression Model (MS001)

Progression postures (stall, inconsistency, decay, thin warrant, acceleration, false readiness risk) are **diagnostic invitations**. They colour urgency and envelope pressure; they do not by themselves select an LI without a diagnosis.

MS001’s seed catalogue (`../learning_coach/LEARNING_INTERVENTIONS.md`) is **superseded for governing educational meaning** by this corpus. LI identifiers remain continuous for audit and cross-reference.

### 8.3 Daily Coach / Session / Reflection

| Consumer | Relationship |
|----------|--------------|
| **Educational Reflection** | May surface session-level reinforcement / continuation / escalation *inside* plan envelopes |
| **Daily Coach** | May modulate today’s emphasis using Learning Coach intervention meaning — still plan-faithful; still owns *today’s* primary objective |
| **Learning Session** | May realise method/emphasis changes locally without absorbing Daily Coach authority |

Learning Coach interventions **inform** day coaching; they do **not** replace Daily Coach decision authority.

### 8.4 Canonical Study Plan / Master Planner / Strategy

The Canonical Study Plan remains the authorised preparation contract.  
Educational Strategy and Planning remain Master Planner authorities.

When local intervention cannot repair progression within plan envelopes, the lawful output is **escalation** (see Catalogue LI-12 and Boundaries) — not silent plan mutation.

### 8.5 Student Educational Profile

Profile capacity, recovery posture, and planning reliability **constrain** which interventions are sustainable. They do not author interventions without diagnosis, and interventions do not silently rewrite Profile meaning.

### 8.6 Architectural Intervention Model

[`knowledge/architecture/INTERVENTION_MODEL.md`](../../architecture/INTERVENTION_MODEL.md) describes orchestration DTOs for a future Strategy Engine. **Educational meaning of what to recommend after diagnosis is owned here.** Architecture may map storage labels onto these meanings; it may not redefine them in code.

## 9. Tutor Posture (Binding Metaphor)

When selecting interventions, the system must behave as an expert IFoA tutor would:

1. **Diagnose before directing.** No tip without a named barrier — or an honest “we need clearer evidence.”
2. **Match response to cause.** Prerequisite gaps get rebuilding; misconceptions get correction; weak retrieval gets retrieval design; thin practice gets attributable practice — not one generic “study harder.”
3. **Name one primary intervention in speech.** Supporting moves may exist; students need a clear next educational step.
4. **Prefer the smallest sufficient change.** Method and emphasis before calendar surgery.
5. **Protect claim honesty.** Never speak mastery into existence because advice was followed.
6. **Respect the plan and the day.** Local coaching changes stay inside Canonical Study Plan and Daily Coach envelopes; escalate when they do not.
7. **Expect future evidence.** Say what improvement would look like — then look for it.
8. **Explain supportively.** Students should understand why this response, what it addresses, what improvement is expected, and what evidence will indicate success.
9. **Change when the trail changes.** Interventions are provisional; clear or revise them as future evidence arrives.

## 11. Success Criteria (Educational)

This Framework succeeds educationally when:

1. Every recommended intervention is traceable to **accumulated educational evidence**, an **explicit diagnosis** (or explicit gather-evidence posture), and a **defined educational objective**.
2. No intervention bypasses diagnosis.
3. No intervention silently alters long-term planning, Daily Coach authority, mastery meaning, or educational strategy.
4. Students can understand why the response was selected, what barrier it addresses, what improvement is expected, and what evidence will indicate success.
5. Future evidence — not compliance theatre — judges whether the intervention worked.
