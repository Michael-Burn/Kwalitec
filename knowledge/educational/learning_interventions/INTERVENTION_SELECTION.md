## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor faced with a named barrier does not grab the first familiar tip. The tutor asks what cause was diagnosed, what objective should advance, what change is smallest and sufficient, whether envelopes allow it, and what future evidence would show success.

This document records that selection discipline so Learning Coach recommendations remain educationally honest.

---

## 2. Selection Principles

1. **Diagnosis first.** No LI without an explicit LOB — or LI-00 under insufficient warrant / conflicting posture.
2. **Match cause, not symptom.** “Wrong answers” never selects an intervention by itself.
3. **One primary in speech.** Supporting interventions may exist; students hear one primary next step.
4. **Smallest sufficient change.** Prefer LI-01…LI-11 / LI-13–LI-14 before LI-12.
5. **Structure before volume.** When LOB-01 or LOB-03 is primary, do not default to LI-02.
6. **Objective specificity.** Name which Learning Objective the response advances.
7. **Envelope awareness.** If the response cannot fit Daily Coach / Canonical Study Plan envelopes, select LI-12 rather than silent mutation.
8. **Confidence-posture humility.** Provisional diagnoses warrant provisional recommendations; Conflicting / Insufficient warrant → LI-00.
9. **Non-punitive.** Selection never chooses an intervention to punish missed ticks.
10. **No numerical scoring.** No weights, ranks, or priority maths as educational law.

---

## 3. Selection Procedure (Educational)

The tutor follows this order. Implementations may later encode equivalents; they may not skip educational steps.

| Step | Action | Output |
|------|--------|--------|
| 1 | Confirm **diagnosis gate** | Named LOB + confidence posture, or Insufficient warrant / Conflicting |
| 2 | If Insufficient warrant / Conflicting | Select **LI-00**; stop tip selection |
| 3 | Name **educational objective(s)** to advance | LO-01…LO-07 mapping |
| 4 | List **candidate interventions** from Catalogue matched to the LOB | Shortlist of LI-XX |
| 5 | Eliminate candidates that **must not be used** given rivals, Profile, envelopes | Reduced shortlist |
| 6 | Prefer **smallest sufficient** local change | Primary LI |
| 7 | If no local LI fits envelopes | Select **LI-12** escalation |
| 8 | State **expected evidence of improvement** | Future-evidence cues |
| 9 | Produce **explainability** under Explainability document | Student-facing account |

Skipping from symptom or progression posture directly to an intervention is unlawful.

---

## 4. Diagnosis Confidence and Selection Firmness

| Diagnostic posture | Lawful selection posture |
|--------------------|--------------------------|
| **Insufficient warrant** | **LI-00 only** — no cure tips |
| **Conflicting** | **LI-00** (distinguishing evidence); dual-hypothesis speech allowed; do not pick a favourite LOB for coaching convenience |
| **Provisional** | Primary LI allowed with provisional speech; prefer reversible, smaller changes |
| **Supported** | Primary LI with clearer recommendation tone — still provisional as educational estimate |

Never convert these postures into scores.

## 5. Per-Intervention Selection Cards

For every catalogue intervention: required diagnosis, educational objective, expected evidence of improvement, and situations where it should not be used.

---

### LI-00 — Evidence Gathering

| Field | Provision |
|-------|-----------|
| **Required diagnosis** | Insufficient warrant or Conflicting (explicit) |
| **Educational objective** | Distinguishing observations that enable honest diagnosis |
| **Expected evidence of improvement** | Rival causes weaken; a primary LOB becomes speakable — or conflict resolves toward a named barrier |
| **Do not use when** | Supported LOB already exists with a matched local LI; used to avoid escalation theatre when LI-12 is already required |

---

### LI-01 — Retrieval Reinforcement / Consolidation Practice

| Field | Provision |
|-------|-----------|
| **Required diagnosis** | Emerging competence with fragility after LOB-08 thinning, post–LOB-03 repair, or LOB-12 consolidation phase — not unrepaired LOB-01/03 |
| **Educational objective** | Stabilise LO-03; early LO-05 |
| **Expected evidence of improvement** | Confirming practice on same scope; fewer fragile collapses |
| **Do not use when** | Foundations missing; misconception unrepaired; used as mastery minting; protected revision windows consumed without authority |

---

### LI-02 — Targeted Question Practice

| Field | Provision |
|-------|-----------|
| **Required diagnosis** | LOB-08 primary (structural rivals weakened) |
| **Educational objective** | LO-05 Application warrant |
| **Expected evidence of improvement** | EC-C density/variety grows on named scope |
| **Do not use when** | LOB-01/03/05/06 better explain the stall; volume without focus when LI-13 is needed |

---

### LI-03 — Retrieval Reinforcement (Cue-Light Production)

| Field | Provision |
|-------|-----------|
| **Required diagnosis** | LOB-02 primary; LOB-11 as co-primary with LI-06 |
| **Educational objective** | LO-04 Retrieval |
| **Expected evidence of improvement** | Prompt-vs-production gap narrows across sessions |
| **Do not use when** | Idea never taught; wrong schema fluently retrieved (LI-07 first); recognition drills mislabelled as retrieval |

### LI-04 — Prerequisite Rebuilding

| Field | Provision |
|-------|-----------|
| **Required diagnosis** | LOB-01 primary |
| **Educational objective** | Rebuild earlier LO-01/LO-03; unlock current LO-05 |
| **Expected evidence of improvement** | Earlier-scope checks strengthen; current items become approachable |
| **Do not use when** | Current misconception with sound foundations; thin practice only; invents unofficial prerequisites |

### LI-05 — Increased Challenge

| Field | Provision |
|-------|-----------|
| **Required diagnosis** | Acceleration with confirming evidence; or LOB-10 with fair content warrant |
| **Educational objective** | Stronger LO-05; careful LO-07 contributions |
| **Expected evidence of improvement** | Stable performance under higher demand |
| **Do not use when** | Soft confidence alone; unresolved LOB-01/03/05; recovery; false readiness risk; coverage theatre |

---

### LI-06 — Spacing Adjustment

| Field | Provision |
|-------|-----------|
| **Required diagnosis** | LOB-09 and/or LOB-11 (and LOB-02 support) |
| **Educational objective** | LO-06 Durable retention |
| **Expected evidence of improvement** | Stronger spaced returns (EC-E); revision contact appears |
| **Do not use when** | Same-day retest labelled as spacing; plan forbids return design without escalation; foundations never existed |

---

### LI-07 — Misconception Correction

| Field | Provision |
|-------|-----------|
| **Required diagnosis** | LOB-03 with namable wrong idea (Supported or strong Provisional) |
| **Educational objective** | LO-03 quality repair; unlock LO-05 |
| **Expected evidence of improvement** | False rule stops recurring; performance flips after correction |
| **Do not use when** | Heterogeneous errors; unnamed “something wrong”; LOB-01 misread as misconception; more questions without repair |

---

### LI-08 — Study Method / Strategy Coaching (incl. Worked-Example Emphasis)

| Field | Provision |
|-------|-----------|
| **Required diagnosis** | LOB-06 and/or LOB-10; LI-08a when scaffolding/fading is the method need |
| **Educational objective** | Efficiency of LO-04/LO-05; LO-07 process |
| **Expected evidence of improvement** | Production-shaped methods appear; gains for effort; method-mark/timing trails improve |
| **Do not use when** | Structure obstacles primary; practice absent (LOB-08 needs LI-02 first); folk syllabus invention |

---

### LI-09 — Consolidation Before Progression

| Field | Provision |
|-------|-----------|
| **Required diagnosis** | LOB-12 primary; LOB-05 when novelty must be deferred |
| **Educational objective** | Convert coverage into understanding/application |
| **Expected evidence of improvement** | Understanding/application catch up; unfinished novelty reduces |
| **Do not use when** | Permanent plan freeze without LI-12; LOB-01 needs LI-04; punishes coverage honesty |

---

### LI-10 — Recovery-Respecting Rebuild

| Field | Provision |
|-------|-----------|
| **Required diagnosis** | Post-interruption LOB-04/LOB-11 colouring; Continuity recovery posture |
| **Educational objective** | Safe return to LO-04/LO-05 |
| **Expected evidence of improvement** | Gentle re-emergence without overload; cadence stabilises |
| **Do not use when** | Punishment catch-up; moralising the break; avoiding needed LI-12 when runway is broken |

---

### LI-11 — Confidence Recalibration

| Field | Provision |
|-------|-----------|
| **Required diagnosis** | LOB-07 primary |
| **Educational objective** | Calibrated self-regulation; protect false LO-07 speech |
| **Expected evidence of improvement** | Soft story aligns with performance trail across sessions |
| **Do not use when** | Minting competence from confidence; shaming; substituting for content/retrieval work when confidence is correctly low |

---

### LI-12 — Escalation for Replan / Reschedule

| Field | Provision |
|-------|-----------|
| **Required diagnosis** | Persistent LOB(s) after local LI attempts; envelope breakage; sitting ambition impossible under remaining calendar |
| **Educational objective** | Restore lawful long-term educational fit |
| **Expected evidence of improvement** | Planning pathways produce an honest revised contract (outside Learning Coach publication authority) |
| **Do not use when** | First response to every mild stall; used to dump responsibility without a clear educational reason |

---

### LI-13 — Deliberate Practice

| Field | Provision |
|-------|-----------|
| **Required diagnosis** | LOB-08 (unfocused practice), LOB-10 (craft elements), post–LOB-03 application, or LOB-06 enactment |
| **Educational objective** | Quality LO-05 (scoped weakness); LO-07 craft elements |
| **Expected evidence of improvement** | Named weakness shrinks; feedback-informed gains transfer modestly |
| **Do not use when** | LOB-01/03 primary; overload primary; marathon dumps without success criteria |

---

### LI-14 — Revision Restructuring

| Field | Provision |
|-------|-----------|
| **Required diagnosis** | LOB-09/11/12/10 as they affect revision *design* |
| **Educational objective** | Structurally achievable LO-06 (and late LO-07 contributions) |
| **Expected evidence of improvement** | Clearer revision mix; inhabited protected revision intent; EC-E improves |
| **Do not use when** | Silent plan reallocation (use LI-12); foundations missing (LI-04); checklist theatre without educational design |

## 6. Rival-Diagnosis Discipline

When diagnosis is **Conflicting**, selection must not “average” incompatible interventions (e.g. LI-02 volume + LI-07 repair + LI-04 rebuild) into one overloaded tip.

Lawful options:

1. **LI-00** with a distinguishing evidence plan; or  
2. Dual-hypothesis speech: “If it is X we will do A; if it is Y we will do B — first we check …”

Unlawful: pick the intervention that feels most product-friendly.

---

## 7. Combining Interventions

Multiple interventions may combine when educationally coherent (e.g. LI-07 then LI-01; LI-03 + LI-06; LI-06 + LI-14).

Rules:

- Prefer **one primary** in student speech.
- Order matters: repair (LI-07) before volume (LI-02); foundations (LI-04) before advanced challenge (LI-05); method (LI-08) before deliberate enactment (LI-13) when strategy was the barrier.
- Do not combine into cognitive overload (LOB-05 risk).
- LI-12 displaces local combinations when envelopes are already broken.

---

## 8. Selection Anti-Patterns (Forbidden)

| Anti-pattern | Why unlawful |
|--------------|--------------|
| Tip from LPS stall without LOB | Skips diagnosis layer |
| “More practice” for every error pattern | Ignores LOB-01/03/06 |
| Challenge to motivate thin warrant | False readiness risk |
| Plan rewrite narrated as a tip | Boundary violation |
| Score-ranked LI picker as educational truth | Numerical scoring forbidden here |
| Declaring success because advice was followed | Collapses future evidence |
