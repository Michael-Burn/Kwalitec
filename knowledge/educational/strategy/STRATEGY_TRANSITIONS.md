## Authority

This document defines **lawful succession among educational strategies** — when a student should move from one primary strategy to another, and why.

Transitions are educational meanings — not automated state-machine code. Future algorithms may detect triggers; they may not invent transition reasons absent from this document.

> **Strategy changes when the educational picture changes.  
> Strategy must not churn for novelty, engagement, or opaque scores.**

---

## 1. Purpose

An expert tutor revises approach when the student’s reality changes: foundations secure, practice evidence arrives, revision window opens, recovery completes, or feasibility collapses.

Students should experience strategy change as **intelligible educational progression** (or honest triage) — not as a mysterious mode flip.

---

## 2. Transition Principles

1. **Profile evolution is the fuel.** Strategy transitions follow material Profile change (see `PROFILE_EVOLUTION.md`).
2. **Reselect, don’t drift.** Material triggers cause fresh selection under `STRATEGY_SELECTION_MODEL.md`, not silent label mutation.
3. **Explain the change.** Every material transition answers “what changed?” (see `STRATEGY_EXPLAINABILITY.md`).
4. **Stability has value.** Absent material trigger, keep the primary strategy (anti-churn).
5. **Escalation and de-escalation are both lawful.** Rescue → Steady Progression is as valid as Steady → Rescue when warrant changes.
6. **Non-punitive.** Moving into Recovery or Exam Rescue is diagnostic honesty, not demotion.
7. **Constraints survive transitions.** A new strategy cannot authorise prerequisite violation or revision erasure.
8. **Understatement on ambiguous exits.** Prefer cautious successor when exit warrant is thin.

## 3. Material Transition Triggers

A trigger is **material** when an expert tutor would reconsider approach. Categories:

### T1 — Coverage & journey progress

| Trigger | Typical educational effect |
|---------|----------------------------|
| First-pass foundations established on early spine | Foundation Building → Steady Progression |
| Mid-syllabus length with rising decay risk | Steady Progression → Knowledge Consolidation |
| Enough coverage to make practice the main need | Build strategies → Practice Intensive |
| First-pass substantially complete for sitting scope | Deepen/Build → Revision Intensive |
| Provisional Exam Ready warrant achieved | Revision / High Performer → Balanced Maintenance |

### T2 — Evidence & understanding

| Trigger | Typical educational effect |
|---------|----------------------------|
| Practice evidence reveals material weak topics | Any deepen/build → Weak Topic Reinforcement |
| Weak topics recover warrant | Weak Topic Reinforcement → Practice Intensive / Steady / Revision |
| Strong mocks / deep practice on solid coverage | Practice / Revision → High Performer Optimisation |
| Thin evidence persists | Stay cautious; do not transit to Balanced Maintenance |

### T3 — Calendar & feasibility

| Trigger | Typical educational effect |
|---------|----------------------------|
| Sitting date brought forward / capacity cut | May enter Late Starter or Exam Rescue |
| Sitting date pushed / capacity increased | Late Starter / Rescue may exit toward Steady / Foundation |
| Revision window now dominant educationally | → Revision Intensive |
| Infeasibility becomes clear | → Exam Rescue (L1) |
| Feasibility stabilises after triage | Exam Rescue → Revision Intensive / Confidence Restoration |

### T4 — Behaviour, interruption, recovery

| Trigger | Typical educational effect |
|---------|----------------------------|
| Sustained missed study / burnout / leave | → Recovery Strategy |
| Return after significant gap | → Recovery Strategy (often via Returning After Break state) |
| Cadence restored for sustained period | Recovery → Steady Progression / prior family |
| Motivation/confidence collapse with stable hard facts | → Confidence Restoration |
| Confidence stabilises | Confidence Restoration → Normal progression strategy (Steady / Practice / Revision) |

### T5 — Assessment shocks

| Trigger | Typical educational effect |
|---------|----------------------------|
| Poor mock on covered material | → Weak Topic Reinforcement and/or Confidence Restoration |
| Fail / previous attempt aftermath | Often Revision Intensive + Weak Topic Reinforcement; Recovery if intensity broke |
| Strong mock under solid warrant | May support High Performer Optimisation / Balanced Maintenance |

### T6 — Intake & assumption breaks

| Trigger | Typical educational effect |
|---------|----------------------------|
| Mandatory inputs newly completed | First lawful selection (exit M1) |
| Planning assumptions break | Reselect; often more cautious |
| Concurrent load declared | May intensify Late Starter / Recovery / intensity downshift within strategy |

Non-material noise (one late night, one soft mood check-in without hard change) does **not** force transition.

## 4. Canonical Transition Paths

Paths below are **common educational successions**. They are not the only lawful paths. Selection lenses (L1–L4) still apply.

### 4.1 Build → Deepen → Approach (standard long journey)

```
Foundation Building
        ↓  (foundations underway, cadence stable)
Steady Progression
        ↓  (retention risk mid first-pass)
Knowledge Consolidation  ←→  Practice Intensive
        ↓  (first-pass largely done; horizon shortens)
Revision Intensive
        ↓  (warrant strong / sitting near)
High Performer Optimisation  and/or  Balanced Maintenance
```

**Educational story:** Build coverage honestly, deepen with practice and return, then revise and stabilise.

### 4.2 Practice ↔ Reinforcement

```
Practice Intensive
        ↓  (evidence shows fragile topics)
Weak Topic Reinforcement
        ↓  (warrant improves)
Practice Intensive  or  Steady Progression  or  Revision Intensive
```

**Educational story:** Practice reveals truth; reinforcement repairs; then return to the honest main story.

### 4.3 Recovery arcs

```
(any strategy)
        ↓  (interruption / burnout / abandoned intensity)
Recovery Strategy
        ↓  (optional if confidence is the remaining fracture)
Confidence Restoration
        ↓  (cadence and confidence restored)
Steady Progression  /  Practice Intensive  /  Foundation Building
```

**Educational story:** Restart that still counts, then resume normal progression.

**Also common:**

```
Returning After Break (state)
        ↓
Recovery Strategy
        ↓
Knowledge Consolidation  (if decay risk)  or  Foundation Building / Steady Progression
```

### 4.4 Confidence restoration exit

```
Confidence Restoration
        ↓  (felt/educational confidence stabilises; hard facts unchanged)
Steady Progression  or  Practice Intensive  or  Revision Intensive
        (“normal progression” — whichever the Profile now warrants)
```

**Educational story:** Restore trust in the process, then continue the journey the Profile already supported.

### 4.5 Triage arcs

```
Late Starter Strategy
        ↓  (horizon eases — date/capacity change)
Steady Progression / Foundation Building
        ↓  (or if compression fails near sitting)
Exam Rescue
```

```
(any Approach/Build strategy)
        ↓  (late infeasibility / At Risk dominates)
Exam Rescue
        ↓  (feasibility narrative stabilises; still near sitting)
Revision Intensive  and/or  Confidence Restoration
        ↓  (rare full recovery of runway)
Steady Progression  (usually only if sitting changes)
```

**Educational story:** Face the calendar honestly; triage; only then resume ordinary progression if the horizon truly improves.

### 4.6 Maintenance vigilance

```
Balanced Maintenance / High Performer Optimisation
        ↓  (new weakness, decay, or shock)
Weak Topic Reinforcement  or  Revision Intensive
        ↓  (or feasibility shock)
Exam Rescue
```

**Educational story:** Maintenance is not immortality — new evidence can reopen deepen/triage.

## 6. Anti-Churn Rules

Do **not** change primary strategy when:

1. Only soft signals flicker without sustained pattern.
2. A single missed session occurs without recovery posture.
3. UI experimentation wants a “fresh theme.”
4. An opaque score crosses a hidden threshold without Profile-narratable change.
5. The student asks for novelty but Profile and horizon are unchanged — coach within strategy; don’t fake a new strategy.
6. Replan is cosmetic (copy tweak) without educational input change.

**Minimum stability expectation:** Primary strategy should persist across ordinary study weeks unless a material trigger fires. Exact calendar thresholds are implementation concerns; educational law requires *material warrant*.

---

## 7. Simultaneous Overlay vs Transition

Some Profile overlays change coaching tone without forcing a full primary strategy change:

| Situation | Prefer |
|-----------|--------|
| Mild At Risk overlay during Steady Progression | Keep ES-02; intensify explainability and feasibility honesty; consider secondary emphasis |
| Dominant At Risk near sitting | Transition to ES-09 |
| Mild confidence dip during Revision Intensive | Keep ES-05; apply Confidence Restoration as secondary tone |
| Confidence collapse blocking study | Transition to ES-07 (or ES-08 if cadence also broken) |

---

## 8. Replan Coupling

Whenever Master Planner replans because of:

- missed weeks / leave / capacity change;
- sitting date change;
- material evidence shock;
- recovery events;
- assumption break;

it must:

1. Refresh the Student Educational Profile.
2. Reselect primary strategy under the Selection Model.
3. Redesign the plan under the Planning Model **using the new strategy**.
4. Explain both strategy change (if any) and plan change.

Replan without strategy reselection invents approach drift.

---

## 9. Forbidden Transition Theatre

| Theatre | Why forbidden |
|---------|----------------|
| Daily strategy roulette | Destroys educational continuity |
| “Level up” gamified strategy ranks | Not tutor reasoning |
| Silent swap into Exam Rescue without telling the student | Violates explainability |
| Moving to Balanced Maintenance from coverage % alone | Readiness theatre |
| Exiting Recovery into High Performer overnight | Understatement violation |
| Using transition to erase Study Progress | Continuity violation |

---

## 10. Success Test

Strategy transition behaviour is educationally compliant when:

1. Every change cites a material trigger category (T1–T6).
2. Reselection follows Selection lenses L1–L4.
3. Student-facing speech answers what changed.
4. Anti-churn rules hold.
5. Planning Constraints remain intact after the change.
