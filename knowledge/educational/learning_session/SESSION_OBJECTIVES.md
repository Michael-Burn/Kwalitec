> **Session objectives specialise *how* today’s Daily Coach objective is pursued.  
> They never authorise replacing or reinventing what the student is expected to accomplish today.**

## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not optimise “pages read” or “minutes survived.” The tutor optimises **educational gains that honestly advance today’s authorised job** — new knowledge when learning is due, durable retrieval when practice is due, calibrated confidence when reflection is due.

These objectives bind every Learning Session composition and adaptation decision.

Identifiers (LSO-XX) exist for traceability. Educational meaning is binding; implementation field names are out of scope.

---

## 2. Governing Constraint — Objective Fidelity

### LSO-00 — Preserve today’s Daily Coach objective

**Definition.** Every session aim, phase choice, and adaptation must remain in service of the authorised Daily Coach primary objective (DCO-01) and disclosed day posture (including DCO-10 when present).

**Includes:**

- Selecting session aims that realise today’s work type (learn / practise / consolidate / revise / checkpoint / recover / rest).
- Narrowing depth or scope when capacity shrinks — without swapping the educational job.
- Escalating when the day’s job cannot be honestly served.

**Excludes:**

- Replacing a revision day with undeclared first-pass coverage.
- Inventing a “more important” topic mid-session.
- Treating rapid mastery of a sub-skill as licence to redefine the day’s syllabus job.

**Tutor rationale.** Method without fidelity is improvisation. The session is how the day is lived — not a second day planner.

**Priority.** LSO-00 is a **hard constraint** on all other session objectives. It is never traded away.

## 3. Primary Session Objective Family

Which primary aims apply depends on today’s Daily Coach work type. A single session typically privileges **one primary aim cluster** with supporting aims — not an unordered wishlist.

### LSO-01 — Acquire new knowledge

**Definition.** Help the student form an initial, accurate representation of authorised new material (concepts, methods, conditions, notations) belonging to today’s learning job.

**When it fits.** First-pass learning days; foundation building under Learning Mode topic authority.

**Tutor rationale.** IFoA topics compound. Weak first representations poison later practice.

**Must not.** Equate exposure with understanding; skip orientation; race coverage without checking sense-making.

---

### LSO-02 — Strengthen understanding

**Definition.** Deepen, connect, and clarify material the student has already met — resolving fragile representations without pretending mastery is complete.

**When it fits.** Consolidation days; after thin first-pass; when recent evidence shows fragile grasp inside today’s objective.

**Tutor rationale.** Professional exams punish shallow familiarity. Strengthening is distinct from both first exposure and exam-speed drilling.

**Must not.** Invent mastery from re-reading; abandon today’s topic for unrelated “interesting” digressions.

---

### LSO-03 — Retrieval practice

**Definition.** Require the student to recall and reconstruct knowledge without the notes in view, so memory traces become usable under exam conditions.

**When it fits.** Practice / consolidation / revision emphasis; any session where durable recall is required to serve today’s objective.

**Tutor rationale.** Passive review creates familiarity illusions. Retrieval creates usable memory.

**Must not.** Treat failed retrieval as moral failure; skip feedback after retrieval; mint mastery from a few successful recalls.

---

### LSO-04 — Deliberate practice

**Definition.** Target specific difficulties with focused, effortful application — worked reasoning, graded questions, or exam-craft drills — with feedback loops that improve the next attempt.

**When it fits.** Authorised practice focus (DCO-03); checkpoint / mock preparation; skill gaps inside today’s objective.

**Tutor rationale.** IFoA success requires method under pressure, not only recognition of notes.

**Must not.** Confuse volume of questions with deliberate practice; ignore feedback; displace protected recovery/revision jobs.

---

### LSO-05 — Identify and repair misconceptions

**Definition.** Surface incorrect models (wrong conditions, swapped formulae, illegal steps) and replace them with accurate ones before they harden.

**When it fits.** Whenever session signals show systematic error inside today’s objective; especially after weak practice.

**Tutor rationale.** An expert tutor hunts misconceptions early. Coverage that leaves false models intact is false progress.

**Must not.** Invent diagnostic certainty from a single slip; expand into a new day’s curriculum of “everything weak.”

---

### LSO-06 — Calibrate confidence

**Definition.** Align the student’s felt confidence with honest educational evidence — raising warranted confidence, and lowering false confidence, without shame theatre.

**When it fits.** Reflection phases; after surprising success or failure; revision and checkpoint preparation.

**Tutor rationale.** Overconfidence and underconfidence both harm exam decisions. Calibration is an educational good.

**Must not.** Fabricate confidence messages that invent competence; equate low confidence with incompetence automatically.

---

### LSO-07 — Reflect on learning

**Definition.** Help the student name what was studied, what remains uncertain, and what the next honest step is — closing the session without false mastery claims.

**When it fits.** Near every ordinary study session close; especially after dense learning or practice.

**Tutor rationale.** Reflection converts experience into self-knowledge. Without it, sessions blur into unexamined hours.

**Must not.** Force the student to declare mastery; introduce Twin jargon; skip reflection solely to “save time” when honesty is at risk.

---

## 4. Supporting Session Objectives

### LSO-08 — Maintain cognitive focus

**Definition.** Structure the sitting so attention can sustain effortful learning — including brief orientation, manageable chunks, and lawful pauses — without abandoning today’s objective.

**Tutor rationale.** Fatigued attention produces coverage theatre. Focus is an educational precondition.

---

### LSO-09 — Transfer to exam-like conditions (when authorised)

**Definition.** When today’s job includes exam craft, shift practice toward timed, less-scaffolded, syllabus-faithful performance — without inventing mock theatre on a first-pass day.

**Tutor rationale.** Transfer must be earned and authorised. Premature exam pressure on fragile first-pass material harms learning.

---

### LSO-10 — Leave an honest residue for tomorrow

**Definition.** End so that Study Progress / continuity narratives can remain truthful: what was attempted, what felt solid, what remains open — without minting understanding.

**Tutor rationale.** Sessions feed the journey. Dishonest closes poison Daily Coach and Profile inputs later.

## 5. Mapping Daily Coach Work Types → Session Aim Clusters

| Today’s Daily Coach work type | Primary aim cluster (typical) | Supporting aims |
|------------------------------|-------------------------------|-----------------|
| First-pass learning | LSO-01, then LSO-02 / LSO-05 as needed | LSO-07, LSO-08, LSO-10 |
| Practice emphasis | LSO-03, LSO-04, LSO-05 | LSO-06, LSO-07, LSO-10 |
| Consolidation | LSO-02, LSO-03, LSO-05 | LSO-06, LSO-07 |
| Revision | LSO-03, LSO-04 (exam-craft as authorised), LSO-06 | LSO-07, LSO-09 |
| Checkpoint / mock prep | LSO-04, LSO-09, LSO-06 | LSO-05, LSO-07 |
| Recovery / lighter load | LSO-02 or light LSO-03 within envelopes; LSO-07 | LSO-08, LSO-10 — **refuse** heroic LSO-04 volume |
| Rest / freshness | Continuity-minimal; reflection-light only if helpful | **Refuse** new first-pass ambition |

This table is educational guidance for composition — not a rigid script. Local signals may reorder supporting aims; they may not swap the work-type row without Daily Coach escalation.

## 6. Objective Priority When They Conflict

When session objectives pull in different directions inside an authorised day:

1. **LSO-00 — Objective fidelity** (hard constraint)
2. **Misconception repair (LSO-05)** when false models block today’s job
3. **Primary aim cluster for today’s work type** (LSO-01–LSO-04 / LSO-09 as authorised)
4. **Confidence calibration (LSO-06)** when false confidence would corrupt practice or close
5. **Reflection & honest residue (LSO-07, LSO-10)**
6. **Focus maintenance (LSO-08)** as enabling condition
7. **Breadth of coverage inside the topic** — lowest when it threatens depth required by today’s job

`SESSION_ADAPTATION.md` operationalises conflicts that threaten LSO-00 (escalation).

---

## 7. Explicit Non-Objectives

The Learning Session must **not** optimise for:

- maximising pages, videos, or minutes regardless of today’s educational job;
- maximising question count without feedback or targeting;
- maximising comfort by avoiding retrieval when retrieval is due;
- maximising short-term confidence by hiding uncertainty;
- inventing a more dramatic educational job mid-session;
- completing every catalogue aim in every session regardless of work type.
