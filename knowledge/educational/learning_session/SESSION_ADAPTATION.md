## 1. Purpose

Live study is messy. Unexpected difficulty, rapid fluency, fatigue, interruptions, and shrinking time are normal — not exceptions.

An expert IFoA tutor **changes method first**, keeps the day’s brief, and only revisits *what today is for* when honesty demands it.

This document draws that boundary permanently.

Identifiers (LSA-XX) exist for traceability. Educational meaning is binding; feature-flag behaviour is out of scope.

---

## 2. Adaptation Principles

1. **Preserve DCO-01.** Local adaptation changes *how*, not *what*.
2. **Least change that restores learning quality.** Prefer scaffolding and scope narrowing over arc redesign.
3. **Disclose material adaptation** in session explainability when the student experience shifts noticeably.
4. **No punishment pedagogy.** Difficulty and missed attempts invite support — not shame intensity.
5. **No reward theatre that rewrites the day.** Rapid success invites deeper practice *within* the job — not a new syllabus agenda.
6. **Fatigue is educational data.** Protect learning quality; do not glorify grinding.
7. **Interruptions suspend; they do not redefine.** Resume the same day’s objective unless Daily Coach revisits the day.
8. **Escalate early enough.** Do not “adapt” into an unlawful day by a thousand cuts.
9. **Evidence humility.** Adaptation may respond to session signals without minting mastery or Profile re-diagnosis.
10. **Deterministic posture.** Same trigger class → same adaptation class (local vs escalate).

---

## 3. Local Adaptation Envelope

Local adaptations are lawful when **all** of the following hold:

1. Today’s Daily Coach primary objective (DCO-01) remains the session warrant.
2. Disclosed day posture / mode authority (DCO-10) remains respected.
3. Capacity and intensity stay inside Daily Coach envelopes (DCO-02 and plan intensity).
4. Topic / syllabus warrant does not expand into undeclared new first-pass ambition (unless today’s job already is that learning).
5. Claim types remain honest — no mastery minting from adapted success.

If any condition fails, classify as **escalation**, not local adaptation.

## 4. Adaptation Catalogue (Local)

### LSA-01 — Unexpected difficulty

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **Trigger** | Encoding sense-checks fail; retrieval collapses; practice errors are conceptual and persistent |
| **Lawful local responses** | Extend LSP-02; insert/strengthen LSP-03; interrupt with LSP-06; reduce independent difficulty; shrink chunk size; increase worked scaffolding |
| **Still serving** | Same topic and same day’s job at reduced pace/depth |
| **Unlawful** | Abandon today’s objective for an easier unrelated topic; invent a remediation curriculum beyond envelopes; shame the student into overload |
| **Escalate when** | Difficulty implies today’s job was wrong (e.g. prerequisite hole requiring plan-level change), or repair cannot fit remaining capacity without stealing protected day posture |

**Tutor rationale.** Struggle is information. The tutor slows and scaffolds before changing the brief.

---

### LSA-02 — Rapid mastery (fluency within the sitting)

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **Trigger** | Retrieval and application succeed stably for the chunk in scope sooner than expected |
| **Lawful local responses** | Advance difficulty within today’s job; shift from examples to independent practice; add retrieval variety; deepen connections **on the same objective**; shorten encoding; move earlier to reflection if the sitting’s aims are honestly met |
| **Must remember** | Sitting fluency ≠ journey mastery; do not narrate “mastered” |
| **Unlawful** | Open a new first-pass topic on a revision/recovery day; redefine DCO-01 because “we finished early”; pack unrelated advisory work as if it were today’s Mission |
| **Escalate when** | Remaining time is large **and** continuing the same job is educationally empty **and** only a Daily Coach secondary/primary rethink can authorise next work |

**Tutor rationale.** Speed is rewarded with depth or earlier honest close — not with silent day rewriting.

---

### LSA-03 — Fatigue

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **Trigger** | Careless errors rise; attention collapses; student reports depletion; effort quality falls |
| **Lawful local responses** | Short focus reset; switch to lighter phase still serving the objective (e.g. gentle retrieval → reflection); reduce question volume; early close with honest residue (LST-08) |
| **Unlawful** | Invent heroic final drills; convert fatigue into moral failure; silently keep dense practice when quality is gone |
| **Escalate when** | Fatigue reveals that today’s authorised intensity was never feasible (recovery posture needed at day level) |

**Tutor rationale.** Protecting learning quality preserves the journey. Exhausted practice teaches errors.

---

### LSA-04 — Interruption

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **Trigger** | External break suspends the sitting |
| **Lawful local responses** | Suspend with optional micro-note of position; on resume, short re-orientation (LSP-01 light); continue the same objective and arc |
| **Unlawful** | Treat interruption as licence to switch educational jobs; erase what was done; pretend the session completed |
| **Escalate when** | Interruption consumes the day’s capacity such that today’s objective cannot be served at all — Daily Coach must reshape the day |

**Tutor rationale.** Life interrupts study. Continuity of *objective* matters more than theatrical completion.

---

### LSA-05 — Reduced time available

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **Trigger** | Available remaining time shrinks below the composed arc’s honest needs |
| **Lawful local responses** | Narrow scope (fewer chunks); prefer complete smaller arc over unfinished heroic arc; keep preparation + one high-value core phase + abbreviated reflection/review; drop optional enrichment |
| **Preserve** | The educational *job class* (learn / practise / revise / recover) even if depth shrinks |
| **Unlawful** | Swap job class to whatever fits the minutes (e.g. replace revision with random new reading); skip all honesty at close |
| **Escalate when** | Remaining time cannot support even a minimal honest instance of today’s job |

**Tutor rationale.** A smaller complete sitting beats a large abandoned theatre — without changing what the day was for.

---

### LSA-06 — Emotional spike (anxiety / false confidence)

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **Trigger** | Panic after errors, or overconfidence despite failed retrieval |
| **Lawful local responses** | Confidence calibration moves (LSO-06); shorten threat load; contrasting evidence; reflective prompts; return to scaffolded success inside the same job |
| **Unlawful** | Fabricate competence; humiliation; invent a different comforting curriculum |
| **Escalate when** | Emotional state makes any further study today educationally unsafe/unusable and day-level recovery/rest is required |

## 5. Escalation to Daily Coach

### 5.1 Meaning of escalation

**Session → Daily Coach escalation** means:

> Local method changes are insufficient or unlawful.  
> The day’s educational objective, posture, or envelopes must be revisited by Daily Coach authority.

It does **not** mean the session becomes Master Planner, reschedules the calendar, or rewrites the Canonical Study Plan. If Daily Coach then requires rescheduling/replanning, Daily Coach uses its own escalation pathways (E1/E2 in `DAILY_COACH_DECISION_MODEL.md`).

### 5.2 Escalation triggers (LSA-E)

| ID | Trigger | Why local adaptation is insufficient |
|----|---------|--------------------------------------|
| **LSA-E1** | Today’s objective cannot be minimally served within remaining capacity | Need day-level reshape of DCO-01 / blocks |
| **LSA-E2** | Serving the sitting honestly requires a different work type than DCO-01 (e.g. discovered need for recovery vs dense practice) | Day posture must change |
| **LSA-E3** | Topic / mode authority conflict discovered mid-session | Mission/day authority must be disclosed/resolved at Daily Coach layer |
| **LSA-E4** | Prerequisite void or plan-level gap blocks today’s job | Beyond session envelopes; may further escalate from Daily Coach upstream |
| **LSA-E5** | Protected recovery/revision posture would be stolen by “needed” intensity | Protection honesty belongs to Daily Coach / plan — session must not cannibalise |
| **LSA-E6** | Rapid completion leaves substantial capacity and no lawful continuation under DCO-01/DCO-09 without day rethink | Optional secondaries are Daily Coach-ranked; session must not invent a new primary |

### 5.3 Escalation output (educational)

When escalating, the session should make available:

1. **What was attempted** under DCO-01 (Observed / Derived Fact posture).
2. **Why continuation is unlawful or empty** (plain educational reason).
3. **What local adaptations already tried**.
4. **What Daily Coach must reconsider** (objective, blocks, posture, escalation upstream).
5. **Interim close** — honest residue + reflection as feasible (do not leave the student in void theatre).

## 6. Lawful vs Unlawful Adaptation Matrix

| Situation | Lawful local | Unlawful |
|-----------|--------------|----------|
| Hard encoding | Scaffold, shrink chunk | Switch topic / rewrite day |
| Easy practice | Deepen within job; earlier close | Start undeclared new first-pass |
| Fatigue | Lighter phase / early close | Punishment finale drills |
| Short time | Minimal complete arc of same job | Swap job class for convenience |
| Interruption | Suspend & resume same objective | Treat as new day design |
| Misconception | Repair inside objective | Expand into multi-day remediation silently |
| Recovery day feels “too easy” | Keep light authorised work | Fill with dense catch-up practice |

## 7. Relationship to Daily Coach Adaptation

| Layer | May adapt | Must not |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| **Learning Session (this doc)** | Method, pace, scaffolding, phase order/depth inside DCO-01 | Redefine DCO-01; steal plan protections; replan journey |
| **Daily Coach (MS001)** | Today’s objective emphasis inside Canonical Study Plan envelopes | Silently rewrite Study Plan; absorb Master Planner authority |
| **Master Planner / Scheduling** | Envelopes, schedule, plan lifecycle | Be bypassed by session improvisation |

Authority flows **up** when envelopes break — never sideways into silent rewrite.

## 8. Examples (Tutor Reasoning)

**Unexpected difficulty, still same topic**  
CM1 method fails sense-checks → more worked examples + misconception contrast → continue learning job. **Local (LSA-01).**

**Rapid fluency on practice day**  
Student clears targeted questions → harder variants on same skill → reflect. **Local (LSA-02).** Not a licence to open next week’s topic as a new primary.

**Time collapses to 15 minutes on a practice day**  
Brief retrieval + one targeted question + reflection. **Local (LSA-05).** Same job, smaller arc.

**Student is exhausted on a dense practice day and recovery was not authorised**  
Early close + escalate: Daily Coach must decide recovery/rest vs remaining envelopes. **Escalate (LSA-E2 / LSA-E1).**

**Revision day, student “wants to learn something new” because revision feels easy**  
Refuse day rewrite; optionally deepen revision retrieval. If large spare capacity remains with no lawful revision continuation, **escalate (LSA-E6)** rather than invent first-pass primary.
