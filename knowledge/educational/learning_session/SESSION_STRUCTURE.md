## 1. Purpose

An expert IFoA tutor does not run every sitting identically. The tutor **composes** a session from recognisable educational moves: orient, teach or revisit, retrieve, work examples, practise, reflect, and close.

This document names those moves as **phases** so algorithms and surfaces share one anatomy — while forbidding cargo-cult templates that ignore today’s Daily Coach job.

Identifiers (LSP-XX) exist for traceability. Educational meaning is binding; UI step names are out of scope.

---

## 2. Structure Principles

1. **Purpose before phase list.** Choose phases that serve LSO aims under DCO-01.
2. **Not every phase every time.** A recovery sitting may omit deliberate question practice; a pure practice sitting may omit first-pass exposition.
3. **Coherence over variety.** Phases must form one educational arc for one objective — not a buffet of unrelated activities.
4. **Active beats passive when the job allows.** Prefer retrieval and deliberate practice over rereading theatre when today’s work type warrants it.
5. **Scaffold then release.** Earlier phases may support; later phases should require more independent performance when readiness appears.
6. **Honest close is structural.** Reflection / session review are educational phases — not optional UI garnish — for ordinary study sittings.
7. **Duration is capacity, not law.** How long a phase lasts is constrained by today’s capacity envelopes; *whether* a phase belongs is an educational decision.
8. **IFoA realism.** Worked examples, conditions of formulae, and exam-craft practice are first-class when authorised — not afterthoughts.
9. **Explainable composition.** Why these phases appear must be narratable (see `SESSION_EXPLAINABILITY.md`).
10. **No mastery from structure.** Completing a phase sequence never equals understanding proof.

## 3. Phase Catalogue

### LSP-01 — Preparation (orientation)

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **Educational job** | Orient the student to today’s session objective, scope, and success sense |
| **Typical moves** | Restate today’s objective in plain language; activate prior knowledge; name the intended aim cluster; set honest expectations for depth vs breadth |
| **Serves** | LSO-00, LSO-08, LSO-01/02 orientation |
| **Omit when** | Continuity-minimal rest days; mid-session resume after a brief pause may use a shorter re-orientation rather than a full preparation |
| **Must not** | Introduce a different educational job; overwhelm with multi-priority lists |

**Tutor rationale.** Students who know *what this sitting is for* waste less attention and judge progress more honestly.

---

### LSP-02 — Focused learning (encoding)

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **Educational job** | Build or rebuild accurate representations of authorised material |
| **Typical moves** | Concept exposition; method walkthrough; condition/assumption highlighting; short sense-checks |
| **Serves** | LSO-01, LSO-02 |
| **Omit when** | Pure retrieval/practice/revision sittings where new encoding is not today’s job |
| **Must not** | Equate watching/reading with understanding; expand beyond today’s authorised topic without warrant |

**Tutor rationale.** First-pass and strengthening need dedicated encoding time — but encoding without later retrieval is fragile.

---

### LSP-03 — Worked examples (guided application)

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **Educational job** | Show expert reasoning on representative problems before or alongside independent practice |
| **Typical moves** | Fully worked solutions with narrated decisions; faded examples; compare/contrast legal vs illegal steps |
| **Serves** | LSO-01, LSO-02, LSO-04 (as scaffolding), LSO-05 |
| **Omit when** | Student already fluent and today’s job is independent retrieval/exam craft; rest/recovery minimal sittings |
| **Must not** | Replace all practice with passive watching of solutions; hide why steps are legal |

**Tutor rationale.** IFoA methods are procedural and conditional. Worked examples teach *how experts decide*, not only *what the answer is*.

---

### LSP-04 — Active retrieval

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **Educational job** | Require recall/reconstruction without notes, then provide corrective feedback |
| **Typical moves** | Closed-book prompts; “write the method from memory”; short oral/written recall; flash reconstruction of definitions/conditions |
| **Serves** | LSO-03, LSO-05, LSO-06 |
| **Omit when** | Material has not yet been encoded at all *and* today’s job is pure first exposure — then retrieval may be light sense-checks only |
| **Must not** | Skip feedback; treat struggle as failure; claim mastery from brief success |

**Tutor rationale.** Retrieval is the bridge from “I recognise this” to “I can produce this.”

---

### LSP-05 — Question practice (deliberate application)

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **Educational job** | Apply knowledge to graded problems with targeting and feedback |
| **Typical moves** | Selected questions aligned to DCO-03; error-focused retries; difficulty progression; exam-craft variants when authorised |
| **Serves** | LSO-04, LSO-05, LSO-09 (when authorised) |
| **Omit when** | First-pass encoding is incomplete and practice would be noise; recovery days forbidding intensity; rest days |
| **Must not** | Maximise question count without targeting; mint mastery from attempts; displace today’s non-practice job |

**Tutor rationale.** Deliberate practice is how exam performance is built — when the day authorises it and encoding is sufficient.

---

### LSP-06 — Misconception check

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **Educational job** | Explicitly probe for systematic errors and repair them |
| **Typical moves** | Contrastive questions; “why is this wrong?”; condition traps; short diagnostic prompts mid-arc |
| **Serves** | LSO-05, LSO-02 |
| **May be** | A distinct phase **or** interleaved moments inside LSP-02–LSP-05 |
| **Must not** | Expand into an undeclared multi-topic remediation day |

**Tutor rationale.** Misconceptions are cheaper to fix when found early inside today’s objective.

---

### LSP-07 — Reflection

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **Educational job** | Help the student articulate learning, difficulty, and uncertainty honestly |
| **Typical moves** | Prompts aligned to DCO-04; separate “what I did” from “what I understand”; name one uncertain point |
| **Serves** | LSO-06, LSO-07, LSO-10 |
| **Omit when** | Only on true rest / zero-capacity days where even light reflection is unhelpful; otherwise prefer a short form over omission |
| **Must not** | Force mastery declarations; shame uncertainty; use Twin/engineering vocabulary |

**Tutor rationale.** Reflection closes the cognitive loop and feeds honest continuity.

---

### LSP-08 — Session review (close)

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|----------------|
| **Educational job** | Summarise what the sitting accomplished relative to today’s objective and what remains for later — without readiness theatre |
| **Typical moves** | Restate objective; note completed educational moves; mark open items; optional confidence-honest close (DCO-05-compatible) |
| **Serves** | LSO-00, LSO-10, LSO-06 |
| **Omit when** | Abrupt external stop may defer review — but the educational debt should be acknowledged when continuity resumes |
| **Must not** | Narrate completion as mastery; invent a new tomorrow’s Daily Coach job |

**Tutor rationale.** A clean educational close preserves trust between session, day, and plan.

## 4. Composition Patterns (Illustrative, Not Mandatory Templates)

These patterns show **principle-led composition**. They are examples of tutor judgement — not required scripts.

### 4.1 First-pass learning sitting

Typical arc:

`LSP-01 Preparation → LSP-02 Focused learning → LSP-03 Worked examples → LSP-04 Light retrieval → LSP-07 Reflection → LSP-08 Session review`

Question practice (LSP-05) appears only lightly or not at all if encoding is still fragile.

### 4.2 Practice / consolidation sitting

Typical arc:

`LSP-01 → (brief LSP-02 refresh if needed) → LSP-04 Retrieval → LSP-05 Question practice ↔ LSP-06 Misconception check → LSP-07 → LSP-08`

### 4.3 Revision sitting

Typical arc:

`LSP-01 → LSP-04 Retrieval-heavy → LSP-05 Exam-craft practice (as authorised) → LSP-06 → LSP-07 Confidence calibration → LSP-08`

New encoding (LSP-02) is minimal and only for repair — not undeclared first-pass expansion.

### 4.4 Recovery / lighter sitting

Typical arc:

`LSP-01 → short LSP-02 or gentle LSP-04 → LSP-07 → LSP-08`

Omit high-volume LSP-05. Intensity stays inside recovery envelopes.

### 4.5 Checkpoint / mock-prep sitting

Typical arc:

`LSP-01 → LSP-05 / LSP-09-oriented practice → LSP-06 → LSP-07 calibration → LSP-08`

Scaffolding is thinner; feedback remains educationally rich.

## 5. Ordering Logic (Educational)

When composing order, prefer:

1. **Orient before load** (LSP-01 early).
2. **Encode before heavy independent practice** when the material is new to this sitting’s job.
3. **Worked examples before or interleaved with early practice** when methods are unfamiliar.
4. **Retrieval before or between practice blocks** to expose false fluency.
5. **Misconception repair when detected** — interrupt the happy-path order if false models are active.
6. **Reflect and review before claiming the sitting closed.**

Re-ordering for local adaptation is lawful; inventing phases that serve a different day’s job is not.

---

## 6. What Structure Is Not

Session structure must **not** be treated as:

- a fixed seven-step product funnel required of every sitting;
- a timer-driven playlist where minutes replace educational judgement;
- a coverage checklist that ignores today’s Daily Coach work type;
- proof of mastery upon reaching LSP-08;
- authority to change DCO-01 mid-arc.
