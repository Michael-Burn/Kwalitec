## Authority

This document is the constitutional overview of **revision strategies** for Kwalitec.

It is subordinate to the Educational Constitution, specialised educational models, and the Educational Revision Model (MS001). It governs **how Kwalitec classifies the kind of consolidating approach appropriate for previously learned material at a given time** while remaining faithful to Learning Coach evidence and the Canonical Study Plan. It does not authorise implementation shortcuts that contradict the Constitution, and it does not invent prior learning, evidence, mastery, or planning meaning absent from upstream models.

Authority order for revision strategies:

> Constitution defines educational truth and curriculum primacy.  
> Knowledge & Mastery defines coverage ≠ understanding ≠ mastery.  
> Evidence Model defines what may warrant educational claims.  
> Continuity Standard preserves rightful educational history across plan change.  
> Master Planner publishes the Canonical Study Plan and owns long-term educational envelopes, including protected revision windows.  
> Daily Coach owns *what is most educationally valuable today* under that contract.  
> Learning Coach owns longitudinal progression, obstacle diagnosis, and learning interventions.  
> Recovery Coach owns restorative coaching after meaningful disruption.  
> Educational Revision Model (MS001) defines *whether* revision is warranted, *which* material and objectives, and *qualitative priority*.  
> **This Revision Strategy Framework (Workstream 5 / MS002) defines what kind of revision is educationally appropriate for this knowledge at this time.**  
> Downstream coaching and narration surfaces must consume this educational reasoning — never replace first learning, recovery, or planning with strategy theatre.

---

## 1. Purpose

Kwalitec coaches students preparing for demanding professional examinations (especially IFoA syllabi such as CM1/CS1 and peers).

An expert IFoA tutor does not apply one generic “do more revision” mode to every consolidating need. Durability risk, weak retrieval, brittle concepts that need stabilising, prerequisites that later work depends on, related topics that must cohere, ordinary maintenance of healthy known material, and exam-facing consolidation each call for a **different consolidating approach** — while remaining honest that the material was already learned, and while never pretending that repeated revision cures a deeper learning problem.

After a lawful revision warrant and material emphasis, the tutor answers one living educational question:

> **“What kind of revision is educationally appropriate for this knowledge at this time?”**

That answer must fit the educational evidence profile, pursue MS001 revision objectives, remain faithful to Canonical Study Plan educational intent, preserve Daily Coach and Learning Coach authorities, refuse Recovery Coach bypass, and escalate honestly to the Learning Coach when revision alone is insufficient.

This document records that tutor posture so every future Revision Coach subsystem has a single educational reference for *strategy meaning*.

> **The Revision Strategy Framework describes educational strategy reasoning.  
> It does not implement Runtime A logic, algorithms, analytics, or Twin writers.**

## 2. What a Revision Strategy Is

A **revision strategy** is a named **educational approach archetype** that describes the *kind* of consolidating coaching appropriate for previously learned knowledge at this time — not a calendar packing algorithm, not a score band, and not a second study plan.

| Concept | Definition | Primary question |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Evidence profile** | Educational reading of what consolidating need the trail shows for this known material | What kind of reinforcement need is this? |
| **Revision strategy** | Named consolidating approach matched to that profile | What kind of revision fits? |
| **Strategy purpose** | Educational job this approach primarily does | What are we trying to strengthen by this route? |
| **Strategy objectives** | MS001 RVO-XX goods this strategy primarily advances | Which consolidating goods lead? |
| **Strategy success** | Educational signs that this approach has done its job | When may we transition or maintain? |
| **Strategy limit** | Situations where another strategy — or Learning Coach / Recovery handoff — is preferable | When is this the wrong approach? |

A strategy is:

- **evidence-matched** — selected from educational need, not operational convenience;
- **objective-bound** — advances RVO-01…RVO-05 rather than revision theatre;
- **prior-exposure-gated** — never applies to unlearned syllabus content;
- **plan-faithful** — respects Canonical Study Plan revision windows and protections;
- **coach-coordinated** — informs Daily Coach; consumes Learning Coach evidence; never replaces Recovery Coach;
- **transition-aware** — may move to another strategy, enter maintenance, or escalate;
- **explainable** — students understand why this approach, what evidence, what improvement, how success is recognised;
- **non-scoring** — qualitative educational judgement only.

A strategy is **not**:

- a substitute for first-pass Learning Mode or Learning Coach progression of unlearned material;
- a licence to label new syllabus content as a “revision strategy”;
- inference of mastery from strategy compliance, revision ticks, or phase labels;
- a bypass of Daily Coach day authority, Learning Coach evidence/diagnosis layers, or Recovery Coach restorative authority;
- a rewrite of the Canonical Study Plan or Master Planner educational strategy;
- a duplicate of MS001 triggers or priorities under new labels without educational distinction;
- a claim that completing a strategy guarantees a pass;
- a cure for deeper learning obstacles through endless consolidating loops.

## 3. Binding Distinction: Strategy vs Neighbours

| Layer | Educational job | Unlawful collapse |
|-------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Trigger (RVT)** | Warrant that revision is needed | Selecting a strategy without a warrant |
| **Objective (RVO)** | Name goods to strengthen | Treating question volume as an objective |
| **Priority (RVP)** | Order which known material leads | Treating priority as a numeric score |
| **Strategy (RVS)** | Name the *kind* of consolidating approach | Equating strategy with a new long-term plan or with first learning |
| **Daily Coach** | Own today’s educational priority under the contract | Letting strategy speech commandeer the day |
| **Learning Coach** | Own progression / obstacle / intervention meaning | Using repeated revision to skip diagnosis or override evidence |
| **Recovery Coach** | Own restorative continuity after disruption | Disguising catch-up as a revision strategy |
| **Master Planner / Scheduling** | Own structural envelope and allocation change | Absorbing replan inside strategy “completion” |

Hard rule:

> **Strategies classify consolidating approaches.  
> They do not mint plans, mastery, day priorities, first learning, or recovery episodes.**

## 4. What the Strategy Framework Produces

Given a **lawful revision warrant**, **material emphasis**, and **accumulated educational evidence**, the Strategy Framework produces:

| Output family | Role |
|---------------|------|
| **Primary strategy** | Named RVS-XX approach type |
| **Supporting strategy (optional)** | Secondary archetype when needs stack — speech still names one primary |
| **Strategy rationale** | Educational link from evidence profile → strategy |
| **Objectives in focus** | MS001 RVO-XX goods this strategy advances |
| **Expected educational outcome** | What consolidation should achieve if the approach is followed |
| **Success recognition** | Educational cues that the strategy is working |
| **Transition posture** | Stay / change strategy / enter maintenance / escalate to Learning Coach (or other handoff) |
| **Strategy explainability** | Plain-language account for the student |

It answers:

| Question | Educational answer |
|----------|-------------------|
| What kind of revision? | Named strategy from the catalogue |
| Why this kind? | Evidence profile + objectives + plan posture |
| What improvement is expected? | Expected consolidating outcome |
| How will success be recognised? | Success signs under the strategy |
| May Revision Coach enact this alone? | Only within MS001 boundaries; else escalate / hand off |
| When does this strategy end or change? | Transition rules |

## 5. Educational Purpose

The Strategy Framework exists so that:

1. **Different consolidating needs receive different approaches** — not one “more past papers” mode.
2. **Strategy choice remains educational** — derived from tutoring principles, not UI or optimiser convenience.
3. **Learning Coach evidence stays binding** — strategies consume progression meaning; they never override it.
4. **Revision stays revision** — strategies never invent first learning or Recovery Coach bypass.
5. **Exam readiness stays honest** — exam-facing strategies consolidate what is known; they do not mint coverage or certainty.
6. **Transitions are honest** — maintenance after success, strategy change, and Learning Coach escalation are named educational events.
7. **Students understand the approach** — why this kind of revision, what evidence, what improvement, how success is recognised.

## 6. Core Responsibilities

The Strategy Framework is educationally responsible for:

| Responsibility | Meaning |
|----------------|---------|
| **Name lawful strategies** | Catalogue consolidating approaches from educational principles |
| **Select after warrant** | Match evidence profile → RVS-XX without inventing warrant |
| **Bind to objectives** | Declare which RVO-XX goods the strategy advances |
| **Govern transitions** | Maintenance, strategy change, Learning Coach escalation, Recovery / planning handoff |
| **Protect authority boundaries** | Never absorb first learning, Recovery Coach, mastery, or plan rewrite |
| **Explain strategy choice** | Make approach selection speakable with evidence and expected outcomes |

It is **not** educationally responsible for:

- inventing prior exposure or revision warrant;
- deciding which topic enters the revision field (MS001 warrant + priority);
- diagnosing learning obstacles or selecting Learning Interventions;
- restoring progress after meaningful disruption;
- rewriting Canonical Study Plans or educational strategy;
- deciding today’s Daily Coach primary objective by commandeering day authority;
- producing opaque “strategy scores” as educational proof.

---

## 7. Binding Educational Doctrine

1. **Strategies reinforce existing learning.** They do not introduce first learning, bypass Recovery Coach, redefine mastery, or modify long-term planning.
2. **Warrant first.** No RVS without an explicit revision warrant and prior exposure (`REVISION_TRIGGERS.md`).
3. **Evidence honesty.** Strategy selection cites accumulated educational evidence — never calendar guilt alone.
4. **One primary in speech.** Supporting strategies may exist; students hear one primary consolidating approach.
5. **Learning Coach evidence is preserved.** Strategies consume, never override, progression / obstacle / intervention meaning.
6. **Deeper learning problems escalate.** Where revision evidence shows revision is insufficient, authority returns to the Learning Coach rather than compensating through repeated revision.
7. **Daily Coach authority is preserved.** Strategy informs how today’s consolidating work is shaped; it does not commandeer day-priority selection.
8. **Canonical Study Plan remains the contract.** Strategies may shape emphasis within authorised revision windows; they may not independently rewrite the plan.
9. **Mastery is never redefined by strategy.** Strategy compliance does not mint understanding or exam readiness (EIP-006).
10. **Explainability is mandatory.** Silent strategy switches and opaque approach speech are forbidden (EIP-003).

```
Revision warrant + material emphasis (MS001)
        │
        ▼
Revision Strategy Framework (this corpus)
        │  primary RVS + rationale + success signs + transitions
        │
        ├─ lawful consolidating approach
        │     → informs Daily Coach emphasis & session design
        │     → respects protected revision windows already published
        │
        └─ wrong-mode / insufficiency detection
              → Learning Coach (deeper learning problem; revision insufficient)
              → first learning (no prior exposure)
              → Recovery Coach (meaningful disruption is primary)
              → Master Planner / Scheduling (structural plan / window change)
```

## 9. Relationship to MS001 Educational Revision Model

| Layer | Question | Owner |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Warrant / objectives / priority** | What should be revised now, and why? | Revision Coach MS001 |
| **Strategy** | What kind of revision is educationally appropriate for this knowledge at this time? | **Revision Coach MS002 (this corpus)** |

MS001 without MS002 risks treating all revision as interchangeable volume.  
MS002 without MS001 risks inventing approaches without warrant or prior exposure.

Hard rule:

> **Strategy selection never invents revision warrant.  
> Warrant never automatically selects a strategy by ID matching alone.**

---

## 10. Relationship to Learning Coach (Architectural Hard Line)

| Situation | Primary authority | Strategy Framework role |
|-----------|-------------------|-------------------------|
| Ordinary consolidating need on known material | Revision Coach strategy | Select RVS; pursue RVO goods |
| Learning obstacle / thin genuine progression on “covered” material | Learning Coach | Do **not** compensate with endless RVS loops; escalate |
| Intervention already in progress | Learning Coach owns intervention meaning | May support consolidating emphasis only when honest and subordinate |
| False readiness theatre | Learning Coach owns honesty of readiness claims | Exam consolidation strategies must not mint readiness |

Architectural requirement restated:

> **Where revision evidence indicates a deeper learning problem,  
> authority must transition back to the Learning Coach  
> rather than compensating through repeated revision.**
