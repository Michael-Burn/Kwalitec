## 1. Purpose

Without transition law, revision becomes a permanent intensive mode, a confusing sequence of unexplained “approaches,” or a loop that papers over Learning Coach problems with more of the same consolidating work.

With transition law, an expert tutor can:

- move to maintenance after successful reinforcement;
- change strategy when the consolidating need evolves (including prerequisite refresh → examination consolidation);
- escalate to the Learning Coach when revision is insufficient;
- hand off to Recovery Coach or planning pathways when those authorities own the problem;
- keep Daily Coach and Canonical Study Plan authorities intact across those moves.

---

## 2. Transition Principles

1. **Continuity of learner history.** Strategy change does not wipe educational identity (EIP-005).  
2. **Explain before switch.** Material transitions are speakable under `STRATEGY_EXPLAINABILITY.md`.  
3. **One primary strategy at a time** in student speech.  
4. **Success lightens intensity** — successful reinforcement often transitions to maintenance, not endless crisis revision.  
5. **Insufficient revision is educational information** — not moral failure, and not a licence for infinite loops.  
6. **Escalate to Learning Coach rather than fake consolidating success** when deeper learning problems appear.  
7. **Do not independently replan** during transition — Master Planner / Scheduling own structural change.  
8. **Daily Coach remains day authority** under the current authorised contract before and after transition.  
9. **Learning Coach evidence persists** across strategy change — completing a strategy is not proof learning progressed or mastery exists.  
10. **Recovery boundaries remain sacred** — strategy transitions never bypass Recovery Coach.  
11. **No streak or score gates.** Transitions are educational judgements, not numeric unlocks.

## 3. Transition Classes

| Class | Meaning | Typical destination |
|-------|---------|---------------------|
| **ST1 — Maintenance after successful reinforcement** | Intensive consolidating job has done enough; keep-alive posture | RVS-07 (or ordinary plan rhythm when no keep-alive needed) |
| **ST2 — Strategy change within revision** | Same revision episode; different RVS better fits evolving profile | Another RVS-XX as primary |
| **ST3 — Escalation to Learning Coach** | Revision evidence shows deeper learning problem; revision insufficient | Learning Coach diagnosis / intervention ownership |
| **ST4 — Recovery Coach handoff** | Meaningful disruption becomes / remains primary | Recovery Coach restorative ownership |
| **ST5 — First-learning handoff** | Target lacks prior exposure or “revision” was misframed | Daily Coach / Learning Mode / first-pass pathways |
| **ST6 — Structural escalation** | Strategy need cannot fit authorised windows / envelopes | Rescheduling / Master Planner pathways |
| **ST7 — Hold / gather** | Warrant or success unclear | Stay on gentle strategy or authorised return; gather evidence — do not invent crisis or false completion |

## 4. ST1 — Maintenance After Successful Reinforcement

### 4.1 Educational meaning

An intensive consolidating strategy (typically RVS-01, RVS-02, RVS-03, or focused RVS-04 / RVS-05) has produced enough honest improvement that light protective return — or ordinary plan rhythm — is now the right educational posture.

### 4.2 Success → maintenance criteria (qualitative)

Move toward **RVS-07** (or end intensive revision emphasis) when **all** of the following can be honestly affirmed for the target material:

1. Primary RVO goods for the intensive strategy show educational improvement on return (retrieval / durability / understanding / integration as applicable).  
2. Evidence no longer supports acute forgetting / fragility as the leading story — **or** remaining issues are owned by Learning Coach (ST3).  
3. Explainability can honestly say the intensive job has done enough for now.  
4. Canonical Study Plan / Daily Coach can absorb lighter consolidating emphasis without inventing crisis.

### 4.3 Lawful effects

- Primary strategy becomes RVS-07, or intensive revision emphasis ends inside ordinary day coaching.  
- Retain learner history; do not narrate success as mastery earned or exam readiness declared.  
- Keep spaced return available under RVT-02 when plan windows warrant.

### 4.4 Unlawful effects

- Declaring success because a revision checklist was completed.  
- Declaring mastery / exam readiness from strategy completion.  
- Silently continuing intensive RVS-03 loops after claiming maintenance.  
- Using “maintenance” as a euphemism while evidence still shows acute fragility.

### 4.5 Worked example

> Spaced return on a known CM1 chapter was weak (RVS-02). After several consolidating sittings, retrieval is workable again under light probing. Transition to **RVS-07** keep-alive inside the next protected window — not perpetual intensive retrieval drills.

## 5. ST2 — Strategy Change Within Revision

### 5.1 Educational meaning

The current primary strategy is no longer the best educational match — either because it was provisionally wrong, or because consolidating progress changed the leading need.

### 5.2 When strategy change is warranted

Change primary strategy when:

1. **Success signs for the current strategy are not emerging** after a fair consolidating attempt, and another RVS better matches the observed profile; or  
2. **Profile evolved** (e.g. retrieval restored → durability across longer spacing remains the job; foundations refreshed → exam-facing consolidation of known material now leads); or  
3. **Trigger co-occurrence shifted** (e.g. RVT-05 resolved enough that RVT-03 exam-facing form of known material becomes primary).

### 5.3 Illustrative lawful evolutions

| From | Toward | Educational story |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| RVS-04 Prerequisite Refresh | RVS-06 Examination Consolidation | Foundations of known material are safe again; exam proximity now makes consolidating *already learned* exam strength the leading job |
| RVS-03 Fragile Knowledge Stabilisation | RVS-01 Durability Reinforcement | Brittleness eased; lasting retention across spacing is now the honest next consolidating job |
| RVS-02 Retrieval Strengthening | RVS-05 Concept Integration | Single-topic retrieval workable; related known topics now need to cohere |
| RVS-01 / RVS-02 / RVS-03 | RVS-07 Maintenance | Intensive job succeeded (ST1 special case of ST2) |
| RVS-07 Maintenance | RVS-01 / RVS-02 / RVS-03 | New evidence of forgetting / fragility — escalate intensity honestly |

### 5.4 Lawful effects

- Rename primary RVS with explainability.  
- Re-state RVO focus and expected outcome.  
- Preserve prior-exposure and warrant honesty.

### 5.5 Unlawful effects

- Silent mode switches without educational reason.  
- Changing to RVS-06 to invent coverage of unlearned material.  
- Using strategy change to avoid Learning Coach escalation when ST3 applies.

## 6. ST3 — Escalation to Learning Coach (Architectural Hard Line)

### 6.1 Educational meaning

Accumulated revision evidence indicates that consolidating approaches are **insufficient** — a deeper learning problem exists (obstacle, thin genuine progression, misconception, false readiness that revision cannot honestly repair). Authority must transition back to the **Learning Coach** rather than compensating through repeated revision.

### 6.2 Escalation criteria (qualitative)

Escalate to Learning Coach when any of the following hold:

1. Fair attempts at appropriate RVS strategies show **no meaningful consolidating improvement**.  
2. Learning Coach posture already indicates **obstacle / intervention** ownership.  
3. “Covered” material behaves as if **understanding never formed**, and consolidating speech has become dishonest.  
4. Revision would need to **override Learning Coach evidence** to continue.  
5. Student needs **diagnosis**, not another consolidating approach label.

### 6.3 Lawful effects

- End primary Revision Coach strategy ownership for that problem (or demote to explicitly supporting emphasis only if Learning Coach allows).  
- Hand educational response to Learning Coach progression / obstacle / intervention pathways.  
- Explain honestly: revision alone is not enough; we need a learning diagnosis / response.  
- Do **not** redefine mastery or invent first learning as “more revision.”

### 6.4 Unlawful effects

- Opening RVS-02 / RVS-03 loops as a substitute for diagnosis.  
- Narrating escalation as student failure rather than educational honesty.  
- Keeping Revision Coach as primary while contradicting Learning Coach evidence.

Architectural requirement restated:

> **Where revision evidence indicates a deeper learning problem,  
> authority must transition back to the Learning Coach  
> rather than compensating through repeated revision.**

### 6.5 Worked example

> RVS-03 on a “covered” topic yields repeated same-pattern failure. Learning Evidence Model shows thin genuine progression. Transition **ST3** — Learning Coach owns diagnosis; stop promising that more revision will fix it.

## 7. ST4 — Recovery Coach Handoff

### 7.1 Educational meaning

Meaningful disruption is (or becomes) the primary educational problem. Revision strategies must not disguise catch-up as consolidation.

### 7.2 When Recovery handoff is warranted

- Active Recovery warrant (RT-* family) is primary.  
- Absence / capacity break makes consolidating strategy speech dishonest.  
- Student returns from disruption needing restorative continuity before ordinary revision strategies.

### 7.3 Lawful effects

- Defer to Recovery Coach; revision strategies pause or become layered only when Recovery allows.  
- After recovery posture ends, MS001 warrant + MS002 strategy selection may resume for ordinary consolidating needs.

### 7.4 Unlawful effects

- Opening RVS-01 / RVS-06 as if disruption were ordinary spacing.  
- Using examination consolidation to bypass Recovery responsibilities.

---

## 8. ST5 — First-Learning Handoff

### 8.1 Educational meaning

Target material lacks prior exposure, or “revision strategy” was misapplied to unfinished first-pass.

### 8.2 Lawful effects

- Refuse RVS; route to first learning / Daily Coach Learning Mode / Learning Coach as appropriate.  
- Protect Canonical Study Plan revision windows from silent first-pass consumption (escalate if conflict).

### 8.3 Unlawful effects

- Relabelling unread content as any RVS.  
- “Examination consolidation” of never-studied units.

---

## 9. ST6 — Structural Escalation (Plan / Scheduling)

### 9.1 Educational meaning

The consolidating need cannot be met honestly inside authorised windows, intensity, or envelopes without structural change.

### 9.2 Lawful effects

- Identify need for Rescheduling / Master Planner pathways.  
- Do not invent unofficial revision windows or steal protections.  
- Daily Coach / plan authorities remain owners of structural change.

### 9.3 Unlawful effects

- Absorbing replan inside strategy “completion.”  
- Claiming strategy success while envelopes are broken.

---

## 10. ST7 — Hold / Gather

### 10.1 Educational meaning

Warrant, success, or Learning Coach posture is unclear. Prefer gentler authorised return and evidence gathering over invented crisis or false completion.

### 10.2 Lawful effects

- Stay on RVS-07 or light authorised return when safe.  
- Gather Educational Evidence; re-enter selection when clear.  
- Do not mint strategies from calendar guilt.

---

## 11. Authority Boundary Summary Across Transitions

| Transition | May Revision Coach complete alone? | Must respect |
|------------|------------------------------------|--------------|
| ST1 Maintenance | Yes, as consolidating emphasis change | Daily Coach day authority; no mastery minting |
| ST2 Strategy change | Yes, within revision field | MS001 warrant honesty; explainability |
| ST3 Learning Coach | **No** — hand off ownership | Learning Coach diagnosis / intervention law |
| ST4 Recovery | **No** — hand off ownership | Recovery Coach boundaries |
| ST5 First learning | **No** — refuse revision | Prior-exposure gate |
| ST6 Structural | **No** — escalate | Master Planner / Scheduling |
| ST7 Hold | Yes, lightly | Evidence honesty; no crisis theatre |

Hard rule:

> **Strategy transitions never rewrite the Canonical Study Plan,  
> never redefine mastery,  
> never bypass Recovery Coach,  
> and never use repeated revision to compensate for Learning Coach problems.**
