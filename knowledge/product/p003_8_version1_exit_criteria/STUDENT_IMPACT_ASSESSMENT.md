## 10. Lessons learned for student value

- Student harm from release-governance failure is usually **overclaim**, not missing checklists — the highest-leverage restatement is hard-gate FAIL → NO GO.  
- Separable verdicts (beta vs effectiveness vs Version 1) remain the student-protection mechanism; exit criteria must not collapse them.  
- Docs-only exit packs correctly score ΔKSI = 0; claiming student-value movement from packaging would itself violate claim honesty.
