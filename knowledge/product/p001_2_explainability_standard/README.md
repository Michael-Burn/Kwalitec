**Programme ID:** P-001.2  
**Parent programme:** P-001 — Product Success Measurement  
**Status:** COMPLETE (documentation & governance)  
**Started:** 2026-07-26  
**Authority:** Product explainability law (subordinate to Vision 2030, Educational Constitution / EIP-003 Educational Explainability Standard, and Architecture Constitution Article IV)  
**Constraints:** Documentation and governance only — no runtime, UI, or API changes  

---

## Mission

Create the permanent product explainability standard that governs every recommendation, prediction, planning decision, and readiness assessment presented to students.

The KSI baseline scores Explainability (K8) at **55** — below the Version 1 floor of **70** (V1-K3). Constitutional law already requires explainability; students still experience opaque Coach / Insight / readiness speech. This programme turns that gap into enforceable product contracts: levels, mandatory schema, patterns, quality guidelines, and a review checklist for future EP/P work.
