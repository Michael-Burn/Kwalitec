# Daily Mission Intelligence — Philosophy

**Programme:** ILE-004 — Daily Mission Intelligence  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Effective:** 2026-07-28  
**Authority:** ILE-010; ILE-011; ILE-001C0; ILE-001C; ILE-002; ILE-003; P-001.2; P-001.3  

---

## Purpose

Define what **Daily Mission Intelligence** is for the learner: one primary educational mission each day that answers *what deserves attention today* — with one educational reason and one expected benefit.

---

## Guiding principle

> One day. One primary mission. One educational reason. One expected benefit.

The Study Sensei reduces uncertainty by selecting **one** meaningful educational mission rather than presenting multiple competing priorities.

---

## Educational goal

Every day the learner should know exactly what deserves attention today.

Opening Kwalitec should immediately answer: **“What should I focus on today?”**  
The student should never need to browse for work.

---

## Optimisation axes (lawful)

Mission composition may cite:

| Axis | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Learning value | Highest educational value given authorised evidence |
| Momentum | Sustaining productive learning progress |
| Knowledge stability | Protecting fragile or provisional knowledge |
| Recovery | Restoring after disruption without shame |
| Consistency | Steady, sustainable study rhythm |

## Forbidden axes

Engagement, screen time, streaks, retention metrics, gamification, or “open the app” theatre.

---

## Authority boundaries

Daily Mission Intelligence **composes** a student-facing brief from authorised Recommendation / MES evidence.

It does **not**:

- Re-run Decision Engine selection  
- Invent ranking or a second educational brain  
- Duplicate Twin, readiness prediction, Tutor, or chat authority  
- Optimise for engagement metrics  

Capability 2.10 structural Mission Intelligence (task operationalisation) remains distinct: this programme owns the **learner-facing primary mission brief** and its journal/timeline continuity.

---

## Mission structure

Each mission contains:

1. Mission title  
2. Educational purpose  
3. Why today  
4. Supporting evidence  
5. Estimated effort  
6. Expected learning outcome  
7. What happens after completion  
8. Reflection prompt  
9. Mission confidence  
10. Optional uncertainty  
11. Mission explanation  

Plus explainability answers: why not something else? what if I skip it?

---

## Lifecycle

```
Created → Presented → Accepted → Completed | Deferred
    → Decision Journal → Educational Timeline → Next mission
```

---

## Out of scope

No Tutor redesign. No chat interface. No exam prediction. No conversational AI. No gamification.
