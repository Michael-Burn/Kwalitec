## Purpose

This document states how Kwalitec thinks about learning for Intelligent Learning Experience design. Binding educational law remains the Educational Constitution. Where conflict appears, the Constitution wins.

---

## Learning beliefs

1. **Learning is normally sequential.** Candidates advance through official syllabus order unless a later, constitutionally authorised mode explicitly and transparently interrupts that order.
2. **Study is not understanding.** Coverage answers “have I touched this?” Competence requires evidence.
3. **Consistency beats intensity.** Sustainable daily progress outperforms heroic cramming for long professional syllabuses.
4. **Feedback and reflection close the loop.** Attempt → outcome → interpretation → next guidance must be visible to the student.
5. **Mistakes are educational fuel** when explained and scheduled for recovery — not shame signals.
6. **Confidence should track evidence.** Calibrated confidence is healthier than inflated certainty.

---

## Assessment philosophy

- Assessment exists to produce **educational evidence**, not vanity scores.
- Prefer instruments that map to syllabus topics and learning objectives.
- Record outcomes immutably; update educational state only through lawful reasoning paths.
- Never let a single quiz declare mastery as fact.
- Surface thin evidence as uncertainty; do not paper over gaps with averages that look precise.

---

## Mission philosophy

- A **Mission** (domain) / **Session** (learner UI) is the day’s authoritative study commitment.
- One primary mission per day beats a buffet of optional tasks.
- Missions must bind to curriculum identity whenever a plan is curriculum-bound.
- Duration and pacing must be realistic relative to published effort and exam date.
- Completion is educationally meaningful only when it advances coverage and/or qualifying evidence — not checkbox theatre.

---

## Revision philosophy

- Revision is scheduled from forgetting risk, syllabus weight, and evidence — not from arbitrary streaks.
- Prefer spaced, explainable revisits over last-minute mass review alone.
- Recovery paths should respect prerequisites and Learning Graph structure.
- Revision must remain honest: “revise because evidence shows weakness” beats “revise to keep engagement high.”

---

## Explainability philosophy

- Guidance is educational speech, not system telemetry.
- Students deserve: what / why / what next / what is uncertain.
- Explainability levels and schema follow the Explainability Standard (P-001.2).
- Tutor explanation explains decisions already made by reasoning authorities; it does not invent new educational authority.

---

## Role of AI

AI exists to **improve judgement and communication**, not to replace educational law.

| Allowed | Forbidden |
|---|---|
| Clearer prose for already-decided recommendations | Opaque ranking or mastery invention |
| Structured explanation envelopes | Hidden LLM calls in core planning/recommendation paths |
| Future replaceable generation adapters behind ports | Treating chat as a second educational brain |

If AI cannot explain itself in educationally defensible terms, it must not recommend.

---

## Role of deterministic reasoning

Deterministic cores are a feature:

- Same inputs → same planning, readiness, and recommendation outputs.
- Auditable Decision Journal and certification harnesses become possible.
- Students and operators can reproduce why a day looked the way it did.

Personalisation adapts parameters and presentation within deterministic, evidence-bound rules — it does not inject randomness into educational authority.

---

## Relationship to certified platform

Educational Intelligence stages (evidence → interpretation → decision → twin → graph → mission → tutor) operationalise this philosophy. ILE programmes must **surface** that pipeline honestly rather than bypass it with experience-layer heuristics.

---

## Final educational test

> Does this change help the candidate learn more truthfully and sustainably toward professional competence?

If it only increases activity, novelty, or marketing claim-ability — reject it.
