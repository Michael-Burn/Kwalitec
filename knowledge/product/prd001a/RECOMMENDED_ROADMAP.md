# PRD-001A — Recommended Roadmap

**Goal:** Make Kwalitec feel like the Education Operating System in the Product Blueprint **without** pretending Twin-first authority is already live.

Investigation only produced this roadmap — no implementation in PRD-001A.

---

## Priority principles

1. **Tell the truth about Version 1** before enabling more intelligence.  
2. **Elevate syllabus + understanding visibility** on the Home decision surface.  
3. **Connect existing backends** before inventing new engines.  
4. Treat Twin adaptive interruption as an **explicit cutover programme**, not a silent expectation.

---

## P0 — Product integrity (highest student value)

| Work | Closes gaps | Why first |
|---|---|---|
| **Educational contract on Home** — one plain-language line stating Learning Mode rule (syllabus order) and what does *not* change today’s topic | G01, G05, G18 | Restores trust immediately; no Twin required |
| **Curriculum position strip** — section, topic code, “N of M complete” on first viewport | G07, syllabus disconnect | Makes Curriculum pillar visible |
| **Guarantee topic-bound mission titles** whenever curriculum_id present; kill generic fallbacks in bound plans | G09 | Fixes “generic mission” perception |
| **Align landing descriptor honesty** with V1 (or add Internal Alpha qualifier that intelligence is syllabus-guided now, Twin deepening next) | G19, Product Promise | Stops over-promise |

## Explicit non-goals (near term)

- Rewriting architecture or adding LLM recommendation paths.  
- Enabling all Twin flags in production without a cutover programme.  
- UI redesign for aesthetics (DEP-003 shell is enough chrome).  
- Claiming Exam Ready guarantees.

## Success test for “Education Operating System”

After P0+P1, a founding-cohort student should answer yes to:

1. What am I studying today?  
2. Where is that in my official syllabus?  
3. Why this before the next topic?  
4. What does the system believe I understand (if anything yet)?  
5. What is still sequential vs adaptive?

Until those five are answerable on Home, Blueprint conformance remains incomplete regardless of service coverage.
