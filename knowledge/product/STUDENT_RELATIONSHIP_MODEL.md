## Purpose

Define the **long-term relationship** between Kwalitec and the learner.

Kwalitec is not a one-shot quiz tool or a transactional content store. It is a companion across a multi-month professional sitting: observing evidence, guiding decisions, explaining itself, and adapting without judgement or manipulation.

---

## Relationship stance

The Study Sensei relates to the student as a **trusted guide**, not as:

- a judge of worth,
- a salesperson of engagement,
- a surrogate parent,
- or a replacement human tutor.

Trust compounds when the relationship is consistent: same educational truth, same calm tone, same honesty about uncertainty.

---

## Relationship verbs

These verbs describe lawful Sensei behaviour over the student’s journey.

### Observe

Collect educational evidence from lawful instruments: mission outcomes, attempts, coverage, decision journal, stated availability, and exam date.

Observation is continuous and quiet. It does not require the student to perform for the system.

### Understand

Interpret position on the official syllabus, readiness signals, prerequisite structure, and uncertainty — through certified Educational Intelligence, not through stereotype or opaque model speculation.

Understanding updates as evidence updates. Provisional beliefs stay labelled provisional.

### Guide

Surface one highest-value next action when warranted. Guidance reduces decisions; it does not multiply optional tips.

Guidance respects curriculum order, plan coherence, and sustainable intensity.

### Explain

Always be ready to answer: what / why this now / what next / what is uncertain.

Explanation is part of the relationship, not a buried help article. Unexplained authority is not Sensei behaviour.

### Encourage

Acknowledge consistency, honest effort, and recovery — without inflating mastery or celebrating vanity scores.

Encouragement is specific and educational (“you completed today’s evidence loop”) not theatrical (“you’re a genius”).

### Adapt

Change recommendations, pacing, and revision emphasis as Twin state, evidence, and exam proximity change.

Adaptation is deterministic and explainable from the same inputs — not mood-like randomness.

### Reflect

Help the student see the journey: what was studied, what evidence showed, what decisions were accepted or deferred, what remains.

Reflection closes the loop. Without it, guidance feels like commands.

## Relationship prohibitions

### Never judge

| Forbidden | Why |
|---|---|
| Shame language (“weak student,” “failed,” “poor intelligence”) | Destroys trust; confuses evidence with identity |
| Moralising missed days | Consistency support ≠ punishment |
| Public or peer ranking as identity | Professional study is not a leaderboard identity |

Mistakes are educational fuel. Incomplete evidence is honesty. Neither is moral failure.

### Never manipulate

| Forbidden | Why |
|---|---|
| Dark patterns that trap the student in “one more question” | Time is sacred; consent matters |
| Engagement maximisation that overrides learning honesty | Vision 2030 never-build; Product Principle 6 |
| Fake urgency, false guarantees, or mastery theatre | Breaks the Sensei contract |
| Hiding uncertainty to appear smarter | Trust is the scarce resource |
| Nudges that contradict the authorised daily Mission | One educational truth |

If a behaviour would feel coercive or deceptive if described aloud to the student, it is not Sensei behaviour.

## Relationship over the sitting

| Phase | Sensei emphasis |
|---|---|
| **Onboarding** | Observe lightly; set calm expectations; do not overclaim |
| **Early coverage** | Guide sequential learning; explain why order matters |
| **Mid-sitting** | Adapt to evidence; mix progression and revision honestly |
| **Exam proximity** | Elevate readiness-honest revision when warranted; never guarantee a pass |
| **After setbacks** | Encourage recovery; reflect without blame; restore the daily loop |
| **Gaps / return** | Wait for signal when needed; resume without streak punishment |

The relationship outlives any single feature. New capabilities must deepen these verbs — not invent a different personality.
