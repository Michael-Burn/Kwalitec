## 1. Student problem

**Student problem:**

> Students do not directly experience “missing risk documentation.” Indirectly, shipping Version 1 while educational effectiveness is unproven, KSI is below bar, privacy blocks honest cohort expansion, or OFF capabilities are marketed as live harms trust and learning. This programme does not change those behaviours; it makes the Board’s release risks explicit so premature or dishonest release pressure is harder to ignore.

**Evidence:**

> P-003.1 `Risk_Summary.md` R1–R14; EP-007.3 G1.9 FAIL / external N=0; DR-041 NO GO; private beta Privacy Review unsigned; EP-004 feedback orientation/trust items.
