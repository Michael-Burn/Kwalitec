# Decision Journal Philosophy

**Programme:** ILE-002 — Decision Journal  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active — permanent educational memory standard  
**Effective:** 2026-07-28  
**Authority:** Product educational continuity (subordinate to Vision 2030, Educational Constitution, ILE-010 Sensei philosophy, ILE-011 Decision Framework, ILE-001C0 Communication Framework, P-001.2 Explainability)  

---

## Purpose

The Decision Journal is the learner’s **persistent educational memory**.

It records significant educational guidance, the evidence available at the time, learner choices, and later outcomes — so the Study Sensei can remember previous guidance and reflect on it honestly.

It is **not** telemetry.  
It is **not** analytics.  
It is an **educational narrative** that strengthens trust through continuity.

---

## Educational goal

Students should feel that the Study Sensei remembers their journey.

Recommendations become conversations over time rather than isolated events. Guidance becomes cumulative.

---

## What belongs in the journal

Each entry represents one **significant educational interaction**, for example:

- Mission recommendation  
- Quick Check recommendation  
- Revision recommendation  
- Recovery recommendation  
- Learning milestone  
- Educational reflection  

Routine micro-events (individual item answers, page views, click telemetry) do **not** belong here.

---

## Hard rules

1. **Encourage reflection** — close the loop with calm, honest language.  
2. **Never shame** — deferred or dismissed guidance is a lawful choice.  
3. **Never criticise** — outcomes describe what happened, not who the student is.  
4. **Never rewrite history** — original observation / meaning / recommendation stay fixed.  
5. **Never remove previous guidance** — archive is soft; entries remain readable.  
6. **If evidence changes, append** — new evidence is an event, not an edit.  
7. **Never expose internals** — no Twin, ranking, warrants, mastery scores, or pipeline jargon.  
8. **Preference ≠ mastery** — journal writes never mutate Estimated Knowledge or Educational Evidence of understanding (Constitution Art. V §2).

---

## Explainability permanence

Every entry preserves the educational arc:

| Layer | Student question |
|---|---|
| Observation | What happened? |
| Meaning | Why? |
| Recommendation | What was suggested? |
| Uncertainty | What remained uncertain? |
| Expected benefit | Why bother? |
| Student action | What did I choose? |
| Outcome | What happened afterwards? |
| Reflection | What should I learn from this? |

This is permanent educational explainability (P-001.2), not a transient tip card.
