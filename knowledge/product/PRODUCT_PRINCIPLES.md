# Kwalitec Product Principles

**Programme:** ILE-000  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active — decision filter for product and engineering  
**Effective:** 2026-07-28  
**Authority:** Product principles (subordinate to Vision 2030 and Educational Constitution)  

---

## Purpose

These principles evaluate every Intelligent Learning Experience proposal. If a feature conflicts with a principle, change the feature — or amend a higher authority first. Do not silently override.

---

## 1. Evidence before inference

Prefer observed educational evidence (attempts, coverage, mission outcomes, decision journal) over assumed knowledge, demographic stereotypes, or model speculation.

Inference is allowed only when labelled provisional and grounded in a declared evidence model.

---

## 2. Explain every decision

Every material recommendation, readiness signal, mission selection, or interruption must answer, in plain language:

- What should I do?
- Why this, now?
- What happens next?
- What is uncertain?

If it cannot be explained, it must not be shown as authoritative guidance.

---

## 3. Respect uncertainty

Unknown, thin, or conflicting evidence must surface as uncertainty — never as false mastery, exam guarantees, or overconfident scores.

Confidence labels and provisional knowledge estimates are features, not defects.

---

## 4. Quality over quantity

Prefer one high-value next action over many competing tips. Prefer fewer subjects done honestly over many half-supported catalogues.

Ship depth for supported syllabuses before breadth that dilutes trust.

---

## 5. Student trust first

Trust is the scarce resource. Break trust once (dual truths, overclaim, opaque AI, broken promises) and educational usefulness collapses.

When speed and honesty conflict, choose honesty.

---

## 6. Never optimise for engagement at the expense of learning

Do not maximise time-on-site, streak vanity, notification pressure, or gamification that distracts from study.

Activity is instrumentation; learning is success.

---

## 7. Educational honesty

Language must match behaviour. Do not market Twin-first adaptivity, Exam Ready guarantees, or “AI tutor” capabilities the product does not lawfully deliver.

Coverage ≠ understanding. Studied ≠ mastered.

---

## 8. Long-term mastery over short-term scores

Optimise for durable understanding, revision timing, and sustainable pacing toward exam competence — not for inflating daily completion percentages or short-term quiz scores.

---

## 9. Curriculum first

Official syllabus structure is the primary organising truth of study. Personalisation adapts *within* lawful curriculum constraints; it does not invent a private syllabus.

---

## 10. Deterministic cores, optional AI prose

Planning, readiness, ranking, and educational decisions remain deterministic and reproducible from the same inputs.

AI (including future LLM adapters) may improve explanation prose only behind ports that cannot invent educational authority.

---

## 11. One educational truth

One Educational State, one student runtime story, one navigation. No parallel “shadow” recommendations that contradict Home.

---

## 12. Sustainable progress

Maximise effective learning while reducing burnout. Intensity without recovery is a failure mode, not a premium feature.

---

## 13. Final Test (binding)

Before building:

> Does this help students become better professionals?

If no — do not build it.

---

## How to use

| Situation | Apply |
|---|---|
| Prioritising ILE programmes | Prefer trust, explainability, and daily decision quality over novelty |
| Accepting PRDs | Reject vanity engagement and opaque AI paths |
| Marketing copy | Align claims with validated evidence and Learning Mode honesty |
| Free vs paid | Monetise depth and integrity, not dark patterns |
