## 10. Lessons learned for student value

- **Presentation pass-through can move perceived explainability** without changing educational math — when fields actually render.  
- **Cold-start incomplete schema** is the new minority opacity mode; curing schema-complete Coach is necessary but not sufficient for all nights.  
- **Home readiness drivers** remain a student-visible gap even after MES-05 intent — Analytics ≠ Home.  
- Measurement programmes create student value by **preventing false K8 claims** as much as by clearing gates.
