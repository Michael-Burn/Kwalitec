## How I used it

My failure mode is spending time on the wrong thing. Schema-complete Home with soft-recall why + explicit Next reduces decision time at the start of the evening. Evidence list helps me accept or reject the priority quickly.

Coach aligned with that decision instead of adding a second vague story — less decision noise.

What MES does **not** decide for me: continue vs move on inside a topic, reread vs practise item selection, sitting strategy. Those remain mine.

Cold-start nights still cost decision time because why is generic.

Dual homes still create a meta-decision (“which home?”) that wastes minutes.
