## How I used it

After three weeks I care less about “what is tonight’s topic” and more about “am I getting better?”

Schema-complete Home now explains *why tonight* with practice soft-recall and evidence. That helps me see a **local** improvement signal: the system noticed weak recall. Review point (“reassess after tonight”) is the first Coach-adjacent cue that tomorrow might differ because of tonight.

Coach now carries that why/next instead of generic highest-value speech — better for “what to improve next.”

Journey still feels like a progress bar, not improvement feedback. Readiness on Home still does not list named drivers; Analytics is where drivers become unpackable. I would need Analytics habits to judge exam preparation — Home alone is not enough for three-month confidence.
