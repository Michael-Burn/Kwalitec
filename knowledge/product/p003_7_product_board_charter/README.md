## Purpose

Canonical Product Board Charter for Version 1.

After reading this folder, a new Product Board member should understand:

- **what** the Board governs (and what it does not),  
- **how** it reaches decisions,  
- **how** evidence is evaluated,  
- **how** releases are approved (GO / NO GO),  

without needing tribal knowledge — and without reading the rest of the repository first. Deep law and registers remain cited for execution.

**Operating model note (GP-001):** Kwalitec is founder-operated. Board roles are capacities currently held by the Founder. See `../gp001_founder_governance_model/`. Product Board authority and evidence standards are unchanged.

## Board control statement

> Only the Product Board may recommend Version 1 production-ready **GO** or **NO GO**. As of 2026-07-26 that recommendation remains **NO GO** (DR-041). This Charter defines procedure; it does not rewrite gates, invent external evidence, or activate runtime features.

## Quick start (new Board member)

1. Read [`PRODUCT_BOARD_CHARTER.md`](PRODUCT_BOARD_CHARTER.md) end-to-end (~20 minutes).  
2. Skim your role in [`BOARD_ROLES_AND_RESPONSIBILITIES.md`](BOARD_ROLES_AND_RESPONSIBILITIES.md).  
3. For the next decision: [`DECISION_PROCESS.md`](DECISION_PROCESS.md).  
4. For “can we say X?”: [`EVIDENCE_REVIEW_PROCESS.md`](EVIDENCE_REVIEW_PROCESS.md) → then P-003.5 claim tree.  
5. For release: [`RELEASE_DECISION_PROCESS.md`](RELEASE_DECISION_PROCESS.md) → confirm dossier still **NO GO** until evidence clears blockers.
