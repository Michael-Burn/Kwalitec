## 10. Lessons learned for student value

- Student harm from governance failure is usually **overclaim**, not missing meeting notes — the Charter’s highest-leverage rule is exclusive Board GO/NO GO authority bound to hard gates.  
- Process maturity (Board Level 3) can coexist with Release Readiness Red; students still need evidence programmes, not more ceremony alone.  
- Docs-only Charter work correctly scores ΔKSI = 0; claiming student-value movement from packaging would itself violate claim honesty.
