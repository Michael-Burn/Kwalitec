# Student Reflection Model

**Programme:** ILE-005 — Educational Feedback Loop  

---

## Purpose

Invite learners to briefly judge whether Study Sensei guidance was educationally useful — without coercion, shame, or engagement scoring.

Aligned with ILE-003 [Student Reflection Principles](../ILE-003/STUDENT_REFLECTION_PRINCIPLES.md).

---

## Principles

1. **Optional** — skip any or all questions.
2. **Invite, don’t lead** — no implied “correct” answer.
3. **No shame** — deferred or quiet periods remain lawful.
4. **Evidence-bound** — questions attach to a real journal recommendation.
5. **Professional tone** — calm, adult, syllabus-serious.
6. **Not engagement** — answers never feed streaks, points, or retention metrics.

---

## Prompt set

| Id | Question |
|---|---|
| `helped` | Did this recommendation help? |
| `timing` | Was the timing appropriate? |
| `understood_why` | Did you understand why it was recommended? |
| `same_decision` | Would you make the same decision again? |

**Answers:** Yes / Mostly / No / Prefer not to say.

Optional free-text: “Anything else worth remembering?”

---

## Persistence

- Stored on the Decision Journal entry via `record_reflection` (structured note).
- An append-only evidence line notes that reflection was recorded.
- Triggers an internal Sensei educational review refresh (not learner-visible).

---

## Surface

Decision Journal entries that can still accept reflection show an **Optional reflection** form.

Mission completion does not force reflection; learners may reflect later from the journal.

---

## Disallowed

- “Why didn’t you…?”
- Score- or streak-shaming
- Mandatory reflection gates on Home CTAs
- Publishing Sensei self-reflection text to the learner
