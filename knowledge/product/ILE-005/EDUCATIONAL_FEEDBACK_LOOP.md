# Educational Feedback Loop

**Programme:** ILE-005  
**Status:** Active  
**Effective:** 2026-07-28  

---

## Purpose

Evaluate the educational effectiveness of Study Sensei guidance over time.

The Study Sensei should become better at understanding whether its own educational guidance was helpful. The objective is **educational calibration**, not behavioural optimisation.

This capability **does not** change recommendation selection. It measures and reflects on recommendation outcomes.

---

## Feedback model

For each significant recommendation record:

```
Recommendation
    ↓
Student response
    ↓
Educational outcome
    ↓
Reflection
    ↓
Evidence quality
    ↓
Recommendation review
```

---

## Principles

**Never optimise for:** engagement, clicks, streaks, screen time.

**Always optimise for:** educational usefulness, learner confidence, decision quality, evidence quality, professional judgement.

---

## Recommendation review states

Each recommendation should eventually reach one of:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Supported by later evidence | Outcome and reflection support the original guidance |
| Partially supported | Mixed or incomplete educational signals |
| Inconclusive | Signals present but not decisive |
| Evidence insufficient | Too little educational evidence to judge |
| Requires future observation | Response / outcome / reflection still pending |

**Never rewrite history.** Append educational evidence only.

---

## Student reflection

Where appropriate, learners may answer brief optional questions:

- Did this recommendation help?
- Was the timing appropriate?
- Did you understand why it was recommended?
- Would you make the same decision again?

Reflection remains **optional**. Skipping is lawful and never shamed.

---

## Sensei self-reflection

Internal educational review records follow:

```
Observation
    ↓
Original recommendation
    ↓
Later evidence
    ↓
Educational assessment
    ↓
Future learning
```

These records are **not** exposed directly to learners. They improve educational quality governance. They do not create a second recommendation engine.
