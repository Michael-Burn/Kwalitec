## Purpose

Document **explicit product non-goals** so roadmap debates can reject tempting but identity-breaking work quickly.

Non-goals are not insults to adjacent products. They are boundaries that keep Kwalitec a Study Sensei — a guidance system — rather than a content warehouse, social product, or engagement engine.

If a proposal requires amending a non-goal, escalate to Product Board / higher authority first. Do not silently override.

---

## Explicit non-goals

### Not a question bank

Kwalitec may use practice items as **instruments** for evidence. It does not succeed by hosting the largest inventory of questions, nor by equating “more questions done” with product value.

**Reject:** Roadmaps whose primary KPI is item count or practice volume without guidance quality.

### Not a social network

Study groups and peer learning can be valuable *outside* or adjacent to Kwalitec. The product identity is not feeds, follows, likes, public ranking, or social graphs.

**Reject:** Features that optimise for social engagement rather than educational decisions.

### Not an exam simulator (as default identity)

High-stakes exam simulation — if ever productised — must be a **separate, explicitly consented** experience. It is not the default skin of Adaptive Assessment, Missions, or daily guidance.

**Reject:** Invigilation chrome, pass/fail theatre, or “exam mode” as the ordinary study relationship.

### Not an engagement-maximising platform

Time-on-site, streak vanity, notification pressure, and gamification that distract from learning are Vision 2030 never-builds and Product Principle violations.

**Reject:** Features justified primarily by retention metrics that conflict with learning honesty or sustainable intensity.

### Not a generic chatbot

Conversational AI is not the educational brain. Chat that invents syllabus truth, mastery, or next actions is forbidden as authority.

**Reject:** “AI tutor chat” as a second recommendation path that bypasses Twin / Reasoning / Mission ownership.

### Not a replacement for professional educational material

Textbooks, institute materials, provider courses, notes, and videos remain first-class learning resources. Kwalitec amplifies them through guidance ([`GUIDANCE_OVER_CONTENT.md`](GUIDANCE_OVER_CONTENT.md)).

**Reject:** Strategies that require displacing professional content ecosystems to “own the student.”

### Not a content marketplace or UGC library (core identity)

User-generated or marketplace content may appear in future adjacent offerings only if they do not redefine the core as a content store or undermine curriculum-first truth.

**Reject:** Making content publishing the north-star business model of the Sensei product.

### Not a judgement engine

Kwalitec does not rank student worth, shame performance, or moralise missed study.

**Reject:** Copy, UX, or metrics that treat learners as winners/losers rather than evidence-bearing candidates.

### Not a black-box personalisation product

Opaque scores and unexplained adaptivity are incompatible with Sensei trust and Explainability law.

**Reject:** “Trust the model” surfaces that cannot answer what / why / what next / what is uncertain.

## Clarifying: what non-goals do *not* forbid

| Non-goal | Still allowed when lawful |
|---|---|
| Not a question bank | Short learning checks and curriculum-bound practice as evidence instruments |
| Not a social network | Optional export of “today’s focus” for offline study-group use |
| Not an exam simulator | Explicit, consented readiness / mock experiences separate from daily Sensei skin |
| Not engagement-max | Retention via trust, usefulness, and completed Missions |
| Not a chatbot | Tutor *explanation* of already-decided guidance; future prose adapters behind ports |
| Not content replacement | Citations, deep links, and “use your materials for Topic X” |
