## 1. Student problem

**Student problem:**

> Students (and Product) cannot know whether clearer explanations, readiness communication, and a coherent journey actually help them study more consistently and feel better prepared over weeks. Without a Stage 1 cohort, the product risks shipping “perception success” as if it were educational effectiveness — which would break trust when study habits do not change.

**Evidence:**

> EP-004 Week 0 external N=0; effectiveness NO-GO; EP-005.2 REM-07; EP-006.3 / EP-006.5 / EP-007.2 perception Pass with explicit “not effectiveness GO” residuals; Gate G1.9 FAIL.

**Student benefit summary:**

**Final Test:** Does this help students become better professionals? **Indirectly yes** — honest measurement discipline is a professional-exam companion requirement; overclaim harms learners.

## 10. Lessons learned for student value

- Perception Pass (explainability, readiness, journey) is necessary but **not sufficient** for educational-effectiveness claims.  
- The binding student-value blocker for G1.9 is **ops/privacy**, not missing Runtime A algorithms.  
- Completing a Stage 1 *design* without invites must be labelled clearly so boards do not confuse programme completion with cohort completion.  
- Next student-value move is invite + measure — not another Tier B pack for G1.9.
