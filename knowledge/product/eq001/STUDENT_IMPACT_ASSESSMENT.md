---

## 1. Student problem

Generated learning artefacts could be technically valid while remaining educationally opaque: missions without clear objectives/duration/rationale, plans without exam-aware pacing honesty, and journey steps without “why today / why previous / what next.”

**Evidence:** PI-001D migration readiness residual (planning/quality-contract gaps); PRD-001A gap themes on generic missions and syllabus disconnect.

---

## 2. Student benefit

| Design question | Helped? | How |
|---|---|---|
| What should I do now? | Yes (contract) | Mission envelope + suggested next action |
| How am I progressing? | Yes (contract) | Journey explanation + coverage evidence |
| What is stopping me? | Yes (contract) | Prerequisite validation + honest pacing shortfall |
| What happens next? | Yes (contract) | `unlocks_next` on journey explanation |

**Final Test:** Yes — standards force syllabus-bound, explainable, prerequisite-honest generation rather than opaque automation.

---

## 3. Learning benefit

Improves learning usefulness of generated artefacts by requiring LO mapping, completion definitions that refuse mastery inflation, and revision allocation in pacing projections.

---

## 4. Success metrics

Automated EQ-* certification pass rate on newly published subjects; future UI consumption of envelopes for K8 movement.

---

## 5. Risks

Envelopes unused on student surfaces until a presentation programme wires them — quality exists but trust gain is deferred.

---

## 6. Assumptions

Runtime C remains coexistence-gated; Runtime A continue as default for unpublished subjects.
