# Tone of Voice

**Programme:** ILE-001C0 — Study Sensei Communication Framework  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active — permanent voice standard  
**Effective:** 2026-07-28  
**Companion:** [`STUDY_SENSEI_COMMUNICATION_FRAMEWORK.md`](STUDY_SENSEI_COMMUNICATION_FRAMEWORK.md)  

---

## Purpose

Describe **how the Study Sensei sounds** — the register that makes every feature feel like one trusted guide.

Tone is not decoration. It is trust infrastructure.

---

## Core attributes

| Attribute | Meaning in practice |
|---|---|
| **Professional** | Speaks like a serious companion for professional exams — competent, restrained, syllabus-aware |
| **Calm** | Steady under pressure; no alarmism near exam dates; no panic chrome |
| **Warm** | Human and considerate; never cold bureaucracy; never fake intimacy |
| **Respectful** | Treats the learner as an adult professional; no condescension, no parent voice |
| **Precise** | Exact nouns; specific educational meaning; no fluffy filler |
| **Quiet confidence** | Authority from evidence and curriculum, not volume or bravado |

Together: **quiet professional warmth** — not cheerleader, not drill sergeant, not chatbot.

---

## What quiet confidence sounds like

- Short sentences that state the situation and the next step.
- Confidence adjectives reserved for High / Reliable levels only.
- Uncertainty named plainly when present.
- No need to “sound smart” with jargon.

**Example:** “Today’s focus is reinforcing Topic X. Recent practice looks fragile, and it blocks the next syllabus step.”

---

## Never theatrical

Avoid:

- Exclamation-mark campaigns  
- Dramatic countdown language  
- Sticker-like praise bursts  
- Storytime metaphors that obscure the educational point  

The Sensei does not perform.

---

## Never motivational hype

Avoid:

- “Crush it!” / “You’ve got this!” as primary guidance  
- Destiny claims (“this will guarantee your pass”)  
- Empty pep talks without evidence  

Motivation, if any, is a side effect of clarity — not a content genre.

---

## Never robotic

Avoid:

- Template stacks that feel machine-generated without care (“Error: null recommendation”)  
- Cold system status as the only voice (“Processing complete”)  
- Over-formal legalese in daily study loops  

Robotic ≠ precise. Precise can still be human: “We don’t have enough yet to recommend a focus.”

---

## Register by context

| Context | Tone nuance |
|---|---|
| Daily Mission / primary tip | Direct, one action, brief why |
| Quick Check / Adaptive Assessment | Orienting, non-exam, anxiety-safe |
| Uncertainty / silence | Honest, short, non-anxious |
| Challenge / calibration | Matter-of-fact, protective — never “gotcha” |
| Encouragement / recovery | Warm, specific, low volume |
| Pre-exam readiness | Bounded honesty; no false reassurance |
| Tutor narration (if present) | Explains authorised decisions; never invents ranking |

---

## Vocabulary preferences

| Prefer | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Evidence, focus, reinforce, provisional, still unsure | Fail, weak student, crush, doom, guaranteed |
| Today’s Mission, Quick Check, readiness guidance | Pop quiz, mock exam (on AA surfaces), destiny language |
| Let’s gather a little more | Prove yourself / test yourself (as threat) |
| Suggested / useful focus | You must / or else |

Platform noun standards (Mission, Study Plan, etc.) remain governed by existing terminology packs. Adaptive Assessment additionally obeys ILE-001 forbidden terms.

---

## Length and rhythm

- Lead with the actionable or the honest status.  
- One supporting sentence for why.  
- Optional third sentence for uncertainty or benefit.  
- Prefer paragraphs of 1–3 short sentences over walls of coach prose.

---

## Voice checklist

Before approving copy:

1. Professional, calm, warm, respectful, precise?  
2. Quiet confidence — or theatre / hype / robot?  
3. Same Sensei as Mission and Insights?  
4. Matches evidence strength?
