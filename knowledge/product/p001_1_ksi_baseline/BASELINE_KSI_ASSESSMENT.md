### 3.1 Scoring rationales

**K1 Planning usefulness — 62 (Partial)**  
Near-universal positive signal that Learning Workspace Dashboard → Session can act as a clear “what to study tonight” director. Counterweights: dual homes / dual start paths, same-day duration mismatch (commonly 30 vs 90), and thin Home → Start Session overview. Strong enough to sit mid-Partial, not Strong.

**K2 Recommendation usefulness — 48 (Weak)**  
Coach / Insight guidance frequently restates “highest-value / learning evidence” language without showing working; recommendation engine effectiveness remains excluded from validated claims. Students often retain external stacks. Below 50 — a Version 1 pillar risk under V1-K2 if not improved.

**K3 Readiness usefulness — 52 (Partial)**  
Honest empty readiness is sometimes trusted; filled composites are often unpackable or anxiety-inducing; overconfidence risk noted for resitters. Twin existence ≠ perceived usefulness. Barely Partial — integrity preserved more than usefulness delivered.

**K4 Personalisation — 55 (Partial)**  
Syllabus-bound missions and Twin foundation exist architecturally; reviewers often did not perceive durable adaptation after poor practice or missed days. Personalisation feels closer to structured default than evidenced individualisation.

**K5 Motivation — 60 (Partial)**  
Director path and session structure support consistency for some personas; dual-home friction, duration distrust, and thin overviews sap motivation. Habit can form without belief in adaptive learning. Mid-Partial.

**K6 Learning analytics — 50 (Partial)**  
Journey / analytics surfaces exist for orientation; quantitative progress claims provisional; reviewers struggle to treat analytics as decision-grade learning evidence. Boundary with vanity risk watched.

**K7 Revision support — 58 (Partial)**  
Version 1 Revision Workspace ships per Blueprint; advanced revision optimisation deferred. Deliberate-practice / exam-transfer questions remain weakly answered in blind reviews. Partial, not a primary strength.

**K8 Explainability — 55 (Partial)**  
Constitutional law is strong; student-perceived explainability is not. Near-universal Coach opacity themes and unpackable readiness keep this below the Version 1 explainability floor (V1-K3 requires ≥ 70). Largest constitutional + usefulness tension.

## 5. Recommended priority order for improvement

### P0 — Unlock Version 1 floors (must move first)

1. **Explainability (K8)** — Make every Coach / Insight / readiness narrative show working (inputs → reason → next action). Eliminate dual conflicting “today” speech. Target: K8 ≥ 70.
2. **Recommendation usefulness (K2)** — Align guidance with authorised Today’s Session; reduce generic restatement; instrument acceptance when PRD allows. Target: K2 ≥ 50 immediately, then → 70+.
3. **Planning coherence (K1)** — Resolve duration mismatch and dual-home hesitation so the director path is the single trusted plan. Target: K1 ≥ 70.

### P1 — Honesty and interpretation

4. **Readiness usefulness (K3)** — Prefer interpretable, evidence-bound readiness over calm composites; keep unknown as unknown. Target: K3 ≥ 65, then 70+.
5. **Learning analytics (K6)** — Decision-grade progress (Journey emit / honest velocity) without vanity metrics. Target: K6 ≥ 65.

### P2 — Depth for exam companion promise

6. **Personalisation (K4)** — Evidenced adaptation after weak practice / missed days (Twin-honest).
7. **Revision support (K7)** — Intelligent revision loops beyond Workspace presence.
8. **Motivation (K5)** — Treat primarily as outcome of P0–P1; avoid gamification shortcuts.
