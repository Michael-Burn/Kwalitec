## Recommendation

---

## Board question

> Would we recommend that the founder begins preparing for CS1 exclusively through Kwalitec?

**Answer:** **Yes — as the exclusive daily study operating system**, provided the constraints below are accepted.  
**Not yet:** as the exclusive source of exam-grade CS1 practice content.

---

## Evidence for GO WITH CONSTRAINTS

1. **Daily loop is intact.** Open → understand mission → start → complete session → know tomorrow → return works under sole runtime (CQ-002–CQ-005).
2. **Critical habit/start blockers closed.** Auto-begin, single primary CTA, Continue resume without re-commitment, topic-threaded session copy, guidance why continuity, premium craft stylesheet fixes (CQ-002–CQ-006).
3. **No Critical or Major adoption blockers remain** that would stop daily use ([`FOUNDER_ADOPTION_BLOCKERS.md`](FOUNDER_ADOPTION_BLOCKERS.md)).
4. **Operational readiness ~67 (Ready band)** for personal founder use ([`OPERATIONAL_READINESS_BOARD.md`](OPERATIONAL_READINESS_BOARD.md)).
5. **Production posture matches the audited path** (`KWALITEC_V2_SOLE_RUNTIME=1` on Render).

## Why not 🟢 GO (unqualified)

1. **Practice substance is scaffolded**, not authored CS1 item banks — exclusive *content* preparation would overclaim CR4/CR8.
2. **Engineering CRI 53% remains provisional Emerging** across CR1–CR6; Strong-band needs Founder Validation.
3. **Evidence Confidence (CR8) and Commercial Envelope (CR9)** remain Weak/Broken — correct for invite-only, but they forbid unqualified commercial “ready” claims.

## Why not 🔴 NO GO

1. No Critical/Major engineering blocker stops daily OS use.
2. Interrupt recovery, session reliability, and next-day return are Ready-band.
3. Remaining gaps are Constraints or Minor polish — acceptable under documented limitations.
