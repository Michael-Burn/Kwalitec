## 4. Re-score gate

A domain may receive a Founder Validated score when:

1. At least one real session log cites that domain with concrete behaviour, **or**  
2. Weekly summary aggregates repeated session signals for that domain, **and**  
3. The score change cites log IDs — not CQ engineering artefacts alone.

Composite Founder Validated CRI uses the same weighted formula as Engineering CRI once domains are scored. Until then, report **0% Open**.
