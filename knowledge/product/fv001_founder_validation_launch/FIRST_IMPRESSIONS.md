# FV-001A — First Impressions

**Date:** 2026-07-28  
**Persona:** First-time founder, preparing CS1, zero prior Kwalitec use  

---

## Opening moment (0–30 seconds)

The first screen is **Sign in**, not a marketing site. Left panel: **Kwalitec** as an “Education Operating System” that helps you “know exactly what to study next,” with Internal Alpha / Founding Cohort badges and six concrete study benefits. Right panel: email, password, Remember me, Sign in.

**Gut feel:** This is a serious study product for hard exams. It does **not** look like a “upload your syllabus and stand up a new subject” tool.

---

## Confidence

| Question | Immediate answer | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| What is Kwalitec? | A study OS that tells me what to do next | Medium |
| Who is it for? | Exam candidates (IFoA-ish from later screens) | Medium |
| What should I do first? | Sign in — but I have no account | Low |
| Would I continue? | Yes *if* I had an invite; otherwise stop | Low–Medium |

---

## Confusion spikes

1. **Where do I create an account?** — Nowhere. Coordinator only.  
2. **After login I’m in a Console full of “Platform Health 0%”** — feels like something is broken, not like day-one setup.  
3. **Am I a student or an operator?** — Both surfaces exist; the product does not greet me as a founder preparing CS1.  
4. **Study Sensei** — sounds like a person/coach; later there is no chat.  
5. **Mission says “this topic”** — I still don’t know *which* CS1 topic.

---

## Emotional arc

| Moment | Feeling |
|---|---|
| Landing | Curious, mildly impressed |
| No registration | Frustrated / locked out |
| Console Home | Overwhelmed, slightly alarmed (0% health) |
| Curriculum Studio empty state | Relieved — clear next step |
| Create Subject success | Competent |
| Upload Failed / 0 nodes | Trust drop |
| Student Mission generic | Doubt (“is this real CS1?”) |
| Session free-text prompt | Disappointed vs actuarial expectation |
| Revision empty honesty | Respect |
| Help instead of Coach | Let down |

---

## Would I continue?

- **As invited Internal Alpha student on a known paper:** Maybe — Mission → Start Session is obvious.  
- **As founder creating CS1 from zero PDFs today:** No — upload/review/publish loop failed to produce a trustworthy curriculum, and study copy stayed generic.

---

## Launch blockers felt in the first hour

1. No account without coordinator.  
2. Console does not lead with “prepare your first subject.”  
3. Uploaded documents do not become a curriculum I can trust.  
4. Studying does not name CS1 topics I recognise.

---

**End of First Impressions**
