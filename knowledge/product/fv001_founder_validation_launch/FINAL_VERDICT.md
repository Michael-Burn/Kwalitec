## Why NO-GO (evidence-only)

1. **Account creation stops the journey** unless a coordinator already provided credentials.  
2. **Curriculum from upload does not become student-ready** in this walkthrough: documents show Failed; preview reports 0 nodes; publish is refused.

5. **Coach as an askable mentor** is not present—Help & Support is orientation and tickets, not Q&A coaching.
