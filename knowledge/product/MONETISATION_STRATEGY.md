# Monetisation Strategy

**Programme:** ILE-000  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active — commercial direction (not a live price list)  
**Effective:** 2026-07-28  

---

## Philosophy

Kwalitec monetises **trustworthy educational usefulness** for high-stakes professional exams.

Pricing must reinforce product principles: no dark-pattern engagement, no paywalling of honesty, no premium “guaranteed pass” claims.

Premium positioning is earned by syllabus fidelity, explainable guidance, and calm daily decision support — not by content volume alone.

---

## Subscription philosophy

| Principle | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Outcome-aligned** | Charge for continuous companion value across an exam cycle, not one-off downloads |
| **Transparent** | Clear what is free vs paid; no bait-and-switch intelligence claims |
| **Sitting-aware** | Packaging should respect exam sitting calendars and multi-month study arcs |
| **Fair upgrades** | Paid tiers deepen guidance and subjects; they do not unlock “secret” educational truths that free users are lied about |

Default commercial motion for individuals: **subscription** (monthly / sitting / annual options to be decided in a later commercial programme).

---

## Premium positioning

Kwalitec is a **premium professional tool**, comparable in seriousness to actuarial study materials and coaching — not a freemium consumer game.

Premium signals:

- Curriculum-bound plans and missions
- Explainable next actions and readiness
- Educational state continuity across the sitting
- Operational reliability and privacy seriousness

Premium does **not** mean:

- Opaque AI upsells
- Artificial scarcity of basic progress honesty
- Gamified status symbols

---

## Free vs paid (directional)

Exact SKUs are deferred; directional boundaries:

| Likely free / limited | Likely paid |
|---|---|
| Orientation, supported-subject honesty, limited trial of daily loop | Full exam-cycle companion for supported subjects |
| Public marketing honesty about Learning Mode | Multi-subject / multi-sitting continuity |
| Foundational explainability (never sell “truth”) | Deeper analytics timeline, collaboration, institution features |

**Hard rule:** Educational honesty and uncertainty labelling are not paywalled. Paid tiers must not create a second, contradictory educational story.

---

## Institution licensing

| Model | Description |
|---|---|
| **Seat / cohort licence** | Providers or employers sponsor N candidates for named subjects/sittings |
| **Curriculum partnership** | Co-publishing or validated packs for provider syllabuses mapped to professional bodies |
| **Success-aligned options (later)** | Explore carefully; never invent pass guarantees as contract terms |

Institution deals require privacy DPAs, admin reporting that respects student data ownership, and no dual-runtime educational forks.

---

## Future enterprise offerings

Indicative (post-ILE foundation):

- Cohort operational health (completion, burnout flags, syllabus progress) without exposing private attempt detail unlawfully
- Curriculum Studio / publishing workflows for partner subjects
- SSO and provisioning
- Exportable audit of recommendation explainability for compliance-minded buyers

Enterprise must remain subordinate to student Educational State integrity.

---

## Pricing principles

1. **Value before volume** — price depth of supported experience, not feature count.
2. **Willingness follows trust** — raise prices only as KSI, retention, and effectiveness evidence improve.
3. **No exam-guarantee pricing** — never sell pass insurance.
4. **Sustainable unit economics** — curriculum publishing and support cost scale with subjects; do not underprice infinite catalogue promises.
5. **Geography sensitivity later** — PPP or regional packs only after beachhead proof.
6. **Founder-governed commercial claims** — marketing claims follow EVF / release honesty gates.

---

## Commercial sequencing

```
Private beta / Stage 1 (invite) → Individual premium (IFoA beachhead)
        → Provider/employer seats
        → Adjacent bodies
        → Broader enterprise suite
```

Do not force enterprise packaging before Stage 1 privacy and effectiveness gates close.
