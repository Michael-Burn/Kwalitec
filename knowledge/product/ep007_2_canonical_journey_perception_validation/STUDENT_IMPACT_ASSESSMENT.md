## 1. Student problem

**Student problem:**

> Students faced two homes and conflicting session clocks (often 30 vs 90 minutes), which reintroduced the decision fatigue the product claims to remove. EP-007.1 fixed the journey structurally; Product could not honestly claim planning-usefulness gains without measuring whether students perceive clearer entry, start, duration, continuity, and lower cognitive load.

**Evidence:**

> EP-004 Meta Analysis V2 (dual homes Near Universal; duration Universal); EP-005.1 K1 **68** capped by EV-PLAN-006; EP-005.2 REM-02 / REM-03; EP-006.3 / EP-006.5 dual-home residuals; EP-007.1 estimated K1 +4 pending Tier B.

## 10. Lessons learned for student value

- **Dual-home is an educational reliability failure**, not a navigation preference — clearing it unlocks planning trust.  
- **One duration fact** is a weeknight survival feature (SV-002), not a polish item.  
- **Cognitive load falls when reconciliation ends** — organisational lightness precedes habit claims.  
- Measurement programmes create student value by **preventing false Strong-band K1 claims** as much as by raising scores.
