## 1. Student problem

**Student problem:**

> Tonight’s guidance often feels opaque. The product “knows” why a topic, plan, or readiness estimate was chosen, but students on the main Home/Coach path cannot see the working — evidence, confidence basis, next step, or when to reassess — so they cannot trust or question the advice like a serious professional would.

**Evidence:**

> EP-005.1 validated K8 **65** (below floor 70); EP-005.2 RC-01 / REM-01 (MES pipeline drop); EP-004 blind corpus Coach opacity Near-Universal; EP-006.1 Traceability Report — Home never binds `explanation_card`; `review_point` unbound on all surfaces; Coach re-narrates from reason codes.

---

## 2. Student benefit

| Design question | Helped? | How |
|---|---|---|
| What should I do now? | Indirect → Yes when implemented | Mandatory L1 next action on Home |
| How am I progressing? | Indirect | Readiness why + drivers unpackability specified |
| What is stopping me? | Yes (diagnostic) | Names exact presentation omissions blocking trust |
| What happens next? | Yes when implemented | Review point + change reasoning disclosure |

**Student benefit summary:**

> This programme does not change the daily loop yet. Benefit is a **precise delivery contract and remediation order** so successors make existing Runtime A explanations visible — students gain inspectability without a new “smarter” brain.

**Final Test:** Does this help students become better professionals? **Indirectly Yes** — professionals justify priorities with evidence; the contract restores that habit in product speech.

## 10. Lessons learned for student value (exit)

> Schema-complete intelligence that students cannot see does not raise validated explainability. The canonical Home currently delivers **less** MES than legacy Dashboard — consolidation without widening DTOs would worsen student trust. Delivery must be pass-through of authored Runtime A fields, with progressive disclosure — not re-narration and hard clips without a Level-2 escape hatch.
