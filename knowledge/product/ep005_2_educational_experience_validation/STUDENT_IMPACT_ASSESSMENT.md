## 1. Student problem

**Student problem:**

> Even after Runtime A “got smarter,” serious students still struggle to trust and use Kwalitec as an educational companion: they cannot always see why tonight’s guidance was chosen, which home/clock is authoritative, what readiness means, or whether the product adapted after a bad session. Validated usefulness (KSI 59) therefore lags Version 1 needs.

**Evidence:**

> EP-005.1 validated KSI 59 / gap 21; EP-004 blind themes (Coach opacity, dual-home, 30-vs-90, readiness unpackability); template inventory showing MES fields unbound; flags OFF for personalisation/feedback.

## 10. Lessons learned for student value (exit)

- **Correct reasoning that students cannot see does not raise KSI.**  
- Dual-home and duration mismatch recreate the decision fatigue the product promises to remove — capping planning value despite schema Pass.  
- Recommendation usefulness is currently **presentation- and trust-limited** more than Decision-Framework-limited.  
- Flag-OFF personalisation programmes cannot close the Version 1 gap on the validated board.  
- G1 needs a **phased experience + evidence** strategy, not another estimated-ΔKSI implementation stack alone.
