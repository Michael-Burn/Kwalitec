## 1. Student problem

**Student problem:**

> Students often do not trust or follow “what to do next” guidance. Tips feel generic (“highest-value / learning evidence”), sometimes conflict with Today’s Mission, and do not clearly improve learning. Recommendation usefulness is the weakest KSI dimension.

**Evidence:**

> KSI baseline K2 = 48 (Weak), below Version 1 floor of no category below 50 (V1-K2). `BASELINE_KSI_ASSESSMENT.md` ranks K2 among largest opportunity areas; Coach / Insight opacity and external-stack retention themes from EP-004 / product validation. Effectiveness marketing remains frozen — usefulness is already scored as weak from qualitative evidence.

## 2. Student benefit

| Design question | Helped? | How |
|---|---|---|
| What should I do now? | Indirect | Decision framework + actionability principle define how primary tips should win |
| How am I progressing? | Indirect | Expected educational impact dimension forbids vanity tips |
| What is stopping me? | Indirect | Priority ladder elevates recovery and blocking deficits |
| What happens next? | Indirect | Review triggers and scorecard require reassessment discipline |

**Student benefit summary:**

> No immediate student-visible change. Establishes the permanent definition of high-quality recommendations, prioritisation law, and review gate so future EP/P work must ship relevant, proportionate, evidence-backed guidance — the prerequisite for raising K2.

**Final Test:** Does this help students become better professionals? **Yes** — professional exam preparation depends on justified study priorities; governance that forbids low-quality tips serves that outcome. Benefit is enabling, not yet experiential.

## 10. Lessons learned for student value

> Recommendation philosophy and constitutional recommendation models already exist, but students still experience weak usefulness (K2 = 48). The gap is enforceable product quality law: principles, dimensions, competing-tip prioritisation, measurable scorecard, and a review gate. Without those, algorithm work risks optimising the wrong objective (engagement, tip volume, or opaque “highest value”). Live K2 gains still wait on implementation.
