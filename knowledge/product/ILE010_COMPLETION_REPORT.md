### Summary

ILE-010 establishes the permanent philosophical foundation that distinguishes Kwalitec as a **trusted Study Sensei** — a guidance companion for learning decisions — rather than a content provider, question bank, exam simulator, social network, engagement engine, or generic AI chatbot. The pack defines the Sensei identity, the guidance-over-content principle, the long-term student relationship model, decision-making principles (suggest / wait / challenge / reassure / revise / progress / admit uncertainty), and explicit non-goals. Documentation only — no production code, architecture, educational reasoning, or UI changes. Future roadmap decisions can be evaluated against these artefacts; every new capability should strengthen guidance rather than content ownership.
