---

## Desired feeling

When a serious candidate opens Kwalitec, they should feel:

| Feeling | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Calm** | The day has one clear path; noise is removed. |
| **Professional** | Tone suits a professional qualification, not a consumer toy. |
| **Premium** | Craft, clarity, and restraint — not chrome for its own sake. |
| **Trustworthy** | What is shown matches what the system believes and can explain. |
| **Encouraging** | Progress and setbacks are framed without shame or judgement. |
| **Never overwhelmed** | Cognitive load falls with every visit, not rises. |

Students should never feel lost, judged, confused, or pressured into unhealthy study.

---

## Design question (every screen)

Every student-facing screen must help answer at least one of:

1. What should I do now?
2. How am I progressing?
3. What is stopping me?
4. What happens next?

If a screen answers none, it does not belong in the primary journey.

---

## Cognitive load doctrine

**Every screen should reduce cognitive load.**

| Do | Do not |
|---|---|
| One primary action | Competing CTAs with equal weight |
| Plain educational language | Internal engine jargon as guidance |
| Progressive disclosure | Dumping Twin/graph internals on Home |
| Honest empty states | Fake completeness or placeholder mastery |
| Stable information architecture | Novelty layouts that retrain the student each release |

---

## Composition principles

- **One job per section** — one purpose, one headline, one short supporting line.
- **Mission owns Next** — supporting insights must not compete with today’s session.
- **Explain in place** — “why” lives next to the decision, not in a buried FAQ.
- **Uncertainty visible** — provisional estimates labelled; confidence never theatrical.
- **Syllabus visible** — students can locate today in the official curriculum without hunting.

---

## Emotional register

| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| Daily Home / Session | Steady coach: clear, brief, respectful |
| Mistakes / recovery | Specific and constructive — never punitive |
| Readiness | Honest and bounded — no false reassurance |
| Empty / early state | Orient and invite evidence — never invent competence |
| Errors / outages | Direct and human — protect trust |

---

## Accessibility and inclusion

Premium does not mean exclusive. Experience must remain usable under real exam-prep conditions: fatigue, limited time, varied devices, and mixed digital literacy.

Prioritise readability, keyboard reachability, and honest loading/empty states over decorative motion.

---

## Motion and polish

Use motion to create presence and hierarchy — not to manufacture engagement. Prefer subtle transitions that confirm completion or guide attention to the next lawful action.

---

## Relationship to brand

Kwalitec presents as an **Education Operating System** companion: serious, syllabus-honest, intelligence-backed. Brand promise must not outrun Learning Mode truth (see Product Principles — Educational honesty).

---

## Non-goals for UX

- Gamification that distracts from learning
- Social feeds or engagement loops as the home experience
- Dashboard sprawl (many cards, equal urgency)
- Aesthetic redesign for its own sake when educational clarity is the gap

---

## Evaluation

A candidate after one week should answer yes to:

1. I know what to do today without deciding from scratch.
2. I understand why the product suggested it.
3. I can see where I am in the syllabus.
4. I do not feel the product is trying to keep me scrolling.
