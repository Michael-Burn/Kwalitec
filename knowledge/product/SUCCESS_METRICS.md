# Success Metrics

**Programme:** ILE-000  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active — product outcome metrics for ILE era  
**Effective:** 2026-07-28  

---

## Purpose

Define measurable outcomes for Intelligent Learning Experience work. Align with Vision 2030 success metrics, EP-003 educational metrics (M1–M9), and KSI categories (K1–K8).

**Avoid vanity metrics:** raw DAU, time-on-site maximisation, notification opens, streak length as success, undifferentiated click counts.

---

## North star (unchanged)

Students who consistently use Kwalitec should have a **materially higher probability of passing** their examinations than students who do not.

Pass-rate lift is measured when lawful cohorts and privacy allow. Until then, leading usefulness metrics and KSI govern claims.

---

## Metric families

### 1. Retention & consistency

| Metric | Intent | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Student retention** | Still active across study weeks toward sitting | Cohort-defined; not vanity DAU |
| **Study-day consistency** | Regular return to complete the daily loop | Align EP-003 consistency metrics |
| **Sitting continuity** | Remains through plan phases (learn → revise) | Drop-off diagnosis over blame |

### 2. Session / mission quality

| Metric | Intent | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Mission / session completion rate** | Started sessions that complete educationally | Domain Mission; UI Session |
| **Session completion quality** | Completions that produce coverage and/or qualifying evidence | Reject checkbox theatre |
| **Time-to-start** | Friction from open → first lawful action | UX load proxy |

### 3. Recommendation & trust

| Metric | Intent | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Recommendation acceptance** | Accept / commit rate for primary next action | Strong-band needs observational Stage data |
| **Recommendation follow-through** | Accepted actions actually started/completed | EP-008.3 family |
| **Trust metrics** | Blind-review / survey trust; “I understand why” | Qualitative + structured |
| **Explainability sufficiency** | Student can answer what/why/next | K8; P-001.2 reviews |

### 4. Learning & calibration

| Metric | Intent | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Learning confidence calibration** | Self-confidence vs evidence-backed estimates | Overconfidence is a risk signal |
| **Time-to-mastery (topic)** | Evidence-backed competence trajectory per topic | Estimated; never self-certified as fact |
| **Revision adherence** | Scheduled revision completed on time | K7 alignment |
| **Readiness signal usefulness** | Students find drivers/confidence actionable | Perception + later predictive accuracy |

### 5. Outcomes (when measurable)

| Metric | Intent | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Exam pass rates** | North-star outcome | Require privacy-safe, consented measurement |
| **Predicted readiness accuracy** | Calibration of readiness vs outcomes | Post-sitting analysis |
| **Educational satisfaction** | Serious candidate NPS/CSAT bounded by honesty | Not a substitute for pass probability |

### 6. Product usefulness index

| Metric | Intent | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **KSI (validated)** | Composite educational usefulness | Version 1 bar ≥ 80; estimated ΔKSI ≠ Gate G1 |
| **K1–K8 category scores** | Diagnose which ILE programmes to fund | P-001.1 framework |

---

## Anti-metrics (do not optimise)

- Maximise session duration regardless of learning  
- Maximise cards/questions answered without evidence quality  
- Maximise push notifications  
- Maximise feature adoption of non-educational chrome  
- Inflate completion by lowering educational standards
