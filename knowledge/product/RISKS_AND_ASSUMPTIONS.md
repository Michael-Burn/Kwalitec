**Programme:** ILE-000  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active

## Purpose

Document educational, commercial, technical, market, and regulatory risks for the Intelligent Learning Experience era — plus assumptions the strategy depends on — with mitigations.

Does not replace the Product Risk Register (P-003.3) or Version 1 Release Dossier; it frames **strategy-level** risk for ILE-000+.

---

## Educational risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Overclaiming intelligence / Exam Ready | Trust collapse; constitutional breach | Claim freezes; EVF; Learning Mode honesty; P-001.2/1.3 reviews |
| Mastery theatre (coverage as competence) | False confidence; failed sittings | Constitution language; uncertainty UX (ILE-005); evidence before inference |
| Dual educational truths on surfaces | Confusion; KSI regression | One runtime / one state; Home decision hygiene |
| Optimising completion vanity | Activity without learning | Success metric anti-list; EQ mission quality |
| Premature Twin interruption of syllabus order | Surprise and distrust | Explicit cutover programme + law amendment before behaviour change |
| Effectiveness unproven (G1.9 / Stage 1) | Strategy built on hope | Hold strong marketing; fund Stage 1 evidence closure |

## Regulatory & privacy considerations

| Area | Consideration | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Student data ownership | Vision data principles; lawful basis | Privacy reviews before cohort expansion; minimise PII in logs (PR-001 patterns) |
| Cross-border / international | Data residency, exam body rules | Expand geography only with legal review |
| Professional exam body IP | Syllabus publishing rights | Curriculum packs via lawful sources; Founder publishing controls |
| Accessibility / consumer law (later public) | Public registration obligations | Invite-only until readiness; then compliance programme |
| AI regulatory trends | Transparency duties | Explainability already strategic; document decision provenance |

Stage 1 enrollment HOLD items (privacy signatures, pilot analytics) remain Board blockers for live effectiveness claims — strategy assumes those close before scale marketing.

---

## Strategy assumptions (explicit)

1. Educational Intelligence remains the sole educational authority path for adaptive guidance.  
2. Vision 2030 and Educational Constitution stay binding; ILE docs do not outrank them.  
3. Version 1 production-ready declaration still requires P-002.1 gates (including validated KSI ≥ 80).  
4. Product Board continues founder-governed approval (GP-001).  
5. No production educational behaviour changes occur in documentation-only programmes.

---

## Risk posture for ILE sequencing

Prefer **Wave A (mission + explain)** even if commercial pressure pushes catalogue or collaboration earlier. Trust risk dominates growth risk in professional exams.
