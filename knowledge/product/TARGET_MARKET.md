## Market thesis

Kwalitec serves **candidates for demanding professional examinations** where syllabuses are long, failure is costly, and daily “what next?” decisions dominate outcomes.

Beachhead: actuarial professional education. Expansion: other professional qualifications and institutional buyers who need syllabus fidelity and honest progress — not generic e-learning.

---

## Initial audience — IFoA

| Attribute | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Body** | Institute and Faculty of Actuaries (IFoA) |
| **Subjects (current product focus)** | Supported papers with published curriculum packages (e.g. CS1, CM1, and other in-scope subjects as published) |
| **Candidate profile** | Serious first- or second-sitting candidates; often employed; limited daily study time |
| **Buying behaviour** | Individual subscription or employer/provider sponsorship; high willingness to pay for trust and clarity |
| **Go-to-market** | Invite-only / private beta → Stage 1 cohorts → broader IFoA acquisition once Version 1 gates and privacy allow |

IFoA remains the proof market: curriculum depth, educational effectiveness evidence, and brand association before aggressive multi-body expansion.

## Expansion opportunities

### Actuarial adjacency (near-term)

| Opportunity | Rationale |
|---|---|
| **SOA / CAS / CAA** | Similar exam culture, long syllabuses, high stakes; curriculum intelligence generalises |
| **Additional IFoA papers** | Depth within beachhead before geography |

Gate: quality of published curriculum packages + EQ certification — not marketing alone.

### Professional qualifications (medium-term)

Examples (illustrative, not committed backlog):

- Legal professional exams
- Medical / clinical professional assessments (where lawful and appropriate)
- Engineering chartered pathways
- Accountancy / CFA-adjacent professional tracks

Each requires curriculum publishing capability, regulatory sensitivity, and subject-matter partnership — not a content scrape.

### University programmes (medium-term)

| Fit | Caution |
|---|---|
| University actuarial/STEM programmes that map to professional syllabuses | Avoid becoming a generic VLE |
| Bridging modules aligned to IFoA/SOA pathways | Institutional procurement cycles are slow; student trust still primary |

### Corporate learning (medium–long-term)

| Buyer | Need |
|---|---|
| Employers funding actuarial trainees | Cohort progress honesty, sustainable pacing, reduced failure cost |
| Training providers | Curriculum fidelity, mission quality, white-label or co-branded companion |

Enterprise offerings must not dilute the student-first Educational State model.

### International markets (long-term)

- English-speaking professional exam markets first (UK, Ireland, selected Commonwealth, North America actuarial).
- Localisation of UX and curriculum packs after beachhead proof.
- Data residency and privacy regimes treated as release blockers, not afterthoughts.

## Segment priorities

| Priority | Segment | Why |
|---:|---|---|
| P0 | IFoA individual candidates (supported subjects) | Beachhead proof; KSI and effectiveness |
| P1 | Actuarial employers / providers sponsoring those candidates | Revenue + retention; still student-outcome aligned |
| P2 | Adjacent actuarial bodies / papers | Same product motion, new curriculum packs |
| P3 | Other professions / universities | Strategy optionality after ILE maturity |

---

## Non-targets (near term)

- Mass-market school homework apps
- Casual language-learning consumers
- Pure content marketplace sellers
- Social study networks as primary users

---

## Implications for ILE roadmap

- ILE programmes optimise the **IFoA daily companion** first.
- Catalogue expansion is a **curriculum publishing + quality** programme family, not a UI skin.
- Enterprise surfaces lag student trust metrics; do not invert the roadmap.
