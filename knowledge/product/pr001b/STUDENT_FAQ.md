# Student FAQ — Pilot (PR-001B)

## Accounts and access

**Q: Why can’t I register myself?**  
A: Kwalitec Internal Alpha is invite-only. Your pilot organiser creates your account.

**Q: I forgot my password.**  
A: Contact your pilot organiser / ops. Self-serve reset may not be exposed in alpha.

**Q: After login I was sent to a long wizard again, but I already enrolled.**  
A: You should land on Home if you have a Runtime C enrolment. If the wizard appears, stop and contact ops — do not create a duplicate plan unless instructed.

## Subjects and enrolment

**Q: What is Published Curriculum?**  
A: Subjects a founder published from Curriculum Studio. That is the Runtime C pilot path.

**Q: My subject says Coming Soon or Not Supported.**  
A: It is not open for Runtime C enrolment yet. Pick another supported published subject or ask the founder.

**Q: I didn’t get a calibration quiz.**  
A: Correct for Runtime C. You go straight to Home with today’s syllabus mission.

**Q: Can I enrol in both a published subject and a legacy paper?**  
A: Technically possible in coexistence, but for this pilot stick to one Published Curriculum subject unless ops says otherwise.

## Studying

**Q: Why isn’t there a Start Session button?**  
A: On the Runtime C pilot path, Home shows **Mark mission complete**. You study with your materials, then confirm completion. Guided Session remains the Runtime A path.

**Q: How do I know what to study?**  
A: Home + Educational context: topic, learning objectives, Done when, Why / Why now.

**Q: Why only one mission per day?**  
A: The educational runtime issues one daily mission per enrolment. After you complete it, return tomorrow for the next topic.

**Q: I completed a mission — when is the next one?**  
A: Next calendar day. Journey already shows what unlocks next.

## Progress and Journey

**Q: Where do I see progress?**  
A: Educational context **Progress**, and the Journey page completed/upcoming lists.

**Q: Revision / History look empty or different.**  
A: During this pilot, trust Home and Journey for Runtime C. Other surfaces may still reflect the older Runtime A framing.

**Q: I finished the whole syllabus.**  
A: Home/Journey show syllabus complete. No further daily missions are generated for that enrolment.

## Errors and recovery

**Q: Mark complete failed.**  
A: Refresh Home and try again. If it still fails, wait a moment and retry. Your organiser can check logs if it persists.

**Q: It said the mission was already complete.**  
A: Progress is already saved. Open Journey or return tomorrow.

**Q: I closed the browser mid-study.**  
A: Sign in again. The same open mission remains until you mark it complete.
