# Student Pilot Guide

**Programme:** PR-001B  
**Who this is for:** First-time students on a Runtime C (published curriculum) pilot  

---

## What you will do this week

1. Sign in with your invite account.  
2. Enrol in a **Published Curriculum** subject.  
3. Each study day: read today’s mission, study the topic, mark it complete.  
4. Check Journey to see progress.  
5. Return the next day for the next topic.

You do **not** need a developer. Use this guide and the in-product Educational context panel.

---

## Before you start

- You have an email and password from the pilot organiser (registration is invite-only).  
- Your organiser has enabled published-subject discovery for the pilot environment.  
- You have your subject study materials (notes, CMP/syllabus references your cohort uses).

---

## Day 0 — Get set up

### 1. Sign in

Open Kwalitec → **Sign in**.

If you see a short product introduction (alpha onboarding), read it and continue.

### 2. Choose your subject

You will enter the study plan wizard.

1. Select the **Published Curriculum** category (not a legacy catalogue paper unless instructed).  
2. Choose your subject — it should show as supported.  
3. Complete the remaining steps (including exam date if asked).  
4. Confirm on the review step.

You land on **Home**. You should see **Educational context** and today’s mission.

Runtime C does **not** send you through calibration. That is expected.

---

## Each study day

### 1. Open Home

Sign in. You should land on Home (not the wizard again).

### 2. Read the mission

On Home, note:

- **What** — today’s topic / mission title  
- **Why** — why this mission  
- **Why now** — why today  
- **You’ll work toward** — expected benefit  
- **Done when** — in Educational context  
- **What comes next** — after this topic  

### 3. Study

Use your materials against the learning objectives and **Done when** criteria.  
There is no separate Guided Session screen in this pilot path — you study, then confirm.

### 4. Mark complete

Click **Mark mission complete** when you meet the Done when criteria.

Home will show that today’s learning day is complete and hint at what unlocks next.

### 5. Optional: open Journey

Journey shows completed topics, the current place in the syllabus, and upcoming topics.

---

## Next day

Sign in again. Home generates the next topic’s mission.  
One mission per calendar day is normal — do not expect a second mission the same day after completing.

---

## If you miss a day

Just return. Your place in the syllabus stays on the unfinished topic until you complete it. Nothing is lost.

---

## If you leave mid-study

Come back the same day. The same mission remains open until you mark it complete.

---

## What “good” looks like after one week

- You enrolled without help.  
- You completed several daily missions.  
- You can explain why today’s topic was chosen.  
- Journey shows increasing progress.  
- Signing in never asks you to re-create a plan for the same subject.
