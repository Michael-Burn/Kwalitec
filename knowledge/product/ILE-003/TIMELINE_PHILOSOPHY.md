# Educational Timeline Philosophy

**Programme:** ILE-003 — Educational Timeline  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active — reflective narrative standard over Decision Journal  
**Effective:** 2026-07-28  
**Authority:** Product educational continuity (subordinate to Vision 2030, Educational Constitution, ILE-010 Sensei philosophy, ILE-002 Decision Journal, ILE-001C0 Communication Framework, P-001.2 Explainability)

---

## Purpose

The Educational Timeline turns Decision Journal educational memories into a **coherent learning story**.

It identifies meaningful patterns across significant educational events and presents them chronologically for reflection.

It is **not** analytics.  
It is **not** a dashboard of scores.  
It is **interpretation for reflection**.

---

## Educational goal

Students should understand how their learning has evolved over time.

The Timeline helps learners recognise:

- growth  
- recovery  
- consistency  
- changing confidence  
- important turning points  

without exaggeration or motivational theatre.

Success criterion: after reading, a student should feel *“I understand how I have developed”* — not *“I’ve looked at a collection of statistics.”*

---

## Relationship to Decision Journal

| Layer | Role |
|---|---|
| **Decision Journal (ILE-002)** | Stores educational memories |
| **Educational Timeline (ILE-003)** | Interprets educational memories |

The Timeline does **not** duplicate journal CRUD, accept/defer controls, or evidence append. It reads journal evidence and generates narrative sections. Journal history is never rewritten by the Timeline.

---

## Hard rules

1. **Evidence first** — every narrative moment cites journal decision IDs.  
2. **No certainty beyond evidence** — thin journals speak tentatively.  
3. **No analytics theatre** — no engagement metrics, streak scores, or gamification.  
4. **No second brain** — no Twin, readiness prediction, Tutor, or recommendation ranking.  
5. **Calm Sensei voice** — qualitative educational language only.  
6. **Reflection over persuasion** — questions invite thought; they do not lead or judge.

---

## Timeline sections

Sections emerge from journal evidence and appear only when moments exist:

1. Learning Journey  
2. Turning Points  
3. Recoveries  
4. Periods of Consistency  
5. Periods of Uncertainty  
6. Mission Milestones  
7. Reflection Highlights  
8. Decision Milestones  
9. Learning Momentum  

Empty sections are omitted — silence is honest.
