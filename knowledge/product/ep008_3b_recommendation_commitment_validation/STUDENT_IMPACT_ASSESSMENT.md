## 1. Student problem

**Student problem:**

> After Trust made tips inspectable, students could still lack a conscious commit / honest defer path, a clear post-session “what changed,” and a readable history of study choices — leaving follow-through and Strong-band K2 unproven. EP-008.3A shipped that chrome; Product could not claim educational benefit without Tier B + observational evidence.

**Evidence:**

> EP-008.1B residual (K2 **68**; Strong-band / acceptance KPI open); EP-008.3 VALIDATION_PLAN; EP-008.3A Tier A Pass with ΔKSI = 0 until this programme.

## 2. Student benefit

| Design question | Helped? | How |
|---|---|---|
| What should I do now? | Yes (measured perception) | Start-vs-defer fork after Trust speech |
| How am I progressing? | Partial | Reflection + History narrative; rates unmeasured |
| What is stopping me? | Yes (measured) | Honest defer catalogue without shame |
| What happens next? | Yes (measured) | Reflection next + continuity lines |

**Student benefit summary:**

> This programme does not change the product; it **validates** that students perceive conscious commitment (soft Pattern A), honest deferral, useful reflection/continuity, and no Twin/shame regressions — and documents that **behavioural follow-through rates and K2 ≥ 75 remain unproven**.

**Final Test:** Does this help students become better professionals? **Indirectly yes** — honest measurement rewards deliberate study choices and refuses conversion theatre, without claiming exam outcomes.
