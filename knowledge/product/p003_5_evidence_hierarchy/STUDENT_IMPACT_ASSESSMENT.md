## 10. Lessons learned for student value (exit)

- Students are protected more by **explicit prohibitions** (C-EDU, C-V1, freezes) than by additional estimated ΔKSI.  
- Splitting E3 (internal perception) from E4 (external) matches how the repo already blocked High confidence — making that split claim-law prevents “Tier B = proven for students” smuggling.  
- Separable verdicts (programme GO / effectiveness GO / V1 ready) remain the highest-leverage honesty tool.
