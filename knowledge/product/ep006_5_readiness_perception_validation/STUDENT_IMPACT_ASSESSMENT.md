## 1. Student problem

**Student problem:**

> Students could see a readiness percentage on Home without knowing what drove it — so Product could not honestly claim readiness usefulness gains from EP-006.4 without measuring whether drivers, confidence, next action, and review points actually improve understanding, trust, and actionability.

**Evidence:**

## 10. Lessons learned for student value

- **Home delivery is required for readiness perception** — Analytics drivers never cleared PERC-01.  
- **Named drivers + Suggested confidence** move calibration more than a prettier percentage.  
- **Sit-advice refusal is a success signal** for honesty, not a K3 failure.  
- Measurement programmes create student value by **preventing false K3 claims** as much as by raising scores.
