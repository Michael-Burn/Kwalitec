## 1. Executive verdict

Post–EP-006.4 Home readiness MES delivery **measurably improves student perception of readiness usefulness on the schema-complete Home path**. The pre-change residual — percentage / “On Track” without named drivers (EP-005.2 RC-04; EP-006.3 **PERC-01**) — is **cleared to a minority residual** when the authored readiness surface is present.

Cold-start / absent readiness explanation nights remain weakly explained. Dual-home / duration frictions continue to tax product-wide trust but are outside readiness delivery scope. Students continue to refuse sit-advice reliance — preserved honesty, not a failure of unpackability.

| Dimension | Baseline (pre–EP-006.4 Home) | Post–EP-006.4 Tier B | Meaningful change? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Readiness explanation visibility | Poor on Home (borrowed rec evidence; empty drivers) | **Strong** when schema-complete | **Yes** |
| Driver comprehension | Unpackability Near-Universal / Fail on Home | **Majority can name drivers** | **Yes** |
| Confidence understanding | Thin; % overclaim risk | **Pass** (Suggested + basis + drivers) | **Yes** |
| Next-action clarity (readiness) | Often borrowed / unclear | **Pass** ≥80% schema-complete | **Yes** |
| Review-point usefulness | Unbound / secondary | **Pass** when shown | **Yes** |
| Student trust | Weak unpackability → distrust or overclaim | **Conditional Pass** (study use; sit refused) | **Yes (bounded)** |

**Unsupported claims:** “Readiness cured for all nights”; “K3 ≥ 70 Strong mid-band”; “KSI ≥ 80”; “educational effectiveness GO”; “Exam Ready marketing cleared”; “dual-home trust cured.”

## 3. Dimension findings

### 3.1 Readiness explanation visibility

**Pass for schema-complete Home.** L1 why + readiness next appear without expand; L2 “Why this estimate?” reaches drivers, evidence, confidence, and review point in one disclosure. Automated EP-006.4 delivery tests align with Tier B observation.

**Fail / residual:** Cold-start Home shows countdown-only readiness with no why/drivers/next/review (`home_cold_start` capture).

### 3.2 Driver comprehension

**Majority Pass.** Reviewers (esp. SV-005, SV-013, SV-014, SV-015, SV-011) could restate coverage / knowledge strength / review discipline in their own words and use them as levers. This is the primary clearance of **PERC-01**.

Unpackability theme status on schema-complete Home: **not Near-Universal** → **minority residual** (cold-start absence; “On Track” if L2 ignored — SV-008/SV-013).

### 3.3 Confidence understanding

**Pass.** “Suggested” + basis tied to coverage/practice density read as provisional (SV-013 calibration Pass/stronger). Drivers falsify over-precise reading of 62% for majority who open disclosure.

### 3.4 Next-action clarity

**Pass.** ≥8/9 reviewers affirmed clear readiness next action on schema-complete Home (dedicated practise cue, distinct from mission copy). Meets ≥80% target for that path.

### 3.5 Review-point usefulness

**Pass when shown.** Reviewers noticed and valued reassessment cues for calibration (SV-013), feedback timing (SV-011), and decision re-check (SV-015). Secondary to Next for some decision-focused users — still Pass.

### 3.6 Student trust

**Conditional Pass (majority).** Schema-complete unpackability earned cautious trust for study choices (SV-005, SV-010, SV-014, SV-015). SV-003 respected honesty but rejected product substitution. SV-008 remained limited by emotional residual on “On Track.” **Universal refusal of sit-advice reliance** among calibration-sensitive reviewers — treated as honesty success.
