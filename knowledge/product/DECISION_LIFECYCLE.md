## Purpose

Describe how learner decisions move through a continuous loop — and how Educational Intelligence **supports but never replaces** learner agency.

```
Observation → Understanding → Guidance → Student action → Reflection → Updated understanding
                                      ↘ (silence / ask) ↗
```

This lifecycle is product philosophy. It does not change runtime algorithms in this programme.

---

## Stages

### 1. Observation

Kwalitec gathers lawful educational evidence:

- Mission and session outcomes  
- Attempts and learning checks  
- Coverage and plan position  
- Decision Journal (accept / defer / dismiss)  
- Stated availability and exam date  
- Workload / intensity signals when present  

Observation is continuous and quiet. The student should not need to “perform for the system” beyond normal study.

**Agency:** Observation does not decide. It records.

### 2. Understanding

Certified Educational Intelligence interprets position on the official syllabus, readiness signals, prerequisites, uncertainty, and plan coherence.

Understanding is:

- Curriculum-first (V1/V2 traversable)  
- Deterministic from the same inputs  
- Provisional when evidence is thin  
- Never invented by chat prose or a second brain  

**Agency:** Understanding is the Sensei’s internal map — not a verdict on the student’s worth.

### 3. Guidance

When confidence and responsibility allow, Kwalitec surfaces guidance:

- Suggest / strongly recommend  
- Challenge / reassure  
- Recommend revision or progression  
- Or: ask a question / wait / remain silent  

Guidance maps to catalogue Decision IDs and obeys the Responsibility Matrix, Confidence Model, Silence Principle, P-001.2, and P-001.3.

**Agency:** Guidance is a proposal. It is not an automatic booking, lockout, or punishment.

### 4. Student action

The learner acts:

- Accepts and studies  
- Defers  
- Dismisses  
- Adjusts scope (shorter session, different resource)  
- Seeks human help  
- Makes Student-only choices (booking, postpone, career) without Sensei ownership  

**Agency:** This stage is non-negotiable. Without student action, there is no learning — only software theatre.

### 5. Reflection

Sensei and student see what happened:

- What was studied  
- What evidence changed  
- Which recommendations were accepted or deferred  
- What remains uncertain  

Reflection closes the emotional and educational loop so guidance does not feel like commands.

**Agency:** Reflection informs the next choice; it does not coerce it.

### 6. Updated understanding

New evidence updates Twin / Educational State. The lifecycle repeats.

Adaptation is explainable from new inputs — not mood-like randomness.

**Agency:** The student remains the decision owner on the next turn.

## Continuous improvement

| Loop benefit | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Better evidence | Checks and Missions improve Understanding quality |
| Better guidance | Confidence levels rise only when warranted |
| Better trust | Silence and honesty when evidence is weak |
| Better agency | Decision Journal teaches the product what the student rejects |

Product improvement programmes may strengthen each stage — but must not collapse Student action into automatic Sensei decision.

---

## How Educational Intelligence supports agency

| Intelligence role | Agency protection |
|---|---|
| Surfaces one highest-value option | Reduces overload without removing choice |
| Explains what / why / uncertain | Student can disagree with eyes open |
| Honours defer/dismiss | Rejection is data, not defiance |
| Admits Insufficient evidence | Student is not gaslit by fake certainty |
| Stays within Responsibility Matrix | High-stakes personal choices remain human |

Educational Intelligence is the **compass**. The student is the **navigator**.

---

## Anti-patterns (lifecycle breaks)

| Anti-pattern | Break |
|---|---|
| Tip without observation | Guidance without warrant |
| Understanding without curriculum truth | Invented syllabus |
| Auto-executing Missions the student never saw | Replaces agency |
| Reflection as shame scoreboard | Judges worth |
| Updated understanding that ignores dismissals | Manipulative re-nag |
| Skipping silence when evidence is thin | Trust erosion |

## Worked micro-example

1. **Observe:** Student completes a Mission check with weak prerequisite signals.  
2. **Understand:** Foundation topic blocks planned progression (Reliable).  
3. **Guide:** Strongly recommend revisit fundamentals (D-L04) with explanation.  
4. **Student action:** Accepts; studies foundation; or dismisses to finish employer homework.  
5. **Reflect:** Journal records choice; summary shows evidence still thin if dismissed.  
6. **Update:** Twin remains provisional; next day Sensei may soft-revisit or wait — not punish.

## Evaluation questions

2. Does Student action remain free?  
3. Will Updated understanding respect silence, deferral, and Student-only boundaries?
