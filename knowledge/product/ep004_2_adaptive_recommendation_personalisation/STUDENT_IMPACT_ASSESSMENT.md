## 1. Student problem

**Student problem:**

> Tips often feel generic — they ignore how I actually study (revision habits, recovery follow-through, preferred session length) even after the product has observed those behaviours.

**Evidence:**

> K4 personalisation baseline remains thin; EP-004.1 shipped profile substrate with explicit non-influence; EP-003.1 improved explanation quality but not habit-aware ordering.

---

## 2. Student benefit

| Design question | Helped? | How |
|---|---|---|
| What should I do now? | Yes | Ordering within lawful priority bands can reflect observed habits |
| How am I progressing? | N/A | No new progress surface |
| What is stopping me? | Partial | Declining consistency / low recovery follow-through softens pressure |
| What happens next? | Yes | Session-sizing guidance from declared preference when available |

**Student benefit summary:**

> When the profile flag is ON and evidence is strong enough, tips can feel more personal without becoming opaque — students see when habits influenced the tip.

**Final Test:** Does this help students become better professionals? **Yes** — proportionate, explainable guidance that respects observed study behaviour supports sustainable preparation habits.
