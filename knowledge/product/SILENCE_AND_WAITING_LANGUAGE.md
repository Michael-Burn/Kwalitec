## Purpose

Define situations where the **best communication is not a recommendation** — and the few words (if any) that may accompany that choice.

Silence is a Sensei behaviour. Empty product that abandons warranted guidance is not.

---

## Stance

> Speaking without warrant is noise. Overclaiming is betrayal.

When evidence, responsibility class, or mid-focus state forbids a tip, prefer:

1. **No recommendation** (true silence on advice)  
2. **A question** (one clarifying or reflective question)  
3. **A request for more evidence**  
4. **An invitation to reflect**  

Do not invent Coach/Insights theatre to fill whitespace.

---

## Mode A — No recommendation

**When:** Insufficient evidence; Observation only; outside educational responsibility; mid-authorised focus; re-push would manipulate; conflicting signals unresolved and no clarifying instrument ready.

**UI speech:** Often none for the tip slot. Optional quiet status only if anxiety rises from a blank that looks like a bug.

| Optional status | Use |
|---|---|
| “No extra tip right now — your Mission is the focus.” | Mid-Mission protection |
| “We don’t have enough yet to recommend a focus.” | Insufficient evidence (honesty reduces anxiety) |
| (Nothing) | When the Mission / plan already provides the lawful next action |

**Do not:** “Here’s something anyway…” speculative tips.

---

## Mode B — A question

**When:** Emerging confidence; close alternatives; a single clarifying input unlocks lawful guidance (exam date, available time); agency should lead.

**Rules:** One question. Answerable. Educational or practical — not interrogation.

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| Constraint | “How much time do you have for today’s session?” |
| Priority | “Which matters more today — new coverage or reinforcing the foundation?” |
| Evidence invitation | “Would a short check help clarify this mixed signal?” |

Questions are not disguised commands. Honour the answer.

---

## Mode C — Request for more evidence

**When:** Guidance would help but evidence strength is below Reliable; conflict needs a clarifying instrument; coverage without understanding signals.

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| Gentle gather | “Let’s gather a little more information before choosing a focus.” |
| Instrument | “A Quick Check on [topic] would help us guide practice.” |
| After study | “Study this idea, then we can check again.” |

Always say **what kind** of evidence would help — not vague “do more.”

---

## Mode D — Invitation to reflect

**When:** Soft calibration; post-session closure; Decision Journal moments; under/overconfidence without needing a firm tip.

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| Journey | “What felt solid today — and what still feels unclear?” |
| Decision | “You deferred yesterday’s tip — still the right call for today?” |
| Confidence | “Did your confidence match how the check felt?” |

Keep reflection optional and brief. Not a journaling product pitch.

---

## Waiting vs neglect

| Waiting / silence (good) | Neglect (bad) |
|---|---|
| Withholds *advice* without warrant | Hides a Reliable primary Mission / plan action |
| Explains “not enough yet” when useful | Dead-end with no path to gather evidence |
| Protects authorised study loop | Leaves learner with no lawful next step when one exists |
| Honours dismissals | Abandons in-scope educational support |

If a Reliable next action exists and responsibility allows guidance, silence on that action is neglect — speak.

---

## Language for transitions into silence

When withdrawing a tip because evidence collapsed or conflict appeared:

> “Signals conflicted, so we’re holding the extra tip. Your Mission focus stays as planned.”

When honouring a dismissal:

> “Understood — we won’t push that suggestion again until something material changes.”

---

## Mapping to confidence levels

| Level | Preferred modes |
|---|---|
| Insufficient | A (silence) or B/C if clarifying |
| Observation only | A; optional status |
| Emerging | B, C, D, or soft tip (not firm primary) |
| Reliable / High | Silence inappropriate if lawful primary exists — unless Responsibility forbids deciding |

---

## Checklist

1. Are we quiet because evidence warrants it — or because the surface looked empty?  
2. If we speak, is it question / evidence request / reflection — not fake certainty?  
3. Is the authorised Mission/plan still visible and usable?  
4. Would a tip be manipulation after defer/dismiss?
