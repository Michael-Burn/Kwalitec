## 1. Student problem

**Student problem:**

> Students could see tonight’s tip after MES delivery, but still hesitated: why *now*, what improvement to expect, whether the tip fights the mission, what else was considered, or how finishing practice changes tomorrow’s tip. EP-008.1A bound those trust surfaces; Product could not claim K2 improvement without Tier B perception evidence.

**Evidence:**

> Validated K2 **55** (EP-007.2 lineage); P-004.1 PP-001 / IMP-01; EP-005.2 REM-06; EP-008.1 design + EP-008.1A Tier A Pass with ΔKSI = 0 until this programme.
