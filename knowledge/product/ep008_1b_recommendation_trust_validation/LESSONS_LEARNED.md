# EP-008.1B — Lessons Learned

**Programme:** EP-008.1B — Recommendation Trust Validation (Tier B)  
**Date:** 2026-07-26  

---

## 1. Student value

1. **Inspectability moves K2 more than new ranking.** Closing why-now, benefit, coherence, alternatives, and refusal on the daily Home path produced a validated K2 lift without touching RecommendationService maths.  
2. **MES pass-through was necessary but not sufficient.** EP-006.2/006.3 cleared Coach opacity enough for K8=70 and K2=55; the remaining trust layer was agency and completeness of the five success answers.  
3. **Honesty beats fabricated confidence.** Refusal nights increased trust for resitter / calibration personas — a learning benefit, not a conversion trick.  
4. **Stated willingness ≠ acceptance KPI.** Tier B shows students *say* they would follow tips; EP-008.3 is required before claiming behavioural acceptance or Strong-band K2.  
5. **Dense L1 is tolerable when one CTA remains.** Alternatives in L2 avoided decision paralysis.

---

## 2. Measurement

1. Prefer-lower correctly parked K2 at **68** (Partial upper) rather than Strong floor **70+** without acceptance evidence.  
2. Persona re-reviews against a live surface pack remain the approved Tier B instrument when external N=0.  
3. Capture fixtures without Start Session forms must disclose the CTA limitation — next-action speech is still judgeable from authored Next lines.

---

## 3. What failed to move (or only weakly moved)

1. **Cold-start opacity** — unchanged; incomplete nights still lack trust speech.  
2. **Adaptation labelling** — students infer adaptation from evidence; no explicit adaptation narrative.  
3. **Educational effectiveness** — not in scope; no M1–M9 movement claimed.  
4. **Composite KSI gap to 80** — only ≈+2 points; Wave A alone cannot close Version 1 usefulness.
