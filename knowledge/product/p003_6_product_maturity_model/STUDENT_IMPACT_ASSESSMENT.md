## 10. Lessons learned for student value

- Organisational maturity can look “green” on Runtime A / Planning / Explainability while students still lack proof that the product improves learning outcomes — **those are different maturity cells**.  
- The largest student-trust gap is not missing architecture docs; it is **Educational Effectiveness Level 1** and **Release Readiness Level 2** under a **NO GO** board.  
- Personalisation and Twin as Level 2 Red correctly discourage treating gated code as student benefit.
