## 1. Purpose

## 2. Board headline

Version 1’s organisational strength is **internal educational-runtime + governance**.  
Version 1’s organisational weakness is **external proof, release-class ops packaging, gated intelligence, and commercial unlock**.

After Version 1 (and still *before* any C-V1 claim), prioritise:

1. **Evidence that unlocks honesty** (Educational Effectiveness → E5; external perception → E4; G1.1/G1.9).  
2. **Declaration packaging** (full G1–G12 Evidence Package; G1.7; G7–G8/G10/G12).  
3. **Activation only with soak evidence** (Personalisation, Learning Twin T7) — not speculative default-ON.  
4. **Commercial** only after claim freezes and release posture permit.
