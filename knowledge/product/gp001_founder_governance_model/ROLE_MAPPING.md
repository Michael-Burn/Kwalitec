## 3. Dual-signature patterns

| Historical pattern | Founder-operated equivalent | Still required? |
|---|---|---|
| Product + Security/ops (Privacy Review) | Two Founder Reviews: Product Owner + Privacy Owner | **Yes** |
| Product + Ops (named owners) | Founder Review confirming Operations Owner designations (may be self) | **Yes** |
| Full V1 sign-off board (6 roles) | Founder Reviews per capacity in Approval Matrix | **Yes** |
| Chair + quorum of distinct people | Founder as Chair + capacity Founder Reviews for meeting class | **Yes** (adapted) |
| Independent G1.7 second assessor | Distinct natural person **or** HOLD | **Yes — not satisfied by dual capacity alone** |

## 4. Phrases to prefer going forward

| Prefer | Avoid (current reality) |
|---|---|
| Founder Review (Product Owner capacity) | “Awaiting Product team signature” implying a separate Product department |
| Founder Review (Privacy Owner capacity) | “Security team countersignature” implying a separate Security department |
| Founder as Product Board Chair | “Board of directors vote” implying multi-director staffing |
| Capacity concentration accepted (PR-027) | Pretending multi-person RACI is staffed |
