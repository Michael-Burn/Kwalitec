---

## 1. Student problem

Students risk receiving a “Version 1” product that is operationally shippable but not educationally useful enough — or marketed as ready while explainability, recommendation quality, planning/readiness honesty, or production flag posture remain incomplete. Prior governance had a KSI usefulness bar (P-001.1) and quality standards (P-001.2 / P-001.3), but no single permanent gate board that required **validated** KSI plus constitutional, quality-contract, and operational evidence before a production-ready declaration.

**Evidence:** Product Success Framework §2.3 / §7 (KSI necessary but not sufficient); VERSION_1_READINESS still IN PROGRESS across educational validation, perf, security residuals; EP-003/EP-004 estimated ΔKSI under-claimed pending live re-score; private-beta GO WITH CONDITIONS ≠ Version 1 GO.

---

## 2. Student benefit

No immediate student-visible change. Indirect benefit: Version 1 will only be declared when objective gates pass, reducing the risk that serious candidates trust an under-ready companion or face dual messaging / false certainty.

| Design question | Helped? | How |
|---|---|---|
| What should I do now? | N/A (governance) | G4/G5 require recommendation + planning quality before V1 claim |
| How am I progressing? | N/A | G6 readiness quality + G1 K3 floor |
| What is stopping me? | N/A | Gate board forces honesty residuals closed |
| What happens next? | N/A | Go / no-go + claim language rules |

**Final Test:** Yes — preventing premature “Version 1 ready” claims protects professional learners from false confidence and protects trust required for consistent study.

---

## 3. Learning benefit

Improves organisational discipline about when educational usefulness and operational safety are sufficient to claim Version 1. Does not change learning algorithms. Explicitly forbids rewarding activity vanity or estimated-only KSI for declaration.

| Check | Answer |
|---|---|
| Reinforces consistency / feedback / reflection / revision / confidence / understanding mistakes? | Indirectly — via gates that keep guidance honest before V1 claim |
| Risks rewarding activity vanity? | No — G1/G9 forbid vanity as success; telemetry must not invent educational scores |
| Educational Constitution / honesty risks? | Mitigated — G2/G3/G6 hard-fail on honesty incidents |

**Learning benefit summary:** Safer Version 1 declaration timing; no direct learning-loop change in this programme.

## 10. Lessons learned for student value

1. Usefulness thresholds (KSI) without operational and quality-contract gates still allow premature “ready” language.  
2. Estimated programme ΔKSI is useful for prioritisation but dangerous as a release surrogate — validation must be explicit.  
3. Separating private-beta GO WITH CONDITIONS from Version 1 GO protects students from overclaim during incomplete cohorts.
