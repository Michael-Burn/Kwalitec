## 10. Lessons learned for student value (exit)

- Perception remediation (MES, readiness, journey) **does** move validated KSI — but only by **~1 point per successful Tier B wave**, far short of the Version 1 bar.  
- **Implemented capability ≠ validated educational value** remains the central lesson (EP-005.2 → EP-007.3).  
- Highest remaining student-value leverage is **recommendation trust + external evidence + gated personalisation realisation**, not new intelligence speech.  
- What failed historically to move value: estimate stacking, checklist Pass without rendered speech, flags OFF claimed as usefulness.
