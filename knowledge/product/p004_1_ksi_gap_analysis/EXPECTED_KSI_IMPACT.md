## 3. Portfolio path to ≥80 (illustrative)

### 3.1 Conservative validated path (preferred Board view)

Assume prefer-lower haircuts and partial overlaps.

| Wave | Improvements | Est. net ΔKSI (de-duped) | Illustrative KSI |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| **Done** | MES + readiness + journey (EP-006/007) | +3 observed | **62** |
| **Wave A — Trust + evidence start** | IMP-01, IMP-02, IMP-07, IMP-10; start IMP-03 | +2.5 to +4.0 | **65–66** |
| **Wave B — Activation + analytics** | IMP-04, IMP-05 (after dogfood) | +2.5 to +4.0 | **68–70** |
| **Wave C — Portfolio depth** | IMP-06, IMP-08; deepen K2/K3 via Stage 1 | +2.5 to +4.0 | **71–74** |
| **Wave D — Strong-band corroboration** | Stage 1 interviews + re-validation; conditional IMP-11 | +2.0 to +6.0 | **74–80+** |

**Board interpretation:** Reaching **80 is plausible but not guaranteed**. Waves A–C alone may stall in the low-70s without Stage 1 corroboration and deeper K2/K4 lifts. Wave D is where prefer-lower + external N either unlock Strong-band or expose that more educational work remains.

## 5. Separating G1.1 (KSI) from G1.9 (effectiveness)

| Gate | Needs | Relation to ΔKSI table |
|---|---|---|
| **G1.1** KSI ≥ 80 | Validated category board | Waves A–D educational improvements + re-score |
| **G1.9** effectiveness not NO-GO | Stage 1 M1–M9 + interviews (IMP-03) | May raise confidence and some scores; **required even if KSI hits 80** |
| **Overall G1** | Both (and other G1.*) | DR-031: any hard FAIL → NO GO |

A product that reaches KSI 80 with effectiveness still NO-GO is **still NO GO** for Version 1 production-ready declaration.
