---

## Strengths that recur across independent reviewers

### 1. A concrete daily study decision
Reviewers repeatedly appreciate a named mission/tip that answers “what should I study next?” This is strongest for first-use, habit, decision-quality, and bounded-adoption personas.

### 2. Commitment and defer without shame
“I’m doing this next,” calm Not today reasons, and continuity language after defer are valued by procrastinators, parents, and emotionally exhausted resitters. Absence of streak punishment is noticed positively.

### 3. Study-plan wizard grounding
Paper / sitting / minute constraints make the product feel exam-real for supported subjects (CS1, CM1, CB2).

### 4. Inspectable recommendation rationale (when present)
Home why / why now / benefit / next speech improves trust for analytical students — when not contradicted elsewhere.

### 5. Operational honesty of Alpha labelling
Internal Alpha / Founding Cohort chrome lowers overclaim risk and accidentally aids calibration-minded resitters.

### 6. Short practice outcome capture
Attempted/correct logging is short enough that many complete it; longer research check-ins are optional.

### 7. Subject-support honesty
Coming Soon / Not Supported gates are preferred to silently broken plans (even when coverage gaps themselves are rejected).
