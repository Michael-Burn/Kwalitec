## Required critique

### Three strengths

- Happy-path Session resume eventually works.
- Plan page is a stable landmark.
- Defer/commit does not punish mistakes.

### Three weaknesses

- Dual homes amplify “am I in the right place?” anxiety.
- Thin Overview after Home Start feels like I broke something.
- No strong “you are here / continue studying” recovery banner.

### One unexpected observation

Browser confirm dialogs on plan archive felt abrupt and easy to mis-click.

### One feature I would remove

Ambiguous second start path that looks broken to anxious users.

### One feature I most appreciate

Study Plan as a calm reference when lost.

### One improvement before daily use

Always surface Resume today’s session from every major screen after a mis-click.
