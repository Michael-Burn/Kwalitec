## Required critique

### Three strengths

- A single named daily mission reduces first-night paralysis.
- Study-plan wizard makes exam/sitting/time constraints concrete.
- Practice outcome capture is short enough to complete.

### Three weaknesses

- Two homes make “where do I start?” ambiguous.
- Home → Start Session can feel emptier than Learning Workspace Session.
- Early Journey/History emptiness makes day one feel unfinished.

### One unexpected observation

I expected Coach to be a nav destination; it is only a Home panel.

### One feature I would remove

The thinner Start Session path — or the duplicate home — until briefings match.

### One feature I most appreciate

The “what should I study next” framing on sign-in and today’s mission.

### One improvement before daily use

One Start Studying path that always opens a full briefing with agreeing time and activities.
