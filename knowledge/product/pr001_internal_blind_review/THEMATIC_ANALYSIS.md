## Cross-cutting themes

### 1. Decision reduction is the primary perceived value
Across beginners, disciplined decision-seekers, habit users, and bounded adopters, the strongest recurring praise is a single concrete “what tonight?” object. Where that object is visible and startable, motivation and continued-use scores rise.

### 2. Product cohesion is the primary perceived risk
Dual homes (Learning Workspace Dashboard vs Student Home), thin Session Overview vs fuller Session briefing, and conflicting durations/status fields are the dominant friction cluster. Time-scarce and cognitively overloaded personas punish this hardest.

### 3. Commitment/defer is polarising in a healthy way
Procrastinators, results-day recoverers, and bounded adopters rate Commitment Workflow highly for agency without shame. Mature-stack and late-urgency personas see less incremental value because calendars/past papers already structure intent.

### 4. Trust is conditional, not blanket
Explainability fields (why / why now) help analytical personas. Contradictions and unsupported papers destroy trust faster than good copy can rebuild it. Resitters refuse to treat readiness chrome as exam truth.

### 5. Reflection is appreciated in principle, skipped in practice
Many reviewers accept practice-count logging; fewer find Product Check-in or thin “what changed” copy worth scarce minutes. Deliberate-practice seekers want mistake analysis the product does not yet force.

### 6. Replacement value is limited for mature systems
High performers and seniors with Anki/Notion/Excel stacks rarely see rip-and-replace justification. Organisational stickiness ≠ educational dependence.

### 7. Urgency and exam-transfer gaps appear near the exam
Four-week personas (SV-006, SV-019) and results-day emotional needs (SV-008) expose missing triage, past-paper centrality, and failure-aware restart narratives.

## Most common praise

1. **Daily mission / “what should I study next”** — strongest shared positive across beginners, habit users, decision-seekers, and bounded adopters
2. **Commitment / defer without shame** — especially valued by procrastinators, parents, and results-day recoverers
3. **Study-plan wizard / exam constraints** — paper, sitting, and minute settings feel concrete on supported subjects

## Most common criticism

1. **Dual homes / navigation complexity** — dominant friction; aligns with lowest Navigation mean (4.6)
2. **Inconsistent briefings / durations / status contradictions** — thin Overview vs full Session; credibility damage
3. **Shallow reflection / feedback loop** — aligns with lowest category mean (Reflection Workflow 4.55); practice counts tolerated, deeper insight missing

## Most polarising feature/category

**Likelihood of Continued Use** (σ=1.5; mean=5.4). Disagreement tracks persona urgency, tool maturity, exam support, and whether the student needs organisation vs exam-mark certainty.

## Reviewer agreement level

| Slice | Count | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Continued-use score ≥ 6 | 11/20 | Inclined to keep using in some bounded form |
| Continued-use score 5 | 4/20 | Undecided / conditional |
| Continued-use score ≤ 4 | 5/20 | Unlikely to continue for this sitting |

**Agreement level:** Moderate agreement on *value of a daily mission* and *pain of dual navigation*; **low agreement** on *whether to adopt now* (depends on exam support, weeks remaining, and existing stack). Overall cohort agreement on continued use is **mixed-to-moderate**, not unanimous.
