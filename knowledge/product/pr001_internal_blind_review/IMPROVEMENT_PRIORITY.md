# PR-001 Improvement Priority

**Programme:** PR-001 — Internal Blind Student Review (Simulation)
**Date:** 2026-07-26
**Method:** Ranked by recurrence of related improvement and weakness statements across the twenty independent reviews (qualitative frequency, not automated keyword counts)

Simulated priorities for usability/trust/workflow before Stage 1 external pilot. Not a claim of educational effectiveness.

## Top ten improvements (by frequency)

1. **Unify dual home / single start path**
2. **Align duration / briefing consistency** (Home vs Learning Workspace Session)
3. **Deeper reflection / mistake insight** (beyond attempted/correct counts)
4. **Richer Coach beyond restatement** of the tip already shown
5. **Visible adaptation after poor performance or study gaps**
6. **Stronger exam-transfer / past-paper linkage** for late sitting
7. **Calibration: separate syllabus coverage from exam readiness**
8. **Restart / late-joiner / gap modes** with reduced chrome
9. **Subject coverage honesty** (CS2 support or stop inviting unsupported papers)
10. **Show tip trade-offs vs top alternatives** (“why not X tonight”)

## Priority rationale (short)

1. **Cohesion first** — dual homes and mismatched briefings/durations appear in the widest set of personas (beginner, parent, anxious, systems thinker, cognitive-load).
2. **Then trust integrity** — contradictions and status mismatches poison explainability gains.
3. **Then learning-loop depth** — reflection/adaptation/exam-transfer matter for continued educational belief, especially resitters.
4. **Coverage honesty** — unsupported papers are rare in count but absolute vetoes when hit.

## What not to prioritise from this corpus alone

- Visual polish unrelated to study blockers
- New gamification (explicitly disliked / unmissed)
- Claims of pass-rate improvement (out of scope for PR-001)
