## Required critique

### Three strengths

- Generally avoids loud false-confidence chrome.
- Empty early states are more honest than fake high scores.
- Commitment ≠ claimed mastery.

### Three weaknesses

- Any readiness number without mock linkage is a trap for people like me.
- Journey progress can be misread as exam readiness.
- Coach can sound assured while evidence is thin.

### One unexpected observation

The Alpha labelling itself helped me discount overclaim — accidental calibration aid.

### One feature I would remove

Any unqualified “on track” vibe without tying to exam-standard evidence.

### One feature I most appreciate

Restraint and empty-state honesty early on.

### One improvement before daily use

Separate syllabus coverage from exam-performance confidence explicitly everywhere.
