# PR-001 Executive Summary

**Programme:** PR-001 — Internal Blind Student Review (Simulation)  
**Date:** 2026-07-26  
**Cohort:** 20 Student Digital Twin personas (SV-001 … SV-020)  
**Product frame:** Version 1 build intended for Stage 1 pilot (pre-Render external deployment)

---

## Verdict

### Would these twenty simulated students choose to continue using Kwalitec?

# Mixed

---

## Why

**Mean Likelihood of Continued Use:** 5.4/10 (σ=1.5)  
**Inclined to continue (≥6):** 11/20  
**Undecided (5):** 4/20  
**Unlikely (≤4):** 5/20

The cohort does **not** speak with one voice — and that disagreement is informative.

- **Yes-leaning personas** value a concrete daily mission, calm commitment/defer, and plan constraints. They treat Kwalitec as a **decision companion** beside CMP and past papers (e.g. first-use, procrastination restart, decision-quality, habit, bounded adoption).
- **No-leaning personas** hit hard vetoes: unsupported paper (CS2), late-sitting switching cost, low replacement value vs mature stacks, weak exam-transfer, or invisible adaptation after poor performance.
- **Conditional personas** will continue only if cohesion problems shrink (single start path, agreeing durations/briefings) and if readiness chrome is not mistaken for exam truth.

So continuation is **plausible for a meaningful minority-to-majority slice under bounded use**, not a unanimous “students will live here.” The honest pre-pilot reading is **mixed adoption intent** in simulation — useful for usability/trust hardening, **not** proof the Stage 1 pilot will succeed educationally.

**Clarity of Study Plan** (mean 6.3)

## Lowest-rated feature

**Reflection Workflow** (mean 4.55)

Daily mission / “what should I study next” decision reduction (with shame-free commitment/defer as a close second)

## Most common criticism

Dual homes / navigation forks and inconsistent session briefings (with thin reflection depth as a close second)

## Most polarising feature

**Likelihood of Continued Use** (σ=1.5)

## Pre-pilot implication (non-prescriptive)

Before external Stage 1, the simulation argues for fixing **cohesion and contradiction** issues that repeatedly tax trust and weeknight time — while preserving **daily mission clarity** and **shame-free commitment/defer**, which are the clearest perceived strengths.
