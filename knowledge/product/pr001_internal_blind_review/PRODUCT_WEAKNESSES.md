## Lowest-rated feature (by category mean)

**Reflection Workflow** — mean **4.55** / 10 (σ=0.8)

---

## Weaknesses that recur across independent reviewers

### 1. Dual homes and navigation forks
Learning Workspace Dashboard vs Student Home creates competing “start here” answers. Time-scarce and anxious personas lose trust and minutes.

### 2. Inconsistent session briefings and durations
Home → Start Session Overview can feel thin; Learning Workspace Session is fuller. Duration disagreements (e.g. ~30 vs ~90 minutes) are cited as credibility damage.

### 3. Reflection depth vs ceremony
Practice counts are tolerated; Product Check-in and thin “what changed” copy are often skipped. Deliberate-practice seekers want mistake analysis.

### 4. Weak visible adaptation
After gaps or poor assessments, several reviewers cannot see that the product “learned” anything specific.

### 5. Limited replacement value
Students with mature Anki/Notion/Excel/past-paper systems rarely find a rip-and-replace reason.

### 6. Subject coverage cliff
CS2 Coming Soon is an adoption veto for that persona. Unsupported wizard entries add noise.

### 7. Exam-transfer uncertainty near the sitting
Final-month and resit personas do not feel convinced that completing missions improves marks under exam conditions.

### 8. Progress surfaces that can mislead or hollow out
Empty Journey/History early; later percentages risk overconfidence if skimmed as exam readiness.

### 9. Profile / status contradictions
Examples such as examination “Not set” while a plan is active undermine explainability narratives.

### 10. Coach restatement
When Trust L1 already explained the tip, Coach often adds little — and becomes ignored after habit forms.

---

## Features reviewers would remove (clustered)

1. Duplicate home / thinner Start Session path
2. Unsupported exam-family noise in wizard
3. Repetitive Coach paraphrase
4. Low-value analytics/progress rabbit holes on weeknights
5. Activity tick theatre without understanding checks
6. Unqualified on-track / progress vibes without exam-standard evidence
