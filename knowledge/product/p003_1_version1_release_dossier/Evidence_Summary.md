## 4. What has been validated vs not

### Validated (with cited limits)

- Structural recommendation / planning / readiness quality contracts on production defaults.  
- MES delivery improves perceived explainability to K8 **70**.  
- Readiness Home unpackability to K3 **65**.  
- Canonical journey (single Home / duration) to K1 **72** on W-PROD.  
- Validated composite KSI **62** (Medium).  
- Gate G1.5 PASS.

### Not validated

- Educational effectiveness / learning outcome improvement.  
- KSI ≥ 80.  
- Personalisation value in production defaults (flags OFF).  
- Twin cutover as production-default student authority.  
- Exam pass probability lift.  
- Full G2–G12 declaration evidence package.
