## Student problem

Product Board and operators risk declaring Version 1 “ready” without a single evidence-bound dossier. Students are harmed when usefulness and effectiveness are overclaimed — false confidence, wasted scarce study time, and eroded trust.

## Student benefit

This programme does not change the student product. Indirect benefit: future release decisions must cite validated KSI, gate status, and effectiveness evidence rather than estimate stacks or operational GA alone — protecting students from premature readiness claims.

## Learning benefit

None directly (documentation only). Preserves educational honesty conditions required by Vision 2030 Final Test and Educational Constitution claim-honesty rules.
