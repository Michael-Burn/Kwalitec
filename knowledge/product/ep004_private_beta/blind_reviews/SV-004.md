# Blind Review SV-004

**Reviewer:** Michael Dube  
**Age:** 24  
**Country:** Zimbabwe  
**Exam:** IFoA CS1 (second sitting)  
**Weeks until exam:** 9  
**Review date:** Monday evening (reluctant return after four skipped study days)  
**Package used:** `knowledge/reviews/V1_REVIEW_PACKAGE/` (student-facing overview, journeys, walkthrough, screens, known limitations, beta expectations)  
**Package check:** Matches the current student-facing app described for Internal Alpha / Founding Cohort (Kwalitec v2.0.0 · Build RC2). CS1 is supported. I used only that student material and the screens — not engineering docs.

---

## Situation I brought into the app

It is Monday evening. I have not studied since Thursday. I own the CMP notes. I know what I should be doing. I still opened YouTube and WhatsApp instead. I feel guilty. I do not want a lecture. I want something that makes starting tonight feel possible.

I signed in, landed on the Learning Workspace Dashboard, looked at today’s session, opened Home, read the Coach panel, opened Journey briefly, then followed the normal study flow: Session → start → work → finish / record.

The whole time I asked myself: *Does this make me want to keep studying tonight?*

---

## Answers

### 1. Did Kwalitec reduce my resistance to starting?

**Somewhat — yes at first, then it wobbled.**

Signing in was fine. I am comfortable with tech. The Dashboard did not open with “you missed four days.” It just showed today’s topic and a big **Resume / Start** button. That helped. Decision removed. Guilt stayed mine; the app did not pile on.

Home was even better for resistance: **Welcome back.** **Today’s Mission.** **30 minutes.** One blue **Start Session** button. For about ten seconds I thought, *okay, I can do thirty minutes.*

Then I went into the Learning Workspace Session path and saw **90 min**. That hit like a wall. Same day, same topic, two different sizes of night. Resistance came back.

So: it reduced the “what should I even open?” resistance. It did not fully reduce the “this is going to eat my whole evening” resistance.

### 2. Did today’s mission feel achievable after several missed days?

**On Home, yes. On Session, no.**

Home said thirty minutes. That felt like something a guilty person could finish before bed.

Session said ninety minutes, with a checklist (read, examples, practice, review mistakes) and a plan that included attempting practice questions. After four missed days, ninety minutes feels like punishment dressed as a plan. I do not need a perfect catch-up block. I need a night I can actually finish so tomorrow is not another “I’ll start properly then.”

The topic itself — CS1-A descriptive statistics foundations — felt real and exam-relevant. The size of the ask did not.

### 3. Did I feel judged or encouraged?

**Not judged. Mildly encouraged. Not really seen.**

No red “you fell behind” banners. No streak broken drama. Journey saying 0% felt empty, not mean. When a session is closed without practice, the feedback talks about the mission staying open until I am ready — that felt adult and calm.

Encouragement is thin. Coach says the next topic is the highest-value step and will strengthen readiness. Fine. Also generic. It could have said that to anyone. After four dead days, I wanted something closer to: *showing up tonight still counts.*

I did not feel shamed. I also did not feel hugged. Neutral is safer than shame — I will take that — but neutral alone will not drag me off WhatsApp.

### 4. Did the app make me feel like I could recover from falling behind?

**It left the door open. It did not help me walk through it.**

Good:

- Today’s mission is still there.
- I can resume without rebuilding a plan.
- Closing without practice does not nuke everything.
- “Comfortable Pace” / surplus hours on the Dashboard told me I am not doomed on the calendar.

Mixed / hard:

- “Comfortable Pace” after I personally know I skipped four days felt slightly unreal. Relieving for a second. Then I thought the app might not understand how I actually study.
- Journey at **0% complete** with empty readiness / milestones does not give me a recovery story. Visible progress is how I stay interested. Empty panels make returning feel like day one again.
- Nothing offered a smaller “restart tonight” mission. Recovery is implied: just do today’s full thing. That is exactly how I fall behind my own timetable.

### 5. Would I complete today’s session?

**Maybe a short one. Not a full ninety-minute one.**

If I stay on the path that feels like thirty minutes and one clear objective, I think I finish — or at least I start, tick a couple of activities, and record something honest.

If I commit to the ninety-minute Learning Workspace session with the full checklist, I probably pause, open WhatsApp “for a minute,” and never finish. Then I feel worse than if I had never opened the app.

Honest answer: I would try. I would complete it only if the night stays small.

### 6. Would I open the app again tomorrow?

**Yes — if tonight ends with a recorded win. Unsure — if tonight ends in a half-open ninety-minute tab.**

I open tools that show me I moved. Recording practice, seeing a session land, anything that proves I showed up — that would pull me back tomorrow.

If tomorrow opens to the same empty Journey, the same generic Coach sentence, and another heavy session estimate, I will tell myself I will study properly on Wednesday. I know that script.

### 7. What moment increased my motivation the most?

**Home: “Today’s Mission · 30 minutes · Start Session.”**

One job. One button. No lecture about the four days I wasted. For a person who always falls behind his own timetable, that moment felt like the app was finally asking for something human-sized.

Second place: the practice capture screen letting me record real numbers — or honestly say I did not practise — without fake perfection. That respects how inconsistent nights actually go.

### 8. What moment made me think about closing the app?

**Estimated duration: 90 min** on the Session screen.

Also:

- Seeing **0%** syllabus / Journey after I already feel behind.
- Two homes / two session stories (Dashboard vs Home, 30 vs 90, full briefing vs thin “Core methods / No activities listed”). When I am already looking for an excuse, confusion is an exit.
- Coach repeating the same “highest-value next step” line — smart enough to notice, bored enough to leave.
- After finishing, being nudged into Product Check-in / Alpha feedback when my brain wants to close the laptop and feel done.

### 9. If I missed another three study days, would I still come back?

**I would still be able to come back. Whether I would is about 50/50.**

The app does not lock me out or shame me for gaps. That matters. Guilt already does enough damage.

But coming back only works if returning feels like a small restart, not like walking into an unfinished ninety-minute debt. Right now the door is open and polite. The room still expects a big night. For me, open + heavy = another delayed start.

### 10. What single change would most increase my consistency?

**Give me a visible, no-shame “restart tonight” that is short and still counts.**

Something like: after quiet days, keep the same topic, but offer a twenty-minute show-up session with one or two activities, mark it complete when I finish, and show a clear progress tick on Journey / Home.

I do not need a stricter timetable. I already fail those. I need the app to make showing up after a bad streak feel small, honest, and rewarding — same topic, smaller door, visible proof I returned.

## Closing note (as Michael)

I am not looking for a perfect planner. I already know what I should study. I fall behind my own timetable. Tonight I needed help keeping the promise of “just start.”

Kwalitec almost gave me that on Home. The Session path asked for a bigger night than I had in me. I would use this again if returning always felt like thirty honest minutes that move a visible needle. Right now it feels more like a polite syllabus queue than a consistency partner.

I would stay in the private beta. I would tell you the truth every week: if it only plans my days and never shrinks the restart, I will keep saying tomorrow.
