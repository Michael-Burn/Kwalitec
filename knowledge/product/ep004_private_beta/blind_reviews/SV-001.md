## How I used it tonight

Got login details from the coordinator. Opened the link. No public sign-up — that was fine; I was not expecting to create my own account.

**Sign in**  
Left side said “Know exactly what to study next.” That is literally my problem right now. I have the CMP. I do not know how to start the twelve weeks. Signed in.

**After login — Student Dashboard**  
Landed on Student Dashboard. Big card: Today’s Study Session — *Review CS1-A: Descriptive statistics foundations*, with Resume / Start. IFoA CS1 was named. Progress 0%, which made sense. There was also a recommendation for “1.1 The Purpose And Function Of Data Analysis.” For someone who has just started, that felt like a sensible beginning of the syllabus, not a random topic.

I did notice the study plan sitting looked further out than my twelve weeks (the plan talked in months / a later sitting). Assumed beta sample account. Still, the *topic for today* was clear.

**The other Home**  
I also opened what felt like another home (Student / Home). Today’s Mission, **30 minutes**, Start Session, plus Readiness / Journey / Coach. Suddenly I had two places both claiming to tell me what to do next. That made me hesitate.

**Start Session from Home**  
I tried Start Session from that Home first, because the button was right there. It opened a Session Overview. It did **not** crash. But the briefing felt thin: “your topic,” topics labelled “Core methods,” **No activities listed**, about 30 minutes. I was not sure what I was meant to study. I backed out and used the Dashboard path instead.

**Today’s Study Session (Dashboard / Session)**  
This screen made sense. Same CS1-A topic, estimated **90 min**, why you are studying this, what success looks like, recommended activities (read, examples, practice, review mistakes). That matches how I already plan to use the CMP — Kwalitec just pointed at the topic.

One annoyance: Home said 30 minutes and this said 90 for the same day. I picked the fuller briefing and treated 90 as “upper bound for a proper first night,” but I did not like the disagreement.

**Active study**  
Started the session. Timer, topic again, checklist, short session plan (read CS1-A notes, attempt practice questions, mark weak areas). I studied from my CMP and a question bank beside the laptop. Ticked activities as I went. The app did not give me the notes — it told me what to do with materials I already have. For night one, that was enough.

**Finish / practice log**  
Finished the session. Practice Outcome Capture asked how many questions I attempted and got right, optional time and notes. Straightforward. I logged a small first set and recorded the session.

**Journey / Coach / Profile (quick look after)**  
Journey: 0% complete, “Explore your journey.” Empty on day one — I get it, but it did not help me tonight.  
Coach on Home: same “highest-value next step… recent learning evidence” line. I barely have evidence yet, so that sounded a bit hollow, even though the topic itself felt right.  
Profile: Current Examination **Not set**, while Dashboard and Study Plan clearly showed IFoA CS1. That shook my confidence for a minute.

## Answers

### 1. What was my first impression?

Hopeful. The sign-in line answered the exact question I brought. It felt like a real study tool for actuarial exams, not a generic to-do app. Invite-only / Internal Alpha was fine.

### 2. Did I understand what Kwalitec wanted me to do?

Mostly yes. On the Dashboard it was obvious: open today’s study session and work the named topic. The second Home made me less sure which screen was “the” start. Once I stuck to Dashboard → Session, the ask was clear.

### 3. Was today’s study task obvious?

Yes — on the Learning Workspace Session path. **CS1-A: Descriptive statistics foundations** is exactly where I would have started myself if someone forced me to pick. The thin Home Session Overview was *not* obvious (“your topic” / “Core methods”).

### 4. Did I ever feel lost?

Yes, a few times:

- Two homes that both felt like the main screen.
- Home → Start Session opened a vague overview with no real activities.
- 30 minutes vs 90 minutes for the same day.
- Profile saying examination “Not set” while everything else said CS1.
- Journey at 0% with nothing to explore yet.

I was never lost about *which topic* once I was on the Dashboard session path.

### 5. Which screen made the most sense?

**Today’s Study Session** (the fuller briefing): named topic, time estimate, why, success checklist, recommended activities, then a clear Start / Resume. That is the screen I would show another student.

### 6. Which screen confused me?

The **Home → Session Overview** after Start Session: placeholder-feeling copy, “Core methods,” no activities. Also the fact that **Profile** disagreed with the Dashboard about my examination.

### 7. Did I understand why today’s mission was chosen?

Partly. The active session explained it as the next syllabus topic I have not finished studying yet — that makes sense for a beginner. Coach’s “based on your recent learning evidence” did **not** make sense on night one. I trusted the syllabus-order story more than the coach wording.

### 8. Would I know how to begin tomorrow?

Yes. Open Kwalitec → Dashboard → Start / Resume today’s study session → study from CMP → finish and log practice. Same loop. I would avoid relying on the thin Home Start Session briefing until it shows the real topic and activities.

### 9. Would I return tomorrow?

**Yes.**

Why: tonight it answered my only question — what should I do next — with a concrete CS1 topic and a simple structure for the evening. That is worth opening again. I would come back for the daily pointer, not because Journey / Readiness already feel full.

### 10. If another new CS1 student asked whether to try Kwalitec, what would I tell them?

I would say **yes, try it** if you already have the CMP (or notes and questions) and you freeze on “what do I do tonight?” Treat it as a daily director, not as your textbook. Use the Dashboard session path. Expect two different “home” screens and some empty panels on day one. Do not expect it to replace past papers. For a first-sitting student twelve weeks out who has just started, the “here is today’s topic” loop is the useful part.
