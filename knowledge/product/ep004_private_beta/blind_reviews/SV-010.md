# Blind Review SV-010

**Reviewer:** Hannah Brooks  
**Age:** 26  
**Country:** United Kingdom  
**Exam:** IFoA CS1 (first sitting)  
**Weeks until exam:** 9  
**Occupation:** Graduate Actuarial Consultant  
**Date:** 24 July 2026  
**Context:** One evening after work. About an hour. CMP open beside me. I used the product normally, and I deliberately made a few ordinary mistakes on purpose — wrong nav clicks, wrong pages, leaving a session, skipping ahead, coming back to find my place.

**Package confirmed:** Student-facing build in the review package — **Kwalitec v2.0.0 · Internal Alpha · Founding Cohort · Build RC2**. CS1 is supported. I reviewed only what a student can see and do. I ignored engineering documentation.

**Question I held all evening:** *If I make a mistake, do I know how to recover?*

---

## How I used it tonight (with mistakes)

Signed in with the credentials I was given. Landed on the Learning Workspace **Student Dashboard**. Today’s Study Session was there for **Review CS1-A: Descriptive statistics foundations**, marked **In Progress**, with a clear **Resume Study Session** button. That already felt like the product expected me to come and go.

### Mistake 1 — wrong navigation

I meant to start studying. I clicked **Journey** instead.

Journey showed 0% complete and “where you are on the path.” Not what I needed tonight. I felt a small spike of “have I left something unfinished?” Then I noticed **Dashboard** still in the top nav. One click back. Fine.

### Mistake 2 — reading the wrong pages

From there I opened **Revision** by accident (I was hunting for “continue studying”). It said there was no revision focus yet, and — this mattered — **“Keep going with today’s session”** plus **Return Home**. That was the first time the app actively steered me back after a wrong turn. I liked that.

I also opened **History / Analytics** on the student side. Empty trend, **Return Home**. Easy to leave. No damage done.

### Mistake 3 — leave a session unexpectedly

I went back to the Learning Workspace Dashboard → Resume → into the real study session for CS1-A. Topic name, checklist, timer, **Pause Study Session**, **Finish Study Session**, **Back to Today’s Study Session**.

Mid-session I did what I actually do when Slack pings: I left. Clicked into **Analytics**, then **Settings**. No warning. No “are you sure?” The session did not scream at me. For a second I worried I had abandoned it and broken something.

I returned to the **Dashboard**. Still **In Progress**. Still the same CS1-A topic. Still **Resume Study Session**, with copy telling me the next step was to resume and finish when done. That settled me.

I also tried **Pause** inside the session. It flipped to **Paused** and the button became **Resume Study Session**. That felt deliberate and safe — like the product expects real life to interrupt you.

**Back to Today’s Study Session** took me out of the live session to the briefing without making me feel I had wiped the evening.

### Mistake 4 — the other Home

I also opened the other **Dashboard / Home** (student experience). It still offered **Start Session** for a **30-minute** mission — not “Resume,” even though the Learning Workspace side clearly knew a session was **In Progress** for **90 min** on the same day’s topic.

I clicked **Start Session** there once. I landed on a thinner **Session Overview** — “your topic,” “Core methods,” “No activities listed,” about 30 minutes. That did not look like the CS1-A session I had just left. I felt a proper flutter of anxiety: *Did I start a second session? Did I lose the first one?*

I abandoned that overview without finishing any reflection steps and went back to the Learning Workspace Dashboard. My CS1-A session was still **In Progress** with **Resume**. Relief — but I had to *choose the right home* to feel that relief.

### Mistake 5 — reopen as if I closed the laptop

I treated a fresh open like closing the tab and coming back. I landed on the Learning Workspace Dashboard again: same topic, **In Progress**, **Resume Study Session**. If I always return there, I would know where I left off.

### Wrong URLs

I also typed a nonsense URL and followed a staff-looking `/console/` path out of curiosity. **404** and **403** both said clearly what happened and offered **Return to Dashboard** / Help. No panic. Clear exits.

I did not invent bugs. I only noticed what appeared when I behaved imperfectly.

---

## Answers

### 1. When I made a mistake, did I know how to recover?

**Usually yes — on the Learning Workspace path.**

Wrong Journey / Revision / History clicks: nav and **Return Home** got me out. Leaving a session for Analytics or Settings: Dashboard **Resume Study Session** found it again. Pause was obvious. 404/403 had a clear return.

**Not always** when I recovered via the other Home. **Start Session** there did not read like recovery of my in-progress CS1-A work. That is where I hesitated.

### 2. Did I ever feel lost?

**Yes, briefly — twice.**

1. After clicking into the student Home while a Learning Workspace session was already in progress: **Start** vs **Resume**, **30** vs **90**, two “what next” surfaces.  
2. After **Start Session** from that Home opened a thinner overview that did not look like my CS1-A briefing.

I was never permanently stuck. I was unsure which door was the real one.

### 3. Did I know where my study session had gone?

**Yes, once I returned to the Learning Workspace Dashboard or Session.**

**In Progress** + named topic + **Resume Study Session** made the session feel parked, not deleted. Pause labelled **Paused**. That matched what I need when I leave a desk mid-hour.

On the student Home, I did **not** get the same “your session is waiting” language. That made the session feel harder to locate if I landed there first.

### 4. Was it obvious how to continue studying?

**Obvious on one path. Ambiguous across two.**

Learning Workspace: **Resume Study Session** is exactly the sentence I want after a mistake.  
Student Home: **Start Session** sounds like beginning again, not continuing. I would continue studying confidently only if I trained myself to open the Learning Workspace Dashboard every evening.

### 5. Did anything make me anxious?

Yes.

- Two homes disagreeing about Start vs Resume and 30 vs 90 minutes.  
- The thinner Session Overview after Home → Start Session — it felt like a different session, and I am the sort of person who will overthink that.  
- Leaving mid-session with no soft acknowledgement (even though the session survived — the silence made me check twice).  
- Not having a clear place called Reflection in the nav if I skip the post-session recording and later wonder whether I “failed” to close the loop.

Pause, Resume, and branded 404/403 *reduced* anxiety. The dual continue-paths *created* it.

### 6. Which part of the application was the easiest to recover from?

**Learning Workspace Dashboard → Today’s Study Session → Resume Study Session.**

Also: Revision’s “Keep going with today’s session,” and the 404/403 **Return to Dashboard** links. Those felt designed for imperfect humans.

### 7. Which part required unnecessary effort?

**Reconciling the two homes after a wrong click.**

I should not need a mental map of “which Dashboard remembers my session.” Once I had wandered into Journey / Revision / student Home, finding the authoritative continue button took more thought than an anxious evening study block should require.

Starting the thinner Session Overview from Home, then abandoning it to find the real CS1-A resume, was extra work I should not have needed.

### 8. If I accidentally closed the app, would I be confident returning to the correct place?

**Mostly yes — if I reopen to the Learning Workspace Dashboard.**

Tonight that surface still held **In Progress** and **Resume** for CS1-A. That is the behaviour I need across months of weeknight study.

I would be **less** confident if my browser restored the other Home, or if I habitually used **Start Session** there. I would worry I was starting fresh instead of continuing.

### 9. Did the application forgive ordinary human mistakes?

**Yes on the main study path. Partially overall.**

Forgiving:

- Wrong nav items with easy exits  
- Leaving a session for other pages without wiping it  
- Pause / Resume  
- Back to Today’s Study Session  
- Clear recovery from 404/403  

Less forgiving (or less clear):

- Offering **Start** on one home while the other knows you are **In Progress**  
- A second session-shaped flow that does not look like the first  

Ordinary mistakes were not punished. Some recoveries still asked me to be careful — and careful is already my default stress setting.

### 10. Would I trust it during months of study?

**Yes, with a condition.**

I would trust it for months **if I always continue from the Learning Workspace Dashboard / Session resume path**. That path forgives leaving, pausing, and coming back the next evening. That is exactly what nine weeks of CS1 after work looks like.

I would **not** fully trust the product as a whole until there is one obvious “continue studying” answer after any wrong turn. Dual homes and dual start buttons would keep me second-guessing — and I lose confidence when an application becomes confusing.

**Why:** My biggest belief is that when I make a mistake, I want to know how to recover. Kwalitec often shows me. On the best path, recovery is named out loud (**Resume**, **In Progress**, **Paused**). On the weaker path, recovery is something I have to invent. For a careful first-sitting student, that difference is the difference between a calm tool and a slightly worrying one.

---

## Bottom line

Kwalitec mostly forgives imperfect behaviour — if you stay on the Learning Workspace study path.

I left a session, clicked the wrong nav, opened the wrong pages, paused, and came back. My CS1-A work was still waiting with **Resume**. That is the resilience I need with nine weeks to go and a brain that panics when software feels ambiguous.

What still costs me trust is not a crash. It is two doors labelled as the way forward, only one of which clearly says I can continue. When I am tired after work, I need one recovery sentence — not a choice between **Resume Study Session** and **Start Session**.
