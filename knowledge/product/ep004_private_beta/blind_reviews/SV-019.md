## How I used it tonight

Signed in with the coordinator credentials. Skipped through Alpha orientation.

Confirmed I could aim a plan at **IFoA CM1** (Supported). For this session I stayed on the Learning Workspace path: **Dashboard → Session → Start/Resume → Finish → Practice Outcome Capture**. That felt like the real study route. I glanced at the other Home (Today’s Mission / Coach / Readiness) once, then came back — I only have one evening and I need the fuller briefing.

**Dashboard**  
Told me today’s topic and that I should resume the study session. Progress was framed as syllabus coverage, with an explicit warning that completing topics is **not** estimated knowledge. That line landed. First sitting I finished chapters and still blanked in the paper. Estimated Knowledge needed practice results first — fair.

**Session briefing**  
Topic, time estimate, “Why you are studying this,” success checklist, recommended activities: read, examples, practice, review mistakes. The activity list matches how I already use ActEd notes and a question bank. The “why” and “success” copy felt more about finishing the session correctly than about what I should be able to **do in the exam** after tonight.

**Active session**  
Timer on. Checklist. Session plan included reading syllabus notes, attempting a set number of practice questions, and marking weak areas. I studied from my own CM1 materials and questions. Kwalitec did not supply content. It directed and timed the block. I ticked as I went.

**Practice Outcome Capture**  
Attempted / correct / optional time and notes. Clear statement that this is practice recording, not Estimated Knowledge. I entered honest numbers. This was the first moment all evening that felt like it might eventually connect study to capability — if the product actually uses those numbers to change what I do next.

**After**  
Session recorded. I opened Journey (exam readiness path — still thin / early). Revision said no revision focus yet. Coach on the other Home repeated “highest-value next step” and “strengthen your exam readiness” without showing why this beat other CM1 topics for marks. Closed the laptop.

Walked away and asked: *If the exam were tomorrow, what did tonight actually improve?*

---

## Answers

### 1. Did today’s work feel connected to exam performance?

**Partially.**

Connected: the topic sat on the official syllabus path, and the session pushed me into practice questions and reviewing mistakes — the parts of study that historically move marks for me.

Disconnected: nothing framed tonight as exam performance. Success was “spend focused time,” “work through activities,” “finish and record results.” That is how you complete a study block. It is not how you know you can score on a CM1 question under time pressure. Four weeks out, that gap matters.

### 2. Did Kwalitec help me understand why today’s study mattered?

**Only at a shallow level.**

I understood *that* this was the next syllabus topic and *that* Coach/Home language claims it strengthens exam readiness. I did not understand *why this topic tonight maximises marks* versus another weak CM1 area I already know I fail in the paper.

“Why you are studying this” read like workflow instruction (resume, finish, record), not educational rationale (exam weight, prior weak practice, sitting timeline). For a resit, I already know I must study. I need to know why *this* study is the highest-yield use of a Tuesday night.

### 3. Did the session strengthen exam technique, knowledge, or both?

**Mostly knowledge-process. Technique only lightly, and only because I brought it.**

Knowledge: the structure nudged read → examples → practice → review mistakes. That supports building topic knowledge if I do the work properly with my materials.

Exam technique: weak from the product itself. No timed conditions, no paper-style multi-part questions, no mark allocation, no “write as the examiner expects.” The prompt to attempt practice questions and review mistakes is the seed of technique — but Kwalitec did not coach technique. I either already know how to practise for CM1, or I do not. The app does not close that gap.

### 4. Which feature made the strongest connection to exam success?

**Practice Outcome Capture — attempted / correct after the session.**

It is the only place that forces me to confront whether tonight produced usable performance, not just time on task. Combined with the Dashboard line that completing a topic is not understanding, it is the clearest signal that Kwalitec *intends* to separate activity from capability.

If those numbers actually steer tomorrow’s mission toward weak areas, this becomes the product’s exam-relevant spine. Tonight I only saw the input side.

### 5. Which feature felt disconnected from real examination performance?

**Journey and Revision in their current state — and Coach wording without evidence.**

Journey talks about the path to exam readiness but gave me almost nothing actionable four weeks out. Revision said there was no revision focus and told me to keep going with today’s session. Coach said “highest-value” and “exam readiness” in language that sounds mark-relevant but did not show working.

Also: defining “success today” as finishing the session and recording results. That is product success, not examination success.

### 6. Did I leave the session believing I was more likely to score higher?

**Slightly more organised. Not clearly more likely to score higher.**

Why:

I left knowing I covered a named topic and logged practice honesty. That is better than a vague “I revised CM1 tonight.”

I did **not** leave with proof that I can convert that topic into marks tomorrow. No before/after on exam-style questions. No link from my wrong answers to a next action that targets the mark-losing pattern. Confidence in *process* went up a notch. Confidence in *score* barely moved.

### 7. Did Kwalitec distinguish between completing study and improving exam capability?

**Yes — in words. Incomplete — in the session experience.**

The Dashboard is unusually honest: syllabus progress is not estimated knowledge; estimated knowledge needs recorded practice; completing a topic alone is not understanding. Practice Outcome Capture repeats that it is not Estimated Knowledge.

But the live session still rewards completion behaviours: tick activities, hit Finish, record numbers. Success criteria do not ask “can you attempt an exam-standard question on this topic?” So the distinction is stated. It is not yet the thing the session is designed around.

### 8. If my goal were simply to maximise marks, would I follow Kwalitec’s guidance?

**Partly — for sequencing. Not as my sole director.**

Why:

I would follow it for *what topic to open tonight* when I am tired and otherwise waste twenty minutes deciding. Syllabus order beats panic-jumping.

I would **not** hand it full control of mark maximisation four weeks from a resit. I still need past papers, timed mocks, and deliberate attack on known weak question types. Kwalitec does not currently feel like it owns that layer. If guidance ever conflicted with “do a timed CM1 paper on Friday,” the paper wins.

### 9. Would I recommend Kwalitec to another resit student specifically because it improves exam preparation?

**Not yet — not on that specific claim.**

I might mention it to another resit student as a way to stop dithering and keep a practice log. I would not say: “Use this, it improves how you prepare for the exam” in the sense of technique and mark conversion.

For resitters, the selling point has to be exam impact. Tonight the product sold study direction. That is related. It is not the same thing.

### 10. What one change would most strengthen the link between Kwalitec and examination success?

**Redefine every session’s success criteria around exam capability, and close the loop from practice results into the next mission.**

Concretely, after tonight I want:

1. Success stated as: “You should be able to attempt exam-style questions on [topic] and know where you lose marks.”  
2. Practice Outcome Capture (and wrong-answer notes) visibly changing tomorrow’s guidance — e.g. revise the failing skill, or schedule a timed set on that topic.  
3. “Why this session” written in marks language: sitting proximity, syllabus weight, or my recorded weakness — not resume/finish instructions.

Until the product shows that yesterday’s wrong answers bought today’s higher-yield work, the exam link stays promised rather than proven.

## Central verdict

**Does Kwalitec convincingly connect everyday study to improved exam performance?**

**Not yet.**

It connects everyday study to a sensible syllabus next step, and it forces a practice confession at the end. It even says the right thing: finishing topics is not understanding.

What it does not yet do — and what I need four weeks from a CM1 resit — is prove that today’s block made me more able to score. Until “why today” and “what success means” are framed in examination terms, and until practice results visibly steer what comes next, Kwalitec remains a disciplined study OS, not yet a marks machine.
