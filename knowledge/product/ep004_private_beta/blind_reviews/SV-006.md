## Situation I brought into the app

It is exactly four weeks before CM1. Second attempt. I know how I study when it matters: past papers under timed conditions, selective CMP when I blank on a method, no multi-month roadmap theatre.

I do not care how the product works. I care whether it raises my chance of passing in the time I have left.

One question the whole evening: *If I only have four weeks left, is Kwalitec still worth adopting?*

---

## How I used it tonight

Signed in with coordinator credentials. No fuss — tech is fine.

### Learning Workspace Dashboard (default after login)

Landed here. Today’s Study Session named a concrete topic and a big **Resume / Start** button. That part is clear.

Then the rest of the page fought my situation:

- Progress at **0%** syllabus coverage with a long remaining-topic count
- Exam card showing a distant sitting and **~200 days remaining**
- Pace labelled **Comfortable** with a large hours surplus

That is not my life. I am four weeks out and late. “Comfortable pace” is the wrong product for me tonight. I treated it as sample-plan data on the beta account, but as a first impression it already answered my question: this surface is built for a long runway, not a final-month crunch.

### Student Home (the other home)

Opened Home via the student nav. Subtitle: “What you should do next, and why.”

Today’s Mission: **30 minutes**, no topic name on the card, “highest-value next step… based on your recent learning evidence.”

Readiness: not enough history.  
Journey: empty.  
Coach: same highest-value / learning-evidence / strengthen readiness language.

So within five minutes I had **two homes**, a mission with no topic name, and a claim of “evidence” while every evidence panel was empty. Impatient student’s verdict: stop selling, show me the triage list.

### Mission / Session path (Learning Workspace)

Fuller briefing. Named topic. **90 min** (not 30). Activity checklist: read, examples, practice questions, review mistakes. Session plan even said attempt practice questions and mark weak areas.

That is the first screen that felt like something I might actually use — as a shell around materials I already own. Still: it is syllabus-order “Current Learning Topic” energy, not “these three past-paper weak spots will cost you marks in four weeks.”

### Home → Start Session (Session Overview)

Thin. “your topic.” Topics: **Core methods.** Activities: **No activities listed.** About 30 minutes. I would not study from this. Clicked out.

### Revision

Subtitle promises “highest-value revision for today.” Content: **No revision focus yet. Keep going with today’s session.**

With four weeks left, Revision is exactly where I needed value. Empty. Told to go back to the learning session. That is a miss for someone in final-month mode.

### Journey / Analytics / Readiness

Journey at 0%. Analytics: less than a minute studied, readiness trend unavailable. Fair for day one — and therefore useless for deciding what to do *tonight*. Readiness did not help me prioritise. It told me to wait until I have history I do not have time to build from scratch.

### Study Plan

Glanced. I do not want a long study plan four weeks out. Creating or nurturing one is not how I pass CM1. Closed it.

## Answers

### 1. Would I invest time learning this app with only four weeks remaining?

**Barely — one evening to test, not a real adoption.**

I am fast with software. The cost is not “learning buttons.” The cost is two homes, conflicting session lengths, empty Revision/Readiness, and a default pace that assumes months. Every minute spent reconciling the app is a minute not spent on a past paper.

If after one evening it is still pointing me at syllabus foundations and “comfortable” runway language, I stop. Tonight that is where I landed.

### 2. Did today’s mission feel urgent enough?

**No.**

Home’s 30-minute mission felt light, not urgent — and it did not even name the topic. The Learning Workspace session felt like a normal study block (90 min, checklist), not a final-four-weeks triage.

Nothing said: *you are short on time; here is the highest-yield cut.* “Comfortable pace” and empty Revision actively undercut urgency.

### 3. Did the Coach help me prioritise what matters most?

**No.**

Coach restated the mission in longer words. No weak-topic ranking. No past-paper focus. No “skip X, hammer Y.” For a second sitting with four weeks left, prioritisation is the whole game. Coach did not play it.

### 4. Did Readiness help me decide what to study next?

**No.**

“Not enough history” is honest. It is also a dead end when I need a decision *now*. I already decide from past-paper mistakes and known weak chapters. Readiness added nothing tonight and asked me to wait for data I will not have time to accumulate before the sitting.

### 5. Would I abandon my current revision strategy for this?

**No.**

My strategy is past papers, timed practice, selective CMP. Kwalitec’s useful bit (session checklist + “attempt practice questions”) can sit *around* that strategy. It does not replace it. Syllabus-order Learning Mode plus empty Revision is not a better plan for week −4.

### 6. Which screen wasted the most time?

**Two-way tie, then a close third.**

1. **Having two homes** — Dashboard vs Student Home. Impatient people should not have to pick which “what next” is real.  
2. **Session Overview** (Home → Start Session) — “Core methods,” “your topic,” no activities. Pure delay.  
3. **Revision** — promised highest-value revision, delivered nothing, sent me home.

Also wasted mental time: **30 min vs 90 min** for the same day. I will not trust a time coach that disagrees with itself.

### 7. Which screen saved the most time?

**Active Study Session (Learning Workspace path)** — once I got there.

Named topic. Timer. Tick-list. Explicit “attempt practice questions” and mark weak areas. That is a usable wrapper: open app, do the block with my papers/notes, finish, record. Minimal ceremony compared to browsing Journey / Study Plan / empty Revision.

Dashboard’s single big **Start / Resume** button is second — when it is the path I follow and I ignore everything else on the page.

### 8. Did anything make me feel more likely to pass?

**Slightly, narrowly — only the practice-shaped session shell.**

Feeling more likely to pass, for me, means: less time deciding, more timed questions, clearer weak spots. The session checklist nudges toward practice and reviewing mistakes. That helps if I already know the topic is worth tonight’s hours.

Nothing else raised pass probability: not Coach, not Readiness, not Journey at 0%, not “comfortable pace,” not an empty Revision tab. Those either wasted time or pointed at the wrong time horizon.

### 9. Would I still be using Kwalitec on exam day minus one?

**No.**

Exam day minus one is past papers, formula sheet, sleep. Not onboarding panels, not 0% journey, not coaching copy. If Kwalitec had become a ruthless daily triage of weak topics from recorded practice, maybe I would open it for thirty seconds to see “do this paper set.” Tonight it is not that product. Day −1 I would not open it.

### 10. If a colleague asked me tomorrow whether to install Kwalitec with only four weeks remaining, what would I say?

**Don’t. Not this late.**

If they are earlier in the season and want someone to remove “what should I study tonight?” friction along a syllabus plan, maybe worth a look — CM1 is supported, session flow is usable.

With four weeks left and a past-paper strategy already in place: the install and orientation cost is too high for the urgency support on offer. Stick to papers. Do not spend a weekend learning a second home and waiting for Readiness to invent a trend.

## Bottom line

The last four weeks matter more than the previous four months. Kwalitec tonight behaves like a long-season planner that has not switched into crunch mode: syllabus next-topic, comfortable runway, empty Revision, Coach without a priority list.

I will not abandon past papers for that. I might steal the session checklist as a thirty-to-ninety-minute shell if the topic is already one I chose. I would not invest serious adoption time with four weeks remaining, and I would tell a colleague in the same boat to skip the install and open a paper instead.
