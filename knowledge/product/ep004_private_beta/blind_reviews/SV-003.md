## How I used it

Signed in with coordinator credentials. No public registration — fine.

**Sign in**  
Claim on the left: “Know exactly what to study next.” That is the only claim I care about. The rest — decision fatigue, sustainable pace — is marketing for people who do not already run a spreadsheet. I do.

**Dashboard**  
Landed on Student Dashboard. Today’s topic: *Review CS1-A: Descriptive statistics foundations* for **IFoA CS1**. I am sitting **CS2** in twelve weeks. Already a problem.

I checked Study Plan Wizard. Explicit: Version 1 supports **CS1, CM1, CB2**. Other papers are **Coming Soon**. So for my actual exam, Kwalitec cannot create a usable plan. I continued on the seeded CS1 account as a proxy for “how the product behaves when it has a syllabus,” not as something I can use for CS2.

Dashboard also said Progress 0%, Estimated Knowledge unavailable until practice is recorded, exam date Feb 2027 / 200 days / Comfortable Pace. That is not my sitting. Recommendation: “Study 1.1 The Purpose And Function Of Data Analysis” marked High / New Topic. That looks like syllabus order, not a diagnosis of my weaknesses.

**Home (Student Experience)**  
Second “home.” Today’s Mission: **30 minutes**. Copy: “highest-value next step for this topic based on your recent learning evidence.” I have essentially no evidence yet. That sentence is asserting a conclusion without showing the working.

Readiness: not enough history. Journey panel: empty story. Coach: same paragraph as the mission, restated. Milestones: none.

**Start Session from Home**  
This opened Session Overview. It worked. Briefing was thin: “Complete today’s learning session,” duration ~30 minutes, topics “Core methods,” **No activities listed**, flash saying “Session started: **your topic**.” That is not intelligence. That is a template with missing fields.

**Learning Workspace Session**  
Went to Session from the sidebar. Fuller briefing: CS1-A descriptive statistics, **90 min** estimated, why this session, success criteria, recommended activities (read / examples / practice / review mistakes). Same day, Home said 30 minutes and Session said 90. I would not trust a system that cannot agree with itself on duration.

Active session: timer, checklist, session plan (read notes, attempt 8 questions, mark weak areas). Honest about prompts vs scored practice. Still requires my CMP and question bank. Practice outcome capture is basically logging attempted/correct into a form — which I already do in Excel, with more columns and better filters.

**Journey**  
0% complete. Explore your journey. Tells me nothing I cannot see on a blank progress bar.

**Revision / History / Analytics**  
Revision: nothing recommended. History: no completed sessions. Analytics is careful that readiness depends on **self-recorded** practice, not verified exam performance. Good caveat. Still: until I feed it data I already track elsewhere, it is empty.

**Profile**  
Current Examination: **Not set**. Dashboard and Study Plan clearly show IFoA CS1. That is enough for me to distrust the data model on day one.

**Study Plan**  
Syllabus roadmap with section weights and estimated hours is the most useful surface I saw — a structured view of the paper. Learning outcomes often “Not available yet.” For CS1 this would be a nicer syllabus browser than my notes index. For **CS2 it does not exist.**

## Answers

### 1. Did Kwalitec teach me something my own study system does not?

No.

It did not teach CS2 content. It did not surface a weakness I had missed. It did not compare my practice accuracy to syllabus weighting in a way my spreadsheet does not already approximate. The activity checklist is standard study hygiene I already use. The only “new” thing was product framing — not educational information.

### 2. Is the Mission selection obviously intelligent?

No.

On the seeded account it pointed at the first CS1 topic / early syllabus item. That is curriculum sequencing, not intelligence. Coach and mission both claim “highest-value next step based on recent learning evidence” while readiness admits there is not enough history. Those two statements cannot both be true. Intelligent would mean: here is the evidence, here are the alternatives considered, here is why this beats topic X. I did not get that.

### 3. Would I trust the recommendations?

Not yet. Probably not for a while.

I do not trust recommendations that (a) are for the wrong paper for my sitting, (b) disagree on session length across screens, (c) use evidence language without evidence, and (d) show Profile examination as unset while Dashboard shows a paper. Trust requires reproducible rationale. I got slogans.

### 4. Did the Coach provide genuinely useful guidance?

No.

Coach insight restated the mission line. No comparison across topics. No citation of my practice outcomes (there were none, which should have made the coach say “insufficient data” instead of sounding certain). Useful coaching for me would look like: “Your last three practice sets on survival models were 55% while the paper weights that section at Y% — prioritise that over flashcards tonight.” This was not that.

### 5. Did Journey provide information I didn't already know?

No.

0% and an empty story. My spreadsheet already shows coverage by syllabus section. Journey did not add a new signal.

### 6. Did Readiness feel evidence-based?

Partially in *intent*, not in *substance* today.

Analytics correctly warns that estimates come from recorded practice, not verified exam scores. That honesty helps. But Readiness on Home is empty, and the product still talks about readiness/exam strengthening before it has data. Evidence-based means show the inputs. Empty + confident copy is the opposite.

### 7. Is this application replacing anything I already use?

No.

- Does not replace **CMP** / notes  
- Does not replace **Anki** / flashcards  
- Does not replace **past papers**  
- Does not replace **Excel** / my spreadsheet for tracking  
- At best it is a session orchestrator and syllabus roadmap *for supported papers*

For CS2 it replaces nothing because it cannot plan my paper.

### 8. What feature would I stop using first?

**Coach** — then the thin Session Overview path.

Coach adds words without information. Session Overview with “your topic” / no activities is strictly worse than the Learning Workspace Session briefing. I would also ignore Journey until it shows topic-level evidence I cannot get from my sheet.

### 9. What feature would become part of my daily routine?

Possibly none for CS2.

Hypothetically, if CS2 were fully supported and recommendations showed working: **Dashboard → today’s topic + Learning Workspace Session checklist** as a forced single next action, and **practice outcome logging** only if it exported cleanly into something I can reconcile with Excel. Daily utility requires (1) my paper, (2) trustworthy topic choice, (3) no contradictory durations. None of those are satisfied today for me.

### 10. If my CS2 exam were twelve weeks away, would Kwalitec improve my probability of passing? Why?

**No.**

Reasons, ranked:

1. **CS2 is not supported.** Coming Soon is not a study tool. Twelve weeks out I need a system that covers *my* syllabus.
2. My pass probability is driven by **past-paper volume, marking discipline, Anki retention, and targeted weak-topic drilling**. Kwalitec does not provide questions, notes, or marking. It asks me to bring those myself and then logs a thin session wrapper.
3. Recommendation quality is not demonstrated. Claiming “highest-value” without evidence would *risk* displacing better decisions I already make from my spreadsheet.
4. Dual homes, 30 vs 90 minutes, Profile exam “Not set” — I would spend cognitive budget verifying the app instead of studying.

Could it help a weaker student who freezes on “what to open tonight”? Possibly. That is not my failure mode. Kwalitec did not earn a permanent place in my workflow.

## Bottom line

Kwalitec’s job is to know what I should study next better than I do. Tonight it could not plan CS2, could not show its working, and could not stay consistent across its own screens. My system stays. The app does not get a seat at the table until it supports my paper and justifies every recommendation with evidence I can audit.
