## Answers

### 1. What role would Kwalitec play in my study routine?

**Evening session director**, not my textbook.

After work I do not want to reopen the CMP contents page and argue with myself about whether to push descriptive stats or jump to distributions. Kwalitec’s job in my week would be: open app → accept today’s CS1 topic → study that topic from materials I already own → log practice → close laptop.

It would sit beside CMP and past papers, not instead of them. Roughly 4–5 weeknights: 60–90 minutes directed by the Session briefing. Weekends for longer past-paper blocks I still plan myself.

### 2. Which feature would make me return tomorrow?

**Today’s Study Session** on the Learning Workspace path — the named CS1 topic, estimated time, “why you are studying this,” success criteria, and the recommended activities checklist.

That is the only surface that consistently answered “what do I do tonight?” without making me think.

### 3. Which feature would I probably ignore?

**Revision** and most of **Analytics / History** for the first stretch — empty or near-empty until I have volume, and I will not invent a ritual around blank panels.

I would also ignore the thinner **Home → Start Session / Session Overview** path once I noticed the 30 vs 90 mismatch and the generic “Core methods” copy. Same for repeated Product Check-in / Alpha feedback forms after the first couple of nights — useful for them, not for my pass mark.

### 4. What is the strongest educational reason to keep using Kwalitec?

It turns a long CS1 syllabus into **one defensible next topic** and a short study ritual that matches how I already learn (read → examples → questions → mistakes), then asks me to **record practice** so progress is not just vibes.

With 12 weeks and a full-time job, removing the nightly planning tax is real educational value — more hours on statistics, fewer hours on meta-planning.

### 5. What is the strongest educational reason to stop using it?

**I cannot fully trust the “highest-value next step” claim** when the product disagrees with itself about how long tonight is (30 vs 90) and when Coach insight does not show its working.

For a first sitting, if the guide feels arbitrary, I will stop opening it and go back to my own spreadsheet. Trust is the product here. Content I already have elsewhere.

### 6. Would I recommend it to another CS1 student today?

**Yes, carefully — to someone who already has CMP (or equivalent) and struggles with “what tonight?”**

I would say: use Dashboard → Session as the daily spine; treat Home/Coach/Journey as experimental; do not expect notes or an in-app question bank; keep past papers as the real exam rehearsal.

I would **not** recommend it today as a complete study system for someone starting from zero materials.

### 7. What would I still rely on outside Kwalitec?

- ActEd **CMP** / Core Reading and my own notes  
- **Past papers** and examiners’ reports  
- **Excel / R** practice for CS1 methods  
- A simple personal tracker for weak topics the app has not earned trust on yet  
- Office study leave / peer discussions when I am stuck on a concept

Kwalitec does not teach the material. It sequences and accounts for study.

### 8. Did Kwalitec make me believe it understands how people learn?

**Partly.**

The session design shows it understands actuarial study behaviour: one topic, focused time, practice, review mistakes, then capture results. The study-plan roadmap mirrors how the syllabus is actually weighted.

What it does **not** yet convince me of: that it understands a tired graduate consultant who needs **one** authoritative path and a coach that can justify the pick. Dual homes and a vague Coach undercut that belief.

### 9. If I had to choose only one study companion alongside the official IFoA material, would I choose Kwalitec?

**No.**

Alongside official IFoA material I still need a companion that carries teaching depth and exam-style practice volume. That is still CMP / tuition / past-paper discipline for me — not a planner that points at topics and asks me to self-report scores.

Kwalitec is a strong *second* layer. It is not my one companion.

### 10. Would I personally commit to using Kwalitec until my exam?

**Yes — as my daily “what next” layer, not as my primary study system.**

Honestly: with 12 weeks, I will not risk replacing CMP and papers. I *will* commit to opening Kwalitec most study nights, following the Learning Workspace Session topic, studying from my usual materials, and logging practice. If the duration conflict and Coach vagueness do not improve, I will keep using it narrowly (Session only) and ignore the rest. If Session ever feels random or unreliable, I will drop it without guilt.

That is still a commitment. It is just a bounded one — which is how I treat any tool this close to a first sitting.

## Bottom line

Kwalitec deserves a place in my preparation as the thing that ends the “what should I do tonight?” argument. It does not yet deserve to be the thing I trust blindly about *why* tonight’s topic won, or how long I should spend. For a working first-sitter, that is still enough to keep opening it — as long as I stay on the Session path that actually briefed CS1-A properly.

I would commit to using Kwalitec until my exam as my nightly study director beside CMP and past papers, not as my only study companion.
