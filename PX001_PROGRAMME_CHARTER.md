## 1. Mission

After Version 1 educational completion is certified, elevate the student-facing product so that a diligent CS1 candidate experiences Kwalitec as calm, coherent, motivating, accessible, and unmistakably premium — without changing what is recommended, what is educational law, or how educational content is produced.

Premium is applied to **finished educational truth**, not to unfinished coverage or experimental guidance.

---

## 2. Why this programme exists now as planning only

Educational dependence must precede experience polish.

| Priority (EF-001 stack) | Role relative to PX-001 |
|-------------------------|-------------------------|
| Educational Volume production (EP-001) | **Must finish first** — PX-001 must not divert capacity or reopen production law |
| Founder study / Private Beta evidence | Informs experience priorities; does not authorise early polish claims |
| Evidence-driven content / execution | Remains content/ops under frozen law — not PX redesign of Excellence |
| Premium Experience (this programme) | Begins **only after** §4 gates |

PX-000 exists so that when those gates close, implementation can start immediately from a frozen plan — without improvising scope against Educational Production.

## 5. In scope

Once started, PX-001 may design and implement experience craft in these focus areas (detail in companions):

1. Student emotional journey  
2. Visual polish  
3. Premium interaction design  
4. Motivation systems (non-coercive; no pass promises; no gamification that undermines calm professionalism)  
5. Accessibility  
6. Performance (perceived and measured student-path latency)  
7. Microcopy  
8. Personalisation (presentation and pacing of *already-authorised* guidance — not new selection logic)  
9. Celebration moments  
10. Long-term engagement  

Surfaces in scope: student-facing shells already in the sole-runtime student experience (auth, onboarding, home, mission/session, reflection, plan, journey/history, help, settings, errors/empty/success/loading states, mobile).

## 8. Principles

1. **Education first** — polish never substitutes for unpublished content.  
2. **Calm professionalism** — serious exam preparation tone; celebration without carnival.  
3. **One composition** — reduce competing panels; one next action.  
4. **Protect proven patterns** — Daily Mission clarity, Commitment/Defer agency, Study Session Feedback explainability remain reference standards unless evidence shows harm.  
5. **Consistency over novelty** — prefer extending house tokens, components, and microcopy standards.  
6. **Accessibility and performance are quality, not afterthoughts.**  
7. **Personalisation presents authorised truth** — does not invent new educational decisions.  
8. **Motivation supports diligence** — never coerces, shames, or promises pass outcomes.  
9. **Evidence before premium claims** — score against `PREMIUM_QUALITY_ATTRIBUTES.md`; do not declare “premium product” from intent alone.  
10. **Smallest effective craft** — prefer local template/CSS/copy fixes over architectural rewrites.
