## 3. Longitudinal trajectories

### 3.1 Mission clarity (Q1 / D1)

**Stable high (9 across all eight days).**  
Every Mission opened with a unique display title, tutor intent, task list, and success criteria that agreed with Session and Reading Guidance. No “Today’s topic” placeholder shell appeared inside the walked inventory. Cold start (J1) and Volume entry (J5) were both immediately actionable.

**Trajectory:** → (hold). Clarity did not degrade with familiarity.

### 3.2 CMP craft (Q2 / D3)

**Stable high (9).**  
House habit formed by Day 2: sketch / retrieve → selective open → hard stop → closed-book return. Revision days correctly refused new chapter opens. Bleed risks (PCA on Alpha exploratory days; continuous on discrete day; 2.1.3 on continuous/Revision) were named and refused.

**Trajectory:** ↑ early (Day 1→2 selectivity), then →. CMP became *easier* to use under Kwalitec direction than under solo chapter tourism.

### 3.3 Decision fatigue (Q3 / D2)

**High, slight dip on densest day (Day 6 = 8).**  
Kwalitec removed evening planning (“what / why / how far”) on every day. Day 6’s six discrete families increased *cognitive* load without returning *decision* load to the student — still clearer than choosing a CMP section alone.

**Trajectory:** → with one watch peak. No dual-home or competing-clock fatigue inside inventory.

### 3.4 Workload appropriateness (Q4 / D5)

**Good; Day 6 is the Pilot Arc peak (7).**  
Duration bands 40–75 min held. Day 6 finished inside band but felt heavy. Day 7 recovered. Revision days felt restorative rather than guilt-padded.

**Trajectory:** flat → peak (J6) → recover. Sustainable for a two-week Pilot Arc; flag for future Volumes with longer LO lists (observation, not redesign request).

### 3.5 Stopping integrity (Q5 / D4)

**Stable high (9).**  
Every Learning day had an explicit stop condition matching wrap-up claims. No invented extra chapters. No mid-fog abandon. Study Progress language refused Topic Complete / mastery theatre.

**Trajectory:** →. Stopping is a trust asset of this inventory.
