Orientation

Your educational journey

Study Sensei is how Kwalitec guides your daily learning decisions. Kwalitec is the product; Study Sensei is your educational mentor for what to focus on, why it matters, and what comes next.

  1. Mission — what deserves your attention today.
  2. Study Session — the practice workflow for that Mission.
  3. Reflection — a short pause that closes practice or calibrates guidance (see the Reflection map below).
  4. Decision Journal — Study Sensei’s durable educational memory of significant guidance and your choices.
  5. Educational Timeline — the chronological learning story drawn from the Journal — not a second memory store.
  6. History — practice archives and progress stats as context; meaning lives in Journal and Timeline.
  7. Study Sensei — the mentor voice across Mission, Session, Journal, Timeline, and guidance that History only bridges to.

Educational memory

One educational memory system

The Decision Journal is Study Sensei’s durable educational memory of significant guidance, your choices, and what followed. The Educational Timeline is the chronological learning story drawn from that Journal — not a second memory store and not a scoreboard. History keeps practice archives and progress stats as context; educational meaning lives in the Journal and Timeline. After a Reflection, Session notes stay with the Session; optional Sensei reflection deepens Journal memory that Timeline can later interpret.

Reflection

One reflection family

Reflection after a Session closes practice. Commitment reflection on Home confirms you finished what you chose. Optional reflection in the Decision Journal helps the Study Sensei learn whether guidance was useful. The Educational Timeline asks deeper questions about your learning story. Product Check-in is feedback for the product team — not educational reflection.

  • Session reflection — closes practice after a Session; optional note; does not score you.
  • Commitment reflection — on Home after you finish what you chose.
  • Sensei reflection — optional questions in the Decision Journal about whether guidance helped.
  • Timeline reflection — deeper questions on the Educational Timeline about your learning story.
  • Guided Reflection preview — Home prompts to think through only; nothing is recorded.
  • Not reflection: Product Check-in (product research), Calibration (declared coverage), Revision (review practice).

Glossary

Educational glossary

Mission
Today’s authorised educational focus — what deserves attention now.
Study Session
The practice workflow you start for a Mission. A Session is not the Mission itself.
Reflection
A family of short pauses for judgement — after practice, on commitment, in the Journal, or on the Timeline. Never Product Check-in.
Decision Journal
Study Sensei’s durable educational memory — significant guidance, your choices, outcomes, and optional Sensei reflection.
Educational Timeline
Your chronological educational record — a learning story from the Journal, not from scores and not a duplicate of History.
History
Practice archives and progress context (Sessions, study time, readiness trends). Numbers orient; meaning lives in Journal and Timeline.
Study Sensei
Your educational mentor inside Kwalitec for focus, reasons, memory, and next steps.
Product Check-in
Optional product experience feedback for the team. It does not teach, remember learning, or change guidance.
Calibration
Declared coverage for a starting position — not mastery and not reflection.
Revision
Review practice on the syllabus — distinct from today’s Mission focus.
