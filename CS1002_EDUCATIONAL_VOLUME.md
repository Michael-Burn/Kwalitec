## 3. Why this Volume (selection)

Campaign Beta is the **successor Educational Volume** after CS1-001. It is not defined by “Week 2.” It is defined by the next coherent professional transformation after Alpha:

> From *exploratory judgement with deferred PCA* → to *PCA placed honestly and a lawful opening of the distributional spine*.

| Selection test | Decision |
|----------------|----------|
| Closes Alpha named handoff (1.2.3) | Yes — CB-D1 |
| Opens Chapter 2 / 2.1 lawfully | Yes — CB-D2 (2.1.1) → CB-D3 (2.1.2) |
| Contiguous Pilot Arc (≥ 3 Learning + Revision) | Yes — three Learning + one Revision |
| Contaminant-free | Yes — only 1.2.3 and 2.1.1–2.1.2 |
| Distinct from CS1-003 mid-spine absorption | Yes — opening continuation, not 4.1→4.2→5.1 |
| Meets Alpha floor | Yes — see Certification + reference-bar worksheet |

**Why not absorb all of 2.1 (through 2.1.6)?**  
Probability/quantile evaluation and generation methods are distinct cognitive loads. Forcing them into Day 3 would either overload continuous placement or create thin days without Revision spacing. **2.1.3–2.1.6 are explicitly out of scope** and named as successor handoff.

**Why include Alpha association on Revision?**  
Series continuity: CS1-001 terminal honesty required a live return target so opening-spine memory is not orphaned when Chapter 2 begins.

### Campaign Purpose

This Campaign exists so the candidate can move from Alpha’s completed exploratory chain to a professional stance: reduce dimensionality with PCA without inventing causes, then recognise basic discrete and continuous univariate distribution families by situation — arriving at that arc under one Study Sensei, with one Revision return before deeper 2.1 calculation work.

### Campaign Educational Objective

**Assessable journey outcome:**  
By Campaign completion, the candidate can **retrieve and connect** (1) PCA purpose with one non-claim, (2) a discrete situation→family match, (3) a continuous placement with Normal refusal, and (4) one Alpha association measure choice with one non-claim — a competence no single day alone guarantees.

**Does not claim:** Topic Complete for all of 2.1; Estimated Mastery of CS1-B; readiness for exam sitting; EA-007 spine PASS; 4.2 absorption.
