## 5. Surface-by-surface longitudinal findings

### Morning Mission

Unique display titles and Tutor Intents every day. Prior bridges name yesterday’s skill, not only yesterday’s topic code. Cold start (J1) is honest (“no prior Campaign day”). Volume start (J5) is series-honest (“Alpha deferred PCA”). **No day rejected.**

### Study Guidance / Session

Session educational + tutor purposes execute the Mission; duration budgets match Mission estimates; wrap-ups use Study Progress / Session complete language only. Confidence prompts demand a warrant. **No day rejected.**

### CMP Reading Guidance

Learning days: lead line, focus questions, misconception watch, open point, stop condition, out-of-scope, annotation task, exit/return/reentry. Revision days: retrieval-first (lawful — no new chapter open). Stop conditions advance the locus and refuse silent bleed (PCA on Alpha D2/D3; continuous on Beta D2; 2.1.3 on Beta D3/R1). **CMP usage becomes easier** because the student learns the house habit: sketch → selective CMP → closed-book return.

### Knowledge Checks

Every Learning day: Active Recall + Checkpoint. Revision: multi-target return checks (Alpha ×3; Beta ×4 including series hinge). Items are situation/judgement, not documentation dumps. **No day rejected.**

### Reflection

Unique sticky-point stems; harvest wobble, not chapter summary. Alpha D3 and Beta D3 Reflections were previously defect-cycled for uniqueness — residual holds in catalogue. **No day rejected.**

### Session Closure

Wrap-ups name what was practised, what is *not* claimed (Topic Complete / PCA / 2.1.3+), and set honest mode. Stopping points are clear. **No day rejected.**

### Tomorrow Bridge

Skill-named continuity lines + optional light prep (headings only). Reciprocal with next day’s `prior_bridge` on all internal boundaries. **Mild finding CF-01** at Alpha R1 terminal (see Continuity Findings) — Beta D1 corrects student expectation on Volume open.

### Revision

Both Revision days are assessable return, not “review yesterday” theatre. Beta R1 retains Alpha association hinge — series memory, not Volume reboot. **No day rejected.**

### Weekly rhythm

Each Volume = 3 Learning + 1 Revision ≈ one study week. Pattern repeats Alpha → Beta without collapsing into stamp pedagogy (substance hinges advance). Sustainable for months **if** successor Volumes keep this cadence — not yet proven beyond J8.
