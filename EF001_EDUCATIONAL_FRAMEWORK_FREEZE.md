## 1. Freeze declaration

From this point onward the **Educational Framework** is treated as **Version 1 Educational Law**.

The Educational Excellence Framework is **no longer under active design**.

It is now under **operational stewardship**.

**Goal shift:**

| Former goal | Version 1 goal |
|-------------|----------------|
| Invent a better framework | Consistently produce educational guidance that helps students use the CMP in the best possible way and maximises their probability of passing professional examinations |

---

## 2. Conditions for new Educational Framework programmes

No further Educational Framework programmes may be created unless **all** of the following hold:

1. A genuine educational failure is observed through **Founder Validation** or **Private Beta**.
2. The failure **cannot** be explained or corrected using the existing framework.
3. Evidence demonstrates a deficiency in the **framework itself** rather than educational content or execution.

Absent those three conditions, proposed EA / EO / TV / EJ / EW successor design programmes are **out of order**. Correct content, execution, volume, reachability, or tooling instead.

---

## 3. What freeze permits and forbids

| Permitted | Forbidden under freeze |
|-----------|------------------------|
| Educational Volume production under EO lifecycle | New Educational Framework design programmes without §2 conditions |
| Founder study using the released product | Redesign of Excellence, Operations, Trust, Justification, or Editorial Workspace law texts |
| Private Beta preparation and evidence collection | Lowering Gate MG/LE/SS/TP, EJ hard rules, joint publication, or Trust criteria to raise throughput |
| Evidence-driven improvements to **content** and **execution** | Treating preference, affinity, or convenience as Educational Trust |
| Educational author tooling that reduces author cognitive load **without** lowering quality (implements EW-001; does not amend frozen exits) | Inventing competing educational law outside EA/EO/TV/EJ/EW |
| Errata and editions per EO-001 | Runtime / SCI / Twin / recommendation redesign labelled as “education framework work” |
| Activation engineering and publication Approver path | Claiming Version 1 production-ready from framework completeness alone |

**One-line law:** Framework creation is complete. The house now publishes, studies, and improves under law already established.

---

## 4. Engineering priority stack (post-freeze)

Engineering effort shall prioritise, in order:

1. **Educational Volume production**
2. **Founder study** using the released product
3. **Private Beta preparation**
4. **Evidence-driven improvements**
5. **Educational author tooling** where it reduces author cognitive load without lowering quality

Framework invention is not on this stack.

---

## 5. Stewardship rules

1. Consume frozen law; do not rewrite it in production or tooling programmes.  
2. Failures first attributed to content, staffing, activation, reachability, or execution discipline.  
3. Framework amendment requires §2 evidence package + Founder Educational Gate Owner capacity decision.  
4. EW-001 remains a productivity shell under Excellence / Operations — software implementation may proceed; Gate/EJ/Trust exits stay hard.  
5. Living ops board remains `PR001_EDUCATIONAL_OPERATIONS_REGISTER.md` (Volumes, blockers, roster) — not a design studio.  
6. Every educational observation must be reviewed with `EF001_OPERATIONAL_REVIEW_TEMPLATE.md` **before** any solution is proposed (classification, severity, evidence, smallest effective intervention, EF-001 check).
