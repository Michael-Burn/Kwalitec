### Lessons learned for student value

1. **Session Philosophy without a Blueprint still leaves “tutor hour” subjective** — authors need stage-level architecture and interruption law, not only Gate SS checklists.  
2. **Silence is teaching** — EV-001 empty shells and future chatbot nags are opposite failures; Reading Guidance must design exit and re-entry.  
3. **Rhythm is the product feel** — guide → CMP → return → reflect → tomorrow is what makes Kwalitec feel like a tutor rather than software.  
4. **Joint Mission + Session certification remains non-negotiable** — a beautiful brief over a broken Session (or vice versa) must not publish.  
5. **Numeric publication threshold prevents taste-test drift** — ≥8.0 with dimension floors stops borderline shells shipping as “good enough.”
