# EA-008 — Campaign Publication Policy

**Programme:** Educational Excellence Programme EA-008 — Educational Campaign Architecture  
**Status:** Binding — Campaign publication policy  
**Effective:** 2026-08-01  
**Parent:** `EA008_EDUCATIONAL_CAMPAIGN_ARCHITECTURE.md` · `EA008_CAMPAIGN_CERTIFICATION.md`  
**Related:** `EA002_PUBLICATION_WORKFLOW.md` · EA-006 Publication Report · EA-007 Campaign Review  
**Nature:** Process law — not educational content, not application code  

---

## 1. Purpose

Define how certified Campaigns move into student exposure — and how Kwalitec **prevents isolated premium (“Golden Day”) publication** from being treated as educational maturity.

### Governing distinction (extended)

| Gate family | Question answered |
|-------------|-------------------|
| Technical / lifecycle publish | Is the artefact schema-valid? |
| Educational package publication (EA-002) | May students rely on this **day**? |
| **Campaign publication (this document)** | May students rely on this **journey** — and may packages publish only as Campaign members? |

---

## 2. Publication primacy principle

> **Educational Packages may only be published for commercial student-reachable Learning Mode pathways when they belong to a certified Educational Campaign.**

Corollaries:

1. The **Campaign** is the primary publication unit.  
2. Mission bundles remain the atomic **day** inventory inside a Campaign.  
3. Isolated “Golden Day” publication is **prohibited** as a commercial primary-study release after EA-008 effective date.  
4. Package PASS without Campaign membership is insufficient for new commercial publication approvals.

This policy does **not** erase EA-002. It **constrains** when EA-002 Publication Approval may be granted for new Learning Mode packages.

---

## 3. Why this policy exists (EA-007 lesson)

EA-006 APPROVED a single certified package (CS1 4.2). EA-007 found:

- Orphan excellence raises expectations the product then betrays.  
- Trust declines by Day 4 under templates; Day 14 relapse after a Golden day is especially damaging.  
- Large-scale publication must not proceed under a continuity PASS claim.

**Policy intent:** Make contiguous journeys the only honest path to student reliance claims.

## 4. Publication units (revised)

| Unit | What publishes together | Primary? |
|------|-------------------------|----------|
| **Educational Campaign** | Ordered certified packages + Revision placements per dossier | **Yes — primary** |
| **Campaign Arc (Pilot Arc)** | Minimum ≥ 3 contiguous Learning packages + Revision Strategy minimum | Yes — minimum commercial unit |
| **Mission bundle** | Mission + Session + Reading Guidance + Checks + Reflection + Tomorrow | Atomic day inside Campaign |
| **Revision bundle** | Revision Session/activity as mapped in Campaign | Member of Campaign |
| **Subject sample / full package** | EA-002 subject aggregates | Must compose from certified Campaigns |

**Rule:** Do not Publication-Approve an orphan Mission bundle for commercial Learning Mode after EA-008 effective date.

## 8. Preconditions to enter Campaign Publication

Before Campaign Publication Approval may be requested:

1. Gate CG **PASS** for the named Campaign version (`EA008_CAMPAIGN_CERTIFICATION.md`)  
2. EA-002 preconditions satisfied for **every** Mission bundle in inventory  
3. Continuity Index and bridge integrity recorded  
4. EV-001 / EA-007 regression checklists complete  
5. Inventory lists only certified members (no template placeholders)  
6. Explicit statement: technical publish ≠ educational Campaign PASS  
7. Explicit statement: isolated Golden Day pattern denied

## 9. Campaign Publication workflow

```text
GATE CG PASS
        ↓
CAMPAIGN PUBLICATION REQUEST
  - campaign_id + version + scope class
  - Full package inventory + certification refs
  - Continuity Index + bridge integrity
  - Revision Strategy evidence
  - EV-001 / EA-007 regression outcomes
        ↓
CAMPAIGN PUBLICATION APPROVAL (human)
  - Approver signs APPROVED / REJECTED / PARTIAL HOLD
  - Records reviewer IDs, dates, grandfather notes if any
        ↓
RELEASE TO STUDENT SURFACE
  - Only inventory in the approved Campaign version
  - Non-members remain unavailable or honest-blocked
        ↓
POST-PUBLISH VERIFICATION
  - Spot-check journey surfaces for One Educational Truth
  - Confirm no orphan excellence framing in student copy
        ↓
MAINTENANCE CYCLE
```

## 10. Approval outcomes

| Result | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **APPROVED** | Scoped Campaign inventory may reach commercial student pathways as a journey |
| **REJECTED** | Nothing new in the request publishes; defects returned |
| **PARTIAL HOLD** | Only explicitly listed PASS packages inside an otherwise PASS Campaign publish; HOLD members blocked with honesty |

PARTIAL HOLD **cannot** approve a single Golden Day as a substitute for Gate CG PASS.  
PARTIAL HOLD **cannot** drop Revision Strategy requirements by omission.

Staging and author training may still use Golden Packages (EA-005 posture). **Production student reliance** follows this policy.

---

## 12. Student exposure rules (Campaign)

1. **Journey beats spike.** Do not market or frame a single published day as Campaign excellence.  
2. **Unavailable beats unfit.** Missing neighbour packages → honest unavailable / recovery for the gap — never pretend continuity.  
3. **Version pinning.** Students see artefacts from an approved Campaign version.  
4. **No mixed draft leak.** Uncertified days inside a claimed span must not appear as premium.  
5. **Truth alignment.** Home / History / Journey / Revision project the same Campaign membership facts.  
6. **Mode honesty.** Revision days labelled as revision.

---

## 13. Maintenance and unpublish

### 13.1 Triggers

All EA-002 maintenance triggers apply, plus:

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Loss of reciprocal bridge after patch | Campaign HOLD until repaired |
| Member package unpublish | Campaign continuity re-audit; possible Campaign unpublish |
| Longitudinal dogfood FAIL (EA-007 method) | Scale claims withdrawn; Gate CG recertify |
| Attempt to add orphan premium day | Reject; require arc redesign |

### 13.2 Unpublish

Publication Approver may HOLD or unpublish a Campaign immediately if trust fails. Member packages may remain certified as packages while Campaign publication is withdrawn — students must not be told the journey still PASSes.

## 16. Closing rules

1. Campaign is the primary publication unit.  
2. Packages publish as Campaign members — not as isolated trophies.  
3. Isolated Golden Day commercial publication is prohibited after effective date.  
4. EA-006 4.2 remains a labelled pre-Campaign pilot until absorbed into a certified arc.  
5. Unpublish remains a feature of trust.

**Students deserve journeys they can finish and recommend — not single days that photograph well.**
