## How to use

1. Keep this checklist open beside the Editorial Workfile.  
2. Tick items **in stage order** (S1 → S10).  
3. Do not mark a stage complete until every box in that stage is ticked.  
4. At S9, re-scan the whole list before submitting for Educational Review.  
5. Law IDs in parentheses are **reference only** — you do not need to open those documents to tick boxes if the Workfile fields already answer the prompt.

**Print / duplicate once per Mission.** Record `mission_id`: _____________ · `author`: _____________ · `date`: _____________
