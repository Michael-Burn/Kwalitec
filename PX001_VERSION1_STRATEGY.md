## 3. Version 1 quality definition (experience)

A Version 1 premium student experience means:

1. **One next action** is always obvious.  
2. **Chrome tells the same story as the package** the student just studied.  
3. **Durations, verbs, and exam identity** are consistent across surfaces.  
4. **Mobile and keyboard** students are first-class.  
5. **Loading and errors** are crafted and dignified.  
6. **Motivation** reinforces diligence without shame or pass promises.  
7. **Identity** is student-grade — not Internal Alpha theatre.  

Measured by `PREMIUM_QUALITY_ATTRIBUTES.md`, not marketing aspiration.
