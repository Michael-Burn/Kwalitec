# TV-001 — Educational Trust Model

**Programme:** Trust Validation Programme TV-001 — Educational Trust Validation  
**Construct:** Educational Trust  
**Status:** Binding — validation philosophy for Founder Validation and successors  
**Effective:** 2026-08-01  
**Authority:** Educational Excellence Framework (Frozen) · Educational Operations (Frozen) · SV-001 PASS · FV-002 Evidence  
**Nature:** Validation philosophy only — **no** redesign of Excellence, Operations, Runtime A/C, SCI, Twin, or Recommendation Engine; **no** application code; **no** educational content; **no** new governance frameworks beyond this redefinition of validation philosophy  

---

## 1. Purpose

Redefine **Founder Validation** as **Educational Trust Validation**.

The objective is **not** to determine whether the Founder personally trusted Kwalitec.

The objective is to determine whether **Kwalitec’s educational decisions are objectively worthy of a student’s trust**.

---

## 2. Governing shift

| Former framing (risk) | TV-001 framing (required) |
|-----------------------|---------------------------|
| Did *I* (Founder) like today’s plan? | Would an educational professional defend today’s pathway? |
| Could *I* have designed a better study day? | Did *Kwalitec* exercise sound educational judgement? |
| Do *I* prefer Kwalitec to the CMP alone? | Are Kwalitec’s decisions *worthy* of ordinary-student trust? |
| Subjective companionship preference | Objective educational trustworthiness of judgements |

FV-002 correctly asked whether companionship rights were earned. TV-001 clarifies what “trust” must mean when that question is asked again:

> Trust is earned when Kwalitec consistently demonstrates educational judgement a serious candidate may rely on — not when the Founder feels affinity for the product.

---

## 3. Responsibility assignment

### 3.1 What the student is **not** expected to be

The student is **not** an educational expert. The ordinary CS1 candidate is not expected to:

- design optimal sequencing,
- calibrate nightly workload,
- invent CMP craft,
- time revision scientifically,
- justify stopping points,
- manage cognitive load under exam pressure,
- or calibrate their own confidence honestly without guidance.

### 3.2 What Kwalitec **owns**

Kwalitec carries professional responsibility for educational decisions that shape the study day:

| Domain | Kwalitec responsibility |
|--------|-------------------------|
| **Sequencing** | What comes next, and why that next step is lawful and educationally justified |
| **Pacing** | How far a day advances without false coverage theatre |
| **Workload** | Volume and intensity appropriate to a scarce study evening |
| **Revision timing** | When retrieval / consolidation is introduced vs deferred |
| **CMP guidance** | How the student opens, aims, and stops inside authoritative materials |
| **Cognitive load** | Whether the day’s design respects working-memory limits |
| **Motivation** | Whether the pathway sustains willing, honest return — without engagement theatre |
| **Confidence calibration** | Whether confidence rises only with evidence (Constitution honesty) |
| **Educational progression** | Whether days form a defensible learning pathway, not isolated checkboxes |

### 3.3 One-line law

> **The student studies. Kwalitec judges. Educational Trust Validation audits Kwalitec’s judgement — not the student’s expertise, and not the Founder’s alternative syllabus design.**

---

## 4. Definition — Educational Trust

### 4.1 Formal definition

**Educational Trust** is the justified reliance a non-expert student may place on Kwalitec’s educational decisions for a study day (and, longitudinally, for a pathway of days), grounded in the **quality of those decisions** rather than in subjective preference, brand affinity, or editorial inventory metrics.

Educational Trust is **earned** when Kwalitec’s judgements pass the Educational Trust Criteria (`TV001_VALIDATION_CRITERIA.md`).

Educational Trust is **not** earned by:

- Coverage %, DSH length, Approver seals, or certification gates alone (SV-001 separation remains binding),
- the Founder’s personal preference for Sensei voice,
- willingness-to-return driven by novelty or loyalty rather than educational quality,
- or the Founder concluding “I could not have planned better myself” as a vanity test.

### 4.2 Relationship to Daily Educational Value (SV-001)

| Construct | Owner programme | Asks | Whose perspective |
|-----------|-----------------|------|-------------------|
| **DEV (D1–D7)** | SV-001 | Was *today’s lived day* valuable for the student? | Student experience |
| **Educational Trust (T1–T7)** | TV-001 | Were *today’s educational decisions* professionally defensible? | Educational judgement audit |

They are complementary:

- **DEV** without Educational Trust can celebrate a pleasant day that was educationally indefensible.  
- **Educational Trust** without DEV can celebrate a tutor-correct day the student cannot sustain.  
- **Both** are required for companion-rights claims after FV-002.

SV-001 remains the primary student-value layer. TV-001 is the **trustworthiness of judgement** layer that Founder Validation must use when interpreting preference, return willingness, and companionship.

### 4.3 Unit of judgement

| Unit | Role |
|------|------|
| **Study day** | Primary — evaluate T1–T7 for that day’s decisions |
| **Pathway / run** | Aggregate — consistency of judgement across days |
| **Horizon tip** | Continuity honesty — trust includes refusing false next-day promises (FV-002 tip discipline preserved) |

## 5. Grounding in educational science (consumed, not redesigned)

TV-001 does **not** invent new educational law. It audits whether lived decisions honour frozen Excellence / Operations expectations already established, including:

- Guidance-Over-Content and CMP craft (EA-001 principles),
- Mission as tutor brief (EA-001 Mission Philosophy),
- Session structure and deliberate practice (Session philosophy / EA-004 lineage),
- Revision as retrieval, not re-read theatre,
- Stopping integrity and workload honesty (DX / Session delivery expectations),
- Anti–false-confidence (Educational Constitution; SV-001 HT gates).

The Founder does not re-author these frameworks during validation. The Founder asks whether **today’s pathway** would be defended by an educational professional operating under them.

## 6. What Educational Trust is not

| Not | Why |
|-----|-----|
| Founder’s personal liking | Preference ≠ professional defensibility |
| “I trust myself as planner” | Wrong evaluator — Kwalitec is the planner under test |
| Editorial inventory success | Lawful ≠ trustworthy judgement (SV-001) |
| Companion affinity alone | FV-002 companionship without educational-judgement audit is incomplete |
| Redesign brief | TV-001 forbids speculative Excellence / Runtime redesign during validation |
| Exam-pass guarantee | Vision north star — later; trust audits daily/path judgement |

## 7. Hierarchy update (validation philosophy)

| Layer | Owns | Role under TV-001 |
|-------|------|-------------------|
| **Educational Trust (TV-001)** | T1–T7 judgement audit | Whether Kwalitec’s decisions deserve student reliance |
| **Student Value (SV-001)** | DEV + SS / LT / HT | Whether the lived day was valuable and honest |
| Product Usefulness (P-001.1) | KSI | Composite usefulness — complementary |
| Delivery (DX-001) | Delivery Quality Index | Editorial expectation — not lived trust proof |
| Reliability (DSH-001) | Dependable Study Horizon | Dependable length — not judgement quality |
| Catalogue / Ops / Excellence | CE / EO / EA | Lawful inventory and authoring — frozen inputs |

**Forbidden claim:** “Founder felt good tonight ⇒ Educational Trust PASS.”  
**Forbidden claim:** “Founder would have sequenced differently ⇒ Educational Trust FAIL.”  
**Allowed claim:** “T1–T7 pass and DEV/HT hold ⇒ Educational Trust earned for the walked days.”

## 8. Treatment of Kwalitec

Treat Kwalitec as an **educational professional**.

The Founder is **not** evaluating whether they could have designed a better study plan.

The Founder is evaluating whether Kwalitec **consistently demonstrates educational judgement worthy of complete trust** throughout the student’s journey.

## Closing

Educational Trust is the justified reliance a non-expert student places on Kwalitec’s professional judgements — sequencing, pacing, workload, revision timing, CMP guidance, cognitive load, motivation, confidence calibration, and progression. Founder Validation measures that judgement. It does not measure whether the Founder is an expert tutor, nor whether the Founder personally likes the product.
