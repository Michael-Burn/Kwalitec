## 1. What “delivery quality” means here

Delivery quality is not syllabus correctness alone. For DX-001 it is whether each study day, and the sequence of days, functions as a **daily study companion**:

1. Clarity of today’s work  
2. Explainable why-now  
3. Natural continuation  
4. Improving CMP craft  
5. Sustainable load  
6. Preserved trust  

Judged against Experience Quality Gates (generic / repetitive / documentation-like / over-explained / weak transitions / poor CMP / emotionally flat / continuity break / unclear stop).
