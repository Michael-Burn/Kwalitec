---

## 1. Purpose

Define what the Founder **is** and **is not** when conducting Educational Trust Validation.

The Founder stands in for the **ordinary non-expert student** as the lived walker of days, while auditing **Kwalitec as the educational professional** responsible for judgement.

---

## 2. Role summary

| The Founder is… | The Founder is not… |
|-----------------|---------------------|
| A diligent CS1 candidate walking real study days | An educational redesign committee |
| An auditor of Kwalitec’s educational judgement (T1–T7) | A competitor tutor inventing a better plan each night |
| A recorder of lived DEV (SV-001) and trust criteria (TV-001) | A coverage / DSH / Approver scorekeeper for “value” |
| A classifier of defect vs preference (FV discipline) | An authoriser of speculative Runtime / Excellence redesign mid-validation |
| A guardian of honesty gates (HT / TF-08) | A soft-grader because the product “feels Sensei” |

---

## 3. Core duty

> Treat Kwalitec as an educational professional.  
> Do **not** evaluate whether you could have designed a better study plan.  
> Evaluate whether Kwalitec consistently demonstrates educational judgement **worthy of complete trust** throughout the student’s journey.

---

## 4. Responsibilities (assigned)

### 4.1 Before the run

1. Stamp **reachability** honestly (student-reachable vs Validation-mode inventory).  
2. Affirm the TV-001 honesty posture: judgement audit ≠ preference.  
3. Keep SV-001 scorecard and TV-001 T1–T7 instrument both available.  
4. Refuse to treat DSH / CE / Approver as Educational Trust proof.

### 4.2 Each study day

1. Study as a student would: open Mission, follow guidance, use CMP as directed, stop as authorised.  
2. After wrap, complete **SV-001** daily scorecard (D1–D7, SS, HT).  
3. After wrap, complete **TV-001** T1–T7 with one-line expert defence / residual.  
4. Tick TF / HF flags without soft-washing.  
5. Classify residuals: EDU-DEFECT / OBS / WATCH / OPS / PREF.

### 4.3 After the run

1. Separate **Educational Trust (ET)** results from **companion-rights** results (FV-002).  
2. Preserve scope labels — Validation-mode PASS is not ordinary-path PASS.  
3. Recommend only what evidence supports; do not invent redesign programmes from preference.  
4. Hand genuine EDU-DEFECTs to the appropriate frozen authority owners — without amending frozen frameworks inside the validation programme.

## 5. Explicit prohibitions

| ID | Prohibition |
|----|-------------|
| **FR-01** | Do not fail a day because “I would have sequenced differently” without an educational defect under T1–T7 |
| **FR-02** | Do not pass a day because “I personally trusted / liked Kwalitec tonight” without T1–T7 |
| **FR-03** | Do not use coverage %, DSH, Approver seals, or certification gates as Educational Trust scores |
| **FR-04** | Do not redesign Educational Excellence, Educational Operations, Runtime A/C, SCI, Twin, or Recommendation Engine during validation |
| **FR-05** | Do not author new educational content as a validation outcome |
| **FR-06** | Do not create new governance frameworks beyond TV-001’s redefinition of validation philosophy |
| **FR-07** | Do not conflate Founder expertise with student expertise — the student remains a non-expert |
| **FR-08** | Do not punish honest horizon cliffs that refuse false next-day promises |

## 6. Responsibility map (who owns educational decisions)

During validation, the Founder **observes** these decisions; Kwalitec **owns** them:

| Decision domain | Owner | Founder action |
|-----------------|-------|----------------|
| Sequencing | Kwalitec | Audit T1 |
| Pacing / workload | Kwalitec | Audit T2 |
| CMP guidance | Kwalitec | Audit T3 |
| Revision timing | Kwalitec | Audit T4 |
| Stopping point | Kwalitec | Audit T5 |
| Cognitive load / science-aligned design | Kwalitec | Audit T6 |
| Motivation / progression integrity | Kwalitec | Audit T6–T7 + DEV D6 |
| Confidence calibration | Kwalitec | Audit via HT + TF-08 + D7 |
| Alternative study-plan invention | **Not the Founder’s job in-role** | Record as PREF if tempted |

If the Founder invents a competing plan mid-sitting, that is a **behavioural observation** (SS-02), not proof that Kwalitec failed — unless T-criteria show the Mission was indefensible.

## 8. Daily posture checklist

Before scoring, the Founder confirms:

- [ ] I walked today as a non-expert student, not as product owner rewriting the syllabus.  
- [ ] I will score T1–T7 on Kwalitec’s judgement.  
- [ ] I will score D1–D7 on lived educational value.  
- [ ] I will separate PREF from EDU-DEFECT.  
- [ ] I will not convert tonight into a redesign ticket unless TF / HF fire.  

---

## 9. Success of the role (definition)

The Founder role succeeds when:

1. Educational Trust measures **Kwalitec’s educational judgement**, not the Founder’s expertise.  
2. Responsibility for educational decisions is **explicitly assigned to Kwalitec**.  
3. Student trust claims are grounded in **educational quality** (T + DEV/HT), not subjective preference.

---

## Closing

The Founder is the student’s proxy and the judgement auditor — never the competing tutor, never the preference oracle, never the redesign engine. Kwalitec carries the educational decisions. Educational Trust Validation asks whether those decisions deserve complete trust.
