**Programme:** Educational Production Enhancement EJ-001  
**Status:** Binding — how to author Educational Justifications  
**Effective:** 2026-08-01  
**Parent:** `EJ001_EDUCATIONAL_JUSTIFICATION_STANDARD.md` · `EJ001_MISSION_JUSTIFICATION_TEMPLATE.md`

**Nature:** Authoring process guidance — **not** content generation, **not** application code, **not** student-facing copy  

---

## 1. Purpose

Teach educational authors and reviewers how to produce Educational Justifications that make every Mission professionally defensible — without redesigning Excellence, Operations, Runtime, Twin, SCI, or recommendations.

These guidelines operationalise the Standard and Template. Prefer the Standard on conflicts of law; prefer the Template for field layout.

---

## 2. Authoring stance

Before filling J1–J10, answer privately (Head Tutor / Editorial Director stance):

1. If an IFoA-aware tutor challenged this Mission, could I defend **placement, load, CMP locus, revision timing, stop, and tomorrow** without inventing excuses?  
2. Which educational science moves did I **actually design** — not which I wish I had?  
3. What misconception am I hunting, and where does the Session force it into the open?  
4. What will I **refuse** to claim at wrap?

If you cannot answer (1), stop. You do not yet have a publishable Mission — polish will not create a rationale.

> **Write Justification to defend the Mission as authored.**  
> **Do not write Justification to decorate a weak Mission.**  
> If defence fails, revise the Mission (under EA-003), then rewrite Justification.

---

## 3. When Justification is required

| Event | Justification required? |
|-------|-------------------------|
| New authored Mission destined for publication | **Yes** — before Publication Approval |
| Mission edition bump that changes educational decisions | **Yes** — update and re-review |
| CMP edition bump affecting open/stop loci | **Yes** — revisit J3/J8 at minimum |
| Mode change (Learning ↔ Revision) | **Yes** — revisit J1/J4/J7/J10 |
| Cosmetic Tutor Voice polish only | Reviewer discretion; usually no full rewrite |
| Student-visible auto-generated chrome without authored Mission | Out of scope — do not invent Justification to launder empty shells |
| Draft exploration never submitted for review | Optional while drafting; mandatory before review submission |

## 4. Recommended authoring sequence

Align with EA-003 Mission authoring, then lock Justification:

```text
EA-003 Steps 1–7 (Intent → misconceptions → load)
      ↓
Draft student-facing prose (EA-003 Steps 8–10)
      ↓
Fill EJ Template J8 → J1 → J6 → J3 → J5 → J2 → J4 → J9 → J7 → J10
      ↓
Write Expert-defence paragraph (Template §D)
      ↓
Cross-check: Justification must not contradict Mission pack
      ↓
Submit Educational Review (EJ-R01–EJ-R13 + Gate MG path)
```

### Why this order

| Step | Reason |
|------|--------|
| **J8 first** | Ground identity in syllabus/CMP truth before narrative defence |
| **J1 / J6 next** | Pathway logic before craft details |
| **J3 / J5** | CMP craft and stop integrity (Guidance-Over-Content) |
| **J2 / J4** | Load and revision timing after scope is known |
| **J9 / J7 / J10** | Teaching design, science citations, honest outcome |

Authors may reorder if stuck — completeness at submission matters more than order.

## 5. Dimension-by-dimension guidance

### J1 — Topic placement

**Do:** Cite lawful Learning Mode order, prerequisite readiness, or disclosed Revision focus. Name what breaks if earlier or later.  
**Don’t:** Use “highest-value next step,” inventory convenience, or Founder preference as the sole reason.  
**Quality bar:** Another author reading only J1 could reconstruct *why this day, not adjacent days*.

### J2 — Workload

**Do:** Match load enum and minute band to Session stages and CMP span; list what was cut to protect the evening.  
**Don’t:** Claim “moderate / 45 minutes” for a chapter-sprawl Session; don’t romanticise grind.  
**Quality bar:** A scarce-evening candidate could finish without theatrical underload or chronic overload.

### J3 — CMP reading

**Do:** Defend open, stop, and out-of-scope; explain leverage (questions, extract, return).  
**Don’t:** Paste CMP paragraphs; don’t select a locus because it “looks important” without LO fit.  
**Quality bar:** Guidance multiplies CMP — it does not replace it (EP-01 / EP-04).

### J4 — Revision timing

**Do:** Explicit binary (or disclosed mixed boundary). If Learning: say why retrieval is not forced yet. If Revision: say what is being retrieved and why due.  
**Don’t:** Call re-reading “revision”; don’t schedule revision with nothing to retrieve.  
**Quality bar:** Passes TV-001 T4 spirit — timing is educational, not theatrical.

### J5 — Stopping point

**Do:** Tie stop to success criteria and tomorrow protection.  
**Don’t:** Stop mid-fog; don’t “just one more LO” beyond authorised stop.  
**Quality bar:** Wrap and authorised stop agree (TV-001 T5).

### J6 — Tomorrow continuity

**Do:** Name the skill bridge; be honest about cliffs / unassigned next.  
**Don’t:** Invent a fake next Mission; don’t reboot the story with no bridge.  
**Quality bar:** EP-02 continuity without false horizon promises.

### J7 — Educational science

**Do:** Cite EP IDs and science moves **enacted** in today’s design (deliberate practice, guided reading, retrieval, reflection, load awareness, exam focus, tutor voice, one truth).  
**Don’t:** Dump all ten EP IDs by habit; don’t claim Active Recall if the Session is read-only.  
**Quality bar:** Each cited principle maps to a concrete stage or demand.

### J8 — Syllabus & CMP support

**Do:** Table lawful codes and CMP sections with honest coverage claims (first-pass ≠ mastery).  
**Don’t:** Contaminant nodes; vague “covers chapter 4.”  
**Quality bar:** U1 identity + edition pin resolve.

### J9 — Misconceptions

**Do:** Name 1–3 misconceptions; state how Session forces them open; link Reflection/Checkpoint.  
**Don’t:** Generic “students get confused”; don’t list errors the day never addresses.  
**Quality bar:** Matches EA-003 `common_misconceptions` and Session design.

### J10 — Expected outcome

**Do:** Assessable closed-book outcome; evidence created; explicit non-claims.  
**Don’t:** Readiness ±%; mastery from one Session; vague “understand the topic.”  
**Quality bar:** Constitution VIII honesty — completion is not mastery.

## 6. Voice and register (Justification vs student)

| Surface | Register |
|---------|----------|
| Student Mission / Session | Tutor Voice (EA-002) — Study Sensei; concise coach |
| Educational Justification | Editorial / professional defence — precise, evidence-cited, audit-ready |

Justification may be denser and more technical than student copy. It must remain readable by Educational Reviewers and Founders acting as judgement auditors.

**Never** ship Justification language to the student as a substitute for Tutor Voice.

---

## 7. Subject flexibility — how to stay explainable

Subjects may emphasise different strategies:

| Strategy emphasis (examples) | Still required |
|------------------------------|----------------|
| Proof / derivation heavy | J2 load honesty; J5 stop before sprawl |
| Calculation / method drill | J7 retrieval and deliberate practice citations must be real |
| Early orientation chapters | J1 cold-start honesty; J10 modest non-claims |
| Late exam-craft chapters | J8 weight cues; J9 examiner-trap misconceptions |
| Revision-dominant days | J4 retrieval basis; J1 disclosed Revision focus |

**Rule:** Strategy variation is welcome; undocumented strategy is a publication block.

## 8. Anti-patterns (hard fail)

| ID | Anti-pattern | Fix |
|----|--------------|-----|
| AJ-01 | Empty or `TBD` J-fields at review | Complete before submit |
| AJ-02 | Identical Justification reused across Missions | Rewrite Mission-unique answers |
| AJ-03 | Justification contradicts Mission `why_now` / scope / mode | Align pack and Justification |
| AJ-04 | J7 cites EP catalogue without enactment | Delete unused IDs; bind to stages |
| AJ-05 | J3 defends a locus the Session never uses | Fix Session or Justification |
| AJ-06 | J4 calls re-read “revision” | Redesign mode/activity |
| AJ-07 | J10 claims mastery | Add non-claims; tighten success criteria |
| AJ-08 | Preference or inventory as J1 sole basis | Replace with educational sequencing defence |
| AJ-09 | Student-facing paste of entire Justification | Keep editorial; shorten Tutor Voice separately |
| AJ-10 | Justification written to pass review while Mission remains empty shell | Block joint publication; fix substance first |

## 9. Reviewer operating notes

Educational Reviewers should:

1. Read Mission pack and Justification **together**.  
2. Apply EJ-R01–EJ-R13 (`EJ001_EDUCATIONAL_JUSTIFICATION_STANDARD.md` §8).  
3. Spot-check TV-001 T1–T7 spirit using Justification as evidence — without treating EJ as a student walk substitute.  
4. Prefer HOLD with precise defect notes over soft PASS.  
5. Refuse publication when Expert-defence (§D) is generic affinity (“students will like this”).

Tutor Reviewers may sample Justification for Tutor Intent coherence but are not required to re-score all J fields unless Educational Review requests it.

---

## 10. Fit tests (author self-check)

Before marking status `complete`, answer **yes** to all:

1. Could I defend this Mission to an educational expert using only this Justification?  
2. Are J1–J10 Mission-unique and evidence-cited?  
3. Does Justification agree with the EA-003 pack?  
4. Is revision timing explicit and non-theatrical?  
5. Is the stopping point protective of learning and tomorrow?  
6. Are outcome claims honest (no mastery theatre)?  
7. Would I still defend this if coverage metrics were zeroed out?

Any **no** → revise before Educational Review.

---

## 11. Relationship to publishing operations

Under EO-001, Justification is part of the Volume / Mission dossier evidence for Educational Audit and Publication Approval. It does not replace EA-002 certification stages or Gate MG. Publication Approvers treat missing or `review_fail` Justification as a hard block equivalent to incomplete certification evidence.

---

## 12. Explicit non-goals

These guidelines do **not**:

- Redesign Excellence, Operations, Runtime A/C, SCI, Twin, or recommendations  
- Require application schema changes in this programme  
- Author live subject content  
- Mandate student display of Justification  
- Weaken frozen EA Mission Philosophy or Quality Gates  

---

## 13. Closing discipline

Every future Mission must be traceable to a documented educational rationale. Reviewers audit decisions against that rationale. Subjects may differ in strategy; none may be unexplainable.
