# Version 1 Product Principles

**Status:** Binding product principles for Version 1 student experience  
**Effective:** 2026-08-04  
**Authority:** Vision 2030 · EF-001 · Educational Content Freeze · `PX001_PROGRAMME_CHARTER.md` · `PREMIUM_QUALITY_ATTRIBUTES.md` · PX-007 Conditional PASS  
**Nature:** Experience and honesty law for Version 1 — does **not** replace Educational Framework, recommendation law, Twin authority, or Version 1 Release Framework gates  

---

## 1. Purpose

State the non-negotiable product principles that Version 1 must honour so “premium” and “ready” never outrun educational honesty.

## 2. Principles

### P1 — Curriculum first

Student guidance starts from official syllabus structure and certified educational packages. Experience craft never invents a second curriculum.

### P2 — One honest next action

At every ordinary sitting, the student should know what to do now. Secondary chrome must not compete with the authorised session.

### P3 — Emotional honesty over theatre

No pass-rate promises, fake XP, streak shame, or until-exam trust claims via UX alone. Progress cues map to real study evidence.

### P4 — Calm professionalism

Urgency is educationally warranted, never manufactured. Gap returns welcome without guilt. Near-exam tone is focused, not panic.

### P5 — Consistency is trust

Same concept → same name, duration, and verb across Home, Mission, Session, Plan, and Revision.

### P6 — Accessibility is first-class

Keyboard, assistive tech, and touch are part of the product — not a later retrofit. Do not claim WCAG levels without recorded audit.

### P7 — Perceived performance without lying

Loading states are crafted and honest. Controls do not look interactive before they work. Infra failures never masquerade as educational failure.

### P8 — Sole runtime truth

Premium claims apply only to the student experience students actually get. Dual-path chrome is retired or redirected — not marketed.

### P9 — Educational Framework stays frozen

EF-001 remains operational stewardship. New educational law requires genuine framework insufficiency evidence — not UX preference.

### P10 — Experience does not rewrite education

Presentation may clarify and calm. It must not change package bodies, selection ranking, Twin conclusions, or Runtime authority.

### P11 — Prefer under-claim

Conditional PASS with owned residuals beats inflated PASS. Version 1 **production-ready** requires P-002.1 gates (G1–G12) — not Premium Certification alone.

### P12 — Founder accountability

Material claim posture, residual waivers, and release declaration remain Founder / Product Board capacities under GP-001.
