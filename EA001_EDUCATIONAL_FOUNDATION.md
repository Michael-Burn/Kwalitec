## 1. Purpose of this foundation

This document establishes the permanent educational identity of Kwalitec for Version 1 onward.

It answers, before any mission, episode, or session is rewritten:

1. What Kwalitec is — and is not — as an educational system.  
2. What the CMP is — and is not — in that system.  
3. How syllabus, missions, episodes, sessions, revision, reflection, tomorrow preview, and the Student Digital Twin fit together.  
4. What every future educational artefact must serve.

**EA-001 is the teaching constitution.**  
It specialises *how teaching and guided study work* when Kwalitec and the CMP operate as one educational system.

It does **not** amend Vision 2030, the Product Blueprint, or the Educational Constitution. Where educational *truth*, mastery, evidence ownership, or decision-mode authority are concerned, the Educational Constitution remains controlling. Where product *why* and never-build rules are concerned, Vision 2030 remains controlling.

## 2. Fundamental principle

> **Kwalitec is NOT the textbook.**  
> **The CMP is NOT the tutor.**  
> **Together they form one educational system.**

| Authority | Owns |
|-----------|------|
| **CMP** (Curriculum Master Pack / approved course materials) | Authoritative exposition, worked theory, official instructional depth |
| **Syllabus** | Official structure, learning outcomes, examination scope and weights |
| **Kwalitec** | Educational guidance: what to study, why now, how to study deliberately, whether understanding is evidenced, what comes next |
| **Student Digital Twin** | Per-student educational state derived from evidence |

Kwalitec exists to **maximise the probability of passing the examination** through disciplined, efficient, and deliberate study — Vision north star expressed as daily highest-value next action (`PRODUCT_BLUEPRINT.md`).

## 4. Definitions — system identity

### 4.1 What Kwalitec is

Kwalitec is a **guided study operating system** for professional examination candidates.

It is:

1. An **educational intelligence platform** that answers, every day: what to study, why it matters, whether understanding is evidenced, and what to do next (Vision 2030 / Blueprint).  
2. An **evidence-driven study companion** — not a content warehouse.  
3. A **personalised decision-support system** whose guidance is explainable from curriculum position, Twin state, and evidence.  
4. The product surface of the Educational Operating System (EOS).  
5. The home of the **Study Sensei** educational mentor voice (Canonical Lexicon) — calm, specific, tutor-like guidance that never explains the platform to itself on primary study surfaces (`PRODUCT_EXPERIENCE_GUIDELINES.md`).

In educational practice terms, Kwalitec is the system that:

- sequences study against the official syllabus;  
- briefs the day’s Mission as a tutor would;  
- structures deliberate study sessions around CMP reading;  
- forces active recall, reflection, and continuity;  
- schedules revision from evidence, not comfort;  
- protects One Educational Truth across Home, Session, History, Revision, and Journey.

### 4.2 What Kwalitec is not

Kwalitec is **not**:

1. **The textbook** — it does not replace CMP exposition, chapter narrative, or publisher worked examples as primary teaching content.  
2. **A CMP dump or syllabus paste engine** — missions and episodes must never restate headings or paste raw CMP stems as if that were teaching (EV-001 TB-002, E1).  
3. **A black-box AI tutor** that invents syllabus order, mastery, or rankings without Twin/evidence warrant.  
4. **A question bank identity** — practice items may exist as instruments; they are not the product.  
5. **A habit tracker, gamification platform, note app, or social network** (Blueprint).  
6. **A second Educational Truth** — progress, readiness, and “what’s next” must not disagree across surfaces.  
7. **Documentation about itself** on student study surfaces — no platform philosophy, runtime names, or “mission ≠ mastery” essays substituting for tutor brief (Experience Guidelines; EV-001 Phase 8).

Those capabilities may appear as supporting tools. They are never Kwalitec’s educational identity.

### 4.3 The role of the CMP

The **CMP** (Curriculum Master Pack / approved course notes and materials for the subject) is the **authoritative content authority**.

| CMP provides | CMP does not provide |
|--------------|----------------------|
| Authoritative theory and definitions | Personalised “what to study today” |
| Worked examples and technical depth | Memory of this student’s evidence |
| Official instructional map into topics | Deliberate-study structure (before / during / after reading) |
| Publisher-quality exposition | Reflection, revision scheduling, tomorrow continuity |
| Source material students open while studying | Tutor voice, trust, or educational sequencing |

**Rule:** When a Learning Episode asks a student to read, the **material lives in the CMP** (or other authorised official materials). Kwalitec supplies **guided reading instructions**, focus questions, checkpoints, and recall — not a substitute textbook chapter.

**Rule:** CMP text must not be pasted wholesale into Kwalitec missions or episodes. Contaminants (addresses, boilerplate, publisher metadata) must never appear as study topics (EV-001 TB-003).

### 4.4 The role of the Syllabus

The **Syllabus** is the **official organising truth** of examination scope.

It provides:

- sections, topics, and learning outcomes;  
- examination weights and eligibility of content;  
- the lawful sequence spine for Learning Mode (Educational Constitution Article VI).

It does **not**:

- narrate a tutor’s daily brief;  
- substitute for CMP depth;  
- become mission copy by title-paste alone.

**Curriculum primacy:** Planning, missions, progress narration, and recommendations remain accountable to official syllabus structure. The Twin references syllabus entities; it never invents a parallel topic tree (`STUDENT_DIGITAL_TWIN.md`).

### 4.5 The role of Daily Missions

A **Mission** is the student’s authorised primary educational focus for a day (or study period): what deserves attention now, with one educational reason and one expected benefit (Canonical Lexicon).

A Mission:

- translates Twin + syllabus position + plan constraints into **one** commitment;  
- briefs like a tutor — bridge from prior learning, today’s purpose, success criterion, handoff to tomorrow;  
- is executed through a **Session** (practice workflow), not confused with it;  
- advances Study Progress when study of a unit is lawfully completed — never mastery by checkbox (Constitution Article VIII).

A Mission must **never** simply restate a syllabus heading. Full authoring law: `EA001_MISSION_PHILOSOPHY.md`.

### 4.6 The role of Learning Episodes

A **Learning Episode** is a bounded, teachable unit of guided study inside a Session — typically one stage such as Guided Reading, Worked Example, Practice, or Checkpoint.

A Learning Episode:

- has **one** clear learning objective;  
- tells the student exactly what to do with CMP (or authorised materials);  
- includes active cognitive work (focus questions, note-one-idea, recall, short check);  
- has meaningful success criteria;  
- never ships as an empty “read the material for Today’s topic” shell (EV-001 TB-001, TB-007).

Episodes **guide**; they do not republish the CMP.

### 4.7 The role of Session Overviews

A **Session Overview** answers one question: **Am I ready to begin?** (`PRODUCT_EXPERIENCE_GUIDELINES.md`).

It provides:

- topic identity (real title, never placeholder);  
- today’s learning objective and concept focus;  
- session structure and expected duration honesty;  
- brief educational context (why this now);  
- one primary action: begin the Session.

It does **not**:

- dump CMP chapters;  
- restack Home’s entire mission brief;  
- explain platform internals;  
- open if topic resolution failed — honest refusal beats placeholder theatre.

### 4.8 The role of Revision

**Revision** is deliberate reworking of previously studied material for retention and exam consolidation (Constitution Article IV §12; Version 2 principles P6 specialised for V1 practice).

Revision:

- is part of the educational journey, not a disconnected island;  
- is scheduled from evidence (decay, gaps, weight, time-to-exam) — not from empty states that deny prior study while Home claims 80% coverage (EV-001 TB-005);  
- is labelled as revision when it is not Current Learning Topic first-pass work;  
- never equates revision completion with first-pass mastery.

### 4.9 The role of Reflection

**Reflection** is the post-study act of naming what became clearer, what remains uncertain, and what to carry forward.

Reflection:

- is mandatory after every completed study Session (V2 P5 specialised as V1 educational law under EA-001);  
- produces soft Educational Evidence (Constitution rank D) — never fabricated by the system as if the student wrote it;  
- is topic-specific, not generic “what is clearer about Today’s topic”;  
- closes the Session educationally before Summary / Tomorrow Preview.

### 4.10 The role of Tomorrow Preview

**Tomorrow Preview** is the continuity bridge that answers: **What happens next?**

It:

- names the next lawful focus when known (topic + continuity line);  
- connects today’s close to tomorrow’s open (“you finish GLM link functions → next Bayesian foundations”);  
- never invents non-syllabus nodes or address-like contaminants;  
- never substitutes for today’s incomplete work.

Absence of a known next topic must be an honest empty state (Experience Guidelines §5) — not a fabricated preview.

### 4.11 The role of the Student Digital Twin

The **Student Digital Twin** is Kwalitec’s single authoritative representation of the learner’s exam-preparation state (`STUDENT_DIGITAL_TWIN.md`).

| Twin owns | Twin does not own |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Knowledge, memory, behaviour, readiness, goals, predictions (as estimates) | Syllabus structure |
| Evidence-backed learner beliefs | CMP exposition |
| Inputs to planning and recommendations | Student-facing “Twin” jargon (Constitution VII §5) |
| Continuity of educational history | Disposable Study Plan rows as identity |

Plans and Missions are **consequences** of intelligence. If the Twin is wrong, guidance is wrong even when the CMP is perfect.

**One Educational Truth:** Twin, Educational State, Evidence, and student surfaces must not present competing stories for the same moment.

```text
                    +-------------------------+
                    |   Official Syllabus     |
                    |  (structure & outcomes) |
                    +-----------+-------------+
                                |
              +-----------------+-----------------+
              v                 v                 v
     +----------------+ +--------------+ +------------------+
     |      CMP       | |   Kwalitec   | | Student Digital  |
     |   (content)    | |  (guidance)  | |      Twin        |
     +-------+--------+ +------+-------+ +--------+---------+
             |                 |                  |
             |    Guided reading / missions /     |
             |    episodes / reflection /         |
             +----> revision <--- evidence -------+
                                |
                                v
                     Higher exam pass probability
```

**Student daily loop (educational, not UI):**

1. Home / Mission — *What should I do now, and why?*  
2. Session Overview — *Am I ready to begin?*  
3. Before / During / After Reading (CMP + Kwalitec guidance) — *Deliberate study.*  
4. Reflection — *What stuck? What didn’t?*  
5. Tomorrow Preview — *What continues?*  
6. Twin / Evidence update — *State evolves honestly.*  
7. Revision when warranted — *Consolidate for the exam.*

## 6. Version 1 Educational Principles (product-level, inherited)

Vision 2030 states that students learn best when they:

- study consistently;  
- receive immediate feedback;  
- reflect regularly;  
- revise intelligently;  
- build confidence gradually;  
- understand mistakes.

EA-001 operationalises those behaviours into permanent teaching principles in `EA001_EDUCATIONAL_PRINCIPLES.md`. Every mission, episode, session, and revision activity must reinforce them.

---

## 7. Non-goals of EA-001

EA-001 does **not**:

- rewrite missions, episodes, or sessions;  
- generate educational content;  
- redesign screens;  
- change Twin algorithms, Runtime, or SCI;  
- declare EV-001 FAIL resolved;  
- claim Version 1 production-ready.

Those belong to successor programmes that must cite and pass EA-001 quality gates before student exposure.

## 8. Binding rule for successors

> Nothing educational reaches students unless it has a clear purpose under this Foundation, complies with EA-001 principles, and passes the relevant gate in `EA001_QUALITY_GATES.md`.

Placeholder shells, syllabus-title missions, empty reading stages, boilerplate explainability, and curriculum contaminants are **constitutional defects**, not acceptable MVP shortcuts.
